# Lottery

**Munshi Premchand**

jaldee se maaladaar ho jaane ki havas kise nahin hoti? una dinon jab laotaree ke tikat aaye, to mere dost, vikram ke pitaa, chachaa, ammaa, aur bhai,sabhi ne ek-ek tikat khareed liya. kaun jaane, kisakee takadeer jor kare? kisee ke naam aaye, rupayaa rahegaa to ghar mein hi. magar vikram ko sabr na hua. auron ke naam rupaye aayenge, phir use kaun poochhta hai? bahut hogaa, das-paanch hajaar use de denge. itane rupayon mein uska kya hogaa? uski zindagi mein bade-bade mansoobe the. pahale to use pooraa jagat ki yaatraa karanee thi, ek-ek kone ki. peeroo aur braajeel aur timbakatoo aur honolooloo, ye sab usake prograam mein the. vah aandhee ki tarah mahine-do-mahine
udakar laut aanevaalon mein na tha. vah ek-ek sthaan mein kaee-kaee din thaharakar wahan ke rahan-sahan, reeti-rivaaj aadi kaa adhyayan karn aur sansaar-yaatraa kaa ek vrihad granth likhna chahta tha. phir use ek bahut bada pustakaalay banavaanaa tha, jisamen duniyaa-bhar ki uttam rachanaaen jamaa ki jaayan. pustakaalay ke lie vah do laakh tak kharch karne ko taiyaar tha, bangalaa, kaar aur pharneechar to maamoolee baaten theen. pitaa yaa chachaa ke naam rupaye aaye, to paanch hajaar se zyada kaa daul nahin, ammaan ke naam aaye, to bees hajaar mil jaayange; lekin bhai sahab ke naam aa gaye, to usake haath dholaa bhi na lagegaa. vah aatmaabhimaanee tha. ghar vaalon se khairaat yaa puraskaar ke roop mein kuchh lene ki baat use beizzatee-see lagti thi. kahaa, kart tha bhai,kisee ke saamane haath
phailaane se to kisee gandhe mein doob maranaa achchha hai. jab aadamee apne lie sansaar mein koi sthaan na nikaal sake, to yahan se ravaanaa hona kar jaay? vah bahut bekaraar tha. ghar mein laotaree-tikat ke lie use kaun rupae
degaa aur vah maangegaa bhi to kaise? usane bahut soch-soch kar kahaa, kyon na ham-tum saajhe mein ek tikat le len? tajaveej mujhe bhi pasand aayee. main una dinon skool-maastar tha. bees rupaye milate the. usamen badi mushkil se gujar hoti thi. das rupaye kaa tikat khareedanaa mere lie suphed haathee khareedanaa tha. haan, ek mahina doodh, ghee, kuchh khaanaa aur oopar ke saare kharch todakar paanch rupaye ki gunjaaish nikal sakatee thi. phir bhi jee darataa tha. kaheen se koi balaaee rakam mil jaay, to kuchh himmat badhe.
vikram ne kahaa, 'kaho to apni angoothee bech daaloon? kah doongaa, ungalee se phisal padee.' angoothee das rupaye se kam ki na thi. usamen pooraa tikat aa sakataa tha; agar kuchh kharch kiye binaa hi tikat mein aadhaa-saajhaa hua jat hai, to kya buraa hai? sahasaa vikram phir bol, lekin bhaee, tumhen nakad dene padenge. main paanch rupaye nakad liye bagair saajhaa na karoongaa. ab mujhe auchity kaa dhyaan aa gaya. bol, nahin dost, yah buree baat hai, choree khul jaayegee, to sharmindaa hona padegaa aur tumhaare saath mujh par bhi daant padegee. aakhir yah tay hua ki puraanee kitaaben kisee sekend haind kitaabon ki dookaan par bech daalee jaayan aur us rupaye se tikat liya jaay. kitaabon
se zyada bejaroorat hamaare paas aur koi cheej na thi. ham donon saath hi maitrik paas hue the aur yah dekhakar ki jinhonne digriyaan leen, apni aankhen phodeen aur ghar ke rupaye barabaad kiye, vah bhi jootiyaan chatakaa rahe the, hamne
vaheen haalt kar diya. main skool-maastar ho gaya aur vikram mataragashtee karne lagaa? hamaaree puraanee pustaken ab deemakon ke sivaa hamaare kisee kaam ki na theen. hamse jitanaa chaatate banaa chaataa; unakaa satt nikaal liya. ab choohe chaaten yaa deemak, hamen paravaah na thi. aaj ham donon ne unhen koodekhaane se nikaalaa aur jhaad-ponchh kar ek bada-saa gatthar baandh. maastar tha, kisee bukaselar ki dookaan par kitaab bechte hue jhenpataa tha. mujhe sabhi pahachaanate the;isalie yah khidamat vikram ke supurd hui aur vah aadhaa ghante mein das rupaye kaa ek not liye uchhalataa-koodataa aa pahunchaa. maine use itanaa khush kabhi na dekh tha. kitaaben chaalees rupaye se kam ki na theen; par yah das rupaye us vakt hamen jaise pade hue mile. ab tikat mein aadhaa saajhaa hogaa. das laakh ki rakam milegee. paanch laakh mere hisse mein aayenge, paanch vikram ke. ham apne isi mein magan the.
maine santosh kaa bhaav dikhaakar kahaa, 'paanch laakh bhi kuchh kam nahin hote jee!' vikram itanaa santoshee na tha. bol, 'paanch laakh kya, hamaare lie to is vakt paanch sau bhi bahut hai bhai, magar zindagi kaa prograam to badalanaa pad gaya. meri yaatraavaalee skeem to tal nahin sakatee. haan, pustakaalay gaayab ho gaya. maine aapatti ki aakhir yaatraa mein tum do laakh se zyada to na kharch karoge?'
'jee nahin, uska bajat hai saadhe teen laakh kaa. saat varsh kaa prograamahai. pachaas hajaar rupaye saal hi to hue?'
'chaar hajaar mahina kaho. main samajhta hoon, do hajaar mein tum bade aaraam se raha sakate ho.'
vikram ne garm hokar kahaa, 'main shaan se rehna chahta hoon; bhikhaariyon ki tarah nahin.'
'do hajaar mein bhi tum shaan se raha sakate ho.'
'jab tak aap apne hisse mein se do laakh mujhe na de denge, pustakaalay na ban sakegaa.'
'koi jarooree nahin ki tumhara pustakaalay shahar mein bejod ho?'
'main to bejod hi banavaaoongaa.'
'isakaa tumhen akhtiyaar hai, lekin mere rupaye mein se tumhen kuchh na mil sakegaa. meri jarooraten dekho. tumhaare ghar mein kaaphee jaayadaad hai. tumhaare sir koi bojh nahin, mere sir to sari ghar-parivaar kaa bojh hai. do bahanon kaa vivaah
hai, do bhaaiyon ki shikshaa hai, nayaa makaan banavaanaa hai. maine to phaisalaa kar liya hai ki sab rupaye seedhe baink mein jamaa kar doongaa. unake sood se kaam chalaaoongaa. kuchh aisee sharten lagaa doongaa, ki mere baad bhi koi is rakam mein haath na lagaa sake.'
vikram ne sahaanubhooti ke bhaav se kahaa, 'haan, aisee dashaa mein tumse kuchh maanganaa anyaay hai. khair, main hi takaleeph uthaa loongaa; lekin baink ke sood ki dar to bahut gir gayi hai. hamne kaee bainkon mein sood ki dar dekhee, asthaayee kosh ki bhi; seving baink ki bhi. beshak dar bahut kam thi. do-dhaaee rupaye saikade byaaj par jamaa karn bekaar hai. kyon na len-den kaa kaarobaar shuru kiya jaay? vikram bhi abhi yaatraa par na jaayagaa. donon ke saajhe mein kothee chalegee,jab kuchh paisaa jamaa ho jaayagaa, tab vah yaatraa karegaa. len-den mein sood bhi achchha milegaa aur apna rob-daab bhi rahegaa. haan, jab tak achchhi jamaanat na ho, kisee ko rupayaa na dena chaahie; chaahe asaamee kitanaa hi maatabar kyon na ho. aur jamaanat par rupaye de hi kyon? jaayadaad rehan likhaakar rupaye denge. phir to
koi khatakaa na rahegaa. yah manjil bhi tay hui.
ab yah sawal uthaa ki tikat par kisakaa naam rahe. vikram ne apna naam rakhne ke lie bada aagrah kiya. agar uska naam na raha, to vah tikat hi na legaa! maine koi upaay na dekhakar manjoor kar liya aur binaa kisee likhaa-padhee ke, jisse aage chalakar mujhe badi pareshaanee hui! ek-ek karake intajaar ke din kaatane lage. bhor hote hi hamaaree aankhen kailendar par jaateen. mera makaan vikram ke makaan se milaa hua tha. skool jaane ke pahale aur skool se aane ke baad ham donon saath baithakar apne-apne mansoobe baandh karte aur is tarah saayan-saayan ki koi sun na le. ham apne tikat khareedane kaa rahasy chhipaaye rakhna chahte the. yah rahasy jab saty kaa roop dhaaran kar legaa, us vakt logon ko kitanaa hairaanee hogaa! us drishy kaa naatakeey khushi ham nahin chhodanaa chahte the. ek din baaton-baaton mein vivaah kaa jikr aa gaya. vikram ne daarshanik gambheerataa se kahaa, bhaee,shaadee-vaadee kaa janjaal to main nahin paalanaa chahta. bekaar ki phikr aur haay-haay. patnee ki naaj baradaaree mein hi bahut-se rupaye uda jaayange.
maine isakaa virodh kiya 'haan, yah to theek hai; lekin jab tak jeevan ke sukh-du:kha kaa koi saathee na ho; jeevan kaa khushi hi kya? main to vivaahit jeevan se itanaa virakt nahin hoon. haan, saathee aisaa chahta hoon jo ant tak saath rahe aur aisaa saathee patnee ke sivaa doosaraa nahin ho sakataa.'
vikram jaroorat se zyada tunukamijaajee se bol, 'Kair, apna-apna drishtikon hai. aapako beevee mubaarak aur kutton ki tarah usake peechhe-peechhe chalna tathaa bachchon ko sansaar ki sabase badi vibhooti aur eeshvar ki sabase badi dayaa samajhna mubaarak. bandaa to aajaad rahegaa, apne maje se chaahaa aur jab chaahaa uda gaye aur jab chaahaa ghar aa gaye. yah nahin ki har vakt ek chaukeedaar aapake sir par savaar ho. zara-see der hui ghar aane mein aur phauran jawab talab hua kahan the ab tak? aap kaheen baahar nikale aur phauran sawal hua kahan jaate ho? aur jo kaheen badakismatee se patneejee bhi saath ho gayeen, tab to doob marane ke sivaa aapake lie koi raastaa hi nahin raha jat. na bhaiyaa, mujhe aapase zara bhi sahaanubhooti nahin. bachche ko zara-saa jukaam hua aur aap betahaashaa daude chale jaa rahe hain homiyopaithik daaktar ke paas. zara umr khisakee aur launde manaane lage ki kab aap ravaanaa hona karen aur vah gulachharre udaayen. maukaa milaa to aapako jahar khilaa diya aur mashahoor kiya ki aapako
kaalaraa ho gaya tha. main is janjaal mein nahin padataa. '
kuntee aa gayi. vah vikram ki chhoti bahan thi, koi gyaarah saal ki. chhathe mein padhti thi aur baraabar phel hoti thi. badi chibillee, badi shokh. itane dhamaake se dvaar kholaa ki ham donon chaunkakar uth khade hue.
vikram ne bigadakar kahaa, 'too badi shaitaan hai kuntee, kisane tujhe bulaayaa yahan?'
kuntee ne khuphiyaa pulis ki tarah kamare mein najar daudaakar kahaa, 'tum log haradam yahan kivaad band kiye baithe kya baaten kiya karte ho? jab dekho,yaheen baithe ho. na kaheen ghoomane jaate ho, na tamaashaa dekhne; koi jaadoo-mantar jagaate honge!'
vikram ne uski garadan pakadakar hilaate hue kahaa, 'haan ek mantar jagaa rahe hain, jisamen tujhe aisaa doolhaa mile, jo roj ginakar paanch hajaar hantar jamaaye sadaasad.'
kuntee uski peeth par baithakar bolee, 'main aise doolhe se byaah karoongee, jo mere saamane khadaa poonchh hilaataa rahegaa. main mithaaee ke done phenk doongee aur vah chaategaa. zara bhi cheen-chapad karegaa, to kaan garm kar doongee. ammaan ko laotaree
ke rupaye milenge, to pachaas hajaar mujhe de den. bas, chain karoongee. main donon vakt thaakurajee se ammaan ke lie praarthanaa karti hoon. ammaan kehti hain, kunvaaree ladakiyon ki duaa kabhi nishphal nahin hoti. mera man to keht hai, ammaan ko zaroor rupaye milenge.'
mujhe yaad aay, ek baar main apne nanihaal dehaat mein gaya tha, to sookhaa padaa hua tha. bhaadon kaa mahina aa gaya tha; magar paani ki boond nahin. sab logon ne chandaa karake gaanv ki sab kunvaaree ladakiyon ki daavat ki thi.
aur usake teesare hi din moosalaadhaar varshaa hui thi. avashy hi kunvaariyon ki duaa mein asar hot hai. maine vikram ko arthapoorn aankhon se dekh, vikram ne mujhe. aankhon hi mein hamne salaah kar lee aur phaisalaa bhi kar liya.
vikram ne kuntee se kahaa, 'achchha, tujhase ek baat kahen kisee se kahogee to nahin? nahin, too to badi achchhi ladakee hai, kisee se na kahegee. main abakee tujhe khoob padhaaoongaa aur paas karaa doongaa. baat yah hai ki ham donon ne bhi laotaree kaa tikat liya hai. ham logon ke lie bhi eeshvar se praarthanaa kiya kar. agar hamen rupaye mile,
to tere lie achchhe-achchhe gahane banavaa denge. sach!' kuntee ko yakeen na aay. hamne kasamen khaayeen. vah nakhare karne lagee. jab hamne use sir se paanv tak sone aur heere se madh dene ki pratijnaa ki, tab vah hamaare lie duaa karne par raajee hui. lekin usake pet mein manon mithaaee pach sakatee thi;vah zara-see baat
na pachee. seedhe andar bhaagee aur ek pal mein saare ghar mein vah khabar phail gayi. ab jise dekhie, vikram ko daant raha hai, ammaan bhi, chachaa bhi, pitaa bhi keval vikram ki shubh-chaah se yaa aur kisee bhaav se,
'kaun jaane baithe-baithe tumhen himaakat hi soojhti hai. rupaye lekar paani mein phenk diye. ghar mein itane aadamiyon ne to tikat liya hi tha, tumhen lene ki kya jaroorat thi? kya tumhen usamen se kuchh na milate? aur tum bhi maastar sahab, bilakul ghonghaa ho. ladake ko achchhi baaten kya sikhaaoge, use aur chaupat kiye daalate ho.'
vikram to laadalaa betaa tha. use aur kya kehte. kaheen roothakar ek-do joon khaanaa na khaaye, to aaphat hi aa jaay. mujh par sara gussaa utaraa. isakee sohabat mein ladakaa bigadaa jat hai.
'par upadesh kushal bahutere' vaalee kahaavat meri aankhon ke saamane thi. mujhe apne bachpan ki ek ghatanaa yaad aayee. holee kaa din tha. sharaab ki ek botal mangavaayee gayi thi. mere maamoon sahab una dinon aaye hue the.
maine chupake se kotharee mein jaakar gilaas mein ek ghoont sharaab daalee aur pee gaya. abhi galaa jal hi raha tha aur aankhen laal hi theen, ki maamoon sahab kotharee mein aa gaye aur mujhe maano sendhaa mein giraphtaar kar liya aur itanaa bigade itanaa bigade ki mera kalejaa sookhakar chhuhaaraa ho gaya. ammaan ne bhi daantaa, pitaajee ne bhi daantaa, mujhe aansuon se unki krodhagni shaant karanee padee; aur dopahar hi ko maamoon sahab nashe mein paagal hokar gaane lage, phir roye, phir ammaan ko gaaliyaan deen, daadaa ke manaa karne par bhi maarane daude aur aakhir mein kai karake jameen par besudh pade najar aaye.
vikram ke pitaa bade thaakur sahab aur taaoo chhote thaakur sahab donon jadavaadee the, poojaa-paath ki hansee udaane vaale, poore naastik; magar ab donon bade nishthaavaan aur eeshvar bhakt ho gaye the. bade thaakur sahab praat:kaal gangaa-snaan karne jaate aur mandiron ke chakkar lagaate hue dopahar ko sari deh mein chandan lapete ghar lautate. chhote thaakur sahab ghar par hi garm paani se snaan karte aur gathiyaa se grast hone par bhi raam-naam likhna shuru kar dete. dhoop nikal aane par paark ki ora nikal jaate aur cheentiyon ko aat khilaate. shaam hote hi donon bhai apne thaakuradvaare mein jaa baithte aur aadhee raat tak bhaagavat ki kathaa tanmay hokar sunate. vikram ke bade bhai prakaash ko saadhu-mahaatmaaon par zyaadaa yakeen tha. vah mathon aur saadhuon ke akhaadon tathaa kutiyon ki khaak chhaanate aur maataajee ko to bhor se aadhee raat tak snaan, poojaa aur vrat ke sivaa doosaraa kaam hi na tha. is umr mein bhi unhen singaar kaa shauk tha; par aajakal pooree tapasvinee banee hui theen. log naahak laalasaa ko buraa kehte hain. main to samajhta hoon, hamamen jo yah bhakti-nishthaa aur dharm-pyaar hai, vah keval hamaaree laalasaa, hamaaree havas ke kaaran. hamaaraa dharm hamaare svaarth ke bal par tikaa hua hai. havas manushy ke man aur samajh kaa itanaa sanskaar kar sakatee hai, yah mere lie bilakul nayaa anubhav tha. ham donon bhi jyotishiyon aur panditon se sawal karake apne ko kabhi dukhee kar liya karte the.
jyon-jyon laotaree kaa divas paas aat jat tha, hamaare chitt ki shaanti udatee jati thi. hameshaa usi ora man tangaa rehta. mujhe aap-hi-aap akaaran shak hone lagaa ki kaheen vikram mujhe hissaa dene se inkaar kar de, to main kya karoongaa. saaph inkaar kar jaay ki tumne tikat mein saajhaa kiya hi nahin. na koi tahareer hai, na koi doosaraa saboot. sabakuchh vikram ki neeyat par hai. uski neeyat zara bhi daavaandol hui ki kaam-tamaam. kaheen phariyaad nahin kar sakataa, munh tak nahin khol sakataa. ab agar kuchh kahoon bhi to kuchh phaayadaa nahin. agar uski neeyat mein phitoor aa gaya hai tab to vah abhi se inkaar kar degaa; agar nahin aay hai, to is shak se use marmaantak dard hogee. aadamee aisaa to nahin hai; magar bhai, daulat paakar eemaan salaamat rakhna mushkil hai. abhi to rupaye nahin mile hain. is vakt eemaanadaar banane mein kya kharch hot hai? pareekshaa kaa samay to tab aayegaa,jab das laakh rupaye haath mein honge. maine apne ant:karan ko tatolaa agar tikat mere naam kaa hot aur mujhe
das laakh mil jaate, to kya main aadhe rupaye binaa kaan-poonchh hilaaye vikram ke havaale kar detaa? kaun kah sakataa hai; magar zyaadaa mumakin yahi tha ki main heele-havaale kart, keht tumne mujhe paanch rupaye udhar diye the. usake das le lo, sau le lo aur kya karoge; magar nahin, mujhse itanee bada-neeyatee na hoti.
doosare din ham donon akhabaar dekh rahe the ki sahasaa vikram ne kahaa, kaheen hamaaraa tikat nikal aaye, to mujhe aphasos hogaa ki naahak tumse saajhaa kiya! vah aasaan bhaav se muskaraayaa, magar yah thi usake aatmaa ki jhalak jise vah vinod ki aad mein chhipaanaa chahta tha. maine chaunkakar kahaa,'sach! lekin isi tarah mujhe bhi to aphasos ho sakataa hai?'
'lekin tikat to mere naam kaa hai?'
'isase kya.'
'achchha, maan lo, main tumhaare saajhe se inkaar kar jaaoon?'
mera khoon sard ho gaya. aankhon ke saamane andheraa chhaa gaya.
'main tumhen itanaa badaneeyat nahin samajhta tha.'
'magar hai bahut sanbhav. paanch laakh. socho! dimaag chakaraa jat hai!'
'to bhai, abhi se kushal hai, likhaa-padhee kar lo! yah sanshay rahe hi kyon?'
vikram ne hansakar kahaa, 'tum bade shakkee ho yaar! main tumhari pareekshaa le raha tha. bhalaa, aisaa kaheen ho sakataa hai? paanch laakh kya, paanch karod bhi hon,tab bhi eeshvar chaahegaa, to neeyat mein khalal na aane doongaa.'
lekin mujhe usake is aashvaasan par bilakul yakeen na aay. man mein ek sanshay baith gaya.
maine kahaa, 'yah to main jaanataa hoon, tumhari neeyat kabhi vichalit nahin ho sakatee, lekin likhaa-padhee kar lene mein kya haraj hai?'
'phajool hai.'
'phajool hi sahee.'
'to pakke kaagaj par likhna padegaa. das laakh ki kort-phees hi saadhe saat hajaar ho jaayagee. kis bhram mein hain aap?'
maine sochaa, 'balaa se saadee likhaa-padhee ke bal par koi kaanoonee kaarravaaee na kar sakoongaa. par inhen lajjit karne kaa, inhen jaleel karne kaa, inhen sabake saamane beeemaan siddh karne kaa avasar to mere haath aayegaa aur duniyaa mein badanaamee kaa dar na ho, to aadamee na jaane kya kare. beizzatee kaa dar kanoon ke dar se kisee tarah kam kriyaasheel nahin hot. bol, 'mujhe saade kaagaj par hi yakeen aa jaayagaa.'
vikram ne laaparavaahee se kahaa, 'zis kaagaj kaa koi kaanoonee mahattv nahin, use likhakar kya samay nasht karen?'
mujhe phaisalaa ho gaya ki vikram ki neeyat mein abhi se phitoor aa gaya. nahin to saadaa kaagaj likhne mein kya baadhaa ho sakatee hai? bigadakar kahaa,'tumhari neeyat to abhi se kharaab ho gayi. usane nirlajjataa se kahaa, to kya tum saabit karn chahte ho ki aisee dashaa mein tumhari neeyat na badalatee?'
'meri neeyat itanee kamajor nahin hai?'
'rehne bhi do. badi neeyatavaale! achchhe-achchhe ko dekh hai!'
'tumhen isi vakt lekhaabaddh hona padegaa. mujhe tumhaare oopar yakeen nahin raha.'
'agar tumhen mere oopar yakeen nahin hai, to main bhi nahin likhta.'
'to kya tum samajhte ho, tum mere rupaye hajam kar jaaoge?'
'kisake rupaye aur kaise rupaye?'
'main kahe detaa hoon vikram, hamaaree dostee kaa hi ant na ho jaayagaa, balki isase kaheen bhayankar nateejaa hogaa.'
hinsaa ki ek jvaalaa-see mere andar dahak uthee. sahasaa deevaanakhaane mein jhadap ki aavaaj sunakar mera dhyaan udhar chalaa gaya. yahan donon thaakur baithaa karte the. unamen aisee maitree thi, jo aadarsh bhaaiyon mein ho sakatee hai. raam aur lakshman mein bhi itanee hi rahi hogee. jhadap ki to baat hi kya, maine unamen kabhi vivaad hote bhi na sunaa tha. bade thaakur jo kah den, vah chhote thaakur ke lie kanoon tha aur chhote thaakur ki ichchhaa dekhakar hi bade thaakur koi baat kehte the. ham donon ko hairaanee hua deevaanakhaane ke dvaar par jaakar khade ho gaye. donon bhai apni-apni kursiyon se uthakar khade ho gaye the,ek-ek kadam aage bhi badh aaye the, aankhen laal, munh vikrit, tyoriyaan chadhee hueen, mutthiyaan bandhee hueen. maaloom hot tha, bas haathaapaaee hui hi chahti hai. chhote thaakur ne hamen dekhakar peechhe hatate hue kahaa, sammilit parivaar mein jo kuchh bhi aur kaheen se bhi aur kisee ke naam bhi aaye, vah sabakaa hai,baraabar.

'isakaa phaisalaa adaalat se hogaa.'
