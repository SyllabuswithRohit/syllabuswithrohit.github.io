# Sharab Ki Dukan

**Munshi Premchand**

1
kaangres kametee mein yah sawal pesh tha-sharaab aur taadee kee dookaanon par kaun dharanaa dene jaay? kametee ke pachchees membar sir jhukaaye baithe the; par kisee ke munh se baat na nikalatee thi. muaamalaa badaa naajuk tha. pulis ke haathon giraphtaar ho jan to zyada mushkil baat na thi. pulis ke karmachaaree apanee jimmedaariyon ko samajhate hain. choonki achchhe aur bure to sabhee jagah hote hain, lekin pulis ke aphasar, kuchh logon ko chhod kar, sabhyataa se itane khaalee nahin hote ki jaati aur desh par jaan denevaalon ke saath durvyavahaar karen; lekin nashebaajon mein yah jimmedaaree kahan? unamen to adhikaansh aise log hote hain, jinhen ghudakee-dhamakee ke sivaa aur kisee shakti ke saamane jhukane kee aadat nahin. maarapeet se nashaa hiran ho sakataa hai; par shaantivaadiyon ke lie to woh daravaajaa band hai. tab kaun is okhalee mein sir de, kaun piyakkadon kee gaaliyaan khaay? bahut mumakin hai ki woh haathaapaaee kar baithen. unake haathon pitanaa kise manjoor ho sakataa tha? phir pulisavaale bhee baithe tamaashaa na dekhenge. unhe aur bhee bhadakaate rahenge. pulis kee shah paa kar ye nashe ke bande jo kuchh na kar daalen, woh thodaa! eent kaa jawab patthar se de nahin sakate aur is samudaay par vinatee kaa koi asar nahin!
ek membar ne kahaa-mere soch mein to ina jaaton mein panchaayaton ko phir sanbhaalanaa chaahie. idhar hamaaree laaparavaahee se unakee panchaayaten nirjeev ho gayee hain. isake sivaa mujhe to aur koi upaay nahin soojhataa.
sabhaapati ne kahaa-haan, yah ek upaay hai. main ise not kiye letaa hoon, par dharanaa denaa jarooree hai.
doosare sahab bole-unake gharon par jaa kar samajhaayaa jaay, to achchhaa asar hogaa.
sabhaapati ne apanee chikanee khopadee sahalaate hue kahaa-yah bhee achchhaa upaay hai; magar dharane ko ham log tyaag nahin sakate.
phir sannaataa ho gaya.
pichhalee kataar mein ek devee bhee chup baithee huee theen. jab koi membar bolataa woh ek najar usakee taraph daal kar phir sir jhukaa letee theen. yahee kaangres kee ledee membar theen. unake pati sahab jee. pee. saksenaa kaangres ke achchhe kaam karanevaalon mein the. unakaa dehaant hue teen saal ho gaye the. misej saksenaa ne idhar ek saal se kaangres ke kaamon mein bhaag lenaa shuroo kar diya tha aur kaangres kametee ne unhe apanaa membar chun liya tha. woh shareeph gharaanon mein jaa kar svadeshee aur khaddar kaa prachaar karti theen. jab kabhee kaangres ke pletaphaarm par bolane khadee hoteen to unakaa josh dekh kar aisaa maaloom hot tha maano aakaash mein uda jan chaahatee hain. kundan kaa-saa rang laal ho jat tha, badee-badee karun aankhen jinamen jal bharaa hua maaloom hot tha, chamakane lagatee theen. badee khushamijaaj aur isake saath balaa kee nirbheek aurat theen. dabee huee chinagaaree thi, jo havaa paakar dahak uthatee hai. unake maamoolee shabdon mein itanaa aakarshan kahan se aa jat tha, kah nahin sakate. kametee ke kaee javaan membar, jo pahale kaangres mein bahut kam aate the, ab bilaa naagaa aane lage the. misej saksenaa koi bhee prastaav karen, unakaa anumodan karanevaalon kee kamee na thi. unakee saadagee, unakaa utsaah, unakee vinay, unakee mridu-vaanee kaangres par unakaa sikkaa jamaaye detee thi. har aadamee unakee khaatir izzat kee seemaa tay kart tha, par unakee svaabhaavik namrataa unhe apne daivee saadhanon se pooraa-pooraa phaayadaa na uthaane detee thi. jab kamare mein aateen, log khade ho jate the; par woh pichhalee saph se aage na badhatee theen.
misej saksenaa ne pradhaan se poochhaa-sharaab kee dookaanon par auraten dharanaa de sakatee hain?
sabakee aankhen unakee ora uth gayeen. is sawal kaa aashay sab samajh gaye.
pradhaan ne kaatar aavaaz mein kahaa-mahaatmaa jee ne to yah kaam auraton hee ko supurd karne par jor diya hai par. ..
misej saksenaa ne unhe apanaa vaaky pooraa na karne diya. boleen-to phir mujhe is kaam par bhej deejie.
logon ne kutoohal kee aankhon se misej saksenaa ko dekh. yah sukumaaree jisake komal angon mein shaayad havaa bhee chubhatee ho, gandee galiyon mein taadee aur sharaab kee durgandh-bharee dookaanon ke saamane jaane aur nashe se paagal aadamiyon kee kalushit aankhon aur baanhon kaa saamanaa karne ko kaise taiyaar ho gayee.
ek sahab ne apne paas ke aadamee ke kaan mein kahaa-balaa kee nidar aurat hai.
una sahab ne jale hue shabdon mein jawab diya-ham logon ko kaanton mein ghaseetanaa chaahatee hain, aur kuchh nahin. woh bechaaree kya piketing karengee. dookaan ke saamane khadaa tak to hua na jaayegaa.
pradhaan ne sir jhukaa kar kahaa-main aapake saahas aur utsarg kee prashansaa kart hoon, lekin mere soch mein abhee is shahar kee dashaa aisee nahin hai ki deviyaan piketing kar saken. aapako khabar nahin, nashebaaj kitane munhaphat hote hain. vinay to woh jaanate hee nahin!
misej saksenaa ne vyangy-bhaav se kahaa-to kya aapakaa soch hai ki koi aisaa jamaanaa bhee aayegaa, jab sharaabee log vinay aur sheel ke putale ban jaayenge? yah dashaa to hameshaa hee rahegee. aakhir mahaatmaa jee ne kuchh samajh kar hee to auraton ko yah kaam saunpaa hai. main nahin kah sakatee ki mujhe kahan tak saphalataa hogee; par is farz ko taalane se kaam na chalegaa.
pradhaan ne pasopesh mein pad kar kahaa-main to aapako is kaam ke lie ghaseetanaa theek nahin samajhataa, aage aapako akhtiyaar hai.
misej saksenaa ne jaise vinay kaa aalingan karte hue kahaa-main aapake paas phariyaad le kar na aaoongee ki mujhe phalaan aadamee ne maaraa yaa gaalee dee. itanaa jaanatee hoon ki agar main kaamayaab ho gayee, to aisee striyon kee kamee na rahegee jo is kaam ko solahon aane apne haath mein na le len.
is par ek naujavaan membar ne kahaa-main sabhaapati jee se binatee karoongaa ki misej saksenaa ko yah kaam de kar aap hinsaa kaa saamanaa kar rahe hain. isase yah kaheen achchhaa hai ki aap mujhe yah kaam saunpen.
misej saksenaa ne garm hokar kahaa-aapake haathon hinsaa hone kaa dar aur bhee zyada hai.
is naujavaan membar kaa naam tha jayaraam. ek baar ek kadaa vyaakhyaan dene ke lie jel ho aaye the, par us vakt unake sir ghar-gharavaale kaa bhaar na tha. kaanoon padhate the. ab unakaa vivaah ho gaya tha, do-teen bachche bhee ho gaye the, dashaa badal gayee thi. dil mein vahee josh, vahee tadap, vahee dard tha, par apanee haalat se majaboor the.
misej saksenaa kee ora namr aagrah se dekh kar bole-aap meree khaatir is gande kaam mein haath na daalen. mujhe ek saptaah kaa avasar deejie. agar is beech mein kaheen dangaa ho jaaye, to aapako mujhe nikaal dene kaa adhikaar hogaa.
misej saksenaa jayaraam ko khoob jaanatee theen. unhe maaloom tha ki yah tyaag aur saahas kaa putalaa hai aur ab tak paristhitiyon ke vajah peechhe dabakaa hua tha. isake saath hee woh yah bhee jaanatee theen ki isamen woh sabr aur bardaasht nahin hai, jo piketing ke lie laajamee hai. jel mein usane daarogaa ko apashabd kehne par chaantaa lagaayaa tha aur usakee sajaa teen maheene aur badh gayee thi. boleen-aapake sir ghar-gharavaale kaa bhaar hai. main ghamand nahin karti par jitane sabr se main yah kaam kar sakatee hoon, aap nahin kar sakate.
jayaraam ne usee namr aagrah ke saath kahaa-aap mere pichhale rekaard par phaisalaa kar rahi hain. aap bhool jati hain ki aadamee kee avasthaa ke saath usakee uddandataa ghatatee jati hai.
pradhaan ne kahaa-main chaahataa hoon, sahab jayaraam is kaam ko apne haathon mein len.
jayaraam ne khush ho kar kahaa-main sachche dil se aapako dhanyavaad detaa hoon.
misej saksenaa ne niraash ho kar kahaa-sahab, jayaraam, aapane mere saath badaa zulm kiya hai aur main ise kabhee maafee na karoongee. aap logon ne is baat kaa aaj nayaa parichay de diya ki purushon ke adheen striyaan apne desh kee sevaa bhee nahin kar sakateen.
2
doosare din, teesare pahar jayaraam paanch svayansevakon ko le kar begamaganj ke sharaabakhaane kee piketing karne jaa pahunchaa. taadee aur sharaab-donon kee dookaanen milee huee theen. theekedaar bhee ek hee tha. dookaan ke saamane, sadak kee pataree par, andar ke aangan mein nashebaajon kee toliyaan vish mein amrit kaa khushi loot rahi theen. koi wahan aphalaatoon se kam na tha. kaheen veerataa kee deeng thi, kaheen apne daan-dakshinaa ke pachade, kaheen apne samajh-kaushal kaa aalaap. ghamand nashe kaa mukhy roop hai.
ek boodhaa sharaabee kah raha tha-bhaiyaa, jindagaanee kaa bharosaa nahin. haan, koi bharosaa nahin. meree baat maan lo, jindagaanee kaa koi bharosaa nahin. bas yahee khana-khilaanaa yaad raha jaayegaa. paisaa-daulat, jagah-jameen sab dharee raha jaayegee!
do taadeevaalon mein ek doosaree bahas chhidee huee thi-
‘ham-tum riaayaa hain bhai! hamaaree majaal hai ki sarakaar ke saamane sir uthaa saken? ’
‘apne ghar mein baithakar baadashaah ko gaaliyaan de lo; lekin maidaan mein aan mushkil hai. ’
‘kahan kee baat bhaiyaa, sarakaar to badee cheej hai, laal pagadee dekh kar to ghar bhaag jate ho. ’
‘chhotaa aadamee bhar-pet khaake baithataa hai, to samajhataa hai, ab baadashaah hameen hain. lekin apanee haisiyat ko bhoolanaa na chaahie. ’
‘bahut pakkee baat kehte ho khaan sahab. apanee asaliyat par date raho. jo raajaa hai, woh raajaa hai, jo parajaa hai, woh parajaa hai. bhalaa parajaa kaheen raajaa ho sakataa hai? ’
itane mein jayaraam ne aa kar kahaa-raam raam bhaaiyo, raam raam!
paanch-chhah khaddaradaaree manushyon ko dekh kar sabhee log unakee ora shankaa aur kutoohal se taakane lage. dookaanadaar ne chupake se apne ek naukar ke kaan mein kuchh kahaa aur naukar dookaan se utar kar chalaa gaya.
jayaraam ne jhande ko jameen par khadaa karake kahaa-bhaaiyo, mahaatmaa gaandhee kaa hukm hai ki aap log taadee-sharaab na piyen. jo rupaye aap yahan udaa dete hain, woh agar apne baal-bachchon ko khilaane-pilaane mein kharch karen, to kitanee achchhee baat ho. zara der ke nashe ke lie aap apne baal-bachchon ko bhookhon maarate hain. gande gharon mein rehte hain, mahaajan kee gaaliyaan khaate hain. sochie, is rupaye se aap apne pyaare bachchon ko kitane aaraam se rakh sakate hain!
ek boodhe sharaabee ne apne saathee se kahaa-bhaiyaa, hai to buree cheej, ghar tabaah karake chhod detee hai. mudaa itanee umir peete kat gayee, to ab marate dam kya chhoden? usake saathee ne samarthan kiya-pakkee baat kehte ho chaudharee! jab itanee umir peete kat gayee, to ab marate dam kya chhoden?
jayaraam ne kahaa-vaah chaudharee, yahee to umir hai chhodane kee. javaanee to deevaanee hoti hai, us vakt sab kuchh muaaph hai.
chaudharee ne to koi jawab na diya; lekin usake saathee ne jo kaalaa, motaa, badee-badee moonchhonvaalaa aadamee tha, aasaan aapatti ke bhaav se kahaa-agar peenaa buraa hai, to angarez kyon peete hain?
jayaraam vakeel tha, usse bahas karn bhid ke chhatte ko chhedanaa tha. bol-yah tumane bahut achchhaa sawal poochhaa bhai. angarejon ke baap-daadaa abhee dedh-do sau saal pahale lutere the. hamaare-tumhaare baap-daadaa rishi-muni the. luteron kee bachchaa piye, to peene do. unake paas na koi dharm hai, na neeti; lekin rishiyon kee bachchaa unakee nakal kyon kare. ham aur tum una mahaatmaaon kee bachchaa hain, jinhonne duniya ko sikhaayaa, jinhonne duniya ko aadamee banaayaa. ham apanaa dharm chhod baithe, usee kaa phal hai ki aaj ham gulaam hain. lekin ab hamane gulaamee kee janjeeron ko todane kaa phaisalaa kar liya hai aur. ..
ekaaek thaanedaar aur chaar-paanch kaanstebal aa khade hue.
thaanedaar ne chaudharee se poochhaa-yah log tumako dhamakaa rahe hain?
chaudharee ne khade ho kar kahaa-nahin hujoor, yah to hamen samajhaa rahe hain. kaise pyaar se samajhaa rahe hain ki vaah!
thaanedaar ne jayaraam se kahaa-agar yahan phisaad ho jaaye, to aap jimmedaar honge?
jayaraam-main us vakt tak zimmedaar hoon, jab tak aap na rahen.
‘aapakaa matalab hai ki main phisaad karaane aay hoon? ’
‘main yah nahin keht, lekin aap aaye hain, to angarejee saamraajy kee atul shakti kaa parichay zaroor hee deejiegaa. janataa mein uttejanaa phailegee. tab aap pil padenge aur das-bees aadamiyon ko maar giraayenge. vahee sab jagah hot hai aur yahan bhee hogaa. ’
sab inspektar ne onth chabaa kar kahaa-main aapase keht hoon, yahan se chale jaaie, varanaa mujhe jaabate kee kaarravaaee karanee padegee.
jayaraam ne avichal bhaav se kahaa-aur main aapase keht hoon ki aap mujhe apanaa kaam karne deejie. mere bahut-se bhai yahan jamaa hain aur mujhe unse baatacheet karne kaa utanaa hee hak hai jitanaa aapako.
is vakt tak saikadon darshak jamaa ho gaye the. daarogaa ne aphasaron se poochhe bagair aur koi kaarravaaee karn theek na samajhaa. akadate hue dookaan par gaye aur kursee par paanv rakh kar bole-ye log to maananevaale nahin hain!
dookaanadaar ne gidagidaakar kahaa-hujoor, meree to badhiyaa baith jaayegee.
daarogaa-do-chaar gunde bulaa kar bhagaa kyon nahin dete? main kuchh na boloongaa. haan, zara ek botal achchhee-see bhej denaa. kal na jaane kya bhej diya, kuchh majaa hee nahin aay.
thaanedaar chalaa gaya, to chaudharee ne apne saathee se kahaa-dekh kalloo, thaanedaar kitanaa bigad raha tha? sarakaar chaahatee hai ki ham log khoob sharaab peeyen aur koi hamen samajhaane na paaye. sharaab kaa paisaa bhee to sarakaar hee mein jat hai?
kalloo ne daarshanik bhaav se kahaa-har ek bahaane se paisaa kheenchate hain sab!
chaudharee-to phir kya salaah hai? hai to buree cheej.
kalloo-bahut buree cheej hai bhaiyaa, mahaatmaa jee kaa hukm hai, to chhod hee denaa chaahie.
chaudharee-achchhaa to yah lo, aaj se agar piye to dogalaa.
yah kehte hue chaudharee ne botal jameen par patak dee. aadhee botal sharaab jameen par bah kar sookh gayee.
jayaraam ko shaayad zindagi mein kabhee itanee khushi na huee thi. jor-jor se taaliyaan bajaa kar uchhal pade.
usee vakt donon taadee peenevaalon ne bhee ‘mahaatmaa jee kee jay’ pukaaree aur apanee haandee jameen par patak dee. ek svayansevak ne lapak kar phoolon kee maalaa lee aur chaaron aadamiyon ke gale mein daal dee.
3
sadak kee pataree par kaee nashebaaj baithe ina chaaron aadamiyon kee taraph us durbal bhakti se taak rahe the, jo purushaarthaheen manushyon kaa lakshan hai. wahan ek bhee aisaa vyakti na tha, jo angarejon kee maans-madiraa yaa taadee ko zindagi ke lie anivaary samajhataa ho aur usake bagair zindagi kee kalpanaa bhee na kar sake. sabhee log nashe ko dooshit samajhate the, keval durbalendriy hone ke vajah nity aa kar pee jate the. chaudharee jaise ghaagh piyakkad ko botal patakate dekh kar unakee aankhen khul gayeen.
ek mariyal daadheevaale aadamee ne aakar chaudharee kee peeth thonkee. chaudharee ne use peechhe dhakel kar kahaa-peeth kya thonkate ho jee, jaakar apanee botal patak do.
daadheevaale ne kahaa-aaj aur pee lene do chaudharee! allaah jaanataa hai, kal se idhar bhool kar bhee na aaoongaa.
chaudharee-jitanee bachee ho, usake paise hamse le lo. ghar jaa kar bachchon ko mithaaee khilaa denaa.
daadheevaale ne jaa kar botal patak dee aur bol-lo, tum bhee kya kahoge? ab to hue khush.
chaudharee-ab to na peeyoge kabhee?
daadheevaale ne kahaa-agar tum na peeyoge, to main bhee na peeoongaa. jis din tumane pee, usee din phir shuroo kar dee.
chaudharee kee tatparataa ne duraagrah kee jaden hilaa deen. baahar abhee paanch-chhah aadamee aur the. woh sachet nirlajjataa se baithe hue abhee tak peete jate the. jayaraam ne unake saamane jaa kar kahaa-bhaaiyo, aapake paanch bhaaiyon ne abhee aapake saamane apanee-apanee botal patak dee. kya aap una logon ko baajee jeet le jaane denge?
ek thigane, kaale aadamee ne jo kisee angarez kaa khaanasaamaa maaloom hot tha, laal-laal aankhen nikaal kar kahaa-ham peete hain tumse matalab? tumse bheekh maangane to nahin jate?
jayaraam ne samajh liya, ab baajee maar lee. gumaraah aadamee jab vivaad karne par utar aaye, to samajh lo, woh raaste par aa jaayegaa. chuppaa aib woh chikanaa ghadaa hai, jis par kisee baat kaa asar nahin hot.
jayaraam ne kahaa-agar main apne ghar mein aag lagaaoon to use dekh kar kya aap meraa haath na pakad lenge? mujhe to isamen rattee bhar shak nahin hai ki aap meraa haath hee na pakad lenge balki mujhe wahan se jabaradastee kheench le jaayenge.
chaudharee ne khaanasaamaa kee taraph mugdh aankhon se dekh, maano kah raha hai-isakaa tumhaare paas kya jawab hai? aur bol-jamaadaar, ab isee baat par botal patak do.
khaanasaamaa ne jaise kaat khaane ke lie daant tej kar liye aur bol-botal kyon patak doon, paise nahin diye hain?
chaudharee paraast ho gaya. jayaraam se bol-inhen chhodie baaboo jee, yah log is tarah maananevaale asaamee nahin hain. aap inake saamane jaan bhee de den to bhee sharaab na chhodenge. haan, pulis kee ek ghudakee paa jaayen to phir kabhee idhar bhool kar bhee na aayen.
khaanasaamaa ne chaudharee kee ora tiraskaar ke bhaav se dekh, jaise kah raha ho-kya tum samajhate ho ki main hee manushy hoon, yah sab pashu hain? phir bol-tumse kya matalab hai jee, kyon beech mein koode padate ho? main to baaboo jee se baat kar raha hoon. tum kaun hote ho beech mein bolanevaale? main tumhaaree tarah nahin hoon ki botal patak kar vaah-vaah karaaoon. kal phir munh mein kaalikh lagaaoon, ghar par mangavaa kar peeoon? jab yahan chhodenge, to sachche dil se chhodenge. phir koi laakh rupaye bhee de to aankh uthaa kar na dekhen.
jayaraam-mujhe aap logon se aisee hee ummeed hai.
chaudharee ne khaanasaamaa kee ora kataaksh karake kahaa-kya samajhate ho, main kal phir peene aaoongaa?
khaanasaamaa ne uddandataa se kahaa-haan-haan, keht hoon tum aaoge aur bada kar aaoge. kaho, pakke kaagaj par likh doon.
chaudharee-achchhaa bhai, tum bade dharamaatmaa ho, main paapee sahee. tum chhodoge to zindagi bhar ke lie chhodoge, main aaj chhod kar kal phir peene lagoongaa, yahee sahee. meree ek baat gaanth baandh lo. tum us bakhat chhodoge, jab zindagi tumhaaraa saath chhod degee. isake pahale tum nahin chhod sakate.
khaanasaamaa-tum mere dil kaa haal kya jaanate ho?
chaudharee-jaanataa hoon, tumhaare jaise saikadon aadamee ko bhugat chukaa hoon.
khaanasaamaa-to tumane aise-vaise besharmon ko dekh hogaa. hayaadaar aadamiyon ko na dekh hogaa.
yah kehte hue usane jaa kar botal patak dee aur bol-ab agar tum is dookaan par dekhanaa, to munh mein kaalikh lagaa denaa.
chaaron taraph taaliyaan bajane lageen. mard aise hote hain.
theekedaar ne dookaan ke neeche utar kar kahaa-tum log apanee-apanee dookaan par kyon nahin jate jee? main to kisee kee dookaan par nahin jat?
ek darshak ne kahaa-khade hain, to tumse matalab? sadak tumhaaree nahin hai? tum gareebon ko loote jaao. kisee ke baal-bachche bhookhon maren tumhaaraa kya bigadataa hai. (doosare sharaabiyon se) kya yaaro, ab bhee peete jaaoge! jaanate ho, yah kisakaa hukm hai? are kuchh bhee to sharm karo?
jayaraam ne darshakon se kahaa-aap log yahan bheed na lagaayen aur na kisee ko bhalaa-buraa kahen!
magar darshakon kaa samooh badhataa jat tha. abhee tak chaar-paanch aadamee be-gam baithe hue kulhad par kulhad chadhaa rahe the. ek manachale aadamee ne jaa kar us botal ko uthaa liya, jo unake beech mein rakhee huee thi aur use patakanaa chaahataa tha ki chaaron sharaabee uth khade hue aur use peetane lage. jayaraam aur usake svayansevak turant wahan pahunch gaye aur use bachaane kee cheshtaa karne lage ki chaaron use chhod kar jayaraam kee taraph lapake. darshakon ne dekh ki jayaraam par maar padaa chaahatee hai, to kaee aadamee jhallaa kar una chaaron sharaabiyon par toot pade. laaten, ghoonse aur dande chalaane lage. jayaraam ko isakaa kuchh avasar na milataa tha ki kisee ko samajhaaye. donon haath phailaaye una chaaron ke vaaron se bach raha tha; woh chaaron bhee aape se baahar ho kar darshakon par dande chalaa rahe the. jayaraam donon taraph se maar khaataa tha. sharaabiyon ke vaar bhee us par padate the, tamaashaaiyon ke vaar bhee usee par padate the, par woh unake beech se hatataa na tha. agar woh is vakt apanee jaan bachaa kar hat jat, to sharaabiyon kee khairiyat na thi. isakaa dosh kaangres par padataa. woh kaangres ko is aakshep se bachaane ke lie apne jaan dene par taiyaar tha. misej saksenaa ko apne oopar hansane kaa maukaa woh na denaa chaahataa tha.
aakhir usake sir par dandaa is jor se padaa ki woh sir pakad kar baith gaya. aankhon ke saamane titaliyaan udane lageen. phir use hosh na raha.
4
jayaraam saaree raat behosh padaa raha. doosare din subah ko jab use hosh aay, to saaree deh mein dard ho rahi thi aur kamajoree itanee thi ki raha-raha kar jee doobataa jat tha. ekaaek sirahaane kee taraph aankh uth gayee, to misej saksenaa baithee najar aayeen. unhe dekhate hee svayansevakon ke manaa karne par bhee uth baithaa. dard aur kamajoree donon jaise gaayab ho gayee. ek-ek ang mein sphoorti daud gayee.
misej saksenaa ne usake sir par haath rakh kar kahaa-aapako badee chot aayee. isakaa saaraa dosh mujh par hai.
jayaraam ne bhaktimay kritajnataa ke bhaav se dekh kar kahaa-chot to aisee zyada na thi, ina logon ne barabas pattee-sattee baandh kar jakhmee banaa diya.
misej saksenaa ne glaanit hokar kahaa-mujhe aapako na jaane denaa chaahie tha.
jayaraam-aapakaa wahan jan theek na tha. main aapase ab bhee yahee binatee karoongaa ki us taraph na jaaiegaa.
misej saksenaa ne jaise una baadhaaon par hans kar kahaa-vaah! mujhe aaj se wahan piket karne kee hukm mil gayee hai.
‘aap meree itanee vinay maan jaaiegaa. shohadon ke lie aavaaj kasanaa bilakul maamoolee baat hai. ’
‘main aavaajon kee paravaah nahin karti! ’
‘to phir main bhee aapake saath chaloongaa. ’
‘aap is haalat mein? ’-misej saksenaa ne hairaanee se kahaa.
‘main bilakul achchhaa hoon, sach! ’
‘yah nahin ho sakataa. jab tak daaktar yah na kah degaa ki ab aap wahan jaane ke yogy hain, aapako na jaane doongee. kisee tarah nahin. ’
‘to main bhee aapako na jaane doongaa. ’
misej saksenaa ne mridu-vyangy ke saath kahaa-aap bhee any purushon hee kee tarah svaarth ke putale hain. sadaa yash khud lootanaa chaahate hain, auraton ko koi maukaa nahin denaa chaahate. kam se kam yah to dekh leejie ki main bhee kuchh kar sakatee hoon yaa nahin.
jayaraam ne vyathit galaa se kahaa-jaisee aapakee ichchhaa.
5
teesare pahar misej saksenaa chaar svayansevakon ke saath begamaganj chaleen.
jayaraam aankhen band kiye chaarapaaee par padaa tha. shor sun kar chaunkaa aur apanee aurat se poochhaa-yah kaisaa shor hai?
aurat ne khidakee se jhaankakar dekh aur bolee-woh aurat, jo kal aayee thi jhandaa liye kaee aadamiyon ke saath jaa rahi hai. ise sharm bhee nahin aati.
jayaraam ne usake chehare par maafee kee nazar daalee aur soch mein doob gaya. phir woh uth khadaa hua aur bol-main bhee vaheen jat hoon.
aurat ne usakaa haath pakad kar kahaa-abhee kal maar khaa kar aaye ho, aaj phir jaane kee soojhee!
jayaraam ne haath chhudaa kar kahaa-tum use maar kehti ho, main use upahaar samajhataa hoon.
aurat ne usakaa raastaa rok liya-kehti hoon, tumhaaraa jee achchhaa nahin hai, mata jaao, kyon meree jaan ke graahak hue ho? usakee deh mein heere nahin jade hain, jo wahan koi noch legaa.
jayaraam ne minnat karake kahaa-meree tabeeyat bilakul achchhee hai chammoo! agar kuchh kasar hai to woh bhee mit jaayegee. bhalaa socho, yah kaise mumakin hai ki ek devee una shohadon ke beech mein piketing karne jaay aur main baithaa rahoon. meraa wahan rahanaa jarooree hai. agar koi baat aa padee, to kam se kam main logon ko samajhaa to sakoongaa.
chammoo ne jal kar kahaa-yah kyon nahin kehte ki koi aur hee cheej kheenche liye jati hai!
jayaraam ne muskaraa kar usakee ora dekh, jaise kah raha ho-yah baat tumhaare dil se nahin, galaa se nikal rahi hai aur kataraa kar nikal gaya. phir dvaar par khadaa ho kar bol-shahar mein teen laakh se kuchh hee kam aadamee hain, kametee mein bhee tees membar hain, magar sab ke sab jee churaa rahe hain. logon ko achchhaa bahaanaa mil gaya ki sharaabakhaanon par dharanaa dene ke lie striyon hee kee jaroorat hai? aakhir kyon striyon hee ko is kaam ke lie upayukt samajhaa jat hai? iseelie ki maradon ke sir bhoot savaar ho jat hai aur jahaan namrataa se kaam lenaa chaahie, wahan log ugrataa se kaam lene lagate hain. woh deviyaan kya isee yogy hain ki shohadon ke phikare sunen aur unakee kudrishti kaa nishaanaa banen? kam se kam main yah nahin dekh sakataa.
woh langadaataa hua ghar se nikal padaa. chammoo ne phir use rokane kaa koshish nahin kiya. raaste mein ek svayansevak mil gaya. jayaraam ne use saath liya aur ek taange par baith kar chalaa. sharaabakhaane se kuchh door idhar ek lemaned-barph kee dookaan thi. usane taange ko chhod diya aur vaalantiyar ko sharaabakhaane bhej kar khud usee dookaan mein jaa baithaa.
dookaanadaar ne lemaned kaa ek gilaas use dete hue kahaa-baaboo jee, kalavaale chaaron badamaash aaj phir aaye hue hain. aapane na bachaayaa hot to aaj sharaab yaa taadee kee jagah haldee-gud peete hote.
jayaraam ne gilaas lekar kahaa-tum log beech mein na kood padate, to mainne una sabon ko theek kar liya hot.
dookaanadaar ne prativaad kiya-nahin baaboo jee, woh sab chhante hue gunde hain. main to unhe apanee dookaan ke saamane khadaa bhee nahin hone detaa. chaaron teen-teen saal kaat aaye hain.
abhee bees minat bhee na gujare honge ki ek svayansevak aakar khadaa ho gaya. jayaraam ne sachint hokar poochhaa-kaho, wahan kya ho raha hai?
svayansevak ne kuchh aisaa munh banaa liya, jaise wahan kee dashaa kahanaa woh theek nahin samajhataa aur bol-kuchh nahin, devee jee aadamiyon ko samajhaa rahi hain.
jayaraam ne usakee ora atript netron se taakaa, maano kah rahe hon-bas itanaa hee! itanaa to main jaanataa hee tha.
svayansevak ne ek pal baad phir kahaa-deviyon kaa aise shohadon ke saamane jan achchhaa nahin.
jayaraam ne adheer hokar poochhaa-saaph-saaph kyon nahin kehte, kya baat hai?
svayansevak darate-darate bol-sab ke sab unse dillagee kar rahe hain. deviyon kaa yahan aan achchhaa nahin.
jayaraam ne aur kuchh na poochhaa. dandaa uthaayaa aur laal-laal aankhen nikaale bijalee kee tarah kaundh kar sharaabakhaane ke saamane jaa pahunchaa aur misej saksenaa kaa haath pakad kar peechhe hataataa hua sharaabiyon se bol-agar tum logon ne deviyon ke saath zara bhee gustaakhee kee, to tumhaare hak mein achchhaa na hogaa. kal mainne tum logon kee jaan bachaayee thi aaj isee dande se tumhaaree khopadee tod kar rakh doongaa.
usake badale hue tevar ko dekh kar sab ke sab nashebaaj ghabadaa gaye. woh kuchh kahanaa chaahate the ki misej saksenaa ne gambheer bhaav se poochhaa-aap yahan kyon aaye? mainne to aapase kahaa tha, apanee jagah se na hiliegaa. mainne to aapase madad na maangee thi?
jayaraam ne lajjit hokar kahaa-main is neeyat se yahan nahin aay tha. ek jaroorat se idhar aa nikalaa tha. yahan jamaav dekh kar aa gaya. mere khayaal mein aap ab yahan se chalen. main aaj kaangres kametee mein yah sawal pesh karoongaa ki is kaam ke lie purushon ko bhejen.
misej saksenaa ne teekhe aavaaz mein kahaa-aapake soch mein duniya ke saare kaam maradon ke lie hain.
jayaraam-meraa yah matalab na tha.
misej saksenaa-to aap jaa kar aaraam se leten aur mujhe apanaa kaam karne den.
jayaraam vaheen sir jhukaaye khadaa raha.
misej saksenaa ne poochhaa-ab aap kyon khade hain?
jayaraam ne vineet aavaaz mein kahaa-main bhee yaheen ek kinaare khadaa rahoongaa.
misej saksenaa ne kathor aavaaz mein kahaa-jee nahin, aap jaayen.
jayaraam dheere-dheere ladee huee gaadee kee tarah chalaa aur aa kar phir usee lemaned kee dookaan par baith gaya. use jor kee pyaas lagee thi. usane ek gilaas sharbat banavaayaa aur saamane mej par rakh kar soch mein doob gaya; magar aankhen aur kaan usee taraph lage hue the.
jab koi aadamee dookaan par aat, woh chaunk kar usakee taraph taakane lagataa-wahan koi nayee baat to nahin ho gayee?
koi aadh ghante baad vahee svayansevak phir daraa hua-saa aa kar khadaa ho gaya. jayaraam ne udaaseen banane kee cheshtaa karake poochhaa-wahan kya ho raha hai jee?
svayansevak ne kaanon par haath rakh kar kahaa-main kuchh nahin jaanataa baaboo jee, mujhse kuchh na poochhie.
jayaraam ne ek saath hee namr aur kathor hokar poochhaa-phir koi chhedachhaad huee?
svayansevak-jee nahin, koi chhedachhaad nahin huee. ek aadamee ne devee jee ko dhakkaa de diya, woh gir padeen.
jayaraam nispand baithaa raha; par usake antaraal mein bhookamp-saa machaa hua tha. bol-unake saath ke svayansevak kya kar rahe hain?
‘khade hain, deveejee unhe bolane hee nahin deteen. ’
‘to kya bade jor se dhakkaa diya? ’
‘jee haan, gir padeen. ghutanon mein chot aa gayee. woh aadamee saath pee rahe the. jab ek botal uda gayee, to unamen se ek aadamee doosaree botal lene chalaa. devee jee ne raastaa rok liya. bas, usane dhakkaa de diya. vahee jo kaalaa-kaalaa motaa saa aadamee hai! kalavaale chaaron aadamiyon kee sharaarat hai. ’
jayaraam unmaad kee dashaa mein wahan se uthaa aur daudataa hua sharaabakhaane ke saamane aay. misej saksenaa sir pakade jameen par baithee huee theen aur woh kaalaa motaa aadamee dookaan ke kathaghare ke saamane khadaa tha. pachaason aadamee jamaa the. jayaraam ne use dekhate hee lapak kar usakee gardan pakad lee aur itane jor se dabaayee ki usakee aankhen baahar nikal aayeen. maaloom hot tha, usake haath phaulaad ke ho gaye hain.
sahasaa misej saksenaa ne aakar usakaa phaulaadee haath pakad liya aur bhaven sikod kar boleen-chhod do isakee gardan! kya isakee jaan le loge?
jayaraam ne aur jor se usakee gardan dabaayee aur bol-haan, le loongaa. aise dusht kee yahee sajaa hai.
misej saksenaa ne adhikaar-garv se gardan uthaa kar kahaa-aapako yahan aane kaa koi adhikaar nahin hai.
ek darshak ne kahaa-aisaa dabaao baaboo jee, ki saalaa thandaa ho jaaye. isane devee jee ko aisaa dhakelaa ki bechaaree gir padeen. hamen to bolane kaa hukm nahin hai, nahin to haddee tod kar rakh dete.
jayaraam ne sharaabee kee gardan chhod dee. woh kisee baaj ke changul se chhootee huee chidiyaa kee tarah sahamaa hua khadaa ho gaya. use ek dhakkaa dete hue usane misej saksenaa se kahaa-aap yahan se chalateen kyon nahin? aap jaayen, main baithataa hoon; agar ek chhataank sharaab bik jaay, to meraa kaan pakad leejiyegaa.
usakaa dam phoolane lagaa, aankhon ke saamane andheraa chhaa raha tha. woh khadaa na raha sakaa. jameen par baith kar roomaal se maathe kaa paseenaa ponchhane lagaa.
misej saksenaa ne parihaas karake kahaa-aap kaangres nahin hain ki main aapakaa hukm maanoon. agar aap yahan se na jaayenge, to main satyaagrah karoongee.
phir ekaaek kathor ho kar boleen-jab tak kaangres ne is kaam kaa bhaar mujh par rakhaa hai, aapako mere beech mein bolane kaa koi hak nahin hai. aap meraa beizzatee kar rahe hain. kaangres kametee ke saamane aapako isakaa jawab denaa hogaa.
jayaraam tilamilaa uthaa. binaa koi jawab diye laut padaa. aur veg se ghar kee taraph chalaa; par jyon-jyon aage badhataa tha usakee gati mand hoti jati thi. yahan tak ki baajaar ke doosare sire par aakar woh ruk gaya. rassee yahan khatm ho gayee. usake aage jan usake lie asaadhy ho gaya. jis jhatake ne use yahan tak bhejaa tha, usakee shakti ab shesh ho gayee thi. una shabdon mein jo katutaa aur chot thi, usamen ab use hamadardee aur pyaar kee sugandh aa rahi thi.
use phir phikr huee na jaane wahan kya ho raha hai. kaheen una badamaashon ne aur koi dushtataa na kee ho, yaa pulis na aa jaaye.
woh baajaar kee taraph mudaa lekin ek kadam hee chal kar phir ruk gaya. aise pasopesh mein woh kabhee na padaa tha.
sahasaa use vahee svayansevak daudataa aat dikhaayee diya. woh badahavaas hokar usse milane ke lie khud bhee usakee taraph daudaa. beech mein donon mil gaye.
jayaraam ne haanphate hue poochhaa-kya hua? kyon bhaage jaa rahe ho?
svayansevak ne dam le kar kahaa-badaa gajab ho gaya baaboo jee! aapake aane ke baad woh kaalaa sharaabee botal le kar dookaan se chalaa, to devee jee daravaaje par baith gayeen. woh baar-baar devee jee ko hataa kar nikalanaa chaahataa hai; par woh phir aa kar baith jati hain. dhakkam-dhakke mein unake kuchh kapade phat gaye hain aur kuchh chot bhee. ..
abhee baat pooree na huee thi ki jayaraam sharaabakhaane kee taraph daudaa.
6
jayaraam sharaabakhaane ke saamane pahunchaa to dekh, misej saksenaa ke chaaron svayansevak dookaan ke saamane lete hue hain aur misej saksenaa ek kinaare sir jhukaaye khadee hain. jayaraam ne darate-darate unake chehare par nigaah daalee. aanchal par rakt kee boonden dikhaayee deen. use phir kuchh sudh na rahi. khoon kee woh chinagaariyaan jaise usake rom-rom mein samaa gayeen. usakaa khoon khaulane lagaa, maano usake sir khoon savaar ho gaya ho. woh una chaaron sharaabiyon par toot padaa aur poore jor ke saath lakadee chalaane lagaa. ek-ek boond kee jagah woh ek-ek ghadaa khoon bahaa denaa chaahataa tha. khoon use kabhee itanaa pyaaraa na tha. khoon mein itanee uttejanaa hai, isakee use khabar na thi.
woh poore jor se lakadee chalaa raha tha. misej saksenaa kab aakar usake saamane khadee ho gayeen use kuchh pataa na chalaa. jab woh jameen par gir padeen, tab use jaise hosh aa gaya ho. usane lakadee phenk dee aur vaheen nishchal, nispand khadaa ho gaya, maano usakaa rakt pravaah ruk gaya hai.
chaaron svayansevakon ne daud kar misej saksenaa ko pankhaa jhalanaa shuroo kiya. dookaanadaar thandaa paani le kar daudaa. ek darshak daaktar ko bulaane bhaagaa, par jayaraam vaheen bejaan khadaa tha jaise svayan apne tiraskaar-bhaav kaa putalaa ban gaya ho. agar is vakt koi usake donon haath kaat daalataa, koi usakee aankhen laal lohe se phod detaa, tab bhee woh choon na kart.
phir vaheen sadak par baith kar usane apne lajjit, tiraskrit, paraajit mastak ko bhoomi par patak diya aur behosh ho gaya.
usee vakt us kaale mote sharaabee ne botal jameen par patak dee aur usake sir par thandaa paani daalane lagaa.
ek sharaabee ne laisansadaar se kahaa-tumhaaraa rojagaar any logon kee jaan le kar rahegaa. ab to abhee doosaraa hee din hai.
laisansadaar ne kahaa-kal se meraa isteephaa hai. ab svadeshee kapade kaa rojagaar karoongaa, jisamen jas bhee hai aur upakaar bhee.
sharaabee ne kahaa-ghaataa to bahut rahegaa.
dookaanadaar ne kismat thonk kar kahaa-ghaataa-naphaa to jindagaanee ke saath hai.
