# Subhagi

**Munshi Premchand**

1
aur logon ke yahan chaahe jo hot ho, tulasee mahato apanee ladakee subhaagee ko ladake raamoo se jau-bhar bhee kam pyaar na karte the. raamoo javaan hokar bhee kaath kaa ulloo tha. subhaagee gyaarah saal kee ladakee hokar bhee ghar ke kaam mein itanee hoshiyaar, aur khetee-baaree ke kaam mein itanee nipun thi ki usakee maan lakshmee dil mein daratee rehti ki kaheen ladakee par devataaon kee aankh na pad jaay. achchhe baalakon se bhagavaan ko bhee to pyaar hai. koi subhaagee kaa bakhaan na kare, isalie woh anaayaas hee use daantatee rehti thi. bakhaan se ladake bigad jate hain, yah dar to na tha, dar tha - najar kaa! vahee subhaagee aaj gyaarah saal kee umr mein vidhavaa ho gayee.
ghar mein kuharaam machaa hua tha. lakshmee pachhaaden khaatee thi. tulasee sir peetate the. unhe rote dekhakar subhaagee bhee rotee thi. baar-baar maan se poochhatee, kyon rotee ho ammaan, main tumhe chhodakar kaheen na jaaoongee, tum kyon rotee ho? usakee bholee baaten sunakar maan kaa dil aur bhee phataa jat tha. woh sochatee thi eeshvar tumhaaree yahee leelaa hai! jo khel khelate ho, woh doosaron ko du: kha dekar. aisaa to paagal karte hain. aadamee paagalapan kare to use paagalakhaane bhejate hain; magar tum jo paagalapan karte ho, usakaa koi sazaa nahin. aisaa khel kis kaam kaa ki doosare royen aur tum hanso. tumhe to log dayaalu kehte hain. yahee tumhaaree raham hai!
aur subhaagee kya soch rahi thi? usake paas kotharee bhar rupaye hote, to woh unhe chhipaakar rakh detee. phir ek din chupake se baajaar chalee jati aur ammaan ke lie achchhe-achchhe kapade laatee, daadaa jab baakee maangane aate, to chat rupaye nikaalakar de detee, ammaan-daadaa kitane khush hote.
2
jab subhaagee javaan huee to log tulasee mahato par dabaav daalane lage ki ladakee kaa ghar kaheen kar do. javaan ladakee kaa yon phiranaa theek nahin. jab hamaaree biraadaree mein isakee koi nindaa nahin hai, to kyon soch-soch karte ho?
tulasee ne kahaa- bhai main to taiyaar hoon; lekin jab subhaagee bhee maane. woh kisee tarah raajee nahin hoti.
harihar ne subhaagee ko samajhaakar kahaa- betee, ham tere hee bhale ko kehte hain. maan-baap ab boodhe hue, unakaa kya bharosaa. tum is tarah kab tak baithee rahogee?
subhaagee ne sir jhukaakar kahaa- chaachaa, main tumhaaree baat samajh rahi hoon; lekin meraa man ghar karne ko nahin keht. mujhe aaraam kee phikr nahin hai. main sab kuchh jhelane ko taiyaar hoon. aur jo kaam tum kaho, woh sir-aankho ke bal karoongee; magar ghar basaane ko mujhse na kaho. jab meraa chaal-kuchaal dekhanaa to meraa sir kaat lenaa. agar main sachche baap kee betee hoongee, to baat kee bhee pakkee hoongee. phir sharm rakhanevaale to bhagavaan hain, meree kya hastee hai ki abhee kuchh kahoon.
ujadd raamoo bol - tum agar sochatee ho ki bhaiyaa kamaavenge aur main baithee mauj karoongee, to is bharose na rahanaa. yahan kisee ne janam bhar kaa theekaa nahin liya hai.
raamoo kee dulhan raamoo se bhee do angul oonchee thi. matakakar bolee- hamane kisee kaa karaj thode hee khaayaa ki janam bhar baithe bharaa karen. yahan to khaane ko bhee maheen chaahie, pahanane ko bhee maheen chaahie, yah hamaare boote kee baat nahin.
subhaagee ne garv se bhare hue aavaaz mein kahaa- bhaabhee, mainne tumhaaraa aasaraa kabhee nahin kiya aur bhagavaan ne chaahaa to kabhee karoongee bhee nahin. tum apanee dekho, meree phikr na karo.
raamoo kee dulhan ko jab maaloom ho gaya ki subhaagee ghar na karegee, to aur bhee usake sir ho gayee. hameshaa ek-na-ek khuchad lagaaye rehti. use rulaane mein jaise usako majaa aat tha. woh bechaaree pahar raat se uthakar kootane-peesane mein lag jati, chaukaa-baratan karti, gobar paathatee. phir khet mein kaam karne chalee jati. dopahar ko aakar jaldee-jaldee khana pakaakar sabako khilaatee. raat ko kabhee maan ke sir mein tel daalatee, kabhee usakee deh dabaatee. tulasee chilam ke bhakt the. unhe baar-baar chilam pilaatee. jahaan tak apanaa bas chalataa, maan-baap ko koi kaam na karne detee. haan, bhai ko na rokatee. sochatee, yah to javaan aadamee hain, yah kaam na karenge to ghar-gharavaale kaise chalegee.
magar raamoo ko yah buraa lagataa. ammaa aur daadaa ko tinakaa tak nahin uthaane detee aur mujhe peesanaa chaahatee hai. yahan tak ki ek din woh jaame se baahar ho gaya. subhaagee se bol- agar una logon kaa badaa moh hai, to kyon nahin alag lekar rehti ho. tab sevaa karo to maaloom ho ki sevaa kadavee lagatee hai ki meethee. doosaron ke bal par vaahavaahee lenaa aasaan hai. bahaadur woh hai, jo apne bal par kaam kare.
subhaagee ne to kuchh jawab na diya. baat badh jaane kaa dar tha. magar usake maan-baap baithe sun rahe the. mahato se na raha gaya. bole- kya hai raamoo, us gareebin se kyon ladate ho?
raamoo paas aakar bol- tum kyon beech mein kood pade, main to usako keht tha.
tulasee- jab tak main jeetaa hoon, tum use kuchh nahin kah sakate. mere peechhe jo chaahe karn. bechaaree kaa ghar mein rahanaa mushkil kar diya.
raamoo- aapako betee bahut pyaaree hai, to use gale baandh kar rakhie. mujhse to nahin sahaa jat.
tulasee- achchhee baat hai. agar tumhaaree yah marajee hai, to yahee hogaa. main kal gaanv ke aadamiyon ko bulaakar bantavaaraa kar doongaa. tum chaahe chhoot jaav, subhaagee nahin chhoot sakatee.
raat ko tulasee lete to woh puraanee baat yaad aayee, jab raamoo ke janmotsav mein unhonne rupaye karj lekar jalasaa kiya tha, aur subhaagee paidaa huee, to ghar mein rupaye rehte hue bhee unhonne ek kaudee na kharch kee. betaa ko ratn samajhaa tha, betee ko pahale-janm ke paapon kaa dand. woh ratn kitanaa kathor nikalaa aur yah sazaa kitanaa mangalamay.
3
doosare din mahato ne gaanv ke aadamiyon ko jamaa karake kahaa- pancho, ab raamoo kaa aur meraa ek mein nibaah nahin hot. main chaahataa hoon ki tum log insaaph se jo kuchh mujhe de do, woh lekar alag ho jaaoon. raat-din kee kich-kich achchhee nahin.
gaanv ke mukhtaar baaboo sajanasinh bade bhalaa aadamee aadamee the. unhonne raamoo ko bulaakar poochhaa- kyon jee, tum apne baap se alag rahanaa chaahate ho? tumhe sharm nahin aati ki aurat ke kehne se maan-baap ko alag kiye dete ho? raam! raam!
raamoo ne dhithaaee ke saath kahaa- jab ek mein gujar na ho, to alag ho jan hee achchhaa hai.
sajanasinh - tumako ek mein kya takaleeph hot hai?
raamoo - ek baat ho to bataaoon.
sajanasinh - kuchh to batalaao.
raamoo - sahab, ek mein meraa inake saath nibaah na hogaa. bas main aur kuchh nahin jaanataa.
yah keht hua raamoo wahan se chalataa banaa.
tulasee - dekh liya aap logon ne isakaa mijaaj! aap chaahe chaar hisson mein teen hisse use de den, par ab main is dusht ke saath na rahoongaa. bhagavaan ne betee ko du: kha de diya, nahin mujhe khetee-baaree lekar kya karn tha. jahaan rehta vaheen kamaataa khaataa! bhagavaan aisaa betaa saataven bairee ko bhee na den. 'ladake se ladakee bhalee, jo kulavantee hoy. '
sahasaa subhaagee aakar bolee - daadaa, yah sab baant-bakharaa mere hee kaaran to ho raha hai, mujhe kyon nahin alag kar dete. main mehanat-majooree karake apanaa pet paal loongee. apne se jo kuchh ban padegaa, tumhaaree sevaa karti rahoongee; par rahoongee alag. yon ghar kaa baaraa-baant hote mujhse nahin dekh jat. main apne maathe yah kalank nahin lenaa chaahatee.
tulasee ne kahaa- betee, ham tujhe na chhodenge chaahe sansaar chhoot jaay! raamoo kaa main munh nahin dekhanaa chaahataa, usake saath rahanaa to door raha.
raamoo kee dulhan bolee- tum kisee kaa munh nahin dekhanaa chaahate, to ham bhee tumhaaree poojaa karne ko vyaakul nahin hain.
mahato daant peesate hue uthe ki bahoo ko maaren, magar logon ne pakad liya.
4
bantavaaraa hote hee mahato aur lakshmee ko maanon penshan mil gayee. pahale to donon saare din, subhaagee ke manaa karne par bhee kuchh-na-kuchh karte hee rehte the; par ab unhe pooraa vishraam tha. pahale donon doodh-ghee ko tarasate the. subhaagee ne kuchh rupaye bachaakar ek bhains le lee. boodhe aadamiyon kee jaan to unakaa khana hai. achchhaa khana na mile to woh kis aadhaar par rahen. chaudharee ne bahut khilaafat kiya. kehne lage, ghar kaa kaam yon hee kya kam hai ki too yah nayaa jhanjhat paal rahi hai. subhaagee unhe bahalaane ke lie kehti - daadaa, doodh ke binaa mujhe khana nahin achchhaa lagataa.
lakshmee ne hansakar kahaa- betee, too jhooth kab se bolane lagee. kabhee doodh haath se to chhootee nahin, khaane kee kaun kahe. saaraa doodh ham logon ke pet mein thoons detee hai.
gaanv mein jahaan dekho sabake munh se subhaagee kee taareeph. ladakee nahin devee hai. do maradon kaa kaam bhee karti hai, us par maan-baap kee sevaa bhee kiye jati hai. sajanasinh to kehte, yah us janm kee devee hai.
magar shaayad mahato ko yah sukh bahut din tak bhoganaa na likhaa tha.
saat-aath din se mahato ko jor kaa jvar chadhaa hua tha. deh par kapade kaa taar bhee nahin rehne dete. lakshmee paas baithee ro rahi thi. subhaagee paani liye khadee hai. abhee ek pal pahale mahato ne paani maangaa tha; par jab tak woh paani laave, unakaa jee doob gaya aur haath-paanv thande ho gaye. subhaagee unakee yah dashaa dekhate hee raamoo ke ghar gayee aur bolee- bhaiyaa, chalo, dekho aaj daadaa na jaane kaise hue jate hain. saat din se jvar nahin utaraa.
raamoo ne chaarapaaee par lete-lete kahaa- to kya main daaktar-hakeem hoon ki dekhane chaloon? jab tak achchhe the, tab tak to tum unake gale kaa haar banee huee theen. ab jab marane lage to mujhe bulaane aayee ho!
usee vakt usakee dulhan andar se nikal aayee aur subhaagee se poochhaa- daadaa ko kya hua hai deedee?
subhaagee ke pahale raamoo bol uthaa- hua kya hai, abhee koi mare thode hee jate hain.
subhaagee ne phir usse kuchh na kahaa, seedhe sajanasinh ke paas gayee. usake jaane ke baad raamoo hansakar aurat se bol - triyaacharitr isee ko kehte hain.
aurat - isamen triyaacharitr kee kaun baat hai? chale kyon nahin jate?
raamoo - main nahin jaane kaa. jaise use lekar alag hue the, vaise use lekar rahen. mar bhee jaayen to na jaaoon.
aurat- mar jaayenge to aag dene to jaaoge, tab kahan bhaagoge?
raamoo - kabhee nahin? sab kuchh unakee pyaaree subhaagee kar legee.
aurat - tumhaare rehte woh kyon karne lagee!
raamoo - jaise mere rehte use lekar alag hue aur kaise!
aurat - nahin jee, yah achchhee baat nahin hai. chalo dekh aaven. kuchh bhee ho, baap hee to hain. phir gaanv mein kaun munh dikhaaoge?
raamoo - chup raho, mujhe upadesh mata do.
udhar baaboo sahab ne jyon hee mahato kee haalat sunee, turant subhaagee ke saath bhaage chale aaye. yahan pahunche to mahato kee dashaa aur kharaab ho chukee thi. naadee dekhee to bahut dheemee thi. samajh gaye ki zindagi ke din poore ho gaye. maut kaa aatank chhaayaa hua tha. sajal aankh hokar bole - mahato bhai, kaisaa jee hai?
mahato jaise neend se jaagakar bole- bahut achchhaa hai bhaiyaa! ab to chalne kee belaa hai. subhaagee ke pitaa ab tumheen ho. use tumheen ko saunpe jat hoon.
sajanasinh ne rote hue kahaa- bhaiyaa mahato, ghabadaao mata, bhagavaan ne chaahaa to tum achchhe ho jaaoge. subhaagee ko to mainne hameshaa apanee betee samajhaa hai aur jab tak jioongaa aisaa hee samajhataa rahoongaa. tum nishchint raho. mere rehte subhaagee yaa lakshmee ko koi tirachhee aankh se na dekh sakegaa. aur kuchh ichchhaa ho to woh bhee kah do.
mahato ne vineet netron se dekhakar kahaa- aur kuchh nahin kahoongaa bhaiyaa. bhagavaan tumhe sadaa sukhee rakhe.
sajanasinh - raamoo ko bulaakar laataa hoon. usse jo bhool-chook ho maafee kar do.
mahato - nahin bhaiyaa. us paapee hatyaare kaa munh main nahin dekhanaa chaahataa.
isake baad godaan kee taiyaaree hone lagee.
5
raamoo ko gaanv bhar ne samajhaayaa; par woh antyeshti karne par raajee na hua. kahaa, jis pitaa ne marate samay meraa munh dekhanaa sveekaar na kiya, woh meraa na pitaa hai, na main usakaa betaa hoon.
lakshmee ne daah-kriyaa kee. ina thode se dinon mein subhaagee ne na jaane kaise rupaye jamaa kar liye the ki jab terahee kaa saamaan aane lagaa to gaanvavaalon kee aankhen khul gayeen. baratan, kapade, ghee, shakkar, sabhee saamaan ipharaat se jamaa ho gaye. raamoo dekh-dekh jalataa tha. aur subhaagee use jalaane ke lie sabako yah saamaan dikhaatee thi.
lakshmee ne kahaa- betee, ghar dekhakar kharch karo. ab koi kamaanevaalaa nahin baithaa hai, aap hee kuaan khodanaa aur paani peenaa hai.
subhaagee bolee- baaboojee kaa kaam to dhoom-dhaam se hee hogaa ammaan, chaahe ghar rahe yaa jaay. baaboojee phir thode hee aavenge. main bhaiyaa ko dikhaa denaa chaahatee hoon ki abalaa kya kar sakatee hai. woh samajhate honge ina donon ke kiye kuchh na hogaa. unakaa yah ghamand tod doongee.
lakshmee chup ho rahi. terahee ke din aath gaanv ke braahmanon kaa bhoj hua. chaaron taraph vaah-vaahee mach gayee.
pichhale pahar kaa samay tha; log khana karake chale gaye the. lakshmee thak kar so gayee thi. keval subhaagee bachee huee cheejen uthaa-uthaakar rakh rahi thi ki thaakur sajanasinh ne aakar kahaa- ab tum bhee aaraam karo betee! sabere yah sab kaam kar lenaa.
subhaagee ne kahaa- abhee thakee nahin hoon daadaa. aapane jod liya kul kitane rupaye uthe?
sajanasinh - woh poochhakar kya karogee betee?
'kuchh nahin yon hee poochhatee thi. '
'koi teen sau rupaye uthe honge. '
subhaagee ne sakuchaate hue kahaa- main ina rupayon kee denadaar hoon.
'tumse to main maangataa nahin. mahato mere mitr aur bhai the. unake saath kuchh meraa bhee to dharm hai. '
'aapakee yahee raham kya kam hai ki mere oopar itanaa yakeen kiya, mujhe kaun 300/- detaa? '
sajanasinh sochane lage. is abalaa kee dharm-samajh kaa kaheen vaarapaar bhee hai yaa nahin.
6
lakshmee una striyon mein thi jinake lie pati-viyog jeevan-srot kaa band ho jan hai. pachaas varsh ke chir-sahavaas ke baad ab yah ekaant jeevan usake lie pahaad ho gaya. use ab pataa hua ki meree samajh, meraa bal, meree sumati maano sabase main vanchit ho gayee.
usane kitanee baar eeshvar se vinatee kee thi, mujhe swami ke saamane uthaa lenaa; magar usane yah vinatee sveekaar na kee. maut par apanaa kaaboo nahin, to kya jeevan par bhee kaaboo nahin hai?
woh lakshmee jo gaanv mein apanee samajh ke lie mashahoor thi, jo doosare ko seekh diya karti thi, ab baurahee ho gayee hai. seedhee-see baat karte nahin banatee.
lakshmee kaa daanaa-paani usee din se chhoot gaya. subhaagee ke aagrah par chauke mein jati; magar kaur galaa ke neeche na utarataa. pachaas varsh hue ek din bhee aisaa na hua ki pati ke binaa khaaye khud khaayaa ho. ab us niyam ko kaise tode?
aakhir use khaansee aane lagee. durbalataa ne jald hee khaat par daal diya. subhaagee ab kya kare! thaakur sahab ke rupaye chukaane ke lie dilojaan se kaam karne kee jaroorat thi. yahan maan beemaar pad gayee. agar baahar jaay to maan akelee rehti hai. unake paas baithe to baahar kaa kaam kaun kare. maan kee dashaa dekhakar subhaagee samajh gayee ki inakaa paravaanaa bhee aa pahunchaa. mahato ko bhee to yahee jvar tha.
gaanv mein aur kise phurasat thi ki daud-dhoop kart. sajanasinh donon vakt aate, lakshmee ko dekhate, davaa pilaate, subhaagee ko samajhaate, aur chale jate; magar lakshmee kee dashaa bigadatee jati thi. yahan tak ki pandrahaven din woh bhee sansaar se sidhaar gayee. antim samay raamoo aay aur usake pair chhoonaa chaahataa tha, par lakshmee ne use aisee jhidakee dee ki woh usake paas na jaa sakaa. subhaagee ko usane aasheervaad diya, tumhaaree-jaisee betee paakar tar gayee. meraa kriyaa-karm tumheen karn. meree bhagavaan se yahee arajee hai ki us janm mein bhee tum meree kokh pavitr karo.
maan ke dehaant ke baad subhaagee ke jeevan kaa keval ek lakshy raha gaya sajanasinh ke rupaye chukaanaa. 300/- pitaa ke kriyaa-karm mein lage the. lagabhag 200/- maan ke kaam mein lage. 500/- kaa rin tha aur usakee akelee jaan! magar woh himmat na haaratee thi. teen saal tak subhaagee ne raat ko raat aur din ko din na samajhaa. usakee kaary-shakti aur paurush dekhakar log daanton tale ungalee dabaate the. din bhar khetee-baaree kaa kaam karne ke baad woh raat ko chaar-chaar paseree aat pees daalatee. teesaven din 15/- lekar woh sajanasinh ke paas pahunch jati. isamen kabhee naagaa na padataa. yah maano prakriti kaa atal niyam tha.
ab chaaron ora se usakee sagaaee ke paigaam aane lage. sabhee usake lie munh phailaaye hue the. jisake ghar subhaagee jaayagee, usake bhaagy phir jaayenge. subhaagee yahee jawab detee, abhee woh din nahin aay.
jis din subhaagee ne aakhiree kist chukaaee, us din usakee khushi kaa thikaanaa na tha. aaj usake jeevan kaa kathor vrat pooraa ho gaya.
woh chalne lagee to sajanasinh ne kahaa- betee, tumse meree ek praarthanaa hai. kaho kahoon, kaho na kahoon, magar vachan do ki maanogee.
subhaagee ne kritajn bhaav se dekhakar kahaa- daadaa, aapakee baat na maanoongee to kisakee baat maanoongee. meraa to royaan-royaan aapakaa gulaam hai.
sajanasinh - agar tumhaare man mein yah bhaav hai, to main na kahoongaa. mainne ab tak tumse isalie nahin kahaa, ki tum apne ko meraa denadaar samajh rahi theen. ab rupaye chuk gaye. meraa tumhaare oopar koi ehasaan nahin hai, rattee bhar bhee nahin. bolo kahoon?
subhaagee - aapakee jo hukm ho.
sajanasinh- dekho inakaar na karn, nahin main phir tumhe apanaa munh na dikhaaoongaa.
subhaagee- kya hukm hai?
sajanasinh- meree ichchhaa hai ki tum meree bahoo banakar mere ghar ko pavitr karo. main jaat-paant kaa kaayal hoon, magar tumane mere saare bandhan tod diye. meraa ladakaa tumhaare naam kaa pujaaree hai. tumane use baarahaa dekh hai. bolo, manjoor karti ho?
subhaagee- daadaa, itanaa izzat paakar paagal ho jaaoongee.
sajanasinh- tumhaaraa izzat bhagavaan kar rahe hain betee! tum saakshaat bhagavatee kaa avataar ho.
subhaagee - main to aapako apanaa pitaa samajhatee hoon. aap jo kuchh karenge, mere bhale hee ke lie karenge. aapake hukm ko kaise inakaar kar sakatee hoon.
sajanasinh ne usake maathe par haath rakhakar kahaa- betee, tumhaaraa sohaag amar ho. tumane meree baat rakh lee. mujh-saa bhaagyashaalee sansaar mein aur kaun hogaa?
