# Bade Bhai Sahab

**Munshi Premchand**

1
mere bhai sahab mujhse paanch saal bade the, lekin teen darje aage. unhone bhi usi umr mein padhna shuru kiya tha jab maine shuru kiya; lekin taaleem jaise mahatva ke maamale mein vah jaldeebaajee se kaam lena pasand na karte the. is bhavan ki buniyad khoob mazboot daalanaa chahte the jis par aalishan mahal ban sake. ek saal kaa kaam do saal mein karte the. kabhi-kabhi teen saal bhi lag jaate the. buniyad hi pukhtaa na ho, to makaan kaise paaedaar bane.
main chhota tha, vah bade the. meri umr nau saal ki,vah chaudah saal ke the. unhen meri tambeeh aur nigaraanee kaa pooraa janmasiddh adhikar tha. aur meri shaaleenataa isi mein thi ki unake hukma ko kanoon samajhoon.
vah svabhaav se bade aghyayanasheel the. haradam kitaab khole baithe rehte aur shaayad dimaag ko aaraam dene ke lie kabhi kaapee par, kabhi kitaab ke haashiyon par chidiyon, kutton, balliyo ki tasveeren banaayaa karte then. kabhi-kabhi ek hi naam yaa shabda yaa vaakya das-bees baar likh daalate. kabhi ek sher ko baar-baar sundar akshar se nakal karte. kabhi aisee shabda-rachanaa karte, jisamen na koi arth hot, na koi saamanjasya! masalan ek baar unki kaapee par maine yah ibaarat dekhee-speshal, ameenaa, bhaaiyon-bhaaiyon, dar-asal, bhai-bhai, raagheshyaam, shreeyut raagheshyaam, ek ghante tak—isake baad ek aadamee kaa cheharaa banaa hua tha. maine cheshtaa ki ki is pahelee kaa koi arth nikaaloon; lekin naakaam raha aur usane poochhne kaa saahas na hua. vah navee jamaat mein the, main paanchavee mein. unaki rachanaao ko samajhna mere lie chhota munh badi baat thi.
mera jee padhne mein bilakul na lagta tha. ek ghanta bhi kitaab lekar baithanaa pahaad tha. maukaa paate hi hostal se nikalakar maidaan mein aa jat aur kabhi kankariyaan uchhaalataa, kabhi kaagaj ki titaliyaan udaataa, aur kaheen koi saathee mil gaya to poochhna hi kya kabhi chaaradeevaaree par chadhakar neeche kood rahe hai, kabhi phaatak par vaar, use aage-peechhe chalaate hue motarakaar kaa khushi uthaa rahe hai. lekin kamare mein aate hi bhai sahab kaa raudr roop dekhakar praan sookh jaate. unakaa pahalaa sawal hot-‘kahan then?‘ hameshaa yahi sawal, isi ghvani mein poochhaa jat tha aur isakaa jawab mere paas keval chup tha. na jaane munh se yah baat kyon na nikalatee ki zara baahar khel raha tha. mera chup kah detaa tha ki mujhe apna aparaadh sveekaar hai aur bhai sahab ke lie isake sivaa aur koi ilaaj na tha ki rosh se mile hue shabdon mein mera satkaar karen.
‘is tarah angrezi padhoge, to zindagi-bhar padhte rahoge aur ek harph na aaegaa. angrezi padhna koi hansee-khel nahin hai ki jo chaahe padh le, nahin, airaa-gairaa natthoo-khairaa sabhi angrezi ki viddhaan ho jaate. yahan raat-din aankhe phodanee padatee hai aur khoon jalaanaa padataa hai, jab kahin yah vidhaa aatee hai. aur aatee kya hai, haan, kehne ko aa jati hai. bade-bade viddhaan bhi shuddh angrezi nahin likh sakate, bolna to dur raha. aur main keht hoon, tum kitane ghonghaa ho ki mujhe dekhakar bhi sabak nahin lete. main kitanee mehnat kart hoon, tum apni aankho dekhte ho, agar nahin dekhte, jo yah tumhari aankho kaa kasoor hai, tumhari samajh kaa kasoor hai. itane mele-tamaashe hote hai, mujhe tumne kabhi dekhne jaate dekh hai, roj hi kriket aur haakee maich hote hain. main paas nahin phatakataa. hameshaa padhta raha hoon, us par bhi ek-ek darje mein do-do, teen-teen saal padaa rehta hoon phir tum kaise ummeed karte ho ki tum yon khel-kud mein vakta ganvaakar paas ho jaaoge? mujhe to do-hi-teen saal lagte hain, tum umr-bhar isi darje mein pade sadate rahoge. agar tumhe is tarah umr ganvaanee hai, to banhatar hai, ghar chale jaao aur maje se gullee-dandaa khelo. daadaa ki gaadhee kamaaee ke rupaye kyo barabaad karte ho?’
main yah lataad sunakar aansoo bahaane lagta. jawab hi kya tha. aparaadh to maine kiya, lataad kaun sahe? bhai sahab upadesh ki kalaa mein nipun the. aisee-aisee lagti baaten kehte, aise-aise sookti-baan chalaate ki mere jigar ke tukade-tukade ho jaate aur himmata chhoot jati. is tarah jaan todakar mehnat karne ki shakti main apne mein na paataa tha aur us maayoosee me zara der ke lie main sochne lagta-kyon na ghar chalaa jaaoon. jo kaam mere boote ke baahar hai, usame haath daalakar kyo apni zindagi kharaab karoon. mujhe apna bevakooph rehna manjoor tha; lekin utanee mehnat se mujhe to chakkar aa jat tha. lekin ghante–do ghante baad maayoosee ke baadal phat jaate aur main iraadaa kart ki aage se khoob jee lagaakar padhoongaa. chatapat ek taaim-tebil banaa daalataa. binaa pahale se nakshaa banaae, binaa koi skim taiyaar kie kaam kaise shuroon karoon? taaim-tebil mein, khel-kood ki mad bilakul uda jati. praat:kaal uthanaa, chh: baje munh-haath dho, naashtaa kar padhne baith jan. chh: se aath tak angrezi, aath se nau tak hisaab, nau se saadhe nau tak itihaas, phir khaanaa aur skool. saadhe teen baje skool se vaapas hokar aadhaa ghanntaa aaraam, chaar se paanch tak bhoogol, paanch se chh: tak graamar, aaghaa ghanta hostal ke saamane tahalanaa, saadhe chh: se saat tak angrezi kampojeeshan, phir khaanaa karake aath se nau tak anuvaad, nau se das tak hindee, das se gyaarah tak vividh vishay, phir vishraam.
magar taaim-tebil banaa lena ek baat hai, us par amal karn doosaree baat. pahale hi din se uski avahelanaa shuru ho jati. maidaan ki vah sukhad hariyaalee, havaa ke vah halake-halake jhoke, phutabaal ki uchhal-kood, kabaddee ke vah daanv-ghaat, vaalee-baal ki vah tejee aur phuratee mujhe anajaan aur anirvaay roop se kheech le jati aur wahan jaate hi main sab kuchh bhool jat. vah jaan-levaa taaim-tebil, vah aankhaphod pustake kisee ki yaad na rehti, aur phir bhai sahab ko naseehat aur phajeehat kaa avasar mil jat. main unake saaye se bhaagataa, unki aankho se door rehne ki cheshtaa kart. kamare me is tarah dabe paanv aat ki unhe khabar na ho. unaki najar meri ora uthee aur mere praan nikale. hameshaa sir par nangee talavaar-see latakatee maaloom hoti. phir bhi jaise maut aur vipatti ke beech me bhi aadamee moh aur maayaa ke bandhan mein jakadaa rehta hai, main phatakaar aur ghudakiyaan khaakar bhi khel-kood kaa tiraskaar na kar sakataa.
2
saalaanaa imtahaan hua. bhai sahab phel ho gae, main paas ho gaya aur darje mein pratham aay. mere aur unake beech keval do saal kaa antar raha gaya. jee mein aay, bhai sahab ko aaden haatho loon—aapakee vah ghor tapasyaa kahan gayi? mujhe dekhie, maje se khelataa bhi raha aur darje mein avval bhi hoon. lekin vah itane du:khee aur udaas the ki mujhe unse dillee hamadardee hui aur unake ghaav par namak chhidakane kaa soch hi lajjaaspad jaan padaa. haan, ab mujhe apne oopar kuchh abhimaan hua aur aatmaabhimaan bhi badhaa bhai sahab kaa vaharob mujh par na raha. aajaadee se khel–kood mein shareek hone lagaa. dil mazboot tha. agar unhone phir meri phajeehat ki, to saaph kah doongaa—aapane apna khoon jalaakar kaun-saa teer maar liya. main to khelate-koodate darje mein avval aa gaya. jabaavaseyah hekadee jataane kaasaahas na hone par bhi mere rang-dhang se saaph jaahir hot tha ki bhai sahab kaa vah aatank ab mujh par nahin hai. bhai sahab ne ise bhaanp liya-unki sasahasat samajh badi teevr thi aur ek din jab mai bhor kaa sara samay gullee-dande ki bhent karake theek khaanaa ke samay lautaa, to bhai saaib ne maano talavaar kheech lee aur mujh par toot pade-dekhta hoon, is saal paas ho gae aur darje mein avval aa gae, to tumhe dimaag ho gaya hai; magar bhaaeejaan, ghamand to bade-bade kaa nahin raha, tumhari kya hastee hai, itihaas mein raavan kaa haal to padha hi hogaa. usake charitr se tumne kaun-saa upadesh liya? yaa yo hi padh gae? mahaj imtahaan paas kar lena koi cheej nahin, asal cheej hai samajh kaa vikaas. jo kuchh padho, uska abhipraay samajho. raavan bhoomandal kaa svaamee tha. aise raajo ko chakravartee kehte hai. aajakal angarejo ke raajya kaa vistaar bahut badhaa hua hai, par inhe chakravartee nahin kah sakate. sansaar mein aneko raashtara angarejon kaa aadhipatya sveekaar nahin karte. bilakul svaadheen hain. raavan chakravartee raajaa tha. sansaar ke sabhi maheep use kar dete the. bade-bade devataa uski gulaamee karte the. aag aur paani ke devataa bhi usake daas the; magar uska ant kya hua, ghamand ne uska naam-nishaan tak mitaa diya, koi use ek chilloo paani denevaalaa bhi na bachaa. aadamee jo kukarm chaahe karen; par abhimaan na kare, itaraae nahin. abhimaan kiya aur deen-duniyaa se gaya.
shaitaan kaa haal bhi padha hi hogaa. use yah anumaan hua tha ki eeshvar kaa usse badhakar sachchaa bhakta koi hai hi nahin. anta mein yah hua ki svarg se narak mein dhakel diya gaya. shaaheroom ne bhi ek baar ahankaar kiya tha. bheekh maang-maangakar mar gaya. tumne to abhi keval ek darja paas kiya hai aur abhi se tumhara sir phir gaya, tab to tum aage badh chuke. yah samajh lo ki tum apni mehnat se nahin paas hue, andhe ke haath bater lag gayi. magar bater keval ek baar haath lag sakatee hai, baar-baar nahin. kabhi-kabhi gullee-dande mein bhi andhaa chot nishaanaa pad jat hai. usse koi kaamayaab khilaadee nahin ho jat. kaamayaab khilaadee vah hai, jisakaa koi nishaan khaalee na jaae.
mere phel hone par na jaao. mere darje mein aaoge, to daanto paseenaa aayagaa. jab alajabaraa aur jaamentree ke lohe ke chane chabaane padenge aur ingalistaan kaa itihaas padhna padengaa! baadashaahon ke naam yaad rakhna aasaan nahin. aath-aath henaree ko gujare hai kaun-saa kaand kis henaree ke samay hua, kya yah yaad kar lena aasaan samajhte ho? henaree saataven ki jagah henaree aathavaan likhaa aur sab nambar gaayab! saphaachat. sirph bhi na milagaa, siphar bhi! ho kis khyaal mein! darajano to jemsa hue hain, darajano viliyam, kodiyon chaarlsa dimaag chakkar khaane lagta hai. aandhee rog ho jat hai. ina abhaago ko naam bhi na judate the. ek hi naam ke peechhe doyam, teyam, chahaaram, pancham nagaate chale gae. muchhase poochhte, to das laakh naam bataa detaa.
aur jaametree to bas khudaa ki panaah! a ba ja ki jagah a ja ba likh diya aur saare nambar kat gae. koi ina nirdayee mumatahinon se nahin poochhta ki aakhir a ba ja aur a ja ba mein kya phark hai aur vyarthakee baat ke lie kyo chhaatro kaa khoon karte ho daal-bhaat-rotee khaayee yaa bhaat-daal-rotee khaayee, isamen kya rakhaa hai; magar ina pareekshako ko kya paravaah! vah to wahi dekhte hai, jo pustak mein likhaa hai. chahte hain ki ladake akshar-akshar rat daale. aur isi ratant kaa naam shikshaa rakh chhodaa hai aur aakhir ina be-sir-pair ki baato ke padhne se kya phaayadaa?
is rekhaa par vah lamba giraa do, to aadhaar lamba se duganaa hogaa. poochhie, isase kaam? duganaa nahin, chaugunaa ho jaae, yaa aadhaa hi rahe, meri balaa se, lekin pareekshaa mein paas hona hai, to yah sab khuraaphaat yaad karanee padegee. kah diya-‘samay ki paabandee’ par ek nibandha likho, jo chaar panno se kam na ho. ab aap kaapee saamane khole, kalam haath mein liye, usake naam ko roie.
kaun nahin jaanataa ki samay ki paabandee bahut achchhi baat hai. isase aadamee ke jeevan mein sanyam aa jat hai, doosaro kaa us par sneh hone lagta hai aur usake karobaar mein unnati hoti hai; zara-see baat par chaar panne kaise likhen? jo baat ek vaakya mein kahin jaa sake, use chaar panne mein likhne ki jaroorat? main to ise himaakat samajhta hoon. yah to samay ki kiphaayat nahin, balki uska duroopayog hai ki vyarth mein kisee baat ko thoons diya. ham chahte hai, aadamee ko jo kuchh kehna ho, chatapat kah de aur apni raah le. magar nahin, aapako chaar panne rangane padenge, chaahe jaise likhie aur panne bhi poore phulsakep aakaar ke. yah chhaatro par atyaachaar nahin to aur kya hai? anarth to yah hai ki kahaa jat hai, sankshep mein likho. samay ki paabandee par sankshep mein ek nibandha likho, jo chaar panno se kam na ho. theek! sankshep mein chaar panne hue, nahin shaayad sau-do sau panne likhavaate. tej bhi daudie aur dheere-dheere bhi. hai ultee baat yaa nahin? ladakaa bhi itanee-see baat samajh sakataa hai, lekin ina adhyaapako ko itanee tameej bhi nahin. us par daavaa hai ki ham adhyaapak hai. mere darje mein aaoge laalaa, to ye saare paapad belane padenge aur tab aate-daal kaa bhaav maaloom hogaa. is darje mein avval aa gae ho, vo jameen par paanv nahin rakhte isalie mera kehna maanie. laakh phel ho gaya hoon, lekin tumse bada hoon, sansaar kaa mujhe tumse zyada anubhav hai. jo kuchh keht hoon, use girah baandhie nahin pachhataaenge.
skool kaa samay paas tha, nahin ishvar jaane, yah upadesh-maalaa kab samaapta hoti. khaanaa aaj mujhe nissvaad-saa lag raha tha. jab paas hone par yah tiraskaar ho raha hai, to phel ho jaane par to shaayad praan hi le lie jaaen. bhai sahab ne apne darje ki padhaaee kaa jo bhayankar chitr kheechaa tha; usane mujhe bhayabheet kar diya. kaise skool chhodakar ghar nahin bhaagaa, yahi taajjub hai; lekin itane tiraskaar par bhi pustakon mein meri aroochi jyo-ki-tyon banee rahi. khel-kood kaa koi avasar haath se na jaane detaa. padhta bhi tha, magar bahut kam. bas, itanaa ki roj kaa taaska pooraa ho jaae aur darje mein jaleel na hona paden. apne oopar jo vishvaas paidaa hua tha, vah phir lupta ho gaya aur phir choro kaa-saa jeevan katane lagaa.
3
phir saalaanaa imtahaan hua, aur kuchh aisaa sanyog hua ki mai iphra paas hua aur bhai sahab phir phel ho gae. maine bahut mehnat na ki par na jaane, kaise darje mein avval aa gaya. mujhe khud acharaj hua. bhai sahab ne praanaantak parishram kiya tha. kors kaa ek-ek shabda chaat gaye the; das baje raat tak idhar, chaar baje bhor se ubhar, chh: se saadhe nau tak skool jaane ke pahale. mudraa kaantiheen ho gayi thi, magar bechaare phel ho gae. mujhe una par dayaa aatee thi. nateejaa sunaayaa gaya, to vah ro pade aur main bhi rone lagaa. apne paas hone vaalee khushi aadhee ho gayi. main bhi phel ho gaya hot, to bhai sahab ko itanaa du:kha na hot, lekin vidhi ki baat kaun taale?
mere aur bhai sahab ke beech mein ab keval ek darje kaa antar aur raha gaya. mere man mein ek kutil bhaavanaa uday hui ki kahin bhai sahab ek saal aur phel ho jaaen, to mai unake baraabar ho jaaoon, iphar vah kis aadhaar par meri phajeehat kar sakege, lekin maine is kameene soch ko dil se balapoorvak nikaal daalaa. aakhir vah mujhe mere hit ke soch se hi to daantate hain. mujhe us vakta apriy lagta hai avashya, magar yah shaayad unake upadeshon kaa hi asar ho ki main danaanad paas hot jat hoon aur itane achchhe nambaron se.
abakee bhai sahab bahut-kuchh narm pad gae the. kaee baar mujhe daantane kaa avasar paakar bhi unhone dheeraj se kaam liya. shaayad ab vah khud samajhne lage the ki mujhe daantane kaa adhikar unhe nahin raha; yaa raha to bahut kam. meri svachchhandataa bhi badhee. main unaki sahishnutaa kaa galat phaayadaa uthaane lagaa. mujhe kuchh aisee dhaaranaa hui ki main to paas hi ho jaaoongaa, padhoo yaa na padhoon meri takadeer balavaan hai, isalie bhai sahab ke dar se jo thodaa-bahut badh liya kart tha, vah bhi band hua. mujhe kanakaue udaane kaa nayaa shauk paidaa ho gaya tha aur ab sara samay patangabaajee hi ki bhent hot tha, iphar bhi main bhai sahab kaa adab kart tha, aur unki najar bachaakar kanakaue udaataa tha. maanjhaa dena, kanne baandhanaa, patang toornaament ki taiyaariyaan aadi samasyaaen ab gupta roop se hal ki jati theen. bhai sahab ko yah shak na karne dena chahta tha ki unakaa sammaan aur lihaaj meri najaro se kam ho gaya hai.
ek din sandhyaa samay hostal se door mai ek kanakauaa lootane bantahaashaa daudaa jaa raha tha. aankhe aasamaan ki ora theen aur man us aakaashagaamee pathik ki ora, jo mand gati se jhoomataa patan ki ora chalaa jaa raha tha, maano koi aatmaa svarg se nikalakar virakta man se nae sanskaar grahan karne jaa rahi ho. baalakon ki ek pooree senaa lagge aur jhadadaar baans liye unakaa svaagat karne ko daudee aa rahi thi. kisee ko apne aage-peechhe ki khabar na thi. sabhi maano us patang ke saath hi aakaash mein uda rahe the, jahon sab kuchh samatal hai, na motarakaare hai, na traam, na gaadiyaan.
sahasaa bhai sahab se meri muthabhed ho gayi, jo shaayad baajaar se laut rahe the. unhone wahi mera haath pakad liya aur ugrabhaav se bole-ina baajaaree laundo ke saath dhele ke kanakaue ke lie daudate tumhen sharm nahin aatee? tumhen isakaa bhi kuchh lihaaj nahin ki ab neechee jamaat mein nahin ho, balki aathaveen jamaat mein aa gaye ho aur mujhse keval ek darja neeche ho. aakhir aadamee ko kuchh to apni pojeeshan kaa khyaal karn chaahie. ek jamaanaa tha ki ki log aathavaan darja paas karake naayab tahaseeladaar ho jaate the. main kitane hi midalachiyon ko jaanataa hoon, jo aaj avval darje ke diptee majistret yaa suparitendent hai. kitane hi aathavee jamaaat vaale hamaare leedar aur khabar-patro ke sampaadak hai. baden-baden viddhaan unki maatahti mein kaam karte hai aur tum usi aathaven darje mein aakar baajaaree laundon ke saath kanakaue ke lie daud rahe ho. mujhe tumhari is kamaakalee par du:kha hot hai. tum jaheen ho, isamen shak nahin: lekin vah jehan kis kaam kaa, jo hamaare aatmagaurav ki hatyaa kar daale? tum apne din mein samajhte hoge, main bhai sahab se mahaj ek darjaa neeche hoon aur ab unhe mujhako kuchh kehne kaa hak nahin hai; lekin yah tumhari galatee hai. main tumse paanch saal bada hoon aur chaahe aaj tum meri hi jamaaat mein aa jaao–aur pareekshakon kaa yahi haal hai, to nisshak agale saal tum mere samakaksh ho jaaoge aur shaayad ek saal baad tum mujhse aage nikal jaao-lekin mujhamen aur jo paanch saal kaa antar hai, use tum kya, khudaa bhi nahin mitaa sakataa. main tumse paanch saal bada hoon aur hameshaa rahoongaa. mujhe duniyaa kaa aur zindagi kaa jo tajarabaa hai, tum uski baraabaree nahin kar sakate, chaahe tum ema. e., dee. phil. aur dee. lit. hi kyo na ho jaao. samajh kitaaben padhne se nahin aatee hai. hamaaree ammaa ne koi darja paas nahin kiya, aur daadaa bhi shaayad paanchavee jamaaat ke aage nahin gaye, lekin ham dono chaahe sari duniyaa ki vidhaa padh le, ammaa aur daadaa ko hamen samajhaane aur sudhaarane kaa adhikar hameshaa rahegaa. keval isalie nahin ki ve hamaare janmadaataa hai, balki isalie ki unhe duniyaa kaa hamse zyada jatarabaa hai aur rahegaa. amerikaa mein kis jarah ki raajya-vyavastha hai aur aathave henaree ne kitane vivaah kiye aur aakaash mein kitane nakshatr hai, yah baate chaahe unhe na maaloom ho, lekin hajaaron aisee aate hai, jinakaa jnaan unhe hamse aur tumse zyada hai.
daiv na karen, aaj main beemaar ho aaoon, to tumhaare haath-paanv phool jaaegen. daadaa ko taar dene ke sivaa tumhe aur kuchh na soojhengaa; lekin tumhari jagah par daadaa ho, to kisee ko taar na den, na ghabaraaen, na badahavaas hon. pahale khud maraj pahachaanakar ilaaj karenge, usamen kaamayaab na hue, to kisee daanktar ko bulaayegen. beemaaree to khair badi cheej hai. ham-tum to itanaa bhi nahin jaanate ki mahine-bhar kaa mahine-bhar kaise chale. jo kuchh daadaa bhejate hai, use ham bees-baaees tak rkhach kar daalate hai aur paise-paise ko mohataaj ho jaate hai. naashtaa band ho jat hai, dhobee aur naaee se munh churaane lagte hai; lekin jitanaa aaj ham aur tum rkhach kar rahe hai, usake aadhe mein daadaa ne apni umr kaa bada bhaag ijjat aur nekanaamee ke saath nibhaayaa hai aur ek kutumba kaa paalan kiya hai, jisame sab milaakar nau aadamee the. apne hedamaastar sahab hi ko dekho. ema. e. hain ki nahin, aur yahaa ke ema. e. nahin, aakyaphord ke. ek hajaar rupaye paate hai, lekin unake ghar intajaam kaun kart hai? unki boodhee maan. hedamaastar sahab ki digree yahan bekaar ho gayi. pahale khud ghar kaa intajaam karte the. kharch pooraa na padataa tha. karajadaar rehte the. jab se unki maataajee ne intazaam apne haath me le liya hai, jaise ghar mein lakshmee aa gayi hai. to bhaaeejaan, yah zaroor dil se nikaal daalo ki tum mere paas aa gaye ho aur ab svatantr ho. mere dekhte tum beraah nahin chal paaoge. agar tum yon na maanoge, to main (thappad dikhaakar) isakaa prayog bhi kar sakataa hoon. main jaanataa hoon, tumhen meri baaten jahar lag rahi hai.
main unki is naee yukti se natamastak ho gaya. mujhe aaj sachamuch apni laghutaa kaa anubhav hua aur bhai sahab ke prati mere tam mein shraddhaa utpanna hueen. maine sajal aankhon se kahaa-haragij nahin. aap jo kuchh pharamaa rahe hai, vah bilakul sach hai aur aapako kehne kaa adhikar hai.
bhai sahab ne mujhe gale lagaa liya aur baal-kanakaae udaan ko manaa nahin kart. mera jee bhi lalachaataa hai, lekin kya karoon, khud beraah chaloon to tumhari rakshaa kaise karoon? yah karttavy bhi to mere sir par hai.
sanyog se usi vakt ek kataa hua kanakauaa hamaare oopar se gujaraa. uski dor latak rahi thi. ladakon kaa ek gol peechhe-peechhe daudaa chalaa aat tha. bhai sahab lanbe hain hi, uchhalakar uski dor pakad lee aur betahaashaa hotal ki taraph daude. main peechhe-peechhe daud raha tha.
