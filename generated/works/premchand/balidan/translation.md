# Balidan

**Munshi Premchand**

manushy ki aarthik avasthaa kaa sabase zyada asar usake naam par padataa hai. mauje belaa ke mangaroo thaakur jab se kaansatebal ho gae hain, inakaa naam mangalasinh ho gaya hai. ab unhen koi mangaroo kehne kaa saahas nahin kar sakataa. kalloo aheer ne jab se halake ke thaanedaar sahab se mitrataa kar lee hai aur gaanv kaa mukhiyaa ho gaya hai, uska naam kaalikaadeen ho gaya hai. ab use kalloo kahen to aankhen laal-peelee kart hai. isi tarah harakhachandr kuramee ab harakhoo ho gaya hai. aaj se bees saal pahale usake yahan shakkar banatee thi, kaee hal ki khetee hoti thi aur kaarobaar khoob phailaa hua tha. lekin videshee shakkar ki aamadanee ne use matiyaamet kar diya. dheer-dheere kaarakhaanaa toot gaya, jameen toot gayi, gaahak toot gae aur vah bhi toot gaya. sattar varsh kaa boodhaa, jo takiyedaar maache par baithaa hua naariyal piyaa kart, ab sir par tokaree liye khaad phenkane jat hai. lekin usake munh par ab bhi ek tarah ki ganbheerataa, baatacheet mein ab bhi ek tarah ki akad, chaal-dhaal se ab bhi ek tarah kaa svaabhimaan bharaa hua hai. is par kaal ki gati kaa prabhaav nahin padaa. rassee jal gayi, par bal nahin tootaa. bhale din manushy ke charitr par hameshaa ke lie apna chihn chhod jaate hain. harakhoo ke paas ab keval paanch beeghaa jameen hai, keval do bail hain. ek hi hal ki khetee hoti hai.

lekin panchaayaton mein, aapas ki kalah mein, uski sammati ab bhi izzat ki nazar se dekhee jati hai. vah jo baat keht hai, belaag keht hai aur gaanv ke anapadhe unake saamane munh nahin khol sakate.
harakhoo ne apne jeevan mein kabhi davaa nahin khaayee thi. vah beemaar zaroor padataa, kuaanr maas mein maleriyaa se kabhi na bachta tha. lekin das-paanch din mein vah binaa davaa khaae hi changaa ho jat tha. is varsh kaartik mein beemaar padaa aur yah samajhakar ki achchha to hi jaaoongaa, usane kuchh paravaah na ki. lekin ab ki jvar maut kaa paravaanaa lekar chalaa tha. ek saptaah beetaa, doosaraa saptaah beetaa, pooraa mahina beet gaya, par harakhoo chaarapaaee se na uthaa. ab use davaa ki jaroorat maaloom hui. uska ladakaa giradhaaree kabhi neem ki seekhe pilaataa, kabhi gurch kaa sat, kabhi gadaapooranaa ki jad. par ina aushadhiyon se koi phaayadaa na hot tha. harakhoo ko yakeen ho gaya ki ab sansaar se chalne ke din aa gae.
ek din mangalasinh use dekhne gae. bechaaraa tootee khaat par padaa raam naam jap raha tha. mangalasinh ne kahaa- baabaa, binaa davaa khaae achchhe na honge; kunain kyon nahin khaate? harakhoo ne udaaseen bhaav se kahaa- to lete aan.

doosare din kaalikaadeen ne aakar kahaa- baabaa, do-chaar din koi davaa khaa lo. ab tumhari javaanee ki deh thode hai ki binaa davaa-darpan ke achchhe ho jaaoge.
harakhoo ne usi mand bhaav se kahaa- to lete aan. lekin rogee ko dekh aan ek baat hai, davaa laakar dena use doosaree baat hai. pahalee baat shishtaachaar se hoti hai, doosaree sachchee samavedanaa se. na mangalasinh ne khabar lee, na kaalikaadeen ne, na kisee teesare hi ne. harakhoo daalaan mein khaat par padaa rehta. mangal sinh kabhi najar aa jaate to keht-bhaiyaa, vah davaa nahin laae?
mangalasinh kataraakar nikal jaate. kaalikaadeen dikhaaee dete, to unse bhi yahi sawal kart. lekin vah bhi najar bachaa jaate. yaa to use soojhta hi nahin tha ki davaa paison ke binaa nahin aatee, yaa vah paison ko bhi jaan se priy samajhta tha, athavaa jeevan se niraash ho gaya tha. usane kabhi davaa ke daam ki baat nahin ki. davaa na aayee. uski dashaa dinondin bigadatee gayi. yahan tak ki paanch mahine takaleeph bhogane ke baad usane theek holee ke din shareer tyaag diya. giradhaaree ne uska shav badi dhoomadhaam ke saath nikaalaa! kriyaakarm bade hausale se kiya. kaee gaanv ke braahmanon ko nimantrit kiya.
belaa mein holee na manaayee gayi, na abeer na gulaal udee, na daphalee bajee, na bhang ki naaliyaan baheen. kuchh log man mein harakhoo ko kosate zaroor the ki is buddhe ko aaj hi maranaa tha; do-chaar din baad marataa.
lekin itanaa nirlajj koi na tha ki shok mein khushi manaataa. vah shareer nahin tha, jahaan koi kisee ke kaam mein shareek nahin hot, jahaan padosee ko rone-peetane ki aavaaj hamaare kaanon tak nahin pahunchti.
harakhoo ke khet gaanvavaalon ki najar par chadhe hue the. paanchon beeghaa jameen kuen ke paas, khaad-paans se ladee hui mend-baandh se theek thi. unamen teen-teen phasalen paidaa hoti theen. harakhoo ke marate hi una par chaaron ora se dhaave hone lage. giradhaaree to kriyaa-karm mein phansaa hua tha. udhar gaanv ke manachale kisaan laalaa onkaaranaath ko chain na lene dete the, najaraane ki badi-badi rakamen pesh ho rahi theen. koi saal-bhar kaa lagaan dene par taiyaar tha, koi najaraane ki doonee rakam kaa dastaavej likhne par tulaa hua tha. lekin onkaaranaath sabako taalate rehte the. unakaa soch tha ki giradhaaree ke baap ne kheton ko bees varsh tak jotaa hai, isalie giradhaaree kaa hak sabase zyada hai. vah agar doosaron se kam bhi najaraanaa de, to khet usi ko dene chaahie. astu, jab giradhaaree kriyaa-karm se nivritt ho gaya aur usse poochhaa- khetee ke baare mein kya kehte ho?
giradhaaree ne rokar kahaa- sarakaar, inheen kheton hi kaa to aasaraa hai, jotoongaa nahin to kya karoongaa.
onkaaranaath- nahin, zaroor joto khet tumhaare hain. main tumse chhodane ko nahin keht. harakhoo ne use bees saal tak jotaa. una par tumhara hak hai. lekin tum dekhte ho, ab jameen ki dar kitanee badh gayi hai. tum aath rupaye beeghe par jotate the, mujhe das rupaye mil rahe hain aur najaraane ke sau alag. tumhaare saath riyaasat karake lagaan wahi rakhta hoon, par najaraane ke rupaye tumhen dene padenge.
giradhaaree- sarakaar, mere ghar mein to is samay rotiyon kaa bhi thikaanaa nahin hai. itane rupaye kahan se laaoongaa? jo kuchh jamaa-jathaa thi, daadaa ke kaam mein uth gayi. anaaj khalihaan mein hai. lekin daadaa ke beemaar ho jaane se upaj bhi achchhi nahin hui. rupaye kahan se laaoon?
onkaraanaath- yah sach hai, lekin main isase zyada riaayat nahin kar sakataa.
giradhaaree-nahin sarakaar! aisaa na kahie. nahin to ham binaa maare mar jaaenge. aap bade hokar kehte hain, to main bail-bachhiyaa bechakar pachaas rupayaa laa sakataa hoon. isase beshee ki himmat meri nahin padatee.
onkaaranaath chidhakar bole- tum samajhte hoge ki ham ye rupaye lekar apne ghar mein rakh lete hain aur chain ki banshee bajaate hain. lekin hamaare oopar jo kuchh gujaratee hai, ham bhi jaanate hain. kaheen yah chandaa, kaheen vah chandaa; kaheen yah najar, kaheen vah najar, kaheen yah inaam, kaheen vah inaam. inake maare kachoomar nikal jat hai. bade din mein saikadon rupaye daaliyon mein uda jaate hain. jise daalee na do, wahi munh phulaataa hai. jin cheejon ke lie ladake tarasakar raha jaate hain, unhen baahar se mangavaakar daaliyaan sajaataa hoon. us par kabhi kaanoonagon aa gae, kabhi tahaseeladaar, kabhi diptee sahab kaa lashkar aa gaya. sab mere mehamaan hote hain. agar na karoon to nakkoo banoon aur sabakee aankhon mein kaantaa ban jaaoon. saal mein hajaar baarah sau modee ko isi rasad-khuraak ke mad mein dene padate hain. yah sab kahan se aaen? bas, yahi jee chahta hai ki chhodakar chalaa jaaoon. lekin hamen paramaatmaa ne isalie banaayaa hai ki ek se rupayaa sataakar len aur doosare ko ro-rokar den, yahi hamaaraa kaam hai. tumhaare saath itanee riyaasat kar raha hoon. magar tum itanee riyaasat par bhi khush nahin hote to hari ichchhaa. nazaraane mein ek paise ki bhi riaayat na hogee. agar ek haphte ke andar rupae daakhil karoge to khet jotane paaoge, nahin to nahin. main koi doosaraa intazaam kar doongaa.

lekin subhaagee yon chupachaap baithanevaalee aurat na thi. vah gussaa se bharee hui kaalikaadeen ke ghar gayi. aur uski aurat ko khoob lathedaa- kal kaa baanee aaj kaa seth, khet jotane chale hain. dekhen, kaun mere khet mein hal le jat hai? apna aur uska lohoo ek kar doon. padosin ne uska paksh liya, to kya gareebon ko kuchalate phirenge? subhaagee ne samajhaa, maine maidaan maar liya. uska chitt bahut shaant ho gaya. lekin wahi vaayu, jo paani mein lahare paidaa karti hain, vrikshon ki jad se ukhaad daalatee hai. subhaagee to padosiyon ki panchaayat mein apne dukhade rotee aur kaalikaadeen ki aurat se chhed-chhed ladatee.
idhar giradhaaree apne dvaar par baithaa hua sochta, ab mera kya haal hogaa? ab yah jeevan kaise kategaa? ye ladake kisake dvaar par jaaenge? majadooree kaa soch karte hi uska dil vyaakul ho jat. itane dinon tak svaadheenataa aur izzat ko sukh bhogane ke baad adham chaakaree ki sharan lene ke badale vah mar jan achchha samajhta tha. vah ab tak grihasth tha, uski gananaa gaanv ke bhale aadamiyon mein thi, use gaanv ke maamale mein bolne kaa adhikar tha. usake ghar mein paisaa na tha, par maan tha. naaee, kumhaar, purohit, bhaat, chaukeedaar ye sab uska munh taakate the. ab yah maryaadaa kahan? ab kaun uski baat poochhegaa? kaun usake dvaar aaegaa? ab use kisee ke baraabar baithane kaa, kisee ke beech mein bolne kaa hak nahin raha. ab use pet ke lie doosaron ki gulaamee karanee padegee. ab pahar raat rahe, kaun bailon ko naand mein lagaaegaa. vah din ab kahan, jab geet gaakar hal chalaataa tha. chotee kaa paseenaa edee tak aat tha, par zara bhi thakaavat na aatee thi. apne lahalahaate hue khet ko dekhakar phoolaa na samaataa tha. khalihaan mein anaaj kaa dher saamane rakhe hue apne ko raajaa samajhta tha. ab anaaj ko tokare bhar-bharakar kaun laaegaa? ab khetee kahan? bakhaar kahan?

yahi sochte-sochte giradhaaree ki aankhon se aansoo ki jhadee lag jati thi. gaanv ke do-chaar sajjan, jo kaalikaadeen se jalate the, kabhi-kabhi giradhaaree ko tasallee dene aay karte the, par vah unse bhi khulakar na bolta. use maaloom hot tha ki main sabakee najaron mein gir gaya hoon.
agar koi samajhta tha ki tumne kriyaa-karm mein bekaar itane rupaye udaa diye, to use bahut du:kha hot. vah apne us kaam par zara bhi na pachhataataa. mere bhaagy mein jo kuchh likhaa hai vah hogaa, par daadaa ke rin se to urin ho gaya; unhone apni zindagi mein chaar ko khilaakar khaayaa. kya marane ke peechhe unhen pinde-paani ko tarasaataa?
is tarah teen maas beet gae aur aashaadh aa pahunchaa. aakaash mein ghataaen aayeen, paani giraa, kisaan hal-jue theek karne lage. badhaee halon ki marammat karne lagaa. giradhaaree paagal ki tarah kabhi ghar ke bheetar jat, kabhi baahar jat, apne halon ko nikaal dekhta, isakee muthiyaa toot gayi hai, isakee phaal dheelee ho gayi hai, jue mein sailaa nahin hai. dekhte-dekhte vah ek pal apne ko bhool gaya. daudaa hui badhaee ke yahan gaya aur bol- rajjoo, mere haal bhi bigade hue hai, chalo banaa do. rajjoo ne uski ora karun-bhaav se dekh aur apna kaam karne lagaa. giradhaaree ko hosh aa gaya, neend se chauk padaa, pachhataavaa se uska sir jhuk gaya, aankhen bhar aayeen. chupachaap ghar chalaa aay.

gaanv mein chaaron ora halachal machee hui thi. koi san ke beej khojataa phirataa tha, koi jameendaar ke chaupaal se dhaan ke beej liye aat tha, kaheen salaah hoti, kis khet mein kya bonaa chaahie. giradhaaree ye baaten sunataa aur jalaheen machhalee ki tarah tadapataa tha.

ek din shaam samay giradhaaree khadaa apne bailon ko khujalaa raha tha ki mangalasinh aaye aur idhar-udhar ki baaten karake bole- goee ko baandhakar kab tak khilaaoge? nikaal kyon nahin dete?
giradhaaree ne malin bhaav se kahaa-haan, koi gaahak aaye to nikaal doon.
mangalasinh- ek gaahak to hameen hain, hameen ko de do.
giradhaaree abhi kuchh jawab na dene paayaa tha ki tulasee baniyaa aay aur garajakar bol- giradhaaree! tumhen rupaye dene hain ki nahin, vaisaa kaho. teen mahine se heel-havaalaa karte chale aate ho. ab kaun khetee karte ho ki tumhari phasal ko agore baith rahen?
giradhaaree ne deenataa se kahaa-saah, jaise itane dinon maane ho, aaj aur maan jaao. kal tumhari ek-ek kaudee chukaa doongaa.
mangal aur tulasee ke ishaaron se baaten ki aur tulasee bhunabhunaataa hua chalaa gaya. tab giradhaaree mangalasinh se bol- tum inhen le lo, to ghar-ke-ghar hi mein raha jaaoon. kabhi-kabhi aankh se dekh to liya karoongaa.
mangalasinh- mujhe abhi to koi aisaa kaam nahin, lekin ghar par salaah karoongaa.
giradhaaree- mujhe tulasee ke rupaye dene hai, nahin to khilaane ko to bhoosaa hai.
mangalasinh- yah bada badamaash hai, kaheen naalish na kar de.
aasaan dil giradhaaree dhamakee mein aa gaya. kaaryakushal mangalasinh ko sastaa saudaa karne kaa achchha suavasar milaa. 80 rupaye ki jodee 60 rupaye mein theek kar lee.
giradhaaree ne ab tak bailon ko na jaane kis ummeed se baandhakar khilaayaa tha. aaj ummeed kaa vah kalpit sootr bhi toot gaya. mangalasinh giradhaaree ki khaat par baithe rupaye gin rahe the aur giradhaaree bailon ke paas vishaadamay netron se unake munh ki ora taak raha tha. aah! ye mere kheton ke kamaane vaale, mere jeevan ke aadhaar, mere annadaataa, meri maan-maryaadaa ki rakshaa karne vaale, jinake lie pahar raat se uthakar chhaantee kaatataa tha, jinake khalee-daane ki phikr apne khaane se zyada rehti thi, jinake lie sara din-bhar hariyaalee ukhaadaa kart tha, ye meri ummeed ki do aankhen, mere iraade ke do taare, mere achchhe dinon ke do chihn, mere do haath, ab mujhse vidaa ho rahe hain.

jab mangalasinh ne rupaye ginakar rakh die aur bailon ko le chale, tab giradhaaree unake kandhon par sir rakhakar khoob phoot-phootakar royaa. jaisaa kanyaa maayake se vidaa hote samay maan-baap ke pairon ko nahin chhodatee, usi tarah giradhaaree ina bailon ko na chhodataa tha. subhaagee bhi daalaan mein padee ro rahi thi aur chhota ladakaa mangalasinh ko ek baans ki chhadee se maar raha tha!
raat ko giradhaaree ne kuchh nahin khaayaa. chaarapaaee par padaa raha. praat:kaal subhaagee chilam bharakar le gayi, to vah chaarapaaee par na tha. usane samajhaa, kaheen gaye honge. lekin jab do-teen ghadee din chadh aay aur vah na lautaa, to usane ronaa-dhonaa shuru kiya. gaanv ke log jamaa ho gae, chaaron ora khoj hone lagee, par giradhaaree kaa pataa na chalaa.
giradhaaree udaas aur niraash hokar ghar aay. 100 rupaye kaa intazaam karn usake kaaboo ke baahar tha. sochne lagaa, agar donon bail bech doon to khet hi lekar kya karoongaa? ghar bechoon to yahan lene vaalaa hi kaun hai? aur phir baap daadon kaa naam doobataa hai. chaar-paanch ped hain, lekin unhen bechakar 25 rupaye yaa 30 rupaye se zyaadaa na milenge. udhaar loon to detaa kaun hai? abhi baniye ke 50 rupaye sir par chadh hain. vah ek paisaa bhi na degaa. ghar mein gahane bhi to nahin hain, nahin unheen ko bechta. le dekar ek hansalee banavaayee thi, vah bhi baniye ke ghar padee hui hai. saal-bhar ho gaya, chhudaane ki naubat na aayee. giradhaaree aur uski aurat subhaagee donon hi isi phikr mein pade rehte, lekin koi upaay na soojhta tha. giradhaaree ko khaanaa-peenaa achchha na lagta, raat ko neend na aatee. kheton ke nikalne kaa dhyaan aate hi usake dil mein hook-see uthane lagti. haay! vah bhoomi jise hamne varshon jotaa, jise khaad se paataa, jisamen meden rakheen, jisakee meden banaayee, uska majaa ab doosaraa uthaaegaa.

ve khet giradhaaree ke jeevan kaa ansh ho gae the. unki ek-ek angul bhoomi usake rakt mein rangee hui thi. unake ek-ek paramaanu usake paseene se tar ho raha tha.
unake naam uski jihvaa par usi tarah aate the, jis tarah apne teenon bachchon ke. koi chaubeeso tha, koi baaeeso tha, koi naalabelaa, koi talaiyaavaalaa. ina naamon ke smaran hote hi kheton kaa chitr uski aankhon ke saamane khinch jat tha. vah ina kheton ki charchaa is, tarah kart, maano ve sajeev hain. maano usake bhale-bure ke saathee hain. usake jeevan ki sari aashaaen, sari ichchhaaen, saare manasoobe, sari mithaaiyaan, saare havaaee kile, inheen kheton par avalambit the.

inake binaa vah jeevan ki kalpanaa hi nahin kar sakataa tha. aur ve hi sab haath se nikale jaate hain. vah ghabaraakar ghar se nikal jat aur ghanton kheton ki medon par baithaa hua rotaa, maano unse vidaa ho raha hai. is tarah ek saptaah beet gaya aur giradhaaree rupaye kaa koi bandobast na kar sakaa. aathaven din use maaloom hua ki kaalikaadeen ne 100 rupaye najaraane dekar 10 rupaye beeghe par khet le liye. giradhaaree ne ek thandee saans lee. ek pal ke baad vah apne daadaa kaa naam lekar bilakh-bilakhakar rone lagaa. us din ghar mein choolhaa nahin jalaa. aisaa maaloom hot tha, maano harakhoo aaj hi maraa hai.
shaam ho gayi thi. andheraa chhaa raha tha. subhaagee ne diya jalaakar giradhaaree ne sirahaane rakh diya tha aur baithee dvaar ki ora taar rahi thi ki sahasaa use pairon ki aahat maaloom hui. subhaagee kaa dil dhadak uthaa. vah daudakar baahar aayee idhar-udhar taakane lagee. usane dekh ki giradhaaree bailon ki naand ke paas sir jhukaaen khadaa hai.
subhaagee bol uthee- ghar aao, wahan khade kya kar rahe ho, aaj saare din kahan rahe?
yah kehte hue vah giradhaaree ki ora chalee. giradhaaree ne kuchh jawab na diya. vah peechhe hatane lagaa aur thodee door jaakar gaayab ho gaya. subhaagee chillaayee aur moorchchhit hokar gir padee.
doosare din kaalikaadeen hal lekar apne nae khet par pahunche, abhi kuchh andheraa tha. vah bailon ko hal mein lagaa rahe the ki yakaayak unhone dekh ki giradhaaree khet ki med par khadaa hai. wahi, mirjaee, wahi pagadee, wahi sontaa.
kaalikaadeen ne kahaa- are giradhaaree! marade aadamee, tum yahan khade ho, aur bechaaree subhaagee hairaan ho rahi hai. kahan se aa rahe ho?
yah kehte hue bailon ko chhodakar giradhaaree ki ora chale. giradhaaree peechhe hatane lagaa aur peechhevaale kuen mein koond padaa. kaalikaadeen ne cheekhamaaree aur hal-bail vaheen chhodakar bhaagaa. saare gaanv mein shor mach gaya, log naanaa tarah ki kalpanaaen karne lage! kaalikaadeen ko giradhaaree vaale kheton mein jaane ki himmat na padee.
giradhaaree ko gaayab hue chh: mahine beet chuke hain. uska bada ladakaa ab ek eent ke bhatte par kaam kart hai aur 20 rupaye mahina ghar aat hai. ab vah kameej aur angrezi jootaa pahanataa hai, ghar mein donon joon tarakaaree pakatee hai aur jau ke badale gehoon khaayaa jat hai, lekin gaanv mein uska kuchh bhi aadar nahin. ab vah majoor hai. subhaagee ab paraaye gaanv mein aaye kutte ki tarah dabakatee phiratee hai. vah ab panchaayat mein bhi nahin baithti, vah ab majadoor ki maan hai.

kaalikaadeen ne giradhaaree ke kheton se isteephaa de diya hai, kyonki giradhaaree abhi tak apne kheton ke chaaron taraph mandaraayaa kart hai. andheraa hote hi vah meind par aakar baith jat hai aur kabhi-kabhi raat ko udhar se usake rone ki aavaaj sunaaee detee hai. vah kisee se bolta nahin, kisee ko chhedataa nahin. use keval apne kheton ko dekhakar santosh hot hai. deeyaa jalane ke baad udhar kaa raastaa band ho jat hai.
laalaa onkaaranaath bahut chahte hain ki ye khet uth jaaen, lekin gaanv ke log una kheton kaa naam lete darate hain.
