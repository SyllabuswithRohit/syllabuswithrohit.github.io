# Balidan

**Munshi Premchand**

manushy kee aarthik avasthaa kaa sabase jyaadaa asar usake naam par padataa hai| mauje belaa ke mangaroo thaakur jab se kaansatebal ho gae hain, inakaa naam mangalasinh ho gaya hai| ab unhen koee mangaroo kehne kaa saahas nahin kar sakataa| kalloo aheer ne jab se halake ke thaanedaar sahab se mitrataa kar lee hai aur gaanv kaa mukhiyaa ho gaya hai, usakaa naam kaalikaadeen ho gaya hai| ab use kalloo kahen to aankhen laal-peelee kart hai| isee tarah harakhachandr kuramee ab harakhoo ho gaya hai| aaj se bees saal pahale usake yahan shakkar banatee thi, kaee hal kee khetee hoti thi aur kaarobaar khoob phailaa hua tha| lekin videshee shakkar kee aamadanee ne use matiyaamet kar diya| dheer-dheere kaarakhaanaa toot gaya, jameen toot gaee, gaahak toot gae aur vah bhee toot gaya| sattar varsh kaa boodhaa, jo takiyedaar maache par baithaa hua naariyal piyaa kart, ab sir par tokaree liye khaad phenkane jat hai| lekin usake munh par ab bhee ek tarah kee ganbheerataa, baatacheet mein ab bhee ek tarah kee akad, chaal-dhaal se ab bhee ek tarah kaa svaabhimaan bharaa hua hai| is par kaal kee gati kaa prabhaav nahin padaa| rassee jal gaee, par bal nahin tootaa| bhale din manushy ke charitr par hameshaa ke lie apanaa chihn chhod jaate hain| harakhoo ke paas ab keval paanch beeghaa jameen hai, keval do bail hain| ek hee hal kee khetee hoti hai|

lekin panchaayaton mein, aapas kee kalah mein, usakee sammati ab bhee izzat kee nazar se dekhee jati hai| vah jo baat keht hai, belaag keht hai aur gaanv ke anapadhe unake saamane munh nahin khol sakate|
harakhoo ne apne jeevan mein kabhee davaa nahin khaayee thi| vah beemaar jaroor padataa, kuaanr maas mein maleriyaa se kabhee na bachataa tha| lekin das-paanch din mein vah binaa davaa khaae hee changaa ho jat tha| is varsh kaartik mein beemaar padaa aur yah samajhakar ki achchhaa to hee jaaoongaa, usane kuchh paravaah na kee| lekin ab kee jvar maut kaa paravaanaa lekar chalaa tha| ek saptaah beetaa, doosaraa saptaah beetaa, pooraa maheenaa beet gaya, par harakhoo chaarapaaee se na uthaa| ab use davaa kee jaroorat maaloom huee| usakaa ladakaa giradhaaree kabhee neem kee seekhe pilaataa, kabhee gurch kaa sat, kabhee gadaapooranaa kee jad| par ina aushadhiyon se koee phaayadaa na hot tha| harakhoo ko yakeen ho gaya ki ab sansaar se chalne ke din aa gae|
ek din mangalasinh use dekhane gae| bechaaraa tootee khaat par padaa raam naam jap raha tha| mangalasinh ne kahaa- baabaa, binaa davaa khaae achchhe na honge; kunain kyon nahin khaate ? harakhoo ne udaaseen bhaav se kahaa- to lete aan|

doosare din kaalikaadeen ne aakar kahaa- baabaa, do-chaar din koee davaa khaa lo| ab tumhaaree javaanee kee deh thode hai ki binaa davaa-darpan ke achchhe ho jaaoge|
harakhoo ne usee mand bhaav se kahaa- to lete aan| lekin rogee ko dekh aan ek baat hai, davaa laakar denaa use doosaree baat hai| pahalee baat shishtaachaar se hoti hai, doosaree sachchee samavedanaa se| na mangalasinh ne khabar lee, na kaalikaadeen ne, na kisee teesare hee ne| harakhoo daalaan mein khaat par padaa rahataa| mangal sinh kabhee najar aa jaate to keht-bhaiyaa, vah davaa nahin laae ?
mangalasinh kataraakar nikal jaate| kaalikaadeen dikhaaee dete, to unse bhee yahee savaal kart| lekin vah bhee najar bachaa jaate| yaa to use soojhataa hee nahin tha ki davaa paison ke binaa nahin aatee, yaa vah paison ko bhee jaan se priy samajhataa tha, athavaa jeevan se niraash ho gaya tha| usane kabhee davaa ke daam kee baat nahin kee| davaa na aayee| usakee dashaa dinondin bigadatee gaee| yahan tak ki paanch maheene takaleeph bhogane ke baad usane theek holee ke din shareer tyaag diya| giradhaaree ne usakaa shav badee dhoomadhaam ke saath nikaalaa ! kriyaakarm bade hausale se kiya| kaee gaanv ke braahmanon ko nimantrit kiya|
belaa mein holee na manaayee gaee, na abeer na gulaal udee, na daphalee bajee, na bhang kee naaliyaan baheen| kuchh log man mein harakhoo ko kosate jaroor the ki is buddhe ko aaj hee maranaa tha; do-chaar din baad marataa|
lekin itanaa nirlajj koee na tha ki shok mein khushee manaataa| vah shareer nahin tha, jahaan koee kisee ke kaam mein shareek nahin hot, jahaan padosee ko rone-peetane kee aavaaj hamaare kaanon tak nahin pahunchatee|
harakhoo ke khet gaanvavaalon kee najar par chadhe hue the| paanchon beeghaa jameen kuen ke paas, khaad-paans se ladee huee mend-baandh se theek thi| unamen teen-teen phasalen paidaa hoti theen| harakhoo ke marate hee una par chaaron ora se dhaave hone lage| giradhaaree to kriyaa-karm mein phansaa hua tha| udhar gaanv ke manachale kisaan laalaa onkaaranaath ko chain na lene dete the, najaraane kee badee-badee rakamen pesh ho rahi theen| koee saal-bhar kaa lagaan dene par taiyaar tha, koee najaraane kee doonee rakam kaa dastaavej likhane par tulaa hua tha| lekin onkaaranaath sabako taalate rahate the| unakaa soch tha ki giradhaaree ke baap ne kheton ko bees varsh tak jotaa hai, isalie giradhaaree kaa hak sabase jyaadaa hai| vah agar doosaron se kam bhee najaraanaa de, to khet usee ko dene chaahie| astu, jab giradhaaree kriyaa-karm se nivritt ho gaya aur usse poochhaa- khetee ke baare mein kyaa kehte ho ?
giradhaaree ne rokar kahaa- sarakaar, inheen kheton hee kaa to aasaraa hai, jotoongaa nahin to kyaa karoongaa|
onkaaranaath- nahin, jaroor joto khet tumhaare hain| main tumse chhodane ko nahin keht| harakhoo ne use bees saal tak jotaa| una par tumhaaraa hak hai| lekin tum dekhate ho, ab jameen kee dar kitanee badh gaee hai| tum aath rupaye beeghe par jotate the, mujhe das rupaye mil rahe hain aur najaraane ke sau alag| tumhaare saath riyaasat karake lagaan vahee rakhataa hoon, par najaraane ke rupaye tumhen dene padenge|
giradhaaree- sarakaar, mere ghar mein to is samay rotiyon kaa bhee thikaanaa nahin hai| itane rupaye kahan se laaoongaa ? jo kuchh jamaa-jathaa thi, daadaa ke kaam mein uth gaee| anaaj khalihaan mein hai| lekin daadaa ke beemaar ho jaane se upaj bhee achchhee nahin huee| rupaye kahan se laaoon ?
onkaraanaath- yah sach hai, lekin main isase jyaadaa riaayat nahin kar sakataa|
giradhaaree-nahin sarakaar ! aisaa na kahie| nahin to ham binaa maare mar jaaenge| aap bade hokar kehte hain, to main bail-bachhiyaa bechakar pachaas rupayaa laa sakataa hoon| isase beshee kee himmat meree nahin padatee|
onkaaranaath chidhakar bole- tum samajhate hoge ki ham ye rupaye lekar apne ghar mein rakh lete hain aur chain kee banshee bajaate hain| lekin hamaare oopar jo kuchh gujaratee hai, ham bhee jaanate hain| kaheen yah chandaa, kaheen vah chandaa; kaheen yah najar, kaheen vah najar, kaheen yah inaam, kaheen vah inaam| inake maare kachoomar nikal jat hai| bade din mein saikadon rupaye daaliyon mein uda jaate hain| jise daalee na do, vahee munh phulaataa hai| jin cheejon ke lie ladake tarasakar raha jaate hain, unhen baahar se mangavaakar daaliyaan sajaataa hoon| us par kabhee kaanoonagon aa gae, kabhee tahaseeladaar, kabhee diptee sahab kaa lashkar aa gaya| sab mere mehamaan hote hain| agar na karoon to nakkoo banoon aur sabakee aankhon mein kaantaa ban jaaoon| saal mein hajaar baarah sau modee ko isee rasad-khuraak ke mad mein dene padate hain| yah sab kahan se aaen ? bas, yahee jee chaahataa hai ki chhodakar chalaa jaaoon| lekin hamen paramaatmaa ne isalie banaayaa hai ki ek se rupayaa sataakar len aur doosare ko ro-rokar den, yahee hamaaraa kaam hai| tumhaare saath itanee riyaasat kar raha hoon| magar tum itanee riyaasat par bhee khush nahin hote to hari ichchhaa| nazaraane mein ek paise kee bhee riaayat na hogee| agar ek haphte ke andar rupae daakhil karoge to khet jotane paaoge, nahin to nahin| main koee doosaraa intazaam kar doongaa|

lekin subhaagee yon chupachaap baithanevaalee aurat na thi| vah gussaa se bharee huee kaalikaadeen ke ghar gayee| aur usakee aurat ko khoob lathedaa- kal kaa baanee aaj kaa seth, khet jotane chale hain| dekhen, kaun mere khet mein hal le jat hai ? apanaa aur usakaa lohoo ek kar doon| padosin ne usakaa paksh liya, to kyaa gareebon ko kuchalate phirenge ? subhaagee ne samajhaa, mainne maidaan maar liya| usakaa chitt bahut shaant ho gaya| lekin vahee vaayu, jo paanee mein lahare paidaa karti hain, vrikshon kee jad se ukhaad daalatee hai| subhaagee to padosiyon kee panchaayat mein apne dukhade rotee aur kaalikaadeen kee aurat se chhed-chhed ladatee|
idhar giradhaaree apne dvaar par baithaa hua sochataa, ab meraa kyaa haal hogaa ? ab yah jeevan kaise kategaa ? ye ladake kisake dvaar par jaaenge ? majadooree kaa soch karte hee usakaa dil vyaakul ho jat| itane dinon tak svaadheenataa aur izzat ko sukh bhogane ke baad adham chaakaree kee sharan lene ke badale vah mar jan achchhaa samajhataa tha| vah ab tak grihasth tha, usakee gananaa gaanv ke bhale aadamiyon mein thi, use gaanv ke maamale mein bolane kaa adhikaar tha| usake ghar mein paisaa na tha, par maan tha| naaee, kumhaar, purohit, bhaat, chaukeedaar ye sab usakaa munh taakate the| ab yah maryaadaa kahan ? ab kaun usakee baat poochhegaa ? kaun usake dvaar aaegaa ? ab use kisee ke baraabar baithane kaa, kisee ke beech mein bolane kaa hak nahin raha| ab use pet ke lie doosaron kee gulaamee karanee padegee| ab pahar raat rahe, kaun bailon ko naand mein lagaaegaa| vah din ab kahaan, jab geet gaakar hal chalaataa tha| chotee kaa paseenaa edee tak aat tha, par jaraa bhee thakaavat na aatee thi| apne lahalahaate hue khet ko dekhakar phoolaa na samaataa tha| khalihaan mein anaaj kaa dher saamane rakhe hue apne ko raajaa samajhataa tha| ab anaaj ko tokare bhar-bharakar kaun laaegaa ? ab khetee kahan ? bakhaar kahan ?

yahee sochate-sochate giradhaaree kee aankhon se aansoo kee jhadee lag jati thi| gaanv ke do-chaar sajjan, jo kaalikaadeen se jalate the, kabhee-kabhee giradhaaree ko tasallee dene aay karte the, par vah unse bhee khulakar na bolataa| use maaloom hot tha ki main sabakee najaron mein gir gaya hoon|
agar koee samajhataa tha ki tumane kriyaa-karm mein bekaar itane rupaye udaa diye, to use bahut du:kha hot| vah apne us kaam par jaraa bhee na pachhataataa| mere bhaagy mein jo kuchh likhaa hai vah hogaa, par daadaa ke rin se to urin ho gaya; unhonne apanee jindagee mein chaar ko khilaakar khaayaa| kyaa marane ke peechhe unhen pinde-paanee ko tarasaataa ?
is tarah teen maas beet gae aur aashaadh aa pahunchaa| aakaash mein ghataaen aayeen, paanee giraa, kisaan hal-jue theek karne lage| badhaee halon kee marammat karne lagaa| giradhaaree paagal kee tarah kabhee ghar ke bheetar jat, kabhee baahar jat, apne halon ko nikaal dekhataa, isakee muthiyaa toot gaee hai, isakee phaal dheelee ho gaee hai, jue mein sailaa nahin hai| dekhate-dekhate vah ek pal apne ko bhool gaya| daudaa huee badhaee ke yahan gaya aur bol- rajjoo, mere haal bhee bigade hue hai, chalo banaa do| rajjoo ne usakee ora karun-bhaav se dekh aur apanaa kaam karne lagaa| giradhaaree ko hosh aa gaya, neend se chauk padaa, pachhataavaa se usakaa sir jhuk gaya, aankhen bhar aayeen| chupachaap ghar chalaa aay|

gaanv mein chaaron ora halachal machee huee thi| koee san ke beej khojataa phirataa tha, koee jameendaar ke chaupaal se dhaan ke beej liye aat tha, kaheen salaah hoti, kis khet mein kyaa bonaa chaahie| giradhaaree ye baaten sunataa aur jalaheen machhalee kee tarah tadapataa tha|

ek din shaam samay giradhaaree khadaa apne bailon ko khujalaa raha tha ki mangalasinh aaye aur idhar-udhar kee baaten karake bole- goee ko baandhakar kab tak khilaaoge ? nikaal kyon nahin dete?
giradhaaree ne malin bhaav se kahaa-haan, koee gaahak aaye to nikaal doon|
mangalasinh- ek gaahak to hameen hain, hameen ko de do|
giradhaaree abhee kuchh javaab na dene paayaa tha ki tulasee baniyaa aay aur garajakar bol- giradhaaree ! tumhen rupaye dene hain ki nahin, vaisaa kaho| teen maheene se heel-havaalaa karte chale aate ho| ab kaun khetee karte ho ki tumhaaree phasal ko agore baith rahen ?
giradhaaree ne deenataa se kahaa-saah, jaise itane dinon maane ho, aaj aur maan jaao| kal tumhaaree ek-ek kaudee chukaa doongaa|
mangal aur tulasee ke ishaaron se baaten kee aur tulasee bhunabhunaataa hua chalaa gaya| tab giradhaaree mangalasinh se bol- tum inhen le lo, to ghar-ke-ghar hee mein raha jaaoon| kabhee-kabhee aankh se dekh to liya karoongaa|
mangalasinh- mujhe abhee to koee aisaa kaam nahin, lekin ghar par salaah karoongaa|
giradhaaree- mujhe tulasee ke rupaye dene hai, nahin to khilaane ko to bhoosaa hai|
mangalasinh- yah badaa badamaash hai, kaheen naalish na kar de|
aasaan dil giradhaaree dhamakee mein aa gaya| kaaryakushal mangalasinh ko sastaa saudaa karne kaa achchhaa suavasar milaa| 80 rupaye kee jodee 60 rupaye mein theek kar lee|
giradhaaree ne ab tak bailon ko na jaane kis ummeed se baandhakar khilaayaa tha| aaj ummeed kaa vah kalpit sootr bhee toot gaya| mangalasinh giradhaaree kee khaat par baithe rupaye gin rahe the aur giradhaaree bailon ke paas vishaadamay netron se unake munh kee ora taak raha tha| aah ! ye mere kheton ke kamaane vaale, mere jeevan ke aadhaar, mere annadaataa, meree maan-maryaadaa kee rakshaa karne vaale, jinake lie pahar raat se uthakar chhaantee kaatataa tha, jinake khalee-daane kee phikr apne khaane se jyaadaa rahatee thi, jinake lie saaraa din-bhar hariyaalee ukhaadaa kart tha, ye meree ummeed kee do aankhen, mere iraade ke do taare, mere achchhe dinon ke do chihn, mere do haath, ab mujhse vidaa ho rahe hain|

jab mangalasinh ne rupaye ginakar rakh die aur bailon ko le chale, tab giradhaaree unake kandhon par sir rakhakar khoob phoot-phootakar royaa| jaisaa kanyaa maayake se vidaa hote samay maan-baap ke pairon ko nahin chhodatee, usee tarah giradhaaree ina bailon ko na chhodataa tha| subhaagee bhee daalaan mein padee ro rahi thi aur chhotaa ladakaa mangalasinh ko ek baans kee chhadee se maar raha tha !
raat ko giradhaaree ne kuchh nahin khaayaa| chaarapaaee par padaa raha| praat:kaal subhaagee chilam bharakar le gayee, to vah chaarapaaee par na tha| usane samajhaa, kaheen gaye honge| lekin jab do-teen ghadee din chadh aay aur vah na lautaa, to usane ronaa-dhonaa shuroo kiya| gaanv ke log jamaa ho gae, chaaron ora khoj hone lagee, par giradhaaree kaa pataa na chalaa|
giradhaaree udaas aur niraash hokar ghar aay| 100 rupaye kaa intazaam karn usake kaaboo ke baahar tha| sochane lagaa, agar donon bail bech doon to khet hee lekar kyaa karoongaa ? ghar bechoon to yahan lene vaalaa hee kaun hai ? aur phir baap daadon kaa naam doobataa hai| chaar-paanch ped hain, lekin unhen bechakar 25 rupaye yaa 30 rupaye se zyaadaa na milenge| udhaar loon to detaa kaun hai ? abhee baniye ke 50 rupaye sir par chadh hain| vah ek paisaa bhee na degaa| ghar mein gahane bhee to nahin hain, nahin unheen ko bechataa| le dekar ek hansalee banavaayee thi, vah bhee baniye ke ghar padee huee hai| saal-bhar ho gaya, chhudaane kee naubat na aayee| giradhaaree aur usakee aurat subhaagee donon hee isee phikr mein pade rahate, lekin koee upaay na soojhataa tha| giradhaaree ko khaanaa-peenaa achchhaa na lagataa, raat ko neend na aatee| kheton ke nikalane kaa dhyaan aate hee usake dil mein hook-see uthane lagatee| haay ! vah bhoomi jise hamane varshon jotaa, jise khaad se paataa, jisamen meden rakheen, jisakee meden banaayee, usakaa majaa ab doosaraa uthaaegaa|

ve khet giradhaaree ke jeevan kaa ansh ho gae the| unakee ek-ek angul bhoomi usake rakt mein rangee huee thi| unake ek-ek paramaanu usake paseene se tar ho raha tha|
unake naam usakee jihvaa par usee tarah aate the, jis tarah apne teenon bachchon ke| koee chaubeeso tha, koee baaeeso tha, koee naalabelaa, koee talaiyaavaalaa| ina naamon ke smaran hote hee kheton kaa chitr usakee aankhon ke saamane khinch jat tha| vah ina kheton kee charchaa is, tarah kart, maano ve sajeev hain| maano usake bhale-bure ke saathee hain| usake jeevan kee saaree aashaaen, saaree ichchhaaen, saare manasoobe, saaree mithaaiyaan, saare havaaee kile, inheen kheton par avalambit the|

inake binaa vah jeevan kee kalpanaa hee nahin kar sakataa tha| aur ve hee sab haath se nikale jaate hain| vah ghabaraakar ghar se nikal jat aur ghanton kheton kee medon par baithaa hua rotaa, maano unse vidaa ho raha hai| is tarah ek saptaah beet gaya aur giradhaaree rupaye kaa koee bandobast na kar sakaa| aathaven din use maaloom hua ki kaalikaadeen ne 100 rupaye najaraane dekar 10 rupaye beeghe par khet le liye| giradhaaree ne ek thandee saans lee| ek pal ke baad vah apne daadaa kaa naam lekar bilakh-bilakhakar rone lagaa| us din ghar mein choolhaa nahin jalaa| aisaa maaloom hot tha, maano harakhoo aaj hee maraa hai|
shaam ho gaee thi| andheraa chhaa raha tha| subhaagee ne diya jalaakar giradhaaree ne sirahaane rakh diya tha aur baithee dvaar kee ora taar rahi thi ki sahasaa use pairon kee aahat maaloom huee| subhaagee kaa dil dhadak uthaa| vah daudakar baahar aayee idhar-udhar taakane lagee| usane dekh ki giradhaaree bailon kee naand ke paas sir jhukaaen khadaa hai|
subhaagee bol uthee- ghar aao, wahan khade kyaa kar rahe ho, aaj saare din kahan rahe ?
yah kehte hue vah giradhaaree kee ora chalee| giradhaaree ne kuchh javaab na diya| vah peechhe hatane lagaa aur thodee door jaakar gaayab ho gaya| subhaagee chillaayee aur moorchchhit hokar gir padee|
doosare din kaalikaadeen hal lekar apne nae khet par pahunche, abhee kuchh andheraa tha| vah bailon ko hal mein lagaa rahe the ki yakaayak unhonne dekh ki giradhaaree khet kee med par khadaa hai| vahee, mirjaee, vahee pagadee, vahee sontaa|
kaalikaadeen ne kahaa- are giradhaaree ! marade aadamee, tum yahan khade ho, aur bechaaree subhaagee hairaan ho rahi hai| kahan se aa rahe ho ?
yah kehte hue bailon ko chhodakar giradhaaree kee ora chale| giradhaaree peechhe hatane lagaa aur peechhevaale kuen mein koond padaa| kaalikaadeen ne cheekhamaaree aur hal-bail vaheen chhodakar bhaagaa| saare gaanv mein shor mach gaya, log naanaa tarah kee kalpanaaen karne lage ! kaalikaadeen ko giradhaaree vaale kheton mein jaane kee himmat na padee|
giradhaaree ko gaayab hue chh: maheene beet chuke hain| usakaa badaa ladakaa ab ek eent ke bhatte par kaam kart hai aur 20 rupaye maheenaa ghar aat hai| ab vah kameej aur angarejee jootaa pahanataa hai, ghar mein donon joon tarakaaree pakatee hai aur jau ke badale gehoon khaayaa jat hai, lekin gaanv mein usakaa kuchh bhee aadar nahin| ab vah majoor hai| subhaagee ab paraaye gaanv mein aaye kutte kee tarah dabakatee phiratee hai| vah ab panchaayat mein bhee nahin baithatee, vah ab majadoor kee maan hai|

kaalikaadeen ne giradhaaree ke kheton se isteephaa de diya hai, kyonki giradhaaree abhee tak apne kheton ke chaaron taraph mandaraayaa kart hai| andheraa hote hee vah meind par aakar baith jat hai aur kabhee-kabhee raat ko udhar se usake rone kee aavaaj sunaaee detee hai| vah kisee se bolataa nahin, kisee ko chhedataa nahin| use keval apne kheton ko dekhakar santosh hot hai| deeyaa jalane ke baad udhar kaa raastaa band ho jat hai|
laalaa onkaaranaath bahut chaahate hain ki ye khet uth jaaen, lekin gaanv ke log una kheton kaa naam lete darate hain|
