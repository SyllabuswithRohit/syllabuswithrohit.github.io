# Khudai Fauzdar

**Munshi Premchand**

seth naanakachand ko aaj phir vahee liphaaphaa milaa aur vahee likhaavat saamaneaayee to unakaa cheharaa peelaa pad gaya. liphaaphaa kholate hue haath aur hradayadonon kaanpane lage. khat mein kya hai, yah unhe khoob maaloom tha. isee tarah kedo khat pahale paa chuke the. is teesare khat mein bhee vahee dhaamakiyaan hain, isamen unhe shak na tha. patr haath mein liye hue aakaash kee ora taakane lage.
woh dil ke majaboot aadamee the, dhamakiyon se daranaa unhonne na seekhaa tha, murdon se bhee apanee rakam vasool kar lete the. raham yaa upakaar jaisee maanaveey durbalataaen unhe chhoo bhee na gayee theen, nahin to mahaajan hee kaise banate! us par dharmanishth bhee the. har poornamaasee ko satyanaaraayan kee kathaa sunate the. har mangal komahaabeerajee ko laddoo chadhaate the, nity-prati jamunaa mein snaan karte the aur har ekaadashee ko vrat rakhate aur braahmanon ko khana karaate the aur idhaar jab se ghee mein karaaraa naphaa hone lagaa tha, ek dharmashaalaa banavaane kee phikr mein the.
jameen theek kar lee thi. unake asaamiyon mein saikadon hee thavaee aur beladaar the, jo keval sood mein kaam ko taiyaar the. intajaar yahee tha ki koi eent aur choone vaalaa phans jaay aur das-bees hajaar kaa dastaavej likhaa le, to sood mein eent aur choonaa bhee mil jaay. is dharmanishthaa ne unakee aatmaa ko aur bhee shakti pradaan kar dee thi. devataaon ke aasheervaad aur prataap se unhe kabhee kisee saude mein ghaataa nahin hua aur bheeshan paristhitiyon mein bhee woh sthirachitt rehne ke aadee the; lekin jab se yah dhamakiyon se bhare hue patr milane lage the, unhe barabas tarah-tarah kee shankaaen vyathit karne lagee theen. kaheen sachamuch daakuon ne chhaapaa maaraa, to kaun unakee madad karegaa? daivee baadhaaon mein
to devataaon kee madad par woh takiyaa kar sakate the, par sir par latakatee huee is talavaar ke saamane woh shraddhaa kuchh kaam na detee thi. raat ko unake dvaar par keval ek chaukeedaar rehta hai. agar das-bees hathiyaaraband aadamee aa jaaye, to woh akelaa kya kar sakataa hai? shaayad unakee aahat paate hee bhaag khadaa ho. padosiyon mein aisaa koi nazar na aat tha, jo is sankat mein kaam aave. yadyapi sabhee unake asaamee the yaa raha chuke the. lekin yah ehasaan-pharaamoshon kaa sampradaay hai, jis pattal mein khaataa hai, usee mein chhed kart hai; jisake dvaar par avasar padane par naak ragadataa hai, usee kaa dushman ho jat hai. inse koi ummeed nahin. haan, kivaaden sudridh hain; unhe todanaa aasaan
nahin, phir andar kaa daravaajaa bhee to hai. sau aadamee lag jaaye to hilaayen hile. aur kisee ora se hamale kaa khatakaa nahin. itanee oonchee sapaat deevaar par koi kya khaa ke chadhegaa? phir unake paas raayaphalen bhee to hain. ek raayaphal se woh darjanon aadamiyon ko bhoonakar rakh denge. magar itane pratibandhon ke hote hue bhee unake man mein ek hook-see samaayee rehti thi. kaun jaane chaukeedaar bhee unheen mein mil gaya ho, khidamatagaar bhee aasteen ke saanp ho gaye hon!
isalie woh ab bahudhaa andar hee rehte the. aur jab tak milanevaalon kaa pataa-thikaanaa na poochh len, unse milate na the. phir bhee do-chaar ghante to chaupaal mein baithane hee padate the; nahin to saaraa kaarobaar mittee mein na mil jat! jitanee der baahar rehte the, unake jaan jaise soolee par tange rehte the. udhaar unake mijaaj mein badee tabdeelee ho gayee thi. itane vinamr aur mishtabhaashee woh kabhee na the. gaaliyaan to kya, kisee se too-takaar bhee na karte. sood kee dar bhee kuchh ghataa dee thi; lekin phir bhee chitt ko shaanti na milatee thi. aakhir kaee minat tak dil ko majaboot karne ke baad unhonne patr kholaa, aur jaise golee lag gayee. sir mein chakkar aa gaya aur saaree cheejen naachatee huee maaloom hueen. saans phoolane lagee, aankhen phail gayeen. likhaa tha, tumane hamaare donon patron par kuchh bhee dhyaan na diya. shaayad tum samajhate hoge ki pulis tumhaaree rakshaa karegee; lekin yah tumhaaraa bhram hai. pulis us vakt aayegee, jab ham apanaa kaam karake sau kos nikal gaye honge. tumhaaree akl par patthar pad gaya hai, isamen hamaaraa koi dosh nahin. ham tumse sirf 25 hajaar rupaye maangate hain. itane rupaye de denaa tumhaare lie kuchh bhee mushkil nahin. hamen pataa hai ki tumhaare paas ek laakh kee moharen rakhee huee hain; lekin vinaashakaale vipareet samajh; ab ham tumhe aur zyada na samajhaayenge. tumako samajhaane kee cheshtaa karn hee bekaar hai. aaj shaam tak agar rupaye na aa gaye, to raat ko tumhaare oopar dhaavaa hogaa. apanee hiphaajat ke lie jise bulaanaa chaaho, bulaa lo, jitane aadamee aur hathiyaar jamaa karn chaaho, jamaa kar lo. ham lalakaar kar aayenge aur dinadahaade aayenge. ham chor nahin hain, ham veer hain aur hamaaraa yakeen baahubal mein hai. ham jaanate hain ki lakshmee usee ke gale mein jayamaal daalatee hai, jo dhanush tod sakataa hai, machhalee ko vedhaa sakataa hai. yadi. ..
seth ne turant bahee-khaate band kar diye aur rokad sanbhaalakar tijoree mein rakh diya aur saamane kaa dvaar bheetar se band karake mare hue se kesar ke paas aakar bole aaj phir vahee khat aay, kesar! ab aaj hee aa rahe hain.
kesar dohare badan kee aurat thi, yauvan beet jaane par bhee naujavaan ladakee, shauk-singaar mein lipt rehne vaalee, us phalaheen vriksh kee tarah, jo patajhad
mein bhee haree-bharee pattiyon se ladaa rehta hai. bachchaa kee viphal chaah mein jeevan kaa badaa bhaag bitaa chukane ke baad, ab use apanee sanchit maayaa ko bhogane kee dhun savaar rehti thi. maaloom nahin, kab aankhen band ho jaaye, phir yah thaatee kisake haath lagegee, kaun jaane? isalie use sabase zyaadaa dar beemaaree kaa tha, jise woh maut kaa paigaam samajhatee thi aur nity hee koi-na-koi davaa khaatee rehti thi. kaayaa ke is kapade ko us samay tak utaaranaa na chaahatee thi, jab tak usamen ek taar bhee baakee rahe. baal-bachche hote to woh mrityu kaa svaagat karti, lekin ab to usake jeevan hee ke saath ant tha, phir kyon na woh zyaadaa-se-zyaadaa samay tak jiye. haan, woh jeevan niraanand avashy tha, us madhur graas kee tarah, jise ham isalie khaa jate hain ki rakhe-rakhe sad jaayagaa.

usane ghabadaakar kahaa main tumse kab se kah rahi hoon ki do-chaar maheenon ke lie yahan se kaheen bhaag chalo, lekin tum sunate hee nahin. aakhir kya karne par tule hue ho?
sethajee sashank to the aur yah svaabhaavik tha aisee dashaa mein kaun shaant raha sakataa tha lekin woh kaayar nahin the. unhe ab bhee yakeen tha ki agar koi sankat aa pade to woh peechhe kadam na hataayenge. jo kuchh kamajoree aa gayee thi, woh sankat ko sir par mandaraate dekhakar bhaag gayee thi. hiran bhee to bhaagane kee raah na paakar shikaaree par chot kar baithataa hai. kabhee-kabhee nahin, aksar sankat padane par hee aadamee ke jauhar khulate hain. itanee der mein sethajee ne ek tarah se bhaavee vipatti kaa saamanaa karne kaa pakkaa iraadaa kar
liya tha. dar kyon, jo kuchh hona hai, woh hokar rahegaa. apanee rakshaa karn hamaaraa farz hai, maranaa-jeenaa vidhi ke haath mein hai. sethaaneejee ko dilaasaa dete hue bole, tum naahak itanaa daratee ho kesar, aakhir woh sab bhee to aadamee hain, apanee jaan kaa moh unhe bhee hai, nahin to yah kukarm hee kyon karte?
main khidakee kee aad se das-bees aadamiyon ko giraa sakataa hoon. pulis ko ittalaa dene bhee jaa raha hoon. pulis kaa farz hai ki hamaaree rakshaa kare. ham das hajaar saalaanaa taiks dete hain, kisalie? main abhee darogaajee ke paas jat hoon. jab sarakaar hamse taiks letee hai, to hamaaree madad karn usakaa dharm ho
jat hai.
raajaneeti kaa yah tattv usakee samajh mein nahin aay. woh to kisee tarah us dar se mukt hona chaahatee thi, jo usake dil mein saanp kee tarah baithaa phuphakaar raha tha. pulis kaa use jo tajurbaa tha, usse chitt ko santosh na hot tha. bolee pulisavaalon ko bahut dekh chukee. vaaradaat ke samay to unakee soorat nahin dikhaaee detee. jab vaaradaat ho chukatee hai, tab alabattaa shaan ke saath aakar rob jamaane lagate hain.
'pulis to sarakaar kaa raaj chalaa rahi hai. tum kya jaano? '
'main to kehti hoon, yon agar kal vaaradaat hone vaalee hogee, to pulis ko khabar dene se aaj hee ho jaayagee. loot ke maal mein inakaa bhee saajhaa hot hai. '
'jaanataa hoon, dekh chukaa hoon, aur roj dekhataa hoon; lekin main sarakaar ko das hajaar kaa saalaanaa taiks detaa hoon. pulisavaalon kaa aadar-satkaar bhee kart rehta hoon. abhee jaadon mein suparintendent sahab aaye the, to mainne kitanee rasad pahunchaayee thi. ek pooraa kanastar ghee aur ek shakkar kee pooree boree bhej dee thi. yah sab khilaanaa-pilaanaa kis din kaam aayegaa. haan, aadamee ko solaho aane doosaron ke bharose na baithanaa chaahie; isalie mainne sochaa hai, tumhe bhee bandook chalaanaa sikhaa doon? ham donon bandooken chhodanaa shuroo karenge, to daakuon kee kya majaal hai ki andar kadam rakh saken? '
prastaav haasyajanak tha. kesar ne musakaraakar kahaa: ‘haan aur kya, ab aaj main bandook chalaanaa seekhoongee! tumako jab dekho, hansee hee soojhatee hai.
'isamen hansee kee kya baat hai? aajakal to auraton kee phaujen ban rahi hain. sipaahiyon kee tarah auraten bhee kavaayad karti hain, bandook chalaatee hain, maidaanon mein khelatee hain. auraton ke ghar mein baithane kaa jamaanaa ab nahin hai. '
'vilaayat kee auraten bandook chalaatee hongee, yahan kee auraten kya chalaayengee.
haan, haath bhar kee jabaan chaahe chalaa len. '
'yahan kee auraton ne bahaaduree ke jo-jo kaam kiye hain, unse itihaas ke panne bhare pade hain. aaj duniya una vrittaanton ko padhakar chakit ho jati hai. '
'puraane jamaane kee baaten chhodo. tab auraten bahaadur rahi hongee. aaj kaun bahaaduree kar rahi hain? '
'vaah! abhee hajaaron auraten ghar-baar chhodakar hansate-hansate jel chalee gayeen. yah bahaaduree nahin thi? abhee panjaab mein haranaad kunvar ne akele chaar sashastr daakuon ko giraphtaar kiya aur laat sahab tak ne usakee prashansaa kee. '
'kya jaane woh kaisee auraten hain. main to daakuon ko dekhate hee chakkar khaakar gir panoongee. '
usee vakt naukar ne aakar kahaa sarakaar, thaane se chaar kaanistibil aaye hain. aapako bulaa rahe hain.
sethajee khush hokar bole thaanedaar bhee hain?
'nahin sarakaar, akele kaanistibil hain. '
'thaanedaar kyon nahin aay? ' yah kehte hue sethajee ne paan khaayaa aur baahar nikale.
sethajee ko dekhate hee chaaron kaanistibilon ne jhukakar salaam kiya, bilakulaangarejee kaayade se, maano apne kisee afasar ko sailyoot kar rahe hon. sethajee ne unhe benchon par baithaayaa aur bole darogaajee kaa mijaaj to achchhaa hai? main to unake paas aanevaalaa tha.
chaaron mein jo sabase praudh tha, jisakee aasteen par kaee bille lage hue the, bol aap kyon takaleeph karte hain, woh to khud hee aa rahe the; par ek badee jarooree tahakeekaat aa gayee, isase ruk gaye. kal aapase milenge. jabase yahan daakuon kee khabaren aayee hain, bechaare bahut ghabaraaye hue hain. aapakee taraph hameshaa unakaa dhyaan rehta hai. kaee baar kah chuke hain ki mujhe sabase zyada phikar sethajee kee hai. gumanaam khat to aapake paas bhee aaye honge? sethajee ne laaparavaahee dikhaakar kahaa ajee, aisee chitthiyaan aati hee rehti hain, inakee kaun paravaah kart hai. mere paas to teen khat aa chuke hain, mainne kisee se jikr bhee nahin kiya.
kaanistibil hansaa darogaajee ko khabar milee thi.
'sach! '
'haan, sahab? rattee-rattee khabar milatee rehti hai. yahan tak maaloom hua hai ki kal aapake makaan par unakaa dhaavaa honevaalaa hai. jabhee to aaj darogaa jee ne mujhe aapakee khidamat mein bhejaa. '
'magar wahan kaise khabar pahunchee? mainne to kisee se kahaa hee nahin. '
kaanistibil ne rahasyamay bhaav se kahaa hujoor, yah na poochhen. ilaake ke sabase bade seth ke paas aise khat aayen aur pulis ko khabar na ho! bhalaa, koi baat hai. phir oopar se baraabar taakeed aati rehti hai ki sethajee ko shikaayat kaa koi maukaa na diya jaay. suparintendent sahab kee khaas taakeedahai aapake lie. aur hujoor, sarakaar bhee to aap hee ke boote par chalatee hai.
seth-saahookaaron ke jaan-maal kee hiphaajat na kare, to rahe kahan? hamaare hote majaal hai ki koi aapakee taraph tirachhee aankhon se dekh sake; magar yah kambakht daakoo itane diler aur taadaad mein itane zyada hain ki thaane ke baahar unse mukaabilaa karn mushkil hai. darogaajee gaarad mangaane kee baat soch rahe the; magar ye hatyaare kaheen ek jagah to rehte nahin, aaj yahan hain, to kal yahan se do sau kos par. gaarad mangaakar hee kya kiya jaay? ilaake kee riaayaa kee to hamen zyada phikr nahin, hujoor maalik hain, aapase kya chhipaayen, kisake paas rakhaa hai itanaa maal-asabaab! aur agar kisee ke paas do-chaar sau kee poonjee nikal hee aayee to usake lie pulis daakuon ke peechhe apanee jaan hathelee par liye na phiregee. unhe kya, woh to chhootate hee golee chalaate hain aur aksar chhipakar. hamaare lie to hajaar bandishen hain. koi baat bigad jaay to ulate apanee hee jaan aaphat mein phans jaay. hamen to aise raaste chalanaa hai kisaanp mare aur laathee na toote, isalie darogaajee ne aapase yah arj karne ko kahaa hai ki aapake paas jokhim kee jo cheezen hon, unhe laakar sarakaaree khajaane mein jamaa kar deejie. aapako usakee raseed de dee jaayagee. taalaa aur muhar aap hee kee rahegee. jab yah hangaamaa thandaa ho jaay to mangavaa leejiegaa. isase aapako bhee bephikree ho jaayagee aur ham bhee jimmedaaree se bach jaayege. nahin khudaa na kare, koi vaaradaat ho jaay, to hujoor ko to jo nukasaan ho woh to ho hee hamaare oopar bhee javaabadehee aa jaay. aur yah jaalim sirph maal-asabaab lekar hee to jaan nahin chhodate Koon karte hain, ghar mein aag lagaa dete hain, yahan tak ki auraton kee beijjatee bhee karte hain. hujoor to jaanate hain, hot hai vahee jo takadeer mein likhaa hai. aap ikabaalavaale aadamee hain, daakoo aapakaa kuchh nahin bigaad sakate. saaraa kasbaa aapake lie jaan dene ko taiyaar hai. aapakaa poojaa-paath, dharm-karm khudaa khud dekh raha hai. yah usee kee barakat hai ki aap mittee bhee chhoo len, to sonaa ho jaay; lekin aadamee bharasak apanee hiphaajat kart hai. hujoor ke paas motar hai hee, jo kuchh rakhanaa ho, us par rakh deejie. ham chaar aadamee aapake saath hain, koi khatakaa nahin. wahan ek minat mein aapako phurasat ho jaayagee. pataa chalaa hai ki is gol mein bees javaan hain. do to bairaagee bane hue hain, do panjaabiyon ke bhes mein dhusse aur alavaan bechate phirate hain. ina donon ke saath do bahangeevaale bhee hain. do aadamee baloochiyon ke bhes mein chhooriyaan aur taale bechate hain. kahan tak ginaaoon, hujoor! hamaare thaane mein to har ek kaa huliyaa rakhaa hua hai.
khatare mein aadamee kaa dil kamajor ho jat hai aur woh aisee baaton par yakeen kar letaa hai, jin par shaayad hosh-havaas mein na kart. jab kisee davaa se rogee ko phaayadaa nahin hot, to ham duaa, taabeej, ojhon aur sayaanon kee sharan lete hain aur wahan to shak karne kaa koi vajah hee na tha. mumakin hai, darogaajee kaa kuchh svaarth ho, magar sethajee isake lie taiyaar the. agar do-chaar sau bal khaane paden, to koi badee baat nahin. aise avasar to jeevan mein aate hee rehte hain aur is paristhiti mein isase achchhaa doosaraa kya intajaam ho sakataa tha; balki ise to eeshvareey preranaa samajhanaa chaahie. maanaa, unake paas do-do bandooken hain, kuchh log madad karne ke lie nikal hee aayenge, lekin hai jaan jokhim. unhonne phaisalaa kiya, darogaajee kee is kripaa se phaayadaa uthaanaa chaahie. inheen aadamiyon ko kuchh de-dilaakar saaree cheejen nikalavaa lenge. doosaron kaa kya bharosaa? kaheen koi cheez udaa den to bas? unhonne is bhaav se kahaa, maano darogaajee ne una par koi khaas kripaa nahin kee hai; woh to unakaa farz hee tha mainne yahan aisaa intazaam kiya tha ki yahan woh sab aate to unake dot khatte kar diye jate. saaraa kasbaa madad ke lie taiyaar tha. sabhee se to apanaa mitr-bhaav hai, lekin darogaajee kee tajaveej mujhe pasand hai. isase woh bhee apanee jimmedaaree se baree ho jate hain aur mere sir se bhee phikr kaa bojh utar jat hai, lekin bheetar se cheejen baahar nikaal-nikaalakar laanaa mere boote kee baat nahin. aap logon kee duaa se naukar-chaakaron kee to kamee nahin hai, magar kisakee neeyat kaisee hai, kaun jaan sakataa hai? aap log kuchh madad karen to kaam aasaan ho jaay.
hed kaanistibil ne badee khushi se yah sevaa sveekaar kar lee aur bol, 'ham sab hujoor ke taabedaar hain, isamen madad kee kaun-see baat hai? talab sarakaar se paate hain, yah theek hai; magar denevaale to aap hee hain. aap keval saamaan hamen dikhaaye jaaye, ham baat-kee-baat mein saaree cheezen nikaal laayenge.
hujoor kee khidamat karenge to kuchh inaam-ikaraam milegaa hee. tanakhvaah mein gujar nahin hot sethajee, aap logon kee raham kee nigaah na ho, to ek din bhee nibaah na ho. baal-bachche bhookhon mar jaaye pandrah-bees rupaye mein kya hot hai hujoor, itanaa to hamaare lie hee pooraa nahin padataa. ' sethajee ne andar jaakar kesar se yah khabar kahaa to use jaise aankhen mil gayeen. bolee, bhagavaan ne madad kee, nahin mere jaan sankat mein pade hue the.
sethajee ne sarvajnataa ke bhaav se pharamaayaa isee ko kehte hain sarakaar kaa intajaam! isee mustaidee ke bal par sarakaar kaa raaj thamaa hua hai. kaisee suvyavasthaa hai ki zara-see koi baat ho, wahan tak khabar pahunch jati hai aur turant usakee rokathaam kaa hukm ho jat hai. aur yahan vaale aise budhdoo hain ki svaraajy-svaraajy chillaa rahe hain. inake haath mein akhtiyaar aa jaay to din-dopahar loot mach jaay, koi kisee kee na sune. oopar se taakeed aayee hai. haakimon kaa aadar-satkaar kabhee nishphal nahin jat. main to sochataa hoon, koi bahumooly vastu ghar mein na chhonoon. saale aayen to apanaa-saa munh lekar raha jaaye.
kesar ne man-hee-man khush hokar kahaa kunjee unake saamane phenk denaa ki jo cheej chaaho nikaal le jaao.
'saale jhenp jaayenge. '
'munh mein kaalikh lag jaayegee. '
'ghamand to dekho ki tithi tak bataa dee. yah nahin samajhe ki angrejee sarakaar kaa raaj hai. tum daal-daal chalo, to woh paat-paat chalate hain. '
'samajhe honge ki dhaamakee mein aa jaayenge. '
teen kaanistibilon ne aakar sandookache aur seph nikaalane shuroo kiye.
ek baahar saamaan ko motar par laad raha tha aur ek harek cheej ko notabuk par tokataa jat tha. gahane, muharen, not, rupaye, keematee kapade, saadiyaan; lahange, shaal-dushaale, sab kaar mein rakh diye. maamoolee baratan, lohe-lakadee ke saamaan, pharsh aadi ke sivaa ghar mein aur kuchh na bachaa. aur daakuon ke lie ye cheezen kaudee kee bhee nahin. kesar kaa singaaradaan khud sethajee laaye aur hed ke haath mein dekar bole ise badee hiphaajat se rakhanaa bhai! hed ne singaaradaan lekar kahaa mere lie ek-ek tinakaa itanaa hee keematee hai.
sethajee ke man mein ek shak uthaa. poochhaa, Kajaane kee kunjee to mere hee paas rahegee?
'aur kya, yah to main pahale hee arj kar chukaa; magar yah sawal aapake dil se kyon paidaa hua? '
'yonhee, poochhaa tha' sethajee lajjit ho gaye.
'nahin, agar aapake dil mein kuchh shubahaa ho, to ham log yahan bhee aap kee khidamat ke lie haajir hain. haan, ham jimmedaar na honge. '
'ajee nahin hed sahab, mainne yonhee poochh liya tha. yah phiharist to mujhe de doge na? '
'phiharist aapako thaane mein darogaajee ke dastakhat se milegee. isakaa kya etabaar? '
kaar par saaraa saamaan rakh diya gaya. kasbe ke saikadon aadamee tamaashaa dekh rahe the. kaar badee thi, par thasaathas bharee huee thi. badee mushkil se sethajee ke lie jagah nikalee. chaaron kaanistibil aage kee seet par simatakar baithe. kaar chalee. kesar dvaar par is tarah khadee thi, maano usakee betee bidaa ho rahi ho. betee sasuraal jaa rahi hai, jahaan woh maalakin banegee; lekin usakaa ghar soonaa kiye jaa rahi hai.
thaanaa yahan se paanch meel par tha. kasbe se baahar nikalate hee pahaadon kaa pathareelaa sannaataa tha, jisake daaman mein haraa-bharaa maidaan tha aur isee maidaan ke beech mein laal moram kee sadak chakkar khaatee huee laal saanp-jaisee nikal gayee thi. hed ne sethajee se poochhaa yah kahan tak sahee hai sethajee, ki aaj se pachchees saal pahale aapake baap keval lotaa-dor lekar yahan khaalee haath aaye the?
sethajee ne garv karte hue kahaa 'bilakul sahee hai. mere paas kul teen rupaye the. usee se aate-daal kee dookaan kholee thi. takadeer kaa khel hai, bhagavaan kee raham chaahie, aadamee ke banate-bigadate der nahin lagatee. lekin mainne kabhee paise ko doton se nahin pakadaa. yathaashakti dharm kaa paalan kart gaya. dhaan kee shobhaa dharm hee se hai, nahin to dhaan se koi phaayadaa nahin. '
'aap bilakul theek kehte hain sethajee. aapako moorat banaakar poojanaa chaahie. teen rupaye se laakh kamaa lenaa maamoolee kaam nahin hai! '
'aadhee raat tak sir uthaane kee phurasat nahin milatee, khaan sahab! '
'aapako to yah sab kaarobaar janjaal-saa lagataa hogaa. '
'janjaal to hai hee, magar bhagavaan kee aisee maayaa hai ki aadamee sabakuchh samajhakar bhee isamen phans jat hai aur saaree umr phansaa rehta hai. maut aa jati hai, tabhee chhuttee milatee hai. bas, yahee chaah hai ki kuchh yaadagaar chhod jaaoon. '
'aapake koi aulaad huee hee nahin? '
'bhaagy mein na thi khaan sahab, aur kya kahoon! jinake ghar mein bhoonee bhaang nahin hai, unake yahan ghaas-phoos kee tarah bachche-hee-bachche dekh lo, jinhen bhagavaan ne khaane ko diya hai, woh bachchaa kaa munh dekhane ko tarasate hain. '
'aap bilakul theek kehte hain, sethajee! zindagi kaa majaa bachchaa se hai. jisake aage andheraa hai, usake lie dhaan-daulat kis kaam kee? '
'eeshvar kee yahee ichchhaa hai to aadamee kya kare. meraa bas chalataa, to maayaajaal se nikal bhaagataa khaan sahab, ek pal bhee yahan na rehta, kaheen teerthasthaan mein baithakar bhagavaan kaa bhajan kart. magar karoon kya? maayaajaal tode nahin tootataa. '
'ek baar dil majaboot karake tod kyon nahin dete? sab uthaakar gareebon ko bot deejie. saadhu-santon ko nahin, na mote braahmanon ko balki unako, jinake lie yah zindagi bojh ho rahi hai, jisakee yahee ek aarajoo hai ki maut aakar unakee vipatti kaa ant kar de. '
'is maayaajaal ko todanaa aadamee kaa kaam nahin hai, khaan sahab! bhagavaan kee ichchhaa hoti hai, tabhee man mein vairaagy aat hai. '
'aaj bhagavaan ne aapake oopar raham kee hai. ham is maayaajaal ko makadee ke jaale kee tarah todakar aapako aajaad karne ke lie bheje gaye hain. bhagavaan aapakee bhakti se khush ho gaye hain aur aapako is bandhan mein nahin rakhanaa chaahate, jeevan-mukt kar denaa chaahate hain. '
'aisee bhagavaan kee raham ho jati, to kya poochhanaa khaan sahab! '
'bhagavaan kee aisee hee raham hai sethajee, yakeen maanie. hamen iseelie unhonne mrityulok mein tainaat kiya hai. ham kitane hee maayaajaal ke kaidiyon kee bediyaan kaat chuke hain. aaj aapakee baaree hai. '
sethajee kee naadiyon mein jaise rakt kaa pravaah band ho gaya. sahamee huee aankhon se sipaahiyon ko dekh. phir bole, aap bade hansod ho, khaan sahab?
'hamaare jeevan kaa siddhaant hai ki kisee ko takaleeph mata do; lekin ye rupaye vaale kuchh aisee aundhee khopadee ke log hain ki jo unakaa uddhaar karne aat hai, usee ke dushman ho jate hain. ham aapakee bediyaan kaatane aaye hain;
lekin agar aapase kahen ki yah sab jamaa-jathaa aur lataa-pataa chhodakar ghar kee raah leejie, to aap cheekhanaa-chillaanaa shuroo kar denge. ham log vahee khudaaee
phaujadaar hain, jinake ittalaaee khat aapake paas pahunch chuke hain. '
sethajee maano aakaash se paataal mein gir pade. saaree jnaanendriyon ne jawab de diya aur isee moorchchhaa kee dashaa mein woh motarakaar se neeche dhakel diye gaye
aur gaadee chal padee.
sethajee kee cheshtaa jaag padee. badahavaas gaadee ke peechhe daude hujoor, sarakaar, tabaah ho jaayege, raham keejie, ghar mein ek kaudee bhee nahin hai. .. hed sahab ne khidakee se baahar haath nikaalaa aur teen rupaye jameen par phenk diye. motar kee chaal tej ho gayee.
sethajee sir pakadakar baith gaye aur vikshipt netron se motarakaar ko dekh, jaise koi shav svargaarohee jaan ko dekhe. unake jeevan kaa svapn udaa chalaa jaa raha tha.
