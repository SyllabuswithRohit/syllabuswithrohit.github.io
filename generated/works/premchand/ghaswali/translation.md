# Ghaswali

**Munshi Premchand**

1
muliyaa haree-haree ghaas kaa gatthaa lekar aayee, to usakaa gehuaan rang kuchh tamatamaayaa hua tha aur badee-badee mad-bharee aankho mein shankaa samaaee huee thi. mahaaveer ne usakaa tamatamaayaa hua cheharaa dekhakar poochhaa- kya hai muliyaa, aaj kaisaa jee hai.
muliyaa ne kuchh jawab na diya- usakee aankhen dabadabaa gayeen!
mahaaveer ne paas aakar poochhaa- kya hua hai, bataatee kyon nahin? kisee ne kuchh kahaa hai, ammaan ne daantaa hai, kyon itanee udaas hai?
muliyaa ne sisakakar kahaa- kuchh nahin, hua kya hai, achchhee to hoon?
mahaaveer ne muliyaa ko sir se paanv tak dekhakar kahaa- chupachaap royegee, bataayegee nahin?
muliyaa ne baat taalakar kahaa- koi baat bhee ho, kya bataaoon.
muliyaa is oosar mein gulaab kaa phool thi. gehuaan rang tha, hiran kee-see aankhen, neeche khinchaa hua chibuk, kapolon par halakee laalimaa, badee-badee nukeelee palaken, aankho mein ek vichitr aardrataa, jisamen ek spasht dard, ek mook vyathaa jhalakatee rehti thi. maaloom nahin, chamaaron ke is ghar mein woh apsaraa kahan se aa gayee thi. kya usakaa komal phool-saa gaat is yogy tha ki sar par ghaas kee tokaree rakhakar bechane jati? us gaanv mein bhee aise log maujood the, jo usake talave ke neeche aankhen bichhaate the, usakee ek chitavan ke lie tarasate the, jinase agar woh ek shabd bhee bolti, to nihaal ho jate; lekin use aaye saal-bhar se zyaadaa ho gaya, kisee ne use yuvakon kee taraph taakate yaa baaten karte nahin dekh. woh ghaas liye nikalatee, to aisaa maaloom hot, maano ushaa kaa prakaash, sunahare aavaran mein ranjit, apanee chhataa bikherataa jat ho. koi gajalen gaataa, koi chhaatee par haath rakhataa; par muliyaa neechee aankh kiye apanee raah chalee jati. log hairaan hokar kehte- itanaa ghamand! mahaaveer mein aise kya surakhaab ke par lage hain, aisaa achchhaa javaan bhee to nahin, na jaane yah kaise usake saath rehti hai!
magar aaj aisee baat ho gayee, jo is jaati kee aur yuvatiyon ke lie chaahe gupt sandesh hoti, muliyaa ke lie dil kaa shool thi. prabhaat kaa samay tha, pavan aam kee baur kee sugandhi se matavaalaa ho raha tha, aakaash prithvee par sone kee varshaa kar raha tha. muliyaa sir par jhauaa rakkhe ghaas chheelane chalee, to usakaa gehuaan rang prabhaat kee sunaharee kiranon se kundan kee tarah damak uthaa. ekaaek naujavaan chainasinh saamane se aat hua dikhaaee diya. muliyaa ne chaahaa ki kataraakar nikal jaay; magar chainasinh ne usakaa haath pakad liya aur bol- muliyaa, tujhe kya mujh par zara bhee raham nahin aati?
muliyaa kaa woh phool-saa khilaa hua cheharaa jvaalaa kee tarah dahak uthaa. woh zara bhee nahin daree, zara bhee na jhijhakee, jhauaa jameen par giraa diya, aur bolee- mujhe chhod do, nahin main chillaatee hoon.
chainasinh ko aaj jeevan mein ek nayaa tajurbaa hua. neechee jaaton mein roop-maadhury kaa isake sivaa aur kaam hee kya hai ki woh oonchee jaatavaalon kaa khilaunaa bane. aise kitane hee maark usane jeete the; par aaj muliyaa ke chehare kaa woh rang, usakaa woh gussaa, woh ghamand dekhakar usake chhakke chhoot gaye. usane lajjit hokar usakaa haath chhod diya. muliyaa veg se aage badh gayee. sangharsh kee garamee mein chot kee vyathaa nahin hoti, peechhe se tees hone lagatee hai. muliyaa jab kuchh door nikal gayee, to gussaa aur dar tathaa apanee bekasee ko tajurbaa karake usakee aankho mein aansoo bhar aaye. usane kuchh der jabt kiya, phir sisak-sisak kar rone lagee. agar woh itanee gareeb na hoti, to kisee kee majaal thi ki is tarah usakaa beizzatee kart! woh rotee jati thi aur ghaas chheelatee jati thi. mahaaveer kaa gussaa woh jaanatee thi. agar usse kah de, to woh is thaakur ke khoon kaa pyaasaa ho jaayagaa. phir na jaane kya ho! is khayaal se usake roen khade ho gaye. iseelie usane mahaaveer ke prashnon kaa koi jawab na diya.
2
doosare din muliyaa ghaas ke lie na gayee. saas ne poochhaa- too kyon nahin jati? aur sab to chalee gayeen?
muliyaa ne sir jhukaakar kahaa- main akelee na jaaoongee.
saas ne bigadakar kahaa- akele kya tujhe baagh uthaa le jaayagaa?
muliyaa ne aur bhee sir jhukaa liya aur dabee huee aavaaj se bolee- sab mujhe chhedate hain.
saas ne daantaa- na too auron ke saath jaayagee, na akelee jaayagee, to phir jaayagee kaise! woh saaph-saaph kyon nahin kehti ki main na jaaoongee. to yahan mere ghar mein raanee ban ke nibaah na hogaa. kisee ko chaam nahin pyaaraa hot, kaam pyaaraa hot hai. too badee sundar hai, to teree sundarataa lekar chaatoon? uthaa jhaabaa aur ghaas laa!
dvaar par neem ke darakht ke saaye mein mahaaveer khadaa ghode ko mal raha tha. usane muliyaa ko ronee soorat banaaye jate dekh; par kuchh bol na sakaa. usakaa bas chalataa to muliyaa ko kaleje mein bithaa letaa, aankho mein chhipaa letaa; lekin ghode kaa pet bharanaa to jarooree tha. ghaas mol lekar khilaaye, to baarah aane roj se kam na pade. aisee majadooree hee kaun hoti hai. mushkil se dedh-do rupaye milate hain, woh bhee kabhee mile, kabhee na mile. jab se yah satyaanaashee laariyaan chalne lagee hain; ekkevaalon kee badhiyaa baith gayee hai. koi sent bhee nahin poochhataa. mahaajan se dedh-sau rupaye udhaar lekar ekkaa aur ghodaa khareedaa tha; magar laariyon ke aage ekke ko kaun poochhataa hai. mahaajan kaa sood bhee to na pahunch sakataa tha, mool kaa kahanaa hee kya! ooparee man se bol- na man ho, to rehne do, dekhee jaayagee.
is dilajoee se muliyaa nihaal ho gayee. bolee- ghodaa khaayegaa kya?
aaj usane kal kaa raastaa chhod diya aur kheton kee medon se hoti huee chalee. baar-baar satark aankho se idhar-udhaar taakatee jati thi. donon taraph ookh ke khet khade the. zara bhee khadakhadaahat hoti, usakaa jee sann ho jat kaheen koi ookh mein chhipaa na baithaa ho. magar koi nayee baat na huee. ookh ke khet nikal gaye, aamon kaa baag nikal gaya; sinche hue khet najar aane lage. door ke kuen par pur chal raha tha. kheton kee medon par haree-haree ghaas jamee huee thi. muliyaa kaa jee lalachaayaa. yahan aadhaa ghante mein jitanee ghaas chhil sakatee hai, sookhe maidaan mein dopahar tak na chhil sakegee! yahan dekhataa hee kaun hai. koi chillaayegaa, to chalee jaaoongee. woh baithakar ghaas chheelane lagee aur ek ghante mein usakaa jhaabaa aadhe se zyada bhar gaya. woh apne kaam mein itanee tanmay thi ki use chainasinh ke aane kee khabar hee na huee. ekaaek usane aahat paakar sir uthaayaa, to chainasinh ko khadaa dekh.
muliyaa kee chhaatee dhak se ho gayee. jee mein aay bhaag jaay, jhaabaa ulat de aur khaalee jhaabaa lekar chalee jaay; par chainasinh ne kaee gaj ke phaasale se hee rukakar kahaa- dar mata, dar mata, bhagavaan jaanataa hai! main tujhase kuchh na boloongaa. jitanee ghaas chaahe chheel le, meraa hee khet hai.
muliyaa ke haath sunn ho gaye, khurapee haath mein jam-see gayee, ghaas najar hee na aati thi. jee chaahataa tha; jameen phat jaay aur main samaa jaaoon. jameen aankho ke saamane tairane lagee.
chainasinh ne aashvaasan diya- chheelatee kyon nahin? main tumse kuchh keht thode hee hoon. yaheen roj chalee aay kar, main chheel diya karoongaa.
muliyaa chitralikhit-see baithee rahi.
chainasinh ne ek kadam aage badhaayaa aur bol- too mujhse itanaa daratee kyon hai! kya too samajhatee hai, main aaj bhee tujhe sataane aay hoon? eeshvar jaanataa hai, kal bhee tujhe sataane ke lie mainne teraa haath nahin pakadaa tha. tujhe dekhakar aap-hee-aap haath badh gaye. mujhe kuchh sudh hee na rahi. too chalee gayee, to main vaheen baithakar ghanton rotaa raha. jee mein aat tha, haath kaat daaloon. kabhee jee chaahataa tha, jahar khaa loon. tabhee se tujhe dhoondh raha hoon aaj too is raaste se chalee aayee. main saaraa haar chhaanataa hua yahan aay hoon. ab jo sajaa tere jee mein aave, de de. agar too meraa sir bhee kaat le, to gardan na hilaaoongaa. main shohadaa tha, luchchaa tha, lekin jab se tujhe dekh hai, mere man se saaree khot mit gayee hai. ab to yahee jee mein aat hai ki teraa kuttaa hot aur tere peechhe-peechhe chalataa, teraa ghodaa hot, tab to too apne haathon se mere saamane ghaas daalatee. kisee tarah yah cholaa tere kaam aave, mere man kee yah sabase badee laalasaa hai. meree javaanee kaam na aave, agar main kisee khot se ye baaten kar raha hoon. badaa bhaagavaan tha mahaaveer, jo aisee devee use milee.
muliyaa chupachaap sunatee rahi, phir neechaa sir karake bholepan se bolee- to tum mujhe kya karne ko kehte ho?
chainasinh aur paas aakar bol- bas, teree raham chaahataa hoon.
muliyaa ne sir uthaakar usakee ora dekh. usakee sharm na jaane kahan gaayab ho gayee. chubhate hue shabdon mein bolee- tumse ek baat kahoon, buraa to na maanoge? tumhaaraa byaah ho gaya hai yaa nahin?
chainasinh ne dabee jabaan se kahaa- byaah to ho gaya, lekin byaah kya hai, khilavaad hai.
muliyaa ke hothon par avahelanaa kee musakaraahat jhalak padee, bolee- phir bhee agar meraa aadamee tumhaaree aurat se isee tarah baaten kart, to tumhe kaisaa lagataa? tum usakee gardan kaatane par taiyaar ho jate ki nahin? bolo! kya samajhate ho ki mahaaveer chamaar hai to usakee deh mein lahoo nahin hai, use sharm nahin hai, apne maryaadaa kaa soch nahin hai? meraa roop-rang tumhe bhaataa hai. kya ghaat ke kinaare mujhse kaheen sundar auraten nahin ghoomaa karateen? main unake talavon kee baraabaree bhee nahin kar sakatee. tum usamen se kisee se kyon nahin raham maangate! kya unake paas raham nahin hai? magar wahan tum na jaaoge; kyonki wahan jate tumhaaree chhaatee dahalatee hai. mujhse raham maangate ho, isalie na ki main chamaarin hoon, neech jaati hoon aur neech jaati kee aurat zara-see ghudakee-dhamakee vaa zara-see laalach se tumhaaree mutthee mein aa jaayagee. kitanaa sastaa saudaa hai. thaakur ho na, aisaa sastaa saudaa kyon chhodane lage?
chainasinh lajjit hokar bol- moolaa, yah baat nahin. main sach keht hoon, isamen oonch-neech kee baat nahin hai. sab aadamee baraabar hain. main to tere charanon par sir rakhane ko taiyaar hoon.
muliyaa- iseelie na ki jaanate ho, main kuchh kar nahin sakatee. jaakar kisee khataraanee ke charanon par sir rakkho, to maaloom ho ki charanon par sir rakhane kaa kya phal milataa hai. phir yah sir tumhaaree gardan par na rahegaa.
chainasinh maare sharm ke jameen mein gadaa jat tha. usakaa munh aisaa sookh gaya tha, maano maheenon kee beemaaree se uthaa ho. munh se baat na nikalatee thi. muliyaa itanee vaak-patu hai, isakaa use gumaan bhee na tha.
muliyaa phir bolee- main bhee roj baajaar jati hoon. bade-bade gharon kaa haal jaanatee hoon. mujhe kisee bade ghar kaa naam bataa do, jisamen koi saaees, koi kochavaan, koi kahaar, koi pandaa, koi mahaaraaj na ghusaa baithaa ho? yah sab bade gharon kee leelaa hai. aur woh auraten jo kuchh karti hain, theek karti hain! unake gharavaale bhee to chamaarinon aur kahaarinon par jaan dete phirate hain. lenaa-denaa baraabar ho jat hai. bechaare gareeb aadamiyon ke lie yah baaten kahan? mere aadamee ke lie sansaar mein jo kuchh hoon, main hoon. woh kisee doosaree mihariyaa kee ora aankh uthaakar bhee nahin dekhataa. sanyog kee baat hai ki main tanik sundar hoon, lekin main kaalee-kalootee bhee hoti, tab bhee woh mujhe isee tarah rakhataa. isakaa mujhe yakeen hai. main chamaarin hokar bhee itanee neech nahin hoon ki yakeen kaa badalaa khot se doon. haan, woh apne man kee karne lage, meree chhaatee par moong dalane lage, to main bhee usakee chhaatee par moong daloongee. tum mere roop hee ke deevaane ho na! aaj mujhe maan nikal aayen, kaanee ho jaaoon, to meree ora taakoge bhee nahin. bolo, jhooth kehti hoon?
chainasinh inakaar na kar sakaa.
muliyaa ne usee garv se bhare hue aavaaz mein kahaa- lekin meree ek nahin, donon aankhen phoot jaayen, tab bhee woh mujhe isee tarah rakkhegaa. mujhe uthaavegaa, baithaavegaa, khilaavegaa. tum chaahate ho, main aise aadamee ke saath kapat karoon? jaao, ab mujhe kabhee na chhedanaa, nahin achchhaa na hogaa.
3
javaanee josh hai, bal hai, raham hai, saahas hai, aatm-yakeen hai, gaurav hai aur sab kuchh jo jeevan ko pavitr, ujjval aur poorn banaa detaa hai. javaanee kaa nashaa ghamand hai, nirdayataa hai, svaarth hai, shekhee hai, vishay-vaasanaa hai, katutaa hai aur woh sab kuchh jo jeevan ko pashutaa, vikaar aur patan kee ora le jat hai. chainasinh par javaanee kaa nashaa tha. muliyaa ke sheetal chheenton ne nashaa utaar diya. jaise ubalatee huee chaashanee mein paani ke chheente pad jaane se phen mit jat hai, mail nikal jat hai aur nirmal, shuddh ras nikal aat hai. javaanee kaa nashaa jat raha, keval javaanee raha gayee. kaaminee ke shabd jitanee aasaanee se gareeb aur eemaan ko gaarat kar sakate hain, utanee hee aasaanee se unakaa uddhaar bhee kar sakate hain.
chainasinh us din se doosaraa hee aadamee ho gaya. gussaa usakee naak par rehta tha, baat-baat par majadooron ko gaaliyaan denaa, daantanaa aur peetanaa usakee aadat thi. asaamee usse thar-thar kaanpate the. majadoor use aate dekhakar apne kaam mein chust ho jate the; par jyon hee usane idhar peeth pheree aur unhonne chilam peenaa shuroo kiya. sab dil mein usse jalate the, use gaaliyaan dete the. magar us din se chainasinh itanaa dayaalu, itanaa ganbheer, itanaa sahanasheel ho gaya ki logon ko hairaanee hot tha.
kaee din gujar gaye the. ek din shaam samay chainasinh khet dekhane gaya. pur chal raha tha. usane dekh ki ek jagah naalee toot gayee hai, aur saaraa paani bahaa chalaa jat hai. kyaariyon mein paani bilakul nahin pahunchataa, magar kyaaree banaane vaalee budhiyaa chupachaap baithee hai. use isakee zara bhee phikr nahin hai ki paani kyon nahin aat. pahale yah dashaa dekhakar chainasinh aape se baahar ho jat. us aurat kee us din majooree kaat letaa aur pur chalaanevaalon ko ghudakiyaan jamaataa, par aaj use gussaa nahin aay. usane mittee lekar naalee baandh dee aur khet mein jaakar budhiyaa se bol- too yahan baithee hai aur paani sab bahaa jaa raha hai.
budhiyaa ghabadaakar bolee- abhee khul gayee hogee. raajaa! main abhee jaakar band kiye detee hoon.
yah kehti huee woh tharathar kaanpane lagee. chainasinh ne usakee dilajoee karte hue kahaa- bhaag mata, bhaag mata. mainne naalee band kar dee. budhaoo kaee din se nahin dikhaaee diye, kaheen kaam par jate hain ki nahin?
budhiyaa gadgad hokar bolee- aajakal to khaalee hee baithe hain bhaiyaa, kaheen kaam nahin lagataa.
chainasinh ne namr bhaav se kahaa- to hamaare yahan lagaa de. thodaa-saa san rakhaa hai, use kaat den.
yah keht hua woh kuen kee ora chalaa gaya. yahan chaar pur chal rahe the; par is vakt do hankave ber khaane gaye the. chainasinh ko dekhate hee majooron ke hosh uda gaye. thaakur ne poochhaa, do aadamee kahan gaye, to kya jawab denge? sab-ke-sab daante jaayenge. bechaare dil mein sahame jaa rahe the. chainasinh ne poochhaa- woh donon kahan chale gaye?
kisee ke munh se aavaaj na nikalee. sahasaa saamane se donon majoor dhotee ke ek kone mein ber bhare aate dikhaaee die. khush-khush baat karte chale aa rahe the. chainasinh par nigaah padee, to donon ke jaan sookh gaye. paanv man-man bhar ke ho gaye. ab na aate banataa hai, na jate. donon samajh gaye ki aaj daant padee, shaayad majooree bhee kat jaay. chaal dheemee pad gayee. itane mein chainasinh ne pukaaraa badh aao, badh aao, kaise ber hain, laao zara mujhe bhee do, mere hee ped ke hain na?
donon aur bhee saham uthe. aaj thaakur jeetaa na chhodegaa. kaisaa mithaa-mithaakar bol raha hai. utanee hee bhigo-bhigokar lagaayegaa. bechaare aur bhee sikud gaye.
chainasinh ne phir kahaa- jaldee se aao jee, pakkee-pakkee sab main le loongaa. zara ek aadamee lapakakar ghar se thodaa-saa namak to le lo! (baakee donon majooron se) tum bhee donon aa jaao, us ped ke ber meethe hote hain. ber khaa le, kaam to karn hee hai.
ab donon bhagodon ko kuchh dhaaras hua. sabhee ne jaakar sab ber chainasinh ke aage daal die aur pakke-pakke chhaantakar use dene lage. ek aadamee namak laane daudaa. aadhaa ghante tak chaaron pur band rahe. jab sab ber uda gaye aur thaakur chalne lage, to donon aparaadhiyon ne haath jodakar kahaa- bhaiyaajee, aaj jaan bakasee ho jaay, badee bhookh lagee thi, nahin to kabhee na jate.
chainasinh ne namrataa se kahaa- to isamen buraaee kya huee? mainne bhee to ber khaaye. ek-aadhaa ghante kaa haraj hua yahee na? tum chaahoge, to ghante bhar kaa kaam aadhaa ghante mein kar doge. na chaahoge, din-bhar mein bhee ghante-bhar kaa kaam na hogaa.
chainasinh chalaa gaya, to chaaron baaten karne lage.
ek ne kahaa- maalik is tarah rahe, to kaam karne mein jee lagataa hai. yah nahin ki haradam chhaatee par savaar.
doosaraa- mainne to samajhaa, aaj kachchaa hee khaa jaayenge.
teesaraa- kaee din se dekhataa hoon, mijaaj naram ho gaya hai.
chauthaa- saanjh ko pooree majooree mile to kahanaa.
pahalaa- tum to ho gobar-ganes. aadamee kaa rukh nahin pahachaanate.
doosaraa- ab khoob dil lagaakar kaam karenge.
teesaraa- aur kya! jab unhonne hamaare oopar chhod diya, to hamaaraa bhee dharam hai ki koi kasar na chhoden.
chauthaa- mujhe to bhaiyaa, thaakur par ab bhee yakeen nahin aat.
4
ek din chainasinh ko kisee kaam se kachaharee jan tha. paanch meel kaa saphar tha. yon to woh baraabar apne ghode par jaayaa kart tha; par aaj dhoop badee tej ho rahi thi, sochaa ekke par chalaa chaloon. mahaaveer ko kahalaa bhejaa mujhe lete jan. koi nau baje mahaaveer ne pukaaraa. chainasinh taiyaar baithaa tha. chatapat ekke par baith gaya. magar ghodaa itanaa dubalaa ho raha tha, ekke kee gaddee itanee mailee aur phatee huee, saaraa saamaan itanaa raddee ki chainasinh ko us par baithate sharm aaee. poochhaa yah saamaan kyon bigadaa hua hai mahaaveer? tumhaaraa ghodaa to itanaa dubalaa kabhee na tha; kya aajakal savaariyaan kam hain kya? mahaaveer ne kahaa, nahin maalik, savaariyaan kaahe nahin hai; magar laariyon ke saamane ekke ko kaun poochhataa hai. kahan do-dhaaee-teen kee majooree karake ghar lautataa tha, kahan ab bees aane paise bhee nahin milate? kya jaanavar ko khilaaoon kya aap khaaoon? badee vipatti mein padaa hoon. sochataa hoon ekkaa-ghodaa bech-baachakar aap logon kee majooree kar loon, par koi gaahak nahin lagataa. zyada nahin to baarah aane to ghode hee ko chaahie, ghaas oopar se. jab apanaa hee pet nahin chalataa, to jaanavar ko kaun poochhe. chainasinh ne usake phate hue kurate kee ora dekhakar kahaa, do-chaar beeghe khetee kyon nahin kar lete?
mahaaveer sir jhukaakar bol- khetee ke lie badaa paurukh chaahie maalik! mainne to yahee sochaa hai ki koi gaahak lag jaay, to ekke ko aune-paune nikaal doon, phir ghaas chheelakar baajaar le jaayaa karoon. aajakal saas-patohoo donon chheelatee hain. tab jaakar das-baarah aane paise naseeb hote hain.
chainasinh ne poochhaa- to budhiyaa baajaar jati hogee?
mahaaveer lajaataa hua bol- nahin bhaiyaa, woh itanee door kahan chal sakatee hai. gharavaalee chalee jati hai. dopahar tak ghaas chheelatee hai, teesare pahar baajaar jati hai. wahan se ghadee raat gaye lautatee hai. halakaan ho jati hai bhaiyaa, magar kya karoon, takadeer se kya jor.
chainasinh kachaharee pahunch gaye aur mahaaveer savaariyon kee toh mein idhar-udhar ikke ko ghumaataa hua shahar kee taraph chalaa gaya. chainasinh ne use paanch baje aane ko kah diya.
koi chaar baje chainasinh kachaharee se phurasat paakar baahar nikale. haate mein paan kee dukaan thi, zara aur aage badhakar ek ghanaa baragad kaa ped tha, usakee chhaanh mein beeson hee taange; ekke, phitanen khadee theen. ghode khol die gaye the. vakeelon, mukhtaaron aur aphasaron kee savaariyaan yaheen khadee rehti theen. chainasinh ne paani piyaa, paan khaayaa aur sochane lagaa koi laaree mil jaay, to zara shahar chalaa jaaoon ki usakee nigaah ek ghaasavaalee par pad gayee. sir par ghaas kaa jhaabaa rakkhe saaeeson se mol-bhaav kar rahi thi. chainasinh kaa dil uchhal padaa yah to muliyaa hai! banee-thanee, ek gulaabee saadee pahane kochavaanon se mol-tol kar rahi thi. kaee kochavaan jamaa ho gaye the. koi usse dillagee kart tha, koi ghoorataa tha, koi hansataa tha.
ek kaale-kaloote kochavaan ne kahaa- moolaa, ghaas to udake zyaadaa se zyaadaa chh: aane kee hai.
muliyaa ne unmaad paidaa karne vaalee aankho se dekhakar kahaa- chh: aane par lenaa hai, to saamane ghasiyaarinen baithee hain, chale jaao, do-chaar paise kam mein paa jaaoge, meree ghaas to baarah aane mein hee jaayagee.
ek adhed kochavaan ne phitan ke oopar se kahaa- teraa jamaanaa hai, baarah aane nahin ek rupayaa maang. lenevaale jhakh maarenge aur lenge. nikalane de vakeelon ko, ab der nahin hai.
ek taangevaale ne, jo gulaabee pagadee baandhe hue tha, bol- budhaoo ke munh mein paani bhar aay, ab muliyaa kaahe ko kisee kee ora dekhegee!
chainasinh ko aisaa gussaa aa raha tha ki ina dushton ko joote se peete. sab-ke-sab kaise usakee ora takatakee lagaaye taak rahe hain, aankho se pee jaayenge. aur muliyaa bhee yahan kitanee khush hai. na lajaatee hai, na jhijhakatee hai, na dabatee hai. kaisaa musakiraa-musakiraakar, raseelee aankho se dekh-dekhakar, sir kaa anchal khisakaa- khisakaakar, munh mod-modakar baaten kar rahi hai. vahee muliyaa, jo sheranee kee tarah tadap uthee thi.
itane mein chaar baje. amale aur vakeel-mukhtaaron kaa ek melaa-saa nikal padaa. amale laariyon par daude. vakeel-mukhtaar ina savaariyon kee ora chale. kochavaanon ne bhee chatapat ghode jote. kaee mahaashayon ne muliyaa ko rasik netron se dekh aur apanee-apanee gaadiyon par jaa baithe.
ekaaek muliyaa ghaas kaa jhaabaa liye us phitan ke peechhe daudee. phitan mein ek angrejee phaishan ke javaan vakeel sahab baithe the. unhonne paavadaan par ghaas rakhavaa lee, jeb se kuchh nikaalakar muliyaa ko diya. muliyaa muskaraaee, donon mein kuchh baaten bhee hueen, jo chainasinh na sun sake.
ek pal mein muliyaa khush-munh ghar kee ora chalee. chainasinh paanavaale kee dukaan par vismriti kee dashaa mein khadaa raha. paanavaale ne dukaan badhaaee, kapade pahine aur kebin kaa dvaar band karake neeche utaraa to chainasinh kee samaadhi tootee. poochhaa kya dukaan band kar dee?
paanavaale ne hamadardee dikhaakar kahaa- isakee davaa karo thaakur sahab, yah beemaaree achchhee nahin hai!
chainasinh ne chakit hokar poochhaa- kaisee beemaaree?
paanavaalaa bol- kaisee beemaaree! aadhaa ghante se yahan khade ho jaise koi muradaa khadaa ho. saaree kachaharee khaalee ho gayee, sab dukaanen band ho gayeen, mehatar tak jhaadoo lagaakar chal diye; tumhe kuchh khabar huee? yah buree beemaaree hai, jaldee davaa kar daalo.
chainasinh ne chhadee sanbhaalee aur phaatak kee ora chalaa ki mahaaveer kaa ekkaa saamane se aat dikhaaee diya.

kuchh door ekkaa nikal gaya, to chainasinh ne poochhaa- aaj kitane paise kamaaye mahaaveer?
mahaaveer ne hansakar kahaa- aaj to maalik, din bhar khadaa hee raha gaya. kisee ne begaar mein bhee na pakadaa. oopar se chaar paise kee beediyaan pee gaya.
chainasinh ne zara der ke baad kahaa- meree ek salaah hai. tum mujhse ek rupayaa roj liya karo. bas, jab main bulaaoon to ekkaa lekar chale aay karo. tab to tumhaaree gharavaalee ko ghaas lekar baajaar na jan padegaa. bolo manjoor hai?
mahaaveer ne sajal aankho se dekhakar kahaa- maalik, aap hee kaa to khaataa hoon. aapakee parajaa hoon. jab marajee ho, pakad mangavaaie. aapase rupaye. ..
chainasinh ne baat kaatakar kahaa- nahin, main tumse begaar nahin lenaa chaahataa. tum mujhse ek rupayaa roj le jaayaa karo. ghaas lekar gharavaalee ko baajaar mata bhejaa karo. tumhaaree aabaroo meree aabaroo hai. aur bhee rupaye-paise kaa jab kaam lage, bekhatake chale aay karo. haan, dekho, muliyaa se is baat kee bhoolakar bhee charchaa na karn. kya phaayadaa!
kaee dinon ke baad shaam samay muliyaa chainasinh se milee. chainasinh asaamiyon se maalagujaaree vasool karake ghar kee ora lapakaa jaa raha tha ki usee jagah jahaan usane muliyaa kee baanh pakadee thi, muliyaa kee aavaaj kaanon mein aayee. usane thithakakar peechhe dekh, to muliyaa daudee aa rahi thi. bol- kya hai moolaa! kyon daudatee ho, main to khadaa hoon?
muliyaa ne haanphate hue kahaa- kaee din se tumse milanaa chaahatee thi. aaj tumhe aate dekh, to daudee. ab main ghaas bechane nahin jati.
chainasinh ne kahaa- bahut achchhee baat hai.
'kya tumane kabhee mujhe ghaas bechate dekh hai? '
'haan, ek din dekh tha. kya mahaaveer ne tujhase sab kah daalaa? mainne to manaa kar diya tha. '
'woh mujhse koi baat nahin chhipaataa. '
donon ek pal chup khade rahe. kisee ko koi baat na soojhatee thi. ekaaek muliyaa ne muskaraakar kahaa- yahan tumane meree baanh pakadee thi.
chainasinh ne lajjit hokar kahaa- usako bhool jaao moolaa. mujh par jaane kaun bhoot savaar tha.
muliyaa gadgad galaa se bolee- use kyon bhool jaaoon. usee baanh gahe kee laaj to nibhaa rahe ho. gareebee aadamee se jo chaahe karaave. tumane mujhe bachaa liya. phir donon chup ho gaye.
zara der ke baad muliyaa ne phir kahaa- tumane samajhaa hogaa, main hansane-bolane mein magan ho rahi thi?
chainasinh ne balapoorvak kahaa- nahin muliyaa, mainne ek pal ke lie bhee nahin samajhaa.
muliyaa muskaraakar bolee- mujhe tumse yahee ummeed thi, aur hai.
pavan sinche hue kheton mein vishraam karne jaa raha tha, soory nishaa kee god mein vishraam karne jaa raha tha, aur us malin prakaash mein chainasinh muliyaa kee vileen hoti huee rekhaa ko khadaa dekh raha tha!
