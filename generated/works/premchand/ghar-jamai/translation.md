# Ghar Jamai

**Munshi Premchand**

1
haridhan jeth kee dupaharee mein ookh mein paani dekar aay aur baahar baithaa raha. ghar mein se dhunaa uthataa nazar aat tha. chhan-chhan kee aavaaj bhee aa rahi thi. usake dono saale usake baad aaye aur ghar mein chale gaye. donon saalon ke ladake bhee aaye aur usee tarah andar daakhil ho gaye; par haridhan andar na jaa sakaa. idhar ek maheene se usake saath yahan jo barataav ho raha tha aur visheshakar kal use jaisee phatakaar sunanee padee thi, woh usake paanv mein bediyaan-see daale hue tha. kal usakee saas hee ne to kahaa tha, meraa dee tumse bhar gaya, main tumhaaree zindagi kaa thekaa liye baithee hoon kya? aur sabase badhakar apanee aurat kee nishthurataa ne usake dil ke tukade kar diye the. woh baithee yah phatakaar sunatee rahi, par ek baar bhee to usake munh se na nikalaa, ammaan, tum kyon inakaa beizzatee kar rahi ho? baithee gat-gat sunatee rahi. shaayad meree durgati par khush ho rahi thi. is ghar mein woh kaise jaay? kya phir vahee gaaliyaan khaane, vahee phatakaar sunane ke lie? aur aaj is ghar mein jeevan ke das saal guzar jaane par yah haal ho raha hain. main kisee se kam kaam nahin keht hoon? donon saale meethee neend sote rehte hain aur main bailon ko saanee-paani detaa hoon, chhaantee kaatataa hoon. wahan sab log pal-pal par chilam peete hai, main aankhe band kiye apne kaam mein lagaa rehta hoon. shaam samay par gharavaale gaane-bajaane chale jate hain. main ghadee raat tak gaayen-bhaise duhataa hoon. usakaa yah puraskaar mil raha hain ki koi khaane ko bhee nahin poochhataa. ulate aur gaaliyaan milatee hain.
usakee aurat ghar se dol lekar nikalee aur bolee - zaraa ise kuen se kheench lo, ek boond paani nahin hain.
haridhan ne dol liya aur kuen se paani bhar laayaa. use jor se bhookh lagee huee tha. samajhaa, ab khaane ko bulaane aayegee, magar aurat dol lekar andar gayee to vaheen kee ho rahi. haridhan thakaa-maandaa, kshudhaa se vyaakul padaa so raha.
sahasaa usakee aurat gumaanee ne aakar use jagaayaa.
haridhan ne pade-pade hee kahaa - kya hai? kya padaa bhee na rehne degee yaa aur paani chaahie?
gumaanee katu aavaaz mein bolee- gurraate kyo ho, khaane ko bulaane aayee hoon.
haridhan ne dekh, usake dono saale aur bade saale ke dono ladake khana kiye chale jaa rahe hai. usakee deh mein aag lag gayee. meree ab yah naubat pahunch gayee ki ina logon ke saath baith kar khaa bhee nahee sakataa. main inakee joothee thaalee chaatanevaalaa hoon. main inakaa kuttaa hoon, jise khaane ke baad tukadaa rotee daal dee jati hai. yahee ghar hain, jahaan aaj se das saal pahale usakaa kitanaa aadar-satkaar hot tha. saale gulaam bane rehte the. saas munh johatee rehti thi. aurat poojaa karti thi. tab usake paas rupaye the, zaayadaad thi. ab woh daridr hain. usakee saaree zaayadaad ko inheen logo ne koodaa kar diya. ab rotiyon ke bhee laale hain. usake jee mein ek jvaalaa-see uthee ki isee vakt andar jaakar saas ko aur saalee ko bhigo-bhigokar lagaae, par jabt karake raha gaya. pade-pade bol- mujhe bhookh nahee hain. aaj na khaaoongaa.
gumaanee ne kahaa - na khaaoge meree balaa se, haan nahee to! khaaoge, tumhaare hee pet mein jaayegaa, kuchh mere pet mein thode hee jaayegaa.
haridhan kaa gussaa aansoo ban gaya. yah meree aurat hain, jisake lie maine apanaa sarvasv mittee mein milaa diya. mujhe ulloo banaakar yah sab mujhe ab nikaal denaa chaahate hain. woh ab kahan jaaye! kya karen!
usakee saas aakar boleen- chalakar khaa kyon nahee lete jee, roothate kis par ho. yahan tumhaare nakhare sahane kaa kisee mein bootaa nahin hain. jo dete hain, woh mata denaa aur kya karoge! tumse betee byaahee hain, kuchh tumhaaree zindagi kaa thekaa nahin liya hain.
haridhan ne marmaahat hokar kahaa - haan ammaan, meree bhool thi ki main yahee samajh raha tha. ab mere paas kya hain ki tum meree zindagi kaa thekaa logee? jab mere paas paisaa bhee tha, tab sab kuchh aat tha. ab daridr hoon, tum kyon baat poochhogee?
boodhee saas bhee munh phulaakar bheetar chalee gayee.
2
bachchon ke lie baap ek phaalatoo-see cheez - ek vilaas kee vastu - hai, jaise ghode ke lie chane yaa baabuon ke lie mohanabhog. mohanabhog umr-bhar na mile, to kisakaa nukasaan hai; magar ek din rotee-daal ke darshan na ho, to phir dekhie kya haal hot hain! pitaa ke darshan kabhee-kabhee shaam-sabere ho jate hai, woh bachche ko uchhaalataa hain, dulaarataa hai, kabhee god mein lekar yaa ungalee pakadakar sair karaane le jat hain aur bas, yahee usake farz kee iti hain. woh paradesh chalaa jaay, bachche ko paravaa nahin hoti; lekin maan to bachche kaa sarvasv hain. ladakaa ek minat ke lie bhee usakaa viyog nahin sah sakataa. pitaa koi ho, use paravaa nahin, keval ek uchhaalane-kudaanevaalaa aadamee hona chaahie; lekin maan to apanee honee chaahie, solahon-aane apanee. vahee roop, vahee rang, vahee pyaar, vahee sab-kuchh. woh agar nahin hain, to ladakaa ke jeevan kaa srot maano sookh jat hain, phir woh shiv kaa nandee hain, par phool yaa jal chadhaanaa laazimee nahin, aKtiyaaree hain. haridhan kee maan kaa aaj das saal hue, dehaant ho gaya tha. us vakt usakaa vivaah ho chukaa tha. woh solah saal kaa kumaar tha. par maan ke marate hee use maaloom hua ki main kitanaa nissahaay hoon. jaise us ghar par usakaa adhikaar hee na raha ho. bahano ke vivaah ho chuke the. bhai koi doosaraa na tha. bechaaraa akele ghar mein jate bhee darataa tha. maan ke lie rotaa tha, par maan kee parachhaaee se darataa tha. jis kotharee mein usame deh-tyaag kiya tha, udhar aankhe tak na uthaataa. ghar mein ek buaa thi, woh haridhan ko bahut dulaar karti. haridhan ko ab doodh zyada milataa, kaam bhee kam karn padataa. buaa baar-baar poochhatee - betaa! kuchh khaaoge? baap bhee ab use zyada pyaar karte. usake lie alag ek gaay mangavaa dee. kabhee-kabhee use kuchh paise dete ki jaise chaahe kharch kare. par ina marahamon se woh ghaav na pooraa hot tha, jisane usakee aatmaa ko aahat kar diya tha. woh dulaar aur pyaar baar-baar maan kee yaad dilaataa. maan kee ghudakiyon mein jo majaa tha, woh kya is dulaar mein tha? maan se maangakar, ladakar, thunakakar, roothakar lene mein jo khushi tha, woh kya bhikshaadaan mein tha? pahale woh svasth tha, maang-maangakar khaataa tha, lad-ladakar khaataa; ab woh beemaar tha, achchhe-se-achchhe padaarth use diye jate the, par bhookh na thi.
saal bhar tak woh isee dashaa mein raha. phir duniya badal gayee. ek naee aurat, jise log usakee maan kehte the, usake ghar mein aayee aur dekhate-dekhate ek kaalee ghataa kee tarah usake sankuchit bhoomandal par chhaa gayee- saaree hariyaalee, saare prakaash par andhakaar kaa paradaa pad gaya. haridhan ne is nakalee maan se baat tak na kee, kabhee usake paas na gaya tak nahin. ek din ghar se nikalaa aur susaraal chalaa aay.
baap ne baar-baar bulaayaa, par usake jeete-jee woh phir us ghar mein na gaya. jis din usake pitaa ke dehaant kee khabar milee, use ek tarah kaa eershyaamay harsh hua. usakee aankhon mein aansoo kee ek boond bhee na aayee.
is naye sansaar mein aakar haridhan ko ek baar phir maatri-pyaar kaa khushi milaa. usakee saas ne rishi-varadaan kee tarah usake shoony jeevan ko vibhootiyon se paripoorn kar diya. marubhoomi mein hariyaalee utpann ho gayee. saaliyon kee chuhal mein, saas ke pyaar mein, saalon ke vaak-vilaas mein aur aurat ke pyaar mein usake jeevan kee saaree aakaankshaaen pooree ho gayeen. saas kehti- betaa, tum is ghar ko apanaa hee samajho, tumheen meree aankhon ke taare ho. woh usse apne ladakon kee, bahuon kee shikaayat karti. woh dil mein samajhataa tha, saasajee mujhe apne beton se bhee zyada chaahatee hain.
baap ke marate hee woh ghar gaya aur apne hisse kee jaaYadaad ko koodaa karake roopayon kee thailee lie phir aa gaya. ab usakaa doonaa aadar-satkaar hone lagaa. usane apanee saaree jaayadaad saas ke charanon par arpan karake apne jeevan ko saarthak kar diya. ab tak use kabhee-kabhee ghar kee yaad aa jati thi, ab bhoolakar bhee usakee yaad na aati, maano woh usake jeevan kaa koi bheeshan kaand tha, jise bhool jan hee usake lie achchhaa tha. woh sabase pahale uthataa, sabase zyada kaam kart. usakaa manoyog, usakaa parishram dekhakar gaanv ke log daanton ungalee dabaate the. usake sasur kaa bhaagy bakhaarate, jise aisaa daamaad mil gaya. lekin jyon-jyon din gujarate gaye, usakaa maan-izzat ghatataa gaya. pahale devataa tha, phir ghar kaa aadamee, ant mein ghar kaa daas ho gaya. rotiyon mein bhee baadhaa pad gayee! beizzatee hone lagaa. agar ghar ke log bhookhon marate aur saath hee use bhee maranaa padataa, to use zara bhee shikaayat na hoti. lekin jab woh dekhataa, aur log moonchhon par taav de rahe hain, keval main hee doodh kee makkhee banaa diya gaya hoon, to usake antastal se ek lambee, thandee aah nikal jati. abhee usakee umr kuchh pachchees saal kee to thi. itanee umr is ghar mein kaise gujaregee? aur to aur, usakee aurat ne bhee aankhen pher lee! yah us vipatti kaa sabase kroor drishy tha.

3
haridhan to udhar bhookhaa-pyaasaa phikr-daah mein jal raha tha, idhar ghar mein saasajee aur dono saalo mein baate ho rahi thi. gumaanee bhee haan-mein-haan milaatee jati thi.
bade saale ne kahaa- ham logon kee baraabaree karte hain. yah nahee samajhate ki kisee ne usakee zindagi-bhar kaa beedaa thode hee liya hain. das saal ho gayan. itane dinon mein kya do-teen hajaar na hadap gayen honge?
chhote saale ne kahaa- majoor ho to aadamee ghudake bhee, daante bhee, ab inse koi kya kahen. na jaane inse kab pind chhootegaa bhee yaa nahin? apne dil mein samajhate honge, mainne do hajaar rupayen nahin diye hain? yah nahin samajhate ki unake do hajaar rupaye kab ke uda chuke hain. savaa ser to ek joon ko chaahie.
saas gambheer bhaav se bolee - badee bhaaree khoraak hai!
gumaanee maan ke sir se joon nikal rahi thi. sulagate hue dil se bolee - nikamme aadamee ko khaane ke sivaa aur kaam hee kya rehta hai!
bade- khaane kee koi baat nahee hain. jisako jitanee bhookh ho utanaa khaaye lekin kuchh paidaa karn chaahie. yah nahee samajhate ki pahunaee mein kisee ke din kate hai!
chhote - main to ek din kah doongaa; ab aap apanee raah leejie, aapakaa karajaa nahee khaayaa hain.
gumaanee gharavaalon kee aisee-aisee baate sunakar apne pati se napharat karne lagee thi. agar woh baahar se chaar paise laataa, to is ghar mein usakaa kitanaa maan-izzat hot, woh bhee raanee ban kar rehti. na jaane kyon kaheen baahar jaakar kamaate usakee naanee maratee hain. gumaanee kee manovrittiyaan abhee tak bilkul baalapan kee-see thi. usakaa apanaa koi ghar na tha. usee ghar kaa hit-ahit usake lie bhee pradhaan tha. woh bhee unheen shabdon mein soch karti, is samasyaa ko unheen aankhon se dekhatee, jaise usake gharavaale dekhate the. sach to hai, do hajaar rupaye mein kya kisee ko mol le lenge? das saal mein do hajaar rupaye hote hee kya hai? do sau hee to saal-bhar ke hue. kya do aadamee saal-bhar mein do sau bhee na khaaenge? phir kapade-latte, doodh-ghee sab kuchh to hain. das saal ho gaye, ek peetal kaa chhallaa nahin banaa. ghar se nikalate to jaise inake jaan nikalate hai. jaanate hai, jaise pahale poojaa hoti thi, vaise hee janm-bhar hoti rahegee. yah nahee sochate ki pahale aur baat thi; ab aur baat hai. bahoo hee pahale susaraal jati hai, to usakaa kitanaa mahaatam hot hai. usake dolee se utarate hee baaje bajate hain, gaanv-muhalle kee auraten usakaa munh dekhane aati hain aur rupaye detee hain. maheenon use ghar-bhar se achchhaa khaane ko milataa hain, achchhaa pahanane ko, koi kaam nahin liya jat; lekin chhah maheene baad koi usakee baat bhee nahin poochhataa, woh ghar-bhar kee laudee ho jati hain. unake ghar mein meree to vahee gati hoti. phir kaahe ko ronaa. jo yah kaho ki main to kaam kart hoon, to tumhaaree bhool hai, majoor aur baat hain. use aadamee daantataa bhee hain, maarataa bhee hai, jab chaahataa hai rakhataa hain, jab jee chaahataa hain nikaal detaa hain. kasakar kaam letaa hain. yah nahin ki jab jee mein aay, kuchh kaam kiya, jab jee mein aay, padakar so rahe.
4
haridhan bhee padaa andar-hee-andar sulag raha tha ki donon saale baahar aaye aur bade sahab bole- bhaiyaa, utho, teesaraa pahar dhal gaya, kab tak sote rahoge? saaraa khet padaa hain.
haridhan chat se uth baithaa aur teevr aavaaz mein bol - kya tum logon mein mujhe ulloo samajh liya hain?
donon saale hakkaa-bakkaa ho gaye. jis aadamee ne kabhee jabaan nahin kholee hameshaa gulaamon kee tarah haath baandhe haajir raha, woh aaj ekaaek itanaa aatmaabhimaanee ho gaya, yah unako chaukaa dene ke lie kaaphee tha. kuchh jawab na soojhaa.
haridhan ne dekh, ina donon ke kadam ukhad gaye hain, to ek dhakkaa aur dene kee prabal ichchhaa ko na rok sakaa. usee dhang se bol- meree bhee aankhen hain, andhaa nahin hoon, na baharaa hee hoon. chhaatee phaadakar kaam karoon aur us par bhee kuttaa samajhaa jaaoon, aise gadhe kaheen aur honge.
ab bade saale bhee garam pade- tumhe kisee ne yahan baandh to nahee rakhaa hain.
abakee haridhan laajavaab hua. koi baat na soojhee.
bade ne phir usee dhang se kahaa- agar tum yah chaaho ki janm-bhar paahunne bane raho aur tumhaaraa vaisaa hee aadar-satkaar hot rahes to hamaare bas kee baat nahin hain.
haridhan ne aankhen nikaal kar kahaa- kya main tum logon se kam kaam kart hoon?
bade- yah kaun keht hain?
haridhan - to tumhaare ghar kee yahee neeti hain ki jo sabase zyada kaam kare, vahee bhookhon maaraa jaay?
bade- tum khud khaane nahin gaye. kya koi tumhaare munh mein kaur daal detaa?
haridhan nen hoth chabaakar kahaa- main khud khaane nahee gaya! kehte tumhe laaj nahee aati.
'nahin aayee thi bahan tumhe bulaane? '
haridhan kee aankhon mein khoon utar gaya, daant peesakar raha gaya.
chhote saale ne kahaa- ammaan bhee aayee thi. tumane kah diya, mujhe bhookh nahee hain, to kya karti.
saas bheetar se lapakee chalee aa rahi theen. yah baat sunakar bolee- kitanaa kahakar haar gayee, koi uthe na to main kya karoon!
haridhan ne vish, khoon aur aag se bhare hue aavaaz mein kahaa- main tumhaare ladakon kaa joothaa khaane ke lie hoon! mai kuttaa hoon ki tum log khaakar mere saamane rukhee rotee kaa tukadaa phenk do?
budhiyaa- nen ainthakar kahaa - to kya tum ladakon kee baraabaree karogen?
haridhan paraast ho gaya! budhiyaa ke ek hee vaakprahaar ne usakaa kaa kaam tamaam kar diya. usakee tanee huee bhaven dheelee pad gayee, aankhon kee aag bujh gayee, phadakate hue nathane shaant ho gaye. kisee aahat manushy kee tarah woh jameen par gir padaa. 'kya tum mere ladakon kee baraabaree karoge? ' yah vaaky ek lambe bhaale kee tarah usake dil mein chubhataa chalaa jat tha - na dil kaa ant tha, na us bhaale kaa!
5
saare dhar ne khaayaa, par haridhan na uthaa. saas ne manaayaa, saaliyon ne manaayaa, sasur ne manaayaa, dono saale manaakar haar gaye. haridhan na uthaa, wahan dvaar par ek taat padaa tha. use uthaakar sabase alag kuen par le gaya aur jagat par bichhaakar padaa raha.
raat bheeg chukee thi. anant aakaash mein ujjaval taare baalakon kee tarah kreedaa kar rahe the. koi naachataa tha, koi uchhalataa tha, koi hansataa tha, koi aankhen meenchakar phir khol detaa tha. raha-rahakar koi saahasee ladakaa sapaataa bharakar ek pal mein us vistrit kshetr ko paar kar letaa tha aur na jaane kahan chhaa jat tha. haridhan ko apanaa bachpan yaad aay, jab woh bhee isee tarah kreedaa kart tha. usakee baal-smritiyaan unheen chamakeele taaron kee tarah prajvalit ho gayeen. woh apanaa chhotaa-saa ghar, woh aam kaa baaG, jahaan woh keriyaan chunaa kart tha; woh maidaan, jahaan kabaddee khelaa kart tha, sab usakaa yaad aane lage. phir apanee snehamayee maan kee saday moorti usake saamane khadee ho gayee. una aankhon mein kitanee karunaa thi, kitanee raham thi. use aisaa jaan padaa, maano maan kee aankhon mein aansoo bhare, use chhaatee se lagaa lene ke lie haath phailaae usakee ora chalee aa rahi hain. woh us madhur bhaavanaa mein apne ko bhool gaya. aisaa jaan padaa, maano maan ne use chhaatee se lagaa liya hain aur usake sir par haath pher rahi hain. woh rone lagaa, phoot-phootakar rone lagaa. usee aatm-sammohit dashaa mein usake munh se yah shabd nikale- ammaan, tumane mujhe itanaa bhulaa diya. dekho, tumhaare pyaare laal kee kya dashaa ho rahi hai? koi us paani ko bhee nahin poochhataa. kya jahaan tum ho, wahan mere lie jagah nahee hain!
sahasaa gumaanee ne aakar pukaaraa- kya so gaye tum, aisee bhee kya raachchhasee neend! chal kar khaa kyon nahin lete? kab tak koi tumhaare lie baithaa rahe.
haridhan us kalpanaa jagat se kroor pratyaksh mein aa gaya. vahee kuen kee jagat thi, vahee phataa hua taat aur gumaanee saamane khadee kah rahi thi- kab tak koi tumhaare lie baithaa rahe!
haridhan uth baithaa aur maano talavaar myaan se nikaalakar bol- bhalaa, tumhe meree sudh to aayee! mainne kah to diya tha, mujhe bhookh nahee hain.
gumaanee- to kitane din tak na khaaoge?
'ab is ghar kaa paani bhee na peeoongaa. tujhe mere saath chalanaa hai yaa nahin? '
dridh sankalp se bhare hue ina shabdon ko sunakar gumaanee saham uthee. bolee- kahan jaa rahe ho?
haridhan ne maano nashe mein kahaa- tujhe isase kya matalab? mere saath chalegee yaa nahin? phir peechhe se na kahanaa, mujhse kahaa nahin.
gumaanee aapatti ke bhaav se bolee- tum bataate kyon nahin, kahan jaa rahe ho?
'too mere saath chalegee yaa nahin'
'jab tak bataa na doge, main na jaaoongee. '
'to maaloom ho gaya, too nahin jan chaahatee. mujhe itanaa hee poochhanaa tha, nahee to ab tak main aadhee door nikal gaya hot. '
yah kahakar uthaa aur apne ghar kee ora chalaa. gumaanee pukaaratee rahi- 'sun lo, sun lo' par usane phirakar bhee na dekh.

6
tees meel kee manjil haridhan nen paanch ghanton mein tay kee. jab woh apne gaanv kee amaraaiyon ke saamane pahunchaa, to usakee maatri-bhaavanaa ushaa kee sunaharee god mein khel rahi thi. una vrikshon ko dekhakar usakaa vihval dil naachane lagaa. mandir kaa sunaharaa kalash dekhakar woh is tarah daudaa, maano ek chhalaang mein usake oopar jaa pahunchegaa. woh veg mein daudaa jaa raha tha, maano usakee maan god phailaae use bulaa rahi ho. jab woh aamon ke baaG mein pahunchaa, jahaan daaliyon par baithakar woh haathee kee savaaree kaa khushi paataa tha, jahaan ke kachche beron aur lisodon mein ek svargeey svaad tha, to woh baith gaya aur bhoomi par sir jhukaakar rone lagaa, maano apanee maan ko apanee vipatti-kathaa sunaa raha ho. wahan kee vaayu mein, wahan ke prakaash mein, maano usakee viraat-roopinee maan vyaapt ho rahi thi. wahan kee angul-angul bhoomi maan ke pad-chihnon se pavitr thi, maan ke pyaar mein doobe hue shabd abhee tak maano aakaash mein goonj rahe the. is vaayu aur is aakaash mein na jaane kaun-see sanjeevanee thi, jisane usake shokaartt dil ko phir baalotsaah se bhar diya. woh ek ped par chadh gaya aur adhar se aam tod-todakar khaane lagaa. saas ke woh kathor shabd, aurat kaa woh nishthur aaghaat, woh saaraa beizzatee use bhool gaya. usake paanv phool gaye the, talavon mein jalan ho rahi thi; par is khushi mein use kisee baat kaa dhyaan na tha.
sahasaa rakhavaale ne pukaaraa- woh kaun oopar chadhaa hain re? utar abhee nahin to aisaa patthar kheenchakar maaroongaa ki vahee thande ho jaaoge.
usane kaee gaaliyaan bhee dee. is phatakaar aur ina gaaliyon mein is samay haridhan ko alaukik khushi mil raha tha. woh daaliyon me chhip gaya, kaee aam kaat-kaat neeche giraaye, aur zor se thattaa maarakar hansaa. aisee ullaas se bharee huee hansee usane bahut din se na hansee thi.
rakhavaale ko yah hansee parichit maaloom huee. magar haridhan yahan kahan? woh sasuraal kee rotiyaan tod raha hain! kaisaa hansodhaa tha, kitanaa chibillaa. na jaane bechaare kaa kya haal hua? ped kee daal se taalaab mein kood padataa tha. ab gaanv mein aisaa kaun hai?
daantakar bol- wahan se baithe-baithe hansoge, to aakar saaree hansee nikaal doongaa, nahin to seedhe utar aao.
woh gaaliyaan dete jaa raha tha ki ek guthalee aakar usake sir par lagee. sir sahalaataa hua bol- yah kaun shaitaan hain, nahee maanataa. thahar to, main aakar teree khabar letaa hoon.
usane apanee lakadee neeche rakh dee aur bandaron kee tarah chat-pat oopar chadh gaya. dekh to haridhan baithaa muskaraa raha hain. chakit hokar bol- are haridhan, tum yahan kab aaye? is ped par kab se baithe ho?
donon bachpan ke sakhaa vaheen gale mile.
'yahan kab aaye? chalo, ghar chalo. bhale aadamee, kya wahan aam bhee mayassar na hote the? '
haridhan ne muskaraakar kahaa- mangaroo, ina aamon mein jo svaad hai, woh aur kaheen ke aamon mein nahin hain. gaan kaa kya rang-dhang hai?
mangaroo- sab chainachaan hai bhaiyaa! tumane to jaise naataa hee tod liya. is tarah koi apanaa gaanv-ghar chhod detaa hain? jab se tumhaare daadaa mare, saaree girastee chaupat ho gayee. do chhote-chhote ladake hai. unake lie kya hot hai?
haridhan- ab us girastee se kya vaastaa hai bhai? main to apanaa le-de chukaa. majooree to milegee na? tumhaaree gaiyaa hee charaa diya karoongaa; mujhe khaane ko de denaa.
mangaroo ne avishvaas ke bhaav se kahaa- are bhaiyaa, kaisee baate karte ho, tumhaare lie jaan haajir hai. kya sasuraal mein ab na rahoge? koi phikr nahin. pahale to tumhaaraa ghar hee hai. use sanbhaalo! chhote-chhote bachche hai, unako paalo. tum nayee ammaan se naahak darate the. badee seedhee hai bechaaree. bas, apanee maan samajho. tumhe paakar to nihaal ho jaayegee. achchhaa, gharavaalee ko bhee to laaoge?
haridhan- usakaa ab munh na dekhoongaa. mere lie woh mar gayee.
mangaroo- to dusaree sagaaee ho jaayegee. abakee aisee mehariyaa laa doongaa ki usake pair dho-dhokar piyoge, lekin kaheen pahalee bhee aa gayee to?
haridhan- woh na aayagee.

7
haridhan apne ghar pahunchaa to dono bhai, 'bhaiyaa aaye! bhaiyaa aaye! ' kahakar bheetar daude, aur maan ko khabar dee.
us ghar mein kadam rakhate hee haridhan ko aisee shaant mahimaa kaa tajurbaa hua maano woh apanee maan kee god mein baithaa hua hain. itane dinon thokaren khaane se usakaa dil komal ho gaya tha. jahaan pahale ghamand tha, aagrah tha, hekadee thi, wahan ab maayoosee thi, paraajay aur yaachanaa thi. beemaaree ko zor kam ho chalaa tha, ab maamoolee davaa bhee asar kar sakatee thi. kile kee deevaaren chhid chukee thi. ab usamen ghus jan asaadhy na tha. vahee ghar jisse woh ek din virakt ho gaya tha, ab god phailaaye use aashray dene ko taiyaar tha. haridhan kaa niravalamb man yah aashray paakar maano tript ho gaya.
shaam ko vimaataa ne kahaa - betaa, tum ghar aa gaye, hamaare dhany bhaag. ab ina bachchon ko paalo. maan kaa naataa na sahee, baap kaa naataa to hai hee. mujhe ek rotee de denaa, khaakar ek kone mein padee rahoongee. tumhaapee ammaan se meree bahan kaa naataa hain. us naate se tum mere ladake hote ho.
haridhan ne maatri-vihval aankhon se vimaataa ke roop me apanee maan ke darshan kiye. ghar ke ek-ek kone se maatri-smritiyon kee chhataa chaandanee kee tarah chhitakee huee thi, vimaataa kaa praudh munkhamandal bhee usee chhataa se ranjit tha.
doosare din haridhan phir kandhe par hal rakhakar khet ko chalaa. usake munh par ullaas tha aur aankhon mein garv. woh ab kisee kaa aashrit nahin, aashrayadaataa tha, kisee ke dvaar kaa bhikshuk nahee, ghar kaa rakshak tha.
ek din usane sunaa, gumaanee ne doosaraa ghar kar liya. maan se bol- tumane sunaa kaakee! gumaanee ne ghar kar liya.
kaakee ne kahaa- ghar kya kar legee, thatthaa hai. biraadaree mein aisaa andher? panchaayat nahee, adaalat to hain?
haridhan ne kahaa- nahee kaakee, bahut achchhaa hua. laa, mahaaveerajee ko laddoo chadhaa aaoon. main to dar raha tha, kaheen mere gale na aa pade. bhagavaan ne meree sun lee. main wahan se yahee thaanakar chalaa tha, ab usakaa munh na dekhoongaa.
