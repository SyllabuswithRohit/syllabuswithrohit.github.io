# Panch Parmeshwar

**Munshi Premchand**

1
jumman shekh alagoo chaudharee mein gaadhee mitrataa thi| saajhe mein khetee hoti thi| kuchh len-den mein bhee saajhaa tha| ek ko doosare par atal yakeen tha| jumman jab haj karne gaye the, tab apanaa ghar alagoo ko saunp gaye the, aur alagoo jab kabhee baahar jaate, to jumman par apanaa ghar chhod dete the| unamen na khaanaa-paanaa kaa vyavahaar tha, na dharm kaa naataa; keval soch milate the| mitrataa kaa moolamantr bhee yahee hai|
is mitrataa kaa janm usee samay hua, jab donon mitr ladakaa hee the, aur jumman ke poojy pitaa, jumaraatee, unhen shikshaa pradaan karte the| alagoo ne guroo jee kee bahut sevaa kee thi, khoob pyaale dhoye| unakaa hukkaa ek pal ke lie bhee vishraam na lene paataa tha, kyonki har chilam alagoo ko aadh ghante tak kitaabon se alag kar detee thi| alagoo ke pitaa puraane vichaaron ke manushy the| unhen shikshaa kee apekshaa guru kee sevaa-shushrooshaa par zyaadaa yakeen tha| vah kehte the ki padhai padhane ne nahin aatee; jo kuchh hot hai, guru ke aasheervaad se| bas, guru jee kee kripaa-nazar chaahie| isalie yadi alagoo par jumaraatee shekh ke aasheervaad athavaa satsang kaa kuchh phal na hua, to yah maanakar santosh kar lenaa ki vidyopaarjan mein mainne yathaashakti koee baat uthaa nahin rakhee, padhai usake bhaagy hee mein na thi, to kaise aatee?
magar jumaraatee shekh svayan aasheervaad ke kaayal na the| unhen apne sote par zyaadaa bharosaa tha, aur usee sote ke prataap se aaj-paas ke gaonvon mein jumman kee poojaa hoti thi| unake likhe hue rehananaame yaa bainaame par kachaharee kaa muharrir bhee kadam na uthaa sakataa tha| halke kaa daakiyaa, kaanstebil aur tahaseel kaa chaparaasee--sab unakee kripaa kee aakaankshaa rakhate the| isalie alagoo kaa maan unake paisaa ke vajah tha, to jumman shekh apanee anamol padhai se hee sabake aadarapaatr bane the|
2
jumman shekh kee ek boodhee khaalaa (mausee) thi| usake paas kuchh thodee-see milakiyat thi; lekin usake paas sanbandhiyon mein koee na tha| jumman ne lambe-chaude vaade karake vah milakiyat apne naam likhavaa lee thi| jab tak daanapatr kee rajistree na huee thi, tab tak khaalaajaan kaa khoob aadar-satkaar kiya gaya; unhen khoob svaadisht padaarth khilaaye gaye| halave-pulaav kee varshaa- see kee gayee; par rajistree kee mohar ne ina khaatiradaariyon par bhee maanon muhar lagaa dee| jumman kee patnee kareeman rotiyon ke saath kadavee baaton ke kuchh tej, teekhe saalan bhee dene lagee| jumman shekh bhee nithur ho gaye| ab bechaaree khaalaajaan ko praay: nity hee aisee baaten sunanee padatee thi|
budhiyaa na jaane kab tak jiyegee| do-teen beeghe oosar kyaa de diya, maanon mol le liya hai ! baghaaree daal ke binaa rotiyon nahin utarateen ! jitanaa rupayaa isake pet mein jhonk chuke, utane se to ab tak gaonva mol le lete|
kuchh din khaalaajaan ne sunaa aur sahaa; par jab na sahaa gaya tab jumman se shikaayat kee| tumman ne sthaaneey karmachaaree—grihasvaanmee—ke intazaam denaa theek na samajhaa| kuchh din tak din tak aur yon hee ro-dhokar kaam chalataa raha| ant mein ek din khaalaa ne jumman se kahaa—betaa ! tumhaare saath meraa nirvaah na hogaa| tum mujhe rupaye de diya karo, main apanaa pakaa-khaa loongee|
jumman ne dhrishtataa ke saath javaab diya-— rupaye kyaa yahan phalate hain?
khaalaa ne namrataa se kahaa—mujhe kuchh rookhaa-sookhaa chaahie bhee ki nahin?
jumman ne gambheer aavaaz se javaab diya—to koee yah thode hee samajhaa tha ki too maut se ladakar aayee ho?
khaalaa bigad gayeen, unhonne panchaayat karne kee dhamakee dee| jumman hanse, jis tarah koee shikaaree hiran ko jaalee kee taraph jaate dekh kar man hee man hansataa hai| vah bole—haon, jaroor panchaayat karo| phaisalaa ho jaay| mujhe bhee yah raat-din kee khatakhat pasand nahin|
panchaayat mein kisakee jeet hogee, is vishay mein jumman ko kuchh bhee shak na tha| aas-paas ke gaonvon mein aisaa kaun tha, usake anugrahon kaa rinee na ho; aisaa kaun tha, jo usako shatru banaane kaa saahas kar sake? kisamen itanaa bal tha, jo usakaa saamanaa kar sake? aasamaan ke pharishte to panchaayat karne aavenge hee nahin|
3
isake baad kaee din tak boodhee khaalaa haath mein ek lakadee liye aas-paas ke gaanvon mein daudatee raheen| kamar jhuk kar kamaan ho gayee thi| ek-ek pag chalanaa doobhar tha; magar baat aa padee thi| usakaa phaisalaa karn jarooree tha|
biralaa hee koee bhalaa aadamee hogaa, jisake samaane budhiyaa ne du:kha ke onsoo na bahaaye hon| kisee ne to yon hee ooparee man se hoon-haon karake taal diya, aur kisee ne is anyaay par jamaane ko gaaliyaan deen| kahaa—kabr mein paonva jatake hue hain, aaj mare, kal doosaraa din, par havas nahin maanatee| ab tumhen kyaa chaahie? rotee khaao aur allaah kaa naam lo| tumhen ab khetee-baaree se kyaa kaam hai? kuchh aise sajjan bhee the, jinhen haasy-ras ke rasaasvaadan kaa achchhaa avasar milaa| jhukee huee kamar, popalaa munh, san ke-se baal itanee saamagree ekatr hon, tab hansee kyon na aave? aise nyaayapriy, dayaalu, deen-vatsal aadamee bahut kam the, jinhonne is abalaa ke dukhade ko gaur se sunaa ho aur usako saantvanaa dee ho| chaaron ora se ghoom-ghaam kar bechaaree alagoo chaudharee ke paas aayee| laathee patak dee aur dam lekar bolee—betaa, tum bhee dam bhar ke liye meree panchaayat mein chale aan|
alagoo-— mujhe bulaa kar kyaa karogee? kaee gaonva ke aadamee to aavenge hee|
khaalaa—apanee vipad to sabake aage ro aayee| ab aane na aane kaa akhtiyaar unako hai|
alagoo—yon aane ko aa jaaoongaa; magar panchaayat mein munh na kholoongaa|
khaalaa—kyon betaa?
alagoo—ab isakaa kayaa javaab doon? apanee khushee| jumman meraa puraanaa mitr hai| usse bigaad nahin kar sakataa|
khaalaa—betaa, kyaa bigaad ke dar se eemaan kee baat na kahoge?
hamaare soye hue dharm-jnaan kee saaree sampatti lut jaay, to use khabar nahin hot, lekin lalakaar sunakar vah sachet ho jat hai| phir use koee jeet nahin sakataa| alagoo is savaal kaa kaaee javaab na de sakaa, par usake dil mein ye shabd goonj rahe the-
kyaa bigaad ke dar se eemaan kee baat na kahoge?
4
shaam samay ek ped ke neeche panchaayat baithee| shekh jumman ne pahale se hee pharsh bichhaa rakhaa tha| unhonne paan, ilaayachee, hukke-tambaakoo aadi kaa intazaam bhee kiya tha| haon, vah svay alabattaa alagoo chaudharee ke saath jaraa door par baithejab panchaayat mein koee aa jat tha, tab dave hue salaam se usakaa svaagat karte the| jab soory ast ho gaya aur chidiyon kee kalaravayukt panchaayat pedon par baithee, tab yahon bhee panchaayat shuroo huee| pharsh kee ek-ek angul jameen bhar gayee; par adhikaansh darshak hee the| nimantrit mahaashayon mein se keval ve hee log padhaare the, jinhen jumman se apanee kuchh kasar nikaalanee thi| ek kone mein aag sulag rahi thi| naaee taabadatod chilam bhar raha tha| yah phaisalaa karn naamumakin tha ki sulagate hue upalon se zyaadaa dhuon nikalataa tha yaa chilam ke damon se| ladake idhar-udhar daud rahe the| koee aapas mein gaalee-galauj karte aur koee rote the| chaaron taraph kolaahal mach raha tha| gaonva ke kutte is jamaav ko bhoj samajhakar jhund ke jhund jamaa ho gae the|
panch log baith gaye, to boodhee khaalaa ne unse vinatee kee--
‘panchon, aaj teen saal hue, mainne apanee saaree jaayadaad apne bhaanaje jumman ke naam likh dee thi| ise aap log jaanate hee honge| jumman ne mujhe taa-hayaat rotee-kapadaa denaa kabool kiya| saal-bhar to mainne isake saath ro-dhokar kaataa| par ab raat-din kaa ronaa nahin sahaa jat| mujhe na pet kee rotee milatee hai na tan kaa kapadaa| bekas bevaa hoon| kachaharee darabaar nahin kar sakatee| tumhaare sivaa aur kisako apanaa du:kha sunaaoon? tum log jo raah nikaal do, usee raah par chaloon| agar mujhamen koee aib dekho, to mere munh par thappad maaree| jumman mein buraaee dekho, to use samajhaaon, kyon ek bekas kee aah letaa hai ! main panchon kaa hukm sir-maathe par chadhaaoongee|’
raamadhan mishr, jinake kaee asaamiyon ko jumman ne apne gaanv mein basaa liya tha, bole—jumman miyaan kise panch badate ho? abhee se isakaa nipataaraa kar lo| phir jo kuchh panch kahenge, vahee maananaa padegaa|
jumman ko is samay sadasyon mein visheshakar ve hee log deekh pade, jinase kisee na kisee vajah unakaa vaimanasy tha| jumman bole—panchon kaa hukm allaah kaa hukm hai| khaalaajaan jise chaahen, use baden| mujhe koee ujr nahin|
khaalaa ne chillaakar kahaa--are allaah ke bande ! panchon kaa naam kyon nahin bataa detaa? kuchh mujhe bhee to maaloom ho|
jumman ne gussaa se kahaa--is vakt meraa munh na khulavaao| tumhaaree ban padee hai, jise chaaho, panch bado|
khaalaajaan jumman ke aakshep ko samajh gayeen, vah bolee--betaa, khudaa se daro| panch na kisee ke dost hote hain, ne kisee ke dushman| kaisee baat kehte ho! aur tumhaaraa kisee par yakeen na ho, to jaane do; alagoo chaudharee ko to maanate ho, lo, main unheen ko sarapanch badatee hoon|
jumman shekh khushee se phool uthe, lekin bhaavon ko chhipaa kar bole--alagoo hee sahee, mere lie jaise raamadhan vaise alagoo|
alagoo is jhamele mein phansanaa nahin chaahate the| ve kannee kaatane lage| bole--khaalaa, tum jaanatee ho ki meree jumman se gaadhee dostee hai|
khaalaa ne gambheer aavaaz mein kahaa--‘betaa, dostee ke lie koee apanaa eemaan nahin bechataa| panch ke dil mein khudaa basataa hai| panchon ke munh se jo baat nikalatee hai, vah khudaa kee taraph se nikalatee hai|’
alagoo chaudharee sarapanch huen raamadhan mishr aur jumman ke doosare virodhiyon ne budhiyaa ko man mein bahut kosaa|
alagoo chaudharee bole--shekh jumman ! ham aur tum puraane dost hain ! jab kaam padaa, tumane hamaaree madad kee hai aur ham bhee jo kuchh ban padaa, tumhaaree sevaa karte rahe hain; magar is samay tum aur budhee khaalaa, donon hamaaree nigaah mein baraabar ho| tumako panchon se jo kuchh arj karanee ho, karo|
jumman ko pooraa yakeen tha ki ab baajee meree hai| alag yah sab dikhaave kee baaten kar raha hai| isalie shaant-chitt ho kar bole--panchon, teen saal hue khaalaajaan ne apanee jaayadaad mere naam hibbaa kar dee thi| mainne unhen taa-hayaat khaanaa-kapadaa denaa kabool kiya tha| khudaa gavaah hai, aaj tak mainne khaalaajaan ko koee takaleeph nahin dee| main unhen apanee maon ke samaan samajhataa hoon| unakee khidamat karn meraa pharj hai; magar auraton mein jaraa anaban rahatee hai, usamen meraa kyaa bas hai? khaalaajaan mujhse maahavaar kharch alag maongatee hai| jaayadaad jitanee hai; vah panchon se chhipee nahin| usse itanaa munaaphaa nahin hot hai ki maahavaar kharch de sakoon| isake alaavaa hibbaanaame mein maahavaar kharch kaa koee jikr nahee| nahin to main bhoolakar bhee is jhamele me na padataa| bas, mujhe yahee kahanaa hai| aaindaa panchon kaa akhtiyaar hai, jo phaisalaa chaahen, kare|
alagoo chaudharee ko hameshaa kachaharee se kaam padataa tha| isalie vah pooraa kaanoonee aadamee tha| usane jumman se jirah shuroo kee| ek-ek savaal jumman ke dil par hathaudee kee chot kee tarah padataa tha| raamadhan mishr is prashnon par mugdh hue jaate the| jumman chakit the ki alagoo ko kyaa ho gaya| abhee yah alagoo mere saath baithee hua kaisee-kaisee baaten kar raha tha ! itanee hee der mein aisee kaayaapalat ho gayee ki meree jad khodane par tulaa hua hai| na maaloom kab kee kasar yah nikaal raha hai? kyaa itane dinon kee dostee kuchh bhee kaam na aavegee?
jumman shekh to isee sankalp-vikalp mein pade hue the ki itane mein alagoo ne phaisalaa sunaayaa--
jumman shekh ! panchon ne is maamale par soch kiya| unhen yah neeti sangat maaloom hot hai ki khaalaajaan ko maahavaar kharch diya jaay| hamaaraa soch hai ki khaalaa kee jaayadaad se itanaa munaaphaa avashy hot hai ki maahavaar kharch diya jaa sake| bas, yahee hamaaraa phaisalaa hai| agar jumman ko kharch denaa manjoor na ho, to hibvaanaamaa radd samajhaa jaay|
yah phaisalaa sunate hee jumman sannaate mein aa gaye| jo apanaa mitr ho, vah shatru kaa vyavahaar kare aur gale par chhuree phere, ise samay ke her-pher ke sivaa aur kyaa kahen? jis par pooraa bharosaa tha, usane samay padane par dhokhaa diya| aise hee avasaron par jhoothe-sachche mitron kee pareekshaa kee jati hai| yahee kaliyug kee dostee hai| agar log aise kapatee-dhokhebaaj na hote, to desh mein aapattiyon kaa prakop kyon hot? yah haijaa-pleg aadi vyaadhiyon dushkarmon ke hee dand hain|
magar raamadhan mishr aur any panch alagoo chaudharee kee is neeti-paraayanataa ko prashansaa jee kholakar kar rahe the| ve kehte the--isakaa naam panchaayat hai ! doodh kaa doodh aur paanee kaa paanee kar diya| dostee, dostee kee jagah hai, lekin dharm kaa paalan karn mukhy hai| aise hee satyavaadiyon ke bal par prithvee thaharee hai, nahin to vah kab kee rasaatal ko chalee jati|
is phaisale ne alagoo aur jumman kee dostee kee jad hilaa dee| ab ve saath-saath baaten karte nahin dikhaayee dete| itanaa puraanaa mitrataa-roopee vriksh
saty kaa ek jhonkaa bhee na sah sakaa| sachamuch vah baaloo kee hee jameen par khadaa tha|
unamen ab shishtaachaar kaa zyaadaa vyavahaar hone lagaa| ek doosare kee aavabhagat jyaadaa karne lagaa| ve milate-julate the, magar usee tarah jaise talavaar se dhaal milatee hai|
jumman ke chitt mein mitr kee kutilataa aathon pahar khatakaa karti thi| use har ghadee yahee phikr rahatee thi ki kisee tarah badalaa lene kaa avasar mile|
5
achchhe kaamon kee siddhi mein badee dare lagatee hai; par bure kaamon kee siddhi mein yah baat nahin hoti; jumman ko bhee badalaa lene kaa avasar jald hee mil gaya| pichhale saal alagoo chaudharee batesar se bailon kee ek bahut achchhee goee mol laaye the| bail pachhaaheen jaati ke sundar, bade-bade seegonvaale the| maheenon tak aas-paas ke gaonva ke log darshan karte rahe| daivayog se jumman kee panchaayat ke ek maheene ke baad is jodee kaa ek bail mar gaya| jumman ne doston se kahaa--yah daGaabaazee kee sajaa hai| insaan sabr bhale hee kar jaay, par khudaa nek-bada sab dekhataa hai| alagoo ko shak hua ki jumman ne bail ko vish dilaa diya hai| chaudharaain ne bhee jumman par hee is durghatanaa kaa doshaaropan kiya usane kahaa--jumman ne kuchh kar-karaa diya hai| chaudharaain aur kareeman mein is vishay par ek din khub hee vaad-vivaad hua donon deviyon ne shabd-baahuly kee nadee bahaa dee| vyangay, vaktokti anyokti aur upamaa aadi alankaaron mein baaten hueen| jumman ne kisee tarah shaanti sthaapit kee| unhonne apanee patnee ko daonta-dapat kar samajhaa diya| vah use us ranabhoomi se hataa bhee le gaye| udhar alagoo chaudharee ne samajhaane-bujhaane kaa kaam apne tark-poorn sonte se liya|
ab akelaa bail kis kaam kaa? usakaa jod bahut dhoondhaa gaya, par na milaa| nidaan yah salaah thaharee ki ise bech daalanaa chaahie| gaonva mein ek samajhoo saahu the, vah ikkaa-gaadee haonkate the| gaonva ke gud-ghee laad kar mandee le jaate, mandee se tel, namak bhar laate, aur gaonva mein bechate| is bail par unakaa man laharaayaa| unhonne sochaa, yah bail haath lage to din-bhar mein bekhatake teen khep hon| aaj-kal to ek hee khep mein laale pade rahate hain| bail dekh, gaadee mein dodaayaa, baal-bhauree kee pahachaan karaayee, mol-tol kiya aur use laa kar dvaar par baondh hee diya| ek maheene mein daam chukaane kaa vaadaa thaharaa| chaudharee ko bhee garaj thi hee, ghaate kee paravaah na kee|
samajhoo saahu ne nayaa bail paayaa, to lage use ragedane| vah din mein teen-teen, chaar-chaar khepen karne lage| na chaare kee phikr thi, na paanee kee, bas khepon se kaam tha| mandee le gaye, vahon kuchh sookhaa bhoosaa saamane daal diya| bechaaraa jaanavar abhee dam bhee na lene paayaa tha ki phir jot diya| alagoo chaudharee ke ghar tha to chain kee banshee bachatee thi| bailaraam chhathe-chhamaahe kabhee bahalee mein jote jaate the| khoob uchhalate-koodate aur koson tak daudate chale jaate the| vahon bailaraam kaa raatib tha, saaph paanee, dalee huee arahar kee daal aur bhoose ke saath khalee, aur yahee nahin, kabhee-kabhee ghee kaa svaad bhee chakhane ko mil jat tha| shaam-sabere ek aadamee kharahare kart, ponchhataa aur sahalaataa tha| kahon vah sukh-chain, kahon yah aathon pahar kahee khapat| maheene-bhar hee mein vah pis-saa gaya| ikke kaa yah juaa dekhate hee usakaa lahoo sookh jat tha| ek-ek pag chalanaa doobhar tha| hadidayon nikal aayee thi; par tha vah paaneedaar, maar kee baradaasht na thi|
ek din chauthee khep mein saahu jee ne doonaa bojh laadaa| din-bharakaa thakaa jaanavar, pair na uthate the| par saahoo jee kode phatakaarane lage| bas, phir kyaa tha, bail kalejaa tod kaa chalaa| kuchh door daudaa aur chaahaa ki jaraa dam le loon; par saahoo jee ko jald pahunchane kee phikr thi; isalie unhonne kaee kode badee nirdayataa se phatakaare| bail ne ek baar phir jor lagaayaa; par abakee baar shakti ne javaab de diya| vah dharatee par gir padaa, aur aisaa giraa ki phir na uthaa| saahu jee ne bahut peetaa, taonga pakadakar kheechaa, nathanon mein lakadee thoons dee; par kaheen mritak bhee uth sakataa hai? tab saahoo jee ko kuchh shak hua| unhonne bail ko gaur se dekh, kholakar alag kiya; aur sochane lage ki gaadee kaise ghar pahunche| bahut cheekhe-chillaaye; par dehaat kaa raastaa bachchon kee onkha kee tarah saojha hote hee band ho jat hai| koee najar na aay| aas-paas koee gaonva bhee na tha| maare gussaa ke unhonne mare hue bail par aur durre lagaaye aur kosane lage--abhaage| tujhe maranaa hee tha, to ghar pahunchakar marataa ! sasuraa beech raaste hee mein mar raha| ab gadee kaun kheeche? is tarah saahoo jee khoob jale-bhune| kaee bore gud aur kaee peepe ghee unhonne beche the, do-dhaaee sau rupaye kamar mein bandhe the| isake sivaa gaadee par kaee bore namak the; isalie chhod kar jaa bhee na sakate the| laachaar vechaare gaadee par hee lete gaye| vaheen ratajagaa karne kee thaan lee| chilam pee, gaayaa| phir hukkaa piyaa| is tarah saah jee aadhee raat tak neend ko bahalaate rahen| apanee jaan mein to vah jaagate hee rahe; par pau phatate hee jo neend tootee aur kamar par haath rakhaa, to thailee gaayab ! ghabaraa kar idhar-udhar dekh to kaee kanastar tel bhee nadaarat ! aphasos mein bechaare ne sir peet liya aur pachhaad khaane lagaa| praat: kaal rote-bilakhate ghar pahanche| sahuaain ne jab yah booree sunaavanee sunee, tab pahale to royee, phir alagoo chaudharee ko gaaliyon dene lagee--nigode ne aisaa kulachchhanee bail diya ki janm-bhar kee kamaaee lut gayee|
is ghatanaa ko hue kaee maheene beet gae| alagoo jab apne bail ke daam maongate tab saahu aur sahuaain, donon hee jhallaaye hue kutte kee tarah chadh baithate aur and-band bakane lagate—vaah ! yahon to saare janm kee kamaaee lut gaee, satyaanaash ho gaya, inhen daamon kee padee hai| murdaa bail diya tha, us par daam maongane chale hain ! onkhon mein dhool jhonk dee, satyaanaashee bail gale baondh diya, hamen niraa pongaa hee samajh liya hai ! ham bhee baniye ke bachche hai, aise buddhoo kaheen aur honge| pahale jaakar kisee gadahe mein munh dho aao, tab daam lenaa| na jee maanataa ho, to hamaaraa bail khol le jaao| maheenaa bhar ke badale do maheenaa jot lo| aur kyaa loge?
chaudharee ke ashubhachintakon kee kamee na thi| aise avasaren par ve bhee ekatr ho jaate aur saahu jee ke baraane kee pushti karte| lekin dedh sau rupaye se is tarah haath dho lenaa aasaan na tha| ek baar vah bhee garam pade| saahu jee bigad kar laathee dhoondhane ghar chale gae| ab sahuaain ne maidaan liya| prashnottar hote-hote haathaapaaee kee naubat aa pahunchee| sahuaain ne ghar mein ghus kar kivaad band kar lie| shoragul sunakar gaonva ke bhalemaanas ghar se nikaalaa| vah paraamarsh dene lage ki is tarah se kaam na chalegaa| panchaayat kar lo| kuchh tay ho jaay, use sveekaar kar lo| saahu jee raajee ho gae| alagoo ne bhee haamee bhar lee|

panchaayat kee taiyaariyaan hone lageen| donon pakshon ne apne-apne dal banaane shuroo kie| isake baad teesare din usee vriksh ke neeche panchaayat baithee| vahee shaam kaa samay tha| kheton mein kaue panchaayat kar rahe the| vivaadagrast vishay tha yah ki matar kee phaliyon par unakaa koee svatv hai yaa nahee, aur jab tak yah savaal hal na ho jaay, tab tak ve rakhavaale kee pukaar par apanee aprasannataa prakat karn aavashyakat samajhate the| ped kee daaliyon par baithee shuk-mandalee mein vah savaal chhidaa hua tha ki manushyon ko unhen vesurauvat kehne kaa kyaa adhikaar hai, jab unhen svayan apne mitron se dagaan karne mein bhee sankoch nahin hot|
panchaayat baith gaee, to raamadhan mishr ne kahaa-ab deree kyaa hai ? panchon kaa chunaav ho jan chaahie| bolo chaudharee ; kis-kis ko panch badate ho|
alagoo ne deen bhaav se kahaa-samajhoo saahu hee chun len|
samajhoo khade hue aur kadakar bole-meree ora se jumman shekh|
jumman kaa naam sunate hee alagoo chaudharee kaa kalejaa dhak-dhak karne lagaa, maanon kisee ne achaanak thappad maaraa diya ho| raamadhan alagoo ke mitr the| vah baat ko taad gae| poochhaa-kyon chaudharee tumhen koee ujr to nahee|
chaudharee ne niraash ho kar kahaa-nahin, mujhe kyaa ujr hogaa?
apne uttaradaayitv kaa jnaan bahudhaa hamaare sankuchit vyavahaaron kaa sudhaarak hot hai| jab ham raah bhool kar bhatakane lagate hain tab yahee jnaan hamaaraa vishvasaneey raastaa-pradarshak ban jat hai|
patr-sanpaadak apanee shaanti kutee mein baithaa hua kitanee dhrishtataa aur svatantrataa ke saath apanee prabal lekhanee se mantrimandal par aakraman kart hai: lekin aise avasar aate hain, jab vah svayan mantrimandal mein sammilit hot hai| mandal ke bhavan mein pag dharate hee usakee lekhanee kitanee marmajn, kitanee vichaarasheel, kitanee nyaay-paraayan ho jati hai| isakaa vajah javaab-daayitv kaa jnaan hai| navayuvak yuvaavasthaa mein kitanaa uddand rahataa hai| maan-pitaa usakee ora se kitane chititi rahate hai! ve use kul-kalank samajhate hainparantu thaudee heeee samay mein parivaar kaa baujh sir par padate hee vah avyavasthit-chitt unmatt yuvak kitanaa dhairyasheel, kaisaa shaantachitt ho jat hai, yah bhee uttaradaayitv ke jnaan kaa phal hai|
jumman shekh ke man mein bhee sarapanch kaa uchch sthaan grahan karte hee apanee jimmedaaree kaa bhaav pedaa hua| usane sochaa, main is vakt nyaay aur dharm ke sarvochch aasan par baithaa hoon| mere munh se is samay jo kuchh nikalegaa, vah devavaanee ke sadrish hai-aur devavaanee mein mere manovikaaron kaa kabhee nahin samaavesh na honaa chaahie| mujhe saty se jau bhar bhee talanaa theek nahee!
panchon ne donon pakshon se savaal-javaab karne shuroo kie| bahut der tak donon dal apne-apne paksh kaa samarthan karte rahe| is vishay mein to sab sahamat the ki samajhoo ko bail kaa mooly denaa chaahie| lekin vo mahaashay is vajah riyaayat karn chaahate the ki bail ke mar jaane se samajhoo ko nukasaan huee| usake pratikool do sabhy mool ke atirikt samajhoo ko dand bhee denaa chaahate the, jisse phir kisee ko pashuon ke saath aisee nirdayataa karne kaa saahas na ho| ant mein jumman ne phaisalaa sunaayaa-
alagoo chaudharee aur samajhoo saahu| panchon ne tumhaare maamale par achchhee tarah soch kiya| samajhoo ko theek hai ki bail kaa pooraa daam den| jis vakt unhonne bail liya, use koee beemaaree na thi| agar usee samay daam de die jaate, to aaj samajhoo use pher lene kaa aagrah na karte| bail kee mrityu keval is vajah huee ki usse badaa mushkil parishram liya gaya aur usake daane-chaare kaa koee intazaam na kiya gaya|
raamadhan mishr bole-samajhoo ne bail ko jaan-boojh kar maaraa hai, isalie usse dand lenaa chaahie|
jumman bole-yah doosaraa savaal hai| hamako isase koee matalab nahin !
jhagadoo saahu ne kahaa-samajhoo ke saathan kuchh riyaayat honee chaahie|
jumman bole-yah alagoo chaudharee kee ichchhaa par nirbhar hai| yah riyaayat karen, to unakee bhalamanasee|
alagoo chaudharee phoole na samaae| uth khade hue aur jor se bole-panch-parameshvar kee jay!
isake saath hee chaaron ora se pratidhvani huee-panch parameshvar kee jay! yah manushy kaa kaam nahin, panch mein parameshvar vaas karte hain, yah unheen kee mahimaa hai| panch ke saamane khote ko kaun kharaa kah sakataa hai?
thodee der baad jumman alagoo ke paas aae aur unake gale lipat kar bole-bhaiyaa, jab se tumane meree panchaayat kee tab se main tumhaaraa praan-ghaatak shatru ban gaya tha; par aaj mujhe pataa hua ki panch ke pad par baith kar na koee kisee kaa dost hai, na dushman| nyaay ke sivaa use aur kuchh nahin soojhataa| aaj mujhe yakeen ho gaya ki panch kee jabaan se khudaa bolataa hai| alagoo rone lage| is paanee se donon ke dilon kaa mail dhul gaya| mitrataa kee murajhaaee huee lataa phir haree ho gaee|
