# Panch Parmeshwar

**Munshi Premchand**

1
jumman shekh alagoo chaudharee mein gaadhee mitrataa thi. saajhe mein khetee hoti thi. kuchh len-den mein bhi saajhaa tha. ek ko doosare par atal yakeen tha. jumman jab haj karne gaye the, tab apna ghar alagoo ko saunp gaye the, aur alagoo jab kabhi baahar jaate, to jumman par apna ghar chhod dete the. unamen na khaanaa-paanaa kaa vyavahaar tha, na dharm kaa naataa; keval soch milate the. mitrataa kaa moolamantr bhi yahi hai.
is mitrataa kaa janm usi samay hua, jab donon mitr ladakaa hi the, aur jumman ke poojy pitaa, jumaraatee, unhen shikshaa pradaan karte the. alagoo ne guroo jee ki bahut sevaa ki thi, khoob pyaale dhoye. unakaa hukkaa ek pal ke lie bhi vishraam na lene paataa tha, kyonki har chilam alagoo ko aadh ghante tak kitaabon se alag kar detee thi. alagoo ke pitaa puraane vichaaron ke manushy the. unhen shikshaa ki apekshaa guru ki sevaa-shushrooshaa par zyaadaa yakeen tha. vah kehte the ki padhai padhne ne nahin aatee; jo kuchh hot hai, guru ke aasheervaad se. bas, guru jee ki kripaa-nazar chaahie. isalie yadi alagoo par jumaraatee shekh ke aasheervaad athavaa satsang kaa kuchh phal na hua, to yah maanakar santosh kar lena ki vidyopaarjan mein maine yathaashakti koi baat uthaa nahin rakhee, padhai usake bhaagy hi mein na thi, to kaise aatee?
magar jumaraatee shekh svayan aasheervaad ke kaayal na the. unhen apne sote par zyaadaa bharosaa tha, aur usi sote ke prataap se aaj-paas ke gaonvon mein jumman ki poojaa hoti thi. unake likhe hue rehananaame yaa bainaame par kachaharee kaa muharrir bhi kadam na uthaa sakataa tha. halke kaa daakiyaa, kaanstebil aur tahaseel kaa chaparaasee--sab unki kripaa ki aakaankshaa rakhte the. isalie alagoo kaa maan unake paisaa ke vajah tha, to jumman shekh apni anamol padhai se hi sabake aadarapaatr bane the.
2
jumman shekh ki ek boodhee khaalaa (mausee) thi. usake paas kuchh thodee-see milakiyat thi; lekin usake paas sanbandhiyon mein koi na tha. jumman ne lambe-chaude vaade karake vah milakiyat apne naam likhavaa lee thi. jab tak daanapatr ki rajistree na hui thi, tab tak khaalaajaan kaa khoob aadar-satkaar kiya gaya; unhen khoob svaadisht padaarth khilaaye gaye. halave-pulaav ki varshaa- see ki gayi; par rajistree ki mohar ne ina khaatiradaariyon par bhi maanon muhar lagaa dee. jumman ki patnee kareeman rotiyon ke saath kadavee baaton ke kuchh tej, teekhe saalan bhi dene lagee. jumman shekh bhi nithur ho gaye. ab bechaaree khaalaajaan ko praay: nity hi aisee baaten sunanee padatee thi.
budhiyaa na jaane kab tak jiyegee. do-teen beeghe oosar kya de diya, maanon mol le liya hai! baghaaree daal ke binaa rotiyon nahin utarateen! jitanaa rupayaa isake pet mein jhonk chuke, utane se to ab tak gaonva mol le lete.
kuchh din khaalaajaan ne sunaa aur sahaa; par jab na sahaa gaya tab jumman se shikaayat ki. tumman ne sthaaneey karmachaaree—grihasvaanmee—ke intazaam dena theek na samajhaa. kuchh din tak din tak aur yon hi ro-dhokar kaam chalta raha. ant mein ek din khaalaa ne jumman se kahaa—betaa! tumhaare saath mera nirvaah na hogaa. tum mujhe rupaye de diya karo, main apna pakaa-khaa loongee.
jumman ne dhrishtataa ke saath jawab diya-— rupaye kya yahan phalate hain?
khaalaa ne namrataa se kahaa—mujhe kuchh rookhaa-sookhaa chaahie bhi ki nahin?
jumman ne gambheer aavaaz se jawab diya—to koi yah thode hi samajhaa tha ki too maut se ladakar aayee ho?
khaalaa bigad gayeen, unhone panchaayat karne ki dhamakee dee. jumman hanse, jis tarah koi shikaaree hiran ko jaalee ki taraph jaate dekh kar man hi man hansataa hai. vah bole—haon, zaroor panchaayat karo. phaisalaa ho jaay. mujhe bhi yah raat-din ki khatakhat pasand nahin.
panchaayat mein kisakee jeet hogee, is vishay mein jumman ko kuchh bhi shak na tha. aas-paas ke gaonvon mein aisaa kaun tha, usake anugrahon kaa rinee na ho; aisaa kaun tha, jo usako shatru banaane kaa saahas kar sake? kisamen itanaa bal tha, jo uska saamanaa kar sake? aasamaan ke pharishte to panchaayat karne aavenge hi nahin.
3
isake baad kaee din tak boodhee khaalaa haath mein ek lakadee liye aas-paas ke gaanvon mein daudatee raheen. kamar jhuk kar kamaan ho gayi thi. ek-ek pag chalna doobhar tha; magar baat aa padee thi. uska phaisalaa karn jarooree tha.
biralaa hi koi bhalaa aadamee hogaa, jisake samaane budhiyaa ne du:kha ke onsoo na bahaaye hon. kisee ne to yon hi ooparee man se hoon-haon karake taal diya, aur kisee ne is anyaay par jamaane ko gaaliyaan deen. kahaa—kabr mein paonva jatake hue hain, aaj mare, kal doosaraa din, par havas nahin maanatee. ab tumhen kya chaahie? rotee khaao aur allaah kaa naam lo. tumhen ab khetee-baaree se kya kaam hai? kuchh aise sajjan bhi the, jinhen haasy-ras ke rasaasvaadan kaa achchha avasar milaa. jhukee hui kamar, popalaa munh, san ke-se baal itanee saamagree ekatr hon, tab hansee kyon na aave? aise nyaayapriy, dayaalu, deen-vatsal aadamee bahut kam the, jinhonne is abalaa ke dukhade ko gaur se sunaa ho aur usako saantvanaa dee ho. chaaron ora se ghoom-ghaam kar bechaaree alagoo chaudharee ke paas aayee. laathee patak dee aur dam lekar bolee—betaa, tum bhi dam bhar ke liye meri panchaayat mein chale aan.
alagoo-— mujhe bulaa kar kya karogee? kaee gaonva ke aadamee to aavenge hi.
khaalaa—apni vipad to sabake aage ro aayee. ab aane na aane kaa akhtiyaar unako hai.
alagoo—yon aane ko aa jaaoongaa; magar panchaayat mein munh na kholoongaa.
khaalaa—kyon betaa?
alagoo—ab isakaa kayaa jawab doon? apni khushi. jumman mera puraanaa mitr hai. usse bigaad nahin kar sakataa.
khaalaa—betaa, kya bigaad ke dar se eemaan ki baat na kahoge?
hamaare soye hue dharm-jnaan ki sari sampatti lut jaay, to use khabar nahin hot, lekin lalakaar sunakar vah sachet ho jat hai. phir use koi jeet nahin sakataa. alagoo is sawal kaa kaaee jawab na de sakaa, par usake dil mein ye shabd goonj rahe the-
kya bigaad ke dar se eemaan ki baat na kahoge?
4
shaam samay ek ped ke neeche panchaayat baithee. shekh jumman ne pahale se hi pharsh bichhaa rakhaa tha. unhone paan, ilaayachee, hukke-tambaakoo aadi kaa intazaam bhi kiya tha. haon, vah svay alabattaa alagoo chaudharee ke saath zara door par baithejab panchaayat mein koi aa jat tha, tab dave hue salaam se uska svaagat karte the. jab soory ast ho gaya aur chidiyon ki kalaravayukt panchaayat pedon par baithee, tab yahon bhi panchaayat shuru hui. pharsh ki ek-ek angul jameen bhar gayi; par adhikaansh darshak hi the. nimantrit mahaashayon mein se keval ve hi log padhaare the, jinhen jumman se apni kuchh kasar nikaalanee thi. ek kone mein aag sulag rahi thi. naaee taabadatod chilam bhar raha tha. yah phaisalaa karn naamumakin tha ki sulagate hue upalon se zyaadaa dhuon nikalataa tha yaa chilam ke damon se. ladake idhar-udhar daud rahe the. koi aapas mein gaalee-galauj karte aur koi rote the. chaaron taraph kolaahal mach raha tha. gaonva ke kutte is jamaav ko bhoj samajhakar jhund ke jhund jamaa ho gae the.
panch log baith gaye, to boodhee khaalaa ne unse vinatee ki--
‘panchon, aaj teen saal hue, maine apni sari jaayadaad apne bhaanaje jumman ke naam likh dee thi. ise aap log jaanate hi honge. jumman ne mujhe taa-hayaat rotee-kapadaa dena kabool kiya. saal-bhar to maine isake saath ro-dhokar kaataa. par ab raat-din kaa ronaa nahin sahaa jat. mujhe na pet ki rotee milatee hai na tan kaa kapadaa. bekas bevaa hoon. kachaharee darabaar nahin kar sakatee. tumhaare sivaa aur kisako apna du:kha sunaaoon? tum log jo raah nikaal do, usi raah par chaloon. agar mujhamen koi aib dekho, to mere munh par thappad maaree. jumman mein buraaee dekho, to use samajhaaon, kyon ek bekas ki aah letaa hai! main panchon kaa hukm sir-maathe par chadhaaoongee.’
raamadhan mishr, jinake kaee asaamiyon ko jumman ne apne gaanv mein basaa liya tha, bole—jumman miyaan kise panch badate ho? abhi se isakaa nipataaraa kar lo. phir jo kuchh panch kahenge, wahi maananaa padegaa.
jumman ko is samay sadasyon mein visheshakar ve hi log deekh pade, jinase kisee na kisee vajah unakaa vaimanasy tha. jumman bole—panchon kaa hukm allaah kaa hukm hai. khaalaajaan jise chaahen, use baden. mujhe koi ujr nahin.
khaalaa ne chillaakar kahaa--are allaah ke bande! panchon kaa naam kyon nahin bataa detaa? kuchh mujhe bhi to maaloom ho.
jumman ne gussaa se kahaa--is vakt mera munh na khulavaao. tumhari ban padee hai, jise chaaho, panch bado.
khaalaajaan jumman ke aakshep ko samajh gayeen, vah bolee--betaa, khudaa se daro. panch na kisee ke dost hote hain, ne kisee ke dushman. kaisee baat kehte ho! aur tumhara kisee par yakeen na ho, to jaane do; alagoo chaudharee ko to maanate ho, lo, main unheen ko sarapanch badatee hoon.
jumman shekh khushi se phool uthe, lekin bhaavon ko chhipaa kar bole--alagoo hi sahee, mere lie jaise raamadhan vaise alagoo.
alagoo is jhamele mein phansanaa nahin chahte the. ve kannee kaatane lage. bole--khaalaa, tum jaanatee ho ki meri jumman se gaadhee dostee hai.
khaalaa ne gambheer aavaaz mein kahaa--‘betaa, dostee ke lie koi apna eemaan nahin bechta. panch ke dil mein khudaa basataa hai. panchon ke munh se jo baat nikalatee hai, vah khudaa ki taraph se nikalatee hai.’
alagoo chaudharee sarapanch huen raamadhan mishr aur jumman ke doosare virodhiyon ne budhiyaa ko man mein bahut kosaa.
alagoo chaudharee bole--shekh jumman! ham aur tum puraane dost hain! jab kaam padaa, tumne hamaaree madad ki hai aur ham bhi jo kuchh ban padaa, tumhari sevaa karte rahe hain; magar is samay tum aur budhee khaalaa, donon hamaaree nigaah mein baraabar ho. tumako panchon se jo kuchh arj karanee ho, karo.
jumman ko pooraa yakeen tha ki ab baajee meri hai. alag yah sab dikhaave ki baaten kar raha hai. isalie shaant-chitt ho kar bole--panchon, teen saal hue khaalaajaan ne apni jaayadaad mere naam hibbaa kar dee thi. maine unhen taa-hayaat khaanaa-kapadaa dena kabool kiya tha. khudaa gavaah hai, aaj tak maine khaalaajaan ko koi takaleeph nahin dee. main unhen apni maon ke samaan samajhta hoon. unki khidamat karn mera pharj hai; magar auraton mein zara anaban rehti hai, usamen mera kya bas hai? khaalaajaan mujhse maahavaar kharch alag maongatee hai. jaayadaad jitanee hai; vah panchon se chhipee nahin. usse itanaa munaaphaa nahin hot hai ki maahavaar kharch de sakoon. isake alaavaa hibbaanaame mein maahavaar kharch kaa koi jikr nahin. nahin to main bhoolakar bhi is jhamele me na padataa. bas, mujhe yahi kehna hai. aaindaa panchon kaa akhtiyaar hai, jo phaisalaa chaahen, kare.
alagoo chaudharee ko hameshaa kachaharee se kaam padataa tha. isalie vah pooraa kaanoonee aadamee tha. usane jumman se jirah shuru ki. ek-ek sawal jumman ke dil par hathaudee ki chot ki tarah padataa tha. raamadhan mishr is prashnon par mugdh hue jaate the. jumman chakit the ki alagoo ko kya ho gaya. abhi yah alagoo mere saath baithee hua kaisee-kaisee baaten kar raha tha! itanee hi der mein aisee kaayaapalat ho gayi ki meri jad khodane par tulaa hua hai. na maaloom kab ki kasar yah nikaal raha hai? kya itane dinon ki dostee kuchh bhi kaam na aavegee?
jumman shekh to isi sankalp-vikalp mein pade hue the ki itane mein alagoo ne phaisalaa sunaayaa--
jumman shekh! panchon ne is maamale par soch kiya. unhen yah neeti sangat maaloom hot hai ki khaalaajaan ko maahavaar kharch diya jaay. hamaaraa soch hai ki khaalaa ki jaayadaad se itanaa munaaphaa avashy hot hai ki maahavaar kharch diya jaa sake. bas, yahi hamaaraa phaisalaa hai. agar jumman ko kharch dena manjoor na ho, to hibvaanaamaa radd samajhaa jaay.
yah phaisalaa sunate hi jumman sannaate mein aa gaye. jo apna mitr ho, vah shatru kaa vyavahaar kare aur gale par chhuree phere, ise samay ke her-pher ke sivaa aur kya kahen? jis par pooraa bharosaa tha, usane samay padane par dhokhaa diya. aise hi avasaron par jhoothe-sachche mitron ki pareekshaa ki jati hai. yahi kaliyug ki dostee hai. agar log aise kapatee-dhokhebaaj na hote, to desh mein aapattiyon kaa prakop kyon hot? yah haijaa-pleg aadi vyaadhiyon dushkarmon ke hi dand hain.
magar raamadhan mishr aur any panch alagoo chaudharee ki is neeti-paraayanataa ko prashansaa jee kholakar kar rahe the. ve kehte the--isakaa naam panchaayat hai! doodh kaa doodh aur paani kaa paani kar diya. dostee, dostee ki jagah hai, lekin dharm kaa paalan karn mukhy hai. aise hi satyavaadiyon ke bal par prithvee thaharee hai, nahin to vah kab ki rasaatal ko chalee jati.
is phaisale ne alagoo aur jumman ki dostee ki jad hilaa dee. ab ve saath-saath baaten karte nahin dikhaayee dete. itanaa puraanaa mitrataa-roopee vriksh
saty kaa ek jhonkaa bhi na sah sakaa. sachamuch vah baaloo ki hi jameen par khadaa tha.
unamen ab shishtaachaar kaa zyaadaa vyavahaar hone lagaa. ek doosare ki aavabhagat zyada karne lagaa. ve milate-julate the, magar usi tarah jaise talavaar se dhaal milatee hai.
jumman ke chitt mein mitr ki kutilataa aathon pahar khatakaa karti thi. use har ghadee yahi phikr rehti thi ki kisee tarah badalaa lene kaa avasar mile.
5
achchhe kaamon ki siddhi mein badi dare lagti hai; par bure kaamon ki siddhi mein yah baat nahin hoti; jumman ko bhi badalaa lene kaa avasar jald hi mil gaya. pichhale saal alagoo chaudharee batesar se bailon ki ek bahut achchhi goee mol laaye the. bail pachhaaheen jaati ke sundar, bade-bade seegonvaale the. maheenon tak aas-paas ke gaonva ke log darshan karte rahe. daivayog se jumman ki panchaayat ke ek mahine ke baad is jodee kaa ek bail mar gaya. jumman ne doston se kahaa--yah daGaabaazee ki sajaa hai. insaan sabr bhale hi kar jaay, par khudaa nek-bada sab dekhta hai. alagoo ko shak hua ki jumman ne bail ko vish dilaa diya hai. chaudharaain ne bhi jumman par hi is durghatanaa kaa doshaaropan kiya usane kahaa--jumman ne kuchh kar-karaa diya hai. chaudharaain aur kareeman mein is vishay par ek din khub hi vaad-vivaad hua donon deviyon ne shabd-baahuly ki nadee bahaa dee. vyangay, vaktokti anyokti aur upamaa aadi alankaaron mein baaten hueen. jumman ne kisee tarah shaanti sthaapit ki. unhone apni patnee ko daonta-dapat kar samajhaa diya. vah use us ranabhoomi se hataa bhi le gaye. udhar alagoo chaudharee ne samajhaane-bujhaane kaa kaam apne tark-poorn sonte se liya.
ab akelaa bail kis kaam kaa? uska jod bahut dhoondhaa gaya, par na milaa. nidaan yah salaah thaharee ki ise bech daalanaa chaahie. gaonva mein ek samajhoo saahu the, vah ikkaa-gaadee haonkate the. gaonva ke gud-ghee laad kar mandee le jaate, mandee se tel, namak bhar laate, aur gaonva mein bechte. is bail par unakaa man laharaayaa. unhone sochaa, yah bail haath lage to din-bhar mein bekhatake teen khep hon. aaj-kal to ek hi khep mein laale pade rehte hain. bail dekh, gaadee mein dodaayaa, baal-bhauree ki pahachaan karaayee, mol-tol kiya aur use laa kar dvaar par baondh hi diya. ek mahine mein daam chukaane kaa vaadaa thaharaa. chaudharee ko bhi garaj thi hi, ghaate ki paravaah na ki.
samajhoo saahu ne nayaa bail paayaa, to lage use ragedane. vah din mein teen-teen, chaar-chaar khepen karne lage. na chaare ki phikr thi, na paani ki, bas khepon se kaam tha. mandee le gaye, vahon kuchh sookhaa bhoosaa saamane daal diya. bechaaraa jaanavar abhi dam bhi na lene paayaa tha ki phir jot diya. alagoo chaudharee ke ghar tha to chain ki banshee bachti thi. bailaraam chhathe-chhamaahe kabhi bahalee mein jote jaate the. khoob uchhalate-koodate aur koson tak daudate chale jaate the. vahon bailaraam kaa raatib tha, saaph paani, dalee hui arahar ki daal aur bhoose ke saath khalee, aur yahi nahin, kabhi-kabhi ghee kaa svaad bhi chakhane ko mil jat tha. shaam-sabere ek aadamee kharahare kart, ponchhta aur sahalaataa tha. kahon vah sukh-chain, kahon yah aathon pahar kahin khapat. mahine-bhar hi mein vah pis-saa gaya. ikke kaa yah juaa dekhte hi uska lahoo sookh jat tha. ek-ek pag chalna doobhar tha. hadidayon nikal aayee thi; par tha vah paaneedaar, maar ki baradaasht na thi.
ek din chauthee khep mein saahu jee ne doonaa bojh laadaa. din-bharakaa thakaa jaanavar, pair na uthte the. par saahoo jee kode phatakaarane lage. bas, phir kya tha, bail kalejaa tod kaa chalaa. kuchh door daudaa aur chaahaa ki zara dam le loon; par saahoo jee ko jald pahunchane ki phikr thi; isalie unhone kaee kode badi nirdayataa se phatakaare. bail ne ek baar phir jor lagaayaa; par abakee baar shakti ne jawab de diya. vah dharatee par gir padaa, aur aisaa giraa ki phir na uthaa. saahu jee ne bahut peetaa, taonga pakadakar kheechaa, nathanon mein lakadee thoons dee; par kaheen mritak bhi uth sakataa hai? tab saahoo jee ko kuchh shak hua. unhone bail ko gaur se dekh, kholakar alag kiya; aur sochne lage ki gaadee kaise ghar pahunche. bahut cheekhe-chillaaye; par dehaat kaa raastaa bachchon ki onkha ki tarah saojha hote hi band ho jat hai. koi najar na aay. aas-paas koi gaonva bhi na tha. maare gussaa ke unhone mare hue bail par aur durre lagaaye aur kosane lage--abhaage. tujhe maranaa hi tha, to ghar pahunchakar marataa! sasuraa beech raaste hi mein mar raha. ab gadee kaun kheeche? is tarah saahoo jee khoob jale-bhune. kaee bore gud aur kaee peepe ghee unhone beche the, do-dhaaee sau rupaye kamar mein bandhe the. isake sivaa gaadee par kaee bore namak the; isalie chhod kar jaa bhi na sakate the. laachaar vechaare gaadee par hi lete gaye. vaheen ratajagaa karne ki thaan lee. chilam pee, gaayaa. phir hukkaa piyaa. is tarah saah jee aadhee raat tak neend ko bahalaate rahen. apni jaan mein to vah jaagate hi rahe; par pau phatate hi jo neend tootee aur kamar par haath rakhaa, to thailee gaayab! ghabaraa kar idhar-udhar dekh to kaee kanastar tel bhi nadaarat! aphasos mein bechaare ne sir peet liya aur pachhaad khaane lagaa. praat: kaal rote-bilakhte ghar pahanche. sahuaain ne jab yah booree sunaavanee sunee, tab pahale to royee, phir alagoo chaudharee ko gaaliyon dene lagee--nigode ne aisaa kulachchhanee bail diya ki janm-bhar ki kamaaee lut gayi.
is ghatanaa ko hue kaee mahine beet gae. alagoo jab apne bail ke daam maongate tab saahu aur sahuaain, donon hi jhallaaye hue kutte ki tarah chadh baithte aur and-band bakane lagte—vaah! yahon to saare janm ki kamaaee lut gayi, satyaanaash ho gaya, inhen daamon ki padee hai. murdaa bail diya tha, us par daam maongane chale hain! onkhon mein dhool jhonk dee, satyaanaashee bail gale baondh diya, hamen niraa pongaa hi samajh liya hai! ham bhi baniye ke bachche hai, aise buddhoo kaheen aur honge. pahale jaakar kisee gadahe mein munh dho aao, tab daam lena. na jee maanataa ho, to hamaaraa bail khol le jaao. mahina bhar ke badale do mahina jot lo. aur kya loge?
chaudharee ke ashubhachintakon ki kamee na thi. aise avasaren par ve bhi ekatr ho jaate aur saahu jee ke baraane ki pushti karte. lekin dedh sau rupaye se is tarah haath dho lena aasaan na tha. ek baar vah bhi garam pade. saahu jee bigad kar laathee dhoondhane ghar chale gae. ab sahuaain ne maidaan liya. prashnottar hote-hote haathaapaaee ki naubat aa pahunchee. sahuaain ne ghar mein ghus kar kivaad band kar lie. shoragul sunakar gaonva ke bhalemaanas ghar se nikaalaa. vah paraamarsh dene lage ki is tarah se kaam na chalegaa. panchaayat kar lo. kuchh tay ho jaay, use sveekaar kar lo. saahu jee raajee ho gae. alagoo ne bhi haamee bhar lee.
6
panchaayat ki taiyaariyaan hone lageen. donon pakshon ne apne-apne dal banaane shuru kie. isake baad teesare din usi vriksh ke neeche panchaayat baithee. wahi shaam kaa samay tha. kheton mein kaue panchaayat kar rahe the. vivaadagrast vishay tha yah ki matar ki phaliyon par unakaa koi svatv hai yaa nahin, aur jab tak yah sawal hal na ho jaay, tab tak ve rakhavaale ki pukaar par apni aprasannataa prakat karn aavashyakat samajhte the. ped ki daaliyon par baithee shuk-mandalee mein vah sawal chhidaa hua tha ki manushyon ko unhen vesurauvat kehne kaa kya adhikar hai, jab unhen svayan apne mitron se dagaan karne mein bhi sankoch nahin hot.
panchaayat baith gayi, to raamadhan mishr ne kahaa-ab deree kya hai? panchon kaa chunaav ho jan chaahie. bolo chaudharee; kis-kis ko panch badate ho.
alagoo ne deen bhaav se kahaa-samajhoo saahu hi chun len.
samajhoo khade hue aur kadakar bole-meri ora se jumman shekh.
jumman kaa naam sunate hi alagoo chaudharee kaa kalejaa dhak-dhak karne lagaa, maanon kisee ne achaanak thappad maaraa diya ho. raamadhan alagoo ke mitr the. vah baat ko taad gae. poochhaa-kyon chaudharee tumhen koi ujr to nahin.
chaudharee ne niraash ho kar kahaa-nahin, mujhe kya ujr hogaa?
apne uttaradaayitv kaa jnaan bahudhaa hamaare sankuchit vyavahaaron kaa sudhaarak hot hai. jab ham raah bhool kar bhatakane lagte hain tab yahi jnaan hamaaraa vishvasaneey raastaa-pradarshak ban jat hai.
patr-sanpaadak apni shaanti kutee mein baithaa hua kitanee dhrishtataa aur svatantrataa ke saath apni prabal lekhanee se mantrimandal par aakraman kart hai: lekin aise avasar aate hain, jab vah svayan mantrimandal mein sammilit hot hai. mandal ke bhavan mein pag dharate hi uski lekhanee kitanee marmajn, kitanee vichaarasheel, kitanee nyaay-paraayan ho jati hai. isakaa vajah jawab-daayitv kaa jnaan hai. navayuvak yuvaavasthaa mein kitanaa uddand rehta hai. maan-pitaa uski ora se kitane chititi rehte hai! ve use kul-kalank samajhte hainparantu thaudee heeee samay mein parivaar kaa baujh sir par padate hi vah avyavasthit-chitt unmatt yuvak kitanaa dhairyasheel, kaisaa shaantachitt ho jat hai, yah bhi uttaradaayitv ke jnaan kaa phal hai.
jumman shekh ke man mein bhi sarapanch kaa uchch sthaan grahan karte hi apni jimmedaaree kaa bhaav pedaa hua. usane sochaa, main is vakt nyaay aur dharm ke sarvochch aasan par baithaa hoon. mere munh se is samay jo kuchh nikalegaa, vah devavaanee ke sadrish hai-aur devavaanee mein mere manovikaaron kaa kabhi nahin samaavesh na hona chaahie. mujhe saty se jau bhar bhi talanaa theek nahin!
panchon ne donon pakshon se sawal-jawab karne shuru kie. bahut der tak donon dal apne-apne paksh kaa samarthan karte rahe. is vishay mein to sab sahamat the ki samajhoo ko bail kaa mooly dena chaahie. lekin vo mahaashay is vajah riyaayat karn chahte the ki bail ke mar jaane se samajhoo ko nukasaan hui. usake pratikool do sabhy mool ke atirikt samajhoo ko dand bhi dena chahte the, jisse phir kisee ko pashuon ke saath aisee nirdayataa karne kaa saahas na ho. ant mein jumman ne phaisalaa sunaayaa-
alagoo chaudharee aur samajhoo saahu. panchon ne tumhaare maamale par achchhi tarah soch kiya. samajhoo ko theek hai ki bail kaa pooraa daam den. jis vakt unhone bail liya, use koi beemaaree na thi. agar usi samay daam de die jaate, to aaj samajhoo use pher lene kaa aagrah na karte. bail ki mrityu keval is vajah hui ki usse bada mushkil parishram liya gaya aur usake daane-chaare kaa koi intazaam na kiya gaya.
raamadhan mishr bole-samajhoo ne bail ko jaan-boojh kar maaraa hai, isalie usse dand lena chaahie.
jumman bole-yah doosaraa sawal hai. hamako isase koi matalab nahin!
jhagadoo saahu ne kahaa-samajhoo ke saathan kuchh riyaayat honee chaahie.
jumman bole-yah alagoo chaudharee ki ichchhaa par nirbhar hai. yah riyaayat karen, to unki bhalamanasee.
alagoo chaudharee phoole na samaae. uth khade hue aur jor se bole-panch-parameshvar ki jay!
isake saath hi chaaron ora se pratidhvani hui-panch parameshvar ki jay! yah manushy kaa kaam nahin, panch mein parameshvar vaas karte hain, yah unheen ki mahimaa hai. panch ke saamane khote ko kaun kharaa kah sakataa hai?
thodee der baad jumman alagoo ke paas aae aur unake gale lipat kar bole-bhaiyaa, jab se tumne meri panchaayat ki tab se main tumhara praan-ghaatak shatru ban gaya tha; par aaj mujhe pataa hua ki panch ke pad par baith kar na koi kisee kaa dost hai, na dushman. nyaay ke sivaa use aur kuchh nahin soojhta. aaj mujhe yakeen ho gaya ki panch ki jabaan se khudaa bolta hai. alagoo rone lage. is paani se donon ke dilon kaa mail dhul gaya. mitrataa ki murajhaaee hui lataa phir haree ho gayi.
