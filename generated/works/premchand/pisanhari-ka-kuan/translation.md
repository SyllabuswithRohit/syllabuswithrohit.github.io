# Pisanhari Ka Kuan

**Munshi Premchand**

gomatee ne mrityu-shayyaa par pade hue chaudharee vinaayakasinh se kahaa —chaudharee, mere jeevan kee yahee laalasaa thi.
chaudharee ne gambheer ho kar kahaa —isakee kuchh phikr na karo kaakee; tumhaaree laalasaa bhagavaan pooree karenge. main aaj hee se majooron ko bulaa kar kaam par lagaaye detaa hoon. daiv ne chaahaa, to tum apne kuen kaa paani piyogee. tumane to ginaa hogaa, kitane rupaye hain?
gomatee ne ek pal aankhen band karake, bikharee huee smriti ko ekatr karake kahaa —bhaiyaa, main kya jaanoon, kitane rupaye hain? jo kuchh hain, woh isee haandee mein hain. itanaa karn ki itane hee mein kaam chal jaae. kisake saamane haath phailaate phiroge?
chaudharee ne band haandee ko uthaa kar haathon se taulate hue kahaa —aisaa to karenge hee kaakee; kaun denevaalaa hai. ek chutakee bheekh to kisee ke ghar se nikalatee nahin, kuaan banavaane ko kaun detaa hai. dhany ho tum ki apanee umr bhar kee kamaaee is dharm-kaaj ke lie de dee.
gomatee ne garv se kahaa —bhaiyaa, tum to tab bahut chhote the. tumhaare kaakaa mare to mere haath mein ek kaudee bhee na thi. din-din bhar bhookhee padee rehti. jo kuchh unake paas tha, woh sab unakee beemaaree mein uth gaya. woh bhagavaan
ke bade bhakt the. iseelie bhagavaan ne unhe jaldee se bulaa liya. us din se aaj tak tum dekh rahe ho ki kis tarah din kaat rahi hoon. mainne ek-ek raat mein man-man bhar anaaj peesaa hai; betaa! dekhanevaale acharaj maanate the. na-jaane itanee taakat mujhamen kahan se aa jati thi. bas, yahee laalasaa rahi ki unake naam kaa ek chhotaa-saa kuaan gaanv mein ban jaae. naam to chalanaa chaahie. iseelie to aadamee bete-betee ko rotaa hai.
is tarah chaudharee vinaayakasinh ko vaseeyat karake, usee raat ko budhiyaa gomatee paralok sidhaaree. marate samay antim shabd, jo usake munh se nikale, woh yahee the, kuaan banavaane mein der na karn. usake paas paisaa hai yah to logon kaa anumaan tha; lekin do hajaar hai, isakaa kisee ko anumaan na tha. budhiyaa apne paisaa ko aib kee tarah chhipaatee thi. chaudharee gaanv kaa mukhiyaa aur neeyat kaa saaph aadamee tha. isalie budhiyaa ne use yah antim aadesh kiya tha. chaudharee ne gomatee ke kriyaa-karm mein bahut rupaye kharch na kiye. jyonhee ina sanskaaron se chhuttee milee, woh apne bete haranaathasinh ko bulaa kar eent, choonaa, patthar kaa takhameenaa karne lage. haranaath anaaj kaa vyaapaar kart tha. kuchh der tak to woh baithaa sunataa raha, phir bol —abhee do-chaar maheene kuaan na bane to
koi badaa haraj hai?
chaudharee ne hunh! karake kahaa —haraj to kuchh nahin, lekin der karne kaa kaam hee kya hai. rupaye usane de hee diye hain, hamen to sent mein yash milegaa. gomatee ne marate-marate jald kuaan banavaane ko kahaa tha.
haranaath, haan, kahaa to tha, lekin aajakal baajaar achchhaa hai. do-teen hajaar kaa anaaj bhar liya jaae, to agahan-poos tak savaayaa ho jaayagaa. main aapako kuchh sood de doongaa. chaudharee kaa man shankaa aur dar ke duvidho mein
pad gaya. do hajaar ke kaheen dhaaee hajaar ho gaye, to kya kahanaa. jagamohan mein kuchh bel-boote banavaa doongaa. lekin dar tha ki kaheen ghaataa ho gaya to?
is shankaa ko woh chhipaa na sake, bole —jo kaheen ghaataa ho gaya to?
haranaath ne tadap kar kahaa —ghaataa kya ho jaayagaa, koi baat hai?
maan lo, ghaataa ho gaya to?
haranaath ne uttejit hokar kahaa —yah kaho ki tum rupaye nahin denaa chaahate, bade dharmaatmaa bane ho!
any vriddhajanon kee tarah chaudharee bhee bete se dabate the. kaatar aavaaz mein bole —main yah kab keht hoon ki rupaye na doongaa. lekin paraayaa paisaa hai, soch-samajh kar hee to usamen haath lagaanaa chaahie. banij-vyaapaar kaa haal kaun jaanataa hai. kaheen bhaav aur gir jaae to? anaaj mein ghun hee lag jaae, koi muddaee ghar mein aag hee lagaa de. sab baaten soch lo achchhee tarah.
haranaath ne vyangy se kahaa —is tarah sochanaa hai, to yah kyon nahin sochate ki koi chor hee uthaa le jaae; yaa banee-banaayee deevaar baith jaae? ye baaten bhee to hoti hee hain.
chaudharee ke paas ab aur koi daleel na thi, kamajor sipaahee ne taal to thonkee, akhaade mein utar padaa; par talavaar kee chamak dekhate hee haath-paanv phool gaye. bagalen jhaank kar chaudharee ne kahaa —to kitanaa loge?
haranaath kushal yoddhaa kee tarah, shatru ko peechhe hatataa dekh kar, baphar kar bol —sab kaa sab deejie, sau-pachaas rupaye le kar kya khilavaad karn hai?
chaudharee raajee ho gaye. gomatee ko unhe rupaye dete kisee ne na dekh tha. lok-nindaa kee sanbhaavanaa bhee na thi. haranaath ne anaaj bharaa. anaajon ke boron kaa dher lag gaya. aaraam kee meethee neend sonevaale chaudharee ab saaree
raat boron kee rakhavaalee karte the, majaal na thi ki koi chuhiyaa boron mein ghus jaae. chaudharee is tarah jhapatate the ki billee bhee haar maan letee. is tarah chh: maheene beet gaye. paush mein anaaj bikaa, poore 500 ru. kaa phaayadaa hua.
haranaath ne kahaa —isamen se 50 ru. aap le len.
chaudharee ne jhallaa kar kahaa —50 ru. kya khairaat le loon? kisee mahaajan se itane rupaye liye hote to kam se kam hma00 ru. sood ke hote; mujhe tum do-chaar rupaye kam de do, aur kya karoge?
haranaath ne zyada batabadhaav na kiya. 150 ru. chaudharee ko de diya. chaudharee kee aatmaa itanee khush kabhee na huee thi. raat ko woh apanee kotharee mein sone gaya, to use aisaa lagaa hua ki budhiyaa gomatee khadee muskaraa rahi
hai. chaudharee kaa kalejaa dhak-dhak karne lagaa. woh neend mein na tha. koi nashaa na khaayaa tha. gomatee saamane khadee muskaraa rahi thi. haan, us murajhaaye hue munh par ek vichitr sphoorti thi.
kaee saal beet gaye! chaudharee baraabar isee phikr mein rehte ki haranaath se rupaye nikaal loon; lekin haranaath hameshaa hee heele-havaale kart rehta tha. woh saal mein thodaa-saa byaaj de detaa, par mool ke lie hajaar baaten banaataa tha. kabhee lene kaa ronaa tha, kabhee chukate kaa. haan, kaarobaar badhataa jat tha. aakhir ek din chaudharee ne usse saaph-saaph kah diya ki tumhaaraa kaam chale yaa doobe, mujhe paravaa nahin, is maheene mein tumhe avashy rupaye chukaane padenge. haranaath ne bahut udanaghaaiyaan bataayeen, par chaudharee apne iraade par jame rahe.
haranaath ne jhunjhalaa kar kahaa —keht hoon ki do maheene aur thaharie. maal bikate hee main rupaye de doongaa.
chaudharee ne dridhataa se kahaa —tumhaaraa maal kabhee na bikegaa, aur na tumhaare do maheene kabhee poore honge. main aaj rupaye loongaa.
haranaath usee vakt gussaa mein bharaa hua uthaa, aur do hajaar rupaye laa kar chaudharee ke saamane jor se patak diye.
chaudharee ne kuchh jhenp kar kahaa —rupaye to tumhaare paas the.
aur kya baaton se rojagaar hot hai?
to mujhe is samay 500 rupaye de do, baakee do maheene mein denaa. sab
aaj hee to kharch na ho jaaenge.
haranaath ne taav dikhaa kar kahaa —aap chaahe kharch keejie, chaahe jamaa keejie, mujhe rupayon kaa kaam nahin. duniya mein kya mahaajan mar gaye hain, jo aapakee dhauns sahoon?
chaudharee ne rupaye uthaa kar ek taak par rakh diye. kuen kee daagabel daalane kaa saaraa utsaah thandaa pad gaya.
haranaath ne rupaye lautaa to diye the, par man mein kuchh aur manasoobaa baandh rakhaa tha. aadhee raat ko jab ghar mein sannaataa chhaa gaya, to haranaath chaudharee ke kotharee kee chool khisakaa kar andar ghusaa. chaudharee bekhabar soye the. haranaath ne chaahaa ki donon thailiyaan uthaa kar baahar nikal aaoon, lekin jyon hee haath badhaayaa use apne saamane gomatee khadee dikhaayee dee. woh donon thailiyon ko donon haathon se pakade hue thi. haranaath bhayabheet ho kar peechhe hat gaya. phir yah soch kar ki shaayad mujhe dhokhaa ho raha ho, usane phir haath badhaayaa, par abakee woh moorti itanee bhayankar ho gayee ki haranaath ek pal bhee wahan khadaa na raha sakaa. bhaagaa, par baraamade hee mein achet hokar gir padaa. haranaath ne chaaron taraph se apne rupaye vasool karake vyaapaariyon ko dene ke lie jamaa kar rakhe the. chaudharee ne aankhen dikhaayeen, to vahee rupaye laa kar patak diya. dil mein usee vakt soch liya tha ki raat ko rupaye udaa laaoongaa. jhoothamooth chor kaa gul machaa doongaa, to meree ora shak bhee na hogaa. par jab yah peshabandee theek na utaree, to us par vyaapaariyon ke tagaade hone lage. vaadon par logon ko kahan tak taalataa, jitane bahaane ho sakate the, sab kiye. aakhir woh naubat aa gayee ki log naalish karne kee dhaamakiyaan dene lage. ek ne to 300 ru. kee naalish kar bhee dee. bechaare chaudharee badee mushkil mein phanse. dookaan par haranaath baithataa tha, chaudharee kaa usse koi vaastaa na tha, par usakee jo saakh thi woh chaudharee ke vajah log chaudharee ko kharaa aur len-den kaa saaph aadamee samajhate the. ab bhee yadyapi koi unse takaajaa na kart tha, par woh sabase munh chhipaate phirate the. lekin unhonne yah phaisalaa kar liya tha ki kuen ke rupaye na chhuoongaa chaahe kuchh aa pade. raat ko ek vyaapaaree ke musalamaan chaparaasee ne chaudharee ke dvaar par aa kar hajaaron gaaliyaan sunaayeen. chaudharee ko baar-baar gussaa aat tha ki chal kar moonchhen ukhaad loon, par man ko samajhaayaa, hamse matalab hee kya hai, bete kaa karj chukaanaa baap kaa dharm nahin hai.
jab khana karne gaye, to patnee ne kahaa —yah sab kya upadrav machaa rakhaa hai?
chaudharee ne kathor aavaaz mein kahaa —mainne machaa rakhaa hai?
aur kisane machaa rakhaa hai? bachchaa kasam khaate hain ki mere paas keval thodaa-saa maal hai, rupaye to sab tumane maang liye.
chaudharee - maang na letaa to kya kart, halavaaee kee dookaan par daadaa kaa phaatehaa padhna mujhe pasand nahin.
aurat, yah naak-kataaee achchhee lagatee hai?
chaudharee - to meraa kya bas hai bhai, kabhee kuaan banegaa ki nahin? paanch saal ho gaye.
aurat, is vakt usane kuchh nahin khaayaa. pahalee joon bhee munh joothaa karake uth gaya tha.
chaudharee - tumane samajhaakar khilaayaa nahin, daanaa-paani chhod dene se to rupaye na milenge.
aurat, tum kyon nahin jaa kar samajhaa dete?
chaudharee - mujhe to is vakt bairee samajh raha hogaa!
aurat, main rupaye le jaa kar bachchaa ko diye aati hoon, haath mein jab rupaye aa jaaen, to kuaan banavaa denaa.
chaudharee - nahin, nahin, aisaa gajab na karn, main itanaa badaa vishvaasaghaat na karoongaa, chaahe ghar mittee hee mein mil jaae.
lekin aurat ne ina baaton kee ora dhyaan na diya. woh lapak kar bheetar gayee aur thailiyon par haath daalanaa chaahatee thi ki ek cheekh maar kar hat gayee. usakee saaree deh sitaar ke taar kee tarah kaanpane lagee.
chaudharee ne ghabaraa kar poochhaa—kya hua, kya? tumhe chakkar to nahin aa gaya?
aurat ne taak kee ora bhayaatur netron se dekh kar kahaa —chudail wahan khadee hai?
chaudharee ne taak kee ora dekh kar kahaa —kaun chudail? mujhe to koi nahin deekhataa.
aurat, meraa to kalejaa dhak-dhak kar raha hai. aisaa maaloom hua, jaise us budhiyaa ne meraa haath pakad liya hai.
chaudharee - yah sab bhram hai. budhiyaa ko mare paanch saal ho gaye, ab tak woh yahan baithee hai?
aurat, mainne saaph dekh, vahee thi. bachchaa bhee kehte the ki unhonne raat ko thailiyon par haath rakhe dekh tha!
chaudharee - woh raat ko meree kotharee mein kab aay?
aurat, tumse kuchh rupayon ke vishay hee mein kehne aay tha. use dekhate hee bhaagaa.
chaudharee - achchhaa; phir to andar jaao, main dekh raha hoon.
aurat ne kaan par haath rakh kar kahaa —na baabaa, ab main us kamare mein kadam na rakhoongee.
chaudharee - achchhaa, main jaa kar dekhataa hoon.
chaudharee ne kotharee mein jaa kar donon thailiyaan taak par se uthaa leen. kisee tarah kee shankaa na huee. gomatee kee chhaayaa kaa kaheen naam bhee na tha. aurat dvaar par khadee jhaank rahi thi. chaudharee ne aa kar garv se kahaa —mujhe to kaheen
kuchh na dikhaayee diya. wahan hoti, to kahan chalee jati?
aurat, kya jaane, tumhe kyon nahin dikhaayee dee? tumse use pyaar tha, isee se hat gayee hogee.
chaudharee - tumhe bhram tha, aur kuchh nahin.
aurat, bachchaa ko bulaa kar puchhaaye detee hoon.
chaudharee - khadaa to hoon, aa kar dekh kyon nahin leteen?
aurat ko kuchh aashvaasan hua. usane taak ke paas jaa kar darate-darate haath badhaayaa, jor se chillaa kar bhaagee aur aangan mein aa kar dam liya. chaudharee bhee usake saath aangan mein aa gaya aur hairaanee se bol —kya tha, kya? bekaar mein bhaagee chalee aayee. mujhe to kuchh na dikhaayee diya. aurat ne haanphate hue tiraskaarapoorn aavaaz mein kahaa —chalo hato, ab tak to tumane meree jaan hee le lee thi. na-jaane tumhaaree aankhon ko kya ho gaya hai.
khadee to hai woh daayan!
itane mein haranaath bhee wahan aa gaya. maan ko aangan mein khade dekh kar bol —kya hai ammaan, kaisaa jee hai?
aurat, woh chudail aaj do baar dikhaayee dee, betaa. mainne kahaa —laao, tumhe rupaye de doon. phir jab haath mein jaa jaaenge, to kuaan banavaa diya jaayagaa. lekin jyon hee thailiyon par haath rakhaa, us chudail ne meraa haath pakad liya. jaan-se nikal gaye.
haranaath ne kahaa —kisee achchhe ojhaa ko bulaanaa chaahie, jo ise maar bhagaaye.
chaudharee - kya raat ko tumhe bhee dikhaaee dee thi?
haranaath, haan, main tumhaare paas ek maamale mein salaah karne aay tha. jyon hee andar kadam rakhaa, woh chudail taak ke paas khadee dikhaayee dee, main badahavaas ho kar bhaagaa.
chaudharee - achchhaa, phir to jaao.
aurat, kaun, ab to main na jaane doon, chaahe koi laakh rupaye hee kyon na de.
haranaath, main aap na jaaoongaa.
chaudharee - magar mujhe kuchh dikhaayee nahin detaa. yah baat kya hai?
haranaath, kya jaane, aapase daratee hogee. aaj kisee ojhaa ko bulaanaa chaahie.
chaudharee - kuchh samajh mein nahin aat, kya maajaraa hai. kya hua baijoo paande kee digree kaa?
haranaath ina dinon chaudharee se itanaa jalataa tha ki apanee dookaan ke vishay kee koi baat unse na keht tha. aangan kee taraph taakataa hua maano havaa se bol —jo hona hogaa, woh hogaa, meree jaan ke sivaa aur koi kya le legaa? jo khaa gaya hoon, woh to ugal nahin sakataa.
chaudharee - kaheen usane digree jaaree kar dee to?
haranaath, to kya? dookaan mein chaar-paanch sau kaa maal hai, woh neelaam ho jaayagaa.
chaudharee - kaarobaar to sab chaupat ho jaayagaa?
haranaath, ab kaarobaar ke naam ko kahan tak rooon. agar pahale se maaloom hot ki kuaan banavaane kee itanee jaldee hai, to yah kaam chhedataa hee kyon? rotee-daal to pahale bhee mil jati thi. bahut hogaa, to-chaar maheene
havaalaat mein rahanaa padegaa. isake sivaa aur kya ho sakataa hai? maan ne kahaa —jo tumhe havaalaat mein le jaae, usakaa munh jhulas doon! hamaare jeete-jee tum havaalaat mein jaaoge!
haranaath ne daarshanik ban kar kahaa —maan-baap janm ke saathee hote hain, kisee ke karm ke saathee nahin hote.
chaudharee ko betaa se pragaadh pyaar tha. unhe shankaa ho gayee thi ki haranaath rupaye hajam karne ke lie taal-matol kar raha hai. isalie unhonne aagrah karake rupaye vasool kar liye the. ab unhe tajurbaa hua ki haranaath ke jaan
sachamuch sankat mein hain. sochaa, agar ladake ko havaalaat ho gayee, yaa dookaan par kurkee aa gayee; to kul-maryaadaa dhool mein mil jaayagee. kya haraj hai, agar gomatee ke rupaye de doon. aakhir dookaan chalatee hee hai, kabhee na kabhee to rupaye haath mein aa hee jaaenge.
ekaaek kisee ne baahar se pukaaraa, haranaathasinh! haranaath ke munh par havaaiyaan udane lageen. chaudharee ne poochhaa—kaun hai?
kurk ameen.
kya dookaan kurk karaane aay hai?
haan, maaloom to hot hai.
kitane rupayon kee digree hai?
1500 ru. kee.
kurk-ameen kuchh lene-dene se na talegaa?
tal to jat par mahaajan bhee to usake saath hogaa. use jo kuchh lenaa hai, udhar se le chukaa hogaa.
na ho, 1500 ru. gomatee ke rupayon mein se de do.
usake rupaye kaun chhuegaa. na-jaane ghar par kya aaphat aaye.
usake rupaye koi hajam thodee hee kiye letaa hai; chalo, main de doon!
chaudharee ko is samay dar hua, kaheen mujhe bhee woh na dikhaayee de. lekin unakee shankaa nirmool thi. unhonne ek thailee se 550 ru. nikaale aur doosaree thailee mein rakh kar haranaath ko de diye. shaam tak ina 5000 ru. mein ek rupayaa bhee na bachaa.
baarah saal gujar gaye. na chaudharee ab is sansaar mein hain, na haranaath. chaudharee jab tak jiye, unhe kuen kee phikr banee rahi; yahan tak ki marate dam bhee unakee jabaan par kuen kee rat lagee huee thi. lekin dookaan mein hameshaa rupayon
kaa todaa raha. chaudharee ke marate hee saaraa kaarobaar chaupat ho gaya. haranaath ne aane rupaye phaayadaa se santusht na ho kar doone-tigune phaayadaa par haath maaraa, juaa khelanaa shuroo kiya. saal bhee na gujarane paayaa tha ki dookaan band ho gayee.
gahane-paatee, baratan bhaande, sab mittee mein mil gaye. chaudharee kee mrityu ke theek saal bhar baad, haranaath ne bhee is nukasaan-phaayadaa ke sansaar se payaan kiya. maan ke jeevan kaa ab koi sahaaraa na raha. beemaar padee, par davaa-darpan na ho sakee. teen-chaar maheene tak naanaa tarah ke takaleeph jhel kar woh bhee chal basee. ab keval bahoo thi, aur woh bhee garbhinee. us bechaaree ke lie ab koi aadhaar na tha. is dashaa mein majadooree bhee na kar sakatee thi. padosiyon ke kapade see-see kar usane kisee tarah paanch-chh: maheene kaate. tere ladakaa hogaa. saare lakshan ladakaa ke-se the. yahee ek jeevan kaa aadhaar tha. jab ladakee huee, to yah aadhaar bhee jat raha. maan ne apanaa dil itanaa kathor kar liya ki navajaat bachchaa ko chhaatee bhee na lagaatee thi. padosiyon ke bahut samajhaane-bujhaane par chhaatee se lagaayaa, par usakee chhaatee mein doodh kee ek boond bhee na thi. us samay abhaaginee maan ke dil mein karunaa, vaatsaly aur moh kaa ek bhookamp-saa aa gaya. agar kisee upaay se usake stan kee antim boond doodh ban jati, to woh apne ko dhany maanatee. ladakee kee woh bholee, gareeb, yaachanaamay, satrishn chhavi dekh kar usakaa
maatri-dil maano sahastr netron se rudan karne lagaa tha. usake dil kee saaree shubhechchhaaen, saaraa aasheervaad, saaree vibhooti, saaraa anuraag maano usakee aankhon se nikaal kar us ladakee ko usee tarah ranjit kar detaa tha jaise indu kaa sheetal prakaash pushp ko ranjit kar detaa hai; par us ladakee ke bhaagy mein maatriprem ke sukh na bade the! maan ne kuchh apanaa rakt, kuchh oopar kaa doodh pilaa kar use jilaayaa; par usakee dashaa dinondin jeern hoti jati thi.
ek din logon ne jaa kar dekh, to woh bhoomi par padee huee thi, aur ladakee usakee chhaatee se chipatee usake stanon ko choos rahi thi. shok aur daridrataa se aahat shareer mein rakt kahan jisse doodh banataa. vahee ladakee padosiyon kee raham-bhikshaa se pal-pal kar ek din ghaas khodatee huee us sthaan par jaa pahunchee, jahaan budhiyaa gomatee kaa ghar tha. chhappar kab ke panchabhooton mein mil chuke the. keval jahaan-tahaan deevaaron ke chihn baakee the. kaheen-kaheen aadhee-aadhee deevaaren khadee theen. ladakee ne na-jaane kya soch kar khurapee se gandhaa khodanaa shuroo kiya. dopahar se saanjh tak woh gandhaa khodatee rahi. na khaane kee sudh thi, na peene kee. na koi shankaa thi, na bhay. andheraa
ho gaya; par woh jyon kee tyon baithee gandhaa khod rahi thi. us samay kisaan log bhool kar bhee udhar se na nikalate the; par ladakee nishshank baithee bhoomi se mittee nikaal rahi thi. jab andheraa ho gaya to woh chalee gayee.
doosare din woh bade sabere uthee aur itanee ghaas khodee, jitanee woh kabhee din bhar mein na khodatee thi. dopahar ke baad woh apanee khaanchee aur khurapee liye phir usee sthaan par pahunchee; par woh aaj akelee na thi, usake saath do ladakaa aur bhee the. teenon wahan saanjh tak kuaan-kuaan khodate rahe. ladakee gandhe ke andar khodatee thi aur donon ladakaa mittee nikaal-nikaal kar phenkate the.
teesare din do ladake aur bhee us khel mein mil gaye. shaam tak khel hot raha. aaj gandhaa do haath gaharaa ho gaya tha. gaanv ke ladakaa-baalikaaon mein is vilakshan khel ne abhoopoorv utsaah bhar diya tha. chauthe din aur bhee kaee ladakaa aa mile. salaah huee, kaun andar jaae, kaun mittee uthaaye, kaun jhauaa kheenche. gandhaa ab chaar haath gaharaa ho gaya tha, par abhee tak baalakon ke sivaa aur kisee ko usakee khabar na thi. ek din raat ko ek kisaan apanee khoyee huee bhains dhoondhataa hua us khandahar mein jaa nikalaa. andar mittee kaa oonchaa dher, ek badaa-saa gandhaa aur ek timatimaataa hua deepak dekh, to dar kar bhaagaa. auron ne bhee aa kar dekh, kaee aadamee the. koi shankaa na thi. paas jaa kar dekh, to ladakee baithee thi. ek aadamee ne poochhaa—are, kya toone yah gandhaa khodaa hai?

jaise itanaa khodaa hai vaise hee itanaa aur khod loongee. gaanv ke sab ladake khelane aate hain.
maaloom hot hai, too apanee jaan degee aur apne saath aur ladakon ko bhee maaregee. khabaradaar, jo kal se gandhaa khodaa!
doosare din aur ladake na aaye, ladakee bhee din bhar majooree karti rahi. lekin shaam-samay wahan phir deepak jalaa aur phir woh khurapee haath mein liye wahan baithee dikhaayee dee.
gaanv vaalon ne use maaraa-peetaa, kotharee mein band kiya, par woh phursat paate hee wahan jaa pahunchatee. gaanv ke log praay: shrriddhaalu hote hee hain, ladakee ke is alaukik anuraag ne aakhir unamen bhee anuraag utpann kiya. kuaan khudane lagaa. idhar kuaan khud raha tha, udhar ladakee mittee se eenten banaatee thi. is khel mein saare gaanv ke ladake shareek hote the. ujaalee raaton mein jab sab logaso jate, tab bhee woh eenten thaapatee dikhaayee detee. na-jaane itanee lagan usamen kahan se aa gayee thi. saat varsh kee umr koi umr hoti hai? lekin saat varsh kee woh ladakee samajh aur baatacheet mein apanee tigunee umr vaalon ke kaan kaatatee thi. aakhir ek din woh bhee aay ki kuaan bandhaa gaya aur usakee pakkee jagat taiyaar ho gayee. us din ladakee usee jagat par soyee. aaj usake harsh kee seemaa na thi. gaatee thi, chahakatee thi. praat: kaal us jagat par keval usakee laash milee. us din se logon ne kahanaa shuroo kiya, yah vahee budhiyaa gomatee thi! is kuen kaa naam pisanahaaree kaa kuaan padaa.
