# Juloos

**Munshi Premchand**

1
poorn svaraajy kaa juloos nikal raha tha. kuchh yuvak, kuchh boodhe, kuchh ladakaa jhandiyaan aur jhande liye vandemaataram gaate hue maal ke saamane se nikale. donon taraph darshakon ki deevaaren khadee theen, maano unhen is lakshy se koi sarokaar nahin hain, maano yah koi tamaashaa hai aur unakaa kaam keval khade-khade dekhna hai.
shanbhoonaath ne dookaan ki pataree par khade hokar apne padosee deenadayaal se kahaa-sab ke sab kaal ke munh mein jaa rahe hain. aage savaaron kaa dal maar-maar bhagaa degaa.
deenadayaal ne kahaa-mahaatmaa jee bhi sathiyaa gaye hain. juloos nikaalane se svaraajy mil jat to ab tak kab kaa mil gaya hot. aur juloos mein hain kaun log, dekho-launde, laphange, siraphire. shahar kaa koi bada aadamee nahin.
maikoo chattiyon aur sleeparon ki maalaa garadan mein latakaaye khadaa tha. ina donon sethon ki baaten sunakar hansaa.
shanbhoo ne poochhaa-kyon hanse maikoo? aaj rang chokhaa maaloom hot hai.
maikoo-hansaa is baat par jo tumne kahin ki koi bada aadamee juloos mein nahin hai. bade aadamee kyon juloos mein aane lage, unhen is raaj mein kaun aaraam nahin hai? bangalon aur mahalon mein rehte hain, motaron par ghoomate hain, saahabon ke saath daavaten khaate hain, kaun takaleeph hai! mar to ham log rahe hain jinhen rotiyon kaa thikaanaa nahin. is bakhat koi tenis khelataa hogaa, koi chaay peetaa hogaa, koi graamophon lie gaanaa sunataa hogaa, koi paarik ki sair kart hogaa, yahan aaye pulis ke kode khaane ke lie? tumne bhi bhalee kahin?
shanbhoo-tum yah sab baaten kya samajhoge maikoo, jis kaam mein chaar bade aadamee aguaa hote hain uski sarakaar par bhi dhaak baith jati hai. laundon-laphangon kaa gol bhalaa haakimon ki nigaah mein kya janchegaa?
maikoo ne aisee nazar se dekh, jo kah rahi thi-ina baaton ke samajhne kaa theekaa kuchh tumheen ne nahin liya hai aur bol-bade aadamee ko to hamee log banaate-bigaadate hain yaa koi aur? kitane hi log jinhen koi poochhta bhi na tha, hamaare hi banaaye bade aadamee ban gaye aur ab motaron par nikalate hain aur hamen neech samajhte hain. yah logon ki takadeer ki khoobee hai ki jisakee zara badhti hui aur usane hamse aankhen phereen. hamaaraa bada aadamee to wahi hai, jo langotee baandhe nange paanv ghoomataa hai, jo hamaaree dashaa ko sudhaarane ke lie apni jaan hathelee par liye phirataa hai. aur hamen kisee bade aadamee ki paravaah nahin hai. sach poochho to ina bade aadamiyon ne hi hamaaree mittee kharaab kar rakhee hai. inhen sarakaar ne koi achchhi-see jagah de dee, bas uska dam bharane lage.
deenadayaal-nayaa daarogaa bada jallaad hai. chauraaste par pahunchte hi hantar lekar pil padegaa. phir dekhna, sab kaise dum dabaakar bhaagate hain. majaa aayegaa.
juloos svaadheenataa ke nashe mein choor chauraaste par pahunchaa to dekh, aage savaaron aur sipaahiyon kaa ek dastaa raastaa roke khadaa hai.
sahasaa daarogaa beerabal sinh ghodaa badhaa kar juloos ke saamane aa gaye aur bole-tum logon ko aage jaane kaa hukm nahin hai.
juloos ke boodhe netaa ibraahim alee ne aage badhakar kahaa-main aapako itmeenaan dilaataa hoon, kisee kism kaa dangaa-phasaad na hogaa. ham dookaanen lootane yaa motaren todane nahin nikale hain. hamaaraa makasad isase kaheen oonchaa hai.
beerabal-mujhe yah hukm hai ki juloos yahan se aage na jaane paaye.
ibraahim-aap apne aphasaron se zara poochh na len.
beerabal-main isakee koi jaroorat nahin samajhta.
ibraahim-to ham log yaheen baithte hain. jab aap log chale jaayange to ham nikal jaayange.
beerabal-yahan khade hone kaa bhi hukm nahin hai. tumako vaapas jan padegaa.
ibraahim ne ganbheer bhaav se kahaa-vaapas to ham na jaayenge. aapako yaa kisee ko bhi, hamen rokane kaa koi hak nahin. aap apne savaaron, sangeenon aur bandookon ke jor se hamen rokanaa chahte hain, rok leejie, magar aap hamen lautaa nahin sakate. na jaane vah din kab aayegaa, jab hamaare bhai-band aise hukmon ki taameel karne se saaph inkaar kar denge, jinakee manshaa mahaj kaum ko gulaamee ki janjeeron mein jakade rakhna hai.
beerabal grejuet tha. uska baap suparintendent pulis tha. uski nas-nas mein rob bharaa hua tha. aphasaron ki nazar mein uska bada izzat tha. khaasaa goraa chittaa, neelee aankhon aur bhoore baalon vaalaa tejasvee aadamee tha. shaayad jis vakt vah kot pahan kar oopar se hait lagaa letaa to vah bhool jat tha ki main bhi yahan kaa rahanevaalaa hoon. shaayad vah apne ko raajy karanevaalee jaati kaa ang samajhne lagta tha; magar ibraahim ke shabdon mein jo tiraskaar bharaa hua tha, usane zara der ke lie use lajjit kar diya. par muaamalaa naajuk tha. juloos ko raastaa de detaa hai, to jawab talab ho jaayegaa; vaheen khadaa rehne detaa hai, to yah sab na jaane kab tak khade rahen. is sankat mein padaa hua tha ki usane dee. esa. pee. ko ghode par aate dekh. ab soch-soch kaa samay na tha. yahi maukaa tha kaaragujaaree dikhaane kaa. usane kamar se betan nikaal liya aur ghode ko eda lagaa kar juloos par chadhaane lagaa. use dekhte hi aur savaaron ne bhi ghodon ko juloos par chadhaanaa shuru kar diya. ibraahim daarogaa ke ghode ke saamane khadaa tha. usake sir par ek betan aise jor se padaa ki uski aankhen tilamilaa gayeen. khadaa na raha sakaa. sir pakad kar baith gaya. usi vakt daarogaa jee ke ghode ne donon paanv uthaaye aur jameen par baithaa hua ibraahim uski taapon ke neeche aa gaya. juloos abhi tak shaant khadaa tha. ibraahim ko girate dekh kar kaee aadamee use uthaane ke lie lapake; magar koi aage na badh sakaa. udhar savaaron ke dande badi nirdayataa se pad rahe the. log haathon par dandon ko rokate the aur avichalit roop se khade the. hinsaa ke bhaavon mein prabhaavit na ho jan usake lie pratikshan mushkil hot jat tha. jab aaghaat aur beizzatee hi sahanaa hai, to phir ham bhi is deevaar ko paar karne ki kyon na cheshtaa karen? logon ko khayaal aay shahar ke laakhon aadamiyon ki nigaahen hamaaree taraph lagee hui hain. yahan se yah jhandaa lekar ham laut jaayan, to phir kis munh se aajaadee kaa naam lenge; magar praan-rakshaa ke lie bhaagane kaa kisee ko dhyaan bhi na aat tha. yah pet ke bhakton, kiraaye ke tattuon kaa dal na tha. yah svaadheenataa ke sachche svayansevakon kaa, aajaadee ke deevaanon kaa sangathit dal tha-apni jimmedaariyon ko khoob samajhta tha. kitane hi ke siron se khoon jaaree tha, kitane hi ke haath jakhmee ho gaye the. ek halle mein yah log savaaron ki saphon ko cheer sakate the, magar pairon mein bediyaan padee hui theen-siddhaant ki, dharm ki, aadarsh ki.
das-baarah minat tak yon hi dandon ki bauchhaar hoti rahi aur log shaant khade rahe.
2
is maar-dhaad ki khabar ek pal mein baajaar mein jaa pahunchee. ibraahim ghode se kuchal gaye, kaee aadamee jakhmee ho gaye, kaee ke haath toot gaye; magar na ve log peechhe phirate hain aur na pulis unhen aage jaane detee hai.
maikoo ne uttejit hokar kahaa-ab to bhai, yahan nahin raha jat. main bhi chalta hoon.
deenadayaal ne kahaa-ham bhi chalte hain bhai, dekhee jaayagee.
shambhoo ek minat tak chup khadaa raha. ekaaek usane bhi dookaan badhaayee aur bol-ek din to maranaa hi hai, jo kuchh hona hai, ho. aakhir ve log sabhi ke lie to jaan de rahe hain. dekhte-dekhte adhikaansh dookaanen band ho gayeen. vah log, jo das minat pahale tamaashaa dekh rahe the idhar-udhar se daud pade aur hajaaron aadamiyon kaa ek viraat dal ghatanaasthal ki ora chalaa. yah unmatt, hinsaamad se bhare hue manushyon kaa samooh tha, jise siddhaant aur aadarsh ki paravaah na thi. jo marane ke lie hi nahin, maarane ke lie bhi taiyaar the. kitanon hi ke haathon mein laathiyaan theen, kitane hi jebon mein patthar bhare hue the. na koi kisee se kuchh bolta tha, na poochhta tha. bas, sab-ke-sab man mein ek dridh sankalp kiye lapake chale jaa rahe the, maano koi ghta umadee chalee aatee ho.
is dal ko door se dekhte hi savaaron mein kuchh halachal padee. beerabal sinh ke chehare par havaaiyaan udane lageen. dee. esa. pee. ne apni motar badhaayee. shaanti aur ahinsaa ke vratadhaariyon par dande barasaanaa aur baat thi, ek unmatt dal se mukaabalaa karn doosaree baat. savaar aur sipaahee peechhe khisak gaye.
ibraahim ki peeth par ghode ne taap rakh dee. vah achet jameen par pade the. ina aadamiyon kaa shoragul sun kar aap hi aap unki aankhen khul gayeen. ek yuvak ko ishaare se bulaakar kahaa-kyon kailaash, kya kuchh log shahar se aa rahe hain?
kailaash ne us badhti hui ghta ki ora dekh kar kahaa-jee haan, hajaaron aadamee hain.
ibraahim-to ab khairiyat nahin hai. jhandaa lautaa do. hamen phauran laut chalna chaahie, nahin toophaan mach jaayagaa. hamen apne bhaaiyon se ladaaee nahin karanee hai. phauran laut chalo.
yah kehte hue unhone uthane ki cheshtaa ki, magar uth na sake.
ishaare ki der thi. sangathit senaa ki tarah log hukm paate hi peechhe phir gaye. jhandiyon ke baanson, saaphon aur roomaalon se chatapat ek strechar taiyaar ho gaya. ibraahim ko logon ne us par litaa diya aur peechhe phire. magar kya vah paraast ho gaye the? agar kuchh logon ko unhen paraast maanane mein hi santosh ho to ho, lekin vaastav mein unhone ek yugaantakaaree vijay praapt ki thi. ve jaanate the, hamaaraa sangharsh apne hi bhaaiyon se hai, jinake hit paristhitiyon ke vajah hamaare hiton se bhinn hain. hamen unse vair nahin karn hai. phir, vah yah bhi nahin chahte ki shahar mein loot aur dange kaa baajaar garm ho jaay aur hamaare dharmayuddh kaa ant lootee hui dookaanen, phoote hue sir hon, unki vijay kaa sabase ujjval chihn yah tha ki unhone janataa ki sahaanubhooti praapt kar lee thi. wahi log, jo pahale una par hansate the; unakaa dhairy aur saahas dekhakar unki madad ke lie nikal pade the. manovritti kaa yah parivartan hi hamaaree asalee vijay hai. hamen kisee se ladaaee karne ki jaroorat nahin, hamaaraa makasad keval janataa ki sahaanubhooti praapt karn hai, uski manovrittiyon ko badal dena hai. jis din ham is lakshy par pahunch jaayenge, usi din svaraajy soory uday hogaa.
3
teen din gujar gaye the. beerabal sinh apne kamare mein baithe chaay pee rahe the. aur unki patnee mitthan baaee bachchaa ko god mein liye saamane khadee theen.
beerabal sinh ne kahaa-main kya kart us vakt. peechhe dee. esa. pee. khadaa tha. agar unhen raastaa de detaa to apni jaan museebat mein phansatee.
mitthan baaee ne sir hilaa kar kahaa-tum kam se kam itanaa to kar hi sakate the ki una par dande na chalaane dete. tumhara kaam aadamiyon par dande chalaanaa hai? tum zyada se zyada unhen rok sakate the. kal ko tumhen aparaadhiyon ko bent lagaane kaa kaam diya jaay, to shaayad tumhen bada khushi aayegaa, kyon? beerabal sinh ne khisiyaa kar kahaa-tum to baat nahin samajhti ho!
mitthan baaee-main khoob samajhti hoon. dee. esa. pee. peechhe khadaa tha. tumne sochaa hogaa aisee kaaragujaaree dikhaane kaa avasar shaayad phir kabhi mile yaa na mile. kya tum samajhte ho, us dal mein koi bhalaa aadamee na tha? usamen kitane aadamee aise the, jo tumhaare jaison ko naukar rakh sakate hain. padhai mein to shaayad adhikaansh tumse badhe hue honge. magar tum una par dande chalaa rahe the aur unhen ghode se kuchal rahe the, vaah ree javaanmardee!
beerabal sinh ne behayaaee ki hansee ke saath kahaa-dee. esa. pee. ne mera naam not kar liya hai. sach!
daarogaa jee ne samajhaa tha ki yah khabar de kar vah mitthan baaee ko khush kar denge. sajjanataa aur bhalamanasee aadi oopar ki baaten hain, dil se nahin, jabaan se kahin jati hain. svaarth dil ki gaharaaiyon mein baithaa hot hai. vah gambheer soch kaa vishay hai.
magar mitthan baaee ke munh par harsh ki koi rekhaa na najar aayee, oopar ki baaten shaayad gaharaaiyon tak pahunch gayi theen! boleen-zaroor kar liya hogaa aur shaayad tumhen jaldee tarakkee bhi mil jaay. magar begunaahon ke khoon se haath rang kar tarakkee paayee, to kya paayee! yah tumhari kaaragujaaree kaa inaam nahin, tumhaare deshadroh ki keemat hai. tumhari kaaragujaaree kaa inaam to tab milegaa, jab tum kisee khoonee ko khoj nikaaloge, kisee doobate hue aadamee ko bachaa loge.
ekaaek ek sipaahee ne baraamade mein khade ho kar kahaa-hujoor, yah liphaaphaa laayaa hoon. beerabal sinh ne baahar nikal kar liphaaphaa le liya aur bheetar ki sarakaaree chitthee nikaal kar padhne lage. padh kar use mej par rakh diya.
mitthan ne poochhaa-kya tarakkee kaa paravaanaa aa gaya?
beerabal sinh ne jhenp kar kahaa-tum to banaatee ho! aaj phir koi juloos nikalanevaalaa hai. mujhe usake saath rehne kaa hukm hua hai.
mitthan-phir to tumhari chaandee hai, taiyaar ho jaao. aaj phir vaise hi shikaar milenge. khoob badh-badhakar haath dikhalaanaa! dee. esa. pee. bhi zaroor aayenge. abakee tum inspektar ho jaaoge. sach!
beerabal sinh ne maathaa sikod kar kahaa-kabhi-kabhi tum be-sir-pair ki baaten karne lagti ho. maan lo, main jaakar chupachaap khadaa rahoon, to kya nateejaa hogaa. main naalaayak samajhaa jaaoongaa aur meri jagah koi doosaraa aadamee bhej diya jaayagaa. kaheen shubahaa ho gaya ki mujhe svaraajyavaadiyon se sahaanubhooti hai, to kaheen kaa na rahoongaa. agar barkhaast bhi na hua to lain ki haajiree to ho hi jaayagee. aadamee jis duniyaa mein rehta hai, usi kaa chalan dekhakar kaam kart hai. main buddhimaan na sahee; par itanaa jaanataa hoon ki ye log desh aur jaati kaa uddhaar karne ke lie hi koshish kar rahe hain. yah bhi jaanataa hoon ki sarakaar is khayaal ko kuchal daalanaa chahti hai. aisaa gadhaa nahin hoon ki gulaamee ki zindagi par garv karoon; lekin paristhiti se majaboor hoon.
baaje ki aavaaj kaanon mein aayee. beerabal sinh ne baahar jaakar poochhaa. maaloom hua svaraajy vaalon kaa juloos aa raha hai. chatapat vardee pahanee, saaphaa baandhaa aur jeb mein pistaul rakhakar baahar aaye. ek pal mein ghodaa taiyaar ho gaya. kaanstebal pahale hi se taiyaar baithe the. sab log dabal maarch karte hue juloos ki taraph chale.
ye log dabal maarch karte hue koi pandrah minat mein juloos ke saamane pahunch gaye. ina logon ko dekhte hi aganit kanthon se ‘vandemaataram’ ki ek dhvani nikalee, maano meghamandal mein garjan kaa shabd hua ho, phir sannaataa chhaa gaya. us juloos mein aur is juloos mein kitanaa antar tha! vah svaraajy ke utsav kaa juloos tha, yah ek shaheed ke maatam kaa. teen din ke bheeshan jvar aur dard ke baad aaj us jeevan kaa ant ho gaya, jisane kabhi pad ki laalasaa nahin ki, kabhi adhikar ke saamane sir nahin jhukaayaa. unhone marate samay vaseeyat ki thi ki meri laash ko gangaa mein nahalaa kar daphan kiya jaay aur mere majaar par svaraajy kaa jhandaa khadaa kiya jaay. unake marane kaa khabar phailate hi saare shahar par maatam kaa pardaa-saa pad gaya. jo sunataa tha, ek baar is tarah chaunk padataa tha, jaise use golee lag gayi ho aur turant unake darshanon ke lie bhaagataa tha. saare baajaar band ho gaye, ikkon aur taangon kaa kaheen pataa na tha jaise shahar lut gaya ho. dekhte-dekhte sara shahar umad padaa. jis vakt janaajaa uthaa, laakh-savaa laakh aadamee saath the. koi aankh aisee na thi, jo aansuon se laal na ho.
beerabal sinh apne kaanstebalon aur savaaron ko paanch-paanch gaj ke phaasale par juloos ke saath chalne kaa hukm de kar khud peechhe chale gaye. pichhalee saphon mein koi pachaas gaj tak mahilaaen theen. daarogaa ne unki taraph taakaa. pahalee hi kataar mein mitthan baaee najar aayee. beerabal ko yakeen na aay. phir dhyaan se dekh, wahi thi. mitthan ne unki taraph ek baar dekh aur aankhen pher leen, par uski ek chitavan mein kuchh aisaa dhikkaar, kuchh aisee sharm, kuchh aisee vyathaa, kuchh aisee napharat bharee hui thi ki beerabal sinh ki deh mein sir se paanv tak sanasanee-see daud gayi. vah apni nazar mein kabhi itane halke, itane durbal, itane jaleel na hue the.
sahasaa ek yuvatee ne daarogaa jee ki taraph dekh kar kahaa-kotavaal sahab, kaheen ham logon par dande na chalaa deejiegaa. aapako dekhakar dar ho raha hai!
doosaree bolee-aap hi ke koi bhai to the, jinhonne us maal ke chauraste par is aadamee par aaghaat kiye the.
mitthan ne kahaa-aapake koi bhai na the, aap khud the.
beesiyon hi munhon se aavaajen nikaleen-achchha, yah wahi mahaashay hain? mahaashay aapako namaskaar hai. yah aap hi ki kripaa kaa phal hai ki aaj ham bhi aapake dande ke darshan ke lie aa khadee hui hain!
beerabal ne mitthan baaee ki ora aankhon kaa bhaalaa chalaayaa; par munh se kuchh na bole. ek teesaree mahilaa ne phir kahaa-ham ek jalasaa karake aapako jayamaal pahanaayenge aur aapakaa yashogaan karenge.
chauthee ne kahaa-aap bilakul angarej maaloom hote hain, jabhee itane gore hain!
ek budhiyaa ne aankhen chadhaa kar kahaa-meri kokh mein aisaa ladakaa janmaa hot, to uski gardan marod detee!
ek yuvatee ne uska tiraskaar karake kahaa-aap bhi khoob kehti hain, maan jee, kutte tak to namak kaa hak adaa karte hain, yah to aadamee hain!
budhiyaa ne jhallaa kar kahaa-pet ke gulaam, haay pet, haay pet!
is par kaee striyon ne budhiyaa ko aade haathon le liya aur vah bechaaree lajjit ho kar bolee-are, main kuchh kehti thode hi hoon. magar aisaa aadamee bhi kya, jo svaarth ke peechhe andhaa ho jaay.
beerabal sinh ab aur na sun sake. ghodaa badhaa kar juloos se kaee gaj peechhe chale gaye. mard lajjit kart hai, to hamen gussaa aat hai; striyaan lajjit karti hain, to pachhataavaa utpann hoti hai. beerabal sinh ki is vakt itanee himmat na thi ki phir una mahilaaon ke saamane jaate. apne aphasaron par gussaa aay. mujhee ko baar-baar kyon ina kaamon par tainaat kiya jat hai? aur log bhi to hain, unhen kyon nahin laayaa jat? kya main hi sabase gaya-beetaa hoon. kya main hi sabase bhaavashoony hoon.
mitthee is vakt mujhe dil mein kitanaa kaayar aur neech samajh rahi hogee! shaayad is vakt mujhe koi maar daale, to vah jabaan bhi na kholegee. shaayad man mein khush hogee ki achchha hua. abhi koi jaa kar sahab se kah de ki beerabal sinh ki aurat juloos mein nikalee thi, to kaheen kaa na rahoon! mitthee jaanatee hai, samajhti hai, phir bhi nikal khadee hui. mujhse poochhaa tak nahin. koi phikr nahin hai na, jabhee ye baaten soojhti hain, yahan sabhi bephikr hain, kaalejon aur skoolon ke ladake, majadoor, peshevar inhen kya phikr? maran to ham logon ki hai, jinake baal-bachche hain aur kul-maryaadaa kaa dhyaan hai. sab ki sab meri taraph kaisaa ghoor rahi theen, maano khaa jaayangee.
juloos shahar ki mukhy sadakon se gujarataa hua chalaa jaa raha tha. donon ora chhaton par, chhajjon par, jangalon par, vrikshon par darshakon ki deevaaren-see khadee theen. beerabal sinh ko aaj unake cheharon par ek nayee sphoorti, ek nayaa utsaah, ek nayaa garv jhalakataa hua maaloom hot tha. sphoorti thi vrikshon ke chehare par, utsaah yuvakon ke aur garv ramaniyon ke. yah svaraajy ke raastaa par chalne kaa ullaas tha. ab unako yaatraa kaa lakshy anajaan na tha, pathabhrashton ki tarah idhar-udhar bhatakanaa na tha, daliton ki tarah sir jhukaa kar ronaa na tha. svaadheenataa kaa sunahalaa shikhar sudoor aakaash mein chamak raha tha. aisaa jaan padataa tha ki logon ko beech ke naalon aur jangalon ki paravaah nahin hai. sab us sunahale lakshy par pahunchane ke lie utsuk ho rahe hain.
gyaarah bajate-bajate juloos nadee ke kinaare jaa pahunchaa, janaajaa utaaraa gaya aur log shav ko gangaa-snaan karaane ke lie chale. usake sheetal, shaant, peele mastak par laathee ki chot saaph najar aa rahi thi. rakt jam kar kaalaa ho gaya tha. sir ke bade-bade baal khoon jam jaane se kisee chitrakaar ki toolikaa ki tarah chimat gaye the. kaee hajaar aadamee is shaheed ke antim darshanon ke lie mandal baandh kar khade ho gaye. beerabal sinh peechhe ghode par savaar khade the. laathee ki chot unhen bhi najar aayee. unki aatmaa ne jor se dhikkaaraa. vah shav ki ora na taak sake. munh pher liya. jis manushy ke darshanon ke lie, jinake charanon ki raj mastak par lagaane ke lie laakhon aadamee vikal ho rahe hain uska maine itanaa beizzatee kiya. unki aatmaa is samay sveekaar kar rahi thi ki us nirday prahaar mein karttavy ke bhaav kaa lesh bhi na tha-keval svaarth tha, kaaragujaaree dikhaane ki havas aur aphasaron ko khush karne ki lipsaa thi. hajaaron aankhen gussaa se bharee hui unki ora dekh rahi theen; par vah saamane taakane kaa saahas na kar sakate the.
ek kaanstebal ne aakar prashansaa ki-hujoor kaa haath gaharaa padaa tha. abhi tak khopadee khulee hui hai. sabakee aankhen khul gayeen.
beerabal ne upekshaa ki-main ise apni javaanmardee nahin, apna kameenaapan samajhta hoon.
kaanstebal ne phir khushaamad ki-bada sarakash aadamee tha hujoor!
beerabal ne teevr bhaav se kahaa-chup raho! jaanate bhi ho, sarakash kise kehte hain? sarakash ve kahalaate hain, jo daake maarate hain, choree karte hain, khoon karte hain. unhen sarakash nahin kehte jo desh ki bhalaaee ke lie apni jaan hathelee par liye phirate hon. hamaaree badanaseebee hai ki jinakee madad karanee chaahie unakaa virodh kar rahe hain. yah ghamand karne aur khush hone ki baat nahin hai, sharm karne aur rone ki baat hai.
snaan khatm hua. juloos yahan se phir ravaanaa hua.

shav ko jab khaak ke neeche sulaa kar log lautane lage to do baj rahe the. mitthan baaee striyon ke saath-saath kuchh door tak to aayee, par kveensapaark mein aa kar thithak gayi. ghar jaane ki ichchhaa na hui. vah jeern, aahat, raktaranjit shav, maano usake antastal mein baithaa use dhikkaar raha tha. pati se uska man itanaa virakt ho gaya tha ki ab use dhikkaarane ki bhi uski ichchhaa na thi. aise svaarthee manushy par dar ke sivaa aur kisee cheej kaa asar ho sakataa hai, isakaa use yakeen hi na tha.
vah badi der tak paark mein ghaas par baithee sochti rahi, par apne karttavy kaa kuchh phaisalaa na kar sakee. maike jaa sakatee thi, lekin wahan se mahine-do mahine mein phir isi ghar aan padegaa. nahin, main kisee ki aashrit na banoongee. kya main apne gujar-basar ko bhi nahin kamaa sakatee? usane svayan tarah-tarah ki kathinaaiyon ki kalpanaa ki; par aaj uski aatmaa mein na jaane itanaa bal kahan se aa gaya. ina kalpanaaon ko dhyaan mein laanaa hi use apni kamajoree maaloom hui.
sahasaa use ibraahim alee ki vriddhaa vidhavaa kaa khayaal aay. usane sunaa tha, unake ladake-baale nahin hain. bechaaree baithee ro rahi hongee. koi tasallee dene vaalaa bhi paas na hogaa. vah unake makaan ki ora chalee. pataa usane pahale hi apne saath ki auraton se poochh liya tha. vah dil mein sochti jati thi-main unse kaise miloongee, unse kya kahoongee, unhen kin shabdon mein samajhaaoongee. inheen vichaaron mein doobee hui vah ibraahim alee ke ghar par pahunch gayi. makaan ek galee mein tha, saaph-sutharaa; lekin dvaar par hasarat baras rahi thi. usane dhadakate hue dil se andar kadam rakhaa. saamane baraamade mein ek khaat par vah vriddhaa baithee hui thi, jisake pati ne aaj svaadheenataa ki vedee par apna balidaan diya tha. usake saamane saade kapade pahane ek yuvak khadaa, aankhon mein aansoo bhare vriddhaa se baaten kar raha tha. mitthan us yuvak ko dekh kar chaunk padee-vah beerabal sinh the.
usane krodhamay hairaanee se poochhaa-tum yahan kaise aaye?
beerabal sinh ne kahaa-usi tarah jaise tum aayeen. apne aparaadh kshamaa karaane aay hoon!
mitthan ke gore mukhade par aaj garv, ullaas aur pyaar ki jo ujjval vibhooti najar aayee, vah akathaneey thi! aisaa jaan padaa, maano usake janm-janmaantar ke klesh mit gaye hain, vah phikr aur maayaa ke bandhanon se mukt ho gayi hai.
