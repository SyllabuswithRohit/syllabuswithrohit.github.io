# Juloos

**Munshi Premchand**

1
poorn svaraajy kaa juloos nikal raha tha| kuchh yuvak, kuchh boodhe, kuchh ladakaa jhandiyaan aur jhande liye vandemaataram gaate hue maal ke saamane se nikale| donon taraph darshakon kee deevaaren khadee theen, maano unhen is lakshy se koee sarokaar nahin hain, maano yah koee tamaashaa hai aur unakaa kaam keval khade-khade dekhanaa hai|
shanbhoonaath ne dookaan kee pataree par khade hokar apne padosee deenadayaal se kahaa-sab ke sab kaal ke munh mein jaa rahe hain| aage savaaron kaa dal maar-maar bhagaa degaa|
deenadayaal ne kahaa-mahaatmaa jee bhee sathiyaa gaye hain| juloos nikaalane se svaraajy mil jat to ab tak kab kaa mil gaya hot| aur juloos mein hain kaun log, dekho-launde, laphange, siraphire| shahar kaa koee badaa aadamee nahin|
maikoo chattiyon aur sleeparon kee maalaa garadan mein latakaaye khadaa tha| ina donon sethon kee baaten sunakar hansaa|
shanbhoo ne poochhaa-kyon hanse maikoo? aaj rang chokhaa maaloom hot hai|
maikoo-hansaa is baat par jo tumane kahee ki koee badaa aadamee juloos mein nahin hai| bade aadamee kyon juloos mein aane lage, unhen is raaj mein kaun aaraam nahin hai? bangalon aur mahalon mein rahate hain, motaron par ghoomate hain, saahabon ke saath daavaten khaate hain, kaun takaleeph hai ! mar to ham log rahe hain jinhen rotiyon kaa thikaanaa nahin| is bakhat koee tenis khelataa hogaa, koee chaay peetaa hogaa, koee graamophon lie gaanaa sunataa hogaa, koee paarik kee sair kart hogaa, yahan aaye pulis ke kode khaane ke lie? tumane bhee bhalee kahee?
shanbhoo-tum yah sab baaten kyaa samajhoge maikoo, jis kaam mein chaar bade aadamee aguaa hote hain usakee sarakaar par bhee dhaak baith jati hai| laundon-laphangon kaa gol bhalaa haakimon kee nigaah mein kyaa janchegaa?
maikoo ne aisee nazar se dekh, jo kah rahi thi-ina baaton ke samajhne kaa theekaa kuchh tumheen ne nahin liya hai aur bol-bade aadamee ko to hamee log banaate-bigaadate hain yaa koee aur? kitane hee log jinhen koee poochhataa bhee na tha, hamaare hee banaaye bade aadamee ban gaye aur ab motaron par nikalate hain aur hamen neech samajhate hain| yah logon kee takadeer kee khoobee hai ki jisakee jaraa badhatee huee aur usane hamse aankhen phereen| hamaaraa badaa aadamee to vahee hai, jo langotee baandhe nange paanv ghoomataa hai, jo hamaaree dashaa ko sudhaarane ke lie apanee jaan hathelee par liye phirataa hai| aur hamen kisee bade aadamee kee paravaah nahin hai| sach poochho to ina bade aadamiyon ne hee hamaaree mittee kharaab kar rakhee hai| inhen sarakaar ne koee achchhee-see jagah de dee, bas usakaa dam bharane lage|
deenadayaal-nayaa daarogaa badaa jallaad hai| chauraaste par pahunchate hee hantar lekar pil padegaa| phir dekhanaa, sab kaise dum dabaakar bhaagate hain| majaa aayegaa|
juloos svaadheenataa ke nashe mein choor chauraaste par pahunchaa to dekh, aage savaaron aur sipaahiyon kaa ek dastaa raastaa roke khadaa hai|
sahasaa daarogaa beerabal sinh ghodaa badhaa kar juloos ke saamane aa gaye aur bole-tum logon ko aage jaane kaa hukm nahin hai|
juloos ke boodhe netaa ibraahim alee ne aage badhakar kahaa-main aapako itmeenaan dilaataa hoon, kisee kism kaa dangaa-phasaad na hogaa| ham dookaanen lootane yaa motaren todane nahin nikale hain| hamaaraa makasad isase kaheen oonchaa hai|
beerabal-mujhe yah hukm hai ki juloos yahan se aage na jaane paaye|
ibraahim-aap apne aphasaron se jaraa poochh na len|
beerabal-main isakee koee jaroorat nahin samajhataa|
ibraahim-to ham log yaheen baithate hain| jab aap log chale jaayange to ham nikal jaayange|
beerabal-yahan khade hone kaa bhee hukm nahin hai| tumako vaapas jan padegaa|
ibraahim ne ganbheer bhaav se kahaa-vaapas to ham na jaayenge| aapako yaa kisee ko bhee, hamen rokane kaa koee hak nahin| aap apne savaaron, sangeenon aur bandookon ke jor se hamen rokanaa chaahate hain, rok leejie, magar aap hamen lautaa nahin sakate| na jaane vah din kab aayegaa, jab hamaare bhai-band aise hukmon kee taameel karne se saaph inkaar kar denge, jinakee manshaa mahaj kaum ko gulaamee kee janjeeron mein jakade rakhanaa hai|
beerabal grejuet tha| usakaa baap suparintendent pulis tha| usakee nas-nas mein rob bharaa hua tha| aphasaron kee nazar mein usakaa badaa izzat tha| khaasaa goraa chittaa, neelee aankhon aur bhoore baalon vaalaa tejasvee aadamee tha| shaayad jis vakt vah kot pahan kar oopar se hait lagaa letaa to vah bhool jat tha ki main bhee yahan kaa rahanevaalaa hoon| shaayad vah apne ko raajy karanevaalee jaati kaa ang samajhne lagataa tha; magar ibraahim ke shabdon mein jo tiraskaar bharaa hua tha, usane jaraa der ke lie use lajjit kar diya| par muaamalaa naajuk tha| juloos ko raastaa de detaa hai, to javaab talab ho jaayegaa; vaheen khadaa rehne detaa hai, to yah sab na jaane kab tak khade rahen| is sankat mein padaa hua tha ki usane dee. esa. pee. ko ghode par aate dekh| ab soch-soch kaa samay na tha| yahee maukaa tha kaaragujaaree dikhaane kaa| usane kamar se betan nikaal liya aur ghode ko eda lagaa kar juloos par chadhaane lagaa| use dekhate hee aur savaaron ne bhee ghodon ko juloos par chadhaanaa shuroo kar diya| ibraahim daarogaa ke ghode ke saamane khadaa tha| usake sir par ek betan aise jor se padaa ki usakee aankhen tilamilaa gayeen| khadaa na raha sakaa| sir pakad kar baith gaya| usee vakt daarogaa jee ke ghode ne donon paanv uthaaye aur jameen par baithaa hua ibraahim usakee taapon ke neeche aa gaya| juloos abhee tak shaant khadaa tha| ibraahim ko girate dekh kar kaee aadamee use uthaane ke lie lapake; magar koee aage na badh sakaa| udhar savaaron ke dande badee nirdayataa se pad rahe the| log haathon par dandon ko rokate the aur avichalit roop se khade the| hinsaa ke bhaavon mein prabhaavit na ho jan usake lie pratikshan mushkil hot jat tha| jab aaghaat aur beizzatee hee sahanaa hai, to phir ham bhee is deevaar ko paar karne kee kyon na cheshtaa karen? logon ko khayaal aay shahar ke laakhon aadamiyon kee nigaahen hamaaree taraph lagee huee hain| yahan se yah jhandaa lekar ham laut jaayan, to phir kis munh se aajaadee kaa naam lenge; magar praan-rakshaa ke lie bhaagane kaa kisee ko dhyaan bhee na aat tha| yah pet ke bhakton, kiraaye ke tattuon kaa dal na tha| yah svaadheenataa ke sachche svayansevakon kaa, aajaadee ke deevaanon kaa sangathit dal tha-apanee jimmedaariyon ko khoob samajhataa tha| kitane hee ke siron se khoon jaaree tha, kitane hee ke haath jakhmee ho gaye the| ek halle mein yah log savaaron kee saphon ko cheer sakate the, magar pairon mein bediyaan padee huee theen-siddhaant kee, dharm kee, aadarsh kee|
das-baarah minat tak yon hee dandon kee bauchhaar hoti rahi aur log shaant khade rahe|
2
is maar-dhaad kee khabar ek pal mein baajaar mein jaa pahunchee| ibraahim ghode se kuchal gaye, kaee aadamee jakhmee ho gaye, kaee ke haath toot gaye; magar na ve log peechhe phirate hain aur na pulis unhen aage jaane detee hai|
maikoo ne uttejit hokar kahaa-ab to bhai, yahan nahin raha jat| main bhee chalataa hoon|
deenadayaal ne kahaa-ham bhee chalate hain bhai, dekhee jaayagee|
shambhoo ek minat tak chup khadaa raha| ekaaek usane bhee dookaan badhaayee aur bol-ek din to maranaa hee hai, jo kuchh honaa hai, ho| aakhir ve log sabhee ke lie to jaan de rahe hain| dekhate-dekhate adhikaansh dookaanen band ho gayeen| vah log, jo das minat pahale tamaashaa dekh rahe the idhar-udhar se daud pade aur hajaaron aadamiyon kaa ek viraat dal ghatanaasthal kee ora chalaa| yah unmatt, hinsaamad se bhare hue manushyon kaa samooh tha, jise siddhaant aur aadarsh kee paravaah na thi| jo marane ke lie hee nahin, maarane ke lie bhee taiyaar the| kitanon hee ke haathon mein laathiyaan theen, kitane hee jebon mein patthar bhare hue the| na koee kisee se kuchh bolataa tha, na poochhataa tha| bas, sab-ke-sab man mein ek dridh sankalp kiye lapake chale jaa rahe the, maano koee ghataa umadee chalee aatee ho|
is dal ko door se dekhate hee savaaron mein kuchh halachal padee| beerabal sinh ke chehare par havaaiyaan udane lageen| dee. esa. pee. ne apanee motar badhaayee| shaanti aur ahinsaa ke vratadhaariyon par dande barasaanaa aur baat thi, ek unmatt dal se mukaabalaa karn doosaree baat| savaar aur sipaahee peechhe khisak gaye|
ibraahim kee peeth par ghode ne taap rakh dee| vah achet jameen par pade the| ina aadamiyon kaa shoragul sun kar aap hee aap unakee aankhen khul gayeen| ek yuvak ko ishaare se bulaakar kahaa-kyon kailaash, kyaa kuchh log shahar se aa rahe hain?
kailaash ne us badhatee huee ghataa kee ora dekh kar kahaa-jee haan, hajaaron aadamee hain|
ibraahim-to ab khairiyat nahin hai| jhandaa lautaa do| hamen phauran laut chalanaa chaahie, nahin toophaan mach jaayagaa| hamen apne bhaaiyon se ladaaee nahin karanee hai| phauran laut chalo|
yah kehte hue unhonne uthane kee cheshtaa kee, magar uth na sake|
ishaare kee der thi| sangathit senaa kee tarah log hukm paate hee peechhe phir gaye| jhandiyon ke baanson, saaphon aur roomaalon se chatapat ek strechar taiyaar ho gaya| ibraahim ko logon ne us par litaa diya aur peechhe phire| magar kyaa vah paraast ho gaye the? agar kuchh logon ko unhen paraast maanane mein hee santosh ho to ho, lekin vaastav mein unhonne ek yugaantakaaree vijay praapt kee thi| ve jaanate the, hamaaraa sangharsh apne hee bhaaiyon se hai, jinake hit paristhitiyon ke vajah hamaare hiton se bhinn hain| hamen unse vair nahin karn hai| phir, vah yah bhee nahin chaahate ki shahar mein loot aur dange kaa baajaar garm ho jaay aur hamaare dharmayuddh kaa ant lootee huee dookaanen, phoote hue sir hon, unakee vijay kaa sabase ujjval chihn yah tha ki unhonne janataa kee sahaanubhooti praapt kar lee thi| vahee log, jo pahale una par hansate the; unakaa dhairy aur saahas dekhakar unakee madad ke lie nikal pade the| manovritti kaa yah parivartan hee hamaaree asalee vijay hai| hamen kisee se ladaaee karne kee jaroorat nahin, hamaaraa makasad keval janataa kee sahaanubhooti praapt karn hai, usakee manovrittiyon ko badal denaa hai| jis din ham is lakshy par pahunch jaayenge, usee din svaraajy soory uday hogaa|
3
teen din gujar gaye the| beerabal sinh apne kamare mein baithe chaay pee rahe the| aur unakee patnee mitthan baaee bachchaa ko god mein liye saamane khadee theen|
beerabal sinh ne kahaa-main kyaa kart us vakt| peechhe dee. esa. pee. khadaa tha| agar unhen raastaa de detaa to apanee jaan museebat mein phansatee|
mitthan baaee ne sir hilaa kar kahaa-tum kam se kam itanaa to kar hee sakate the ki una par dande na chalaane dete| tumhaaraa kaam aadamiyon par dande chalaanaa hai? tum jyaadaa se jyaadaa unhen rok sakate the| kal ko tumhen aparaadhiyon ko bent lagaane kaa kaam diya jaay, to shaayad tumhen badaa khushee aayegaa, kyon? beerabal sinh ne khisiyaa kar kahaa-tum to baat nahin samajhatee ho !
mitthan baaee-main khoob samajhatee hoon| dee. esa. pee. peechhe khadaa tha| tumane sochaa hogaa aisee kaaragujaaree dikhaane kaa avasar shaayad phir kabhee mile yaa na mile| kyaa tum samajhate ho, us dal mein koee bhalaa aadamee na tha? usamen kitane aadamee aise the, jo tumhaare jaison ko naukar rakh sakate hain| padhai mein to shaayad adhikaansh tumse badhe hue honge| magar tum una par dande chalaa rahe the aur unhen ghode se kuchal rahe the, vaah ree javaanmardee !
beerabal sinh ne behayaaee kee hansee ke saath kahaa-dee. esa. pee. ne meraa naam not kar liya hai| sach !
daarogaa jee ne samajhaa tha ki yah khabar de kar vah mitthan baaee ko khush kar denge| sajjanataa aur bhalamanasee aadi oopar kee baaten hain, dil se nahin, jabaan se kahee jati hain| svaarth dil kee gaharaaiyon mein baithaa hot hai| vah gambheer soch kaa vishay hai|
magar mitthan baaee ke munh par harsh kee koee rekhaa na najar aayee, oopar kee baaten shaayad gaharaaiyon tak pahunch gayee theen ! boleen-jaroor kar liya hogaa aur shaayad tumhen jaldee tarakkee bhee mil jaay| magar begunaahon ke khoon se haath rang kar tarakkee paayee, to kyaa paayee ! yah tumhaaree kaaragujaaree kaa inaam nahin, tumhaare deshadroh kee keemat hai| tumhaaree kaaragujaaree kaa inaam to tab milegaa, jab tum kisee khoonee ko khoj nikaaloge, kisee doobate hue aadamee ko bachaa loge|
ekaaek ek sipaahee ne baraamade mein khade ho kar kahaa-hujoor, yah liphaaphaa laayaa hoon| beerabal sinh ne baahar nikal kar liphaaphaa le liya aur bheetar kee sarakaaree chitthee nikaal kar padhane lage| padh kar use mej par rakh diya|
mitthan ne poochhaa-kyaa tarakkee kaa paravaanaa aa gaya?
beerabal sinh ne jhenp kar kahaa-tum to banaatee ho ! aaj phir koee juloos nikalanevaalaa hai| mujhe usake saath rehne kaa hukm hua hai|
mitthan-phir to tumhaaree chaandee hai, taiyaar ho jaao| aaj phir vaise hee shikaar milenge| khoob badh-badhakar haath dikhalaanaa ! dee. esa. pee. bhee jaroor aayenge| abakee tum inspektar ho jaaoge| sach !
beerabal sinh ne maathaa sikod kar kahaa-kabhee-kabhee tum be-sir-pair kee baaten karne lagatee ho| maan lo, main jaakar chupachaap khadaa rahoon, to kyaa nateejaa hogaa| main naalaayak samajhaa jaaoongaa aur meree jagah koee doosaraa aadamee bhej diya jaayagaa| kaheen shubahaa ho gaya ki mujhe svaraajyavaadiyon se sahaanubhooti hai, to kaheen kaa na rahoongaa| agar barkhaast bhee na hua to lain kee haajiree to ho hee jaayagee| aadamee jis duniyaa mein rahataa hai, usee kaa chalan dekhakar kaam kart hai| main buddhimaan na sahee; par itanaa jaanataa hoon ki ye log desh aur jaati kaa uddhaar karne ke lie hee koshish kar rahe hain| yah bhee jaanataa hoon ki sarakaar is khayaal ko kuchal daalanaa chaahatee hai| aisaa gadhaa nahin hoon ki gulaamee kee jindagee par garv karoon; lekin paristhiti se majaboor hoon|
baaje kee aavaaj kaanon mein aayee| beerabal sinh ne baahar jaakar poochhaa| maaloom hua svaraajy vaalon kaa juloos aa raha hai| chatapat vardee pahanee, saaphaa baandhaa aur jeb mein pistaul rakhakar baahar aaye| ek pal mein ghodaa taiyaar ho gaya| kaanstebal pahale hee se taiyaar baithe the| sab log dabal maarch karte hue juloos kee taraph chale|
ye log dabal maarch karte hue koee pandrah minat mein juloos ke saamane pahunch gaye| ina logon ko dekhate hee aganit kanthon se ‘vandemaataram’ kee ek dhvani nikalee, maano meghamandal mein garjan kaa shabd hua ho, phir sannaataa chhaa gaya| us juloos mein aur is juloos mein kitanaa antar tha ! vah svaraajy ke utsav kaa juloos tha, yah ek shaheed ke maatam kaa| teen din ke bheeshan jvar aur dard ke baad aaj us jeevan kaa ant ho gaya, jisane kabhee pad kee laalasaa nahin kee, kabhee adhikaar ke saamane sir nahin jhukaayaa| unhonne marate samay vaseeyat kee thi ki meree laash ko gangaa mein nahalaa kar daphan kiya jaay aur mere majaar par svaraajy kaa jhandaa khadaa kiya jaay| unake marane kaa khabar phailate hee saare shahar par maatam kaa pardaa-saa pad gaya| jo sunataa tha, ek baar is tarah chaunk padataa tha, jaise use golee lag gayee ho aur turant unake darshanon ke lie bhaagataa tha| saare baajaar band ho gaye, ikkon aur taangon kaa kaheen pataa na tha jaise shahar lut gaya ho| dekhate-dekhate saaraa shahar umad padaa| jis vakt janaajaa uthaa, laakh-savaa laakh aadamee saath the| koee aankh aisee na thi, jo aansuon se laal na ho|
beerabal sinh apne kaanstebalon aur savaaron ko paanch-paanch gaj ke phaasale par juloos ke saath chalne kaa hukm de kar khud peechhe chale gaye| pichhalee saphon mein koee pachaas gaj tak mahilaaen theen| daarogaa ne unakee taraph taakaa| pahalee hee kataar mein mitthan baaee najar aayee| beerabal ko yakeen na aay| phir dhyaan se dekh, vahee thi| mitthan ne unakee taraph ek baar dekh aur aankhen pher leen, par usakee ek chitavan mein kuchh aisaa dhikkaar, kuchh aisee sharm, kuchh aisee vyathaa, kuchh aisee napharat bharee huee thi ki beerabal sinh kee deh mein sir se paanv tak sanasanee-see daud gayee| vah apanee nazar mein kabhee itane halke, itane durbal, itane jaleel na hue the|
sahasaa ek yuvatee ne daarogaa jee kee taraph dekh kar kahaa-kotavaal sahab, kaheen ham logon par dande na chalaa deejiegaa| aapako dekhakar dar ho raha hai !
doosaree bolee-aap hee ke koee bhai to the, jinhonne us maal ke chauraste par is aadamee par aaghaat kiye the|
mitthan ne kahaa-aapake koee bhai na the, aap khud the|
beesiyon hee munhon se aavaajen nikaleen-achchhaa, yah vahee mahaashay hain? mahaashay aapako namaskaar hai| yah aap hee kee kripaa kaa phal hai ki aaj ham bhee aapake dande ke darshan ke lie aa khadee huee hain !
beerabal ne mitthan baaee kee ora aankhon kaa bhaalaa chalaayaa; par munh se kuchh na bole| ek teesaree mahilaa ne phir kahaa-ham ek jalasaa karake aapako jayamaal pahanaayenge aur aapakaa yashogaan karenge|
chauthee ne kahaa-aap bilakul angarej maaloom hote hain, jabhee itane gore hain !
ek budhiyaa ne aankhen chadhaa kar kahaa-meree kokh mein aisaa ladakaa janmaa hot, to usakee gardan marod detee !
ek yuvatee ne usakaa tiraskaar karake kahaa-aap bhee khoob kehti hain, maan jee, kutte tak to namak kaa hak adaa karte hain, yah to aadamee hain !
budhiyaa ne jhallaa kar kahaa-pet ke gulaam, haay pet, haay pet !
is par kaee striyon ne budhiyaa ko aade haathon le liya aur vah bechaaree lajjit ho kar bolee-are, main kuchh kehti thode hee hoon| magar aisaa aadamee bhee kyaa, jo svaarth ke peechhe andhaa ho jaay|
beerabal sinh ab aur na sun sake| ghodaa badhaa kar juloos se kaee gaj peechhe chale gaye| mard lajjit kart hai, to hamen gussaa aat hai; striyaan lajjit karti hain, to pachhataavaa utpann hoti hai| beerabal sinh kee is vakt itanee himmat na thi ki phir una mahilaaon ke saamane jaate| apne aphasaron par gussaa aay| mujhee ko baar-baar kyon ina kaamon par tainaat kiya jat hai? aur log bhee to hain, unhen kyon nahin laayaa jat? kyaa main hee sabase gaya-beetaa hoon| kyaa main hee sabase bhaavashoony hoon|
mitthee is vakt mujhe dil mein kitanaa kaayar aur neech samajh rahi hogee ! shaayad is vakt mujhe koee maar daale, to vah jabaan bhee na kholegee| shaayad man mein khush hogee ki achchhaa hua| abhee koee jaa kar sahab se kah de ki beerabal sinh kee aurat juloos mein nikalee thi, to kaheen kaa na rahoon ! mitthee jaanatee hai, samajhatee hai, phir bhee nikal khadee huee| mujhse poochhaa tak nahin| koee phikr nahin hai na, jabhee ye baaten soojhatee hain, yahan sabhee bephikr hain, kaalejon aur skoolon ke ladake, majadoor, peshevar inhen kyaa phikr? maran to ham logon kee hai, jinake baal-bachche hain aur kul-maryaadaa kaa dhyaan hai| sab kee sab meree taraph kaisaa ghoor rahi theen, maano khaa jaayangee|
juloos shahar kee mukhy sadakon se gujarataa hua chalaa jaa raha tha| donon ora chhaton par, chhajjon par, jangalon par, vrikshon par darshakon kee deevaaren-see khadee theen| beerabal sinh ko aaj unake cheharon par ek nayee sphoorti, ek nayaa utsaah, ek nayaa garv jhalakataa hua maaloom hot tha| sphoorti thi vrikshon ke chehare par, utsaah yuvakon ke aur garv ramaniyon ke| yah svaraajy ke raastaa par chalne kaa ullaas tha| ab unako yaatraa kaa lakshy anajaan na tha, pathabhrashton kee tarah idhar-udhar bhatakanaa na tha, daliton kee tarah sir jhukaa kar ronaa na tha| svaadheenataa kaa sunahalaa shikhar sudoor aakaash mein chamak raha tha| aisaa jaan padataa tha ki logon ko beech ke naalon aur jangalon kee paravaah nahin hai| sab us sunahale lakshy par pahunchane ke lie utsuk ho rahe hain|
gyaarah bajate-bajate juloos nadee ke kinaare jaa pahunchaa, janaajaa utaaraa gaya aur log shav ko gangaa-snaan karaane ke lie chale| usake sheetal, shaant, peele mastak par laathee kee chot saaph najar aa rahi thi| rakt jam kar kaalaa ho gaya tha| sir ke bade-bade baal khoon jam jaane se kisee chitrakaar kee toolikaa kee tarah chimat gaye the| kaee hajaar aadamee is shaheed ke antim darshanon ke lie mandal baandh kar khade ho gaye| beerabal sinh peechhe ghode par savaar khade the| laathee kee chot unhen bhee najar aayee| unakee aatmaa ne jor se dhikkaaraa| vah shav kee ora na taak sake| munh pher liya| jis manushy ke darshanon ke lie, jinake charanon kee raj mastak par lagaane ke lie laakhon aadamee vikal ho rahe hain usakaa mainne itanaa beizzatee kiya| unakee aatmaa is samay sveekaar kar rahi thi ki us nirday prahaar mein karttavy ke bhaav kaa lesh bhee na tha-keval svaarth tha, kaaragujaaree dikhaane kee havas aur aphasaron ko khush karne kee lipsaa thi| hajaaron aankhen gussaa se bharee huee unakee ora dekh rahi theen; par vah saamane taakane kaa saahas na kar sakate the|
ek kaanstebal ne aakar prashansaa kee-hujoor kaa haath gaharaa padaa tha| abhee tak khopadee khulee huee hai| sabakee aankhen khul gayeen|
beerabal ne upekshaa kee-main ise apanee javaanmardee nahin, apanaa kameenaapan samajhataa hoon|
kaanstebal ne phir khushaamad kee-badaa sarakash aadamee tha hujoor !
beerabal ne teevr bhaav se kahaa-chup raho ! jaanate bhee ho, sarakash kise kehte hain? sarakash ve kahalaate hain, jo daake maarate hain, choree karte hain, khoon karte hain| unhen sarakash nahin kehte jo desh kee bhalaaee ke lie apanee jaan hathelee par liye phirate hon| hamaaree badanaseebee hai ki jinakee madad karanee chaahie unakaa virodh kar rahe hain| yah ghamand karne aur khush hone kee baat nahin hai, sharm karne aur rone kee baat hai|
snaan khatm hua| juloos yahan se phir ravaanaa hua|

shav ko jab khaak ke neeche sulaa kar log lautane lage to do baj rahe the| mitthan baaee striyon ke saath-saath kuchh door tak to aayee, par kveensapaark mein aa kar thithak gayee| ghar jaane kee ichchhaa na huee| vah jeern, aahat, raktaranjit shav, maano usake antastal mein baithaa use dhikkaar raha tha| pati se usakaa man itanaa virakt ho gaya tha ki ab use dhikkaarane kee bhee usakee ichchhaa na thi| aise svaarthee manushy par dar ke sivaa aur kisee cheej kaa asar ho sakataa hai, isakaa use yakeen hee na tha|
vah badee der tak paark mein ghaas par baithee sochatee rahi, par apne karttavy kaa kuchh phaisalaa na kar sakee| maike jaa sakatee thi, lekin wahan se maheene-do maheene mein phir isee ghar aan padegaa| nahin, main kisee kee aashrit na banoongee| kyaa main apne gujar-basar ko bhee nahin kamaa sakatee? usane svayan tarah-tarah kee kathinaaiyon kee kalpanaa kee; par aaj usakee aatmaa mein na jaane itanaa bal kahan se aa gaya| ina kalpanaaon ko dhyaan mein laanaa hee use apanee kamajoree maaloom huee|
sahasaa use ibraahim alee kee vriddhaa vidhavaa kaa khayaal aay| usane sunaa tha, unake ladake-baale nahin hain| bechaaree baithee ro rahi hongee| koee tasallee dene vaalaa bhee paas na hogaa| vah unake makaan kee ora chalee| pataa usane pahale hee apne saath kee auraton se poochh liya tha| vah dil mein sochatee jati thi-main unse kaise miloongee, unse kyaa kahoongee, unhen kin shabdon mein samajhaaoongee| inheen vichaaron mein doobee huee vah ibraahim alee ke ghar par pahunch gayee| makaan ek galee mein tha, saaph-sutharaa; lekin dvaar par hasarat baras rahi thi| usane dhadakate hue dil se andar kadam rakhaa| saamane baraamade mein ek khaat par vah vriddhaa baithee huee thi, jisake pati ne aaj svaadheenataa kee vedee par apanaa balidaan diya tha| usake saamane saade kapade pahane ek yuvak khadaa, aankhon mein aansoo bhare vriddhaa se baaten kar raha tha| mitthan us yuvak ko dekh kar chaunk padee-vah beerabal sinh the|
usane krodhamay hairaanee se poochhaa-tum yahan kaise aaye?
beerabal sinh ne kahaa-usee tarah jaise tum aayeen| apne aparaadh kshamaa karaane aay hoon !
mitthan ke gore mukhade par aaj garv, ullaas aur pyaar kee jo ujjval vibhooti najar aayee, vah akathaneey thi ! aisaa jaan padaa, maano usake janm-janmaantar ke klesh mit gaye hain, vah phikr aur maayaa ke bandhanon se mukt ho gayee hai|
