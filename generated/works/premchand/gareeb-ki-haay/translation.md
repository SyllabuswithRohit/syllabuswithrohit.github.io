# Gareeb Ki Haay

**Munshi Premchand**

munshee raamasevak bhaunhe chadhaae hue ghar se nikale aur bole- ‘is jeene se to maranaa bhalaa hai. ’ mrityu ko praayah is tarah ke jitane nimantran diye jate hain, yadi woh sabako sveekaar karti, to aaj saaraa sansaar ujaad dikhaaee detaa.
munshee raamasevak chaandapur gaanv ke ek bade raees the. raeeson ke sabhee gun inamen bharapoor the. maanav charitr kee durbalataaen unake jeevan kaa aadhaar theen. woh nity munsiphee kachaharee ke haate mein ek neem ke ped ke neeche kaagajon kaa bastaa khol ek tootee-see chaukee par baithe dikhaaee dete the. kisee ne kabhee unhe kisee ijalaas par kaanoonee bahas yaa mukadame kee pairavee karte nahin dekh. lekin unhe sab log mukhtaar sahab kahakar purakaarate the. chaahe toophaan aaye, paani barase, ole giren par mukhtaar sahab wahan se tas se mas na hote. jab woh kachaharee chalate to dehaatiyon ke jhund-ke-jhund unake saath ho lete. chaaron ora se una par yakeen aur aadar kee nazar padatee. sabamen prasiddh tha ki unakee jeebh par sarasvatee viraajatee hain. ise vakaalat kaho yaa mukhtaaree, lekin yah keval kul-maryaadaa kee pratishthaa kaa paalan tha. aamadanee zyaadaa na hoti thi. chaandee ke sikkon kee to charchaa hee kya, kabhee-kabhee taanbe ke sikke bhee nirbhay unake paas aane se hichakate the.

munsheejee kee kaanoonadaanee mein koi shak na tha. lekin ‘paas’ ke bakhede ne unhe majaboor kar diya tha. khair, jo ho, unakaa yah peshaa keval pratishthaa-paalan ke nimitt tha; nahin to unake guzaaraa kaa mukhy saadhan aas-paas kee anaath, par khaane-peene mein sukhee vidhavaaon aur bhole-bhaale lekin dhanee vriddhon kee shraddhaa thi. vidhavaaen apanaa rupayaa unake yahan amaanat rakhateen. boodhe apne kapooton ke dar se apanaa paisaa unhe saunp dete. par rupayaa ek baar unakee muththee mein jaakar phir nikalanaa bhool jat tha. woh jaroorat padane par kabhee-kabhee karj le lete the. bhalaa, binaa karj lie kisee kaa kaam chal sakataa hai? bhor ko saanjh ke karaar par rupayaa lete, par woh saanjh kabhee nahin aati thi. saaraansh munsheejee karj lekar denaa seekhe nahin the. yah unakee kul-prathaa thi.
yahee sab maamale bahudhaa munshee jee ke sukh-chain mein vighn daalate the. kaanoon aur adaalat se to unhe koi dar na tha. is maidaan mein usakaa saamanaa karn paani mein magar se ladanaa tha. lekin jab koi dusht unse bhid jat, unakee eemaanadaaree par shak kart aur unake munh par buraa-bhalaa kehne par utaaroo ho jat, tab munsheejee ke dil par badee chot lagatee. is tarah kee durghatanaaen praayah hoti theen. har jagah aise ochhe log rehte hain, jinhen doosaron ko neechaa dikhaane mein hee khushi aat hai. aise logon kaa sahaaraa paakar kabhee-kabhee chhote aadamee munsheejee ke munh lag jate the. nahin to, ek kunjadin kee itanee majaal nahin thi ki aangan mein jaakar unhe buraa-bhalaa kahe. munsheejee usake puraane gaahak the; barason tak usse saag-bhaajee lee thi. yadi daam na diya jaay, to kunjadin ko santosh karn chaahie tha. daam jaldee yaa der se mil hee jate. lekin woh munhaphat kunjadin do hee barason mein ghabaraa gaee, aur usane kuchh aane paison ke lie ek pratishthit aadamee kaa paani utaar liya. jhunjhalaakar munsheejee apne ko mrityu kaa kalevaa banaane par utaaroo ho gae, to isamen unakaa kuchh dosh na tha.

isee gaanv mein moongaa naam kee ek vidhavaa braahmanee rehti thi. usakaa pati brahmaa kee kaalee palatan mein havaladaar tha aur ladaaee mein vaheen maaraa gaya. sarakaar kee ora se usake achchhe kaamon ke badale moongaa ko paanch sau rupaye mile the. vidhavaa aurat, jamaanaa naajuk tha, bechaaree ne sab rupaye munshee raamasevak ko saunp die, aur maheene-maheene thodaa-thodaa usamen se maangakar apanaa guzaaraa karti rahi.
munsheejee ne yah farz kaee varsh tak to badee eemaanadaaree ke saath pooraa kiya par jab boodh hone par bhee moongaa nahin maree aur munshee jee ko yah phikr huee ki shaayad usamen se aadhee rakam bhee svarg-yaatraa ke lie nahin chhodanaa chaahatee, to ek din unhonne kahaa—moongaa! tumhe maranaa hai yaa nahin? saaph-saaph kah do ki main apne marane kee phikr karoon? us din moongaa kee aankhe khuleen, usakee neend tootee, bolee—meraa hisaab kar do. hisaab kaa chitthaa taiyaar tha. ‘amaanat’ mein ab ek kaudee baakee na thi. moongaa ne badee kadaaee se munsheejee kaa haath pakad kar kahaa—abhee mere dhaaee sau rupaye tumane dabaa rakhe hain. main ek kaudee bhee na chhodoongee.
lekin anaathon kaa gussaa pataakhe kee aavaaj hai, jisse bachche dar jate hain aur asar kuchh nahin hot. adaalat mein usakaa kuchh jor na tha. na likhaa-padhee thi, na hisaab-kitaab. haan, panchaayat se kuchh aasaraa tha. panchaayat baithee, kaee gaanv ke log ikatthe hue. munsheejee neeyat aur maamale ke saaph the, unhe panchon kaa kya dar! sabhaa mein khade hokar panchon se kahaa— ‘bhaaiyon! aap log satyanaaraayan aur kuleen hain. main aap sab saahabon kaa daas hoon. aap sab saahabon kee udaarataa aur kripaa se, raham aur pyaar se meraa rom-rom kritajn hai aur aap log sochate hain ki is anaathinee aur vidhavaa aurat ke rupaye hadap kar gaya hoon? ’
panchon ne ek aavaaz se kahaa—nahin, nahin! aapase aisaa nahin ho sakataa.
raamasevak—yadi aap sab sajjanon kaa soch ho ki mainne rupaye dabaa liye, to mere lie doob marane ke sivaa aur koi upaay nahin. main dhanaadhy nahin hoon, na mujhe udaar hone kaa ghamand hai, par apanee kalam kee kripaa se, aap logon kee kripaa se kisee kaa mohataaj nahin hoon kya main aisaa ochhaa ho jaaoongaa ki ek anaathinee ke rupaye pachaa loon?
panchon ne ek aavaaz se phir kahaa—nahin, nahin! aapase aisaa nahin ho sakataa. munh dekhakar teekaa kaadhaa jat hai. panchon ne munsheejee ko chhod diya. panchaayat uth gaee. moongaa ne aah bharakar santosh kiya aur man mein kahaa—achchhaa, achchhaa! yahaa na milaa to na sahee, wahan kahan jaayegaa?

ab koi moongaa kaa duhkh sunanevaalaa aur sahaayak na tha. daridrataa se jo kuchh duhkh bhogane padate hain, woh sab use jhelane pade. woh shareer se pusht thi, chaahatee to parishram kar sakatee thi; par jis din panchaayat pooree huee, usee din usane kaam na karne kee kasam khaa lee. ab use raat-din rupayon kee rat lagee rehti. uthate-baithate, sote-jaagate, use keval ek kaam tha aur woh munshee raamasevak kaa bhalaa manaanaa. apne jhopade ke daravaaje par baithee huee woh raat-din unhe sachche man se aseesaa karti. bahudhaa apne asees ke vaakyon mein aise kavitaa ke vaaky aur upamaaon kaa vyavahaar karti ki log sunakar achambhe mein aa jate.
dheere-dheere moongaa pagalee ho chalee. nange-sir, nange shareer, haath mein ek kulhaadee liye hue sunasaan sthaanon mein jaa baithatee.
jhopadee ke badale ab woh maraghat par, nadee ke kinaare khandaharon mein ghoomatee dikhaaee detee. bikharee huee laten, laal-laal aankhen, paagalon-saa cheharaa, sookhe hue haath-paanv. usakaa yah svaroop dekhakar log dar jate the. ab koi use hansee mein bhee nahin chhedataa. yadi woh kabhee gaanv mein nikal aati, to striyaan gharon ke kivaad band kar leteen. aadamee kataraakar idhar-udhar se nikal jate aur bachche cheekh maarakar bhaagate. yadi koi ladakaa bhaagataa na tha, to woh munshee raamasevak kaa suputr raamagulaam tha. baap mein jo kuchh kor-kasar raha gaee thi, woh bete mein pooree ho gaee thi! ladakon kaa usake maare naak mein dam tha. gaanv ke kaane langade aadamee usakee soorat se chidhate the aur gaaliyaan khaane mein to shaayad sasuraal mein aanevaale damaad ko bhee itanaa khushi na aat ho! woh moongaa ke peechhe taaliyaan bajaataa, kutton ko saath lie hue us samay tak rehta, jab tak woh bechaaree tang aakar gaanv se nikal na jati. rupayaa-paisaa, hosh-havaas khokar use pagalee kee padavee milee aur ab woh sachamuch pagalee thi. akelee baithee apne-aap ghanton baaten kiya karti jisamen raamasevak ke maans, haddee, chamade, aankhen, kalejaa aadi ko khaane, masalane, nochane, khasotane kee badee utkat ichchhaa prakat kee jati thi aur jab usakee yah ichchhaa seemaa tak pahunch jati, to woh raamasevak ke ghar kee ora munh karake khoob chillaakar aur daraavane shabdon mein haank lagaatee, teraa lohoo peeoongee.

praayah raat ke sannaate mein yah garajatee huee aavaaj sunakar striyaan chaunk padatee theen. lekin is aavaaj se bhayaanak usakaa thathaakar hansanaa tha! munsheejee ke lahoo peene kee kalpit khushi mein woh jor se hansaa karti thi. is, thathaane se aisee aasurik uddandataa, aisee paashavik ugrataa tapakatee thi ki raat ko sunakar logon kaa khoon thandaa ho jat tha. maaloom hot, maano, saikadon ulloo ek saath hans rahe hain.
munshee raamasevak bade hausale aur kaleje ke aadamee the. na unhe deevaanee kaa dar tha na phaujadaaree kaa. lekin moongaa ke ina daraavane shabdon ko sunakar woh bhee saham jate. hamen manushy ke insaaf kaa dar na ho, lekin eeshvar ke insaaf kaa dar har manushy ke man mein kabhee-kabhee aisee hee bhaavanaa utpann kar detaa—unse zyaadaa unakee aurat ke man mein. unakee aurat badee hee hoshiyaar thi. woh unako ina sab baaton mein praayah salaah diya karti thi. una logon kee bhool thi, jo log kehte the ki munsheejee kee jeebh par sarasvatee viraajatee hain. woh gun to unakee aurat ko praapt tha. bolane mein woh utanee hee tej thi, jitanaa munsheejee likhane mein the aur yah donon aurat-aadamee praayah apanee avash dashaa mein salaah karte ki ab kya karn chaahie?

aadhee raat kaa samay tha. munsheejee nity niyam ke anusaar apanee phikr door karne ke lie sharaab ke do-chaar ghoont peekar so gae the. yakaayak moongaa ne unake daravaaje par aakar jor se haank lagaayee, ‘teraa lahoo peeoongee’ aur khoob khilakhilaakar hansee.
munsheejee yah bhayaavah thahaakaa sunakar chaunk pade. dar ke maare pair thar-thar kaanpane lage. kalejaa dhak-dhak karne lagaa dil par bahut jor daal kar unhonne daravaajaa kholaa, jaakar naagin ko jagaayaa. naagin ne jhunjhalaakar kahaa—kya hai; kya kehte ho?
munsheejee ne dabee aavaaj se kahaa—woh daravaaje par khadee hai. naagin uth baithee—kya kehti hai?
‘tumhaaraa sir. ’
‘kya daravaaje par aa gaee? ’
‘haan, aavaaj nahin sunatee ho. ’
naagin moongaa se nahin, lekin usake dhyaan se bahut daratee thi, to bhee use yakeen tha ki main bolane mein use zaroor neechaa dikhaa sakatee hoon. sanbhalakar bolee—kaho to main usse do-do baaten kar loon? lekin munsheejee ne manaa kiya.
donon aadamee pair dabaae dyodhee mein gaye aur daravaaje se jhaankakar dekh moongaa kee dhundhalee moorat dharatee par padee thi aur usakee saans tejee se chalatee huee sunaaee detee thi. raamasevak ke lahoo maans kee bhookh mein woh apanaa lahoo aur maans sukhaa chukee thi. ek bachchaa bhee use giraa sakataa tha. lekin usse saaraa gaanv thar-thar kaanpataa tha. ham jeete manushy se nahin darate, par murde se darate hain. raat gujaree. daravaajaa band tha, par munsheejee aur naagin ne baithakar raat kaatee, moongaa bheetar nahin ghus sakatee thi, par usakee aavaaj ko kaun rok sakataa tha moongaa se zyaadaa daraavanee usakee aavaaj thi.
bhor ko munsheejee baahar nikale aur moongaa se bole—yahan kyon padee hai?
moongaa bolee— teraa lahoo peeoongee.
naagin ne bal khaakar kahaa—teraa munh jhulas doongee.
par naagin ke vish ne moongaa par kuchh asar na kiya. usane jor se thahaakaa lagaayaa, naagin khisiyaanee-see ho gaee. hansee ke saamane munh band ho jat hai. munsheejee phir bole— yahan se uth jaa.
‘na uthoongee. ’
‘kab tak padee rahegee? ’
‘teraa lahoo peekar jaaoongee. ’
munsheejee kee prakhar lekhanee kaa yahan kuchh jor na chalaa aur naagin kee aag-bharee baaten yahan sard ho gaeen. donon ghar mein jaakar salaah karne lage, yah balaa kaise talegee? is aapatti se kaise chhutakaaraa hogaa?

devee aati hai to bakare kaa khoon peekar chalee jati hai, par yah daain manushy kaa khoon peene aayee hai. woh khoon, jisakaa agar ek boond bhee kalam banaane ke samay nikal padatee thi, to athavaaron aur maheenon saare kunabe ko aphasos rehta aur yah ghatanaa gaanv mein ghar-ghar phail jati. kya vahee lahoo peekar moongaa kaa sookhaa shareer haraa ho jaaegaa?
gaanv mein yah charchaa phail gaee, moongaa munsheejee ke daravaaje par dharanaa diye baithee hai. munsheejee ke aan mein gaanvavaalon ko badaa majaa aat tha. dekhate-dekhate saikadon aadamiyon kee bheed lag gaee. is daravaaje par kabhee-kabhee bheed lagee rehti thi. yah bheed raamagulaam ko pasand na thi. moongaa par use aisaa gussaa aa raha tha ki yadi usakaa vash chalataa, to woh ise kuen mein dhakel detaa. is tarah kaa soch uthate hee raamagulaam ke man mein gudagudee samaa gaee aur woh badee kathinataa se apanee hansee rok sakaa. ahaa! woh kuen mein giratee to kya maje kee baat hoti! lekin yah chudail yahan se talatee hee nahin kya karoon?
munsheejee ke ghar mein ek gaay thi, jise khaalee, daanaa aur bhoosaa to khoob khilaayaa jat, par woh sab usakee haddiyon mein mil jat, usakaa dhaanchaa pusht hot jat tha. raamagulaam ne usee gaay kaa gobar ek haandee mein gholaa aur sabakaa-sab bechaaree moongaa par undel diya. usake thode-bahut chheente darshakon par bhee daal diye. bechaaree moongaa ladaphad ho gaee aur log bhaag khade hue. kehne lage, yah munshee raamagulaam kaa daravaajaa hai. yahan isee tarah kaa shishtaachaar kiya jat hai. jald bhaag chalo, nahin to ab isase bhee badh kar khaatir kee jaayagee. idhar bheed kam huee, udhar raamagulaam ghar mein jaakar khoob hansaa aur khoob taaliyaan bajaayeen. munsheejee ne bekaar kee bheed ko aise sahaj mein aur aise sundar roop se hataa dene ke upaay par apne susheel ladake kee peeth thokee. sab log to champat ho gae, par bechaaree moongaa jyon-kee-tyon baithee raha gaee.

dopahar huee. moongaa ne kuchh nahin khaayaa. saanjh huee. hajaar kehne-sune se bhee khana nahin khaayaa. gaanv ke chaudharee ne badee khushaamad kee. yahan tak ki munsheejee ne haath tak jode, par devee khush na huee. nidaan munsheejee uthakar bheetar chale gae. woh kehte the ki roothane vaale ko bhookh aap hee manaa liya karti hai. moongaa ne yah raat bhee binaa daanaa-paani ke kaat dee. laalaajee aur lalaain ne aaj phir jaag-jaagakar bhor kiya. aaj moongaa kee garaj aur hansee bahut kam sunaaee padatee thi. gharavaalon ne samajhaa, balaa talee, saberaa hote hee jo daravaajaa kholakar dekh, to woh achet padee thi, munh par makkhiyaan bhinabhinaa rahi hain aur usake jaan-pakheroo uda chuke hain. woh is daravaaje par marane hee aayee thi. jisane usake jeevan kee jamaa-poonjee har lee thi, usee ko apanee jaan bhee saunp dee. apne shareer kee mittee tak usako bhent kar dee. paisaa se manushy ko kitanaa pyaar hot hai! paisaa apanee jaan se bhee zyada pyaaraa hot hai, visheshakar budhaape mein. rin chukaane ke din jyon-jyon paas aate jate hain, tyon-tyon usakaa byaaj badhataa jat hai.

yah kahanaa yahan bekaar hai ki gaanv mein is ghatanaa se kaisee halachal machee aur munshee raamasevak kaise apamaanit hue. ek chhote-se gaanv mein aisee asaadhaaran ghatanaa hone par jitanee halachal ho sakatee, usse zyaadaa hee huee. munsheejee kaa beizzatee jitanaa hona chaahie tha, usse baal baraabar bhee kam na hua. unakaa bachaa-khuchaa paani bhee is ghatanaa se chalaa gaya. ab gaanv kaa chamaar bhee unake haath kaa paani peene kaa, unhe chhoone kaa ravaadaar na tha. yadi kisee ghar se koi gaay khoonte par mar jati hai, to woh aadamee maheenon dvaar-dvaar bhee maangataa phirataa hai. na naaee usakee hajaamat banaave, na kahaar usakaa paani bhare, na koi use chhue. yah gohatyaa kaa prayaashchit tha. brahmahatyaa kaa sazaa to isase bhee kadaa hai aur isamen beizzatee bhee bahut hai. moongaa yah jaanatee thi aur iseelie is daravaaje par aakar maree thi. woh jaanatee thi main jeete-jee to kuchh nahin kar sakatee, marakar usse bahut kuchh kar sakatee hoon. gobar kaa upalaa jab jal kar khaak ho jat hai, , tab saadhu-sant use maathe par chadhaate hain; patthar kaa dhelaa aag mein jalaakar aag se zyaadaa teekhaa aur maarak hot hai.

munshee raamasevak kaanoonadaan the. kaanoon ne una par koi dosh nahin lagaayaa tha. moongaa kisee kaanoonee daphaa ke anusaar nahin maree thi. taajeeraat hind mein usakaa koi udaaharan nahin milataa tha. isalie jo log unse praayashchit karavaanaa chaahate the, unakee bhaaree bhool thi. kuchh harj nahin, kahaar paani na bhare, na sahee. woh paani bhar lenge. apanaa kaam aap karne mein bhalaa laaj hee kya? balaa se naaee baal na banaavegaa. hajaamat banaane kaa kaam hee kya hai? daadhee bahut sundar vastu hai. daadhee mard kee shobhaa aur singaar hai aur jo phir baalon se aisee ghin hogee, to ek-ek aane mein to asture milate hain. dhobee kapade na dhoegaa, isakee bhee kuchh paravaah nahin. saabun to galee-galee kaudiyon ke mol aat hai. ek battee saabun mein darjanon kapade aise saaph ho jate hain, jaise bagule ke par. dhobee kya khaakar aisaa saaph kapadaa dhoegaa? patthar par patak-patakar kapadon kaa lattaa nikaal letaa hai. aap pahane, doosaron ko bhaade par pahanaae, bhattee mein chadhaae, reh mein bhigoe! kapadon kee to durgati kar daalataa hai. jabhee to kurte do-teen saal se zyaadaa nahin chalate. nahin to daadaa har paanchaven baras do-teen achakan aur do kurate banavaayaa karte the. munshee raamasevak aur unakee aurat ne din-bhar to yon hee kahakar apne man ko samajhaayaa. saanjh hote hee unakee tarkanaaen shithil ho gaeen.

ab unake man par dar ne chadhaaee kee. jaise-taise raat beetatee thi, dar bhee badhataa jat tha. baahar kaa daravaajaa bhool se khulaa raha gaya tha, par kisee kee himmat na padatee thi ki jaakar band to kar aaye. nidaan naagin ne haath mein deeyaa liya. munsheejee ne kulhaadaa, raamagulaam ne gandaasaa, is dhang se teenon aadamee chaunkate-hichakate daravaaje par aaye. yahan munsheejee ne bahaaduree se kaam liya. unhonne nidhadak daravaaje se baahar nikalane kee koshish kee. kaanpate hue, par oonchee aavaaj mein naagin se bole—tum bekaar daratee ho, woh kya yahan baithee hai? par unakee pyaaree naagin ne unhe andar kheench liya aur jhunjhalaakar bolee—tumhaaraa yahee ladakapan to achchhaa nahin. yah dangal jeetakar teenon aadamee rasoee ke kamare mein aaye aur khana pakane lagaa.
lekin moongaa unakee aankhon mein ghusee huee thi. apanee parachhaaee ko dekhakar moongaa kaa dar hot tha. andhere kone mein moongaa baithee maaloom hoti thi. vahee haddiyon kaa dhaanchaa, vahee bikhare hue baal, vahee paagalapan, vahee daraavanee aankh, moongaa kaa nakhashikh dikhaaee detaa tha. isee kotharee mein aate daal ke kaee matake rakhe hue the, wahan kuchh puraane chithade bhee pade hue the. ek choohe ko bhookh ne bechain kiya (matakon ne kabhee anaaj kee soorat na dekhee thi; par saare gaanv mein mashahoor tha ki is ghar ke choohe gajab ke daakoo hain), to woh una daanon kee khoj mein, jo matakon se kabhee nahin gire the, rengataa hua is chithade ke neeche aa nikalaa. kapade mein khadakhadaahat huee. phaile hue chithade moongaa kee patalee taange ban gaeen, naagin dekhakar jhijhakee aur cheek uthee. munsheejee badahavaas hokar daravaaje kee ora lapake, raamagulaam daudakar unakee taange se lipat gaya. choohaa baahar nikal aay. use dekhakar ina logon ke hosh thikaane hue. ab munsheejee saahas karake matake kee ora chale. naagin ne kahaa—rehne bhee do, dekh lee tumhaaree maradaanagee.
munsheejee apanee priyaa naagin ke is anaadar par bahut bigade—kya tum samajhatee ho, main dar gaya? bhalaa, dar kee kya baat thi! moongaa mar gayee; kya woh baithee hai? main kal nahin daravaaje ke baahar nikal gaya tha. tum rokatee raheen main na maanaa.
munsheejee kee is daleel ne naagin ko niruttar kar diya. kal daravaaje ke baahar nikal jan yaa nikalane kee koshish karn aam kaam na tha. jisake saahas kaa aisaa pramaan mil chukaa ho, use darapok kaun kah sakataa hai? yah naagin kee hathadharmee thi.
khana khaakar teenon aadamee sone ke kamare mein aaye. lekin moongaa ne yahan bhee peechhaa na chhodaa. baaten karte the, dil ko bahalaate the, naagin ne raajaa haradaul aur raanee saarandhaa kee kahaaniyaan kaheen, munsheejee ne phaujadaaree ke kaee mukadamon kaa haal kah sunaayaa. lekin to bhee, ina upaayon se bhee moongaa kee moorti unakee aankhon ke saamane se na hatatee thi. zara khatakhataahat hoti tab teenon chaunk padate. udhar pattiyon mein sanasanaahat huee ki idhar teenon ke rongate khade ho gae. raha-hakar ek dheemee aavaaj dharatee ke bheetar se unake kaanon mein aati thi—‘teraa lahoo peeoongee’.

aadhee raat ko naagin neend se chaunk padee. woh ina dinon garbhavatee thi laal-laal aankhonvaalee, tej aur nukeele daantonvaalee moongaa usee kee chhaatee par baithee huee jaan padatee thi. naagin cheekh uthee. baavalee kee tarah aangan mein bhaag aayee aur yakaayak dharatee par chitt gir padee. saaraa shareer paseene-paseene ho gaya. munsheejee bhee usakee cheekh sunakar chaunke, par dar ke maare aankhen na khuleen. andhon kee tarah daravaajaa tatolate rahe. bahut der ke baad unhe daravaajaa milaa. aangan mein aaye naagin jameen par padee haath-paanv patak rahi thi. use uthaakar bheetar laaye, par raat-bhar usane aankhen na kholeen. bhor ko akabak bakane lagee. thodee der mein jvar ho aay. badan laal tavaa-saa ho gaya. saanj hote-hote sannipaat ho aay aur aadhee raat ke samay jab sansaar mein sannaataa chhaayaa hua tha, naagin is sansaar se chal basee. moongaa ke dar ne usakee jaan lee jab tak moongaa jeetee rahi, woh naagin kee phuphakaar se sadaa daratee rahi. pagalee hone par bhee usane kabhee naagin kaa saamanaa nahin kiya, par apanee jaan dekar usane aaj naagin kee jaan lee dar mein badee shakti hai. manushy havaa mein ek girah bhee nahin lagaa sakataa, par isane havaa mein ek sansaar rach daalaa hai.
raat beet gayee. din chadhataa aat tha, par gaanv kaa koi aadamee naagin kee laash uthaane ko aat na dikhaaee diya. munsheejee ghar-ghar ghoome par koi na nikalaa. bhalaa, hatyaare ke daravaaje par kaun jaae? hatyaare kee laash kaun uthaae? is samay munsheejee kaa robadaab, unakee prabal lekhanee kaa dar aur unakee kaanoonee pratibhaa ek bhee kaam na aayee. chaaron ora se haarakar munsheejee phir apne ghar aaye. yahan unhe andhakaar-hee-andhakaar deekhataa tha, daravaaje tak to aaye, par bheetar pair nahin rakhaa jat tha. na baahar hee khade raha sakate the. baahar moongaa thi, bheetar naagin. jee ko kadaa karake ‘hanumaan chaaleesaa’ kaa paath karte hue ghar mein ghuse. us samay unake man par jo beetatee thi, vahee jaanate the. unakaa anumaan karn mushkil hai. ghar mein laash padee huee; na koi aage, na peechhe. doosaraa byaah to ho sakataa tha. abhee isee phaagun mein to pachaasavaan lagaa hai. par aisee suyogy aur meethee boleevaalee aurat kahan milegee? aphasos! ab tagaadaa karne vaalon se bahas kaun karegaa, kaun unhe niruttar karegaa? len-den kaa hisaab-kitaab kaun itanee khoobee se karegaa? kisakee kadee aavaaj teer kee tarah tagaadedaaron kee chhaatee mein chubhegee? yah nukasaan ab pooraa nahin ho sakataa. doosare din munsheejee laash ko ek thelegaadee par laadakar gangaajee kee taraph chale.

shav ke saath jaane vaalon kee sankhyaa kuchh bhee na thi. ek svayan munsheejee, doosare unake putraratn raamagulaamajee! is beijjatee se moongaa kee laash bhee nahin uthee thi. moongaa ne naagin kee jaan lekar bhee munsheejee kaa pind na chhodaa. unake man mein har ghadee moongaa kee moorti viraajamaan rehti thi. kaheen rehte, unakaa dhyaan isee ora raha kart tha. yadi dil-bahalaav kaa koi upaay hot, to shaayad woh itane bechain na hote; par gaanv kaa ek putalee bhee unake daravaaje kee ora na jhaankataa tha. bechaare apne haathon paani bharate, aap hee baratan dhote. soch aur gussaa, phikr aur dar, itane shatruon ke saamane ek dimaag kab tak thahar sakataa? visheshakar woh dimaag, jo roj, -roj kaanoon kee bahason mein kharch ho jat tha.
akele kaidee kee tarah unake das-baarah din to jyon-tyon kar kate. chaudahaven din munsheejee ne kapade badale aur boriyaa-bastaa liye hue kachaharee chale. aaj unakaa cheharaa kuchh khilaa hua tha. jate hee mere muvakkil mujhe gher lenge. meree maatamapursee karenge. main aansuon kee do-chaar boonden giraa doongaa. phir bainaamon, rehanaamon aur sulahanaamon kee bharamaar ho jaaegee. mutthee garam hogee. shaam ko zara nashepaanee kaa rang jam jaaegaa, jisake chhoot jaane se jee aur bhee uchaat ho raha tha. inheen vichaaron mein magn munsheejee kachaharee pahunche.

par wahan rehanaamon kee bharamaar aur bainaamon kee baadh aur muvakkilon kee chahal-pahal ke badale maayoosee kee reteelee bhoomi najar aayee. bastaa khole ghanto baithe rahe, par koi najadeek bhee na aay. kisee ne itanaa bhee na poochhaa ki aap kaise hain? nae muvakkil to khair, bade-bade puraane muvakkil, jinakaa munsheejee se kaee peedhiyon se sarokaar tha, aaj unse munh chhipaane lage. woh naalaayak aur anaadee ramajaan, jisakee munsheejee hansee udaate the aur jise shuddh likhanaa bhee na aat tha, gopiyon mein kanhaiyaa banaa hua tha. vaah re bhaagy! muvakkil yon munh phere chale jate hain, maano kabhee kee jaan-pahachaan hee nahin. din-bhar kachaharee kee khaak chhaanane ke baad munsheejee apne ghar chale. maayoosee aur phikr mein doobe hue jyon-jyon ghar ke paas aate the, moongaa kaa chitr saamane aat jat tha. yahan tak ki jab ghar kaa dvaar kholaa aur do kutte, jinhen raamagulaam ne band kar rakhaa tha, jhatapat baahar nikale, to munsheejee ke hosh uda gae; ek cheekh maarakar jameen par gir pade.

manushy ke man aur dimaag par dar kaa jitanaa prabhaav hot hai, utanaa aur kisee shakti kaa nahin! pyaar, phikr, maayoosee, nukasaan yah sab man ko avashy dukhit karte hain; yah havaa ke halake jhonke hain aur dar prachand aandhee hai. munsheejee par isake baad kya beetee, maaloom nahin. kaee din tak logon ne unhe kachaharee jate aur wahan se murajhaae hue lautate dekh. kachaharee jan unakaa farz tha aur yadyapi wahan muvakkilon kaa akaal tha, to bhee tagaadevaalon se galaa chhudaane aur unako bharosaa dilaane ke lie ab yahee ek latakaa raha gaya tha. isake baad woh kaee maheene tak deekh na pade. badreenaath chale gaye.
ek din gaanv mein ek saadhu aay, bhabhoot ramaae, lambee-lambee jataaen, haath mein kamandal. isakaa cheharaa munshee raamasevak se bahut milataa-julataa tha. bolachaal bhee zyaadaa bhed na tha. woh ek ped ke neeche dhoonee ramaae baithaa raha. usee raat ko munshee raamasevak ke ghar dhuaan uthaa, phir aag kee jvaalaa deekhane lagee aur aag bhadak uthee. gaanv ke saikadon aadamee daude, aag bujhaane ke lie nahin, tamaashaa dekhane ke lie. ek gareeb kee haay mein kitanaa prabhaav hai! raamagulaam munsheejee ke gaayab ho jaane par apne maamaa ke yahan chal gaya aur wahan kuchh dinon raha, par wahan usakee chaal-dhaal kisee ko pasand na aayee.
ek din usane kisee ke khet mein moolee nochee. usane do-chaar dhaul lagaae. us par woh is tarah bigadaa ki jab usake chane khalihaan mein aaye, to usane aag lagaa dee. saaraa-kaa-saaraa khalihaan jalakar khaak ho gaya. hajaaron rupayon kaa nukasaan hua. pulis ne tahakeekaatakee, raamagulaam pakadaa gaya. isee jurm mein woh chunaar ke riphaarmetaree skool mein maujood hai
