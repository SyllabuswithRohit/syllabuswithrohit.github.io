# Atmaram

**Munshi Premchand**

1
vedon-graam mein mahaadev sonaar ek suvikhyaat aadamee tha| vah apne saayabaan mein praat: se shaam tak angeethee ke saamane baithaa hua khatakhat kiya kart tha| yah lagaataar dhvani sunane ke log itane abhyast ho gaye the ki jab kisee vajah se vah band ho jati, to jaan padataa tha, koee cheej gaayab ho gayee| vah nity-prati ek baar praat:kaal apne tote kaa pinjadaa lie koee bhajan gaataa hua taalaab kee ora jat tha| us dhandhale prakaash mein usakaa jarjar shareer, popalaa munh aur jhukee huee kamar dekhakar kisee aparichit manushy ko usake pishaach hone kaa bhram ho sakataa tha| jyon hee logon ke kaanon mein aavaaj aatee—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,’ log samajh jaate ki bhor ho gayee|
mahaadev kaa paarivaarik jeevan sookhamay na tha| usake teen betaa the, teen bahuen theen, darjanon naatee-paate the, lekin usake bojh ko halkaa karne-vaalaa koee na tha| ladake kehte—‘tab tak daadaa jeete hain, ham jeevan kaa khushee bhog le, phir to yah dhol gale padegee hee|’ bechaare mahaadev ko kabhee-kabhee niraahaar hee rahanaa padataa| khaanaa ke samay usake ghar mein saamyavaad kaa aisaa gaganabhedee nirghosh hot ki vah bhookhaa hee uth aat, aur naariyal kaa hukkaa peetaa hua so jat| unakaa vyaapasaayik jeevan aur bhee aashaantikaarak tha| yadyapi vah apne kaam mein nipun tha, usakee khataaee auron se kaheen jyaadaa shuddhikaarak aur usakee raasayanik kriyaaen kaheen jyaadaa kashtasaadhy theen, phir bhee use aaye din shakkee aur dhairy-shoony praaniyon ke apashabd sunane padate the, par mahaadev avichilit gaambheery se sir jhukaaye sab kuchh sunaa kart tha| jyon hee yah kalah shaant hot, vah apne tote kee ora dekhakar pukaar uthataa—‘satt gurudatt shivadattadaataa|’ is mantr ko japate hee usake chitt ko poorn shaanti praapt ho jati thi|
2
ek din sanyogavash kisee ladake ne pinjade kaa dvaar khol diya| totaa uda gaya| mahaadev ne sih uthaakar jo pinjade kee ora dekh, to usakaa kalejaa sann-se ho gaya| totaa kahan gaya| usane phir pinjade ko dekh, totaa gaayab tha| mahaadev ghabadaa kar uthaa aur idhar-udhar khaparailon par nigaah daudaane lagaa| use sansaar mein koee vastu agar pyaaree thi, to vah yahee totaa| ladake-baalon, naatee-poton se usakaa jee bhar gaya tha| ladako kee chulabul se usake kaam mein vighn padataa tha| beton se use pyaar na tha; isalie nahin ki ve nikamme the; balki isalie ki unake vajah vah apne aanandadaayee kulhadon kee niyamit sankhyaa se vanchit raha jat tha| padosiyon se use chidh thi, isalie ki ve angeethee se aag nikaal le jaate the| ina sab vighn-baadhaaon se usake lie koee panaah thi, to yahee totaa tha| isase use kisee tarah kaa takaleeph na hot tha| vah ab us avasthaa mein tha jab manushy ko shaanti bhog ke sivaa aur koee ichchhaa nahin rahatee|
totaa ek khaparail par baithaa tha| mahaadev ne pinjaraa utaar liya aur use dikhaakar kehne lagaa—‘aa aa’ satt gurudatt shivadaataa|’ lekin gaonva aur ghar ke ladake ekatr ho kar chillaane aur taaliyon bajaane lage| oopar se kauon ne kaonva-kaonva kee rat lagaayee? totaa udaa aur gaonva se baahar nikal kar ek ped par jaa baithaa| mahaadev khaalee pinjadaa liye usake peechhe daudaa, so daudaa| logo ko usakee drutigaamitaa par achambhaa ho raha tha| moh kee isase sundar, isase sajeev, isase bhaavamay kalpanaa nahin kee jaa sakatee|
dopahar ho gayee thi| kisaan log kheton se chale aa rahe the| unhen vinod kaa achchhaa avasar milaa| mahaadev ko chidhaane mein sabhee ko majaa aat tha| kisee ne kankad phenke, kisee ne taaliyon bajaayeen| totaa phir udaa aur wahan se door aam ke baag mein ek ped kee phunagee par jaa baithaa | mahaadev phir khaalee pinjadaa liye mendhak kee bhaonti uchakataa chalaa| baag mein pahunchaa to pair ke taluon se aag nikal rahi thi, sir chakkar khaa raha tha| jab jaraa saavadhaan hua, to phir pinjadaa uthaa kar kehne lage—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa’ totaa phunagee se utar kar neeche kee ek daal pee aa baithaa, lekin mahaadev kee ora sashank netron se taak raha tha| mahaadev ne samajhaa, dar raha hai| vah pinjade ko chhod kar aap ek doosare ped kee aad mein chhip gaya| tote ne chaaron ora gaur se dekh, nishshank ho gaya, ataraa aur aa kar pinjade ke oopar baith gaya| mahaadev kaa dil uchhalane lagaa| ‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa’ kaa mantr japataa hua dheere-dheere tote ke paas aay aur lapakaa ki tote ko pakad len, lekin totaa haath na aay, phir ped par aa baithaa|
shaam tak yahee haal raha| totaa kabhee is daal par jat, kabhee us daal par| kabhee pinjade par aa baithataa, kabhee pinjade ke dvaar par baithe apne daanaa-paanee kee pyaaliyon ko dekhataa, aur phir uda jat| buddhaa agar moortimaan moh tha, to totaa moortimayee maayaa| yahan tak ki shaam ho gayee| maayaa aur moh kaa yah sangraam andhakaar mein vileen ho gaya|
3
raat ho gayee ! chaaron ora nibid andhakaar chhaa gaya| totaa na jaane patton mein kahan chhipaa baithaa tha| mahaadev jaanataa tha ki raat ko totaa kahee udakar nahin jaa sakataa, aur na pinjade hee mein aa sakataa hain, phir bhee vah us jagah se hilane kaa naam na letaa tha| aaj usane din bhar kuchh nahin khaayaa| raat ke khaanaa kaa samay bhee nikal gaya, paanee kee boond bhee usake galaa mein na gayee, lekin use na bhookh thi, na pyaas ! tote ke binaa use apanaa jeevan nissaar, shushk aur soonaa jaan padataa tha| vah din-raat kaam kart tha; isalie ki yah usakee ant:preranaa thi; jeevan ke aur kaam isalie kart tha ki aadat thi| ina kaamon me use apanee sajeevataa kaa lesh-maatr bhee jnaan na hot tha| totaa hee vah vastu tha, jo use chetanaa kee yaad dilaataa tha| usakaa haath se jan jeev kaa deh-tyaag karn tha|
mahaadev din-bhar kaa bhookh-pyaasaa, thakaa-maondaa, raha-raha kar jhapakiyon le letaa tha; lekin ek pal mein phir chaunk kar onkhe khol detaa aur us vistrit andhakaar mein usakee aavaaj sunaayee detee—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa|’
aadhee raat gujar gayee thi| sahasaa vah koee aahat paa kar chaukaa| dekh, ek doosare vriksh ke neeche ek dhundhalaa deepak jal raha hai, aur kaee aadamee bainthe hue aapas mein kuchh baaten kar rahe hain| ve sab chilam pee rahe the| tamaakhoo kee mahak ne use adheer kar diya| uchch aavaaz se bol—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa’ aur una aadamiyon kee ora chilam peene chalaa gaya; lekin jis tarah bandook kee aavaaj sunate hee hiran bhaag jaate hain usee tarah use aate dekh sab-ke-sab uth kar bhaage| koee idhar gaya, koee udhar| mahaadev chillaane lagaa—‘thaharo-thaharo !’ ekaaek use dhyaan aa gaya, ye sab chor hain| vah jaare se chillaa uthaa—‘chor-chor, pakado-pakado !’ choron ne peechhe phir kar na dekh|
mahaadev deepak ke paas gaya, to use ek malasaa rakhaa hua milaa jo morche se kaalaa ho raha tha| mahaadev kaa dil uchhalane lagaa| usane kalase me haath daalaa, to moharen theen| usane ek mohare baahar nikaalee aur deepak ke ujaale mein dekh| haan mohar thi| usane turant kalasaa uthaa liya, aur deepak bujhaa diya aur ped ke neeche chhip kar baith raha| saah se chor ban gaya|
use phir shankaa huee, aisaa na ho, chor laut aaven, aur mujhe akelaa dekh kar moharen chheen len| usane kuchh mohar kamar mein baondhee, phir ek sookhee lakadee se jameen kee kee mitatee hataa kar kaee gaddhe banaaye, unhen maaharon se bhar kar mitatee se dhank diya|
4
mahaadev ke atarnetron ke saamane ab ek doosaraa jagat tha, chintaaon aur kalpanaa se paripoorn| yadyapi abhee kosh ke haath se nikal jaane kaa dar tha; par abhilaashaaon ne apanaa kaam shuru kar diya| ek pakkaa makaan ban gaya, saraaphe kee ek bhaaree dookaan khul gayee, nij sambandhiyon se phir naataa jud gaya, vilaas kee saamagriyon ekatrit ho gayeen| tab teerth-yaatraa karne chale, aur wahan se laut kar bade samaaroh se yajn, brahmabhoj hua| isake baad ek shivaalay aur kuon ban gaya, ek baag bhee lag gaya aur vah nityaprati kathaa-puraan sunane lagaa| saadhu-santon kaa aadar-satkaar hone lagaa|
akasmaat use dhyaan aay, kaheen chor aa jaayan , to main bhaagoongaa kyon-kar? usane pareekshaa karne ke lie kalasaa uthaayaa| aur do sau pag tak betahaashaa bhaagaa hua chalaa gaya| jaan padataa tha, usake pairo mein par lag gaye hain| phikr shaant ho gayee| inheen kalpanaaon mein raat vyateet ho gayee| ushaa kaa aan hua, havaa jaagee, chidiyon gaane lageen| sahasaa mahaadev ke kaanon mein aavaaj aayee—
‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,
raam ke charan mein chitt lagaa|’
yah bol hameshaa mahaadev kee jihvaa par rahataa tha| din mein sahasron hee baar ye shabd usake munh se nikalate the, par unakaa dhaarmik bhaav kabhee bhee usake ant:vajah ko sparsh na kart tha| jaise kisee baaje se raag nikalataa hain, usee tarah usake munh se yah bol nikalataa tha| bekaar aur prabhaav-shoony| tab usakaa dil-rupee vriksh patr-pallav viheen tha| yah nirmal vaayu use gunjarit na kar sakatee thi; par ab us vriksh mein kopalen aur shaakhaaen nikal aayee theen| ina vaayu-pravaah se jhoom uthaa, gunjit ho gaya|
arunoday kaa samay tha| prakriti ek anuraagamay prakaash mein doobee huee thi| usee samay totaa pairon ko jode hue oonchee daal se utaraa, jaise aakaash se koee taaraa toote aur aa kar pinjade mein baith gaya| mahaadev praphullit ho kar daudaa aur pinjade ko uthaa kar bol—aao aatmaaraam tumane takaleeph to bahut diya, par meraa jeevan bhee kaamayaab kar diya| ab tumhen chaondee ke pinjade mein rakhoongaa aur sone se madh doongaa|’ usake rom-rom ke paramaatmaa ke gunaanuvaad kee dhvani nikalane lagee| prabhu tum kitane dayaavaan ho ! yah tumhaaraa aseem vaatsaly hai, nahin to mujh paapee, patit praanee kab is kripaa ke yogy tha ! is pavitr bhaavon se aatmaa vinhal ho gayee ! vah anurakt ho kar kah uthaa—
‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,
raam ke charan mein chitt laagaa|’
usane ek haath mein pinjadaa latakaayaa, bagal mein kalasaa dabaayaa aur ghar chalaa|
5
mahaadev ghar pahunchaa, to abhee kuchh andheraa tha| raaste mein ek kutte ke sivaa aur kisee se bhent na huee, aur kutte ko moharon se khaas pyaar nahin hot| usane kalase ko ek naad mein chhipaa diya, aur koyale se achchhee tarah dhank kar apanee kotharee mein rakh aay| jab din nikal aay to vah seedhe puraahit ke ghar pahunchaa| purohit poojaa par baithe soch rahe the—kal hee mukadamen kee peshee hain aur abhee tak haath mein kaudee bhee nahin—yajamaano mein koee saonsa bhee letaa| itane mein mahaadev ne paalaagan kee| pandit jee ne munh pher liya| yah amangalamoorti kahan se aa pahunchee, maalamoo nahin, daanaa bhee mayassar hogaa yaa nahin| rusht ho kar poochhaa—kyaa hai jee, kyaa kehte ho| jaanate nahin, ham is samay poojaa par rahate hain|
mahaadev ne kahaa—mahaaraaj, aaj mere yahan satyanaaraan kee kathaa hai|
purohit jee vismit ho gaye| kaanon par yakeen na hua| mahaadev ke ghar kathaa kaa honaa utanee hee asaadhaaran ghatanaa thi, jitanee apne ghar se kisee bhikhaaree ke lie bheekh nikaalanaa| poochhaa—aaj kyaa hai?
mahaadev bol—kuchh nahin, aisaa ichchhaa huee ki aaj bhagavaan kee kathaa sun loon|
prabhaat hee se taiyaaree hone lagee| vedon ke nikatavartee gaonvo mein soopaaree phiree| kathaa ke uparaant bhoj kaa bhee nevataa tha| jo sunataa hairaanee kart aaj ret mein doob kaise jamee|
shaam samay jab sab log jamaa ho, aur pandit jee apne sinhaasan par viraajamaan hue, to mahaadev khadaa hokar uchch aavaaz mein bol—bhaaiyon meree saaree umr chhal-kapat mein kat gayee| mainne na jaane kitane aadamiyon ko dagaa dee, kitane khare ko khotaa kiya; par ab bhagavaan ne mujh par dayaa kee hai, vah mere munh kee kaalikh ko mitaanaa chaahate hain| main aap sab bhaaiyon se lalakaar kar keht hoon ki jisakaa mere jimme jo kuchh nikalataa ho, jisakee jamaa mainne maar lee ho, jisake chokhe maal kaa khotaa kar diya ho, vah aakar apanee ek-ek kaudee chukaa le, agar koee yahan na aa sakaa ho, to aap log usse jaakar kah deejie, kal se ek maheene tak, jab jee chaahe, aaye aur apanaa hisaab chukataa kar le| gavaahee-saakhee kaa kaam nahin|
sab log sannaate mein aa gaye| koee maarmik bhaav se sir hilaa kar bol—ham kehte na the| kisee ne avishvaas se kahaa—kyaa khaa kar bharegaa, hajaaron ko total ho jaayagaa|
ek thaakur ne thatholee kee—aur jo log suradhaam chale gaye|
mahaadev ne javaab diya—usake ghar vaale to honge|
lekin is samay logon ko vasoolee kee itanee ichchhaa na thi, jitanee yah jaanane kee ki ise itanaa paisaa mil kahan se
gaya| kisee ko mahaadev ke paas aane kaa saahas na hua| dehaat ke aadamee the, gade murde ukhaadanaa kyaa jaanen| phir praay: logon ko yaad bhee na tha ki unhen mahaadev se kyaa paanaa hain, aur aise pavitr avasar par bhool-chook ho jaane kaa dar unakaa munh band kiye hue tha| sabase badee baat yah thi ki mahaadev kee saadhutaa ne unheen vasheebhoot kar liya tha|
achaanak purohit jee bole—tumhen yaad hain, mainne ek kanthaa banaane ke lie sonaa diya tha, tumane kaee maashe taul mein udaa diye the|
mahaadev—haan, yaad hain, aapakaa kitanaa nukasaan hua hog|
purohit—pachaas rupaye se kam na hogaa|
mahaadev ne kamar se do moharen nikaaleen aur purohit jee ke saamane rakh deen|
purohitajee kee lolupataa par teekaaen hone lageen| yah beeemaanee hain, bahut ho, to do-chaar rupaye kaa nukasaan hua hogaa| bechaare se pachaas rupaye ainth lie| naaraayan kaa bhee dar nahin| banane ko pandit, par niyat aisee kharaab raam-raam !
logon ko mahaadev par ek shraddhaa-see ho gaee| ek ghantaa beet gaya par una sahasron manushyon mein se ek bhee khadaa na hua| tab mahaadev ne phir kahan—maaloom hot hai, aap log apanaa-apanaa hisaab bhool gaye hain, isalie aaj kathaa hone deejie| main ek maheene tak aapakee raah dekhoongaa| isake peechhe teerth yaatraa karne chalaa jaaoongaa| aap sab bhaaiyon se meree vinatee hai ki aap meraa uddhaar karen|
ek maheene tak mahaadev lenadaaron kee raah dekhataa raha| raat ko chonro ke dar se neend na aatee| ab vah koee kaam na kart| sharaab kaa chasakaa bhee chhootaa| saadhu-abhyaagat jo dvaar par aa jaate, unakaa yathaayogy satkaar kart| door-door usakaa suyash phail gaya| yahan tak ki maheenaa pooraa ho gaya aur ek aadamee bhee hisaab lene na aay| ab mahaadev ko jnaan hua ki sansaar mein kitanaa dharm, kitanaa sadvyavahaar hain| ab use maaloom hua ki sansaar buron ke lie buraa hain aur achchhe ke lie achchhaa|
6
is ghatanaa ko hue pachaas varsh beet chuke hain| aap vedon jaaiye, to door hee se ek sunahalaa kalas dikhaayee detaa hai| vah thaakuradvaare kaa kalas hai| usse milaa hua ek pakkaa taalaab hain, jisamen khoob kamal khile rahate hain| usakee machhaliyon koee nahin pakadataa; taalaab ke kinaare ek vishaal samaadhi hai| yahee aatmaaraam kaa smriti-chinh hai, usake sambandh mein vibhinn kinvadantiyon prachalit hai| koee keht hain, vah ratnajatit pinjadaa svarg ko chalaa gaya, koee keht, vah ‘satt gurudatt’ keht hua antardhyaan ho gaya, par yarthaath yah hain ki us pakshee-rupee chandr ko kisee billee-rupee raahu ne gras liya| log kehte hain, aadhee raat ko abhee tak taalaab ke kinaare aavaaj aatee hai—
‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,
raam ke charan mein chitt laagaa|’
mahaadev ke vishay mein bhee kitanee hee jan-shrutiyon hai| unamen sabase maany yah hai ki aatmaaraam ke samaadhisth hone ke baad vah kaee sannyaasiyon ke saath himaalay chalaa gaya, aur wahan se laut kar na aay| usakaa naam aatmaaraam prasiddh ho gaya|
