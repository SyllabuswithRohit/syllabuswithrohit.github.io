# Atmaram

**Munshi Premchand**

1
vedon-graam mein mahaadev sonaar ek suvikhyaat aadamee tha. vah apne saayabaan mein praat: se shaam tak angeethee ke saamane baithaa hua khatakhat kiya kart tha. yah lagaataar dhvani sunane ke log itane abhyast ho gaye the ki jab kisee vajah se vah band ho jati, to jaan padataa tha, koi cheej gaayab ho gayi. vah nity-prati ek baar praat:kaal apne tote kaa pinjadaa lie koi bhajan gaataa hua taalaab ki ora jat tha. us dhandhale prakaash mein uska jarjar shareer, popalaa munh aur jhukee hui kamar dekhakar kisee aparichit manushy ko usake pishaach hone kaa bhram ho sakataa tha. jyon hi logon ke kaanon mein aavaaj aatee—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,’ log samajh jaate ki bhor ho gayi.
mahaadev kaa paarivaarik jeevan sookhamay na tha. usake teen betaa the, teen bahuen theen, darjanon naatee-paate the, lekin usake bojh ko halkaa karne-vaalaa koi na tha. ladake kehte—‘tab tak daadaa jeete hain, ham jeevan kaa khushi bhog le, phir to yah dhol gale padegee hi.’ bechaare mahaadev ko kabhi-kabhi niraahaar hi rehna padataa. khaanaa ke samay usake ghar mein saamyavaad kaa aisaa gaganabhedee nirghosh hot ki vah bhookhaa hi uth aat, aur naariyal kaa hukkaa peetaa hua so jat. unakaa vyaapasaayik jeevan aur bhi aashaantikaarak tha. yadyapi vah apne kaam mein nipun tha, uski khataaee auron se kaheen zyada shuddhikaarak aur uski raasayanik kriyaaen kaheen zyada kashtasaadhy theen, phir bhi use aaye din shakkee aur dhairy-shoony praaniyon ke apashabd sunane padate the, par mahaadev avichilit gaambheery se sir jhukaaye sab kuchh sunaa kart tha. jyon hi yah kalah shaant hot, vah apne tote ki ora dekhakar pukaar uthta—‘satt gurudatt shivadattadaataa.’ is mantr ko japate hi usake chitt ko poorn shaanti praapt ho jati thi.
2
ek din sanyogavash kisee ladake ne pinjade kaa dvaar khol diya. totaa uda gaya. mahaadev ne sih uthaakar jo pinjade ki ora dekh, to uska kalejaa sann-se ho gaya. totaa kahan gaya. usane phir pinjade ko dekh, totaa gaayab tha. mahaadev ghabadaa kar uthaa aur idhar-udhar khaparailon par nigaah daudaane lagaa. use sansaar mein koi vastu agar pyaaree thi, to vah yahi totaa. ladake-baalon, naatee-poton se uska jee bhar gaya tha. ladako ki chulabul se usake kaam mein vighn padataa tha. beton se use pyaar na tha; isalie nahin ki ve nikamme the; balki isalie ki unake vajah vah apne aanandadaayee kulhadon ki niyamit sankhyaa se vanchit raha jat tha. padosiyon se use chidh thi, isalie ki ve angeethee se aag nikaal le jaate the. ina sab vighn-baadhaaon se usake lie koi panaah thi, to yahi totaa tha. isase use kisee tarah kaa takaleeph na hot tha. vah ab us avasthaa mein tha jab manushy ko shaanti bhog ke sivaa aur koi ichchhaa nahin rehti.
totaa ek khaparail par baithaa tha. mahaadev ne pinjaraa utaar liya aur use dikhaakar kehne lagaa—‘aa aa’ satt gurudatt shivadaataa.’ lekin gaonva aur ghar ke ladake ekatr ho kar chillaane aur taaliyon bajaane lage. oopar se kauon ne kaonva-kaonva ki rat lagaayee? totaa udaa aur gaonva se baahar nikal kar ek ped par jaa baithaa. mahaadev khaalee pinjadaa liye usake peechhe daudaa, so daudaa. logo ko uski drutigaamitaa par achambhaa ho raha tha. moh ki isase sundar, isase sajeev, isase bhaavamay kalpanaa nahin ki jaa sakatee.
dopahar ho gayi thi. kisaan log kheton se chale aa rahe the. unhen vinod kaa achchha avasar milaa. mahaadev ko chidhaane mein sabhi ko majaa aat tha. kisee ne kankad phenke, kisee ne taaliyon bajaayeen. totaa phir udaa aur wahan se door aam ke baag mein ek ped ki phunagee par jaa baithaa. mahaadev phir khaalee pinjadaa liye mendhak ki bhaonti uchakataa chalaa. baag mein pahunchaa to pair ke taluon se aag nikal rahi thi, sir chakkar khaa raha tha. jab zara saavadhaan hua, to phir pinjadaa uthaa kar kehne lage—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa’ totaa phunagee se utar kar neeche ki ek daal pee aa baithaa, lekin mahaadev ki ora sashank netron se taak raha tha. mahaadev ne samajhaa, dar raha hai. vah pinjade ko chhod kar aap ek doosare ped ki aad mein chhip gaya. tote ne chaaron ora gaur se dekh, nishshank ho gaya, ataraa aur aa kar pinjade ke oopar baith gaya. mahaadev kaa dil uchhalane lagaa. ‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa’ kaa mantr japataa hua dheere-dheere tote ke paas aay aur lapakaa ki tote ko pakad len, lekin totaa haath na aay, phir ped par aa baithaa.
shaam tak yahi haal raha. totaa kabhi is daal par jat, kabhi us daal par. kabhi pinjade par aa baithta, kabhi pinjade ke dvaar par baithe apne daanaa-paani ki pyaaliyon ko dekhta, aur phir uda jat. buddhaa agar moortimaan moh tha, to totaa moortimayee maayaa. yahan tak ki shaam ho gayi. maayaa aur moh kaa yah sangraam andhakaar mein vileen ho gaya.
3
raat ho gayi! chaaron ora nibid andhakaar chhaa gaya. totaa na jaane patton mein kahan chhipaa baithaa tha. mahaadev jaanataa tha ki raat ko totaa kahin udakar nahin jaa sakataa, aur na pinjade hi mein aa sakataa hain, phir bhi vah us jagah se hilane kaa naam na letaa tha. aaj usane din bhar kuchh nahin khaayaa. raat ke khaanaa kaa samay bhi nikal gaya, paani ki boond bhi usake galaa mein na gayi, lekin use na bhookh thi, na pyaas! tote ke binaa use apna jeevan nissaar, shushk aur soonaa jaan padataa tha. vah din-raat kaam kart tha; isalie ki yah uski ant:preranaa thi; jeevan ke aur kaam isalie kart tha ki aadat thi. ina kaamon me use apni sajeevataa kaa lesh-maatr bhi jnaan na hot tha. totaa hi vah vastu tha, jo use chetanaa ki yaad dilaataa tha. uska haath se jan jeev kaa deh-tyaag karn tha.
mahaadev din-bhar kaa bhookh-pyaasaa, thakaa-maondaa, raha-raha kar jhapakiyon le letaa tha; lekin ek pal mein phir chaunk kar onkhe khol detaa aur us vistrit andhakaar mein uski aavaaj sunaayee detee—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa.’
aadhee raat gujar gayi thi. sahasaa vah koi aahat paa kar chaukaa. dekh, ek doosare vriksh ke neeche ek dhundhalaa deepak jal raha hai, aur kaee aadamee bainthe hue aapas mein kuchh baaten kar rahe hain. ve sab chilam pee rahe the. tamaakhoo ki mahak ne use adheer kar diya. uchch aavaaz se bol—‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa’ aur una aadamiyon ki ora chilam peene chalaa gaya; lekin jis tarah bandook ki aavaaj sunate hi hiran bhaag jaate hain usi tarah use aate dekh sab-ke-sab uth kar bhaage. koi idhar gaya, koi udhar. mahaadev chillaane lagaa—‘thaharo-thaharo!’ ekaaek use dhyaan aa gaya, ye sab chor hain. vah jaare se chillaa uthaa—‘chor-chor, pakado-pakado!’ choron ne peechhe phir kar na dekh.
mahaadev deepak ke paas gaya, to use ek malasaa rakhaa hua milaa jo morche se kaalaa ho raha tha. mahaadev kaa dil uchhalane lagaa. usane kalase me haath daalaa, to moharen theen. usane ek mohare baahar nikaalee aur deepak ke ujaale mein dekh. haan mohar thi. usane turant kalasaa uthaa liya, aur deepak bujhaa diya aur ped ke neeche chhip kar baith raha. saah se chor ban gaya.
use phir shankaa hui, aisaa na ho, chor laut aaven, aur mujhe akelaa dekh kar moharen chheen len. usane kuchh mohar kamar mein baondhee, phir ek sookhee lakadee se jameen ki ki mitatee hataa kar kaee gaddhe banaaye, unhen maaharon se bhar kar mitatee se dhank diya.
4
mahaadev ke atarnetron ke saamane ab ek doosaraa jagat tha, chintaaon aur kalpanaa se paripoorn. yadyapi abhi kosh ke haath se nikal jaane kaa dar tha; par abhilaashaaon ne apna kaam shuru kar diya. ek pakkaa makaan ban gaya, saraaphe ki ek bhaaree dookaan khul gayi, nij sambandhiyon se phir naataa jud gaya, vilaas ki saamagriyon ekatrit ho gayeen. tab teerth-yaatraa karne chale, aur wahan se laut kar bade samaaroh se yajn, brahmabhoj hua. isake baad ek shivaalay aur kuon ban gaya, ek baag bhi lag gaya aur vah nityaprati kathaa-puraan sunane lagaa. saadhu-santon kaa aadar-satkaar hone lagaa.
akasmaat use dhyaan aay, kaheen chor aa jaayan, to main bhaagoongaa kyon-kar? usane pareekshaa karne ke lie kalasaa uthaayaa. aur do sau pag tak betahaashaa bhaagaa hua chalaa gaya. jaan padataa tha, usake pairo mein par lag gaye hain. phikr shaant ho gayi. inheen kalpanaaon mein raat vyateet ho gayi. ushaa kaa aan hua, havaa jaagee, chidiyon gaane lageen. sahasaa mahaadev ke kaanon mein aavaaj aayee—
‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,
raam ke charan mein chitt lagaa.’
yah bol hameshaa mahaadev ki jihvaa par rehta tha. din mein sahasron hi baar ye shabd usake munh se nikalate the, par unakaa dhaarmik bhaav kabhi bhi usake ant:vajah ko sparsh na kart tha. jaise kisee baaje se raag nikalataa hain, usi tarah usake munh se yah bol nikalataa tha. bekaar aur prabhaav-shoony. tab uska dil-rupee vriksh patr-pallav viheen tha. yah nirmal vaayu use gunjarit na kar sakatee thi; par ab us vriksh mein kopalen aur shaakhaaen nikal aayee theen. ina vaayu-pravaah se jhoom uthaa, gunjit ho gaya.
arunoday kaa samay tha. prakriti ek anuraagamay prakaash mein doobee hui thi. usi samay totaa pairon ko jode hue oonchee daal se utaraa, jaise aakaash se koi taaraa toote aur aa kar pinjade mein baith gaya. mahaadev praphullit ho kar daudaa aur pinjade ko uthaa kar bol—aao aatmaaraam tumne takaleeph to bahut diya, par mera jeevan bhi kaamayaab kar diya. ab tumhen chaondee ke pinjade mein rakhoongaa aur sone se madh doongaa.’ usake rom-rom ke paramaatmaa ke gunaanuvaad ki dhvani nikalne lagee. prabhu tum kitane dayaavaan ho! yah tumhara aseem vaatsaly hai, nahin to mujh paapee, patit praanee kab is kripaa ke yogy tha! is pavitr bhaavon se aatmaa vinhal ho gayi! vah anurakt ho kar kah uthaa—
‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,
raam ke charan mein chitt laagaa.’
usane ek haath mein pinjadaa latakaayaa, bagal mein kalasaa dabaayaa aur ghar chalaa.
5
mahaadev ghar pahunchaa, to abhi kuchh andheraa tha. raaste mein ek kutte ke sivaa aur kisee se bhent na hui, aur kutte ko moharon se khaas pyaar nahin hot. usane kalase ko ek naad mein chhipaa diya, aur koyale se achchhi tarah dhank kar apni kotharee mein rakh aay. jab din nikal aay to vah seedhe puraahit ke ghar pahunchaa. purohit poojaa par baithe soch rahe the—kal hi mukadamen ki peshee hain aur abhi tak haath mein kaudee bhi nahin—yajamaano mein koi saonsa bhi letaa. itane mein mahaadev ne paalaagan ki. pandit jee ne munh pher liya. yah amangalamoorti kahan se aa pahunchee, maalamoo nahin, daanaa bhi mayassar hogaa yaa nahin. rusht ho kar poochhaa—kya hai jee, kya kehte ho. jaanate nahin, ham is samay poojaa par rehte hain.
mahaadev ne kahaa—mahaaraaj, aaj mere yahan satyanaaraan ki kathaa hai.
purohit jee vismit ho gaye. kaanon par yakeen na hua. mahaadev ke ghar kathaa kaa hona utanee hi asaadhaaran ghatanaa thi, jitanee apne ghar se kisee bhikhaaree ke lie bheekh nikaalanaa. poochhaa—aaj kya hai?
mahaadev bol—kuchh nahin, aisaa ichchhaa hui ki aaj bhagavaan ki kathaa sun loon.
prabhaat hi se taiyaaree hone lagee. vedon ke nikatavartee gaonvo mein soopaaree phiree. kathaa ke uparaant bhoj kaa bhi nevataa tha. jo sunataa hairaanee kart aaj ret mein doob kaise jamee.
shaam samay jab sab log jamaa ho, aur pandit jee apne sinhaasan par viraajamaan hue, to mahaadev khadaa hokar uchch aavaaz mein bol—bhaaiyon meri sari umr chhal-kapat mein kat gayi. maine na jaane kitane aadamiyon ko dagaa dee, kitane khare ko khotaa kiya; par ab bhagavaan ne mujh par dayaa ki hai, vah mere munh ki kaalikh ko mitaanaa chahte hain. main aap sab bhaaiyon se lalakaar kar keht hoon ki jisakaa mere jimme jo kuchh nikalataa ho, jisakee jamaa maine maar lee ho, jisake chokhe maal kaa khotaa kar diya ho, vah aakar apni ek-ek kaudee chukaa le, agar koi yahan na aa sakaa ho, to aap log usse jaakar kah deejie, kal se ek mahine tak, jab jee chaahe, aaye aur apna hisaab chukataa kar le. gavaahee-saakhee kaa kaam nahin.
sab log sannaate mein aa gaye. koi maarmik bhaav se sir hilaa kar bol—ham kehte na the. kisee ne avishvaas se kahaa—kya khaa kar bharegaa, hajaaron ko total ho jaayagaa.
ek thaakur ne thatholee ki—aur jo log suradhaam chale gaye.
mahaadev ne jawab diya—usake ghar vaale to honge.
lekin is samay logon ko vasoolee ki itanee ichchhaa na thi, jitanee yah jaanane ki ki ise itanaa paisaa mil kahan se
gaya. kisee ko mahaadev ke paas aane kaa saahas na hua. dehaat ke aadamee the, gade murde ukhaadanaa kya jaanen. phir praay: logon ko yaad bhi na tha ki unhen mahaadev se kya paanaa hain, aur aise pavitr avasar par bhool-chook ho jaane kaa dar unakaa munh band kiye hue tha. sabase badi baat yah thi ki mahaadev ki saadhutaa ne unheen vasheebhoot kar liya tha.
achaanak purohit jee bole—tumhen yaad hain, maine ek kanthaa banaane ke lie sonaa diya tha, tumne kaee maashe taul mein udaa diye the.
mahaadev—haan, yaad hain, aapakaa kitanaa nukasaan hua hog.
purohit—pachaas rupaye se kam na hogaa.
mahaadev ne kamar se do moharen nikaaleen aur purohit jee ke saamane rakh deen.
purohitajee ki lolupataa par teekaaen hone lageen. yah beeemaanee hain, bahut ho, to do-chaar rupaye kaa nukasaan hua hogaa. bechaare se pachaas rupaye ainth lie. naaraayan kaa bhi dar nahin. banane ko pandit, par niyat aisee kharaab raam-raam!
logon ko mahaadev par ek shraddhaa-see ho gayi. ek ghanta beet gaya par una sahasron manushyon mein se ek bhi khadaa na hua. tab mahaadev ne phir kahan—maaloom hot hai, aap log apna-apna hisaab bhool gaye hain, isalie aaj kathaa hone deejie. main ek mahine tak aapakee raah dekhoongaa. isake peechhe teerth yaatraa karne chalaa jaaoongaa. aap sab bhaaiyon se meri vinatee hai ki aap mera uddhaar karen.
ek mahine tak mahaadev lenadaaron ki raah dekhta raha. raat ko chonro ke dar se neend na aatee. ab vah koi kaam na kart. sharaab kaa chasakaa bhi chhootaa. saadhu-abhyaagat jo dvaar par aa jaate, unakaa yathaayogy satkaar kart. door-door uska suyash phail gaya. yahan tak ki mahina pooraa ho gaya aur ek aadamee bhi hisaab lene na aay. ab mahaadev ko jnaan hua ki sansaar mein kitanaa dharm, kitanaa sadvyavahaar hain. ab use maaloom hua ki sansaar buron ke lie buraa hain aur achchhe ke lie achchha.
6
is ghatanaa ko hue pachaas varsh beet chuke hain. aap vedon jaaiye, to door hi se ek sunahalaa kalas dikhaayee detaa hai. vah thaakuradvaare kaa kalas hai. usse milaa hua ek pakkaa taalaab hain, jisamen khoob kamal khile rehte hain. uski machhaliyon koi nahin pakadataa; taalaab ke kinaare ek vishaal samaadhi hai. yahi aatmaaraam kaa smriti-chinh hai, usake sambandh mein vibhinn kinvadantiyon prachalit hai. koi keht hain, vah ratnajatit pinjadaa svarg ko chalaa gaya, koi keht, vah ‘satt gurudatt’ keht hua antardhyaan ho gaya, par yarthaath yah hain ki us pakshee-rupee chandr ko kisee billee-rupee raahu ne gras liya. log kehte hain, aadhee raat ko abhi tak taalaab ke kinaare aavaaj aatee hai—
‘satt gurudatt shivadatt daataa,
raam ke charan mein chitt laagaa.’
mahaadev ke vishay mein bhi kitanee hi jan-shrutiyon hai. unamen sabase maany yah hai ki aatmaaraam ke samaadhisth hone ke baad vah kaee sannyaasiyon ke saath himaalay chalaa gaya, aur wahan se laut kar na aay. uska naam aatmaaraam prasiddh ho gaya.
