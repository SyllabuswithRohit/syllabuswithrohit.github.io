# Tagada

**Munshi Premchand**

seth chetaraam ne snaan kiya, shivajee ko jal chadhaayaa, do daane mirch chabaaye, do lote paani piyaa aur sotaa lekar tagaade par chale. sethajee kee umr koi pachaas kee thi. sir ke baal jhad gaye the aur khopadee aisee saaph-sutharee nikal aaee thi, jaise oosar khet. aapakee aankhen theen to chhotee lekin bilakul gol. chehare ke neeche pet tha aur pet ke neeche taangen, maano kisee peepe mein do mekhen gaad dee gaee hon. lekin, yah khaalee peepaa na tha. isamen sajeevataa aur karmasheelataa koot-kootakar bharee huee thi. kisee baakeedaar asaamee ke saamane is peepe kaa uchhalanaa-koodanaa aur paintare badalanaa dekhakar kisee nat kaa chigiyaa bhee lajjit ho jat. aisee aankhen laal-peelee karte, aise garajate ki darshakon kee bheed lag jati thi. unhe kanjoos to nahin kahaa, jaa sakataa, kyonki jab woh dookaan par hote, to harek bhikhamange ke saamane ek kaudee phenk dete. haan, us samay unake maathe par kuchh aisaa bal pad jat, aankhen kuchh aisee prachand ho jaateen, naak kuchh aisee sikud jati ki bhikhaaree phir unakee dookaan par na aat. lahane kaa baap tagaadaa hai, is siddhaant ke woh anany bhakt the. kuchh khana karne ke baad shaam tak woh baraabar tagaadaa karte rehte the. isamen ek to ghar kaa khana bachataa tha, doosare asaamiyon ke maathe doodh, pooree, mithaaee aadi padaarth khaane ko mil jate the. ek vakt kaa khana bach jan koi aam baat nahin hai! ek khana kaa ek aan bhee rakh len, to keval isee mad mein unhonne apne tees varshon ke mahaajanee jeevan mein koi aath sau rupaye bachaa liye the. phir lautate samay doosaree belaa ke lie bhee doodh, dahee, tel, tarakaaree, upale, eendhan mil jate the. bahudhaa shaam kaa khana bhee na karn padataa tha. isalie tagaade se na chookate the. aasamaan phataa padataa ho, aag baras rahi ho, aandhee aati ho; par sethajee prakriti ke atal niyam kee tarah tagaade par zaroor nikal jate.
sethaanee ne poochhaa, 'khana? '
sethajee ne garajakar kahaa, 'nahin. '
'saanjh kaa? '
'aane par dekhee jaayagee. '
sethajee ke ek kisaan par paanch rupaye aate the. chh: maheene se dusht ne sood-byaaj kuchh na diya tha aur na kabhee koi saugaat lekar haajir hua tha. usakaa ghar teen kos se kam na tha, isaliye sethajee taalate aate the. aaj unhonne usee gaanv chalne kaa phaisalaa kar liya. aaj binaa dusht se rupaye liye na maanoongaa, chaahe kitanaa hee roye, ghighiyaaye. magar itanee lambee yaatraa paidal karn nindaaspad tha. log kahenge naam bade darshan thode. kahalaate seth, chalate hain paidal isalie manthar gati se idhar-udhar taakate, raahageeron se baaten karte chale jate the ki log samajhen vaayu-sevan karne jaa rahe hain. sahasaa ek khaalee ikkaa unakee taraph jat hua mil gaya. ikkevaan ne poochhaa, 'kahaan laalaa, kahan jan hai?
sethajee ne kahaa, 'zaanaa to kaheen nahin hai, do parag to aur hai; lekin laao baith jaayan. '
ikkevaale ne chubhatee huee aankhon se sethajee ko dekh, sethajee ne bhee apanee laal aankhon se use ghooraa. donon samajh gaye, 'aaj lohe ke chane chabaane padenge. '
ikkaa chalaa. sethajee ne pahalaa vaar kiya, 'kahaan ghar hai miyaan sahab? '
'ghar kahan hai hujoor, jahaan pad rahoon, vaheen ghar hai. jab ghar tha tab tha. ab to beghar, bedar hoon aur sabase badee baat yah hai ki bepar hoon. takadeer ne par kaat liye. ledooraa banaakar chhod diya. mere daadaa navaabee mein chakaledaar the hujoor, saat jile ke maalik, jise chaahen top-dam kar den, phaansee par latakaa den. nikalane ke pahale laakhon kee thailiyaan najar chadh jati theen hujoor. navaab sahab bhai kee tarah maanate the. ek din woh the, ek din yah hai ki ham aap logon kee gulaamee kar rahe hain. dinon kaa pher hai. '
sethajee ko haath milaate hee maaloom ho gaya, pakkaa phikait hai, akhaadebaaj, isase pesh paanaa mushkil hai, par ab to kushtee bada gaee thi, akhaade mein utar pade the.
bole -'to yah kaho ki baadashaahee gharaane ke ho. yah soorat hee gavaahee de rahi hai. dinon kaa pher hai bhai, sab din baraabar nahin jate. hamaare yahan lakshmee ko chanchalaa kehte hain, baraabar chalatee rehti hain, aaj mere ghar, kal tumhaare ghar. tumhaare daadaa ne rupaye to khoob chhode honge? '
ikkevaalaa -'are seth, us daulat kaa koi hisaab na tha. na jaane kitane tahakhaane bhare hue the! boron mein sone-chaandee ke dale rakhe hue the. javaaharaat tokariyon mein bhare pade the. ek-ek patthar pachaas-pachaas laakh kaa. chamak-damak aisee thi, ki chiraag maat. magar takadeer bhee to koi cheej hai. idhar daadaa kaa chaaleesavaan hua; udhar navaabee burd huee. saaraa khajaanaa lut gaya. chhakadon par laad-laadakar log javaaharaat le gaye phir bhee ghar mein itanaa bach raha tha ki abbaajaan ne zindagi bhar aish kiya aisaa aish kiya, ki kya koi bhakuvaa karegaa. solah kahaaron ke sukhapaal par nikalate the. aage-peechhe chobadaar daudate chalate the. phir bhee mere gujar bhar ko unhonne bahut chhodaa. agar hisaab-kitaab se rehta to aaj bhalaa aadamee hot; lekin raees kaa betaa raees hee to hogaa. ek botal chadhaakar bistar se uthataa tha. raat-raat bhar mujare hote rehte the. kya jaanataa tha, ek din yah thokaren khaanee padengee. '
seth -'allaa miyaan kaa sukur karo bhai ki eemaanadaaree se apne baal-bachchon kee paravarish to karte ho. nahin to hamaare-tumhaare kitane hee bhai raat-din kukarm karte rehte hain, phir bhee daane-daane ko muhataaj rehte hain. eemaan kee salaamatee chaahie, nahin din to sabhee ke kat jate hain, doodh-rotee khaakar kate to kya, sookhe chane chabaakar kate to kya. badee baat to eemaan hai. mujhe to tumhaaree soorat dekhate hee maaloom ho gaya tha, ki neeyat ke saaph sachche aadamee ho. beeemaanon kee to soorat hee se phatakaar barasatee hai. '
ikkevaalaa -'sethajee, aapane theek kahaa, ki eemaan salaamat rahe, to sab kuchh hai. aap logon se chaar paise mil jate hain, vahee baal-bachchon ko khilaa-pilaakar pad rehta hoon. hujoor, aur ikkevaalon ko dekhie, to koi kisee marj mein mubtilaa hai, koi kisee marj mein. mainne tobaa bol, ! aisaa kaam hee kyon karen, ki museebat mein phesen. badaa kunabaa hai hujoor, maan hai, bachche hain, kaee bevaaen hain, aur kamaaee yahee ikkaa hai. phir bhee allaah miyaan kisee tarah nibaahe jate hain. '
seth -'woh badaa kaarasaaj hai khaan sahab, tumhaaree kamaaee mein hameshaa barakat hogee. '
ikkevaalaa-' aap logon kee meharabaanee chaahie. '
seth -'bhagavaan kee meharabaanee chaahie. tumse khoob bhent ho gaee. main ikkevaalon se bahut ghabaraataa hoon; lekin ab maaloom hua, achchhe-bure sabhee jagah hote hain. tumhaare jaisaa sachchaa, deenadaar aadamee mainne nahin dekh. kaisee saaph tabiyat paaee hai tumane ki vaah! '
sethajee kee ye lachchhedaar baaten sunakar ikkevaalaa samajh gaya ki yah sahab parale sire ke baithakabaaj hain. yah sirph meree taareeph karake mujhe chakamaa diya chaahate hain. ab aur kisee pahaloo se apanaa matalab nikaalanaa chaahie. inakee raham se to kuchh le maranaa mushkil hai, shaayad inse dar se kuchh le maroon. bol, 'magar laalaa, yah na samajhie ki main jitanaa seedhaa aur nek najar aat hoon, utanaa seedhaa aur nek hoon bhee. nekon ke saath nek hoon lekin buron ke saath pakkaa badamaash hoon. yon kahie aapakee jootiyaan seedhee kar doon; lekin kiraaye ke maamale mein kisee ke saath riaayat nahin kart. riaayat karoon to khaaoon kya? '
sethajee ne samajhaa tha, ikkevaale ko hatthe par chadhaa liya, ab yaatraa nirvighn aur ni: shulk khatm ho jaayagee! lekin yah alaap sunaa, to kaan khade hue.
bole -'bhai, rupaye-paise ke maamale mein main bhee kisee se riaayat nahin kart; lekin kabhee-kabhee jab yaar-doston kaa maamalaa aa padataa hai to jhak maarakar dabanaa hee padataa hai. tumhe bhee kabhee-kabhee bal khana hee padataa hogaa. doston se bemurauvatee to nahin kee jati. '
ikkevaale ne rookhepan se kahaa, 'main kisee ke saath murauvat nahin kart. murauvat kaa sabak to ustaad ne padhaayaa hee nahin. ek hee chanchool hoon. majaal kya ki koi ek paisaa dabaa le. gharavaalee tak ko to main ek paisaa detaa nahin, doosaron kee baat hee kya hai. aur ikkevaale apne mahaajan kee khushaamad karte hain. usake daravaaje par khade rehte hain. yahan mahaajanon ko bhee dhataa bataataa hoon. sab mere naam ko rote hain. rupaye liye aur saaph dakaar gaya. dekhen, ab kaise vasool karte ho bachchaa, naalish karo, ghar mein kya dharaa hai, jo le loge. '
sethajee ko maano joodee chadh aaee. samajh gaye, yah shaitaan binaa paise liye na maanegaa. jaanate ki yah vipatti gale padegee, to bhoolakar bhee ikke par paanv na rakhate. itanee door paidal chalne mein kaun pair toote jate the. agar is tarah roj paise dene pade, to phir len-den kar chukaa. sethajee bhakt jeev the. shivajee ko jal chadhaane mein, jab se hosh sanbhaalaa, ek naagaa bhee na kiya. kya bhaktavatsal shankar bhagavaan is avasar par meree madad na karenge. ishtadev kaa sumiran karake bole, 'Ko sahab aur kisee se chaahe na dabo; par pulis se to dabanaa hee padataa hogaa. woh to kisee ke sage nahin hote. '
ikkevaale ne kahakahaa, 'maaraa kabhee nahin, unse ulte aur kuchh-na-kuchh vasool kart hoon. jahaan koi shikaar milaa, jhat saste bhaade baithaataa hoon aur thaane par pahunchaa detaa hoon. kiraayaa bhee mil jat hai aur inaam bhee. kya majaal ki koi bol sake. laaisan nahin liya aaj tak, laaisan! maje mein sadar mein ikkaa daudaataa phirataa hoon. koi saalaa choon nahin kar sakataa. mele-thelon mein apanee khoob ban aati hai. achchhe-achchhe maal chunakar kotavaalee pahunchaataa hoon. wahan kaun kisee kee daal galatee hai. jise chaahen rok len, ek din, do din, teen din. bees bahaane hain. kah diya, shak tha ki yah aurat ko bhagaaye liye jat tha. phir kaun bol sakataa hai. sahab bhee chhodanaa chaahen; to nahin chhod sakate. mujhe seedhaa na samajhiegaa. ek hee haraamee hoon. savaariyon se pahale kiraayaa tay nahin kart, thikaane par pahunchakar ek ke do letaa hoon. zara bhee cheen-chapad kiya, to aasteen chadhaa, paintare badalakar khadaa ho jat hoon. phir kaun hai jo saamane thahar sake. '
sethajee ko romaanch ho aay. haath mein ek sotaa to tha, par usakaa vyavahaar karne kee shakti kaa unamen abhaav tha. aaj bure phese, na jaane kis manahoos kaa munh dekhakar ghar se chale the. kaheen yah dusht ulajh pade, to das-paanch din haldee-sonth peenaa pade. ab se bhee kushal hai, yahan utar jaaoon, jo bach jaay vahee sahee. bheegee billee banakar bole, 'achchhaa, ab rok lo khaan sahab, meraa gaanv aa gaya. bolo, tumhe kya de doon? '
ikkevaale ne ghodee ko ek chaabuk aur lagaayaa aur nirdayataa se bol, 'majooree soch lo bhai. tumako na baithaayaa hot to teen savaariyaan baithaa letaa. teenon chaar-chaar aane bhee dete, to baarah aane ho jate. tum aath hee aane de do. '
sethajee kee badhiyaa baith gaee. itanee badee rakam unhonne umr bhar is mad mein nahin kharch kee thi. itanee-see door ke lie itanaa kiraayaa, woh kisee tarah na de sakate the. manushy ke jeevan mein ek aisaa avasar bhee aat hai, jab nateejaa kee use phikr nahin rehti. sethajee ke jeevan mein yah aisaa hee avasar tha. agar aane-do-aane kee baat hoti, to khoon kaa ghoont peekar de dete, lekin aath aane ke lie ki jisakaa dvigun ek kaladaar hot hai, agar too-too main-main hee nahin haathaapaaee kee bhee naubat aaye, to woh karne ko taiyaar the. yah phaisalaa karake woh dridhataa ke saath baithe rahe. sahasaa sadak ke kinaare ek jhonpadaa najar aay. ikkaa ruk gaya, seth jee utar pade aur kamar se ek duannee nikaalakar ikkevaan kee ora badhaaee.
ikkevaan ne sethajee ke tevar dekhe, to samajh gaya, taav bigad gaya. chaashanee kadee hokar kathor ho gaee. ab yah daanton se ladegee. ise chubal kar hee mithaas kaa khushi liya jaa sakataa hai. namrataa se bol, 'meree ora se isakee revadiyaan lekar baal-bachchon ko khilaa deejiegaa. allaah aapako salaamat rakhe. '
sethajee ne ek aan aur nikaalaa aur bole -'bas, ab jabaan na hilaanaa, ek kaudee bhee besee na doongaa. ' ikkevaalaa -'nahin maalik, aap hee aisaa kahenge, to ham gareebon ke baal-bachche kahan se palenge. ham log bhee aadamee pahachaanate hain hujoor. ' itane mein jhonpadee mein se ek aurat gulaabee saadee pahane, paan chabaatee huee nikal aaee aur bolee, aaj badee der lagaaee (yakaayak sethajee ko dekhakar), 'achchhaa aaj laalaajee tumhaare ikke par the. phir aaj tumhaaraa mijaaj kaahe ko milegaa. ek chehareshaahee to milee hee hogee. idhar badhaa do seedhe se. '
yah kahakar woh sethajee ke paas aakar bolee, 'aaraam se charapaiyaa par baitho laalaa! bade bhaag the ki aaj sabere-sabere aapake darshan hue. '
usake kapade mand-mand mahak rahe the. sethajee kaa dimaag taajaa ho gaya. usakee ora kanakhiyon se dekh. aurat chanchal, baankee-kateelee, tej-tarraar thi. sethaaneejee kee moorti aankhon ke saamane aa gaee bhaddee, thal-thal, pil-pil, pairon mein bevaay phatee huee, kapadon se durgandh udatee huee. sethajee naamamaatr ko bhee rasik na the, par is samay aankhon se haar gaye. aankhon ko udhar se hataane kee cheshtaa karake chaarapaaee par baith gaye. abhee kos bhar kee manjil baakee hai, isakaa khyaal hee na raha. aurat ek chhotee-see pankhiyaa uthaa laaee aur sethajee ko jhalane lagee. haath kee har gati ke saath sugandhaa kaa ek jhonkaa aakar sethajee ko unmatt karne lagaa.
sethajee ne jeevan mein aisaa ullaas kabhee tajurbaa na kiya tha. unhe praay: sabhee napharat kee nazar se dekhate the. cholaa mast ho gaya. usake haath se pankhiyaa chheen lenee chaahee.
'tumhe takaleeph ho raha hai, laao main jhal loon. '
'yah kaisee baat hai laalaajee! aap hamaare daravaaje par aaye hain. kya itanee khaatir bhee na karne deejiyegaa. aur ham kis laayak hain. idhar kaheen door jan hai? ab to bahut der ho gaee. kahan jaaiegaa. '
sethajee ne paapee aankhon ko pher kar aur paapee man ko dabaa kar kahaa, 'yahan se thodee door par ek gaanv hai, vaheen jan hai. saanjh ko idhar hee se lautoongaa. '
sundaree ne khush hokar kahaa, 'to phir aaj yaheen rahiegaa. saanjh ko phir kahan jaaiegaa. ek din ghar ke baahar kee havaa bhee khaaie. phir na jaane kab mulaakaat hogee. '
ikkevaale ne aakar sethajee ke kaan mein kahaa, 'paise nikaalie to daane-chaare kaa intajaam karoon. '
sethajee ne chupake se athannee nikaalakar de dee.
ikkevaale ne phir poochhaa, 'aapake lie kuchh mithaaee letaa aaoon? yahan aapake laayak mithaaee to kya milegee, haan munh meethaa ho jaayagaa. '
sethajee bole, 'mere liye koi jaroorat nahin, haan bachchon ke lie yah chaar aane kee mithaaee livaate aan.
chavannee nikaalakar sethajee ne usake saamane aise garv se phenkee maano isakee unake saamane koi hakeekat nahin hai. sundaree ke munh kaa bhaav to dekhanaa chaahate the; par darate the ki kaheen woh yah na samajhe, laalaa chavannee kya de rahe hain, maano kisee ko mol le rahe hain.
ikkevaalaa chavannee uthaakar jaa hee raha tha ki sundaree ne kahaa, 'sethajee kee chavannee lautaa do. lapakakar uthaa lee, sharm nahin aati. yah mujhse rupayaa le lo. aath aane kee taajee mithaaee banavaakar laao. ' usane rupayaa nikaalakar phenkaa! sethajee maare laaj ke gad gaye. ek ikkevaan kee bhathiyaarin jisakee take kee bhee aukaat nahin, itanee khaatiradaaree kare ki unake lie pooraa rupayaa nikaalakar de de, yah bhalaa woh kaise sah sakate the. bole -'nahin-nahin, yah nahin ho sakataa. tum apanaa rupayaa rakh lo. (rasik aankhon ko tript karake) main rupayaa diye detaa hoon. yah lo, aath aane kee le lenaa. '
ikkevaan to udhar mithaaee aur daanaa-chaare kee phikr mein chalaa, 'idhar sundaree ne seth se kahaa, woh to abhee der mein aayegaa laalaa, tab tak paan to khaao. '
sethajee ne idhar-udhar taakakar kahaa, 'yahan to koi tambolee nahin hai. '
sundaree unakee ora kataakshapoorn netron se dekhakar bolee, 'kya mere lagaaye paan tambolee ke paanon se bhee kharaab honge? '
sethajee ne lajjit hokar kahaa, 'nahin-nahin, yah baat nahin, tum musalamaan ho na? '
sundaree ne vinodamay aagrah se kahaa, 'Kudaa kee kasam, isee baat par main tumhe paan khilaakar chhodoongee!
yah kahakar usane paanadaan se ek beedaa nikaalaa aur sethajee kee taraph chalee. sethajee ne ek minit tak to haan! haan! kiya, phir donon haath badhaakar use hataane kee cheshtaa kee, phir jor se donon onth band kar lie par jab sundaree kisee tarah na maanee, to sethajee apanaa dharm lekar betahaashaa bhaage. sontaa vaheen chaarapaaee par raha gaya. bees kadam par jaakar aap ruk gaye aur haanphakar bole -'dekho, is tarah kisee kaa dharm nahin liya jat. ham log tumhaaraa chhuaa paani pee len to dharm bhrasht ho jaay. '
sundaree ne phir daudaayaa. sethajee phir bhaage. idhar pachaas varsh se unhe is tarah bhaagane kaa avasar na padaa tha. dhotee khisakakar girane lagee magar itanaa phursat na tha ki dhotee baandh len. bechaare dharm ko kandho par rakhe daude chale jate the. na maaloom kab kamar se rupayon kaa batuaa khisak padaa. jab ek pachaas kadam par phir ruke aur dhotee oopar uthaaee, to batuaa nadaarad. peechhe phirakar dekh. sundaree haath mein batuaa liye unhe dikhaa rahi thi aur ishaare se bulaa rahi thi. magar sethajee ko dharm rupaye se kaheen pyaaraa tha. do-chaar kadam chale phir ruk gaye.
yakaayak dharm-samajh ne dot bataaee. thode rupaye ke lie dharm chhode dete ho. rupaye bahut milenge. dharm kahan milegaa.
yah sochate hue woh apanee raah chale, jaise koi kuttaa jhagadaaloo kutton ke beech se aahat, dum dabaaye bhaagaa jat ho aur baar-baar peechhe phirakar dekh letaa ho ki kaheen woh dusht aa to nahin rahe hain.
