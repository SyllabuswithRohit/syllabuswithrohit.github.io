keshav ke pitaa sarakaaree penshanar the, mijaaj ke chidachide aur kripan. dharm ke aadambaron mein hee unake chitt ko shaanti milatee thi. kalpanaa-shakti kaa abhaav tha. kisee ke manobhaavon kaa izzat na kar sakate the. woh ab bhee us sansaar mein rehte the, jisamen unhonne apne bachpan aur javaanee ke din kaate the. navayug kee badhatee huee lahar ko woh sarvanaash kehte the, aur kam-se-kam apne ghar ko donon haathon aur donon pairon kaa jor lagaakar usse bachaae rakhanaa chaahate the; isalie jab ek din premaa ke pitaa usake paas pahunche aur keshav se premaa ke vivaah kaa prastaav kiya, to boodhe pandit jee apne aap mein na raha sake. dhundhalee aankhen phaadakar bole-aap bhang to nahin khaa gaye hain? is tarah kaa sambandh aur chaahe jo kuchh ho, vivaah nahin hai. maaloom hot hai, aapako bhee naye jamaane kee havaa lag gayee.

doosare din premaa ne keshav ke naam yah patr likhaa-
'priy keshav! '
tumhaare poojy pitaajee ne laalaa jee ke saath jo ashisht aur apamaanajanak vyavahaar kiya hai, usakaa haal sunakar mere man mein badee shankaa utpann ho rahi hai. shaayad unhonne tumhe bhee daant-phatakaar bataayee hogee, aisee dashaa mein main tumhaaraa phaisalaa sunane ke lie vikal ho rahi hoon. tumhaare saath har tarah kaa takaleeph jhelane ko taiyaar hoon. mujhe tumhaare pitaajee kee jaayadaad kaa moh nahin hai, main to keval tumhaaraa pyaar chaahatee hoon aur usee mein khush hoon. aaj shaam ko yaheen aakar khana karo. daadaa aur maan donon tumse milane ke lie bahut ichchhuk hain. main woh svapn dekhane mein magn hoon jab ham donon us sootr mein bandh jaayange, jo tootanaa nahin jaanataa. jo badee-se-badee aapatti mein bhee atoot rehta hai.
tumhaaree-
premaa!
shaam ho gayee aur is patr kaa koi jawab na aay. usakee maan baar-baar poochhatee thi-keshav aaye nahin? boodhe laalaa bhee dvaar kee ora aankh lagaaye baithe the. yahan tak ki raat ke nau baj gaye, par na to keshav hee aaye, na unakaa patr.
premaa ke man mein tarah-tarah ke sankalp-vikalp uth rahe the, shaayad unhe patr likhane kaa phursat na milaa hogaa, yaa aaj aane kee phurasat na milee hogee, kal avashy aa jaaenge. keshav ne pahale usake paas jo pyaar-patr likhe the, una sabako usane phir padhaa. unake ek-ek shabd se kitanaa anuraag tapak raha tha, unamen kitanaa kampan tha, kitanee vikalataa, kitanee teevr aakaankshaa! phir use keshav ke woh vaaky yaad aae; jo usane saikadon hee baar kahe the. kitanee baar woh usake saamane royaa tha. itane pramaanon ke hote hue maayoosee ke lie kahan sthaan tha, magar phir bhee saaree raat usakaa man jaise soolee par tangaa raha.
praat: kaal keshav kaa jawab aay. premaa ne kaanpate hue haathon se patr lekar padhaa. patr haath se gir gaya. aisaa jaan padaa, maano usakee deh kaa rakt sthir ho gaya ho. likhaa tha-
'main bade sankat mein hoon, ki tumhe kya jawab doon! mainne idhar is samasyaa par khoob thande dil se soch kiya hai aur is nateeje par pahunchaa hoon ki abhee dashaaon mein mere lie pitaa kee hukm kee upekshaa karn du: sah hai. mujhe kaayar na samajhanaa. main svaarthee bhee nahin hoon, lekin mere saamane jo baadhaaen hain una par vijay paane kee shakti mujhamen nahin hai. puraanee baaton ko bhool jaao. us samay mainne ina baadhaaon kee kalpanaa na kee thi! '
premaa ne ek lambee, gaharee, jalatee huee saans kheenchee aur us khat ko phaadakar phenk diya. usakee aankhon se ashrudhaar bahane lagee. jis keshav ko usane apne ant: karan se var liya tha, woh itanaa nishthur ho jaayagaa, isakee usako rattee bhar bhee ummeed na thi. aisaa maaloom padaa, maano, ab tak woh koi sunahalaa svapn dekh rahi thi; par aankh khulane par woh sab kuchh adrishy ho gaya. jeevan mein jab ummeed hee lupt ho gayee, to ab andhakaar ke sivaa aur kya raha! apne dil kee saaree jaayadaad lagaakar usane ek naav ladavaayee thi, woh naav jalamagn ho gayee. ab doosaree naav kaun wahan se ladavaaye; agar woh naav tootee hai to usake saath woh bhee doob jaayagee.
maan ne poochhaa-kya keshav kaa patr hai?
premaa ne bhoomi kee ora taakate hue kahaa-haan, unakee tabeeyat achchhee nahin hai-isake sivaa woh aur kya kahe? keshav kee nishthurataa aur bevaphaaee kaa khabar kahakar lajjit hone kaa saahas usamen na tha.
din bhar woh ghar ke kaam-dhandhon mein lagee rahi, maano use koi phikr hee nahin hai. raat ko usane sabako khana karaayaa, khud bhee khana kiya aur badee der haaramoniyam par gaatee rahi.
magar saberaa hua, to usake kamare mein usakee laash padee huee thi. prabhaat kee sunaharee kiranen usake peele munh ko jeevan kee aabhaa pradaan kar rahi theen.
