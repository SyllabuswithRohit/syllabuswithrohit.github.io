# Budhi Kaki

**Munshi Premchand**

1
jihvaa-svaad ke sivaa aur koi cheshtaa shesh na thi aur na apne kashton ki ora aakarshit karne kaa, rone ke atirikt koi doosaraa sahaaraa hi. sab indriyaan, aankh, haath aur pair jawab de chuke the. prithvee par padee rahateen aur ghar vaale koi baat unki ichchhaa ke pratikool karte, khaanaa kaa samay tal jat yaa uska parimaan poorn na hot athavaa baazaar se koi vastu aatee aur na milatee to ye rone lagti theen. unakaa ronaa-sisakanaa aam ronaa na tha, ve galaa phaad-phaadakar rotee theen.
unake patidev ko svarg sidhaare kaalaantar ho chukaa tha. bete tarun ho-hokar chal base the. ab ek bhateeje ke alaavaa aur koi na tha. usi bhateeje ke naam unhone apni sari sampatti likh dee. bhateeje ne sari sampatti likhaate samay Koob lambe-chaude vaade kie, lekin ve sab vaade keval kulee-dipo ke dalaalon ke dikhaae hue sabzabaag the. yadyapi us sampatti ki vaarshik aay dedh-do sau rupae se kam na thi phir bhi boodhee kaakee ko pet bhar khaanaa bhi kathinaaee se milataa tha. isamen unake bhateeje pandit buddhiraam kaa aparaadh tha athavaa unki ardhaanginee shreematee roopaa kaa, isakaa phaisalaa karn sahaj nahin. buddhiraam svabhaav ke sajjan the, lekin usi samay tak jab ki unake kosh par aanch na aae. roopaa svabhaav se teevr thi sahee, par eeshvar se daratee thi. isalie boodhee kaakee ko uski teevrataa utanee na khalatee thi jitanee buddhiraam ki bhalamanasaahat.
buddhiraam ko kabhi-kabhi apne atyaachaar kaa khed hot tha. vichaarate ki isi sampatti ke vajah main is samay bhalaamaanush banaa baithaa hoon. yadi bhautik aashvaasan aur sookhee sahaanubhooti se sthiti mein sudhaar ho sakataa ho, unhen kadaachit koi aapatti na hoti, lekin khaas vyay kaa dar unki sucheshtaa ko dabaae rakhta tha. yahan tak ki yadi dvaar par koi bhalaa aadamee baithaa hot aur boodhee kaakee us samay apna raag alaapane lagateen to vah aag ho jaate aur ghar mein aakar unhen jor se daantate. ladakon ko buddhon se svaabhaavik vidvesh hot hi hai aur phir jab maan-pitaa kaa yah rang dekhte to ve boodhee kaakee ko aur sataayaa karte. koi chutakee kaatakar bhaagataa, koi ina par paani ki kullee kar detaa. kaakee cheeK maarakar roteen lekin yah baat prasiddh thi ki vah keval khaane ke lie rotee hain, isalie unake santaap aur aartanaad par koi dhyaan nahin detaa tha. haan, kaakee krodhaatur hokar bachchon ko gaaliyaan dene lagateen to roopaa ghatanaasthal par aa pahunchti. is dar se kaakee apni jihvaa kripaan kaa kadaachit hi prayog karti theen, yadyapi upadrav-shaanti kaa yah upaay rone se kaheen zyaadaa upayukt tha.
pooraa parivaar mein yadi kaakee se kisee ko anuraag tha, to vah buddhiraam ki chhoti ladakee laadalee thi. laadalee apne donon bhaaiyon ke dar se apne hisse ki mithaaee-chabainaa boodhee kaakee ke paas baithakar khaayaa karti thi. yahi uska rakshaagaar tha aur yadyapi kaakee ki sharan unki lolupataa ke vajah bahut manhagee padatee thi, phir bhi bhaaiyon ke anyaay se surakshaa kaheen sulabh thi to bas yaheen. isi svaarthaanukoolataa ne una donon mein sahaanubhooti kaa aaropan kar diya tha.
2
raat kaa samay tha. buddhiraam ke dvaar par shahanaaee baj rahi thi aur gaanv ke bachchon kaa jhund vismayapoorn netron se gaane kaa rasaasvaadan kar raha tha. chaarapaaiyon par mehamaan vishraam karte hue naaiyon se mukkiyaan lagavaa rahe the. paas khadaa bhaat virudaavalee sunaa raha tha aur kuchh bhaavajn mehamaanon ki 'vaah, vaah' par aisaa Kush ho raha tha maano is 'vaah-vaah' kaa yathaarth mein wahi adhikaaree hai. do-ek angrezee padhe hue navayuvak ina vyavahaaron se udaaseen the. ve is ganvaar mandalee mein bolna athavaa sammilit hona apni pratishthaa ke pratikool samajhte the.
aaj buddhiraam ke bade ladake mukharaam kaa tilak aay hai. yah usi kaa utsav hai. ghar ke bheetar striyaan gaa rahi theen aur roopaa mehamaanon ke lie khaanaa mein vyast thi. bhattiyon par kadaah chadh rahe the. ek mein poodiyaan-kachaudiyaan nikal rahi theen, doosare mein any pakavaan banate the. ek bade hande mein masaaledaar tarakaaree pak rahi thi. ghee aur masaale ki kshudhaavardhak sugandhi chaaron ora phailee hui thi.
boodhee kaakee apni kotharee mein shokamay soch ki tarah baithee hui theen. yah svaad mishrit sugandhi unhen bechain kar rahi thi. ve man-hi-man soch kar rahi theen, sanbhavatah mujhe poodiyaan na milengeen. itanee der ho gayi, koi khaanaa lekar nahin aay. maaloom hot hai sab log khaanaa kar chuke hain. mere lie kuchh na bachaa. yah sochakar unhen ronaa aay, lekin apashakun ke dar se vah ro na sakeen.
'aahaa... kaisee sugandhi hai? ab mujhe kaun poochhta hai. jab rotiyon ke hi laale pade hain tab aise bhaagy kahan ki bharapet poodiyaan milen?' yah soch kar unhen ronaa aay, kaleje mein hook-see uthane lagee. lekin roopaa ke dar se unhone phir chup dhaaran kar liya.
boodhee kaakee der tak inhee dukhadaayak vichaaron mein doobee raheen. ghee aur masaalon ki sugandhi raha-rahakar man ko aape se baahar kie detee thi. munh mein paani bhar-bhar aat tha. poodiyon kaa svaad smaran karake dil mein gudagudee hone lagti thi. kise pukaaroon, aaj laadalee betee bhi nahin aaee. donon chhokare sadaa dik diya karte hain. aaj unakaa bhi kaheen pataa nahin. kuchh maaloom to hot ki kya ban raha hai.
boodhee kaakee ki kalpanaa mein poodiyon ki tasveer naachane lagee. Koob laal-laal, phoolee-phoolee, naram-naram hongeen. roopaa ne bhalee-tarah khaanaa kiya hogaa. kachaudiyon mein ajavaain aur ilaayachee ki mahak aa rahi hogee. ek poodee milatee to zara haath mein lekar dekhti. kyon na chal kar kadaah ke saamane hi baithoon. poodiyaan chhan-chhanakar taiyaar hongee. kadaah se garam-garam nikaalakar thaal mein rakhee jati hongee. phool ham ghar mein bhi soongh sakate hain, lekin vaatikaa mein kuchh aur baat hoti hai. is tarah phaisalaa karake boodhee kaakee ukadoon baithakar haathon ke bal sarakatee hui badi kathinaaee se chaukhat se utareen aur dheere-dheere rengatee hui kadaah ke paas jaa baitheen. yahan aane par unhen utanaa hi dhairy hua jitanaa bhookhe kutte ko khaane vaale ke sammukh baithane mein hot hai.
roopaa us samay kaaryabhaar se udvign ho rahi thi. kabhi is kothe mein jati, kabhi us kothe mein, kabhi kadaah ke paas jati, kabhi bhandaar mein jati. kisee ne baahar se aakar kahaa--'mahaaraaj thandaee maang rahe hain.' thandaee dene lagee. itane mein phir kisee ne aakar kahaa--'bhaat aay hai, use kuchh de do.' bhaat ke lie seedhaa nikaal rahi thi ki ek teesare aadamee ne aakar poochhaa--'abhi khaanaa taiyaar hone mein kitanaa vilamb hai? zara dhol, majeeraa utaar do.' bechaaree akelee aurat daudate-daudate vyaakul ho rahi thi, jhunjhalaatee thi, kudhti thi, lekin gussaa prakat karne kaa avasar na paatee thi. dar hot, kaheen padosinen yah na kehne lagen ki itane mein ubal padeen. pyaas se svayan galaa sookh raha tha. garmee ke maare phunkee jati thi, lekin itanaa phursat na tha ki zara paani pee le athavaa pankhaa lekar jhale. yah bhi khatakaa tha ki zara aankh hatee aur cheezon ki loot machee. is avasthaa mein usane boodhee kaakee ko kadaah ke paas baithee dekh to jal gayi. gussaa na ruk sakaa. isakaa bhi dhyaan na raha ki padosinen baithee hui hain, man mein kya kahengeen. purushon mein log sunenge to kya kahenge. jis tarah mendhak kenchue par jhapatataa hai, usi tarah vah boodhee kaakee par jhapatee aur unhen donon haathon se jhatak kar bolee-- aise pet mein aag lage, pet hai yaa bhaad? kotharee mein baithte hue kya dam ghutataa tha? abhi mehamaanon ne nahin khaayaa, bhagavaan ko bhog nahin lagaa, tab tak dhairy na ho sakaa? aakar chhaatee par savar ho gayi. jal jaae aisee jeebh. din bhar khaatee na hoti to jaane kisakee haandee mein munh daalatee? gaanv dekhegaa to kahegaa ki budhiyaa bharapet khaane ko nahin paatee tabhee to is tarah munh baae phiratee hai. daayan na mare na maanchaa chhode. naam bechane par lagee hai. naak katavaa kar dam legee. itanee thoonsatee hai na jaane kahan bhasm ho jat hai. bhalaa chahti ho to jaakar kotharee mein baitho, jab ghar ke log khaane lagenge, tab tumhe bhi milegaa. tum koi devee nahin ho ki chaahe kisee ke munh mein paani na jaae, lekin tumhari poojaa pahale hi ho jaae.
boodhee kaakee ne sir uthaayaa, na roeen na boleen. chupachaap rengatee hui apni kotharee mein chalee gaeen. aavaaz aisee kathor thi ki dil aur mashtishk ki pooraa shaktiyaan, pooraa soch aur pooraa bhaar usi ora aakarshit ho gae the. nadee mein jab kagaar kaa koi vrihad khand katakar girataa hai to aas-paas kaa jal samooh chaaron ora se usi sthaan ko pooraa karne ke lie daudataa hai.
3
khaanaa taiyaar ho gaya hai. aangan mein pattalen pad gaeen, mehamaan khaane lage. striyon ne jevanaar-geet gaanaa shuru kar diya. mehamaanon ke naaee aur sevakagan bhi usi mandalee ke saath, lekin kuchh hatakar khaanaa karne baithe the, lekin sabhyataanusaar jab tak sab-ke-sab khaa na chuken koi uth nahin sakataa tha. do-ek mehamaan jo kuchh padhe-likhe the, sevakon ke deerghaahaar par jhunjhalaa rahe the. ve is bandhan ko bekaar aur bekaar ki baat samajhte the.
boodhee kaakee apni kotharee mein jaakar pashchaataap kar rahi thi ki main kahan-se-kahan aa gayi. unhen roopaa par gussaa nahin tha. apni jaldabaazee par dukh tha. sach hi to hai jab tak mehamaan log khaanaa na kar chukenge, ghar vaale kaise khaaenge. mujh se itanee der bhi na raha gaya. sabake saamane paani utar gaya. ab jab tak koi bulaane nahin aaegaa, na jaaoongee.
man-hi-man is tarah kaa soch kar vah bulaane ki prateekshaa karne lageen. lekin ghee ki ruchikar suvaas badi dhairY-pareekshak lagaa ho rahi thi. unhen ek-ek pal ek-ek yug ke samaan maaloom hot tha. ab pattal bichh gayi hogee. ab mehamaan aa gae honge. log haath pair dho rahe hain, naaee paani de raha hai. maaloom hot hai log khaane baith gae. jevanaar gaayaa jaa raha hai, yah soch kar vah man ko bahalaane ke lie let gaeen. dheere-dheere ek geet gunagunaane lageen. unhen maaloom hua ki mujhe gaate der ho gayi. kya itanee der tak log khaanaa kar hi rahe honge. kisee ki aavaaz sunaaee nahin detee. avashy hi log khaa-peekar chale gae. mujhe koi bulaane nahin aay hai. roopaa chidh gayi hai, kya jaane na bulaae. sochti ho ki aap hi aavengeen, vah koi mehamaan to nahin jo unhen bulaaoon. boodhee kaakee chalne ko taiyaar hueen. yah yakeen ki ek minat mein poodiyaan aur masaaledaar tarakaariyaan saamane aaengeen, unki svaadendriyon ko gudagudaane lagaa. unhone man mein tarah-tarah ke mansoobe baandhe-- pahale tarakaaree se poodiyaan khaaoongee, phir dahee aur shakkar se, kachauriyaan raayate ke saath mazedaar maaloom hongee. chaahe koi buraa maane chaahe bhalaa, main to maang-maangakar khaaoongee. yahi na log kahenge ki inhen soch nahin? kahaa karen, itane din ke baad poodiyaan mil rahi hain to munh jhoothaa karake thode hi uth jaaoongee.
vah ukadoon baithakar sarakate hue aangan mein aaeen. lekin haay badakismatee! chaah ne apne puraane svabhaav ke anusaar samay ki mithyaa kalpanaa ki thi. mehamaan-mandalee abhi baithee hui thi. koi khaakar ungaliyaan chaatataa tha, koi tirachhe netron se dekhta tha ki aur log abhi khaa rahe hain yaa nahin. koi is phikr mein tha ki pattal par poodiyaan chhootee jati hain kisee tarah inhen bheetar rakh letaa. koi dahee khaakar chatakaarataa tha, lekin doosaraa donaa maangate sankoch kart tha ki itane mein boodhee kaakee rengatee hui unake beech mein aa pahunchee. kaee aadamee chaunkakar uth khade hue. pukaarane lage-- are, yah budhiyaa kaun hai? yahan kahan se aa gayi? dekho, kisee ko chhoo na de.
pandit buddhiraam kaakee ko dekhte hi gussaa se tilamilaa gae. poodiyon kaa thaal lie khade the. thaal ko zameen par patak diya aur jis tarah nirdayee mahaajan apne kisee beimaan aur bhagode karzadaar ko dekhte hi uska tentuaa pakad letaa hai usi tarah lapak kar unhone kaakee ke donon haath pakade aur ghaseetate hue laakar unhen andheree kotharee mein dham se patak diya. aashaaroopee vatikaa loo ke ek jhonke mein vinasht ho gayi.
mehamaanon ne khaanaa kiya. gharavaalon ne khaanaa kiya. baaje vaale, dhobee, chamaar bhi khaanaa kar chuke, lekin boodhee kaakee ko kisee ne na poochhaa. buddhiraam aur roopaa donon hi boodhee kaakee ko unki nirlajjataa ke lie dand dene ka phaisalaa kar chuke the. unake budhaape par, deenataa par, hatjnaan par kisee ko karunaa na aaee thi. akelee laadalee unake lie kudh rahi thi.
laadalee ko kaakee se bahut pyaar tha. bechaaree bholee ladakee thi. baal-vinod aur chanchalataa ki usamen gandh tak na thi. donon baar jab usake maan-pitaa ne kaakee ko nirdayataa se ghaseetaa to laadalee kaa dil ènthakara raha gaya. vah jhunjhalaa rahi thi ki ham log kaakee ko kyon bahut-see poodiyaan nahin dete. kya mehamaan sab-ki-sab khaa jaaenge? aur yadi kaakee ne mehamaanon se pahale khaa liya to kya bigad jaaegaa? vah kaakee ke paas jaakar unhen dhairy dena chahti thi, lekin maan ke dar se na jati thi. usane apne hisse ki poodiyaan bilkul na khaaeen theen. apni gudiyaa ki pitaaree mein band kar rakhee theen. una poodiyon ko kaakee ke paas le jan chahti thi. uska dil adheer ho raha tha. boodhee kaakee meri baat sunate hi uth baithengeen, poodiyaan dekhakar kaisee khush hongeen! mujhe khoob pyaar karengeen.
4
raat ko gyaarah baj gae the. roopaa aangan mein padee so rahi thi. laadalee ki aankhon mein neend na aatee thi. kaakee ko poodiyaan khilaane ki khushi use sone na detee thi. usane gudiyon ki pitaaree saamane rakhee thi. jab yakeen ho gaya ki ammaa so rahi hain, to vah chupake se uthee aur vichaarane lagee, kaise chaloon. chaaron ora andheraa tha. keval choolhon mein aag chamak rahi thi aur choolhon ke paas ek kuttaa letaa hua tha. laadalee ki nazar saamane vaale neem par gayi. use maaloom hua ki us par hanumaan jee baithe hue hain. unki poonchh, unki gadaa, vah spasht dikhalaaee de rahi hai. maare dar ke usane aankhen band kar leen. itane mein kuttaa uth baithaa, laadalee ko dhaadhas hua. kaee soe hue manushyon ke badale ek bhaagataa hua kuttaa usake lie zyaadaa dhairy kaa vajah hua. usane pitaaree uthaaee aur boodhee kaakee ki kotharee ki ora chalee.
5
boodhee kaakee ko keval itanaa smaran tha ki kisee ne mere haath pakadakar ghaseete, phir aisaa maaloom hua ki jaise koi pahaad par udaae lie jat hai. unake pair baar-baar pattharon se takaraae tab kisee ne unhen pahaad par se patakaa, ve moorchhit ho gaeen.
jab ve sachet hueen to kisee ki zaraa bhi aahat na milatee thi. samajhee ki sab log khaa-peekar so gae aur unake saath meri takadeer bhi so gayi. raat kaise kategee? raam! kya khaaoon? pet mein agni dhadhak rahi hai. haa! kisee ne meri sudhi na lee. kya mera pet kaatane se paisaa jud jaaegaa? ina logon ko itanee bhi dayaa nahin aatee ki na jaane budhiyaa kab mar jaae? uska jee kyon dukhaaven? main pet ki rotiyaan hi khaatee hoon ki aur kuchh? is par yah haal. main andhee, apaahij thaharee, na kuchh sunoon, na boojhoon. yadi aangan mein chalee gayi to kya buddhiraam se itanaa kehte na banataa tha ki kaakee abhi log khaanaa khaa rahe hain phir aan. mujhe ghaseetaa, patakaa. unhee poodiyon ke lie roopaa ne sabake saamane gaaliyaan deen. unheen poodiyon ke lie itanee durgati karne par bhi unakaa patthar kaa kalejaa na paseejaa. sabako khilaayaa, meri baat tak na poochhee. jab tab hi na deen, tab ab kya denge? yah soch kar kaakee niraashaamay santosh ke saath let gayi. pachhataavaa se galaa bhar-bhar aat tha, lekin mehamaanon ke dar se rotee na theen. sahasaa kaanon mein aavaaz aaee-- 'kaakee utho, main poodiyaan laaee hoon.' kaakee ne laadalee ki bolee pahachaanee. chatapat uth baitheen. donon haathon se laadalee ko tatolaa aur use god mein bithaa liya. laadalee ne poodiyaan nikaalakar deen.
kaakee ne poochhaa-- kya tumhari ammaa ne dee hai?
laadalee ne kahaa-- nahin, yah mere hisse ki hain.
kaakee poodiyon par toot padeen. paanch minat mein pitaaree khaalee ho gayi. laadalee ne poochhaa-- kaakee pet bhar gaya.
jaise thodee-see varshaa thandak ke sthaan par aur bhi garmee paidaa kar detee hai us tarah ina thodee poodiyon ne kaakee ki kshudhaa aur ikshaa ko aur uttejit kar diya tha. boleen-- nahin betee, jaakar ammaa se aur maang laao.
laadalee ne kahaa-- ammaa sotee hain, jagaaoongee to maarengeen.
kaakee ne pitaaree ko phir tatolaa. usamen kuchh khurchan giree thi. baar-baar honth chaatatee theen, chatakhaare bharatee theen.
dil masos raha tha ki aur poodiyaan kaise paaoon. santosh-setu jab toot jat hai tab ichchhaa kaa bahaav aparimit ho jat hai. matavaalon ko mad kaa smaran karn unhen madaandh banaataa hai. kaakee kaa adheer man ichchhaaon ke prabal pravaah mein bah gaya. theek aur galat kaa soch jat raha. ve kuchh der tak us ichchhaa ko rokatee raheen. sahasaa laadalee se boleen-- mera haath pakadakar wahan le chalo, jahaan mehamaanon ne baithakar khaanaa kiya hai.
laadalee unakaa abhipraay samajh na sakee. usane kaakee kaa haath pakadaa aur le jaakar jhoothe pattalon ke paas bithaa diya. deen, kshudhaatur, hat jnaan budhiyaa pattalon se poodiyon ke tukade chun-chunakar bhakshan karne lagee. oha... dahee kitanaa svaadisht tha, kachaudiyaan kitanee salonee, Kastaa kitane sukomal. kaakee buddhiheen hote hue bhi itanaa jaanatee theen ki main vah kaam kar rahi hoon, jo mujhe kabhi nahin na karn chaahie. main doosaron ki jhoothee pattal chaat rahi hoon. lekin budhaapaa trishnaa rog kaa antim samay hai, jab pooraa ichchhaaen ek hi kendr par aa lagti hain. boodhee kaakee mein yah kendr unki svaadendriy thi.
theek usi samay roopaa ki aankh khulee. use maaloom hua ki laadalee mere paas nahin hai. vah chaunkee, chaarapaaee ke idhar-udhar taakane lagee ki kaheen neeche to nahin gir padee. use wahan na paakar vah uthee to kya dekhti hai ki laadalee joothe pattalon ke paas chupachaap khadee hai aur boodhee kaakee pattalon par se poodiyon ke tukade uthaa-uthaakar khaa rahi hai. roopaa kaa dil sann ho gaya. kisee gaay ki garadan par chhuree chalte dekhakar jo avasthaa uski hoti, wahi us samay hui. ek braahmanee doosaron ki jhoothee pattal tatole, isase zyaadaa shokamay drishy naamumakin tha. poodiyon ke kuchh graason ke lie uski chacheree saas aisaa nishkrisht karm kar rahi hai. yah vah drishy tha jise dekhakar dekhne vaalon ke dil kaanp uthte hain. aisaa lagaa hot maano zameen ruk gayi, aasamaan chakkar khaa raha hai. sansaar par koi aapatti aane vaalee hai. roopaa ko gussaa na aay. shok ke sammukh gussaa kahan? karunaa aur dar se uski aankhen bhar aaeen. is adharm kaa bhaagee kaun hai? usane sachche dil se gagan mandal ki ora haath uthaakar kahaa-- paramaatmaa, mere bachchon par dayaa karo. is adharm kaa dand mujhe mata do, nahin to mera satyaanaash ho jaaegaa.
roopaa ko apni svaarthaparataa aur anyaay is tarah pratyaksh roop mein kabhi na dikh pade the. vah sochne lagee-- haay! kitanee nirday hoon. jisakee sampati se mujhe do sau rupayaa aay ho rahi hai, uski yah durgati. aur mere kaaran. he dayaamay bhagavaan! mujhse badi bhaaree chook hui hai, mujhe kshamaa karo. aaj mere bete kaa tilak tha. saikadon manushyon ne khaanaa paayaa. main unake ishaaron ki daasee banee rahi. apne naam ke lie saikadon rupae vyay kar die, lekin jisakee badaulat hazaaron rupae khaae, use is utsav mein bhi bharapet khaanaa na de sakee. keval isi vajah to, vah vriddhaa asahaay hai.
roopaa ne diya jalaayaa, apne bhandaar kaa dvaar kholaa aur ek thaalee mein pooraa saamagriyaan sajaakar boodhee kaakee ki ora chalee.
aadhee raat jaa chukee thi, aakaash par taaron ke thaal saje hue the aur una par baithe hue devagan svargeey padaarth sajaa rahe the, lekin usamen kisee ko vah paramaanand praapt na ho sakataa tha, jo boodhee kaakee ko apne sammukh thaal dekhakar praapt hua. roopaa ne kanthaaruddh aavaaz mein kahaa---kaakee utho, khaanaa kar lo. mujhse aaj badi bhool hui, uska buraa na maananaa. paramaatmaa se praarthanaa kar do ki vah mera aparaadh kshamaa kar den.
bhole-bhole bachchon ki tarah, jo mithaaiyaan paakar maar aur tiraskaar sab bhool jat hai, boodhee kaakee vaise hi sab bhulaakar baithee hui khaanaa khaa rahi thi. unake ek-ek rone se sachchee sadichchhaaen nikal rahi theen aur roopaa baithee svargeey drishy kaa khushi lene mein nimagn thi.
