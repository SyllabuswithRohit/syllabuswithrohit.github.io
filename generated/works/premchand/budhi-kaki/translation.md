# Budhi Kaki

**Munshi Premchand**

1
jihvaa-svaad ke sivaa aur koee cheshtaa shesh na thi aur na apne kashton kee ora aakarshit karne kaa, rone ke atirikt koee doosaraa sahaaraa hee| sab indriyaan, aankh, haath aur pair javaab de chuke the| prithvee par padee rahateen aur ghar vaale koee baat unakee ichchhaa ke pratikool karte, khaanaa kaa samay tal jat yaa usakaa parimaan poorn na hot athavaa baazaar se koee vastu aatee aur na milatee to ye rone lagatee theen| unakaa ronaa-sisakanaa aam ronaa na tha, ve galaa phaad-phaadakar rotee theen|
unake patidev ko svarg sidhaare kaalaantar ho chukaa tha| bete tarun ho-hokar chal base the| ab ek bhateeje ke alaavaa aur koee na tha| usee bhateeje ke naam unhonne apanee saaree sampatti likh dee| bhateeje ne saaree sampatti likhaate samay Koob lambe-chaude vaade kie, lekin ve sab vaade keval kulee-dipo ke dalaalon ke dikhaae hue sabzabaag the| yadyapi us sampatti kee vaarshik aay dedh-do sau rupae se kam na thi phir bhee boodhee kaakee ko pet bhar khaanaa bhee kathinaaee se milataa tha| isamen unake bhateeje pandit buddhiraam kaa aparaadh tha athavaa unakee ardhaanginee shreematee roopaa kaa, isakaa phaisalaa karn sahaj nahin| buddhiraam svabhaav ke sajjan the, lekin usee samay tak jab ki unake kosh par aanch na aae| roopaa svabhaav se teevr thi sahee, par eeshvar se daratee thi| isalie boodhee kaakee ko usakee teevrataa utanee na khalatee thi jitanee buddhiraam kee bhalamanasaahat|
buddhiraam ko kabhee-kabhee apne atyaachaar kaa khed hot tha| vichaarate ki isee sampatti ke vajah main is samay bhalaamaanush banaa baithaa hoon| yadi bhautik aashvaasan aur sookhee sahaanubhooti se sthiti mein sudhaar ho sakataa ho, unhen kadaachit koee aapatti na hoti, lekin khaas vyay kaa dar unakee sucheshtaa ko dabaae rakhataa tha| yahan tak ki yadi dvaar par koee bhalaa aadamee baithaa hot aur boodhee kaakee us samay apanaa raag alaapane lagateen to vah aag ho jaate aur ghar mein aakar unhen jor se daantate| ladakon ko buddhon se svaabhaavik vidvesh hot hee hai aur phir jab maan-pitaa kaa yah rang dekhate to ve boodhee kaakee ko aur sataayaa karte| koee chutakee kaatakar bhaagataa, koee ina par paanee kee kullee kar detaa| kaakee cheeK maarakar roteen lekin yah baat prasiddh thi ki vah keval khaane ke lie rotee hain, isalie unake santaap aur aartanaad par koee dhyaan nahin detaa tha| haan, kaakee krodhaatur hokar bachchon ko gaaliyaan dene lagateen to roopaa ghatanaasthal par aa pahunchatee| is dar se kaakee apanee jihvaa kripaan kaa kadaachit hee prayog karti theen, yadyapi upadrav-shaanti kaa yah upaay rone se kaheen zyaadaa upayukt tha|
pooraa parivaar mein yadi kaakee se kisee ko anuraag tha, to vah buddhiraam kee chhotee ladakee laadalee thi| laadalee apne donon bhaaiyon ke dar se apne hisse kee mithaaee-chabainaa boodhee kaakee ke paas baithakar khaayaa karti thi| yahee usakaa rakshaagaar tha aur yadyapi kaakee kee sharan unakee lolupataa ke vajah bahut manhagee padatee thi, phir bhee bhaaiyon ke anyaay se surakshaa kaheen sulabh thi to bas yaheen| isee svaarthaanukoolataa ne una donon mein sahaanubhooti kaa aaropan kar diya tha|
2
raat kaa samay tha| buddhiraam ke dvaar par shahanaaee baj rahi thi aur gaanv ke bachchon kaa jhund vismayapoorn netron se gaane kaa rasaasvaadan kar raha tha| chaarapaaiyon par mehamaan vishraam karte hue naaiyon se mukkiyaan lagavaa rahe the| paas khadaa bhaat virudaavalee sunaa raha tha aur kuchh bhaavajn mehamaanon kee 'vaah, vaah' par aisaa Kush ho raha tha maano is 'vaah-vaah' kaa yathaarth mein vahee adhikaaree hai| do-ek angrezee padhe hue navayuvak ina vyavahaaron se udaaseen the| ve is ganvaar mandalee mein bolanaa athavaa sammilit honaa apanee pratishthaa ke pratikool samajhate the|
aaj buddhiraam ke bade ladake mukharaam kaa tilak aay hai| yah usee kaa utsav hai| ghar ke bheetar striyaan gaa rahi theen aur roopaa mehamaanon ke lie khaanaa mein vyast thi| bhattiyon par kadaah chadh rahe the| ek mein poodiyaan-kachaudiyaan nikal rahi theen, doosare mein any pakavaan banate the| ek bade hande mein masaaledaar tarakaaree pak rahi thi| ghee aur masaale kee kshudhaavardhak sugandhi chaaron ora phailee huee thi|
boodhee kaakee apanee kotharee mein shokamay soch kee tarah baithee huee theen| yah svaad mishrit sugandhi unhen bechain kar rahi thi| ve man-hee-man soch kar rahi theen, sanbhavatah mujhe poodiyaan na milengeen| itanee der ho gaee, koee khaanaa lekar nahin aay| maaloom hot hai sab log khaanaa kar chuke hain| mere lie kuchh na bachaa| yah sochakar unhen ronaa aay, lekin apashakun ke dar se vah ro na sakeen|
'aahaa... kaisee sugandhi hai? ab mujhe kaun poochhataa hai| jab rotiyon ke hee laale pade hain tab aise bhaagy kahan ki bharapet poodiyaan milen?' yah soch kar unhen ronaa aay, kaleje mein hook-see uthane lagee| lekin roopaa ke dar se unhonne phir chup dhaaran kar liya|
boodhee kaakee der tak inhee dukhadaayak vichaaron mein doobee raheen| ghee aur masaalon kee sugandhi raha-rahakar man ko aape se baahar kie detee thi| munh mein paanee bhar-bhar aat tha| poodiyon kaa svaad smaran karake dil mein gudagudee hone lagatee thi| kise pukaaroon, aaj laadalee betee bhee nahin aaee| donon chhokare sadaa dik diya karte hain| aaj unakaa bhee kaheen pataa nahin| kuchh maaloom to hot ki kyaa ban raha hai|
boodhee kaakee kee kalpanaa mein poodiyon kee tasveer naachane lagee| Koob laal-laal, phoolee-phoolee, naram-naram hongeen| roopaa ne bhalee-tarah khaanaa kiya hogaa| kachaudiyon mein ajavaain aur ilaayachee kee mahak aa rahi hogee| ek poodee milatee to jaraa haath mein lekar dekhatee| kyon na chal kar kadaah ke saamane hee baithoon| poodiyaan chhan-chhanakar taiyaar hongee| kadaah se garam-garam nikaalakar thaal mein rakhee jati hongee| phool ham ghar mein bhee soongh sakate hain, lekin vaatikaa mein kuchh aur baat hoti hai| is tarah phaisalaa karake boodhee kaakee ukadoon baithakar haathon ke bal sarakatee huee badee kathinaaee se chaukhat se utareen aur dheere-dheere rengatee huee kadaah ke paas jaa baitheen| yahan aane par unhen utanaa hee dhairy hua jitanaa bhookhe kutte ko khaane vaale ke sammukh baithane mein hot hai|
roopaa us samay kaaryabhaar se udvign ho rahi thi| kabhee is kothe mein jati, kabhee us kothe mein, kabhee kadaah ke paas jati, kabhee bhandaar mein jati| kisee ne baahar se aakar kahaa--'mahaaraaj thandaee maang rahe hain|' thandaee dene lagee| itane mein phir kisee ne aakar kahaa--'bhaat aay hai, use kuchh de do|' bhaat ke lie seedhaa nikaal rahi thi ki ek teesare aadamee ne aakar poochhaa--'abhee khaanaa taiyaar hone mein kitanaa vilamb hai? jaraa dhol, majeeraa utaar do|' bechaaree akelee aurat daudate-daudate vyaakul ho rahi thi, jhunjhalaatee thi, kudhatee thi, lekin gussaa prakat karne kaa avasar na paatee thi| dar hot, kaheen padosinen yah na kehne lagen ki itane mein ubal padeen| pyaas se svayan galaa sookh raha tha| garmee ke maare phunkee jati thi, lekin itanaa phursat na tha ki jaraa paanee pee le athavaa pankhaa lekar jhale| yah bhee khatakaa tha ki jaraa aankh hatee aur cheezon kee loot machee| is avasthaa mein usane boodhee kaakee ko kadaah ke paas baithee dekh to jal gaee| gussaa na ruk sakaa| isakaa bhee dhyaan na raha ki padosinen baithee huee hain, man mein kyaa kahengeen| purushon mein log sunenge to kyaa kahenge| jis tarah mendhak kenchue par jhapatataa hai, usee tarah vah boodhee kaakee par jhapatee aur unhen donon haathon se jhatak kar bolee-- aise pet mein aag lage, pet hai yaa bhaad? kotharee mein baithate hue kyaa dam ghutataa tha? abhee mehamaanon ne nahin khaayaa, bhagavaan ko bhog nahin lagaa, tab tak dhairy na ho sakaa? aakar chhaatee par savar ho gaee| jal jaae aisee jeebh| din bhar khaatee na hoti to jaane kisakee haandee mein munh daalatee? gaanv dekhegaa to kahegaa ki budhiyaa bharapet khaane ko nahin paatee tabhee to is tarah munh baae phiratee hai| daayan na mare na maanchaa chhode| naam bechane par lagee hai| naak katavaa kar dam legee| itanee thoonsatee hai na jaane kahaan bhasm ho jat hai| bhalaa chaahatee ho to jaakar kotharee mein baitho, jab ghar ke log khaane lagenge, tab tumhe bhee milegaa| tum koee devee nahin ho ki chaahe kisee ke munh mein paanee na jaae, lekin tumhaaree poojaa pahale hee ho jaae|
boodhee kaakee ne sir uthaayaa, na roeen na boleen| chupachaap rengatee huee apanee kotharee mein chalee gaeen| aavaaz aisee kathor thi ki dil aur mashtishk kee pooraa shaktiyaan, pooraa soch aur pooraa bhaar usee ora aakarshit ho gae the| nadee mein jab kagaar kaa koee vrihad khand katakar girataa hai to aas-paas kaa jal samooh chaaron ora se usee sthaan ko pooraa karne ke lie daudataa hai|
3
khaanaa taiyaar ho gaya hai| aangan mein pattalen pad gaeen, mehamaan khaane lage| striyon ne jevanaar-geet gaanaa shuroo kar diya| mehamaanon ke naaee aur sevakagan bhee usee mandalee ke saath, lekin kuchh hatakar khaanaa karne baithe the, lekin sabhyataanusaar jab tak sab-ke-sab khaa na chuken koee uth nahin sakataa tha| do-ek mehamaan jo kuchh padhe-likhe the, sevakon ke deerghaahaar par jhunjhalaa rahe the| ve is bandhan ko bekaar aur bekaar kee baat samajhate the|
boodhee kaakee apanee kotharee mein jaakar pashchaataap kar rahi thi ki main kahan-se-kahan aa gaee| unhen roopaa par gussaa nahin tha| apanee jaldabaazee par dukh tha| sach hee to hai jab tak mehamaan log khaanaa na kar chukenge, ghar vaale kaise khaaenge| mujh se itanee der bhee na raha gaya| sabake saamane paanee utar gaya| ab jab tak koee bulaane nahin aaegaa, na jaaoongee|
man-hee-man is tarah kaa soch kar vah bulaane kee prateekshaa karne lageen| lekin ghee kee ruchikar suvaas badee dhairY-pareekshak lagaa ho rahi thi| unhen ek-ek pal ek-ek yug ke samaan maaloom hot tha| ab pattal bichh gaee hogee| ab mehamaan aa gae honge| log haath pair dho rahe hain, naaee paanee de raha hai| maaloom hot hai log khaane baith gae| jevanaar gaayaa jaa raha hai, yah soch kar vah man ko bahalaane ke lie let gaeen| dheere-dheere ek geet gunagunaane lageen| unhen maaloom hua ki mujhe gaate der ho gaee| kyaa itanee der tak log khaanaa kar hee rahe honge| kisee kee aavaaz sunaaee nahin detee| avashy hee log khaa-peekar chale gae| mujhe koee bulaane nahin aay hai| roopaa chidh gaee hai, kyaa jaane na bulaae| sochatee ho ki aap hee aavengeen, vah koee mehamaan to nahin jo unhen bulaaoon| boodhee kaakee chalne ko taiyaar hueen| yah yakeen ki ek minat mein poodiyaan aur masaaledaar tarakaariyaan saamane aaengeen, unakee svaadendriyon ko gudagudaane lagaa| unhonne man mein tarah-tarah ke mansoobe baandhe-- pahale tarakaaree se poodiyaan khaaoongee, phir dahee aur shakkar se, kachauriyaan raayate ke saath mazedaar maaloom hongee| chaahe koee buraa maane chaahe bhalaa, main to maang-maangakar khaaoongee| yahee na log kahenge ki inhen soch nahin? kahaa karen, itane din ke baad poodiyaan mil rahi hain to munh jhoothaa karake thode hee uth jaaoongee |
vah ukadoon baithakar sarakate hue aangan mein aaeen| lekin haay badakismatee! chaah ne apne puraane svabhaav ke anusaar samay kee mithyaa kalpanaa kee thi| mehamaan-mandalee abhee baithee huee thi| koee khaakar ungaliyaan chaatataa tha, koee tirachhe netron se dekhataa tha ki aur log abhee khaa rahe hain yaa nahin| koee is phikr mein tha ki pattal par poodiyaan chhootee jati hain kisee tarah inhen bheetar rakh letaa| koee dahee khaakar chatakaarataa tha, lekin doosaraa donaa maangate sankoch kart tha ki itane mein boodhee kaakee rengatee huee unake beech mein aa pahunchee| kaee aadamee chaunkakar uth khade hue| pukaarane lage-- are, yah budhiyaa kaun hai? yahan kahan se aa gaee? dekho, kisee ko chhoo na de|
pandit buddhiraam kaakee ko dekhate hee gussaa se tilamilaa gae| poodiyon kaa thaal lie khade the| thaal ko zameen par patak diya aur jis tarah nirdayee mahaajan apne kisee beimaan aur bhagode karzadaar ko dekhate hee usakaa tentuaa pakad letaa hai usee tarah lapak kar unhonne kaakee ke donon haath pakade aur ghaseetate hue laakar unhen andheree kotharee mein dham se patak diya| aashaaroopee vatikaa loo ke ek jhonke mein vinasht ho gaee|
mehamaanon ne khaanaa kiya| gharavaalon ne khaanaa kiya| baaje vaale, dhobee, chamaar bhee khaanaa kar chuke, lekin boodhee kaakee ko kisee ne na poochhaa| buddhiraam aur roopaa donon hee boodhee kaakee ko unakee nirlajjataa ke lie dand dene ka phaisalaa kar chuke the| unake budhaape par, deenataa par, hatjnaan par kisee ko karunaa na aaee thi| akelee laadalee unake lie kudh rahi thi|
laadalee ko kaakee se bahut pyaar tha| bechaaree bholee ladakee thi| baal-vinod aur chanchalataa kee usamen gandh tak na thi| donon baar jab usake maan-pitaa ne kaakee ko nirdayataa se ghaseetaa to laadalee kaa dil ènthakara raha gaya| vah jhunjhalaa rahi thi ki ham log kaakee ko kyon bahut-see poodiyaan nahin dete| kyaa mehamaan sab-kee-sab khaa jaaenge? aur yadi kaakee ne mehamaanon se pahale khaa liya to kyaa bigad jaaegaa? vah kaakee ke paas jaakar unhen dhairy denaa chaahatee thi, lekin maan ke dar se na jati thi| usane apne hisse kee poodiyaan bilkul na khaaeen theen| apanee gudiyaa kee pitaaree mein band kar rakhee theen| una poodiyon ko kaakee ke paas le jan chaahatee thi| usakaa dil adheer ho raha tha| boodhee kaakee meree baat sunate hee uth baithengeen, poodiyaan dekhakar kaisee khush hongeen! mujhe khoob pyaar karengeen|
4
raat ko gyaarah baj gae the| roopaa aangan mein padee so rahi thi| laadalee kee aankhon mein neend na aatee thi| kaakee ko poodiyaan khilaane kee khushee use sone na detee thi| usane gudiyon kee pitaaree saamane rakhee thi| jab yakeen ho gaya ki ammaa so rahi hain, to vah chupake se uthee aur vichaarane lagee, kaise chaloon| chaaron ora andheraa tha| keval choolhon mein aag chamak rahi thi aur choolhon ke paas ek kuttaa letaa hua tha| laadalee kee nazar saamane vaale neem par gaee| use maaloom hua ki us par hanumaan jee baithe hue hain| unakee poonchh, unakee gadaa, vah spasht dikhalaaee de rahi hai| maare dar ke usane aankhen band kar leen| itane mein kuttaa uth baithaa, laadalee ko dhaadhas hua| kaee soe hue manushyon ke badale ek bhaagataa hua kuttaa usake lie zyaadaa dhairy kaa vajah hua| usane pitaaree uthaaee aur boodhee kaakee kee kotharee kee ora chalee|
5
boodhee kaakee ko keval itanaa smaran tha ki kisee ne mere haath pakadakar ghaseete, phir aisaa maaloom hua ki jaise koee pahaad par udaae lie jat hai| unake pair baar-baar pattharon se takaraae tab kisee ne unhen pahaad par se patakaa, ve moorchhit ho gaeen|
jab ve sachet hueen to kisee kee zaraa bhee aahat na milatee thi| samajhee ki sab log khaa-peekar so gae aur unake saath meree takadeer bhee so gaee| raat kaise kategee? raam! kyaa khaaoon? pet mein agni dhadhak rahi hai| haa! kisee ne meree sudhi na lee| kyaa meraa pet kaatane se paisaa jud jaaegaa? ina logon ko itanee bhee dayaa nahin aatee ki na jaane budhiyaa kab mar jaae? usakaa jee kyon dukhaaven? main pet kee rotiyaan hee khaatee hoon ki aur kuchh? is par yah haal| main andhee, apaahij thaharee, na kuchh sunoon, na boojhoon| yadi aangan mein chalee gaee to kyaa buddhiraam se itanaa kehte na banataa tha ki kaakee abhee log khaanaa khaa rahe hain phir aan| mujhe ghaseetaa, patakaa| unhee poodiyon ke lie roopaa ne sabake saamane gaaliyaan deen| unheen poodiyon ke lie itanee durgati karne par bhee unakaa patthar kaa kalejaa na paseejaa| sabako khilaayaa, meree baat tak na poochhee| jab tab hee na deen, tab ab kyaa denge? yah soch kar kaakee niraashaamay santosh ke saath let gaee| pachhataavaa se galaa bhar-bhar aat tha, lekin mehamaanon ke dar se rotee na theen| sahasaa kaanon mein aavaaz aaee-- 'kaakee utho, main poodiyaan laaee hoon|' kaakee ne laadalee kee bolee pahachaanee| chatapat uth baitheen| donon haathon se laadalee ko tatolaa aur use god mein bithaa liya| laadalee ne poodiyaan nikaalakar deen|
kaakee ne poochhaa-- kyaa tumhaaree ammaa ne dee hai?
laadalee ne kahaa-- nahin, yah mere hisse kee hain|
kaakee poodiyon par toot padeen| paanch minat mein pitaaree khaalee ho gaee| laadalee ne poochhaa-- kaakee pet bhar gaya|
jaise thodee-see varshaa thandak ke sthaan par aur bhee garmee paidaa kar detee hai us tarah ina thodee poodiyon ne kaakee kee kshudhaa aur ikshaa ko aur uttejit kar diya tha| boleen-- nahin betee, jaakar ammaa se aur maang laao|
laadalee ne kahaa-- ammaa sotee hain, jagaaoongee to maarengeen|
kaakee ne pitaaree ko phir tatolaa| usamen kuchh khurchan giree thi| baar-baar honth chaatatee theen, chatakhaare bharatee theen|
dil masos raha tha ki aur poodiyaan kaise paaoon| santosh-setu jab toot jat hai tab ichchhaa kaa bahaav aparimit ho jat hai| matavaalon ko mad kaa smaran karn unhen madaandh banaataa hai| kaakee kaa adheer man ichchhaaon ke prabal pravaah mein bah gaya| theek aur galat kaa soch jat raha| ve kuchh der tak us ichchhaa ko rokatee raheen| sahasaa laadalee se boleen-- meraa haath pakadakar wahan le chalo, jahaan mehamaanon ne baithakar khaanaa kiya hai|
laadalee unakaa abhipraay samajh na sakee| usane kaakee kaa haath pakadaa aur le jaakar jhoothe pattalon ke paas bithaa diya| deen, kshudhaatur, hat jnaan budhiyaa pattalon se poodiyon ke tukade chun-chunakar bhakshan karne lagee| oha... dahee kitanaa svaadisht tha, kachaudiyaan kitanee salonee, Kastaa kitane sukomal| kaakee buddhiheen hote hue bhee itanaa jaanatee theen ki main vah kaam kar rahi hoon, jo mujhe kabhee nahin na karn chaahie| main doosaron kee jhoothee pattal chaat rahi hoon| lekin budhaapaa trishnaa rog kaa antim samay hai, jab pooraa ichchhaaen ek hee kendr par aa lagatee hain| boodhee kaakee mein yah kendr unakee svaadendriy thi|
theek usee samay roopaa kee aankh khulee| use maaloom hua ki laadalee mere paas nahin hai| vah chaunkee, chaarapaaee ke idhar-udhar taakane lagee ki kaheen neeche to nahin gir padee| use wahan na paakar vah uthee to kyaa dekhatee hai ki laadalee joothe pattalon ke paas chupachaap khadee hai aur boodhee kaakee pattalon par se poodiyon ke tukade uthaa-uthaakar khaa rahi hai| roopaa kaa dil sann ho gaya| kisee gaay kee garadan par chhuree chalate dekhakar jo avasthaa usakee hoti, vahee us samay huee| ek braahmanee doosaron kee jhoothee pattal tatole, isase zyaadaa shokamay drishy naamumakin tha| poodiyon ke kuchh graason ke lie usakee chacheree saas aisaa nishkrisht karm kar rahi hai| yah vah drishy tha jise dekhakar dekhane vaalon ke dil kaanp uthate hain| aisaa lagaa hot maano zameen ruk gaee, aasamaan chakkar khaa raha hai| sansaar par koee aapatti aane vaalee hai| roopaa ko gussaa na aay| shok ke sammukh gussaa kahan? karunaa aur dar se usakee aankhen bhar aaeen| is adharm kaa bhaagee kaun hai? usane sachche dil se gagan mandal kee ora haath uthaakar kahaa-- paramaatmaa, mere bachchon par dayaa karo| is adharm kaa dand mujhe mata do, nahin to meraa satyaanaash ho jaaegaa|
roopaa ko apanee svaarthaparataa aur anyaay is tarah pratyaksh roop mein kabhee na dikh pade the| vah sochane lagee-- haay! kitanee nirday hoon| jisakee sampati se mujhe do sau rupayaa aay ho rahi hai, usakee yah durgati| aur mere kaaran| he dayaamay bhagavaan! mujhse badee bhaaree chook huee hai, mujhe kshamaa karo| aaj mere bete kaa tilak tha| saikadon manushyon ne khaanaa paayaa| main unake ishaaron kee daasee banee rahi| apne naam ke lie saikadon rupae vyay kar die, lekin jisakee badaulat hazaaron rupae khaae, use is utsav mein bhee bharapet khaanaa na de sakee| keval isee vajah to, vah vriddhaa asahaay hai|
roopaa ne diya jalaayaa, apne bhandaar kaa dvaar kholaa aur ek thaalee mein pooraa saamagriyaan sajaakar boodhee kaakee kee ora chalee|
aadhee raat jaa chukee thi, aakaash par taaron ke thaal saje hue the aur una par baithe hue devagan svargeey padaarth sajaa rahe the, lekin usamen kisee ko vah paramaanand praapt na ho sakataa tha, jo boodhee kaakee ko apne sammukh thaal dekhakar praapt hua| roopaa ne kanthaaruddh aavaaz mein kahaa---kaakee utho, khaanaa kar lo| mujhse aaj badee bhool huee, usakaa buraa na maananaa| paramaatmaa se praarthanaa kar do ki vah meraa aparaadh kshamaa kar den|
bhole-bhole bachchon kee tarah, jo mithaaiyaan paakar maar aur tiraskaar sab bhool jat hai, boodhee kaakee vaise hee sab bhulaakar baithee huee khaanaa khaa rahi thi| unake ek-ek rone se sachchee sadichchhaaen nikal rahi theen aur roopaa baithee svargeey drishy kaa khushee lene mein nimagn thi|
