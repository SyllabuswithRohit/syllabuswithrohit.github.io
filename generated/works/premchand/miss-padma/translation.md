# Miss Padma

**Munshi Premchand**

1
kaanoon mein achchhee saphalataa praapt kar lene ke baad mis padmaa ko ek nayaa tajurbaa hua, woh tha jeevan kaa soonaapan. vivaah ko unhonne ek apraakritik bandhan samajhaa tha aur phaisalaa kar liya tha ki svatantr rahakar jeevan kaa upabhog karoongee. ema. e. kee digree lee, phir kaanoon paas kiya aur praiktis shuroo kar dee. roopavatee thi, naujavaan ladakee thi, mridubhaashinee thi aur pratibhaashaalinee bhee thi. raastaa mein koi baadhaa na thi. dekhate-dekhate woh apne saathee naujavaan mard vakeelon ko peechhe chhodakar aage nikal gayee aur ab usakee aamadanee kabhee-kabhee ek hajaar se bhee oopar badh jati. ab utane parishram aur sir-magajan kee aavashyakataa na rahi. mukadamen adhikatar vahee hote the, jinakaa use pooraa tajurbaa ho chukaa tha, usake vishay mein kisee tarah kee taiyaaree kee use jaroorat na maaloom hoti. apanee shaktiyon par kuchh yakeen bhee ho gaya tha. kaanoon mein kaise vijay milaa karti hain, isake kuchh latake bhee use maaloom ho gaye the. isaliye use ab use bahut phursat milataa tha aur ise woh kisse-kahaaniyaan padhne, sair karne, sinemaa dekhane, milane-milaane mein kharch karti thi. jeevan ko sukhee banaane ke lie kisee vyasan kee jaroorat ko woh khoob samajhatee thi. usane phool aur paude lagaane kaa vyasan paal liya tha. tarah-tarah ke beej aur paude mangaatee aur unhe ugate-badhate, phoolate-phalate dekhakar khush hoti; magar jeevan mein soonapane kaa tajurbaa hot rehta tha. yah baat na thi ki use purushon se virakti ho. nahin, usake premiyon kee kamee na thi. agar usake paas keval roop aur yauvan hot, to bhee upaasakon kaa abhaav na rehta; magar yahan to roop aur yauvan ke saath paisaa bhee thi. phir rasik-vrind kyon chook jate? padmaa ko vilaas se napharat thi nahin, napharat thi paraadheenataa se, vivaah ko jeevan kaa vyavasaay banaane se. jab svatantr rahakar bhog-vilaas kaa khushi uthaayaa jaa sakataa hain, to phir kyon na udaayaa jaay? bhog mein use koi naitik baadhaa na thi, ise keval deh kee ek bhookh samajhatee thi. is bhookh ko kisee saaph-sutharee dukaan se bhee shaant kiya jaa sakataa hain. aur padmaa ko saaph-sutharee dukaam kee hameshaa talaash rehti thi. graahak dukaan mein vaheen cheej letaa hain, jo use pasand aati hain. padmaa bhee vaheen cheej chaahatee thi. yon usake darjanon aashik the- kaee vakeel, kaee prophesar, kaee doktar, kaee raees. magar ye sab-ke-sab aiyyaas the- bephikr, keval bhaure kee tarah ras lekar uda jaane vaale. aisaa ek bhee na tha, jis par woh yakeen kar sakatee. ab use maaloom hua ki usakaa man keval bhog nahin chaahataa, kuchh aur bhee chaahataa hain. woh cheej kya thi? pooraa aatm-samarpan aur yah use na milataa tha.
usake premiyon mein ek mi. prasaad tha- badaa hee roopavaan aur dhurandhar vidvaan. ek kolej mein prophesar tha. woh bhee mukt-bhog kaa aadarsh kaa upaasak tha aur padmaa us par phidaa thi. chaahatee thi use baandhakar rakhe, sampoornatah apanaa banaa le; lekin prasaad changul mein na aat tha.
shaam ho gayee thi. padmaa sair karne jaa rahi thi ki prasaad aa gaye. sair karn multavee ho gaya. baatacheet mein sair se kaheen zyada khushi tha aur padmaa aaj prasaad se kuchh dil kee baat kehne vaalee thi. kaee din ke soch-soch ke baad usane kah daalane kaa phaisalaa kiya.
usane prasaad kee nasheelee aankhon se aankhen milaakar kahaa- tum yaheen mere bangale mein aakar kyon nahin rehte?
prasaad ne kutil-vinod ke saath kahaa- nateejaa yah hogaa ki do-chaar maheene mein yah mulaakaat band ho jaayegee.
'meree samajh mein nahee aay, tumhaaraa aashay kya hai. '
'aashay vaheen hain, jo main kah raha hoon. '
'aakhir kyon? '
'main apanee svaadheenataa na khonaa chaahoongaa, tum apanee svatantrataa na khonaa chaahogee. tumhaare paas tumhaare aashik aayenge, mujhe jalan hogee. mere paas meree premikaaen aayengee, tumhe jalan hogee. manamutaav hogaa, phir vaimanasy hogaa aur tum mujhe ghar se nikaal dogee. ghar tumhaaraa hai hee! mujhe buraa lagegaa hee, phir yah maitree kaise nibhegee? '
dono kaee minat tak chup rahe. prasaad ne paristhiti ko itane spasht, belaag, latthamaar shabdon mein kholakar rakh diya tha ki kuchh kehne kee jagah na milatee thi.
aakhir prasaad hee ko nukataa soojhaa. bol- jab tab ham donon yah pratijnaa na kar len ki aaj se main tumhaaraa hoon aur tum meree ho, tab tak ek saath guzaaraa nahin ho sakataa!
'tum yah pratijnaa karonge? '
'pahale tum batalaao. '
'main karoongee. '
'to main bhee karoongaa. '
'magar is ek baat ke sivaa main aur sabhee baaton mein svatantr rahoongee! '
'aur main bhee is ek baat ke sivaa har baat mein svatantr rahoongaa. '
'manjoor. '
'manjoor! '
'to kab se? '
'jab se tum kaho. '
'main to kehti hoon, kal hee se. '
'tay. lekin agar tumane isake viruddh aachaaran kiya to? '
'aur tumane kiya to? '
'tum mujhe ghar se nikaal sakatee ho; lekin main tumhe kya sajaa doongaa? '
'tum mujhe tyaag denaa, aur kya karoge? '
'jee nahin, tab itane se chitt ko shaanti na milegee. tab main chaahoongaa tumhe jaleel karn; balki tumhaaree hatyaa karn. '
'tum bahut nirdayee ho, prasaad? '
'jab tak ham donon svaadheen hain, ham kisee ko kuchh kehne kaa hak nahin, lekin ek baar pratijnaa mein bandh jaane ke baad phir ne main isakee avajnaa sah sakoongaa, na tum sah sakogee. tumhaare paas sazaa kaa saadhan hain, mere paas nahin. kaanoon mujhe koi bhee adhikaar nahin degaa. main to keval apanee pashubal se pratijnaa kaa paalan karaaoongaa aur tumhaare itane naukaron ke saamane main akelaa kya kar sakoongaa? '
'tum to chitr kaa shyaam paksh hee dekhate ho! jab main tumhaaree ho rahi hoon, to yah makaan, naukar-chaakar aur jaayadaad sab kuchh tumhaaraa hain. ham-tum donon jaanate hain ki jalan se zyada ghrinit koi saamaajik paap nahin hain. tumhe mujhasen pyaar hain yaa nahin, main nahin kah sakatee; lekin tumhaare lie main sab kuchh sahane, sab kuchh karne ke lie taiyaar hoon. '
'dil se kehti ho padmaa? '
'sachche dil se. '
'magar na-jaane kyon tumhaare oopar yakeen nahin aa raha hain? '
'main to tumhaare oopar yakeen kar rahi hoon. '
'yah samajh lo, mai mehamaan banakar tumhaare ghar mein na rahoongaa, swami banakar rahoongaa. '
'tum ghar ke swami hee nahee, mere swami banakar rahoge. main tumhaaree svaaminee ban kar rahoongee. '

2
pro. prasaad aur mis padmaa donon saath rehte hain aur khush hain. donon hee ne jeevan kaa jo aadarsh man mein sthir kar liya tha, woh saty ban gaya hain. prasaat ko keval do-sau rupaye vetan milataa hain; magar apa woh apanee aamadanee kaa dugunaa bhee kharch kar de to paravaah nahin. pahale woh kabhee-kabhee sharaab peetaa tha, ab raat-din sharaab mein mast rehta hain. ab usake lie alag apanee kaar hain, alag naukar hain, tarah-tarah kee bahumooly cheejen mangavaataa rehta hain aur padmaa bade harsh se usakee saaree phijool-kharjiyaan bardaasht karti hain. nahin, bardaasht karne kaa sawal nahin. woh khud use achchhe-se-achchhe soot pahanaakar achchhe-se-achchhe thaath mein rakhakar, khush hoti hain. jaisee ghadee is vakt prasaad ke paas hain, shahar ke bade-bade raees ke paas na hogee aur padmaa jitanaa hee usse dabatee hain, prasaad utanaa hee use dabaataa hain. kabhee-kabhee use naagavaar bhee lagataa hain, par kisee anajaan vajah se apne ko usake vash mein paatee hain. prasaad ko zara bhee udaas yaa chintit dekhakar usakaa man chanchal ho jat hain. us par aavaajen kasee jati hain; phabatiyaan chust kee jati hain. jo usake puraane premee hain, woh use jalaane aur kudhaane kaa koshish bhee karte hain; par woh prasaad ke paas aate hee sab kuchh bhool jati hain. prasaad ne us par pooraa aadhipaty paa liya hain aur use isakaa jnaan padmaa ko usane baareek aankhon se padhaa hain aur usakaa shaasan achchhee tarah paa gaya hain.
magar jaise raajaneeti ke kshetr mein adhikaar durupayog kee ora jat hain, usee tarah pyaar ke kshetr mein bhee woh durupayog kee ora hee jat hain aur jo kamajor hai, use taavaan denaa padataa hain. aatmaabhimaanee padmaa ab prasaad kee laudee thi aur prasaad usakee durbalataa kaa phaayadaa uthaane se kyon chookataa? usane keel kee patalee nok chubhaa lee thi aur badee kushalataa se uttarottar use andar thonkataa jat tha. yahan tak ki usane raat ko der se aan shuroo kiya. padmaa ko apne saath na le jat, usse bahaanaa kart ki mere sir mein dard hain aur jab padmaa ghumane jati to apanee kaar nikaal letaa aur uda jat. do saal gujar gaye aur padmaa ko garbh tha. woh sthool bhee ho chalee thi. usake roop mein pahale kee-see naveenataa aur maadakataa na raha gaee thi. woh ghar kee murgee thi, saag baraabar.
ek din isee tarah padmaa lautakar aayee, to prasaad gaayab the. woh jhunjhalaa uthee. idhar kaee din se woh prasaad kaa rang badalaa hua dekh rahi thi. aaj usane kuchh spasht baate kehne kaa saahas batoraa. das baj gaye, gyaarah baj gaye, baarah baj gaye, padmaa usake intajaar mein baithee thi. khana thandaa ho gaya, naukar-chaakar so gaye. woh baar-baar uthatee, phaatak par jaakar najar daudaatee. baahar-ek baje ke kareeb prasaad ghar aaye.
padmaa ne saahas batoraa tha; par prasaad ke saamane jate hee use itanee kamajoree maaloom huee. phir bhee usane zara kade aavaaz mein poochhaa- aaj itanee raat tak kahan the? kuchh khabar hain, kitanee raat hain?
prasaad ko woh is vakt asundarataa kee moorti-see lagee. woh ek skool kee chhaatraa ke saath sinemaa dekhane gaya tha. bol- tumako aaraam se so jan chaahie tha. tum jis dashaa mein ho, usamen tumhe, jahaan tak ho sake, aaraam se rahanaa chaahie.
padmaa kaa saahas kuchh prabal hua- tumse poochhatee hoon, usakaa jawab do. mujhe jahannum mein bhejo!
'to tum bhee mujhe jahannum mein jaane do. '
'tum mere saath dagaa kar rahe ho, yah main saaph dekh rahi hoon. '
'tumhaaree aankhon kee jyoti kuchh badh gayee hogee! '
'main idhar kaee dino se tumhaaraa mijaaj kuchh badalaa hua dekh rahi hoon. '
'main tumhaare saath apne ko bechaa nahee hain. agar tumhaaraa jee mujhse bhar gaya ho, to main aaj jaane ko taiyaar hoon. '
'tum jaane kee dhamakee kya dete ho! yahan tumane aakar koi badaa tyaag nahin kiya hain. '
'maine tyaag nahin kiya hain? tum yah kehne kaa saahas kar rahi ho. main dekhataa hoon, tumhaaraa mijaaz bigad raha hain. tum samajhatee ho, maine ise apang kar diya. magar main isee vakt tumhe thokar maarane ko taiyaar hoon, isee vakt, isee vakt! '
padmaa kaa saahas jaise bujh gaya tha. prasaad apanaa trank sanbhaal raha tha. padmaa ne gareeb-bhaav se kahaa- maine to aisee koi baat nahin kaheen, jo tum itanaa bigad uthe. main to keval tumse poochh raheen thi, kahan the. kya mujhe itanaa bhee adhikaar nahin denaa chaahate? main kabhee tumhaaree ichchhaa ke viruddh koi kaam nahin karti aur tum mujhe baat-baat par daantate rehte ho. tumhe mujh par zara bhee raham nahin aati. mujhe tumse kuchh bhee to hamadardee milanee chaahie. main tumhaare lie kya kuchh karne ko taiyaar nahin hoon? aur aaj jo meree dashaa ho gayee hain, to tum mujhse aankhe pher lete ho. ..
usakaa galaa rundh gaya aur mej par sir rakhakar phoot-phootakar rone lagee. prasaad ne pooree vijay paayee.
3
padmaa ke lie maatritv ab badaa hee apriy prasang tha. us par phikr mandaraatee rehti. kabhee-kabhee woh dar se kaanp uthatee aur pachhataatee. prasaad kee nirankushataa din-din badhatee jati thi. kya kare, kya na karen. garbh pooraa ho gaya tha, woh kort na jati thi. din-bhar akelee baithee rehti thi. prasaad shaam samay aate, chaay-vaay peekar phir uda jate, to gyaarah-baarah baje se pahale na lautate. woh kahan jate hain, yah bhee usse chhipaa na tha. prasaad ko to jaise usakee soorat se napharat thi. poorn garbh, peelaa munh, chintit, sashank, udaas. phir bhee woh prasaad ko shrringaar aur gahane se baandhane kee cheshtaa se baaj na aati thi. magar woh jitanaa hee koshish karti, utanaa hee prasaad kaa man usakee ora se phirataa tha. is avasthaa mein shrringaar use aur bhee bhaddaa lagataa.
prasav-dard ho rahi thi. prasaad kaa pataa nahin. nars maujood thi, ledee doktar maujood thi; magar prasaad kaa na rahanaa padmaa kee prasav dard ko aur bhee daaroon banaa raha tha.
ladakaa ko god mein dekhakar usakaa kalejaa phool uthaa, magar phir prasaad ko saamane na paakar usane ladakaa kee ora se munh pher liya. meethe phal mein jaise keede pad gaye hon.
paanch din saur-ghar mein kaatane ke baad jaise padmaa jelakhaane se nikalee- nangee talavaar banee huee. maan banakar woh apne mein ek adbhoot shakti kaa tajurbaa kar rahi thi.
usane chaparaasee ko chek dekar baink bhejaa. prasav-sambandhee kaee bil adaa karne the. chaparaasee khaalee haath laut aay.
padmaa ne poochhaa- rupaye?
'baink baaboo ne kahaa, rupaye prasaad baaboo nikaal le gaye. '
padmaa ko golee lag gayee. bees hajaar rupaye praanon kee tarah sanchit kar rakhe the, isee bachchaa ke lie. haay! saur se nikalane par maaloom hua, prasaad skool kee ek ladakee ko lekar ingalaind kee sair karne chale gaye. jhallaayee hua ghar mein aayee, prasaad kee tasaveer uthaakar jameen par patak dee aur use pairon se kuchalaa. usakaa jitanaa saamaan tha, use jamaa karake diyaasalaaee lagaa dee aur usake naam par thook diya.
ek maheenaa beet gaya tha. padmaa apne bangale ke phaatak par bachchaa ko god mein lie khadee thi. usakaa gussaa ab shokamay maayoosee ban chukaa tha. ladakaa par kabhee raham aati, kabhee pyaar aat, kabhee napharat hoti. usane sadak par dekh, ek yooropiyan ledee apne pati ke saath apne ladakaa ko gaadee mein bithaaye liye chalee jaa rahi thi. usane hasarat-bharee aankhon se us khushanaseeb jode ko dekh aur usakee aankhe sajal ho gayeen.
