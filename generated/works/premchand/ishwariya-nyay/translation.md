# Ishwariya Nyay

**Munshi Premchand**

1
kaanapur jile mein pandit bhrigudatt naamak ek bade jameendaar the. munshee satyanaaraayan unake kaarindaa the. vah bade svaamibhakt aur sachcharitr manushy the. laakhon rupaye ki tahaseel aur hajaaron man anaaj kaa len-den unake haath mein tha; par kabhi unki niyat daavondol na hoti. unake suprabandh se riyaasat dinondin unnati karti jati thi. aise kattarvyaparaayan sevak kaa jitanaa izzat hona chaahie, usse zyaadaa hi hot tha. du:kha-sukh ke har avasar par pandit jee unake saath badi udaarataa se pesh aate. dheere-dheere munshee jee kaa yakeen itanaa badhaa ki pandit jee ne hisaab-kitaab kaa samajhna bhi chhod diya. mumakin hai, unse aajeevan isi tarah nibh jati, par bhaavee prabal hai. prayaag mein kumbh lagaa, to pandit jee bhi snaan karne gaye. vahon se lautakar phir ve ghar na aaye. maaloom nahin, kisee gadhe mein phisal pade yaa koi jal-jantu unhen kheench le gaya, unakaa phir kuchh pataa hi na chalaa. ab munshee satyanaaraan ke adhikar aur bhi badhe. ek hatabhaaginee vidhavaa aur do chhote-chhote bachchon ke sivaa pandit jee ke ghar mein aur koi na tha. antyeshti-kriyaa se nivritt hokar ek din shokaatur panditaain ne unhen bulaayaa aur rokar kahaa—laalaa, pandit jee hamen manjhadhaar mein chhodakar surapur ko sidhar gaye, ab yah naiyaa tumhee paar lagaaoge to lag sakatee hai. yah sab khetee tumhari lagaayee hui hai, ise tumhaare hi oopar chhodatee hoon. ye tumhaare bachche hain, inhen apanaao. jab tak maalik jiye, tumhen apna bhai samajhte rahe. mujhe yakeen hai ki tum usi tarah is bhaar ko sanbhaale rahoge.
satyanaaraan ne rote hue jawab diya—bhaabhee, bhaiyaa kya uth gaye, mere to bhaagy hi phoot gaye, nahin to mujhe aadamee banaa dete. main unheen kaa namak khaakar jiyaa hoon aur unheen ki chaakaree mein marungaa bhi. aap dheeraj rakhen. kisee tarah ki phikr na karen. main jeete-jee aapakee sevaa se munh na modoongaa. aap keval itanaa keejiegaa ki main jis kisee ki shikaayat karun, use daonta deejiegaa; nahin to ye log sir chadh jaayenge.
2
is ghatanaa ke baad kaee varsho tak munsheejee ne riyaasat ko sanbhaalaa. vah apne kaam mein bade kushal the. kabhi ek kaudee kaa bhi bal nahin padaa. saare jile mein unakaa izzat hone lagaa. log pandit jee ko bhool-saa gaye. darabaaron aur kametiyon mein ve sammilit hote, jile ke adhikaaree unheen ko jameendaar samajhte. any raeeson mein unakaa aadar tha; par maan-vridvi ki mahangee vastu hai. aur bhaanukunvari, any striyon ke sadrish paise ko khoob pakadatee. vah manushy ki manovrittiyon se parichit na thi. pandit jee hameshaa laalaa jee ko inaam ikaraam dete rehte the. ve jaanate the ki jnaan ke baad eemaan kaa doosaraa stambh apni sudashaa hai. isake sivaa ve khud bhi kabhi kaagajon ki jaonch kar liya karte the. naamamaatr hi ko sahee, par is nigaraanee kaa dar jarur banaa rehta tha; kyonki eemaan kaa sabase bada shatru avasar hai. bhaanukunvari ina baaton ko jaanatee na thi. isalie avasar tathaa dhanaabhaav-jaise prabal shatruon ke panje mein pad kar munsheejee kaa eemaan kaise bedaag bachta?
kaanapur shahar se milaa hua, theek gangaa ke kinaare, ek bahut aajaad aur upajaaoo gaonva tha. pandit jee is gaonva ko lekar nadee-kinaare pakkaa ghaat, mandir, baag, makaan aadi banavaanaa chahte the; par unki yah chaah kaamayaab na ho sakee. sanyog se ab yah gaonva bikane lagaa. unake jameendaar ek thaakur sahab the. kisee phaujadaaree ke maamale mein phanse hue the. mukadamaa ladane ke lie rupaye ki chaah thi. munsheejee ne kachaharee mein yah khabar sunaa. chatapat mol-tol hua. donon taraph garaj thi. saudaa patane mein der na lagee, bainaamaa likhaa gaya. rajistree hui. rupaye maujood na the, par shahar mein saakh thi. ek mahaajan ke yahon se tees hajaar rupaye mangavaaye gaye aur thaakur sahab ko najar kiye gaye. haon, kaam-kaaj ki aasaanee ke khayaal se yah sab likhaa-padhee munsheejee ne apne hi naam ki; kyonki maalik ke ladake abhi naabaalig the. unake naam se lene mein bahut jhanjhat hoti aur vilamb hone se shikaar haath se nikal jat. munsheejee bainaamaa liye aseem khushi mein magn
bhaanukunvari ke paas aaye. pardaa karaayaa aur yah shubh-khabar sunaayaa. bhaanukunvari ne sajal netron se unako dhanyavaad diya. pandit jee ke naam par mandir aur ghaat banavaane kaa iraadaa pakkaa ho gaya.
munshee jee doosare hi din us gaonva mein aaye. aasaamee najaraane lekar naye svaamee ke svaagat ko haajir hue. shahar ke raeeson ki daavat hui. logon ke naavon par baith kar gangaa ki khoob sair ki. mandir aadi banavaane ke lie aabaadee se hat kar ramaneek sthaan chunaa gaya.
3
yadyapi is gaonva ko apne naam lete samay munshee jee ke man mein kapat kaa bhaav na tha, phir bhi do-chaar din mein hi unakaa ankur jam gaya aur dheere-dheere badhane lagaa. munshee jee is gaonva ke aay-vyay kaa hisaab alag rakhte aur apne svaaminon ko uska byoro samajhaane ki jarurat na samajhte. bhaanukunvari ina baaton mein dakhal dena theek na samajhti thi; par doosare kaarindon se baaten sun-sun kar use shankaa hoti thi ki kaheen munshee jee dagaa to na denge. apne man kaa bhaav munshee se chhipaatee thi, is khayaal se ki kaheen kaarindon ne unhen nukasaan pahunchaane ke lie yah shadayantr na rachaa ho.
is tarah kaee saal gujar gaye. ab us kapat ke ankur ne vriksh kaa rup dhaaran kiya. bhaanukunvari ko munshee jee ke us raastaa ke lakshan dikhaayee dene lage. udhar munshee jee ke man ne kanoon se neeti par vijay paayee, unhone apne man mein phaisalaa kiya ki gaonva mera hai. haon, main bhaanukunvari kaa tees hajaar kaa rinee avashy hoon. ve bahut karengee to apne rupaye le lengee aur kya kar sakatee hain? magar donon taraph yah aag andar hi andar sulagatee rahi. munshee jee astrasajjit hokar aakraman ke intajaar mein the aur bhaanukunvari isake lie avasar dhoondh rahi thi. ek din usane saahas karake munshee jee ko andar bulaayaa aur kahaa—laalaa jee ‘baragadaa’ ke mandir kaa kaam kab se lagavaaiegaa? use liye aath saal ho gaye, ab kaam lag jaay to achchha ho. zindagi kaa kaun thikaanaa hai, jo kaam karn hai; use kar hi daalanaa chaahie.
is dhang se is vishay ko uthaa kar bhaanukunvari ne apni chaturaaee kaa achchha parichay diya. munshee jee bhi dil mein isake kaayal ho gaye. zara soch kar bole—iraadaa to mera kaee baar hua, par mauke ki jameen nahin milatee. gangaatat ki jameen asaamiyon ke jot mein hai aur ve kisee tarah chhodane par raajee nahin.
bhaanukunvari—yah baat to aaj mujhe maaloom hui. aath saal hue, is gaonva ke vishay mein aapane kabhi bhool kar bhi dee to charchaa nahin ki. maaloom nahin, kitanee tahaseel hai, kya munaaphaa hai, kaisaa gaonva hai, kuchh seer hoti hai yaa nahin. jo kuchh karte hain, aap hi karte hain aur karenge. par mujhe bhi to maaloom hona chaahie?
munshee jee sanbhal uthe. unhen maaloom ho gaya ki is hoshiyaar aurat se baajee le jan mushkil hai. gaonva lena hi hai to ab kya dar. khul kar bole—aapako isase koi sarokaar na tha, isalie maine bekaar takaleeph dena munaasib na samajhaa.
bhaanukunvari ke dil mein kuthaar-saa lagaa. parde se nikal aayee aur munshee jee ki taraph tej onkhon se dekh kar bolee—aap kya kehte hain! aapane gaonva mere liye liya tha yaa apne lie! rupaye maine diye yaa aapane? us par jo kharch padaa, vah mera tha yaa aapakaa? meri samajh mein nahin aat ki aap kaisee baaten karte hain.
munshee jee ne saavadhaanee se jawab diya—yah to aap jaanatee hain ki gaonva hamaare naam se basaa hua hai. rupayaa jarur aapakaa lagaa, par main uska denadaar hoon. raha tahaseel-vasool kaa kharch, yah sab maine apne paas se diya hai. uska hisaab-kitaab, aay-vyay sab rakhta gaya hoon.
bhaanukunvari ne gussaa se kaonpate hue kahaa—is kapat kaa phal aapako avashy milegaa. aap is nirdayataa se mere bachchon kaa galaa nahin kaat sakate. mujhe nahin maaloom tha ki aapane dil mein chhuree chhipaa rakhee hai, nahin to yah naubat hi kyon aatee. khair, ab se meri rokad aur bahee khaataa aap kuchh na chhuen. mera jo kuchh hogaa, le loongee. jaaie, ekaant mein baith kar sochie. paap se kisee kaa bhalaa nahin hot. tum samajhte hoge ki ladakaa anaath hain, inakee sampatti hajam kar loongaa. is bhool mein na rehna, main tumhaare ghar ki eet tak bikavaa loongee.
yah kahakar bhaanukunvari phir parde ki aad mein aa baithee aur rone lagee. striyon gussaa ke baad kisee na kisee bahaane royaa karti hain. laalaa sahab ko koi jawab na soojhaa. yahon se uth aaye aur daphtar jaakar kaagaj ulat-palat karne lage, par bhaanukunvari bhi unake peechhe-peechhe daphtar mein pahunchee aur daonta kar bolee—mera koi kaagaj mata chhoonaa. nahin to buraa hogaa. tum vishaile saanp ho, main tumhara munh nahin dekhna chahti.
munshee jee kaagajon mein kuchh kaat-chhonta karn chahte the, par vivash ho gaye. khajaane ki kunjee nikaal kar phenk dee, bahee-khaate patak diye, kivaad dhadaake-se band kiye aur havaa ki tarah sann-se nikal gaye. kapat mein haath to daalaa, par kapat mantr na jan.
doosaren kaarindon ne yah kaiphiyat sunee, to phoole na samaaye. munshee jee ke saamane unki daal na galane paatee. bhaanukunvari ke paas aakar ve aag par tel chhidakane lage. sab log is vishay mein sahamat the ki munshee satyanaaraayan ne vishvaasaghaat kiya hai. maalik kaa namak unki haddiyon se phoot-phoot kar nikalegaa.
donon ora se mukadamebaajee ki taiyaariyon hone lageen! ek taraph nyaay kaa shareer tha, doosaree ora nyaay ki aatmaa. prakriti kaa aadamee se ladane kaa saahas hua.
bhaanakunvari ne laalaa chhakkan laal se poochhaa—hamaaraa vakeel kaun hai? chhakkan laal ne idhar-udhar jhaonka kar kahaa—vakeel to seth jee hain, par satyanaaraayan ne unhen pahale gaonth rakhaa hogaa. is mukadamen ke lie bade hoshiyaar vakeel ki jarurat hai. meharaa baaboo ki aajakal khoob chal rahi hai. haakim ki kalam pakad lete hain. bolte hain to jaise motarakaar chhoot jati hai sarakaar! aur kya kahen, kaee aadamiyon ko phaonsee se utaar liya hai, unake saamane koi vakeel jabaan to khol nahin sakataa. sarakaar kahen to wahi kar liye jaayan.
chhakkan laal ki atyukti se shak paidaa kar liya. bhaanukunvari ne kahaa—nahin, pahale seth jee se poochh liya jaay. usake baad dekh jaayagaa. aap jaaie, unhen bulaa laaie.
chhakkanalaal apni takadeer ko thonkate hue seth jee ke paas gaye. seth jee pandit bhrigudatt ke jeevan-kaal se hi unakaa kanoon-sambandhee sab kaam kiya karte the. mukadame kaa haal sunaa to sannaate mein aa gaye. satyanaaraan ko yah bada nekaneeyat aadamee samajhte the. unake patan se bada khed hua. usi vakt aaye. bhaanukunvari ne ro-ro kar unse apni vipatti ki kathaa kahin aur apne donon ladakon ko unake saamane khadaa karake bolee—aap ina anaathon ki rakshaa keejie. inhen main aapako saunpatee hoon.
seth jee ne samajhaute ki baat chhedee. bole—aapas ki ladaaee achchhi nahin.
bhaanukunvari—anyaayee ke saath ladanaa hi achchha hai.
seth jee—par hamaaraa paksh nirbal hai.
bhaanukunvari phir parde se nikal aayee aur vismit hokar bolee—kya hamaaraa paksh nirbal hai? duniyaa jaanatee hai ki gaonva hamaaraa hai. use hamse kaun le sakataa hai? nahin, main sulah kabhi na karungee, aap kaagajon ko dekhen. mere bachchon ki khaatir yah takaleeph uthaayen. aapakaa parishram nishphal na jaayagaa. satyanaaraayan ki neeyat pahale kharaab na thi. dekhie jis mitee mein gaonva liya gaya hai, us mitee mein tees hajaar kaa kya kharch dikhaayaa gaya hai. agar usane apne naam udhaar likhaa ho, to dekhie, vaarshik sood chukaayaa gaya yaa nahin. aise narapishaach se main kabhi sulah na karungee.
seth jee ne samajh liya ki is samay samajhaane-bujhaane se kuchh kaam na chalegaa. kaagajaat dekhen, abhiyog chalaane ki taiyaariyon hone lageen.
4
munshee satyanaaraayanalaal khisiyaaye hue makaan pahunche. ladake ne mithaaee maongee. use peetaa. aurat par isalie baras pade ki usane kyon ladake ko unake paas jaane diya. apni vriddhaa maan ko daonta kar kahaa—tumse itanaa bhi nahin ho sakataa ki zara ladake ko bahalaao? ek to main din-bhar kaa thakaa-maondaa ghar aaoon aur phir ladake ko khelaaoon? mujhe duniyaa mein na aur koi kaam hai, na dhandhaa. is tarah ghar mein baavailaa machaa kar baahar aaye, sochne lage—mujhse badi bhool hui. main kaisaa bevakooph hoon. aur itane din tak saare kaagaj-patr apne haath mein the. chahta, kar sakataa tha, par haath par haath dhare baithe raha. aaj sir par aa padee, to soojhee. main chahta to bahee-khaate sab naye banaa sakataa tha, jisamen is gaonva kaa aur rupaye kaa jikr hi na hot, par meri moorkhta ke vajah ghar mein aayee hui lakshmee ruthee jati hain. mujhe kya maaloom tha ki vah chudail mujhse is tarah pesh aayegee, kaagajon mein haath tak na lagaane degee.
isi udhedabun mein munshee jee ekaaek uchhal pade. ek upaay soojh gaya—kyon na kaaryakarttaaon ko milaa loon? yadyapi meri sakhtee ke vajah ve sab mujhse naaraaj the aur is samay seedhe baat bhi na karenge, phir bhi unamen aisaa koi bhi nahin, jo pralobhan se muththee mein na aa jaay. haon, isamen rupaye paani ki tarah bahaanaa padegaa, par itanaa rupayaa aayegaa kahon se? haay badakismatee? do-chaar din pahale chet gaya hot, to koi kathinaaee na padatee. kya jaanataa tha ki vah daain is tarah vajr-prahaar karegee. bas, ab ek hi upaay hai. kisee tarah kaagajaat gum kar doon. badi jokhim kaa kaam hai, par karn hi padegaa.
dushkaamanaaon ke saamane ek baar sir jhukaane par phir sanbhalanaa mushkil ho jat hai. paap ke athaah daladal mein jahon ek baar pade ki phir pratikshan neeche hi chale jaate hain. munshee satyanaaraayan-saa vichaarasheel manushy is samay is phikr mein tha ki kaise sendh lagaa paaoon!
munshee jee ne sochaa—kya sendh lagaanaa aasaan hai? isake vaaste kitanee chaturataa, kitanaa sahab, kitanee budvi, kitanee veerataa chaahie! kaun keht hai ki choree karn aasaan kaam hai? main jo kaheen pakadaa gaya, to marane ke sivaa aur koi raastaa na rahegaa.
bahut sochne-vichaarane par bhi munshee jee ko apne oopar aisaa dussaahas kar sakane kaa yakeen na ho sakaa. haon, isamen sugam ek doosaree tadabeer najar aayee—kyon na daphtar mein aag lagaa doon? ek botal mittee kaa tel aur diyaasalaaee ki jarurat hain kisee badamaash ko milaa loon, magar yah kya maaloom ki wahi usi kamare mein rakhee hai yaa nahin. chudail ne use jarur apne paas rakh liya hogaa. nahin; aag lagaanaa gunaah belajjat hogaa.
bahut der munshee jee karavaten badalate rahe. naye-naye manasoobe sochte; par phir apne hi tarko se kaat dete. varshaakaal mein baadalon ki nayee-nayee sooraten banatee aur phir havaa ke veg se bigad jati hain; wahi dashaa is samay unake manasoobon ki ho rahi thi.
par is maanasik ashaanti mein bhi ek soch poornarup se sthir tha—kisee tarah ina kaagajaat ko apne haath mein laanaa chaahie. kaam mushkil hai—maanaa! par himmat na thi, to raar kyon mol lee? kya tees hajaar ki jaayadaad daal-bhaat kaa kaur hai?—chaahe jis tarah ho, chor bane binaa kaam nahin chal sakataa. aakhir jo log choriyon karte hain, ve bhi to manushy hi hote hain. bas, ek chhalonga kaa kaam hai. agar paar ho gaye, to raaj karenge, gir pade, to jaan se haath dhoyenge.
5
raat ke das baj gaye. munshee satyanaaraan kunjiyon kaa ek guchchhaa kamar mein dabaaye ghar se baahar nikale. dvaar par thodaa-saa puaal rakhaa hua tha. use dekhte hi ve chaunk pade. maare dar ke chhaatee dhadakane lagee. jaan padaa ki koi chhipaa baithaa hai. kadam ruk gaye. puaal ki taraph dhyaan se dekh. usamen bilakul harakat na hui! tab himmat baondhee, aage bade aur man ko samajhaane lage—main kaisaa baukhal hoon
apne dvaar par kisakaa dar aur sadak par bhi mujhe kisakaa dar hai? main apni raah jat hoon. koi meri taraph tirachhee onkha se nahin dekh sakataa. haon, jab mujhe sendh lagaate dekh le—nahin, pakad le tab alabatte darane ki baat hai. tis par bhi bachaav ki yukti nikal sakatee hai.
akasmaat unhone bhaanukunvari ke ek chaparaasee ko aate hue dekh. kalejaa dhadak uthaa. lapak kar ek andheree galee mein ghus gaye. badi der tak vahon khade rahe. jab vah sipaahee onkhon se ojhal ho gaya, tab phir sadak par aaye. vah sipaahee aaj subah tak inakaa gulaam tha, use unhone kitanee hi baar gaaliyon dee theen, laaten maaree theen, par aaj use dekhakar unake praan sookh gaye.
unhone phir tark ki sharan lee. main maanon bhang khaakar aay hoon. is chaparaasee se itanaa daraa maano ki vah mujhe dekh letaa, par mera kar kya sakataa tha? hajaaron aadamee raastaa chal rahe hain. unheen mein main bhi ek hoon. kya vah antaryaamee hai? sabake dil kaa haal jaanataa hai? mujhe dekhakar vah adab se salaam kart aur vahon kaa kuchh haal bhi keht; par main usse aisaa daraa ki soorat tak na dikhaayee. is tarah man ko samajhaa kar ve aage badhe. sach hai, paap ke panjon mein phansaa hua man patajhad kaa pattaa hai, jo havaa ke zara-se jhonke se gir padataa hai.
munshee jee baajaar pahunche. adhikatar dookaanen band ho chukee theen. unamen saonda aur gaayen baithee hui jugaalee kar rahi thi. keval halavaaiyon ki dookaanen khulee thi aur kaheen-kaheen gajarevaale haar ki haonka lagaate phirate the. sab halavaaee munshee jee ko pahachaanate the, isalie munshee jee ne sir jhukaa liya. kuchh chaal badalee aur lapakate hue chale. ekaaek unhen ek bagghee aatee dikhaayee dee. yah seth ballabhadaas savakeel ki bagghee thi. isamen baithakar hajaaron baar seth jee ke saath kachaharee gaye the, par aaj vah bagghee kaaladev ke samaan bhayankar maaloom hui. phauran ek khaalee dookaan par chadh gaye. vahon vishraam karne vaale saonda ne samajhaa, ve mujhe padachyut karne aaye hain! maathaa jhukaaye phunkaarataa hua uth baithaa; par isi beech mein bagghee nikal gayi aur munshee jee ki jaan mein jaan aayee. abakee unhone tark kaa aashray na liya. samajh gaye ki is samay isase koi phaayadaa nahin, khairiyat yah hui ki vakeel ne dekh nahin. yah ek ghaagh hain. mere chehare se taad jat.
kuchh vidvaanon kaa kathan hai ki manushy ki svaabhaavik pravritti paap ki ora hoti hai, par yah koraa anumaan hi anumaan hai, anubhav-siddh baat nahin. sach baat to yah hai ki manushy svabhaavat: paap-bheeru hot hai aur ham pratyaksh dekh rahe hain ki paap se use kaisee napharat hoti hai.
ek pharlaang aage chal kar munshee jee ko ek galee milee. vah bhaanukunvari ke ghar kaa ek raastaa tha. dhundhalee-see laalaten jal rahi thi. jaisaa munshee jee ne anumaan kiya tha, paharedaar kaa pataa na tha. astabal mein chamaaron ke yahon naach ho raha tha. kaee chamaarinen banaav-singaar karake naach rahi theen. chamaar mridang bajaa-bajaa kar gaate the—
‘naaheen ghare shyaam, gheri aaye badaraa.
sovat raheun, sapan ek dekheun, raamaa.
khuli gayi neend, dharak gaye kajaraa.
naaheen ghare shyaam, gheri aaye badaraa.’
donon paharedaar wahi tamaashaa dekh rahe the. munshee jee dabe-paonva laalaten ke paas gae aur jis tarah billee choohe par jhapatatee hai, usi tarah unhone jhapat kar laalaten ko bujhaa diya. ek padaav pooraa ho gaya, par ve us kaary ko jitanaa dushkar samajhte the, utanaa na jaan padaa. dil kuchh mazboot hua. daphtar ke baraamade mein pahunche aur khoob kaan lagaakar aahat lee. chaaron ora sannaataa chhaayaa hua tha. keval chamaaron kaa kolaahal sunaayee detaa tha. is samay munshee jee ke dil mein dhadakan thi, par sir dhamadham kar raha tha; haath-paonva kaonpa rahe the, saonsa bade veg se chal rahi thi. shareer kaa ek-ek rom onkha aur kaan banaa hua tha. ve sajeevataa ki moorti ho rahe the. unamen jitanaa paurush, jitanee chapalataa, jitanaa-saahas, jitanee chetanaa, jitanee budvi, jitanaa ausaan tha, ve sab is vakt sajag aur sachet hokar ichchhaa-shakti ki madad kar rahe the.
daphtar ke daravaaje par wahi puraanaa taalaa lagaa hua tha. isakee kunjee aaj bahut talaash karake ve baajaar se laaye the. taalaa khul gaya, kivaado ne bahut dabee jabaan se pratirodh kiya. is par kisee ne dhyaan na diya. munshee jee daphtar mein daakhil hue. bheetar chiraag jal raha tha. munshee jee ko dekh kar usane ek daphe sir hilaayaa, maano unhen bheetar aane se rokaa.
munshee jee ke pair thar-thar kaonpa rahe the. ediyon jameen se uchhalee padatee theen. paap kaa bojh unhen asahy tha.
pal-bhar mein munshee jee ne bahiyon ko ulataa-palataa. likhaavat unki onkhon mein tair rahi thi. itanaa phursat kahon tha ki jaruree kaagajaat chhonta lete. unhonnen sari bahiyon ko samet kar ek gatthar banaayaa aur sir par rakh kar teer ke samaan kamare ke baahar nikal aaye. us paap ki gatharee ko laade hue vah andheree galee se gaayab ho gae.
tang, andheree, durgandhapoorn keechad se bharee hui galiyon mein ve nange paonva, svaarth, lobh aur kapat kaa bojh lie chale jaate the. maano paapamay aatmaa narak ki naaliyon mein bahee chalee jati thi.
bahut door tak bhatakane ke baad ve gangaa kinaare pahunche. jis tarah kalushit hridayon mein kaheen-kaheen dharm kaa dhundhalaa prakaash rehta hai, usi tarah nadee ki kaalee satah par taare jhilamilaa rahe the. tat par kaee saadhu dhoonee jamaaye pade the. jnaan ki jvaalaa man ki jagah baahar dahak rahi thi. munshee jee ne apna gatthar utaaraa aur chaadar se khoob mazboot baondh kar balapoorvak nadee mein phenk diya. sotee hui laharon mein kuchh halachal hui aur phir sannaataa ho gaya.
6
munshee satyanaaraayan laal ke ghar mein do striyon theen—maan aur patnee. ve donon ashikshitaa theen. tis par bhi munshee jee ko gangaa mein doob marane yaa kaheen bhaag jaane ki jarurat na hoti thi! na ve baodee pahanatee thi, na moje-joote, na haaramoniyam par gaa sakatee thi. yahon tak ki unhen saabun lagaanaa bhi na aat tha. heyarapin, bruchej, jaaket aadi paramaavashyak cheejon kaa to naam hi nahin sunaa tha. bahoo mein aatm-izzat zara bhi nahin tha; na saas mein aatm-gaurav kaa josh. bahoo ab tak saas ki ghudakiyon bheegee billee ki tarah sah letee thi—haa moorkhe! saas ko bachche ke nahalaane-dhulaane, yahon tak ki ghar mein jhaadoo dene se bhi napharat na thi, haa jnaanaandhe! bahoo aurat kya thi, mittee kaa londaa thi. ek paise ki jarurat hoti to saas se maongatee. saaraansh yah ki donon striyon apne adhikaaron se bekhabar, andhakaar mein padee hui pashuvat jeevan vyateet karti theen. aisee phoohad thi ki rotiyaan bhi apne haathon se banaa letee thi. kanjoosee ke maare daalamot, samose kabhi baajaar se na mangaateen. aagare vaale ki dookaan ki cheejen khaayee hoti to unakaa majaa jaanateen. budhiyaa khoosat davaa-darapan bhi jaanatee thi. baithee-baithee ghaas-paat kootaa karti.
munshee jee ne maon ke paas jaakar kahaa—ammon! ab kya hogaa? bhaanukunvari ne mujhe jawab de diya.
maan ne ghabaraa kar poochhaa—jawab de diya?
munshee—haon, bilakul bekasoor!
maan—kya baat hui? bhaanukunvari kaa mijaaj to aisaa na tha.
munshee—baat kuchh na thi. maine apne naam se jo gaonva liya tha, use maine apne adhikar mein kar liya. kal mujhse
aur unse saaph-saaph baaten hui. maine kah diya ki gaonva mera hai. maine apne naam se liya hai, usamen tumhara koi ijaaraa nahin. bas, bigad gayeen, jo munh mein aay, bakatee raheen. usi vakt mujhe nikaal diya aur dhamakaa kar kahaa—main tumse lad kar apna gaonva le loongee. ab aaj hi unki taraph se mere oopar mukadamaa daayar hogaa; magar isase hot kya hai? gaonva mera hai. us par mera kabjaa hai. ek nahin, hajaar mukadamen chalaaen, digaree meri hogee?
maan ne bahoo ki taraph marmaantak nazar se dekh aur bolee—kyon bhaiyaa? vah gaonva liya to tha tumne unheen ke rupaye se aur unheen ke vaaste?
munshee—liya tha, tab liya tha. ab mujhse aisaa aabaad aur maaladaar gaonva nahin chhodaa jat. vah mera kuchh nahin kar sakatee. mujhse apna rupayaa bhi nahin le sakatee. dedh sau gaonva to hain. tab bhi havas nahin maanatee.
maanaa—betaa, kisee ke paisaa zyada hot hai, to vah use phenk thode hi detaa hai? tumne apni neeyat bigaadee, yah achchha kaam nahin kiya. duniyaa tumhen kya kahegee? aur duniyaa chaahe kahe yaa na kahe, tumako bhalaa aisaa karn chaahie ki jisakee god mein itane din pale, jisakaa itane dinon tak namak khaayaa, ab usi se dagaa karo? naaraayan ne tumhen kya nahin diya? maje se khaate ho, pahanate ho, ghar mein naaraayan kaa diya chaar paisaa hai, baal-bachche hain, aur kya chaahie? mera kehna maano, is kalank kaa teekaa apne maathe na lagaao. yah apajas mata lo. barakkat apni kamaaee mein hoti hai; haraam ki kaudee kabhi nahin phalatee.
munshee—oonh! aisee baaten bahut sun chukaa hoon. duniyaa una par chalne lage, to saare kaam band ho jaayan. maine itane dinon inakee sevaa ki, meri hi badaulat aise-aise chaar-paanch gaonva badh gae. jab tak pandit jee the, meri neeyat kaa maan tha. mujhe onkha mein dhool daalane ki jarurat na thi, ve aap hi meri khaatir kar diya karte the. unhen mare aath saal ho gae; magar musammaat ke ek beede paan ki kasam khaataa hoon; meri jaat se unako hajaaron rupaye-maasik ki bachat hoti thi. kya unako itanee bhi samajh na thi ki yah bechaaraa, jo itanee eemaanadaaree se mera kaam kart hai, is naphe mein kuchh use bhi milna chaahie? yah kah kar na do, inaam kah kar do, kisee tarah do to, magar ve to samajhti thi ki maine ise bees rupaye mahine par mol le liya hai. maine aath saal tak sab kiya, ab kya isi bees rupaye mein gulaamee kart rahoon aur apne bachchon ko doosaron kaa munh taakane ke lie chhod jaaoon? ab mujhe yah avasar milaa hai. ise kyon chhodoon? jameendaaree ki laalasaa liye hue kyon marun? jab tak jeeoongaa, khud khaaoongaa. mere peechhe mere bachche chain udaayenge.
maan ki onkhon mein onsoo bhar aaye. bolee—betaa, maine tumhaare munh se aisee baaten kabhi nahin sunee theen, tumhen kya ho gaya hai? tumhaare aage baal-bachche hain. aag mein haath na daalo.
bahoo ne saas ki ora dekh kar kahaa—hamako aisaa paisaa na chaahie, ham apni daal-rotee mein magan hain.
munshee—achchhi baat hai, tum log rotee-daal khaanaa, gaadhaa pahananaa, mujhe ab halve-pooree ki ichchhaa hai.
maan—yah adharm mujhse na dekh jaayagaa. main gangaa mein doob marungee.
patnee—tumhen yah sab kaontaa bonaa hai, to mujhe maayake pahunchaa do, main apne bachchon ko lekar is ghar mein na rahoongee!
munshee ne jhunjhalaa kar kahaa—tum logon ki budvi to bhaonga khaa gayi hai. laakhon sarakaaree naukar raat-din doosaron kaa galaa
dabaa-dabaa kar rishvaten lete hain aur chain karte hain. na unake baal-bachchon hi ko kuchh hot hai, na unheen ko haijaa pakadataa hai. adharm unako kyon nahin khaa jat, jo mujhee ko khaa jaayagaa. maine to satyavaadiyon ko sadaa du:kha jhelate hi dekh hai. maine jo kuchh kiya hai, sukh lootoongaa. tumhaare man mein jo aaye, karo.
praat:kaal daphtar khulaa to kaagajaat sab gaayab the. munshee chhakkanalaal baukhalaaye se ghar mein gaye aur maalakin se poochhaa—kaagajaat aapane uthavaa lie hain.
bhaanukunvari ne kahaa—mujhe kya khabar, jahon aapane rakhe honge, vaheen honge.
phir saare ghar mein khalabalee pad gayi. paharedaaron par maar padane lagee. bhaanukunvari ko turant munshee satyanaaraayan par shak hua, magar unki samajh mein chhakkanalaal ki madad ke binaa yah kaam hona naamumakin tha. pulis mein rapat hui. ek ojhaa naam nikaalane ke lie bulaayaa gaya. maulavee sahab ne kurraa phenkaa. ojhaa ne bataayaa, yah kisee puraane bairee kaa kaam hai. maulavee sahab ne pharamaayaa, kisee ghar ke bhediye ne yah harakat ki hai. shaam tak yah daud-dhoop rahi. phir yah salaah hone lagee ki ina kaagajaaton ke bagair mukadamaa kaise chale. paksh to pahale se hi nirbal tha. jo kuchh bal tha, vah isi bahee-khaate kaa tha. ab to saboot bhi haath se gaye. daave mein kuchh jaan hi na rahi, magar bhaanakunvari ne kahaa—balaa se haar jaaenge. hamaaree cheej koi chheen le, to hamaaraa dharm hai ki usse yathaashakti laden, haar kar baithanaa kaayaron kaa kaam hai. seth jee (vakeel) ko is durghatanaa kaa khabar milaa to unhone bhi yahi kahaa ki ab daave mein zara bhi jaan nahin hai. keval anumaan aur tark kaa bharosaa hai. adaalat ne maanaa to maanaa, nahin to haar maananee padegee. par bhaanukunvari ne ek na maanee. lakhanaoo aur ilaahaabaad se do hoshiyaar bairistir bulaaye. mukadamaa shuru ho gaya.
saare shahar mein is mukadamen ki dhoom thi. kitane hi raeeson ko bhaanukunvari ne saathee banaayaa tha. mukadamaa shuru hone ke samay hajaaron aadamiyon ki bheed ho jati thi. logon ke is khinchaav kaa mukhy vajah yah tha ki bhaanukunvari ek parde ki aad mein baithee hui adaalat ki kaaravaaee dekh karti thi, kyonki use ab apne naukaron par zara bhi yakeen na tha.
vaadee bairistar ne ek badi maarmik vaktritaa dee. usane satyanaaraan ki poorvaavasthaa kaa khoob achchha chitr kheenchaa. usane dikhalaayaa ki ve kaise svaamibhakt, kaise kaary-kushal, kaise karm-sheel the; aur svargavaasee pandit bhrigudatt kaa us par poorn yakeen ho jan, kis tarah svaabhaavik tha. isake baad usane siddh kiya ki munshee satyanaaraayan ki aarthik intazaam kabhi aisee na thi ki ve itanaa paisaa-sanchay karte. ant mein usane munshee jee ki svaarthaparataa, kootaneeti, nirdayataa aur yakeen-ghaatakataa kaa aisaa ghrinotpaadak chitr kheenchaa ki log munshee jee ko goliyon dene lage. isake saath hi usane pandit jee ke anaath baalakon ki dashaa kaa bada karoonotpaadak varnan kiya—kaise shok aur sharm ki baat hai ki aisaa charitravaan, aisaa neeti-kushal manushy itanaa gir jaay ki apne svaamee ke anaath baalakon ki gardan par chhuree chalaane par sankoch na kare. maanav-patan kaa aisaa karun, aisaa dil-vidaarak udaaharan milna mushkil hai. is kutil kaary ke nateejaa ki nazar se is manushy ke pahale parichit sadagunon kaa gaurav lupt ho jat hai. kyonki ve asalee motee nahin, nakalee kaonch ke daane the, jo keval yakeen jamaane ke nimitt darshaaye gaye the. vah keval sundar jaal tha, jo ek aasaan dil aur chhal-chhand se door rehne vaale raees ko phansaane ke lie phailaayaa gaya tha. is nar-pashu kaa ant:karan kitanaa andhakaaramay, kitanaa kapatapoorn, kitanaa kathor hai; aur isakee dushtataa kitanee ghor, kitanee apaavan hai. apne shatru ke saath dayaa karn ek baar to kshamy hai, magar is malin dil manushy ne una bekason ke saath dagaa diya hai, jin par maanav-svabhaav ke anusaar dayaa karn theek hai! yadi aaj hamaare paas bahee-khaate maujood hote, adaalat par satyanaaraayan ki satyataa spasht rup se prakat ho jati, par munshee jee ke barakhaast hote hi daphtar se unakaa lupt ho jan bhi adaalat ke lie ek bada saboot hai.
shahar mein kaee raeeson ne gavaahee dee, par sunee-sunaayee baaten jirah mein ukhad gayeen. doosare din phir mukadamaa pesh hua.
prativaadee ke vakeel ne apni vaktritaa shuru ki. usamen ganbheer vichaaron ki apekshaa haasy kaa aadhiky tha—yah ek vilakshan nyaay-siddhaant hai ki kisee dhanaadhay manushy kaa naukar jo kuchh khareede, vah usake svaamee ki cheej samajhee jaay. is siddhaant ke anusaar hamaaree gavarnament ko apne karmachaariyon ki sari sampatti par kabjaa kar lena chaahie. yah sveekaar karne mein hamako koi aapatti nahin ki ham itane rupayon kaa intazaam na kar sakate the aur yah paisaa hamne svaamee hi se rin liya; par hamse rin chukaane kaa koi takaajaa na karake vah jaayadaad hi maongee jati hai. yadi hisaab ke kaagajaat dikhalaaye jaayan, to ve saaph bataa denge ki main sara rin de chukaa. hamaare mitr ne kahaa ki aisee avasthaa mein bahiyon kaa gum ho jan bhi adaalat ke liye ek saboot hona chaahie. main bhi unki yukti kaa samarthan kart hoon. yadi main aapase rin le kar apna vivaah karun to kya mujhse meri nav-vivaahit vadhoo ko chheen lenge?
‘hamaare suyog mitr ne hamaare oopar anaathon ke saath dagaa karne kaa dosh lagaayaa hai. agar munshee satyanaaraan ki neeyat kharaab hoti, to unake lie sabase achchha avasar vah tha jab pandit bhrigudatt kaa svargavaas hua tha. itane vilamb ki kya jarurat thi? yadi aap sher ko phansaa kar usake bachche ko usi vakt nahin pakad lete, use badhane aur sabal hone kaa avasar dete hain, to main aapako budvimaan na kahoongaa. yathaarth baat yah hai ki munshee satyanaaraan ne namak kaa jo kuchh hak tha, vah pooraa kar diya. aath varsh tak tan-man se svaamee ke bachchaa ki sevaa ki. aaj unhen apni saadhutaa kaa jo phal mil raha hai, vah bahut hi du:khajanak aur dil-vidaarak hai. isamen bhaanukunvari kaa dosh nahin. ve ek gun-sampann mahilaa hain; magar apni jaati ke avagun unamen bhi vidyamaan hain! eemaanadaar manushy svabhaavat: spashtabhaashee hot hai; use apni baaton mein namak-mirch lagaane ki jarurat nahin hoti. yahi vajah hai ki munshee jee ke mridubhaashee maatahaton ko una par aakshep karne kaa maukaa mil gaya. is daave ki jad keval itanee hi hai, aur kuchh nahin. bhaanukunvari yahon upasthit hain. kya ve kah sakatee hain ki is aath varsh ki muddat mein kabhi is gaonva kaa jikr unake saamane aay? kabhi usake nukasaan-phaayadaa, aay-vyay, len-den ki charchaa unse ki gayi? maan leejie ki main gavarnament kaa mulaajim hoon. yadi main aaj daphtar mein aakar apni patnee ke aay-vyay aur apne tahaluon ke taikson kaa pachadaa gaane lagoon, to shaayad mujhe jaldee hi apne pad se prithak hona pade, aur mumakin hai, kuchh dinon tak barelee ki atithishaalaa mein bhi rakhaa jaaoon. jis gaonva se bhaanukunvari kaa sarovaar na tha, uski charchaa unse kyon ki jati?’
isake baad bahut se gavaah pesh hue; jinamen adhikaansh aas-paas ke dehaaton ke jameendaar the. unhone bayaan kiya ki hamne munshee satyanaaraayan asaamiyon ko apni dastakhti raseeden aur apne naam se khajaane mein rupayaa daakhil karte dekh hai.
itane mein shaam ho gayi. adaalat ne ek saptaah mein phaisalaa sunaane kaa hukm diya.
7
satyanaaraayan ko ab apni jeet mein koi shak na tha. vaadee paksh ke gavaah bhi ukhad gaye the aur bahas bhi saboot se khaalee thi. ab inakee ginatee bhi jameendaaron mein hogee aur mumakin hai, yah kuchh dinon mein raees kahalaane lagenge. par kisee na kisee vajah se ab shahar ke ganamaany purushon se onkhen milaate sharmaate the. unhen dekhte hi unakaa sir neechaa ho jat tha. vah man mein darate the ki ve log kaheen is vishay par kuchh poochh-taachh na kar baithen. vah baajaar mein nikalate to dookaanadaaron mein kuchh kaanaaphoosee hone lagti aur log unhen tirachhee nazar se dekhne lagte. ab tak log unhen vivekasheel aur sachcharitr manushy samajhte, shahar ke dhanee-maanee unhen ijjat ki nigaah se dekhte aur unakaa bada aadar karte the. yadyapi munshee jee ko ab tak inse tedhee-tirachhee sunane kaa sanyog na padaa tha, phir bhi unakaa man keht tha ki sachchee baat kisee se chhipee nahin hai. chaahe adaalat se unki jeet ho jaay, par unki saakh ab jati rahi. ab unhen log svaarthee, kapatee aur dagaabaaj samajhenge. doosaron ki baat to alag rahi, svayan unake gharavaale unki upekshaa karte the. boodhee maan ne teen din se munh mein paani nahin daalaa! aurat baar-baar haath jod kar kehti thi ki apne pyaare baalakon par dayaa karo. bure kaam kaa phal kabhi achchha nahin hot! nahin to pahale mujhee ko vish khilaa do.
jis din phaisalaa sunaayaa jaanevaalaa tha, praat:kaal ek kunjadin tarakaariyon lekar aayee aur munshiyaain se bolee—
‘bahoo jee! hamne baajaar mein ek baat sunee hai. buraa na maanon to kahoon? jisako dekho, usake munh se yahi baat nikalatee hai ki laalaa baaboo ne jaalasaajee se panditaain kaa koi halakaa le liya. hamen to is par yakeen nahin aat. laalaa baaboo ne na sanbhaalaa hot, to ab tak panditaain kaa kaheen pataa na lagta. ek angul jameen na bachti. inheen mein ek saradaar tha ki sabako sanbhaal liya. to kya ab unheen ke saath badi karenge? are bahoo! koi kuchh saath laayaa hai ki le jaayagaa? yahi nek-badi raha jati hai. bure kaa phal buraa hot hai. aadamee na dekhe, par allaah sab kuchh dekhta hai.’
bahoo jee par ghadon paani pad gaya. jee chahta tha ki dharatee phat jati, to usamen samaa jati. striyon svabhaavat: lajjaavatee hoti hain. unamen aatmaabhimaan ki maatraa zyaadaa hoti hai. nindaa-beizzatee unse sahan nahin ho sakataa hai. sir jhukaaye hue bolee—buaa! main ina baaton ko kya jaanoon? maine to aaj hi tumhaare munh se sunee hai. kaun-see tarakaariyon hain?
munshee satyanaaraayan apne kamare mein lete hue kunjadin ki baaten sun rahe the, usake chale jaane ke baad aakar aurat se poochhne lage—yah shaitaan ki khaalaa kya kah rahi thi.
aurat ne pati ki ora se munh pher liya aur jameen ki ora taakate hue bolee—kya tumne nahin sunaa? tumhara gun-gaan kar rahi thi. tumhaare peechhe dekho, kis-kisake munh se ye baaten sunanee padatee hain aur kis-kisase munh chhipaanaa padataa hai.
munshee jee apne kamare mein laut aaye. aurat ko kuchh jawab nahin diya. aatmaa sharm se paraast ho gayi. jo manushy hameshaa sarv-sammaanit raha ho; jo sadaa aatmaabhimaan se sir uthaa kar chalta raha ho, jisakee sukriti ki saare shahar mein charchaa hoti ho, vah kabhi sarvathaa lajjaashoony nahin ho sakataa; sharm kupath ki sabase badi shatru hai. kuvaasanaaon ke bhram mein pad kar munshee jee ne samajhaa tha, main is kaam ko aisee gupt-reeti se pooraa kar le jaaoongaa ki kisee ko kaanon-kaan khabar na hogee, par unakaa yah manorath siddh na hua. baadhaaen aa khadee hui. unake hataane mein unhen bade dussaahas se kaam lena padaa; par yah bhi unhone sharm se bachane ke nimitt kiya. jisamen yah koi na kahe ki apni svaaminee ko dhokhaa diya. itanaa yatn karne par bhi nindaa se na bach sake. baajaar kaa saudaa bechanevaaliyon bhi ab beizzatee karateen hain. kuvaasanaaon se dabee hui sharm-shakti is kadee chot ko sahan na kar sakee. munshee jee sochne lage, ab mujhe paisaa-sampatti mil jaayagee, aishvaryavaan ho jaaoongaa, lekin nindaa se mera peechhaa na chhootegaa. adaalat kaa phaisalaa mujhe lok-nindaa se na bachaa sakegaa. aishvary kaa phal kya hai?—maan aur maryaadaa. usse haath dho baithaa, to aishvary ko lekar kya karungaa? chitt ki shakti khokar, lok-sharm sahakar, janasamudaay mein neech ban kar aur apne ghar mein kalah kaa beej bokar yah sampatti mere kis kaam aayegee? aur yadi vaastav mein koi nyaay-shakti ho aur vah mujhe is kukrity kaa dand de, to mere lie sivaa munh mein kaalikh lagaa kar nikal jaane ke aur koi raastaa na rahegaa. satyavaadee manushy par koi vipatt padatee hain, to log unake saath sahaanubhooti karte hain. dushton ki vipatti logon ke lie vyangy ki saamagree ban jati hai. us avasthaa mein eeshvar anyaayee thaharaayaa jat hai; magar dushton ki vipatti eeshvar ke nyaay ko siddh karti hai. paramaatman! is durdashaa se kisee tarah mera uddhaar karo! kyon na jaakar main bhaanukunvari ke pairon par gir padoon aur vinay karun ki yah mukadamaa uthaa lo? shok! pahale yah baat mujhe kyon na soojhee? agar kal tak mein unake paas chalaa gaya hot, to baat ban jati; par ab kya ho sakataa hai. aaj to phaisalaa sunaayaa jaayagaa.
munshee jee der tak isi soch mein pade rahe, par kuchh phaisalaa na kar sake ki kya karen.
bhaanukunvari ko bhi yakeen ho gaya ki ab gaonva haath se gaya. bechaaree haath mal kar raha gayi. raat-bhar use neend na aayee, raha-raha kar munshee satyanaaraayan par gussaa aat tha. haay paapee! dhol bajaa kar mera pachaas hajaar kaa maal lie jat hai aur main kuchh nahin kar sakatee. aajakal ke nyaay karne vaale bilakul onkha ke andhe hain. jis baat ko sari duniyaa jaanatee hai, usamen bhi unki nazar nahin pahunchti. bas, doosaron ko onkhon se dekhte hain. kore kaagajon ke gulaam hain. nyaay vah hai jo doodh kaa doodh, paani kaa paani kar de; yah nahin ki khud hi kaagajon ke dhokhe mein aa jaay, khud hi paakhandiyon ke jaal mein phans jaay. isi se to aisee chhalee, kapatee, dagaabaaj, aur duraatmaaon kaa saahas badh gaya hai. khair, gaonva jat hai to jaay; lekin satyanaaraayan, tum shahar mein kaheen munh dikhaane ke laayak bhi na rahe.
is khayaal se bhaanukunvari ko kuchh shaanti hui. shatru ki nukasaan manushy ko apne phaayadaa se bhi zyaadaa priy hoti hai, maanav-svabhaav hi kuchh aisaa hai. tum hamaaraa ek gaonva le gaye, naaraayan chaahenge to tum bhi isase sukh na paaoge. tum aap narak ki aag mein jaloge, tumhaare ghar mein koi diya jalaane vaalaa na raha jaayagaa.
phaisale kaa din aa gaya. aaj ijalaas mein badi bheed thi. aise-aise mahaanubhaav upasthit the, jo bagulon ki tarah aphasaron ki badhaaee aur bidaaee ke avasaron hi mein najar aay karte hain. vakeelon aur mukhtaaron ki palatan bhi jamaa thi. niyat samay par jaj sahab ne ijalaas sushobhit kiya. vistrit nyaay bhavan mein sannaataa chhaa gaya. ahalamad ne
sandook se tajabeej nikaalee. log utsuk hokar ek-ek kadam aur aage khisak gae.
jaj ne phaisalaa sunaayaa—muddaee kaa daavaa khaarij. donon paksh apna-apna kharch sah len.
yadyapi phaisalaa logon ke anumaan ke anusaar hi tha, phir bhi jaj ke munh se use sun kar logon mein halachal-see mach gayi. udaaseen bhaav se phaisale par aalochanaaen karte hue log dheere-dheere kamare se nikalne lage.
ekaaek bhaanukunvari ghoonghat nikaale ijalaas par aa kar khadee ho gayi. jaanevaale laut pade. jo baahar nikal gaye the, daud kar aa gaye. aur kautoohalapoorvak bhaanukunvari ki taraph taakane lage.
bhaanukunvari ne kanpit aavaaz mein jaj se kahaa—sarakaar, yadi hukm den, to main munshee jee se kuchh poochhoon.
yadyapi yah baat niyam ke viruddh thi, phir bhi jaj ne dayaapoorvak hukm de dee.
tab bhaanukunvari ne satyanaaraayan ki taraph dekh kar kahaa—laalaa jee, sarakaar ne tumhari digree to kar hi dee. gaonva tumhen mubaarak rahe; magar eemaan aadamee kaa sab kuchh hai. eemaan se kah do, gaonva kisakaa hai?
hajaaron aadamee yah sawal sun kar kautoohal se satyanaaraayan ki taraph dekhne lage. munshee jee soch-saagar mein doob gaye. dil mein sankalp aur vikalp mein ghor sangraam hone lagaa. hajaaron manushyon ki onkhen unki taraph jamee hui theen. yathaarth baat ab kisee se chhipee na thi. itane aadamiyon ke saamane asaty baat munh se nikal na sakee. sharm se jabaan band kar lee—‘mera’ kehne mein kaam banataa tha. koi baat na thi; lekin ghoratam paap kaa dand samaaj de sakataa hai, usake milne kaa pooraa dar tha. ‘aapakaa’ kehne se kaam bigadataa tha. jeetee-jitaayee baajee haath se nikalee jati thi, sarvotkrisht kaam ke lie samaaj se jo inaam mil sakataa hai, usake milne ki pooree ummeed thi. ummeed ke dar ko jeet liya. unhen aisaa lagaa hua, jaise eeshvar ne mujhe apna munh ujjaval karne kaa yah antim avasar diya hai. main ab bhi maanav-izzat kaa paatr ban sakataa hoon. ab apni aatmaa ki rakshaa kar sakataa hoon. unhone aage badh kar bhaanukunvari ko pranaam kiya aur kaonpate hue aavaaz se bole—aapakaa!
hajaaron manushyon ke munh se ek gaganasparshee dhvani nikalee—saty ki jay!
jaj ne khade hokar kahaa—yah kanoon kaa nyaay nahin, eeshvareey nyaay hai! ise kathaa na samajhiegaa; yah sachchee ghatanaa hai. bhaanukunvari aur saty naaraayan ab bhi jeevit hain. munshee jee ke is naitik saahas par log mugadh ho gae.
maanaveey nyaay par eeshvareey nyaay ne jo vilakshan vijay paayee, uski charchaa shahar bhar mein maheenon rahi. bhaanukunvari munshee jee ke ghar gayi, unhen manaa kar laayeen. phir apna sara kaarobaar unhen saunpaa aur kuchh dinon uparaant yah gaonva unheen ke naam hibbaa kar diya. munshee jee ne bhi use apne adhikar mein rakhna theek na samajhaa, krishnaarpan kar diya. ab isakee aamadanee deen-dukhiyon aur vidyaarthiyon ki madad mein kharch hoti.
