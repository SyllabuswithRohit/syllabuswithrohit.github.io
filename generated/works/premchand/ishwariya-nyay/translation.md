# Ishwariya Nyay

**Munshi Premchand**

1
kaanapur jile mein pandit bhrigudatt naamak ek bade jameendaar the| munshee satyanaaraayan unake kaarindaa the| vah bade svaamibhakt aur sachcharitr manushy the| laakhon rupaye kee tahaseel aur hajaaron man anaaj kaa len-den unake haath mein tha; par kabhee unakee niyat daavondol na hoti| unake suprabandh se riyaasat dinondin unnati karti jati thi| aise kattarvyaparaayan sevak kaa jitanaa izzat honaa chaahie, usse zyaadaa hee hot tha| du:kha-sukh ke har avasar par pandit jee unake saath badee udaarataa se pesh aate| dheere-dheere munshee jee kaa yakeen itanaa badhaa ki pandit jee ne hisaab-kitaab kaa samajhanaa bhee chhod diya| mumakin hai, unse aajeevan isee tarah nibh jati, par bhaavee prabal hai| prayaag mein kumbh lagaa, to pandit jee bhee snaan karne gaye| vahon se lautakar phir ve ghar na aaye| maaloom nahin, kisee gadhe mein phisal pade yaa koee jal-jantu unhen kheench le gaya, unakaa phir kuchh pataa hee na chalaa| ab munshee satyanaaraan ke adhikaar aur bhee badhe| ek hatabhaaginee vidhavaa aur do chhote-chhote bachchon ke sivaa pandit jee ke ghar mein aur koee na tha| antyeshti-kriyaa se nivritt hokar ek din shokaatur panditaain ne unhen bulaayaa aur rokar kahaa—laalaa, pandit jee hamen manjhadhaar mein chhodakar surapur ko sidhar gaye, ab yah naiyaa tumhee paar lagaaoge to lag sakatee hai| yah sab khetee tumhaaree lagaayee huee hai, ise tumhaare hee oopar chhodatee hoon| ye tumhaare bachche hain, inhen apanaao| jab tak maalik jiye, tumhen apanaa bhai samajhate rahe| mujhe yakeen hai ki tum usee tarah is bhaar ko sanbhaale rahoge|
satyanaaraan ne rote hue javaab diya—bhaabhee, bhaiyaa kyaa uth gaye, mere to bhaagy hee phoot gaye, nahin to mujhe aadamee banaa dete| main unheen kaa namak khaakar jiyaa hoon aur unheen kee chaakaree mein marungaa bhee| aap dheeraj rakhen| kisee tarah kee phikr na karen| main jeete-jee aapakee sevaa se munh na modoongaa| aap keval itanaa keejiegaa ki main jis kisee kee shikaayat karun, use daonta deejiegaa; nahin to ye log sir chadh jaayenge|
2
is ghatanaa ke baad kaee varsho tak munsheejee ne riyaasat ko sanbhaalaa| vah apne kaam mein bade kushal the| kabhee ek kaudee kaa bhee bal nahin padaa| saare jile mein unakaa izzat hone lagaa| log pandit jee ko bhool-saa gaye| darabaaron aur kametiyon mein ve sammilit hote, jile ke adhikaaree unheen ko jameendaar samajhate| any raeeson mein unakaa aadar tha; par maan-vridvi kee mahangee vastu hai| aur bhaanukunvari, any striyon ke sadrish paise ko khoob pakadatee| vah manushy kee manovrittiyon se parichit na thi| pandit jee hameshaa laalaa jee ko inaam ikaraam dete rahate the| ve jaanate the ki jnaan ke baad eemaan kaa doosaraa stambh apanee sudashaa hai| isake sivaa ve khud bhee kabhee kaagajon kee jaonch kar liya karte the| naamamaatr hee ko sahee, par is nigaraanee kaa dar jarur banaa rahataa tha; kyonki eemaan kaa sabase badaa shatru avasar hai| bhaanukunvari ina baaton ko jaanatee na thi| isalie avasar tathaa dhanaabhaav-jaise prabal shatruon ke panje mein pad kar munsheejee kaa eemaan kaise bedaag bachataa?
kaanapur shahar se milaa hua, theek gangaa ke kinaare, ek bahut aajaad aur upajaaoo gaonva tha| pandit jee is gaonva ko lekar nadee-kinaare pakkaa ghaat, mandir, baag, makaan aadi banavaanaa chaahate the; par unakee yah chaah kaamayaab na ho sakee| sanyog se ab yah gaonva bikane lagaa| unake jameendaar ek thaakur sahab the| kisee phaujadaaree ke maamale mein phanse hue the| mukadamaa ladane ke lie rupaye kee chaah thi| munsheejee ne kachaharee mein yah khabar sunaa| chatapat mol-tol hua| donon taraph garaj thi| saudaa patane mein der na lagee, bainaamaa likhaa gaya| rajistree huee| rupaye maujood na the, par shahar mein saakh thi| ek mahaajan ke yahon se tees hajaar rupaye mangavaaye gaye aur thaakur sahab ko najar kiye gaye| haon, kaam-kaaj kee aasaanee ke khayaal se yah sab likhaa-padhee munsheejee ne apne hee naam kee; kyonki maalik ke ladake abhee naabaalig the| unake naam se lene mein bahut jhanjhat hoti aur vilamb hone se shikaar haath se nikal jat| munsheejee bainaamaa liye aseem khushee mein magn
bhaanukunvari ke paas aaye| pardaa karaayaa aur yah shubh-khabar sunaayaa| bhaanukunvari ne sajal netron se unako dhanyavaad diya| pandit jee ke naam par mandir aur ghaat banavaane kaa iraadaa pakkaa ho gaya|
munshee jee doosare hee din us gaonva mein aaye| aasaamee najaraane lekar naye svaamee ke svaagat ko haajir hue| shahar ke raeeson kee daavat huee| logon ke naavon par baith kar gangaa kee khoob sair kee| mandir aadi banavaane ke lie aabaadee se hat kar ramaneek sthaan chunaa gaya|
3
yadyapi is gaonva ko apne naam lete samay munshee jee ke man mein kapat kaa bhaav na tha, phir bhee do-chaar din mein hee unakaa ankur jam gaya aur dheere-dheere badhane lagaa| munshee jee is gaonva ke aay-vyay kaa hisaab alag rakhate aur apne svaaminon ko usakaa byoro samajhaane kee jarurat na samajhate| bhaanukunvari ina baaton mein dakhal denaa theek na samajhatee thi; par doosare kaarindon se baaten sun-sun kar use shankaa hoti thi ki kaheen munshee jee dagaa to na denge| apne man kaa bhaav munshee se chhipaatee thi, is khayaal se ki kaheen kaarindon ne unhen nukasaan pahunchaane ke lie yah shadayantr na rachaa ho|
is tarah kaee saal gujar gaye| ab us kapat ke ankur ne vriksh kaa rup dhaaran kiya| bhaanukunvari ko munshee jee ke us raastaa ke lakshan dikhaayee dene lage| udhar munshee jee ke man ne kaanoon se neeti par vijay paayee, unhonne apne man mein phaisalaa kiya ki gaonva meraa hai| haon, main bhaanukunvari kaa tees hajaar kaa rinee avashy hoon| ve bahut karengee to apne rupaye le lengee aur kyaa kar sakatee hain? magar donon taraph yah aag andar hee andar sulagatee rahi| munshee jee astrasajjit hokar aakraman ke intajaar mein the aur bhaanukunvari isake lie avasar dhoondh rahi thi| ek din usane saahas karake munshee jee ko andar bulaayaa aur kahaa—laalaa jee ‘baragadaa’ ke mandir kaa kaam kab se lagavaaiegaa? use liye aath saal ho gaye, ab kaam lag jaay to achchhaa ho| jindagee kaa kaun thikaanaa hai, jo kaam karn hai; use kar hee daalanaa chaahie|
is dhang se is vishay ko uthaa kar bhaanukunvari ne apanee chaturaaee kaa achchhaa parichay diya| munshee jee bhee dil mein isake kaayal ho gaye| jaraa soch kar bole—iraadaa to meraa kaee baar hua, par mauke kee jameen nahin milatee| gangaatat kee jameen asaamiyon ke jot mein hai aur ve kisee tarah chhodane par raajee nahin|
bhaanukunvari—yah baat to aaj mujhe maaloom huee| aath saal hue, is gaonva ke vishay mein aapane kabhee bhool kar bhee dee to charchaa nahin kee| maaloom nahin, kitanee tahaseel hai, kyaa munaaphaa hai, kaisaa gaonva hai, kuchh seer hoti hai yaa nahin| jo kuchh karte hain, aap hee karte hain aur karenge| par mujhe bhee to maaloom honaa chaahie?
munshee jee sanbhal uthe| unhen maaloom ho gaya ki is hoshiyaar aurat se baajee le jan mushkil hai| gaonva lenaa hee hai to ab kyaa dar| khul kar bole—aapako isase koee sarokaar na tha, isalie mainne bekaar takaleeph denaa munaasib na samajhaa|
bhaanukunvari ke dil mein kuthaar-saa lagaa| parde se nikal aayee aur munshee jee kee taraph tej onkhon se dekh kar bolee—aap kyaa kehte hain! aapane gaonva mere liye liya tha yaa apne lie! rupaye mainne diye yaa aapane? us par jo kharch padaa, vah meraa tha yaa aapakaa? meree samajh mein nahin aat ki aap kaisee baaten karte hain|
munshee jee ne saavadhaanee se javaab diya—yah to aap jaanatee hain ki gaonva hamaare naam se basaa hua hai| rupayaa jarur aapakaa lagaa, par main usakaa denadaar hoon| raha tahaseel-vasool kaa kharch, yah sab mainne apne paas se diya hai| usakaa hisaab-kitaab, aay-vyay sab rakhataa gaya hoon|
bhaanukunvari ne gussaa se kaonpate hue kahaa—is kapat kaa phal aapako avashy milegaa| aap is nirdayataa se mere bachchon kaa galaa nahin kaat sakate| mujhe nahin maaloom tha ki aapane dil mein chhuree chhipaa rakhee hai, nahin to yah naubat hee kyon aatee| khair, ab se meree rokad aur bahee khaataa aap kuchh na chhuen| meraa jo kuchh hogaa, le loongee| jaaie, ekaant mein baith kar sochie| paap se kisee kaa bhalaa nahin hot| tum samajhate hoge ki ladakaa anaath hain, inakee sampatti hajam kar loongaa| is bhool mein na rahanaa, main tumhaare ghar kee eet tak bikavaa loongee|
yah kahakar bhaanukunvari phir parde kee aad mein aa baithee aur rone lagee| striyon gussaa ke baad kisee na kisee bahaane royaa karti hain| laalaa sahab ko koee javaab na soojhaa| yahon se uth aaye aur daphtar jaakar kaagaj ulat-palat karne lage, par bhaanukunvari bhee unake peechhe-peechhe daphtar mein pahunchee aur daonta kar bolee—meraa koee kaagaj mata chhoonaa| nahin to buraa hogaa| tum vishaile saanp ho, main tumhaaraa munh nahin dekhanaa chaahatee|
munshee jee kaagajon mein kuchh kaat-chhonta karn chaahate the, par vivash ho gaye| khajaane kee kunjee nikaal kar phenk dee, bahee-khaate patak diye, kivaad dhadaake-se band kiye aur havaa kee tarah sann-se nikal gaye| kapat mein haath to daalaa, par kapat mantr na jan|
doosaren kaarindon ne yah kaiphiyat sunee, to phoole na samaaye| munshee jee ke saamane unakee daal na galane paatee| bhaanukunvari ke paas aakar ve aag par tel chhidakane lage| sab log is vishay mein sahamat the ki munshee satyanaaraayan ne vishvaasaghaat kiya hai| maalik kaa namak unakee haddiyon se phoot-phoot kar nikalegaa|
donon ora se mukadamebaajee kee taiyaariyon hone lageen! ek taraph nyaay kaa shareer tha, doosaree ora nyaay kee aatmaa| prakriti kaa aadamee se ladane kaa saahas hua|
bhaanakunvari ne laalaa chhakkan laal se poochhaa—hamaaraa vakeel kaun hai? chhakkan laal ne idhar-udhar jhaonka kar kahaa—vakeel to seth jee hain, par satyanaaraayan ne unhen pahale gaonth rakhaa hogaa| is mukadamen ke lie bade hoshiyaar vakeel kee jarurat hai| meharaa baaboo kee aajakal khoob chal rahi hai| haakim kee kalam pakad lete hain| bolte hain to jaise motarakaar chhoot jati hai sarakaar! aur kyaa kahen, kaee aadamiyon ko phaonsee se utaar liya hai, unake saamane koee vakeel jabaan to khol nahin sakataa| sarakaar kahen to vahee kar liye jaayan|
chhakkan laal kee atyukti se shak paidaa kar liya| bhaanukunvari ne kahaa—nahin, pahale seth jee se poochh liya jaay| usake baad dekh jaayagaa| aap jaaie, unhen bulaa laaie|
chhakkanalaal apanee takadeer ko thonkate hue seth jee ke paas gaye| seth jee pandit bhrigudatt ke jeevan-kaal se hee unakaa kaanoon-sambandhee sab kaam kiya karte the| mukadame kaa haal sunaa to sannaate mein aa gaye| satyanaaraan ko yah badaa nekaneeyat aadamee samajhate the| unake patan se badaa khed hua| usee vakt aaye| bhaanukunvari ne ro-ro kar unse apanee vipatti kee kathaa kahee aur apne donon ladakon ko unake saamane khadaa karake bolee—aap ina anaathon kee rakshaa keejie| inhen main aapako saunpatee hoon|
seth jee ne samajhaute kee baat chhedee| bole—aapas kee ladaaee achchhee nahin|
bhaanukunvari—anyaayee ke saath ladanaa hee achchhaa hai|
seth jee—par hamaaraa paksh nirbal hai|
bhaanukunvari phir parde se nikal aayee aur vismit hokar bolee—kyaa hamaaraa paksh nirbal hai? duniyaa jaanatee hai ki gaonva hamaaraa hai| use hamse kaun le sakataa hai? nahin, main sulah kabhee na karungee, aap kaagajon ko dekhen| mere bachchon kee khaatir yah takaleeph uthaayen| aapakaa parishram nishphal na jaayagaa| satyanaaraayan kee neeyat pahale kharaab na thi| dekhie jis mitee mein gaonva liya gaya hai, us mitee mein tees hajaar kaa kyaa kharch dikhaayaa gaya hai| agar usane apne naam udhaar likhaa ho, to dekhie, vaarshik sood chukaayaa gaya yaa nahin| aise narapishaach se main kabhee sulah na karungee|
seth jee ne samajh liya ki is samay samajhaane-bujhaane se kuchh kaam na chalegaa| kaagajaat dekhen, abhiyog chalaane kee taiyaariyon hone lageen|
4
munshee satyanaaraayanalaal khisiyaaye hue makaan pahunche| ladake ne mithaaee maongee| use peetaa| aurat par isalie baras pade ki usane kyon ladake ko unake paas jaane diya| apanee vriddhaa maan ko daonta kar kahaa—tumse itanaa bhee nahin ho sakataa ki jaraa ladake ko bahalaao? ek to main din-bhar kaa thakaa-maondaa ghar aaoon aur phir ladake ko khelaaoon? mujhe duniyaa mein na aur koee kaam hai, na dhandhaa| is tarah ghar mein baavailaa machaa kar baahar aaye, sochane lage—mujhse badee bhool huee| main kaisaa bevakooph hoon| aur itane din tak saare kaagaj-patr apne haath mein the| chaahataa, kar sakataa tha, par haath par haath dhare baithe raha| aaj sir par aa padee, to soojhee| main chaahataa to bahee-khaate sab naye banaa sakataa tha, jisamen is gaonva kaa aur rupaye kaa jikr hee na hot, par meree moorkhataa ke vajah ghar mein aayee huee lakshmee ruthee jati hain| mujhe kyaa maaloom tha ki vah chudail mujhse is tarah pesh aayegee, kaagajon mein haath tak na lagaane degee|
isee udhedabun mein munshee jee ekaaek uchhal pade| ek upaay soojh gaya—kyon na kaaryakarttaaon ko milaa loon? yadyapi meree sakhtee ke vajah ve sab mujhse naaraaj the aur is samay seedhe baat bhee na karenge, phir bhee unamen aisaa koee bhee nahin, jo pralobhan se muththee mein na aa jaay| haon, isamen rupaye paanee kee tarah bahaanaa padegaa, par itanaa rupayaa aayegaa kahon se? haay badakismatee? do-chaar din pahale chet gaya hot, to koee kathinaaee na padatee| kyaa jaanataa tha ki vah daain is tarah vajr-prahaar karegee| bas, ab ek hee upaay hai| kisee tarah kaagajaat gum kar doon| badee jokhim kaa kaam hai, par karn hee padegaa|
dushkaamanaaon ke saamane ek baar sir jhukaane par phir sanbhalanaa mushkil ho jat hai| paap ke athaah daladal mein jahon ek baar pade ki phir pratikshan neeche hee chale jaate hain| munshee satyanaaraayan-saa vichaarasheel manushy is samay is phikr mein tha ki kaise sendh lagaa paaoon!
munshee jee ne sochaa—kyaa sendh lagaanaa aasaan hai? isake vaaste kitanee chaturataa, kitanaa sahab, kitanee budvi, kitanee veerataa chaahie! kaun keht hai ki choree karn aasaan kaam hai? main jo kaheen pakadaa gaya, to marane ke sivaa aur koee raastaa na rahegaa|
bahut sochane-vichaarane par bhee munshee jee ko apne oopar aisaa dussaahas kar sakane kaa yakeen na ho sakaa| haon, isamen sugam ek doosaree tadabeer najar aayee—kyon na daphtar mein aag lagaa doon? ek botal mittee kaa tel aur diyaasalaaee kee jarurat hain kisee badamaash ko milaa loon, magar yah kyaa maaloom ki vahee usee kamare mein rakhee hai yaa nahin| chudail ne use jarur apne paas rakh liya hogaa| nahin; aag lagaanaa gunaah belajjat hogaa|
bahut der munshee jee karavaten badalate rahe| naye-naye manasoobe sochate; par phir apne hee tarko se kaat dete| varshaakaal mein baadalon kee nayee-nayee sooraten banatee aur phir havaa ke veg se bigad jati hain; vahee dashaa is samay unake manasoobon kee ho rahi thi|
par is maanasik ashaanti mein bhee ek soch poornarup se sthir tha—kisee tarah ina kaagajaat ko apne haath mein laanaa chaahie| kaam mushkil hai—maanaa! par himmat na thi, to raar kyon mol lee? kyaa tees hajaar kee jaayadaad daal-bhaat kaa kaur hai?—chaahe jis tarah ho, chor bane binaa kaam nahin chal sakataa| aakhir jo log choriyon karte hain, ve bhee to manushy hee hote hain| bas, ek chhalonga kaa kaam hai| agar paar ho gaye, to raaj karenge, gir pade, to jaan se haath dhoyenge|
5
raat ke das baj gaye| munshee satyanaaraan kunjiyon kaa ek guchchhaa kamar mein dabaaye ghar se baahar nikale| dvaar par thodaa-saa puaal rakhaa hua tha| use dekhate hee ve chaunk pade| maare dar ke chhaatee dhadakane lagee| jaan padaa ki koee chhipaa baithaa hai| kadam ruk gaye| puaal kee taraph dhyaan se dekh| usamen bilakul harakat na huee! tab himmat baondhee, aage bade aur man ko samajhaane lage—main kaisaa baukhal hoon
apne dvaar par kisakaa dar aur sadak par bhee mujhe kisakaa dar hai? main apanee raah jat hoon| koee meree taraph tirachhee onkha se nahin dekh sakataa| haon, jab mujhe sendh lagaate dekh le—nahin, pakad le tab alabatte darane kee baat hai| tis par bhee bachaav kee yukti nikal sakatee hai|
akasmaat unhonne bhaanukunvari ke ek chaparaasee ko aate hue dekh| kalejaa dhadak uthaa| lapak kar ek andheree galee mein ghus gaye| badee der tak vahon khade rahe| jab vah sipaahee onkhon se ojhal ho gaya, tab phir sadak par aaye| vah sipaahee aaj subah tak inakaa gulaam tha, use unhonne kitanee hee baar gaaliyon dee theen, laaten maaree theen, par aaj use dekhakar unake praan sookh gaye|
unhonne phir tark kee sharan lee| main maanon bhang khaakar aay hoon| is chaparaasee se itanaa daraa maano ki vah mujhe dekh letaa, par meraa kar kyaa sakataa tha? hajaaron aadamee raastaa chal rahe hain| unheen mein main bhee ek hoon| kyaa vah antaryaamee hai? sabake dil kaa haal jaanataa hai? mujhe dekhakar vah adab se salaam kart aur vahon kaa kuchh haal bhee keht; par main usse aisaa daraa ki soorat tak na dikhaayee| is tarah man ko samajhaa kar ve aage badhe| sach hai, paap ke panjon mein phansaa hua man patajhad kaa pattaa hai, jo havaa ke jaraa-se jhonke se gir padataa hai|
munshee jee baajaar pahunche| adhikatar dookaanen band ho chukee theen| unamen saonda aur gaayen baithee huee jugaalee kar rahi thi| keval halavaaiyon kee dookaanen khulee thi aur kaheen-kaheen gajarevaale haar kee haonka lagaate phirate the| sab halavaaee munshee jee ko pahachaanate the, isalie munshee jee ne sir jhukaa liya| kuchh chaal badalee aur lapakate hue chale| ekaaek unhen ek bagghee aatee dikhaayee dee| yah seth ballabhadaas savakeel kee bagghee thi| isamen baithakar hajaaron baar seth jee ke saath kachaharee gaye the, par aaj vah bagghee kaaladev ke samaan bhayankar maaloom huee| phauran ek khaalee dookaan par chadh gaye| vahon vishraam karne vaale saonda ne samajhaa, ve mujhe padachyut karne aaye hain! maathaa jhukaaye phunkaarataa hua uth baithaa; par isee beech mein bagghee nikal gayee aur munshee jee kee jaan mein jaan aayee| abakee unhonne tark kaa aashray na liya| samajh gaye ki is samay isase koee phaayadaa nahin, khairiyat yah huee ki vakeel ne dekh nahin| yah ek ghaagh hain| mere chehare se taad jat|
kuchh vidvaanon kaa kathan hai ki manushy kee svaabhaavik pravritti paap kee ora hoti hai, par yah koraa anumaan hee anumaan hai, anubhav-siddh baat nahin| sach baat to yah hai ki manushy svabhaavat: paap-bheeru hot hai aur ham pratyaksh dekh rahe hain ki paap se use kaisee napharat hoti hai|
ek pharlaang aage chal kar munshee jee ko ek galee milee| vah bhaanukunvari ke ghar kaa ek raastaa tha| dhundhalee-see laalaten jal rahi thi| jaisaa munshee jee ne anumaan kiya tha, paharedaar kaa pataa na tha| astabal mein chamaaron ke yahon naach ho raha tha| kaee chamaarinen banaav-singaar karake naach rahi theen| chamaar mridang bajaa-bajaa kar gaate the—
‘naaheen ghare shyaam, gheri aaye badaraa|
sovat raheun, sapan ek dekheun, raamaa|
khuli gayee neend, dharak gaye kajaraa|
naaheen ghare shyaam, gheri aaye badaraa|’
donon paharedaar vahee tamaashaa dekh rahe the| munshee jee dabe-paonva laalaten ke paas gae aur jis tarah billee choohe par jhapatatee hai, usee tarah unhonne jhapat kar laalaten ko bujhaa diya| ek padaav pooraa ho gaya, par ve us kaary ko jitanaa dushkar samajhate the, utanaa na jaan padaa| dil kuchh majaboot hua| daphtar ke baraamade mein pahunche aur khoob kaan lagaakar aahat lee| chaaron ora sannaataa chhaayaa hua tha| keval chamaaron kaa kolaahal sunaayee detaa tha| is samay munshee jee ke dil mein dhadakan thi, par sir dhamadham kar raha tha; haath-paonva kaonpa rahe the, saonsa bade veg se chal rahi thi| shareer kaa ek-ek rom onkha aur kaan banaa hua tha| ve sajeevataa kee moorti ho rahe the| unamen jitanaa paurush, jitanee chapalataa, jitanaa-saahas, jitanee chetanaa, jitanee budvi, jitanaa ausaan tha, ve sab is vakt sajag aur sachet hokar ichchhaa-shakti kee madad kar rahe the|

shok! pahale yah baat mujhe kyon na soojhee? agar kal tak mein unake paas chalaa gaya hot, to baat ban jati; par ab kyaa ho sakataa hai| aaj to phaisalaa sunaayaa jaayagaa|
munshee jee der tak isee soch mein pade rahe, par kuchh phaisalaa na kar sake ki kyaa karen|
bhaanukunvari ko bhee yakeen ho gaya ki ab gaonva haath se gaya| bechaaree haath mal kar raha gayee| raat-bhar use neend na aayee, raha-raha kar munshee satyanaaraayan par gussaa aat tha| haay paapee! dhol bajaa kar meraa pachaas hajaar kaa maal lie jat hai aur main kuchh nahin kar sakatee| aajakal ke nyaay karne vaale bilakul onkha ke andhe hain| jis baat ko saaree duniyaa jaanatee hai, usamen bhee unakee nazar nahin pahunchatee| bas, doosaron ko onkhon se dekhate hain| kore kaagajon ke gulaam hain| nyaay vah hai jo doodh kaa doodh, paanee kaa paanee kar de; yah nahin ki khud hee kaagajon ke dhokhe mein aa jaay, khud hee paakhandiyon ke jaal mein phans jaay| isee se to aisee chhalee, kapatee, dagaabaaj, aur duraatmaaon kaa saahas badh gaya hai| khair, gaonva jat hai to jaay; lekin satyanaaraayan, tum shahar mein kaheen munh dikhaane ke laayak bhee na rahe|
is khayaal se bhaanukunvari ko kuchh shaanti huee| shatru kee nukasaan manushy ko apne phaayadaa se bhee zyaadaa priy hoti hai, maanav-svabhaav hee kuchh aisaa hai| tum hamaaraa ek gaonva le gaye, naaraayan chaahenge to tum bhee isase sukh na paaoge| tum aap narak kee aag mein jaloge, tumhaare ghar mein koee diya jalaane vaalaa na raha jaayagaa|
phaisale kaa din aa gaya| aaj ijalaas mein badee bheed thi| aise-aise mahaanubhaav upasthit the, jo bagulon kee tarah aphasaron kee badhaaee aur bidaaee ke avasaron hee mein najar aay karte hain| vakeelon aur mukhtaaron kee palatan bhee jamaa thi| niyat samay par jaj sahab ne ijalaas sushobhit kiya| vistrit nyaay bhavan mein sannaataa chhaa gaya| ahalamad ne
sandook se tajabeej nikaalee| log utsuk hokar ek-ek kadam aur aage khisak gae|
jaj ne phaisalaa sunaayaa—muddaee kaa daavaa khaarij| donon paksh apanaa-apanaa kharch sah len|
yadyapi phaisalaa logon ke anumaan ke anusaar hee tha, phir bhee jaj ke munh se use sun kar logon mein halachal-see mach gayee| udaaseen bhaav se phaisale par aalochanaaen karte hue log dheere-dheere kamare se nikalane lage|
ekaaek bhaanukunvari ghoonghat nikaale ijalaas par aa kar khadee ho gayee| jaanevaale laut pade| jo baahar nikal gaye the, daud kar aa gaye| aur kautoohalapoorvak bhaanukunvari kee taraph taakane lage|
bhaanukunvari ne kanpit aavaaz mein jaj se kahaa—sarakaar, yadi hukm den, to main munshee jee se kuchh poochhoon|
yadyapi yah baat niyam ke viruddh thi, phir bhee jaj ne dayaapoorvak hukm de dee|
tab bhaanukunvari ne satyanaaraayan kee taraph dekh kar kahaa—laalaa jee, sarakaar ne tumhaaree digree to kar hee dee| gaonva tumhen mubaarak rahe; magar eemaan aadamee kaa sab kuchh hai| eemaan se kah do, gaonva kisakaa hai?
hajaaron aadamee yah savaal sun kar kautoohal se satyanaaraayan kee taraph dekhane lage| munshee jee soch-saagar mein doob gaye| dil mein sankalp aur vikalp mein ghor sangraam hone lagaa| hajaaron manushyon kee onkhen unakee taraph jamee huee theen| yathaarth baat ab kisee se chhipee na thi| itane aadamiyon ke saamane asaty baat munh se nikal na sakee| sharm se jabaan band kar lee—‘meraa’ kehne mein kaam banataa tha| koee baat na thi; lekin ghoratam paap kaa dand samaaj de sakataa hai, usake milane kaa pooraa dar tha| ‘aapakaa’ kehne se kaam bigadataa tha| jeetee-jitaayee baajee haath se nikalee jati thi, sarvotkrisht kaam ke lie samaaj se jo inaam mil sakataa hai, usake milane kee pooree ummeed thi| ummeed ke dar ko jeet liya| unhen aisaa lagaa hua, jaise eeshvar ne mujhe apanaa munh ujjaval karne kaa yah antim avasar diya hai| main ab bhee maanav-izzat kaa paatr ban sakataa hoon| ab apanee aatmaa kee rakshaa kar sakataa hoon| unhonne aage badh kar bhaanukunvari ko pranaam kiya aur kaonpate hue aavaaz se bole—aapakaa!
hajaaron manushyon ke munh se ek gaganasparshee dhvani nikalee—saty kee jay!
jaj ne khade hokar kahaa—yah kaanoon kaa nyaay nahin, eeshvareey nyaay hai! ise kathaa na samajhiegaa; yah sachchee ghatanaa hai| bhaanukunvari aur saty naaraayan ab bhee jeevit hain| munshee jee ke is naitik saahas par log mugadh ho gae|
maanaveey nyaay par eeshvareey nyaay ne jo vilakshan vijay paayee, usakee charchaa shahar bhar mein maheenon rahi| bhaanukunvari munshee jee ke ghar gayee, unhen manaa kar laayeen| phir apanaa saaraa kaarobaar unhen saunpaa aur kuchh dinon uparaant yah gaonva unheen ke naam hibbaa kar diya| munshee jee ne bhee use apne adhikaar mein rakhanaa theek na samajhaa, krishnaarpan kar diya| ab isakee aamadanee deen-dukhiyon aur vidyaarthiyon kee madad mein kharch hoti|
