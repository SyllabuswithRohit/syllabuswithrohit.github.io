# Do Bailon Ki Katha

**Munshi Premchand**

jaanavaron mein gadhaa sabase jyaadaa buddhimaan samajhaa jat hai| ham jab kisee aadamee ko pahale darje kaa bevakooph kahanaa chaahate hain, to use gadhaa kehte hain| gadhaa sachamuch bevakooph hai yaa
usake seedhepan, usakee niraapad sahishnutaa ne use yah padavee de dee hai, isakaa phaisalaa nahin kiya jaa sakataa| gaayen seeng maaratee hain, byaahee huee gaay to anaayaas hee sinhanee kaa
roop dhaaran kar letee hai| kuttaa bhee bahut gareeb jaanavar hai, lekin kabhee-kabhee use bhee gussaa aa hee jat hai, lekin gadhe ko kabhee gussaa karte nahin sunaa, na dekh| jitanaa chaaho
gareeb ko maaro, chaahe jaisee kharaab, sadee huee ghaas saamane daal do, usake chehare par kabhee asantosh kee chhaayaa bhee nahin dikhaaee degee| vaishaakh mein chaahe ekaadh baar kulel kar letaa hai,
par hamane to use kabhee khush hote nahin dekh| usake chehare par sthaaee udaasee sthaayee roop se chhaayaa rahataa hai| sukh-duhkh, nukasaan-phaayadaa kisee bhee dashaa mein use badalate nahin dekh|
rishiyon-muniyon ke jitane gun hain, ve sabhee usamen paraakaashthaa ko pahunch gae hain, par aadamee use bevakooph keht hai| sadgunon kaa itanaa anaadar!

shaayad seedhaapan sansaar ke lie upayukt nahin hai| dekhie na, bhaaratavaasiyon kee aphreekaa mein kyaa durdashaa ho rahi hai ? kyon amareekaa mein unhen ghusane nahin diya jat?
bechaare sharaab nahin peete,chaar paise kusamay ke lie bachaakar rakhate hain, jee todakar kaam karte hain, kisee se ladaaee-jhagadaa nahin karte, chaar baaten sunakar gam khaa jaate hain phir
bhee badanaam hain| kahaa jat hai, ve jeevan ke aadarsh ko neechaa karte hain| agar ve eent kaa javaab patthar se denaa seekh jaate to shaayad sabhy kahalaane lagate| jaapaan kee misaal saamane
hai| ek hee vijay ne use sansaar kee sabhy jaatiyon mein gany banaa diya| lekin gadhe kaa ek chhotaa bhai aur bhee hai, jo usse kam hee gadhaa hai| aur vah hai 'bail'| jis arth mein ham
'gadhaa' kaa prayog karte hain, kuchh usee se milate-julate arth mein 'bachhiyaa ke taaoo' kaa bhee prayog karte hain| kuchh log bail ko shaayad bevakoophee mein sarvashreshth kahenge, magar
hamaaraa soch aisaa nahin hai| bail kabhee-kabhee maarataa bhee hai, kabhee-kabhee adiyal bail bhee dekhane mein aat hai| aur bhee kaee reetiyon se apanaa asantosh prakat kar detaa hai, ataevan usakaa
sthaan gadhe se neechaa hai|

jhooree ka paas do bail the- heeraa aur motee| dekhane mein sundar, kaam mein chaukas, deel mein oonche| bahut dinon saath rahate-rahate donon mein bhaaeechaaraa ho gaya tha| donon aamane-saamane
yaa aas-paas baithe hue ek-doosare se mook bhaashaa mein soch-vinimay kiya karte the| ek-doosare ke man kee baat ko kaise samajhaa jat hai, ham kah nahin sakate| avashy hee unamen koee
aisee gupt shakti thi,jisse jeevon mein shreshthataa kaa daavaa karne vaalaa manushy vanchit hai| donon ek-doosare ko chaatakar soonghakar apanaa pyaar prakat karte, kabhee-kabhee donon
seeng bhee milaa liya karte the, vigrah ke naate se nahin, keval vinod ke bhaav se, aatmeeyataa ke bhaav se, jaise donon mein ghanishthataa hote hee dhaul-dhappaa hone lagataa hai|
isake binaa dostee kuchh phusaphasee, kuchh halkee-see rahatee hai, phir jyaadaa yakeen nahin kiya jaa sakataa| jis vakt ye donon bail hal yaa gaadee mein jot die jaate aur garadan
hilaa-hilaakar chalate, us samay har ek kee cheshtaa hoti ki jyaadaa-se-jyaadaa bojh meree hee gardan par rahe|

din-bhar ke baad dopahar yaa shaam ko donon khulate to ek-doosare ko chaat-choot kar apanee thakaan mitaa liya karte, naand mein khalee-bhoosaa pad jaane ke baad donon saath uthate, saath
naand mein munh daalate aur saath hee baithate the| ek munh hataa letaa to doosaraa bhee hataa letaa tha|

sanyog kee baat, jhooree ne ek baar goeen ko sasuraal bhej diya| bailon ko kyaa maaloom, ve kahan bheje jaa rahe hain| samajhe, maalik ne hamen bech diya| apanaa yon bechaa jan
unhen achchhaa lagaa yaa buraa, kaun jaane, par jhooree ke saale gaya ko ghar tak goeen le jaane mein daanton paseenaa aa gaya| peechhe se haankataa to donon daaen-baane bhaagate, pagahiyaa
pakadakar aage se kheenchataa to donon peechhe kee ora jor lagaate| maarataa to donon seenge neechee karake hunkaarate| agar eeshvar ne unhen vaanee dee hoti to jhooree se poochhate-tum ham
gareebon ko kyon nikaal rahe ho ?

hamane to tumhaaree sevaa karne mein koee kasar nahin uthaa rakhee| agar itanee mehanat se kaam na chalataa tha, aur kaam le lete| hamen to tumhaaree chaakaree mein mar jan kabool tha| hamane
kabhee daane-chaare kee shikaayat nahin kee| tumane jo kuchh khilaayaa, vah sir jhukaakar khaa liya, phir tumane hamen is jaalim ke haath kyon bench diya ?

shaam samay donon bail apne nae sthaan par pahunche| din-bhar ke bhookhe the, lekin jab naand mein lagaae gae to ek ne bhee usamen munh nahin daalaa| dil bhaaree ho raha tha| jise
unhonne apanaa ghar samajh rakhaa tha, vah aaj unse chhoot gaya| yah nayaa ghar, nayaa gaanv, nae aadamee unhen begaane-se lagate the|

donon ne apanee mook bhaashaa mein salaah kee, ek-doosare ko kanakhiyon se dekh aur let gaye| jab gaanv mein sotaa pad gaya to donon ne jor maarakar pagahaa tudaa daale aur ghar kee taraph
chale| pagahe bahut majaboot the| anumaan na ho sakataa tha ki koee bail unhen tod sakegaa, par ina donon mein is samay doonee shakti aa gaee thi| ek-ek jhatake mein rassiyaan toot gaeen|

jhooree subah kaal so kar uthaa to dekh ki donon bail charanee par khade hain| donon kee garadanon mein aadhaa-aadhaa garaanv latak raha tha| ghutane tak paanv keechad se bhare hain aur donon
kee aankhon mein vidrohamay pyaar jhalak raha hai|

jhooree bailon ko dekhakar pyaar se gadgad ho gaya| daudakar unhen gale lagaa liya| premaalingan aur chumban kaa vah drishy badaa hee manohar tha|

ghar aur gaanv ke ladake jamaa ho gae| aur taaliyaan bajaa-bajaakar unakaa svaagat karne lage| gaanv ke itihaas mein yah ghatanaa abhootapoorv na hone par bhee mahattvapoorn thi, baal-sabhaa ne
phaisalaa kiya, donon pashu-veeron kaa abhinandan patr denaa chaahie| koee apne ghar se rotiyaan laayaa, koee gud, koee chokar, koee bhoosee|

ek ladakaa ne kahaa- ''aise bail kisee ke paas na honge|''

doosare ne samarthan kiya- ''itanee door se donon akele chale aae|'

teesaraa bol- 'bail nahin hain ve, us janm ke aadamee hain|'

isakaa prativaad karne kaa kisee ko saahas nahin hua| jhooree kee aurat ne bailon ko dvaar par dekh to jal uthee| bolee -'kaise namak-haraam bail hain ki ek din vahaan kaam na
kiya, bhaag khade hue|'

jhooree apne bailon par yah aakshep na sun sakaa-'namak haraam kyon hain ? chaaraa-daanaa na diya hogaa to kyaa karte ?'

aurat ne rob ke saath kahaa-'bas, tumheen to bailon ko khilaanaa jaanate ho, aur to sabhee paanee pilaa-pilaakar rakhate hain|'

jhooree ne chidhaayaa-'chaaraa milataa to kyon bhaagate ?'

aurat chidh gayee-'bhaage isalie ki ve log tum jaise buddhuon kee tarah bail ko sahalaate nahin, khilaate hain to ragadakar jotate bhee hain| ye donon thahare kaamachor, bhaag nikale|
ab dekhoon kahaan se khalee aur chokar milataa hai| sookhe bhoose ke sivaa kuchh na doongee, khaaen chaahen maren|'

vahee hua| majoor kee badee taakeed kee gaee ki bailon ko khaalee sookhaa bhoosaa diya jaae|

bailon ne naand mein munh daalaa to pheekaa-pheekaa, na koee chikanaahat, na koee ras !

kyaa khaaen ? ummeed-bharee aankhon se dvaar kee ora taakane lage| jhooree ne majoor se kahaa-'thodee-see khalee kyon nahin daal detaa be ?'

'maalakin mujhe maar hee daalengee|'

'churaakar daal aa|'

'naa daadaa, peechhe se tum bhee unheen kee-see kahoge|'

doosare din jhooree kaa saalaa phir aay aur bailon ko le chalaa| abakee usane donon ko gaadee mein jotaa|

do-chaar baar motee ne gaadee ko khaaee mein giraanaa chaahaa, par heeraa ne sanbhaal liya| vah jyaadaa sahanasheel tha|

shaam-samay ghar pahunchakar usane donon ko motee rassiyon se baandhaa aur kal kee sharaarat kaa majaa chakhaayaa phir vahee sookhaa bhoosaa daal diya| apne donon baalon ko khalee choonee sab kuchh
dee|

donon bailon kaa aisaa beizzatee kabhee na hua tha| jhooree ne inhen phool kee chhadee se bhee chhootaa tha| usakee titakaar par donon udane lagate the| yahaan maar padee| aahat izzat kee vyathaa
to thi hee,us par milaa sookhaa bhoosaa !

naand kee taraph aankhen tak na uthaaeen|

doosare din gaya ne bailon ko hal mein jotaa, par ina donon ne jaise paanv na uthaane kee kasam khaa lee thi| vah maarate-maarate thak gaya, par donon ne paanv na uthaayaa| ek baar jab us
nirdayee ne heeraa kee naak par khoob dande jamaaye to motee ko gussaa kaaboo se baahar ho gaya| hal lekar bhaagaa| hal, rassee, juaa, jot, sab toot-taatakar baraabar ho gaya| gale mein
badee-badee rassiyaan na hoteen to donon pakadaaee mein na aate|

heeraa ne mook-bhaashaa mein kahaa-bhaaganaa bekaar hai|'

motee ne javaab diya-'tumhaaree to isane jaan hee le lee thi|'

'abakee badee maar padegee|'

'padane do, bail kaa janm liya hai to maar se kahaan tak bachenge ?'

'gaya do aadamiyon ke saath daudaa aa raha hai, donon ke haathon mein laathiyaan hain|'

motee bol-'kaho to dikhaa doon majaa main bhee, laathee lekar aa raha hai|'

heeraa ne samajhaayaa-'nahin bhai ! khade ho jaao|'

'mujhe maaregaa to main ek-do ko giraa doongaa|'

'nahin hamaaree jaati kaa yah dharm nahin hai|'

motee dil mein ainthakar raha gaya| gaya aa pahunchaa aur donon ko pakad kar le chalaa| kushal huee ki usane is vakt maarapeet na kee, nahin to motee palat padataa| usake tevar dekh gaya aur
usake sahaayak samajh gae ki is vakt taal jan hee bhalamanasaahat hai|

aaj donon ke saamane phir vahee sookhaa bhoosaa laayaa gaya, donon chupachaap khade rahe|

ghar mein log khaanaa karne lage| us vakt chhotee-see ladakee do rotiyaan lie nikalee aur donon ke munh mein dekar chalee gaee| us ek rotee se inakee bhookh to kyaa shaant hoti, par
donon ke dil ko maano khaanaa mil gaya| yahaan bhee kisee sajjan kaa vaas hai| ladakee bhairo kee thi| usakee maan mar chukee thi| sautelee maan use maaratee rahatee thi, isalie ina bailon
se ek tarah kee aatmeeyataa ho gaee thi|

donon din-bhar jaate, dande khaate, adate, shaam ko thaan par baandh die jaate aur raat ko vahee ladakee unhen do rotiyaan khilaa jati| pyaar ke is prasaad kee yah barakat thi ki
do-do gaal sookhaa bhoosaa khaakar bhee donon durbal na hote the, magar donon kee aankhon mein rom-rom mein vidroh bharaa hua tha|

ek din motee ne mook-bhaashaa mein kahaa-'ab to nahin sahaa jat heeraa !

'kyaa karn chaahate ho ?'

'ekaadh ko seengon par uthaakar phenk doongaa|'

'lekin jaanate ho, vah pyaaree ladakee, jo hamen rotiyaan khilaatee hai, usee kee ladakee hai, jo is ghar kaa maalik hai, yah bechaaree anaath ho jaaegee|'

'to maalakin ko phenk doon, vahee to is ladakee ko maaratee hai|

'lekin aurat jaat par seeng chalaanaa manaa hai, yah bhool jaate ho|'

'tum to kisee tarah nikalane hee nahin dete, bataao, tudaakar bhaag chalen|'

'haan, yah main sveekaar kart, lekin itanee motee rassee tootegee kaise|'

isakaa ek upaay hai, pahale rassee ko thodaa chabaa lo| phir ek jhatake mein jati hai|'

raat ko jab ladakee rotiyaan khilaa kar chalee gaee to donon rassiyaan chabane lage, par motee rassee munh mein na aatee thi| bechaare baar-baar jor lagaakar raha jaate the|

saahasaa ghar kaa dvaar khulaa aur vah ladakee nikalee| donon sir jhukaakar usakaa haath chaatane lage| donon kee poonchhen khadee ho gaeen| usane unake maathe sahalaae aur bolee-'khol detee hoon,
chupake se bhaag jaao, nahin to ye log maar daalenge| aaj ghar mein salaah ho rahi hai ki inakee naakon mein naath daal dee jaaen|'

usane garaanv khol diya, par donon chup khade rahe|

motee ne apanee bhaashaa mein poonchhaa-'ab chalate kyon nahin ?'

heeraa ne kahaa-'chalen to, lekin kal is anaath par aaphat aaegee, sab isee par shak karenge|

saahasaa ladakee chillaaee-'donon phoophaa vaale bail bhaage je rahe hain, o daadaa! daadaa! donon bail bhaage jaa rahe hain, o daadaa! daadaa! donon bail bhaage jaa rahe hain, jaldee
daudo|

gaya hadabadaakar bheetar se nikalaa aur bailon ko pakadane chalaa| ve donon bhaage| gaya ne peechhaa kiya, aur bhee tej hue, gaya ne shor machaayaa| phir gaanv ke kuchh aadamiyon ko bhee saath
lene ke lie lautaa| donon mitron ko bhaagane kaa maukaa mil gaya| seedhe daudate chale gae| yahaan tak ki raastaa kaa jnaan raha| jis parichit raastaa se aae the, usakaa yahaan pataa na
tha| nae-nae gaanv milane lage| tab donon ek khet ke kinaare khade hokar sochane lage, ab kyaa karn chaahie|

heeraa ne kahaa-'mujhe maaloom hot hai, raah bhool gae|'

'tum bhee betahaashaa bhaage, vaheen use maar giraanaa tha|'
'use maar giraate to duniyaa kyaa kehti ? vah apne dharm chhod de, lekin ham apanaa dharm kyon chhoden ?'

donon bhookh se vyaakul ho rahe the| khet mein matar khadee thi| charane lage| raha-rahakar aahat lete rahe the| koee aat to nahin hai|

jab pet bhar gaya, donon ne aajaadee kaa anubhav kiya to mast hokar uchhalane-koodane lage| pahale donon ne dakaar lee| phir seeng milaae aur ek-doosare ko thekane lage| motee ne heeraa
ko kaee kadam peechhe hataa diya, yahaan tak ki vah khaaee mein gir gaya| tab use bhee gussaa aa gaya| sanbhalakar uthaa aur motee se bhid gaya| motee ne dekh ki khel mein jhagadaa hua chaahataa
hai to kinaare hat gaya|

are ! yah kyaa ? koee saand daunkataa chalaa aa raha hai| haan, saand hee hai| vah saamane aa pahunchaa| donon mitr bagalen jhaank rahe the| saand pooraa haathee tha| usse bhidanaa jaan
se haath dhonaa hai, lekin na bhidane par bhee jaan bachatee najar nahin aatee| inheen kee taraph aa bhee raha hai| kitanee bhayankar soorat hai !

motee ne mook-bhaashaa mein kahaa-'bure phanse, jaan bachegee ? koee upaay socho|'

heeraa ne chintit aavaaz mein kahaa-'apne ghamand mein phoolaa hua hai, aarajoo-vinatee na sunegaa|'

'bhaag kyon na chalen?'

'bhaaganaa kaayarataa hai|'

'to phir yaheen maro| bandaa to nau do gyaarah hot hai|'

'aur jo daudaae?'

' to phir koee upaae socho jald!'

'upaay yah hai ki us par donon jane ek saath chot karen| main aage se ragedataa hoon, tum peechhe se ragedo, doharee maar padegee to bhaag khadaa hogaa| meree ora jhapate, tum bagal se
usake pet mein seeng ghused denaa| jaan jokhim hai, par doosaraa upaay nahin hai|

donon mitr jaan hathelee par lekar lapake| saand ko bhee sangathit shatruon se ladane kaa tajurabaa na tha|

vah to ek shatru se mallayuddh karne kaa aadee tha| jyon-hee heeraa par jhapataa, motee ne peechhe se daudaayaa| saand usakee taraph mudaa to heeraa ne ragedaa| saand chaahataa tha, ki
ek-ek karake donon ko giraa le, par ye donon bhee ustaad the| use vah avasar na dete the| ek baar saand jhallaakar heeraa kaa ant kar dene ke lie chalaa ki motee ne bagal se
aakar usake pet mein seeng bhonk diya| saand gussaa mein aakar peechhe phiraa to heeraa ne doosare pahaloo mein seenge chubhaa diya|

aakhir bechaaraa jakhmee hokar bhaagaa aur donon mitron ne door tak usakaa peechhaa kiya| yahaan tak ki saand bedam hokar gir padaa| tab donon ne use chhod diya| donon mitr jeet
ke nashe mein jhoomate chale jaate the|

motee ne saanketik bhaashaa mein kahaa-'meraa jee chaahataa tha ki bachaa ko maar hee daaloon|'

heeraa ne tiraskaar kiya-'gire hue bairee par seeng na chalaanaa chaahie|'

'yah sab dhong hai, bairee ko aisaa maaranaa chaahie ki phir na uthe|'

'ab ghar kaise pahunchoge vah socho|'

'pahale kuchh khaa len, to sochen|'

saamane matar kaa khet tha hee, motee usamen ghus gaya| heeraa manaa kart raha, par usane ek na sunee| abhee do hee chaar graas khaaye the ki aadamee laathiyaan lie daud pade aur donon
mitr ko gher liya, heeraa to med par tha nikal gaya| motee seenche hue khet mein tha| usake khur keechad mein dhansane lage| na bhaag sakaa| pakad liya| heeraa ne dekh, sangee sankat
mein hai to laut padaa| phansenge to donon phansenge| rakhavaalon ne use bhee pakad liya|

subah donon mitr kaanjee haus mein band kar die gae|

donon mitron ko jeevan mein pahalee baar aisaa saabikaa padaa tha ki saaraa din beet gaya aur khaane ko ek tinakaa bhee na milaa| samajh mein na aat tha, yah kaisaa svaamee hai| isase to
gaya phir bhee achchhaa tha| yahaan kaee bhainse theen, kaee bakariyaan, kaee ghode, kaee gadhe, par kisee ke saamane chaaraa na tha, sab jameen par murdon kee tarah pade the|

kaee to itane kamajor ho gaye the ki khade bhee na ho sakate the| saaraa din mitr phaatak kee ora takatakee lagaae rahate, par koee chaaraa na lekar aat dikhaaee diya| tab donon ne deevaar kee
namakeen mittee chaatanee shuroo kee, par isase kyaa tripti hoti|

raat ko bhee jab kuchh khaanaa na milaa to heeraa ke dil mein vidroh kee jvaalaa dahak uthee| motee se bol-'ab nahin raha jat motee !

motee ne sir latakaae hue javaab diya-'mujhe to maaloom hot hai ki praan nikal rahe hain|'

'aao deevaar tod daalen|'

'mujhse to ab kuchh nahin hogaa|'

'bas isee boot par akadate the !'

'saaree akad nikal gaee|'

baade kee deevaar kachchee thi| heeraa majaboot to tha hee, apne nukeele seeng deevaar mein gadaa die aur jor maaraa to mittee kaa ek chippad nikal aay| phir to usakaa saahas badhaa
usane daud-daudakar deevaar par choten keen aur har chot mein thodee-thodee mittee giraane lagaa|

usee samay kaanjee haus kaa chaukeedaar laalaten lekar jaanavaron kee haajiree lene aa nikalaa| heeraa kaa uddanddapann dekhakar use kaee dande raseed kie aur motee-see rassee se baandh diya|

motee ne pade-pade kahaa-'aakhir maar khaaee, kyaa milaa?'

'apne boote-bhar jor to maar diya|'

'aisaa jor maaranaa kis kaam kaa ki aur bandhan mein pad gae|'

'jor to maarataa hee jaaoongaa, chaahe kitane hee bandhan padate jaaen|'

'jaan se haath dhonaa padegaa|'

'kuchh paravaah nahin| yon bhee to maranaa hee hai| socho, deevaar khud jati to kitanee jaane bach jaateen| itane bhai yahaan band hain| kisee kee deh mein jaan nahin hai| do-chaar din yahee
haal raha to mar jaaenge|'

'haan, yah baat to hai| achchhaa, to laa phir main bhee jor lagaataa hoon|'

motee ne bhee deevaar mein seeng maaraa, thodee-see mittee giree aur phir himmat badhee, phir to vah deevaar mein seeng lagaakar is tarah jor karne lagaa, maano kisee pratidvandee se
lad raha hai| aakhir koee do ghante kee jor-aajamaaee ke baad deevaar oopar se lagabhag ek haath gir gaee, usane doonee shakti se doosaraa dhakkaa maaraa to aadhee deevaar gir padee|

deevaar kaa giranaa tha ki adhamare-se pade hue sabhee jaanavar chet uthe, teenon ghodiyaan sarapat bhaag nikaleen| phir bakariyaan nikaleen, isake baad bhains bhee khasak gaee, par gadhe abhee tak
jyon ke tyon khade the|

heeraa ne poochhaa-'tum donon kyon nahin bhaag jaate?'

ek gadhe ne kahaa-'jo kaheen phir pakad lie jaaen|'

'to kyaa haraj hai, abhee to bhaagane kaa avasar hai|'

'hamen to dar lagataa hai| ham yaheen pade rahenge|'

aadhee raat se oopar jaa chukee thi| donon gadhe abhee tak khade soch rahe the ki bhaagen, yaa na bhaagen, aur motee apne mitr kee rassee todane mein lagaa hua tha| jab vah haar gaya to
heeraa ne kahaa-'tum jaao, mujhe yaheen padaa rehne do, shaayad kaheen bhent ho jaae|'

motee ne aankhon mein aansoo laakar kahaa-'tum mujhe itanaa svaarthee samajhate ho, heeraa ham aur tum itane dinon ek saath rahe hain| aaj tum vipatti mein pad gae ho to main tumhen
chhodakar alag ho jaaoon ?'

heeraa ne kahaa-'bahut maar padegee, log samajh jaaenge, yah tumhaaree sharaarat hai|'

motee ne garv se bol-'jis aparaadh ke lie tumhaare gale mein bandhanaa padaa, usake lie agar mujhe maar pade, to kyaa chintaa| itanaa to ho hee gaya ki nau-das praaniyon kee
jaan bach gaee, ve sab to aasheervaad denge|'

yah kehte hue motee ne donon gadhon ko seengon se maar-maar kar baade se baahar nikaalaa aur tab apne bandhu ke paas aakar so raha|

bhor hote hee munshee aur chaukeedaar tathaa any karmachaariyon mein kaisee khalabalee machee, isake likhane kee jaroorat nahin| bas, itanaa hee kaaphee hai ki motee kee khoob marammat huee aur use bhee
motee rassee se baandh diya gaya|

ek saptaah tak donon mitr vahaan bandhe pade rahe| kisee ne chaare kaa ek trin bhee na daalaa| haan, ek baar paanee dikhaa diya jat tha| yahee unakaa aadhaar tha| donon itane durbal
ho gae the ki uthaa tak nahin jat tha, thathariyaan nikal aaeen theen| ek din baade ke saamane duggee bajane lagee aur dopahar hote-hote vahaan pachaas-saath aadamee jamaa ho gae| tab donon
mitr nikaale gae aur log aakar unakee soorat dekhate aur man pheekaa karake chale jaate|

aise mritak bailon kaa kaun khareedadaar hot ? sahasaa ek dadhiyal aadamee, jisakee aankhen laal theen aur mudraa bahut kathor, aay aur donon mitr ke koolhon mein ungalee godakar
munsheejee se baaten karne lagaa| cheharaa dekhakar antarjnaan se donon mitron kaa dil kaanp uthe| vah kyon hai aur kyon tatol raha hai, is vishay mein unhen koee shak na
hua| donon ne ek-doosare ko bheet netron se dekh aur sir jhukaa liya|

heeraa ne kahaa-'gaya ke ghar se naahak bhaage, ab to jaan na bachegee|' motee ne ashraddhaa ke bhaav se javaab diya-'kehte hain, bhagavaan sabake oopar dayaa karte hain, unhen hamaare oopar
dayaa kyon nahin aatee ?'

'bhagavaan ke lie hamaaraa jeenaa maranaa donon baraabar hai| chalo, achchhaa hee hai, kuchh din usake paas to rahenge| ek baar us bhagavaan ne us ladakee ke roop mein hamen bachaayaa tha| kyaa
ab na bachaaenge ?'

'yah aadamee chhuree chalaaegaa, dekh lenaa|'

'to kyaa phikr hai ? maans, khaal, seeng, haddee sab kisee ke kaam aa jaaegaa|'

neelaam ho jaane ke baad donon mitr us dadhiyal ke saath chale| donon kee botee-botee kaanp rahi thi| bechaare paanv tak na uthaa sakate the, par dar ke maare girate-padate bhaage
jaate the, kyonki vah jaraa bhee chaal dheemee ho jaane par dandaa jamaa detaa tha|

raah mein gaay-bailon kaa ek revad hare-bhare haar mein charataa najar aay| sabhee jaanavar khush the, chikane, chapal| koee uchhalataa tha, koee khushee se baithaa paagur kart tha kitanaa sukhee
jeevan tha inakaa, par kitane svaarthee hain sab| kisee ko phikr nahin ki unake do baaee badhik ke haath pade kaise duhkhee hain|

sahasaa donon ko aisaa maaloom hua ki parichit raah hai| haan, isee raaste se gaya unhen le gaya tha| vahee khet, vahee baag, vahee gaanv milane lage, pratikshan unakee chaal tej
hone lagee| saaree thakaan, saaree durbalataa gaayab ho gaee| aah ! yah lo ! apanaa hee haar aa gaya| isee kuen par ham pur chalaane aay karte the, yahee kuaan hai|

motee ne kahaa-'hamaaraa ghar najadeek aa gaya hai|'

heeraa bol -'bhagavaan kee dayaa hai|'

'main to ab ghar bhaagataa hoon|'

'yah jaane degaa ?'

ise main maar giraataa hoon|

'nahin-nahin, daudakar thaan par chalo| vahaan se aage ham na jaaenge|'

donon unmatt hokar bachhadon kee tarah kulelen karte hue ghar kee ora daude| vah hamaaraa thaan hai| donon daudakar apne thaan par aae aur khade ho gae| dadhiyal bhee peechhe-peechhe
daudaa chalaa aat tha|

jhooree dvaar par baithaa dhoop khaa raha tha| bailon ko dekhate hee daudaa aur unhen baaree-baaree se gale lagaane lagaa| mitron kee aankhon se khushee ke aansoo bahane lage| ek jhooree kaa
haath chaat raha tha|

dadhiyal ne jaakar bailon kee rassiyaan pakad leen| jhooree ne kahaa-'mere bail hain|'

'tumhaare bail kaise hain ? main maveseekhaane se neelaam lie aat hoon|'

''main to samajhataa hoon, churaae lie jaate ho! chupake se chale jaao, mere bail hain| main bechoongaa to bikenge| kisee ko mere bail neelaam karne kaa kyaa akhtiyaar hain ?'

'jaakar thaane mein rapat kar doongaa|'

'mere bail hain| isakaa saboot yah hai ki mere dvaar par khade hain|

dadhiyal jhallaakar bailon ko jabaradastee pakad le jaane ke lie badhaa| usee vakt motee ne seeng chalaayaa| dadhiyal peechhe hataa| motee ne peechhaa kiya| dadhiyal bhaagaa| motee peechhe
daudaa, gaanv ke baahar nikal jaane par vah rukaa, par khadaa dadhiyal kaa raastaa vah dekh raha tha, dadhiyal door khadaa dhamakiyaan de raha tha, gaaliyaan nikaal raha tha, patthar phenk
raha tha, aur motee vijayee shoor kee tarah usakaa raastaa roke khadaa tha| gaanv ke log yah tamaashaa dekhate the aur hansate the| jab dadhiyal haarakar chalaa gaya to motee akadataa hua
lautaa| heeraa ne kahaa-'main to dar gaya tha ki kaheen tum gusse mein aakar maar na baitho|'

'ab na aaegaa|'

'aaegaa to door se hee khabar loongaa| dekhoon, kaise le jat hai|'

'jo golee maravaa de ?'

'mar jaaoongaa, par usake kaam na aaoongaa|'

'hamaaree jaan ko koee jaan hee nahin samajhataa|'

'isalie ki ham itane seedhe hain|'

jaraa der mein naandon mein khalee bhoosaa, chokar aur daanaa bhar diya gaya aur donon mitr khaane lage| jhooree khadaa donon ko sahalaa raha tha| vah unse lipat gaya|

jhooree kee patnee bhee bheetar se daudee-daudee aaee| usane ne aakar donon bailon ke maathe choom lie|
