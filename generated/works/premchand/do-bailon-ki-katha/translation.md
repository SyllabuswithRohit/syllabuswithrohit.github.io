# Do Bailon Ki Katha

**Munshi Premchand**

jaanavaron mein gadhaa sabase zyada buddhimaan samajhaa jat hai. ham jab kisee aadamee ko pahale darje kaa bevakooph kehna chahte hain, to use gadhaa kehte hain. gadhaa sachamuch bevakooph hai yaa
usake seedhepan, uski niraapad sahishnutaa ne use yah padavee de dee hai, isakaa phaisalaa nahin kiya jaa sakataa. gaayen seeng maaratee hain, byaahee hui gaay to anaayaas hi sinhanee kaa
roop dhaaran kar letee hai. kuttaa bhi bahut gareeb jaanavar hai, lekin kabhi-kabhi use bhi gussaa aa hi jat hai, lekin gadhe ko kabhi gussaa karte nahin sunaa, na dekh. jitanaa chaaho
gareeb ko maaro, chaahe jaisee kharaab, sadee hui ghaas saamane daal do, usake chehare par kabhi asantosh ki chhaayaa bhi nahin dikhaaee degee. vaishaakh mein chaahe ekaadh baar kulel kar letaa hai,
par hamne to use kabhi khush hote nahin dekh. usake chehare par sthaaee udaasee sthaayee roop se chhaayaa rehta hai. sukh-duhkh, nukasaan-phaayadaa kisee bhi dashaa mein use badalate nahin dekh.
rishiyon-muniyon ke jitane gun hain, ve sabhi usamen paraakaashthaa ko pahunch gae hain, par aadamee use bevakooph keht hai. sadgunon kaa itanaa anaadar!

shaayad seedhaapan sansaar ke lie upayukt nahin hai. dekhie na, bhaaratavaasiyon ki aphreekaa mein kya durdashaa ho rahi hai? kyon amareekaa mein unhen ghusane nahin diya jat?
bechaare sharaab nahin peete,chaar paise kusamay ke lie bachaakar rakhte hain, jee todakar kaam karte hain, kisee se ladaaee-jhagadaa nahin karte, chaar baaten sunakar gam khaa jaate hain phir
bhi badanaam hain. kahaa jat hai, ve jeevan ke aadarsh ko neechaa karte hain. agar ve eent kaa jawab patthar se dena seekh jaate to shaayad sabhy kahalaane lagte. jaapaan ki misaal saamane
hai. ek hi vijay ne use sansaar ki sabhy jaatiyon mein gany banaa diya. lekin gadhe kaa ek chhota bhai aur bhi hai, jo usse kam hi gadhaa hai. aur vah hai 'bail'. jis arth mein ham
'gadhaa' kaa prayog karte hain, kuchh usi se milate-julate arth mein 'bachhiyaa ke taaoo' kaa bhi prayog karte hain. kuchh log bail ko shaayad bevakoophee mein sarvashreshth kahenge, magar
hamaaraa soch aisaa nahin hai. bail kabhi-kabhi maarataa bhi hai, kabhi-kabhi adiyal bail bhi dekhne mein aat hai. aur bhi kaee reetiyon se apna asantosh prakat kar detaa hai, ataevan uska
sthaan gadhe se neechaa hai.

jhooree ka paas do bail the- heeraa aur motee. dekhne mein sundar, kaam mein chaukas, deel mein oonche. bahut dinon saath rehte-rehte donon mein bhaaeechaaraa ho gaya tha. donon aamane-saamane
yaa aas-paas baithe hue ek-doosare se mook bhaashaa mein soch-vinimay kiya karte the. ek-doosare ke man ki baat ko kaise samajhaa jat hai, ham kah nahin sakate. avashy hi unamen koi
aisee gupt shakti thi,jisse jeevon mein shreshthta kaa daavaa karne vaalaa manushy vanchit hai. donon ek-doosare ko chaatakar soonghakar apna pyaar prakat karte, kabhi-kabhi donon
seeng bhi milaa liya karte the, vigrah ke naate se nahin, keval vinod ke bhaav se, aatmeeyataa ke bhaav se, jaise donon mein ghanishthta hote hi dhaul-dhappaa hone lagta hai.
isake binaa dostee kuchh phusaphasee, kuchh halkee-see rehti hai, phir zyada yakeen nahin kiya jaa sakataa. jis vakt ye donon bail hal yaa gaadee mein jot die jaate aur garadan
hilaa-hilaakar chalte, us samay har ek ki cheshtaa hoti ki zyada-se-zyada bojh meri hi gardan par rahe.

din-bhar ke baad dopahar yaa shaam ko donon khulate to ek-doosare ko chaat-choot kar apni thakaan mitaa liya karte, naand mein khalee-bhoosaa pad jaane ke baad donon saath uthte, saath
naand mein munh daalate aur saath hi baithte the. ek munh hataa letaa to doosaraa bhi hataa letaa tha.

sanyog ki baat, jhooree ne ek baar goeen ko sasuraal bhej diya. bailon ko kya maaloom, ve kahan bheje jaa rahe hain. samajhe, maalik ne hamen bech diya. apna yon bechaa jan
unhen achchha lagaa yaa buraa, kaun jaane, par jhooree ke saale gaya ko ghar tak goeen le jaane mein daanton paseenaa aa gaya. peechhe se haankataa to donon daaen-baane bhaagate, pagahiyaa
pakadakar aage se kheenchta to donon peechhe ki ora jor lagaate. maarataa to donon seenge neechee karake hunkaarate. agar eeshvar ne unhen vaanee dee hoti to jhooree se poochhte-tum ham
gareebon ko kyon nikaal rahe ho?

hamne to tumhari sevaa karne mein koi kasar nahin uthaa rakhee. agar itanee mehnat se kaam na chalta tha, aur kaam le lete. hamen to tumhari chaakaree mein mar jan kabool tha. hamne
kabhi daane-chaare ki shikaayat nahin ki. tumne jo kuchh khilaayaa, vah sir jhukaakar khaa liya, phir tumne hamen is jaalim ke haath kyon bench diya?

shaam samay donon bail apne nae sthaan par pahunche. din-bhar ke bhookhe the, lekin jab naand mein lagaae gae to ek ne bhi usamen munh nahin daalaa. dil bhaaree ho raha tha. jise
unhone apna ghar samajh rakhaa tha, vah aaj unse chhoot gaya. yah nayaa ghar, nayaa gaanv, nae aadamee unhen begaane-se lagte the.

donon ne apni mook bhaashaa mein salaah ki, ek-doosare ko kanakhiyon se dekh aur let gaye. jab gaanv mein sotaa pad gaya to donon ne jor maarakar pagahaa tudaa daale aur ghar ki taraph
chale. pagahe bahut mazboot the. anumaan na ho sakataa tha ki koi bail unhen tod sakegaa, par ina donon mein is samay doonee shakti aa gayi thi. ek-ek jhatake mein rassiyaan toot gaeen.

jhooree subah kaal so kar uthaa to dekh ki donon bail charanee par khade hain. donon ki garadanon mein aadhaa-aadhaa garaanv latak raha tha. ghutane tak paanv keechad se bhare hain aur donon
ki aankhon mein vidrohamay pyaar jhalak raha hai.

jhooree bailon ko dekhakar pyaar se gadgad ho gaya. daudakar unhen gale lagaa liya. premaalingan aur chumban kaa vah drishy bada hi manohar tha.

ghar aur gaanv ke ladake jamaa ho gae. aur taaliyaan bajaa-bajaakar unakaa svaagat karne lage. gaanv ke itihaas mein yah ghatanaa abhootapoorv na hone par bhi mahattvapoorn thi, baal-sabhaa ne
phaisalaa kiya, donon pashu-veeron kaa abhinandan patr dena chaahie. koi apne ghar se rotiyaan laayaa, koi gud, koi chokar, koi bhoosee.

ek ladakaa ne kahaa- ''aise bail kisee ke paas na honge.''

doosare ne samarthan kiya- ''itanee door se donon akele chale aae.'

teesaraa bol- 'bail nahin hain ve, us janm ke aadamee hain.'

isakaa prativaad karne kaa kisee ko saahas nahin hua. jhooree ki aurat ne bailon ko dvaar par dekh to jal uthee. bolee -'kaise namak-haraam bail hain ki ek din wahan kaam na
kiya, bhaag khade hue.'

jhooree apne bailon par yah aakshep na sun sakaa-'namak haraam kyon hain? chaaraa-daanaa na diya hogaa to kya karte?'

aurat ne rob ke saath kahaa-'bas, tumheen to bailon ko khilaanaa jaanate ho, aur to sabhi paani pilaa-pilaakar rakhte hain.'

jhooree ne chidhaayaa-'chaaraa milataa to kyon bhaagate?'

aurat chidh gayi-'bhaage isalie ki ve log tum jaise buddhuon ki tarah bail ko sahalaate nahin, khilaate hain to ragadakar jotate bhi hain. ye donon thahare kaamachor, bhaag nikale.
ab dekhoon kahan se khalee aur chokar milataa hai. sookhe bhoose ke sivaa kuchh na doongee, khaaen chaahen maren.'

wahi hua. majoor ki badi taakeed ki gayi ki bailon ko khaalee sookhaa bhoosaa diya jaae.

bailon ne naand mein munh daalaa to pheekaa-pheekaa, na koi chikanaahat, na koi ras!

kya khaaen? ummeed-bharee aankhon se dvaar ki ora taakane lage. jhooree ne majoor se kahaa-'thodee-see khalee kyon nahin daal detaa be?'

'maalakin mujhe maar hi daalengee.'

'churaakar daal aa.'

'naa daadaa, peechhe se tum bhi unheen ki-see kahoge.'

doosare din jhooree kaa saalaa phir aay aur bailon ko le chalaa. abakee usane donon ko gaadee mein jotaa.

do-chaar baar motee ne gaadee ko khaaee mein giraanaa chaahaa, par heeraa ne sanbhaal liya. vah zyada sahanasheel tha.

shaam-samay ghar pahunchakar usane donon ko motee rassiyon se baandhaa aur kal ki sharaarat kaa majaa chakhaayaa phir wahi sookhaa bhoosaa daal diya. apne donon baalon ko khalee choonee sab kuchh
dee.

donon bailon kaa aisaa beizzatee kabhi na hua tha. jhooree ne inhen phool ki chhadee se bhi chhootaa tha. uski titakaar par donon udane lagte the. yahan maar padee. aahat izzat ki vyathaa
to thi hi,us par milaa sookhaa bhoosaa!

naand ki taraph aankhen tak na uthaaeen.

doosare din gaya ne bailon ko hal mein jotaa, par ina donon ne jaise paanv na uthaane ki kasam khaa lee thi. vah maarate-maarate thak gaya, par donon ne paanv na uthaayaa. ek baar jab us
nirdayee ne heeraa ki naak par khoob dande jamaaye to motee ko gussaa kaaboo se baahar ho gaya. hal lekar bhaagaa. hal, rassee, juaa, jot, sab toot-taatakar baraabar ho gaya. gale mein
badi-badi rassiyaan na hoteen to donon pakadaaee mein na aate.

heeraa ne mook-bhaashaa mein kahaa-bhaaganaa bekaar hai.'

motee ne jawab diya-'tumhari to isane jaan hi le lee thi.'

'abakee badi maar padegee.'

'padane do, bail kaa janm liya hai to maar se kahan tak bachenge?'

'gaya do aadamiyon ke saath daudaa aa raha hai, donon ke haathon mein laathiyaan hain.'

motee bol-'kaho to dikhaa doon majaa main bhi, laathee lekar aa raha hai.'

heeraa ne samajhaayaa-'nahin bhai! khade ho jaao.'

'mujhe maaregaa to main ek-do ko giraa doongaa.'

'nahin hamaaree jaati kaa yah dharm nahin hai.'

motee dil mein ainthakar raha gaya. gaya aa pahunchaa aur donon ko pakad kar le chalaa. kushal hui ki usane is vakt maarapeet na ki, nahin to motee palat padataa. usake tevar dekh gaya aur
usake sahaayak samajh gae ki is vakt taal jan hi bhalamanasaahat hai.

aaj donon ke saamane phir wahi sookhaa bhoosaa laayaa gaya, donon chupachaap khade rahe.

ghar mein log khaanaa karne lage. us vakt chhoti-see ladakee do rotiyaan lie nikalee aur donon ke munh mein dekar chalee gayi. us ek rotee se inakee bhookh to kya shaant hoti, par
donon ke dil ko maano khaanaa mil gaya. yahan bhi kisee sajjan kaa vaas hai. ladakee bhairo ki thi. uski maan mar chukee thi. sautelee maan use maaratee rehti thi, isalie ina bailon
se ek tarah ki aatmeeyataa ho gayi thi.

donon din-bhar jaate, dande khaate, adate, shaam ko thaan par baandh die jaate aur raat ko wahi ladakee unhen do rotiyaan khilaa jati. pyaar ke is prasaad ki yah barakat thi ki
do-do gaal sookhaa bhoosaa khaakar bhi donon durbal na hote the, magar donon ki aankhon mein rom-rom mein vidroh bharaa hua tha.

ek din motee ne mook-bhaashaa mein kahaa-'ab to nahin sahaa jat heeraa!

'kya karn chahte ho?'

'ekaadh ko seengon par uthaakar phenk doongaa.'

'lekin jaanate ho, vah pyaaree ladakee, jo hamen rotiyaan khilaatee hai, usi ki ladakee hai, jo is ghar kaa maalik hai, yah bechaaree anaath ho jaaegee.'

'to maalakin ko phenk doon, wahi to is ladakee ko maaratee hai.

'lekin aurat jaat par seeng chalaanaa manaa hai, yah bhool jaate ho.'

'tum to kisee tarah nikalne hi nahin dete, bataao, tudaakar bhaag chalen.'

'haan, yah main sveekaar kart, lekin itanee motee rassee tootegee kaise.'

isakaa ek upaay hai, pahale rassee ko thodaa chabaa lo. phir ek jhatake mein jati hai.'

raat ko jab ladakee rotiyaan khilaa kar chalee gayi to donon rassiyaan chabane lage, par motee rassee munh mein na aatee thi. bechaare baar-baar jor lagaakar raha jaate the.

saahasaa ghar kaa dvaar khulaa aur vah ladakee nikalee. donon sir jhukaakar uska haath chaatane lage. donon ki poonchhen khadee ho gaeen. usane unake maathe sahalaae aur bolee-'khol detee hoon,
chupake se bhaag jaao, nahin to ye log maar daalenge. aaj ghar mein salaah ho rahi hai ki inakee naakon mein naath daal dee jaaen.'

usane garaanv khol diya, par donon chup khade rahe.

motee ne apni bhaashaa mein poonchhaa-'ab chalte kyon nahin?'

heeraa ne kahaa-'chalen to, lekin kal is anaath par aaphat aaegee, sab isi par shak karenge.

saahasaa ladakee chillaaee-'donon phoophaa vaale bail bhaage je rahe hain, o daadaa! daadaa! donon bail bhaage jaa rahe hain, o daadaa! daadaa! donon bail bhaage jaa rahe hain, jaldee
daudo.

gaya hadabadaakar bheetar se nikalaa aur bailon ko pakadane chalaa. ve donon bhaage. gaya ne peechhaa kiya, aur bhi tej hue, gaya ne shor machaayaa. phir gaanv ke kuchh aadamiyon ko bhi saath
lene ke lie lautaa. donon mitron ko bhaagane kaa maukaa mil gaya. seedhe daudate chale gae. yahan tak ki raastaa kaa jnaan raha. jis parichit raastaa se aae the, uska yahan pataa na
tha. nae-nae gaanv milne lage. tab donon ek khet ke kinaare khade hokar sochne lage, ab kya karn chaahie.

heeraa ne kahaa-'mujhe maaloom hot hai, raah bhool gae.'

'tum bhi betahaashaa bhaage, vaheen use maar giraanaa tha.'
'use maar giraate to duniyaa kya kehti? vah apne dharm chhod de, lekin ham apna dharm kyon chhoden?'

donon bhookh se vyaakul ho rahe the. khet mein matar khadee thi. charane lage. raha-rahakar aahat lete rahe the. koi aat to nahin hai.

jab pet bhar gaya, donon ne aajaadee kaa anubhav kiya to mast hokar uchhalane-koodane lage. pahale donon ne dakaar lee. phir seeng milaae aur ek-doosare ko thekane lage. motee ne heeraa
ko kaee kadam peechhe hataa diya, yahan tak ki vah khaaee mein gir gaya. tab use bhi gussaa aa gaya. sanbhalakar uthaa aur motee se bhid gaya. motee ne dekh ki khel mein jhagadaa hua chahta
hai to kinaare hat gaya.

are! yah kya? koi saand daunkataa chalaa aa raha hai. haan, saand hi hai. vah saamane aa pahunchaa. donon mitr bagalen jhaank rahe the. saand pooraa haathee tha. usse bhidanaa jaan
se haath dhonaa hai, lekin na bhidane par bhi jaan bachti najar nahin aatee. inheen ki taraph aa bhi raha hai. kitanee bhayankar soorat hai!

motee ne mook-bhaashaa mein kahaa-'bure phanse, jaan bachegee? koi upaay socho.'

heeraa ne chintit aavaaz mein kahaa-'apne ghamand mein phoolaa hua hai, aarajoo-vinatee na sunegaa.'

'bhaag kyon na chalen?'

'bhaaganaa kaayarataa hai.'

'to phir yaheen maro. bandaa to nau do gyaarah hot hai.'

'aur jo daudaae?'

' to phir koi upaae socho jald!'

'upaay yah hai ki us par donon jane ek saath chot karen. main aage se ragedataa hoon, tum peechhe se ragedo, doharee maar padegee to bhaag khadaa hogaa. meri ora jhapate, tum bagal se
usake pet mein seeng ghused dena. jaan jokhim hai, par doosaraa upaay nahin hai.

donon mitr jaan hathelee par lekar lapake. saand ko bhi sangathit shatruon se ladane kaa tajurabaa na tha.

vah to ek shatru se mallayuddh karne kaa aadee tha. jyon-hi heeraa par jhapataa, motee ne peechhe se daudaayaa. saand uski taraph mudaa to heeraa ne ragedaa. saand chahta tha, ki
ek-ek karake donon ko giraa le, par ye donon bhi ustaad the. use vah avasar na dete the. ek baar saand jhallaakar heeraa kaa ant kar dene ke lie chalaa ki motee ne bagal se
aakar usake pet mein seeng bhonk diya. saand gussaa mein aakar peechhe phiraa to heeraa ne doosare pahaloo mein seenge chubhaa diya.

aakhir bechaaraa jakhmee hokar bhaagaa aur donon mitron ne door tak uska peechhaa kiya. yahan tak ki saand bedam hokar gir padaa. tab donon ne use chhod diya. donon mitr jeet
ke nashe mein jhoomate chale jaate the.

motee ne saanketik bhaashaa mein kahaa-'mera jee chahta tha ki bachaa ko maar hi daaloon.'

heeraa ne tiraskaar kiya-'gire hue bairee par seeng na chalaanaa chaahie.'

'yah sab dhong hai, bairee ko aisaa maaranaa chaahie ki phir na uthe.'

'ab ghar kaise pahunchoge vah socho.'

'pahale kuchh khaa len, to sochen.'

saamane matar kaa khet tha hi, motee usamen ghus gaya. heeraa manaa kart raha, par usane ek na sunee. abhi do hi chaar graas khaaye the ki aadamee laathiyaan lie daud pade aur donon
mitr ko gher liya, heeraa to med par tha nikal gaya. motee seenche hue khet mein tha. usake khur keechad mein dhansane lage. na bhaag sakaa. pakad liya. heeraa ne dekh, sangee sankat
mein hai to laut padaa. phansenge to donon phansenge. rakhavaalon ne use bhi pakad liya.

subah donon mitr kaanjee haus mein band kar die gae.

donon mitron ko jeevan mein pahalee baar aisaa saabikaa padaa tha ki sara din beet gaya aur khaane ko ek tinakaa bhi na milaa. samajh mein na aat tha, yah kaisaa svaamee hai. isase to
gaya phir bhi achchha tha. yahan kaee bhainse theen, kaee bakariyaan, kaee ghode, kaee gadhe, par kisee ke saamane chaaraa na tha, sab jameen par murdon ki tarah pade the.

kaee to itane kamajor ho gaye the ki khade bhi na ho sakate the. sara din mitr phaatak ki ora takatakee lagaae rehte, par koi chaaraa na lekar aat dikhaaee diya. tab donon ne deevaar ki
namakeen mittee chaatanee shuru ki, par isase kya tripti hoti.

raat ko bhi jab kuchh khaanaa na milaa to heeraa ke dil mein vidroh ki jvaalaa dahak uthee. motee se bol-'ab nahin raha jat motee!

motee ne sir latakaae hue jawab diya-'mujhe to maaloom hot hai ki praan nikal rahe hain.'

'aao deevaar tod daalen.'

'mujhse to ab kuchh nahin hogaa.'

'bas isi boot par akadate the!'

'sari akad nikal gayi.'

baade ki deevaar kachchee thi. heeraa mazboot to tha hi, apne nukeele seeng deevaar mein gadaa die aur jor maaraa to mittee kaa ek chippad nikal aay. phir to uska saahas badhaa
usane daud-daudakar deevaar par choten keen aur har chot mein thodee-thodee mittee giraane lagaa.

usi samay kaanjee haus kaa chaukeedaar laalaten lekar jaanavaron ki haajiree lene aa nikalaa. heeraa kaa uddanddapann dekhakar use kaee dande raseed kie aur motee-see rassee se baandh diya.

motee ne pade-pade kahaa-'aakhir maar khaaee, kya milaa?'

'apne boote-bhar jor to maar diya.'

'aisaa jor maaranaa kis kaam kaa ki aur bandhan mein pad gae.'

'jor to maarataa hi jaaoongaa, chaahe kitane hi bandhan padate jaaen.'

'jaan se haath dhonaa padegaa.'

'kuchh paravaah nahin. yon bhi to maranaa hi hai. socho, deevaar khud jati to kitanee jaane bach jaateen. itane bhai yahan band hain. kisee ki deh mein jaan nahin hai. do-chaar din yahi
haal raha to mar jaaenge.'

'haan, yah baat to hai. achchha, to laa phir main bhi jor lagaataa hoon.'

motee ne bhi deevaar mein seeng maaraa, thodee-see mittee giree aur phir himmat badhee, phir to vah deevaar mein seeng lagaakar is tarah jor karne lagaa, maano kisee pratidvandee se
lad raha hai. aakhir koi do ghante ki jor-aajamaaee ke baad deevaar oopar se lagabhag ek haath gir gayi, usane doonee shakti se doosaraa dhakkaa maaraa to aadhee deevaar gir padee.

deevaar kaa giranaa tha ki adhamare-se pade hue sabhi jaanavar chet uthe, teenon ghodiyaan sarapat bhaag nikaleen. phir bakariyaan nikaleen, isake baad bhains bhi khasak gayi, par gadhe abhi tak
jyon ke tyon khade the.

heeraa ne poochhaa-'tum donon kyon nahin bhaag jaate?'

ek gadhe ne kahaa-'jo kaheen phir pakad lie jaaen.'

'to kya haraj hai, abhi to bhaagane kaa avasar hai.'

'hamen to dar lagta hai. ham yaheen pade rahenge.'

aadhee raat se oopar jaa chukee thi. donon gadhe abhi tak khade soch rahe the ki bhaagen, yaa na bhaagen, aur motee apne mitr ki rassee todane mein lagaa hua tha. jab vah haar gaya to
heeraa ne kahaa-'tum jaao, mujhe yaheen padaa rehne do, shaayad kaheen bhent ho jaae.'

motee ne aankhon mein aansoo laakar kahaa-'tum mujhe itanaa svaarthee samajhte ho, heeraa ham aur tum itane dinon ek saath rahe hain. aaj tum vipatti mein pad gae ho to main tumhen
chhodakar alag ho jaaoon?'

heeraa ne kahaa-'bahut maar padegee, log samajh jaaenge, yah tumhari sharaarat hai.'

motee ne garv se bol-'jis aparaadh ke lie tumhaare gale mein bandhanaa padaa, usake lie agar mujhe maar pade, to kya chintaa. itanaa to ho hi gaya ki nau-das praaniyon ki
jaan bach gayi, ve sab to aasheervaad denge.'

yah kehte hue motee ne donon gadhon ko seengon se maar-maar kar baade se baahar nikaalaa aur tab apne bandhu ke paas aakar so raha.

bhor hote hi munshee aur chaukeedaar tathaa any karmachaariyon mein kaisee khalabalee machee, isake likhne ki jaroorat nahin. bas, itanaa hi kaaphee hai ki motee ki khoob marammat hui aur use bhi
motee rassee se baandh diya gaya.

ek saptaah tak donon mitr wahan bandhe pade rahe. kisee ne chaare kaa ek trin bhi na daalaa. haan, ek baar paani dikhaa diya jat tha. yahi unakaa aadhaar tha. donon itane durbal
ho gae the ki uthaa tak nahin jat tha, thathariyaan nikal aaeen theen. ek din baade ke saamane duggee bajane lagee aur dopahar hote-hote wahan pachaas-saath aadamee jamaa ho gae. tab donon
mitr nikaale gae aur log aakar unki soorat dekhte aur man pheekaa karake chale jaate.

aise mritak bailon kaa kaun khareedadaar hot? sahasaa ek dadhiyal aadamee, jisakee aankhen laal theen aur mudraa bahut kathor, aay aur donon mitr ke koolhon mein ungalee godakar
munsheejee se baaten karne lagaa. cheharaa dekhakar antarjnaan se donon mitron kaa dil kaanp uthe. vah kyon hai aur kyon tatol raha hai, is vishay mein unhen koi shak na
hua. donon ne ek-doosare ko bheet netron se dekh aur sir jhukaa liya.

heeraa ne kahaa-'gaya ke ghar se naahak bhaage, ab to jaan na bachegee.' motee ne ashraddhaa ke bhaav se jawab diya-'kehte hain, bhagavaan sabake oopar dayaa karte hain, unhen hamaare oopar
dayaa kyon nahin aatee?'

'bhagavaan ke lie hamaaraa jeenaa maranaa donon baraabar hai. chalo, achchha hi hai, kuchh din usake paas to rahenge. ek baar us bhagavaan ne us ladakee ke roop mein hamen bachaayaa tha. kya
ab na bachaaenge?'

'yah aadamee chhuree chalaaegaa, dekh lena.'

'to kya phikr hai? maans, khaal, seeng, haddee sab kisee ke kaam aa jaaegaa.'

neelaam ho jaane ke baad donon mitr us dadhiyal ke saath chale. donon ki botee-botee kaanp rahi thi. bechaare paanv tak na uthaa sakate the, par dar ke maare girate-padate bhaage
jaate the, kyonki vah zara bhi chaal dheemee ho jaane par dandaa jamaa detaa tha.

raah mein gaay-bailon kaa ek revad hare-bhare haar mein charataa najar aay. sabhi jaanavar khush the, chikane, chapal. koi uchhalataa tha, koi khushi se baithaa paagur kart tha kitanaa sukhee
jeevan tha inakaa, par kitane svaarthee hain sab. kisee ko phikr nahin ki unake do baaee badhik ke haath pade kaise duhkhee hain.

sahasaa donon ko aisaa maaloom hua ki parichit raah hai. haan, isi raaste se gaya unhen le gaya tha. wahi khet, wahi baag, wahi gaanv milne lage, pratikshan unki chaal tej
hone lagee. sari thakaan, sari durbalataa gaayab ho gayi. aah! yah lo! apna hi haar aa gaya. isi kuen par ham pur chalaane aay karte the, yahi kuaan hai.

motee ne kahaa-'hamaaraa ghar najadeek aa gaya hai.'

heeraa bol -'bhagavaan ki dayaa hai.'

'main to ab ghar bhaagataa hoon.'

'yah jaane degaa?'

ise main maar giraataa hoon.

'nahin-nahin, daudakar thaan par chalo. wahan se aage ham na jaaenge.'

donon unmatt hokar bachhadon ki tarah kulelen karte hue ghar ki ora daude. vah hamaaraa thaan hai. donon daudakar apne thaan par aae aur khade ho gae. dadhiyal bhi peechhe-peechhe
daudaa chalaa aat tha.

jhooree dvaar par baithaa dhoop khaa raha tha. bailon ko dekhte hi daudaa aur unhen baaree-baaree se gale lagaane lagaa. mitron ki aankhon se khushi ke aansoo bahane lage. ek jhooree kaa
haath chaat raha tha.

dadhiyal ne jaakar bailon ki rassiyaan pakad leen. jhooree ne kahaa-'mere bail hain.'

'tumhaare bail kaise hain? main maveseekhaane se neelaam lie aat hoon.'

''main to samajhta hoon, churaae lie jaate ho! chupake se chale jaao, mere bail hain. main bechoongaa to bikenge. kisee ko mere bail neelaam karne kaa kya akhtiyaar hain?'

'jaakar thaane mein rapat kar doongaa.'

'mere bail hain. isakaa saboot yah hai ki mere dvaar par khade hain.

dadhiyal jhallaakar bailon ko jabaradastee pakad le jaane ke lie badhaa. usi vakt motee ne seeng chalaayaa. dadhiyal peechhe hataa. motee ne peechhaa kiya. dadhiyal bhaagaa. motee peechhe
daudaa, gaanv ke baahar nikal jaane par vah rukaa, par khadaa dadhiyal kaa raastaa vah dekh raha tha, dadhiyal door khadaa dhamakiyaan de raha tha, gaaliyaan nikaal raha tha, patthar phenk
raha tha, aur motee vijayee shoor ki tarah uska raastaa roke khadaa tha. gaanv ke log yah tamaashaa dekhte the aur hansate the. jab dadhiyal haarakar chalaa gaya to motee akadataa hua
lautaa. heeraa ne kahaa-'main to dar gaya tha ki kaheen tum gusse mein aakar maar na baitho.'

'ab na aaegaa.'

'aaegaa to door se hi khabar loongaa. dekhoon, kaise le jat hai.'

'jo golee maravaa de?'

'mar jaaoongaa, par usake kaam na aaoongaa.'

'hamaaree jaan ko koi jaan hi nahin samajhta.'

'isalie ki ham itane seedhe hain.'

zara der mein naandon mein khalee bhoosaa, chokar aur daanaa bhar diya gaya aur donon mitr khaane lage. jhooree khadaa donon ko sahalaa raha tha. vah unse lipat gaya.

jhooree ki patnee bhi bheetar se daudee-daudee aaee. usane ne aakar donon bailon ke maathe choom lie.
