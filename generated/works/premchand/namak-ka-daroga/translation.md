# Namak Ka Daroga

**Munshi Premchand**

jab namak kaa nayaa vibhaag banaa aur eeshvarapradatt vastu ke vyavahaar karne kaa nishedh ho gaya to log choree-chhipe isakaa vyaapaar karne lage| anek tarah ke chhal-prapanchon kaa sootrapaat hua, koee ghoos se kaam nikaalataa tha, koee chaalaakee se| adhikaariyon ke pau-baarah the| patavaareegiree kaa sarvasammaanit pad chhod-chhodakar log is vibhaag kee barakandaajee karte the| isake daarogaa pad ke lie to vakeelon kaa bhee jee lalachaataa tha|
yah vah samay tha jab engarejee shikshaa aur eesaaee mata ko log ek hee vastu samajhate the| phaarasee kaa praabaly tha| pyaar kee kathaaen aur shringaar ras ke kaavy padhakar phaaraseedaan log sarvochch padon par niyukt ho jaayaa karte the|
munshee vansheedhar bhee julekhaa kee virah-kathaa khatm karake seeree aur pharahaad ke pyaar-vrittaant ko nal aur neel kee ladaaee aur amerikaa ke aavishkaar se zyaadaa mahatv kee baaten samajhate hue rojagaar kee khoj mein nikale|
unake pitaa ek anubhavee aadamee the| samajhaane lage, 'betaa! ghar kee durdashaa dekh rahe ho| reen ke bojh se dabe hue hain| ladakiyaan hain, ve ghaas-phoos kee tarah badhatee chalee jati hain| main kagaare par kaa vriksh ho raha hoon, na maaloom kab gir padoon! ab tumheen ghar ke maalik-mukhtaar ho|
'naukaree mein ohade kee ora dhyaan mata denaa, yah to peer kaa majaar hai| nigaah chadhaave aur chaadar par rakhanee chaahie| aisaa kaam dhoondhanaa jahaan kuchh ooparee aay ho| maasik vetan to poornamaasee kaa chaand hai, jo ek din dikhaaee detaa hai aur ghatate-ghatate lupt ho jat hai| ooparee aay bahataa hua srot hai jisse hameshaa pyaas bujhatee hai| vetan manushy detaa hai, isee se usamen vridhdi nahin hoti| ooparee aamadanee eeshvar detaa hai, isee se usakee barakat hoti hain, tum svayan vidvaan ho, tumhen kyaa samajhaaoon|
'is vishay mein vivek kee badee aavashyakataa hai| manushy ko dekho, usakee aavashyakataa ko dekho aur avasar ko dekho, usake uparaant jo theek samajho, karo| garajavaale aadamee ke saath kathorataa karne mein phaayadaa hee phaayadaa hai| lekin begaraj ko daanv par paanaa jaraa mushkil hai| ina baaton ko nigaah mein baandh lo yah meree janm bhar kee kamaaee hai|
is upadesh ke baad pitaajee ne aasheervaad diya| vansheedhar aajnaakaaree betaa the| ye baaten dhyaan se suneen aur tab ghar se chal khade hue| is vistrit sansaar mein unake lie dhairy apanaa mitr, budhdi apanee pathapradarshak aur aatmaavalamban hee apanaa sahaayak tha| lekin achchhe shakun se chale the, jaate hee jaate namak vibhaag ke daarogaa pad par pratishthit ho gae| vetan achchhaa aur ooparee aay kaa to thikaanaa hee na tha| vridhd munsheejee ko sukh-baatacheet milaa to phoole na samaae| mahaajan kuchh naram pade, kalavaar kee aashaalataa lahalahaaee| padosiyon ke dil mein shool uthane lage|
jaade ke din the aur raat kaa samay| namak ke sipaahee, chaukeedaar nashe mein mast the| munshee vansheedhar ko yahan aae abhee chhah maheenon se zyaadaa na hue the, lekin is thode samay mein hee unhonne apanee kaaryakushalataa aur uttam aachaar se aphasaron ko mohit kar liya tha| aphasar log una par bahut yakeen karne lage|
namak ke daphtar se ek meel pahale kee ora jamunaa bahatee thi, us par naavon kaa ek pul banaa hua tha| daarogaajee kivaad band kie meethee neend so rahe the| achaanak onkha khulee to nadee ke pravaah kee jagah gaadiyon kee gadagadaahat tathaa mallaahon kaa kolaahal sunaaee diya| uth baithe|
itanee raat gae gaadiyaan kyon nadee ke paar jati hain? avashy kuchh na kuchh golamaal hai| tark ne bhram ko pusht kiya| varadee pahanee, tamanchaa jeb mein rakhaa aur baat kee baat mein ghodaa badhaae hue pul par aa pahunche| gaadiyon kee ek lambee kataar pul ke paar jati dekhee| daantakar poochhaa, 'kisakee gaadiyaan hain|
thodee der tak sannaataa raha| aadamiyon mein kuchh kaanaaphoosee huee tab aage vaale ne kahaa-'pandit alopeedeen kee|
'kaun pandit alopeedeen?
'daataaganj ke|
munshee vansheedhar chaunke| pandit alopeedeen is ilaake ke sabase pratishthit jameendaar the| laakhon rupae kaa len-den karte the, idhar chhote se bade kaun aise the jo unake reenee na hon| vyaapaar bhee badaa lambaa-chaudaa tha| bade chalate-puraje aadamee the| engareja aphasar unake ilaake mein shikaar khelane aate aur unake mehamaan hote| baarahon maas sadaavrat chalataa tha|
munshee ne poochhaa, 'gaadiyaan kahan jaaengee? javaab milaa, 'kaanapur | lekin is savaal par ki inamen kyaa hai, sannaataa chhaa gaya| daarogaa sahab kaa shak aur bhee badhaa| kuchh der tak javaab kee baat dekhakar vah jor se bole, 'kyaa tum sab goonge ho gae ho? ham poochhate hain inamen kyaa ladaa hai?
jab is baar bhee koee javaab na milaa to unhonne ghode ko ek gaadee se milaakar bore ko tatolaa| bhram door ho gaya| yah namak ke dele the|
pandit alopeedeen apne sajeele rath par savaar, kuchh sote, kuchh jaagate chale aate the| achaanak kaee gaadeevaanon ne ghabaraae hue aakar jagaayaa aur bole-'mahaaraaj! daarogaa ne gaadiyaan rok dee hain aur ghaat par khade aapako bulaate hain|
pandit alopeedeen kaa lakshmeejee par akhand yakeen tha| vah kahaa karte the ki sansaar kaa to kahanaa hee kyaa, svarg mein bhee lakshmee kaa hee raajy hai| unakaa yah kahanaa yathaarth hee tha| nyaay aur neeti sab lakshmee ke hee khilaune hain, inhen vah jaise chaahatee hain nachaatee hain| lete hee lete garv se bole, chalo ham aate hain| yah kahakar panditajee ne badee nishnchitataa se paan ke beede lagaakar khaae| phir lihaaph odhe hue daarogaa ke paas aakar bole, 'baaboojee aasheervaad! kahie, hamse aisaa kaun saa aparaadh hua ki gaadiyaan rok dee gaeen| ham braahmanon par to aapakee kripaa-nazar rahanee chaahie|
vansheedhar rukhaaee se bole, 'sarakaaree hukm|
pan. alopeedeen ne hansakar kahaa, 'ham sarakaaree hukm ko nahin jaanate aur na sarakaar ko| hamaare sarakaar to aap hee hain| hamaaraa aur aapakaa to ghar kaa maamalaa hai, ham kabhee aapase baahar ho sakate hain? aapane bekaar kaa takaleeph uthaayaa| yah ho nahin sakataa ki idhar se jaaen aur is ghaat ke devataa ko bhent na chadhaaven| main to aapakee sevaa mein svayan hee aa raha tha| vansheedhar par aishvary kee mohinee vanshee kaa kuchh prabhaav na padaa| eemaanadaaree kee naee umang thi| kadakakar bole, 'ham una namakaharaamon mein nahin hai jo kaudiyon par apanaa eemaan bechate phirate hain| aap is samay hiraasat mein hain| aapako kaayade ke anusaar chaalaan hogaa| bas, mujhe zyaadaa baaton kee phursat nahin hai| jamaadaar badaloosinh! tum inhen hiraasat mein le chalo, main hukm detaa hoon|
pan. alopeedeen stambhit ho gae| gaadeevaanon mein halachal mach gaee| panditajee ke jeevan mein shaayad yah pahalaa hee avasar tha ki panditajee ko aisee kathor baaten sunanee padeen| badaloosinh aage badhaa, lekin rob ke maare yah saahas na hua ki unakaa haath pakad sake| panditajee ne dharm ko paisaa kaa aisaa niraadar karte kabhee na dekh tha| soch kiya ki yah abhee uddand ladakaa hai| maayaa-moh ke jaal mein abhee nahin padaa| alhad hai, jhijhakataa hai| bahut deenabhaav se bole, 'baaboo sahab, aisaa na keejie, ham mit jaaenge| ijjat dhool mein mil jaaegee| hamaaraa beizzatee karne se aapake haath kyaa aaegaa| ham kisee tarah aapase baahar thode hee hain|
vansheedhar ne kathor aavaaz mein kahaa, 'ham aisee baaten nahin sunanaa chaahate|
alopeedeen ne jis sahaare ko chattaan samajh rakhaa tha, vah pairon ke neeche khisakataa hua maaloom hua| svaabhimaan aur paisaa-aishvary kee kadee chot lagee| lekin abhee tak paisaa kee saankhyik shakti kaa pooraa bharosaa tha| apne mukhtaar se bole, 'laalaajee, ek hajaar ke not baaboo sahab kee bhent karo, aap is samay bhookhe sinh ho rahe hain|
vansheedhar ne garam hokar kahaa, 'ek hajaar nahin, ek laakh bhee mujhe sachche raastaa se nahin hataa sakate|
dharm kee is budhdiheen dridhataa aur dev-durlabh tyaag par man bahut jhunjhalaayaa| ab donon shaktiyon mein sangraam hone lagaa| paisaa ne uchhal-uchhalakar aakraman karne shuroo kie| ek se paanch, paanch se das, das se pandrah aur pandrah se bees hajaar tak naubat pahunchee, lekin dharm alaukik veerataa ke saath bahusankhyak senaa ke sammukh akelaa parvat kee tarah atal, avichalit khadaa tha|
alopeedeen niraash hokar bole, 'ab isase zyaadaa meraa saahas nahin| aage aapako adhikaar hai|
vansheedhar ne apne jamaadaar ko lalakaaraa| badaloosinh man mein daarogaajee ko gaaliyaan detaa hua pandit alopeedeen kee ora badhaa| panditajee ghabadaakar do-teen kadam peechhe hat gae| bahut deenataa se bole, 'baaboo sahab, eeshvar ke lie mujh par dayaa keejie, main pachchees hajaar par nipataaraa karne kaa taiyaar hoon|
'naamumakin baat hai|
'tees hajaar par?
'kisee tarah bhee mumakin nahin|
'kyaa chaalees hajaar par bhee nahin|
'chaalees hajaar nahin, chaalees laakh par bhee naamumakin hai|
'badaloosinh, is aadamee ko hiraasat mein le lo| ab main ek shabd bhee nahin sunanaa chaahataa|
dharm ne paisaa ko pairon tale kuchal daalaa| alopeedeen ne ek hrisht-pusht manushy ko hathakadiyaan lie hue apanee taraph aate dekh| chaaron ora niraash aur kaatar nazar se dekhane lage| isake baad moorchhit hokar gir pade|
duniyaa sotee thi par duniyaa kee jeebh jaagatee thi| savere dekhie to ladakaa-vridhd sabake muhan se yahee baat sunaaee detee thi| jise dekhie vahee panditajee ke is vyavahaar par teekaa-tippanee kar raha tha, nindaa kee bauchhaaren ho rahi theen, maano sansaar se ab paapee kaa paap kat gaya|
paanee ko doodh ke naam se bechane vaalaa gvaalaa, kalpit rojanaamache bharane vaale adhikaaree varg, rel mein binaa tikat saphar karne vaale baaboo log, jaalee dastaavej banaane vaale seth aur saakaar yah sab ke sab devataaon kee tarah gardanen chalaa rahe the|
jab doosare din pandit alopeedeen abhiyukt hokar kaanstebalon ke saath, haathon mein hathakadiyaan, dil mein pachhataavaa aur kshobh bhare, sharm se gardan jhukaae adaalat kee taraph chale to saare shahar mein halachal mach gaee| melon mein shaayad onkhen itanee vyagr na hoti hongee| bheed ke maare chhat aur deevaar mein koee bhed na raha|
lekin adaalat mein pahunchane kee der thi| pan. alopeedeen is agaadh van ke sinh the| adhikaaree varg unake bhakt, amale unake sevak, vakeel-mukhtaar unake hukm paalak aur aradalee, chaparaasee tathaa chaukeedaar to unake binaa mol ke gulaam the|
unhen dekhate hee log chaaron taraph se daude| sabhee log vismit ho rahe the| isalie nahin ki alopeedeen ne yah karm kiya, balki isalie ki vah kaanoon ke panje mein kaise aae? aisaa manushy jisake paas asaadhy saadhan karne vaalaa paisaa aur anany vaachaalataa ho, vah kyon kaanoon ke panje mein aae? har manushy unse sahaanubhooti prakat kart tha|
badee tatparataa se is aakraman ko rokane ke nimitt vakeelon kee ek senaa taiyaar kee gaee| nyaay ke maidaan mein dharm aur paisaa mein yadhd than gaya| vansheedhar chupachaap khade the| unake paas saty ke sivaa na koee bal tha, na spasht bhaashan ke atirikt koee shastr| gavaah the, lekin lobh se daanvaadol|
yahan tak ki munsheejee ko nyaay bhee apanee ora kuchh khinchaa hua deekh padataa tha| vah nyaay kaa darabaar tha, lekin usake karmachaariyon par pakshapaat kaa nashaa chhaayaa hua tha| lekin pakshapaat aur nyaay kaa kyaa mel? jahaan pakshapaat ho, wahan nyaay kee kalpanaa nahin kee jaa sakatee| mukadamaa jaldee hee khatm ho gaya|
diptee majistret ne apanee tajaveej mein likhaa, pan. alopeedeen ke virudhd die gae pramaan nirmool aur bhramaatmak hain| vah ek bade bhaaree aadamee hain| yah baat kalpanaa ke baahar hai ki unhonne thode phaayadaa ke lie aisaa dussaahas kiya ho| yadyapi namak ke darogaa munshee vansheedhar kaa zyaadaa dosh nahin hai, lekin yah bade khed kee baat hai ki usakee uddandataa aur vichaaraheenataa ke vajah ek bhalemaanus ko takaleeph jhelanaa padaa| ham khush hain ki vah apne kaam mein sajag aur sachet rahataa hai, lekin namak ke mukadame kee badhee huee namak se halaalee ne usake vivek aur budhdi ko bhrasht kar diya| aane vaalaa samay mein use hoshiyaar rahanaa chaahie|
vakeelon ne yah phaisalaa sunaa aur uchhal pade| pan. alopeedeen muskuraate hue baahar nikale| svajan baandhavon ne rupae kee loot kee| udaarataa kaa saagar umad padaa| usakee laharon ne adaalat kee neenv tak hilaa dee|
jab vansheedhar baahar nikale to chaaron ora unake oopar vyangyabaanon kee varshaa hone lagee| chaparaasiyon ne jhuk-jhukakar salaam kie| lekin is samay ek katu vaaky, ek-ek sanket unakee garvaagni ko prajjvalit kar raha tha|
shaayad is mukadame mein kaamayaab hokar vah is tarah akadate hue na chalate| aaj unhen sansaar kaa ek khedajanak vichitr anubhav hua| nyaay aur vidvattaa, lanbee-chaudee upaadhiyaan, badee-badee daadhiyaan, dheele choge ek bhee sachche aadar kaa paatr nahin hai|
vansheedhar ne paisaa se bair mol liya tha, usakaa mooly chukaanaa anivaary tha| kathinataa se ek saptaah beetaa hogaa ki muattalee kaa paravaanaa aa pahunchaa| kaary-paraayanataa kaa dand milaa| bechaare bhagn dil, shok aur khed se vyathit ghar ko chale| boodhe munsheejee to pahale hee se kudabudaa rahe the ki chalate-chalate is ladake ko samajhaayaa tha, lekin isane ek na sunee| sab manamaanee kart hai| ham to kalavaar aur kasaaee ke tagaade sahen, budhaape mein bhagat banakar baithen aur wahan bas vahee sookhee tanakhvaah! hamane bhee to naukaree kee hai aur koee ohadedaar nahin the| lekin kaam kiya, dil kholakar kiya aur aap eemaanadaar banane chale hain| ghar mein chaahe endheraa ho, masjid mein avashy diya jalaaenge| khed aisee samajh par! padhanaa-likhanaa sab akaarath gaya|
isake thode hee dinon baad, jab munshee vansheedhar is duraavasthaa mein ghar pahunche aur boodhe pitaajee ne khabar sunaa to sir peet liya| bole- 'jee chaahataa hai ki tumhaaraa aur apanaa sir phod loon| bahut der tak pachhataa-pachhataakar haath malate rahe| gussaa mein kuchh kathor baaten bhee kaheen aur yadi vansheedhar wahan se tal na jat to avashy hee yah gussaa vikat roop dhaaran kart| vridhd maan ko bhee du:kha hua| jagannaath aur raameshvar yaatraa kee kaamanaaen mittee mein mil gaeen| patnee ne kaee dinon tak seedhe munh baat tak nahin kee|
isee tarah ek saptaah beet gaya| saandhy kaa samay tha| boodhe munsheejee baithe-baithe raam naam kee maalaa jap rahe the| isee samay unake dvaar par sajaa hua rath aakar rukaa| hare aur gulaabee parade, pachhahie bailon kee jodee, unakee gardan mein neele dhaage, seeng peetal se jade hue| kaee naukar laathiyaan kandhon par rakhe saath the|
munsheejee agavaanee ko daude dekh to pandit alopeedeen hain| jhukakar dandavat kee aur lallo-chappo kee baaten karne lage- 'hamaaraa bhaagy uday hua, jo aapake charan is dvaar par aae| aap hamaare poojy devataa hain, aapako kaun saa munh dikhaaven, munh mein to kaalikh lagee huee hai| lekin kyaa karen, ladakaa abhaagaa kapoot hai, nahin to aapase kyaa munh chhipaanaa padataa? eeshvar nissantaan chaahe rakkhe par aisee bachchaa na de|
alopeedeen ne kahaa- 'nahin bhai sahab, aisaa na kahie|
munsheejee ne chakit hokar kahaa- 'aisee bachchaa ko aur kyaa kan?
alopeedeen ne vaatsalyapoorn aavaaz mein kahaa- 'kulatilak aur purukhon kee keerti ujjval karne vaale sansaar mein aise kitane dharmaparaayan manushy hain jo dharm par apanaa sab kuchh arpan kar saken!
pan. alopeedeen ne vansheedhar se kahaa- 'darogaajee, ise khushaamad na samajhie, khushaamad karne ke lie mujhe itanaa takaleeph uthaane kee jaroorat na thi| us raat ko aapane apne adhikaar-bal se apanee hiraasat mein liya tha, lekin aaj main svechchhaa se aapakee hiraasat mein aay hoon| mainne hajaaron raees aur ameer dekhe, hajaaron uchch padaadhikaariyon se kaam padaa lekin paraast kiya to aapane| mainne sabako apanaa aur apne paisaa kaa gulaam banaakar chhod diya| mujhe hukm deejie ki aapase kuchh vinay karoon|
vansheedhar ne alopeedeen ko aate dekh to uthakar satkaar kiya, lekin svaabhimaan sahit| samajh gae ki yah mahaashay mujhe lajjit karne aur jalaane aae hain| kshamaa-praarthanaa kee cheshtaa nahin kee, varan unhen apne pitaa kee yah thakurasuhaatee kee baat asahy see lagaa huee| par panditajee kee baaten sunee to man kee mail mit gaee|
panditajee kee ora udatee huee nazar se dekh| sadbhaav jhalak raha tha| garv ne ab sharm ke saamane sir jhukaa diya| sharmaate hue bole- 'yah aapakee udaarataa hai jo aisaa kehte hain| mujhse jo kuchh avinay huee hai, use kshamaa keejie| main dharm kee bedee mein jakadaa hua tha, nahin to vaise main aapakaa daas hoon| jo hukm hogee vah mere sir-maathe par|
alopeedeen ne vineet bhaav se kahaa- 'nadee tat par aapane meree praarthanaa nahin sveekaar kee thi, lekin aaj sveekaar karanee padegee|
vansheedhar bole- 'main kis yogy hoon, lekin jo kuchh sevaa mujhse ho sakatee hai, usamen truti na hogee|
alopeedeen ne ek staamp lagaa hua patr nikaalaa aur use vansheedhar ke saamane rakhakar bole- 'is pad ko sveekaar keejie aur apne hastaakshar kar deejie| main braahman hoon, jab tak yah savaal pooraa na keejiegaa, dvaar se na hatoongaa|
munshee vansheedhar ne us kaagaj ko padhaa to kritajnataa se onkhon mein onsoo bhar aae| pan. alopeedeen ne unako apanee saaree jaayadaad kaa sthaayee mainejar niyat kiya tha| chhah hajaar vaashak vetan ke atirikt rojaanaa kharch alag, savaaree ke lie ghodaa, rehne ko bangalaa, naukar-chaakar mupht| kampit aavaaz mein bole- 'panditajee mujhamen itanee saamarthy nahin hai ki aapakee udaarataa kee prashansaa kar sakoon! lekin aise uchch pad ke yogy nahin hoon|
alopeedeen hansakar bole- 'mujhe is samay ek ayogy manushy kee hee jaroorat hai|
vansheedhar ne ganbheer bhaav se kahaa- 'yon main aapakaa daas hoon| aap jaise keertivaan, sajjan aadamee kee sevaa karn mere lie achchhee kismat kee baat hai| lekin mujhamen na padhai hai, na budhdi, na vah svabhaav jo ina trutiyon kee poorti kar detaa hai| aise mahaan kaary ke lie ek bade marmajn anubhavee manushy kee jaroorat hai|
alopeedeen ne kalamadaan se kalam nikaalee aur use vansheedhar ke haath mein dekar bole- 'na mujhe vidvattaa kee chaah hai, na anubhav kee, na marmajnataa kee, na kaaryakushalataa kee| ina gunon ke mahatv ko khoob paa chukaa hoon| ab achchhee kismat aur suavasar ne mujhe vah motee de diya jisake saamane yogyataa aur vidvattaa kee chamak pheekee pad jati hai| yah kalam leejie, zyaadaa soch-soch na keejie, dastakhat kar deejie| paramaatmaa se yahee praarthanaa hai ki vah aapako hameshaa vahee nadee ke kinaare vaalaa, bemurauvat, uddand, kathor lekin dharmanishth daarogaa banaae rakhe|
vansheedhar kee onkhen dabadabaa aaeen| dil ke sankuchit paatr mein itanaa ehasaan na samaa sakaa| ek baar phir panditajee kee ora bhakti aur shradhdaa kee nazar se dekh aur kaanpate hue haath se mainejaree ke kaagaj par hastaakshar kar die|
alopeedeen ne praphullit hokar unhen gale lagaa liya
