# Namak Ka Daroga

**Munshi Premchand**

jab namak kaa nayaa vibhaag banaa aur eeshvarapradatt vastu ke vyavahaar karne kaa nishedh ho gaya to log choree-chhipe isakaa vyaapaar karne lage. anek tarah ke chhal-prapanchon kaa sootrapaat hua, koi ghoos se kaam nikaalataa tha, koi chaalaakee se. adhikaariyon ke pau-baarah the. patavaareegiree kaa sarvasammaanit pad chhod-chhodakar log is vibhaag ki barakandaajee karte the. isake daarogaa pad ke lie to vakeelon kaa bhi jee lalachaataa tha.
yah vah samay tha jab engarejee shikshaa aur eesaaee mata ko log ek hi vastu samajhte the. phaarasee kaa praabaly tha. pyaar ki kathaaen aur shringaar ras ke kaavy padhakar phaaraseedaan log sarvochch padon par niyukt ho jaayaa karte the.
munshee vansheedhar bhi julekhaa ki virah-kathaa khatm karake seeree aur pharahaad ke pyaar-vrittaant ko nal aur neel ki ladaaee aur amerikaa ke aavishkaar se zyaadaa mahatv ki baaten samajhte hue rojagaar ki khoj mein nikale.
unake pitaa ek anubhavee aadamee the. samajhaane lage, 'betaa! ghar ki durdashaa dekh rahe ho. reen ke bojh se dabe hue hain. ladakiyaan hain, ve ghaas-phoos ki tarah badhti chalee jati hain. main kagaare par kaa vriksh ho raha hoon, na maaloom kab gir padoon! ab tumheen ghar ke maalik-mukhtaar ho.
'naukaree mein ohade ki ora dhyaan mata dena, yah to peer kaa majaar hai. nigaah chadhaave aur chaadar par rakhanee chaahie. aisaa kaam dhoondhanaa jahaan kuchh ooparee aay ho. maasik vetan to poornamaasee kaa chaand hai, jo ek din dikhaaee detaa hai aur ghatate-ghatate lupt ho jat hai. ooparee aay bahta hua srot hai jisse hameshaa pyaas bujhti hai. vetan manushy detaa hai, isi se usamen vridhdi nahin hoti. ooparee aamadanee eeshvar detaa hai, isi se uski barakat hoti hain, tum svayan vidvaan ho, tumhen kya samajhaaoon.
'is vishay mein vivek ki badi aavashyakataa hai. manushy ko dekho, uski aavashyakataa ko dekho aur avasar ko dekho, usake uparaant jo theek samajho, karo. garajavaale aadamee ke saath kathorataa karne mein phaayadaa hi phaayadaa hai. lekin begaraj ko daanv par paanaa zara mushkil hai. ina baaton ko nigaah mein baandh lo yah meri janm bhar ki kamaaee hai.
is upadesh ke baad pitaajee ne aasheervaad diya. vansheedhar aajnaakaaree betaa the. ye baaten dhyaan se suneen aur tab ghar se chal khade hue. is vistrit sansaar mein unake lie dhairy apna mitr, budhdi apni pathapradarshak aur aatmaavalamban hi apna sahaayak tha. lekin achchhe shakun se chale the, jaate hi jaate namak vibhaag ke daarogaa pad par pratishthit ho gae. vetan achchha aur ooparee aay kaa to thikaanaa hi na tha. vridhd munsheejee ko sukh-baatacheet milaa to phoole na samaae. mahaajan kuchh naram pade, kalavaar ki aashaalataa lahalahaaee. padosiyon ke dil mein shool uthane lage.
jaade ke din the aur raat kaa samay. namak ke sipaahee, chaukeedaar nashe mein mast the. munshee vansheedhar ko yahan aae abhi chhah maheenon se zyaadaa na hue the, lekin is thode samay mein hi unhone apni kaaryakushalataa aur uttam aachaar se aphasaron ko mohit kar liya tha. aphasar log una par bahut yakeen karne lage.
namak ke daphtar se ek meel pahale ki ora jamunaa bahti thi, us par naavon kaa ek pul banaa hua tha. daarogaajee kivaad band kie meethee neend so rahe the. achaanak onkha khulee to nadee ke pravaah ki jagah gaadiyon ki gadagadaahat tathaa mallaahon kaa kolaahal sunaaee diya. uth baithe.
itanee raat gae gaadiyaan kyon nadee ke paar jati hain? avashy kuchh na kuchh golamaal hai. tark ne bhram ko pusht kiya. varadee pahanee, tamanchaa jeb mein rakhaa aur baat ki baat mein ghodaa badhaae hue pul par aa pahunche. gaadiyon ki ek lambee kataar pul ke paar jati dekhee. daantakar poochhaa, 'kisakee gaadiyaan hain.
thodee der tak sannaataa raha. aadamiyon mein kuchh kaanaaphoosee hui tab aage vaale ne kahaa-'pandit alopeedeen ki.
'kaun pandit alopeedeen?
'daataaganj ke.
munshee vansheedhar chaunke. pandit alopeedeen is ilaake ke sabase pratishthit jameendaar the. laakhon rupae kaa len-den karte the, idhar chhote se bade kaun aise the jo unake reenee na hon. vyaapaar bhi bada lambaa-chaudaa tha. bade chalte-puraje aadamee the. engareja aphasar unake ilaake mein shikaar khelne aate aur unake mehamaan hote. baarahon maas sadaavrat chalta tha.
munshee ne poochhaa, 'gaadiyaan kahan jaaengee? jawab milaa, 'kaanapur. lekin is sawal par ki inamen kya hai, sannaataa chhaa gaya. daarogaa sahab kaa shak aur bhi badhaa. kuchh der tak jawab ki baat dekhakar vah jor se bole, 'kya tum sab goonge ho gae ho? ham poochhte hain inamen kya ladaa hai?
jab is baar bhi koi jawab na milaa to unhone ghode ko ek gaadee se milaakar bore ko tatolaa. bhram door ho gaya. yah namak ke dele the.
pandit alopeedeen apne sajeele rath par savaar, kuchh sote, kuchh jaagate chale aate the. achaanak kaee gaadeevaanon ne ghabaraae hue aakar jagaayaa aur bole-'mahaaraaj! daarogaa ne gaadiyaan rok dee hain aur ghaat par khade aapako bulaate hain.
pandit alopeedeen kaa lakshmeejee par akhand yakeen tha. vah kahaa karte the ki sansaar kaa to kehna hi kya, svarg mein bhi lakshmee kaa hi raajy hai. unakaa yah kehna yathaarth hi tha. nyaay aur neeti sab lakshmee ke hi khilaune hain, inhen vah jaise chahti hain nachaatee hain. lete hi lete garv se bole, chalo ham aate hain. yah kahakar panditajee ne badi nishnchitataa se paan ke beede lagaakar khaae. phir lihaaph odhe hue daarogaa ke paas aakar bole, 'baaboojee aasheervaad! kahie, hamse aisaa kaun saa aparaadh hua ki gaadiyaan rok dee gaeen. ham braahmanon par to aapakee kripaa-nazar rahanee chaahie.
vansheedhar rukhaaee se bole, 'sarakaaree hukm.
pan. alopeedeen ne hansakar kahaa, 'ham sarakaaree hukm ko nahin jaanate aur na sarakaar ko. hamaare sarakaar to aap hi hain. hamaaraa aur aapakaa to ghar kaa maamalaa hai, ham kabhi aapase baahar ho sakate hain? aapane bekaar kaa takaleeph uthaayaa. yah ho nahin sakataa ki idhar se jaaen aur is ghaat ke devataa ko bhent na chadhaaven. main to aapakee sevaa mein svayan hi aa raha tha. vansheedhar par aishvary ki mohinee vanshee kaa kuchh prabhaav na padaa. eemaanadaaree ki naee umang thi. kadakakar bole, 'ham una namakaharaamon mein nahin hai jo kaudiyon par apna eemaan bechte phirate hain. aap is samay hiraasat mein hain. aapako kaayade ke anusaar chaalaan hogaa. bas, mujhe zyaadaa baaton ki phursat nahin hai. jamaadaar badaloosinh! tum inhen hiraasat mein le chalo, main hukm detaa hoon.
pan. alopeedeen stambhit ho gae. gaadeevaanon mein halachal mach gayi. panditajee ke jeevan mein shaayad yah pahalaa hi avasar tha ki panditajee ko aisee kathor baaten sunanee padeen. badaloosinh aage badhaa, lekin rob ke maare yah saahas na hua ki unakaa haath pakad sake. panditajee ne dharm ko paisaa kaa aisaa niraadar karte kabhi na dekh tha. soch kiya ki yah abhi uddand ladakaa hai. maayaa-moh ke jaal mein abhi nahin padaa. alhad hai, jhijhakataa hai. bahut deenabhaav se bole, 'baaboo sahab, aisaa na keejie, ham mit jaaenge. ijjat dhool mein mil jaaegee. hamaaraa beizzatee karne se aapake haath kya aaegaa. ham kisee tarah aapase baahar thode hi hain.
vansheedhar ne kathor aavaaz mein kahaa, 'ham aisee baaten nahin sunanaa chahte.
alopeedeen ne jis sahaare ko chattaan samajh rakhaa tha, vah pairon ke neeche khisakataa hua maaloom hua. svaabhimaan aur paisaa-aishvary ki kadee chot lagee. lekin abhi tak paisaa ki saankhyik shakti kaa pooraa bharosaa tha. apne mukhtaar se bole, 'laalaajee, ek hajaar ke not baaboo sahab ki bhent karo, aap is samay bhookhe sinh ho rahe hain.
vansheedhar ne garam hokar kahaa, 'ek hajaar nahin, ek laakh bhi mujhe sachche raastaa se nahin hataa sakate.
dharm ki is budhdiheen dridhta aur dev-durlabh tyaag par man bahut jhunjhalaayaa. ab donon shaktiyon mein sangraam hone lagaa. paisaa ne uchhal-uchhalakar aakraman karne shuru kie. ek se paanch, paanch se das, das se pandrah aur pandrah se bees hajaar tak naubat pahunchee, lekin dharm alaukik veerataa ke saath bahusankhyak senaa ke sammukh akelaa parvat ki tarah atal, avichalit khadaa tha.
alopeedeen niraash hokar bole, 'ab isase zyaadaa mera saahas nahin. aage aapako adhikar hai.
vansheedhar ne apne jamaadaar ko lalakaaraa. badaloosinh man mein daarogaajee ko gaaliyaan detaa hua pandit alopeedeen ki ora badhaa. panditajee ghabadaakar do-teen kadam peechhe hat gae. bahut deenataa se bole, 'baaboo sahab, eeshvar ke lie mujh par dayaa keejie, main pachchees hajaar par nipataaraa karne kaa taiyaar hoon.
'naamumakin baat hai.
'tees hajaar par?
'kisee tarah bhi mumakin nahin.
'kya chaalees hajaar par bhi nahin.
'chaalees hajaar nahin, chaalees laakh par bhi naamumakin hai.
'badaloosinh, is aadamee ko hiraasat mein le lo. ab main ek shabd bhi nahin sunanaa chahta.
dharm ne paisaa ko pairon tale kuchal daalaa. alopeedeen ne ek hrisht-pusht manushy ko hathakadiyaan lie hue apni taraph aate dekh. chaaron ora niraash aur kaatar nazar se dekhne lage. isake baad moorchhit hokar gir pade.
duniyaa sotee thi par duniyaa ki jeebh jaagatee thi. savere dekhie to ladakaa-vridhd sabake muhan se yahi baat sunaaee detee thi. jise dekhie wahi panditajee ke is vyavahaar par teekaa-tippanee kar raha tha, nindaa ki bauchhaaren ho rahi theen, maano sansaar se ab paapee kaa paap kat gaya.
paani ko doodh ke naam se bechane vaalaa gvaalaa, kalpit rojanaamache bharane vaale adhikaaree varg, rel mein binaa tikat saphar karne vaale baaboo log, jaalee dastaavej banaane vaale seth aur saakaar yah sab ke sab devataaon ki tarah gardanen chalaa rahe the.
jab doosare din pandit alopeedeen abhiyukt hokar kaanstebalon ke saath, haathon mein hathakadiyaan, dil mein pachhataavaa aur kshobh bhare, sharm se gardan jhukaae adaalat ki taraph chale to saare shahar mein halachal mach gayi. melon mein shaayad onkhen itanee vyagr na hoti hongee. bheed ke maare chhat aur deevaar mein koi bhed na raha.
lekin adaalat mein pahunchane ki der thi. pan. alopeedeen is agaadh van ke sinh the. adhikaaree varg unake bhakt, amale unake sevak, vakeel-mukhtaar unake hukm paalak aur aradalee, chaparaasee tathaa chaukeedaar to unake binaa mol ke gulaam the.
unhen dekhte hi log chaaron taraph se daude. sabhi log vismit ho rahe the. isalie nahin ki alopeedeen ne yah karm kiya, balki isalie ki vah kanoon ke panje mein kaise aae? aisaa manushy jisake paas asaadhy saadhan karne vaalaa paisaa aur anany vaachaalataa ho, vah kyon kanoon ke panje mein aae? har manushy unse sahaanubhooti prakat kart tha.
badi tatparataa se is aakraman ko rokane ke nimitt vakeelon ki ek senaa taiyaar ki gayi. nyaay ke maidaan mein dharm aur paisaa mein yadhd than gaya. vansheedhar chupachaap khade the. unake paas saty ke sivaa na koi bal tha, na spasht bhaashan ke atirikt koi shastr. gavaah the, lekin lobh se daanvaadol.
yahan tak ki munsheejee ko nyaay bhi apni ora kuchh khinchaa hua deekh padataa tha. vah nyaay kaa darabaar tha, lekin usake karmachaariyon par pakshapaat kaa nashaa chhaayaa hua tha. lekin pakshapaat aur nyaay kaa kya mel? jahaan pakshapaat ho, wahan nyaay ki kalpanaa nahin ki jaa sakatee. mukadamaa jaldee hi khatm ho gaya.
diptee majistret ne apni tajaveej mein likhaa, pan. alopeedeen ke virudhd die gae pramaan nirmool aur bhramaatmak hain. vah ek bade bhaaree aadamee hain. yah baat kalpanaa ke baahar hai ki unhone thode phaayadaa ke lie aisaa dussaahas kiya ho. yadyapi namak ke darogaa munshee vansheedhar kaa zyaadaa dosh nahin hai, lekin yah bade khed ki baat hai ki uski uddandataa aur vichaaraheenataa ke vajah ek bhalemaanus ko takaleeph jhelanaa padaa. ham khush hain ki vah apne kaam mein sajag aur sachet rehta hai, lekin namak ke mukadame ki badhee hui namak se halaalee ne usake vivek aur budhdi ko bhrasht kar diya. aane vaalaa samay mein use hoshiyaar rehna chaahie.
vakeelon ne yah phaisalaa sunaa aur uchhal pade. pan. alopeedeen muskuraate hue baahar nikale. svajan baandhavon ne rupae ki loot ki. udaarataa kaa saagar umad padaa. uski laharon ne adaalat ki neenv tak hilaa dee.
jab vansheedhar baahar nikale to chaaron ora unake oopar vyangyabaanon ki varshaa hone lagee. chaparaasiyon ne jhuk-jhukakar salaam kie. lekin is samay ek katu vaaky, ek-ek sanket unki garvaagni ko prajjvalit kar raha tha.
shaayad is mukadame mein kaamayaab hokar vah is tarah akadate hue na chalte. aaj unhen sansaar kaa ek khedajanak vichitr anubhav hua. nyaay aur vidvattaa, lanbee-chaudee upaadhiyaan, badi-badi daadhiyaan, dheele choge ek bhi sachche aadar kaa paatr nahin hai.
vansheedhar ne paisaa se bair mol liya tha, uska mooly chukaanaa anivaary tha. kathinataa se ek saptaah beetaa hogaa ki muattalee kaa paravaanaa aa pahunchaa. kaary-paraayanataa kaa dand milaa. bechaare bhagn dil, shok aur khed se vyathit ghar ko chale. boodhe munsheejee to pahale hi se kudabudaa rahe the ki chalte-chalte is ladake ko samajhaayaa tha, lekin isane ek na sunee. sab manamaanee kart hai. ham to kalavaar aur kasaaee ke tagaade sahen, budhaape mein bhagat banakar baithen aur wahan bas wahi sookhee tanakhvaah! hamne bhi to naukaree ki hai aur koi ohadedaar nahin the. lekin kaam kiya, dil kholakar kiya aur aap eemaanadaar banane chale hain. ghar mein chaahe endheraa ho, masjid mein avashy diya jalaaenge. khed aisee samajh par! padhna-likhna sab akaarath gaya.
isake thode hi dinon baad, jab munshee vansheedhar is duraavasthaa mein ghar pahunche aur boodhe pitaajee ne khabar sunaa to sir peet liya. bole- 'jee chahta hai ki tumhara aur apna sir phod loon. bahut der tak pachhta-pachhataakar haath malate rahe. gussaa mein kuchh kathor baaten bhi kaheen aur yadi vansheedhar wahan se tal na jat to avashy hi yah gussaa vikat roop dhaaran kart. vridhd maan ko bhi du:kha hua. jagannaath aur raameshvar yaatraa ki kaamanaaen mittee mein mil gaeen. patnee ne kaee dinon tak seedhe munh baat tak nahin ki.
isi tarah ek saptaah beet gaya. saandhy kaa samay tha. boodhe munsheejee baithe-baithe raam naam ki maalaa jap rahe the. isi samay unake dvaar par sajaa hua rath aakar rukaa. hare aur gulaabee parade, pachhahie bailon ki jodee, unki gardan mein neele dhaage, seeng peetal se jade hue. kaee naukar laathiyaan kandhon par rakhe saath the.
munsheejee agavaanee ko daude dekh to pandit alopeedeen hain. jhukakar dandavat ki aur lallo-chappo ki baaten karne lage- 'hamaaraa bhaagy uday hua, jo aapake charan is dvaar par aae. aap hamaare poojy devataa hain, aapako kaun saa munh dikhaaven, munh mein to kaalikh lagee hui hai. lekin kya karen, ladakaa abhaagaa kapoot hai, nahin to aapase kya munh chhipaanaa padataa? eeshvar nissantaan chaahe rakkhe par aisee bachchaa na de.
alopeedeen ne kahaa- 'nahin bhai sahab, aisaa na kahie.
munsheejee ne chakit hokar kahaa- 'aisee bachchaa ko aur kya kan?
alopeedeen ne vaatsalyapoorn aavaaz mein kahaa- 'kulatilak aur purukhon ki keerti ujjval karne vaale sansaar mein aise kitane dharmaparaayan manushy hain jo dharm par apna sab kuchh arpan kar saken!
pan. alopeedeen ne vansheedhar se kahaa- 'darogaajee, ise khushaamad na samajhie, khushaamad karne ke lie mujhe itanaa takaleeph uthaane ki jaroorat na thi. us raat ko aapane apne adhikar-bal se apni hiraasat mein liya tha, lekin aaj main svechchhaa se aapakee hiraasat mein aay hoon. maine hajaaron raees aur ameer dekhe, hajaaron uchch padaadhikaariyon se kaam padaa lekin paraast kiya to aapane. maine sabako apna aur apne paisaa kaa gulaam banaakar chhod diya. mujhe hukm deejie ki aapase kuchh vinay karoon.
vansheedhar ne alopeedeen ko aate dekh to uthakar satkaar kiya, lekin svaabhimaan sahit. samajh gae ki yah mahaashay mujhe lajjit karne aur jalaane aae hain. kshamaa-praarthanaa ki cheshtaa nahin ki, varan unhen apne pitaa ki yah thakurasuhaatee ki baat asahy see lagaa hui. par panditajee ki baaten sunee to man ki mail mit gayi.
panditajee ki ora udatee hui nazar se dekh. sadbhaav jhalak raha tha. garv ne ab sharm ke saamane sir jhukaa diya. sharmaate hue bole- 'yah aapakee udaarataa hai jo aisaa kehte hain. mujhse jo kuchh avinay hui hai, use kshamaa keejie. main dharm ki bedee mein jakadaa hua tha, nahin to vaise main aapakaa daas hoon. jo hukm hogee vah mere sir-maathe par.
alopeedeen ne vineet bhaav se kahaa- 'nadee tat par aapane meri praarthanaa nahin sveekaar ki thi, lekin aaj sveekaar karanee padegee.
vansheedhar bole- 'main kis yogy hoon, lekin jo kuchh sevaa mujhse ho sakatee hai, usamen truti na hogee.
alopeedeen ne ek staamp lagaa hua patr nikaalaa aur use vansheedhar ke saamane rakhakar bole- 'is pad ko sveekaar keejie aur apne hastaakshar kar deejie. main braahman hoon, jab tak yah sawal pooraa na keejiegaa, dvaar se na hatoongaa.
munshee vansheedhar ne us kaagaj ko padha to kritajnataa se onkhon mein onsoo bhar aae. pan. alopeedeen ne unako apni sari jaayadaad kaa sthaayee mainejar niyat kiya tha. chhah hajaar vaashak vetan ke atirikt rojaanaa kharch alag, savaaree ke lie ghodaa, rehne ko bangalaa, naukar-chaakar mupht. kampit aavaaz mein bole- 'panditajee mujhamen itanee saamarthy nahin hai ki aapakee udaarataa ki prashansaa kar sakoon! lekin aise uchch pad ke yogy nahin hoon.
alopeedeen hansakar bole- 'mujhe is samay ek ayogy manushy ki hi jaroorat hai.
vansheedhar ne ganbheer bhaav se kahaa- 'yon main aapakaa daas hoon. aap jaise keertivaan, sajjan aadamee ki sevaa karn mere lie achchhi kismat ki baat hai. lekin mujhamen na padhai hai, na budhdi, na vah svabhaav jo ina trutiyon ki poorti kar detaa hai. aise mahaan kaary ke lie ek bade marmajn anubhavee manushy ki jaroorat hai.
alopeedeen ne kalamadaan se kalam nikaalee aur use vansheedhar ke haath mein dekar bole- 'na mujhe vidvattaa ki chaah hai, na anubhav ki, na marmajnataa ki, na kaaryakushalataa ki. ina gunon ke mahatv ko khoob paa chukaa hoon. ab achchhi kismat aur suavasar ne mujhe vah motee de diya jisake saamane yogyataa aur vidvattaa ki chamak pheekee pad jati hai. yah kalam leejie, zyaadaa soch-soch na keejie, dastakhat kar deejie. paramaatmaa se yahi praarthanaa hai ki vah aapako hameshaa wahi nadee ke kinaare vaalaa, bemurauvat, uddand, kathor lekin dharmanishth daarogaa banaae rakhe.
vansheedhar ki onkhen dabadabaa aaeen. dil ke sankuchit paatr mein itanaa ehasaan na samaa sakaa. ek baar phir panditajee ki ora bhakti aur shradhdaa ki nazar se dekh aur kaanpate hue haath se mainejaree ke kaagaj par hastaakshar kar die.
alopeedeen ne praphullit hokar unhen gale lagaa liya
