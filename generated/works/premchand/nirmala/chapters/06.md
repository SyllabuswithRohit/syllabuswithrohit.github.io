# Nirmala — Adhyay 6

**Munshi Premchand**

chhathaa parichchheda

us din apane praga.dha pranaya kaa sabala pramaana dene ke baada munshee totaaraama ko aashaa hui thi ki nirmalaa ke marmasthala par meraa sikkaa jama jaayaga; lekin unakee yaha aashaa leshamaatra bhee pooree na hui; balki pahale to vaha kabhee-kabhee unase hansa kar bola bhee karti thi, ab bachchon hee ke laalana-paalana men vyasta rahane lagee| jab ghar men jaate, bachchon ko usake paasa baithe paate| kabhee dekhate ki unhen khilaa rahi hai, kabhee kapa.de pahana rahi hai, kabhee koee khela khela rahi hai, aur kabhee koee kahaanee kaha rahi hai| nirmalaa kaa trishita hridaya pranaya kee ora se niraasha hokara is avalamba hee ko Ganeemata samajhane lagaa| bachchon ke saatha hansane-bolane men usakee maatri-kalpana tripta hoti thi| pati ke saatha hansane-bolane men use jo sankocha, jo aruchi, tathaa jo anichchhaa hoti thi, yahaan taka ki vaha utha kar bhaaga jana chaahatee; usake badale yahaan baalakon ke sachche, sarala sneha se chitta prasanna ho jata tha| pahale mansaaraama usake paasa aate hue jhijhakata tha lekin ab vaha bhee kabhee-kabhee aa vaithata| vaha nirmalaa kaa hamasina tha;lekin maanasika vikaasa men paancha saala chhota| haokee aur phutavaala hee usakaa sansaara usakee kalpanaaon kaa mukta kshetra tathaa usakee kaamanaaon kaa haraa-bharaa vaaraa tha| ikahare vadana ka,chhareera,sundara,hansamukha,lajjaasheela baalaka tha, jisakaa ghar se kevala bhojana kaa naataa tha, vaaqee saare din na jaane kahaan ghoomata rahata| nirmalaa usake munha se khela kaa baaten suna kar tho.dee dera ke lie apanee chintaaon ko bhoola jati;aur chaahatee ki ek baara phir vahee din aa jaate,jab vaha gu.diyaan khelatee aur unake vyaaha rachaayaa karti thi,aur jise abhee tho.de,aaha! bahut hee tho.de din gujare the!

totaaraama ne gambheera bhaava se kaha-kaheen aisee himaakata na kar baithana,naheen to pachhataaoge|laudiyaaen kuchha laundonhee se khusha rahatee hain| hama tuma ab us kaama ke naheen rahe| sacha kahata hoon,main to shaadee karake pachhata raha hoon| buree balaa gale pa.dee| sochaa tha, do-chaara saala aur zindagi kaa mazaa uthaa loon par ulatee aanten gale pa.dee|

nayanasukha-tuma kyaa baaten karte ho| laundiyon ko paje men laanaa kyaa mushkila hai, jaraa saira-tamaashe dikhaa do, usake roopa-ranga kee taareepha kar do; basa, ranga jama gaya!

totaao-yaha sab kar-dhara ke haara gaya!

nayanao-achchha! kuchha itra-tela, phoola-patte, chaata-vaata kaa bhee majaa chakhaaya? totaao--ajee yaha sab kar chukaa| dampati-shaastra ke saare mantron kaa imtahaana le chukaa ; sab koree gappen hain|

nayanao--achchha to ab meree ek salaaha maano| zaraa apanee soorata vanavaalo| aajakala yahaan ek bijalee ke daoktara aae hue hain, jo bu.dhaape ke saare nishaana mitaa dete hain| kyaa majaala ki chehare par ek jhurree yaa sira kaa koee baala pakaa raha jaaya| na jaane aisaa kyaa jaadoo kar dete hain ki aadmi kaa cholaa hee badala jata hai|

totaa--pheesa kyaa lete hain?

nayanao–pheesa to sunaa zyada lete hain; shaayada paancha sau rupaye|

totaao--ajee koee paakhandee hoga, bevaqoophon ko loota raha hogaa| koee roGana lagaa kar do-chaara din ke lie jaraa cheharaa chikana kar detaa hogaa| ishtahaaree daoktaron par to apana vishvaasa hee naheen| dasa-paancha kee baat hoti, to kahata jaraa dillagee hee sahee| 500) ba.dee rakama hai|

nayanao--tumhaare lie 500) kaun ba.dee baat hai| ek maheene kee aamadanee hai| mere paasa to bhaee agar 500) hote, to sab se pahalaa kaama yahee karata| javaanee ke ek ghante kee keemata 500) se kaheen zyada hai|

totaao--ajee koee sastaa nuskhaa bataao, koee phakeeree ja.dee-bootee ho ki binaa harra-phitakaree ke ranga chokhaa ho jaaya| bijalee aur rediyama ba.de aadamiyon ke lie rahane do| unheen ko mubaaraka hon|

nayanao-to phir rangeelepana kaa svaanga racho, yaha dheelaa-dhaalaa kota phenko| tanzeba kee chusta achakana ho,chunnatadaara paajaama,gale men sone kee zanjeera pa.dee hui,sira par jayapuree saafaa bandhaa hua aankhon men surmaa aur baalon men hinaa kaa tela pada hua| tonda kaa pachakana bhee jarooree hai| doharaa kamarabanda baandho| jaraa takaleepha to hogee; par achakana saja uthegee| khizaaba main laa doongaa| sau-pachaasa Gazalen yaada kar lo; aur mauqe-mauqe se shaira pa.dho| baaton men rasa bharaa ho| aisaa maalooma ho ki tumhen deena aur duniya kee koee phikra naheen hai, basa jo kuchha hai priyatamaa hee hai| javaanmardee aur saahasa ke kaama karane kaa maukaa dhoon.dhate raho| raat ko jhootha-mootha shora karo--chora-chora, aur talavaara lekara akele pila pa.do| haan, zaraa maukaa dekha lena, aisaa na ho ki sachamucha koee chora aajaaya; aur tuma usake peechhe dau.do, naheen to saaree qalaee khula jaayagee aur mufta men ulloo banoge| us vakta to javaanmardee isee men hai ki dama saadhe pa.de raho, jisamen vaha samajhe ki tumhen khabara hee naheen hui; lekin jyonhee chora bhaaga kha.daa ho, tuma bhee uchhala kar baahara nikalo aur talavaara lekara kahaan-kahaan kehte dau.do| zyada naheen, ek hee maheene meree baaton kaa imtahaana karake dekho| agar vaha tumhaaraa dama na bharane lage, to jo jurmaanaa kaho vaha doon|

totaaraama ne us vakta to yaha baaten hansee men u.daa deen, jaisaa ki ek vyavahaara-kushala manushya ko karana chaahie tha, lekin inamen kee kuchha baaten unake mann men baitha gaee| unakaa asara pa.dane men koee sandeha na tha| dheere-dheere ranga badalane lage, jisamen log khataka na jaayan| pahale vaalon se shuroo kiya, phir surme kee vaaree aaee; yahaan taka ki ek-do maheene men unakaa kalevara hee badala gaya| gajalen yaada karane kaa prastaava to haasyaaspada tha lekin veerata kee deenga maarane men koee haani na thi|

us din se vaha roja apanee javaanmardee kaa koee na koee prasanga avashya chhe.da dete| nirmalaa ko sandeha hone lagaa ki kaheen unhen unmaada kaa roga to naheen ho raha hai| jo aadmi moonga kee daala aur mote aate ke do phulke khaakara bhee namaka sulemaanee kaa muhataaza ho, usake chhailepana par unmaada kaa sandeha ho to aashcharya hee kya? nirmalaa par is paagalapana kaa aur to kyaa ranga jamata, haan use una par dayaa aane lagee| krodha aur ghrinaa kaa bhaava jata raha| krodha aur ghrinaa us par hoti hai, jo apane hosha men ho| paagala aadmi to dayaa hee kaa paatra hai| vaha baat-baat men unakee chutakiyaan letee, unakaa majaaka u.daatee, jaise log paagalon ke saatha kiya karte hain| haan, isakaa dhyaana rakhatee thi ki yaha samajha na jaayan| vaha sochatee, bechaaraa apane paapa kaa praayashchitta kar raha hai| yaha saaraa svaanga kevala isalie to hai ki main apana dukha bhoola jaaoon| aakhira ab bhaagya to badala sakata naheen, is bechaare ko kyon jalaaoon|

ek din raat ko nau baje totaaraama baanke bane hue saira karake laute; aur nirmalaa se bole---aaja teena choron se saamana ho gaya| main jaraa shivapura kee tarafa chalaa gaya tha| andheraa tha hee| jyonhee rela kee sa.daka ke paasa pahuncha, to teena aadmi talavaara lie hue na jaane kidhara se nikala pa.de| yaqeena maano, teenon kaale deva the! main bilakula akela, haath men sirfa yaha chha.dee thi| udhara teenon talavaara baandhe hue, hosha u.da gae| samajha gaya ki zindagi kaa yaheen taka saatha tha| magar mainne bhee socha; marata hee hoon to veeron kee mauta kyon na maroon?

itane men ek aadmi ne lalakaara kaha––rakha de tere paasa jo kuchha ho; aur chupake se chalaa ja!

main chha.dee sanbhaala kar kha.daa ho gaya aur bola––mere paasa to sirpha yaha chha.dee hai aur isakaa moolya ek aadmi kaa sira hai|

mere munha se itana nikalana tha ki teenon talavaara kheencha kar mujha par jhapata pa.de; aur main unake vaaron ko chha.dee par rokane lagaa| teenon jhallaa-jhallaa kar vaara karte the, khataake kee aavaaza hoti thi; aur main bijalee kee tarah jhapata kar unake vaaron ko kaata detaa tha| koee dasa minita taka teenon ne Kooba talavaara ke jauhara dikhaae; par mujha par refa taka na aaee| majabooree yahee thi ki mere haath men talavaara na thi| yadi kaheen talavaara hoti, to ek ko jeetaa na chho.data| Kaira, kahaan taka bayaana karoon? us vakta mere haathon kee saphaaee dekhane qaabila thi| mujhe Kuda aashcharya ho raha tha ki yaha chapalata mujhamen kahaan se aa gaee| jab teenon ne dekha ki yahaan daala naheen galane kee, to talavaara myaana men rakha lee aur meree peetha thonka kar bole––javaana, tuma-saa veera aaja taka naheen dekha| hama teenon teena sau par bhaaree hain,gaon ke gaon dhola vajaa kar lootate hain,par aaja tumane hamen neechaa dikhaa diya| hama tumhaaraa lohaa maana gae| yaha kaha kar teenon phir najaron se gaayaba ho gae!

nirmalaa ne gambheera bhaava se muskaraa kar kaha-is chha.dee par to talavaaron ke bahut se nishaana bane hue honge|

munshee jee is shankaa ke lie taiyaara na the;par koee javaaba dena aavashyaka tha| vole—main vaaron ko varaavara khaalee detaa tha| do-chaara choten chha.dee par pa.dee theen,to uchatatee hui, jinase koee nishaana na pa.da sakata tha|

abhee unake munha se pooree vaata bhee na nikalee thi ki sahasaa rukminee devee vadahavaasa dau.datee hui aaee aur haanphate hue boli-tota,tota,hai ki naheen?mere kamare men ek saanpa nikala aaya hai| meree chaarapaaee ke neeche baithaa hua hai| main utha kar bhaagee| mooaa koee do gaja kaa hogaa| phana nikaale phuphakaara raha hai,jaraa chalo to,dandaa lete chalana|

totaaraama ke chehare kaa ranga u.da gaya, munha par havaaiyaan chhootane lagee; magar mann ke bhaavon ko chhipaa kar bole-saanpa yahaan kahaan? tumhen dhokhaa hua hogaa| koee rassee pa.dee hogee|

rukminee-are mainne apanee aakhon dekha hai| jaraa chala kar dekha na lo| hai-hai!marda hokara darate ho!!

munshee jee ghar men se to nikale; lekin baraamade men phir thithaka gae| unake paanva hee na uthate the| kalejaa dha.da-dha.da kar raha tha| saanpa bada krodhee jaanavara hai| kaheen kaata le, to muphta men praana se haath dhonaa pa.de| bole-darata naheen hoon| saanpa hee to hai,shera to naheen;magar saanpa par laathee naheen asara karti| jaakara kisee ko bheja,kisee ke ghar se bhaalaa laae|

yaha kaha kar munshee jee lapake hue baahara chale| mansaaraama baithaa khaanaa khaa raha tha | munshee jee to baahara gae, idhara vaha khaanaa chho.da, apana haokee kaa dandaa haath men le kamare men ghusa hee to pa.da;aur turanta chaarapaaee kheencha lee | saanpa masta tha, bhaagane ke badale phana nikaala kar kha.daa ho gaya | mansaaraama ne chatapata chaarapaaee kee chaadara uthaa kar saanpa ke oopara phenka dee, aur taaba.da to.da teena-chaara dande kasa kar jamaae | saanpa chaadara ke andara ta.dapa kar raha gaya| tab usedande par uthaae hue baahara chalaa | munshee jee kaee aadamiyon ko saatha lie chale aa rahe the| mansaaraama ko saanpa ko latakaae dekha, to sahasaa unake munha se ek cheekha nikala pa.dee| magar phir 'sanbhala gaye aur bole-main to aa hee raha tha, tumane kyon jaldee kee | de do koee phenka aae|

yaha kaha kar vaha ba.dee bahaaduree ke saatha rukminee ke kamare ke dvaara par jaakara kha.de ho gae; aur kamare ko khooba dekha-bhaala kar moonchhon par taava dete hue nirmalaa ke paasa aakara bole-main jab taka jaaoon jaaoon mansaaraama ne maara daalaa | besamajha ladka dandaa lekara dau.da pada | saanpa ko hameshaa bhaale se maarana chaahie | yahee to . ladakon men aiba hai| mainne aise-aise kitane saanpa maare hain| saanpa ko khilaa-khilaa kar maarata hoon| kitanon hee kee to mutthee se paka.da kar masala diya hai|

rukminee ne kaha-jaao bhee,dekha lee tumhaaree mardaanagee!
munshee jee jhepa kar vole-achchha jaao,main darapoka hee sahee;tumase kuchha inaama to naheen maoga raha hoon| jaakara maharaaja se kaho khaanaa nikaale|

munshee jee to bhojana karane gae aur nirmalaa dvaara kee chaukhata par kha.dee socha rahi thi-bhagavan!kyaa inhen sachamucha koee bheepana roga ho raha hai? kyaa meree dashaa ko aur bhee daaruna banaanaa chaahate ho? main inakee sevaa kar sakatee hoon;sammaana kar sakatee hoon,apana jeevana inake charanon par arpana kar sakatee hoon;lekin vaha naheen kar sakatee,jo mere kie naheen ho sakata| avasthaa kaa bheda mitaanaa mere vasha kee baat naheen! aakhira yaha mujhase kyaa chaahate hain-samajha gaee! samajha gaee!! aaha yaha baat pahale hee naheen samajhee thi, naheen to inako kyon itanee tapasyaa karanee pa.datee, kyon itane svaanga bharane pa.date?
