# Nirmala — Adhyay 6

**Munshi Premchand**

chhatha parichchhed

us din apne pragarh pranay ka sabal sabut dene ke bad munshi NAmETotaram ko ummid hui thi ki NAmENirmala ke marmasthal par mera sikka jam jayga; lekin unki yah ummid leshamatra bhi puri na hui; balki pahle to vah kabhi-kabhi unse hams kar bola bhi karti thi, ab bachchon hi ke lalan-palan men vyast rahne lagi. jab ghar men jate, bachchon ko uske pas baithe pate. kabhi dekhte ki unhen khila rahi hai, kabhi kapde pahna rahi hai, kabhi koi khel khel rahi hai, aur kabhi koi kahani kah rahi hai. NAmENirmala ka trishit dil pranay ki or se nirash hokar is avalamb hi ko ganimat samjhane laga. bachchon ke sath hamsne-bolne men uski matri-kalpana tript hoti thi. pati ke sath hamsne-bolne men use jo sankoch, jo aruchi, tatha jo anichchha hoti thi, yaham tak ki vah uth kar bhag jana chahti; uske badle yaham balkon ke sachche, asan pyaar se chitt khush ho jata tha. pahle NAmEmansaram uske
pas ate hue jhijhkata tha lekin ab vah bhi kabhi-kabhi a vaithta. vah NAmENirmala ka hamsin tha; lekin mansik vikas men panch sal chhota. hoki aur phutval hi uska samsar uski kalpanaon ka mukt kshetra tatha uski kamanaon ka hara-bhara vara tha. ikhare vadan ka, chharira, sundar, hamsmukh, lajjashil larka tha, jiska ghar se sirph khana ka nata tha, vaqi sare din na jane kaham ghumta rahta. NAmENirmala uske mumh se khel ka baten sun kar thodi der ke lie apni chintaon ko bhul jati; aur chahti ki ek bar phir vahi din a jate, jab vah guriyam khelti aur unke vyah rachaya karti thi, aur jise abhi thore, ah! bahut hi thore din gujre the!

munshi NAmETotaram anya ekant-sevi manushyon ki tarah vipyi jiv the. kuchh din to vah NAmENirmala kosair-tamashe dikhate rahe lekin jab dekha ki iska kuchh phal nahin hota, to phir ekant-samyam karne lage. din bhar ke mushkil mansik mehnat ke vad unka chitt amod-pramod ke lie lalayit ho jata tha, lekin jav apni vinod-vatika men pravesh karte aur uske phulon ko murjhaya, paudon ko sukha aur kyariyon men dhul urti hui dekhte, to unka ji chahta kyon na is vatika ko ujar dum? NAmENirmala unse kyon virakt rahti hai, iska rahasya unki samajh men na ata tha. dampattishastra ke sare mantron ki pariksha kar chuke, par manorath na pura hua. ab kya karna chahiye, yah unki samajh men na ata tha.
ek din vah isi chinta men baithe hue the ki unke ek sahpathi dost munshi nayanasukhram akar baith gae; aur salam-kalam ke bad muskara kar bole-ajkal to khub gahri chhanti hogi, nai bibi ka alingan karke javani ka maza a jata hoga. bade bhagyavan ho! bhai, ruthi hui javani ko manane ka isse achchha koi upay nahin ki naya shadi hojay. yaham to jindagi vabal ho rahi hai. patni ji is buri tarah chimti haim ki kisi tarah pind hi nahin chhorti. maim to dusri shadi ki phikra men hum. kahim daul ho to thik-thak kar do. dasturi men ek din tumhen uske hath ke bane hue pan khila denge.

NAmETotaram ne gambhir bhav se kaha-kahim aisi himakat na kar baithna, nahin to pachhataoge. laudiyaen kuchh laundomhi se khush rahti haim. ham tum ab us kam ke nahin rahe. sach kahta hum, maim to shadi karke pachhta raha hum. buri bala gale padi. socha tha, do-char sal aur jindagi ka maza utha lum par ulti anten gale padi.

nayansukh-tum kya baten karte ho. laundiyon ko paje men lana kya mushkil hai, jara sair-tamashe dikha do, uske rup-ranga ki tariph kar do; bas, ranga jam gaya!

totao-yah sab kar-dhar ke har gaya!

nayano-achchha! kuchh itra-tel, phul-patte, chat-vat ka bhi maja chakhaya?
totao--aji yah sab kar chuka. dampati-shastra ke sare mantron ka imtahan le chuka; sab kori gappen haim.

nayano--achchha to ab meri ek salah mano. zara apni surat vanvalo. ajkal yaham ek bijli ke doktar ae hue haim, jo burhape ke sare nishan mita dete haim. kya majal ki chehre par ek jhurri ya sir ka koi bal paka rah jay. na jane aisa kya jadu kar dete haim ki aadmi ka chola hi badal jata hai.

tota--phis kya lete haim?

nayanophis to suna jyada lete haim; shayad panch sau rupye.

totao--aji koi pakhandi hoga, bevqupho ko lut raha hoga. koi rogan laga kar do-char din ke lie jara chehra chikna kar deta hoga. ishtahari doktaron par to apna yakin hi nahin. das-panch ki bat hoti, to kahta jara dillagi hi sahi. 500) badi rakam hai.

nayano--tumhare lie 500) kaun badi bat hai. ek mahine ki amdani hai. mere pas to bhai agar 500) hote, to sab se pahla kam yahi karta. javani ke ek ghante ki kimat 500) se kahim jyada hai.

totao--aji koi sasta nuskha batao, koi phakiri jari-buti ho ki bina harr-phitkari ke ranga chokha ho jay. bijli aur rediyam bade admiyo ke lie rahne do. unhim ko mubarak hon.

nayano-to phir rangilepan ka svang racho, yah dhila-dhala
kot phenko. tanzeb ki chust achkan ho, chunnatdar pajama, gale men sone ki zanjir padi hui, sir par jaypuri safa bandha hua ankhon men surma aur balon men hina ka tel pada hua. tond ka pachkana bhi jaruri hai. dohra kamaraband bandho. jara takliph to hogi; par achkan saj uthegi. khizab maim la dunga. sau-pachas gazlen yad kar lo; aur mauqe-mauqe se shair parho. baton men ras bhara ho. aisa malum ho ki tumhen bechara aur duniya ki koi phikra nahin hai, bas jo kuchh hai priytama hi hai. javammardi aur himmat ke kam karne ka mauka dhumrhte raho. rat ko jhuth-muth shor karo--chor-chor, aur talvar lekar akele pil paro. ham, zara mauka dekh lena, aisa na ho ki sachmuch koi chor ajay; aur tum uske pichhe dauro, nahin to sari qalai khul jaygi aur mufta men ullu banoge. us vakt to javammardi isi men hai ki dam sadhe pade raho, jismen vah samjhe ki tumhen khabar hi nahin hui; lekin jyomhi chor bhag khada ho, tum bhi uchhal kar bahar niklo aur talvar lekar kaham-kaham kahte dauro. jyada nahin, ek hi mahine meri baton ka imtahan karke dekho. agar vah tumhara dam na bharne lage, to jo jurmana kaho vah dum.

NAmETotaram ne us vakt to yah baten hamsi men ura dim, jaisa ki ek bartav-kushal manushya ko karna chahiye tha, lekin inmen ki kuchh baten unke man men baith gai. unka asar parne men koi shak na tha. dhire-dhire ranga badlane lage, jismen log khatak na
jayam. pahle valon se shuru kiya, phir surme ki vari ai; yaham tak ki ek-do mahine men unka kalevar hi badal gaya. gajlen yad karne ka prastav to hasyaspad tha lekin virta ki ding marne men koi nuksan na thi.

us din se vah roj apni javammardi ka koi na koi prasanga zarur chher dete. NAmENirmala ko shak hone laga ki kahim unhen unmad ka bimari to nahin ho raha hai. jo aadmi mung ki dal aur mote ate ke do phulke khakar bhi namak sulemani ka muhtaz ho, uske chhailepan par unmad ka shak ho to hairani hi kya? NAmENirmala par is pagalpan ka aur to kya ranga jamta, ham use un par daya ane lagi. gussa aur naphrat ka bhav jata raha. gussa aur naphrat us par hoti hai, jo apne hosh men ho. pagal aadmi to daya hi ka chitthi hai. vah bat-bat men unki chutkiya leti, unka majak urati, jaise log paglon ke sath kiya karte haim. ham, iska dhyan rakhti thi ki yah samajh na jayam. vah sochti, bechara apne pap ka prayashchitt kar raha hai. yah sara svang sirph isalie to hai ki maim apna dukh bhul jaum. akhir ab bhagya to badal sakta nahin, is bechare ko kyon jalaum.

ek din rat ko nau baje NAmETotaram banke bane hue sair karke laute; aur NAmENirmala se bole---aj tin choron se samna ho gaya. maim jara shivpur ki taraf chala gaya tha. andhera tha hi. jyomhi
rel ki sarak ke pas pahuncha, to tin aadmi talvar lie hue na jane kidhar se nikal pade. yaqin mano, tinon kale dev the! maim bilkul akela, hath men sirfa yah chhari thi. udhar tinon talvar bandhe hue, hosh ud gae. samajh gaya ki zindagi ka yahim tak sath tha. magar maimne bhi socha; marta hi hum to viron ki maut kyon na marum?

mere mumh se itna niklana tha ki tinon talvar khinch kar mujh par jhapat pade; aur maim unke varon ko chhari par rokne laga. tinon jhalla-jhalla kar var karte the, khatake ki avaz hoti thi; aur maim bijli ki tarah jhapat kar unke varon ko kat deta tha. koi das minit tak tinon ne khub talvar ke jauhar dikhae; par mujh par ref tak na ai. majburi yahi thi ki mere hath men talvar na thi. yadi kahim talvar hoti, to ek ko jita na chhorta. khair, kaham tak bayan karum? us vakta mere hathon ki saphai dekhne qabil thi. mujhe khud hairani ho raha tha ki yah chaplata mujhmen kaham se a gai. jab tinon ne dekha ki yaham dal nahin galne ki, to talvar myan men rakh li aur meri pith thonk kar bolejavan, tum-sa vir aj tak nahin dekha. ham tinon tin sau par bhari
