# Nirmala — Adhyay 6

**Munshi Premchand**

chhathaa parichchhed

us din apane pragaadh pranay ka sabal saboot dene ke baad munshee totaaraam ko ummeed huee thi ki Nirmala ke marmasthal par meraa sikkaa jam jaayagaa; lekin unakee yah ummeed leshamaatr bhi pooree n huee; balki pahale to vah kabhee-kabhee unase hans kar bolaa bhi karatee thi, ab bachchon hee ke laalan-paalan mein vyast rahane lagee. jab ghar mein jaate, bachchon ko usake paas baithe paate. kabhee dekhate ki unhen khilaa rahee hai, kabhee kapde pahanaa rahee hai, kabhee koee khel khel rahee hai, aur kabhee koee kahaanee kah rahee hai. Nirmala ka trishit dil pranay ki or se niraash hokar is avalamb hee ko ganeemat samajhane lagaa. bachchon ke saath hansane-bolane mein usakee maatri-khayaal tript hotee thee. shauhar ke saath hansane-bolane mein use jo sanggkoch, jo aruchi, tathaa jo anichchhaa hotee thi, yahan tak ki vah uth kar bhaag jaanaa chaahatee; usake badale yahan bachchaaon ke sachche, saral pyaar se chitt khush ho jaataa thaa. pahale mansaaraam usake paas aate hue jhijhakataa tha lekin ab vah bhi kabhee-kabhee aa vaithataa. vah Nirmala ka hamasin tha; lekin maanasik vikaas mein paanch saal chhotaa. hokee aur phutavaal hee usakaa sansaar usakee khayaalaon ka mukt ilaakaa tathaa usakee kaamanaaon ka haraa-bharaa vaaraa thaa. ikahare vadan ka, chhareeraa, sundar, hansamunh, sharmasheel bachcha tha, jisakaa ghar se keval khana ka naataa tha, vaakee saare din n jaane kahaan ghoomataa rahataa. Nirmala usake munh se khel ka baaten sun kar thodee der ke lie apanee phikraon ko bhool jaatee; aur chaahatee ki ek baar phir vahee din aa jaate, jab vah gudiyaan khelatee aur unake vyaah rachaayaa karatee thi, aur jise abhee thode, aah! bahut hee thode din gujare the!

totaaraam ne seeriyas bhaav se kahaa-kaheen aisee himaakat n kar baithanaa, nahin to pachhataaoge. laudiyaaen kuchh laundonhee se khush rahatee hain. ham tum ab us kaam ke nahin rahe. sach kahataa hoon, main to shaadee karake pachhataa rahaa hoon. buree balaa gale padee. sochaa tha, do-chaar saal aur jindagee ka majaa uthaa loon par ulatee aanten gale padee.

nayanasukh-tum kyaa baaten karate ho. laundiyon ko paje mein laanaa kyaa mushkil hai, jaraa sair-tamaashe dikhaa do, usake roop-ranggg ki taareeph kar do; bas, ranggg jam gayaa!

totaao-yah sab kar-dhar ke haar gayaa!

nayano-achchha! kuchh itr-tel, phool-patte, chaat-vaat ka bhi majaa chakhaayaa? totaao--ajee yah sab kar chukaa. damshauhar-shaastr ke saare mantron ka imtahaan le chukaa; sab koree gappen hain.

nayano--achchha to ab meree ek salaah maano. jaraa apanee soorat vanavaalo. aajakal yahan ek bijalee ke doktar aae hue hain, jo budhaape ke saare nishaan mitaa dete hain. kyaa majaal ki chehare par ek jhurree ya sir ka koee baal pakaa rah jaaya. n jaane aisaa kyaa jaadoo kar dete hain ki aadmi ka cholaa hee badal jaataa hai.

totaa--phees kyaa lete hain?

nayano — phees to sunaa jyaadaa lete hain; shaayad paanch sau rupaye.

totaao--ajee koee paakhandee hogaa, bevakoophon ko loot rahaa hogaa. koee beemaareen lagaa kar do-chaar din ke lie jaraa cheharaa chikanaa kar detaa hogaa. ishtahaaree doktaron par to apanaa bharosaa hee naheen. das-paanch ki baat hotee, to kahataa jaraa dillagee hee sahee. 500) badee rakam hai.

nayano--tumhaare lie 500) kaun badee baat hai. ek maheene ki aamadanee hai. mere paas to bhaee agar 500) hote, to sab se pahalaa kaam yahee karataa. javaanee ke ek ghante ki keemat 500) se kaheen jyaadaa hai.

totaao--ajee koee sastaa nuskhaa bataao, koee phakeeree jadee-bootee ho ki binaa harr-phitakaree ke ranggg chokhaa ho jaaya. bijalee aur rediyam bade aadamiyon ke lie rahane do. unheen ko mubaarak hon.

nayano-to phir ranggeelepan ka svaangg racho, yah dheelaa-dhaalaa kot phengko. tanjeb ki chust achakan ho, chunnatadaar paajaamaa, gale mein sone ki janyjeer padee huee, sir par jayapuree saaphaa bandhaa huaa aangkhon mein surmaa aur baalon mein hinaa ka tel padaa huaa. tond ka pachakanaa bhi jarooree hai. doharaa kamaraband baandho. jaraa takaleeph to hogee; par achakan saj uthegee. khijaab main laa doonggaa. sau-pachaas gajalen yaad kar lo; aur mauke-mauke se shair padho. baaton mein ras bharaa ho. aisaa maaloom ho ki tumhe deen aur duniya ki koee fikr nahin hai, bas jo kuchh hai priyatamaa hee hai. javaanmardee aur himmat ke kaam karane ka maukaa dhoondhate raho. raat ko jhooth-mooth shor karo--chor-chor, aur talavaar lekar akele pil pado. haan, jaraa maukaa dekh lenaa, aisaa n ho ki sachamuch koee chor aajaay; aur tum usake peechhe daudo, nahin to saaree kalaee khul jaayagee aur mupht mein ulloo banoge. us vakt to javaanmardee isee mein hai ki dam saadhe pade raho, jisamen vah samajhe ki tumhe khabar hee nahin huee; lekin jyonhee chor bhaag khadaa ho, tum bhi uchhal kar baahar nikalo aur talavaar lekar kahaan-kahaan kahate daudo. jyaadaa nahin, ek hee maheene meree baaton ka imtahaan karake dekho. agar vah tumhaaraa dam n bharane lage, to jo jurmaanaa kaho vah doon.

totaaraam ne us vakt to yah baaten hansee mein udaa deen, jaisaa ki ek duniya ki samajh rakhane vaalee insaan ko karanaa chahiye tha, lekin inamen ki kuchh baaten unake man mein baith gaee. unakaa asar padane mein koee shak n thaa. dheere-dheere ranggg badalane lage, jisamen log khatak n jaayan. pahale vaalon se shuroo kiyaa, phir surme ki vaaree aaee; yahan tak ki ek-do maheene mein unakaa kalevar hee badal gayaa. gajalen yaad karane ka prastaav to haasyaaspad tha lekin veerataa ki deengg maarane mein koee haani n thee.

us din se vah roj apanee javaanmardee ka koee n koee prasanggg zaroor chhed dete. Nirmala ko shak hone lagaa ki kaheen unhen unmaad ka beemaaree to nahin ho rahaa hai. jo aadmi moongg ki daal aur mote aate ke do phulke khaakar bhi namak sulemaanee ka muhataaj ho, usake chhailepan par unmaad ka shak ho to hairaanee hee kyaa? Nirmala par is paagalapan ka aur to kyaa ranggg jamataa, haan use un par taras aane lagee. krodh aur nafrat ka bhaav jaataa rahaa. krodh aur nafrat us par hotee hai, jo apane hosh mein ho. paagal aadmi to taras hee ka paatr hai. vah baat-baat mein unakee chutakiyaan letee, unakaa majaak udaatee, jaise log paagalon ke saath kiyaa karate hain. haan, isakaa dhyaan rakhatee thi ki yah samajh n jaayan. vah sochatee, bechaaraa apane paap ka praayashchitt kar rahaa hai. yah saaraa svaangg keval isalie to hai ki main apanaa dukh bhool jaaoon. aakhir ab kismat to badal sakataa nahin, is bechaare ko kyon jalaaoon.

ek din raat ko nau baje totaaraam baangke bane hue sair karake laute; aur Nirmala se bole---aaj teen choron se saamanaa ho gayaa. main jaraa shivapur ki taraph chalaa gayaa thaa. andheraa tha hee. jyonhee rel ki sadak ke paas pahunchaa, to teen aadmi talavaar lie hue n jaane kidhar se nikal pade. yakeen maano, teenon kaale dev the! main bilakul akelaa, haath mein sirph yah chhadee thee. udhar teenon talavaar baandhe hue, hosh ud gae. samajh gayaa ki jindagee ka yaheen tak saath thaa. magar mainne bhi sochaa; marataa hee hoon to veeron ki maut kyon n maroon?

itane mein ek aadmi ne lalakaar kahaa — — rakh de tere paas jo kuchh ho; aur chupake se chalaa jaa!

main chhadee sambhaal kar khadaa ho gayaa aur bolaa — — mere paas to sirph yah chhadee hai aur isakaa mooly ek aadmi ka sir hai.

mere munh se itanaa nikalanaa tha ki teenon talavaar kheench kar mujh par jhapat pade; aur main unake vaaron ko chhadee par rokane lagaa. teenon jhallaa-jhallaa kar vaar karate the, khataake ki aavaaj hotee thi; aur main bijalee ki tarah jhapat kar unake vaaron ko kaat detaa thaa. koee das minit tak teenon ne khoob talavaar ke jauhar dikhaae; par mujh par reph tak n aaee. majabooree yahee thi ki mere haath mein talavaar n thee. yadi kaheen talavaar hotee, to ek ko jeetaa n chhodataa. khair, kahaan tak bayaan karoon? us vakt mere haathon ki saphaaee dekhane kaabil thee. mujhe khud hairaanee ho rahaa tha ki yah chapalataa mujhamen kahaan se aa gaee. jab teenon ne dekhaa ki yahan daal nahin galane ki, to talavaar myaan mein rakh lee aur meree peeth thongk kar bole — — javaan, tum-saa veer aaj tak nahin dekhaa. ham teenon teen sau par bhaaree hain, gaon ke gaon dhol vajaa kar lootate hain, par aaj tumane hamen neechaa dikhaa diyaa. ham tumhaaraa lohaa maan gae. yah kah kar teenon phir najaron se gaayab ho gae!

Nirmala ne seeriyas bhaav se muskaraa kar kahaa-is chhadee par to talavaaron ke bahut se nishaan bane hue hongge.

munshee jee is shanggkaa ke lie taiyaar n the; par koee javaab denaa zaroori thaa. vole — main vaaron ko varaavar khaalee detaa thaa. do-chaar choten chhadee par padee theen, to uchatatee huee, jinase koee nishaan n pad sakataa thaa.

abhee unake munh se pooree vaat bhi n nikalee thi ki sahasaa rukminee devee vadahavaas daudatee huee aaee aur haamphate hue bolee-totaa, totaa, hai ki nahin? mere kamare mein ek saamp nikal kamaaeeaa hai. meree chaarapaaee ke neeche baithaa huaa hai. main uth kar bhaagee. mooaa koee do gaj ka hogaa. phan nikaale phuphakaar rahaa hai, jaraa chalo to, dandaa lete chalanaa.

totaaraam ke chehare ka ranggg ud gayaa, munh par havaaiyaan chhootane lagee; magar man ke bhaavon ko chhipaa kar bole-saamp yahan kahaan? tumhe dhokhaa huaa hogaa. koee rassee padee hogee.

rukminee-are mainne apanee aakhon dekhaa hai. jaraa chal kar dekh n lo. hai-hai! mard hokar darate ho!!

munshee jee ghar mein se to nikale; lekin baraamade mein phir thithak gae. unake paanv hee n uthate the. kalejaa dhad-dhad kar rahaa thaa. saamp badaa krodhee jaanavar hai. kaheen kaat le, to mupht mein praan se haath dhonaa pade. bole-darataa nahin hoon. saamp hee to hai, sher to nahin; magar saamp par laathee nahin asar karatee. jaakar kisee ko bhej, kisee ke ghar se bhaalaa laae.

yah kah kar munshee jee lapake hue baahar chale. mansaaraam baithaa khana khaa rahaa tha. munshee jee to baahar gae, idhar vah khana chhod, apanaa hokee ka dandaa haath mein le kamare mein ghus hee to padaa; aur turant chaarapaaee kheench lee. saamp mast tha, bhaagane ke badale phan nikaal kar khadaa ho gayaa. mansaaraam ne chatapat chaarapaaee ki chaadar uthaa kar saamp ke oopar phengk dee, aur taabad tod teen-chaar dande kas kar jamaae. saamp chaadar ke andar tadap kar rah gayaa. tab usedande par uthaae hue baahar chalaa. munshee jee kaee aadamiyon ko saath lie chale aa rahe the. mansaaraam ko saamp ko latakaae dekhaa, to sahasaa unake munh se ek cheekh nikal padee. magar phir 'sambhal gaye aur bole-main to aa hee rahaa tha, tumane kyon jaldee ki. de do koee phengk aae.

yah kah kar vah badee bahaaduree ke saath rukminee ke kamare ke dvaar par jaakar khade ho gae; aur kamare ko khoob dekh-bhaal kar moonchhon par taav dete hue Nirmala ke paas aakar bole-main jab tak jaaoon jaaoon mansaaraam ne maar daalaa | besamajh ladakaa dandaa lekar daud padaa. saamp ko hameshaa bhaale se maaranaa chahiye. yahee to. ladakon mein aib hai. mainne aise-aise kitane saamp maare hain. saamp ko khilaa-khilaa kar maarataa hoon. kitanon hee ki to mutthee se pakad kar masal diyaa hai.

rukminee ne kahaa-jaao bhi, dekh lee tumhaaree mardaanagee!
munshee jee jhep kar vole-achchha jaao, main darapok hee sahee; tumase kuchh inaam to nahin mog rahaa hoon. jaakar maharaaj se kaho khana nikaale.

munshee jee to khana karane gae aur Nirmala dvaar ki chaukhat par khadee soch rahee thi-Bhagwan! kyaa inhen sachamuch koee bheepan beemaaree ho rahaa hai? kyaa meree dashaa ko aur bhi daarun banaanaa chaahate ho? main inakee sevaa kar sakatee hoon; izzat kar sakatee hoon, apanaa zindagi inake charanon par arpan kar sakatee hoon; lekin vah nahin kar sakatee, jo mere kie nahin ho sakataa. avasthaa ka bhed mitaanaa mere vash ki baat nahin! aakhir yah mujhase kyaa chaahate hain-samajh gaee! samajh gaee!! aah yah baat pahale hee nahin samajhee thi, nahin to inako kyon itanee tapasyaa karanee padatee, kyon itane svaangg bharane padate?
