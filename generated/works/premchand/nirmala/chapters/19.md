# Chapter 19

unneesavaa.N parichchheda

aba kee sudhaa ke saatha nirmalaa ko bhee aana padaa| vaha to maike men kuchha din aur rahanaa chaahatee thi, lekin shokaaturaa sudhaa akele kaise rahatee| usakee khaatira aana hee padaa|

rukmiNee ne bhungee se kahaa-dekhatee hai, bahoo maike se kaisee nikhara kar aaee hai|

bhungee ne kahaa-deedee, maa.N ke haath kee rotiyaa.N ladakiyon ko bahuta achchhee lagatee hain|

rukmiNee-theeka kehti hai bhungee, khilaanaa to kuchha maa.N hee jaanatee hai|

nirmalaa ko aisaa maalooma hua ki ghar kaa koee aadamee usake aane se khusha naheen| munshee jee ne khushee to bahuta dikhaaee; para hridayagata chintaa ko na chhipaa sake| bachchee kaa naama sudhaa ne ummeeda rakha diya tha| vaha ummeeda kee moorti see thi bhee| dekha kar saaree

chintaa bhaaga jati thi| munshee jee ne use goda men lenaa chaahaa, to rone lagee, dauda kar maa.N se lipata gaee; maano pitaa ko pahachaanatee hee naheen| munshee jee ne mithaaiyon se use parachaanaa chaahaa! ghar men koee naukara to tha naheen, jaakara siyaaraama se do aane kee mithaaiyaae laane ko kahaa| jiyaaraama bhee baithaa hua tha| bola uthaa -- hama logon ke lie to kabhee mithaaiyaa.N naheen aatee|

munshee jee ne jhu.Njhalaakara kahaa -- tuma log bachche naheen ho|

jiyaaraama -- aur kyaa boodhe hain| mithaaiyaa.N ma.Ngavaa kar rakha deejie, to maalooma ho ki bachche hain yaa boodhe| nikaalie chaara aane aur| ummeeda kee badaulata hamaare naseeba bhee jaagen|

munshee jee -- mere paasa is vakta paise naheen hain| jaao siyaa, jalda aana|

jiyaa 0-- siyaa naheen jaayagaa| kisee kaa gulaama naheen hai| ummeeda apane baapa kee betee hai, to vaha bhee apane baapa kaa betaa hai|

munshee jee -- kyaa phujoola kee baaten karte ho| nanheen see bachchee kee baraabaree karte tumhen sharma naheen aatee| jaao siyaaraama, ye paise lo|

jiyaa 0-- mata jana siyaa! tuma kisee ke naukara naheen ho|
siyaa badee duvidhaa men pada gaya| kisakaa kahanaa maane? anta men usane jiyaaraama kaa kahanaa maanane kaa phaisalaa kiya| baapa jyaadaa se jyaadaa ghudaka denge, jiyaa to maaregaa, phir vaha kisake paasa phariyaada lekara jaayagaa| bola -- main na jaaoo.Ngaa|
munshee jee ne dhamakaa kar kahaa-achchhaa, to mere paasa phir koee cheeja maa.Ngane mata aana|

munshee jee khuda baajaara chale gae; aur ek rupae kee mithaaee lekara laute| do aane ko mithaaee maangate hue unhen sharma aaee| halavaaee unhen pahachaanataa tha| dil men kyaa kahegaa ?

mithaaee lie hue munshee jee andara chale gae| siyaaraama ne mithaaee kaa badaa saa donaa dekha, to vaapa kaa kahanaa na maanane kaa use duhkha hua| aba vaha kisa munha se mithaaee lene andara jaayagaa| vadee bhoola huee | vaha mana hee mana jiyaaraama ke chaa.Nton kee chota aur mithaaee kee mithaasa men tulanaa karane lagaa|

sahasaa bhungee ne do tashtariyo donon ke saamane laakara rakha dee| jiyaaraama ne bigada kar kahaa-ise uthaa le jaa|

mujhee kaahe ko vigadate ho vaaboo, kyaa mithaaee achchhee naheen lagatee?

jiyaao-mithaaee ummeeda ke lie aaee hai, hamaare lie naheen aaee | le jaa, naheen to main sadaka para phenka doongaa| hama to paise-paise ke lie ratate rahate hain, aur yahaa.N rupayon kee mithaaee aatee hai|

bhungee-tuma le lo siyaa vaaboo, yaha na lenge na sahee| siyaaraama ne darate-darate haath badhaayaa tha ki jiyaaraama ne daa.Nta kar kahaa-mata chhoonaa mithaaee, naheen to haath toda kar rakha doongaa| laalachee kaheen kaa | siyaaraama yaha ghudakee suna kar sahama uthaa| mithaaee khaane kee himmata na padee | nirmalaa ne yaha kathaa sunee to
donon ladakon ko manaane chalee| munshee jee ne kadee kasama rakhaa dee|

nirmalaa-aapa samajhate naheen hain | yaha saaraa gussaa mujha para hai|

munshee jee-gustaakha ho gaya hai | is khyaala se koee sakhtee naheen karta ki log kahenge binaa maa.N ke bachchon ko sataate hain, naheen to saaree sharaarata ghadee bhara men nikaala doo.N|

nirmalaa-isee badanaamee kaa mujhe bhee to dara hai|

munshee jee-ava na daroo.Ngaa, jisake jee men jo aae kahe|

nirmalaa-pahale to yaha aise na the|

munshee jee-ajee kehta hai ki aapake ladake maujooda the, aapane shaadee kyon kee? yaha kehte bhee ise sankocha naheen hota ki aapa logon ne mansaaraama ko visha de diya| ladakaa naheen hai, shatru hai|

jiyaaraama dvaara para chhipa kar khadaa tha| aurata-aadamee men mithaaee ke vishaya men kyaa baaten hoti hain, yahee sunane vaha aaya tha| munshee jee kaa antima vaakya suna kar usase na raha gaya | bola uthaa-shatru na hota to aapa usake peechhe kyon padate | aapa jo is vakta kaha rahe hain, vaha main bahuta pahale se samajhe baithaa hoo.N | bhaiyaa na samajhe the, dhokhaa khaa gae| hamaare saatha aapa kee daala na galegee| saaraa jamaanaa kaha raha hai ki bhaaee saahava ko jahara diya gaya| main kehta hoo.N to kyon aapako gussaa aata hai ?

nirmalaa to sannaate men aa gaee | maalooma hua kisee ne usakee deha para angaare daala die | munshee jee ne daa.Nta kar jiyaaraama ko
chupa karna chaahaa; para jiyaaraama nihshanka khadaa eeton kaa javaaba patthara se detaa raha| yahaa.N taka ki nirmalaa ko bhee us para gussaa aa gaya| yaha kala kaa chhokaraa na kisee kaama kaa na kaaja kaa, yon khadaa tarraa raha hai; jaise ghar bhara kaa paalana-poshaNa yahee karta ho| tyoriyaa.N chadhaa kar bolee-basa, aba bahuta hua jiyaaraama, maalooma ho gaya ki tuma bade laayaka ho, vaahara jaakara vaitho|

munshee jee ava taka to jaraa daba-daba kar bolate rahe, nirmalaa kee shaha paaee to dil badha gaya| daa.Nta peesa kar lapake aur isake pahale ki nirmalaa unake haath pakada sake, ek thappada chalaahee diya| thappada nirmalaa ke mu.Nha para padaa, vahee saamane padee| maathaa chakaraa gaya| munshee jee ke sookhe hue haathon men bhee itanee shakti hai, isakaa vaha anumaana na kar sakatee thi| sira pakada kar baitha gaee| munshee jee kaa gussaa aur bhee bhadaka uthaa, phir ghoonsaa chalaayaa;para aba kee jiyaaraama ne unakaa haath pakada liya; aur peechhe dhakela kar bola-doora se baaten keejie; kyon naahaka apanee beijjatee karavaate hain| ammaa.N jee kaa lihaaja kar raha hoo.N, naheen to dikhaa detaa|

yaha kehta hua vaha baahara chalaa gaya| munshee jee sanjnaa-shoonya •se khade rahe| is vakta agar jiyaaraama para daivee vajra gira padataa,
to shaayada unhen haardika khushee hota| jisa betaa ko kabhee goda men lekara nihaala ho jaate the, usee ke prati aaja taraha-taraha kee dushkalpanaae.N mana men aa rahi theen|

rukmiNee aba taka to apanee kotharee men thi| aba aakara
bolee-betaa apane baraabara kaa ho gaya, to us para haath na chhodanaa chaahie|

munshee jee ne ontha chabaa kar kahaa-main ise ghar se nikaala kar chhodoongaa| bheekha maa.Nge yaa choree kare, mujhase koee matalaba naheen|

rukmiNee-naaka kisakee kategee?

munshee-isakee chintaa naheen|

nirmalaa-main jaanatee ki mere aane se yaha toophaana khadaa ho jaayagaa, to bhoola kar bhee na aatee| aba bhee bhalaa hai, mujhe bheja deejie| is ghar men mujhase raha na jaayagaa|

rukmiNee-tumhaaraa bahuta lihaaja karta hai bahoo! naheen, to aaja anartha hee ho jata|

nirmalaa-aba aur kyaa anartha hogaa deedee jee? main to phoonka-phoonka kar paa.Nva rakhatee hoo.N; phir bhee apayasha laga hee jata hai| abhee ghar men paa.Nva rakhate dera naheen huee; aur yaha haala ho gaya| eeshvara hee kushala karen|

raat ko khaanaa karane koee na uthaa| akele munshee jee ne khaayaa| nirmalaa ko aaja ek naee chintaa ho gaee thi-jeevana kaise paara lagegaa? apanaa hee peta hota, to khaasa chintaa na thi| aba to ek naee vipatti gale pada gaee thi| vaha socha rahi thi-meree bachchee ke bhaagya men kyaa likhaa hai raama?
