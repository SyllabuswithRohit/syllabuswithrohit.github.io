# Nirmala — Adhyay 19

**Munshi Premchand**

unneesavaan parichchhed

ab ki sudhaa ke saath Nirmala ko bhi aanaa padaa. vah to maike mein kuchh din aur rahanaa chaahatee thi, lekin shokaaturaa sudhaa akele kaise rahatee. usakee khaatir aanaa hee padaa.

rukminee ne bhungggee se kahaa-dekhatee hai, bahoo maike se kaisee nikhar kar aaee hai.

bhungggee ne kahaa-deedee, maan ke haath ki rotiyaan ladakiyon ko bahut achchhi lagatee hain.

rukminee-theek kahatee hai bhungggee, khilaanaa to kuchh maan hee jaanatee hai.

Nirmala ko aisaa maaloom huaa ki ghar ka koee aadmi usake aane se khush naheen. munshee jee ne khushi to bahut dikhaaee; par dilagat fikr ko n chhipaa sake. bachchi ka naam sudhaa ne ummeed rakh diyaa thaa. vah ummeed ki moorti see thi bhee. dekh kar saaree fikr bhaag jaatee thee. munshee jee ne use god mein lenaa chaahaa, to rone lagee, daud kar maan se lipat gaee; maano pitaa ko pahachaanatee hee naheen. munshee jee ne mithaaiyon se use parachaanaa chaahaa! ghar mein koee naukar to tha nahin, jaakar siyaaraam se do aane ki mithaaiyaae laane ko kahaa. jiyaaraam bhi baithaa huaa thaa. bol uthaa -- ham logon ke lie to kabhee mithaaiyaan nahin aatee.

munshee jee ne jhunjhalaakar kahaa -- tum log bachche nahin ho.

jiyaaraam -- aur kyaa boodhe hain. mithaaiyaan manggavaa kar rakh deejie, to maaloom ho ki bachche hain ya boodhe. nikaalie chaar aane aura. ummeed ki badaulat hamaare naseeb bhi jaagen.

munshee jee -- mere paas is vakt paise nahin hain. jaao siyaa, jald aanaa.

jiyaa 0-- siyaa nahin jaayagaa. kisee ka gulaam nahin hai. ummeed apane baap ki betee hai, to vah bhi apane baap ka betaa hai.

munshee jee -- kyaa phujool ki baaten karate ho. nanheen see bachchi ki baraabaree karate tumhe sharm nahin aatee. jaao siyaaraam, ye paise lo.

jiyaa 0-- mat jaanaa siyaa! tum kisee ke naukar nahin ho.
siyaa badee duvidhaa mein pad gayaa. kisakaa kahanaa maane? ant mein usane jiyaaraam ka kahanaa maanane ka pakkaa faisla kiyaa. baap jyaadaa se jyaadaa ghudak dengge, jiyaa to maaregaa, phir vah kisake paas phariyaad lekar jaayagaa. bolaa -- main n jaaoonggaa. munshee jee ne dhamakaa kar kahaa-achchha, to mere paas phir koee cheej maanggane mat aanaa.

munshee jee khud baajaar chale gae; aur ek rupaye ki mithaaee lekar laute. do aane ko mithaaee maanggate hue unhen sharm aaee. halavaaee unhen pahachaanataa thaa. dil mein kyaa kahegaa?

mithaaee lie hue munshee jee andar chale gae. siyaaraam ne mithaaee ka badaa saa donaa dekhaa, to vaap ka kahanaa n maanane ka use duhkh huaa. ab vah kis munh se mithaaee lene andar jaayagaa. vadee bhool huee. vah man hee man jiyaaraam ke chaanton ki chot aur mithaaee ki mithaas mein tulanaa karane lagaa.

sahasaa bhungggee ne do tashtariyo donon ke saamane laakar rakh dee. jiyaaraam ne bigad kar kahaa-ise uthaa le jaa.

mujhee kaahe ko vigadate ho vaaboo, kyaa mithaaee achchhi nahin lagatee?

jiyaao-mithaaee ummeed ke lie aaee hai, hamaare lie nahin aaee. le jaa, nahin to main sadak par phengk doonggaa. ham to paise-paise ke lie ratate rahate hain, aur yahan rupayon ki mithaaee aatee hai.

bhungggee-tum le lo siyaa vaaboo, yah n lengge n sahee. siyaaraam ne darate-darate haath badhaayaa tha ki jiyaaraam ne daant kar kahaa-mat chhoonaa mithaaee, nahin to haath tod kar rakh doonggaa. laalachee kaheen ka. siyaaraam yah ghudakee sun kar saham uthaa. mithaaee khaane ki himmat n padee. Nirmala ne yah kathaa sunee to donon ladakon ko manaane chalee. munshee jee ne kadee kasam rakhaa dee.

Nirmala-aap samajhate nahin hain. yah saaraa gussaa mujh par hai.

munshee jee-gustaakh ho gayaa hai. is khyaal se koee sakhtee nahin karataa ki log kahengge binaa maan ke bachchon ko sataate hain, nahin to saaree sharaarat ghadee bhar mein nikaal doon.

Nirmala-isee badanaamee ka mujhe bhi to dar hai.

munshee jee-av n daroonggaa, jisake jee mein jo aae kahe.

Nirmala-pahale to yah aise n the.

munshee jee-ajee kahataa hai ki aapake ladake maujood the, aapane shaadee kyon ki? yah kahate bhi ise sanggkoch nahin hotaa ki aap logon ne mansaaraam ko vish de diyaa. ladakaa nahin hai, shatru hai.

jiyaaraam dvaar par chhip kar khadaa thaa. aurat-mard mein mithaaee ke vishay mein kyaa baaten hotee hain, yahee sunane vah kamaaeeaa thaa. munshee jee ka antim vaaky sun kar usase n rahaa gayaa. bol uthaa-shatru n hotaa to aap usake peechhe kyon padate. aap jo is vakt kah rahe hain, vah main bahut pahale se samajhe baithaa hoon. bhaiyaa n samajhe the, dhokhaa khaa gae. hamaare saath aap ki daal n galegee. saaraa jamaanaa kah rahaa hai ki bhaaee saahav ko jahar diyaa gayaa. main kahataa hoon to kyon aapko gussaa aataa hai?

Nirmala to sannaate mein aa gaee. maaloom huaa kisee ne usakee deh par angggaare daal die. munshee jee ne daant kar jiyaaraam ko chup karanaa chaahaa; par jiyaaraam nihshanggk khadaa eeton ka javaab patthar se detaa rahaa. yahan tak ki Nirmala ko bhi us par krodh aa gayaa. yah kal ka chhokaraa n kisee kaam ka n kaaj ka, yon khadaa tarraa rahaa hai; jaise ghar bhar ka paalan-poshan yahee karataa ho. tyoriyaan chadhaa kar bolee-bas, ab bahut huaa jiyaaraam, maaloom ho gayaa ki tum bade laayak ho, vaahar jaakar vaitho.

munshee jee av tak to jaraa dab-dab kar bolate rahe, Nirmala ki shah paaee to dil badh gayaa. daant pees kar lapake aur isake pahale ki Nirmala unake haath pakad sake, ek thappad chalaahee diyaa. thappad Nirmala ke munh par padaa, vahee saamane padee. maathaa chakaraa gayaa. munshee jee ke sookhe hue haathon mein bhi itanee taakat hai, isakaa vah anumaan n kar sakatee thee. sir pakad kar baith gaee. munshee jee ka krodh aur bhi bhadak uthaa, phir ghoonsaa chalaayaa; par ab ki jiyaaraam ne unakaa haath pakad liyaa; aur peechhe dhakel kar bolaa-door se baaten keejie; kyon naahak apanee beijjatee karavaate hain. ammaan jee ka lihaaj kar rahaa hoon, nahin to dikhaa detaa.

yah kahataa huaa vah baahar chalaa gayaa. munshee jee sangyaa-shoony •se khade rahe. is vakt agar jiyaaraam par daivee vajr gir padataa,
to shaayad unhen haardik khushi hotaa. jis putr ko kabhee god mein lekar nihaal ho jaate the, usee ke prati aaj bhaanti-bhaanti ki dushkhayaalaen man mein aa rahee theen.

rukminee ab tak to apanee kotharee mein thee. ab aakar bolee-betaa apane baraabar ka ho gayaa, to us par haath n chhodanaa chaahie.

munshee jee ne onth chabaa kar kahaa-main ise ghar se nikaal kar chhodoonggaa. bheekh maangge ya choree kare, mujhase koee matalab naheen.

rukminee-naak kisakee kategee?

munshee-isakee fikr naheen.

Nirmala-main jaanatee ki mere aane se yah toophaan khadaa ho jaayagaa, to bhool kar bhi n aatee. ab bhi bhalaa hai, mujhe bhej deejie. is ghar mein mujhase rahaa n jaayagaa.

rukminee-tumhaaraa bahut lihaaj karataa hai bahoo! nahin, to aaj anarth hee ho jaataa.

Nirmala-ab aur kyaa anarth hogaa deedee jee? main to phoongk-phoongk kar paanv rakhatee hoon; phir bhi apayash lag hee jaataa hai. abhee ghar mein paanv rakhate der nahin huee; aur yah haal ho gayaa. Bhagwan hee kushal karen.

raat ko khana karane koee n uthaa. akele munshee jee ne khaayaa. Nirmala ko aaj ek naee fikr ho gaee thi-zindagi kaise paar lagegaa? apanaa hee pet hotaa, to vishesh fikr n thee. ab to ek naee vipatti gale pad gaee thee. vah soch rahee thi-meree bachchi ke kismat mein kyaa likhaa hai raam?
