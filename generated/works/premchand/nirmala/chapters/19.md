# Nirmala — Adhyay 19

**Munshi Premchand**

unnisvam parichchhed

ab ki NAmESudha ke sath NAmENirmala ko bhi ana pada. vah to maike men kuchh din aur rahna chahti thi, lekin shokatura NAmESudha akele kaise rahti. uski khatir ana hi pada.

NAmERukmini ne bhungi se kaha-dekhti hai, bahu maike se kaisi nikhar kar ai hai.

bhungi ne kaha-didi, mam ke hath ki rotiyam larkiyo ko bahut achchhi lagti haim.

NAmERukmini -thik kahti hai bhungi, khilana to kuchh mam hi janti hai.

NAmENirmala ko aisa malum hua ki ghar ka koi aadmi uske ane se khush nahin. munshi ji ne khushi to bahut dikhai; par hridaygat chinta ko na chhipa sake. bachchi ka nam NAmESudha ne ummid rakh diya tha. vah ummid ki murti si thi bhi. dekh kar sari
chinta bhag jati thi. munshi ji ne use god men lena chaha, to rone lagi, daur kar mam se lipat gai; mano pita ko pahachanti hi nahin. munshi ji ne mithaiyon se use parchana chaha! ghar men koi naukar to tha nahin, jakar NAmESiyaram se do ane ki mithaiyaa lane ko kaha. NAmEJiyaram bhi baitha hua tha. bol utha -- ham logon ke lie to kabhi mithaiyam nahin ati.

munshi ji ne jhunjhalakar kaha -- tum log bachche nahin ho.

NAmEJiyaram -- aur kya burhe haim. mithaiyam mangva kar rakh dijie, to malum ho ki bachche haim ya burhe. nikalie char ane aur. ummid ki badaulat hamare nasib bhi jagen.

munshi ji -- mere pas is vakta paise nahin haim. jao siya, jald ana.

jiya 0-- siya nahin jayga. kisi ka gulam nahin hai. ummid apne bap ki beti hai, to vah bhi apne bap ka beta hai.

munshi ji -- kya fuzul ki baten karte ho. nanhim si bachchi ki barabri karte tumhen sharm nahin ati. jao NAmESiyaram, ye paise lo.

jiya 0-- mat jana siya! tum kisi ke naukar nahin ho.
siya badi duvidha men par gaya. kiska kahna mane? ant men usne NAmEJiyaram ka kahna manne ka phaisla kiya. bap jyada se zyada ghurak denge, jiya to marega, phir vah kiske pas fariyad lekar jayga. bola -- maim na jaunga.
munshi ji ne dhamka kar kaha-achchha, to mere pas phir koi chiz mangne mat ana.

munshi ji khud bajar chale gae; aur ek rupae ki mithai lekar laute. do ane ko mithai mangte hue unhen sharm ai. halavai unhen pahachanta tha. dil men kya kahega?

mithai lie hue munshi ji andar chale gae. NAmESiyaram ne mithai ka bada sa dona dekha, to vap ka kahna na manne ka use duhkh hua. ab vah kis mumh se mithai lene andar jayga. vari bhul hui. vah man hi man NAmEJiyaram ke chanton ki chot aur mithai ki mithas men tulna karne laga.

sahsa bhungi ne do tashtariyo donon ke samne lakar rakh di. NAmEJiyaram ne bigar kar kaha-ise utha le ja.

mujhi kahe ko vigrate ho vabu, kya mithai achchhi nahin lagti?

jiyao-mithai ummid ke lie ai hai, hamare lie nahin ai. le ja, nahin to maim sarak par phenk dunga. ham to paise-paise ke lie ratte rahte haim, aur yaham rupyon ki mithai ati hai.

bhungi-tum le lo siya vabu, yah na lenge na sahi. NAmESiyaram ne darte-darte hath barhaya tha ki NAmEJiyaram ne dant kar kaha-mat chhuna mithai, nahin to hath tod kar rakh dunga. lalchi kahim ka. NAmESiyaram yah ghurki sun kar saham utha. mithai khane ki himmat na padi. NAmENirmala ne yah katha suni to
donon larkon ko manane chali. munshi ji ne kari kasam rakha di.

NAmENirmala -ap samjhate nahin haim. yah sara gussa mujh par hai.

munshi ji-gustakh ho gaya hai. is khyal se koi sakhti nahin karta ki log kahenge bina mam ke bachchon ko satate haim, nahin to sari shararat ghadi bhar men nikal dum.

NAmENirmala -isi badnami ka mujhe bhi to dar hai.

munshi ji-av na darunga, jiske ji men jo ae kahe.

NAmENirmala -pahle to yah aise na the.

munshi ji-aji kahta hai ki apke larke maujud the, apne shadi kyon ki? yah kahte bhi ise sankoch nahin hota ki ap logon ne NAmEmansaram ko vish de diya. larka nahin hai, dushman hai.

NAmEJiyaram dvar par chhip kar khada tha. aurat-aadmi men mithai ke vishay men kya baten hoti haim, yahi sunne vah aya tha. munshi ji ka antim vakya sun kar usse na raha gaya. bol utha-dushman na hota to ap uske pichhe kyon parte. ap jo is vakt kah rahe haim, vah maim bahut pahle se samjhe baitha hum. bhaiya na samjhe the, dhokha kha gae. hamare sath ap ki dal na galegi. sara jamana kah raha hai ki bhai sahav ko jahar diya gaya. maim kahta hum to kyon apko gussa ata hai?

NAmENirmala to sannate men a gai. malum hua kisi ne uski deh par angare dal die. munshi ji ne dant kar NAmEJiyaram ko
chup karna chaha; par NAmEJiyaram nihshanka khada iton ka javab patthr se deta raha. yaham tak ki NAmENirmala ko bhi us par gussa a gaya. yah kal ka chhokra na kisi kam ka na kaj ka, yon khada tarra raha hai; jaise ghar bhar ka palan-poshan yahi karta ho. tyoriyam chadha kar boli-bas, ab bahut hua NAmEJiyaram, malum ho gaya ki tum bade layak ho, vahar jakar vaitho.

munshi ji av tak to jara dab-dab kar bolte rahe, NAmENirmala ki shah pai to dil barh gaya. dant pis kar lapke aur iske pahle ki NAmENirmala unke hath pakad sake, ek thappar chalahi diya. thappar NAmENirmala ke mumh par pada, vahi samne padi. matha chakra gaya. munshi ji ke sukhe hue hathon men bhi itni shakti hai, iska vah anuman na kar sakti thi. sir pakad kar baith gai. munshi ji ka gussa aur bhi bharak utha, phir ghumsa chalaya; par ab ki NAmEJiyaram ne unka hath pakad liya; aur pichhe dhakel kar bola-dur se baten kijie; kyon nahak apni beijjati karvate haim. ammam ji ka lihaz kar raha hum, nahin to dikha deta.

yah kahta hua vah bahar chala gaya. munshi ji sanjnya-shunya se khade rahe. is vakt agar NAmEJiyaram par daivi vajra gir parta,
to shayad unhen hardik khushi hota. jis beta ko kabhi god men lekar nihal ho jate the, usi ke prati aj tarah-tarah ki dushkalpanaen man men a rahi thim.

NAmERukmini ab tak to apni kothri men thi. ab akar
boli-beta apne barabar ka ho gaya, to us par hath na chhorna chahiye.

munshi ji ne onth chaba kar kaha-maim ise ghar se nikal kar chhodunga. bhikh mange ya chori kare, mujhse koi matlab nahin.

NAmERukmini -nak kiski kategi?

munshi-iski chinta nahin.

NAmENirmala -maim janti ki mere ane se yah tuphan khada ho jayga, to bhul kar bhi na ati. ab bhi bhala hai, mujhe bhej dijie. is ghar men mujhse raha na jayga.

NAmERukmini -tumhara bahut lihaz karta hai bahu! nahin, to aj anarth hi ho jata.

NAmENirmala -ab aur kya anarth hoga didi ji? maim to phunk-phunk kar pamv rakhti hum; phir bhi apyash lag hi jata hai. abhi ghar men pamv rakhte der nahin hui; aur yah hal ho gaya. ishvar hi kushal karen.

rat ko khana karne koi na utha. akele munshi ji ne khaya. NAmENirmala ko aj ek nai chinta ho gai thi-jivan kaise par lagega? apna hi pet hota, to khas chinta na thi. ab to ek nai musibat gale par gai thi. vah soch rahi thi-meri bachchi ke bhagya men kya likha hai ram?
