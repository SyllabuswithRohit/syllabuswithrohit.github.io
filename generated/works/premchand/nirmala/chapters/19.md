# Nirmala — Adhyay 19

**Munshi Premchand**

unneesavaan parichchheda

ab kee sudhaa ke saatha nirmalaa ko bhee aana pada| vaha to maike men kuchha din aur rahana chaahatee thi, lekin shokaaturaa sudhaa akele kaise rahatee| usakee khaatira aana hee pada|

rukminee ne bhungee se kaha-dekhatee hai, bahoo maike se kaisi nikhara kar aaee hai|

bhungee ne kaha-deedee, maan ke haath kee rotiyaan ladakiyon ko bahut achchhi lagatee hain|

rukminee-theeka kehti hai bhungee, khilaanaa to kuchha maan hee jaanatee hai|

nirmalaa ko aisaa maalooma hua ki ghar kaa koee aadmi usake aane se khusha naheen| munshee jee ne khushi to bahut dikhaaee; par hridayagata chintaa ko na chhipaa sake| bachchi kaa naama sudhaa ne aashaa rakha diya tha| vaha aashaa kee moorti see thi bhee| dekha kar saaree chintaa bhaaga jati thi| munshee jee ne use goda men lena chaaha, to rone lagee, dau.da kar maan se lipata gaee; maano pitaa ko pahachaanatee hee naheen| munshee jee ne mithaaiyon se use parachaanaa chaaha! ghar men koee naukara to tha naheen, jaakara siyaaraama se do aane kee mithaaiya.c laane ko kaha| jiyaaraama bhee baithaa hua tha| bola uthaa -- hama logon ke lie to kabhee mithaaiyaan naheen aati|

munshee jee ne jhunjhalaakara kaha -- tuma log bachche naheen ho|

jiyaaraama -- aur kyaa boo.dhe hain| mithaaiyaan mangavaa kar rakha deejie, to maalooma ho ki bachche hain yaa boo.dhe| nikaalie chaara aane aur| aashaa kee badaulata hamaare naseeba bhee jaagen|

munshee jee -- mere paasa is vakta paise naheen hain| jaao siya, jalda aana|

jiya 0-- siya naheen jaayagaa| kisee kaa gulaama naheen hai| aashaa apane baapa kee betee hai, to vaha bhee apane baapa kaa betaa hai|

munshee jee -- kyaa fuzoola kee baaten karte ho| nanheen see bachchi kee baraabaree karte tumhen sharma naheen aati| jaao siyaaraama, ye paise lo|

jiya 0-- mata jana siya! tuma kisee ke naukara naheen ho|
siya ba.dee duvidhaa men pa.da gaya| kisakaa kahana maane? anta men usane jiyaaraama kaa kahana maanane kaa nishchaya kiya| baapa zyada se zyada ghu.daka denge, jiya to maarega, phir vaha kisake paasa fariyaada lekara jaayagaa| bola -- main na jaaoongaa| munshee jee ne dhamakaa kar kaha-achchha, to mere paasa phir koee cheeza maangane mata aana|

munshee jee khuda baajaara chale gae; aur ek rupae kee mithaaee lekara laute| do aane ko mithaaee maangate hue unhen sharma aaee| halavaaee unhen pahachaanata tha| dil men kyaa kahegaa ?

mithaaee lie hue munshee jee andara chale gae| siyaaraama ne mithaaee kaa bada saa donaa dekha, to vaapa kaa kahana na maanane kaa use duhkha hua| ab vaha kisa munha se mithaaee lene andara jaayagaa| va.dee bhoola hui | vaha mann hee mann jiyaaraama ke chaanton kee chota aur mithaaee kee mithaasa men tulana karane lagaa|

sahasaa bhungee ne do tashtariyao donon ke saamane laakara rakha dee| jiyaaraama ne biga.da kar kaha-ise uthaa le jaa|

mujhee kaahe ko viga.date ho vaaboo, kyaa mithaaee achchhi naheen lagatee?

jiyaao-mithaaee aashaa ke lie aaee hai, hamaare lie naheen aaee | le ja, naheen to main sa.daka par phenka doongaa| hama to paise-paise ke lie ratate rahate hain, aur yahaan rupayon kee mithaaee aati hai|

bhungee-tuma le lo siya vaaboo, yaha na lenge na sahee| siyaaraama ne darate-darate haath ba.dhaayaa tha ki jiyaaraama ne daanta kar kaha-mata chhoonaa mithaaee, naheen to haath to.da kar rakha doongaa| laalachee kaheen kaa | siyaaraama yaha ghu.dakee suna kar sahama uthaa| mithaaee khaane kee himmata na pa.dee | nirmalaa ne yaha kathaa sunee to donon ladakon ko manaane chalee| munshee jee ne ka.dee kasama rakhaa dee|

nirmalaa-aapa samajhate naheen hain | yaha saaraa gussaa mujha par hai|

munshee jee-gustaakha ho gaya hai | is khayal se koee sakhtee naheen karata ki log kahenge binaa maan ke bachchon ko sataate hain, naheen to saaree sharaarata gha.dee bhara men nikaala doon|

nirmalaa-isee badanaamee kaa mujhe bhee to dara hai|

munshee jee-ava na daroonga, jisake jee men jo aae kahe|

nirmalaa-pahale to yaha aise na the|

munshee jee-ajee kahata hai ki aapake ladke maujooda the, aapane shaadee kyon kee? yaha kehte bhee ise sankocha naheen hota ki aapa logon ne mansaaraama ko visha de diya| ladka naheen hai, shatru hai|

jiyaaraama dvaara par chhipa kar kha.daa tha| stree-purusha men mithaaee ke vishaya men kyaa baaten hoti hain, yahee sunane vaha aaya tha| munshee jee kaa antima vaakya suna kar usase na raha gaya | bola uthaa-shatru na hota to aapa usake peechhe kyon pa.date | aapa jo is vakta kaha rahe hain, vaha main bahut pahale se samajhe baithaa hoon | bhaiyaa na samajhe the, dhokhaa khaa gae| hamaare saatha aapa kee daala na galegee| saaraa jamaanaa kaha raha hai ki bhaaee saahava ko jahara diya gaya| main kahata hoon to kyon aapako gussaa aata hai ?

nirmalaa to sannaate men aa gaee | maalooma hua kisee ne usakee deha par angaare daala die | munshee jee ne daanta kar jiyaaraama ko chupa karana chaaha; par jiyaaraama nihshanka kha.daa eeton kaa javaaba patthara se detaa raha| yahaan taka ki nirmalaa ko bhee us par krodha aa gaya| yaha kala kaa chhokaraa na kisee kaama kaa na kaaja ka, yon kha.daa tarraa raha hai; jaise ghar bhara kaa paalana-poshana yahee karata ho| tyoriyaan cha.dhaa kar boli-basa, ab bahut hua jiyaaraama, maalooma ho gaya ki tuma ba.de laayaka ho, vaahara jaakara vaitho|

munshee jee ava taka to jaraa daba-daba kar bolte rahe, nirmalaa kee shaha paaee to dil ba.dha gaya| daanta peesa kar lapake aur isake pahale ki nirmalaa unake haath paka.da sake, ek thappa.da chalaahee diya| thappa.da nirmalaa ke munha par pa.da, vahee saamane pa.dee| maathaa chakaraa gaya| munshee jee ke sookhe hue haathon men bhee itanee shakti hai, isakaa vaha anumaana na kar sakatee thi| sira paka.da kar baitha gaee| munshee jee kaa krodha aur bhee bha.daka utha, phir ghoonsaa chalaaya;par ab kee jiyaaraama ne unakaa haath paka.da liya; aur peechhe dhakela kar bola-doora se baaten keejie; kyon naahaka apanee beijjatee karavaate hain| ammaan jee kaa lihaaza kar raha hoon, naheen to dikhaa detaa|

yaha kahata hua vaha baahara chalaa gaya| munshee jee sanjnaa-shoonya •se kha.de rahe| is vakta agar jiyaaraama par daivee vajra gira pa.data,
to shaayada unhen haardika aananda hota| jisa putra ko kabhee goda men lekara nihaala ho jaate the, usee ke prati aaja bhaanti-bhaanti kee dushkalpanaaen mann men aa rahi theen|

rukminee ab taka to apanee kotharee men thi| ab aakara boli-betaa apane baraabara kaa ho gaya, to us par haath na chho.dana chaahie|

munshee jee ne ontha chabaa kar kaha-main ise ghar se nikaala kar chhodoongaa| bheekha maange yaa choree kare, mujhase koee matalaba naheen|

rukminee-naaka kisakee kategee?

munshee-isakee chintaa naheen|

nirmalaa-main jaanatee ki mere aane se yaha toophaana kha.daa ho jaayaga, to bhoola kar bhee na aati| ab bhee bhalaa hai, mujhe bheja deejie| is ghar men mujhase raha na jaayagaa|

rukminee-tumhaaraa bahut lihaaza karata hai bahoo! naheen, to aaja anartha hee ho jata|

nirmalaa-ab aur kyaa anartha hogaa deedee jee? main to phoonka-phoonka kar paanva rakhatee hoon; phir bhee apayasha laga hee jata hai| abhee ghar men paanva rakhate dera naheen hui; aur yaha haala ho gaya| eeshvara hee kushala karen|

raat ko bhojana karane koee na uthaa| akele munshee jee ne khaayaa| nirmalaa ko aaja ek naee chintaa ho gaee thi-jeevana kaise paara lagega? apana hee peta hota, to vishesha chintaa na thi| ab to ek naee vipatti gale pa.da gaee thi| vaha socha rahi thi-meree bachchi ke bhaagya men kyaa likhaa hai raama?
