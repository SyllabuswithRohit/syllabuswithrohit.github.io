# Nirmala — Adhyay 24

**Munshi Premchand**

chaubeesavaan parichchhed

Nirmala saaree raat rotee rahee. itanaa badaa kalanggk! usane jiyaaraam ko gahane le jaate dekhane par bhi munh kholane ka himmat nahin kiyaa. kyon? iseelie to ki log samajhengge ki vah mithyaa doshaaropan karake ladake se bair saadh rahee hai. aaj usake maun rahane par use jurminee thaharaayaa jaa rahaa hai. yadi vah jiyaaraam ko usee pal rok detee; aur jiyaaraam sharmavash kaheen bhaag jaataa, to kyaa usake sir jurm n madhaa jaataa?

siyaaraam hee ke saath usane kaun saa durvyavahaar kiyaa thaa. vah kuchh bachat karane hee ke soch se to siyaaraam se saudaa manggavaayaa karatee thee. kyaa vah bachat karake apane lie gahane gadhavaanaa chaahatee thi? jab aamadanee ka yah haal ho rahaa tha, to paise-paise par nigaah rakhane ke sivaay kuchh jamaa karane ka usake paas aur saadhan hee kyaa tha? javaanon ki jindagee ka to koee bharosaa hee nahin, boodhon ki jindagee ka kyaa thikaanaa? bachchi ke shaadee ke lie vah kisake saamane haath phailaatee? bachchi ka bhaar kuchh usee par to nahaa thaa. vah keval shauhar ki suvidhaa hee ke lie kuchh batorane ka prayatn kar rahee thee. shauhar ho ki kyon? siyaaraam hee to pitaa ke baad ghar ka svaamee hotaa. bahin ke shaadee karane ka bhaar kyaa usake sir n padataa? Nirmala saaree katar-vyaat shauhar aur putr ka sanggkat mochan karane hee ke lie kar rahee thee. bachchi ka shaadee is haal mein sanggkat ke sivaay
aur kyaa tha? par isake lie bhi usake kismat mein apayash hee badaa thaa.

dopahar ho gayaa tha; par aaj bhi choolhaa nahin jalaa. khana bhi zindagi ka kaam hai-isakee kisee ko sudh hee n thee. munshee jee baahar bejaan-se pade the, aur Nirmala bheetar. bachchi kabhee bheetar jaatee, kabhee baahar. koee usase bolane vaalaa n thaa. baar-baar siyaaraam ke kamare ke dvaar par jaakar khadee hotee; aur 'baiyaa-vaiyaa' pukaaratee; par 'vaiyaa' koee javaab n detaa tha!

sandhyaa samay munshee jee aakar Nirmala se bole tumhaare paas kuchh rupaye hain?

Nirmala ne chaungk kar poochhaa-kyaa keejiegaa?

munshee jee-main jo poochhataa hoon usakaa javaab do. Nirmala -- kyaa aapko nahin maaloom hai? dene vaale to aap hee hain.

munshee -- tumhaare paas kuchh rupaye hain ya nahin? agar hon to mujhe de do, n hon to saaph javaab do.

Nirmala ne ab bhi saaph jabaav n diyaa. bolee -- hongge to ghar hee mein n hongge. main ne kaheen aur to nahin bhej die.

munshee jee baahar chale gae. vah jaanate the ki -- Nirmala ke paas rupaye hain. vaastav mein the bhee. Nirmala ne yah bhi nahin kahaa ki nahin hain, ya main n doonggee; par usakee baaton se saamane ho gayaa ki vah denaa nahin chaahatee.

nau baje raat ko munshee jee ne aakar rukminee se kahaa -- bahin, main jaraa baahar jaa rahaa hooe. meraa vistaraa bhungggee se bandhavaa denaa; aur trak mein kuchh kapde rakhavaa kar band kar denaa.

rukminee khana banaa rahee thi; bolee -- bahoo to kamare mein hai, kah kyon nahin dete? kahaan jaane ka iraadaa hai?

munshee jee -- main tumase kahataa hoon; bahoo se kahanaa hotaa, to tumase kyon kahataa? aaj tum kyon khana pakaa rahee ho?

rukminee -- kaun pakaave? bahoo ke sir mein dard ho rahaa hai. aakhir is vakt kahaan jaa rahe ho? sabere n chale jaanaa.

munshee jee -- isee tarah taalate-taalate to aaj teen din ho gae. idhar-udhar ghoom-dhaam kar dekhoon, shaayad kaheen siyaaraam ka

rukminee -- to lautoge kab tak?

munshee jee -- kah nahin sakataa. haphtaa bhar lag jaay, maheenaa bhar lag jaay; kyaa thikaanaa hai?

rukminee -- aaj kaun din hai? kisee pandit se poochh liyaa hai, yaatraa hai ki nahin?

munshee jee khana karane baithe. Nirmala ko is vakt un par badee taras aaee. usakaa saaraa krodh shaant ho gayaa. khud to n bolee, bachchi ko jagaa kar chumakaaratee huee bolee -- dekh, tere baaboo jee kahaan jaa rahe hain? poochh to.

bachchi ne dvaar se jhaangk kar poochhaa -- baaboo dee, tahaan daate ho?

bachchi ne vaheen se khade-khade kahaa -- am bee talengge.

munshee jee -- badeen door jaate hain bachchee. tumhaare vaaste cheejen laavengge! yahan kyon nahin aatee?

bachchi muskaraa kar chhip gaee; aur ek pal mein phir kivaad se sir nikaal kar bolee -- am bee talengge.

munshee jee ne usee svar mein kahaa -- tum ko nanee le talengge.

bachchi -- am ko kyon naeen le taloge?

munshee jee -- tum to hamaare paachh aatee nahin ho. ladakee thumakatee huee aakar pitaa ki god mein baith gaee. thodee der ke lie munshee jee usakee baal-kreedaa mein apanee antardard bhool gae.

khana karake munshee jee baahar chale gae. Nirmala khadee taakatee rahee. kahanaa chaahatee thi-vyarth jaa rahe ho; par kah n sakatee thee. kuchh rupaye nikaal kar dene ka soch karatee thi; par de n sakatee thee.

rookminee ne karun-soochak aangkhenon se dekhaa; aur apane kamare mein chalee gaee.

Nirmala bachchi ko god mein lie soch rahee thi ki shaayad jaane ke pahale bachchi ko dekhane ya mujhase milane ke lie aaven; par usakee ummeed viphal ho gaee. munshee jee ne vistar uthaayaa aur taangge par jaa baithe.

usee vakt Nirmala ka kalejaa masosane lagaa. use aisaa jaan padaa ki ab inase bhent n hogee. vah adheer hokar dvaar par aaee ki munshee jee ko rok le; par taanggaa chal diyaa tha!
