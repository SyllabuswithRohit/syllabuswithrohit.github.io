# Nirmala — Adhyay 24

**Munshi Premchand**

chaubisvam parichchhed

NAmENirmala sari rat roti rahi. itna bada kalanka! usne NAmEJiyaram ko gahne le jate dekhne par bhi mumh kholne ka himmat nahi kiya. kyon? isilie to ki log samjhenge ki vah mithya dosharopan karke larke se bair sadh rahi hai. aj uske chup rahne par use aparadhini thahraya ja raha hai. yadi vah NAmEJiyaram ko usi pal rok deti; aur NAmEJiyaram lajjavash kahim bhag jata, to kya uske sir jurm na marha jata?

NAmESiyaram hi ke sath usne kaun sa durvyavhar kiya tha. vah kuchh bachat karne hi ke soch se to NAmESiyaram se sauda mangvaya karti thi. kya vah bachat karke apne lie gahne garhvana chahti thi? jab amdani ka yah hal ho raha tha, to paise-paise par nigah rakhne ke sivay kuchh jama karne ka
uske pas aur sadhan hi kya tha? javanon ki jindagi ka to koi bharosa hi nahin, burhon ki jindagi ka kya thikana? bachchi ke shadi ke lie vah kiske samne hath phailati? bachchi ka bhar kuchh usi par to naha tha. vah sirph pati ki suvidha hi ke lie kuchh batorne ka koshish kar rahi thi. pati ho ki kyon? NAmESiyaram hi to pita ke bad ghar ka malik hota. bahin ke shadi karne ka bhar kya uske sir na parta? NAmENirmala sari katar-vyat pati aur beta ka museebat mochan karne hi ke lie kar rahi thi. bachchi ka shadi is hal men museebat ke sivay
aur kya tha? par iske lie bhi uske bhagya men apyash hi bada tha.

dophar ho gaya tha; par aj bhi chulha nahin jala. khana bhi jivan ka kam hai-iski kisi ko sudh hi na thi. munshi ji bahar bejan-se pade the, aur NAmENirmala bhitar. bachchi kabhi bhitar jati, kabhi bahar. koi usse bolne vala na tha. bar-bar NAmESiyaram ke kamre ke dvar par jakar khadi hoti; aur baiya-vaiya pukarti; par vaiya koi javab na deta tha!

sham samay munshi ji akar NAmENirmala se bole tumhare pas kuchh rupae haim?

NAmENirmala ne chaunk kar puchha-kya kijiega?

munshi ji-maim jo puchhta hum uska javab do.
NAmENirmala -- kya apko nahin malum hai? dene vale to ap hi haim.

munshi -- tumhare pas kuchh rupae haim ya nahin? agar hon to mujhe de do, na hon to saf javab do.

NAmENirmala ne ab bhi saf jabav na diya. boli -- honge to ghar hi men na honge. maim ne kahim aur to nahin bhej die.

munshi ji bahar chale gae. vah jante the ki -- NAmENirmala ke pas rupae haim. sach men the bhi. NAmENirmala ne yah bhi nahin kaha ki nahin haim, ya maim na dungi; par uski baton se prakat ho gaya ki vah dena nahin chahti.

nau baje rat ko munshi ji ne akar NAmERukmini se kaha -- bahin, maim zara bahar ja raha hua. mera vistara bhungi se bandhva dena; aur trak men kuchh kapde rakhva kar band kar dena.

NAmERukmini khana bana rahi thi; boli -- bahu to kamre men hai, kah kyon nahin dete? kaham jane ka irada hai?

munshi ji -- maim tumse kahta hum; bahu se kahna hota, to tumse kyon kahta? aj tum kyon khana paka rahi ho?

NAmERukmini -- kaun pakave? bahu ke sir men dard ho raha hai. akhir is vakta kaham ja rahe ho? sabere na chale jana.

munshi ji -- isi tarah talte-talte to aj tin din ho gae. idhar-udhar ghum-dham kar dekhum, shayad kahim NAmESiyaram ka

pata mil jay. kuchh log kahte haim ki ek sadhu ke sath baten kar raha tha. shayad vahi kahim bahka le gaya ho.

NAmERukmini -- to lautoge kab tak?

munshi ji -- kah nahin sakta. haphta bhar lag jay, mahina bhar lag jay; kya thikana hai?

NAmERukmini -- aj kaun din hai? kisi pandit se puchh liya hai, yatra hai ki nahin?

munshi ji khana karne baithe. NAmENirmala ko is vakta un par badi daya ai. uska sara gussa shant ho gaya. khud to na boli, bachchi ko jaga kar chumakarti hui boli -- dekh, tere babu ji kaham ja rahe haim? puchh to.

bachchi ne dvar se jhank kar puchha -- babu di, taham date ho?

munshi ji -- badi dur jata hum, beti. tumhare baiya ko khojne jata hum.

bachchi ne vahim se khade-khade kaha -- am bi talenge.

munshi ji -- barim dur jate haim bachchi. tumhare vaste chizen lavenge! yaham kyon nahin ati?

bachchi muskara kar chhip gai; aur ek pal men phir kivar se sir nikal kar boli -- am bi talenge.

munshi ji ne usi avaz men kaha -- tum ko nami le talenge.

bachchi -- am ko kyon naim le taloge?

munshi ji -- tum to hamare pachh ati nahin ho.
ladki thumkati hui akar pita ki god men baith gai. thodi der ke lie munshi ji uski bal-krira men apni antarvedna bhul gae.

khana karke munshi ji bahar chale gae. NAmENirmala khadi takti rahi. kahna chahti thi-bekar ja rahe ho; par kah na sakti thi. kuchh rupae nikal kar dene ka soch karti thi; par de na sakti thi.

ant ko na raha gaya. NAmERukmini se boli-didi ji, jara samjha dijie, kaham ja rahe haim. meri to jaban pakri jaygi; par bina bole raha nahin jata. bina thikane kaham khojenge? bekar ki hairani hogi.

rukmini ne karun-suchak netron se dekha; aur apne kamre men chali gai.

NAmENirmala bachchi ko god men lie soch rahi thi ki shayad jane ke pahle bachchi ko dekhne ya mujhse milne ke lie aven; par uski ummid viphal ho gai. munshi ji ne vistar uthaya aur tange par ja baithe.

usi vakt NAmENirmala ka kaleja masosne laga. use aisa jan pada ki ab inse bhent na hogi. vah adhir hokar dvar par ai ki munshi ji ko rok le; par tanga chal diya tha!
