# Chapter 24

chaubeesavaa.N parichchheda

nirmalaa saaree raat rotee rahi| itanaa badaa kalanka! usane jiyaaraama ko gahane le jaate dekhane para bhee mu.Nha kholane kaa saahasa nahee kiya| kyon? iseelie to ki log samajhenge ki vaha mithyaa doshaaropaNa karake ladake se baira saadha rahi hai| aaja usake chupa rahane para use aparaadhinee thaharaayaa jaa raha hai| yadi vaha jiyaaraama ko usee pala roka detee; aur jiyaaraama lajjaavasha kaheen bhaaga jata, to kyaa usake sira aparaadha na madhaa jata?

siyaaraama hee ke saatha usane kauna saa durvyavahaara kiya tha| vaha kuchha bachata karane hee ke socha se to siyaaraama se saudaa ma.Ngavaayaa karti thi| kyaa vaha bachata karake apane lie gahane gadhavaanaa chaahatee thi? jaba aamadanee kaa yaha haala ho raha tha, to paise-paise para nigaaha rakhane ke sivaaya kuchha jamaa karane kaa
usake paasa aur saadhana hee kyaa tha ? javaanon kee jindagee kaa to koee bharosaa hee naheen, boodhon kee jindagee kaa kyaa thikaanaa ? bachchee ke vivaaha ke lie vaha kisake saamane haath phailaatee ? bachchee kaa bhaara kuchha usee para to nahaa tha| vaha kevala pati kee suvidhaa hee ke lie kuchha batorane kaa koshisha kar rahi thi| pati ho kee kyon ? siyaaraama hee to pitaa ke baada ghar kaa svaamee hota | bahina ke vivaaha karane kaa bhaara kyaa usake sira na padataa ? nirmalaa saaree katara-vyaata pati aur betaa kaa sankata mochana karane hee ke lie kar rahi thi| bachchee kaa vivaaha is paristhiti men sankata ke sivaaya
aur kyaa tha ? para isake lie bhee usake bhaagya men apayasha hee badaa tha|

dopahara ho gaya tha ; para aaja bhee choolhaa naheen jalaa | khaanaa bhee jeevana kaa kaama hai-isakee kisee ko sudha hee na thi| munshee jee baahara bejaana-se pade the, aur nirmalaa bheetara | bachchee kabhee bheetara jati, kabhee baahara | koee usase bolane vaalaa na tha| baara-baara siyaaraama ke kamare ke dvaara para jaakara khadee hoti; aur 'baiyaa-vaiyaa' pukaaratee ; para 'vaiyaa' koee javaaba na detaa tha!

shaama samaya munshee jee aakara nirmalaa se bole tumhaare paasa kuchha rupae hain ?

nirmalaa ne chaunka kar poochhaa-kyaa keejiegaa ?

munshee jee-main jo poochhataa hoo.N usakaa javaaba do|
nirmalaa -- kyaa aapako naheen maalooma hai? dene vaale to aapa hee hain|

munshee -- tumhaare paasa kuchha rupae hain yaa naheen? agar hon to mujhe de do, na hon to saapha javaaba do|

nirmalaa ne aba bhee saapha jabaava na diya| bolee -- honge to ghar hee men na honge| main ne kaheen aur to naheen bheja die|

munshee jee baahara chale gae| vaha jaanate the ki -- nirmalaa ke paasa rupae hain| vaastava men the bhee| nirmalaa ne yaha bhee naheen kahaa ki naheen hain, yaa main na doo.Ngee; para usakee baaton se prakata ho gaya ki vaha denaa naheen chaahatee|

nau baje raat ko munshee jee ne aakara rukmiNee se kahaa -- bahina, main jaraa baahara jaa raha hooe| meraa vistaraa bhungee se ba.Ndhavaa denaa; aur traka men kuchha kapade rakhavaa kar banda kar denaa|

rukmiNee khaanaa banaa rahi thi; bolee -- bahoo to kamare men hai, kaha kyon naheen dete? kahaa.N jaane kaa iraadaa hai?

munshee jee -- main tumase kehta hoo.N; bahoo se kahanaa hota,to tumase kyon kehta? aaja tuma kyon khaanaa pakaa rahi ho?

rukmiNee -- kauna pakaave? bahoo ke sira men darda ho raha hai| aakhira is vakta kahaa.N jaa rahe ho? sabere na chale jana|

munshee jee -- isee taraha taalate-taalate to aaja teena din ho gae| idhara-udhara ghooma-dhaama kar dekhoo.N, shaayada kaheen siyaaraama kaa

pataa mila jaaya| kuchha log kehte hain ki ek saadhoo ke saatha baaten kar raha tha| shaayada vahee kaheen bahakaa le gaya ho|

rukmiNee -- to lautoge kaba taka?

munshee jee -- kaha naheen sakataa| haphtaa bhara laga jaaya, maheenaa bhara laga jaaya; kyaa thikaanaa hai?

rukmiNee -- aaja kauna din hai? kisee paNdita se poochha liya hai, yaatraa hai ki naheen?

munshee jee khaanaa karane baithe| nirmalaa ko is vakta una para badee dayaa aaee| usakaa saaraa gussaa shaanta ho gaya| khuda to na bolee, bachchee ko jagaa kar chumakaaratee huee bolee -- dekha,tere baaboo jee kahaa.N jaa rahe hain? poochha to|

bachchee ne dvaara se jhaa.Nka kar poochhaa -- baaboo dee,tahaa.N daate ho?

munshee jee -- badee doora jata hoo.N,betee| tumhaare baiyaa ko khojane jata hoo.N|

bachchee ne vaheen se khade-khade kahaa -- ama bee talenge|

munshee jee -- badeen doora jaate hain bachchee| tumhaare vaaste cheejen laavenge! yahaa.N kyon naheen aatee?

bachchee muskaraa kar chhipa gaee; aur ek pala men phir kivaada se sira nikaala kar bolee -- ama bee talenge|

munshee jee ne usee aavaaja men kahaa -- tuma ko nanee le talenge|

bachchee -- ama ko kyon naeen le taloge?

munshee jee -- tuma to hamaare paachha aatee naheen ho|
ladakee thumakatee huee aakara pitaa kee goda men baitha gaee| thodee dera ke lie munshee jee usakee baala-kreedaa men apanee antarvedanaa bhoola gae|

khaanaa karake munshee jee baahara chale gae| nirmalaa khadee taakatee rahi| kahanaa chaahatee thi-bekaara jaa rahe ho;para kaha na sakatee thi| kuchha rupae nikaala kar dene kaa socha karti thi;para de na sakatee thi|

anta ko na raha gaya| rukmiNee se bolee-deedee jee,jaraa samajhaa deejie,kahaa.N jaa rahe hain| meree to jabaana pakadee jaayagee;para binaa bole raha naheen jata| binaa thikaane kahaa.N khojenge? bekaara kee hairaanee hogee|

rookmiNee ne karuNa-soochaka netron se dekha;aur apane kamare men chalee gaee|

nirmalaa bachchee ko goda men lie socha rahi thi ki shaayada jaane ke pahale bachchee ko dekhane yaa mujhase milane ke lie aaven; para usakee ummeeda viphala ho gaee| munshee jee ne vistara uthaayaa aur taa.Nge para jaa baithe|

usee vakta nirmalaa kaa kalejaa masosane lagaa| use aisaa jaana padaa ki aba inase bhenta na hogee| vaha adheera hokara dvaara para aaee ki munshee jee ko roka le; para taa.Ngaa chala diya tha!
