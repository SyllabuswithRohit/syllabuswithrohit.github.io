# Nirmala — Adhyay 24

**Munshi Premchand**

chaubeesavaan parichchhed

nirml saaree raat rotee rahee. itn ba. daa kalank! usane jiyaaraam ko gahane le jaate dekhane par bhee munh kholane kaa himmat nahee kiyaa. kyon? iseelie to ki log samajhenge ki woh mithyaa doshaaropan karake la. dake se bair saadh rahee hai. aaj usake chup rahane par use aparaadhinee thaharaayaa jaa rahaa hai. yadi woh jiyaaraam ko usee pal rok detee; aur jiyaaraam lajjaavash kaheen bhaag jaataa, to kyaa usake sir jurm na ma. dhaa jaataa?

siyaaraam hee ke saath usane kaun saa durvyavahaar kiyaa thaa. woh kuchh bachat karane hee ke soch se to siyaaraam se saudaa mangavaayaa karatee thee. kyaa woh bachat karake apane lie gahane ga. dhavaanaa chaahatee thee? jab aamadanee kaa yeh haal ho rahaa thaa, to paise-paise par nigaah rakhane ke sivaay kuchh jamaa karane kaa
usake paas aur saadhan hee kyaa thaa? javaanon kee jindagee kaa to koee bharosaa hee naheen, boo. dhon kee jindagee kaa kyaa thikaanaa? bachchee ke shaadee ke lie woh kisake saamane haath phailaatee? bachchee kaa bhaar kuchh usee par to nahaa thaa. woh sirph pati kee suvidhaa hee ke lie kuchh batorane kaa koshish kar rahee thee. pati ho kee kyon? siyaaraam hee to pitaa ke baad ghar kaa maalik hotaa. bahin ke shaadee karane kaa bhaar kyaa usake sir na pa. dataa? nirml saaree katara-vyaat pati aur betaa kaa sankat mochan karane hee ke lie kar rahee thee. bachchee kaa shaadee is haal men sankat ke sivaay
aur kyaa thaa? par isake lie bhee usake bhaagy men apayash hee badaa thaa.

dopahar ho gayaa thaa; par aaj bhee choolhaa naheen jl. khaanaa bhee jeevan kaa kaam hai-isakee kisee ko sudh hee na thee. munshee jee baahar bejaana-se pa. de the, aur nirml bheetar. bachchee kabhee bheetar jaatee, kabhee baahar. koee usase bolane vaalaa na thaa. baara-baar siyaaraam ke kamare ke dvaar par jaakar kha. dee hotee; aur 'baiyaa-vaiyaa' pukaaratee; par 'vaiyaa' koee javaab na detaa thaa!

shaam samay munshee jee aakar nirml se bole tumhaare paas kuchh rupae hain?

nirml ne chaunk kar poochhaa-kyaa keejiegaa?

munshee jee-main jo poochhataa hoon usakaa javaab do.
nirml -- kyaa aapako naheen maaloom hai? dene vaale to aap hee hain.

munshee -- tumhaare paas kuchh rupae hain yaa naheen? agar hon to mujhe de do, na hon to saaf javaab do.

nirml ne ab bhee saaf jabaav na diyaa. bolee -- honge to ghar hee men na honge. main ne kaheen aur to naheen bhej die.

munshee jee baahar chale gae. woh jaanate the ki -- nirml ke paas rupae hain. sach men the bhee. nirml ne yeh bhee naheen kahaa ki naheen hain, yaa main na doongee; par usakee baaton se prakat ho gayaa ki woh denaa naheen chaahatee.

nau baje raat ko munshee jee ne aakar rukminee se kahaa -- bahin, main zr baahar jaa rahaa hoo. c. meraa vistr bhungee se bandhv denaa; aur trak men kuchh kapa. de rakhv kar band kar denaa.

rukminee khaanaa bn rahee thee; bolee -- bahoo to kamare men hai, kah kyon naheen dete? kahaan jaane kaa iraadaa hai?

munshee jee -- main tumase kahataa hoon; bahoo se kahn hotaa, to tumase kyon kahataa? aaj tum kyon khaanaa pakaa rahee ho?

rukminee -- kaun pakaave? bahoo ke sir men dard ho rahaa hai. aakhir is vakt़ kahaan jaa rahe ho? sabere na chale jaanaa.

munshee jee -- isee tarah taalate-taalate to aaj teen din ho gae. idhara-udhar ghooma-dhaam kar dekhoon, shaayad kaheen siyaaraam kaa

pataa mil jaaya. kuchh log kahate hain ki ek saadhoo ke saath baaten kar rahaa thaa. shaayad vahee kaheen bahakaa le gayaa ho.

rukminee -- to lautoge kab tak?

munshee jee -- kah naheen sakataa. hapht़aa bhar lag jaay, maheenaa bhar lag jaay; kyaa thikaanaa hai?

rukminee -- aaj kaun din hai? kisee pandit se poochh liyaa hai, yaatraa hai ki naheen?

munshee jee khaanaa karane baithe. nirml ko is vakt़ una par ba. dee dayaa aaee. usakaa saaraa gussaa shaant ho gayaa. Kud to na bolee, bachchee ko jagaa kar chumakaaratee huee bolee -- dekh, tere baaboo jee kahaan jaa rahe hain? poochh to.

bachchee ne dvaar se jhaank kar poochhaa -- baaboo dee, tahaan daate ho?

munshee jee -- ba. dee door jaataa hoon, betee. tumhaare baiyaa ko khojane jaataa hoon.

bachchee ne vaheen se kha. de-kha. de kahaa -- ama bee talenge.

munshee jee -- ba. deen door jaate hain bachchee. tumhaare vaaste cheezen laavenge! yahaan kyon naheen aatee?

bachchee muskr kar chhip gaee; aur ek pal men phir kivaa. d se sir nikaal kar bolee -- ama bee talenge.

munshee jee ne usee aavaaz men kahaa -- tum ko nanee le talenge.

bachchee -- ama ko kyon naeen le taloge?

munshee jee -- tum to hamaare paachh aatee naheen ho.
la. dakee thumakatee huee aakar pitaa kee god men baith gaee. tho. dee der ke lie munshee jee usakee baala-kree. daa men apanee antarvedn bhool gae.

khaanaa karake munshee jee baahar chale gae. nirml kha. dee taakatee rahee. kahn chaahatee thee-bekaar jaa rahe ho; par kah na sakatee thee. kuchh rupae nikaal kar dene kaa soch karatee thee; par de na sakatee thee.

ant ko na rahaa gayaa. rukminee se bolee-deedee jee, jr samajhaa deejie, kahaan jaa rahe hain. meree to jabaan paka. dee jaayagee; par binaa bole rahaa naheen jaataa. binaa thikaane kahaan khojenge? bekaar kee hairaanee hogee.

rookminee ne karuna-soochak netron se dekhaa; aur apane kamare men chalee gaee.

nirml bachchee ko god men lie soch rahee thee ki shaayad jaane ke pahale bachchee ko dekhane yaa mujhase milane ke lie aaven; par usakee ummeed viphal ho gaee. munshee jee ne vistar uthaayaa aur taange par jaa baithe.

usee vakt nirml kaa kalejaa masosane lagaa. use aisaa jaan pa. daa ki ab inase bhent na hogee. woh adheer hokar dvaar par aaee ki munshee jee ko rok le; par taangaa chal diyaa thaa!
