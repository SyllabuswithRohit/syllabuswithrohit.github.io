# Nirmala — Adhyay 24

**Munshi Premchand**

chaubeesavaan parichchheda

nirmalaa saaree raat rotee rahi| itana bada kalanka! usane jiyaaraama ko gahane le jaate dekhane par bhee munha kholane kaa saahasa nahee kiya| kyon? iseelie to ki log samajhenge ki vaha mithyaa doshaaropana karake ladke se baira saadha rahi hai| aaja usake mauna rahane par use aparaadhinee thaharaayaa jaa raha hai| yadi vaha jiyaaraama ko usee kshana roka detee; aur jiyaaraama lajjaavasha kaheen bhaaga jaata, to kyaa usake sira aparaadha na ma.dhaa jaata?

siyaaraama hee ke saatha usane kaun saa durvyavahaara kiya tha| vaha kuchha bachata karane hee ke vichaara se to siyaaraama se saudaa mangavaayaa karti thi| kyaa vaha bachata karake apane lie gahane ga.dhavaanaa chaahatee thi? jab aamadanee kaa yaha haala ho raha tha, to paise-paise par nigaaha rakhane ke sivaaya kuchha jamaa karane kaa usake paasa aur saadhana hee kyaa tha ? javaanon kee zindagi kaa to koee bharosaa hee naheen, boo.dhon kee zindagi kaa kyaa thikaanaa ? bachchi ke vivaaha ke lie vaha kisake saamane haath phailaatee ? bachchi kaa bhaara kuchha usee par to nahaa tha| vaha kevala pati kee suvidhaa hee ke lie kuchha batorane kaa prayatna kar rahi thi| pati ho kee kyon ? siyaaraama hee to pitaa ke baada ghar kaa svaamee hota | bahina ke vivaaha karane kaa bhaara kyaa usake sira na pa.data ? nirmalaa saaree katara-vyaata pati aur putra kaa sankata mochana karane hee ke lie kar rahi thi| bachchi kaa vivaaha is paristhiti men sankata ke sivaaya
aur kyaa tha ? par isake lie bhee usake bhaagya men apayasha hee bada tha|

dopahara ho gaya tha ; par aaja bhee choolhaa naheen jalaa | khaanaa bhee jeevana kaa kaama hai-isakee kisee ko sudha hee na thi| munshee jee baahara bejaana-se pa.de the, aur nirmalaa bheetara | bachchi kabhee bheetara jati, kabhee baahara | koee usase bolane vaalaa na tha| baara-baara siyaaraama ke kamare ke dvaara par jaakara kha.dee hoti; aur 'baiyaa-vaiyaa' pukaaratee ; par 'vaiyaa' koee javaaba na detaa tha!

sandhyaa samay munshee jee aakara nirmalaa se bole tumhaare paasa kuchha rupae hain ?

nirmalaa ne chaunka kar poochhaa-kyaa keejiegaa ?

munshee jee-main jo poochhata hoon usakaa javaaba do| nirmalaa -- kyaa aapako naheen maalooma hai? dene vaale to aapa hee hain|

munshee -- tumhaare paasa kuchha rupae hain yaa naheen? agar hon to mujhe de do, na hon to saafa javaaba do|

nirmalaa ne ab bhee saafa jabaava na diya| boli -- honge to ghar hee men na honge| main ne kaheen aur to naheen bheja die|

munshee jee baahara chale gae| vaha jaanate the ki -- nirmalaa ke paasa rupae hain| vaastava men the bhee| nirmalaa ne yaha bhee naheen kaha ki naheen hain, yaa main na doongee; par usakee baaton se prakata ho gaya ki vaha dena naheen chaahatee|

nau baje raat ko munshee jee ne aakara rukminee se kaha -- bahina, main zaraa baahara jaa raha hoo.c| meraa vistaraa bhungee se bandhavaa dena; aur traka men kuchha kapa.de rakhavaa kar banda kar dena|

rukminee bhojana bana rahi thi; boli -- bahoo to kamare men hai, kaha kyon naheen dete? kahaan jaane kaa iraadaa hai?

munshee jee -- main tumase kahata hoon; bahoo se kahana hota,to tumase kyon kahata? aaja tuma kyon khaanaa pakaa rahi ho?

rukminee -- kaun pakaave? bahoo ke sira men darda ho raha hai| aakhira is vakta kahaan jaa rahe ho? sabere na chale jana|

munshee jee -- isee tarah taalate-taalate to aaja teena din ho gae| idhara-udhara ghooma-dhaama kar dekhoon, shaayada kaheen siyaaraama kaa

rukminee -- to lautoge kab taka?

munshee jee -- kaha naheen sakata| haphtaaa bhara laga jaaya, maheenaa bhara laga jaaya; kyaa thikaanaa hai?

rukminee -- aaja kaun din hai? kisee pandita se poochha liya hai, yaatraa hai ki naheen?

munshee jee bhojana karane baithe| nirmalaa ko is vakta una par ba.dee dayaa aaee| usakaa saaraa krodha shaanta ho gaya| Kuda to na boli, bachchi ko jagaa kar chumakaaratee hui boli -- dekha,tere baaboo jee kahaan jaa rahe hain? poochha to|

bachchi ne dvaara se jhaanka kar poochhaa -- baaboo dee,tahaan daate ho?

bachchi ne vaheen se kha.de-kha.de kaha -- ama bee talenge|

munshee jee -- ba.deen doora jaate hain bachchi| tumhaare vaaste cheezen laavenge! yahaan kyon naheen aati?

bachchi muskaraa kar chhipa gaee; aur ek kshana men phir kiva.da se sira nikaala kar boli -- ama bee talenge|

munshee jee ne usee svara men kaha -- tuma ko nanee le talenge|

bachchi -- ama ko kyon naeen le taloge?

munshee jee -- tuma to hamaare paachha aati naheen ho| ladki thumakatee hui aakara pitaa kee goda men baitha gaee| tho.dee dera ke lie munshee jee usakee baala-kree.daa men apanee antarvedana bhoola gae|

bhojana karake munshee jee baahara chale gae| nirmalaa kha.dee taakatee rahi| kahana chaahatee thi-vyartha jaa rahe ho;par kaha na sakatee thi| kuchha rupae nikaala kar dene kaa vichaara karti thi;par de na sakatee thi|

rookminee ne karuna-soochaka netron se dekha;aur apane kamare men chalee gaee|

nirmalaa bachchi ko goda men lie socha rahi thi ki shaayada jaane ke pahale bachchi ko dekhane yaa mujhase milane ke lie aaven; par usakee aashaa viphala ho gaee| munshee jee ne vistara uthaayaa aur taange par jaa baithe|

usee vakta nirmalaa kaa kalejaa masosane lagaa| use aisaa jaana pada ki ab inase bhenta na hogee| vaha adheera hokara dvaara par aaee ki munshee jee ko roka le; par taangaa chala diya tha!
