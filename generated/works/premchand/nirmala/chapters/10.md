itne men adhyaksh ne akar kaha-maim to samjhata hum, ap inhen apne sath le jayam. gari hai hi, koi takliph na hogi. yaham achchhi tarah dekh-bhal na ho sakegi.

munshi ji-ham, aya to maim isi khyal se tha; lekin inki halat bahut hi najuk malum hoti hai. jara si asavdhani hone se sarsam ho jane ka dar hai.

adhyaksh-yaham se unhen le jane men to thodi si dikkat jarur hai. lekin yah to ap khud hi soch sakte haim ki ghar par jo aram mil sakta hai, vah yaham kisi tarah nahin mil sakta. iske atirikt kisi bimar larke ko yaham rakhna, niyam-viruddh bhi hai.

munshi ji-kahie to maim hedamastar se hukma le lum. mujh inko yaham se is halat men le jana kisi tarah munasib nahin. malum hota.
adhyaksh ne hedamastar ka nam suna, to samjhe ki yah mahashay mujhe dhamki de rahe haim. jara tinak kar bole-hedamastar niyam-viruddh koi bat nahin kar sakte. maim itni badi jimmedari kaise le sakta hum?

ab kya ho? kya ghar le jana hi parega? yaham rakhne ka to yah vahana tha ki le jane se bimari barh jane ki shanka hai. yaham se le jakar aspatal men thahrane ke lie koi bahana nahin hai. jo sunega vah yahi kahega ki doktar ki phis bachane ke lie larke ko aspatal phenk ae; par ab le jane ke siva
aur koi upay na tha. agar adhyaksh mahoday is vakt rishvat lene par taiyar ho jate, to shayad do-char sal ka tanakhvah le lete; lekin kayde ke paband logon men itni akal, itni chaturai kaham! agar is vakt munshi ji ko koi aadmi aisa unn sujha deta, jismen unhen NAmEmansaram ko ghar na le jana pade, to vah ajivan uska ehsan mante. sochne ka samay bhi nahin tha. adhyaksh mahoday shaitan ki tarah sir par savar the. majbur hokar munshi ji ne donon saison ko bulaya aur NAmEmansaram ko uthane lage. NAmEmansaram arddha-chetna ki hal men tha. chaunk kar bola-kya hai? kaun hai?

munshi ji-koi nahin hai; beta! maim tumhen dhar le chalna chahta hum; ao maim god men utha lum.

NAmEmansaram -mujhe ghar kyon le chalte haim? maim vaham nahin jaunga.
munshi--ji yaham to rah nahin sakte, niyam hi aisa hai.

NAmEmansaram --kuchh bhi ho, maim vaham na jaunga. mujhe aur kahim le chalie-kisi per ke niche, kisi jhopre men, jaham chahe rakhie; par ghar na le chalie.

adhyaksh ne munshi ji se kaha-ap in baton ka khyal na karen, yah to hosh men nahin haim.

NAmEmansaram -kaun hosh men nahin hai? maim hosh men nahin hum? kisi ko galiyam deta hum, dant katta hum? kyon hosh men nahin hum? mujhe yahim pada rahne dijie, jo kuchh hona hoga yahim hoga, agar aisa hi hai to mujhe aspatal le chalie. maim vaham pada rahunga. jina hoga jiunga, marna hoga marunga; lekin ghar kisi tarah bhi na jaunga.

yah jod pakad munshi ji phir adhyaksh se minnaten karne lage; lekin vah kayde ka pavand aadmi kuchh sunta hi na tha. agar chhut ki bimari hui aur kisi dusre larke ko chhut lag gai, to kaun uska jabavdeh hoga? is tark ke samne munshiji ki kanuni dalilen bhi mat ho gai.

akhir munshi ji ne NAmEmansaram se kaha-veta, tumhen ghar chalne se kyon inkar ho raha hai? vaham to sabhi tarah ka aram rahega. munshi ji ne kahne ko to yah bat kah di; lekin dar rahe the ki kahim sachmuch NAmEmansaram chalne par raji na ho jay. vah NAmEmansaram ko aspatal men rakhne ka koi vahana khoj rahe the; aur
uski sari jimmedari NAmEmansaram hi ke sir dalna chahte the. yah adhyaksh ke samne ki bat thi, yah is bat ki gavah de sakte the ki NAmEmansaram apni jidd se aspatal ja raha hai. munshi ji ka ismen leshamatra bhi dosh nahin hai.

NAmEmansaram ne bhalla kar kaha-nahin, nahin, nahin, sau bar nahin. maim ghar nahin jaunga. mujhe aspatal le chalie aur ghar ke sab admiyo ko mana kar dijie ki mujhe dekhne na aven. mujhe kuchh nahin hua hai, vilkul vimar nahin hum. ap sujhe chhod dijie, maim apne pamv se chal sakta hum.

vah uth khada hua aur unmatt ki tarah dvar ki or chala; lekin pair larkhara gae. yadi munshi ji ne sambhal na liya hota, to use badi chot ati. donon naukron ki madad se munshi ji use bagghi ke pas lae aur andar vitha diya.

gari aspatal ki or chali. vahi hua jo munshi ji. chahte the. is shok men bhi unka chitt santusht tha. larka apni chah se aspatal ja raha tha, kya yah is bat ka sabut nahin tha ki ghar se ise koi pyaar nahin hai? kya isse yah siddh nahin hota ki NAmEmansaram nirdosh hai? vah us par akaran hi bhram kar rahe the.

lekin jara hi der men is tushti ki jagah unke man men pachhtava ka bhav jagrat hua. vah apne jan-priy beta ko ghar na le jakar aspatal liye ja rahe the. unke bahut bada bhavan men unke
beta ke lie bhi jagah na thi, is hal men bhi, jabki uska jivan museebat men pada hua tha, kitni vidambana hai?

ek pal ke bad ekaek munshi ji ke man men saval utha--kahim NAmEmansaram unke bhavon ko tar to nahin gay? isilie to use ghar se naphrat nahin ho gai! agar aisa hai, to gazab ho jayga!
us anarth ki kalpana hi se munshi ji ke roen khade ho gae aur kaleja dhak-dhak karne laga. dil men ek dhakka sa laga. agar is jvar ka yahi vajah hai, to ishvar hi malik hai. is samay unki hal bahut bechara thi. vah ag jo unhomne apne thidure hue hathon ko senkne ke lie jalai thi, ab unke ghar men lagi ja rahi thi. is daya, shok, pashchattap aur
shanka se unka chitt ghabra utha. unke gupt rudan ki dhvani vahar nikal sakti, to sunne vale ro parte! unke amsu bahar nikal sakte, to unka tar badh jata! unhomne beta ke varn-hin mumh: ki or ek bar vatsalyapurn netron se dekha, dard se vikal hokar use chhati se laga liya; aur itna roe ki hichki bandh gai.

samne aspatal ka phatak dikhai de raha tha!
