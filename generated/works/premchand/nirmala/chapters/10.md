# Nirmala — Adhyay 10

**Munshi Premchand**

dasavaan parichchhed

mansaaraam do din tak gaharee fikr mein doobaa rahaa. baar-baar apanee maataa ki yaad aatee; n khana achchha lagataa, n padhane hee mein jee lagataa. usakee kaayaa-palat see ho gaee. do din gujar gae aur chhaatraalay mein rahate hue bhi usane vah kaam n kiyaa, jo skool ke maastaron ne ghar se kar laane ko diyaa thaa. nateejaa-svaroop use bainych par khadaa rahanaa padaa. jo baat kabhee n huee thi, vah aaj ho gaee! yah asahy beijjatee bhi use sahanaa padaa!

teesare din vah inheen phikraon mein magn huaa apane man ko samajhaa rahaa tha — kyaa sansaar mein akele meree hee maataa maree hai. vimaataaen to sabhee isee svabhaav ki hotee hain. mere saath koee naee baat nahin ho rahee hai. ab mujhe mardon ki bhaanti dvigun parimehanat se apanaa kaam karanaa chahiye; jaise maataa-pitaa raajee rahen, vaise unhen raajee rakhanaa chaahie. is saal agar chhaatravritti mil gaee, to mujhe ghar se kuchh lene ki zaroorat hee n rahegee. kitane hee ladake apane hee bal par badee-badee upaadhiyaan milaa kar lete hain. baadhaaon par vijay paanaa aur avasar dekh kar kaam karanaa hee insaan ka farz hai. kismat ke naam ko rone aur kosane se kyaa hogaa?

itane mein jiyaaraam aakar khadaa ho gayaa.

mansaaraam ne poochhaa-ghar ka kyaa haal hai jiyaa? naee ammaan jee to bahut khush honggee?

jiyaa0-unake man ka haal to main nahin jaanataa; lekin jav se tum aae ho, unhonne ek joon bhi khana nahin khaayaa. jab dekho tab royaa hee karatee hain. jab baaboo jee aate hain, tab alabattaa hansane lagatee hain. tum chale aae, to main ne bhi shaam ko apanee kitaaben sambhaalee. yaheen tumhaare saath rahanaa chaahataa thaa. bhungggee chudailane jaakar ammaan jee se kah diyaa. baaboo jee baithe the, unake saamane hee ammaan jee ne aakar meree kitaaben chheen lee; aur rokar bolontum bhi chale jaaoge, to is ghar mein kaun rahegaa? agar mere 'vajah tum log ghar chhod-chhod kar bhaage jaa rahe ho, to lo main hee kaheen chalee jaatee hoon! main to bhallaayaa huaa tha hee, wahan baaboo jee bhi n the; bigad kar bolaa-aap kyon kaheen chalee jaaenggee! aapakaa to ghar hai, aap aaraam se rahie. gair to hamee log hain. ham n rahengge, tab to aapko aaraam hee aaraam rahegaa.

mansaaraam-tumane khoob kahaa, bahut hee achchha kahaa. is par aur bhi bhalaaee honggee aur jaakar baaboo jee se shikaayat ki hogee? jiyaaraam-nahin, yah kuchh nahin huaa. bechaaree zameen par vaith kar rone lagee, mujhe bhi taras aa gaee. main bhi ro padaa, tab unhonne aanchal se mere aansoo ponchhe aur boleen-jiyaa! main Bhagwan ko saakshee dekar kahatee hoon ki mainne tumhaare bhaiyaa ke vishay mein tumhaare vaaboo jee se ek shabd bhi nahin kahaa. mere kismat mein kalanggk likhaa huaa hai, vahee bhog rahee hoon. phir aur n jaane kyaa-kyaa kahaa, jo meree samajh mein nahin kamaaeeaa. kuchh baaboo jee ki baat thee.

mansaaraam ne udvignataa se poochhaa-baaboo jee ke vishay mein kyaa kahaa, kuchh yaad hai?

jiyaaraam-baaten to bhaee mujhe yaad nahin aatee. meree maimoree(Memory) kaun badee tej hai; lekin unakee baaton ka matalab kuchh aisaa maaloom hotaa tha ki unhen baaboo jee ko khush rakhane ke lie yah svog bharanaa pad rahaa hai. n jaane dharm-adharm ki kaisee baaten karatee theen, jo main bilakul n samajh sakaa. mujhe to ab isakaa bharosaa aa gayaa hai ki unakee ichchhaa tumhe yahan bhejane ki n thee.

mansaaraam-tum in chaalon ka matalab nahin samajh sakate. ye badee gaharee chaalen hain.

jiyaaraam-tumhaaree samajh mein honggee, meree samajh mein to nahin hain.
mansaaraam-jab tum jyometaree (Geometry) nahin samajh sakate, to in baaton ko kyaa samajh sakoge! us raat ko jab mujhe khana khaane ke lie bulaane aaee theen; aur main unake jor dekar kahanaa par jaane ko taiyaar bhi ho gayaa tha, us vakt baaboo jee ko dekhate hee unhonne jo kaindaa badalaa vah kyaa main kabhee bhool sakataa hoon?

jiyaaraam- yahee baat meree samajh mein bhi nahin aatee. abhee kal hee main yahan se gayaa to lagee tumhaaraa haal poochhane. mainne kahaa-vah to kahate the ki ab kabhee is ghar mein kadam n rakhoonggaa. mainne kuchh jhooth to kahaa nahin, tumane mujhase kahaa hee thaa. itanaa sunanaa tha ki phoot-phoot kar rone lagee. main dil mein bahut pachhataayaa ki kahaan se kahaan mainne yah baat kah dee. baar-baar yahee kahatee thi, kyaa vah mere vajah ghar chhod dengge? mujhase itane naaraaj hain? chale gae
aur mujhase mile tak nahin! khana taiyaar tha, khaane tak nahin aae! haay! main kyaa bataaoon, kis vipatti mein hoon! itane mein baaboo jee aa gae. bas, turant aankhen ponchh kar muskaraatee huee unake paas chalee gaee. yah baat meree samajh mein nahin aatee. aaj mujhase badee minnat ki ki unako saath lete aanaa. aaj main tumhe kheench le chaloonggaa. do din mein vah kitanee dubalee ho gaee hain, tumhe yah dekh kar un par taras aaegee. to chaloge n?

mansaaraam ne kuchh jabaab n diyaa. usake pair kaamp rahe the. jiyaaraam to haajiree ki ghantee sun kar bhaagaa; par vah bainych par let gayaa aur itanee lambee saans lee, maano bahut der se usane saans nahin lee hai. usake munh se dussah dard mein doobe hue yah shabd nikale-haay Bhagwan! is naam ke sivaa use ab apanaa zindagi niraadhaar maaloom hotaa thaa. is ek uchchhavaas mein kitanaa nairaashy, kitanee samadard, kitanee taras, kitanee bechaaragee-dua bharee huee thi, isakaa kaun anumaan kar sakataa hai? ab saaraa rahasy usakee samajh mein aa rahaa tha; aur baar-baar usakaa peedit dil aartanaad kar rahaa tha-haay Bhagwan! itanaa ghor kalanggk!!

kyaa zindagi mein isase badee vipatti ki khayaal ki jaa sakatee hai? kyaa sansaar mein isase ghoratar neechataa ki khayaal ho sakatee hai? aaj tak kisee pitaa ne apane putr par itanaa nirday kalanggkan lagaayaa hogaa! jisake charitr ki sabhee prashansaa karate the, jo any javaan aadameeon ke lie aadarsh samajhaa jaataa tha, jisane kabhee apavitr soch ko apane paas nahin phatakane diyaa, usee par yah ghoratam kalanggk! mansaaraam ko aisaa maaloom huaa, maano usakaa dil phataa jaataa hai!

doosaree ghantee bhi vaj gaee. ladake apane-apane kamaron mein gae; par mansaaraam hathelee par haath rakkhe animesh aangkhenon se zameen ki
or dekh rahaa tha, maano usakaa sarvasv jal-magn ho gayaa ho; maano vah kisee ko munh n dikhaa sakataa ho. skool mein gair-haajiree ho jaayagee, jurmaanaa ho jaayagaa, isakee use fikr nahin! jab usakaa sarvasv lut gayaa, to ab in chhotee-chhotee baaton ka kyaa dar? itanaa badaa kalanggk lagane par bhi agar jeetaa rahoon, to mere jeene ko dhikkaar hai!

usee shokaatirek ki dashaa mein vah chillaa padaa-maataa jee, tum kahaan ho? tumhaaraa betaa, jis par tum praan detee theen-jise tum apane zindagi ka aadhaar samajhatee theen, aaj ghor sanggkat mein hai! usee ka pitaa usakee gardan par chhuree pher rahaa hai! haay, tum kahaan ho!!

mansaaraam phir shaant chitt se sochane lagaa-mujh par yah shak kyon ho rahaa hai? isakaa kyaa vajah hai? mujhamen aisee, kaun see baat unhonne dekhee jisase unhen yah shak huaa. vah mere pitaa hain; mere shatru nahin hain, jo anaayaas hee mujh par jurm lagaane baith jaayan. zaroor unhonne koee n koee baat dekhee ya sunee hai. unakaa mujh par kitanaa pyaar tha! mere bagair khana karane n jaate the, vahee mere shatru ho jaayen, yah baat avajah nahin ho sakatee!

achchha is shak ka beejaaropan kis din huaa? mujhe bordinggg haaus mein thaharaane ko baat to peechhe ki hai. jis din raat ko vah mere kamare mein aakar meree pareeksh lene lage the, usee din unakee tyoriyaan badalee huee thee. us din aisee kaun see baat huee, jo unhen apriy lagee ho! main naee ammaan se kuchh khaane ko maanggane gayaa thaa. baaboo jee us vakt wahan baithe thaa. haan, ab yaad aataa hai usee vakt. unakaa cheharaa tamatamaa gayaa thaa. usee din se naee ammaan ne mujhase padhanaa chhod diyaa. agar main jaanataa ki meraa ghar mein aanaa-jaanaa, ammaan jee se kuchh kahanaa-sunanaa aur unhen padhaanaa-likhaanaa pitaa jee ko buraa lagataa hai, to aaj kyon yah naubat aatee? aur naee ammaan! un par kyaa beet rahee hogee? mansaaraam ne ab tak Nirmala ki or dhyaan hee nahin diyaa thaa. Nirmala ka dhyaan aate hee usake roen khade ho gae! haay, unakaa saral pyaarasheel dil yah aaghaat kaise sah sakegaa? aah! main kitane bhram mein tha? main unake pyaar ko kaushal samajhataa tha! mujhe kyaa maaloom tha ki unhen pitaa jee ka bhram shaant karane ke lie mere prati itanaa katu vyavahaar karanaa padataa hai. aah! mainne un par kitanaa naainsaaphee kiyaa hai. unakee dashaa to mujhase bhi kharaab ho rahee hogee. main to yahan chalaa kamaaeeaa, magar vah kahaan jaaenggee. jiyaa kahataa tha, unhonne do din se khana nahin kiyaa! har dam royaa karatee hain! kaise jaakar samajhaaoon? vah is abhaage ke peechhe kyon apane sir yah vipatti le rahee hain! vah kyon baar-baar meraa haal poochhatee hain? kyon baar-baar mujhe bulaatee hai? kaise kah doon ki maataa mujhe tumase jaraa bhi shikaayat nahin, meraa dil tumhaaree taraph se saaph hai.

vah ab bhi baithee ro rahee hogee! kitanaa badaa anarth hai? baaboo jee ko yah kyaa ho gayaa hai? kyaa iseelie shaadee kiyaa tha? ek ladakee ki hatyaa karane hee ke lie use laae the! is komal pushp ko masal daalane hee ke lie todaa tha?

unakaa uddhaar kaise hogaa? us niraparaadhinee ka munh kaise ujjval hogaa? unhe keval mere saath pyaar ka vyavahaar karane ke lie yah sajaa diyaa jaa rahaa hai! unakee sajjanataa ka unhen yah upahaar mil rahaa hai! main unhe is prakaar nirday aaghaat sahate dekh kar baithaa rahoonggaa! apanee maan-bachaav ke lie n sahee unakee
aatm-bachaav ke lie in praanon ko balidaan karanaa padegaa! isake sivaay uddhaar ka aur koee raastaa naheen. aah! dil mein kaise kaise aramaan the. ve sab khaak mein milaa dene hongge. ek satee par shak kiyaa jaa rahaa hai; aur mere vajah! mujhe apane praanon se usakee bachaav karanee hogee, yahee meraa farz hai. isee mein sachchee veerataa hai! maataa, main apane rakt se is kaalimaa ko dho doonggaa. isee mein meraa aur tumhaaraa donon ka kalyaan hai!

vah din bhar inheen soch mein doobaa rahaa. shaam ko usake donon bhaaee aakar ghar chalane ke lie jor dekar kahanaa karane lage.

siyaaraam-chalate kyon nahin? mere bhaiyaa jee, chale chalo n!

mansaaraam-mujhe phurasat nahin hai ki tumhaare kahane se chalaa chaloon.

jiyaa0-aakhir kal to itavaar hee hai!

mansaa0-itavaar ko bhi kaam hai.

jiyaa-achchha, kal aaoge n?

mansaa0-nahin, kal mujhe ek maich mein jaanaa hai.

siyaa0-ammaan jee moongg ke laddoo banaa rahee hain. n chaloge to ek bhi n paaoge. ham-tum mil ke khaa jaayangge;'jiyaa, inhen n dengge.

jiyaa0-bhaiyaa, agar tum kal n gae, to shaayad ammaan jee yaheen chalee kamaaeeen. mansaa-sach! nahin, aisaa kyon karenggee. yahan aaee, to badee pareshaanee hogee. tum kah denaa, vah kaheen maich dekhane gae hain.

jiyaa-main jhooth kyo bolane lagaa. main kah doonggaa vah munh phulaae baithe the. dekh lenaa unhen saath laataa hoon ki nahin!

siyaa0-ham kah dengge ki aaj padhane nahin gae. pade-pade sote rahe. mansaaraam ne in dooton se kal aane ka vaadaa karake galaa chhudaayaa. jab donon chale gae, to phir fikr mein doobaa. raat bhar use karavat badalate gujaree. chhattee ka din bhi baithe hee baithe kat gayaa, use din bhar shanggkaa hotee rahee ki kaheen ammaan jee sachamuch n chalee aaven. kisee gaadee ki khadakhadaahat sunataa, to usakaa kalejaa dhakadhak karane lagataa. kaheen aa to nahin gaee!

chhaatraalay mein ek chhotaa-saa aushadhaalay thaa. ek doktar saahav sandhyaa samay ek ghante ke lie aa jaayaa karate the. agar koee ladakaa beemaar hotaa, to use davaa dete. aaj vah aae to mansaaraam kuchh sochataa huaa unake paas jaakar khadaa ho gayaa. vah mansaaraam ko achchhi tarah jaanate the. use dekh kar hairaanee se vole-yah tumhaaree kyaa haalat hai jo? tum to maano gale jaa rahe ho. kaheen vaajaar ka chaskaa to nahin pad gayaa? aakhir tumhe huaa kyaa? jaraa yahan to aao!

mansaaraam ne muskaraa kar kahaa-mujhe jindagee ka beemaaree hai. aapake paas isakee bhi koee davaa hai? doktar-main tumhaaree pareeksh karanaa chaahataa hoon. tumhaaree to soorat hee badal gaee jee, pahachaane bhi nahin jaate!

yah kah kar unhonne mansaaraam ka haath pakad liyaa aur chhaatee, peeth, aankhen, jeebh sab baaree-baaree se dekhee. tab chintit hokar bole-vakeel Sahab se main aaj hee miloonggaa. tumhe thaaisis ho rahaa hai. saare lapal usee ke hain.

mansaaraam ne badee utsukataa se poochhaa-bhalaa kitane dinon mein kaam tamaam ho jaayagaa; doktar Sahab?

doktar-kaisee baaten karate ho jee! main vakeel Sahab se mil kar tumhe kisee pahaadee jagah bhejane ki salaah doonggaa. Bhagwan ne chaahaa to tum bahut jald achchhe ho jaaoge. beemaaree abhee pahale hee stej mein hai.

mansaaraam-tab to abhee saal-do saal ki der maaloom hotee hai. main to itanaa intajaar nahin kar sakataa. sunie, mujhe thaaisisavaaisis kuchh nahin hai n koee doosaree shikaayat hee hai. aap baaboojee ko naahak taradut mein n daaliegaa. is vakt mere sir mein dard hai, koee davaa deejie. koee aisee davaa ho, jisase need bhi aa jaaya. mujhe do raat se neend nahin aatee.

doktar Sahab ne jahareelee davaaiyon ki aalamaaree kholee aur ek sheeshee mein theedee see davaa nikaal kar mansaaraam ko dee. mansaaraam ne poochhaa- yah to koee jahar hai? bhalaa koee ise peele to mar jaay? doktar-nahin, mar to n jaay; par sir mein chakar jalar aa jaaya.

mansaa0-koee aisee davaa bhi isamen hai, jise peet hee praan nikal jaayen?

doktar-aisee ek-do nahin, kitanee hee davaaen hain. yah jo sheeshee dekh rahe ho, isakee ek boond bhi pet mein chalee jaay no jaan n vache aanan-phaanan mein maut ho jaaya.

mansaa0-kyon doktar Sahab, jo log jahar khaa lete hain, unhen badee takaleeph hotee hogee?

doktar-sabhee jaharon se takaleeph nahin hotee. vaaj to aise hain ki peete hee aadmi thandaa ho jaataa hai. yah sheeshee isee kism ki hai. ise peete hee aadmi vehosh ho jaataa hai, phir use hosh nahin aataa.

mansaaraam ne sochaa-tab to praan denaa bahut aasaan hai. phir log kyon itanaa darate hain? yah sheeshee kaise milegee? agar davaa ka naam poochh kar shahar ke kisee davaa-karosh se lenaa chaahoon, to vah kabhee n degaa. unh, isake milane mein koee dikkat naheen. yah to maaloom ho gayaa ki praanon ka ant badee aasaanee se kiyaa jaa sakataa hai. mansaaraam itanaa khush huaa, maano koee inaam paa gayaa ho. usake dil par se ek vojh saa hat gayaa. fikr ki megh-rakam, jo sir par mandalaa rahee thi, chhinn-bhinn ho gaee. maheenon ke baad aaj use man mein ek sphoorti ka tajurbaa huaa. kaee ladake thiyetar dekhane jaa rahe the, nireekshk se aagyaa le lee thee. mansaaraam bhi unake saath thiyetar dekhane chalaa gayaa. aisaa khush tha, maano usase jyaadaa sukhee jeev sansaar mein koee nahin hai. thiyetar mein nakal dekh kar to vah hansate-hansate lot gayaa. baarabaar taaliyaan bajaane aur 'vansamor!' ki haangk lagaane mein sabase pahalaa nambar usee ka tha. gaanaa sun kar vah mast ho jaataa tha,
aur 'oho ho!' karake chillaa uthataa thaa. darshakon ki nigaahen baar-baar. usakee taraph uth jaatee theen. thiyetar ke paatr bhi usee ki or taakate the; aur yah jaanane ko utsuk the ki kaun mahaashay itane rasik aur bhaavuk hain. usake mitron ko usakee uchchhanggkhalataa par hairaanee ho rahaa thaa. vah bahut hee shaantachitt, seeriyas svabhaav ka javaan aadmi thaa. aaj vah kyon itanaa haasyasheel ho gayaa hai, kyon usake vinod ka vaaraapaar nahin hai?

do baje raat ko thiyetar se lautane par bhi usakaa haasyonmaad kam nahin huaa. usane ek ladake ki chaarapaaee ulat dee, kaee ladakon ke kamaron ke dvaar baahar se band kar die; aur unhen bheetar se khatakhat karate sun kar hansataa rahaa. yahan tak ki chhaatraalay ke adhyaksh mahoday ki neend bhi shor-gul sun kar khul gaee;
aur unhonne mansaaraam ki sharaarat par khed saamane kiyaa. kaun jaanataa hai ki usake antastal mein kitanee bheeshan kraanti ho rahee hai? shak ke nirday aaghaat ne usakee. sharm aur apanee izzat ka ehasaas ko kuchal daalaa hai! use beijjatee aur nafrat ka lesh maatr bhi dar nahaa hai. yah vinod nahin-usakee aatmaa ka karun vilaap hai. jab aur sab ladake so gae, to vah bhi chaarapaaee par letaa, lekin use neend nahin aaee. ek pal ke baad vah uth baithaa; aur apanee saaree pustaken baandh kar sandook mein rakh dee. jab maranaa hee hai to padh kar kyaa hogaa? jis zindagi mein aisee-aisee vaadhaaen hain aisee-aisee yaatanaaen hain, usase maut kaheen achchhi!

yahee sochate-sochate tadakaa ho gayaa. teen raat se vah ek pal bhi n soyaa thaa. is vakt vah uthaa to usake pair thar-thar kop rahe the; aur sir mein chakkar saa aa rahaa thaa. aankhen jal rahee theen; aur shareer ke saare anggg shithil ho rahe the. din chadhataa jaataa tha; aur usamen itanee taakat bhi n thi ki uth kar munh-haath dho daale. ekaaek usane bhugee ko roomaal mein kuchh lie hue ek kahaar ke saath aate dekhaa. usakaa kalejaa san ho gayaa. haay Bhagwan! vah aa gaee! ab kyaa hogaa? bhungggee akele nahin aaee hogee, vagdhee zaroor baahar khadee hogee. kahaan to usase uthaa n jaataa thaa. kahaan bhugee ko dekhate hee daudaa aur ghavaraaee huee aavaaj mein bolaa-ammaan jee bhi aaee hain kyaa re? jab maaloom huaa ki ammaan jee nahin aaee, tab usakaa chitt shaant huyaa. bhungggee ne kahaa-bhaiyaa! tum kal gae nahin, vahoo jee tumhaaree raah dekhatee rah gaee. unase kyon roothe ho bhaiyaa? vah to kahatee hain, mainne unakee kuchh bhi shikaayat nahin ki hai. mujhase aaj rokar kahane lagee-unake paas yah mithaaee letee jaa aur kahanaa mere vajah ghar kyon chhod diyaa hai? kahaan rakh doonn yah thaalee?

mansaaraam ne rukhaaee se kahaa-thaalee apane sir par patak de, chudail wahan se chalee hai mithaaee lekar! khabaradaar, jo phir kamee idhar aaee. saugaat lekar chalee hain! jaakar kah denaa mujhe unakee mithaaee nahin chahiye! jaakar kah denaa tumhaaraa ghar hai, tum raho; wahan ve bade aaraam se hain. khoob khaate aur mauj karate hain. sunatee hai, baaboo jee ke munh par kahanaa, samajh gaee? mujhe kisee ka dar nahin hai; aur jo karanaa chaahen kar daalen, jisase dil mein koee aramaan n rah jaay. kahen to ilaahaabaad, lakhanaoo, kalakattaa chalaa jaaoon. mere lie jaise banaaras vaise doosaraa shahar! yahan kyaa rakkhaa hai.

bhungggee-bhaiyaa, mithaaee rakh lo, nahin ro-rokar mar jaayenggee. sachan maano, rokar mar jaayanggee.

mansaaraam ne aansuon ke uthate hue veg ko davaa kar kahaamar jaayanggee, meree balaa se! kaun mujhe badaa sukh de diyaa hai, jisake lie pachhataaoon. meraa to unhonne sarvanaash kar diyaa. kah denaa mere paas koee sandeshaa n bhejen, kuchh jaroorat nahin!

bhungggee-bhaiyaa, tum to kahate ho yahan khoob khaataa hoon aur mauj karataa hoon; magar deh to aadhee bhi nahin rahee. jaise aae the usake aadhe bhi nahin rahe!

mansaaraam-yah teree aangkhon ka pher hai, dekhanaa do-chaar din mein mutaa kar kolhoo ho jaataa hoon ki naheen. unase yah bhi kah denaa ki ronaa-dhonaa band karen. jo mainne sunaa ki rotee hain aur khana nahin khaatee, to mujhase buraa koee naheen. mujhe ghar se nikaalaa hai, to ab chain se rahen. chalee hain pyaar dikhaane! main aise triyaa-charitr vahut padhe vaithaa hoon.

bhungggee chalee gaee. mansaaraam ko usase baaten karate hee karate kuchh thand maaloom hone lagee thee. yah abhinay karane ke lie use apane manobhaavon ko jitanaa davaanaa padaa tha, vah usake lie asaadhy thaa. usakaa apanee izzat ka ehasaas use is kutil vyaapaar ka jald se jald ant kar dene ke lie vaadhy kar rahaa tha; par isakaa nateejaa kyaa hogaa? Nirmala kyaa yah aaghaat sah sakegee? ab tak vah maut-khayaal karate samay kisee any praanee ka soch n karataa tha, par aaj ekaaek use gyaat huaa ki mere zindagi ke saath ek aur praanee ka zindagi-sootr bhi bandhaa huaa hai. Nirmala yahee samajhegee ki meree nishthurataa hee ne inakee jaan lee. yah samajh kar usakaa komal dil kyaa phat n jaaegaa? usakaa zindagi to ab bhi sanggkat mein hai. shak ke sakht paje mein phansee huee avalaa kyaa apane ko hatyaarinee samajh kar bahut din zinda rah sakatee hai?

mansaaraam ne chaarapaaee par let kar lihaaph odh liyaa, phir bhi maare sardee se kalejaa kop rahaa tha. thodee hee der mein use jor se jvar chadh kamaaeeaa-vah behosh ho gayaa. is achet dashaa mein use bhaanti-bhaanti ke svapn dikhaaee dene lage. thodee der ke baad chaungk padataa-aankhen khul jaatee; phir behosh ho jaataa.

sahasaa vakeel Sahab ki aavaaj sun kar vah chaungk padaa. haan, vakeel Sahab hee ki aavaaj thee. usane lihaaph phengk diyaa
aur chaarapaaee se utar kar neeche khadaa ho gayaa. usake man mein ek aaveg huaa ki isee vakt inake saamane praan de doon. use aisaa maaloom huaa ki main mar jaaoon, to inhen sachchee khushi hogee. shaayad iseelie yah dekhane aae hain ki mere marane mein kitanee der hai. vakeel Sahab ne usakaa haath pakad liyaa, jisamen vah gir n pade aur poochhaa-kaisee tabeeyat hai lalloo, lete kyon n rahe? let jaao, let jaao, tum khade kyon ho gae?

mansaaraam-meree tabeeyat to bahut achchhi hai. aapko vyarth hee takaleeph huaa.

munshee jee ne kuchh javaab n diyaa. ladake ki dashaa dekh kar unakee aangkhon se aansoo nikal aae. vah hrisht-pusht bachcha, jise dekh kar chitt khush ho jaataa tha, ab sookh kar kaantaa ho gayaa thaa. paanch-chhah din mein hee vah itanaa dubalaa ho gayaa tha ki use pahachaananaa kathin thaa. munshee jee ne use aahistaa se chaarapaaee par litaa diyaa; aur lihaaph achchhi tarah odhaa kar sochane lage ki ab kyaa karanaa chaahie. kaheen ladakaa haath se to n jaayagaa? yah khyaal karake vah shok se vihval ho gae; aur stool par baith kar phoot-phoot kar rone lage. mansaaraam bhi lihaaph mein munh lapete ro rahaa thaa. abhee thode hee dinon pahale use dekh kar pitaa ka dil garv se phool uthataa tha lekin aaj use is daarun dashaa mein dekh kar bhi vah soch rahaa hai ki ise ghar le chaloon ya naheen. kyaa yahan davaa nahin ho sakatee? main yahan chaubeeson ghante baithaa rahoonggaa. doktar Sahab yahan hain hee, koee dikkat n hogee. ghar le chalane mein unhen baadhaaen hee baadhaaen dikhaaee detee theen; sabase badhaa dar yah tha ki wahan Nirmala isake paas haradam vaithee rahegee; aur main manaa n kar sakoonggaa yah unake lie asahy thaa.

itane mein adhyaksh ne aakar kahaa-main to samajhataa hoon, aap inhen apane saath le jaayan. gaadee hai hee, koee takaleeph n hogee. yahan achchhi tarah dekh-bhaal n ho sakegee.

munshee jee-haan, kamaaeeaa to main isee khyaal se tha; lekin inakee haalat bahut hee naajuk maaloom hotee hai. jaraa see asaavadhaanee hone se sarasaam ho jaane ka dar hai.

adhyaksh-yahan se unhen le jaane mein to thodee see dikkat jaroor hai. lekin yah to aap khud hee soch sakate hain ki ghar par jo aaraam mil sakataa hai, vah yahan kisee tarah nahin mil sakataa. isake atirikt kisee beemaar ladake ko yahan rakhanaa, niyam-viruddh bhi hai.

'munshee jee-kahie to main hedamaastar se aagyaa le loon. mujh inako yahan se is haalat mein le jaanaa kisee tarah munaasib nahin. maaloom hotaa. adhyaksh ne hedamaastar ka naam sunaa, to samajhe ki yah mahaashay mujhe dhamakee de rahe hain. jaraa tinak kar bole-hedamaastar niyam-viruddh koee baat nahin kar sakate. main itanee badee jimmedaaree kaise le sakataa hoon?

ab kyaa ho? kyaa ghar le jaanaa hee padegaa? yahan rakhane ka to yah vahaanaa tha ki le jaane se beemaaree badh jaane ki shanggkaa hai. yahan se le jaakar aspataal mein thaharaane ke lie koee bahaanaa nahin hai. jo sunegaa vah yahee kahegaa ki doktar ki phees bachaane ke lie ladake ko aspataal phengk aae; par ab le jaane ke sivaa
aur koee raastaa n thaa. agar adhyaksh mahoday is vakt rishvat lene par taiyaar ho jaate, to shaayad do-chaar saal ka vetan le lete; lekin kaayade ke paaband logon mein itanee buddhi, itanee chaturaaee kahaan! agar is vakt munshee jee ko koee aadmi aisaa unn sujhaa detaa, jisamen unhen mansaaraam ko ghar n le jaanaa pade, to vah aajindagee usakaa ehasaan maanate. sochane ka samay bhi nahin thaa. adhyaksh mahoday shaitaan ki tarah sir par savaar the. vivash hokar munshee jee ne donon saeeson ko bulaayaa aur mansaaraam ko uthaane lage. mansaaraam arddh-chetanaa ki dashaa mein thaa. chaungk kar bolaa-kyaa hai? kaun hai?

munshee jee-koee nahin hai; betaa! main tumhe dhar le chalanaa chaahataa hoon; aao main god mein uthaa loon.

mansaaraam-mujhe ghar kyon le chalate hain? main wahan nahin jaaoonggaa. munshee--jee yahan to rah nahin sakate, niyam hee aisaa hai.

mansaaraam--kuchh bhi ho, main wahan n jaaoonggaa. mujhe aur kaheen le chalie-kisee ped ke neeche, kisee jhopade mein, jahaan chaahe rakhie; par ghar n le chalie.

adhyaksh ne munshee jee se kahaa-aap in baaton ka khyaal n karen, yah to hosh mein nahin hain.

mansaaraam-kaun hosh mein nahin hai? main hosh mein nahin hoon? kisee ko gaaliyaan detaa hoon, daant kaatataa hoon? kyon hosh mein nahin hoon? mujhe yaheen padaa rahane deejie, jo kuchh honaa hogaa yaheen hogaa, agar aisaa hee hai to mujhe aspataal le chalie. main wahan padaa rahoonggaa. jeenaa hogaa jioonggaa, maranaa hogaa maroonggaa; lekin ghar kisee tarah bhi n jaaoonggaa.

yah jor paakar munshee jee phir adhyaksh se minnaten karane lage; lekin vah kaayade ka paavand aadmi kuchh sunataa hee n thaa. agar chhoot ki beemaaree huee aur kisee doosare ladake ko chhoot lag gaee, to kaun usakaa jabaavadeh hogaa? is tark ke saamane munsheejee ki kaanoonee daleelen bhi maat ho gaee.

mansaaraam ne bhallaa kar kahaa-nahin, nahin, nahin, sau baar nahin. main ghar nahin jaaoonggaa. mujhe aspataal le chalie aur ghar ke sab aadamiyon ko manaa kar deejie ki mujhe dekhane n aaven. mujhe kuchh nahin huaa hai, vilakul veemaar nahin hoon. aap sujhe chhod deejie, main apane paanv se chal sakataa hoon.

vah uth khadaa huaa aur unmatt ki bhaanti dvaar ki or chalaa; lekin pair ladakhadaa gae. yadi munshee jee ne sambhaal n liyaa hotaa, to use badee chot aatee. donon naukaron ki madad se munshee jee use bagghee ke paas laae aur andar vithaa diyaa.

gaadee aspataal ki or chalee. vahee huaa jo munshee jee. chaahate the. is shok mein bhi unakaa chitt santusht thaa. ladakaa apanee ichchhaa se aspataal jaa rahaa tha, kyaa yah is baat ka saboot nahin tha ki ghar se ise koee pyaar nahin hai? kyaa isase yah siddh nahin hotaa ki mansaaraam nirdosh hai? vah us par avajah hee bhram kar rahe the.

lekin jaraa hee der mein is tushti ki jagah unake man mein glaani ka bhaav jaagrat huaa. vah apane praan-priy putr ko ghar n le jaakar aspataal liye jaa rahe the. unake bahut badaa bhavan mein unake putr ke lie bhi jagah n thi, is dashaa mein bhi, jabaki usakaa zindagi sanggkat mein padaa huaa tha, kitanee vidambanaa hai?

ek pal ke baad ekaaek munshee jee ke man mein savaal uthaa--kaheen mansaaraam unake bhaavon ko taad to nahin gay? iseelie to use ghar se nafrat nahin ho gaee! agar aisaa hai, to gajab ho jaayagaa!
us anarth ki khayaal hee se munshee jee ke roen khade ho gae aur kalejaa dhak-dhak karane lagaa. dil mein ek dhakkaa saa lagaa. agar is jvar ka yahee vajah hai, to Bhagwan hee maalik hai. is samay unakee dashaa bahut dayaneey thee. vah aag jo unhonne apane thidure hue haathon ko sengkane ke lie jalaaee thi, ab unake 'ghar mein lagee jaa rahee thee. is taras, shok, baadataap aur
shanggkaa se unakaa chitt ghabaraa uthaa. unake gupt rudan ki dhvani vaahar nikal sakatee, to sunane vaale ro padate! unake aansoo baahar nikal sakate, to unakaa taar badh jaataa! unhonne putr ke varn-heen munh: ki or ek baar vaatsalyapoorn aangkhenon se dekhaa, dard se vikal hokar use chhaatee se lagaa liyaa; aur itanaa roe ki hichakee bandh gaee.
