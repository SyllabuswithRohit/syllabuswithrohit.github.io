# Nirmala — Adhyay 10

**Munshi Premchand**

dasavaan parichchheda

mansaaraama do din taka gaharee chintaa men doobaa raha| baara-baara apanee maataa kee yaada aati; na khaanaa achchha lagata, na pa.dhane hee men jee lagata| usakee kaayaa-palata see ho gaee| do din guzara gae aur chhaatraalaya men rahate hue bhee usane vaha kaama na kiya, jo skoola ke maastaron ne ghar se kar laane ko diya tha| parinaama-svaroopa use baincha par kha.daa rahana pada| jo baat kabhee na hui thi, vaha aaja ho gaee! yaha asahya apamaana bhee use sahana pa.da!

teesare din vaha inheen chintaaon men magna hua apane mann ko samajhaa raha tha—kyaa sansaara men akele meree hee maataa maree hai| vimaataaen to sabhee isee svabhaava kee hoti hain| mere saatha koee naee baat naheen ho rahi hai| ab mujhe purushon kee bhaanti dviguna parishrama se apana kaama karana chaahie; jaise maataa-pitaa raazee rahen, vaise unhen raazee rakhana chaahie| is saala agar chhaatravritti mila gaee, to mujhe ghar se kuchha lene kee zaroorat hee na rahegee| kitane hee ladke apane hee bala par ba.dee-ba.dee upaadhiyaan praapta kar lete hain| baadhaaon par vijaya paanaa aur avasara dekha kar kaama karana hee manushya kaa kartavya hai| bhaagya ke naama ko rone aur kosane se kyaa hoga?

itane men jiyaaraama aakara kha.daa ho gaya|

mansaaraama ne poochhaa-ghar kaa kyaa haala hai jiya? naee ammaan jee to bahut prasanna hongee?

jiyaa0-unake mann kaa haala to main naheen jaanata; lekin java se tuma aae ho, unhonne ek joona bhee khaanaa naheen khaayaa| jab dekho tab royaa hee karti hain| jab baaboo jee aate hain, tab alabattaa hansane lagatee hain| tuma chale aae, to main ne bhee shaama ko apanee kitaaben sanbhaalee| yaheen tumhaare saatha rahana chaahata tha| bhungee chudailane jaakara ammaan jee se kaha diya| baaboo jee baithe the, unake saamane hee ammaan jee ne aakara meree kitaaben chheena lee;aur rokara bolontuma bhee chale jaaoge, to is ghar men kaun rahega? agar mere 'kaarana tuma log ghar chho.da-chho.da kar bhaage jaa rahe ho,to lo main hee kaheen chalee jati hoon! main to bhallaayaa hua tha hee, vahaan baaboo jee bhee na the; biga.da kar bola-aapa kyon kaheen chalee jaaengee! aapakaa to ghar hai, aapa aaraama se rahie| gaira to hamee log hain| hama na rahenge, tab to aapako aaraama hee aaraama rahegaa|

mansaaraama-tumane khooba kaha,bahut hee achchha kaha| is par aur bhee bhalaaee hongee aur jaakara baaboo jee se shikaayata kee hogee? jiyaaraama-naheen, yaha kuchha naheen hua| bechaaree zameen par vaitha kar rone lagee, mujhe bhee karunaa aa gaee| main bhee ro pa.da,tab unhonne aanchala se mere aansoo ponchhe aur boleen-jiya! main eeshvara ko saakshee dekara kehti hoon ki mainne tumhaare bhaiyaa ke vishaya men tumhaare vaaboo jee se ek shabda bhee naheen kaha| mere bhaagya men kalanka likhaa hua hai, vahee bhoga rahi hoon| phir aur na jaane kyaa-kyaa kaha, jo meree samajha men naheen aaya| kuchha baaboo jee kee baat thi|

mansaaraama ne udvignata se poochhaa-baaboo jee ke vishaya men kyaa kaha, kuchha yaada hai?

jiyaaraama-baaten to bhaee mujhe yaada naheen aati| meree maimoree(nemory) kaun ba.dee teza hai;lekin unakee baaton kaa matalaba kuchha aisaa maalooma hota tha ki unhen baaboo jee ko prasanna rakhane ke lie yaha svaoga bharana pa.da raha hai| na jaane dharma-adharma kee kaisi baaten karti theen,jo main bilakula na samajha sakaa| mujhe to ab isakaa vishvaasa aa gaya hai ki unakee ichchhaa tumhen yahaan bhejane kee na thi|

mansaaraama-tuma ina chaalon kaa matalaba naheen samajha sakate| ye ba.dee gaharee chaalen hain|

jiyaaraama-tumhaaree samajha men hongee, meree samajha men to naheen hain|
mansaaraama-jab tuma jyometaree (Geometry) naheen samajha sakate, to ina baaton ko kyaa samajha sakoge! us raat ko jab mujhe khaanaa khaane ke lie bulaane aaee theen; aur main unake aagraha par jaane ko taiyaara bhee ho gaya tha, us vakta baaboo jee ko dekhate hee unhonne jo kaindaa badalaa vaha kyaa main kabhee bhoola sakata hoon?

jiyaaraama- yahee baat meree samajha men bhee naheen aati| abhee kala hee main yahaan se gaya to lagee tumhaaraa haala poochhane| mainne kaha-vaha to kehte the ki ab kabhee is ghar men kadama na rakhoongaa| mainne kuchha jhootha to kaha naheen, tumane mujhase kaha hee tha| itana sunana tha ki phoota-phoota kar rone lagee| main dil men bahut pachhataayaa ki kahaan se kahaan mainne yaha baat kaha dee| baara-baara yahee kehti thi, kyaa vaha mere kaarana ghar chho.da denge? mujhase itane naaraaja hain? chale gae
aur mujhase mile taka naheen! khaanaa taiyaara tha,khaane taka naheen aae! haaya! main kyaa bataaoon, kisa vipatti men hoon! itane men baaboo jee aa gae| basa, turanta aankhen ponchha kar muskaraatee hui unake paasa chalee gaee| yaha baat meree samajha men naheen aati| aaja mujhase ba.dee minnata kee ki unako saatha lete aana| aaja main tumhen kheencha le chaloongaa| do din men vaha kitanee dubalee ho gaee hain, tumhen yaha dekha kar una par dayaa aaegee| to chaloge na ?

mansaaraama ne kuchha jabaaba na diya| usake pair kaanpa rahe the| jiyaaraama to haajiree kee ghantee suna kar bhaaga; par vaha baincha par leta gaya aur itanee lambee saansa lee, maano bahut dera se usane saansa naheen lee hai| usake mukha se dussaha vedana men doobe hue yaha shabda nikale-haaya eeshvara! is naama ke sivaa use ab apana jeevana niraadhaara maalooma hota tha| is ek uchchhavaasa men kitana nairaashya, kitanee samavedana, kitanee karuna, kitanee deenata-praarthana bharee hui thi,isakaa kaun anumaana kar sakata hai? ab saaraa rahasya usakee samajha men aa raha tha; aur baara-baara usakaa pee.dita hridaya aartanaada kar raha tha-haaya eeshvara! itana ghora kalanka!!

kyaa jeevana men isase ba.dee vipatti kee kalpana kee jaa sakatee hai? kyaa sansaara men isase ghoratara neechata kee kalpana ho sakatee hai? aaja taka kisee pitaa ne apane putra par itana nirdaya kalankana lagaayaa hoga! jisake charitra kee sabhee prashansaa karte the,jo anya yuvakon ke lie aadarsha samajhaa jata tha, jisane kabhee apavitra vichaaron ko apane paasa naheen phatakane diya, usee par yaha ghoratama kalanka! mansaaraama ko aisaa maalooma hua, maano usakaa dil phata jata hai!

doosaree ghantee bhee vaja gaee| ladke apane-apane kamaron men gae; par mansaaraama hathelee par haath rakkhe animesha netron se bhoomi kee
ora dekha raha tha,maano usakaa sarvasva jala-magna ho gaya ho; maano vaha kisee ko munha na dikhaa sakata ho| skoola men gaira-haajiree ho jaayagee, jurmaanaa ho jaayaga, isakee use chintaa naheen! jab usakaa sarvasva luta gaya, to ab ina chhotee-chhotee baaton kaa kyaa bhaya? itana bada kalanka lagane par bhee agar jeetaa rahoon, to mere jeene ko dhikkaara hai!

usee shokaatireka kee dashaa men vaha chillaa pada-maataa jee, tuma kahaan ho? tumhaaraa beta, jisa par tuma praana detee theen-jise tuma apane jeevana kaa aadhaara samajhatee theen, aaja ghora sankata men hai! usee kaa pitaa usakee gardana par chhuree phera raha hai! haaya, tuma kahaan ho!!

mansaaraama phir shaanta chitta se sochane lagaa-mujha par yaha sandeha kyon ho raha hai? isakaa kyaa kaarana hai? mujhamen aisee,kaun see baat unhonne dekhi jisase unhen yaha sandeha hua| vaha mere pitaa hain;mere shatru naheen hain, jo anaayaasa hee mujha par aparaadha lagaane baitha jaayan | zaroora unhonne koee na koee baat dekhi yaa sunee hai| unakaa mujha par kitana sneha tha! mere bagaira bhojana karane na jaate the,vahee mere shatru ho jayen, yaha baat akaarana naheen ho sakatee!

achchha is sandeha kaa beejaaropana kisa din hua? mujhe bordinga haausa men thaharaane ko baat to peechhe kee hai| jisa din raat ko vaha mere kamare men aakara meree pareekshaa lene lage the,usee din unakee tyoriyaan badalee hui thi| us din aisee kaun see baat hui, jo unhen apriya lagee ho! main naee ammaan se kuchha khaane ko maangane gaya tha| baaboo jee us vakta vahaan baithe tha| haan, ab yaada aata hai usee vakta. unakaa cheharaa tamatamaa gaya tha| usee din se naee ammaan ne mujhase pa.dhana chho.da diya| agar main jaanata ki meraa ghar men aana-jaana,ammaan jee se kuchha kahana-sunana aur unhen pa.dhaanaa-likhaanaa pitaa jee ko buraa lagata hai, to aaja kyon yaha naubata aati? aur naee ammaan! una par kyaa beeta rahi hogee? mansaaraama ne ab taka nirmalaa kee ora dhyaana hee naheen diya tha| nirmalaa kaa dhyaana aate hee usake roen kha.de ho gae! haaya, unakaa sarala snehasheela hridaya yaha aaghaata kaise saha sakega? aaha! main kitane bhrama men tha? main unake sneha ko kaushala samajhata tha! mujhe kyaa maalooma tha ki unhen pitaa jee kaa bhrama shaanta karane ke lie mere prati itana katu vyavahaara karana pa.data hai| aaha! mainne una par kitana anyaaya kiya hai| unakee dashaa to mujhase bhee kharaaba ho rahi hogee| main to yahaan chalaa aaya, magar vaha kahaan jaaengee| jiya kahata tha,unhonne do din se bhojana naheen kiya! hara dama royaa karti hain! kaise jaakara samajhaaoon? vaha is abhaage ke peechhe kyon apane sira yaha vipatti le rahi hain! vaha kyon baara-baara meraa haala poochhatee hain? kyon baara-baara mujhe bulaatee hai? kaise kaha doon ki maataa mujhe tumase zaraa bhee shikaayata naheen,meraa dil tumhaaree tarapha se saapha hai|

vaha ab bhee baithee ro rahi hogee! kitana bada anartha hai? baaboo jee ko yaha kyaa ho gaya hai? kyaa iseelie vivaaha kiya tha? ek baalikaa kee hatyaa karane hee ke lie use laae the! is komala pushpa ko masala daalane hee ke lie to.daa tha?

unakaa uddhaara kaise hoga? us niraparaadhinee kaa mukha kaise ujjvala hoga? unhe kevala mere saatha sneha kaa vyavahaara karane ke lie yaha danda diya jaa raha hai! unakee sajjanata kaa unhen yaha upahaara mila raha hai! main unhe is prakaara nirdaya aaghaata sahate dekha kar baithaa rahoonga! apanee maana-rakshaa ke lie na sahee unakee
aatma-rakshaa ke lie ina praanon ko balidaana karana pa.dega! isake sivaaya uddhaara kaa aur koee upaaya naheen| aaha! dil men kaise kaise aramaana the| ve sab khaaka men milaa dene honge| ek satee par sandeha kiya jaa raha hai;aur mere kaarana! mujhe apane praanon se usakee rakshaa karanee hogee,yahee meraa kartavya hai| isee men sachchee veerata hai! maata,main apane rakta se is kaalimaa ko dho doongaa| isee men meraa aur tumhaaraa donon kaa kalyaana hai!

vaha din bhara inheen vichaaron men doobaa raha| shaama ko usake donon bhaaee aakara ghar chalane ke lie aagraha karane lage|

siyaaraama-chalate kyon naheen? mere bhaiyaa jee, chale chalo na!

mansaaraama-mujhe phurasata naheen hai ki tumhaare kahane se chalaa chaloon|

jiyaa0-aakhira kala to itavaara hee hai!

mansaa0-itavaara ko bhee kaama hai|

jiya-achchha,kala aaoge na?

mansaa0-naheen,kala mujhe ek maicha men jana hai|

siyaa0-ammaan jee moonga ke laddoo bana rahi hain| na chaloge to ek bhee na paaoge| hama-tuma mila ke khaa jaayange;'jiya, inhen na denge|

jiyaa0-bhaiya, agar tuma kala na gae,to shaayada ammaan jee yaheen chalee aayen| mansaa-sacha! naheen,aisaa kyon karengee| yahaan aaee,to ba.dee pareshaanee hogee| tuma kaha dena,vaha kaheen maicha dekhane gae hain|

jiya-main jhootha kyo bolane lagaa| main kaha doongaa vaha munha phulaae baithe the| dekha lena unhen saatha laataa hoon ki naheen!

siyaa0-hama kaha denge ki aaja pa.dhane naheen gae| pa.de-pa.de sote rahe| mansaaraama ne ina dooton se kala aane kaa vaadaa karake galaa chhu.daayaa| jab donon chale gae,to phir chintaa men doobaa| raat bhara use karavata badalate gujaree| chhattee kaa din bhee baithe hee baithe kata gaya,use din bhara shankaa hoti rahi ki kaheen ammaan jee sachamucha na chalee aaven| kisee ga.dee kee kha.dakha.daahata sunata,to usakaa kalejaa dhakadhaka karane lagata| kaheen aa to naheen gaee!

chhaatraalaya men ek chhota-saa aushadhaalaya tha| ek daoktara saahava sandhyaa samay ek ghante ke lie aa jaayaa karte the| agar koee ladka beemaara hota,to use davaa dete| aaja vaha aae to mansaaraama kuchha sochata hua unake paasa jaakara kha.daa ho gaya| vaha mansaaraama ko achchhi tarah jaanate the| use dekha kar aashcharya se vole-yaha tumhaaree kyaa haalata hai jo? tuma to maano gale jaa rahe ho| kaheen vaazaara kaa chaskaa to naheen pa.da gaya? aakhira tumhen hua kya? jaraa yahaan to aao!

mansaaraama ne muskaraa kar kaha-mujhe zindagi kaa roga hai| aapake paasa isakee bhee koee davaa hai? daoktara-main tumhaaree pareekshaa karana chaahata hoon| tumhaaree to soorata hee badala gaee jee,pahachaane bhee naheen jaate!

yaha kaha kar unhonne mansaaraama kaa haath paka.da liya aur chhaatee, peetha,aankhen, jeebha sab baaree-baaree se dekhi | tab chintita hokara bole-vakeela saahaba se main aaja hee miloongaa| tumhen thaaisisa ho raha hai| saare lakshana usee ke hain|

mansaaraama ne ba.dee utsukata se poochhaa-bhalaa kitane dinon men kaama tamaama ho jaayaga;daoktara saahaba?

daoktara-kaisi baaten karte ho jee! main vakeela saahaba se mila kar tumhen kisee paha.dee jagaha bhejane kee salaaha doongaa| eeshvara ne chaahaa to tuma bahut jalda achchhe ho jaaoge| beemaaree abhee pahale hee steja men hai|

mansaaraama-tab to abhee saala-do saala kee dera maalooma hoti hai| main to itana intezar naheen kar sakata| sunie,mujhe thaaisisavaaisisa kuchha naheen hai na koee doosaree shikaayata hee hai| aapa baaboojee ko naahaka taraduta men na daaliegaa| is vakta mere sira men darda hai, koee davaa deejie| koee aisee davaa ho,jisase needa bhee aa jaaya| mujhe do raat se neenda naheen aati|

daoktara saahaba ne jahareelee davaaiyon kee aalamaaree kholee aur ek sheeshee men thi.dee see davaa nikaala kar mansaaraama ko dee| mansaaraama ne poochhaa- yaha to koee jahara hai? bhalaa koee ise peele to mara jaaya? daoktara-naheen,mara to na jaaya;par sira men chakara jalara aa jaaya|

mansaa0-koee aisee davaa bhee isamen hai,jise peeta hee praana nikala jayen?

daoktara-aisee ek-do naheen,kitanee hee davaaen hain| yaha jo sheeshee dekha rahe ho,isakee ek boonda bhee peta men chalee jaaya no jaana na vache aanana-phaanana men mauta ho jaaya|

mansaa0-kyon daoktara saahaba,jo log jahara khaa lete hain,unhen ba.dee takaleepha hoti hogee?

daoktara-sabhee jaharon se takaleepha naheen hoti| vaaza to aise hain ki peete hee aadmi thandaa ho jata hai| yaha sheeshee isee kisma kee hai| ise peete hee aadmi vehosha ho jata hai,phir use hosha naheen aata|

mansaaraama ne sochaa-tab to praana dena bahut aasaana hai| phir log kyon itana darate hain? yaha sheeshee kaise milegee? agar davaa kaa naama poochha kar shehar ke kisee davaa-karosha se lena chaahoon, to vaha kabhee na degaa| unha, isake milane men koee dikkata naheen| yaha to maalooma ho gaya ki praanon kaa anta ba.dee aasaanee se kiya jaa sakata hai | mansaaraama itana prasanna hua, maano koee inaama paa gaya ho| usake dil par se ek vojha saa hata gaya| chintaa kee megha-raashi, jo sira par mandalaa rahi thi,chhinna-bhinna ho gaee| maheenon ke baada aaja use mann men ek sphoorti kaa anubhava hua| kaee ladke thiyetara dekhane jaa rahe the, nireekshaka se aajnaa le lee thi| mansaaraama bhee unake saatha thiyetara dekhane chalaa gaya | aisaa khusha tha, maano usase zyada sukhee jeeva sansaara men koee naheen hai| thiyetara men nakala dekha kar to vaha hansate-hansate lota gaya | baarabaara taaliyaan bajaane aur 'vansamora !' kee haanka lagaane men sabase pahalaa nambara usee kaa tha | gaanaa suna kar vaha masta ho jata tha,
aur 'oho ho !' karake chillaa uthata tha| darshakon kee nigaahen baara-baara. usakee tarapha utha jati theen| thiyetara ke paatra bhee usee kee ora taakate the; aur yaha jaanane ko utsuka the ki kaun mahaashaya itane rasika aur bhaavuka hain| usake mitron ko usakee uchchhankhalata par aashcharya ho raha tha| vaha bahut hee shaantachitta, gambheera svabhaava kaa yuvaka tha| aaja vaha kyon itana haasyasheela ho gaya hai, kyon usake vinoda kaa vaaraapaara naheen hai ?

do baje raat ko thiyetara se lautane par bhee usakaa haasyonmaada kama naheen hua| usane ek ladke kee chaarapaaee ulata dee, kaee ladakon ke kamaron ke dvaara baahara se banda kar die; aur unhen bheetara se khatakhata karte suna kar hansata raha| yahaan taka ki chhaatraalaya ke adhyaksha mahodaya kee neenda bhee shora-gula suna kar khula gaee;
aur unhonne mansaaraama kee sharaarata par kheda prakata kiya | kaun jaanata hai ki usake antastala men kitanee bheeshana kraanti ho rahi hai ? sandeha ke nirdaya aaghaata ne usakee. lajjaa aur aatmasammaana ko kuchala daalaa hai ! use apamaana aur tiraskaara kaa lesha maatra bhee bhaya nahaa hai| yaha vinoda naheen-usakee aatmaa kaa karuna vilaapa hai| jab aur sab ladke so gae, to vaha bhee chaarapaaee par leta, lekin use neenda naheen aaee| ek kshana ke baada vaha utha baitha; aur apanee saaree pustaken baandha kar sandooka men rakha dee| jab marana hee hai to pa.dha kar kyaa hogaa ? jisa jeevana men aisee-aisee vaadhaaen hain aisee-aisee yaatanaaen hain, usase mrityu kaheen achchhi!

yahee sochate-sochate ta.dakaa ho gaya| teena raat se vaha ek kshana bhee na soyaa tha| is vakta vaha uthaa to usake pair thara-thara kaopa rahe the; aur sira men chakkara saa aa raha tha| aankhen jala rahi theen; aur shareera ke saare anga shithila ho rahe the| din cha.dhata jata tha; aur usamen itanee shakti bhee na thi ki utha kar munha-haath dho daale | ekaaeka usane bhugee ko roomaala men kuchha lie hue ek kahaara ke saatha aate dekha | usakaa kalejaa sana ho gaya| haaya eeshvara ! vaha aa gaee ! ab kyaa hogaa ? bhungee akele naheen aaee hogee, vagdhee zaroora baahara kha.dee hogee| kahaan to usase uthaa na jata tha| kahaan bhugee ko dekhate hee dau.daa aur ghavaraaee hui aavaaza men bola-ammaan jee bhee aaee hain kyaa re ? jab maalooma hua ki ammaan jee naheen aaee, tab usakaa chitta shaanta huyaa | bhungee ne kaha-bhaiyaa ! tuma kala gae naheen, vahoo jee tumhaaree raaha dekhatee raha gaee| unase kyon roothe ho bhaiyaa ? vaha to kehti hain, mainne unakee kuchha bhee shikaayata naheen kee hai | mujhase aaja rokara kahane lagee-unake paasa yaha mithaaee letee jaa aur kahana mere kaarana ghar kyon chho.da diya hai ? kahaan rakha doonn yaha thaalee ?

mansaaraama ne rukhaaee se kaha-thaalee apane sira par pataka de, chudaila vahaan se chalee hai mithaaee lekara ! khabaradaara, jo phir kamee idhara aaee | saugaata lekara chalee hain ! jaakara kaha dena mujhe unakee mithaaee naheen chaahie! jaakara kaha dena tumhaaraa ghar hai, tuma raho; vahaan ve ba.de aaraama se hain| khooba khaate aur mauja karte hain| sunatee hai, baaboo jee ke munha par kahana, samajha gaee ? mujhe kisee kaa dara naheen hai ; aur jo karana chaahen kar daalen, jisase dil men koee aramaana na raha jaaya | kahen to ilaahaabaada, lakhanaoo, kalakattaa chalaa jaaoon | mere lie jaise banaarasa vaise doosaraa shehar ! yahaan kyaa rakkhaa hai|

bhungee-bhaiya, mithaaee rakha lo, naheen ro-rokara mara jaayengee| sachan maano, rokara mara jaayangee|

mansaaraama ne aansuon ke uthate hue vega ko davaa kar kahaamara jaayangee, meree balaa se ! kaun mujhe bada sukha de diya hai, jisake lie pachhataaoon| meraa to unhonne sarvanaasha kar diya| kaha dena mere paasa koee sandeshaa na bhejen, kuchha jaroorata naheen !

bhungee-bhaiya, tuma to kehte ho yahaan khooba khaataa hoon aur mauja karata hoon; magar deha to aadhee bhee naheen rahi| jaise aae the usake aadhe bhee naheen rahe !

mansaaraama-yaha teree aankhon kaa phera hai, dekhana do-chaara din men mutaa kar kolhoo ho jata hoon ki naheen| unase yaha bhee kaha dena ki ronaa-dhonaa banda karen| jo mainne sunaa ki rotee hain aur khaanaa naheen khaatee, to mujhase buraa koee naheen| mujhe ghar se nikaalaa hai, to ab chaina se rahen | chalee hain prema dikhaane ! main aise triya-charitra vahuta pa.dhe vaithaa hoon|

bhungee chalee gaee| mansaaraama ko usase baaten karte hee karte kuchha thanda maalooma hone lagee thi| yaha abhinaya karane ke lie use apane manobhaavon ko jitana davaanaa pada tha, vaha usake lie asaadhya tha| usakaa aatma-sammaana use is kutila vyaapaara kaa jalda se jalda anta kar dene ke lie vaadhya kar raha tha; par isakaa parinaama kyaa hogaa ? nirmalaa kyaa yaha aaghaata saha sakegee ? ab taka vaha mrityu-kalpana karte samay kisee anya praanee kaa vichaara na karata tha, par aaja ekaaeka use jnaata hua ki mere jeevana ke saatha ek aur praanee kaa jeevana-sootra bhee bandhaa hua hai| nirmalaa yahee samajhegee ki meree nishthurata hee ne inakee jaana lee| yaha samajha kar usakaa komala hridaya kyaa phata na jaaegaa ? usakaa jeevana to ab bhee sankata men hai| sandeha ke kathora paje men phansee hui avalaa kyaa apane ko hatyaarinee samajha kar bahut din jeevita raha sakatee hai ?

mansaaraama ne chaarapaaee par leta kar lihaafa o.dha liya, phir bhee maare sardee se kalejaa kaopa raha tha | tho.dee hee dera men use jora se jvara cha.dha aaya-vaha behosha ho gaya| is acheta dashaa men use bhaanti-bhaanti ke svapna dikhaaee dene lage| tho.dee dera ke baada chaunka pa.data-aankhen khula jati;phir behosha ho jata|

sahasaa vakeela saahaba kee aavaaza suna kar vaha chaunka pada| haan, vakeela saahaba hee kee aavaaza thi| usane lihaafa phenka diya
aur chaarapaaee se utara kar neeche kha.daa ho gaya| usake mann men ek aavega hua ki isee vakta inake saamane praana de doon| use aisaa maalooma hua ki main mara jaaoon,to inhen sachchee khushi hogee| shaayada iseelie yaha dekhane aae hain ki mere marane men kitanee dera hai| vakeela saahaba ne usakaa haath paka.da liya,jisamen vaha gira na pa.de aur poochhaa-kaisi tabeeyata hai lalloo,lete kyon na rahe? leta jaao, leta jaao,tuma kha.de kyon ho gae?

mansaaraama-meree tabeeyata to bahut achchhi hai| aapako vyartha hee kashta hua|

munshee jee ne kuchha javaaba na diya| ladke kee dashaa dekha kar unakee aankhon se aansoo nikala aae| vaha hrishta-pushta baalaka,jise dekha kar chitta prasanna ho jata tha,ab sookha kar kaantaa ho gaya tha| paancha-chhah din men hee vaha itana dubalaa ho gaya tha ki use pahachaanana kathina tha| munshee jee ne use aahistaa se chaarapaaee par litaa diya;aur lihaapha achchhi tarah o.dhaa kar sochane lage ki ab kyaa karana chaahie| kaheen ladka haath se to na jaayaga? yaha khayal karake vaha shoka se vihvala ho gae;aur stoola par baitha kar phoota-phoota kar rone lage| mansaaraama bhee lihaapha men munha lapete ro raha tha| abhee tho.de hee dinon pahale use dekha kar pitaa kaa hridaya garva se phoola uthata tha lekin aaja use is daaruna dashaa men dekha kar bhee vaha socha raha hai ki ise ghar le chaloon yaa naheen| kyaa yahaan davaa naheen ho sakatee? main yahaan chaubeeson ghante baithaa rahoongaa| daoktara saahaba yahaan hain hee,koee dikkata na hogee| ghar le chalane men unhen baadhaaen hee baadhaaen dikhaaee detee theen;sabase ba.dhaa bhaya yaha tha ki vahaan nirmalaa isake paasa haradama vaithee rahegee; aur main mana na kar sakoongaa yaha unake lie asahya tha|

itane men adhyaksha ne aakara kaha-main to samajhata hoon,aapa inhen apane saatha le jaayan| ga.dee hai hee, koee takaleepha na hogee| yahaan achchhi tarah dekha-bhaala na ho sakegee|

munshee jee-haan,aaya to main isee khayal se tha;lekin inakee haalata bahut hee naajuka maalooma hoti hai| jaraa see asaavadhaanee hone se sarasaama ho jaane kaa bhaya hai|

adhyaksha-yahaan se unhen le jaane men to tho.dee see dikkata jaroora hai| lekin yaha to aapa khuda hee socha sakate hain ki ghar par jo aaraama mila sakata hai,vaha yahaan kisee tarah naheen mila sakata| isake atirikta kisee beemaara ladke ko yahaan rakhana,niyama-viruddha bhee hai|

'munshee jee-kahie to main hedamaastara se aajnaa le loon| mujha inako yahaan se is haalata men le jana kisee tarah munaasiba naheen. maalooma hota| adhyaksha ne hedamaastara kaa naama suna,to samajhe ki yaha mahaashaya mujhe dhamakee de rahe hain| jaraa tinaka kar bole-hedamaastara niyama-viruddha koee baat naheen kar sakate| main itanee ba.dee jimmedaaree kaise le sakata hoon?

ab kyaa ho? kyaa ghar le jana hee pa.dega? yahaan rakhane kaa to yaha vahaanaa tha ki le jaane se beemaaree ba.dha jaane kee shankaa hai| yahaan se le jaakara aspataala men thaharaane ke lie koee bahaanaa naheen hai| jo sunegaa vaha yahee kahegaa ki daoktara kee pheesa bachaane ke lie ladke ko aspataala phenka aae;par ab le jaane ke sivaa
aur koee upaaya na tha| agar adhyaksha mahodaya is vakta rishvata lene par taiyaara ho jaate,to shaayada do-chaara saala kaa vetana le lete; lekin kaayade ke paabanda logon men itanee buddhi,itanee chaturaaee kahaan! agar is vakta munshee jee ko koee aadmi aisaa unna sujhaa deta,jisamen unhen mansaaraama ko ghar na le jana pa.de,to vaha aajeevana usakaa ehasaana maanate| sochane kaa samay bhee naheen tha| adhyaksha mahodaya shaitaana kee tarah sira par savaara the| vivasha hokara munshee jee ne donon saeeson ko bulaayaa aur mansaaraama ko uthaane lage| mansaaraama arddha-chetana kee dashaa men tha| chaunka kar bola-kyaa hai? kaun hai?

munshee jee-koee naheen hai;beta! main tumhen dhara le chalana chaahata hoon; aao main goda men uthaa loon|

mansaaraama-mujhe ghar kyon le chalate hain? main vahaan naheen jaaoongaa| munshee--jee yahaan to raha naheen sakate,niyama hee aisaa hai|

mansaaraama--kuchha bhee ho,main vahaan na jaaoongaa| mujhe aur kaheen le chalie-kisee pe.da ke neeche,kisee jhopa.de men,jahaan chaahe rakhie;par ghar na le chalie|

adhyaksha ne munshee jee se kaha-aapa ina baaton kaa khayal na karen, yaha to hosha men naheen hain|

mansaaraama-kaun hosha men naheen hai? main hosha men naheen hoon? kisee ko gaaliyaan detaa hoon,daanta kaatata hoon? kyon hosha men naheen hoon? mujhe yaheen pada rahane deejie,jo kuchha honaa hogaa yaheen hoga,agar aisaa hee hai to mujhe aspataala le chalie| main vahaan pada rahoongaa| jeenaa hogaa jioonga,marana hogaa maroonga;lekin ghar kisee tarah bhee na jaaoongaa|

yaha jora paakara munshee jee phir adhyaksha se minnaten karane lage; lekin vaha kaayade kaa paavanda aadmi kuchha sunata hee na tha| agar chhoota kee beemaaree hui aur kisee doosare ladke ko chhoota laga gaee, to kaun usakaa jabaavadeha hoga? is tarka ke saamane munsheejee kee kaanoonee daleelen bhee maata ho gaee|

mansaaraama ne bhallaa kar kaha-naheen, naheen, naheen, sau baara naheen | main ghar naheen jaaoongaa| mujhe aspataala le chalie aur ghar ke sab aadamiyon ko mana kar deejie ki mujhe dekhane na aaven| mujhe kuchha naheen hua hai, vilakula veemaara naheen hoon| aapa sujhe chho.da deejie, main apane paanva se chala sakata hoon|

vaha utha kha.daa hua aur unmatta kee bhaanti dvaara kee ora chala; lekin pair ladakha.daa gae| yadi munshee jee ne sanbhaala na liya hota, to use ba.dee chota aati| donon naukaron kee madada se munshee jee use bagghee ke paasa laae aur andara vithaa diya|

ga.dee aspataala kee ora chalee| vahee hua jo munshee jee. chaahate the| is shoka men bhee unakaa chitta santushta tha| ladka apanee ichchhaa se aspataala jaa raha tha,kyaa yaha is baat kaa pramaana naheen tha ki ghar se ise koee sneha naheen hai? kyaa isase yaha siddha naheen hota ki mansaaraama nirdosha hai? vaha us par akaarana hee bhrama kar rahe the|

lekin jaraa hee dera men is tushti kee jagaha unake mann men glaani kaa bhaava jaagrata hua| vaha apane praana-priya putra ko ghar na le jaakara aspataala liye jaa rahe the| unake vishaala bhavana men unake putra ke lie bhee jagaha na thi,is dashaa men bhee,jabaki usakaa jeevana sankata men pada hua tha,kitanee vidambana hai?

ek kshana ke baada ekaaeka munshee jee ke mann men prashna uthaa--kaheen mansaaraama unake bhaavon ko ta.da to naheen gaya ? iseelie to use ghar se ghrinaa naheen ho gaee! agar aisaa hai,to Gazaba ho jaayaga!
