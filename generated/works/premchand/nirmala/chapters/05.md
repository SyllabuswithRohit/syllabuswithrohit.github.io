# Nirmala — Adhyay 5

**Munshi Premchand**

paanchavaan parichchhed

Nirmala ka shaadee ho gayaa. sasuraal aa gaee. vakeel Sahab ka naam tha munshee totaaraama. saanvale ranggg ke mote-taaje aadmi the. umr to abhee chaalees se adhik n thi, par vakaalat ke kathin parimehanat ne sir ke baal pakaa die the. vyaayaam karane ka unhen avakaash n milataa thaa. yahan tak ki kabhee kaheen ghoomane bhi n jaate, isalie tond nikal aaee thee. deh ke sthool hote hue bhi aae din koee n koee shikaayat hotee rahatee! mandaagni aur bavaaseer se to unakaa bahut lambe samay ki rishtaa thaa. ataev bahut phoongk-phoongk kar kadam rakhate the. unake teen ladake the. badaa mansaaraam solah varsh ka tha, manjhalaa jiyaaraam baarah aur chhotaa siyaaraam saat varsh kaa. teenon angggarejee padhate the. ghar mein vakeel Sahab ki vidhavaa bahin ke sivaa koee aurat n thee. vahee ghar ki maalikin theen. unakaa naam tha rukminee; aur avasthaa pachaas se oopar thee. sasuraal mein koee n thaa. sthaayee reeti se yaheen rahatee theen.

totaaraam damshauhar-vigyaan mein kushal the. Nirmala ko khush rakhane ke lie unamen jo svaabhaavik kamee thi, use vah upahaaron se pooree karanee chaahate the. halaanki bahut hee mitakharchee mard the; par Nirmala ke lie koee n koee tohaphaa roj laayaa karate. mauke par dhan ki paravaah n karate the. khud kabhee naashtaa n karate the, ladake ke lie thodaa-thodaa doodh aataa tha; par Nirmala ke lie meve, murabbe, mithaaiyaan-kisee cheej ki kamee n thee. apanee jindagee mein kabhee sair-tamaashe dekhane n gae the; par ab chhuttiyon mein Nirmala ko sinemaa, sarakas, thiyetar dikhaane le jaate. apane bahumooly samay ka thodaa saa hissaa usake saath baith kar gaanvophon bajaane mein bhi vyateet kiyaa karate the.

lekin Nirmala ko n jaane kyon totaaraam ke paas baithane aur hansane-bolane mein sanggkoch hotaa thaa. isakaa shaayad yah vajah tha ki ab tak aisaa hee ek aadmi usakaa pitaa tha, jisake saamane vah sir jhukaa kar, deh churaa kar, nikalatee thi; ab usakee avasthaa ka ek aadmi usakaa shauhar thaa. vah use pyaar ki vastu nahin, izzat ki vastu samajhatee thee. unase bhaagatee phiratee, unako dekhate hee usakee praphullataa palaayan kar jaatee thi!

vakeel Sahab ko unake damshauhar-vigyaan ne sikhaayaa tha ka javaan ladakee ke saamane khoob pyaar ki baaten karanee chahiye-dil nikaal kar rakh denaa chaahie. yahee usake vasheekarna ka munhy mantr hai. isalie vakeel saahav apane pyaar-pradarshan mein koee kasar n rakhate the; lekin Nirmala ko in baaton se nafrat hotee thee. vahee baaten, jinhen kisee javaan aadmi ke munh se sun kar usakaa dil pyaar se unmatt ho jaataa, vakeel Sahab ke munh se nikal kar usake dil par shar ke samaan aaghaat karatee theen! unamen ras n tha, ullaas n tha, unmaad n tha, dil n tha, keval banaavat thi, dhokhaa tha; aur tha shushk, neeras shabdaadambar! use itr aur tel buraa n lagataa, sair-tamaashe bure n lagate, banaav-singgaar bhi buraa n lagataa; buraa lagataa tha keval totaaraam ke paas baithanaa. vah apanaa roop aur yauvan unhen n dikhaanaa chaahatee thi, kyunki wahan dekhane vaalee aankhen n theen. vah unhen in rason ka aasvaadan karane ke yogy hee n samajhatee thee. kalee prabhaat-sameer hee ke sparsh se khilatee hain. donon mein samaan saarasy hai. Nirmala ke lie vah prabhaat-sameer kahaan thi?

pahalaa maheenaa gujarate hee totaaraam ne Nirmala ko apanaa khajaanychee banaa liyaa. kachaharee se aakar din bhar ki kamaaee use de dete. unakaa khyaal tha ki Nirmala in rupayon ko dekh kar phoolee n samaaegee. Nirmala bade shauk se is pad ka kaam ajaam detee. ek-ek paise ka hisaab likhatee, agar kabhee rupaye kam milate, to poochhatee-aaj kam kyon hain? ghar-baar ke rishtaa mein unase khoob vaaten karatee. inheen baaton ke laayak vah unako samajhatee thee. jyonhee koee vinod ki baat unake nikal jaatee, usakaa munh malin ho jaataa tha!

Nirmala jab kapadeaabhooshanon se alanggkat hokar aaeene ke saamane khadee hotee; aur usamen apane saundary ki sushamaapoorn aabhaa dekhatee, to usakaa dil ek satrishn kaamanaa se tadap uthataa thaa. us vakt usake dil mein ek jvaalaa see uthatee. man mein aataa is ghar mein aag lagaa doon. apanee maataa par krodh aataa, pitaa par krodh aataa, apane kismat par krodh aataa; par sabase adhik krodh bechaare niraparaadh totaaraam par aataa, vah hameshaa is taap se jalaa karatee thee. vaangkaa savaar boodhe ladd, tatt par savaar honaa kav pasand karegaa, chaahe use paidal hee kyon n chalanaa pade. Nirmala ki dashaa usee baangke savaar ki thee. vah us par savaar hokar udanaa chaahatee thi, us ullaasamayee vidyut-gati ka khushi uthaanaa chaahatee thi, tatoo ke hinahinaane aur kanautiyaan khadee karane se kyaa ummeed hotee? sambhav tha ki bachchon ke saath hansane-khelane se vah apanee dashaa ko thodee der ke lie bhool jaatee, kuchh man haraa ho jaataa; lekin rukminee devee ladakon ko usake paas phatakane bhi n deteen, maano vah koee pishaachinee hai, jo unhen nigal jaayagee. ruknimee devee ka svabhaav saare sansaar se niraalaa tha; yah pataa lagaanaa kathin tha ki vah kis baat se khush hotee theen; aur kis baat se naaraaj! ek baar jis baat se khush ho jaatee theen, doosaree: baar usee baat se jal jaatee theen. agar Nirmala apane kamare mein vaithee rahatee, to kahateen n jaane kahaan ki manahoosin hai, agar vah kothe par chadh jaatee ya mahariyon se baaten karatee, to chhaatee peetane lagateen. laaj hai n sharam nigodee ne hayaa bhoon khaaee; ab kyaa? kuchh dinon mein baajaar mein naachegee. jab se vakeel Sahab ne Nirmala ke haath mein rupaye-paise dene shuroo kie, rukminee usakee aalochanaa karane par aaroodh ho gaee thee. use maaloom hotaa tha ki ab pralay hone mein bahut-thodee kasar rah gaee hai. ladakon ko baar-baar paison ki jaroorat padatee. jab tak khud svaaminee thi, unhen vahalaa diyaa karatee thee. ab seedhe Nirmala ke paas bhej detee. Nirmala ko ladakon ka chatoraapan achchha n lagataa thaa. kabhee-kabhee paise dene se ingkaar kar detee. rukminee ko apane vaagbaan sar karane ka avasar mil jaataa-ab to maalikin huee hain, ladake kaahe ko jiengge. bin maan ke bachche ko kaun poochhe? rupayon ki mithaaiyaan khaa jaate the, ab dhele-dhele ko tarasate hain. Nirmala agaran chidh kar kisee din vinaa kuchh poochhe-guchhe paise de detee, to devee jee usakee doosaree ho aalochanaa karateen inhen kyaa, ladake maren ya jien, inakee valaa se; maan ke vinaa kaun samajhaave ki betaa bahut mithaaiyaan mat khaao? aaeenaaee to mere sir jaayagee, unhen kyaa! yaheen tak hotaa to Nirmala shaayad japt kar jaatee; par devee jee khuphiyaa pulees ke sipaahee ki bhaanti Nirmala ka peechhaa karatee rahatee theen. agar vah kothe par khadee hai, to zaroor kisee par nigaah daal rahee hogee; maharee se baat karatee hai, to zaroor hee unakee nindaa karatee hogee; baajaar se kuchh manggavaatee hai, to zaroor koee vilaas-vastu hogee. vah baraabar usake patr padhane ki koshish kiyaa karatee, chhip-chhip kar usakee baaten sunaa karateen. Nirmala unakee dodhaaree talavaar se kaampatee rahatee thee. yahan tak ki usane ek din shauhar se kahaa-aap jaraa jeejee ko samajhaa deejie, kyon mere peechhe padee rahatee hain?

totaaraam ne tej hokar kahaa-kyaa tumhe kuchh kahaa hai kyaa?

roj hee kahatee hain. baat munh se nikalanee mushkil hai. agar unhen is baat ki jalan ho ki yah maalikin kyon banee huee hai, to aap unheen ko rupaye-paise deejie, mujhe n chahiye; vahee maalikin banee rahen. main to keval itanaa chaahatee hoon ki koee mujhe taane-mehane n diyaa kare.

yah kahate-kahate Nirmala ki aangkhon se aansoo bahane lage. totaaraam ko apanaa pyaar dikhaane ka yah bahut hee achchha maukaa milaa. bole-main aaj hee unakee khabar loonggaa. saaph kah doonggaa, agar munh band karake rahanaa hai, to raho; nahin to apanee raah lo. is ghar ki svaaminee vah nahin hain, tum ho. vah keval tumhaaree madad ke lie hain. agar madad karane ke badale tumhe dikat karatee hain, to unake yahan rahane ki jaroorat hee naheen. mainne to sochaa tha vidhavaa hain, anaath hain, paavabhar aataa khaayanggee; padee rahenggee. jab aur naukar-chaakar khaa rahe hain, to yah to apanee bahin hee hain. ladakon ki dekh-bhaal ke lie ek aurat ki jaroorat bhi thi, rakh liyaa; lekin isake yah maane nahin hain ki vah tumhaare oopar shaasan karen.

Nirmala ne phir kahaa-ladakon ko sikhaa detee hain ki jaakar maan se paise maanggo, kabhee kuchh-kabhee kuchha. ladake aakar meree jaan khaate hain. ghadee bhar letanaa mushkil ho jaataa hai. daatatee hoon, to vah okhen laal-peelee karake daudatee hain. mujhe samajhatee hain ki ladakon ko dekh kar jalatee hai. Bhagwan jaanate hongge ki main bachchon ko kitanaa pyaar karatee hoon. aakhir mere hee bachche to hain. mujhe unase kyon jalan hone lagee.

totaaraam krodh se kaamp uthe. vole-tumhe jo ladakaa dik kare, use peet diyaa karo. main bhi dekhataa hoon ki launde shareer ho gae hain. mansaaraam ko to main bordinggg haaus mein bhej doonggaa. baaknee donon ko aaj hee theek kie detaa hoon.

us vakt totaaraam kachaharee jaa rahe the. daat-dapat karane ka maukaa n tha; lekin kachaharee se lautate hee unhonne ghar mein aakar rukminee se kahaa-kyon vahin, tumhe is ghar mein rahanaa hai ya naheen. agar rahanaa hai, to shaant hokar raho. yah kyaa ki doosaron ka rahanaa mushkil kar do.

rukminee samajh gaee ki bahoo ne apanaa vaar kiyaa; par vah davane vaalee aurat n thi. ek to umr mein badee, tis par isee ghar ki sevaa mein jindagee kaat dee thee. kisakee majaal thi ki unhen vedakhal kar de. unhen bhaaee ki is kshudrataa par hairaanee huaa. boleen-to kyaa laundee banaa kar rakkhoge. laundee ban kar rahanaa hai, to is ghar ki laundee n banoonggee. agar tumhaaree yah ichchhaa ho ki ghar mein koeeaag lagaa de aur main khadee dekhaa karoon. kisee ko veraah chalate dekh, to chup saadh loon. jo jisake man mein aae kare; main mittee ki devee banee baithee rahoon, to yah mujhase n hogaa. yah huaa kyaa, jo tum itanaa aape se baahar ho rahe ho. nikal gaee saaree buddhimaanee, kal ki laudiyaa chotee pakad kar nachaane lagee. kuchh poochhanaa n goochhanaa, bas usane taar kheenchaa; aur tum kaath ke sipaahee ki tarah talavaar nikaal kar khade ho gae.

totaao-sunataa to hoon ki tum hameshaa khuchar nikaalatee rahatee ho, baat-baat par taane detee ho. agar kuchh seekh denee ho, to use pyaar se, meethe shabdon mein denee chaahie. taanon se seekh milane ke badale ulataa aur jee jalane lagataa hai.

rukminee-to tumhaaree yahee marjee hai ki kisee baat mein n boloon, yahee sahee! lekin phir yah n kahanaa ki tum to ghar mein baithee thi, kyon nahin salaah dee. jab meree baaten jahar lagatee hain, to mujhe kyaa kutte ne kaataa hai, jo boloon. masal hai-'naaton khetee, bahuriyon ghar' main bhi dekhoo bahuriyaa kaise ghar chalaatee hai.

itane mein siyaaraam aur jiyaaraam skool se aa gae. aate hee aate donon buaa jee ke paas jaakar khaane ko maanggane lage.
rukminee ne kahaa — jaakar apanee naee ammaan se kyon nahin maanggate, mujhe bolane ka hukm nahin hai. totaao-agar tum logon ne us ghar mein kadam rakkhe, to taangg tod doonnggaa. vadamaashee par kamar bodhee hai.

jiyaaraam jaraa shaangkh thaa. bolaa-unako to aap kuchh nahin kahate, hee ko dhamakaate hain. kabhee paise nahin detee.

siyaaraam ne is baat ka anumodan kiyaa-kahatee hain mujhe dik kabeemaareee, to kaan kaat loonggee. kahatee hain ki nahin jiyaa?

Nirmala apane kamare se bolee-main ne kab kahaa tha ki tumhaare kaan kaat loonggee. abhee se jhooth bolane lage.

itanaa sunanaa tha ki totaaraam ne siyaaraam ke donon kaan pakad kar uthaa liyaa. ladakaa jor se cheekh maar kar rone lagaa.

rukminee ne daud kar bachche ko munshee jee ke haath se chhudaa liyaa aur voleen-vas, rahane bhi do; kyaa vachche ko maar hee daaloge? haay-haay! kaan laal ho gayaa. sach kahaa hai, naee beevee paakar
aadmi andhaa ho jaataa hai. abhee se yah haal hai, to is ghar ke Bhagwan hee maalik hain.

Nirmala apanee vijay par man hee man khush ho rahee thi; lekinajavamunsheejee ne bachche ka kaan pakad kar uthaa liyaa, to usase n rahaa gayaa. chhudaane ko daudee; par rukminee pahale hee pahunch gaee thee. bolee-pahale aag lagaa dee, ab, bujhaane daudee ho. jab apane ladake hongge, tab aankhen khulenggee. paraaee peer kyaa jaano?
Nirmala-khade to hain; poochh lo n, mainne kyaa aag lagaa dee? main ne itanaa hee kahaa tha ki ladake mujhe paison ke lie baar-baar dik karate hain. isake sivaa jo mere munh se kuchh aur nikalaa ho, to meree aankhen phoot jaaen.

totaao-main khud in laudon ki sharaarat dekhaa karataa hoon, andhaa thode hee hoon. teenon jiddee aur shareer ho gae hain. bade miyaan ko to main aaj hee hostal mein bhejataa hoon.

rukminee-ab tak to tumhe inakee koee sharaarat n soojhatee thi, aaj aankhen kyon itanee tej ho gaee?

totaaraam-tumheen ne inhen itanaa shokh kar rakkhaa hai.

rukminee-to main hee vish ki gaanth hoon. mere hee kaaran tumhaaraa ghar chaupat ho rahaa hai. lo, main jaatee hoon. tumhaare ladake hain; maaro chaahe kaato, main n boloonggee.

yah kah kar wahan se chalee gaee. Nirmala bachche ko rote dekh kar vihval ho uthee. usane use chhaatee se lagaa liyaa, aur god mein lie hue apane kamare mein laakar use chusakaarane lagee, lekin bachcha aur bhi sisak-sisak kar rone lagaa. usakaa abodh dil is pyaar mein vah maatri-pyaar n paataa tha, jisase dev ne use vanychit kar diyaa thaa. yah vaatsaly n tha, keval taras thee. yah vah vastu thi, jis par usakaa koee hak n tha, jo keval bhiksh ke roop mein use dee jaa rahee thi, pitaa ne pahale bhi do-ek baar maaraa tha, jab usakee maan zinda thee. lekin tab usakee maan use chhaatee se lagaa kar rotee n thee. vah akhush hokar usase bolanaa chhod detee, yahan tak ki vah svayan thodee hee der ke baad sab kuchh bhool kar phir maataa ke paas daudaa jaataa thaa. sharaarat ke liye sajaa paanaa to usakee samajh mein aataa tha, lekin maar khaane par chumakaaraa jaanaa usakee samajh mein n aataa thaa. maatri-pyaar mein sakhtataa hotee thi, lekin mridulataa se milee huee. is pyaar mein taras thi; par vah sakhtataa n thi, jo aatmeeyataa ka gupt sandesh hai. svasth anggg ki paravaah kaun karataa hai? lekin vahee anggg jav kisee dard se tapakane lagataa hai, to use thes aur dhakke se bachaane ka yatn kiyaa jaataa hai. Nirmala ka karna rodan bachcha ko usake anaath hone ki khabar de rahaa thaa. vah badee der tak Nirmala ki god mein baithaa rotaa rahaa aur rote-rote so gayaa. Nirmala ne use chaarapaaee par sulaanaa chaahaa to bachcha ne sushuptaavasthaa mein apanee donon komal vaahen usakee gardan mein daal dee; aur aisaa chipat gayaa, maano neeche koee gadhaa hai. shanggkaa aur dar se usakaa munh vikrit ho gayaa. Nirmala ne phir bachcha ko god mein uthaa liyaa, chaarapaaee par n sulaa sakee. is samay bachcha ko god mein lie hue use vah tushti ho rahee thi, jo ab tak kabhee n huee thee. aaj pahalee baar use vah aatm-dard huee, jisake binaa aankhen nahin khulatee, apanaa farz-raastaa nahin soojhataa. vah raastaa av dikhaaee dene lagaa.
