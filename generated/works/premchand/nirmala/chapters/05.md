# Nirmala — Adhyay 5

**Munshi Premchand**

panchvam parichchhed

NAmENirmala ka shadi ho gaya. sasural a gai. vakil sahab ka nam tha munshi NAmETotaram. samvle ranga ke mote-taze aadmi the. umra to abhi chalis se zyada na thi, par vakalat ke mushkil mehnat ne sir ke bal paka die the. vyayam karne ka unhen phursat na milta tha. yaham tak ki kabhi kahim ghumne bhi na jate, isalie tond nikal ai thi. deh ke sthul hote hue bhi ae din koi na koi shikayat hoti rahti! mandagni aur bavasir se to unka chirasthayi rishta tha. isalie bahut phunk-phunk kar qadam rakhte the. unke tin larke the. bada NAmEmansaram solah varsh ka tha, manjhla NAmEJiyaram barah aur chhota NAmESiyaram sat varsh ka. tinon angarezi parhte the. ghar men vakil sahab ki vidhva bahin ke siva koi aurat na thi.
vahi ghar ki malikin thim. unka nam tha NAmERukmini; aur avastha pachas se upar thi. sasural men koi na tha. sthayi riti se yahim rahti thim.

NAmETotaram dampati-vijnyan men kushal the. NAmENirmala ko khush rakhne ke lie unmen jo svabhavik kami thi, use vah upharo se puri karni chahte the. halanki bahut hi mitavyayi aadmi the; par NAmENirmala ke lie koi na koi tohpha roz laya karte. mauqe par paisa ki parvah na karte the. khud kabhi nashta na karte the, larke ke lie thoda-thoda dudh ata tha; par NAmENirmala ke lie meve, murabbe, mithaiyam-kisi chiz ki kami na thi. apni jindagi men kabhi sair-tamashe dekhne na gae the; par ab chhuttiyon men NAmENirmala ko sinema, sarkas, thiyetar dikhane le jate. apne bahumulya samay ka thoda sa hissa uske sath baith kar gramofon bajane men bhi vyatit kiya karte the.

lekin NAmENirmala ko na jane kyon NAmETotaram ke pas baithne aur hamsne-bolne men sankocha hota tha. iska shayad yah vajah tha ki ab tak aisa hi ek aadmi uska pita tha, jiske samne vah sir jhuka kar, deh chura kar, niklati thi; ab uski avastha ka ek aadmi uska pati tha. vah use pyaar ki vastu nahin, izzat ki vastu samjhati thi. unse bhagti phirti, unko dekhte hi uski praphullata palayan kar jati thi!

vakil sahab ko unke dampati-vijnyan ne sikhaya tha ka
yuvti ke samne khub pyaar ki baten karni chahiye-dil nikal kar rakh dena chahiye. yahi uske vashikran ka mukhya mantra hai. isalie vakil sahav apne pyaar-pradarshan men koi kasar na rakhte the; lekin NAmENirmala ko in baton se naphrat hoti thi. vahi baten, jinhen kisi yuvak ke mumh se sun kar uska dil pyaar se unmatt ho jata, vakil sahab ke mumh se nikal kar uske dil par shar ke saman aghat karti thim! unmen ras na tha, khushi na tha, unmad na tha, dil na tha, sirph banavat thi, dhokha tha; aur tha shushk, niras shabdadambar! use itra aur tel bura na lagta, sair-tamashe bure na lagte, banav-singar bhi bura na lagta; bura lagta tha sirph NAmETotaram ke pas baithna. vah apna rup aur yauvan unhen na dikhana chahti thi, kyunki vaham dekhne vali ankhen na thim. vah unhen in rason ka asvadan karne ke yogya hi na samjhati thi. kali prabhat-samir hi ke sparsh se khilti haim. donon men saman sarasya hai. NAmENirmala ke lie vah prabhat-samir kaham thi?

pahla mahina gujrate hi NAmETotaram ne NAmENirmala ko apna khajanchi bana liya. kachhari se akar din bhar ki kamai use de dete. unka khyal tha ki NAmENirmala in rupyon ko dekh kar phuli na samaegi. NAmENirmala bade shauk se is pad ka kam ajam deti. ek-ek paise ka hisab likhti, agar kabhi rupye kam milte, to puchhti-aj kam kyon haim? ghar-parivar ke rishta men unse
khub vaten karti. inhim baton ke layak vah unko samjhati thi. jyomhi koi vinod ki bat unke nikal jati, uska mumh malin ho jata tha!

NAmENirmala jab vastrabhushnon se alankat hokar aine ke samne khadi hoti; aur usmen apne saundarya ki sushamapurn abha dekhti, to uska dil ek satrishna chah se tarap uthta tha. us vakt uske dil men ek jvala si uthti. man men ata is ghar men ag laga dum. apni mam par gussa ata, pita par gussa ata, apne bhagya par gussa ata; par sabse zyada gussa bechare nirapradh NAmETotaram par ata, vah hamesha is tap se jala karti thi. vanka savar burhe ladd, tatt par savar hona kav pasand karega, chahe use paidal hi kyon na chalna pade. NAmENirmala ki hal usi banke savar ki thi. vah us par savar hokar udna chahti thi, us ullasmayi vidyut-gati ka khushi uthana chahti thi, tatu ke hinahinane aur kanautiyam khadi karne se kya ummid hoti? mumkin tha ki bachchon ke sath hamsne-khelne se vah apni hal ko thodi der ke lie bhul jati, kuchh man hara ho jata; lekin NAmERukmini devi larkon ko uske pas phatkane bhi na detim, mano vah koi pishachini hai, jo unhen nigal jaygi. ruknimi devi ka svabhav sare samsar se nirala tha; yah pata lagana mushkil tha ki vah kis bat se khush hoti thim; aur kis bat se naraj! ek bar jis bat se khush ho jati thim, dusri:

bar usi bat se jal jati thim. agar NAmENirmala apne kamre men vaithi rahti, to kahtim na jane kaham ki manahusin hai, agar vah kothe par charh jati ya mahriyo se baten karti, to chhati pitne lagtim. laj hai na sharam nigori ne haya bhun khai; ab kya? kuchh dinon men bajar men nachegi. jab se vakil sahab ne NAmENirmala ke hath men rupye-paise dene shuru kie, NAmERukmini uski alochna karne par arurh ho gai thi. use malum hota tha ki ab pralay hone men bahut-thodi kasar rah gai hai. larkon ko bar-bar paison ki jarurat parti. jab tak khud svamini thi, unhen vahla diya karti thi. ab sidhe NAmENirmala ke pas bhej deti. NAmENirmala ko larkon ka chatorapan achchha na lagta tha. kabhi-kabhi paise dene se inkar kar deti. NAmERukmini ko apne vagban sar karne ka avsar mil jata-ab to malikin hui haim, larke kahe ko jienge. bin mam ke bachche ko kaun puchhe? rupyon ki mithaiyam kha jate the, ab dhele-dhele ko tarsate haim. NAmENirmala agaram chirh kar kisi din vina kuchh puchhe-guchhe paise de deti, to devi ji uski dusri ho alochna kartim inhen kya, larke maren ya jien, inki vala se; mam ke vina kaun samjhave ki beta bahut mithaiyam mat khao? ainai to mere sir jaygi, unhen kya! yahim tak hota to NAmENirmala shayad japt kar jati; par devi ji khuphiya pulis ke sipahi ki tarah NAmENirmala ka pichha karti rahti thim. agar vah kothe par khadi hai, to zarur kisi par nigah dal rahi hogi;
mahri se bat karti hai, to zarur hi unki ninda karti hogi; bajar se kuchh mangvati hai, to zarur koi vilas-vastu hogi. vah barabar uske chitthi parhne ki cheshta kiya karti, chhip-chhip kar uski baten suna kartim. NAmENirmala unki dodhari talvar se kampti rahti thi. yaham tak ki usne ek din pati se kaha-ap jara jiji ko samjha dijie, kyon mere pichhe padi rahti haim?

NAmETotaram ne tez hokar kaha-kya tumhen kuchh kaha hai kya?

roj hi kahti haim. bat mumh se niklani mushkil hai. agar unhen is bat ki jalan ho ki yah malikin kyon bani hui hai, to ap unhim ko rupae-paise dijie, mujhe na chahiye; vahi malikin bani rahen. maim to sirph itna chahti hum ki koi mujhe tane-mehne na diya kare.

yah kahte-kahte NAmENirmala ki ankhon se amsu bahne lage. NAmETotaram ko apna pyaar dikhane ka yah bahut hi achchha mauka mila. bole-maim aj hi unki khabar lunga. saph kah dunga, agar mumh band karke rahna hai, to raho; nahin to apni rah lo. is ghar ki svamini vah nahin haim, tum ho. vah sirph tumhari madad ke lie haim. agar madad karne ke badle tumhen dikat karti haim, to unke yaham rahne ki jarurat hi nahin. maimne to socha tha vidhva haim, anath haim, pavbhar ata khayangi; padi rahengi. jab aur naukar-chakar kha rahe haim, to yah to apni bahin hi haim. larkon
ki dekh-bhal ke lie ek aurat ki jarurat bhi thi, rakh liya; lekin iske yah mane nahin haim ki vah tumhare upar shasan karen.

NAmENirmala ne phir kaha-larkon ko sikha deti haim ki jakar mam se paise mango, kabhi kuchh-kabhi kuchh. larke akar meri jan khate haim. ghadi bhar letna mushkil ho jata hai. datti hum, to vah okhen lal-pili karke daurti haim. mujhe samjhati haim ki larkon ko dekh kar jalti hai. ishvar jante honge ki maim bachchon ko kitna pyaar karti hum. akhir mere hi bachche to haim. mujhe unse kyon jalan hone lagi.

NAmETotaram gussa se kamp uthe. vole-tumhen jo larka diq kare, use pit diya karo. maim bhi dekhta hum ki launde sharir ho gae haim. NAmEmansaram ko to maim bordinga haus men bhej dunga. bakni donon ko aj hi thik kie deta hum.

us vakt NAmETotaram kachhari ja rahe the. dat-dapat karne ka mauka na tha; lekin kachhari se lautte hi unhomne ghar men akar NAmERukmini se kaha-kyon vahin, tumhen is ghar men rahna hai ya nahin. agar rahna hai, to shant hokar raho. yah kya ki dusron ka rahna mushkil kar do.

NAmERukmini samajh gai ki bahu ne apna var kiya; par vah davne vali aurat na thi. ek to umra men badi, tis par isi ghar ki seva men jindagi kat di thi. kiski majal thi ki unhen vedkhal kar de. unhen bhai ki is kshudrata par hairani hua.
bolim-to kya laundi bana kar rakkhoge. laundi ban kar rahna hai, to is ghar ki laundi na banungi. agar tumhari yah chah ho ki ghar men koiag laga de aur maim khadi dekha karum. kisi ko verah chalte dekh, to chup sadh lum. jo jiske man men ae kare; maim mitti ki devi bani baithi rahum, to yah mujhse na hoga. yah hua kya, jo tum itna ape se bahar ho rahe ho. nikal gai sari buddhimani, kal ki laudiya choti pakad kar nachane lagi. kuchh puchhna na guchhna, bas usne tar khincha; aur tum kath ke sipahi ki tarah talvar nikal kar khade ho gae.

totao-sunta to hum ki tum hamesha khuchar nikalti rahti ho, bat-bat par tane deti ho. agar kuchh sikh deni ho, to use pyaar se, mithe shabdon men deni chahiye. tanon se sikh milne ke badle ulta aur ji jalne lagta hai.

NAmERukmini -to tumhari yahi marji hai ki kisi bat men na bolum, yahi sahi! lekin phir yah na kahna ki tum to ghar men baithi thi, kyon nahin salah di. jab meri baten jahar lagti haim, to mujhe kya kutte ne kata hai, jo bolum. masal hai-naton kheti, bahuriyon ghar maim bhi dekhu bahuriya kaise ghar chalati hai.

itne men NAmESiyaram aur NAmEJiyaram skul se a gae. ate hi ate donon bua ji ke pas jakar khane ko mangne lage.
NAmERukmini ne kahajakar apni nai ammam se kyon nahin mangte, mujhe bolne ka hukma nahin hai.
totao-agar tum logon ne us ghar men qadam rakkhe, to tang tod dumnga. vadmashi par kamar bodhi hai.

NAmEJiyaram jara shankh tha. bola-unko to ap kuchh nahin kahte, hi ko dhamkate haim. kabhi paise nahin deti.

NAmESiyaram ne is kathan ka anumodan kiya-kahti haim mujhe diq karoge, to kan kat lungi. kahti haim ki nahin jiya?

NAmENirmala apne kamre se boli-maim ne kab kaha tha ki tumhare kan kat lungi. abhi se jhuth bolne lage.

itna sunna tha ki NAmETotaram ne NAmESiyaram ke donon kan pakad kar utha liya. larka zor se chikh mar kar rone laga.

NAmERukmini ne daur kar bachche ko munshi ji ke hath se chhura liya aur volim-vas, rahne bhi do; kya vachche ko mar hi daloge? hay-hay! kan lal ho gaya. sach kaha hai, nai bivi pakad
aadmi andha ho jata hai. abhi se yah hal hai, to is ghar ke bhagavan hi malik haim.

NAmENirmala apni vijay par man hi man khush ho rahi thi; lekinajavamunshiji ne bachche ka kan pakad kar utha liya, to usse na raha gaya. chhurane ko dauri; par NAmERukmini pahle hi pahunch gai thi. boli-pahle ag laga di, ab, bujhane dauri ho. jab apne larke honge, tab ankhen khulengi. parai pir kya jano?
NAmENirmala -khade to haim; puchh lo na, maimne kya ag laga di? maim ne itna hi kaha tha ki larke mujhe paison ke lie bar-bar diq
karte haim. iske siva jo mere mumh se kuchh aur nikla ho, to meri ankhen phut jaen.

totao-maim khud in laudon ki shararat dekha karta hum, andha thore hi hum. tinon jiddi aur sharir ho gae haim. bade miyam ko to maim aj hi hostal men bhejta hum.

NAmERukmini -ab tak to tumhen inki koi shararat na sujhti thi, aj ankhen kyon itni tez ho gai?

NAmETotaram -tumhim ne inhen itna shokh kar rakkha hai.

NAmERukmini -to maim hi vish ki ganth hum. mere hi karan tumhara ghar chaupat ho raha hai. lo, maim jati hum. tumhare larke haim; maro chahe kato, maim na bolungi.

yah kah kar vaham se chali gai. NAmENirmala bachche ko rote dekh kar vihval ho uthi. usne use chhati se laga liya, aur god men lie hue apne kamre men lakar use chusakarne lagi, lekin larka aur bhi sisak-sisak kar rone laga. uska nasmajh dil is pyaar men vah matri-pyaar na pata tha, jisse dev ne use vanchit kar diya tha. yah vatsalya na tha, sirph daya thi. yah vah vastu thi, jis par uska koi adhikar na tha, jo sirph bhiksha ke rup men use di ja rahi thi, pita ne pahle bhi do-ek bar mara tha, jab uski mam zinda thi. lekin tab uski mam use chhati se laga kar roti na thi. vah aprasann hokar usse bolna chhod deti, yaham tak ki vah svayam thodi hi der ke bad
sab kuchh bhul kar phir mam ke pas daura jata tha. shararat ke liye saja pana to uski samajh men ata tha, lekin mar khane par chumkara jana uski samajh men na ata tha. matri-pyaar men kathorta hoti thi, lekin mridulta se mili hui. is pyaar men daya thi; par vah kathorta na thi, jo atmiyta ka gupt paigam hai. svasth anga ki parvah kaun karta hai? lekin vahi anga jav kisi dard se tapkane lagta hai, to use thes aur dhakke se bachane ka yatna kiya jata hai. NAmENirmala ka karan rodan larka ko uske anath hone ki khabar de raha tha. vah badi der tak NAmENirmala ki god men baitha rota raha aur rote-rote so gaya. NAmENirmala ne use charapai par sulana chaha to larka ne sushuptavastha men apni donon komal vahen uski gardan men dal di; aur aisa chipat gaya, mano niche koi garha hai. shanka aur dar se uska mumh vikrit ho gaya. NAmENirmala ne phir larka ko god men utha liya, charapai par na sula saki. is samay larka ko god men lie hue use vah tushti ho rahi thi, jo ab tak kabhi na hui thi. aj pahli bar use vah atma-dard hui, jiske bina ankhen nahin khulti, apna pharz-rasta nahin sujhta. vah rasta av dikhai dene laga.
