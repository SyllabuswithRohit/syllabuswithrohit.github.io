# Nirmala — Adhyay 5

**Munshi Premchand**

paanchavaan parichchheda

nirmalaa kaa vivaaha ho gaya| sasuraala aa gaee| vakeela saahaba kaa naama tha munshee totaaraama| saanvale ranga ke mote-taaze aadmi the| umra to abhee chaaleesa se adhika na thi, par vakaalata ke kathina parishrama ne sira ke baala pakaa die the| vyaayaama karane kaa unhen avakaasha na milata tha| yahaan taka ki kabhee kaheen ghoomane bhee na jaate, isalie tonda nikala aaee thi| deha ke sthoola hote hue bhee aae din koee na koee shikaayata hoti rahatee! mandaagni aur bavaaseera se to unakaa chirasthaayee sambandha tha| ataeva bahut phoonka-phoonka kar qadama rakhate the| unake teena ladke the| bada mansaaraama solaha varsha kaa tha, manjhalaa jiyaaraama baaraha aur chhota siyaaraama saata varsha kaa| teenon angarezee pa.dhate the| ghar men vakeela saahaba kee vidhavaa bahina ke sivaa koee aurat na thi| vahee ghar kee maalikina theen| unakaa naama tha rukminee; aur avasthaa pachaasa se oopara thi| sasuraala men koee na tha| sthaayee reeti se yaheen rahatee theen|

totaaraama dampati-vijnaana men kushala the| nirmalaa ko prasanna rakhane ke lie unamen jo svaabhaavika kamee thi, use vaha upahaaron se pooree karanee chaahate the| yadyapi bahut hee mitavyayee purusha the; par nirmalaa ke lie koee na koee tohaphaa roza laayaa karte| mauqe par dhana kee paravaaha na karte the| khuda kabhee naashtaa na karte the, ladke ke lie tho.daa-tho.daa doodha aata tha; par nirmalaa ke lie meve, murabbe, mithaaiyaan-kisee cheeza kee kamee na thi| apanee zindagi men kabhee saira-tamaashe dekhane na gae the; par ab chhuttiyon men nirmalaa ko sinema, sarakasa, thiyetara dikhaane le jaate| apane bahumoolya samay kaa tho.daa saa hissaa usake saatha baitha kar graamofona bajaane men bhee vyateeta kiya karte the|

lekin nirmalaa ko na jaane kyon totaaraama ke paasa baithane aur hansane-bolane men sankocha hota tha| isakaa kadaachit yaha kaarana tha ki ab taka aisaa hee ek aadmi usakaa pitaa tha, jisake saamane vaha sira jhukaa kar, deha churaa kar, nikalatee thi; ab usakee avasthaa kaa ek aadmi usakaa pati tha| vaha use prema kee vastu naheen, sammaana kee vastu samajhatee thi| unase bhaagatee phiratee, unako dekhate hee usakee praphullata palaayana kar jati thi!

vakeela saahaba ko unake dampati-vijnaana ne sikhaayaa tha kaa yuvatee ke saamane khooba prema kee baaten karanee chaahie-dil nikaala kar rakha dena chaahie| yahee usake vasheekarana kaa mukhya mantra hai| isalie vakeela saahava apane prema-pradarshana men koee kasara na rakhate the; lekin nirmalaa ko ina baaton se ghrinaa hoti thi| vahee baaten, jinhen kisee yuvaka ke mukha se suna kar usakaa hridaya prema se unmatta ho jaata, vakeela saahaba ke munha se nikala kar usake hridaya par shara ke samaana aaghaata karti theen! unamen rasa na tha, ullaasa na tha, unmaada na tha, hridaya na tha, kevala banaavata thi, dhokhaa tha; aur tha shushka, neerasa shabdaadambara ! use itra aur tela buraa na lagata, saira-tamaashe bure na lagate, banaava-singaara bhee buraa na lagata; buraa lagata tha kevala totaaraama ke paasa baithana | vaha apana roopa aur yauvana unhen na dikhaanaa chaahatee thi, kyunki vahaan dekhane vaalee aankhen na theen| vaha unhen ina rason kaa aasvaadana karane ke yogya hee na samajhatee thi| kalee prabhaata-sameera hee ke sparsha se khilatee hain| donon men samaana saarasya hai| nirmalaa ke lie vaha prabhaata-sameera kahaan thi?

pahalaa maheenaa gujarate hee totaaraama ne nirmalaa ko apana khajaanchee bana liya| kachaharee se aakara din bhara kee kamaaee use de dete | unakaa khayal tha ki nirmalaa ina rupayon ko dekha kar phoolee na samaaegee| nirmalaa ba.de shauka se is pada kaa kaama ajaama detee| ek-ek paise kaa hisaaba likhatee, agar kabhee rupaye kama milate, to poochhatee-aaja kama kyon hain ? grihasthee ke sambandha men unase khooba vaaten karti| inheen baaton ke laayaka vaha unako samajhatee thi| jyonhee koee vinoda kee baat unake nikala jati, usakaa mukha malina ho jata tha!

nirmalaa jab vastraabhooshanon se alankata hokara aaeene ke saamane kha.dee hoti; aur usamen apane saundarya kee sushamaapoorna aabhaa dekhatee, to usakaa hridaya ek satrishna kaamana se ta.dapa uthata tha|us vakta usake hridaya men ek jvaalaa see uthatee| mann men aata is ghar men aaga lagaa doon| apanee maataa par krodha aata, pitaa par krodha aata, apane bhaagya par krodha aata; par sabase adhika krodha bechaare niraparaadha totaaraama par aata,vaha sadaiva is taapa se jalaa karti thi| vaankaa savaara boo.dhe ladda, tatta par savaara honaa kava pasanda karega, chaahe use paidala hee kyon na chalana pa.de| nirmalaa kee dashaa usee baanke savaara kee thi| vaha us par savaara hokara u.dana chaahatee thi, us ullaasamayee vidyuta-gati kaa aananda uthaanaa chaahatee thi, tatoo ke hinahinaane aur kanautiyaan kha.dee karane se kyaa aashaa hoti?sambhava tha ki bachchon ke saatha hansane-khelane se vaha apanee dashaa ko tho.dee dera ke lie bhoola jati, kuchha mann haraa ho jaata; lekin rukminee devee ladakon ko usake paasa phatakane bhee na deteen, maano vaha koee pishaachinee hai, jo unhen nigala jaayagee| ruknimee devee kaa svabhaava saare sansaara se niraalaa tha; yaha pata lagaanaa kathina tha ki vaha kisa baat se khusha hoti theen; aur kisa baat se naaraaja!ek baara jisa baat se khusha ho jati theen, doosaree: baara usee baat se jala jati theen| agar nirmalaa apane kamare men vaithee rahatee, to kahateen na jaane kahaan kee manahoosina hai, agar vaha kothe par cha.dha jati yaa mahariyon se baaten karti, to chhaatee peetane lagateen| laaja hai na sharama nigo.dee ne hayaa bhoona khaaee; ab kyaa ? kuchha dinon men baajaara men naachegee| jab se vakeela saahaba ne nirmalaa ke haath men rupaye-paise dene shuroo kie, rukminee usakee aalochana karane par aaroo.dha ho gaee thi| use maalooma hota tha ki ab pralaya hone men bahut-tho.dee kasara raha gaee hai| ladakon ko baara-baara paison kee jaroorata pa.datee| jab taka khuda svaaminee thi, unhen vahalaa diya karti thi| ab seedhe nirmalaa ke paasa bheja detee| nirmalaa ko ladakon kaa chatoraapana achchha na lagata tha| kabhee-kabhee paise dene se inkaara kar detee| rukminee ko apane vaagbaana sara karane kaa avasara mila jata-ab to maalikina hui hain, ladke kaahe ko jienge| bina maan ke bachche ko kaun poochhe ?rupayon kee mithaaiyaan khaa jaate the, ab dhele-dhele ko tarasate hain| nirmalaa agaran chi.dha kar kisee din vinaa kuchha poochhe-guchhe paise de detee, to devee jee usakee doosaree ho aalochana karateen inhen kya, ladke maren yaa jien, inakee valaa se; maan ke vinaa kaun samajhaave ki betaa bahut mithaaiyaan mata khaao ? aaeenaaee to mere sira jaayagee, unhen kyaa ! yaheen taka hota to nirmalaa shaayada japta kar jati; par devee jee khuphiya puleesa ke sipaahee kee bhaanti nirmalaa kaa peechhaa karti rahatee theen | agar vaha kothe par kha.dee hai, to avashya kisee par nigaaha daala rahi hogee; maharee se baat karti hai, to avashya hee unakee nindaa karti hogee; baajaara se kuchha mangavaatee hai, to avashya koee vilaasa-vastu hogee| vaha baraabara usake patra pa.dhane kee cheshtaa kiya karti, chhipa-chhipa kar usakee baaten sunaa karateen| nirmalaa unakee dodhaaree talavaara se kaanpatee rahatee thi| yahaan taka ki usane ek din pati se kaha-aapa jaraa jeejee ko samajhaa deejie, kyon mere peechhe pa.dee rahatee hain?

totaaraama ne teza hokara kaha-kyaa tumhen kuchha kaha hai kya?

roja hee kehti hain| baat munha se nikalanee mushkila hai| agar unhen is baat kee jalana ho ki yaha maalikina kyon banee hui hai, to aapa unheen ko rupae-paise deejie, mujhe na chaahie; vahee maalikina banee rahen| main to kevala itana chaahatee hoon ki koee mujhe taane-mehane na diya kare|

yaha kehte-kehte nirmalaa kee aankhon se aansoo bahane lage| totaaraama ko apana prema dikhaane kaa yaha bahut hee achchha maukaa milaa| bole-main aaja hee unakee khabara loongaa| saapha kaha doonga,agar munha banda karake rahana hai, to raho; naheen to apanee raaha lo| is ghar kee svaaminee vaha naheen hain, tuma ho| vaha kevala tumhaaree sahaayata ke lie hain| agar sahaayata karane ke badale tumhen dikata karti hain, to unake yahaan rahane kee jaroorata hee naheen| mainne to sochaa tha vidhavaa hain, anaatha hain, paavabhara aata khaayangee; pa.dee rahengee| jab aur naukara-chaakara khaa rahe hain, to yaha to apanee bahina hee hain| ladakon kee dekha-bhaala ke lie ek aurat kee jaroorata bhee thi, rakha liya; lekin isake yaha maane naheen hain ki vaha tumhaare oopara shaasana karen|

nirmalaa ne phir kaha-ladakon ko sikhaa detee hain ki jaakara maan se paise maango, kabhee kuchha-kabhee kuchha| ladke aakara meree jaana khaate hain| gha.dee bhara letana mushkila ho jata hai| daatatee hoon, to vaha okhen laala-peelee karake dau.datee hain| mujhe samajhatee hain ki ladakon ko dekha kar jalatee hai| eeshvara jaanate honge ki main bachchon ko kitana pyaar karti hoon| aakhira mere hee bachche to hain|mujhe unase kyon jalana hone lagee|

totaaraama krodha se kaanpa uthe| vole-tumhen jo ladka diqa kare, use peeta diya karo| main bhee dekhata hoon ki launde shareera ho gae hain| mansaaraama ko to main bordinga haausa men bheja doongaa | baaknee donon ko aaja hee theeka kie detaa hoon|

us vakta totaaraama kachaharee jaa rahe the | daata-dapata karane kaa maukaa na tha; lekin kachaharee se lautate hee unhonne ghar men aakara rukminee se kaha-kyon vahina, tumhen is ghar men rahana hai yaa naheen| agar rahana hai, to shaanta hokara raho| yaha kyaa ki doosaron kaa rahana mushkila kar do|

rukminee samajha gaee ki bahoo ne apana vaara kiya; par vaha davane vaalee aurat na thi | ek to umra men ba.dee, tisa par isee ghar kee sevaa men zindagi kaata dee thi| kisakee majaala thi ki unhen vedakhala kar de| unhen bhaaee kee is kshudrata par aashcharya hua | boleen-to kyaa laundee bana kar rakkhoge| laundee bana kar rahana hai, to is ghar kee laundee na banoongee| agar tumhaaree yaha ichchhaa ho ki ghar men koeeaaga lagaa de aur main kha.dee dekha karoon| kisee ko veraaha chalate dekha, to chupa saadha loon| jo jisake mann men aae kare; main mittee kee devee banee baithee rahoon, to yaha mujhase na hogaa| yaha hua kya, jo tuma itana aape se baahara ho rahe ho| nikala gaee saaree buddhimaanee, kala kee laudiya chotee paka.da kar nachaane lagee| kuchha poochhana na goochhana, basa usane taara kheencha; aur tuma kaatha ke sipaahee kee tarah talavaara nikaala kar kha.de ho gae|

totaao-sunata to hoon ki tuma hameshaa khuchara nikaalatee rahatee ho, baat-baat par taane detee ho|agar kuchha seekha denee ho, to use pyaar se, meethe shabdon men denee chaahie| taanon se seekha milane ke badale ulata aur jee jalane lagata hai|

rukminee-to tumhaaree yahee marjee hai ki kisee baat men na boloon, yahee sahee!lekin phir yaha na kahana ki tuma to ghar men baithee thi, kyon naheen salaaha dee|jab meree baaten jahara lagatee hain, to mujhe kyaa kutte ne kaataa hai, jo boloon|masala hai-'naaton khetee,bahuriyon ghar' main bhee dekhoo bahuriya kaise ghar chalaatee hai|

itane men siyaaraama aur jiyaaraama skoola se aa gae|aate hee aate donon buaa jee ke paasa jaakara khaane ko maangane lage|
rukminee ne kaha—jaakara apanee naee ammaan se kyon naheen maangate, mujhe bolane kaa hukma naheen hai| totaao-agar tuma logon ne us ghar men qadama rakkhe,to taanga to.da doonngaa|vadamaashee par kamara baodhee hai|

jiyaaraama jaraa shaankha tha| bola-unako to aapa kuchha naheen kehte, hee ko dhamakaate hain| kabhee paise naheen detee|

siyaaraama ne is kathana kaa anumodana kiya-kehti hain mujhe diqa karoge, to kaana kaata loongee | kehti hain ki naheen jiya?

nirmalaa apane kamare se boli-main ne kab kaha tha ki tumhaare kaana kaata loongee| abhee se jhootha bolane lage|

itana sunana tha ki totaaraama ne siyaaraama ke donon kaana paka.da kar uthaa liya| ladka zora se cheekha maara kar rone lagaa|

rukminee ne dau.da kar bachche ko munshee jee ke haath se chhu.daa liya aur voleen-vasa,rahane bhee do; kyaa vachche ko maara hee daaloge?haaya-haaya!kaana laala ho gaya| sacha kaha hai, naee beevee paakara
aadmi andhaa ho jata hai|abhee se yaha haala hai, to is ghar ke bhagavaan hee maalika hain|

nirmalaa apanee vijaya par mann hee mann prasanna ho rahi thi; lekinajavamunsheejee ne bachche kaa kaana paka.da kar uthaa liya, to usase na raha gaya|chhu.daane ko dau.dee; par rukminee pahale hee pahuncha gaee thi| boli-pahale aaga lagaa dee, ab, bujhaane dau.dee ho | jab apane ladke honge, tab aankhen khulengee| paraaee peera kyaa jaano?
nirmalaa-kha.de to hain; poochha lo na, mainne kyaa aaga lagaa dee? main ne itana hee kaha tha ki ladke mujhe paison ke lie baara-baara diqa karte hain| isake sivaa jo mere munha se kuchha aur nikalaa ho, to meree aankhen phoota jaaen|

totaao-main khuda ina laudon kee sharaarata dekha karata hoon,andhaa tho.de hee hoon| teenon jiddee aur shareera ho gae hain| ba.de miyaan ko to main aaja hee hostala men bhejata hoon|

rukminee-ab taka to tumhen inakee koee sharaarata na soojhatee thi, aaja aankhen kyon itanee teza ho gaee?

totaaraama-tumheen ne inhen itana shokha kar rakkhaa hai|

rukminee-to main hee visha kee gaantha hoon| mere hee kaarana tumhaaraa ghar chaupata ho raha hai| lo, main jati hoon| tumhaare ladke hain; maaro chaahe kaato, main na boloongee|

yaha kaha kar vahaan se chalee gaee| nirmalaa bachche ko rote dekha kar vihvala ho uthee| usane use chhaatee se lagaa liya, aur goda men lie hue apane kamare men laakara use chusakaarane lagee, lekin baalaka aur bhee sisaka-sisaka kar rone lagaa| usakaa abodha hridaya is pyaar men vaha maatri-sneha na paataa tha,jisase deva ne use vanchita kar diya tha| yaha vaatsalya na tha,kevala dayaa thi| yaha vaha vastu thi, jisa par usakaa koee adhikaara na tha,jo kevala bhikshaa ke roopa men use dee jaa rahi thi, pitaa ne pahale bhee do-ek baara maaraa tha, jab usakee maan jeevita thi| lekin tab usakee maan use chhaatee se lagaa kar rotee na thi| vaha aprasanna hokara usase bolana chho.da detee, yahaan taka ki vaha svayan tho.dee hee dera ke baada sab kuchha bhoola kar phir maataa ke paasa dau.daa jata tha| sharaarata ke liye sajaa paanaa to usakee samajha men aata tha, lekin maara khaane par chumakaaraa jana usakee samajha men na aata tha| maatri-prema men kathorata hoti thi, lekin mridulata se milee hui| is prema men karunaa thi; par vaha kathorata na thi, jo aatmeeyata kaa gupta sandesha hai| svastha anga kee paravaaha kaun karata hai? lekin vahee anga java kisee vedana se tapakane lagata hai, to use thesa aur dhakke se bachaane kaa yatna kiya jata hai| nirmalaa kaa karana rodana baalaka ko usake anaatha hone kee soochana de raha tha| vaha ba.dee dera taka nirmalaa kee goda men baithaa rotaa raha aur rote-rote so gaya| nirmalaa ne use chaarapaaee par sulaanaa chaahaa to baalaka ne sushuptaavasthaa men apanee donon komala vaahen usakee gardana men daala dee; aur aisaa chipata gaya, maano neeche koee ga.dhaa hai| shankaa aur bhaya se usakaa mukha vikrita ho gaya| nirmalaa ne phir baalaka ko goda men uthaa liya, chaarapaaee par na sulaa sakee| is samay baalaka ko goda men lie hue use vaha tushti ho rahi thi, jo ab taka kabhee na hui thi| aaja pahalee baara use vaha aatma-vedana hui, jisake binaa aankhen naheen khulatee, apana kartavya-maarga naheen soojhata| vaha maarga ava dikhaaee dene lagaa|
