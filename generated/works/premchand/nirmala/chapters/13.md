# Chapter 13

terahavaan parichchheda

jo kuchha honaa tha ho gaya, kisee kee kuchha na chalee doktara saahaba nirmalaa kee deha se rakta nikaalane kee cheshtaa kar hee rahe the ki mansaaraama apane ujjvala charitra kee antima jhalaka dikhaa kar is bhrama-loka se vidaa ho gaya! kadaachit itanee dera taka usake praaNa nirmalaa hee kee raaha dekha rahe the| use nishkalanka siddha kie binaa ve deha ko kaise tyaaga dete? aba unakaa makasada pooraa ho gaya! munshee jee ko nirmalaa ke nirdosha hone kaa yakeena ho gaya; para kaba? jaba haath se teera nikala chukaa tha—jaba gusaaphira ne rikaaba men paa.Nva daala liye the!

betaa-shoka se munshee jee ko jeevana bhaara-svaroopa ho gaya! us din se phir unake onthon para ha.Nsee na aaee! yaha jeevana aba unhen bekaara saa jaana padataa tha! kachaharee jaate; magara mukadamon kee pairavee karane naheen, kevala dil bahalaane ke lie! ghaNte-do ghaNte men vahaa.N se ukataa kar chale aate| khaane baithate to kaura mu.Nha men na jata!
nirmalaa achchhee se achchhee cheejen pakaatee;para munshee jee do-chaara kaura se jyaadaa na khaa sakate! aisaa jaana padataa ki kaura mu.Nha se nikalaa aata hai! mansaaraama ke kamare kee ora jaate hee unakaa dil tooka-tooka ho jata tha! jahaa.N unakee aashaagon kaa deepaka jalataa rahataa tha,vahaa.N aba andhakaara chhaayaa hua tha! unake do betaa aba bhee the;lekin jaba doodha detee huee gaaya mara gaee,to bachhiyaa kaa kyaa bharosaa? java phalane-phoolane vaalaa vriksha gira padaa, to nanhe-nanhe paudhon se kyaa ummeeda? yon to javaana-boodhe sabhee marate hain, lekin dukha is baat kaa tha ki unhonne svayan ladake kee jaana lee| jisa dama yaha baat yaada aa jati,to aisaa maalooma hota tha ki unakee chhaatee phata jaayagee-maano dil baahara nikala padegaa!

nirmalaa ko pati se sachchee sahaanubhooti thi| jahaa.N taka ho sakataa tha,vaha unako khusha rakhane kee phikra rakhatee thi;aur bhoola kar bhee pichhalee baaten jabaana para na laatee thi| munshee jee usase mansaaraama kee koee charchaa karte sharamaate the| unakee kabhee-kabhee aisee ichchhaa hoti ki ek baara nirmalaa se apane mana ke saare bhaava khola kar kaha doon;lekin sharma jabaana roka letee thi| is taraha unhen vaha saantvanaa bhee na milatee thi,jo apanee vyathaa kaha daalane se-doosaron ko apane gama men shareeka kar lene se-praapta hoti hai| mavaada baahara na nikala kar andara hee andara apanaa visha phailaataa' jata tha-din-din deha ghulatee jati thi!

'idhara kuchha dinon se munshee jee aur una doktara saahaba men
jinhonne mansaaraama kee davaa kee thi,yaaraanaa ho gaya tha| vechaare kabhee-kabhee aakara munshee jee ko samajhaayaa karte,kabhee-kabhee apane saatha havaa khilaane ke lie kheencha le jaate!unakee aurata bhee do-chaara baara nirmalaa se milane aaee thi| nirmalaa bhee kaee baara unake ghar ho aaee thi;magara vahaa.N se jaba vaha lautatee,to kaee din taka udaasa rahatee|us dampati kaa sukhamaya jeevana dekha kar use apanee dashaa para duhkha hue binaa na rahataa tha| doktara saahava ko kula 200milate the;para itane hee men donon khushee se jeevana vyateeta karte the| ghar men kevala ek maharee thi,ghar-parivaara kaa bahuta saa kaama aurata ko apane hee haathon karna padataa tha| gahane bhee usakee deha para bahuta kama the;para una donon men vaha pyaara tha,jo paisaa kee triNa varaavara parvaaha naheen karta! aadamee ko dekha kar aurata kaa cheharaa khila uthataa tha| aurata ko dekha kar purupa nihaala ho jata tha| nirmalaa ke ghar men paisaa isase kaheen jyaadaa tha-aabhooshaNon se usakee deha phatee padatee thi-ghar kaa koee kaama use apane haath se na karna padataa tha; para nirmalaa sampanna hone para bhee jyaadaa dukhee thi;aur sudhaa vipanna hone para bhee sukhee! sudhaa ke paasa koee aisee vastu thi,jo nirmalaa ke paasa na thi;jisake saamane use apanaa vaibhava tuchchha jaana padataa tha| yahaa.N taka ki vaha sudhaa ke ghar gahane pahana kar jaate sharamaatee thi!

ek din nirmalaa doktara saahaba ke ghar aaee,to use bahuta udaasa dekha kar sudhaa ne poochhaa-bahina,aaja bahuta udaasa ho,vakeela saahaba kee tabeeyata to achchhee hai na?
nirmalaa-kyaa kahoo.N sudhaa!unakee dashaa din-din kharaaba hoti jati hai-kuchha kehte naheen banataa-na jaane eeshvara ko kyaa manjoora hai?

sudhaa-hamaare baaboo jee to kehte hain ki unhen kaheen jala-vaayu badalane ke lie jana jarooree hai,naheen to koee bhayankara roga khadaa ho jaayagaa| kaee baara vakeela saahaba se kaha bhee chuke hain;para vaha yahee kaha diya karte hain ki main to bahuta achchhee taraha hoo.N-mujhe koee shikaayata naheen| aaja tuma kahanaa!

nirmalaa-jaba doktara saahaba kee naheen sunate,to meree kyaa sunenge?

yaha kehte-kehte nirmalaa kee aa.Nkhen dabadabaa gaee;aur vaha shankaa, jo idhara maheenon se usake dil ko vikala karti rahatee thi,munha se nikala padee| aba taka usane us shankaa ko chhipaayaa tha;para aba na chhipaa sakee| bolee-bahina,mujhe to lakshaNa kuchha achchhe naheen maalooma hote! dekhen,bhagavaan kyaa karte hain?

sudhaa-tuma aaja unase khooba jora dekara kahanaa ki kaheen jala-vaayu badalane chalie| do-chaara maheene baahara rahane se bahuta see baaten bhoola jaae.Ngee| main to samajhatee hoo.N,shaayada makaana badala daalane se bhee unakaa shoka kuchha kama ho jaayagaa| tuma kaheen baahara jaa bhee to na sakogee! yaha kauna saa maheenaa hai?

nirmalaa-aathavaa.N maheenaa beeta raha hai| yaha chintaa to mujhe
aur bhee maare daalatee hai| mainne to isake lie eeshvara se kabhee
praarthanaa naheen kee thi! yaha balaa mere sira na jaane kyon madha dee? main badee abhaaginee hoo.N bahina! vivaaha ke ek maheene pahale pitaa jee kaa dehaanta ho gaya| unake marate hee mere sira saneechara savaara hue! jahaa.N pahale vivaaha kee baatacheeta pakee huee thi,una logon ne
aa.Nkhen phera lee| vechaaree ammaa.N jee ko haara kar meraa vivaaha yahaa.N karna padaa| ava chhotee bahina kaa vivaaha hone vaalaa hai| dekhen, usakee naava kisa ghaata jati hai?

sudhaa-jahaa.N pahale vivaaha kee baatacheeta huee thi,una logon ne inkaara kyon kar diya?

nirmalaa-yaha to ve hee jaana;pitaa jee hee na rahe,to sone kee gatharee kauna detaa?

sudhaa-yaha to neechataa hai! kahaa.N ke rahane vaale the?
nirmalaa-lakhanaoo ke| naama to yaada naheen,aavakaaree ke koee bade aphasara the|

sudhaa ne gambheera bhaava se poochhaa-aur unakaa ladakaa kyaa karta tha?

nirmalaa kuchha naheen,kaheen padhataa tha;para badaa honahaara tha|

sughaa ne sira neechaa karake kahaa-usane apane pitaa se kuchha na kahaa? vaha to javaana tha,kyaa apane baapa ko davaa na sakataa tha?

nirmalaa-aba yaha main kyaa jaane bahina| sone kee gatharee kise pyaaree naheen hoti? jo paNdita mere yahaa.N se sandeshaa lekara
gaya tha, usane to kahaa tha ki ladakaa hee inkaara kar raha hai ! ladane kee maa.N alabattaa devee thi | usane betaa aur pati donon hee ko janannayaa: para usakee kuchha na chalee !

sudhaa- main to us ladake ko paatee; to khooba aade haarthon letee !

nirmalaa--re bhaagya men to jo likhaa tha, vaha ho chukaa ! bechaaree krishNa para na jaane kyaa beetegee ?

shaama sanya nirmalaa ke jaane ke baada jaba doktara saahaba baahara le aae to sudhaa ne kahaa kyon jee, tuma us aadamee ko kyaa kahoge, jo ek jagaha vivaaha theeka kar lene ke baada phir lobhavasha kisee doosaree jagaha sanbandha kar le ?

doktara sinhaa ne aurata kee aur kutuhala se dekha kar kahaa aisaa naheen karna chaahie aur kyaa ?

sudhaa-yaha kyon naheen kehte ki yaha ghora neechataa hai-parale sire kaa kameenaapana hai!

sinhaa-haa.N, yaha kahane men bhee sune inkaara naheen !

sudhaa-kilakaa aparaadha badaa hai ? vara kaa yaa ghar ke pitaa kaa?

sinhaa kee samajha men abhee taka naheen aaya ki sudhaa ke ina prashnon kaa aashaya kyaa hai | hairaanee se bole-jaisee sthiti ho| agar vara pitaa ke aadheena ho, to pitaa hee kaa aparaadha samajho !

sudhaa-aadheena hone para bhee kyaa javaana aadamee kaa apanaa koee kartavya naheen hai ? agar use apane lie nae kota kee jaroorata ho,
to vaha pitaa ke virodha karane para bhee use ro-dhokara vanavaa letaa hai!kyaa aise mahatva ke vishaya men vaha apanee aavaaja pitaa ke kaanon taka naheen pahunchaa sakataa? yaha kaho ki vara aur usakaa pitaa donon hee aparaadhee hain,lekin vara adhika| boodhaa aadamee sochataa hai-mujhe to saaraa kharcha sa.Nbhaalanaa padegaa, kanyaa-paksha se jitanaa aintha sakoo.N, utanaa hee achchhaa! magara yaha vara kaa dharma hai ki vaha yadi bilakula svaartha ke haathon vika naheen gaya hai,to apane aatma-bala kaa parichaya de! agar vaha aisaa naheen karta, to main kahoo.Ngee ki vaha lobhee bhee hai aur kaayara bhee! durbhaagyavasha aisaa hee ek praaNee meraa pati hai;aur meree samajha men naheen aata ki kina shabdon men usakaa tiraskaara karoon?

sinhaa ne hichakichaate hue kahaa-vaha... vaha... vaha doosaree baat thi| lena-dena kaa vajaha naheen tha; bilakula doosaree baat thi! kanyaa ke pitaa kaa dehaanta ho gaya tha| aisee dashaa men hama log kyaa karte? yaha bhee sunane men aaya tha ki kanyaa men koee aiva hai| vaha bilakula doosaree baat thi; magara tumase yaha kathaa kisane kahee?

sudhaa-kaha do ki vaha kanyaa kaanee thi,kubadee thi yaa naaina ke peta kee thi yaa bhrashtaa thi! itanee kasara kyon chhoda dee? bhalaa suna to, us kanyaa men kyaa aiva tha?

sinhaa-main ne dekha to tha naheen, sunane men aaya tha ki us men koee aiva hai|
sughaa--sabase badaa eva yahee tha ki usake pitaa kaa svargavaasa ho gaya tha,aur vaha koee lambee-chaudee rakama na de sakatee thi! itanaa sveekaara karte kyon jhepate ho? main kuchha tumhaare kaana to na kaata loo.Ngee| agar do-chaara phikare kahoo.N,to is kaana se suna kar us kaana se udaa denaa! jyaadaa chee-chapada karoo.N,to chhadee se kaama le sakate ho| aurata-jaata daNde hee se theeka rahatee hai| agar us kanyaa men koee aiva tha,to main kahoo.Ngee ki lakshmee bhee ve aiva naheen ! tumhaaree takadeera khotee thi, basa! aur kyaa? tumhen to mere paale padanaa tha!

sinhaa-tumase kisane kahaa ki vaha aisee thi aur vaisee thi ? jaise tumane kisee se suna kar maana liya, vaise hee hama logon ne bhee suna kar maana liya!

sudhaa-main ne suna kar naheen maana liya! apanee aa.Nkhon dekha| jyaadaa bakhaana kyaa karoo.N,main ne aisee sundara aurata kabhee naheen dekhee thi!!

sinhaa ne vyagra hokara poochhaa-kyaa vaha yaheen kaheen hai? sacha bataao use kahaa.N dekha? kyaa tumhaare ghar aaee thi?

sudhaa-haa.N,mere ghar aaee thi;aur ek baara naheen,kaee baara aa chukee hai| main bhee usake yahaa.N kaee baara jaa chukee hoo.N| vakeela saahaba kee veevee vahee kanyaa hai,jise aapane aivon ke vajaha tyaaga diya!

sinhaa-sacha?

sudhaa-vilakula sacha! aaja agar use maalooma ho jaaya ki
aapa vahee mahaa-purupa hain, to shaayada phir is ghar men kadamana rakkhe| aisee susheelaa, ghar ke kaamon men aisee nipuNa aur aisee parama sundaree aurata is shahara men do hee chaara hongee| tuma meraa bakhaana karte ho! main usakee laundee banane ke yogya bhee naheen hoo.N! ghar men eeshvara kaa diya hua sab kuchha hai,magara jaba praaNee hee mela kaa naheen, to aur sab raha kar kyaa karegaa? dhanya hai, usake dhairya ko ki us buddhe khoosata vakeela ke saatha jeevana ke din kaata rahi hai! mainne to kava kaa jahara khaa liya hota! magara mana kee vyathaa kahane hee se thode hee prakata hoti hai| ha.Nsatee hai, volatee hai,gahanekapade pahanatee hai;para royaa.N-royaa.N royaa karti hai|

sinhaa-vakeela saahaba kee khooba shikaayata karti hogee?

sudhaa-shikaayata kyon karegee? kyaa vaha usake pati naheen hain? sansaara men aba usake lie jo kuchha hain, vakeela saahava hain! vaha budhe hon yaa rogee,para hain to usake svaamee hee! kulavatee striyaa.N pati kee nindaa naheen karateen-yaha kulataaon kaa kaama hai| vaha unakee dashaa dekha kar kudhatee hain| para munha se kuchha naheen kehti!

sinhaa-ina vakeela saahava ko kyaa soojhee thi,jo is umra men vyaaha karane chale?

sudhaa-aise aadamee na hon,to gareeba koriyon kee naava kauna paara lagaaye? tuma aur tumhaare saathee vinaa bhaaree gatharee lie vaata naheen karte,to phir ye bechaaree kisake ghar jaaya.N! tumane
yaha badaa bhaaree anyaaya kiya hai;aur tumhen isakaa praayashchitta karna padegaa! eeshvara usakaa suhaaga amara kare;lekin vakeela saahaba ko kaheen kuchha ho gaya,to bechaaree kaa jeevana hee nashta ho jaayagaa| aaja to vaha bahuta rotee thi| tuma log sachamucha bade nirdayee ho! main to apane sohana kaa vivaaha kisee gareeba ladakee se karoo.Ngee|

doktara saahaba ne yaha pichhalaa vaakya naheen sunaa! vaha ghora chintaa men pada gae| unake mana men yaha savaala utha-utha kar unhen vikala karane lagaa-kaheen vakeela saahaba ko kuchha ho gaya to? aaja unhen apane svaartha kaa bhayankara svaroopa dikhaaee diya! vaastava men yaha unheen kaa aparaadha tha| agar unhonne pitaa se jora dekara kahaa hota ki main aur kaheen vivaaha na karoo.Ngaa,to kyaa vaha unakee' ichchhaa ke viruddha unakaa vivaaha kar dete?

sahasaa sudhaa ne kahaa-kaho to kala nirmalaa se tumhaaree mulaakaata karaa doon| vaha bhee jaraa tumhaaree soorata dekha le| vaha kuchha bolegee to na;para kadaachit ek drishtin men vaha tumhaaraa itanaa tiraskaara kar degee,jise tuma kabhee na bhoola sakoge! bolo, kala milaa doo.N? tumhaaraa bahuta sankshipta parichaya bhee karaa doongee|

sinhaa ne kahaa-naheen sudhaa,tumhaare haath jodataa hoo.N,kaheen aisaa gajaba na karna! naheen to main sacha kehta hoo.N,ghar chhoda kar bhaaga jaaoo.Ngaa!
