# Nirmala — Adhyay 13

**Munshi Premchand**

terahavaan parichchhed

jo kuchh honaa tha ho gayaa, kisee ki kuchh n chalee doktar Sahab Nirmala ki deh se rakt nikaalane ki koshish kar hee rahe the ki mansaaraam apane ujjval charitr ki antim jhalak dikhaa kar is bhram-lok se vidaa ho gayaa! shaayad itanee der tak usake praan Nirmala hee ki raah dekh rahe the. use nishkalanggk siddh kie binaa ve deh ko kaise chhodanaa dete? ab unakaa makasad pooraa ho gayaa! munshee jee ko Nirmala ke nirdosh hone ka bharosaa ho gayaa; par kab? jab haath se teer nikal chukaa tha — jab gusaaphir ne rikaab mein paanv daal liye the!

putr-shok se munshee jee ko zindagi bhaar-svaroop ho gayaa! us din se phir unake onthon par hansee n aaee! yah zindagi ab unhen vyarth saa jaan padataa tha! kachaharee jaate; magar mukadamon ki pairavee karane nahin, keval dil bahalaane ke lie! ghante-do ghante mein wahan se ukataa kar chale aate. khaane baithate to kaur munh mein n jaataa! Nirmala achchhi se achchhi cheejen pakaatee; par munshee jee do-chaar kaur se adhik n khaa sakate! aisaa jaan padataa ki kaur munh se nikalaa aataa hai! mansaaraam ke kamare ki or jaate hee unakaa dil took-took ho jaataa tha! jahaan unakee ummeedagon ka deepak jalataa rahataa tha, wahan ab andhakaar chhaayaa huaa tha! unake do putr ab bhi the; lekin jab doodh detee huee gaay mar gaee, to bachhiyaa ka kyaa bharosaa? jav phalane-phoolane vaalaa vriksh gir padaa, to nanhe-nanhe paudhon se kyaa ummeed? yon to javaan-boodhe sabhee marate hain, lekin dukh is baat ka tha ki unhonne svayan ladake ki jaan lee. jis dam yah baat yaad aa jaatee, to aisaa maaloom hotaa tha ki unakee chhaatee phat jaayagee-maano dil baahar nikal padegaa!

Nirmala ko shauhar se sachchee sahaanubhooti thee. jahaan tak ho sakataa tha, vah unako khush rakhane ki fikr rakhatee thi; aur bhool kar bhi pichhalee baaten jabaan par n laatee thee. munshee jee usase mansaaraam ki koee charchaa karate sharamaate the. unakee kabhee-kabhee aisee ichchhaa hotee ki ek baar Nirmala se apane man ke saare bhaav khol kar kah doon; lekin sharm jabaan rok letee thee. is bhaanti unhen vah saantvanaa bhi n milatee thi, jo apanee vyathaa kah daalane se-doosaron ko apane gam mein shareek kar lene se-milaa hotee hai. mavaad baahar n nikal kar andar hee andar apanaa vish phailaataa' jaataa tha-din-din deh ghulatee jaatee thi!

'idhar kuchh dinon se munshee jee aur un doktar Sahab mein jinhonne mansaaraam ki davaa ki thi, yaaraanaa ho gayaa thaa. vechaare kabhee-kabhee aakar munshee jee ko samajhaayaa karate, kabhee-kabhee apane saath havaa khilaane ke lie kheench le jaate! unakee aurat bhi do-chaar baar Nirmala se milane aaee thee. Nirmala bhi kaee baar unake ghar ho aaee thi; magar wahan se jab vah lautatee, to kaee din tak udaas rahatee. us damshauhar ka sukhamay zindagi dekh kar use apanee dashaa par duhkh hue binaa n rahataa thaa. doktar saahav ko kul 200milate the; par itane hee mein donon khushi se zindagi vyateet karate the. ghar mein keval ek maharee thi, ghar-baar ka bahut saa kaam aurat ko apane hee haathon karanaa padataa thaa. gahane bhi usakee deh par bahut kam the; par un donon mein vah pyaar tha, jo dhan ki trin varaavar parvaah nahin karataa! mard ko dekh kar aurat ka cheharaa khil uthataa thaa. aurat ko dekh kar purup nihaal ho jaataa thaa. Nirmala ke ghar mein dhan isase kaheen adhik tha-aabhooshanon se usakee deh phatee padatee thi-ghar ka koee kaam use apane haath se n karanaa padataa tha; par Nirmala sampann hone par bhi adhik dukhee thi; aur sudhaa vipann hone par bhi sukhee! sudhaa ke paas koee aisee vastu thi, jo Nirmala ke paas n thi; jisake saamane use apanaa vaibhav tuchchh jaan padataa thaa. yahan tak ki vah sudhaa ke ghar gahane pahan kar jaate sharamaatee thi!

ek din Nirmala doktar Sahab ke ghar aaee, to use bahut udaas dekh kar sudhaa ne poochhaa-bahin, aaj bahut udaas ho, vakeel Sahab ki tabeeyat to achchhi hai n? Nirmala-kyaa kahoon sudhaa! unakee dashaa din-din kharaab hotee jaatee hai-kuchh kahate nahin banataa-n jaane Bhagwan ko kyaa manjoor hai?

sudhaa-hamaare baaboo jee to kahate hain ki unhen kaheen jal-vaayu badalane ke lie jaanaa jarooree hai, nahin to koee daranggkar beemaaree khadaa ho jaayagaa. kaee baar vakeel Sahab se kah bhi chuke hain; par vah yahee kah diyaa karate hain ki main to bahut achchhi tarah hoon-mujhe koee shikaayat naheen. aaj tum kahanaa!

Nirmala-jab doktar Sahab ki nahin sunate, to meree kyaa sunengge?

yah kahate-kahate Nirmala ki aankhen dabadabaa gaee; aur vah shanggkaa, jo idhar maheenon se usake dil ko vikal karatee rahatee thi, munh se nikal padee. ab tak usane us shanggkaa ko chhipaayaa tha; par ab n chhipaa sakee. bolee-bahin, mujhe to lapal kuchh achchhe nahin maaloom hote! dekhen, Bhagwan kyaa karate hain?

sudhaa-tum aaj unase khoob jor dekar kahanaa ki kaheen jal-vaayu badalane chalie. do-chaar maheene baahar rahane se bahut see baaten bhool jaaenggee. main to samajhatee hoon, shaayad makaan badal daalane se bhi unakaa shok kuchh kam ho jaayagaa. tum kaheen baahar jaa bhi to n sakogee! yah kaun saa maheenaa hai?

Nirmala-aathavaan maheenaa beet rahaa hai. yah fikr to mujhe
aur bhi maare daalatee hai. mainne to isake lie Bhagwan se kabhee dua nahin ki thi! yah balaa mere sir n jaane kyon madh dee? main badee abhaaginee hoon bahin! shaadee ke ek maheene pahale pitaa jee ka dehaant ho gayaa. unake marate hee mere sir saneechar savaar hue! jahaan pahale shaadee ki baatacheet pakee huee thi, un logon ne
aankhen pher lee. vechaaree ammaan jee ko haar kar meraa shaadee yahan karanaa padaa. av chhotee bahin ka shaadee hone vaalaa hai. dekhen, usakee naav kis ghaat jaatee hai?

sudhaa-jahaan pahale shaadee ki baatacheet huee thi, un logon ne ingkaar kyon kar diyaa?

Nirmala-yah to ve hee jaan; pitaa jee hee n rahe, to sone ki gatharee kaun detaa?

sudhaa-yah to neechataa hai! kahaan ke rahane vaale the?
Nirmala-lakhanaoo ke. naam to yaad nahin, aavakaaree ke koee bade aphasar the.

sudhaa ne seeriyas bhaav se poochhaa-aur unakaa ladakaa kyaa karataa tha?

Nirmala kuchh nahin, kaheen padhataa tha; par badaa honahaar thaa.

sughaa ne sir neechaa karake kahaa-usane apane pitaa se kuchh n kahaa? vah to javaan tha, kyaa apane baap ko davaa n sakataa tha?

Nirmala-ab yah main kyaa jaane bahina. sone ki gatharee kise pyaaree nahin hotee? jo pandit mere yahan se sandeshaa lekar gayaa tha, usane to kahaa tha ki ladakaa hee ingkaar kar rahaa hai! ladane ki maan alabattaa devee thi. usane putr aur shauhar donon hee ko janannayaa: par usakee kuchh n chalee!

sudhaa- main to us ladake ko paatee; to khoob aade haarthon letee!

Nirmala--re kismat mein to jo likhaa tha, vah ho chukaa! bechaaree krishn par n jaane kyaa beetegee?

sandhyaa sany Nirmala ke jaane ke baad jab doktar Sahab baahar le aae to sudhaa ne kahaa kyon jee, tum us aadmi ko kyaa kahoge, jo ek jagah shaadee theek kar lene ke baad phir lobhavash kisee doosaree jagah sambandh kar le?

doktar sinhaa ne aurat ki aur kutuhal se dekh kar kahaa aisaa nahin karanaa chahiye aur kyaa?

sudhaa-yah kyon nahin kahate ki yah ghor neechataa hai-parale sire ka kameenaapan hai!

sinhaa-haan, yah kahane mein bhi sune ingkaar nahin!

sudhaa-kilakaa jurm badaa hai? var ka ya ghar ke pitaa ka?

sinhaa ki samajh mein abhee tak nahin kamaaeeaa ki sudhaa ke in savaalon ka aashay kyaa hai. hairaanee se bole-jaisee sthiti ho. agar var pitaa ke aadheen ho, to pitaa hee ka jurm samajho!

sudhaa-aadheen hone par bhi kyaa javaan aadmi ka apanaa koee farz nahin hai? agar use apane lie nae kot ki jaroorat ho, to vah pitaa ke virodh karane par bhi use ro-dhokar vanavaa letaa hai! kyaa aise mahatv ke vishay mein vah apanee aavaaj pitaa ke kaanon tak nahin pahunchaa sakataa? yah kaho ki var aur usakaa pitaa donon hee jurmee hain, lekin var adhika. boodhaa aadmi sochataa hai-mujhe to saaraa kharch sambhaalanaa padegaa, kanyaa-paksh se jitanaa ainth sakoon, utanaa hee achchha! magar yah var ka dharm hai ki vah yadi bilakul svaarth ke haathon vik nahin gayaa hai, to apane aatm-bal ka parichay de! agar vah aisaa nahin karataa, to main kahoonggee ki vah lobhee bhi hai aur kaayar bhi! badakismateevash aisaa hee ek praanee meraa shauhar hai; aur meree samajh mein nahin aataa ki kin shabdon mein usakaa nafrat karoon?

sinhaa ne hichakichaate hue kahaa-vah... vah... vah doosaree baat thee. len-den ka vajah nahin tha; bilakul doosaree baat thi! kanyaa ke pitaa ka dehaant ho gayaa thaa. aisee dashaa mein ham log kyaa karate? yah bhi sunane mein kamaaeeaa tha ki kanyaa mein koee aiv hai. vah bilakul doosaree baat thi; magar tumase yah kathaa kisane kahee?

sudhaa-kah do ki vah kanyaa kaanee thi, kubadee thi ya naain ke pet ki thi ya bhrashtaa thi! itanee kasar kyon chhod dee? bhalaa sun to, us kanyaa mein kyaa aiv tha?

sinhaa-main ne dekhaa to tha nahin, sunane mein kamaaeeaa tha ki us mein koee aiv hai. sughaa--sabase badaa ev yahee tha ki usake pitaa ka svargavaas ho gayaa tha, aur vah koee lambee-chaudee rakam n de sakatee thi! itanaa maan lenaa karate kyon jhepate ho? main kuchh tumhaare kaan to n kaat loonggee. agar do-chaar phikare kahoon, to is kaan se sun kar us kaan se udaa denaa! jyaadaa chee-chapad karoon, to chhadee se kaam le sakate ho. aurat-jaat dande hee se theek rahatee hai. agar us kanyaa mein koee aiv tha, to main kahoonggee ki lakshmee bhi ve aiv nahin! tumhaaree takadeer khotee thi, bas! aur kyaa? tumhe to mere paale padanaa tha!

sinhaa-tumase kisane kahaa ki vah aisee thi aur vaisee thi? jaise tumane kisee se sun kar maan liyaa, vaise hee ham logon ne bhi sun kar maan liyaa!

sudhaa-main ne sun kar nahin maan liyaa! apanee aangkhon dekhaa. jyaadaa bakhaan kyaa karoon, main ne aisee sundar aurat kabhee nahin dekhee thi!!

sinhaa ne vyagr hokar poochhaa-kyaa vah yaheen kaheen hai? sach bataao use kahaan dekhaa? kyaa tumhaare ghar aaee thi?

sudhaa-haan, mere ghar aaee thi; aur ek baar nahin, kaee baar aa chukee hai. main bhi usake yahan kaee baar jaa chukee hoon. vakeel Sahab ki veevee vahee kanyaa hai, jise aapane aivon ke vajah chhodanaa diyaa!

sinhaa-sach?

sudhaa-vilakul sach! aaj agar use maaloom ho jaay ki aap vahee mahaa-purup hain, to shaayad phir is ghar mein kadaman rakkhe. aisee susheelaa, ghar ke kaamon mein aisee nipun aur aisee param sundaree aurat is shahar mein do hee chaar honggee. tum meraa bakhaan karate ho! main usakee laundee banane ke yogy bhi nahin hoon! ghar mein Bhagwan ka diyaa huaa sab kuchh hai, magar jab praanee hee mel ka nahin, to aur sab rah kar kyaa karegaa? dhany hai, usake dhairy ko ki us buddhe khoosat vakeel ke saath zindagi ke din kaat rahee hai! mainne to kav ka jahar khaa liyaa hotaa! magar man ki vyathaa kahane hee se thode hee saamane hotee hai. hansatee hai, volatee hai, gahanekapade pahanatee hai; par royaan-royaan royaa karatee hai.

sinhaa-vakeel Sahab ki khoob shikaayat karatee hogee?

sudhaa-shikaayat kyon karegee? kyaa vah usake shauhar nahin hain? sansaar mein ab usake lie jo kuchh hain, vakeel saahav hain! vah budhe hon ya beemaareeee, par hain to usake svaamee hee! kulavatee striyaan shauhar ki nindaa nahin karateen-yah kulataaon ka kaam hai. vah unakee dashaa dekh kar kudhatee hain. par munh se kuchh nahin kahatee!

sinhaa-in vakeel saahav ko kyaa soojhee thi, jo is umr mein vyaah karane chale?

sudhaa-aise aadmi n hon, to gareeb koriyon ki naav kaun paar lagaaye? tum aur tumhaare saathee vinaa bhaaree gatharee lie vaat nahin karate, to phir ye bechaaree kisake ghar jaayan! tumane yah badaa bhaaree naainsaaphee kiyaa hai; aur tumhe isakaa praayashchitt karanaa padegaa! Bhagwan usakaa suhaag amar kare; lekin vakeel Sahab ko kaheen kuchh ho gayaa, to bechaaree ka zindagi hee barabaad ho jaayagaa. aaj to vah bahut rotee thee. tum log sachamuch bade nirdayee ho! main to apane sohan ka shaadee kisee gareeb ladakee se karoonggee.

doktar Sahab ne yah pichhalaa vaaky nahin sunaa! vah ghor fikr mein pad gae. unake man mein yah savaal uth-uth kar unhen vikal karane lagaa-kaheen vakeel Sahab ko kuchh ho gayaa to? aaj unhen apane svaarth ka daranggkar svaroop dikhaaee diyaa! vaastav mein yah unheen ka jurm thaa. agar unhonne pitaa se jor dekar kahaa hotaa ki main aur kaheen shaadee n karoonggaa, to kyaa vah unakee' ichchhaa ke viruddh unakaa shaadee kar dete?

sahasaa sudhaa ne kahaa-kaho to kal Nirmala se tumhaaree mulaakaat karaa doon. vah bhi jaraa tumhaaree soorat dekh le. vah kuchh bolegee to n; par shaayad ek drishtin mein vah tumhaaraa itanaa nafrat kar degee, jise tum kabhee n bhool sakoge! bolo, kal milaa doon? tumhaaraa bahut sangkshipt parichay bhi karaa doonggee.

sinhaa ne kahaa-nahin sudhaa, tumhaare haath jodataa hoon, kaheen aisaa gajab n karanaa! nahin to main sach kahataa hoon, ghar chhod kar bhaag jaaoonggaa!

sudhaa-jo kaantaa boyaa hai, usakaa phal khaate kyon itanaa darate ho? jisakee gardan par kataar chalaaee hai, jaraa use tadapate bhi to dekho! mere daadaa jee ne paanch hajaar diye n! abhee chhote bhaaee ke shaadee mein paanch-chhah hajaar aur mil jaayengge. phir to tumhaare baraab‌r dhanee sansaar mein koee doosaraa n hogaa? gyaarah hajaar bahut hote hain; vaap re vaap! gyaarah hajaar!! uthaa-uthaa kar rakhane lage, to maheenon lag jaayan. agar ladake udaane bhi lage, to teen peedhiyon tak chale. kaheen se baatacheet ho rahee hai ya nahin? is parihaas se doktar Sahab itanaa jhepe ki sir tak n uthaa sake. unakaa saaraa vaak-chaatury gaayab ho gayaa. nanhaa saa munh nikal kamaaeeaa, maano maar pad gaee ho. isee vakt kisee ne doktar Sahab ko vaahar se pukaaraa. vechaare jaan lekar bhaage. aurat kitanee parihaas-kushal hotee hai isakaa aaj parichay mil gayaa!

raat ko doktar saahav shayan karate hue sudhaa se bole-Nirmala ki to koee vahin aur hai n?

sudhaa-haan, aaj usakee charchaa to karatee thee. usakee fikr abhee se savaar ho rahee hai. apane oopar to jo kuchh beetanaa tha, veet chukaa; bahin ki fikr mein padee huee hai! maan ke paas to av aur bhi kuchh nahin rahaa; majabooran kisee aise hee boodhe baabaa ke gale vah bhi madh dee jaayagee.

sinhaa-Nirmala to ab apanee maan ki madad kar sakatee hai.
sudhaa ne teekshn svar mein kahaa-tum bhi kabhee-kabhee bilakul ve sir-pair ki baaten karane lagate ho. Nirmala bahut karegee, to do chaar sau rupaye de degee, aur kyaa kar sakatee hai? vakeel Sahab ka yah haal ho rahaa hai, use bhi to abhee pahaad see umr kaatanee hai. phir kaun jaane unake ghar ka kyaa haal hai. idhar chhahmaheene se bechaare ghar baithe hain. rupaye aakaash se thode hee barasate hain. das-, bees hajaar hongge bhi to bainggk mein hongge, kuchh Nirmala ke paas to rakkhe n hongge. hamaaraa 200) maheene ka kharch hai, to kyaa unakaa 400) maheene ka bhi n hogaa?

sudhaa ko to neend aa gaee; par doktar Sahab bahut der tak karavaten badalate rahe! phir kuchh soch kar uthe aur mej par baith kar ek patr likhane lage!
