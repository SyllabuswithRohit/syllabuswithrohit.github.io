# Nirmala — Adhyay 13

**Munshi Premchand**

terhavam parichchhed

jo kuchh hona tha ho gaya, kisi ki kuchh na chali doktar sahab NAmENirmala ki deh se rakt nikalne ki cheshta kar hi rahe the ki NAmEmansaram apne ujjval svabhav ki antim jhalak dikha kar is bhram-lok se vida ho gaya! shayad itni der tak uske jan NAmENirmala hi ki rah dekh rahe the. use nishkalanka siddh kie bina ve deh ko kaise tyag dete? ab unka maksad pura ho gaya! munshi ji ko NAmENirmala ke nirdosh hone ka yakin ho gaya; par kab? jab hath se tir nikal chuka thajab gusafir ne rikab men pamv dal liye the!

beta-shok se munshi ji ko jivan bhar-svarup ho gaya! us din se phir unke onthon par hamsi na ai! yah jivan ab unhen bekar sa jan parta tha! kachhari jate; magar muqdamon ki pairvi karne nahin, sirph dil bahlane ke lie! ghante-do ghante men vaham se ukta kar chale ate. khane baithte to kaur mumh men na jata!
NAmENirmala achchhi se achchhi chijen pakati; par munshi ji do-char kaur se zyada na kha sakte! aisa jan parta ki kaur mumh se nikla ata hai! NAmEmansaram ke kamre ki or jate hi unka dil tuk-tuk ho jata tha! jaham unki ashagon ka dipak jalta rahta tha, vaham ab andhakar chhaya hua tha! unke do beta ab bhi the; lekin jab dudh deti hui gay mar gai, to bachhiya ka kya bharosa? jav phalne-phulne vala vriksh gir pada, to nanhe-nanhe paudhon se kya ummid? yon to javan-burhe sabhi marte haim, lekin dukh is bat ka tha ki unhomne svayam larke ki jan li. jis dam yah bat yad a jati, to aisa malum hota tha ki unki chhati phat jaygi-mano dil bahar nikal parega!

NAmENirmala ko pati se sachchi hamadardi thi. jaham tak ho sakta tha, vah unko khush rakhne ki phikra rakhti thi; aur bhul kar bhi pichhli baten zaban par na lati thi. munshi ji usse NAmEmansaram ki koi charcha karte sharmate the. unki kabhi-kabhi aisi chah hoti ki ek bar NAmENirmala se apne man ke sare bhav khol kar kah dum; lekin sharm jaban rok leti thi. is tarah unhen vah santvana bhi na milti thi, jo apni vyatha kah dalne se-dusron ko apne gam men sharik kar lene se-prapt hoti hai. mavad bahar na nikal kar andar hi andar apna vish phailata jata tha-din-din deh ghulti jati thi!

idhar kuchh dinon se munshi ji aur un doktar sahab men
jinhomne NAmEmansaram ki dava ki thi, yarana ho gaya tha. vechare kabhi-kabhi akar munshi ji ko samjhaya karte, kabhi-kabhi apne sath hava khilane ke lie khinch le jate! unki aurat bhi do-char bar NAmENirmala se milne ai thi. NAmENirmala bhi kai bar unke ghar ho ai thi; magar vaham se jab vah lautti, to kai din tak udas rahti. us dampati ka sukhmay jivan dekh kar use apni hal par duhkh hue bina na rahta tha. doktar sahav ko kul 200milte the; par itne hi men donon khushi se jivan vyatit karte the. ghar men sirph ek mahri thi, ghar-parivar ka bahut sa kam aurat ko apne hi hathon karna parta tha. gahne bhi uski deh par bahut kam the; par un donon men vah pyaar tha, jo paisa ki trin varavar parvah nahin karta! aadmi ko dekh kar aurat ka chehra khil uthta tha. aurat ko dekh kar purup nihal ho jata tha. NAmENirmala ke ghar men paisa isse kahim zyada tha-abhushnon se uski deh phati parti thi-ghar ka koi kam use apne hath se na karna parta tha; par NAmENirmala sampann hone par bhi zyada dukhi thi; aur NAmESudha vipann hone par bhi sukhi! NAmESudha ke pas koi aisi vastu thi, jo NAmENirmala ke pas na thi; jiske samne use apna vaibhav tuchchh jan parta tha. yaham tak ki vah NAmESudha ke ghar gahne pahan kar jate sharmati thi!

ek din NAmENirmala doktar sahab ke ghar ai, to use bahut udas dekh kar NAmESudha ne puchha-bahin, aj bahut udas ho, vakil sahab ki tabiyat to achchhi hai na?
NAmENirmala -kya kahum NAmESudha! unki hal din-din kharab hoti jati hai-kuchh kahte nahin banta-na jane ishvar ko kya manyur hai?

NAmESudha -hamare babu ji to kahte haim ki unhen kahim jal-vayu badlane ke lie jana jaruri hai, nahin to koi bhayankar bimari khada ho jayga. kai bar vakil sahab se kah bhi chuke haim; par vah yahi kah diya karte haim ki maim to bahut achchhi tarah hum-mujhe koi shikayat nahin. aj tum kahna!

NAmENirmala -jab doktar sahab ki nahin sunte, to meri kya sunenge?

yah kahte-kahte NAmENirmala ki ankhen dabdaba gai; aur vah shanka, jo idhar mahinon se uske dil ko vikal karti rahti thi, mumh se nikal padi. ab tak usne us shanka ko chhipaya tha; par ab na chhipa saki. boli-bahin, mujhe to lakshan kuchh achchhe nahin malum hote! dekhen, bhagavan kya karte haim?

NAmESudha -tum aj unse khub jod dekar kahna ki kahim jal-vayu badlane chalie. do-char mahine bahar rahne se bahut si baten bhul jaengi. maim to samjhati hum, shayad makan badal dalne se bhi unka shok kuchh kam ho jayga. tum kahim bahar ja bhi to na sakogi! yah kaun sa mahina hai?

NAmENirmala -athvam mahina bit raha hai. yah chinta to mujhe
aur bhi mare dalti hai. maimne to iske lie ishvar se kabhi
prarthana nahin ki thi! yah bala mere sir na jane kyon marh di? maim badi abhagini hum bahin! shadi ke ek mahine pahle pita ji ka maut ho gaya. unke marte hi mere sir sanichar savar hue! jaham pahle shadi ki batchit paki hui thi, un logon ne
ankhen pher li. vechari ammam ji ko har kar mera shadi yaham karna pada. av chhoti bahin ka shadi hone vala hai. dekhen, uski nav kis ghat jati hai?

NAmESudha -jaham pahle shadi ki batchit hui thi, un logon ne inkar kyon kar diya?

NAmENirmala -yah to ve hi jan; pita ji hi na rahe, to sone ki gathri kaun deta?

NAmESudha -yah to nichta hai! kaham ke rahne vale the?
NAmENirmala -lakhanau ke. nam to yad nahin, avkari ke koi bade aphsar the.

NAmESudha ne gambhir bhav se puchha-aur unka larka kya karta tha?

NAmENirmala kuchh nahin, kahim parhta tha; par bada honhar tha.

sugha ne sir nicha karke kaha-usne apne pita se kuchh na kaha? vah to javan tha, kya apne bap ko dava na sakta tha?

NAmENirmala -ab yah maim kya jane bahin. sone ki gathri kise pyari nahin hoti? jo pandit mere yaham se sandesha lekar
gaya tha, usne to kaha tha ki larka hi inkar kar raha hai! larne ki mam alabatta devi thi. usne beta aur pati donon hi ko janannaya: par uski kuchh na chali!

NAmESudha - maim to us larke ko pati; to khub are harthon leti!

NAmENirmala --re bhagya men to jo likha tha, vah ho chuka! bechari krishna par na jane kya bitegi?

sham sanya NAmENirmala ke jane ke bad jab doktar sahab bahar le ae to NAmESudha ne kaha kyon ji, tum us aadmi ko kya kahoge, jo ek jagah shadi thik kar lene ke bad phir lobhvash kisi dusri jagah sanbandh kar le?

NAmEDoctor Sinha ne aurat ki aur kutuhal se dekh kar kaha aisa nahin karna chahiye aur kya?

NAmESudha -yah kyon nahin kahte ki yah ghor nichta hai-parle sire ka kaminapan hai!

NAmESinha -ham, yah kahne men bhi sune inkar nahin!

NAmESudha -kilka jurm bada hai? dulha ka ya ghar ke pita ka?

NAmESinha ki samajh men abhi tak nahin aya ki NAmESudha ke in prashnon ka ashay kya hai. hairani se bole-jaisi hal ho. agar dulha pita ke adhin ho, to pita hi ka jurm samjho!

NAmESudha -adhin hone par bhi kya javan aadmi ka apna koi pharz nahin hai? agar use apne lie nae kot ki jarurat ho,
to vah pita ke khilaf hona karne par bhi use ro-dhokar vanva leta hai! kya aise mahatva ke vishay men vah apni avaz pita ke kanon tak nahin pahuncha sakta? yah kaho ki dulha aur uska pita donon hi apradhi haim, lekin dulha adhik. burha aadmi sochta hai-mujhe to sara kharch sambhalna parega, ladki-paksh se jitna ainth sakum, utna hi achchha! magar yah dulha ka dharm hai ki vah yadi bilkul svarth ke hathon vik nahin gaya hai, to apne atma-bal ka parichay de! agar vah aisa nahin karta, to maim kahungi ki vah lobhi bhi hai aur kayar bhi! badakismati se aisa hi ek insaan mera pati hai; aur meri samajh men nahin ata ki kin shabdon men uska beizzati karum?

NAmESinha ne hichakichate hue kaha-vah... vah... vah dusri bat thi. len-den ka vajah nahin tha; bilkul dusri bat thi! ladki ke pita ka maut ho gaya tha. aisi hal men ham log kya karte? yah bhi sunne men aya tha ki ladki men koi aiv hai. vah bilkul dusri bat thi; magar tumse yah katha kisne kahi?

NAmESudha -kah do ki vah ladki kani thi, kubri thi ya nain ke pet ki thi ya bhrashta thi! itni kasar kyon chhod di? bhala sun to, us ladki men kya aiv tha?

NAmESinha -maim ne dekha to tha nahin, sunne men aya tha ki us men koi aiv hai.
sugha--sabse bada ev yahi tha ki uske pita ka svargavas ho gaya tha, aur vah koi lambi-chauri rakam na de sakti thi! itna svikar karte kyon jhepte ho? maim kuchh tumhare kan to na kat lungi. agar do-char phikre kahum, to is kan se sun kar us kan se ura dena! jyada chi-chapar karum, to chhari se kam le sakte ho. aurat-jat dande hi se thik rahti hai. agar us ladki men koi aiv tha, to maim kahungi ki lakshmi bhi ve aiv nahin! tumhari takdir khoti thi, bas! aur kya? tumhen to mere pale parna tha!

NAmESinha -tumse kisne kaha ki vah aisi thi aur vaisi thi? jaise tumne kisi se sun kar man liya, vaise hi ham logon ne bhi sun kar man liya!

NAmESudha -maim ne sun kar nahin man liya! apni ankhon dekha. zyada bakhan kya karum, maim ne aisi sundar aurat kabhi nahin dekhi thi!!

NAmESinha ne vyagra hokar puchha-kya vah yahim kahim hai? sach batao use kaham dekha? kya tumhare ghar ai thi?

NAmESudha -ham, mere ghar ai thi; aur ek bar nahin, kai bar a chuki hai. maim bhi uske yaham kai bar ja chuki hum. vakil sahab ki vivi vahi ladki hai, jise apne aivon ke vajah tyag diya!

NAmESinha -sach?

NAmESudha -vilkul sach! aj agar use malum ho jay ki
ap vahi maha-purup haim, to shayad phir is ghar men kadman rakkhe. aisi sushila, ghar ke kamon men aisi nipun aur aisi param sundari aurat is shahar men do hi char hongi. tum mera bakhan karte ho! maim uski laundi banne ke yogya bhi nahin hum! ghar men ishvar ka diya hua sab kuchh hai, magar jab insaan hi mel ka nahin, to aur sab rah kar kya karega? dhanya hai, uske sabra ko ki us buddhe khusat vakil ke sath jivan ke din kat rahi hai! maimne to kav ka jahar kha liya hota! magar man ki vyatha kahne hi se thore hi prakat hoti hai. hamsti hai, volti hai, gahanekpare pahnati hai; par royam-royam roya karti hai.

NAmESinha -vakil sahab ki khub shikayat karti hogi?

NAmESudha -shikayat kyon karegi? kya vah uske pati nahin haim? samsar men ab uske lie jo kuchh haim, vakil sahav haim! vah burhe hon ya rogi, par haim to uske malik hi! kulvati striyam pati ki ninda nahin kartim-yah kulataon ka kam hai. vah unki hal dekh kar kurhti haim. par mumh se kuchh nahin kahti!

NAmESinha -in vakil sahav ko kya sujhi thi, jo is umra men vyah karne chale?

NAmESudha -aise aadmi na hon, to garib koriyon ki nav kaun par lagaye? tum aur tumhare sathi vina bhari gathri lie vat nahin karte, to phir ye bechari kiske ghar jayam! tumne
yah bada bhari anyay kiya hai; aur tumhen iska prayashchitt karna parega! ishvar uska suhag amar kare; lekin vakil sahab ko kahim kuchh ho gaya, to bechari ka jivan hi nasht ho jayga. aj to vah bahut roti thi. tum log sachmuch bade bedard ho! maim to apne sohan ka shadi kisi garib ladki se karungi.

doktar sahab ne yah pichhla vakya nahin suna! vah ghor chinta men par gae. unke man men yah saval uth-uth kar unhen vikal karne laga-kahim vakil sahab ko kuchh ho gaya to? aj unhen apne svarth ka bhayankar svarup dikhai diya! sach men yah unhim ka jurm tha. agar unhomne pita se jod dekar kaha hota ki maim aur kahim shadi na karunga, to kya vah unki chah ke viruddh unka shadi kar dete?

sahsa NAmESudha ne kaha-kaho to kal NAmENirmala se tumhari mulakat kara dum. vah bhi jara tumhari surat dekh le. vah kuchh bolegi to na; par shayad ek drishtim men vah tumhara itna beizzati kar degi, jise tum kabhi na bhul sakoge! bolo, kal mila dum? tumhara bahut sankshipt parichay bhi kara dungi.

NAmESinha ne kaha-nahin NAmESudha, tumhare hath jorta hum, kahim aisa gajab na karna! nahin to maim sach kahta hum, ghar chhod kar bhag jaunga!

NAmESudha -jo kanta boya hai, uska phal khate kyon itna darte
ho? jiski gardan par katar chalai hai, jara use tarpate bhi to dekho! mere dada ji ne panch hajar diye na! abhi chhote bhai ke shadi men panch-chhah hajar aur mil jayenge. phir to tumhare barabra dhani samsar men koi dusra na hoga? gyarah hajar bahut hote haim; vap re vap! gyarah hajar!! utha-utha kar rakhne lage, to mahinon lag jayam. agar larke urane bhi lage, to tin pirhiyon tak chale. kahim se batchit ho rahi hai ya nahin? is parihas se doktar sahab itna jhepe ki sir tak na utha sake. unka sara vak-chaturya gayab ho gaya. nanha sa mumh nikal aya, mano mar par gai ho. isi vakt kisi ne doktar sahab ko vahar se pukara. vechare jan lekar bhage. aurat kitni parihas-kushal hoti hai iska aj parichay mil gaya!

rat ko doktar sahav shayan karte hue NAmESudha se bole- NAmENirmala ki to koi vahin aur hai na?

NAmESudha -ham, aj uski charcha to karti thi. uski chinta abhi se savar ho rahi hai. apne upar to jo kuchh bitna tha, vit chuka; bahin ki phikra men padi hui hai! mam ke pas to av aur bhi kuchh nahin raha; majaburan kisi aise hi burhe baba ke gale vah bhi marh di jaygi.

NAmESinha - NAmENirmala to ab apni mam ki madad kar sakti hai.
NAmESudha ne tikshna avaz men kaha-tum bhi kabhi-kabhi bilkul ve sir-pair ki baten karne lagte ho. NAmENirmala bahut karegi, to do
char sau rupye de degi, aur kya kar sakti hai? vakil sahab ka yah hal ho raha hai, use bhi to abhi pahar si umra katni hai. phir kaun jane unke ghar ka kya hal hai. idhar chhahmhine se bechare ghar baithe haim. rupye akash se thore hi barsate haim. das-, bis hajar honge bhi to bainka men honge, kuchh NAmENirmala ke pas to rakkhe na honge. hamara 200) mahine ka kharch hai, to kya unka 400) mahine ka bhi na hoga?

NAmESudha ko to nind a gai; par doktar sahab bahut der tak karvaten badlate rahe! phir kuchh soch kar uthe aur mez par baith kar ek chitthi likhne lage!
