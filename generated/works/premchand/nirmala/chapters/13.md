# Nirmala — Adhyay 13

**Munshi Premchand**

terahavaan parichchheda

jo kuchha honaa tha ho gaya, kisee kee kuchha na chalee daoktara saahaba nirmalaa kee deha se rakta nikaalane kee cheshtaa kar hee rahe the ki mansaaraama apane ujjvala charitra kee antima jhalaka dikhaa kar is bhrama-loka se vidaa ho gaya! kadaachit itanee dera taka usake praana nirmalaa hee kee raaha dekha rahe the| use nishkalanka siddha kie binaa ve deha ko kaise tyaaga dete? ab unakaa uddeshya pooraa ho gaya! munshee jee ko nirmalaa ke nirdosha hone kaa vishvaasa ho gaya; par kab? jab haath se teera nikala chukaa tha—jab gusaafira ne rikaaba men paanva daala liye the!

putra-shoka se munshee jee ko jeevana bhaara-svaroopa ho gaya! us din se phir unake onthon par hansee na aaee! yaha jeevana ab unhen vyartha saa jaana pa.data tha! kachaharee jaate; magar muqadamon kee pairavee karane naheen, kevala dil bahalaane ke lie! ghante-do ghante men vahaan se ukata kar chale aate| khaane baithate to kaura munha men na jaata! nirmalaa achchhi se achchhi cheejen pakaatee;par munshee jee do-chaara kaura se adhika na khaa sakate! aisaa jaana pa.data ki kaura munha se nikalaa aata hai! mansaaraama ke kamare kee ora jaate hee unakaa hridaya tooka-tooka ho jata tha! jahaan unakee aashaagon kaa deepaka jalata rahata tha,vahaan ab andhakaara chhaayaa hua tha! unake do putra ab bhee the;lekin jab doodha detee hui gaaya mara gaee,to bachhiya kaa kyaa bharosa? java phalane-phoolane vaalaa vriksha gira pa.da, to nanhe-nanhe paudhon se kyaa aasha? yon to javaana-boo.dhe sabhee marate hain, lekin dukha is baat kaa tha ki unhonne svayan ladke kee jaana lee| jisa dama yaha baat yaada aa jati,to aisaa maalooma hota tha ki unakee chhaatee phata jaayagee-maano hridaya baahara nikala pa.dega!

nirmalaa ko pati se sachchee sahaanubhooti thi| jahaan taka ho sakata tha,vaha unako prasanna rakhane kee phikra rakhatee thi;aur bhoola kar bhee pichhalee baaten zabaana par na laatee thi| munshee jee usase mansaaraama kee koee charchaa karte sharamaate the| unakee kabhee-kabhee aisee ichchhaa hoti ki ek baara nirmalaa se apane mann ke saare bhaava khola kar kaha doon;lekin lajjaa jabaana roka letee thi| is bhaanti unhen vaha saantvana bhee na milatee thi,jo apanee vyathaa kaha daalane se-doosaron ko apane Gama men shareeka kar lene se-praapta hoti hai| mavaada baahara na nikala kar andara hee andara apana visha phailaataa' jata tha-din-din deha ghulatee jati thi!

'idhara kuchha dinon se munshee jee aur una daoktara saahaba men jinhonne mansaaraama kee davaa kee thi,yaaraanaa ho gaya tha| vechaare kabhee-kabhee aakara munshee jee ko samajhaayaa karte,kabhee-kabhee apane saatha havaa khilaane ke lie kheencha le jaate!unakee stree bhee do-chaara baara nirmalaa se milane aaee thi| nirmalaa bhee kaee baara unake ghar ho aaee thi;magar vahaan se jab vaha lautatee,to kaee din taka udaasa rahatee|us dampati kaa sukhamaya jeevana dekha kar use apanee dashaa par duhkha hue binaa na rahata tha| daoktara saahava ko kula 200milate the;par itane hee men donon aananda se jeevana vyateeta karte the| ghar men kevala ek maharee thi,grihasthee kaa bahut saa kaama stree ko apane hee haathon karana pa.data tha| gahane bhee usakee deha par bahut kama the;par una donon men vaha prema tha,jo dhana kee trina varaavara parvaaha naheen karata! purusha ko dekha kar stree kaa cheharaa khila uthata tha| stree ko dekha kar purupa nihaala ho jata tha| nirmalaa ke ghar men dhana isase kaheen adhika tha-aabhooshanon se usakee deha phatee pa.datee thi-ghar kaa koee kaama use apane haath se na karana pa.data tha; par nirmalaa sampanna hone par bhee adhika dukhee thi;aur sudhaa vipanna hone par bhee sukhee! sudhaa ke paasa koee aisee vastu thi,jo nirmalaa ke paasa na thi;jisake saamane use apana vaibhava tuchchha jaana pa.data tha| yahaan taka ki vaha sudhaa ke ghar gahane pahana kar jaate sharamaatee thi!

ek din nirmalaa daoktara saahaba ke ghar aaee,to use bahut udaasa dekha kar sudhaa ne poochhaa-bahina,aaja bahut udaasa ho,vakeela saahaba kee tabeeyata to achchhi hai na? nirmalaa-kyaa kahoon sudha!unakee dashaa din-din kharaaba hoti jati hai-kuchha kehte naheen banata-na jaane eeshvara ko kyaa manjoora hai?

sudhaa-hamaare baaboo jee to kehte hain ki unhen kaheen jala-vaayu badalane ke lie jana jarooree hai,naheen to koee bhayankara roga kha.daa ho jaayagaa| kaee baara vakeela saahaba se kaha bhee chuke hain;par vaha yahee kaha diya karte hain ki main to bahut achchhi tarah hoon-mujhe koee shikaayata naheen| aaja tuma kahana!

nirmalaa-jab daoktara saahaba kee naheen sunate,to meree kyaa sunenge?

yaha kehte-kehte nirmalaa kee aankhen dabadabaa gaee;aur vaha shanka, jo idhara maheenon se usake hridaya ko vikala karti rahatee thi,munha se nikala pa.dee| ab taka usane us shankaa ko chhipaayaa tha;par ab na chhipaa sakee| boli-bahina,mujhe to lakshana kuchha achchhe naheen maalooma hote! dekhen,bhagavaan kyaa karte hain?

sudhaa-tuma aaja unase khooba jora dekara kahana ki kaheen jala-vaayu badalane chalie| do-chaara maheene baahara rahane se bahut see baaten bhoola jaaengee| main to samajhatee hoon,shaayada makaana badala daalane se bhee unakaa shoka kuchha kama ho jaayagaa| tuma kaheen baahara jaa bhee to na sakogee! yaha kaun saa maheenaa hai?

nirmalaa-aathavaan maheenaa beeta raha hai| yaha chintaa to mujhe
aur bhee maare daalatee hai| mainne to isake lie eeshvara se kabhee praarthana naheen kee thi! yaha balaa mere sira na jaane kyon ma.dha dee? main ba.dee abhaaginee hoon bahina! vivaaha ke ek maheene pahale pitaa jee kaa dehaanta ho gaya| unake marate hee mere sira saneechara savaara hue! jahaan pahale vivaaha kee baatacheeta pakee hui thi,una logon ne
aankhen phera lee| vechaaree ammaan jee ko haara kar meraa vivaaha yahaan karana pada| ava chhotee bahina kaa vivaaha hone vaalaa hai| dekhen, usakee naava kisa ghaata jati hai?

sudhaa-jahaan pahale vivaaha kee baatacheeta hui thi,una logon ne inkaara kyon kar diya?

nirmalaa-yaha to ve hee jaana;pitaa jee hee na rahe,to sone kee gatharee kaun deta?

sudhaa-yaha to neechata hai! kahaan ke rahane vaale the?
nirmalaa-lakhanaoo ke| naama to yaada naheen,aavakaaree ke koee ba.de afsar the|

sudhaa ne gambheera bhaava se poochhaa-aur unakaa ladka kyaa karata tha?

nirmalaa kuchha naheen,kaheen pa.dhata tha;par bada honahaara tha|

sughaa ne sira neechaa karake kaha-usane apane pitaa se kuchha na kaha? vaha to javaana tha,kyaa apane baapa ko davaa na sakata tha?

nirmalaa-ab yaha main kyaa jaane bahina| sone kee gatharee kise pyaaree naheen hoti? jo pandita mere yahaan se sandeshaa lekara gaya tha, usane to kaha tha ki ladka hee inkaara kar raha hai ! la.dane kee maan alabattaa devee thi | usane putra aur pati donon hee ko janannaya: par usakee kuchha na chalee !

sudhaa- main to us ladke ko paatee; to khooba a.de haarthon letee !

nirmalaa--re bhaagya men to jo likhaa tha, vaha ho chukaa ! bechaaree krishna par na jaane kyaa beetegee ?

sandhyaa sanya nirmalaa ke jaane ke baada jab daoktara saahaba baahara le aae to sudhaa ne kaha kyon jee, tuma us aadmi ko kyaa kahoge, jo ek jagaha vivaaha theeka kar lene ke baada phir lobhavasha kisee doosaree jagaha sanbandha kar le ?

daoktara sinhaa ne stree kee aur kutuhala se dekha kar kaha aisaa naheen karana chaahie aur kyaa ?

sudhaa-yaha kyon naheen kehte ki yaha ghora neechata hai-parale sire kaa kameenaapana hai!

sinhaa-haan, yaha kahane men bhee sune inkaara naheen !

sudhaa-kilakaa aparaadha bada hai ? vara kaa yaa ghar ke pitaa ka?

sinhaa kee samajha men abhee taka naheen aaya ki sudhaa ke ina prashnon kaa aashaya kyaa hai | vismaya se bole-jaisee sthiti ho| agar vara pitaa ke aadheena ho, to pitaa hee kaa aparaadha samajho !

sudhaa-aadheena hone par bhee kyaa javaana aadmi kaa apana koee kartavya naheen hai ? agar use apane lie nae kota kee jaroorata ho, to vaha pitaa ke virodha karane par bhee use ro-dhokara vanavaa letaa hai!kyaa aise mahatva ke vishaya men vaha apanee aavaaza pitaa ke kaanon taka naheen pahunchaa sakata? yaha kaho ki vara aur usakaa pitaa donon hee aparaadhee hain,parantu vara adhika| boo.dhaa aadmi sochata hai-mujhe to saaraa kharcha sanbhaalana pa.dega, kanyaa-paksha se jitana aintha sakoon, utana hee achchha! magar yaha vara kaa dharma hai ki vaha yadi bilakula svaartha ke haathon vika naheen gaya hai,to apane aatma-bala kaa parichaya de! agar vaha aisaa naheen karata, to main kahoongee ki vaha lobhee bhee hai aur kaayara bhee! durbhaagyavasha aisaa hee ek praanee meraa pati hai;aur meree samajha men naheen aata ki kina shabdon men usakaa tiraskaara karoon?

sinhaa ne hichakichaate hue kaha-vaha... vaha... vaha doosaree baat thi| lena-dena kaa kaarana naheen tha; bilakula doosaree baat thi! kanyaa ke pitaa kaa dehaanta ho gaya tha| aisee dashaa men hama log kyaa karte? yaha bhee sunane men aaya tha ki kanyaa men koee aiva hai| vaha bilakula doosaree baat thi; magar tumase yaha kathaa kisane kahee?

sudhaa-kaha do ki vaha kanyaa kaanee thi,kuba.dee thi yaa naaina ke peta kee thi yaa bhrashtaa thi! itanee kasara kyon chho.da dee? bhalaa suna to, us kanyaa men kyaa aiva tha?

sinhaa-main ne dekha to tha naheen, sunane men aaya tha ki us men koee aiva hai| sughaa--sabase bada eva yahee tha ki usake pitaa kaa svargavaasa ho gaya tha,aur vaha koee lambee-chau.dee rakama na de sakatee thi! itana sveekaara karte kyon jhepate ho? main kuchha tumhaare kaana to na kaata loongee| agar do-chaara phikare kahoon,to is kaana se suna kar us kaana se u.daa dena! zyada chee-chapa.da karoon,to chha.dee se kaama le sakate ho| aurat-jaata dande hee se theeka rahatee hai| agar us kanyaa men koee aiva tha,to main kahoongee ki lakshmee bhee ve aiva naheen ! tumhaaree takadeera khotee thi, basa! aur kya? tumhen to mere paale pa.dana tha!

sinhaa-tumase kisane kaha ki vaha aisee thi aur vaisee thi ? jaise tumane kisee se suna kar maana liya, vaise hee hama logon ne bhee suna kar maana liya!

sudhaa-main ne suna kar naheen maana liya! apanee aankhon dekha| zyada bakhaana kyaa karoon,main ne aisee sundara stree kabhee naheen dekhi thi!!

sinhaa ne vyagra hokara poochhaa-kyaa vaha yaheen kaheen hai? sacha bataao use kahaan dekha? kyaa tumhaare ghar aaee thi?

sudhaa-haan,mere ghar aaee thi;aur ek baara naheen,kaee baara aa chukee hai| main bhee usake yahaan kaee baara jaa chukee hoon| vakeela saahaba kee veevee vahee kanyaa hai,jise aapane aivon ke kaarana tyaaga diya!

sinhaa-sacha?

sudhaa-vilakula sacha! aaja agar use maalooma ho jaaya ki aapa vahee mahaa-purupa hain, to shaayada phir is ghar men kadamana rakkhe| aisee susheela, ghar ke kaamon men aisee nipuna aur aisee parama sundaree stree is shehar men do hee chaara hongee| tuma meraa bakhaana karte ho! main usakee laundee banane ke yogya bhee naheen hoon! ghar men eeshvara kaa diya hua sab kuchha hai,magar jab praanee hee mela kaa naheen, to aur sab raha kar kyaa karega? dhanya hai, usake dhairya ko ki us buddhe khoosata vakeela ke saatha jeevana ke din kaata rahi hai! mainne to kava kaa jahara khaa liya hota! magar mann kee vyathaa kahane hee se tho.de hee prakata hoti hai| hansatee hai, volatee hai,gahanekapa.de pahanatee hai;par royaan-royaan royaa karti hai|

sinhaa-vakeela saahaba kee khooba shikaayata karti hogee?

sudhaa-shikaayata kyon karegee? kyaa vaha usake pati naheen hain? sansaara men ab usake lie jo kuchha hain, vakeela saahava hain! vaha bu.dhe hon yaa rogee,par hain to usake svaamee hee! kulavatee striyaan pati kee nindaa naheen karateen-yaha kulataaon kaa kaama hai| vaha unakee dashaa dekha kar ku.dhatee hain| par munha se kuchha naheen kehti!

sinhaa-ina vakeela saahava ko kyaa soojhee thi,jo is umra men vyaaha karane chale?

sudhaa-aise aadmi na hon,to gareeba kaoriyon kee naava kaun paara lagaaye? tuma aur tumhaare saathee vinaa bhaaree gatharee lie vaata naheen karte,to phir ye bechaaree kisake ghar jaayan! tumane yaha bada bhaaree anyaaya kiya hai;aur tumhen isakaa praayashchitta karana pa.dega! eeshvara usakaa suhaaga amara kare;lekin vakeela saahaba ko kaheen kuchha ho gaya,to bechaaree kaa jeevana hee nashta ho jaayagaa| aaja to vaha bahut rotee thi| tuma log sachamucha ba.de nirdayee ho! main to apane sohana kaa vivaaha kisee gareeba ladki se karoongee|

daoktara saahaba ne yaha pichhalaa vaakya naheen suna! vaha ghora chintaa men pa.da gae| unake mann men yaha prashna utha-utha kar unhen vikala karane lagaa-kaheen vakeela saahaba ko kuchha ho gaya to? aaja unhen apane svaartha kaa bhayankara svaroopa dikhaaee diya! vaastava men yaha unheen kaa aparaadha tha| agar unhonne pitaa se jora dekara kaha hota ki main aur kaheen vivaaha na karoonga,to kyaa vaha unakee' ichchhaa ke viruddha unakaa vivaaha kar dete?

sahasaa sudhaa ne kaha-kaho to kala nirmalaa se tumhaaree mulaakaata karaa doon| vaha bhee jaraa tumhaaree soorata dekha le| vaha kuchha bolegee to na;par kadaachit ek drishtin men vaha tumhaaraa itana tiraskaara kar degee,jise tuma kabhee na bhoola sakoge! bolo, kala milaa doon? tumhaaraa bahut sankshipta parichaya bhee karaa doongee|

sinhaa ne kaha-naheen sudha,tumhaare haath jo.data hoon,kaheen aisaa gajaba na karana! naheen to main sacha kahata hoon,ghar chho.da kar bhaaga jaaoonga!

sudhaa-jo kaantaa boyaa hai,usakaa phala khaate kyon itana darate ho? jisakee gardana par kataara chalaaee hai,jaraa use ta.dapate bhee to dekho! mere daadaa jee ne paancha hajaara diye na!abhee chhote bhaaee ke vivaaha men paancha-chhah hajaara aur mila jaayenge| phir to tumhaare baraaba‌ra dhanee sansaara men koee doosaraa na hoga? gyaaraha hajaara bahut hote hain; vaapa re vaapa! gyaaraha hajaara!! uthaa-uthaa kar rakhane lage,to maheenon laga jaayan| agar ladke u.daane bhee lage,to teena pee.dhiyon taka chale| kaheen se baatacheeta ho rahi hai yaa naheen? is parihaasa se daoktara saahaba itana jhepe ki sira taka na uthaa sake| unakaa saaraa vaak-chaaturya gaayaba ho gaya|nanhaa saa munha nikala aaya,maano maara pa.da gaee ho| isee vakta kisee ne daoktara saahaba ko vaahara se pukaaraa| vechaare jaana lekara bhaage| stree kitanee parihaasa-kushala hoti hai isakaa aaja parichaya mila gaya!

raat ko daoktara saahava shayana karte hue sudhaa se bole-nirmalaa kee to koee vahina aur hai na?

sudhaa-haan,aaja usakee charchaa to karti thi| usakee chintaa abhee se savaara ho rahi hai| apane oopara to jo kuchha beetana tha,veeta chuka; bahina kee phikra men pa.dee hui hai! maan ke paasa to ava aur bhee kuchha naheen raha;majaboorana kisee aise hee boo.dhe baabaa ke gale vaha bhee ma.dha dee jaayagee|

sinhaa-nirmalaa to ab apanee maan kee madada kar sakatee hai|
sudhaa ne teekshna svara men kaha-tuma bhee kabhee-kabhee bilakula ve sira-pair kee baaten karane lagate ho| nirmalaa bahut karegee,to do chaara sau rupaye de degee,aur kyaa kar sakatee hai? vakeela saahaba kaa yaha haala ho raha hai,use bhee to abhee paha.da see umra kaatanee hai| phir kaun jaane unake ghar kaa kyaa haala hai| idhara chhahmaheene se bechaare ghar baithe hain| rupaye aakaasha se tho.de hee barasate hain|dasa-,beesa hajaara honge bhee to bainka men honge,kuchha nirmalaa ke paasa to rakkhe na honge| hamaaraa 200) maheene kaa kharcha hai,to kyaa unakaa 400) maheene kaa bhee na hoga?

sudhaa ko to neenda aa gaee;par daoktara saahaba bahut dera taka karavaten badalate rahe! phir kuchha socha kar uthe aur meza par baitha kar ek patra likhane lage!
