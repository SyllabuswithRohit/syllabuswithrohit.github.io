# Nirmala — Adhyay 4

**Munshi Premchand**

chautha parichchhed

NAmEKalyani ke samne ab ek visham samasya a khadi hui. pati ke maut ke bad use apni duravastha ka yah pahla aur bahut hi karva anubhav hua. garib vidhva ke lie isse badi aur kya musibat ho sakti hai ki javan beti sir par savar ho? larke nange pamv parhne ja sakte haim, chauka-bartan bhi apne hath se kiya ja sakta hai, rukha-sukha khakar guzara kiya ja sakta hai, jhopre men din kate ja sakte haim; lekin yuvti ladki ghar men nahin bithai ja sakti. NAmEKalyani ko NAmEBhalchandra par aisa gussa ata tha ki svayam jakar uske mumh men kalikh lagaum, sir ke bal noch lum. kahum, tu apni bat se phir gaya, tu apne bap ka beta nahin. pandit moteram ne unki kapat-lila ka nagna vrittant suna diya tha.

vah isi gussa men bhari baithi thi ki NAmEKrishna khelti hui ai, aur bolikai din men barat aegi; ammam! pandit ji to a gae.
NAmEKalyani --barat ka sapna dekh rahi hai kya?

NAmEKrishna --vahi chandar to kah raha hai ki do-tin din men barat aegi. kya na aegi ammam?

NAmEKalyani --ek bar to kah diya, sir kyon khati hai?

NAmEKrishna --sab ke ghar to barat a rahi hai, hamare yaham kyon nahin ati?

NAmEKalyani --tere yaham jo barat lane vala tha, uske ghar men ag lag gai.

krishna--sach ammam? tab to sara ghar jal gaya hoga. kaham rahte honge? bahin kaham jakar rahegi?

NAmEKalyani --are pagli, tu to bat hi nahin samjhati. ag nahin lagi. vah hamare yaham vyah na karega.

NAmEKrishna --yah kyon ammam? pahle to vaham thik ho gaya tha na?

NAmEKalyani --bahut se rupye mangta hai. mere pas use dene ko rupye nahin haim.

NAmEKrishna --kya bade lalchi haim ammam?

NAmEKalyani --lalchi nahin to aur kya hai! pura kasai, nirdai, dagabaz!

NAmEKrishna --tab to ammam bahut achchha hua ki uske ghar bahin ka vyah nahin hua. bahin unke sath kaise rahti. yah to khash hone ki bat hai ammam, tum ranja kyon karti ho?
NAmEKalyani ne beti ko snehmay nazar se dekha. iska kathan kitna satya hai. bhole shabdon men samasya ka kitna marmik nirupan hai. sachmuch yah to khush hone ki bat hai ki aise kupatron se rishta nahin hua, ranja ki koi bat nahin. aise kumanuson ke bich men vechari NAmENirmala ki na jane kya gati ho? apne nasivon ko roti. jara sa ghi dal men zyada par jata, to sare ghar men shor mach jata; jara khana jyada pak jata, to sas duniya sir par utha leti. larka bhi aisa hi lobhi hai. badi achchhi bat hui, nahin to bechari ko unn bhar rona parta. NAmEKalyani yaham se tathi, to uska dil halka ho gaya tha.

lekin shadi to karna hi tha, aur ho sake to isi sal; nahin to dusre sal phir nae sire se taiyariyam karni parengi. ab achchhe ghar ki jarurat na thi, achchhe dulha ki zarurat na thi. abhagini ko achchha ghar-ghar kaham milta hai. ab to kisi bhoti sir ka bojh utarna tha, kisi tarah ladki ko par lagana tha--use kuen men jhonkna tha. vah rupvati hai, gunshila hai, hoshiyar hai, kulina hai, to hua kare; dahej nahin to uske sare gun dosh haim. dahej ho to sare dop, gun haim. insaan ka koi kimat nahin, sirph dahej ka kimat hai. kitni visham bhagyalila hai?

NAmEKalyani ka dosh kuchh kam na tha. abla aur vidhva hona hi use doshon se mukt nahin kar sakta. use apne larke

apni larkiyo se kahim pyare the. larke hal ke bail haim, bhuse-khali par pahla haq unka hai, unke khane se jo bache vah gayon ka! makan tha, kuchh naqad tha, kai hajar ke gahne the; lekin use abhi do larkon ka palan-poshan karna tha, unhen parhana-likhana tha, ek ladki aur bhi char-panch sal men shadi karne ke yogya ho jaygi. isalie vah koi badi rakam dahej men na de sakti thi. akhir larkon ko bhi to kuchh chahiye. ve kya samjhege ki hamara bhi koi bap tha.

pandit moteram ko lakhanau se laute pandrah din bit chuke the. lautne ke bad dusre hi din se vah dulha ki khoj men nikle the. unhomne pran kiya tha, maim in lakhanau valon ko dikha dunga ki samsar men tumhim akele nahin ho, tumhare aise aur kitne pade hue haim. NAmEKalyani roz din gina karti thi. aj usne unhen chitthi likhne ka phaisla kiya aur kalam davat lekar baithi hi thi ki pandit moteram ne padarpan kiya.

NAmEKalyani --aie pandit ji, maim to apko khat likhne ja rahi thi. kab laut?

moteram--lauta to subah hi tha; par usi samay ek seth ke yaham se nimantran a gaya. kai din se tar mal na mile the. maimne kaha ki lage hath yah kam bhi niptata chalum. abhi udhar hi se lauta a raha hum, koi panch sau brahmanon ki pangat thi.
NAmEKalyani --kuchh karya bhi siddh hua, ya rasta hi napna pada?

mote0--karya kyon na siddh hota, bhala yah bhi koi bat hai? panch jagah batchit kar aya hum. panchon ki naqal laya hum. usmen se ap jise chahen pasand karen. yah dekhie, is larke ka bap dak ke sege men 100 mahine ka naukar hai. larka abhi kolej men parh raha hai. magar naukri hi ka bharosa hai, ghar men koi jaydad nahin. larka honhar malum hota hai. khandan bhi achchha hai. 2000 men bat tay ho jaygi. mangte to vah tin hazar haim.

NAmEKalyani --larke ke aur bhi bhai haim?

mote0--nahin, magar tin bahinen haim; aur tinon kari! mam zinda hai. achchha, ab dusri nakal dekhie. yah larka rel ke sege men 50 mahina pata hai. mam-bap nahin haim. bahut hi rupavan, sushil aur sharir se khuv hrisht-pusht, kasrati javan hai. magar khandan achchha nahin koi kahta hai, mam nain thi, koi kahta hai thakurain thi. bap kisi riyasat men mukhtar the. ghar par thodi si zamindari hai. magar us par kai hajar ka karja hai. yaham kuchh lena-dena na parega. umra koi vis sal hogi.

NAmEKalyani --khandan men dara na hota, to manjur kar leti. dekh kar to makkhi nahin nigli jati.
mote0--tisri nakkal dekhie. ek zamindar ka larka hai. koi ek hajar salana napha hai. kuchh kheti-badi bhi hoti hai. larka parha-likha to thoda hi hai; par kachhari adalat ke kam men hoshiyar hai. duhaju hai. pahli aurat ko mare do sal hue. usse koi bachcha nahin hai. lekin rahan-sahan mota hai. pisna-kutna ghar hi men hota hai.

NAmEKalyani --kuchh dahej bhi mangte haim?

moteram--iski kuchh na puchhie. char hajar sunate haim. achchha, yah chauthi nakal dekhie. larka vakil hai, umra koi paitis sal hogi. tin-char sau ki amdani hai. pahli aurat mar chuki hai. usse tin larke bhi haim. apna ghar banvaya hai. kuchh jaydad bhi kharidi hai. yaham bhi len-den ka jhagra nahin hai.

NAmEKalyani --khandan kaisa hai?

mote0--bahut hi uttam, purane rais haim. achchha, yah panchvim nakal dekhie. bap ka chhapakhana hai. larka parha to bi0 e0 tak hai, par usichhapekhane men kam karta hai. umra atharah sal hogi. ghar men pres ke sivay koi jaydad nahin hai; magar kisi ka qarja sir par nahin. khandan na bahut achchha hai, na bura. larka bahut sundar aur sachcharitra hai. magar ek hajar se kam men mamla tay na hoga, mangte to vah tin-hazar haim. ab bataie ap kaun sa dulha pasand karti haim.

NAmEKalyani --apko sabon men kaun pasand hai?
mote0--mujhe to do dulha pasand haim. ek vah jo relavai men hai, aur dusra yah jo chhopekhane men kam karta hai.

NAmEKalyani --magar pahle ke to khandan men ap dop batate haim.

mote0--ham, yah dop to hai. to chhapekhane vale hi ko rahne dijie.

NAmEKalyani --yaham ek hazar dene ko kaham se aega? ek hajar to ap ka anuman hai, shayad vah aur bhi mumh phailae. ap to ghar ki hal dekh hi rahe haim, khana milta jay, yahi ganimat hai. rupye kaham se aenge. jamindar sahab char hajar sunate haim, dak vabu bhi do hajar ka saval karte haim. inko jane dijie. bas, vakil sahav hi bach rahte haim; paitis sal ki umra bhi kuchh aisi jyada nahin, inhim ko kyon na rakhie.

moteram--ap khub soch-soch lo, maim to ap ki marji ka tavedar hum. jaham kahiega, vaham jakar tika kar aunga. magar hazar derh-hajar ka mumh na dekhie, chhapekhane vala larka ratna hai. uske sath ladki ka jivan kamyab ho javega. jaisi yah rup aur gun ki puri hai, vaisa hi larka bhi sundar aur sushil hai.

NAmEKalyani -pasand to mujhe bhi yahi hai; maharaj! par rupye kiske gharse aen? kaun dene vala hai? hai koi aisa dan? khane

vale to kha-pikar champat hue. ab kisi ki surat bhi nahin dikhai deti; balki aur mujhse bura mante haim ki hamen nikal diya. jo bat apne bas ke bahar hai, uske lie hath hi kyon phailaum. bachcha kisko pyari nahin hoti? kaun use sukhi nahin dekhna chahta; par jab apna qabu bhi ho. ap ishvar ka nam lekar vakil sahab ko tika kar aie. ayu kuchh zyada hai; lekin marna-jina vidhi ke hath hai. paintis sal ka aadmi buddha nahin kahlata. agar ladki ke bhagya men sukh bhogna bada hai, to jaham jaygi sukhi rahegi; dukh bhogna hai, to jaham jaygi dukh jhelegi. hamari NAmENirmala ko bachchon se pyaar hai. unke bachchon ko apna samjhegi. ap shubh-muhurt dekh kar tika kar aen!!
