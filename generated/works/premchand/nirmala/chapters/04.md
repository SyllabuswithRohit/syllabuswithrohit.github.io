# Chapter 4

chauthaa parichchheda

kalyaaNee ke saamane aba ek vishama samasyaa aa khadee huee| pati ke dehaanta ke baada use apanee duraavasthaa kaa yaha pahalaa aur bahuta hee kadavaa anubhava hua| daridra vidhavaa ke lie isase badee aur kyaa vipatti ho sakatee hai ki javaana betee sira para savaara ho? ladake nange paa.Nva padhane jaa sakate hain, chaukaa-bartana bhee apane haath se kiya jaa sakataa hai, rookhaa-sookhaa khaakara nirvaaha kiya jaa sakataa hai, jhopade men din kaate jaa sakate hain; lekin yuvatee kanyaa ghar men naheen bithaaee jaa sakatee| kalyaaNee ko bhaalachandra para aisaa gussaa aata tha ki svayan jaakara usake mu.Nha men kaalikha lagaaoo.N, sira ke baala nocha loo.N| kahoo.N, too apanee baat se phir gaya, too apane baapa kaa betaa naheen| paNdita moteraama ne unakee kapata-leelaa kaa nagna vrittaanta sunaa diya tha|

vaha isee gussaa men bharee baithee thi ki krishNaa khelatee huee aaee, aur bolee—kai din men baaraata aaegee; ammaa.N! paNdita jee to aa gae|
kalyaaNee--baaraata kaa sapanaa dekha rahi hai kyaa?

krishNaa--vahee chandara to kaha raha hai ki do-teena din men baaraata aaegee| kyaa na aaegee ammaa.N?

kalyaaNee--ek baara to kaha diya, sira kyon khaatee hai?

krishNaa--sab ke ghar to baaraata aa rahi hai, hamaare yahaa.N kyon naheen aatee?

kalyaaNee--tere yahaa.N jo baaraata laane vaalaa tha, usake ghar men aaga laga gaee|

krishNa--sacha ammaa.N? taba to saaraa ghar jala gaya hogaa| kahaa.N rahate honge? bahina kahaa.N jaakara rahegee?

kalyaaNee--are pagalee, too to baat hee naheen samajhatee| aaga naheen lagee| vaha hamaare yahaa.N vyaaha na karegaa|

krishNaa--yaha kyon ammaa.N? pahale to vahaa.N theeka ho gaya tha na?

kalyaaNee--bahuta se rupaye maa.Ngataa hai| mere paasa use dene ko rupaye naheen hain|

krishNaa--kyaa bade laalachee hain ammaa.N?

kalyaaNee--laalachee naheen to aur kyaa hai! pooraa kasaaee, nirdaee, dagaabaaja!

krishNaa--taba to ammaa.N bahuta achchhaa hua ki usake ghar bahina kaa vyaaha naheen hua|bahina unake saatha kaise rahatee| yaha to khasha hone kee baat hai ammaa.N, tuma ranja kyon karti ho?
kalyaaNee ne betee ko snehamaya nazara se dekha| isakaa kathana kitanaa satya hai| bhole shabdon men samasyaa kaa kitanaa maarmika niroopaNa hai| sachamucha yaha to khusha hone kee baat hai ki aise kupaatron se sambandha naheen hua, ranja kee koee baat naheen| aise kumaanuson ke beecha men vechaaree nirmalaa kee na jaane kyaa gati ho? apane naseevon ko rotee| jaraa saa ghee daala men zyaadaa pada jata, to saare ghar men shora macha jata; jaraa khaanaa jyaadaa paka jata, to saasa duniyaa sira para uthaa letee| ladakaa bhee aisaa hee lobhee hai| badee achchhee baat huee, naheen to bechaaree ko unna bhara ronaa padataa| kalyaaNee yahaa.N se tathee, to usakaa dil halakaa ho gaya tha|

lekin vivaaha to karna hee tha, aur ho sake to isee saala; naheen to doosare saala phir nae sire se taiyaariyaa.N karanee padengee| aba achchhe ghar kee jaroorata na thi, achchhe vara kee jaroorata na thi| abhaaginee ko achchhaa ghar-ghar kahaa.N milataa hai| aba to kisee bhoti sira kaa bojha utaaranaa tha, kisee taraha ladakee ko paara lagaanaa tha--use kue.N men jhonkanaa tha| vaha roopavatee hai, guNasheelaa hai, hoshiyaara hai, kuleenaa hai, to hua kare; daheja naheen to usake saare guNa dosha hain| daheja ho to saare dopa, guNa hain| praaNee kaa koee moolya naheen, kevala daheja kaa moolya hai| kitanee vishama bhaagyaleelaa hai?

kalyaaNee kaa dosha kuchha kama na tha| abalaa aur vidhavaa honaa hee use doshon se mukta naheen kar sakataa| use apane ladake

apanee ladakiyon se kaheen pyaare the| ladake hala ke baila hain, bhoose-khalee para pahalaa haka unakaa hai, unake khaane se jo bache vaha gaayon kaa! makaana tha, kuchha nakada tha, kaee hajaara ke gahane the; lekin use abhee do ladakon kaa paalana-poshaNa karna tha, unhen padhaanaa-likhaanaa tha, ek kanyaa aur bhee chaara-paa.Ncha saala men vivaaha karane ke yogya ho jaayagee| isalie vaha koee badee rakama daheja men na de sakatee thi| aakhira ladakon ko bhee to kuchha chaahie| ve kyaa samajhege ki hamaaraa bhee koee baapa tha|

paNdita moteraama ko lakhanaoo se laute pandraha din beeta chuke the| lautane ke baada doosare hee din se vaha vara kee khoja men nikale the| unhonne praNa kiya tha, main ina lakhanaoo vaalon ko dikhaa doongaa ki sansaara men tumheen akele naheen ho, tumhaare aise aur kitane pade hue hain| kalyaaNee roja din ginaa karti thi| aaja usane unhen patra likhane kaa phaisalaa kiya aur kalama daavaata lekara baithee hee thi ki paNdita moteraama ne padaarpaNa kiya|

kalyaaNee--aaie paNdita jee, main to aapako khata likhane jaa rahi thi| kaba lauta?

moteraama--lautaa to subaha hee tha; para usee samaya ek setha ke yahaa.N se nimantraNa aa gaya| kaee din se tara maala na mile the| mainne kahaa ki lage haath yaha kaama bhee nipataataa chaloo.N| abhee udhara hee se lautaa aa raha hoo.N, koee paa.Ncha sau braahmaNon kee pangata thi|
kalyaaNee--kuchha kaarya bhee siddha hua, yaa raastaa hee naapanaa padaa?

mote0--kaarya kyon na siddha hota, bhalaa yaha bhee koee baat hai? paa.Ncha jagaha baatacheeta kar aaya hoo.N| paa.Nchon kee nakala laayaa hoo.N| usamen se aapa jise chaahen pasanda karen| yaha dekhie, is ladake kaa baapa daaka ke sege men 100 maheene kaa naukara hai| ladakaa abhee koleja men padha raha hai| magara naukaree hee kaa bharosaa hai, ghar men koee jaayadaada naheen| ladakaa honahaara maalooma hota hai| khaanadaana bhee achchhaa hai| 2000 men baat taya ho jaayagee| maa.Ngate to vaha teena hajaara hain|

kalyaaNee--ladake ke aur bhee bhaaee hain?

mote0--naheen, magara teena bahinen hain; aur teenon kaaree! maa.N jeevita hai| achchhaa, aba doosaree nakala dekhie| yaha ladakaa rela ke sege men 50 maheenaa paataa hai| maa.N-baapa naheen hain| bahuta hee roopavaan, susheela aur shareera se khoova hrishta-pushta, kasaratee javaana hai| magara khaanadaana achchhaa naheen koee kehta hai, maa.N naaina thi, koee kehta hai thakuraaina thi| baapa kisee riyaasata men mukhtaara the| ghar para thodee see jameendaaree hai| magara us para kaee hajaara kaa karjaa hai| yahaa.N kuchha lenaa-denaa na padegaa| umra koee veesa saala hogee|

kalyaaNee--khaanadaana men daaraa na hota, to manjoora kar letee| dekha kar to makkhee naheen nigalee jati|
mote0--teesaree nakkala dekhie| ek jameendaara kaa ladakaa hai| koee ek hajaara saalaanaa naphaa hai| kuchha khetee-baaree bhee hoti hai| ladakaa padhaa-likhaa to thodaa hee hai; para kachaharee adaalata ke kaama men hoshiyaara hai| duhaajoo hai| pahalee aurata ko mare do saala hue| usase koee bachchaa naheen hai| lekin rahana-sahana motaa hai| peesanaa-kootanaa ghar hee men hota hai|

kalyaaNee--kuchha daheja bhee maa.Ngate hain?

moteraama--isakee kuchha na poochhie| chaara hajaara sunaate hain| achchhaa,yaha chauthee nakala dekhie| ladakaa vakeela hai, umra koee paiteesa saala hogee| teena-chaara sau kee aamadanee hai| pahalee aurata mara chukee hai| usase teena ladake bhee hain| apanaa ghar banavaayaa hai| kuchha jaayadaada bhee khareedee hai| yahaa.N bhee lena-dena kaa jhagadaa naheen hai|

kalyaaNee--khaanadaana kaisaa hai?

mote0--bahuta hee uttama, puraane raeesa hain| achchhaa, yaha paa.Nchaveen nakala dekhie| baapa kaa chhaapaakhaanaa hai| ladakaa padhaa to bee0 e0 taka hai, para useechhaapekhaane men kaama karta hai| umra athaaraha saala hogee| ghar men presa ke sivaaya koee jaayadaada naheen hai; magara kisee kaa karjaa sira para naheen| khaanadaana na bahuta achchhaa hai, na buraa|ladakaa bahuta sundara aur sachcharitra hai| magara ek hajaara se kama men maamalaa taya na hogaa, maa.Ngate to vaha teena-hajaara hain| aba bataaie aapa kauna saa vara pasanda karti hain|

kalyaaNee--aapako sabon men kauna pasanda hai?
mote0--mujhe to do vara pasanda hain| ek vaha jo relavaee men hai, aur doosaraa yaha jo chhopekhaane men kaama karta hai|

kalyaaNee--magara pahale ke to khaanadaana men aapa dopa bataate hain|

mote0--haa.N, yaha dopa to hai| to chhaapekhaane vaale hee ko rahane deejie|

kalyaaNee--yahaa.N ek hajaara dene ko kahaa.N se aaegaa? ek hajaara to aapa kaa anumaana hai, shaayada vaha aur bhee mu.Nha phailaae| aapa to ghar kee dashaa dekha hee rahe hain, khaanaa milataa jaaya, yahee ganeemata hai| rupaye kahaa.N se aae.Nge| jameendaara saahaba chaara hajaara sunaate hain, daaka vaaboo bhee do hajaara kaa savaala karte hain| inako jaane deejie| basa, vakeela saahava hee bacha rahate hain; paiteesa saala kee umra bhee kuchha aisee jyaadaa naheen, inheen ko kyon na rakhie|

moteraama--aapa khooba socha-socha lo, main to aapa kee marjee kaa taavedaara hoo.N| jahaa.N kahiegaa, vahaa.N jaakara teekaa kar aaoo.Ngaa| magara hajaara dedha-hajaara kaa mu.Nha na dekhie, chhaapekhaane vaalaa ladakaa ratna hai| usake saatha kanyaa kaa jeevana kaamayaaba ho jaavegaa| jaisee yaha roopa aur guNa kee pooree hai, vaisaa hee ladakaa bhee sundara aur susheela hai|

kalyaaNee-'pasanda to mujhe bhee yahee hai; mahaaraaja! para rupaye kisake gharase aae.N? kauna dene vaalaa hai? hai koee aisaa daana? khaane

vaale to khaa-peekara champata hue| aba kisee kee soorata bhee naheen dikhaaee detee; balki aur mujhase buraa maanate hain ki hamen nikaala diya| jo baat apane basa ke baahara hai, usake lie haath hee kyon phailaaoo.N| bachchaa kisako pyaaree naheen hoti? kauna use sukhee naheen dekhanaa chaahataa; para jaba apanaa kaaboo bhee ho| aapa eeshvara kaa naama lekara vakeela saahaba ko teekaa kar aaie| aayu kuchha zyaadaa hai; lekin maranaa-jeenaa vidhi ke haath hai| painteesa saala kaa aadamee buddhaa naheen kahalaataa| agar ladakee ke bhaagya men sukha bhoganaa badaa hai, to jahaa.N jaayagee sukhee rahegee; dukha bhoganaa hai, to jahaa.N jaayagee dukha jhelegee| hamaaree nirmalaa ko bachchon se pyaara hai| unake bachchon ko apanaa samajhegee| aapa shubha-muhoorta dekha kar teekaa kar aae.N!!
