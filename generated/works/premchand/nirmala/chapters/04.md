kalyaanee ke saamane ab ek visham samasyaa aa kha. dee huee. pati ke maut ke baad use apanee duraavasthaa kaa yeh pahl aur bahut hee ka. dv anubhav huaa. gareeb vidhv ke lie isase ba. dee aur kyaa museebat ho sakatee hai ki javaan betee sir par savaar ho? la. dake nange paanv pa. dhane jaa sakate hain, chaukaa-bartan bhee apane haath se kiyaa jaa sakataa hai, rookhaa-sookhaa khaakar guzaaraa kiyaa jaa sakataa hai, jhopa. de men din kaate jaa sakate hain; lekin yuvatee kanyaa ghar men naheen bithaaee jaa sakatee. kalyaanee ko bhaalachandr par aisaa gussaa aataa thaa ki svayan jaakar usake munh men kaalikh lagaaoon, sir ke baal noch loon. kahoon, too apanee baat se phir gayaa, too apane baap kaa betaa naheen. pandit moteraam ne unakee kapata-leelaa kaa nagn vrittaant sunaa diyaa thaa.

woh isee gussaa men bharee baithee thee ki krishnaa khelatee huee aaee, aur bolee—kai din men baaraat aaegee; ammaan! pandit jee to aa gae.
kalyaanee--baaraat kaa sapn dekh rahee hai kyaa?

krishnaa--vahee chandar to kah rahaa hai ki do-teen din men baaraat aaegee. kyaa na aaegee ammaan?

kalyaanee--ek baar to kah diyaa, sir kyon khaatee hai?

krishnaa--sab ke ghar to baaraat aa rahee hai, hamaare yahaan kyon naheen aatee?

kalyaanee--tere yahaan jo baaraat laane vaalaa thaa, usake ghar men aag lag gaee.

krishna--sach ammaan? tab to saaraa ghar jal gayaa hogaa. kahaan rahate honge? bahin kahaan jaakar rahegee?

kalyaanee--are pagalee, too to baat hee naheen samajhatee. aag naheen lagee. woh hamaare yahaan vyaah na karegaa.

krishnaa--yeh kyon ammaan? pahale to vahaan theek ho gayaa thaa na?

kalyaanee--bahut se rupaye maangataa hai. mere paas use dene ko rupaye naheen hain.

krishnaa--kyaa ba. de laalachee hain ammaan?

kalyaanee--laalachee naheen to aur kyaa hai! pooraa kasaaee, nirdaee, dagaabaaz!

krishnaa--tab to ammaan bahut achchhaa huaa ki usake ghar bahin kaa vyaah naheen huaa. bahin unake saath kaise rahatee. yeh to khash hone kee baat hai ammaan, tum ranj kyon karatee ho?
kalyaanee ne betee ko snehamay nazar se dekhaa. isakaa kathan kitn saty hai. bhole shabdon men samasyaa kaa kitn maarmik niroopan hai. sachamuch yeh to khush hone kee baat hai ki aise kupaatron se rishtaa naheen huaa, ranj kee koee baat naheen. aise kumaanuson ke beech men vechaaree nirml kee na jaane kyaa gati ho? apane naseevon ko rotee. jr saa ghee daal men zyaadaa pa. d jaataa, to saare ghar men shor mach jaataa; jr khaanaa jyaadaa pak jaataa, to saas duniyaa sir par uthaa letee. la. dakaa bhee aisaa hee lobhee hai. ba. dee achchhee baat huee, naheen to bechaaree ko unn bhar ronaa pa. dataa. kalyaanee yahaan se tathee, to usakaa dil halakaa ho gayaa thaa.

lekin shaadee to karn hee thaa, aur ho sake to isee saal; naheen to doosare saal phir nae sire se taiyaariyaan karanee pa. dengee. ab achchhe ghar kee jaroorat na thee, achchhe doolhaa kee zaroorat na thee. abhaaginee ko achchhaa ghar-ghar kahaan milataa hai. ab to kisee bhaॉti sir kaa bojh utaarn thaa, kisee tarah la. dakee ko paar lagaanaa thaa--use kuen men jhonkn thaa. woh roopavatee hai, gunasheelaa hai, hoshiyaar hai, kuleenaa hai, to huaa kare; dahej naheen to usake saare gun dosh hain. dahej ho to saare dop, gun hain. praanee kaa koee keemat naheen, sirph dahej kaa keemat hai. kitanee visham bhaagyaleelaa hai?

kalyaanee kaa dosh kuchh kam na thaa. abl aur vidhv honaa hee use doshon se mukt naheen kar sakataa. use apane la. dake

apanee la. dakiyon se kaheen pyaare the. la. dake hal ke bail hain, bhoose-khalee par pahl haq unakaa hai, unake khaane se jo bache woh gaayon kaa! makaan thaa, kuchh naqad thaa, kaee hajaar ke gahane the; lekin use abhee do la. dakon kaa paalana-poshan karn thaa, unhen pa. dhaanaa-likhaanaa thaa, ek kanyaa aur bhee chaara-paanch saal men shaadee karane ke yogy ho jaayagee. isalie woh koee ba. dee rakam dahej men na de sakatee thee. aakhir la. dakon ko bhee to kuchh chaahie. ve kyaa samajhege ki hamaaraa bhee koee baap thaa.

pandit moteraam ko lakhanaoo se laute pandrah din beet chuke the. lautane ke baad doosare hee din se woh doolhaa kee khoj men nikale the. unhonne pran kiyaa thaa, main ina lakhanaoo vaalon ko dikhaa doongaa ki sansaar men tumheen akele naheen ho, tumhaare aise aur kitane pa. de hue hain. kalyaanee roz din ginaa karatee thee. aaj usane unhen chitthee likhane kaa phaisl kiyaa aur kalam daavaat lekar baithee hee thee ki pandit moteraam ne padaarpan kiyaa.

kalyaanee--aaie pandit jee, main to aapako khat likhane jaa rahee thee. kab laut?

moteraama--lautaa to subah hee thaa; par usee samay ek seth ke yahaan se nimantran aa gayaa. kaee din se tar maal na mile the. mainne kahaa ki lage haath yeh kaam bhee nipataataa chaloon. abhee udhar hee se lautaa aa rahaa hoon, koee paanch sau braahmanon kee pangat thee.
kalyaanee--kuchh kaary bhee siddh huaa, yaa raastaa hee naapn pa. daa?

mote0--kaary kyon na siddh hotaa, bhl yeh bhee koee baat hai? paanch jagah baatacheet kar aayaa hoon. paanchon kee naqal laayaa hoon. usamen se aap jise chaahen pasand karen. yeh dekhie, is la. dake kaa baap daak ke sege men 100 maheene kaa naukar hai. la. dakaa abhee kaॉlej men pa. dh rahaa hai. magar naukaree hee kaa bharosaa hai, ghar men koee jaayadaad naheen. la. dakaa honahaar maaloom hotaa hai. khaanadaan bhee achchhaa hai. 2000 men baat tay ho jaayagee. maangate to woh teen hazaar hain.

kalyaanee--la. dake ke aur bhee bhaaee hain?

mote0--naheen, magar teen bahinen hain; aur teenon kaaree! maan zindaa hai. achchhaa, ab doosaree nakal dekhie. yeh la. dakaa rel ke sege men 50 maheenaa paataa hai. maan-baap naheen hain. bahut hee roopavaan, susheel aur shareer se khoov hrishta-pusht, kasaratee javaan hai. magar khaanadaan achchhaa naheen koee kahataa hai, maan naain thee, koee kahataa hai thakuraain thee. baap kisee riyaasat men mukhtaar the. ghar par tho. dee see zameendaaree hai. magar us par kaee hajaar kaa karjaa hai. yahaan kuchh lenaa-denaa na pa. degaa. umr koee vees saal hogee.

kalyaanee--khaanadaan men daaraa na hotaa, to manjoor kar letee. dekh kar to makkhee naheen nigalee jaatee.
mote0--teesaree nakkal dekhie. ek zameendaar kaa la. dakaa hai. koee ek hajaar saalaanaa naphaa hai. kuchh khetee-baaree bhee hotee hai. la. dakaa pa. dhaa-likhaa to tho. daa hee hai; par kachaharee adaalat ke kaam men hoshiyaar hai. duhaajoo hai. pahalee aurat ko mare do saal hue. usase koee bachchaa naheen hai. lekin rahana-sahan motaa hai. peesanaa-kootn ghar hee men hotaa hai.

kalyaanee--kuchh dahej bhee maangate hain?

moteraama--isakee kuchh na poochhie. chaar hajaar sunaate hain. achchhaa, yeh chauthee nakal dekhie. la. dakaa vakeel hai, umr koee paitees saal hogee. teena-chaar sau kee aamadanee hai. pahalee aurat mar chukee hai. usase teen la. dake bhee hain. apn ghar banavaayaa hai. kuchh jaayadaad bhee khareedee hai. yahaan bhee lena-den kaa jhaga. daa naheen hai.

kalyaanee--khaanadaan kaisaa hai?

mote0--bahut hee uttam, puraane raees hain. achchhaa, yeh paanchaveen nakal dekhie. baap kaa chhaapaakhaanaa hai. la. dakaa pa. dhaa to bee0 e0 tak hai, par useechhaapekhaane men kaam karataa hai. umr athaarah saal hogee. ghar men pres ke sivaay koee jaayadaad naheen hai; magar kisee kaa qarjaa sir par naheen. khaanadaan na bahut achchhaa hai, na buraa. la. dakaa bahut sundar aur sachcharitr hai. magar ek hajaar se kam men maaml tay na hogaa, maangate to woh teena-hazaar hain. ab bataaie aap kaun saa doolhaa pasand karatee hain.

kalyaanee--aapako sabon men kaun pasand hai?
mote0--mujhe to do doolhaa pasand hain. ek woh jo relavaee men hai, aur doosr yeh jo chhopekhaane men kaam karataa hai.

kalyaanee--magar pahale ke to khaanadaan men aap dop bataate hain.

mote0--haan, yeh dop to hai. to chhaapekhaane vaale hee ko rahane deejie.

kalyaanee--yahaan ek hazaar dene ko kahaan se aaegaa? ek hajaar to aap kaa anumaan hai, shaayad woh aur bhee munh phailaae. aap to ghar kee haal dekh hee rahe hain, khaanaa milataa jaay, yahee ganeemat hai. rupaye kahaan se aaenge. jameendaar saahab chaar hajaar sunaate hain, daak vaaboo bhee do hajaar kaa savaal karate hain. inako jaane deejie. bas, vakeel saahav hee bach rahate hain; paitees saal kee umr bhee kuchh aisee jyaadaa naheen, inheen ko kyon na rakhie.

moteraama--aap khoob socha-soch lo, main to aap kee marjee kaa taavedaar hoon. jahaan kahiegaa, vahaan jaakar teekaa kar aaoongaa. magar hazaar de. dha-hajaar kaa munh na dekhie, chhaapekhaane vaalaa la. dakaa ratn hai. usake saath kanyaa kaa jeevan kaamayaab ho jaavegaa. jaisee yeh roop aur gun kee pooree hai, vaisaa hee la. dakaa bhee sundar aur susheel hai.

kalyaanee-'pasand to mujhe bhee yahee hai; mahaaraaj! par rupaye kisake gharase aaen? kaun dene vaalaa hai? hai koee aisaa daan? khaane

vaale to khaa-peekar champat hue. ab kisee kee soorat bhee naheen dikhaaee detee; balki aur mujhase buraa maanate hain ki hamen nikaal diyaa. jo baat apane bas ke baahar hai, usake lie haath hee kyon phailaaoon. bachchaa kisako pyaaree naheen hotee? kaun use sukhee naheen dekhn chaahataa; par jab apn qaaboo bhee ho. aap eeshvar kaa naam lekar vakeel saahab ko teekaa kar aaie. aayu kuchh zyaadaa hai; lekin maranaa-jeenaa vidhi ke haath hai. paintees saal kaa aadamee buddhaa naheen kahalaataa. agar la. dakee ke bhaagy men sukh bhogn badaa hai, to jahaan jaayagee sukhee rahegee; dukh bhogn hai, to jahaan jaayagee dukh jhelegee. hamaaree nirml ko bachchon se pyaar hai. unake bachchon ko apn samajhegee. aap shubha-muhoort dekh kar teekaa kar aaen!!
