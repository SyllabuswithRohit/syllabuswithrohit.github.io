# Nirmala — Adhyay 4

**Munshi Premchand**

chauthaa parichchheda

kalyaanee ke saamane ab ek vishama samasyaa aa kha.dee hui| pati ke dehaanta ke baada use apanee duraavasthaa kaa yaha pahalaa aur bahut hee ka.davaa anubhava hua| daridra vidhavaa ke lie isase ba.dee aur kyaa vipatti ho sakatee hai ki javaana betee sira par savaara ho? ladke nange paanva pa.dhane jaa sakate hain, chaukaa-bartana bhee apane haath se kiya jaa sakata hai, rookhaa-sookhaa khaakara nirvaaha kiya jaa sakata hai, jhopa.de men din kaate jaa sakate hain; lekin yuvatee kanyaa ghar men naheen bithaaee jaa sakatee| kalyaanee ko bhaalachandra par aisaa krodha aata tha ki svayan jaakara usake munha men kaalikha lagaaoon, sira ke baala nocha loon| kahoon, too apanee baat se phir gaya, too apane baapa kaa betaa naheen| pandita moteraama ne unakee kapata-leelaa kaa nagna vrittaanta sunaa diya tha|

vaha isee krodha men bharee baithee thi ki krishnaa khelatee hui aaee, aur boli—kai din men baaraata aaegee; ammaan! pandita jee to aa gae| kalyaanee--baaraata kaa sapana dekha rahi hai kya?

krishnaa--vahee chandara to kaha raha hai ki do-teena din men baaraata aaegee| kyaa na aaegee ammaan?

kalyaanee--ek baara to kaha diya, sira kyon khaatee hai?

krishnaa--sab ke ghar to baaraata aa rahi hai, hamaare yahaan kyon naheen aati?

kalyaanee--tere yahaan jo baaraata laane vaalaa tha, usake ghar men aaga laga gaee|

krishna--sacha ammaan? tab to saaraa ghar jala gaya hogaa| kahaan rahate honge? bahina kahaan jaakara rahegee?

kalyaanee--are pagalee, too to baat hee naheen samajhatee| aaga naheen lagee| vaha hamaare yahaan vyaaha na karegaa|

krishnaa--yaha kyon ammaan? pahale to vahaan theeka ho gaya tha na?

kalyaanee--bahut se rupaye maangata hai| mere paasa use dene ko rupaye naheen hain|

krishnaa--kyaa ba.de laalachee hain ammaan?

kalyaanee--laalachee naheen to aur kyaa hai! pooraa kasaaee, nirdaee, dagaabaaza!

krishnaa--tab to ammaan bahut achchha hua ki usake ghar bahina kaa vyaaha naheen hua|bahina unake saatha kaise rahatee| yaha to khasha hone kee baat hai ammaan, tuma ranja kyon karti ho? kalyaanee ne putree ko snehamaya drishti se dekha| isakaa kathana kitana satya hai| bhole shabdon men samasyaa kaa kitana maarmika niroopana hai| sachamucha yaha to prasanna hone kee baat hai ki aise kupaatron se sambandha naheen hua, ranja kee koee baat naheen| aise kumaanuson ke beecha men vechaaree nirmalaa kee na jaane kyaa gati ho? apane naseevon ko rotee| jaraa saa ghee daala men adhika pa.da jaata, to saare ghar men shora macha jaata; jaraa khaanaa zyada paka jaata, to saasa duniya sira par uthaa letee| ladka bhee aisaa hee lobhee hai| ba.dee achchhi baat hui, naheen to bechaaree ko unna bhara ronaa pa.data| kalyaanee yahaan se tathee, to usakaa hridaya halakaa ho gaya tha|

lekin vivaaha to karana hee tha, aur ho sake to isee saala; naheen to doosare saala phir nae sire se taiyaariyaan karanee pa.dengee| ab achchhe ghar kee jaroorata na thi, achchhe vara kee zaroorat na thi| abhaaginee ko achchha ghar-ghar kahaan milata hai| ab to kisee bhaoti sira kaa bojha utaarana tha, kisee bhaanti ladki ko paara lagaanaa tha--use kuen men jhonkana tha| vaha roopavatee hai, gunasheelaa hai, chatura hai, kuleenaa hai, to hua kare; daheja naheen to usake saare guna dosha hain| daheja ho to saare dopa, guna hain| praanee kaa koee moolya naheen, kevala daheja kaa moolya hai| kitanee vishama bhaagyaleelaa hai?

kalyaanee kaa dosha kuchha kama na tha| abalaa aur vidhavaa honaa hee use doshon se mukta naheen kar sakata| use apane ladke apanee ladakiyon se kaheen pyaare the| ladke hala ke baila hain, bhoose-khalee par pahalaa haqa unakaa hai, unake khaane se jo bache vaha gaayon ka! makaana tha, kuchha naqada tha, kaee hajaara ke gahane the; lekin use abhee do ladakon kaa paalana-poshana karana tha, unhen pa.dhaanaa-likhaanaa tha, ek kanyaa aur bhee chaara-paancha saala men vivaaha karane ke yogya ho jaayagee| isalie vaha koee ba.dee rakama daheja men na de sakatee thi| aakhira ladakon ko bhee to kuchha chaahie| ve kyaa samajhege ki hamaaraa bhee koee baapa tha|

kalyaanee--aaie pandita jee, main to aapako khata likhane jaa rahi thi| kab lauta?

moteraama--lautaa to praatahkaala hee tha; par usee samay ek setha ke yahaan se nimantrana aa gaya| kaee din se tara maala na mile the| mainne kaha ki lage haath yaha kaama bhee nipataataa chaloon| abhee udhara hee se lautaa aa raha hoon, koee paancha sau braahmanon kee pangata thi| kalyaanee--kuchha kaarya bhee siddha hua, yaa raastaa hee naapana pa.da?

mote0--kaarya kyon na siddha hota, bhalaa yaha bhee koee baat hai? paancha jagaha baatacheeta kar aaya hoon| paanchon kee naqala laayaa hoon| usamen se aapa jise chaahen pasanda karen| yaha dekhie, is ladke kaa baapa daaka ke sege men 100 maheene kaa naukara hai| ladka abhee kaoleja men pa.dha raha hai| magar naukaree hee kaa bharosaa hai, ghar men koee jaayadaada naheen| ladka honahaara maalooma hota hai| khaanadaana bhee achchha hai| 2000 men baat taya ho jaayagee| maangate to vaha teena hazaara hain|

kalyaanee--ladke ke aur bhee bhaaee hain?

mote0--naheen, magar teena bahinen hain; aur teenon kaaree! maataa jeevita hai| achchha, ab doosaree nakala dekhie| yaha ladka rela ke sege men 50 maheenaa paataa hai| maan-baapa naheen hain| bahut hee roopavaan, susheela aur shareera se khoova hrishta-pushta, kasaratee javaana hai| magar khaanadaana achchha naheen koee kahata hai, maan naaina thi, koee kahata hai thakuraaina thi| baapa kisee riyaasata men mukhtaara the| ghar par tho.dee see zameendaaree hai| magar us par kaee hajaara kaa karjaa hai| yahaan kuchha lena-dena na pa.degaa| umra koee veesa saala hogee|

kalyaanee--khaanadaana men daaraa na hota, to manjoora kar letee| dekha kar to makkhee naheen nigalee jati| mote0--teesaree nakkala dekhie| ek zameendaara kaa ladka hai| koee ek hajaara saalaanaa naphaa hai| kuchha khetee-baaree bhee hoti hai| ladka pa.dhaa-likhaa to tho.daa hee hai; par kachaharee adaalata ke kaama men chatura hai| duhaajoo hai| pahalee stree ko mare do saala hue| usase koee santaana naheen hai| lekin rahana-sahana motaa hai| peesana-kootana ghar hee men hota hai|

kalyaanee--kuchha daheja bhee maangate hain?

moteraama--isakee kuchha na poochhie| chaara hajaara sunaate hain| achchha,yaha chauthee nakala dekhie| ladka vakeela hai, umra koee paiteesa saala hogee| teena-chaara sau kee aamadanee hai| pahalee stree mara chukee hai| usase teena ladke bhee hain| apana ghar banavaayaa hai| kuchha jaayadaada bhee khareedee hai| yahaan bhee lena-dena kaa jhaga.daa naheen hai|

kalyaanee--khaanadaana kaisaa hai?

mote0--bahut hee uttama, puraane raeesa hain| achchha, yaha paanchaveen nakala dekhie| baapa kaa chhaapaakhaanaa hai| ladka pa.dhaa to bee0 e0 taka hai, par useechhaapekhaane men kaama karata hai| umra athaaraha saala hogee| ghar men presa ke sivaaya koee jaayadaada naheen hai; magar kisee kaa qarjaa sira par naheen| khaanadaana na bahut achchha hai, na buraa|ladka bahut sundara aur sachcharitra hai| magar ek hajaara se kama men maamalaa taya na hoga, maangate to vaha teena-hazaara hain| ab bataaie aapa kaun saa vara pasanda karti hain|

kalyaanee--aapako sabon men kaun pasanda hai? mote0--mujhe to do vara pasanda hain| ek vaha jo relavaee men hai, aur doosaraa yaha jo chhopekhaane men kaama karata hai|

kalyaanee--magar pahale ke to khaanadaana men aapa dopa bataate hain|

mote0--haan, yaha dopa to hai| to chhaapekhaane vaale hee ko rahane deejie|

kalyaanee--yahaan ek hazaara dene ko kahaan se aaega? ek hajaara to aapa kaa anumaana hai, shaayada vaha aur bhee munha phailaae| aapa to ghar kee dashaa dekha hee rahe hain, bhojana milata jaaya, yahee ganeemata hai| rupaye kahaan se aaenge| jameendaara saahaba chaara hajaara sunaate hain, daaka vaaboo bhee do hajaara kaa savaala karte hain| inako jaane deejie| basa, vakeela saahava hee bacha rahate hain; paiteesa saala kee umra bhee kuchha aisee zyada naheen, inheen ko kyon na rakhie|

moteraama--aapa khooba socha-vichaara lo, main to aapa kee marjee kaa taavedaara hoon| jahaan kahiega, vahaan jaakara teekaa kar aaoongaa| magar hazaara de.dha-hajaara kaa munha na dekhie, chhaapekhaane vaalaa ladka ratna hai| usake saatha kanyaa kaa jeevana saphala ho jaavegaa| jaisee yaha roopa aur guna kee pooree hai, vaisaa hee ladka bhee sundara aur susheela hai|

kalyaanee-'pasanda to mujhe bhee yahee hai; mahaaraaja! par rupaye kisake gharase aaen? kaun dene vaalaa hai? hai koee aisaa daana? khaane vaale to khaa-peekara champata hue| ab kisee kee soorata bhee naheen dikhaaee detee; balki aur mujhase buraa maanate hain ki hamen nikaala diya| jo baat apane basa ke baahara hai, usake lie haath hee kyon phailaaoon| santaana kisako pyaaree naheen hoti? kaun use sukhee naheen dekhana chaahata; par jab apana qaaboo bhee ho| aapa eeshvara kaa naama lekara vakeela saahaba ko teekaa kar aaie| aayu kuchha adhika hai; lekin marana-jeenaa vidhi ke haath hai| painteesa saala kaa aadmi buddhaa naheen kahalaataa| agar ladki ke bhaagya men sukha bhogana bada hai, to jahaan jaayagee sukhee rahegee; dukha bhogana hai, to jahaan jaayagee dukha jhelegee| hamaaree nirmalaa ko bachchon se prema hai| unake bachchon ko apana samajhegee| aapa shubha-muhoorta dekha kar teekaa kar aaen!!
