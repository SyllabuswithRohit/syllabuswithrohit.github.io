# Nirmala — Adhyay 4

**Munshi Premchand**

chauthaa parichchhed

kalyaanee ke saamane ab ek visham mushkil aa khadee huee. shauhar ke dehaant ke baad use apanee duraavasthaa ka yah pahalaa aur bahut hee kadavaa tajurbaa huaa. daridr vidhavaa ke lie isase badee aur kyaa vipatti ho sakatee hai ki javaan betee sir par savaar ho? ladake nanggge paanv padhane jaa sakate hain, chaukaa-bartan bhi apane haath se kiyaa jaa sakataa hai, rookhaa-sookhaa khaakar nirvaah kiyaa jaa sakataa hai, jhopade mein din kaate jaa sakate hain; lekin javaan ladakee kanyaa ghar mein nahin bithaaee jaa sakatee. kalyaanee ko bhaalachandr par aisaa krodh aataa tha ki svayan jaakar usake munh mein kaalikh lagaaoon, sir ke baal noch loon. kahoon, too apanee baat se phir gayaa, too apane baap ka betaa naheen. pandit moteraam ne unakee kapat-leelaa ka nagn vrittaant sunaa diyaa thaa.

vah isee krodh mein bharee baithee thi ki krishnaa khelatee huee aaee, aur bolee — kai din mein baaraat aaegee; ammaan! pandit jee to aa gae. kalyaanee--baaraat ka sapanaa dekh rahee hai kyaa?

krishnaa--vahee chandar to kah rahaa hai ki do-teen din mein baaraat aaegee. kyaa n aaegee ammaan?

kalyaanee--ek baar to kah diyaa, sir kyon khaatee hai?

krishnaa--sab ke ghar to baaraat aa rahee hai, hamaare yahan kyon nahin aatee?

kalyaanee--tere yahan jo baaraat laane vaalaa tha, usake ghar mein aag lag gaee.

krishn--sach ammaan? tab to saaraa ghar jal gayaa hogaa. kahaan rahate hongge? bahin kahaan jaakar rahegee?

kalyaanee--are pagalee, too to baat hee nahin samajhatee. aag nahin lagee. vah hamaare yahan vyaah n karegaa.

krishnaa--yah kyon ammaan? pahale to wahan theek ho gayaa tha n?

kalyaanee--bahut se rupaye maanggataa hai. mere paas use dene ko rupaye nahin hain.

krishnaa--kyaa bade laalachee hain ammaan?

kalyaanee--laalachee nahin to aur kyaa hai! pooraa kasaaee, nirdaee, dagaabaaj!

krishnaa--tab to ammaan bahut achchha huaa ki usake ghar bahin ka vyaah nahin huaa. bahin unake saath kaise rahatee. yah to khash hone ki baat hai ammaan, tum ranyj kyon karatee ho? kalyaanee ne putree ko pyaaramay drishti se dekhaa. isakaa baat kitanaa sach hai. bhole shabdon mein mushkil ka kitanaa maarmik niroopan hai. sachamuch yah to khush hone ki baat hai ki aise kupaatron se rishtaa nahin huaa, ranyj ki koee baat naheen. aise kumaanuson ke beech mein vechaaree Nirmala ki n jaane kyaa gati ho? apane naseevon ko rotee. jaraa saa ghee daal mein adhik pad jaataa, to saare ghar mein shor mach jaataa; jaraa khana jyaadaa pak jaataa, to saas duniya sir par uthaa letee. ladakaa bhi aisaa hee lobhee hai. badee achchhi baat huee, nahin to bechaaree ko unn bhar ronaa padataa. kalyaanee yahan se tathee, to usakaa dil halakaa ho gayaa thaa.

lekin shaadee to karanaa hee tha, aur ho sake to isee saal; nahin to doosare saal phir nae sire se taiyaariyaan karanee padenggee. ab achchhe ghar ki jaroorat n thi, achchhe var ki zaroorat n thee. abhaaginee ko achchha ghar-ghar kahaan milataa hai. ab to kisee bhoti sir ka bojh utaaranaa tha, kisee bhaanti ladakee ko paar lagaanaa tha--use kuen mein jhongkanaa thaa. vah roopavatee hai, gunasheelaa hai, chatur hai, kuleenaa hai, to huaa kare; dahej nahin to usake saare gun dosh hain. dahej ho to saare dop, gun hain. praanee ka koee mooly nahin, keval dahej ka mooly hai. kitanee visham kismataleelaa hai?

kalyaanee ka dosh kuchh kam n thaa. abalaa aur vidhavaa honaa hee use doshon se mukt nahin kar sakataa. use apane ladake apanee ladakiyon se kaheen pyaare the. ladake hal ke bail hain, bhoose-khalee par pahalaa hak unakaa hai, unake khaane se jo bache vah gaayon ka! makaan tha, kuchh nakad tha, kaee hajaar ke gahane the; lekin use abhee do ladakon ka paalan-poshan karanaa tha, unhen padhaanaa-likhaanaa tha, ek kanyaa aur bhi chaar-paanch saal mein shaadee karane ke yogy ho jaayagee. isalie vah koee badee rakam dahej mein n de sakatee thee. aakhir ladakon ko bhi to kuchh chaahie. ve kyaa samajhege ki hamaaraa bhi koee baap thaa.

kalyaanee--aaie pandit jee, main to aapko khat likhane jaa rahee thee. kab laut?

moteraam--lautaa to subah hee tha; par usee samay ek seth ke yahan se nimantran aa gayaa. kaee din se tar maal n mile the. mainne kahaa ki lage haath yah kaam bhi nipataataa chaloon. abhee udhar hee se lautaa aa rahaa hoon, koee paanch sau braahmanon ki pangggat thee. kalyaanee--kuchh kaary bhi siddh huaa, ya raastaa hee naapanaa padaa?

mote0--kaary kyon n siddh hotaa, bhalaa yah bhi koee baat hai? paanch jagah baatacheet kar kamaaeeaa hoon. paanchon ki nakal laayaa hoon. usamen se aap jise chaahen pasand karen. yah dekhie, is ladake ka baap daak ke sege mein 100 maheene ka naukar hai. ladakaa abhee kolej mein padh rahaa hai. magar naukaree hee ka bharosaa hai, ghar mein koee jaayadaad naheen. ladakaa honahaar maaloom hotaa hai. khaanadaan bhi achchha hai. 2000 mein baat tay ho jaayagee. maanggate to vah teen hajaar hain.

kalyaanee--ladake ke aur bhi bhaaee hain?

mote0--nahin, magar teen bahinen hain; aur teenon kaaree! maataa zinda hai. achchha, ab doosaree nakal dekhie. yah ladakaa rel ke sege mein 50 maheenaa paataa hai. maan-baap nahin hain. bahut hee roopavaan, susheel aur shareer se khoov hrisht-pusht, kasaratee javaan hai. magar khaanadaan achchha nahin koee kahataa hai, maan naain thi, koee kahataa hai thakuraain thee. baap kisee riyaasat mein munhtaar the. ghar par thodee see jameendaaree hai. magar us par kaee hajaar ka karjaa hai. yahan kuchh lenaa-denaa n padegaa. umr koee vees saal hogee.

kalyaanee--khaanadaan mein daaraa n hotaa, to manyjoor kar letee. dekh kar to makkhee nahin nigalee jaatee. mote0--teesaree nakkal dekhie. ek jameendaar ka ladakaa hai. koee ek hajaar saalaanaa naphaa hai. kuchh khetee-baaree bhi hotee hai. ladakaa padhaa-likhaa to thodaa hee hai; par kachaharee adaalat ke kaam mein chatur hai. duhaajoo hai. pahalee aurat ko mare do saal hue. usase koee santaan nahin hai. lekin rahna-sahan motaa hai. peesanaa-kootanaa ghar hee mein hotaa hai.

kalyaanee--kuchh dahej bhi maanggate hain?

moteraam--isakee kuchh n poochhie. chaar hajaar sunaate hain. achchha, yah chauthee nakal dekhie. ladakaa vakeel hai, umr koee paitees saal hogee. teen-chaar sau ki aamadanee hai. pahalee aurat mar chukee hai. usase teen ladake bhi hain. apanaa ghar banavaayaa hai. kuchh jaayadaad bhi khareedee hai. yahan bhi len-den ka jhagadaa nahin hai.

kalyaanee--khaanadaan kaisaa hai?

mote0--bahut hee uttam, puraane raees hain. achchha, yah paanchaveen nakal dekhie. baap ka chhaapaakhaanaa hai. ladakaa padhaa to bee0 e0 tak hai, par useechhaapekhaane mein kaam karataa hai. umr athaarah saal hogee. ghar mein pres ke sivaay koee jaayadaad nahin hai; magar kisee ka karjaa sir par naheen. khaanadaan n bahut achchha hai, n buraa. ladakaa bahut sundar aur sachcharitr hai. magar ek hajaar se kam mein maamalaa tay n hogaa, maanggate to vah teen-hajaar hain. ab bataaie aap kaun saa var pasand karatee hain.

kalyaanee--aapko sabon mein kaun pasand hai? mote0--mujhe to do var pasand hain. ek vah jo relavaee mein hai, aur doosaraa yah jo chhopekhaane mein kaam karataa hai.

kalyaanee--magar pahale ke to khaanadaan mein aap dop bataate hain.

mote0--haan, yah dop to hai. to chhaapekhaane vaale hee ko rahane deejie.

kalyaanee--yahan ek hajaar dene ko kahaan se aaegaa? ek hajaar to aap ka anumaan hai, shaayad vah aur bhi munh phailaae. aap to ghar ki dashaa dekh hee rahe hain, khana milataa jaay, yahee ganeemat hai. rupaye kahaan se aaengge. jameendaar Sahab chaar hajaar sunaate hain, daak vaaboo bhi do hajaar ka savaal karate hain. inako jaane deejie. bas, vakeel saahav hee bach rahate hain; paitees saal ki umr bhi kuchh aisee jyaadaa nahin, inheen ko kyon n rakhie.

moteraam--aap khoob soch-soch lo, main to aap ki marjee ka taavedaar hoon. jahaan kahiegaa, wahan jaakar teekaa kar aaoonggaa. magar hajaar dedh-hajaar ka munh n dekhie, chhaapekhaane vaalaa ladakaa ratn hai. usake saath kanyaa ka zindagi kaamayaab ho jaavegaa. jaisee yah roop aur gun ki pooree hai, vaisaa hee ladakaa bhi sundar aur susheel hai.

kalyaanee-'pasand to mujhe bhi yahee hai; mahaaraaj! par rupaye kisake gharase aaen? kaun dene vaalaa hai? hai koee aisaa daan? khaane vaale to khaa-peekar champat hue. ab kisee ki soorat bhi nahin dikhaaee detee; balki aur mujhase buraa maanate hain ki hamen nikaal diyaa. jo baat apane bas ke baahar hai, usake lie haath hee kyon phailaaoon. santaan kisako pyaaree nahin hotee? kaun use sukhee nahin dekhanaa chaahataa; par jab apanaa kaaboo bhi ho. aap Bhagwan ka naam lekar vakeel Sahab ko teekaa kar aaie. kamaaeeu kuchh adhik hai; lekin maranaa-jeenaa vidhi ke haath hai. paintees saal ka aadmi buddhaa nahin kahalaataa. agar ladakee ke kismat mein sukh bhoganaa badaa hai, to jahaan jaayagee sukhee rahegee; dukh bhoganaa hai, to jahaan jaayagee dukh jhelegee. hamaaree Nirmala ko bachchon se pyaar hai. unake bachchon ko apanaa samajhegee. aap shubh-muhoort dekh kar teekaa kar aaen!!
