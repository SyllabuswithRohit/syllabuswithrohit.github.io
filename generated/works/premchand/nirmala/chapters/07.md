saatavaan parichchheda

us din se nirmalaa kaa ranga-dhanga badalane lagaa| usane apane ko karttavya par mitaa dene kaa nishchaya kar liya| ab taka nairaashya ke santaapa men usane karttavya par dhyaana hee na diya tha| usake hridaya men viplava kee jvaalaa see dahakatee rahatee thi, jisakee asahya vedana ne use sanjnaaheena saa kar rakkhaa tha| ab us vedana kaa vega shaanta hone lagaa| use jnaana hua ki mere lie jeevana men koee aananda naheen| usakaa svapna dekha kar kyon is jeevana ko nashta karoon| sansaara men sab ke sab praanee sukha-seja hee par to naheen sote? main bhee unheen abhaagon men hoon| mujhe bhee vidhaataa ne dukha kee gatharee dhone ke lie chunaa hai| vaha bojha sira se utara naheen sakata| use phenkana bhee chaahoon, to naheen phenka sakatee| us kathina bhaara se chaahe aankhon men andheraa aa jaaya,chaahe gardana tootane lage,chaahe pair uthaanaa dustara ho jaaya;lekin vaha gatharee dhonee hee pa.degee| umra bhara kaa qadee kahaan taka roega? roe bhee to kaun dekhata hai? kise us par dayaa aati hai? rone se kaama men harja hone ke kaarana use aur yaatanaaen hee to sahanee pa.datee hain!

nirmalaa ne kaha-aaja itanee dera kahaan lagaaee| din bhara raaha dekhate-dekhate aankhen phoota jati hain|

totaaraama ne khi.dakee kee ora taakate hue javaaba diya-muqadamon ke maare dama maarane kee chhuttee naheen milatee| abhee ek mukadamaa aur tha,lekin main sira-darda kaa bahaanaa karake bhaaga kha.daa hua|

nirmalaa-to kyon itane mukadame lete ho? kaama utana hee karana chaahie,jitana aaraama se ho sake| praana dekara tho.de hee kaama kiya jata hai!mata liya karo bahut mukadame,mujhe rupayon kaa laalacha naheen| tuma aaraama se rahoge, to bahut rupae milenge|

totaa-bhaee,aati hui lakshmee bhee to naheen thukaraaee jati|

nirmalaa—lakshmee agar rakta aur maansa kee bhenta lekara aati hai, to usakaa na aana hee achchha main dhana kee bhookhee naheen hoon|

isee vakta mansaaraama bhee skoola se lautaa|dhoopa men chalane ke kaarana makha par paseene kee boonden aaee hui theen, gore mukha.de par jana kee laalee dau.da rahi thi, aankhon se jyoti see nikalatee maalooma hoti thi| dvaara par kha.daa hokara bola-ammaan jee, laaie kuchha khaane ko 'nikaalie, jaraa khelane jana hai| nirmalaa jaakara glaasa men paanee laaee;aur ek tashtaree men kuchha meve rakha kar mansaaraama ko die|mansaaraama khaakara chalane laga,to nirmalaa ne poochhaa-kab taka aaoge?

totaaraama ne kuchha chi.dha kar kaha-yaha tumhaare paasa khaane-peene kee cheejen maangane kyon aata hai? deedee se kyon naheen kahata?

nirmalaa ne yaha vaata prashansaa paane ke lobha se kahee thi| vaha yaha dikhaanaa chaahatee thi ki main tumhaare ladakon ko kitana chaahatee hoon| yaha koee vanaavatee prema na tha| use ladakon se sachamucha sneha tha| usake charitra men abhee taka baala-bhaava hee pradhaana tha usamen vahee utsukata,vaheeaashaavaadita,vahee chanchalata,vahee vinodapriyata vidyamaana thi aur vaalakon ke saatha usakee ye vaalavrittiyaan prasphutita hoti rahatee theen| sapani-sulabha eershaa abhee taka usake mann men udaya naheen hui thi, lekin pati ke prasanna hone ke badale naaka-bhaun siko.dane kaa aashaya na samajha kar boli-main kyaa jaanoon unase kyon naheen maangate| mere paasa aate hain,to dutakaara naheen detee| agar aisaa karoon,to yahee hogaa ki yaha to ladakon ko dekha kar jalatee hai|

mansaaraama ne nirbheekata se kaha-main shaama ko ek ghantaa khelane ke lie jaane ke sivaa din bhara kaheen naheen jata|aapa ammaan yaa vuaa jee se poochha len| mujhe khuda is tarah ghoomana pasanda naheen| haan,khelane ke lie hedamaastara saahaba aagraha karake bulaate hain,to majaboorana jana pa.data hai|agar aapa ko meraa khelane jana pasanda naheen hai, to kala se na jaaoongaa|

munshee jee ne dekha ki baaten doosare hee rukha par aa rahi hain,to teena svara men vole-mujhe is baat kaa itameenaana kyon kar ho ki khelane ke sivaa aur kaheen naheen ghoomane jaate| main baraabara shikaayaten sunata hoon| sansaaraama ne uttejita hokara kaha―kina mahaashaya ne aapa se yaha shikaayata kee hai,jaraa main bhee to sunoon|

vakeela―koee ho,isase tumhen koee matalaba naheen|tunhen itana vishvaasa honaa chaahie ki main jhoothaa aakshepa naheen karata|

mansaaraama―agar mere saamane koee aakara kaha de ki main ne inhen kaheen ghoomate dekha hai,to munha na dikhaaoon|

vakeela―kisee ko aisee kyaa garaja pa.dee hai ki tumhaare munnha par tumhaaree shikaayata kare; aur tumase vaira mola le? tuma apane do-chaara saadhiyon ko lekara usake ghar ke khaparaila pho.date phiro| mujhase is kisma kee shikaayata ek aadmi ne naheen, kaee aadamiyon ne kee hai; aur koee vajaha naheen hai ki main apane doston kee baat kaa vishvaasa na karoon| main chaahata hoon ki tuma skoola hee men raha karo|

mansaaraama ne munha giraa kar kaha―mujhe vahaan rahane men koee aapatti naheen hai, jab se kahie chalaa jaaoonn|

vakeela―tumane munha kyon latakaa liya, kyaa vahaan rahana achchha naheen lagata? aisaa maalooma hota hai, maano vahaan jaane ke bhaya se tumhaaree naanee maree jaa rahi hai| aakhira baat kyaa hai, vahaan tumhen kyaa takaleefa hogee?

mansaaraama chhaatraalaya men rahane ke lie utsuka naheen tha; lekin jab munshee jee ne yahee baat kaha dee; aur isakaa kaarana poochha, to vaha apanee jhepa mitaane ke lie prasannachita hokara bola―munha kyon latakaaoon? mere lie jaise ghar, vese vordinga haausa| takaleefa bhee koee naheen;aur ho bhee to use sanha sakata hoon| main kala se chalaa jaaoongaa| haan,agar jagaha na khaalee hui,to majabooree hai|
munshee jee vakeela the| samajha gae ki yaha laundaa koee aisaa bahaanaa dhoon.dha raha hai,jisamen mujhe vahaan jana bhee na pa.de;aur koee iljaama bhee sira par na aae|bole-sab ladakon ke lie jagaha hai,tumhaare hee lie jagaha na hogee|

mansaaraama-kitane hee ladakon ko jagaha naheen milee;aur ve baahara kiraae ke makaanon men pa.de hue hain| abhee bordinga haausa se ek ladke kaa naama kata gaya tha,to pachaasa arjiyaan us jagaha ke lie aaee theen|

vakeela saahaba ne zyada tarka-vitarka karana uchita na samajhaa| mansaaraama ko kala taiyaara rahane kee aajnaa dekara aapa ne bagghee taiyaara karaaee, aur saira karane chale gae| idhara kuchha dinon se vaha shaama ko praayah saira karane chale jaayaa karte the| kisee anubhavee praanee ne batalaayaa tha ki deergha jeevana ke lie isase ba.dha kar koee mantra naheen hai| unake jaane ke baada mansaaraama aakara rukminee se bola vuaa jee,baaboo jee ne mujhe kala se skoola hee men rahane ko kaha hai| rukminee ne vismita hokara poochhaa-kyon?

mansaa0-main kyaa jaanoon| kahane lage ki tuma yahaan aavaaron kee tarah idhara-udhara phiraa karte ho||

rukmi0-toone kaha ki main kaheen naheen jaata? mansaa0-kaha kyon naheen,magar jab vaha maanen bhee! rukmi-tumhaaree naee ammaan jee kee kripaa hogee;aur kya?

mansaa-naheen buaa jee,mujhe una par sandeha naheen hai,vaha bechaaree to bhoola se bhee kabhee kuchha naheen kahateen| koee cheeza maangane jata hoon, to turanta utha kar detee hain|

rukmi-too yaha triyaacharitra kyaa jaane,yaha unheen kee lagaaee aaga hai| dekha main jaakara poochhata hoon na|

rukminee jhallaaee hui nirmalaa ke paasa jaa pahunchee| use a.de haathon lene ka,kaanton men ghaseetane ka,taanon se chhedane ka,rulaane kaa koee suavasara vaha haath se na jaane detee theen| nirmalaa unaka,aadara karti thi,unase dabatee thi,unakee baaton kaa javaaba taka na detee thi| vaha chaahatee thi ki yaha mujhe sikhaavana kee baaten kaheen jahaan main bhooloon vahaan sudhaaren,sab kaamon kee dekha-rekha karti rahen;par rukminee usase tanee hee rahatee theen|

nirmalaa chaarapaaee se utha kar boli-aaie deedee,baithie!

nirmalaa ne kaatara bhaava se kaha-kyaa hua;deedee jee? main ne to kisee se kuchha naheen kaha|

rukmi0-mansaaraama ko ghar se nikaale detee ho,tisa par kehti ho mainne to kisee ko kuchha naheen kaha| kyaa tumase itana| bhee naheen dekha jaata?

nirmalaa-deedee jee,main tumhaare charanon ko chhookara kehti hoon, mujhe kuchha naheen maalooma| meree aankhen phoota jaayan, agar mainne usake vishaya men munha taka kholaa ho|

rukmi0— kyon vyartha kasmen khaatee ho| ab taka totaaraama kabhee ladke se naheen bolte the| ek haphte ke lie mansaaraama nanihaala chalaa gaya tha, to itane ghabaraae ki khuda jaakara livaa laae| ava usee mansaaraama ko vaha ghar se nikaala kar skoola men rakkhe dete hain| agar ladke kaa baala bhee vaankaa hua, to tuma jaanogee| vaha kabhee vaahara naheen raha, use na khaane kee sudha rahatee hai,na pahanane kee— jahaan baitha, vaheen so jata hai| kahane ko javaana ho gaya,par svabhaava baalakon kaa saa hai| skoola men to usakaa marana ho jaayagaa| vahaan kise phikra hai ki isane khaayaa yaa naheen, kahaan kapa.de utaare, kahaan so raha hai| jab ghar men koee poochhane vaalaa naheen, to vaahara kaun poochhega? main ne tumhen chetaa diya; aage tuma jaano, tumhaaraa kaama jaane!

yaha kaha kar rukminee vahaan se chalee gaee|
