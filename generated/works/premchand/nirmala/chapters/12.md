# Chapter 12

baarahavaa.N parichchheda

teena din gujara gae; aur munshee jee ghar na aae! rukmiNee donon vakta aspataala jati; aur mansaaraama ko dekha aatee thi| donon ladake bhee jaate the; para nirmalaa kaise jati? usake pairon men to bediyaa.N padee huee theen| vaha mansaaraama kee beemaaree kaa haala-chaala jaanane ke lie vyagra rahatee thi, yadi rukmiNee se kuchha poochhatee thi, to taane milate the; aur ladakon se poochhatee, to ve besira-pair kee baaten karane lagate! ek baara khuda jaakara dekhane ke lie usakaa chitta vyaakula ho raha tha| use yaha dara hota tha ki shaka ne kaheen munshee jee ke putraprema ko shithila na kar diya ho, kaheen unakee kripaNataa hee to mansaaraama ke achchhe hone men baadhaka naheen ho rahi hai? doktara kisee ke sage naheen hote, inhen to apane paison se kaama hai, murdaa dojakha men jaaya yaa bihishta men| usake mana men prabala ichchhaa hoti
thi ki jaakara aspataala ke doktara ko ek hajaara kee thailee dekara kahen-inhen bachaa deejie,yaha thailee aapakee bhenta hai;para usake paasa na to itane rupaye hee the,na itanaa saahasa hee tha| aba bhee yadi vaha vahaa.N pahu.Ncha sakatee,to mansaaraama achchhaa ho jata| usakee jaisee sevaa-sushrooshaa honee chaahie,vaisee naheen ho rahi hai,naheen to kyaa teena din taka jvara hee na utarataa? yaha daihika jvara naheen, maanasika jvara hai aur chitta ke shaanta hone hee se isakaa prakopa shaanta ho sakataa hai| agar vaha vahaa.N raat bhara bhee baithee raha sakatee;aur munshee jee jaraa bhee mana mailaa na karte,to kadaachit mansaaraama ko yakeena ho jata ki pitaa jee kaa dil saapha hai; aur phir usake achchhe hone men dera na lagatee; lekin aisaa hogaa? munshee jee use vahaa.N dekha kar prasannachitta raha sakenge? kyaa aba bhee unakaa dil saapha naheen hua? yahaa.N se jaate samaya to aisaa pataa hua tha| ki vaha apane pramaada para pachhataa rahe hain| aisaa to na hogaa ki usake vahaa.N jaate hee munshee jee kaa shaka phir bhadaka uthe aur vaha bete kee jaana lekara hee chhode!

isee duvidhaa men pade-pade teena din gujara gae;aur na ghar men choolhaa jalaa,na kisee ne kuchha khaayaa| ladakon ke liye baajaara se pooriyaa.N ma.Ngaa lee jati theen| rukmiNee aur nirmalaa bhookhee hee.so jati theen| unhen khaanaa kee ichchhaa hee na hoti thi|

chauthe din jiyaaraama skoola se lautaa, to aspataala hota hua ghar aaya| nirmalaa ne poochhaa-kyon bhaiyaa,aspataala
bhee gae the? aaja kyaa haala hai? tumhaare bhaiyaa uthe yaa naheen?

jiyaaraama ruaa.Nsaa hokara bola-ammaa.N jee, aaja to vaha kuchha bolate-chaalate hee na the| chupachaapa chaarapaaee para pade jora-jora se haath-paa.Nva pataka rahe the|

nirmalaa ke chehare kaa ranga uda gaya| ghabaraa kar poochhaa-tumhaare vaaboo jee vahaa.N na the?

jiyaaraama-the kyon naheen| aaja vaha bahuta rote the! nirmalaa kaa kalejaa dhaka-dhaka karane lagaa| poochhaa-doktara log vahaa.N na the?

jiyaaraama-doktara bhee khade the aur aapasa men kuchha salaaha kar rahe the| sabase badaa sivila sarjana angrejee men kaha raha tha ki mareeja ke deha men kuchha taajaa khoona daalanaa chaahie| is para vaaboo jee ne kahaa-meree deha se jitanaa khoona chaahe le leejie| sivilasarjana ne ha.Nsa kar kahaa-aapake bleda ( Blood ) se kaama naheen chalegaa| kisee javaana aadamee kaa vleda chaahie,aakhira usane pichakaaree se koee davaa bhaiyaa ke vaajoo men daala dee| chaara angula se kama kee suee na rahi hogee;para bhaiyaa minake taka naheen| mainne to maare dara ke aa.Nkhen banda kar lee!

bade-bade mahaan sankalpa aavesha me hee janma lete hain| kahaa.N to nirmalaa dara se sookhee jati thi,kahaa.N usake mu.Nha para dridha sankalpa: kee aabhaa jhalaka padee| usane apanee deha kaa taajaa khoona dene kaa
phaisalaa kar liya| agar usake rakta se mansaaraama ke praaNa bacha jaaya.N,to vaha badee khushee se usakee antima boonda taka de daalegee| aba jisakaa jo jee chaahe samajhe,vaha kuchha parvaaha na karegee| usane jiyaaraama se kahaa-tuma lapaka kar ek ekkaa bulaa lo,main aspataala jaaoo.Ngee|

jiyaaraama-vahaa.N to is vakta bahuta se aadamee honge| jaraa raat ho jaane deejie!

nirmalaa-naheen,tuma abhee ekkaa bulaa lo!

jiyaaraama-kaheen baaboo jee bigaden na!

nirmalaa-bigadane do!tuma abhee jaakara savaaree laao|

jiyaaraama-main kaha doongaa,ammaa.N jee hee ne mujhase savaaree ma.Ngavaaee thi|

nirmalaa-kaha denaa!

jiyaaraama to udhara taa.Ngaa laane gaya,idhara itanee dera men nirmalaa ne sira men kachee kee,joodaa baa.Ndhaa,kapade badale,gahane pahane,paana khaayaa aur dvaara para aakara taa.Nge kee raaha dekhane lagee|

rukmiNee apane kamare men baithee huee thi| use is taiyaaree se aate dekha kar bolee-kahaa.N jati ho bahoo?

nirmalaa-jaraa aspataala taka jati hoo.N|

ruvimaNee-vahaa.N jaakara kyaa karogee?

nirmalaa kuchha naheen,karoo.Ngee kyaa? karane vaale to bhagavaana hain| dekhane ko jee chaahataa hai|
rukmiNee-main kehti hoo.N mata jaao!

nirmalaa ne vineeta bhaava se kahaa-abhee chalee aaoo.Ngee,deedee jee! jiyaaraama kaha rahe hain ki is vakta unakee haalata achchhee naheen hai! jee naheen maanataa,aapa bhee chalie na!

rookminee-main dekhaaaee hoo.N| itanaa hee samajha lo ki aba baaharee khoona pahu.Nchane hee para jeevana kee ummeeda hai| kauna apanaa taajaa khoona degaa; aur kyon degaa? usamen bhee to praaNon kaa dara hai|

nirmalaa-iseelie to main jati hoo.N| mere khoona se kyaa kaama na chalegaa?

rukmiNee-chalegaa kyon naheen,javaana hee kaa khoona to chaahie;lekin tumhaare khoona se mansaa kee jaana bache, isase yaha kaheen achchhaa hai ki use paanee men bahaa diya jaaya!

taa.Ngaa aa gaya| nirmalaa aur jiyaaraama donon jaa baithe! taa.Ngaa chalaa!

rukmiNee dvaara para khadee dera taka rotee rahi| aaja pahalee baara use nirmalaa para dayaa aaee| usakaa basa hota to vaha nirmalaa ko baa.Ndha rakhatee| karuNaa aur sahaanubhooti kaa aavesha use kahaa.N lie jata hai,yaha vaha aprakata roopa se dekha rahi thi| aaha! yaha badakismatee-preraNaa hai,yaha sarvanaasha kaa raastaa hai!

nirmalaa aspataala pahunchee,to deepaka jala chuke the| doktara log apanee-apanee raaya dekara bidaa ho chuke the| mansaaraama kaa jvara kuchha kama ho gaya tha| vaha takatakee lagaae dvaara kee ora dekha
rihaa tha| usakee nazara unmukta aakaasha kee ora lagee huee thi, maano kisee devataa kee prateekshaa kar raha ho| vaha kahaa.N hai, kisa dashaa men hai,isakaa use kuchha jnaana na tha!

sahasaa nirmalaa ko dekhate hee vaha chaunka kar utha baithaa| usakee samaadhi toota gaee| usakee vilupta chetana pradeepta ho gaee| use apanee sthiti kaa-apanee dashaa kaa jnaana ho gaya,maano koee bhoolee huee baat yaada aa gaee ho| usane aa.Nkhen phaada kar nirmalaa ko dekha aur mu.Nha phera liya|

ekaaeka munshee jee teevra aavaaza men bole-tuma yahaa.N kyaa karane aaee? nirmalaa avaak raha gaee| vaha batalaae ki kyaa karane aaee! itane seedhe se savaala kaa bhee vaha kyaa koee javaaba na de sakee! vaha kyaa karane aaee? itanaa jatila savaala kisake saamane aaya hogaa? ghar kaa aadamee beemaara hai,use dekhane aaee hai,yaha baat kyaa binaa poochhe maalooma na ho sakatee thi? phir yaha savaala kyon?

vaha hatbuddhi see khadee rahi,maano sanjnaaheena ho gaee ho! usane dono ladakon se munshee jee ke shoka aur santosa kee baaten suna kar yaha anumaana kiya tha ki aba unakaa dil saapha ho gaya hai| aba use pataa hua ki vaha bhrama tha| haa.N, vaha mahaa bhrama tha| agar vaha jaanatee ki aa.Nsuon kee vrishti ne bhee shaka kee agni shaanta naheen kee, to vaha yahaa.N kabhee naheen na aatee| vaha kudhakudha kar mara jati; para ghar se baahara paa.Nva na nikaalatee!!

munshee jee ne phir vahee savaala kiya-tuma yahaa.N kyon aaee?
nirmalaa ne nihshanka bhaava se javaaba diya-aapa yahaa.N kyaa: karane aae hain?

munshee jee ke nathane phadakane lage| vaha jhallaa kar chaarapaaee se utha aur nirmalaa kaa haath pakada kar vole-tumhaare yahaa.N aane kee koee jaroorata naheen| jaba main bulaaoo.N tava aana,samajha gaee|

are! yaha kyaa anartha hua? mansaaraama jo chaarapaaee se hila bhee na sakataa tha,utha kar khadaa ho gaya aur nirmalaa ke pairon para gira kar rote hue bola-ammaan jee,is abhaage ke lie aapako bekaara itanaa takaleepha hua? main aapakaa pyaara kabhee na bhooloo.Ngaa| eeshvara se meree yahee praarthanaa hai ki meraa punarjanma aapake garbha se ho,jisase main aapake riNa se uriNa ho sakoo.N| eeshvara jaanataa hai,mainne aapako vimaataa naheen samajhaann| main aapako apanee maa.N samajhataa raha| aapakee unna mujhase bahuta jyaadaa na ho;lekin aapa meree maa.N ke sthaana para theen;aur mainne aapako hameshaa isee nazara dekha.....va naheen volaa jata;ammaa.N jee,kshamaa keejie! yaha antima bheta hai!!

nirmalaa ne ashru-pravaaha ko rokate hue kahaa-tuma aisee baaten kyon karte ho? do-chaara din men achchhe ho jaaoge!

mansaaraama ne ksheeNa aavaaza men kahaa-aba jeene kee ichchhaa naheen;aur na volane kee shakti hee hai!

yaha kehte-kehte mansaaraama ashakta hokara vaheen jameena para leta
gaya| nirmalaa ne pati kee ora nirbhaya netron se dekhate hue kahaa-doktaron ne kyaa salaaha dee?

munshee jee-sab ke sab bhanga khaa gae hain| kehte hain taajaa khoona chaahie|

nirmalaa-taajaa khoona mila jaaya,to praaNa-rakshaa ho sakatee hai?

munshee jee ne nirmalaa kee ora teevra netron se dekha kar kahaa-main eeshvara naheen hoo.N; aur na doktaron hee ko eeshvara samajhataa hoo.N|

nirmalaa-taajaa khuna to aisee alabhya vastu naheen!

manshee jee-aakaasha ke taare bhee to alabhya naheen! mu.Nha ke saamane khandaka kyaa cheeja hai!

nirmalaa-main apanaa khoona dene ko taiyaara hoo.N! doktara ko bulaaie!!

munshee jee ne vismita hokara kahaa-tuma!

nirmalaa-haa.N! kyaa mere khoona se kaama na chalegaa?

munshee jee-tuma apanaa khoona dogee! naheen,tumhaare khoona kee jaroorata naheen! isamen praaNon kaa dara hai!!

nirmalaa-mere praaNa aur kisa din kaama aavenge?

munshee jee ne sajala aa.Nkha hokara kahaa-naheen nirmalaa,unakaa moolya aba meree nigaahon men bahuta badha gaya hai!aaja taka vaha mere bhoga kee vastu thi;aaja se vaha meree bhakti kee vastu hai| mainne tumhaare saatha badaa anyaaya kiya hai,kshamaa karo!
