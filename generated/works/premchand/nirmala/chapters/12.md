# Nirmala — Adhyay 12

**Munshi Premchand**

baarahavaan parichchheda

teena din guzara gae; aur munshee jee ghar na aae! rukminee donon vakta aspataala jati; aur mansaaraama ko dekha aati thi| donon ladke bhee jaate the; par nirmalaa kaise jati? usake pairon men to be.diyaan pa.dee hui theen| vaha mansaaraama kee beemaaree kaa haala-chaala jaanane ke lie vyagra rahatee thi, yadi rukminee se kuchha poochhatee thi, to taane milate the; aur ladakon se poochhatee, to ve besira-pair kee baaten karane lagate! ek baara khuda jaakara dekhane ke lie usakaa chitta vyaakula ho raha tha| use yaha bhaya hota tha ki sandeha ne kaheen munshee jee ke putraprema ko shithila na kar diya ho, kaheen unakee kripanata hee to mansaaraama ke achchhe hone men baadhaka naheen ho rahi hai? daoktara kisee ke sage naheen hote, inhen to apane paison se kaama hai, murdaa dozaKa men jaaya yaa bihishta men| usake mann men prabala ichchhaa hoti thi ki jaakara aspataala ke daoktara ko ek hajaara kee thailee dekara kahen-inhen bachaa deejie,yaha thailee aapakee bhenta hai;par usake paasa na to itane rupaye hee the,na itana saahasa hee tha| ab bhee yadi vaha vahaan pahuncha sakatee,to mansaaraama achchha ho jata| usakee jaisee sevaa-sushrooshaa honee chaahie,vaisee naheen ho rahi hai,naheen to kyaa teena din taka jvara hee na utarata? yaha daihika jvara naheen, maanasika jvara hai aur chitta ke shaanta hone hee se isakaa prakopa shaanta ho sakata hai| agar vaha vahaan raat bhara bhee baithee raha sakatee;aur munshee jee jaraa bhee mann mailaa na karte,to kadaachit mansaaraama ko vishvaasa ho jata ki pitaa jee kaa dil saapha hai; aur phir usake achchhe hone men dera na lagatee; lekin aisaa hoga? munshee jee use vahaan dekha kar prasannachitta raha sakenge? kyaa ab bhee unakaa dil saapha naheen hua? yahaan se jaate samay to aisaa jnaata hua tha| ki vaha apane pramaada par pachhata rahe hain| aisaa to na hogaa ki usake vahaan jaate hee munshee jee kaa sandeha phir bha.daka uthe aur vaha bete kee jaana lekara hee chho.de!

isee duvidhaa men pa.de-pa.de teena din guzara gae;aur na ghar men choolhaa jala,na kisee ne kuchha khaayaa| ladakon ke liye baajaara se pooriyaan mangaa lee jati theen| rukminee aur nirmalaa bhookhee hee.so jati theen| unhen bhojana kee ichchhaa hee na hoti thi|

chauthe din jiyaaraama skoola se lauta, to aspataala hota hua ghar aaya| nirmalaa ne poochhaa-kyon bhaiya,aspataala bhee gae the? aaja kyaa haala hai? tumhaare bhaiyaa uthe yaa naheen?

jiyaaraama ruaansaa hokara bola-ammaan jee, aaja to vaha kuchha bolte-chaalate hee na the| chupachaapa chaarapaaee par pa.de jora-jora se haath-paanva pataka rahe the|

nirmalaa ke chehare kaa ranga u.da gaya| ghabaraa kar poochhaa-tumhaare vaaboo jee vahaan na the?

jiyaaraama-the kyon naheen| aaja vaha bahut rote the! nirmalaa kaa kalejaa dhaka-dhaka karane lagaa| poochhaa-daoktara log vahaan na the?

jiyaaraama-daoktara bhee kha.de the aur aapasa men kuchha salaaha kar rahe the| sabase bada sivila sarjana angrejee men kaha raha tha ki mareeza ke deha men kuchha taazaa khoona daalana chaahie| is par vaaboo jee ne kaha-meree deha se jitana khoona chaahe le leejie| sivilasarjana ne hansa kar kaha-aapake bla.cda ( Blood ) se kaama naheen chalegaa| kisee javaana aadmi kaa vla.cda chaahie,aakhira usane pichakaaree se koee davaa bhaiyaa ke vaajoo men daala dee| chaara angula se kama kee suee na rahi hogee;par bhaiyaa minake taka naheen| mainne to maare dara ke aankhen banda kar lee!

ba.de-ba.de mahaan sankalpa aavesha me hee janma lete hain| kahaan to nirmalaa bhaya se sookhee jati thi,kahaan usake mukha par dri.dha sankalpa: kee aabhaa jhalaka pa.dee| usane apanee deha kaa taajaa khoona dene kaa nishchaya kar liya| agar usake rakta se mansaaraama ke praana bacha jaayan,to vaha ba.dee khushi se usakee antima boonda taka de daalegee| ab jisakaa jo jee chaahe samajhe,vaha kuchha parvaaha na karegee| usane jiyaaraama se kaha-tuma lapaka kar ek ekkaa bulaa lo,main aspataala jaaoongee|

jiyaaraama-vahaan to is vakta bahut se aadmi honge| jaraa raat ho jaane deejie!

nirmalaa-naheen,tuma abhee ekkaa bulaa lo!

jiyaaraama-kaheen baaboo jee biga.den na!

nirmalaa-biga.dane do!tuma abhee jaakara savaaree laao|

jiyaaraama-main kaha doonga,ammaan jee hee ne mujhase savaaree mangavaaee thi|

nirmalaa-kaha dena!

jiyaaraama to udhara taangaa laane gaya,idhara itanee dera men nirmalaa ne sira men kachee kee,joo.daa baandha,kapa.de badale,aabhooshana pahane,paana khaayaa aur dvaara par aakara taange kee raaha dekhane lagee|

rukminee apane kamare men baithee hui thi| use is taiyaaree se aate dekha kar boli-kahaan jati ho bahoo?

nirmalaa-jaraa aspataala taka jati hoon|

ruvimanee-vahaan jaakara kyaa karogee?

nirmalaa kuchha naheen,karoongee kya? karane vaale to bhagavaana hain| dekhane ko jee chaahata hai| rukminee-main kehti hoon mata jaao!

nirmalaa ne vineeta bhaava se kaha-abhee chalee aaoongee,deedee jee! jiyaaraama kaha rahe hain ki is vakta unakee haalata achchhi naheen hai! jee naheen maanata,aapa bhee chalie na!

rookminee-main dekhaaaee hoon| itana hee samajha lo ki ab baaharee khoona pahunchane hee par jeevana kee aashaa hai| kaun apana taazaa khoona dega; aur kyon dega? usamen bhee to praanon kaa bhaya hai|

nirmalaa-iseelie to main jati hoon| mere khoona se kyaa kaama na chalega?

rukminee-chalegaa kyon naheen,javaana hee kaa khoona to chaahie;lekin tumhaare khoona se mansaa kee jaana bache, isase yaha kaheen achchha hai ki use paanee men bahaa diya jaaya!

taangaa aa gaya| nirmalaa aur jiyaaraama donon jaa baithe! taangaa chala!

rukminee dvaara par kha.dee dera taka rotee rahi| aaja pahalee baara use nirmalaa par dayaa aaee| usakaa basa hota to vaha nirmalaa ko baandha rakhatee| karunaa aur sahaanubhooti kaa aavesha use kahaan lie jata hai,yaha vaha aprakata roopa se dekha rahi thi| aaha! yaha durbhaagya-prerana hai,yaha sarvanaasha kaa maarga hai!

nirmalaa aspataala pahunchee,to deepaka jala chuke the| daoktara log apanee-apanee raaya dekara bidaa ho chuke the| mansaaraama kaa jvara kuchha kama ho gaya tha| vaha takatakee lagaae dvaara kee ora dekha rihaa tha| usakee drishti unmukta aakaasha kee ora lagee hui thi, maano kisee devata kee prateekshaa kar raha ho| vaha kahaan hai, kisa dashaa men hai,isakaa use kuchha jnaana na tha!

sahasaa nirmalaa ko dekhate hee vaha chaunka kar utha baithaa| usakee samaadhi toota gaee| usakee vilupta chetana pradeepta ho gaee| use apanee sthiti kaa-apanee dashaa kaa jnaana ho gaya,maano koee bhoolee hui baat yaada aa gaee ho| usane aankhen pha.da kar nirmalaa ko dekha aur munha phera liya|

ekaaeka munshee jee teevra svara men bole-tuma yahaan kyaa karane aaee? nirmalaa avaak raha gaee| vaha batalaae ki kyaa karane aaee! itane seedhe se prashna kaa bhee vaha kyaa koee javaaba na de sakee! vaha kyaa karane aaee? itana jatila prashna kisake saamane aaya hoga? ghar kaa aadmi beemaara hai,use dekhane aaee hai,yaha baat kyaa binaa poochhe maalooma na ho sakatee thi? phir yaha prashna kyon?

vaha hatbuddhi see kha.dee rahi,maano sanjnaaheena ho gaee ho! usane dono ladakon se munshee jee ke shoka aur santosa kee baaten suna kar yaha anumaana kiya tha ki ab unakaa dil saapha ho gaya hai| ab use jnaata hua ki vaha bhrama tha| haan, vaha mahaa bhrama tha| agar vaha jaanatee ki aansuon kee vrishti ne bhee sandeha kee agni shaanta naheen kee, to vaha yahaan kadaapi na aati| vaha ku.dhaku.dha kar mara jati; par ghar se baahara paanva na nikaalatee!!

munshee jee ne phir vahee prashna kiya-tuma yahaan kyon aaee? nirmalaa ne nihshanka bhaava se uttara diya-aapa yahaan kya: karane aae hain?

munshee jee ke nathane pha.dakane lage| vaha jhallaa kar chaarapaaee se utha aur nirmalaa kaa haath paka.da kar vole-tumhaare yahaan aane kee koee zaroorat naheen| jab main bulaaoon tava aana,samajha gaee|

are! yaha kyaa anartha hua? mansaaraama jo chaarapaaee se hila bhee na sakata tha,utha kar kha.daa ho gaya aur nirmalaa ke pairon par gira kar rote hue bola-ammaan jee,is abhaage ke lie aapako vyartha itana kashta hua? main aapakaa sneha kabhee na bhooloongaa| eeshvara se meree yahee praarthana hai ki meraa punarjanma aapake garbha se ho,jisase main aapake rina se urina ho sakoon| eeshvara jaanata hai,mainne aapako vimaataa naheen samajhaann| main aapako apanee maataa samajhata raha| aapakee unna mujhase bahut zyada na ho;lekin aapa meree maataa ke sthaana par theen;aur mainne aapako sadaiva isee drishti dekha...va naheen volaa jaata;ammaan jee,kshamaa keejie! yaha antima bheta hai!!

nirmalaa ne ashru-pravaaha ko rokate hue kaha-tuma aisee baaten kyon karte ho? do-chaara din men achchhe ho jaaoge!

mansaaraama ne ksheena svara men kaha-ab jeene kee ichchhaa naheen;aur na volane kee shakti hee hai!

yaha kehte-kehte mansaaraama ashakta hokara vaheen zameen par leta gaya| nirmalaa ne pati kee ora nirbhaya netron se dekhate hue kaha-daoktaron ne kyaa salaaha dee?

munshee jee-sab ke sab bhanga khaa gae hain| kehte hain taajaa khoona chaahie|

nirmalaa-taajaa khoona mila jaaya,to praana-rakshaa ho sakatee hai?

munshee jee ne nirmalaa kee ora teevra netron se dekha kar kaha-main eeshvara naheen hoon; aur na daoktaron hee ko eeshvara samajhata hoon|

nirmalaa-taajaa khuna to aisee alabhya vastu naheen!

manshee jee-aakaasha ke taare bhee to alabhya naheen! munha ke saamane khandaka kyaa cheeza hai!

nirmalaa-main apana khoona dene ko taiyaara hoon! daoktara ko bulaaie!!

munshee jee ne vismita hokara kaha-tuma!

nirmalaa-haan! kyaa mere khoona se kaama na chalega?

munshee jee-tuma apana khoona dogee! naheen,tumhaare khoona kee jaroorata naheen! isamen praanon kaa bhaya hai!!

nirmalaa-mere praana aur kisa din kaama aavenge?

munshee jee ne sajala netra hokara kaha-naheen nirmala,unakaa moolya ab meree nigaahon men bahut ba.dha gaya hai!aaja taka vaha mere bhoga kee vastu thi;aaja se vaha meree bhakti kee vastu hai| mainne tumhaare saatha bada anyaaya kiya hai,kshamaa karo!
