# Nirmala — Adhyay 12

**Munshi Premchand**

baarahavaan parichchhed

teen din gujar gae; aur munshee jee ghar n aae! rukminee donon vakt aspataal jaatee; aur mansaaraam ko dekh aatee thee. donon ladake bhi jaate the; par Nirmala kaise jaatee? usake pairon mein to bediyaan padee huee theen. vah mansaaraam ki beemaaree ka haal-chaal jaanane ke lie vyagr rahatee thi, yadi rukminee se kuchh poochhatee thi, to taane milate the; aur ladakon se poochhatee, to ve besir-pair ki baaten karane lagate! ek baar khud jaakar dekhane ke lie usakaa chitt vyaakul ho rahaa thaa. use yah dar hotaa tha ki shak ne kaheen munshee jee ke putrapyaar ko shithil n kar diyaa ho, kaheen unakee kripanataa hee to mansaaraam ke achchhe hone mein baadhak nahin ho rahee hai? doktar kisee ke sage nahin hote, inhen to apane paison se kaam hai, murdaa dojakh mein jaay ya bihisht men. usake man mein prabal ichchhaa hotee thi ki jaakar aspataal ke doktar ko ek hajaar ki thailee dekar kahen-inhen bachaa deejie, yah thailee aapakee bhent hai; par usake paas n to itane rupaye hee the, n itanaa himmat hee thaa. ab bhi yadi vah wahan pahunch sakatee, to mansaaraam achchha ho jaataa. usakee jaisee sevaa-sushrooshaa honee chahiye, vaisee nahin ho rahee hai, nahin to kyaa teen din tak jvar hee n utarataa? yah daihik jvar nahin, maanasik jvar hai aur chitt ke shaant hone hee se isakaa prakop shaant ho sakataa hai. agar vah wahan raat bhar bhi baithee rah sakatee; aur munshee jee jaraa bhi man mailaa n karate, to shaayad mansaaraam ko bharosaa ho jaataa ki pitaa jee ka dil saaph hai; aur phir usake achchhe hone mein der n lagatee; lekin aisaa hogaa? munshee jee use wahan dekh kar khushachitt rah sakengge? kyaa ab bhi unakaa dil saaph nahin huaa? yahan se jaate samay to aisaa gyaat huaa thaa. ki vah apane pramaad par pachhataa rahe hain. aisaa to n hogaa ki usake wahan jaate hee munshee jee ka shak phir bhadak uthe aur vah bete ki jaan lekar hee chhode!

isee duvidhaa mein pade-pade teen din gujar gae; aur n ghar mein choolhaa jalaa, n kisee ne kuchh khaayaa. ladakon ke liye baajaar se pooriyaan manggaa lee jaatee theen. rukminee aur Nirmala bhookhee hee. so jaatee theen. unhen khana ki ichchhaa hee n hotee thee.

chauthe din jiyaaraam skool se lautaa, to aspataal hotaa huaa ghar kamaaeeaa. Nirmala ne poochhaa-kyon bhaiyaa, aspataal bhi gae the? aaj kyaa haal hai? tumhaare bhaiyaa uthe ya nahin?

jiyaaraam ruaansaa hokar bolaa-ammaan jee, aaj to vah kuchh bolate-chaalate hee n the. chupachaap chaarapaaee par pade jor-jor se haath-paanv patak rahe the.

Nirmala ke chehare ka ranggg ud gayaa. ghabaraa kar poochhaa-tumhaare vaaboo jee wahan n the?

jiyaaraam-the kyon naheen. aaj vah bahut rote the! Nirmala ka kalejaa dhak-dhak karane lagaa. poochhaa-doktar log wahan n the?

jiyaaraam-doktar bhi khade the aur aapas mein kuchh salaah kar rahe the. sabase badaa sivil sarjan anggrejee mein kah rahaa tha ki mareej ke deh mein kuchh taajaa khoon daalanaa chaahie. is par vaaboo jee ne kahaa-meree deh se jitanaa khoon chaahe le leejie. sivilasarjan ne hans kar kahaa-aapake bled ( Blood ) se kaam nahin chalegaa. kisee javaan aadmi ka vled chahiye, aakhir usane pichakaaree se koee davaa bhaiyaa ke vaajoo mein daal dee. chaar angggul se kam ki suee n rahee hogee; par bhaiyaa minake tak naheen. mainne to maare dar ke aankhen band kar lee!

bade-bade mahaan sanggkalp aavesh me hee paidaa honaa lete hain. kahaan to Nirmala dar se sookhee jaatee thi, kahaan usake munh par dridh sanggkalp: ki aabhaa jhalak padee. usane apanee deh ka taajaa khoon dene ka pakkaa faisla kar liyaa. agar usake rakt se mansaaraam ke praan bach jaayan, to vah badee khushi se usakee antim boond tak de daalegee. ab jisakaa jo jee chaahe samajhe, vah kuchh parvaah n karegee. usane jiyaaraam se kahaa-tum lapak kar ek ekkaa bulaa lo, main aspataal jaaoonggee.

jiyaaraam-wahan to is vakt bahut se aadmi hongge. jaraa raat ho jaane deejie!

Nirmala-nahin, tum abhee ekkaa bulaa lo!

jiyaaraam-kaheen baaboo jee bigaden n!

Nirmala-bigadane do! tum abhee jaakar savaaree laao.

jiyaaraam-main kah doonggaa, ammaan jee hee ne mujhase savaaree manggavaaee thee.

Nirmala-kah denaa!

jiyaaraam to udhar taanggaa laane gayaa, idhar itanee der mein Nirmala ne sir mein kachee ki, joodaa baandhaa, kapde badale, aabhooshan pahane, paan khaayaa aur dvaar par aakar taangge ki raah dekhane lagee.

rukminee apane kamare mein baithee huee thee. use is taiyaaree se aate dekh kar bolee-kahaan jaatee ho bahoo?

Nirmala-jaraa aspataal tak jaatee hoon.

ruvimanee-wahan jaakar kyaa kabeemaareeee?

Nirmala kuchh nahin, karoonggee kyaa? karane vaale to Bhagwan hain. dekhane ko jee chaahataa hai. rukminee-main kahatee hoon mat jaao!

Nirmala ne vineet bhaav se kahaa-abhee chalee aaoonggee, deedee jee! jiyaaraam kah rahe hain ki is vakt unakee haalat achchhi nahin hai! jee nahin maanataa, aap bhi chalie n!

rookminee-main dekhaaaee hoon. itanaa hee samajh lo ki ab baaharee khoon pahunchane hee par zindagi ki ummeed hai. kaun apanaa taajaa khoon degaa; aur kyon degaa? usamen bhi to praanon ka dar hai.

Nirmala-iseelie to main jaatee hoon. mere khoon se kyaa kaam n chalegaa?

rukminee-chalegaa kyon nahin, javaan hee ka khoon to chahiye; lekin tumhaare khoon se mansaa ki jaan bache, isase yah kaheen achchha hai ki use paani mein bahaa diyaa jaay!

taanggaa aa gayaa. Nirmala aur jiyaaraam donon jaa baithe! taanggaa chalaa!

rukminee dvaar par khadee der tak rotee rahee. aaj pahalee baar use Nirmala par taras aaee. usakaa bas hotaa to vah Nirmala ko baandh rakhatee. taras aur sahaanubhooti ka aavesh use kahaan lie jaataa hai, yah vah asaamane roop se dekh rahee thee. aah! yah badakismatee-preranaa hai, yah sarvanaash ka raastaa hai!

Nirmala aspataal pahunchee, to deepak jal chuke the. doktar log apanee-apanee Rai dekar bidaa ho chuke the. mansaaraam ka jvar kuchh kam ho gayaa thaa. vah takatakee lagaae dvaar ki or dekh rihaa thaa. usakee drishti unmukt aakaash ki or lagee huee thi, maano kisee devataa ki intezar kar rahaa ho. vah kahaan hai, kis dashaa mein hai, isakaa use kuchh gyaan n tha!

sahasaa Nirmala ko dekhate hee vah chaungk kar uth baithaa. usakee samaadhi toot gaee. usakee vigaayab chetan pradeept ho gaee. use apanee sthiti ka-apanee dashaa ka gyaan ho gayaa, maano koee bhoolee huee baat yaad aa gaee ho. usane aankhen phaad kar Nirmala ko dekhaa aur munh pher liyaa.

ekaaek munshee jee teevr svar mein bole-tum yahan kyaa karane aaee? Nirmala avaak rah gaee. vah batalaae ki kyaa karane aaee! itane seedhe se savaal ka bhi vah kyaa koee javaab n de sakee! vah kyaa karane aaee? itanaa jatil savaal kisake saamane kamaaeeaa hogaa? ghar ka aadmi beemaar hai, use dekhane aaee hai, yah baat kyaa binaa poochhe maaloom n ho sakatee thi? phir yah savaal kyon?

vah hatbuddhi see khadee rahee, maano sangyaaheen ho gaee ho! usane dono ladakon se munshee jee ke shok aur santos ki baaten sun kar yah anumaan kiyaa tha ki ab unakaa dil saaph ho gayaa hai. ab use gyaat huaa ki vah bhram thaa. haan, vah mahaa bhram thaa. agar vah jaanatee ki aansuon ki vrishti ne bhi shak ki agni shaant nahin ki, to vah yahan kadaapi n aatee. vah kudhakudh kar mar jaatee; par ghar se baahar paanv n nikaalatee!!

munshee jee ne phir vahee savaal kiyaa-tum yahan kyon aaee? Nirmala ne nihshanggk bhaav se javaab diyaa-aap yahan kyaa: karane aae hain?

munshee jee ke nathane phadakane lage. vah jhallaa kar chaarapaaee se uth aur Nirmala ka haath pakad kar vole-tumhaare yahan aane ki koee zaroorat naheen. jab main bulaaoon tav aanaa, samajh gaee.

are! yah kyaa anarth huaa? mansaaraam jo chaarapaaee se hil bhi n sakataa tha, uth kar khadaa ho gayaa aur Nirmala ke pairon par gir kar rote hue bolaa-ammaan jee, is abhaage ke lie aapko vyarth itanaa takaleeph huaa? main aapakaa pyaar kabhee n bhooloonggaa. Bhagwan se meree yahee dua hai ki meraa punarpaidaa honaa aapake garbh se ho, jisase main aapake karz se ukarj ho sakoon. Bhagwan jaanataa hai, mainne aapko vimaataa nahin samajhaann. main aapko apanee maataa samajhataa rahaa. aapakee unn mujhase bahut jyaadaa n ho; lekin aap meree maataa ke jagah par theen; aur mainne aapko hameshaa isee drishti dekhaa...... av nahin volaa jaataa; ammaan jee, kshmaa keejie! yah antim bhet hai!!

Nirmala ne ashru-pravaah ko rokate hue kahaa-tum aisee baaten kyon karate ho? do-chaar din mein achchhe ho jaaoge!

mansaaraam ne ksheen svar mein kahaa-ab jeene ki ichchhaa nahin; aur n volane ki taakat hee hai!

yah kahate-kahate mansaaraam ashakt hokar vaheen zameen par let gayaa. Nirmala ne shauhar ki or nirdar aangkhenon se dekhate hue kahaa-doktaron ne kyaa salaah dee?

munshee jee-sab ke sab bhanggg khaa gae hain. kahate hain taajaa khoon chaahie.

Nirmala-taajaa khoon mil jaay, to praan-bachaav ho sakatee hai?

munshee jee ne Nirmala ki or teevr aangkhenon se dekh kar kahaa-main Bhagwan nahin hoon; aur n doktaron hee ko Bhagwan samajhataa hoon.

Nirmala-taajaa khun to aisee alabhy vastu nahin!

manshee jee-aakaash ke taare bhi to alabhy nahin! munh ke saamane khandak kyaa cheej hai!

Nirmala-main apanaa khoon dene ko taiyaar hoon! doktar ko bulaaie!!

munshee jee ne vismit hokar kahaa-tum!

Nirmala-haan! kyaa mere khoon se kaam n chalegaa?

munshee jee-tum apanaa khoon dogee! nahin, tumhaare khoon ki jaroorat nahin! isamen praanon ka dar hai!!

Nirmala-mere praan aur kis din kaam aavengge?

munshee jee ne sajal aankhen hokar kahaa-nahin Nirmala, unakaa mooly ab meree nigaahon mein bahut badh gayaa hai! aaj tak vah mere bhog ki vastu thi; aaj se vah meree bhakti ki vastu hai. mainne tumhaare saath badaa naainsaaphee kiyaa hai, kshmaa karo!
