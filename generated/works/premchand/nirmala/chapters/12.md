# Nirmala — Adhyay 12

**Munshi Premchand**

baarahavaan parichchhed

teen din guzar gae; aur munshee jee ghar na aae! rukminee donon vakt aspataal jaatee; aur mansaaraam ko dekh aatee thee. donon la. dake bhee jaate the; par nirml kaise jaatee? usake pairon men to be. diyaan pa. dee huee theen. woh mansaaraam kee beemaaree kaa haala-chaal jaanane ke lie vyagr rahatee thee, yadi rukminee se kuchh poochhatee thee, to taane milate the; aur la. dakon se poochhatee, to ve besira-pair kee baaten karane lagate! ek baar khud jaakar dekhane ke lie usakaa chitt bechain ho rahaa thaa. use yeh dar hotaa thaa ki shak ne kaheen munshee jee ke putraprem ko shithil na kar diyaa ho, kaheen unakee kripanataa hee to mansaaraam ke achchhe hone men baadhak naheen ho rahee hai? daॉktar kisee ke sage naheen hote, inhen to apane paison se kaam hai, murdaa dozaK men jaay yaa bihisht men. usake man men prabal chaah hotee
thee ki jaakar aspataal ke daॉktar ko ek hajaar kee thailee dekar kahen-inhen bachaa deejie, yeh thailee aapakee bhent hai; par usake paas na to itane rupaye hee the, na itn himmat hee thaa. ab bhee yadi woh vahaan pahunch sakatee, to mansaaraam achchhaa ho jaataa. usakee jaisee sevaa-sushrooshaa honee chaahie, vaisee naheen ho rahee hai, naheen to kyaa teen din tak jvar hee na utarataa? yeh daihik jvar naheen, maanasik jvar hai aur chitt ke shaant hone hee se isakaa prakop shaant ho sakataa hai. agar woh vahaan raat bhar bhee baithee rah sakatee; aur munshee jee jr bhee man mailaa na karate, to shaayad mansaaraam ko yakeen ho jaataa ki pitaa jee kaa dil saaph hai; aur phir usake achchhe hone men der na lagatee; lekin aisaa hogaa? munshee jee use vahaan dekh kar prasannachitt rah sakenge? kyaa ab bhee unakaa dil saaph naheen huaa? yahaan se jaate samay to aisaa pataa huaa thaa. ki woh apane pramaad par pachhataa rahe hain. aisaa to na hogaa ki usake vahaan jaate hee munshee jee kaa shak phir bha. dak uthe aur woh bete kee jaan lekar hee chho. de!

isee duvidhaa men pa. de-pa. de teen din guzar gae; aur na ghar men choolhaa jl, na kisee ne kuchh khaayaa. la. dakon ke liye baajaar se pooriyaan mangaa lee jaatee theen. rukminee aur nirml bhookhee hee. so jaatee theen. unhen khaanaa kee chaah hee na hotee thee.

chauthe din jiyaaraam skool se lautaa, to aspataal hotaa huaa ghar aayaa. nirml ne poochhaa-kyon bhaiyaa, aspataal
bhee gae the? aaj kyaa haal hai? tumhaare bhaiyaa uthe yaa naheen?

jiyaaraam ruaansaa hokar bolaa-ammaan jee, aaj to woh kuchh bolate-chaalate hee na the. chupachaap chaarapaaee par pa. de jora-jor se haath-paanv patak rahe the.

nirml ke chehare kaa rang u. d gayaa. ghabr kar poochhaa-tumhaare vaaboo jee vahaan na the?

jiyaaraama-the kyon naheen. aaj woh bahut rote the! nirml kaa kalejaa dhaka-dhak karane lagaa. poochhaa-dॉktar log vahaan na the?

jiyaaraama-dॉktar bhee kha. de the aur aapas men kuchh salaah kar rahe the. sabase ba. daa sivil sarjan angrejee men kah rahaa thaa ki mareez ke deh men kuchh taazaa khoon daaln chaahie. is par vaaboo jee ne kahaa-meree deh se jitn khoon chaahe le leejie. sivilasarjan ne hans kar kahaa-aapake bla. cd ( Blood ) se kaam naheen chalegaa. kisee javaan aadamee kaa vla. cd chaahie, aakhir usane pichakaaree se koee dv bhaiyaa ke vaajoo men daal dee. chaar angul se kam kee suee na rahee hogee; par bhaiyaa minake tak naheen. mainne to maare dar ke aankhen band kar lee!

ba. de-ba. de mahaan sankalp aavesh me hee janm lete hain. kahaan to nirml dar se sookhee jaatee thee, kahaan usake munh par dri. dh sankalp: kee aabhaa jhalak pa. dee. usane apanee deh kaa taajaa khoon dene kaa
phaisl kar liyaa. agar usake rakt se mansaaraam ke jaan bach jaayan, to woh ba. dee khushee se usakee antim boond tak de daalegee. ab jisakaa jo jee chaahe samajhe, woh kuchh parvaah na karegee. usane jiyaaraam se kahaa-tum lapak kar ek ekkaa bulaa lo, main aspataal jaaoongee.

jiyaaraama-vahaan to is vakt bahut se aadamee honge. jr raat ho jaane deejie!

nirmalaa-naheen, tum abhee ekkaa bulaa lo!

jiyaaraama-kaheen baaboo jee biga. den na!

nirmalaa-biga. dane do! tum abhee jaakar savaaree laao.

jiyaaraama-main kah doongaa, ammaan jee hee ne mujhase savaaree mangavaaee thee.

nirmalaa-kah denaa!

jiyaaraam to udhar taangaa laane gayaa, idhar itanee der men nirml ne sir men kachee kee, joo. daa baandhaa, kapa. de badale, gahane pahane, paan khaayaa aur dvaar par aakar taange kee raah dekhane lagee.

rukminee apane kamare men baithee huee thee. use is taiyaaree se aate dekh kar bolee-kahaan jaatee ho bahoo?

nirmalaa-jr aspataal tak jaatee hoon.

ruvimanee-vahaan jaakar kyaa karogee?

nirml kuchh naheen, karoongee kyaa? karane vaale to bhagavaan hain. dekhane ko jee chaahataa hai.
rukminee-main kahatee hoon mat jaao!

nirml ne vineet bhaav se kahaa-abhee chalee aaoongee, deedee jee! jiyaaraam kah rahe hain ki is vakt unakee haalat achchhee naheen hai! jee naheen maanataa, aap bhee chalie na!

rookminee-main dekhaaaee hoon. itn hee samajh lo ki ab baaharee khoon pahunchane hee par jeevan kee ummeed hai. kaun apn taazaa khoon degaa; aur kyon degaa? usamen bhee to praanon kaa dar hai.

nirmalaa-iseelie to main jaatee hoon. mere khoon se kyaa kaam na chalegaa?

rukminee-chalegaa kyon naheen, javaan hee kaa khoon to chaahie; lekin tumhaare khoon se mansaa kee jaan bache, isase yeh kaheen achchhaa hai ki use paanee men bahaa diyaa jaay!

taangaa aa gayaa. nirml aur jiyaaraam donon jaa baithe! taangaa chl!

rukminee dvaar par kha. dee der tak rotee rahee. aaj pahalee baar use nirml par dayaa aaee. usakaa bas hotaa to woh nirml ko baandh rakhatee. dayaa aur hamadardee kaa aavesh use kahaan lie jaataa hai, yeh woh aprakat roop se dekh rahee thee. aah! yeh badakismatee-prern hai, yeh sarvanaash kaa raastaa hai!

nirml aspataal pahunchee, to deepak jal chuke the. daॉktar log apanee-apanee raay dekar bidaa ho chuke the. mansaaraam kaa jvar kuchh kam ho gayaa thaa. woh takatakee lagaae dvaar kee ora dekh
rihaa thaa. usakee nazar unmukt aakaash kee ora lagee huee thee, maano kisee devataa kee intazaar kar rahaa ho. woh kahaan hai, kis haal men hai, isakaa use kuchh jnaan na thaa!

sahasaa nirml ko dekhate hee woh chaunk kar uth baithaa. usakee samaadhi toot gaee. usakee vilupt chetan pradeept ho gaee. use apanee haal kaa-apanee haal kaa jnaan ho gayaa, maano koee bhoolee huee baat yaad aa gaee ho. usane aankhen phaa. d kar nirml ko dekhaa aur munh pher liyaa.

ekaaek munshee jee teevr aavaaz men bole-tum yahaan kyaa karane aaee? nirml avaak rah gaee. woh batalaae ki kyaa karane aaee! itane seedhe se savaal kaa bhee woh kyaa koee javaab na de sakee! woh kyaa karane aaee? itn jatil savaal kisake saamane aayaa hogaa? ghar kaa aadamee beemaar hai, use dekhane aaee hai, yeh baat kyaa binaa poochhe maaloom na ho sakatee thee? phir yeh savaal kyon?

woh hatbuddhi see kha. dee rahee, maano sanjnaaheen ho gaee ho! usane dono la. dakon se munshee jee ke shok aur santos kee baaten sun kar yeh anumaan kiyaa thaa ki ab unakaa dil saaph ho gayaa hai. ab use pataa huaa ki woh bhram thaa. haan, woh mahaa bhram thaa. agar woh jaanatee ki aansuon kee vrishti ne bhee shak kee agni shaant naheen kee, to woh yahaan kabhee naheen na aatee. woh ku. dhaku. dh kar mar jaatee; par ghar se baahar paanv na nikaalatee!!

munshee jee ne phir vahee savaal kiyaa-tum yahaan kyon aaee?
nirml ne nihshank bhaav se javaab diyaa-aap yahaan kyaa: karane aae hain?

munshee jee ke nathane pha. dakane lage. woh jhallaa kar chaarapaaee se uth aur nirml kaa haath paka. d kar vole-tumhaare yahaan aane kee koee zaroorat naheen. jab main bulaaoon tav aanaa, samajh gaee.

are! yeh kyaa anarth huaa? mansaaraam jo chaarapaaee se hil bhee na sakataa thaa, uth kar kha. daa ho gayaa aur nirml ke pairon par gir kar rote hue bolaa-ammaan jee, is abhaage ke lie aapako bekaar itn takaleeph huaa? main aapakaa pyaar kabhee na bhooloongaa. eeshvar se meree yahee praarthn hai ki meraa punarjanm aapake pet men bachchaa se ho, jisase main aapake karz se urin ho sakoon. eeshvar jaanataa hai, mainne aapako vimaataa naheen samajhaann. main aapako apanee maan samajhataa rahaa. aapakee unn mujhase bahut jyaadaa na ho; lekin aap meree maan ke sthaan par theen; aur mainne aapako hameshaa isee nazar dekhaa..... v naheen volaa jaataa; ammaan jee, kshamaa keejie! yeh antim bhet hai!!

nirml ne aansoo-pravaah ko rokate hue kahaa-tum aisee baaten kyon karate ho? do-chaar din men achchhe ho jaaoge!

mansaaraam ne ksheen aavaaz men kahaa-ab jeene kee chaah naheen; aur na volane kee shakti hee hai!

yeh kahate-kahate mansaaraam ashakt hokar vaheen jameen par let
gayaa. nirml ne pati kee ora nirbhay netron se dekhate hue kahaa-dॉktaron ne kyaa salaah dee?

munshee jee-sab ke sab bhang khaa gae hain. kahate hain taajaa khoon chaahie.

nirmalaa-taajaa khoon mil jaay, to jaana-rakshaa ho sakatee hai?

munshee jee ne nirml kee ora teevr netron se dekh kar kahaa-main eeshvar naheen hoon; aur na daॉktaron hee ko eeshvar samajhataa hoon.

nirmalaa-taajaa khun to aisee alabhy vastu naheen!

manshee jee-aakaash ke taare bhee to alabhy naheen! munh ke saamane khandak kyaa cheez hai!

nirmalaa-main apn khoon dene ko taiyaar hoon! daॉktar ko bulaaie!!

munshee jee ne vismit hokar kahaa-tum!

nirmalaa-haan! kyaa mere khoon se kaam na chalegaa?

munshee jee-tum apn khoon dogee! naheen, tumhaare khoon kee jaroorat naheen! isamen praanon kaa dar hai!!

nirmalaa-mere jaan aur kis din kaam aavenge?

munshee jee ne sajal aankh hokar kahaa-naheen nirml, unakaa keemat ab meree nigaahon men bahut ba. dh gayaa hai! aaj tak woh mere bhog kee vastu thee; aaj se woh meree bhakti kee vastu hai. mainne tumhaare saath ba. daa anyaay kiyaa hai, kshamaa karo!
