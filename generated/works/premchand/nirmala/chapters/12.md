# Nirmala — Adhyay 12

**Munshi Premchand**

barhavam parichchhed

tin din guzar gae; aur munshi ji ghar na ae! NAmERukmini donon vakt aspatal jati; aur NAmEmansaram ko dekh ati thi. donon larke bhi jate the; par NAmENirmala kaise jati? uske pairon men to beriyam padi hui thim. vah NAmEmansaram ki bimari ka hal-chal janne ke lie vyagra rahti thi, yadi NAmERukmini se kuchh puchhti thi, to tane milte the; aur larkon se puchhti, to ve besir-pair ki baten karne lagte! ek bar khud jakar dekhne ke lie uska chitt bechain ho raha tha. use yah dar hota tha ki shak ne kahim munshi ji ke putrapren ko shithil na kar diya ho, kahim unki kripnata hi to NAmEmansaram ke achchhe hone men badhak nahin ho rahi hai? doktar kisi ke sage nahin hote, inhen to apne paison se kam hai, murda dozakh men jay ya bihisht men. uske man men prabal chah hoti
thi ki jakar aspatal ke doktar ko ek hajar ki thaili dekar kahen-inhen bacha dijie, yah thaili apki bhent hai; par uske pas na to itne rupye hi the, na itna himmat hi tha. ab bhi yadi vah vaham pahunch sakti, to NAmEmansaram achchha ho jata. uski jaisi seva-sushrusha honi chahiye, vaisi nahin ho rahi hai, nahin to kya tin din tak jvar hi na utrata? yah daihik jvar nahin, mansik jvar hai aur chitt ke shant hone hi se iska prakop shant ho sakta hai. agar vah vaham rat bhar bhi baithi rah sakti; aur munshi ji jara bhi man maila na karte, to shayad NAmEmansaram ko yakin ho jata ki pita ji ka dil saph hai; aur phir uske achchhe hone men der na lagti; lekin aisa hoga? munshi ji use vaham dekh kar prasannachitt rah sakenge? kya ab bhi unka dil saph nahin hua? yaham se jate samay to aisa pata hua tha. ki vah apne pramad par pachhta rahe haim. aisa to na hoga ki uske vaham jate hi munshi ji ka shak phir bharak uthe aur vah bete ki jan lekar hi chhore!

isi duvidha men pade-pade tin din guzar gae; aur na ghar men chulha jala, na kisi ne kuchh khaya. larkon ke liye bajar se puriyam manga li jati thim. NAmERukmini aur NAmENirmala bhukhi hi. so jati thim. unhen khana ki chah hi na hoti thi.

chauthe din NAmEJiyaram skul se lauta, to aspatal hota hua ghar aya. NAmENirmala ne puchha-kyon bhaiya, aspatal
bhi gae the? aj kya hal hai? tumhare bhaiya uthe ya nahin?

NAmEJiyaram ruamsa hokar bola-ammam ji, aj to vah kuchh bolte-chalte hi na the. chupchap charapai par pade jod-jod se hath-pamv patak rahe the.

NAmENirmala ke chehre ka ranga ud gaya. ghabra kar puchha-tumhare vabu ji vaham na the?

NAmEJiyaram -the kyon nahin. aj vah bahut rote the! NAmENirmala ka kaleja dhak-dhak karne laga. puchha-doktar log vaham na the?

NAmEJiyaram -doktar bhi khade the aur apas men kuchh salah kar rahe the. sabse bada sivil sarjan angreji men kah raha tha ki mariz ke deh men kuchh taza khun dalna chahiye. is par vabu ji ne kaha-meri deh se jitna khun chahe le lijie. sivilasarjan ne hams kar kaha-apke blad ( Blood ) se kam nahin chalega. kisi javan aadmi ka vlad chahiye, akhir usne pichkari se koi dava bhaiya ke vaju men dal di. char angul se kam ki sui na rahi hogi; par bhaiya minke tak nahin. maimne to mare dar ke ankhen band kar li!

bade-bade mahan sankalp avesh me hi janma lete haim. kaham to NAmENirmala dar se sukhi jati thi, kaham uske mumh par drirh sankalp: ki abha jhalak padi. usne apni deh ka taja khun dene ka
phaisla kar liya. agar uske rakt se NAmEmansaram ke jan bach jayam, to vah badi khushi se uski antim bund tak de dalegi. ab jiska jo ji chahe samjhe, vah kuchh parvah na karegi. usne NAmEJiyaram se kaha-tum lapak kar ek ekka bula lo, maim aspatal jaungi.

NAmEJiyaram -vaham to is vakt bahut se aadmi honge. jara rat ho jane dijie!

NAmENirmala -nahin, tum abhi ekka bula lo!

NAmEJiyaram -kahim babu ji bigren na!

NAmENirmala -bigrane do! tum abhi jakar savari lao.

NAmEJiyaram -maim kah dunga, ammam ji hi ne mujhse savari mangavai thi.

NAmENirmala -kah dena!

NAmEJiyaram to udhar tanga lane gaya, idhar itni der men NAmENirmala ne sir men kachi ki, jura bandha, kapde badle, gahne pahne, pan khaya aur dvar par akar tange ki rah dekhne lagi.

NAmERukmini apne kamre men baithi hui thi. use is taiyari se ate dekh kar boli-kaham jati ho bahu?

NAmENirmala -jara aspatal tak jati hum.

ruvimni-vaham jakar kya karogi?

NAmENirmala kuchh nahin, karungi kya? karne vale to bhagvan haim. dekhne ko ji chahta hai.
NAmERukmini -maim kahti hum mat jao!

NAmENirmala ne vinit bhav se kaha-abhi chali aungi, didi ji! NAmEJiyaram kah rahe haim ki is vakt unki halat achchhi nahin hai! ji nahin manta, ap bhi chalie na!

rukmini-maim dekhaai hum. itna hi samajh lo ki ab bahri khun pahunchne hi par jivan ki ummid hai. kaun apna taza khun dega; aur kyon dega? usmen bhi to pranon ka dar hai.

NAmENirmala -isilie to maim jati hum. mere khun se kya kam na chalega?

NAmERukmini -chalega kyon nahin, javan hi ka khun to chahiye; lekin tumhare khun se mansa ki jan bache, isse yah kahim achchha hai ki use pani men baha diya jay!

tanga a gaya. NAmENirmala aur NAmEJiyaram donon ja baithe! tanga chala!

NAmERukmini dvar par khadi der tak roti rahi. aj pahli bar use NAmENirmala par daya ai. uska bas hota to vah NAmENirmala ko bandh rakhti. daya aur hamadardi ka avesh use kaham lie jata hai, yah vah aprakat rup se dekh rahi thi. ah! yah badakismati-prerna hai, yah sarvanash ka rasta hai!

NAmENirmala aspatal pahunchi, to dipak jal chuke the. doktar log apni-apni ray dekar bida ho chuke the. NAmEmansaram ka jvar kuchh kam ho gaya tha. vah taktaki lagae dvar ki or dekh
riha tha. uski nazar unmukt akash ki or lagi hui thi, mano kisi devta ki intzar kar raha ho. vah kaham hai, kis hal men hai, iska use kuchh jnyan na tha!

sahsa NAmENirmala ko dekhte hi vah chaunk kar uth baitha. uski samadhi tut gai. uski vilupt chetan pradipt ho gai. use apni hal ka-apni hal ka jnyan ho gaya, mano koi bhuli hui bat yad a gai ho. usne ankhen phar kar NAmENirmala ko dekha aur mumh pher liya.

ekaek munshi ji tivra avaz men bole-tum yaham kya karne ai? NAmENirmala avak rah gai. vah batalae ki kya karne ai! itne sidhe se saval ka bhi vah kya koi javab na de saki! vah kya karne ai? itna jatil saval kiske samne aya hoga? ghar ka aadmi bimar hai, use dekhne ai hai, yah bat kya bina puchhe malum na ho sakti thi? phir yah saval kyon?

vah hatbuddhi si khadi rahi, mano sanjnyahin ho gai ho! usne dono larkon se munshi ji ke shok aur santos ki baten sun kar yah anuman kiya tha ki ab unka dil saph ho gaya hai. ab use pata hua ki vah bhram tha. ham, vah maha bhram tha. agar vah janti ki amsuon ki vrishti ne bhi shak ki agni shant nahin ki, to vah yaham kabhi nahin na ati. vah kurhkurh kar mar jati; par ghar se bahar pamv na nikalti!!

munshi ji ne phir vahi saval kiya-tum yaham kyon ai?
NAmENirmala ne nihshanka bhav se javab diya-ap yaham kya: karne ae haim?

munshi ji ke nathne pharkane lage. vah jhalla kar charapai se uth aur NAmENirmala ka hath pakad kar vole-tumhare yaham ane ki koi zarurat nahin. jab maim bulaum tav ana, samajh gai.

are! yah kya anarth hua? NAmEmansaram jo charapai se hil bhi na sakta tha, uth kar khada ho gaya aur NAmENirmala ke pairon par gir kar rote hue bola-ammam ji, is abhage ke lie apko bekar itna takliph hua? maim apka pyaar kabhi na bhulunga. ishvar se meri yahi prarthana hai ki mera punarjanma apke pet men bachcha se ho, jisse maim apke karz se urin ho sakum. ishvar janta hai, maimne apko vimata nahin samajhamm. maim apko apni mam samjhata raha. apki unn mujhse bahut jyada na ho; lekin ap meri mam ke sthan par thim; aur maimne apko hamesha isi nazar dekha...... av nahin vola jata; ammam ji, kshama kijie! yah antim bhet hai!!

NAmENirmala ne amsu-pravah ko rokte hue kaha-tum aisi baten kyon karte ho? do-char din men achchhe ho jaoge!

NAmEmansaram ne kshin avaz men kaha-ab jine ki chah nahin; aur na volne ki shakti hi hai!

yah kahte-kahte NAmEmansaram ashakt hokar vahim zameen par let
gaya. NAmENirmala ne pati ki or nirbhay netron se dekhte hue kaha-doktaron ne kya salah di?

munshi ji-sab ke sab bhanga kha gae haim. kahte haim taja khun chahiye.

NAmENirmala -taja khun mil jay, to jan-raksha ho sakti hai?

munshi ji ne NAmENirmala ki or tivra netron se dekh kar kaha-maim ishvar nahin hum; aur na doktaron hi ko ishvar samjhata hum.

NAmENirmala -taja khun to aisi alabhya vastu nahin!

manshi ji-akash ke tare bhi to alabhya nahin! mumh ke samne khandak kya chiz hai!

NAmENirmala -maim apna khun dene ko taiyar hum! doktar ko bulaie!!

munshi ji ne vismit hokar kaha-tum!

NAmENirmala -ham! kya mere khun se kam na chalega?

munshi ji-tum apna khun dogi! nahin, tumhare khun ki jarurat nahin! ismen pranon ka dar hai!!

NAmENirmala -mere jan aur kis din kam avenge?

munshi ji ne sajal ankh hokar kaha-nahin NAmENirmala, unka kimat ab meri nigahon men bahut barh gaya hai! aj tak vah mere bhog ki vastu thi; aj se vah meri bhakti ki vastu hai. maimne tumhare sath bada anyay kiya hai, kshama karo!
