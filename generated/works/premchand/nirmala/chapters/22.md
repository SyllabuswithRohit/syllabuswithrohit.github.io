# Nirmala — Adhyay 22

**Munshi Premchand**

baaeesavaan

parichchhed

Nirmala n bigad kar poochhaa-itanee der kahaan lagaaee?

siyaaraam ne dhithaaee se kahaa-raaste mein ek jagah so gayaa thaa.

Nirmala-yah to main nahin kahatee; par jaanate ho kai baj gae hain? das kabhee ke baj gae. baajaar kuchh door bhi to nahin hai.

siyaa0-kuchh door naheen. daravaaje hee par to hai.

Nirmala-seedhe se kyon nahin bolate? aisaa bigad rahe ho jaise meraa hee koee kaam karane gae ho.

siyaa0-to aap vyarth ki bakavaad kyon karatee hain? liyaa huaa saudaa lautaanaa kyaa aasaan kaam hai. baniye se ghanton hujjat karanee padee. vah to kaho ek baabaa jee ne kah-sun kar pheravaa diyaa; nahin to kisee tarah n pherataa. raaste mein ek minit bhi kaheen nahin rukaa, seedhaa chalaa aataa hoon. Nirmala-ghee ke lie gae-gae to tum gyaarah baje laute ho, lakadee ke lie jaaoge to saanjh hee kar doge!'tumhaare baaboo jee, vinaa khaae hee chale gae. tumhe itanee der lagaanaa tha, to pahale hee kyon n kah diyaa. jaate ho lakadee ke lie?

siyaaraam ab apane ko n sambhaal sakaa. jhallaa kar bolaa-lakadee kisee aur se manggaaie. mujhe skool jaane ki der ho rahee hai.

Nirmala-khana n khaaoge?

siyaa-n khaaoonggaa.

Nirmala-main khana banaane ko taiyaar hoon. haan, lakadee laane nahin jaa sakatee.
siyaa0-bhugee ko kyon nahin bhejatee?

Nirmala-bhungggee ka laayaa saudaa kyaa tumane kabhee dekhaa. nahin hai?

siyaa-to main to is vakt n jaaoonggaa.

Nirmala-phir mujhe dop n denaa.

siyaaraam kaee dinon se skool nahin gayaa thaa. baajaar-haat ke maare use kitaaben dekhane ka samay hee n milataa thaa. skool jaakar jhidakiyaan khaane, vainych par khade hone ya oonchee topee dene ke sivaa aur kyaa milataa. vah ghar se kitaaven lekar chalataa; par shahar ke baahar jaa kar kisee vriksh ki chhaanh mein baithaa rahataa ya paltanon ki kavaayad dekhataa. teen baje ghar laut aataa. aaj bhi vah ghar se chalaa; lekin baithane mein usakaa jee n lagaa-us par aten alag jal rahee theen. haa! ab use rotiyon ke bhi laale pad gae. das baje kyaa khana n ban sakataa thaa. maanaa ki baaboo jee chale gae the. kyaa mere lie ghar me do-chaar paise bhi n the? ammo hotee, to is tarah binaa kuchh khaae-pie aane detee? meraa ab koee nahin rahaa!

siyaaraam ka man baabaa jee ke darshanon ke lie vyaakul ho uthaa. usane sochaa-is vakt vah kahaan milengge? kahaan chal kar das? unakee manohar vaanee, unakee josh-prad saantvanaa usake man ko kheenchane lagee. usane aatur hokar kahaa-main unake saath hee kyon n chalaa gayaa? ghar par mere lie kyaa rakkhaa tha?

vah aaj yahan se chalaa, to ghar n jaakar seedhaa ghee vaale saah jee ki dukaan par gayaa. shaayad baabaa jee se wahan mulaakaat ho jaaya. par wahan baabaa jee n the. badee der tak khadaa-khadaa laut kamaaeeaa.

ghar aakar baithaa hee tha ki Nirmala ne aakar kahaa-aaj der kahaan lagaaee. savere khana nahin banaa, kyaa is vakt bhi upavaas hogaa. jaakar baajaar se koee tarakaaree laao.

Nirmala 258

nahin hoon. aakhir rotiyaan hee to khilaatee ho ya aur kuchh? aisee rotiyaan jahaan mehanat karoonggaa, vaheen mil jaayanggee. jab majooree hee karanee hai, to aapakee n karoonggaa. jaaie, mere lie khana n banaaiegaa.

Nirmala avaak rah gaee. ladake ko aaj yah kyaa ho gayaa? aur din to chupake se jaakar kaam kar laataa tha, aaj kyon tyoriyaan badal rahaa hai? ab bhi usako yah n soojhee ki siyaaraam ko do-chaar paise kuchh khaane ko de de. usakaa svabhaav itanaa kripan ho gayaa thaa. bolee―ghar ka kaam karanaa to majooree nahin kahalaatee. isee tarah main bhi kah ki doonn main khana nahin pakaatee; tumhaare baaboo jee kah den―main kachaharee nahin jaataa, to kyaa ho; bataao? nahin jaanaa chaahate mat jaao, bhungggee se manggaa loonggee. main kyaa jaanatee thi ki tumhe baajaar jaanaa buraa lagataa hai, nahin to balaa se paise ki cheej dhele ki aatee, tumhe n bhejatee. lo, aaj se kaan pakadatee hoon.

dheere-dheere sadakon par sannaataa chhaa gayaa, gharon ke dvaar vand hone lage. sadak ki patariyon par aur galiyon mein basakhate ya bore vichhaa-vichhaa kar bhaarat ki prajaa sukh-nidraa mein magn hone lagee; lekin siyaaraam ghar n lautaa. us ghar se usakaa dil phat gayaa tha, jahaan kisee ko usase pyaar n tha, jahaan vah kisee paraashrit ki bhaanti padaa huaa tha. keval iseelie ki use aur kaheen sharan nahin thee. is vakt bhi usake ghar n jaane ki kise fikr hogee? baaboo jee khana karake lote hongge, ammaan jee bhi aaraam karane jaa rahee honggee. kisee ne mere kamare ki or jhaangk kar dekhaa bhi n hogaa. haan, buaa jee ghabadaa rahee honggee. vahee abhee tak meree raah dekhatee honggee. jab tak main n jaaoonggaa, khana n karenggee.

rukminee ki yaad aate hee siyaaraam ghar ki or chalaa. vah agar aur kuchh n kar sakatee thi, to kam se kam use god mein chimataa kar rotee to thee. usake vaahar se aane par haath-munh dhone ke lie paani to rakh detee thee. sansaar mein sabhee vaalak doodh ki kulliyaan nahin karate, sabhee sone ke kaur nahin khaate. kitanon ko pet bhar khana bhi nahin milataa; par ghar se virakt vahee hote hain, jo maatri-pyaar se vanychit hain.

siyaaraam ghar ki or chalaa hee tha ki sahasaa baabaa paramaanand ek galee se aate dikhaaee die.

siyaaraam ne jaakar unakaa haath pakad liyaa. paramaanand ne chaungk kar poochhaa -- bachcha, tum yahan kahaan?

siyaaraam ne baat banaa kar kahaa -- ek dost se milane kamaaeeaa thaa. aapakaa jagah yahan se kitanee door hai?

paramaanand -- ham log to aaj yahan se jaa rahe hain, bachcha haridvaar ki yaatraa hai.

siyaaraam ne hatotsaah hokar kahaa -- kyaa aaj hee, chale jaaiegaa?

paramaanand -- haan bachcha, ab laut kar aaoonggaa to darshan doonggaa.

siyaaraam ne kaatar kanth se kahaa -- laut kar!

paramaanand -- jald hee aaoonggaa; bachcha!

siyaaraam ne deen-bhaav se kahaa -- main bhi aapake saath chaloonggaa.

paramaanand -- mere saath! tumhaare ghar ke log jaane dengge?

siyaaraam -- ghar ke logon ko meree kyaa parvaah hai. isake aage siyaaraam aur kuchh n kah sakaa. usake ashru-pooriv aangkhenon ne usakee karun-gaathaa usase kaheen vistaar ke saath sunaa dee, jitanee usakee vaanee kar sakatee thee. paramaanand ne bachcha ko kanth se lagaa kar kahaa-achchha bachcha, teree ichchhaa hai to chala. saadhu santon ki sangggati ka bhi khushi uthaa. Bhagwan ki ichchhaa hogee, to teree ichchhaa pooree hogee.

daane par mandaraataa huaa pattee ant ko daane par gir padaa. usake zindagi ka ant pinjare mein hogaa ya vyaadh ki chhuree ke tale — yah kaun jaanataa hai?
