# Nirmala — Adhyay 22

**Munshi Premchand**

parichchheda

nirmalaa na biga.da kar poochhaa-itanee dera kahaan lagaaee?

siyaaraama ne dhithaaee se kaha-raaste men ek jagaha so gaya tha|

nirmalaa-yaha to main naheen kehti;par jaanate ho kai baja gae hain? dasa kabhee ke baja gae| baajaara kuchha doora bhee to naheen hai|

siyaa0-kuchha doora naheen| daravaaje hee par to hai|

nirmalaa-seedhe se kyon naheen bolte? aisaa biga.da rahe ho jaise meraa hee koee kaama karane gae ho|

siyaa0-to aapa vyartha kee bakavaada kyon karti hain? liya hua saudaa lautaanaa kyaa aasaana kaama hai| baniye se ghanton hujjata karanee pa.dee| vaha to kaho ek baabaa jee ne kaha-suna kar pheravaa diya; naheen to kisee tarah na pherata| raaste men ek minita bhee kaheen naheen ruka,seedhaa chalaa aata hoon| nirmalaa-ghee ke lie gae-gae to tuma gyaaraha baje laute ho,laka.dee ke lie jaaoge to saanjha hee kar doge!'tumhaare baaboo jee,vinaa khaae hee chale gae| tumhen itanee dera lagaanaa tha,to pahale hee kyon na kaha diya| jaate ho laka.dee ke lie?

siyaaraama ab apane ko na sanbhaala sakaa|jhallaa kar bola-laka.dee kisee aur se mangaaie|mujhe skoola jaane kee dera ho rahi hai|

nirmalaa-khaanaa na khaaoge?

siya-na khaaoongaa|

nirmalaa-main khaanaa banaane ko taiyaara hoon| haan,laka.dee laane naheen jaa sakatee|
siyaa0-bhugee ko kyon naheen bhejatee?

nirmalaa-bhungee kaa laayaa saudaa kyaa tumane kabhee dekha| naheen hai?

siya-to main to is vakta na jaaoongaa|

nirmalaa-phir mujhe dopa na dena|

siyaaraama kaee dinon se skoola naheen gaya tha| baajaara-haata ke maare use kitaaben dekhane kaa samay hee na milata tha| skoola jaakara jhi.dakiyaan khaane,vaincha par kha.de hone yaa oonchee topee dene ke sivaa aur kyaa milata| vaha ghar se kitaaven lekara chalata; par shehar ke baahara jaa kar kisee vriksha kee chhaanha men baithaa rahata yaa paltanon kee kavaayada dekhata| teena baje ghar lauta aata|aaja bhee vaha ghar se chala;lekin baithane men usakaa jee na lagaa-us par aten alaga jala rahi theen| ha!ab use rotiyon ke bhee laale pa.da gae| dasa baje kyaa khaanaa na bana sakata tha| maanaa ki baaboo jee chale gae the| kyaa mere lie ghar me do-chaara paise bhee na the?ammao hoti,to is tarah binaa kuchha khaae-pie aane detee? meraa ab koee naheen raha!

siyaaraama kaa mann baabaa jee ke darshanon ke lie vyaakula ho uthaa| usane sochaa-is vakta vaha kahaan milenge? kahaan chala kar dasa? unakee manohara vaanee,unakee utsaaha-prada saantvana usake mann ko kheenchane lagee| usane aatura hokara kaha-main unake saatha hee kyon na chalaa gaya? ghar par mere lie kyaa rakkhaa tha?

vaha aaja yahaan se chala,to ghar na jaakara seedhaa ghee vaale saaha jee kee dukaana par gaya| shaayada baabaa jee se vahaan mulaaqaata ho jaaya| par vahaan baabaa jee na the| ba.dee dera taka kha.daa-kha.daa lauta aaya|

ghar aakara baithaa hee tha ki nirmalaa ne aakara kaha-aaja dera kahaan lagaaee| savere khaanaa naheen bana,kyaa is vakta bhee upavaasa hogaa| jaakara baajaara se koee tarakaaree laao|

nirmalaa 258

naheen hoon| aakhira rotiyaan hee to khilaatee ho yaa aur kuchha? aisee rotiyaan jahaan mehanata karoonga, vaheen mila jaayangee| jab majooree hee karanee hai, to aapakee na karoongaa| jaaie, mere lie khaanaa na banaaiegaa|

nirmalaa avaak raha gaee| ladke ko aaja yaha kyaa ho gaya? aur din to chupake se jaakara kaama kar laataa tha, aaja kyon tyoriyaan badala raha hai? ab bhee usako yaha na soojhee ki siyaaraama ko do-chaara paise kuchha khaane ko de de| usakaa svabhaava itana kripana ho gaya tha| boli―ghar kaa kaama karana to majooree naheen kahalaatee| isee tarah main bhee kaha ki doonn main khaanaa naheen pakaatee; tumhaare baaboo jee kaha den―main kachaharee naheen jaata, to kyaa ho; bataao? naheen jana chaahate mata jaao, bhungee se mangaa loongee| main kyaa jaanatee thi ki tumhen baazaara jana buraa lagata hai, naheen to balaa se paise kee cheeza dhele kee aati, tumhen na bhejatee| lo, aaja se kaana paka.datee hoon|

dheere-dheere sa.dakon par sannaataa chhaa gaya,gharon ke dvaara vanda hone lage| sa.daka kee patariyon par aur galiyon men basakhate yaa bore vichhaa-vichhaa kar bhaarata kee prajaa sukha-nidraa men magna hone lagee;lekin siyaaraama ghar na lautaa|us ghar se usakaa dil phata gaya tha,jahaan kisee ko usase prema na tha,jahaan vaha kisee paraashrita kee bhaanti pada hua tha | kevala iseelie ki use aur kaheen sharana naheen thi| is vakta bhee usake ghar na jaane kee kise chintaa hogee? baaboo jee bhojana karake lote honge,ammaan jee bhee aaraama karane jaa rahi hongee| kisee ne mere kamare kee ora jhaanka kar dekha bhee na hogaa| haan,buaa jee ghaba.daa rahi hongee| vahee abhee taka meree raaha dekhatee hongee| jab taka main na jaaoonga,bhojana na karengee|

rukminee kee yaada aate hee siyaaraama ghar kee ora chalaa| vaha agar aur kuchha na kar sakatee thi,to kama se kama use goda men chimata kar rotee to thi| usake vaahara se aane par haath-munha dhone ke lie paanee to rakha detee thi| sansaara men sabhee vaalaka doodha kee kulliyaan naheen karte, sabhee sone ke kaura naheen khaate| kitanon ko peta bhara bhojana bhee naheen milata; par ghar se virakta vahee hote hain, jo maatri-sneha se vanchita hain|

siyaaraama ghar kee ora chalaa hee tha ki sahasaa baabaa paramaananda ek galee se aate dikhaaee die|

siyaaraama ne jaakara unakaa haath paka.da liya| paramaananda ne chaunka kar poochhaa -- bachcha, tuma yahaan kahaan?

siyaaraama ne baat bana kar kaha -- ek dosta se milane aaya tha| aapakaa sthaana yahaan se kitanee doora hai?

paramaananda -- hama log to aaja yahaan se jaa rahe hain, bachcha haridvaara kee yaatraa hai|

siyaaraama ne hatotsaaha hokara kaha -- kyaa aaja hee, chale jaaiega?

paramaananda -- haan bachcha, ab lauta kar aaoongaa to darshana doongaa|

siyaaraama ne kaatara kantha se kaha -- lauta kar!

paramaananda -- jalda hee aaoonga; bachcha!

siyaaraama ne deena-bhaava se kaha -- main bhee aapake saatha chaloongaa|

paramaananda -- mere saatha! tumhaare ghar ke log jaane denge?

siyaaraama -- ghar ke logon ko meree kyaa parvaaha hai| isake aage siyaaraama aur kuchha na kaha sakaa| usake ashru-pooriva netron ne usakee karuna-gaathaa usase kaheen vistaara ke saatha sunaa dee, jitanee usakee vaanee kar sakatee thi| paramaananda ne baalaka ko kantha se lagaa kar kaha-achchha bachcha, teree ichchhaa hai to chala| saadhu santon kee sangati kaa bhee aananda uthaa| bhagavaan kee ichchhaa hogee, to teree ichchhaa pooree hogee|

daane par mandaraataa hua pattee anta ko daane par gira pada| usake jeevana kaa anta pinjare men hogaa yaa vyaadha kee chhuree ke tale–yaha kaun jaanata hai?
