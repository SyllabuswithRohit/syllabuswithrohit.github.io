# Nirmala — Adhyay 22

**Munshi Premchand**

baisvam

parichchhed

NAmENirmala na bigar kar puchha-itni der kaham lagai?

NAmESiyaram ne dhithai se kaha-raste men ek jagah so gaya tha.

NAmENirmala -yah to maim nahin kahti; par jante ho kai baj gae haim? das kabhi ke baj gae. bajar kuchh dur bhi to nahin hai.

siya0-kuchh dur nahin. darvaje hi par to hai.

NAmENirmala -sidhe se kyon nahin bolte? aisa bigar rahe ho jaise mera hi koi kam karne gae ho.

siya0-to ap bekar ki bakvad kyon karti haim? liya hua sauda lautana kya asan kam hai. baniye se ghanton hujjat karni padi. vah to kaho ek baba ji ne kah-sun kar pherva diya; nahin to kisi tarah na pherta. raste men ek minit bhi kahim nahin ruka, sidha chala ata hum.
NAmENirmala -ghi ke lie gae-gae to tum gyarah baje laute ho, lakri ke lie jaoge to sanjh hi kar doge! tumhare babu ji, vina khae hi chale gae. tumhen itni der lagana tha, to pahle hi kyon na kah diya. jate ho lakri ke lie?

NAmESiyaram ab apne ko na sambhal saka. jhalla kar bola-lakri kisi aur se mangaie. mujhe skul jane ki der ho rahi hai.

NAmENirmala -khana na khaoge?

siya-na khaunga.

NAmENirmala -maim khana banane ko taiyar hum. ham, lakri lane nahin ja sakti.
siya0-bhugi ko kyon nahin bhejti?

NAmENirmala -bhungi ka laya sauda kya tumne kabhi dekha. nahin hai?

siya-to maim to is vakt na jaunga.

NAmENirmala -phir mujhe dop na dena.

NAmESiyaram kai dinon se skul nahin gaya tha. bajar-hat ke mare use kitaben dekhne ka samay hi na milta tha. skul jakar jhirkiya khane, vaincha par khade hone ya unchi topi dene ke siva aur kya milta. vah ghar se kitaven lekar chalta; par shahar ke bahar ja kar kisi
vriksh ki chhamh men baitha rahta ya paltanon ki kavayad dekhta. tin baje ghar laut ata. aj bhi vah ghar se chala; lekin baithne men uska ji na laga-us par aten alag jal rahi thim. ha! ab use rotiyon ke bhi lale par gae. das baje kya khana na ban sakta tha. mana ki babu ji chale gae the. kya mere lie ghar me do-char paise bhi na the? ammo hoti, to is tarah bina kuchh khae-pie ane deti? mera ab koi nahin raha!

NAmESiyaram ka man baba ji ke darshanon ke lie bechain ho utha. usne socha-is vakt vah kaham milenge? kaham chal kar das? unki manohar vani, unki utsah-prad santvana uske man ko khinchne lagi. usne atur hokar kaha-maim unke sath hi kyon na chala gaya? ghar par mere lie kya rakkha tha?

vah aj yaham se chala, to ghar na jakar sidha ghi vale sah ji ki dukan par gaya. shayad baba ji se vaham mulaqat ho jay. par vaham baba ji na the. badi der tak khada-khada laut aya.

ghar akar baitha hi tha ki NAmENirmala ne akar kaha-aj der kaham lagai. savere khana nahin bana, kya is vakt bhi upvas hoga. jakar bajar se koi tarkari lao.

NAmESiyaram ne jhalla kar kaha-din bhar ka bhukha chala ata hum, kuchh pani pine tak ko nahin lai. upar se bajar jane ka hukma de diya. maim nahin jata bajar! kisi ka naukar

nahin hum. akhir rotiyam hi to khilati ho ya aur kuchh? aisi rotiyam jaham mehnat karunga, vahim mil jayangi. jab majuri hi karni hai, to apki na karunga. jaie, mere lie khana na banaiega.

NAmENirmala avak rah gai. larke ko aj yah kya ho gaya? aur din to chupke se jakar kam kar lata tha, aj kyon tyoriyam badal raha hai? ab bhi usko yah na sujhi ki NAmESiyaram ko do-char paise kuchh khane ko de de. uska svabhav itna kripan ho gaya tha. bolighar ka kam karna to majuri nahin kahlati. isi tarah maim bhi kah ki dumm maim khana nahin pakati; tumhare babu ji kah denmaim kachhari nahin jata, to kya ho; batao? nahin jana chahte mat jao, bhungi se manga lungi. maim kya janti thi ki tumhen bazar jana bura lagta hai, nahin to bala se paise ki chiz dhele ki ati, tumhen na bhejti. lo, aj se kan pakrati hum.

NAmESiyaram dil men kuchh lajjit to hua; par bazar na gaya. uska dhyan baba ji ki or laga hua tha. apne sare dukhon ka ant aur jivan ki sari ashaen use ab baba ji ke ashirvad men malum hoti thim. unhim ki sharan jakar uska yah adharit jivan sarthak hoga. suryast ke samay vah adhir ho gaya. sara bazar chhan mara; lekin baba ji ka kahim pata na mila. din bhar ka bhukha-pyasa, vah nasmajh larka dukhte hue dil ko hathon se dabae, ummid aur dar ki murti bana hua
dukanon, galiyon aur mandiron men us ashay ko khojta phirta tha, jiske bina use apna jivan dussah ho raha tha. ek var ek mandir ke samne use koi sadhu khada dikhai diya. usne samjha vahi haim. harshollas se vah phul utha. daura aur jakar sadhu ke pas khada ho gaya; par yah koi aur hi mahatma the. nirash hokar age barh gaya.

dhire-dhire sarkon par sannata chha gaya, gharon ke dvar vand hone lage. sarak ki patriyo par aur galiyon men baskhate ya bore vichha-vichha kar bharat ki praja sukh-nidra men magna hone lagi; lekin NAmESiyaram ghar na lauta. us ghar se uska dil phat gaya tha, jaham kisi ko usse pyaar na tha, jaham vah kisi parashrit ki tarah pada hua tha. sirph isilie ki use aur kahim sharan nahin thi. is vakt bhi uske ghar na jane ki kise chinta hogi? babu ji khana karke lote honge, ammam ji bhi aram karne ja rahi hongi. kisi ne mere kamre ki or jhank kar dekha bhi na hoga. ham, bua ji ghabra rahi hongi. vahi abhi tak meri rah dekhti hongi. jab tak maim na jaunga, khana na karengi.

NAmERukmini ki yad ate hi NAmESiyaram ghar ki or chala. vah agar aur kuchh na kar sakti thi, to kam se kam use god men chimta kar roti to thi. uske vahar se ane par hath-mumh dhone ke lie pani to rakh deti thi. samsar men sabhi valak dudh ki kulliyam nahin karte, sabhi sone ke kaur nahin khate. kitnon
ko pet bhar khana bhi nahin milta; par ghar se virakt vahi hote haim, jo matri-pyaar se vanchit haim.

NAmESiyaram ghar ki or chala hi tha ki sahsa baba paramanand ek gali se ate dikhai die.

NAmESiyaram ne jakar unka hath pakad liya. paramanand ne chaunk kar puchha -- bachcha, tum yaham kaham?

NAmESiyaram ne bat bana kar kaha -- ek dost se milne aya tha. apka sthan yaham se kitni dur hai?

paramanand -- ham log to aj yaham se ja rahe haim, bachcha haridvar ki yatra hai.

NAmESiyaram ne hatotsah hokar kaha -- kya aj hi, chale jaiega?

paramanand -- ham bachcha, ab laut kar aunga to darshan dunga.

NAmESiyaram ne katar gala se kaha -- laut kar!

paramanand -- jald hi aunga; bachcha!

NAmESiyaram ne bechara-bhav se kaha -- maim bhi apke sath chalunga.

paramanand -- mere sath! tumhare ghar ke log jane denge?

NAmESiyaram -- ghar ke logon ko meri kya parvah hai. iske age NAmESiyaram aur kuchh na kah saka. uske amsu-puriv netron ne uski karun-gatha usse kahim vistar ke sath suna di, jitni uski vani kar sakti thi.
paramanand ne larka ko gala se laga kar kaha-achchha bachcha, teri chah hai to chal. sadhu santon ki sangati ka bhi khushi utha. bhagavan ki chah hogi, to teri chah puri hogi.

dane par mandrata hua patti ant ko dane par gir pada. uske jivan ka ant pinjre men hoga ya vyadh ki chhuri ke taleyah kaun janta hai?
