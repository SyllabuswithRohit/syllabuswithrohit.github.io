doktar-jee nahin, abhee kamaaeeaa hoon. aap is vakt kahaan jaa rahe hain? saadhe baarah ho gae hain.

munshee jee-aap hee ki taraph aa rahaa thaa. jiyaaraam abhee tak ghoom kar nahin kamaaeeaa. aapakee taraph to nahin gayaa tha? doktar sinhaa ne munshee jee ke donon haath pakad lie; aur itanaa kah paae the "bhaaee Sahab, av dhairy se kaam......" ki munshee jee golee khaae hue insaan ki bhaanti zameen par gir pade!!
