dusra parichchhed

babu NAmEUdaybhanulal ka makan bazar men bana hua hai. baramde men sunar ke hathaure, aur kamre men darzi ki suiyam chal rahi haim. samne nim ke niche barhai charapaiyam bana raha hai. khaprail men halavai ke lie bhattha khoda gaya hai. mehmano ke lie alag ek makan thik kiya gaya hai. yah intzam kiya ja raha hai ki harek mehman ke lie ek-ek charapai, ek-ek kursi aur ek-ek mez ho. har tin mehmano ke lie ek-ek kahar rakhne ki tajviz ho rahi hai. abhi barat ane men ek mahine ki der hai, lekin taiyariyam abhi se ho rahi haim. baratiyon ka aisa satkar kiya jay ki kisi ko zaban hilane ka mauqa na mile. ve log bhi yad karen ki kisi ke yaham barat men gae the. ek pura makan bartanon se bhara hua hai. chay ke set haim, nashte ki
tashtariyam, thal, lote, gilas! jo log nitya khat par par hukka pite rahte the, badi tatparta se kam men lage hue haim. apni upayogita ko siddh karne ka aisa achchha avsar unhen phir bahut dino ke vad milega. jaham ek aadmi ko jana hota hai, panch daurte haim. kam kam hota hai, hullar adhik. jara-jara si bat par ghanton tark-vitark hota hai; aur ant men vakil sahab ko akar phaisla karna parta hai. ek kahta hai, yah ghi kharab hai. dusra kahta hai, isse achchha bajar men mil jay, to tang ki rah nikal jaum. tisra kahta hai, ismen to hik ati hai. chautha kahta hai, tumhari nak hi sar gai hai, tum kya jano ghi kise kahte haim. jav se yaham ae ho, ghi milne laga hai; nahin to ghi ke darshan bhi na hote the. is par takrar barh jati hai; aur vakil sahab ko jhagra chukana parta hai.

rat ke nau baje the. NAmEUdaybhanulal andar baithe hue kharch ka takhmina laga rahe the. vah prayah roj hi takhmina lagate the; par roz hi usmen kuchh na kuchh parivartan aur parivarddhan karna parta tha. samne NAmEKalyani bhauhaim sikore hue khadi thi. babu sahav ne badi der ke bad sir uthaya; aur bole--das hajar se kam nahin hota, balki shayad aur barh jay.

NAmEKalyani --das din men panch hajar se das hajar hue. ek mahine men to shayad ek lakh ki naubat a jay!

udaybhanu--kya karum, jag-hamsai bhi to achchhi nahin lagti. koi shikayat hui, to log kahenge nam bade, darshan thore. phir jab vah mujhse dahej ek pai nahin lete, to mera bhi to yah pharz hai ki mehmano ke adar-satkar men koi bat utha na rakkhum!

NAmEKalyani --jab se brahma ne srishti rachi, tab se aj tak kabhi baratiyon ko koi khush nahin kar saka. unhen dosh nikalne aur ninda karne ka koi na koi avsar mil hi jata hai. jise apne ghar sukhi rotiyam bhi muyassar nahin, vah bhi barat men jakar nanashah ban baithta hai. tel khushabudar nahin, sabun take ser ka jane kaham se bator lae, kahar bat nahin sunte, laltene dhuam deti haim, kursiyon men khatmal haim, charapaiyam dhili haim, janvase ki jagah havadar nahin; aisi-aisi hajaron shikayten hoti rahti haim. unhen ap kaham tak rokiega? agar yah mauka na mila, to aur koi aib nikal lie jayange. bhai, yah tel to randiyon ke lagane layak hai, hamen to sada tel chahiye; janab yah sabun nahin bheja hai, apni amiri ki shan dikhai hai; mano hamne sabun dekha hi nahin. ye kahar nahin, yamdut haim; jab dekhie sir par savar; laltene aisi bheji haim ki ankhen chamkane lagti haim. agar das-panch din is roshni men baithna pade, to ankhen phut jayam. janvasa kya hai, abhage ka bhagya hai, jis par charon taraf se jhonke ate rahte haim. maim to phir yahi kahungi ki baratiyon ke nakhron ka soch hi chhod do.

udaybhanu--to akhir tum mujhe kya karne kahti ho?

NAmEKalyani --kah to rahi hum, pakka irada kar lo ki panch hajar se zyada na kharch karenge. ghar men to taka hai nahin; karz hi ka bharosa thahra, to itna qarj kyon le lo ki jindagi men ada na ho. akhir mere aur bachche bhi to haim, unke lie bhi to kuchh chahiye.

udaybhanu--to kya aj maim mara jata hum?

NAmEKalyani --jine-marne ka hal koi nahin janta.

udaybhanu--to tum baithi yahi manaya karti ho?

NAmEKalyani --ismen bigrane ki to koi bat nahin hai. marna ek din sabhi ko hai. koi yaham amar hokar thore hi aya hai. ankhen band kar lene se to hone vali bat na talegi. roj okhon dekhti hum, bap ka maut ho jata hai, uske bachche gali-gali thokren khate phirte haim. aadmi aisa kam hi kyon kare?

udaybhanu ne jal kar kaha-- to ab samajh lum ki mere marne ke din pas a gae, yah tumhari bhavishyavani hai. suhag se striyon ka ji uvte nahin suna tha, aj yah nai bat malum hui! randape men bhi koi sukh hoga hi!!

NAmEKalyani --tum se duniya ki bhi koi bat kahi jati hai, to jahar uglane lagte ho. isilie na ki jante ho ise kahim thikana nahin hai--meri hi rotiyon par padi hui hai; ya aur

kuchh? jaham koi bat kahi, bas sir ho gae; mano maim ghar ki laundi hum, mera sirph roti aur kapde ka nata hai. jitna hi maim dabti hum, tum aur bhi dabate ho. muphtakhore mal uraen, koi mumh na khole: sharab-kabab men rupye luten, koi jaban na hilae. ye sare kante mere bachchon hi ke sir to boe ja rahe hai!

udaybhanu--to maim kya tumhara gulam hum?

NAmEKalyani --to kya maim tumhari laundi hum?

udaybhanu--aise mard aur honge, jo aurton ke isharon par nachte haim.

NAmEKalyani --to aisi striyam bhi aur hongi, jo mardon ki jutiyam saha karti haim.

udaybhanu--maim kama kar lata hum; jaise chahum kharch kar sakta hum. kisi ko bolne ka adhikar nahin hai.

NAmEKalyani --to ap apna ghar sambhalie. aise ghar ko mera dur hi se salam hai, jaham meri koi puchh nahin. ghar men tumhara jitna adhikar hai, utna hi mera bhi. isse jo bhar bhi kam nahin. agar tum apne man ke raja ho, to maim bhi apne man ki rani hum. tumhara ghar tumhen mubarak rahe, mere lie pet ki rotiyon ki kami nahin hai. tumhare bachche haim; maro ya jilao. na ankhon se dekhugi, na dard hogi. ankhen phutim, pir gai!

udaybhanu--kya tum samjhati ho ki tum na sambhalogi, vo

mera ghar hi na sambhlega? maim akele aise-aise das ghar sambhal sakta hum.

NAmEKalyani --kaun! agar aj ke mahinven din mitti men na mil jay, to kahna koi kahti thi.

yah kahte-kahte NAmEKalyani ka chehra tamtama utha. vah jhamak kar uthi; aur kamre se dvar ki or chali. vakil sahab muqdamon men to khub min-mekh nikalte the; lekin khiyon ke svabhav ka unhen kuchh yon hi sa jnyan tha. yahi ek aisi vidya hai, jismen aadmi burha hone par bhi kora rah jata hai. agar ab bhi vah naram par jate; aur NAmEKalyani ka hath pakad kar vitha lete, to shayad vah ruk jati, lekin ap se yah to naho saka; ulte chalte-chalate ek aur charka diya.

bole--maike ka ghamand hoga.

NAmEKalyani ne dvar par ruk kar pati ki or lal-lal netron se dekha; aur baphar kar boli--maike vale meri taqdir ke sathi nahin haim; aur na maim itni nich hum ki unki rotiyon par ja padum.

udaybhanu--tab kaham ja rahi ho?

NAmEKalyani --tum yah puchhne vale kaun hote ho? ishvar ki srishti men asankhya praniyon ke lie jagah hai, kya mere hi lie jagah nahin hai?

yah kah kar NAmEKalyani kamre ke bahar nikal gai. angan men
akar usne ek bar akash ki or dekha, mano taragan ko gavah de rahi hai ki maim is ghar se kitni nirdayta se nikali ja rahi hum. rat ke gyarah baj gae the. ghar men sannata chha gaya tha, donon beton ki charapai usi ke kamre men rahti thi. vah apne kamre men ai, dekha chandrabhanu soya hai. sab se chhota suryabhanu charapai par se uth baitha hai. mam ko dekhte hi vah bola--tum taham daiti ammam? NAmEKalyani dur ho khade-khade boli--kahim to nahin beta, tumhare babu ji ke pas gai thi.

yah kah bachche ne god charhne ke lie donon hath phaila die. NAmEKalyani ab apne ko na rok saki. matri-pyaar ke NAmESudha -pravah se uska santapt dil pariplavit ho gaya. dil ke komal paudhe, jo gussa ke tap se murjha gae the, phir hare ho gae. ankhen sajal ho gai. usne bachche ko god men utha liya; aur use chhati se laga kar boli--tumne mujhe pukar kyon na liya; beta?

surya0--pukalta to ta, tum chhunti hi na tim. batao, ab to tabi na dabogi?

NAmEKalyani --nahin bhaiya, ab kabhi na jaungi.

yah kah kar NAmEKalyani suryabhanu ko lekar charapai par leti. mam ke dil se liptate hi larka nihshanka hokar so gaya. NAmEKalyani ke man men sankalp-vikalp hone lage. pati ki baten yad

ati, to man hota--ghar ko tilanjali dekar chali jaum; lekin bachchon ka mumh dekhti, to vatsalya se chitt gadgad ho jata. bachchon ko kis par chhod kar jaum! mere in lal ko kaun palega, ye kiske hokar rahenge. kaun subah inhen dudh aur halva khilaega; kaun inki nind soega, inki nind jagega? vechare kauri ke tin ho jaenge. nahin pyaro, maim tumhen chhod kar na jaungi. tumhare lie sab kuchh sah lungi. niradar-beizzati, jali-kati, khoti-khadi, ghurki-jhirki sab tumhare lie sahungi.

NAmEKalyani to bachche ko lekar leti; par babu sahab ko nind na ai. unhen chot karne vali baten badi mushkil se bhulti thim. uph! yah mijaj! mano maim hi inki aurat hum! bat mumh se nikalni mushkil hai. ab maim inka gulam hokar rahum. ghar men akeli yah rahen; aur vaki jitne apne-vegane haim, sab nikal die jayen. jala karti haim. manati haim ki yah kisi tarah mare, to maim akeli aram karum. dil ki bat mumh se nikal hi ati hai, chahe koi kitna hi chhipae. kai din se dekh raha hum, aisi jali-kati sunaya karti haim. maike ka ghamand hoga; lekin vaham koi vat bhi na puchhega. abhi savaavabhgat karte haim. jabajakar sir par jayangi, to ate-dal ka bhav malum ho jayga. roti hui aengi. vah re ghamand, sochti haim--maim hi yah ghar-parivar chalati hum. abhi char din ko kahim chala jaum, to malum ho. tav devu kya karti haim? bas, char din hi men to malum ho jayga. sari shekhi kirkiri.

ho jaygi. ek bar inka ghamand tod hi dum, jara vaidhavya ka maja bhi chakha dum. na jane inki himmat kaise parti hai ki mujhe yon kosne lagti haim? malum hota hai pyaar inhen chhu nahin gaya ya samjhati haim yah ghar se itna chimta hua hai; ki ise chahe jitna kosum, talne ka nam na lega. yahi bat hai; par yaham samsar se chiptane vale jiv nahin haim. jahannum men jay vah ghar; jaham aise praniyon se pala pade. ghar hai ya narak! aadmi bahar se thaka-manda ata hai, to ghar men use aram milta hai. yaham aram ke badle kosne sunne parte haim. meri maut ke lie vrat rakkhe jate haim. yah hai pachis baras ke dampatya jivan ka ant! bas chal hi dum. jab dekh lunga ki inka sara ghamand dhul men mil gaya; aur mizaj thandha ho gaya, to laut aunga. char-panch din kafi honge. le, tum bhi kya yad karogi ki kisi se pala pada tha.

par yah kaun janta tha ki yah sari lila vidhi ke hathon rachi ja rahi hai. jivan-rangashala ka vah nirday sutradhar kisi agamya, gupt sthan par baitha hua apni jatil bedard-krira dikha raha hai? yah kaun janta tha ki nakal asal hone ja rahi hai, abhinay satya ka rup grahan karne vala hai?

rat ne indu ko parast karke apna samrajya sthapit kar liya tha. uski paishachik sena ne prakriti par atanka jama rakkha tha! sadvavrittiyam mumh chhipae padi thim; aur kuvrittiyam vijay-garva se ithlati phirti thim. van men vanya-jantu shikar ki khoj men vichar rahe the; aur nagron men nar-pishach galiyon men mandlate phirte the.

tha. udaybhanu ne us muqdame men sarkar ki or se pairvi ki thi; aur is badmash ko tin sal ki saja dilai thi. tabhi se vah inke khun ka pyasa ho raha tha. kal hi vah chhut kar aya tha. aj daivat babu sahab akele rat ko dikhai die, to usne socha yah inse damv chukane ka achchha mauka hai. aisa mauka shayad hi phir kabhi mile. turant pichhe ho liya; aur var karne ke ghat hi men tha ki babu sahab ne jebi lalten jalai. badmash zara thithak kar bola--kyon babu ji, pahachante ho na? maim hum matai.
