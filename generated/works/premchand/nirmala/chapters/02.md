# Nirmala — Adhyay 2

**Munshi Premchand**

doosaraa parichchhed

baaboo udayabhaanulaal ka makaan baajaar mein banaa huaa hai. baraamade mein sunaar ke hathaude, aur kamare mein darjee ki suiyaan chal rahee hain. saamane neem ke neeche badhaee chaarapaaiyaan banaa rahaa hai. khaparail mein halavaaee ke lie bhatthaa khodaa gayaa hai. mehamaanon ke lie alag ek makaan theek kiyaa gayaa hai. yah intezam kiyaa jaa rahaa hai ki harek mehamaan ke lie ek-ek chaarapaaee, ek-ek kursee aur ek-ek mej ho. har teen mehamaanon ke lie ek-ek kahaar rakhane ki tajaveej ho rahee hai. abhee baaraat aane mein ek maheene ki der hai, lekin taiyaariyaan abhee se ho rahee hain. baaraatiyon ka aisaa satkaar kiyaa jaay ki kisee ko jabaan hilaane ka maukaa n mile. ve log bhi yaad karen ki kisee ke yahan baaraat mein gae the. ek pooraa makaan bartanon se bharaa huaa hai. chaay ke set hain, naashte ki tashtariyaan, thaal, lote, gilaas! jo log nity khaat par pad hukkaa peete rahate the, badee tatparataa se kaam mein lage hue hain. apanee upayogitaa ko siddh karane ka aisaa achchha avasar unhen phir bahut dino ke vaad milegaa. jahaan ek aadmi ko jaanaa hotaa hai, paanch daudate hain. kaam kam hotaa hai, hullad adhika. jaraa-jaraa see baat par ghanton tark-vitark hotaa hai; aur ant mein vakeel Sahab ko aakar pakkaa faisla karanaa padataa hai. ek kahataa hai, yah ghee kharaab hai. doosaraa kahataa hai, isase achchha baajaar mein mil jaay, to taangg ki raah nikal jaaoon. teesaraa kahataa hai, isamen to heek aatee hai. chauthaa kahataa hai, tumhaaree naak hee sad gaee hai, tum kyaa jaano ghee kise kahate hain. jav se yahan aae ho, ghee milane lagaa hai; nahin to ghee ke darshan bhi n hote the. is par takaraar badh jaatee hai; aur vakeel Sahab ko jhagadaa chukaanaa padataa hai.

raat ke nau baje the. udayabhaanulaal andar baithe hue kharch ka takhameenaa lagaa rahe the. vah praayah roj hee takhameenaa lagaate the; par roj hee usamen kuchh n kuchh parivartan aur parivarddhan karanaa padataa thaa. saamane kalyaanee bhauhain sikode hue khadee thee. baaboo saahav ne badee der ke baad sir uthaayaa; aur bole--das hajaar se kam nahin hotaa, balki shaayad aur badh jaaya.

kalyaanee--das din mein paanch hajaar se das hajaar hue. ek maheene mein to shaayad ek laakh ki naubat aa jaay! udayabhaanu--kyaa karoon, jag-hansaaee bhi to achchhi nahin lagatee. koee shikaayat huee, to log kahengge naam bade, darshan thode. phir jab vah mujhase dahej ek paaee nahin lete, to meraa bhi to yah karttavy hai ki mehamaanon ke aadar-satkaar mein koee baat uthaa n rakkhoon!

kalyaanee--jab se brahmaa ne srishti rachee, tab se aaj tak kabhee baaraatiyon ko koee khush nahin kar sakaa. unhen dosh nikaalane aur nindaa karane ka koee n koee avasar mil hee jaataa hai. jise apane ghar sookhee rotiyaan bhi muyassar nahin, vah bhi baaraat mein jaakar naanaashaah ban baithataa hai. tel khushaboodaar nahin, saabun take ser ka jaane kahaan se bator laae, kahaar baat nahin sunate, laalatenen dhuaan detee hain, kursiyon mein khatamal hain, chaarapaaiyaan dheelee hain, janavaase ki jagah havaadaar nahin; aisee-aisee hajaaron shikaayaten hotee rahatee hain. unhen aap kahaan tak rokiegaa? agar yah maukaa n milaa, to aur koee aib nikaal lie jaayangge. bhaee, yah tel to randiyon ke lagaane laayak hai, hamen to saadaa tel chahiye; janaab yah saabun nahin bhejaa hai, apanee ameeree ki shaan dikhaaee hai; maano hamane saabun dekhaa hee naheen. ye kahaar nahin, yamadoot hain; jab dekhie sir par savaar; laalatenen aisee bhejee hain ki aankhen chamakane lagatee hain. agar das-paanch din is roshanee mein baithanaa pade, to aankhen phoot jaayan. janavaasaa kyaa hai, abhaage ka kismat hai, jis par chaaron taraph se jhongke aate rahate hain. main to phir yahee kahoonggee ki baaraatiyon ke nakharon ka soch hee chhod do. udayabhaanu--to aakhir tum mujhe kyaa karane kahatee ho?

kalyaanee--kah to rahee hoon, pakkaa iraadaa kar lo ki paanch hajaar se adhik n kharch karengge. ghar mein to takaa hai nahin; karz hee ka bharosaa thaharaa, to itanaa karj kyon le lo ki jindagee mein adaa n ho. aakhir mere aur bachche bhi to hain, unake lie bhi to kuchh chaahie.

udayabhaanu--to kyaa aaj main maraa jaataa hoon?

kalyaanee--jeene-marane ka haal koee nahin jaanataa.

udayabhaanu--to tum baithee yahee manaayaa karatee ho?

kalyaanee--isamen bigadane ki to koee baat nahin hai. maranaa ek din sabhee ko hai. koee yahan amar hokar thode hee kamaaeeaa hai. aankhen band kar lene se to hone vaalee baat n talegee. roj okhon dekhatee hoon, baap ka dehaant ho jaataa hai, usake bachche galee-galee thokaren khaate phirate hain. aadmi aisaa kaam hee kyon kare?

udayabhaanu ne jal kar kahaa-- to ab samajh loon ki mere marane ke din nikat aa gae, yah tumhaaree bhavishyavaanee hai. suhaag se striyon ka jee oovate nahin sunaa tha, aaj yah naee baat maaloom huee! randaape mein bhi koee sukh hogaa hee!!

kalyaanee--tum se duniya ki bhi koee baat kahee jaatee hai, to jahar ugalane lagate ho. iseelie n ki jaanate ho ise kaheen thikaanaa nahin hai--meree hee rotiyon par padee huee hai; ya aur kuchh? jahaan koee baat kahee, bas sir ho gae; maano main ghar ki laundee hoon, meraa keval roti aur kapde ka naataa hai. jitanaa hee main dabatee hoon, tum aur bhi dabaate ho. muphtakhore maal udaaen, koee munh n khole: sharaab-kabaab mein rupaye luten, koee jabaan n hilaae. ye saare kaante mere bachchon hee ke sir to boe jaa rahe hai!

udayabhaanu--to main kyaa tumhaaraa gulaam hoon?

kalyaanee--to kyaa main tumhaaree laundee hoon?

udayabhaanu--aise mard aur hongge, jo auraton ke ishaaron par naachate hain.

kalyaanee--to aisee striyaan bhi aur honggee, jo mardon ki jootiyaan sahaa karatee hain.

udayabhaanu--main kamaa kar laataa hoon; jaise chaahoon kharch kar sakataa hoon. kisee ko bolane ka hak nahin hai.

kalyaanee--to aap apanaa ghar sambhaalie. aise ghar ko meraa door hee se salaam hai, jahaan meree koee poochh naheen. ghar mein tumhaaraa jitanaa hak hai, utanaa hee meraa bhee. isase jo bhar bhi kam naheen. agar tum apane man ke raajaa ho, to main bhi apane man ki raanee hoon. tumhaaraa ghar tumhe mubaarak rahe, mere lie pet ki rotiyon ki kamee nahin hai. tumhaare bachche hain; maaro ya jilaao. n aangkhon se dekhoogee, n dard hogee. aankhen phooteen, peer gaee!

udayabhaanu--kyaa tum samajhatee ho ki tum n sambhaalogee, vo meraa ghar hee n sambhalegaa? main akele aise-aise das ghar sambhaal sakataa hoon.

kalyaanee--kaun! agar aaj ke maheenaven din mittee mein n mil jaay, to kahanaa koee kahatee thee.

yah kahate-kahate kalyaanee ka cheharaa tamatamaa uthaa. vah jhamak kar uthee; aur kamare se dvaar ki or chalee. vakeel Sahab mukadamon mein to khoob meen-mekh nikaalate the; lekin khiyon ke svabhaav ka unhen kuchh yon hee saa gyaan thaa. yahee ek aisee vidyaa hai, jisamen aadmi boodhaa hone par bhi koraa rah jaataa hai. agar ab bhi vah naram pad jaate; aur kalyaanee ka haath pakad kar vithaa lete, to shaayad vah ruk jaatee, lekin aap se yah to naho sakaa; ulate chalate-chalaate ek aur charkaa diyaa.

bole--maike ka ghamand hogaa.

kalyaanee ne dvaar par ruk kar shauhar ki or laal-laal aangkhenon se dekhaa; aur baphar kar bolee--maike vaale meree takadeer ke saathee nahin hain; aur n main itanee neech hoon ki unakee rotiyon par jaa padoon.

udayabhaanu--tab kahaan jaa rahee ho?

kalyaanee--tum yah poochhane vaale kaun hote ho? Bhagwan ki srishti mein asangkhy praaniyon ke lie jagah hai, kyaa mere hee lie jagah nahin hai?

yah kah kar kalyaanee kamare ke baahar nikal gaee. aanggan mein aakar usane ek baar aakaash ki or dekhaa, maano taaraagan ko saakshee de rahee hai ki main is ghar se kitanee nirdayataa se nikaalee jaa rahee hoon. raat ke gyaarah baj gae the. ghar mein sannaataa chhaa gayaa tha, donon beton ki chaarapaaee usee ke kamare mein rahatee thee. vah apane kamare mein aaee, dekhaa chandrabhaanu soyaa hai. sab se chhotaa sooryabhaanu chaarapaaee par se uth baithaa hai. maataa ko dekhate hee vah bolaa--tum tahaan daeetee ammaan? kalyaanee door ho khade-khade bolee--kaheen to nahin betaa, tumhaare baaboo jee ke paas gaee thee.

soorya0--tum talee daee, muje atele dar ladataa taa. tum tyon talee duee tee; bataayo?

yah kah bachche ne god chadhane ke lie donon haath phailaa die. kalyaanee ab apane ko n rok sakee. maatri-pyaar ke sudhaa-pravaah se usakaa santapt dil pariplaavit ho gayaa. dil ke komal paudhe, jo krodh ke taap se murajhaa gae the, phir hare ho gae. aankhen sajal ho gaee. usane bachche ko god mein uthaa liyaa; aur use chhaatee se lagaa kar bolee--tumane mujhe pukaar kyon n liyaa; betaa?

soorya0--pukaalataa to taa, tum chhunatee hee n teen. bataao, ab to tabee n daabogee?

kalyaanee--nahin bhaiyaa, ab kabhee n jaaoonggee.

yah kah kar kalyaanee sooryabhaanu ko lekar chaarapaaee par letee. maan ke dil se lipatate hee bachcha nihshanggk hokar so gayaa. kalyaanee ke man mein sanggkalp-vikalp hone lage. shauhar ki baaten yaad aatee, to man hotaa--ghar ko tilaanyjalee dekar chalee jaaoon; lekin bachchon ka munh dekhatee, to vaatsaly se chitt gadgad ho jaataa. bachchon ko kis par chhod kar jaaoon! mere in laal ko kaun paalegaa, ye kisake hokar rahengge. kaun subah inhen doodh aur halavaa khilaaegaa; kaun inakee neend soegaa, inakee neend jaagegaa? vechaare kaudee ke teen ho jaaengge. nahin pyaaro, main tumhe chhod kar n jaaoonggee. tumhaare lie sab kuchh sah loonggee. niraadar-beijjatee, jalee-katee, khotee-kharee, ghudakee-jhidakee sab tumhaare lie sahoonggee.

kalyaanee to bachche ko lekar letee; par baaboo Sahab ko neend n aaee. unhen chot karane vaalee baaten badee mushkil se bhoolatee theen. uph! yah mijaaj! maano main hee inakee aurat hoon! baat munh se nikaalanee mushkil hai. ab main inakaa gulaam hokar rahoon. ghar mein akelee yah rahen; aur vaakee jitane apane-vegaane hain, sab nikaal die jaayen. jalaa karatee hain. manaatee hain ki yah kisee tarah mare, to main akelee aaraam karoon. dil ki baat munh se nikal hee aatee hai, chaahe koee kitanaa hee chhipaae. kaee din se dekh rahaa hoon, aisee jalee-katee sunaayaa karatee hain. maike ka ghamand hogaa; lekin wahan koee vaat bhi n poochhegaa. abhee savaaavabhagat karate hain. jabajaakar sir pad jaayanggee, to aate-daal ka bhaav maaloom ho jaayagaa. rotee huee aaenggee. vaah re ghamand, sochatee hain--main hee yah ghar-baar chalaatee hoon. abhee chaar din ko kaheen chalaa jaaoon, to maaloom ho. tav devoo kyaa karatee hain? bas, chaar din hee mein to maaloom ho jaayagaa. saaree shekhee kirakiree.

yahee sochate hue baaboo Sahab uthe, reshamee chaadar gale mein daalee, kuchh rupaye lie, apanaa kaard nikaal kar ek doosare kurte ke jeb mein rakkhaa, chhadee uthaaee; aur chupake se baahar nikale. sab naukar neend mein mast the. kuttaa aahat paakar chaungk padaa; aur unake saath ho liyaa.

vaaboo udayabhaanulaal lapake hue gangggaa ki or chale jaa rahe the. unhonne apanaa kurtaa ghaat ke kinaare rakh kar paanch din ke lie mirjaapoor chale jaane ka pakkaa faisla kiyaa thaa. unake kapde dekh kar logon ko unake doob jaane ka bharosaa ho jaayagaa. kaard kurte ki jeb mein thaa. pataa lagane mein koee dikkat n ho sakatee thee. dam ke dam mein saare shahar mein khavar mashahoor ho jaayagee. aath bajate-bajate to mere dvaar par saaraa shahar jamaa ho jaayagaa, tab dekhoon devee jee kyaa karatee hain?

yahee sochate hue baaboo Sahab galiyon mein chale jaa rahe the, sahasaa unhen apane peechhe kisee doosare aadmi ke aane ki aahat milee; samajhe koee hogaa. aage badhe; lekin jis galee mein vah mudate, usee taraph vah aadmi bhi mudataa thaa. tab to baaboo Sahab ko aashanggkaa huee ki yah aadmi meraa peechhaa kar rahaa hai. aisaa aabhaas huaa ki isakee neeyat saaph nahin hai. unhonne turant jebee laalaten nikaalee; aur usake prakaash mein us aadmi ko dekhaa. ek balisht insaan kandhe par laathee rakkhe chalaa aataa thaa. baaboo Sahab use dekhate hee chaungk pade. yah shahar ka chhataa huaa badamaash thaa. teen saal pahale us par daake ka abhiyog chalaa thaa. udayabhaanu ne us mukadame mein sarakaar ki or se pairavee ki thi; aur is badamaash ko teen saal ki sajaa dilaaee thee. tabhee se vah inake khoon ka pyaasaa ho rahaa thaa. kal hee vah chhoot kar kamaaeeaa thaa. aaj daivaat baaboo Sahab akele raat ko dikhaaee die, to usane sochaa yah inase daanv chukaane ka achchha maukaa hai. aisaa maukaa shaayad hee phir kabhee mile. turant peechhe ho liyaa; aur vaar karane ke ghaat hee mein tha ki baaboo Sahab ne jebee laalaten jalaaee. badamaash jaraa thithak kar bolaa--kyon baaboo jee, pahachaanate ho n? main hoon mataee.

baaboo Sahab ne dapat kar kahaa--tum mere peechhe-peechhe kyon aa rahe ho?

mataee--kyon, kisee ko raastaa chalane ki manaahee hai. yah galee tumhaare baap ki hai?

baaboo Sahab javaanee mein kushtee lade the, ab bhi hrisht-pusht aadmi the. dil ke bhi kachche n the. chhadee sambhaal kar bole--abhee shaayad man nahin bharaa. ab ki saat saal ko jaaoge.

mataee--main saat saal ko jaaoon ya chaudah saal ko; par tumhe jeetaa n chhodanggaa. haan, agar tum mere pairon par gir kar kasam khaao ki ab kisee ki sajaa n karaaoonggaa, to chhod doon. bolo manyjoor hai?

udayabhaanu--teree shaamat to nahin aaee hai?

mataee--shaamat meree nahin aaee, tumhaaree aaee hai. bolo khaate! ho kasam--ek!

udayabhaanu--tum hatate ho ki main puleesamain ko bulaaoon? mataee-do!

udayabhaanu-(garaj kar ) hat jaa badamaash saamane se.

mataee-teen!

munh se 'teen' ka shabd nikalate hee baaboo Sahab ke sir par laathee ka aisaa tulaa huaa haath padaa ki vah achet hokar zameen par gir pade. munh se keval itanaahee nikalaa-haay! maar daalaa! mataee ne sameep aakar dekhaa, to sir phat gayaa tha; aur khoon ki dhaar nikal rahee thee. naadee ka kaheen pataa n thaa. samajhagayaa ki kaam tamaam ho gayaa. usane kalaaee se sone ki ghadee khol lee, kurte se sone ke batan nikaal lie, unggalee se anggoothee utaaree aur apanee raah chalaa gayaa; maano kuchh huaa hee nahin. haan, itanee taras ki ki laash raaste se ghaseet kar kinaare daal dee. haay! bechaare ghar se kyaa soch kar chale the; aur kyaa ho gayaa, zindagi! tujhase jyaadah asaar bhi duniya mein koee vastu hai? kyaa vah us deepak ki bhaanti hee palabhangggur nahin hai, jo havaa ke ek jhongke se bujh jaataa hai? paani ke us bulabule ko dekhate ho; lekin use tootate bhi kuchh der lagatee hai; zindagi mein utanaa saar bhi naheen. saans ka bharosaa hee kyaa? aur isee nashvarataa par ham abhilaashaaon ke kitane bahut badaa bhavan banaate hain! nahin jaanate neeche jaane vaalee saans oopar aaegee ya nahin; par sochate itanee door ki hain, maano ham amar hain.
