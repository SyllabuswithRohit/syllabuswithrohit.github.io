# Nirmala — Adhyay 2

**Munshi Premchand**

doosaraa parichchheda

baaboo udayabhaanulaala kaa makaana baazaara men bana hua hai| baraamade men sunaara ke hathau.de, aur kamare men darzee kee suiyaan chala rahi hain| saamane neema ke neeche ba.dhaee chaarapaaiyaan bana raha hai| khaparaila men halavaaee ke lie bhatthaa khodaa gaya hai| mehamaanon ke lie alaga ek makaana theeka kiya gaya hai| yaha prabandha kiya jaa raha hai ki hareka mehamaana ke lie ek-ek chaarapaaee, ek-ek kursee aur ek-ek meza ho| hara teena mehamaanon ke lie ek-ek kahaara rakhane kee tajaveeza ho rahi hai| abhee baaraata aane men ek maheene kee dera hai, lekin taiyaariyaan abhee se ho rahi hain| baaraatiyon kaa aisaa satkaara kiya jaaya ki kisee ko zabaana hilaane kaa mauqaa na mile| ve log bhee yaada karen ki kisee ke yahaan baaraata men gae the| ek pooraa makaana bartanon se bharaa hua hai| chaaya ke seta hain, naashte kee tashtariyaan, thaala, lote, gilaasa! jo log nitya khaata par pa.da hukkaa peete rahate the, ba.dee tatparata se kaama men lage hue hain| apanee upayogitaa ko siddha karane kaa aisaa achchha avasara unhen phir bahut dino ke vaada milegaa| jahaan ek aadmi ko jana hota hai, paancha dau.date hain| kaama kama hota hai, hulla.da adhika| jaraa-jaraa see baat par ghanton tarka-vitarka hota hai; aur anta men vakeela saahaba ko aakara nishchaya karana pa.data hai| ek kahata hai, yaha ghee kharaaba hai| doosaraa kahata hai, isase achchha baajaara men mila jaaya, to taanga kee raaha nikala jaaoon| teesaraa kahata hai, isamen to heeka aati hai| chauthaa kahata hai, tumhaaree naaka hee sa.da gaee hai, tuma kyaa jaano ghee kise kehte hain| java se yahaan aae ho, ghee milane lagaa hai; naheen to ghee ke darshana bhee na hote the| is par takaraara ba.dha jati hai; aur vakeela saahaba ko jhaga.daa chukaanaa pa.data hai|

raat ke nau baje the| udayabhaanulaala andara baithe hue kharcha kaa takhameenaa lagaa rahe the| vaha praayah roja hee takhameenaa lagaate the; par roza hee usamen kuchha na kuchha parivartana aur parivarddhana karana pa.data tha| saamane kalyaanee bhauhain siko.de hue kha.dee thi| baaboo saahava ne ba.dee dera ke baada sira uthaaya; aur bole--dasa hajaara se kama naheen hota, balki shaayada aur ba.dha jaaya|

kalyaanee--dasa din men paancha hajaara se dasa hajaara hue| ek maheene men to shaayada ek laakha kee naubata aa jaaya! udayabhaanu--kyaa karoon, jaga-hansaaee bhee to achchhi naheen lagatee| koee shikaayata hui, to log kahenge naama ba.de, darshana tho.de| phir jab vaha mujhase daheja ek paaee naheen lete, to meraa bhee to yaha karttavya hai ki mehamaanon ke aadara-satkaara men koee baat uthaa na rakkhoon!

kalyaanee--jab se brahmaa ne srishti rachee, tab se aaja taka kabhee baaraatiyon ko koee prasanna naheen kar sakaa| unhen dosha nikaalane aur nindaa karane kaa koee na koee avasara mila hee jata hai| jise apane ghar sookhee rotiyaan bhee muyassara naheen, vaha bhee baaraata men jaakara naanaashaaha bana baithata hai| tela khushaboodaara naheen, saabuna take sera kaa jaane kahaan se batora laae, kahaara baat naheen sunate, laalatenen dhuaan detee hain, kursiyon men khatamala hain,chaarapaaiyaan dheelee hain, janavaase kee jagaha havaadaara naheen; aisee-aisee hajaaron shikaayaten hoti rahatee hain| unhen aapa kahaan taka rokiega? agar yaha maukaa na mila, to aur koee aiba nikaala lie jaayange| bhaee, yaha tela to randiyon ke lagaane laayaka hai, hamen to saadaa tela chaahie; janaaba yaha saabuna naheen bhejaa hai, apanee ameeree kee shaana dikhaaee hai; maano hamane saabuna dekha hee naheen| ye kahaara naheen, yamadoota hain; jab dekhie sira par savaara; laalatenen aisee bhejee hain ki aankhen chamakane lagatee hain| agar dasa-paancha din is roshanee men baithana pa.de, to aankhen phoota jaayan| janavaasaa kyaa hai, abhaage kaa bhaagya hai, jisa par chaaron tarafa se jhonke aate rahate hain| main to phir yahee kahoongee ki baaraatiyon ke nakharon kaa vichaara hee chho.da do| udayabhaanu--to aakhira tuma mujhe kyaa karane kehti ho?

kalyaanee--kaha to rahi hoon, pakkaa iraadaa kar lo ki paancha hajaara se adhika na kharcha karenge| ghar men to takaa hai naheen; karja hee kaa bharosaa thahara, to itana qarja kyon le lo ki zindagi men adaa na ho| aakhira mere aur bachche bhee to hain, unake lie bhee to kuchha chaahie|

udayabhaanu--to kyaa aaja main maraa jata hoon?

kalyaanee--jeene-marane kaa haala koee naheen jaanata|

udayabhaanu--to tuma baithee yahee manaayaa karti ho?

kalyaanee--isamen biga.dane kee to koee baat naheen hai| marana ek din sabhee ko hai| koee yahaan amara hokara tho.de hee aaya hai| aankhen banda kar lene se to hone vaalee baat na talegee| roja okhon dekhatee hoon, baapa kaa dehaanta ho jata hai, usake bachche galee-galee thokaren khaate phirate hain| aadmi aisaa kaama hee kyon kare?

udayabhaanu ne jala kar kaha-- to ab samajha loon ki mere marane ke din nikata aa gae, yaha tumhaaree bhavishyavaanee hai| suhaaga se striyon kaa jee oovate naheen sunaa tha, aaja yaha naee baat maalooma hui! randaape men bhee koee sukha hogaa hee!!

kalyaanee--tuma se duniya kee bhee koee baat kahee jati hai, to jahara ugalane lagate ho| iseelie na ki jaanate ho ise kaheen thikaanaa naheen hai--meree hee rotiyon par pa.dee hui hai; yaa aur kuchha? jahaan koee baat kahee, basa sira ho gae; maano main ghar kee laundee hoon, meraa kevala rotee aur kapa.de kaa naataa hai| jitana hee main dabatee hoon, tuma aur bhee dabaate ho| muphtaKore maala u.daaen, koee munha na khole: sharaaba-kabaaba men rupaye luten, koee jabaana na hilaae| ye saare kaante mere bachchon hee ke sira to boe jaa rahe hai!

udayabhaanu--to main kyaa tumhaaraa gulaama hoon?

kalyaanee--to kyaa main tumhaaree laundee hoon?

udayabhaanu--aise marda aur honge, jo auraton ke ishaaron par naachate hain|

kalyaanee--to aisee striyaan bhee aur hongee, jo mardon kee jootiyaan sahaa karti hain|

udayabhaanu--main kamaa kar laataa hoon; jaise chaahoon kharcha kar sakata hoon| kisee ko bolane kaa adhikaara naheen hai|

kalyaanee--to aapa apana ghar sanbhaalie| aise ghar ko meraa doora hee se salaama hai, jahaan meree koee poochha naheen| ghar men tumhaaraa jitana adhikaara hai, utana hee meraa bhee| isase jo bhara bhee kama naheen| agar tuma apane mann ke raajaa ho, to main bhee apane mann kee raanee hoon| tumhaaraa ghar tumhen mubaaraka rahe, mere lie peta kee rotiyon kee kamee naheen hai | tumhaare bachche hain; maaro yaa jilaao| na aankhon se dekhoogee, na pee.daa hogee| aankhen phooteen, peera gaee!

udayabhaanu--kyaa tuma samajhatee ho ki tuma na sanbhaalogee, vo meraa ghar hee na sanbhalega? main akele aise-aise dasa ghar sanbhaala sakata hoon|

kalyaanee--kaun! agar aaja ke maheenaven din mittee men na mila jaaya, to kahana koee kehti thi|

yaha kehte-kehte kalyaanee kaa cheharaa tamatamaa uthaa| vaha jhamaka kar uthee; aur kamare se dvaara kee ora chalee| vakeela saahaba muqadamon men to khooba meena-mekha nikaalate the;lekin khiyon ke svabhaava kaa unhen kuchha yon hee saa jnaana tha| yahee ek aisee vidyaa hai, jisamen aadmi boo.dhaa hone par bhee koraa raha jata hai| agar ab bhee vaha narama pa.da jaate; aur kalyaanee kaa haath paka.da kar vithaa lete, to shaayada vaha ruka jati, lekin aapa se yaha to naho saka; ulate chalate-chalaate ek aur charkaa diya|

bole--maike kaa ghamanda hogaa|

kalyaanee ne dvaara par ruka kar pati kee ora laala-laala netron se dekha; aur baphara kar boli--maike vaale meree taqadeera ke saathee naheen hain; aur na main itanee neecha hoon ki unakee rotiyon par jaa padoon|

udayabhaanu--tab kahaan jaa rahi ho?

kalyaanee--tuma yaha poochhane vaale kaun hote ho? eeshvara kee srishti men asankhya praaniyon ke lie jagaha hai, kyaa mere hee lie jagaha naheen hai?

yaha kaha kar kalyaanee kamare ke baahara nikala gaee| aangana men aakara usane ek baara aakaasha kee ora dekha, maano taaraagana ko saakshee de rahi hai ki main is ghar se kitanee nirdayata se nikaalee jaa rahi hoon| raat ke gyaaraha baja gae the| ghar men sannaataa chhaa gaya tha, donon beton kee chaarapaaee usee ke kamare men rahatee thi| vaha apane kamare men aaee, dekha chandrabhaanu soyaa hai| sab se chhota sooryabhaanu chaarapaaee par se utha baithaa hai| maataa ko dekhate hee vaha bola--tuma tahaan daeetee ammaan? kalyaanee doora ho kha.de-kha.de boli--kaheen to naheen beta, tumhaare baaboo jee ke paasa gaee thi|

soorya0--tuma talee daee, muje atele dara ladata taa| tuma tyon talee duee tee; bataayo?

yaha kaha bachche ne goda cha.dhane ke lie donon haath phailaa die| kalyaanee ab apane ko na roka sakee| maatri-sneha ke sudhaa-pravaaha se usakaa santapta hridaya pariplaavita ho gaya| hridaya ke komala paudhe, jo krodha ke taapa se murajhaa gae the, phir hare ho gae| aankhen sajala ho gaee| usane bachche ko goda men uthaa liya; aur use chhaatee se lagaa kar boli--tumane mujhe pukaara kyon na liya; beta?

soorya0--pukaalata to ta, tuma chhunatee hee na teen| bataao, ab to tabee na daabogee?

kalyaanee--naheen bhaiya, ab kabhee na jaaoongee|

yaha kaha kar kalyaanee sooryabhaanu ko lekara chaarapaaee par letee| maan ke hridaya se lipatate hee baalaka nihshanka hokara so gaya| kalyaanee ke mann men sankalpa-vikalpa hone lage| pati kee baaten yaada aati, to mann hota--ghar ko tilaanjalee dekara chalee jaaoon; lekin bachchon kaa munha dekhatee, to vaatsalya se chitta gadgad ho jata| bachchon ko kisa par chho.da kar jaaoon! mere ina laala ko kaun paalega, ye kisake hokara rahenge| kaun praatahkaala inhen doodha aur halavaa khilaaega; kaun inakee neenda soega, inakee neenda jaagega? vechaare kau.dee ke teena ho jaaenge| naheen pyaaro, main tumhen chho.da kar na jaaoongee| tumhaare lie sab kuchha saha loongee| niraadara-apamaana, jalee-katee, khotee-kharee, ghu.dakee-jhi.dakee sab tumhaare lie sahoongee|

kalyaanee to bachche ko lekara letee; par baaboo saahaba ko neenda na aaee| unhen chota karane vaalee baaten ba.dee mushkila se bhoolatee theen| upha! yaha mijaaja! maano main hee inakee stree hoon! baat munha se nikaalanee mushkila hai| ab main inakaa gulaama hokara rahoon| ghar men akelee yaha rahen; aur vaakee jitane apane-vegaane hain, sab nikaala die jayen| jalaa karti hain| manaatee hain ki yaha kisee tarah mare, to main akelee aaraama karoon| dil kee baat munha se nikala hee aati hai, chaahe koee kitana hee chhipaae| kaee din se dekha raha hoon, aisee jalee-katee sunaayaa karti hain| maike kaa ghamanda hoga; lekin vahaan koee vaata bhee na poochhegaa| abhee savaaavabhagata karte hain| jabajaakara sira pa.da jaayangee, to aate-daala kaa bhaava maalooma ho jaayagaa| rotee hui aaengee| vaaha re ghamanda,sochatee hain--main hee yaha grihasthee chalaatee hoon| abhee chaara din ko kaheen chalaa jaaoon, to maalooma ho| tava devoo kyaa karti hain? basa, chaara din hee men to maalooma ho jaayagaa| saaree shekhee kirakiree|

yahee sochate hue baaboo saahaba uthe, reshamee chaadara gale men daalee, kuchha rupaye lie, apana kaarda nikaala kar ek doosare kurte ke jeba men rakkha, chha.dee uthaaee; aur chupake se baahara nikale| sab naukara neenda men masta the| kuttaa aahata paakara chaunka pa.da; aur unake saatha ho liya|

vaaboo udayabhaanulaala lapake hue gangaa kee ora chale jaa rahe the| unhonne apana kurtaa ghaata ke kinaare rakha kar paancha din ke lie mirjaapoora chale jaane kaa nishchaya kiya tha| unake kapa.de dekha kar logon ko unake dooba jaane kaa vishvaasa ho jaayagaa| kaarda kurte kee jeba men tha| pata lagane men koee dikkata na ho sakatee thi| dama ke dama men saare shehar men khavara mashahoora ho jaayagee| aatha bajate-bajate to mere dvaara par saaraa shehar jamaa ho jaayaga, tab dekhoon devee jee kyaa karti hain?

yahee sochate hue baaboo saahaba galiyon men chale jaa rahe the, sahasaa unhen apane peechhe kisee doosare aadmi ke aane kee aahata milee; samajhe koee hogaa| aage ba.dhe; lekin jisa galee men vaha mu.date, usee tarapha vaha aadmi bhee mu.data tha| tab to baaboo saahaba ko aashankaa hui ki yaha aadmi meraa peechhaa kar raha hai| aisaa aabhaasa hua ki isakee neeyata saapha naheen hai| unhonne turanta jebee laalatena nikaalee; aur usake prakaasha men us aadmi ko dekha| ek balishta manushya kandhe par laathee rakkhe chalaa aata tha| baaboo saahaba use dekhate hee chaunka pa.de| yaha shehar kaa chhata hua badamaasha tha| teena saala pahale us par daake kaa abhiyoga chalaa tha| udayabhaanu ne us muqadame men sarkar kee ora se pairavee kee thi; aur is badamaasha ko teena saala kee sajaa dilaaee thi| tabhee se vaha inake khoona kaa pyaasaa ho raha tha| kala hee vaha chhoota kar aaya tha| aaja daivaat baaboo saahaba akele raat ko dikhaaee die, to usane sochaa yaha inase daanva chukaane kaa achchha maukaa hai| aisaa maukaa shaayada hee phir kabhee mile| turanta peechhe ho liya; aur vaara karane ke ghaata hee men tha ki baaboo saahaba ne jebee laalatena jalaaee| badamaasha zaraa thithaka kar bola--kyon baaboo jee, pahachaanate ho na? main hoon mataee|

baaboo saahaba ne dapata kar kaha--tuma mere peechhe-peechhe kyon aa rahe ho?

mataee--kyon, kisee ko raastaa chalane kee manaahee hai| yaha galee tumhaare baapa kee hai?

baaboo saahaba javaanee men kushtee la.de the, ab bhee hrishta-pushta aadmi the| dil ke bhee kachche na the| chha.dee sanbhaala kar bole--abhee shaayada mann naheen bharaa| ab kee saata saala ko jaaoge|

mataee--main saata saala ko jaaoon yaa chaudaha saala ko; par tumhen jeetaa na chho.dangaa| haan, agar tuma mere pairon par gira kar qasama khaao ki ab kisee kee sajaa na karaaoonga, to chho.da doon| bolo manjoora hai?

udayabhaanu--teree shaamata to naheen aaee hai?

mataee--shaamata meree naheen aaee, tumhaaree aaee hai| bolo khaate! ho kasama--ek!

udayabhaanu--tuma hatate ho ki main puleesamaina ko bulaaoon? mataee-do!

udayabhaanu-(garaja kar ) hata jaa badamaasha saamane se |

mataee-teena!

munha se 'teena' kaa shabda nikalate hee baaboo saahaba ke sira par laathee kaa aisaa tulaa hua haath pada ki vaha acheta hokara zameen par gira pa.de| munha se kevala itanaahee nikalaa-haaya ! maara daalaa ! mataee ne sameepa aakara dekha, to sira phata gaya tha; aur khoona kee dhaara nikala rahi thi| na.dee kaa kaheen pata na tha| samajhagayaa ki kaama tamaama ho gaya| usane kalaaee se sone kee gha.dee khola lee, kurte se sone ke batana nikaala lie, ungalee se angoothee utaaree aur apanee raaha chalaa gaya; maano kuchha hua hee naheen | haan, itanee dayaa kee ki laasha raaste se ghaseeta kar kinaare daala dee| haaya ! bechaare ghar se kyaa socha kar chale the; aur kyaa ho gaya, jeevana ! tujhase jyaadaha asaara bhee duniya men koee vastu hai ? kyaa vaha us deepaka kee bhaanti hee kshanabhangura naheen hai, jo havaa ke ek jhonke se bujha jata hai ? paanee ke us bulabule ko dekhate ho; lekin use tootate bhee kuchha dera lagatee hai ; jeevana men utana saara bhee naheen| saansa kaa bharosaa hee kyaa ? aur isee nashvarata par hama abhilaashaaon ke kitane vishaala bhavana banaate hain ! naheen jaanate neeche jaane vaalee saansa oopara aaegee yaa naheen; par sochate itanee doora kee hain, maano hama amara hain| '
