# Chapter 23

teeesavaa.N parichchheda

munshee jee paa.Ncha baje kachaharee se laute; aur andara aakara chaarapaaee para gira pade| budhaape kee deha us para aaja saare din khaanaa na milaa| mu.Nha sookha gaya tha| nirmalaa samajha gaee, aaja din khaalee gaya|

nirmalaa ne poochhaa—aaja kuchha na milaa?

munshee jee—saaraa din daudate gujaraa; para haath kuchha na lagaa|

nirmalaa—phaujadaaree vaale maamale men kyaa hua?

munshee jee—mere muvakkila ko sajaa ho gaee|

nirmalaa—aur paNdita vaale mukadame men?

munshee jee—paNdita para digree ho gaee|

nirmalaa—aapa to kehte the daavaa khaarija ho jaayagaa|

munshee jee—kehta to tha; aur aba bhee kehta hoo.N ki daavaa khaarija ho jana chaahie tha; magara utanaa sira-magajana kauna kare?
nirmalaa -- aur us seera vaale daave men?

munshee jee -- usamen bhee haara ho gaee|

nirmalaa -- to aaja aapa kisee abhaage kaa mu.Nha dekha kar uthe the|

munshee jee se aba kaama bilakula na ho sakataa tha| ek to unake paasa mukadame aate hee na the; aur jo aate bhee the, vaha bigada jaate the| magara apanee asaphalataaon ko vaha nirmalaa se chhipaate rahate the| jisa din kuchha haath na lagataa, us din kisee se do-chaara rupae udhaara laakara nirmalaa ko de dete| praayah sabhee mitron se kuchha na kuchha le chuke the| aaja vaha daula bhee na lagaa|

nirmalaa ne chintaapoorNa aavaaza men kahaa -- aamadanee kaa yaha haala hai, to eeshvara hee maalika hai; us para bete kaa yaha haala hai ki baajaara jana mushkila| bhungee hee se sab kaama karaane kaa jee chaahataa hai| ghee lekara gyaaraha baje laute| kitanaa kaha kar haara gaee ki lakadee lete aao; para sunaa hee naheen|

munshee jee -- to khaanaa naheen pakaayaa?

nirmalaa -- aisee hee baaton se to aapa mukadame haarate hain| eendhana ke binaa kisee ne khaanaa banaayaa hai ki main hee banaa letee!

munshee jee -- to binaa kuchha khaae hee chalaa gaya?

nirmalaa -- ghar men aur kyaa rakkhaa tha jo khilaa detee?

munshee jee ne darate-darate kahaa -- kuchha paise-vaise na de die?

nirmalaa ne bhaunhen sikoda kar kahaa -- ghar men paise phalate hain na?

munshee jee ne kuchha javaaba na diya| jaraa dera taka to prateekshaa karte rahe ki shaayada jala-paana ke lie kuchha milegaa; lekin jaba nirmalaa ne paanee taka na ma.Ngavaayaa, to bechaare niraasha hokara baahara chale gae| siyaaraama ke takaleepha kaa anumaana karake unakaa chitta chanchala ho uthaa| saaraa din gujara gaya, bechaare ne abhee taka kuchha naheen khaayaa| kamare men padaa hogaa| ek baara bhungee hee se lakadee ma.Ngaa lee jati, to aisaa kyaa nukasaana ho jata| aisee kiphaayata bhee kisa kaama kee ki ghar ke aadamee bhookhe raha jaaya.N| apanaa sandookachaa khola kar tatolane lage ki shaayada do-chaara aane paise mila jaaya.N| usake andara ke saare kaagaja nikaala daale, ek-ek khaanaa dekha,neeche haath daala kar dekha; para kuchha na milaa| agar nirmalaa ke sandooka men paise na phalate the,to is sandookache men shaayada isake phoola bhee na lagate hon; lekin sanyoga hee kahiye ki kaagajon ko jhaadate hue ek chavannee gira padee| maare harsha ke munshee jee uchhala pade| badee-badee rakamen isake pahale kamaa chuke the; para yaha chavannee paakara is samaya unhen jitanaa aahlaada hua, utanaa pahale kabhee na hua tha| chavannee haath men lie hue siyaaraama ke kamare ke saamane aakara pukaaraa| koee jabaaba na milaa| taba kamare men jaakara dekha| siyaaraama kaa kaheen pataa naheen -- kyaa abhee skoola se naheen lautaa? mana men yaha savaala uthate hee munshee jee ne andara jaakara bhungee se poochhaa| maalooma hua ki skoola se lauta aae|

munshee jee ne poochhaa -- kuchha paanee piyaa hai?

bhungee ne kuchha javaaba na diya| naaka sikoda kar mu.Nha phere hue chalee gaee|

munshee jee aahistaa-aahistaa aakara apane kamare men baitha gae| aaja pahalee baara unhen nirmalaa para gussaa aaya; lekin ek hee pala men gussaa kaa aaghaata apane oopara hone lagaa| us a.Ndhere kamare men pharsha para lete hue vaha apane ko betaa kee ora se itane udaaseena ho jaane para dhikkaarane lage| din bhara ke thake the| thodee hee dera men unhen neenda aa gaee|

bhungee ne aakara pukaaraa -- baaboo jee, rasoee taiyaara hai| munshee jee chaunka kar utha baithe| kamare men laimpa jala raha tha|

poochhaa -- kai baja gae bhungee| mujhe to neenda aa gaee thi|

bhungee ne kahaa -- kotavaalee ke ghaNte men to nau baja gae hain; aur hama naaheen jaanita|

munshee jee -- siyaa baaboo aae?

bhungee -- aae honge to ghar hee men na honge|

munshee jee ne jhuejhalaa kar poochhaa -- main poochhataa hooe aae ki naheen? aur too na jaane kyaa-kyaa javaaba detee hai? aae ki naheen?

bhungee -- mainne to naheen dekha, jhootha kaise kaha dooe|

munshee jee phir leta gae aur bole -- unako aa jaane de,taba chalataa hoo.N|

aadha ghaNte taka dvaara kee ora aa.Nkha lagaae munshee jee lete rahe| taba vaha utha kar baahara aae; aur daahine haath koee do

pharlaanga taka chale| taba lauta kar dvaara para aae aur; poochhaa -- siyaa baaboo aa gae?

andara se javaaba aaya -- abhee naheen|

munshee jee phir baa.Neen ora chale aur galee ke nukkada taka gae| siyaaraama kaheen na dikhaaee diya| vahaa.N se phir ghar laute; aur dvaara para khade hokara poochhaa -- siyaa baaboo aa gae?

andara se javaaba milaa -- naheen|

kotavaalee ke ghaNte men dasa bajane lage|

munshee jee bade vega se kampanee baaga kee tarapha chale| sochane lage shaayada vahaa.N ghoomane gaya ho; aur ghaasa para lete-lete neenda aa gaee ho| baaraa men pahu.Ncha kar unhonne hareka vaincha ko dekha, chaaron tarapha ghoome, bahuta se aadamee ghaasa para pade hue the; para siyaaraama kaa nishaana na tha| unhonne siyaaraama kaa naama le lekara jora se pukaaraa; para kaheen se aavaaja na aaee|

khyaala aaya shaayada skoola men koee tamaashaa ho raha ho| skoola ek meela se kuchha jyaadaa hee tha| skoola kee tarapha chale; para aadhe raaste hee se lauta pade| baajaara banda ho gaya tha| skoola men itanee raat taka tamaashaa naheen ho sakataa| aba kee unhen ummeeda ho rahi thi ki siyaaraama lauta aaya hogaa| dvaara para aakara unhonne pukaaraa -- siyaa baaboo aae? kivaada banda the| koee aavaaja na aaee| phir jora se pukaaraa| bhungee kivaada khola kar bolee -- abhee to naheen aae| munshee jee ne dheere se bhungee ko apane paasa

bulaayaa;aur karuNa-aavaaza men bole too to ghar kee sab baat jaanatee hai;bataa aaja kyaa hua tha?

bhungee-baaboo jee,jhootha na boloo.Ngee; maalikina chhudaa dengee aur kyaa, doosare kaa ladakaa is taraha naheen rakkhaa jata| jahaa.N koee kaama hua,basa baajaara bheja diya| din bhara baajaara daudate beetataa tha| aaja lakadee laane na gae, to choolhaa hee naheen jalaa| kaho to munha phulaaven| jaba aapa hee naheen dekhate,to doosaraa kauna dekhegaa| chalie khaanaa kar leejie,bahoo jee kaba se baithee hain|

munshee jee kaha de is vakta na khaayenge|

munshee jee phir apane kamare men chale gae; aur ek lambee saa.Nsa lee| darda se bhare hue ye shabda unake mu.Nha se nikala pade-eeshvara, kyaa abhee daNda pooraa naheen hua? kyaa is andhe kee lakadee kaa bhee haath se chheena loge?

nirmalaa ne aakara kahaa-aaja siyaaraama abhee taka naheen aae| kehti rahi ki khaanaa banaae detee hoo.N,khaa lo; magara na jaane kaba utha kar chala die| na jaane kahaa.N ghooma rahe hain| baat to sunate hee naheen| aba kaba taka unakee raaha dekha karoo.N| aapa chala kar khaa leejie| unake lie khaanaa uthaa kar rakha doongee|

munshee jee ne nirmalaa kee ora kathora netron se dekha kar kahaa-abhee kai baje honge?

nirmalaa-kyaa jaane,shaayada dasa baje honge|

munshee jee-jee naheen,baaraha baje hain|
nirmalaa-baaraha! baaraha baja gae! itanee dera to kabhee na karte the| to aba kaba taka unakee raaha dekhoge! dopahara ko bhee to kuchha naheen khaayaa tha| aisaa sailaanee ladakaa mainne naheen dekha|

munshee jee-jee tumhen bahuta dika karta hai,kyon?

nirmalaa-dekhie na,itanee raat gaee; aur ghar kee sudha hee naheen|

munshee jee-shaayada yaha aakhiree sharaarata ho|

nirmalaa-kaisee baaten munha se nikaalate hain| jaaya.Nge kahaa.N? kisee yaara dosta ke ghar pada rahe honge|

munshee jee-shaayada aisaa hee ho| eeshvara kare aisaa hee ho|

nirmalaa-sabere aaven; to jaraa tambeeha keejiegaa|

munshee jee-khooba achchhee taraha karoo.Ngaa|

nirmalaa-chalie khaa leejie,dera bahuta huee|

munshee jee-savere usakee tamveeha karake khaaoo.Ngaa| kaheen na aaya, to tumhen aisaa eemaanadaara naukara kahaa.N milegaa|

nirmalaa ne aintha kar kahaa-to kyaamainne bhagaa diya?

munshee jee-naheen, yaha kauna kehta hai? tuma use kyon bhagaane lagee? tumhaaraa to kaama karta tha| shaamata aa gaee hogee|

nirmalaa ne aur kuchha naheen kahaa| baat badha jaane kaa dara tha| bheetara chalee aaee| sone ko bhee na kahaa| jaraa dera men bhungee ne andara se kivaada bhee banda kar die!
kyaa munshee jee ko neenda aa sakatee thi? teena ladakon men kevala ek bacha raha tha| vaha haath se nikala gaya, to phir jeevana men andhakaara ke sivaa aur kyaa hai? koee naama lene vaalaa bhee na rahegaa| haa! kaise-kaise ratna haath se nikala gae| munshee jee kee aa.Nkhon se yadi is samaya ashrudhaaraa vaha rahi thi, to koee hairaanee hai? us vyaapaka pashchaattaapa, us saghana pachhataavaa-timira men ummeeda kee ek halakee-see rekhaa unhen sanbhaale hue thi! jisa pala yaha rekhaa lupa ho jaayagee, kauna kaha sakataa hai-una para kyaa veetegee? unakee us darda kee kalpanaa kauna kar sakataa hai?

kaee baara munshee jee kee aa.Nkhen jhapakee, lekin hara baarasiyaaraama kee aahata ke dhokhe men chaunka pade!

saberaa hote hee munshee jee phir siyaaraama ko khojane nikale| kisee se poochhate sharma aatee thi| kisa munha se poochhe? unhen kisee se sahaanubhooti kee ummeeda na thi| prakata na kaha kar mana men sada yahee kahege-jaisaa kiya, vaisaa bhogo| saare din vaha skoola ke maidaanon, baajaaron aur vaageechon kaa chakara lagaate rahe| do din niraahaara rahane para bhee unhen itanee shakti kaise huee,yaha vahee jaanen|

raat ke baaraha vaje munshee jee ghar laute,daravaaje para laalatena jala rahi thi,nirmalaa dvaara para khadee thi| dekhate hee bolee-kahaa bhee naheen, na jaane kaba chala die? kuchha pataa chalaa?

munshee jee ne aagneya netron se taakate hue kahaa-hata jaao saamane se, naheen to buraa hogaa| main aape men naheen hoo.N| yaha tumhaaree
karanee hai| tumhaare hee vajaha aaja meree yaha dashaa ho rahi hai| aaja se chhah saala pahale kyaa is ghar kee yahee dashaa thi? tumane meraa banaa-banaayaa ghar bigaada diya,tumane mere lahalahaate hue baaraa ko ujaada daalaa| kevala ek dhoo.Ntha raha gaya hai| usakaa nishaana mitaa kar tabhee tumhen santosha hogaa| main apanaa sarvanaasha karane ke lie tumhen apane ghar naheen laayaa tha| sukhee jeevana ko aur bhee sukhamaya banaanaa chaahataa tha| yaha usee kaa praayashchitta hai| jo ladake paana kee taraha phere jaate the,unhen mere jeete jee tumane chaakara samajha liya;aur main aa.Nkhon se sab kuchha dekhate hue bhee andhaa banaa baithaa raha| jaao,mere lie thodaa saa sahiyaa bheja do| basa,yahee kasara gaee hai; vaha bhee pooree ho jaaya|

nirmalaa ne rote hue kahaa-main to abhaagina hoo.N hee,aapa kahenge taba jaanoo.Ngee? na jaane eeshvara ne meraa janma kyon diya tha| magara yaha aapane kaise samajha liya ki siyaaraama aba aage hee naheen?

munshee jee ne apane kamare kee ora jaate hue kahaa-jalaao mata, jaakara khushiyaa.N manaao| tumhaaree manokaamanaa pooree hogaee!
