# Nirmala — Adhyay 23

**Munshi Premchand**

teeesavaan parichchheda

munshee jee paancha baje kachaharee se laute; aur andara aakara chaarapaaee par gira pa.de| bu.dhaape kee deha us par aaja saare din bhojana na milaa| munha sookha gaya tha| nirmalaa samajha gaee, aaja din khaalee gaya|

nirmalaa ne poochhaa—aaja kuchha na mila?

munshee jee—saaraa din dau.date guzara; par haath kuchha na lagaa|

nirmalaa—phauzadaaree vaale maamale men kyaa hua?

munshee jee—mere muvakkila ko sazaa ho gaee|

nirmalaa—aur pandita vaale muqadame men?

munshee jee—pandita par digree ho gaee|

nirmalaa—aapa to kehte the daavaa Kaarija ho jaayagaa|

munshee jee—kahata to tha; aur ab bhee kahata hoon ki daavaa Kaarija ho jana chaahie tha; magar utana sira-maGazana kaun kare? nirmalaa -- aur us seera vaale daave men?

munshee jee -- usamen bhee haara ho gaee|

nirmalaa -- to aaja aapa kisee abhaage kaa munha dekha kar uthe the|

munshee jee se ab kaama bilakula na ho sakata tha| ek to unake paasa muqadame aate hee na the; aur jo aate bhee the, vaha biga.da jaate the| magar apanee asaphalataaon ko vaha nirmalaa se chhipaate rahate the| jisa din kuchha haath na lagata, us din kisee se do-chaara rupae udhaara laakara nirmalaa ko de dete| praayah sabhee mitron se kuchha na kuchha le chuke the| aaja vaha daula bhee na lagaa|

nirmalaa ne chintaapoorna svara men kaha -- aamadanee kaa yaha haala hai, to eeshvara hee maalika hai; us par bete kaa yaha haala hai ki baazaara jana mushkila| bhungee hee se sab kaama karaane kaa jee chaahata hai| ghee lekara gyaaraha baje laute| kitana kaha kar haara gaee ki laka.dee lete aao; par sunaa hee naheen|

munshee jee -- to khaanaa naheen pakaaya?

nirmalaa -- aisee hee baaton se to aapa muqadame haarate hain| eendhana ke binaa kisee ne khaanaa banaayaa hai ki main hee bana letee!

munshee jee -- to binaa kuchha khaae hee chalaa gaya?

nirmalaa -- ghar men aur kyaa rakkhaa tha jo khilaa detee?

munshee jee ne darate-darate kaha -- kuchha paise-vaise na de die?

nirmalaa ne bhaunhen siko.da kar kaha -- ghar men paise phalate hain na? munshee jee ne kuchha javaaba na diya| jaraa dera taka to prateekshaa karte rahe ki shaayada jala-paana ke lie kuchha milega; lekin jab nirmalaa ne paanee taka na mangavaaya, to bechaare niraasha hokara baahara chale gae| siyaaraama ke kashta kaa anumaana karake unakaa chitta chanchala ho uthaa| saaraa din guzara gaya, bechaare ne abhee taka kuchha naheen khaayaa| kamare men pada hogaa| ek baara bhungee hee se laka.dee mangaa lee jati, to aisaa kyaa nukasaana ho jata| aisee kifaayata bhee kisa kaama kee ki ghar ke aadmi bhookhe raha jaayan| apana sandooqachaa khola kar tatolane lage ki shaayada do-chaara aane paise mila jaayan| usake andara ke saare kaaGaza nikaala daale, ek-ek khaanaa dekha,neeche haath daala kar dekha; par kuchha na milaa| agar nirmalaa ke sandooka men paise na phalate the,to is sandooqache men shaayada isake phoola bhee na lagate hon; lekin sanyoga hee kahiye ki kaaGazon ko jha.date hue ek chavannee gira pa.dee| maare harsha ke munshee jee uchhala pa.de| ba.dee-ba.dee raqamen isake pahale kamaa chuke the; par yaha chavannee paakara is samay unhen jitana aahlaada hua, utana pahale kabhee na hua tha| chavannee haath men lie hue siyaaraama ke kamare ke saamane aakara pukaaraa| koee jabaaba na milaa| tab kamare men jaakara dekha| siyaaraama kaa kaheen pata naheen -- kyaa abhee skoola se naheen lauta? mann men yaha prashna uthate hee munshee jee ne andara jaakara bhungee se poochhaa| maalooma hua ki skoola se lauta aae|

munshee jee ne poochhaa -- kuchha paanee piya hai? bhungee ne kuchha javaaba na diya| naaka siko.da kar munha phere hue chalee gaee|

munshee jee aahistaa-aahistaa aakara apane kamare men baitha gae| aaja pahalee baara unhen nirmalaa par krodha aaya; lekin ek hee kshana men krodha kaa aaghaata apane oopara hone lagaa| us andhere kamare men farsha par lete hue vaha apane ko putra kee ora se itane udaaseena ho jaane par dhikkaarane lage| din bhara ke thake the| tho.dee hee dera men unhen neenda aa gaee|

bhungee ne aakara pukaaraa -- baaboo jee, rasoee taiyaara hai| munshee jee chaunka kar utha baithe| kamare men laimpa jala raha tha|

poochhaa -- kai baja gae bhungee| mujhe to neenda aa gaee thi|

bhungee ne kaha -- kotavaalee ke ghante men to nau baja gae hain; aur hama naaheen jaanita|

munshee jee -- siya baaboo aae?

bhungee -- aae honge to ghar hee men na honge|

munshee jee ne jhu.cjhalaa kar poochhaa -- main poochhata hoo.c aae ki naheen? aur too na jaane kyaa-kyaa javaaba detee hai? aae ki naheen?

bhungee -- mainne to naheen dekha, jhootha kaise kaha doo.c|

munshee jee phir leta gae aur bole -- unako aa jaane de,tab chalata hoon|

aadha ghante taka dvaara kee ora aankh lagaae munshee jee lete rahe| tab vaha utha kar baahara aae; aur daahine haath koee do farlaanga taka chale| tab lauta kar dvaara par aae aur; poochhaa -- siya baaboo aa gae?

andara se javaaba aaya -- abhee naheen|

munshee jee phir baaneen ora chale aur galee ke nukka.da taka gae| siyaaraama kaheen na dikhaaee diya| vahaan se phir ghar laute; aur dvaara par kha.de hokara poochhaa -- siya baaboo aa gae?

andara se javaaba milaa -- naheen|

kotavaalee ke ghante men dasa bajane lage|

munshee jee ba.de vega se kampanee baaga kee tarafa chale| sochane lage shaayada vahaan ghoomane gaya ho; aur ghaasa par lete-lete neenda aa gaee ho| baaraa men pahuncha kar unhonne hareka vaincha ko dekha, chaaron tarafa ghoome, bahut se aadmi ghaasa par pa.de hue the; par siyaaraama kaa nishaana na tha| unhonne siyaaraama kaa naama le lekara zora se pukaara; par kaheen se aavaaza na aaee|

khayal aaya shaayada skoola men koee tamaashaa ho raha ho| skoola ek meela se kuchha zyada hee tha| skoola kee tarafa chale; par aadhe raaste hee se lauta pa.de| baazaara banda ho gaya tha| skoola men itanee raat taka tamaashaa naheen ho sakata| ab kee unhen aashaa ho rahi thi ki siyaaraama lauta aaya hogaa| dvaara par aakara unhonne pukaaraa -- siya baaboo aae? kiva.da banda the| koee aavaaza na aaee| phir zora se pukaaraa| bhungee kiva.da khola kar boli -- abhee to naheen aae| munshee jee ne dheere se bhungee ko apane paasa bulaaya;aur karuna-svara men bole too to ghar kee sab baat jaanatee hai;bata aaja kyaa hua tha?

bhungee-baaboo jee,jhootha na boloongee; maalikina chhu.daa dengee aur kya, doosare kaa ladka is tarah naheen rakkhaa jata| jahaan koee kaama hua,basa baajaara bheja diya| din bhara baazaara dau.date beetata tha| aaja laka.dee laane na gae, to choolhaa hee naheen jalaa| kaho to munha phulaaven| jab aapa hee naheen dekhate,to doosaraa kaun dekhegaa| chalie bhojana kar leejie,bahoo jee kab se baithee hain|

munshee jee kaha de is vakta na khaayenge|

munshee jee phir apane kamare men chale gae; aur ek lambee saansa lee| vedana se bhare hue ye shabda unake munha se nikala pa.de-eeshvara, kyaa abhee danda pooraa naheen hua? kyaa is andhe kee laka.dee kaa bhee haath se chheena loge?

nirmalaa ne aakara kaha-aaja siyaaraama abhee taka naheen aae| kehti rahi ki khaanaa banaae detee hoon,khaa lo; magar na jaane kab utha kar chala die| na jaane kahaan ghooma rahe hain| baat to sunate hee naheen| ab kab taka unakee raaha dekha karoon| aapa chala kar khaa leejie| unake lie khaanaa uthaa kar rakha doongee|

munshee jee ne nirmalaa kee ora kathora netron se dekha kar kaha-abhee kai baje honge?

nirmalaa-kyaa jaane,shaayada dasa baje honge|

munshee jee-jee naheen,baaraha baje hain| nirmalaa-baaraha! baaraha baja gae! itanee dera to kabhee na karte the| to ab kab taka unakee raaha dekhoge! dopahara ko bhee to kuchha naheen khaayaa tha| aisaa sailaanee ladka mainne naheen dekha|

munshee jee-jee tumhen bahut diqa karata hai,kyon?

nirmalaa-dekhie na,itanee raat gaee; aur ghar kee sudha hee naheen|

munshee jee-shaayada yaha aakhiree sharaarata ho|

nirmalaa-kaisi baaten munha se nikaalate hain| jaayange kahaan? kisee yaara dosta ke ghar pa.da rahe honge|

munshee jee-shaayada aisaa hee ho| eeshvara kare aisaa hee ho|

nirmalaa-sabere aaven; to zaraa tambeeha keejiegaa|

munshee jee-khooba achchhi tarah karoongaa|

nirmalaa-chalie khaa leejie,dera bahut hui|

munshee jee-savere usakee tamveeha karake khaaoongaa| kaheen na aaya, to tumhen aisaa eemaanadaara naukara kahaan milegaa|

nirmalaa ne aintha kar kaha-to kyaamainne bhagaa diya?

munshee jee-naheen, yaha kaun kahata hai? tuma use kyon bhagaane lagee? tumhaaraa to kaama karata tha| shaamata aa gaee hogee|

nirmalaa ne aur kuchha naheen kaha| baat ba.dha jaane kaa bhaya tha| bheetara chalee aaee| sone ko bhee na kaha| jaraa dera men bhungee ne andara se kiva.da bhee banda kar die! kyaa munshee jee ko neenda aa sakatee thi? teena ladakon men kevala ek bacha raha tha| vaha haath se nikala gaya, to phir jeevana men andhakaara ke sivaa aur kyaa hai? koee naama lene vaalaa bhee na rahegaa| ha! kaise-kaise ratna haath se nikala gae| munshee jee kee aankhon se yadi is samay ashrudhaaraa vaha rahi thi, to koee aashcharya hai? us vyaapaka pashchaattaapa, us saghana glaani-timira men aashaa kee ek halakee-see rekhaa unhen sanbhaale hue thi! jisa kshana yaha rekhaa lupa ho jaayagee, kaun kaha sakata hai-una par kyaa veetegee? unakee us vedana kee kalpana kaun kar sakata hai?

kaee baara munshee jee kee aankhen jhapakee, lekin hara baarasiyaaraama kee aahata ke dhokhe men chaunka pa.de!

raat ke baaraha vaje munshee jee ghar laute,daravaaje par laalatena jala rahi thi,nirmalaa dvaara par kha.dee thi| dekhate hee boli-kaha bhee naheen, na jaane kab chala die? kuchha pata chala?

munshee jee ne aagneya netron se taakate hue kaha-hata jaao saamane se, naheen to buraa hogaa| main aape men naheen hoon| yaha tumhaaree karanee hai| tumhaare hee kaarana aaja meree yaha dashaa ho rahi hai| aaja se chhah saala pahale kyaa is ghar kee yahee dashaa thi? tumane meraa bana-banaayaa ghar biga.da diya,tumane mere lahalahaate hue baaraa ko uja.da daalaa| kevala ek dhoontha raha gaya hai| usakaa nishaana mitaa kar tabhee tumhen santosha hogaa| main apana sarvanaasha karane ke lie tumhen apane ghar naheen laayaa tha| sukhee jeevana ko aur bhee sukhamaya banaanaa chaahata tha| yaha usee kaa praayashchitta hai| jo ladke paana kee tarah phere jaate the,unhen mere jeete jee tumane chaakara samajha liya;aur main aankhon se sab kuchha dekhate hue bhee andhaa bana baithaa raha| jaao,mere lie tho.daa saa sahiya bheja do| basa,yahee kasara gaee hai; vaha bhee pooree ho jaaya|

nirmalaa ne rote hue kaha-main to abhaagina hoon hee,aapa kahenge tab jaanoongee? na jaane eeshvara ne meraa janma kyon diya tha| magar yaha aapane kaise samajha liya ki siyaaraama ab aage hee naheen?

munshee jee ne apane kamare kee ora jaate hue kaha-jalaao mata, jaakara khushiyaan manaao| tumhaaree manokaamana pooree hogaee!
