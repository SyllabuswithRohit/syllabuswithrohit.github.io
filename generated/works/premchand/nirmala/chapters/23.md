# Nirmala — Adhyay 23

**Munshi Premchand**

teisvam parichchhed

munshi ji panch baje kachhari se laute; aur andar akar charapai par gir pade. burhape ki deh us par aj sare din khana na mila. mumh sukh gaya tha. NAmENirmala samajh gai, aj din khali gaya.

NAmENirmala ne puchhaaj kuchh na mila?

munshi jisara din daurte guzra; par hath kuchh na laga.

NAmENirmala phauzdari vale mamle men kya hua?

munshi jimere muvakkil ko saza ho gai.

NAmENirmala aur pandit vale muqdame men?

munshi jipandit par digri ho gai.

NAmENirmala ap to kahte the dava kharij ho jayga.

munshi jikahta to tha; aur ab bhi kahta hum ki dava kharij ho jana chahiye tha; magar utna sir-magzan kaun kare?
NAmENirmala -- aur us sir vale dave men?

munshi ji -- usmen bhi har ho gai.

NAmENirmala -- to aj ap kisi abhage ka mumh dekh kar uthe the.

munshi ji se ab kam bilkul na ho sakta tha. ek to unke pas muqdame ate hi na the; aur jo ate bhi the, vah bigar jate the. magar apni asaphalataon ko vah NAmENirmala se chhipate rahte the. jis din kuchh hath na lagta, us din kisi se do-char rupae udhar lakar NAmENirmala ko de dete. prayah sabhi mitron se kuchh na kuchh le chuke the. aj vah daul bhi na laga.

NAmENirmala ne chintapurn avaz men kaha -- amdani ka yah hal hai, to ishvar hi malik hai; us par bete ka yah hal hai ki bazar jana mushkil. bhungi hi se sab kam karane ka ji chahta hai. ghi lekar gyarah baje laute. kitna kah kar har gai ki lakri lete ao; par suna hi nahin.

munshi ji -- to khana nahin pakaya?

NAmENirmala -- aisi hi baton se to ap muqdame harte haim. indhan ke bina kisi ne khana banaya hai ki maim hi bana leti!

munshi ji -- to bina kuchh khae hi chala gaya?

NAmENirmala -- ghar men aur kya rakkha tha jo khila deti?

munshi ji ne darte-darte kaha -- kuchh paise-vaise na de die?

NAmENirmala ne bhaumhen sikor kar kaha -- ghar men paise phalte haim na?

munshi ji ne kuchh javab na diya. jara der tak to intzar karte rahe ki shayad jal-pan ke lie kuchh milega; lekin jab NAmENirmala ne pani tak na mangvaya, to bechare nirash hokar bahar chale gae. NAmESiyaram ke takliph ka anuman karke unka chitt chanchal ho utha. sara din guzar gaya, bechare ne abhi tak kuchh nahin khaya. kamre men pada hoga. ek bar bhungi hi se lakri manga li jati, to aisa kya nuksan ho jata. aisi kifayat bhi kis kam ki ki ghar ke aadmi bhukhe rah jayam. apna sanduqcha khol kar tatolne lage ki shayad do-char ane paise mil jayam. uske andar ke sare kagaz nikal dale, ek-ek khana dekha, niche hath dal kar dekha; par kuchh na mila. agar NAmENirmala ke sanduk men paise na phalte the, to is sanduqche men shayad iske phul bhi na lagte hon; lekin samyog hi kahiye ki kagzon ko jharte hue ek chavanni gir padi. mare harsh ke munshi ji uchhal pade. badi-badi raqmen iske pahle kama chuke the; par yah chavanni pakad is samay unhen jitna ahlad hua, utna pahle kabhi na hua tha. chavanni hath men lie hue NAmESiyaram ke kamre ke samne akar pukara. koi jabab na mila. tab kamre men jakar dekha. NAmESiyaram ka kahim pata nahin -- kya abhi skul se nahin lauta? man men yah saval uthte hi munshi ji ne andar jakar bhungi se puchha. malum hua ki skul se laut ae.

munshi ji ne puchha -- kuchh pani piya hai?

bhungi ne kuchh javab na diya. nak sikor kar mumh phere hue chali gai.

munshi ji ahista-ahista akar apne kamre men baith gae. aj pahli bar unhen NAmENirmala par gussa aya; lekin ek hi pal men gussa ka aghat apne upar hone laga. us andhere kamre men farsh par lete hue vah apne ko beta ki or se itne udasin ho jane par dhikkarne lage. din bhar ke thake the. thodi hi der men unhen nind a gai.

bhungi ne akar pukara -- babu ji, rasoi taiyar hai. munshi ji chaunk kar uth baithe. kamre men laimp jal raha tha.

puchha -- kai baj gae bhungi. mujhe to nind a gai thi.

bhungi ne kaha -- kotvali ke ghante men to nau baj gae haim; aur ham nahin janit.

munshi ji -- siya babu ae?

bhungi -- ae honge to ghar hi men na honge.

munshi ji ne jhuajhala kar puchha -- maim puchhta hua ae ki nahin? aur tu na jane kya-kya javab deti hai? ae ki nahin?

bhungi -- maimne to nahin dekha, jhuth kaise kah dua.

munshi ji phir let gae aur bole -- unko a jane de, tab chalta hum.

adh ghante tak dvar ki or ankh lagae munshi ji lete rahe. tab vah uth kar bahar ae; aur dahine hath koi do

farlanga tak chale. tab laut kar dvar par ae aur; puchha -- siya babu a gae?

andar se javab aya -- abhi nahin.

munshi ji phir bamim or chale aur gali ke nukkar tak gae. NAmESiyaram kahim na dikhai diya. vaham se phir ghar laute; aur dvar par khade hokar puchha -- siya babu a gae?

andar se javab mila -- nahin.

kotvali ke ghante men das bajne lage.

munshi ji bade veg se kampani bag ki taraf chale. sochne lage shayad vaham ghumne gaya ho; aur ghas par lete-lete nind a gai ho. bada men pahunch kar unhomne harek vaincha ko dekha, charon taraf ghume, bahut se aadmi ghas par pade hue the; par NAmESiyaram ka nishan na tha. unhomne NAmESiyaram ka nam le lekar zor se pukara; par kahim se avaz na ai.

khyal aya shayad skul men koi tamasha ho raha ho. skul ek mil se kuchh jyada hi tha. skul ki taraf chale; par adhe raste hi se laut pade. bazar band ho gaya tha. skul men itni rat tak tamasha nahin ho sakta. ab ki unhen ummid ho rahi thi ki NAmESiyaram laut aya hoga. dvar par akar unhomne pukara -- siya babu ae? kivar band the. koi avaz na ai. phir zor se pukara. bhungi kivar khol kar boli -- abhi to nahin ae. munshi ji ne dhire se bhungi ko apne pas

bulaya; aur karun-avaz men bole tu to ghar ki sab bat janti hai; bata aj kya hua tha?

bhungi-babu ji, jhuth na bolungi; malikin chhura dengi aur kya, dusre ka larka is tarah nahin rakkha jata. jaham koi kam hua, bas bajar bhej diya. din bhar bazar daurte bitta tha. aj lakri lane na gae, to chulha hi nahin jala. kaho to mumh phulaven. jab ap hi nahin dekhte, to dusra kaun dekhega. chalie khana kar lijie, bahu ji kab se baithi haim.

munshi ji kah de is vakt na khayenge.

munshi ji phir apne kamre men chale gae; aur ek lambi sams li. dard se bhare hue ye shabd unke mumh se nikal pade-ishvar, kya abhi saza pura nahin hua? kya is andhe ki lakri ka bhi hath se chhin loge?

NAmENirmala ne akar kaha-aj NAmESiyaram abhi tak nahin ae. kahti rahi ki khana banae deti hum, kha lo; magar na jane kab uth kar chal die. na jane kaham ghum rahe haim. bat to sunte hi nahin. ab kab tak unki rah dekha karum. ap chal kar kha lijie. unke lie khana utha kar rakh dungi.

munshi ji ne NAmENirmala ki or kathor netron se dekh kar kaha-abhi kai baje honge?

NAmENirmala -kya jane, shayad das baje honge.

munshi ji-ji nahin, barah baje haim.
NAmENirmala -barah! barah baj gae! itni der to kabhi na karte the. to ab kab tak unki rah dekhoge! dophar ko bhi to kuchh nahin khaya tha. aisa sailani larka maimne nahin dekha.

munshi ji-ji tumhen bahut diq karta hai, kyon?

NAmENirmala -dekhie na, itni rat gai; aur ghar ki sudh hi nahin.

munshi ji-shayad yah akhiri shararat ho.

NAmENirmala -kaisi baten mumh se nikalte haim. jayange kaham? kisi yar dost ke ghar par rahe honge.

munshi ji-shayad aisa hi ho. ishvar kare aisa hi ho.

NAmENirmala -sabere aven; to zara tambih kijiega.

munshi ji-khub achchhi tarah karunga.

NAmENirmala -chalie kha lijie, der bahut hui.

munshi ji-savere uski tamvih karke khaunga. kahim na aya, to tumhen aisa imandar naukar kaham milega.

NAmENirmala ne ainth kar kaha-to kyamaimne bhaga diya?

munshi ji-nahin, yah kaun kahta hai? tum use kyon bhagane lagi? tumhara to kam karta tha. shamat a gai hogi.

NAmENirmala ne aur kuchh nahin kaha. bat barh jane ka dar tha. bhitar chali ai. sone ko bhi na kaha. jara der men bhungi ne andar se kivar bhi band kar die!
kya munshi ji ko nind a sakti thi? tin larkon men sirph ek bach raha tha. vah hath se nikal gaya, to phir jivan men andhakar ke siva aur kya hai? koi nam lene vala bhi na rahega. ha! kaise-kaise ratna hath se nikal gae. munshi ji ki ankhon se yadi is samay ashrudhara vah rahi thi, to koi hairani hai? us vyapak pashchattap, us saghan pachhtava-timir men ummid ki ek halki-si rekha unhen sambhale hue thi! jis pal yah rekha lup ho jaygi, kaun kah sakta hai-un par kya vitegi? unki us dard ki kalpana kaun kar sakta hai?

kai bar munshi ji ki ankhen jhapki, lekin har bar NAmESiyaram ki ahat ke dhokhe men chaunk pade!

sabera hote hi munshi ji phir NAmESiyaram ko khojne nikle. kisi se puchhte sharm ati thi. kis mumh se puchhe? unhen kisi se hamadardi ki ummid na thi. prakat na kah kar man men sad yahi kahege-jaisa kiya, vaisa bhogo. sare din vah skul ke maidanon, bajaron aur vagichon ka chakar lagate rahe. do din nirahar rahne par bhi unhen itni shakti kaise hui, yah vahi janen.

rat ke barah vaje munshi ji ghar laute, darvaje par lalten jal rahi thi, NAmENirmala dvar par khadi thi. dekhte hi boli-kaha bhi nahin, na jane kab chal die? kuchh pata chala?

munshi ji ne agney netron se takte hue kaha-hat jao samne se, nahin to bura hoga. maim ape men nahin hum. yah tumhari
karni hai. tumhare hi vajah aj meri yah hal ho rahi hai. aj se chhah sal pahle kya is ghar ki yahi hal thi? tumne mera bana-banaya ghar bigar diya, tumne mere lahalhate hue bada ko ujar dala. sirph ek dhunth rah gaya hai. uska nishan mita kar tabhi tumhen santosh hoga. maim apna sarvanash karne ke lie tumhen apne ghar nahin laya tha. sukhi jivan ko aur bhi sukhmay banana chahta tha. yah usi ka prayashchitt hai. jo larke pan ki tarah phere jate the, unhen mere jite ji tumne chakar samajh liya; aur maim ankhon se sab kuchh dekhte hue bhi andha bana baitha raha. jao, mere lie thoda sa sahiya bhej do. bas, yahi kasar gai hai; vah bhi puri ho jay.

NAmENirmala ne rote hue kaha-maim to abhagin hum hi, ap kahenge tab janungi? na jane ishvar ne mera janma kyon diya tha. magar yah apne kaise samajh liya ki NAmESiyaram ab age hi nahin?

munshi ji ne apne kamre ki or jate hue kaha-jalao mat, jakar khushiyam manao. tumhari manokamna puri hogai!
