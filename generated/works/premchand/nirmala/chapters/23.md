# Nirmala — Adhyay 23

**Munshi Premchand**

teeesavaan parichchhed

munshee jee paanch baje kachaharee se laute; aur andar aakar chaarapaaee par gir pade. budhaape ki deh us par aaj saare din khana n milaa. munh sookh gayaa thaa. Nirmala samajh gaee, aaj din khaalee gayaa.

Nirmala ne poochhaa — aaj kuchh n milaa?

munshee jee — saaraa din daudate gujaraa; par haath kuchh n lagaa.

Nirmala — phaujadaaree vaale maamale mein kyaa huaa?

munshee jee — mere muvakkil ko sajaa ho gaee.

Nirmala — aur pandit vaale mukadame mein?

munshee jee — pandit par digree ho gaee.

Nirmala — aap to kahate the daavaa khaarij ho jaayagaa.

munshee jee — kahataa to tha; aur ab bhi kahataa hoon ki daavaa khaarij ho jaanaa chahiye tha; magar utanaa sir-magajan kaun kare? Nirmala -- aur us seer vaale daave mein?

munshee jee -- usamen bhi haar ho gaee.

Nirmala -- to aaj aap kisee abhaage ka munh dekh kar uthe the.

munshee jee se ab kaam bilakul n ho sakataa thaa. ek to unake paas mukadame aate hee n the; aur jo aate bhi the, vah bigad jaate the. magar apanee naakaameeon ko vah Nirmala se chhipaate rahate the. jis din kuchh haath n lagataa, us din kisee se do-chaar rupaye udhaar laakar Nirmala ko de dete. praayah sabhee mitron se kuchh n kuchh le chuke the. aaj vah daul bhi n lagaa.

Nirmala ne phikrapoorn svar mein kahaa -- aamadanee ka yah haal hai, to Bhagwan hee maalik hai; us par bete ka yah haal hai ki baajaar jaanaa mushkila. bhungggee hee se sab kaam karaane ka jee chaahataa hai. ghee lekar gyaarah baje laute. kitanaa kah kar haar gaee ki lakadee lete aao; par sunaa hee naheen.

munshee jee -- to khana nahin pakaayaa?

Nirmala -- aisee hee baaton se to aap mukadame haarate hain. eendhan ke binaa kisee ne khana banaayaa hai ki main hee banaa letee!

munshee jee -- to binaa kuchh khaae hee chalaa gayaa?

Nirmala -- ghar mein aur kyaa rakkhaa tha jo khilaa detee?

munshee jee ne darate-darate kahaa -- kuchh paise-vaise n de die?

Nirmala ne bhaunhen sikod kar kahaa -- ghar mein paise phalate hain n? munshee jee ne kuchh javaab n diyaa. jaraa der tak to intezar karate rahe ki shaayad jal-paan ke lie kuchh milegaa; lekin jab Nirmala ne paani tak n manggavaayaa, to bechaare niraash hokar baahar chale gae. siyaaraam ke takaleeph ka anumaan karake unakaa chitt chanychal ho uthaa. saaraa din gujar gayaa, bechaare ne abhee tak kuchh nahin khaayaa. kamare mein padaa hogaa. ek baar bhungggee hee se lakadee manggaa lee jaatee, to aisaa kyaa nukasaan ho jaataa. aisee kiphaayat bhi kis kaam ki ki ghar ke aadmi bhookhe rah jaayan. apanaa sandookachaa khol kar tatolane lage ki shaayad do-chaar aane paise mil jaayan. usake andar ke saare kaagaj nikaal daale, ek-ek khana dekhaa, neeche haath daal kar dekhaa; par kuchh n milaa. agar Nirmala ke sandook mein paise n phalate the, to is sandookache mein shaayad isake phool bhi n lagate hon; lekin sanyog hee kahiye ki kaagajon ko jhaadate hue ek chavannee gir padee. maare harsh ke munshee jee uchhal pade. badee-badee rakamen isake pahale kamaa chuke the; par yah chavannee paakar is samay unhen jitanaa aahlaad huaa, utanaa pahale kabhee n huaa thaa. chavannee haath mein lie hue siyaaraam ke kamare ke saamane aakar pukaaraa. koee jabaab n milaa. tab kamare mein jaakar dekhaa. siyaaraam ka kaheen pataa nahin -- kyaa abhee skool se nahin lautaa? man mein yah savaal uthate hee munshee jee ne andar jaakar bhungggee se poochhaa. maaloom huaa ki skool se laut aae.

munshee jee ne poochhaa -- kuchh paani piyaa hai? bhungggee ne kuchh javaab n diyaa. naak sikod kar munh phere hue chalee gaee.

munshee jee aahistaa-aahistaa aakar apane kamare mein baith gae. aaj pahalee baar unhen Nirmala par krodh kamaaeeaa; lekin ek hee pal mein krodh ka aaghaat apane oopar hone lagaa. us andhere kamare mein pharsh par lete hue vah apane ko putr ki or se itane udaaseen ho jaane par dhikkaarane lage. din bhar ke thake the. thodee hee der mein unhen neend aa gaee.

bhungggee ne aakar pukaaraa -- baaboo jee, rasoee taiyaar hai. munshee jee chaungk kar uth baithe. kamare mein laimp jal rahaa thaa.

poochhaa -- kai baj gae bhungggee. mujhe to neend aa gaee thee.

bhungggee ne kahaa -- kotavaalee ke ghante mein to nau baj gae hain; aur ham naaheen jaanita.

munshee jee -- siyaa baaboo aae?

bhungggee -- aae hongge to ghar hee mein n hongge.

munshee jee ne jhuejhalaa kar poochhaa -- main poochhataa hooe aae ki nahin? aur too n jaane kyaa-kyaa javaab detee hai? aae ki nahin?

bhungggee -- mainne to nahin dekhaa, jhooth kaise kah dooe.

munshee jee phir let gae aur bole -- unako aa jaane de, tab chalataa hoon.

aadh ghante tak dvaar ki or aankh lagaae munshee jee lete rahe. tab vah uth kar baahar aae; aur daahine haath koee do pharlaanggg tak chale. tab laut kar dvaar par aae aur; poochhaa -- siyaa baaboo aa gae?

andar se javaab kamaaeeaa -- abhee naheen.

munshee jee phir baaneen or chale aur galee ke nukkad tak gae. siyaaraam kaheen n dikhaaee diyaa. wahan se phir ghar laute; aur dvaar par khade hokar poochhaa -- siyaa baaboo aa gae?

andar se javaab milaa -- naheen.

kotavaalee ke ghante mein das bajane lage.

munshee jee bade veg se kampanee baag ki taraph chale. sochane lage shaayad wahan ghoomane gayaa ho; aur ghaas par lete-lete neend aa gaee ho. baaraa mein pahunch kar unhonne harek vainych ko dekhaa, chaaron taraph ghoome, bahut se aadmi ghaas par pade hue the; par siyaaraam ka nishaan n thaa. unhonne siyaaraam ka naam le lekar jor se pukaaraa; par kaheen se aavaaj n aaee.

khyaal kamaaeeaa shaayad skool mein koee tamaashaa ho rahaa ho. skool ek meel se kuchh jyaadaa hee thaa. skool ki taraph chale; par aadhe raaste hee se laut pade. baajaar band ho gayaa thaa. skool mein itanee raat tak tamaashaa nahin ho sakataa. ab ki unhen ummeed ho rahee thi ki siyaaraam laut kamaaeeaa hogaa. dvaar par aakar unhonne pukaaraa -- siyaa baaboo aae? kivaad band the. koee aavaaj n aaee. phir jor se pukaaraa. bhungggee kivaad khol kar bolee -- abhee to nahin aae. munshee jee ne dheere se bhungggee ko apane paas bulaayaa; aur karun-svar mein bole too to ghar ki sab baat jaanatee hai; bataa aaj kyaa huaa tha?

bhungggee-baaboo jee, jhooth n boloonggee; maalikin chhudaa denggee aur kyaa, doosare ka ladakaa is tarah nahin rakkhaa jaataa. jahaan koee kaam huaa, bas baajaar bhej diyaa. din bhar baajaar daudate beetataa thaa. aaj lakadee laane n gae, to choolhaa hee nahin jalaa. kaho to munh phulaaven. jab aap hee nahin dekhate, to doosaraa kaun dekhegaa. chalie khana kar leejie, bahoo jee kab se baithee hain.

munshee jee kah de is vakt n khaayengge.

munshee jee phir apane kamare mein chale gae; aur ek lambee saans lee. dard se bhare hue ye shabd unake munh se nikal pade-Bhagwan, kyaa abhee sajaa pooraa nahin huaa? kyaa is andhe ki lakadee ka bhi haath se chheen loge?

Nirmala ne aakar kahaa-aaj siyaaraam abhee tak nahin aae. kahatee rahee ki khana banaae detee hoon, khaa lo; magar n jaane kab uth kar chal die. n jaane kahaan ghoom rahe hain. baat to sunate hee naheen. ab kab tak unakee raah dekhaa karoon. aap chal kar khaa leejie. unake lie khana uthaa kar rakh doonggee.

munshee jee ne Nirmala ki or sakht aangkhenon se dekh kar kahaa-abhee kai baje hongge?

Nirmala-kyaa jaane, shaayad das baje hongge.

munshee jee-jee nahin, baarah baje hain. Nirmala-baarah! baarah baj gae! itanee der to kabhee n karate the. to ab kab tak unakee raah dekhoge! dopahar ko bhi to kuchh nahin khaayaa thaa. aisaa sailaanee ladakaa mainne nahin dekhaa.

munshee jee-jee tumhe bahut dik karataa hai, kyon?

Nirmala-dekhie n, itanee raat gaee; aur ghar ki sudh hee naheen.

munshee jee-shaayad yah aakhiree sharaarat ho.

Nirmala-kaisee baaten munh se nikaalate hain. jaayangge kahaan? kisee yaar dost ke ghar pad rahe hongge.

munshee jee-shaayad aisaa hee ho. Bhagwan kare aisaa hee ho.

Nirmala-sabere aaven; to jaraa tambeeh keejiegaa.

munshee jee-khoob achchhi tarah karoonggaa.

Nirmala-chalie khaa leejie, der bahut huee.

munshee jee-savere usakee tamveeh karake khaaoonggaa. kaheen n kamaaeeaa, to tumhe aisaa eemaanadaar naukar kahaan milegaa.

Nirmala ne ainth kar kahaa-to kyaamainne bhagaa diyaa?

munshee jee-nahin, yah kaun kahataa hai? tum use kyon bhagaane lagee? tumhaaraa to kaam karataa thaa. shaamat aa gaee hogee.

Nirmala ne aur kuchh nahin kahaa. baat badh jaane ka dar thaa. bheetar chalee aaee. sone ko bhi n kahaa. jaraa der mein bhungggee ne andar se kivaad bhi band kar die! kyaa munshee jee ko neend aa sakatee thi? teen ladakon mein keval ek bach rahaa thaa. vah haath se nikal gayaa, to phir zindagi mein andhakaar ke sivaa aur kyaa hai? koee naam lene vaalaa bhi n rahegaa. haa! kaise-kaise ratn haath se nikal gae. munshee jee ki aangkhon se yadi is samay ashrudhaaraa vah rahee thi, to koee hairaanee hai? us vyaapak baadataap, us saghan glaani-timir mein ummeed ki ek halakee-see rekhaa unhen sambhaale hue thi! jis pal yah rekhaa lup ho jaayagee, kaun kah sakataa hai-un par kyaa veetegee? unakee us dard ki khayaal kaun kar sakataa hai?

kaee baar munshee jee ki aankhen jhapakee, lekin har baarasiyaaraam ki aahat ke dhokhe mein chaungk pade!

raat ke baarah vaje munshee jee ghar laute, daravaaje par laalaten jal rahee thi, Nirmala dvaar par khadee thee. dekhate hee bolee-kahaa bhi nahin, n jaane kab chal die? kuchh pataa chalaa?

munshee jee ne aagney aangkhenon se taakate hue kahaa-hat jaao saamane se, nahin to buraa hogaa. main aape mein nahin hoon. yah tumhaaree karanee hai. tumhaare hee vajah aaj meree yah dashaa ho rahee hai. aaj se chhah saal pahale kyaa is ghar ki yahee dashaa thi? tumane meraa banaa-banaayaa ghar bigaad diyaa, tumane mere lahalahaate hue baaraa ko ujaad daalaa. keval ek dhoonth rah gayaa hai. usakaa nishaan mitaa kar tabhee tumhe santosh hogaa. main apanaa sarvanaash karane ke lie tumhe apane ghar nahin laayaa thaa. sukhee zindagi ko aur bhi sukhamay banaanaa chaahataa thaa. yah usee ka praayashchitt hai. jo ladake paan ki tarah phere jaate the, unhen mere jeete jee tumane chaakar samajh liyaa; aur main aangkhon se sab kuchh dekhate hue bhi andhaa banaa baithaa rahaa. jaao, mere lie thodaa saa sahiyaa bhej do. bas, yahee kasar gaee hai; vah bhi pooree ho jaaya.

Nirmala ne rote hue kahaa-main to abhaagin hoon hee, aap kahengge tab jaanoonggee? n jaane Bhagwan ne meraa paidaa honaa kyon diyaa thaa. magar yah aapane kaise samajh liyaa ki siyaaraam ab aage hee nahin?

munshee jee ne apane kamare ki or jaate hue kahaa-jalaao mat, jaakar khushiyaan manaao. tumhaaree manokaamanaa pooree hogaee!
