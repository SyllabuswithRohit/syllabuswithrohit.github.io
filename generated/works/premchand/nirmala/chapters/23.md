# Nirmala — Adhyay 23

**Munshi Premchand**

teeesavaan parichchhed

munshee jee paanch baje kachaharee se laute; aur andar aakar chaarapaaee par gir pa. de. bu. dhaape kee deh us par aaj saare din khaanaa na milaa. munh sookh gayaa thaa. nirml samajh gaee, aaj din khaalee gayaa.

nirml ne poochhaa—aaj kuchh na milaa?

munshee jee—saaraa din dau. date guzr; par haath kuchh na lagaa.

nirml—phauzadaaree vaale maamale men kyaa huaa?

munshee jee—mere muvakkil ko sazaa ho gaee.

nirml—aur pandit vaale muqadame men?

munshee jee—pandit par digree ho gaee.

nirml—aap to kahate the daavaa Kaarij ho jaayagaa.

munshee jee—kahataa to thaa; aur ab bhee kahataa hoon ki daavaa Kaarij ho jaanaa chaahie thaa; magar utn sira-maGazan kaun kare?
nirml -- aur us seer vaale daave men?

munshee jee -- usamen bhee haar ho gaee.

nirml -- to aaj aap kisee abhaage kaa munh dekh kar uthe the.

munshee jee se ab kaam bilakul na ho sakataa thaa. ek to unake paas muqadame aate hee na the; aur jo aate bhee the, woh biga. d jaate the. magar apanee asaphalataaon ko woh nirml se chhipaate rahate the. jis din kuchh haath na lagataa, us din kisee se do-chaar rupae udhaar laakar nirml ko de dete. praayah sabhee mitron se kuchh na kuchh le chuke the. aaj woh daul bhee na lagaa.

nirml ne chintaapoorn aavaaz men kahaa -- aamadanee kaa yeh haal hai, to eeshvar hee maalik hai; us par bete kaa yeh haal hai ki baazaar jaanaa mushkila. bhungee hee se sab kaam karaane kaa jee chaahataa hai. ghee lekar gyaarah baje laute. kitn kah kar haar gaee ki laka. dee lete aao; par sunaa hee naheen.

munshee jee -- to khaanaa naheen pakaayaa?

nirml -- aisee hee baaton se to aap muqadame haarate hain. eendhan ke binaa kisee ne khaanaa banaayaa hai ki main hee bn letee!

munshee jee -- to binaa kuchh khaae hee chl gayaa?

nirml -- ghar men aur kyaa rakkhaa thaa jo khilaa detee?

munshee jee ne darate-darate kahaa -- kuchh paise-vaise na de die?

nirml ne bhaunhen siko. d kar kahaa -- ghar men paise phalate hain na?

munshee jee ne kuchh javaab na diyaa. jr der tak to intazaar karate rahe ki shaayad jala-paan ke lie kuchh milegaa; lekin jab nirml ne paanee tak na mangavaayaa, to bechaare niraash hokar baahar chale gae. siyaaraam ke takaleeph kaa anumaan karake unakaa chitt chanchal ho uthaa. saaraa din guzar gayaa, bechaare ne abhee tak kuchh naheen khaayaa. kamare men pa. daa hogaa. ek baar bhungee hee se laka. dee mangaa lee jaatee, to aisaa kyaa nukasaan ho jaataa. aisee kifaayat bhee kis kaam kee ki ghar ke aadamee bhookhe rah jaayan. apn sandooqachaa khol kar tatolane lage ki shaayad do-chaar aane paise mil jaayan. usake andar ke saare kaaGaz nikaal daale, ek-ek khaanaa dekhaa, neeche haath daal kar dekhaa; par kuchh na milaa. agar nirml ke sandook men paise na phalate the, to is sandooqache men shaayad isake phool bhee na lagate hon; lekin sanyog hee kahiye ki kaaGazon ko jhaa. date hue ek chavannee gir pa. dee. maare harsh ke munshee jee uchhal pa. de. ba. dee-ba. dee raqamen isake pahale kamaa chuke the; par yeh chavannee paakar is samay unhen jitn aahlaad huaa, utn pahale kabhee na huaa thaa. chavannee haath men lie hue siyaaraam ke kamare ke saamane aakar pukaaraa. koee jabaab na milaa. tab kamare men jaakar dekhaa. siyaaraam kaa kaheen pataa naheen -- kyaa abhee skool se naheen lautaa? man men yeh savaal uthate hee munshee jee ne andar jaakar bhungee se poochhaa. maaloom huaa ki skool se laut aae.

munshee jee ne poochhaa -- kuchh paanee piyaa hai?

bhungee ne kuchh javaab na diyaa. naak siko. d kar munh phere hue chalee gaee.

munshee jee aahistaa-aahistaa aakar apane kamare men baith gae. aaj pahalee baar unhen nirml par gussaa aayaa; lekin ek hee pal men gussaa kaa aaghaat apane oopar hone lagaa. us andhere kamare men farsh par lete hue woh apane ko betaa kee ora se itane udaaseen ho jaane par dhikkaarane lage. din bhar ke thake the. tho. dee hee der men unhen neend aa gaee.

bhungee ne aakar pukaaraa -- baaboo jee, rasoee taiyaar hai. munshee jee chaunk kar uth baithe. kamare men laimp jal rahaa thaa.

poochhaa -- kai baj gae bhungee. mujhe to neend aa gaee thee.

bhungee ne kahaa -- kotavaalee ke ghante men to nau baj gae hain; aur hum naaheen jaanita.

munshee jee -- siyaa baaboo aae?

bhungee -- aae honge to ghar hee men na honge.

munshee jee ne jhu. cjhl kar poochhaa -- main poochhataa hoo. c aae ki naheen? aur too na jaane kyaa-kyaa javaab detee hai? aae ki naheen?

bhungee -- mainne to naheen dekhaa, jhooth kaise kah doo. c.

munshee jee phir let gae aur bole -- unako aa jaane de, tab chalataa hoon.

aadh ghante tak dvaar kee ora aankh lagaae munshee jee lete rahe. tab woh uth kar baahar aae; aur daahine haath koee do

farlaang tak chale. tab laut kar dvaar par aae aur; poochhaa -- siyaa baaboo aa gae?

andar se javaab aayaa -- abhee naheen.

munshee jee phir baaneen ora chale aur galee ke nukka. d tak gae. siyaaraam kaheen na dikhaaee diyaa. vahaan se phir ghar laute; aur dvaar par kha. de hokar poochhaa -- siyaa baaboo aa gae?

andar se javaab milaa -- naheen.

kotavaalee ke ghante men das bajane lage.

munshee jee ba. de veg se kampanee baag kee taraf chale. sochane lage shaayad vahaan ghoomane gayaa ho; aur ghaas par lete-lete neend aa gaee ho. baaraa men pahunch kar unhonne harek vainch ko dekhaa, chaaron taraf ghoome, bahut se aadamee ghaas par pa. de hue the; par siyaaraam kaa nishaan na thaa. unhonne siyaaraam kaa naam le lekar zor se pukaaraa; par kaheen se aavaaz na aaee.

khyaal aayaa shaayad skool men koee tamaashaa ho rahaa ho. skool ek meel se kuchh jyaadaa hee thaa. skool kee taraf chale; par aadhe raaste hee se laut pa. de. baazaar band ho gayaa thaa. skool men itanee raat tak tamaashaa naheen ho sakataa. ab kee unhen ummeed ho rahee thee ki siyaaraam laut aayaa hogaa. dvaar par aakar unhonne pukaaraa -- siyaa baaboo aae? kivaa. d band the. koee aavaaz na aaee. phir zor se pukaaraa. bhungee kivaa. d khol kar bolee -- abhee to naheen aae. munshee jee ne dheere se bhungee ko apane paas

bulaayaa; aur karuna-aavaaz men bole too to ghar kee sab baat jaanatee hai; bataa aaj kyaa huaa thaa?

bhungee-baaboo jee, jhooth na boloongee; maalikin chhu. daa dengee aur kyaa, doosare kaa la. dakaa is tarah naheen rakkhaa jaataa. jahaan koee kaam huaa, bas baajaar bhej diyaa. din bhar baazaar dau. date beetataa thaa. aaj laka. dee laane na gae, to choolhaa hee naheen jalaa. kaho to munh phulaaven. jab aap hee naheen dekhate, to doosr kaun dekhegaa. chalie khaanaa kar leejie, bahoo jee kab se baithee hain.

munshee jee kah de is vakt na khaayenge.

munshee jee phir apane kamare men chale gae; aur ek lambee saans lee. dard se bhare hue ye shabd unake munh se nikal pa. de-eeshvar, kyaa abhee sazaa pooraa naheen huaa? kyaa is andhe kee laka. dee kaa bhee haath se chheen loge?

nirml ne aakar kahaa-aaj siyaaraam abhee tak naheen aae. kahatee rahee ki khaanaa banaae detee hoon, khaa lo; magar na jaane kab uth kar chal die. na jaane kahaan ghoom rahe hain. baat to sunate hee naheen. ab kab tak unakee raah dekhaa karoon. aap chal kar khaa leejie. unake lie khaanaa uthaa kar rakh doongee.

munshee jee ne nirml kee ora kathor netron se dekh kar kahaa-abhee kai baje honge?

nirmalaa-kyaa jaane, shaayad das baje honge.

munshee jee-jee naheen, baarah baje hain.
nirmalaa-baarah! baarah baj gae! itanee der to kabhee na karate the. to ab kab tak unakee raah dekhoge! dopahar ko bhee to kuchh naheen khaayaa thaa. aisaa sailaanee la. dakaa mainne naheen dekhaa.

munshee jee-jee tumhen bahut diq karataa hai, kyon?

nirmalaa-dekhie na, itanee raat gaee; aur ghar kee sudh hee naheen.

munshee jee-shaayad yeh aakhiree sharaarat ho.

nirmalaa-kaisee baaten munh se nikaalate hain. jaayange kahaan? kisee yaar dost ke ghar pa. d rahe honge.

munshee jee-shaayad aisaa hee ho. eeshvar kare aisaa hee ho.

nirmalaa-sabere aaven; to zr tambeeh keejiegaa.

munshee jee-khoob achchhee tarah karoongaa.

nirmalaa-chalie khaa leejie, der bahut huee.

munshee jee-savere usakee tamveeh karake khaaoongaa. kaheen na aayaa, to tumhen aisaa eemaanadaar naukar kahaan milegaa.

nirml ne ainth kar kahaa-to kyaamainne bhagaa diyaa?

munshee jee-naheen, yeh kaun kahataa hai? tum use kyon bhagaane lagee? tumhaaraa to kaam karataa thaa. shaamat aa gaee hogee.

nirml ne aur kuchh naheen kahaa. baat ba. dh jaane kaa dar thaa. bheetar chalee aaee. sone ko bhee na kahaa. jr der men bhungee ne andar se kivaa. d bhee band kar die!
kyaa munshee jee ko neend aa sakatee thee? teen la. dakon men sirph ek bach rahaa thaa. woh haath se nikal gayaa, to phir jeevan men andhakaar ke sivaa aur kyaa hai? koee naam lene vaalaa bhee na rahegaa. haa! kaise-kaise ratn haath se nikal gae. munshee jee kee aankhon se yadi is samay ashrudhaaraa woh rahee thee, to koee hairaanee hai? us vyaapak pashchaattaap, us saghan pachhataavaa-timir men ummeed kee ek halakee-see rekhaa unhen sanbhaale hue thee! jis pal yeh rekhaa lup ho jaayagee, kaun kah sakataa hai-un par kyaa veetegee? unakee us dard kee kalpn kaun kar sakataa hai?

kaee baar munshee jee kee aankhen jhapakee, lekin har baarasiyaaraam kee aahat ke dhokhe men chaunk pa. de!

saberaa hote hee munshee jee phir siyaaraam ko khojane nikale. kisee se poochhate sharm aatee thee. kis munh se poochhe? unhen kisee se hamadardee kee ummeed na thee. prakat na kah kar man men sad yahee kahege-jaisaa kiyaa, vaisaa bhogo. saare din woh skool ke maidaanon, baajaaron aur vaageechon kaa chakar lagaate rahe. do din niraahaar rahane par bhee unhen itanee shakti kaise huee, yeh vahee jaanen.

raat ke baarah vaje munshee jee ghar laute, daravaaje par laalaten jal rahee thee, nirml dvaar par kha. dee thee. dekhate hee bolee-kahaa bhee naheen, na jaane kab chal die? kuchh pataa chl?

munshee jee ne aagney netron se taakate hue kahaa-hat jaao saamane se, naheen to buraa hogaa. main aape men naheen hoon. yeh tumhaaree
karanee hai. tumhaare hee vajah aaj meree yeh haal ho rahee hai. aaj se chhah saal pahale kyaa is ghar kee yahee haal thee? tumane meraa banaa-banaayaa ghar bigaa. d diyaa, tumane mere lahalahaate hue baaraa ko ujaa. d daalaa. sirph ek dhoonth rah gayaa hai. usakaa nishaan mitaa kar tabhee tumhen santosh hogaa. main apn sarvanaash karane ke lie tumhen apane ghar naheen laayaa thaa. sukhee jeevan ko aur bhee sukhamay banaanaa chaahataa thaa. yeh usee kaa praayashchitt hai. jo la. dake paan kee tarah phere jaate the, unhen mere jeete jee tumane chaakar samajh liyaa; aur main aankhon se sab kuchh dekhate hue bhee andhaa bn baithaa rahaa. jaao, mere lie tho. daa saa sahiyaa bhej do. bas, yahee kasar gaee hai; woh bhee pooree ho jaaya.

nirml ne rote hue kahaa-main to abhaagin hoon hee, aap kahenge tab jaanoongee? na jaane eeshvar ne meraa janm kyon diyaa thaa. magar yeh aapane kaise samajh liyaa ki siyaaraam ab aage hee naheen?

munshee jee ne apane kamare kee ora jaate hue kahaa-jalaao mat, jaakar khushiyaan manaao. tumhaaree manokaamn pooree hogaee!
