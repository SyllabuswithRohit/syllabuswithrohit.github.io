# Nirmala — Adhyay 11

**Munshi Premchand**

gyaarahavaan parichchhed

munshee totaaraam sandhyaa samay kachaharee se ghar pahunche, to Nirmala ne poochhaa — unhen dekhaa, kyaa haal hai? munshee jee ne dekhaa ki Nirmala ke munh par naamamaatr ko bhi shok ya fikr ka chihn nahin hai, usakaa banaav-singggaar aur dinon se bhi kuchh gaadhaa huaa hai — masalan vah gale mein haar n pahanatee thi; par aaj vah bhi gale mein shobhaa de rahaa thaa. jhoomar se bhi use bahut pyaar n tha; par aaj vah bhi maheen reshamee saadee ke neeche, kaale-kaale keshon ke oopar, phaanoos ke deepak ki bhaanti chamak rahaa thaa.

munshee jee ne munh pher kar kahaa — beemaar hai, aur kyaa haal bataaoon?

Nirmala — tum to unhen yahan laane gae the?

munshee jee ne jhunjhalaa kar kahaa — vah nahin kamaaeeaa, to kyaa main jabaradastee uthaa laataa? kitanaa samajhaayaa ki betaa ghar chalo, wahan tumhe koee takaleeph n hone paavegee; lekin ghar ka naam sun kar use jaise doonaa jvar ho jaataa thaa. kahane lagaa -- main yahan mar jaaoonggaa; lekin ghar n jaaooegaa. aakhir majaboor hokar aspataal pahunchaa kamaaeeaa; aur kyaa karataa?

rukminee bhi aakar baraamade mein khadee ho ganee theen; boleen -- vah janam ka hathee hai, yahan kisee tarah n aavegaa aur yah bhi dekh lenaa, wahan achchha bhi n hogaa!

munshee jee ne kaatar svar mein kahaa -- tum do-chaar din ke liye vahaae chalee jaao, to badaa achchha ho; bahin! tumhaare rahane se use taskeen hotee rahegee. meree vahin, meree yah vinay maan lo. akele vah ro-rokar praan de degaa. bas, haay ammaan! haay ammaan! ki rat lagaa-lagaa kar royaa karataa hai. main vaheen jaa rahaa hoon, mere saath hee chalee chalo! usakee dashaa achchhi nahin! bahin, vah soorat hee nahin rahee! dekhen Bhagwan kyaa karate hain?

yah kahate-kahate munshee jee ki aangkhon se aansoo bahane lage; lekin rukminee avichalit bhaav se boleen -- main jaane ko taiyaar hoon. mere vaho rahane se agar mere laal ke praan bach jaayan, to main sir ke bal daudee jaaoon; lekin meraa kahanaa girah baaedh lo bhaiyaa! wahan vah achchha n hogaa. main use khoob pahachaanatee hoon. use koee beemaaree nahin hai, keval ghar se nikaale jaane ka shok hai. yahee dukh jvar ke roop mein saamane huaa hai. tum ek nahin, laakh davaa karo -- sivil-sarjan ko hee kyon n dikhaao, use koee davaa asar n karegee! munshee jee -- bahin, use ghar se nikaalaa kisane hai? mainne to keval usakee padhaaee ke khyaal se use wahan bhejaa thaa.

rukminee -- tumane chaahe jis khyaal se bhejaa ho; lekin yah baat use lag gaee hai. main to ab kisee ginatee mein nahin hoon, mujhe kisee baat mein bolane ka koee hak nahin! maalik tum, maalikin tumhaaree aurat!! main to keval tumhaaree rotiyon par padee huee abhaaginee vidhavaa hoon! meree kaun sunegaa aur kaun parvaah karegaa? lekin binaa bole rahaa nahin jaataa! mansaa tabhee achchha hogaa, jab ghar aavegaa -- jab tumhaaraa dil vahee ho jaayagaa, jo pahale thaa.

yah kah kar rukminee wahan se chalee gaee. unakee jyoti-heen par tajurbaapoorn aangkhon ke saamane jo charitr ho rahe the, unakaa rahasy vah khoob samajhatee thi; aur unakaa saaraa krodh niraparaadhinee Nirmala hee par utarataa thaa. is samay bhi vah yah kahate-kahate ruk gaee ki jab tak yah lakshmee is ghar mein rahenggee, is ghar ki dashaa bigadatee hee jaayagee; par usake saamane roop se n kahane par bhi usakaa aashay munshee jee se chhipaa nahin rahaa. unake chale jaane par munshee jee ne sir jhukaa liyaa aur sochane lage. unhen apane oopar is samay itanaa krodh aa rahaa tha ki deevaar se sir patak kar praanon ka ant kar den. unhonne kyon shaadee kiyaa tha? shaadee karane ki kyaa zaroorat thi, Bhagwan ne unhen ek nahin, teen-teen putr die the. unakee avasthaa bhi 50 ke lagabhag pahunch gaee thi, phir unhonne kyon shaadee kiyaa? kyaa isee bahaane Bhagwan ko unakaa sarvanaash karanaa manjoor tha? unhonne sir uthaakar ek baar Nirmala ki sahaas -- par nishchal moorti dekhee aur aspataal chale gae. Nirmala ki sahaas-chhavi ne unakaa chitt shaant kar diyaa thaa. aaj kaee dinon ke baad unhen yah shaanti muyassar huee thee. pyaar-peedit dil is dashaa mein kyaa itanaa shaant aur avichalit rah sakataa hai? nahin, kabhee nahin! dil ki chot bhaav-kaushal se nahin chhipaaee jaa sakatee. apane chitt ki kamajorataa par is samay unhen bahut kshobh huaa. unhonne avajah hee shak ko dil mein jagah dekar itanaa anarth kiyaa. mansaaraam ki or se bhi unakaa man nihshanggk ho gayaa. ho, usakee jagah ab ek naee shanggkaa utpann ho gaee. kyaa mansaaraam bhaamp to nahin gayaa? kyaa bhaamp kar hee to ghar aane se ingkaar nahin kar rahaa hai? agar vah bhop gayaa hai, to mahaan anarth ho jaayagaa. usakee khayaal hee se unakaa man dahal uthaa. unakee deh ki saaree haddiyaan maano is haahaakaar par paani daalane ke lie vyaakul ho uthee! unhonne kochavaan se ghode ko tej chalaane ko kahaa. aaj kaee dinon ke baad unake dilamandal par chhaayaa huaa saghan-ghan phat gayaa tha; aur prakaash ki laharen andar se nikalane ke liye vyagr ho rahee theen. unhonne baahar sir nikaal kar dekhaa, kochavaan so to nahin rahaa hai. ghode ki chaal unhen itanee mand kabhee n maaloom huee thee.

aspataal pahunch kar vah lapake hue mansaaraam ke paas gae. dekhaa to doktar Sahab usake saamane fikr mein magn khade the. munshee jee ke haath-paanv phool gae. munh se shabd n nikal sakaa. bharabharaaee huee aavaaj mein badee mushkil se bole -- kyaa haal hai, doktar Sahab? yah kahate-kahate vah ro pade aur jab doktar Sahab ko unake savaal ka javaab dene mein ek pal ka der huaa, tab to unake praan nahon mein samaa gae. unhonne palanggg par baith kar achet bachcha ko god mein uthaa liyaa; aur bachchaaon ki bhaanti sisak-sisak kar rone lage!! mansaaraam ki deh tave ki tarah jal rahee thee. mansaaraam ne ek baar aankhen kholeen. aah! kitanee daranggkar aur usake saath hee kitanee deen drishti thi! munshee jee ne bachcha ko kanth se lagaa kar doktar se poochhaa -- kyaa haal hai Sahab, aap chup kyon hain?

doktar ne sandigdh svar mein kahaa -- haal jo kuchh hai, vah aap dekh hee rahe hain. 106 digree ka jvar hai; aur main kyaa batalaaoon? abhee jvar ka prakop badhataa hee jaataa hai. mere kie jo kuchh ho sakataa hai, kar rahaa hoon. Bhagwan maalik hain! jab se aap gae hain, main ek minit ke lie bhi yahan se nahin hilaa. khana tak nahin kar sakaa. haalat itanee naajuk hai ki ek minit mein kyaa ho jaayagaa, nahin kahaa jaa sakataa. yah mahaajvar hai, bilakul hosh nahin hai. rah-rah kar dileeriyam (Delirium) ka dauraa saa ho jaataa hai. kyaa ghar mein inhen kisee ne kuchh kahaa hai! baar-baar ammaan jee, tum kah ho? yahee aavaaj munh se nikalatee hai!

doktar Sahab yah kah hee rahe the ki sahasaa mansaaraam uth kar baith gayaa; aur ek dhakk se munshee jee ko chaarapaaee ke naa dil dhakel kar unmatt svar mein bolaa -- kyon dhamakaate hain? aap maar daalie, maar daalie, abhee maar daalie!! talavaar nahin milatee? rassee ka phandaa hai ya vah bhi nahin? mai apane gale mein lagaa loonggaa. haay ammaae jee, tum kahaan ho? yah kahate-kahate vah phir achet hokar gir padaa!

munshee jee ek pal tak mansaaraam ki shithil mudraa ki or vyathit aangkhenon se taakate rahe; phir sahasaa unhonne doktar Sahab ka haath pakad liyaa; aur bahut bechaarageepoorn jor dekar kahanaa se bole -- doktar Sahab, is ladake ko bachaa leejie -- Bhagwan ke lie bachaa leejie, nahin meraa sarvanaash ho jaayagaa! main ameer nahin hoon; lekin
aap jo kuchh kahengge, vah haajir karoonggaa; ise bachaa leejie! aap bade se bade doktaron ko bulaaie, aur unakee Rai leejie; main sab kharch de doonggaa! isakee yah dashaa ab nahin dekhee jaatee! haay, meraa honahaar betaa!

doktar Sahab ne karun-svar mein kahaa -- baaboo Sahab, main aap se sach kahataa hoon ki main inake lie apanee taraph se koee baat uthaa nahin rakh rahaa hoon. ab aap doosare doktaron se salaah lene kahate hain. main abhee doktar lahiree, doktar bhaatiyaa aur doktar maathur ko bulaataa hoon. vinaayak shaaaurat ko bhi bulaae letaa hoon; lekin main aapko vyarth ka aashvaasan nahin denaa chaahataa -- haalat naajuk hai. maa munshee jee ne rote hue kahaa-nahin, doktar Sahab, yah shabd munh se n nikaalie. haalat isake dushmanon ki naajuk ho. Bhagwan mujh par itanaa kop n karengge. aap kalakattaa aur bambaee ke doktaron ko taar deejie. main jindagee bhar aapakee gulaamee karoonggaa. yahee mere kul ka deepak hai! yahee mere zindagi ka aadhaar hai! meraa dil phataa jaa rahaa hai! koee aisee davaa deejie, jisase ise hosh aa jaaya. main jaraa apane kaanon se usakee baaten sunen! jaanoon ki use kyaa takaleeph ho rahaa hai! haay, meraa bachcha!!

doktar-aap jaraa dil ko taskeen deejie. aap bujurg aadmi hain, yon haay-haay karane se aur doktaron ki phauj jamaa karane se koee nateejaa n nikalegaa. shaant hokar baithie, main shahar ke logon ko bulaa rahaa hoon; dekhie, ve kyaa kahate hain? aap to khud hee badahavaas hue jaate hain.

munshee jee-achchha doktar Sahab, main ab n boloonggaa, jabaan tak n kholoonggaa, aap jo chaahen karen, bachcha ab aapake haath mein hai. aap hee usakee bachaav kar sakate hain. main itanaa hee chaahataa hoon ki jaraa ise hosh aa jaay, mujhe pahachaan le, meree baaten samajhane lage. kyaa koee aisee davaa nahin? koee aisee sajindageeee bootee nahin? bas, main isase do-chaar baaten kar letaa!

yah kahate-kahate munshee jee phir aavesh mein aakar mansaaraam se bole-betaa, jaraa aankhen kholo, kaisaa jee hai? main tumhaare paas baithaa huaa ro rahaa hoon, mujhe tumase koee shikaayat nahin hai, meraa dil tumhaaree or se saaph hai.

doktar-phir aapane anargal baaten karanee shuroo kee. are Sahab, aap bachche nahin hain-bujurg aadmi hain, jaraa dhairy se kaam leejie!

munshee jee-achchha doktar Sahab, ab n boloonggaa, khataa huee. aap jo chaahen keejie. mainne sab kuchh aap par chhod diyaa. koee aisaa raastaa nahin hai, jisase main ise itanaa samajhaa sakoon ki meraa dil saaph hai. aap hee kah deejie doktar Sahab! kah deejie, tumhaaraa abhaagaa pitaa baithaa ro rahaa hai. usakaa dil tumhaaree taraph se bilakul saaph hai. use kuchh bhram huaa tha, vah ab door ho gayaa. bas, itanaa hee kah deejie. main aur kuchh nahin chaahataa. main chupachaap baithaa hoon. jabaan tak nahin kholataa; lekin aap itanaa jaroor kah deejie!

doktar-Bhagwan ke lie baaboo Sahab! jaraa sabr keejie, varanaa mujhe majaboor hokar aapase kahanaa padegaa ki ghar jaaie. main jaraa daphtar mein jaakar doktaron ko khat likh rahaa hoon. aap chupachaap baithe rahiegaa.

nirdayee doktar! javaan bete ki yah dashaa dekh kar kaun pitaa hai, jo dhairy se kaam legaa? munshee jee bahut seeriyas svabhaav ke insaan the. yah bhi jaanate the ki is vakt haay-haay machaane se koee nateejaa nahin; lekin phir bhi is samay shaant baithanaa unake lie asambhav thaa. agar daiv-gati se yah beemaaree hotee, to vah shaant ho sakate the, doosaron ko samajhaa sakate the, khud doktaron ko bulaa sakate the, lekin kyaa yah jaan kar bhi vah dhairy rakh sakate the ki yah sab aag meree hee lagaaee huee hai? koee pitaa itanaa vajr-dil ho sakataa hai? unakaa rom-rom is vakt unhen. dhikkaar rahaa thaa. unhonne sochaa, mujhamen yah durehasaas utpann hee kyon huee? mainne kyon binaa kisee pratyaksh saboot ke aisee bheeshan khayaal kar daalee? achchha, mujhe us dashaa mein kyaa karanaa chahiye tha? jo kuchh unhonne kiyaa, usake sivaa vah aur kyaa karate isakaa vah pakkaa faisla n kar sake. vaastav mein shaadee ke bandhan mein padanaa hee apane pairon mein kulhaadee maaranaa thaa. haan, yahee saare upadrav ki jad hai!

magar mainne yah koee anokhee baat nahin kee. sabhee aurat-mard shaadee karate hain. unakaa zindagi khushi se katataa hai. khushi ki ichchhaa se hee to ham shaadee karate hain. isee muhalle mein saikadon aadamiyon ne doosaree, teesaree, chauthee yahan tak ki saataveen shaadiyaan ki hain; aur mujhase bhi kaheen adhik avasthaa mein! vah jab tak jiye aaraam hee se jiye. yah bhi nahin huaa ki sabhee aurat se pahale mar gae hon. duhaaj-tihaaj hone par bhi kitane hee phir randuve ho gae. agar meree jaisee dashaa sab ki hotee, shaadee ka naam hee kaun letaa? mere pitaa jee hee ne pachapanaven varsh mein shaadee kiyaa tha aur mere paidaa honaa ke samay unakee avasthaa saath se kam n thee. haan, itanee vaat zaroor hai ki tab aur ab mein kuchh antar ho gayaa hai. pahale striyaan padhee-likhee n hotee theen. shauhar chaahe kaisaa hee ho, use poojy samajhatee theen ya yah baat ho ki mard sab kuchh dekh kar bhi behayaaee se kaam letaa ho; zaroor yahee baat hai. jab javaan aadmi boodhee aurat ke saath khush nahin rah sakataa, to javaan ladakee kyon kisee boodhaa ke saath khush rahane lagee? lekin main to kuchh aisaa buddhaa n thaa. mujhe dekh kar koee chaalees se adhik nahin bataa sakataa. kuchh bhi ho, javaanee dhal jaane par navaan aurat se shaadee karake kuchh n kuchh vehayaaee jaroor karanee padatee hai. isamen shak nahin! aurat svabhaav se sharmasheelaa hotee hai. kulataaon ki baat to doosaree hai; par aamatah aurat mard se kaheen jyaadaa sanyamasheelaa hotee hai. jod ka shauhar paakar vah chaahe par-mard se hansee-dillagee kar le; par usakaa man shuddh rahataa hai. vejod shaadee ho jaane se vah chaahe kisee ki or aankhen uthaa kar n dekhe, par usakaa chitt dukhee rahataa hai. vah pakkee deevaar hai, usamen sabaree ka asar nahin hotaa. yah kachchee deevaar hai aur usee vakt tak khadee rahatee hai, jab tak us par sabaree n chalaaee jaay!

inheen soch mein pade-pade munshee jee ko ek jhapakee aa gaee. man ke bhaavon ne turant svapn ka roop dhaaran kar liyaa. kyaa dekhate hain ki unakee pahalee bee mansaaraam ke saamane khadee kah rahee haisvaamee, yah tumane kyaa kiyaa? jis bachcha ko mainne apanaa rakt pilaa-pilaa kar paalaa, usako tumane itanee nirdayataa se maar daalaa| aise aadarsh-charitr bachcha par tumane itanaa ghor kalanggk lagaa diyaa! ab baithe kyaa bisoorate ho? tumane usase haath dho liyaa. main tumhaare nirday haathon se chheen kar use apane saath lie jaatee hoon. tum to itane shakee kabhee n the, kyaa shaadee karate hee shak ko bhi gale baandh laae? is komal dil par itanaa sakht aaghaat! itanaa bheeshan kalanggk! itanaa badaa beijjatee sah kar jeene vaale koee behayaa hongge! meraa betaa nahin sah sakataa. yah kahate-kahate usane bachcha ko god mein uthaa liyaa aur chalee. munshee jee ne rote hue usakee god se mansaaraam ko chheenane ke lie haath badhaayaa, to aankhen khul gaee aur doktar lahiree; doktar bhaatiyaa aadi aadhe darjan doktar unake saamane khade dikhaaee die!
