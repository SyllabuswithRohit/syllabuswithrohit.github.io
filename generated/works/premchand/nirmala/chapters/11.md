# Nirmala — Adhyay 11

**Munshi Premchand**

gyarhavam parichchhed

munshi NAmETotaram sham samay kachhari se ghar pahunche, to NAmENirmala ne puchhaunhen dekha, kya hal hai? munshi ji ne dekha ki NAmENirmala ke mumh par namamatra ko bhi shok ya chinta ka chihna nahin hai, uska banav-singar aur dinon se bhi kuchh garha hua haimasalan vah gale men har na pahnati thi; par aj vah bhi gale men shobha de raha tha. jhumar se bhi use bahut pyaar na tha; par aj vah bhi mahin reshmi sari ke niche, kale-kale keshon ke upar, fanus ke dipak ki tarah chamak raha tha.

munshi ji ne mumh pher kar kahabimar hai, aur kya hal bataum?

NAmENirmala tum to unhen yaham lane gae the?

munshi ji ne jhunjhla kar kahavah nahin aya, to kya maim zabaradasti utha lata? kitna samjhaya ki beta ghar chalo, vaham tumhen koi taklif na hone pavegi; lekin ghar ka nam sun
kar use jaise duna jvar ho jata tha. kahne laga -- maim yaham mar jaunga; lekin ghar na jauaga. akhir majbur hokar aspatal pahuncha aya; aur kya karta?

NAmERukmini bhi akar baramde men khadi ho gami thim; bolim -- vah janam ka hathi hai, yaham kisi tarah na avega aur yah bhi dekh lena, vaham achchha bhi na hoga!

munshi ji ne katar avaz men kaha -- tum do-char din ke liye vahaa chali jao, to bada achchha ho; bahin! tumhare rahne se use taskin hoti rahegi. meri vahin, meri yah narmi man lo. akele vah ro-rokar jan de dega. bas, hay ammam! hay ammam! ki rat laga-laga kar roya karta hai. maim vahim ja raha hum, mere sath hi chali chalo! uski hal achchhi nahin! bahin, vah surat hi nahin rahi! dekhen ishvar kya karte haim?

yah kahte-kahte munshi ji ki ankhon se amsu bahne lage; lekin NAmERukmini avichlit bhav se bolim -- maim jane ko taiyar hum. mere vaho rahne se agar mere lal ke jan bach jayam, to maim sir ke bal dauri jaum; lekin mera kahna girah baadha lo bhaiya! vaham vah achchha na hoga. maim use khub pahachanti hum. use koi bimari nahin hai, sirph ghar se nikale jane ka shok hai. yahi dukh jvar ke rup men prakat hua hai. tum ek nahin, lakh dava karo -- sivil-sarjan ko hi kyon na dikhao, use koi dava asar na karegi!

munshi ji -- bahin, use ghar se nikala kisne hai? maimne to sirph uski parhai ke khyal se use vaham bheja tha.

NAmERukmini -- tumne chahe jis khyal se bheja ho; lekin yah bat use lag gai hai. maim to ab kisi ginti men nahin hum, mujhe kisi bat men bolne ka koi adhikar nahin! malik tum, malikin tumhari aurat!! maim to sirph tumhari rotiyon par padi hui abhagini vidhva hum! meri kaun sunega aur kaun parvah karega? lekin bina bole raha nahin jata! mansa tabhi achchha hoga, jab ghar avega -- jab tumhara dil vahi ho jayga, jo pahle tha.

yah kah kar NAmERukmini vaham se chali gai. unki jyoti-hin par anubhavapurn ankhon ke samne jo svabhav ho rahe the, unka rahasya vah khub samjhati thi; aur unka sara gussa niraparadhini NAmENirmala hi par utrata tha. is samay bhi vah yah kahte-kahte ruk gai ki jab tak yah lakshmi is ghar men rahengi, is ghar ki hal bigrati hi jaygi; par uske prakat rup se na kahne par bhi uska ashay munshi ji se chhipa nahin raha. unke chale jane par munshi ji ne sir jhuka liya aur sochne lage. unhen apne upar is samay itna gussa a raha tha ki divar se sir patak kar pranon ka ant kar den. unhomne kyon shadi kiya tha? shadi karne ki kya zarurat thi, ishvar ne unhen ek nahin, tin-tin beta die the. unki avastha bhi 50 ke lagbhag pahunch gai thi, phir unhomne kyon shadi kiya? kya isi bahane ishvar ko unka

sarvanash karna manyur tha? unhomne sir uthakar ek bar NAmENirmala ki himmat -- par nishchal murti dekhi aur aspatal chale gae. NAmENirmala ki himmat-chhavi ne unka chitt shant kar diya tha. aj kai dinon ke bad unhen yah shanti muyassar hui thi. pyaar-pirit dil is hal men kya itna shant aur avichlit rah sakta hai? nahin, kabhi nahin! dil ki chot bhav-kaushal se nahin chhipai ja sakti. apne chitt ki durbalta par is samay unhen bahut kshobh hua. unhomne akaran hi shak ko dil men sthan dekar itna anarth kiya. NAmEmansaram ki or se bhi unka man nihshanka ho gaya. ho, uski jagah ab ek nai shanka utpann ho gai. kya NAmEmansaram bhamp to nahin gaya? kya bhamp kar hi to ghar ane se inkar nahin kar raha hai? agar vah bhop gaya hai, to mahan anarth ho jayga. uski kalpana hi se unka man dahal utha. unki deh ki sari haddiyam mano is hahakar par pani dalne ke lie bechain ho uthi! unhomne kochvan se ghore ko tez chalane ko kaha. aj kai dinon ke bad unke hridayamandal par chhaya hua saghan-ghan phat gaya tha; aur prakash ki lahren andar se niklane ke liye vyagra ho rahi thim. unhomne bahar sir nikal kar dekha, kochvan so to nahin raha hai. ghore ki chal unhen itni mand kabhi na malum hui thi.

aspatal pahunch kar vah lapke hue NAmEmansaram ke pas gae. dekha to doktar sahab uske samne chinta men magna khade the.
munshi ji ke hath-pamv phul gae. mumh se shabd na nikal saka. bharabharai hui avaj men badi mushkil se bole -- kya hal hai, doktar sahab? yah kahte-kahte vah ro pade aur jab doktar sahab ko unke saval ka javab dene men ek pal ka vilamb hua, tab to unke jan nahon men sama gae. unhomne palanga par baith kar achet larka ko god men utha liya; aur balkon ki tarah sisak-sisak kar rone lage!! NAmEmansaram ki deh tave ki tarah jal rahi thi. NAmEmansaram ne ek bar ankhen kholim. ah! kitni bhayankar aur uske sath hi kitni bechara nazar thi! munshi ji ne larka ko gala se laga kar doktar se puchha -- kya hal hai sahab, ap chup kyon haim?

doktar ne sandigdh avaz men kaha -- hal jo kuchh hai, vah ap dekh hi rahe haim. 106 digri ka jvar hai; aur maim kya batalaum? abhi jvar ka prakop barhta hi jata hai. mere kie jo kuchh ho sakta hai, kar raha hum. ishvar malik haim! jab se ap gae haim, maim ek minit ke lie bhi yaham se nahin hila. khana tak nahin kar saka. halat itni najuk hai ki ek minit men kya ho jayga, nahin kaha ja sakta. yah mahajvar hai, bilkul hosh nahin hai. rah-rah kar diliriyam (Delirium) ka daura sa ho jata hai. kya ghar men inhen kisi ne kuchh kaha hai! bar-bar ammam ji, tum kah ho? yahi avaz mumh se niklati hai!

doktar sahab yah kah hi rahe the ki sahsa NAmEmansaram uth

kar baith gaya; aur ek dhakk se munshi ji ko charapai ke na dil dhakel kar unmatt avaz men bola -- kyon dhamkate haim? ap mar dalie, mar dalie, abhi mar dalie!! talvar nahin milti? rassi ka phanda hai ya vah bhi nahin? mai apne gale men laga lunga. hay ammaa ji, tum kaham ho? yah kahte-kahte vah phir achet hokar gir pada!

munshi ji ek pal tak NAmEmansaram ki shithil mudra ki or vyathit netron se takte rahe; phir sahsa unhomne doktar sahab ka hath pakad liya; aur bahut dinatapurn agrah se bole -- doktar sahab, is larke ko bacha lijie -- ishvar ke lie bacha lijie, nahin mera sarvanash ho jayga! maim amir nahin hum; lekin
ap jo kuchh kahenge, vah hazir karunga; ise bacha lijie! ap bade se bade doktaron ko bulaie, aur unki ray lijie; maim sab kharch de dunga! iski yah hal ab nahin dekhi jati! hay, mera honhar beta!

doktar sahab ne karun-avaz men kaha -- babu sahab, maim ap se satya kahta hum ki maim inke lie apni taraph se koi bat utha nahin rakh raha hum. ab ap dusre doktaron se salah lene kahte haim. maim abhi doktar lahiri, doktar bhatiya aur doktar mathur ko bulata hum. vinayak shastri ko bhi bulae leta hum; lekin maim apko bekar ka ashvasan nahin dena chahta -- halat nazuk hai.
ma munshi ji ne rote hue kaha-nahin, doktar sahab, yah shabd mumh se na nikalie. halat iske dushmanon ki najuk ho. ishvar mujh par itna kop na karenge. ap kalakatta aur bambai ke doktaron ko tar dijie. maim zindagi bhar apki gulami karunga. yahi mere kul ka dipak hai! yahi mere jivan ka adhar hai! mera dil phata ja raha hai! koi aisi dava dijie, jisse ise hosh a jay. maim jara apne kanon se uski baten sunen! janum ki use kya takliph ho raha hai! hay, mera bachcha!!

doktar-ap jara dil ko taskin dijie. ap bujurg aadmi haim, yon hay-hay karne se aur doktaron ki phauj jama karne se koi natija na niklega. shant hokar baithie, maim shahar ke logon ko bula raha hum; dekhie, ve kya kahte haim? ap to khud hi badahvas hue jate haim.

munshi ji-achchha doktar sahab, maim ab na bolunga, jaban tak na kholunga, ap jo chahen karen, bachcha ab apke hath men hai. ap hi uski raksha kar sakte haim. maim itna hi chahta hum ki jara ise hosh a jay, mujhe pahchan le, meri baten samjhane lage. kya koi aisi dava nahin? koi aisi sajivni buti nahin? bas, maim isse do-char baten kar leta!

yah kahte-kahte munshi ji phir avesh men akar NAmEmansaram se bole-beta, jara ankhen kholo, kaisa ji hai? maim tumhare pas
baitha hua ro raha hum, mujhe tumse koi shikayat nahin hai, mera dil tumhari or se saph hai.

doktar-phir apne anargal baten karni shuru ki. are sahab, ap bachche nahin haim-bujurg aadmi haim, jara sabra se kam lijie!

munshi ji-achchha doktar sahab, ab na bolunga, khata hui. ap jo chahen kijie. maimne sab kuchh ap par chhod diya. koi aisa upay nahin hai, jisse maim ise itna samjha sakum ki mera dil saph hai. ap hi kah dijie doktar sahab! kah dijie, tumhara abhaga pita baitha ro raha hai. uska dil tumhari taraph se bilkul saf hai. use kuchh bhram hua tha, vah ab dur ho gaya. bas, itna hi kah dijie. maim aur kuchh nahin chahta. maim chupchap baitha hum. zaban tak nahin kholta; lekin ap itna jarur kah dijie!

doktar-ishvar ke lie babu sahab! jara sabra kijie, varna mujhe majbur hokar apse kahna parega ki ghar jaie. maim jara daphtar men jakar doktaron ko khat likh raha hum. ap chupchap baithe rahiega.

bedard doktar! javan bete ki yah hal dekh kar kaun pita hai, jo sabra se kam lega? munshi ji bahut gambhir svabhav ke manushya the. yah bhi jante the ki is vakt hay-hay machane se koi natija nahin; lekin phir bhi is samay shant baithna unke
lie namumkin tha. agar daiv-gati se yah bimari hoti, to vah shant ho sakte the, dusron ko samjha sakte the, khud doktaron ko bula sakte the, lekin kya yah jan kar bhi vah sabra rakh sakte the ki yah sab ag meri hi lagai hui hai? koi pita itna vajra-dil ho sakta hai? unka ron-ron is vakt unhen. dhikkar raha tha. unhomne socha, mujhmen yah durbhavna utpann hi kyon hui? maimne kyon bina kisi pratyaksh sabut ke aisi bhishan kalpana kar dali? achchha, mujhe us hal men kya karna chahiye tha? jo kuchh unhomne kiya, uske siva vah aur kya karte iska vah phaisla na kar sake. sach men shadi ke bandhan men parna hi apne pairon men kulhari marna tha. ham, yahi sare upadrav ki jar hai!

magar maimne yah koi anokhi bat nahin ki. sabhi aurat-aadmi shadi karte haim. unka jivan khushi se katta hai. khushi ki chah se hi to ham shadi karte haim. isi muhalle men saikron admiyo ne dusri, tisri, chauthi yaham tak ki satvim shadiyam ki haim; aur mujhse bhi kahim zyada avastha men! vah jab tak jiye aram hi se jiye. yah bhi nahin hua ki sabhi aurat se pahle mar gae hon. duhaj-tihaj hone par bhi kitne hi phir randuve ho gae. agar meri jaisi hal sab ki hoti, shadi ka nam hi kaun leta? mere pita ji hi ne pachapnaven varsh men shadi kiya tha aur mere janma ke samay unki avastha sath se kam na
thi. ham, itni vat zarur hai ki tab aur ab men kuchh antar ho gaya hai. pahle striyam parhi-likhi na hoti thim. pati chahe kaisa hi ho, use pujya samjhati thim ya yah bat ho ki aadmi sab kuchh dekh kar bhi behayai se kam leta ho; zarur yahi bat hai. jab yuvak vriddha ke sath khush nahin rah sakta, to yuvti kyon kisi vriddh ke sath khush rahne lagi? lekin maim to kuchh aisa buddha na tha. mujhe dekh kar koi chalis se zyada nahin bata sakta. kuchh bhi ho, javani dhal jane par navan aurat se shadi karke kuchh na kuchh vehayai jarur karni parti hai. ismen shak nahin! aurat svabhav se lajjashila hoti hai. kulataon ki bat to dusri hai; par sadharnatah aurat aadmi se kahim jyada samyamshila hoti hai. jod ka pati pakad vah chahe par-aadmi se hamsi-dillagi kar le; par uska man shuddh rahta hai. vejor shadi ho jane se vah chahe kisi ki or ankhen utha kar na dekhe, par uska chitt dukhi rahta hai. vah pakki divar hai, usmen sabri ka asar nahin hota. yah kachchi divar hai aur usi vakt tak khadi rahti hai, jab tak us par sabri na chalai jay!

inhim vicharon men pade-pade munshi ji ko ek jhapki a gai. man ke bhavon ne turant svapna ka rup dharan kar liya. kya dekhte haim ki unki pahli bi NAmEmansaram ke samne khadi kah rahi haisvami, yah tumne kya kiya? jis larka ko maimne apna rakt pila-pila kar pala, usko tumne itni nirdayta se mar dala|
aise adarsh-svabhav larka par tumne itna ghor kalanka laga diya! ab baithe kya bisurte ho? tumne usse hath dho liya. maim tumhare nirday hathon se chhin kar use apne sath lie jati hum. tum to itne shaki kabhi na the, kya shadi karte hi shak ko bhi gale bandh lae? is komal dil par itna kathor aghat! itna bhishan kalanka! itna bada beizzati sah kar jine vale koi behya honge! mera beta nahin sah sakta. yah kahte-kahte usne larka ko god men utha liya aur chali. munshi ji ne rote hue uski god se NAmEmansaram ko chhinne ke lie hath barhaya, to ankhen khul gai aur doktar lahiri; doktar bhatiya adi adhe darjan doktar unke samne khade dikhai die!
