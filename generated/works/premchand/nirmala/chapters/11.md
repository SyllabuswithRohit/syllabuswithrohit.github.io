# Chapter 11

gyaarahavaa.N parichchheda

munshee totaaraama shaama samaya kachaharee se ghar pahu.Nche, to nirmalaa ne poochhaa—unhen dekha, kyaa haala hai? munshee jee ne dekha ki nirmalaa ke mu.Nha para naamamaatra ko bhee shoka yaa chintaa kaa chihna naheen hai, usakaa banaava-singaara aur dinon se bhee kuchha gaadhaa hua hai—masalan vaha gale men haara na pahanatee thi; para aaja vaha bhee gale men shobhaa de raha tha| jhoomara se bhee use bahuta pyaara na tha; para aaja vaha bhee maheena reshamee saadee ke neeche, kaale-kaale keshon ke oopara, phaanoosa ke deepaka kee taraha chamaka raha tha|

munshee jee ne mu.Nha phera kar kahaa—beemaara hai, aur kyaa haala bataaoo.N?

nirmalaa—tuma to unhen yahaa.N laane gae the?

munshee jee ne jhu.Njhalaa kar kahaa—vaha naheen aaya, to kyaa main jabaradastee uthaa laataa? kitanaa samajhaayaa ki betaa ghar chalo, vahaa.N tumhen koee takaleepha na hone paavegee; lekin ghar kaa naama suna

kar use jaise doonaa jvara ho jata tha| kahane lagaa -- main yahaa.N mara jaaoo.Ngaa; lekin ghar na jaaooegaa| aakhira majaboora hokara aspataala pahu.Nchaa aaya; aur kyaa karta?

rukmiNee bhee aakara baraamade men khadee ho ganee theen;boleen -- vaha janama kaa hathee hai,yahaa.N kisee taraha na aavegaa aur yaha bhee dekha lenaa, vahaa.N achchhaa bhee na hogaa!

munshee jee ne kaatara aavaaza men kahaa -- tuma do-chaara din ke liye vahaae chalee jaao,to badaa achchhaa ho; bahina! tumhaare rahane se use taskeena hoti rahegee| meree vahina,meree yaha vinaya maana lo| akele vaha ro-rokara praaNa de degaa| basa,haaya ammaa.N! haaya ammaa.N! kee rata lagaa-lagaa kar royaa karta hai| main vaheen jaa raha hoo.N, mere saatha hee chalee chalo! usakee dashaa achchhee naheen! bahina,vaha soorata hee naheen rahi! dekhen eeshvara kyaa karte hain?

yaha kehte-kehte munshee jee kee aa.Nkhon se aa.Nsoo bahane lage; lekin rukmiNee avichalita bhaava se boleen -- main jaane ko taiyaara hoo.N| mere vaho rahane se agar mere laala ke praaNa bacha jaaya.N, to main sira ke bala daudee jaaoo.N; lekin meraa kahanaa giraha baaedha lo bhaiyaa! vahaa.N vaha achchhaa na hogaa| main use khooba pahachaanatee hoo.N| use koee beemaaree naheen hai, kevala ghar se nikaale jaane kaa shoka hai| yahee dukha jvara ke roopa men prakata hua hai| tuma ek naheen, laakha davaa karo -- sivila-sarjana ko hee kyon na dikhaao,use koee davaa asara na karegee!

munshee jee -- bahina, use ghar se nikaalaa kisane hai? mainne to kevala usakee padhaaee ke khyaala se use vahaa.N bhejaa tha|

rukmiNee -- tumane chaahe jisa khyaala se bhejaa ho; lekin yaha baat use laga gaee hai| main to aba kisee ginatee men naheen hoo.N, mujhe kisee baat men bolane kaa koee adhikaara naheen! maalika tuma, maalikina tumhaaree aurata!! main to kevala tumhaaree rotiyon para padee huee abhaaginee vidhavaa hoo.N! meree kauna sunegaa aur kauna parvaaha karegaa? lekin binaa bole raha naheen jata! mansaa tabhee achchhaa hogaa, jaba ghar aavegaa -- jaba tumhaaraa dil vahee ho jaayagaa, jo pahale tha|

yaha kaha kar rukmiNee vahaa.N se chalee gaee| unakee jyoti-heena para anubhavapoorNa aa.Nkhon ke saamane jo charitra ho rahe the, unakaa rahasya vaha khooba samajhatee thi; aur unakaa saaraa gussaa niraparaadhinee nirmalaa hee para utarataa tha| is samaya bhee vaha yaha kehte-kehte ruka gaee ki jaba taka yaha lakshmee is ghar men rahengee, is ghar kee dashaa bigadatee hee jaayagee; para usake prakata roopa se na kahane para bhee usakaa aashaya munshee jee se chhipaa naheen raha| unake chale jaane para munshee jee ne sira jhukaa liya aur sochane lage| unhen apane oopara is samaya itanaa gussaa aa raha tha ki deevaara se sira pataka kar praaNon kaa anta kar den| unhonne kyon vivaaha kiya tha? vivaaha karane kee kyaa jaroorata thi, eeshvara ne unhen ek naheen, teena-teena betaa die the| unakee avasthaa bhee 50 ke lagabhaga pahu.Ncha gaee thi, phir unhonne kyon vivaaha kiya? kyaa isee bahaane eeshvara ko unakaa

sarvanaasha karna manjoora tha? unhonne sira uthaakara ek baara nirmalaa kee sahaasa -- para nishchala moorti dekhee aur aspataala chale gae| nirmalaa kee sahaasa-chhavi ne unakaa chitta shaanta kar diya tha| aaja kaee dinon ke baada unhen yaha shaanti muyassara huee thi| pyaara-peedita dil is dashaa men kyaa itanaa shaanta aur avichalita raha sakataa hai? naheen,kabhee naheen! dil kee chota bhaava-kaushala se naheen chhipaaee jaa sakatee| apane chitta kee durbalataa para is samaya unhen bahuta kshobha hua| unhonne akaaraNa hee shaka ko dil men sthaana dekara itanaa anartha kiya| mansaaraama kee ora se bhee unakaa mana nihshanka ho gaya| ho,usakee jagaha aba ek naee shankaa utpanna ho gaee| kyaa mansaaraama bhaa.Npa to naheen gaya? kyaa bhaa.Npa kar hee to ghar aane se inkaara naheen kar raha hai? agar vaha bhopa gaya hai,to mahaan anartha ho jaayagaa| usakee kalpanaa hee se unakaa mana dahala uthaa| unakee deha kee saaree haddiyaa.N maano is haahaakaara para paanee daalane ke lie vyaakula ho uthee! unhonne kochavaana se ghode ko teja chalaane ko kahaa| aaja kaee dinon ke baada unake hridayamaNdala para chhaayaa hua saghana-ghana phata gaya tha; aur prakaasha kee laharen andara se nikalane ke liye vyagra ho rahi theen| unhonne baahara sira nikaala kar dekha, kochavaana so to naheen raha hai| ghode kee chaala unhen itanee manda kabhee na maalooma huee thi|

aspataala pahu.Ncha kar vaha lapake hue mansaaraama ke paasa gae| dekha to doktara saahaba usake saamane chintaa men magna khade the|
munshee jee ke haath-paa.Nva phoola gae| mu.Nha se shabda na nikala sakaa| bharabharaaee huee aavaaja men badee mushkila se bole -- kyaa haala hai, doktara saahaba? yaha kehte-kehte vaha ro pade aur jaba doktara saahaba ko unake savaala kaa javaaba dene men ek pala kaa vilamba hua,taba to unake praaNa nahon men samaa gae| unhonne palanga para baitha kar acheta ladakaa ko goda men uthaa liya; aur baalakon kee taraha sisaka-sisaka kar rone lage!! mansaaraama kee deha tave kee taraha jala rahi thi| mansaaraama ne ek baara aa.Nkhen kholeen| aaha! kitanee bhayankara aur usake saatha hee kitanee deena nazara thi! munshee jee ne ladakaa ko galaa se lagaa kar doktara se poochhaa -- kyaa haala hai saahaba,aapa chupa kyon hain?

doktara ne sandigdha aavaaza men kahaa -- haala jo kuchha hai,vaha aapa dekha hee rahe hain| 106 digree kaa jvara hai; aur main kyaa batalaaoo.N? abhee jvara kaa prakopa badhataa hee jata hai| mere kie jo kuchha ho sakataa hai, kar raha hoo.N| eeshvara maalika hain! jaba se aapa gae hain, main ek minita ke lie bhee yahaa.N se naheen hilaa| khaanaa taka naheen kar sakaa| haalata itanee naajuka hai ki ek minita men kyaa ho jaayagaa, naheen kahaa jaa sakataa| yaha mahaajvara hai,bilakula hosha naheen hai| raha-raha kar dileeriyama (delirium) kaa dauraa saa ho jata hai| kyaa ghar men inhen kisee ne kuchha kahaa hai! baara-baara ammaa.N jee, tuma kaha ho? yahee aavaaja mu.Nha se nikalatee hai!

doktara saahaba yaha kaha hee rahe the ki sahasaa mansaaraama utha

kar baitha gaya; aur ek dhakka se munshee jee ko chaarapaaee ke naa dil dhakela kar unmatta aavaaza men bola -- kyon dhamakaate hain? aapa maara daalie, maara daalie, abhee maara daalie!! talavaara naheen milatee? rassee kaa phandaa hai yaa vaha bhee naheen? mai apane gale men lagaa loo.Ngaa| haaya ammaae jee, tuma kahaa.N ho? yaha kehte-kehte vaha phir acheta hokara gira padaa!

munshee jee ek pala taka mansaaraama kee shithila mudraa kee ora vyathita netron se taakate rahe; phir sahasaa unhonne doktara saahaba kaa haath pakada liya; aur bahuta deenataapoorNa aagraha se bole -- doktara saahaba, is ladake ko bachaa leejie -- eeshvara ke lie bachaa leejie, naheen meraa sarvanaasha ho jaayagaa! main ameera naheen hoo.N; lekin
aapa jo kuchha kahenge, vaha haajira karoo.Ngaa; ise bachaa leejie! aapa bade se bade doktaron ko bulaaie,aur unakee raaya leejie; main sab kharcha de doo.Ngaa! isakee yaha dashaa aba naheen dekhee jati! haaya, meraa honahaara betaa!

doktara saahaba ne karuNa-aavaaza men kahaa -- baaboo saahaba, main aapa se satya kehta hoo.N ki main inake lie apanee tarapha se koee baat uthaa naheen rakha raha hoo.N| aba aapa doosare doktaron se salaaha lene kehte hain| main abhee doktara lahiree, doktara bhaatiyaa aur doktara maathura ko bulaataa hoo.N| vinaayaka shaastree ko bhee bulaae letaa hoo.N; lekin main aapako bekaara kaa aashvaasana naheen denaa chaahataa -- haalata naajuka hai|
maa munshee jee ne rote hue kahaa-naheen,doktara saahaba,yaha shabda mu.Nha se na nikaalie| haalata isake dushmanon kee naajuka ho| eeshvara mujha para itanaa kopa na karenge| aapa kalakattaa aur bambaee ke doktaron ko taara deejie| main jindagee bhara aapakee gulaamee karoo.Ngaa| yahee mere kula kaa deepaka hai! yahee mere jeevana kaa aadhaara hai! meraa dil phataa jaa raha hai! koee aisee davaa deejie,jisase ise hosha aa jaaya| main jaraa apane kaanon se usakee baaten sunen! jaanoo.N ki use kyaa takaleepha ho raha hai! haaya,meraa bachchaa!!

doktara-aapa jaraa dil ko taskeena deejie| aapa bujurga aadamee hain, yon haaya-haaya karane se aur doktaron kee phauja jamaa karane se koee nateejaa na nikalegaa| shaanta hokara baithie,main shahara ke logon ko bulaa raha hoo.N; dekhie,ve kyaa kehte hain? aapa to khuda hee badahavaasa hue jaate hain|

munshee jee-achchhaa doktara saahaba,main aba na boloo.Ngaa,jabaana taka na kholoongaa,aapa jo chaahen karen,bachchaa aba aapake haath men hai| aapa hee usakee rakshaa kar sakate hain| main itanaa hee chaahataa hoo.N ki jaraa ise hosha aa jaaya, mujhe pahachaana le,meree baaten samajhane lage| kyaa koee aisee davaa naheen? koee aisee sajeevanee bootee naheen? basa,main isase do-chaara baaten kar letaa!

yaha kehte-kehte munshee jee phir aavesha men aakara mansaaraama se bole-betaa,jaraa aa.Nkhen kholo,kaisaa jee hai? main tumhaare paasa
baithaa hua ro raha hoo.N,mujhe tumase koee shikaayata naheen hai,meraa dil tumhaaree ora se saapha hai|

doktara-phir aapane anargala baaten karanee shuroo kee| are saahaba, aapa bachche naheen hain-bujurga aadamee hain,jaraa dhairya se kaama leejie!

munshee jee-achchhaa doktara saahaba,aba na boloo.Ngaa,khataa huee| aapa jo chaahen keejie| mainne sab kuchha aapa para chhoda diya|koee aisaa upaaya naheen hai,jisase main ise itanaa samajhaa sakoo.N ki meraa dil saapha hai| aapa hee kaha deejie doktara saahaba!kaha deejie,tumhaaraa abhaagaa pitaa baithaa ro raha hai|usakaa dil tumhaaree tarapha se bilakula saapha hai| use kuchha bhrama hua tha,vaha aba doora ho gaya|basa,itanaa hee kaha deejie| main aur kuchha naheen chaahataa| main chupachaapa baithaa hoo.N|jabaana taka naheen kholataa;lekin aapa itanaa jaroora kaha deejie!

doktara-eeshvara ke lie baaboo saahaba!jaraa sabra keejie,varanaa mujhe majaboora hokara aapase kahanaa padegaa ki ghar jaaie| main jaraa daphtara men jaakara doktaron ko khata likha raha hoo.N| aapa chupachaapa baithe rahiegaa|

nirdayee doktara! javaana bete kee yaha dashaa dekha kar kauna pitaa hai, jo dhairya se kaama legaa? munshee jee bahuta gambheera svabhaava ke manushya the| yaha bhee jaanate the ki is vakta haaya-haaya machaane se koee nateejaa naheen;lekin phir bhee is samaya shaanta baithanaa unake
lie naamumakina tha| agar daiva-gati se yaha beemaaree hoti, to vaha shaanta ho sakate the, doosaron ko samajhaa sakate the, khuda doktaron ko bulaa sakate the, lekin kyaa yaha jaana kar bhee vaha dhairya rakha sakate the ki yaha sab aaga meree hee lagaaee huee hai ? koee pitaa itanaa vajra-dil ho sakataa hai ? unakaa roma-roma is vakta unhen | dhikkaara raha tha| unhonne sochaa, mujhamen yaha durbhaavanaa utpanna hee kyon huee ? mainne kyon binaa kisee pratyaksha pramaaNa ke aisee bheeshaNa kalpanaa kar daalee ? achchhaa, mujhe us dashaa men kyaa karna chaahie tha ? jo kuchha unhonne kiya, usake sivaa vaha aur kyaa karte isakaa vaha phaisalaa na kar sake | vaastava men vivaaha ke bandhana men padanaa hee apane pairon men kulhaadee maaranaa tha| haa.N, yahee saare upadrava kee jada hai!

magara mainne yaha koee anokhee baat naheen kee| sabhee aurata-aadamee vivaaha karte hain | unakaa jeevana khushee se katataa hai| khushee kee ichchhaa se hee to hama vivaaha karte hain| isee muhalle men saikadon aadamiyon ne doosaree, teesaree, chauthee yahaa.N taka ki saataveen shaadiyaa.N kee hain; aur mujhase bhee kaheen zyaadaa avasthaa men ! vaha jaba taka jiye aaraama hee se jiye| yaha bhee naheen hua ki sabhee aurata se pahale mara gae hon| duhaaja-tihaaja hone para bhee kitane hee phir ra.Nduve ho gae | agar meree jaisee dashaa sab kee hoti, vivaaha kaa naama hee kauna letaa ? mere pitaa jee hee ne pachapanaven varsha men vivaaha kiya tha aur mere janma ke samaya unakee avasthaa saatha se kama na
thi| haa.N, itanee vaata jaroora hai ki taba aur aba men kuchha antara ho gaya hai| pahale striyaa.N padhee-likhee na hoti theen| pati chaahe kaisaa hee ho,use poojya samajhatee theen yaa yaha baat ho ki aadamee sab kuchha dekha kar bhee behayaaee se kaama letaa ho;avashya yahee baat hai| jaba yuvaka vriddhaa ke saatha khusha naheen raha sakataa,to yuvatee kyon kisee vriddha ke saatha khusha rahane lagee? lekin main to kuchha aisaa buddhaa na tha| mujhe dekha kar koee chaaleesa se zyaadaa naheen bataa sakataa| kuchha bhee ho,javaanee dhala jaane para navaana aurata se vivaaha karake kuchha na kuchha vehayaaee jaroora karanee padatee hai| isamen shaka naheen! aurata svabhaava se lajjaasheelaa hoti hai| kulataaon kee baat to doosaree hai; para saadhaaraNatah aurata aadamee se kaheen jyaadaa sanyamasheelaa hoti hai| joda kaa pati paakara vaha chaahe para-aadamee se ha.Nsee-dillagee kar le; para usakaa mana shuddha rahataa hai| vejoda vivaaha ho jaane se vaha chaahe kisee kee ora aankhen uthaa kar na dekhe,para usakaa chitta dukhee rahataa hai| vaha pakkee deevaara hai,usamen sabaree kaa asara naheen hota| yaha kachchee deevaara hai aur usee vakta taka khadee rahatee hai,jaba taka us para sabaree na chalaaee jaaya!

inheen vichaaron men pade-pade munshee jee ko ek jhapakee aa gaee| mana ke bhaavon ne turanta svapna kaa roopa dhaaraNa kar liya| kyaa dekhate hain ki unakee pahalee bee mansaaraama ke saamane khadee kaha rahi haisvaamee, yaha tumane kyaa kiya? jisa ladakaa ko mainne apanaa rakta pilaa-pilaa kar paalaa,usako tumane itanee nirdayataa se maara daalaa|
aise aadarsha-charitra ladakaa para tumane itanaa ghora kalanka lagaa diya! aba baithe kyaa bisoorate ho? tumane usase haath dho liya| main tumhaare nirdaya haathon se chheena kar use apane saatha lie jati hoo.N| tuma to itane shakee kabhee na the,kyaa vivaaha karte hee shaka ko bhee gale baa.Ndha laae? is komala dil para itanaa kathora aaghaata!itanaa bheeshaNa kalanka! itanaa badaa beizzatee saha kar jeene vaale koee behayaa honge! meraa betaa naheen saha sakataa| yaha kehte-kehte usane ladakaa ko goda men uthaa liya aur chalee| munshee jee ne rote hue usakee goda se mansaaraama ko chheenane ke lie haath badhaayaa,to aa.Nkhen khula gaee aur doktara lahiree; doktara bhaatiyaa aadi aadhe darjana doktara unake saamane khade dikhaaee die!
