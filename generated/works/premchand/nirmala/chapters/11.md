# Nirmala — Adhyay 11

**Munshi Premchand**

gyaarahavaan parichchheda

munshee totaaraama sandhyaa samay kachaharee se ghar pahunche, to nirmalaa ne poochhaa—unhen dekha, kyaa haala hai? munshee jee ne dekha ki nirmalaa ke mukha par naamamaatra ko bhee shoka yaa chintaa kaa chihna naheen hai, usakaa banaava-singaara aur dinon se bhee kuchha ga.dhaa hua hai—masalan vaha gale men haara na pahanatee thi; par aaja vaha bhee gale men shobhaa de raha tha| jhoomara se bhee use bahut prema na tha; par aaja vaha bhee maheena reshamee sa.dee ke neeche, kaale-kaale keshon ke oopara, faanoosa ke deepaka kee bhaanti chamaka raha tha|

munshee jee ne munha phera kar kaha—beemaara hai, aur kyaa haala bataaoon?

nirmalaa—tuma to unhen yahaan laane gae the?

munshee jee ne jhunjhalaa kar kaha—vaha naheen aaya, to kyaa main zabaradastee uthaa laata? kitana samajhaayaa ki betaa ghar chalo, vahaan tumhen koee takaleefa na hone paavegee; lekin ghar kaa naama suna kar use jaise doonaa jvara ho jata tha| kahane lagaa -- main yahaan mara jaaoonga; lekin ghar na jaaoo.cgaa| aakhira majaboora hokara aspataala pahunchaa aaya; aur kyaa karata?

rukminee bhee aakara baraamade men kha.dee ho ganee theen;boleen -- vaha janama kaa hathee hai,yahaan kisee tarah na aavegaa aur yaha bhee dekha lena, vahaan achchha bhee na hoga!

munshee jee ne kaatara svara men kaha -- tuma do-chaara din ke liye vaha.c chalee jaao,to bada achchha ho; bahina! tumhaare rahane se use taskeena hoti rahegee| meree vahina,meree yaha vinaya maana lo| akele vaha ro-rokara praana de degaa| basa,haaya ammaan! haaya ammaan! kee rata lagaa-lagaa kar royaa karata hai| main vaheen jaa raha hoon, mere saatha hee chalee chalo! usakee dashaa achchhi naheen! bahina,vaha soorata hee naheen rahi! dekhen eeshvara kyaa karte hain?

yaha kehte-kehte munshee jee kee aankhon se aansoo bahane lage; lekin rukminee avichalita bhaava se boleen -- main jaane ko taiyaara hoon| mere vahao rahane se agar mere laala ke praana bacha jaayan, to main sira ke bala dau.dee jaaoon; lekin meraa kahana giraha ba.cdha lo bhaiya! vahaan vaha achchha na hogaa| main use khooba pahachaanatee hoon| use koee beemaaree naheen hai, kevala ghar se nikaale jaane kaa shoka hai| yahee dukha jvara ke roopa men prakata hua hai| tuma ek naheen, laakha davaa karo -- sivila-sarjana ko hee kyon na dikhaao,use koee davaa asara na karegee! munshee jee -- bahina, use ghar se nikaalaa kisane hai? mainne to kevala usakee pa.dhaaee ke khayal se use vahaan bhejaa tha|

rukminee -- tumane chaahe jisa khayal se bhejaa ho; lekin yaha baat use laga gaee hai| main to ab kisee ginatee men naheen hoon, mujhe kisee baat men bolane kaa koee adhikaara naheen! maalika tuma, maalikina tumhaaree stree!! main to kevala tumhaaree rotiyon par pa.dee hui abhaaginee vidhavaa hoon! meree kaun sunegaa aur kaun parvaaha karega? lekin binaa bole raha naheen jaata! mansaa tabhee achchha hoga, jab ghar aavegaa -- jab tumhaaraa hridaya vahee ho jaayaga, jo pahale tha|

yaha kaha kar rukminee vahaan se chalee gaee| unakee jyoti-heena par anubhavapoorna aankhon ke saamane jo charitra ho rahe the, unakaa rahasya vaha khooba samajhatee thi; aur unakaa saaraa krodha niraparaadhinee nirmalaa hee par utarata tha| is samay bhee vaha yaha kehte-kehte ruka gaee ki jab taka yaha lakshmee is ghar men rahengee, is ghar kee dashaa biga.datee hee jaayagee; par usake prakata roopa se na kahane par bhee usakaa aashaya munshee jee se chhipaa naheen raha| unake chale jaane par munshee jee ne sira jhukaa liya aur sochane lage| unhen apane oopara is samay itana krodha aa raha tha ki deevaara se sira pataka kar praanon kaa anta kar den| unhonne kyon vivaaha kiya tha? vivaaha karane kee kyaa zaroorat thi, eeshvara ne unhen ek naheen, teena-teena putra die the| unakee avasthaa bhee 50 ke lagabhaga pahuncha gaee thi, phir unhonne kyon vivaaha kiya? kyaa isee bahaane eeshvara ko unakaa sarvanaasha karana manjoora tha? unhonne sira uthaakara ek baara nirmalaa kee sahaasa -- par nishchala moorti dekhi aur aspataala chale gae| nirmalaa kee sahaasa-chhavi ne unakaa chitta shaanta kar diya tha| aaja kaee dinon ke baada unhen yaha shaanti muyassara hui thi| prema-pee.dita hridaya is dashaa men kyaa itana shaanta aur avichalita raha sakata hai? naheen,kabhee naheen! hridaya kee chota bhaava-kaushala se naheen chhipaaee jaa sakatee| apane chitta kee durbalata par is samay unhen atyanta kshobha hua| unhonne akaarana hee sandeha ko hridaya men sthaana dekara itana anartha kiya| mansaaraama kee ora se bhee unakaa mann nihshanka ho gaya| hao,usakee jagaha ab ek naee shankaa utpanna ho gaee| kyaa mansaaraama bhaanpa to naheen gaya? kyaa bhaanpa kar hee to ghar aane se inkaara naheen kar raha hai? agar vaha bhaopa gaya hai,to mahaan anartha ho jaayagaa| usakee kalpana hee se unakaa mann dahala uthaa| unakee deha kee saaree haddiyaan maano is haahaakaara par paanee daalane ke lie vyaakula ho uthee! unhonne kochavaana se gho.de ko teza chalaane ko kaha| aaja kaee dinon ke baada unake hridayamandala par chhaayaa hua saghana-ghana phata gaya tha; aur prakaasha kee laharen andara se nikalane ke liye vyagra ho rahi theen| unhonne baahara sira nikaala kar dekha, kochavaana so to naheen raha hai| gho.de kee chaala unhen itanee manda kabhee na maalooma hui thi|

aspataala pahuncha kar vaha lapake hue mansaaraama ke paasa gae| dekha to daoktara saahaba usake saamane chintaa men magna kha.de the| munshee jee ke haath-paanva phoola gae| munha se shabda na nikala sakaa| bharabharaaee hui aavaaja men ba.dee mushkila se bole -- kyaa haala hai, daoktara saahaba? yaha kehte-kehte vaha ro pa.de aur jab daoktara saahaba ko unake prashna kaa uttara dene men ek kshana kaa vilamba hua,tab to unake praana nahon men samaa gae| unhonne palanga par baitha kar acheta baalaka ko goda men uthaa liya; aur baalakon kee bhaanti sisaka-sisaka kar rone lage!! mansaaraama kee deha tave kee tarah jala rahi thi| mansaaraama ne ek baara aankhen kholeen| aaha! kitanee bhayankara aur usake saatha hee kitanee deena drishti thi! munshee jee ne baalaka ko kantha se lagaa kar daoktara se poochhaa -- kyaa haala hai saahaba,aapa chupa kyon hain?

daoktara ne sandigdha svara men kaha -- haala jo kuchha hai,vaha aapa dekha hee rahe hain| 106 digree kaa jvara hai; aur main kyaa batalaaoon? abhee jvara kaa prakopa ba.dhata hee jata hai| mere kie jo kuchha ho sakata hai, kar raha hoon| eeshvara maalika hain! jab se aapa gae hain, main ek minita ke lie bhee yahaan se naheen hilaa| bhojana taka naheen kar sakaa| haalata itanee naajuka hai ki ek minita men kyaa ho jaayaga, naheen kaha jaa sakata| yaha mahaajvara hai,bilakula hosha naheen hai| raha-raha kar dileeriyama (delirium) kaa dauraa saa ho jata hai| kyaa ghar men inhen kisee ne kuchha kaha hai! baara-baara ammaan jee, tuma kaha ho? yahee aavaaza munha se nikalatee hai!

daoktara saahaba yaha kaha hee rahe the ki sahasaa mansaaraama utha kar baitha gaya; aur ek dhakka se munshee jee ko chaarapaaee ke naa dil dhakela kar unmatta svara men bola -- kyon dhamakaate hain? aapa maara daalie, maara daalie, abhee maara daalie!! talavaara naheen milatee? rassee kaa phandaa hai yaa vaha bhee naheen? mai apane gale men lagaa loongaa| haaya amma.c jee, tuma kahaan ho? yaha kehte-kehte vaha phir acheta hokara gira pa.da!

munshee jee ek kshana taka mansaaraama kee shithila mudraa kee ora vyathita netron se taakate rahe; phir sahasaa unhonne daoktara saahaba kaa haath paka.da liya; aur atyanta deenataapoorna aagraha se bole -- daoktara saahaba, is ladke ko bachaa leejie -- eeshvara ke lie bachaa leejie, naheen meraa sarvanaasha ho jaayaga! main ameera naheen hoon; lekin
aapa jo kuchha kahenge, vaha haazira karoonga; ise bachaa leejie! aapa ba.de se ba.de daoktaron ko bulaaie,aur unakee raaya leejie; main sab kharcha de doonga! isakee yaha dashaa ab naheen dekhi jati! haaya, meraa honahaara beta!

daoktara saahaba ne karuna-svara men kaha -- baaboo saahaba, main aapa se satya kahata hoon ki main inake lie apanee tarapha se koee baat uthaa naheen rakha raha hoon| ab aapa doosare daoktaron se salaaha lene kehte hain| main abhee daoktara lahiree, daoktara bhaatiya aur daoktara maathura ko bulaataa hoon| vinaayaka shaastree ko bhee bulaae letaa hoon; lekin main aapako vyartha kaa aashvaasana naheen dena chaahata -- haalata naazuka hai| maa munshee jee ne rote hue kaha-naheen,daoktara saahaba,yaha shabda munha se na nikaalie| haalata isake dushmanon kee naajuka ho| eeshvara mujha par itana kopa na karenge| aapa kalakattaa aur bambaee ke daoktaron ko taara deejie| main zindagi bhara aapakee gulaamee karoongaa| yahee mere kula kaa deepaka hai! yahee mere jeevana kaa aadhaara hai! meraa hridaya phata jaa raha hai! koee aisee davaa deejie,jisase ise hosha aa jaaya| main jaraa apane kaanon se usakee baaten sunen! jaanoon ki use kyaa kashta ho raha hai! haaya,meraa bachcha!!

daoktara-aapa jaraa dil ko taskeena deejie| aapa bujurga aadmi hain, yon haaya-haaya karane se aur daoktaron kee phauja jamaa karane se koee nateejaa na nikalegaa| shaanta hokara baithie,main shehar ke logon ko bulaa raha hoon; dekhie,ve kyaa kehte hain? aapa to khuda hee badahavaasa hue jaate hain|

munshee jee-achchha daoktara saahaba,main ab na boloonga,jabaana taka na kholoonga,aapa jo chaahen karen,bachcha ab aapake haath men hai| aapa hee usakee rakshaa kar sakate hain| main itana hee chaahata hoon ki jaraa ise hosha aa jaaya, mujhe pahachaana le,meree baaten samajhane lage| kyaa koee aisee davaa naheen? koee aisee sajeevanee bootee naheen? basa,main isase do-chaara baaten kar leta!

yaha kehte-kehte munshee jee phir aavesha men aakara mansaaraama se bole-beta,jaraa aankhen kholo,kaisaa jee hai? main tumhaare paasa baithaa hua ro raha hoon,mujhe tumase koee shikaayata naheen hai,meraa dil tumhaaree ora se saapha hai|

daoktara-phir aapane anargala baaten karanee shuroo kee| are saahaba, aapa bachche naheen hain-bujurga aadmi hain,jaraa dhairya se kaama leejie!

munshee jee-achchha daoktara saahaba,ab na boloonga,khata hui| aapa jo chaahen keejie| mainne sab kuchha aapa par chho.da diya|koee aisaa upaaya naheen hai,jisase main ise itana samajhaa sakoon ki meraa dil saapha hai| aapa hee kaha deejie daoktara saahaba!kaha deejie,tumhaaraa abhaagaa pitaa baithaa ro raha hai|usakaa dil tumhaaree tarapha se bilakula saafa hai| use kuchha bhrama hua tha,vaha ab doora ho gaya|basa,itana hee kaha deejie| main aur kuchha naheen chaahata| main chupachaapa baithaa hoon|zabaana taka naheen kholata;lekin aapa itana jaroora kaha deejie!

daoktara-eeshvara ke lie baaboo saahaba!jaraa sabra keejie,varana mujhe majaboora hokara aapase kahana pa.degaa ki ghar jaaie| main jaraa daphtara men jaakara daoktaron ko khata likha raha hoon| aapa chupachaapa baithe rahiegaa|

nirdayee daoktara! javaana bete kee yaha dashaa dekha kar kaun pitaa hai, jo dhairya se kaama lega? munshee jee bahut gambheera svabhaava ke manushya the| yaha bhee jaanate the ki is vakta haaya-haaya machaane se koee nateejaa naheen;lekin phir bhee is samay shaanta baithana unake lie asambhava tha| agar daiva-gati se yaha beemaaree hoti, to vaha shaanta ho sakate the, doosaron ko samajhaa sakate the, khuda daoktaron ko bulaa sakate the, lekin kyaa yaha jaana kar bhee vaha dhairya rakha sakate the ki yaha sab aaga meree hee lagaaee hui hai ? koee pitaa itana vajra-hridaya ho sakata hai ? unakaa roma-roma is vakta unhen | dhikkaara raha tha| unhonne socha, mujhamen yaha durbhaavana utpanna hee kyon hui ? mainne kyon binaa kisee pratyaksha pramaana ke aisee bheeshana kalpana kar daalee ? achchha, mujhe us dashaa men kyaa karana chaahie tha ? jo kuchha unhonne kiya, usake sivaa vaha aur kyaa karte isakaa vaha nishchaya na kar sake | vaastava men vivaaha ke bandhana men pa.dana hee apane pairon men kulha.dee maarana tha| haan, yahee saare upadrava kee ja.da hai!

magar mainne yaha koee anokhee baat naheen kee| sabhee stree-purusha vivaaha karte hain | unakaa jeevana aananda se katata hai| aananda kee ichchhaa se hee to hama vivaaha karte hain| isee muhalle men saika.don aadamiyon ne doosaree, teesaree, chauthee yahaan taka ki saataveen shaadiyaan kee hain; aur mujhase bhee kaheen adhika avasthaa men ! vaha jab taka jiye aaraama hee se jiye| yaha bhee naheen hua ki sabhee stree se pahale mara gae hon| duhaaja-tihaaja hone par bhee kitane hee phir randuve ho gae | agar meree jaisee dashaa sab kee hoti, vivaaha kaa naama hee kaun letaa ? mere pitaa jee hee ne pachapanaven varsha men vivaaha kiya tha aur mere janma ke samay unakee avasthaa saatha se kama na thi| haan, itanee vaata zaroora hai ki tab aur ab men kuchha antara ho gaya hai| pahale striyaan pa.dhee-likhee na hoti theen| pati chaahe kaisaa hee ho,use poojya samajhatee theen yaa yaha baat ho ki purusha sab kuchha dekha kar bhee behayaaee se kaama letaa ho;avashya yahee baat hai| jab yuvaka vriddhaa ke saatha prasanna naheen raha sakata,to yuvatee kyon kisee vriddha ke saatha prasanna rahane lagee? lekin main to kuchha aisaa buddhaa na tha| mujhe dekha kar koee chaaleesa se adhika naheen bata sakata| kuchha bhee ho,javaanee dhala jaane par navaana aurat se vivaaha karake kuchha na kuchha vehayaaee jaroora karanee pa.datee hai| isamen sandeha naheen! stree svabhaava se lajjaasheelaa hoti hai| kulataaon kee baat to doosaree hai; par saadhaaranatah stree purusha se kaheen zyada sanyamasheelaa hoti hai| jo.da kaa pati paakara vaha chaahe par-purusha se hansee-dillagee kar le; par usakaa mann shuddha rahata hai| vejo.da vivaaha ho jaane se vaha chaahe kisee kee ora aankhen uthaa kar na dekhe,par usakaa chitta dukhee rahata hai| vaha pakkee deevaara hai,usamen sabaree kaa asara naheen hota| yaha kachchee deevaara hai aur usee vakta taka kha.dee rahatee hai,jab taka us par sabaree na chalaaee jaaya!

inheen vichaaron men pa.de-pa.de munshee jee ko ek jhapakee aa gaee| mann ke bhaavon ne tatkaala svapna kaa roopa dhaarana kar liya| kyaa dekhate hain ki unakee pahalee bee mansaaraama ke saamane kha.dee kaha rahi haisvaamee, yaha tumane kyaa kiya? jisa baalaka ko mainne apana rakta pilaa-pilaa kar paala,usako tumane itanee nirdayata se maara daalaa| aise aadarsha-charitra baalaka par tumane itana ghora kalanka lagaa diya! ab baithe kyaa bisoorate ho? tumane usase haath dho liya| main tumhaare nirdaya haathon se chheena kar use apane saatha lie jati hoon| tuma to itane shakee kabhee na the,kyaa vivaaha karte hee shaka ko bhee gale baandha laae? is komala hridaya par itana kathora aaghaata!itana bheeshana kalanka! itana bada apamaana saha kar jeene vaale koee behayaa honge! meraa betaa naheen saha sakata| yaha kehte-kehte usane baalaka ko goda men uthaa liya aur chalee| munshee jee ne rote hue usakee goda se mansaaraama ko chheenane ke lie haath ba.dhaaya,to aankhen khula gaee aur daoktara lahiree; daoktara bhaatiya aadi aadhe darjana daoktara unake saamane kha.de dikhaaee die!
