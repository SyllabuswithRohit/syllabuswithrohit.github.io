# Nirmala — Adhyay 15

**Munshi Premchand**

pandrahavaan parichchheda

nirmalaa ko yadyapi apane hee ghar ke jhanjhaton se avakaasha na tha; par krishnaa ke vivaaha kaa sandeshaa paakara vaha kisee tarah na ruka sakee| usakee maataa ne bahut aagraha karake bulaayaa tha| sabase bada aakarshana yaha tha ki krishnaa kaa vivaaha usee ghar men ho raha tha, jahaan nirmalaa kaa vivaaha pahale taya hua tha| aashcharya yahee tha ki is baara binaa kuchha daheja lie kaise vivaaha hone par taiyaara ho gae| nirmalaa ko krishnaa ke vishaya men ba.dee chintaa rahatee thi| samajhatee thi—meree hee tarah vaha bhee kisee ke gale ma.dha dee jaayagee| bahut chaahatee thi ki maataa kee kuchha sahaayata karoon, jisase krishnaa ke lie koee yogya vara mile; lekin idhara vakeela saahaba ke ghar baitha jaane aur mahaajana ke naalisha kar dene se usakaa haath bhee tanga tha| aisee dashaa men yaha Kabara paakara use ba.dee shaanti milee| chalane kee taiyaaree kar dee, vakeela saahaba steshana taka pahunchaane aae| nanheen bachchi se unhen bahut prema tha| chho.date hee na the;yahaan taka ki nirmalaa ke saatha chalane ko taiyaara ho gae lekin vivaaha se ek maheene pahale unakaa sasuraala jaa baithana nirmalaa ko uchita na maalooma hua|

nirmalaa ne apanee maataa se ab taka apanee vipatti-kathaa na kahee thi| jo baat ho gaee,usakaa ronaa rokara maataa ko kashta dene aur rulaane se kyaa phaayada? isalie usakee maataa samajhatee thi,nirmalaa ba.de aananda se hai| ab jo nirmalaa kee soorata dekhi,to maano usake hridaya par dhakkaa saa laga gaya| ladakiyaan sasuraala se ghula kar naheen aati; phir nirmalaa jaisee ladki,jisako sukha kee sabhee saamagriyaan praapta theen! usane kitanee hee ladakiyon ko dauja kee chandramaa kee bhaanti sasuraala jaate aur poornachandra vana kar aate dekha tha! mann men kalpana kar rakkhee thi,nirmalaa kaa ranga nikhara gaya hoga, deha bhara kar sudaula ho gaee hogee-angapratyanga kee shobhaa kuchha aur hee ho gaee hogee| ab jo dekha,to vaha aadhee bhee na rahi thi! na yauvana kee chanchalata thi,na vaha vihansita chhavi,jo hridaya ko moha letee hai! vaha kamaneeyata,vaha sukumaarata jo vilaasamaya jeevana se aa jati hai,yahaan naama ko na thi| mukha peela,cheshtaa giree hui, anga shithila,unneesaven hee varsha men vuddhee ho gaee thi| java maan-betiyaan ro-dhokara shaanta hui, to maataa ne poochhaa-kyon ree, tujhe vahaan khaane ko na milata tha? isase kaheen achchhi to too yaheen thi| vahaan tujhe kyaa takaleepha thi? krishnaa ne hansa kar kaha-vahaan maalikina thi ki naheen| maalikina ko duniya bhara kee chintaaen rahatee hain,bhojana kab karen?

nirmalaa-naheen ammaan, vahaan kaa paanee mujhe raasa naheen aata-tabeeyata bhaaree rahatee hai|

maataa-vakeela saahaba nyaute men aaenge na? tab poochhoongee ki aapane phoola see ladki le jaakara usakee yaha gata bana daalee| achchha, ab yaha bata ki toone yahaan rupae kyon bheje the? mainne to tujhase kabhee na maange the| laakha gaee-gujaree hoon lekin betee kaa dhana khaane kee neeyata naheen!

nirmalaa ne chakita hokara poochhaa-kisane rupae bheje the? ammaan! mainne to naheen bheje!

maataa-jhootha na bola! toone 500) ke nota naheen bheje the?

krishnaa-bheje naheen the,to kyaa aasamaana se aagae? tumhaaraa naama saapha likhaa tha| mohara bhee vaheen kee thi|

nirmalaa-tumhaare charana chhookara kehti hoon, mainne rupae naheen bheje| yaha kab kee baat hai?

maataa-are yahee do-dhaaee maheene hue honge| magar toone naheen bheje,to aae kahaan se?

nirmalaa yaha main kyaa jaanoon? magar mainne rupae naheen bheje| hamaare yahaan to jab se javaana betaa maraa hai, kachaharee hee naheen jaate| meraa haath to aapa hee tanga tha, rupae kahaan se aate?

maataa-yaha to ba.de aashcharya kee baat hai! vahaan aur koee teraa sagaa-sambandhee to naheen hai? vakeela saahaba ne tujhase chhipaa kar to naheen bheje?

nirmalaa- naheen ammaan,mujhe to vishvaasa naheen|

maataa-isakaa pata lagaanaa chaahie| maine saare rupae krishnaa ke gahane kapa.de men kharcha kar daale| yahee ba.dee mushkila hui|

donon ladki men kisee vishaya par vivaada utha kha.daa hua;aur krishnaa udhara phaisalaa karane chalee gaee,to nirmalaa ne maataa se kaha is vivaaha kee baat suna kar mujhe bada aashcharya hua| yaha kaise hua ammao?

maataa-yahaan jo sunata hai,daanton ungalee davaataa hai| jina logon ne pakee kee-karaaee vaata phera dee; aur kevala tho.de se rupae ke lobha se! ve ava vinaa kuchha lie kaise vivaaha karane par taiyaara ho gaye, samajha men naheen aata| unhonne khuda hee patra bhejaa| mainne saapha likha diya ki mere paasa dene lene ko kuchha naheen hai; kusha-kanyaa hee se aapakee sevaa kar sakatee hoon|

nirmalaa-isakaa kuchha javaaba naheen diya?

maataa-shaastree jee patra lekara gae the| vaha to yaha kehte the ki ava munshee jee kuchha lene ke ichchhuka naheen hain| apanee pahalee vaadaa- khilaaphee par kuchha lajjita bhee hain| munshee jee se to itanee udaarata kee aashaa na thi, magar sunatee hoon unake ba.de putra bahut sajana aadmi hain| unhonne kaha-suna kar baapa ko raajee kiya hai|
nirmalaa-pahale to vaha mahaashaya bhee thailee chaahate the na? maataa-haan, magar ab to shaastree jee kehte the ki daheja ke naama se chi.dhate hain| sunaa hai, yahaan vivaaha na karane par pachhataate bhee the| rupae ke lie baat chho.dee thi; aur rupae khooba paae; par stree pasanda naheen!

nirmalaa ke mann men us purusha ko dekhane kee ba.dee prabala utkanthaa hui, jo usakee avahelana karake ab usakee bahina kaa uddhaara karana chaahata hai| praayashchitta sahee; lekin kitane aise praanee hain, jo is tarah praayashchitta karane ko taiyaara hain| unase baaten karane ke lie namra shabdon men unakaa tiraskaara karane ke lie, apanee anupama chhabi dikhaa kar unhen aur bhee jalaane ke lie nirmalaa kaa hridaya adheera ho uthaa| raat ko donon bahinen ek hee kamare men soee| muhalle men kina-kina ladakiyon kaa vivaaha ho gaya, kaun-kaun see ladakoree hueen; kisa-kisa ko vivaaha dhooma-dhaama se hua, kisa-kisa ke pati kanyaa kee ichchhaanukoola mile, kaun kitane aur kaise gahane cha.dhaave men laayaa- inheen vishayon par donon men ba.dee dera taka baaten hoti raheen| krishnaa baara-baara chaahatee thi ki bahina ke ghar kaa kuchha haala poochha; magar nirmalaa use kuchha poochhane kaa avasara na detee thi| vaha jaanatee thi ki yaha jo baaten poochhegee, usake bataane men mujhe sankocha hogaa| aakhira ek baara krishnaa poochha hee baithee-jeejaa jee bhee aaenge na?

nirmalaa-aane ko kaha to hai|

krishnaa-ab to tumase prasanna rahate hain na, yaa ab bhee vahee haala hai| main to sunaa karti thi, duhaaja pati apanee stree ko praanon se bhee priya samajhata hai, yahaan vilakula ulatee vaata dekhi| aakhira kisa vaata par biga.date rahate hain?

nirmalaa-ava main kisee ke mann kee baat kyaa jaanoon?

krishnaa-main to samajhatee hoon, tumhaaree rukhaaee se vaha chi.dhate honge| tuma to yaheen se jalee hui gaee theen| vahaan bhee unhen kuchha kaha hoga?

nirmalaa--yaha vaata naheen hai krishna; main sauganda khaakara kehti hoon, jo mere mann men unakee ora se jaraa bhee maila ho| mujhase jahaan taka ho sakata hai,unakee sevaa karti hoon| agar unakee jagaha koee devata bhee hota, to bhee main isase zyada aur kuchha na kar sakatee| unhen bhee mujhase prema hai,varaavara meraa munha dekhate rahate hain;lekin jo baat unake aur mere kaaboo se baahara hai, usake lie vaha kyaa kar sakate hain;aur main kyaa kar sakatee hoon? na vaha javaana ho sakate hain, na main bu.dhiya ho sakatee hoon| javaana banane ke lie vaha na jaane kitane rasa aur bhasma khaate rahate hain, main bu.dhiya banane ke lie doodha-ghee sava chho.de baithee hoon| sochatee hoon, mere duvalepana hee se avasthaa kaa bheda kuchha kama ho jaaya; lekin na unhen paushtika padaarthon se koee laabha hota hai; na mujhe upavaason se! jab se mansaaraama kaa dehaanta ho gaya hai, tabase unakee dashaa aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee hai|

krishnaa-mansaaraama ko to tuma bhee bahut pyaar kiya karti theen|

nirmalaa-vaha ladka hee aisaa tha ki jo dekhata tha, pyaar karata tha| aisee ba.dee-ba.dee doredaara okhen main ne kisee kee naheen dekhi| kamala kee bhaanti mukha haradama khilaa rahata tha| aisaa saahasee ki agar avasara aa pa.data, to aaga men phaanda jata| krishna, main tujhase sacha kehti hoon, jab vaha mere paasa aakara baitha jata tha, to main apane ko bhoola jati thi| jee chaahata tha, yaha haradama saamane baithaa rahe aur main dekha karoon! mere mann men paapa kaa lesha bhee na tha|agar ek kshana ke lie bhee main ne usakee ora kisee aur bhaava se dekha ho, to meree aankhen phoota jaaya; par na jaane kyon use apane paasa dekha kar meraa hridaya phoolaa na samaataa tha| iseelie mainne pa.dhane kaa svaanga racha, naheen to vaha ghar men aata hee na tha| yaha main jaanatee hoon ki agar usake mann men paapa hota, to main usake lie sabakuchha kar sakatee thi|

krishna-are bahina, chupa raho; kaisi baaten munha se nikaalatee ho|

nirmalaa-haan, yaha baat sunane men buree maalooma hoti hai aur hai bhee buree; lekin manushya kee prakriti ko to koee badala naheen sakata|' toohee bata-ek pachaasa varsha ke marda se teraa vivaaha ho jaaya, to too kyaa karegee?

krishnaa-bahina, main to jahara khaakara so rahoon| mujhase to usakaa munha bhee na dekhate bane!

nirmalaa-to basa yahee samajha le| us ladke ne kabhee meree ora aankh uthaa kar naheen dekha; lekin buddhe shakkee to hote hee hain-tumhaare jeejaa us ladke ke dushmana ho gae; aur aakhira usakee jaana lekara hee chho.dee| jisa din use maalooma ho gaya ki pitaa jee ke mann men meree ora se sandeha hai,usee din se use jvara cha.dha, jo jaana lekara hee utaraa| haaya! us antima samay kaa drishya aankhon se naheen utarata! main aspataala gaee thi, vaha jvara men behosha pada tha-uthane kee shakti na thi, lekin jyonhee meree aavaaja sunee, chaunka kar utha baithaa aur maataa-maataa kaha kar mere pairon par gira pa.da! (ro kar ) krishna,us samay aisaa jee chaahata tha ki apane praana nikaala kar use de doon| mere pairon par hee vaha moorchhita ho gaya; aur phir aankhen na kholee| daoktara ne usakee deha men taajaa
khoona daalane kaa prastaava kiya tha, yahee suna kar main dau.dee gaee thi; lekin jab taka daoktara log vaha kriya aarambha karen, usake praana nikala gae!

krishnaa-taajaa rakta pa.da jaane se usakee jaana bacha jati ?

nirmalaa-kaun jaanata hai ! lekin main to apane rudhira kee antima boonda dene kee taiyaara thi| us dashaa men bhee usakaa mukha-mandala deepaka kee bhaanti chamakata tha| agar vaha mujhe dekhate hee dau.da kar mere pairon par na gira pa.data-pahale kuchha rakta deha men pahuncha jaata, to shaayada bacha jata|

krishnaa-to tumane unhen usee vakta letaa kyon na diya?

nirmalaa-are pagalee, too abhee taka baat naheen samajhee| vaha mere pairon par gira kar aur maataa-putra kaa sambandha dikhaa kar, apane baapa ke dil se vaha sandeha nikaala dena chaahata tha| kevala iseelie vaha uthaa tha| meraa klesha mitaane ke lie usane praana die aur usakee vaha ichchhaa pooree ho gaee| tumhaare jeejaa jee usee din se seedhe ho gae | ab to unakee dashaa par mujhe dayaa aati hai | putra-shoka unake praana lekaran chho.degaa | mujha par sandeha karake mere saatha jo anyaaya kiya hai, ab usakaa pratishodha kar rahe hain| ab kee unakee soorata dekha kar too dara jaayagee| boo.dhe baabaa ho gae hain | kamara bhee kuchha jhuka chalee hai|

krishnaa-buddhe log itane shakee kyon hote hain; vahina ?

nirmalaa-yaha jaakara buddhon se poochha !

krishnaa-main to samajhatee hoon, unake dil men haradama ek chora saa baithaa rahata hogaa ki main is yuvatee ko prasanna naheen rakha sakata| iseelie jaraa-jaraa see baat par unhen shaka hone lagata hai |

nirmalaa-jaanatee to hai, phir mujhase kyon poochhatee hai ?

krishnaa-iseelie bechaaraa stree se davata bhee hogaa| dekhane vaale samajhate honge ki yaha bahut prema karata hai|

nirmalaa toone itane hee dinon men itanee baaten kahaan se seekha lee ? ina baaton ko jaane de, bata tujhe apana vara pasanda hai ? usakee tasveera to dekhi hogee?

krishnaa-haan, aaee to thi| laaoon, dekhogee ?

ek kshana men krishnaa ne tasveera laakara nirmalaa ke haath men rakha dee| nirmalaa ne muskaraa kar kaha-too ba.dee bhaagyavaana hai !

krishnaa-ammaan jee ne bhee bahut pasanda kiya |

nirmalaa-tujhe pasanda hai ki naheen, so kaha ; doosaron kee baat na chalaa| krishnaa-(lajaatee hui ) shakla-soorata to buree naheen hai,svabhaava kaa haala eeshvara jaane| shaastree jee to kehte the,aise susheela aur charitravaan yuvaka kama honge|

nirmalaa--yahaan se teree tasveera bhee gaee thi?

krishnaa-gaee to thi,shaastree jee hee to le gae the|

nirmalaa-unhen pasanda aaee?

krishnaa-ab kisee ke mann kee baat main kyaa jaanoon? shaastree jee to kehte the, bahut khusha hue the|

nirmalaa-achchha vata, tume kyaa upahaara doon| abhee se vata de, jisamen banavaa rakkhoon |

krishnaa-jo tumhaaraa jee chaahe de dena| unhen pustakon se bahut prema hai| achchhi-achchhi pustaken mangavaa dena|

nirmalaa-unake lie naheen poochhatee, tere lie poochhatee hoon|

krishnaa apane hee lie to main bhee kaha rahi hoon|

nirmalaa-(tasveera kee tarapha dekhatee hui) kapa.de sava khaddara ke maalooma hote hain|

krishnaa-haan, khadara ke ba.de premee hain| sunatee hoon.ki peetha par khaddara laada kar dehaaton men vechane jaayaa karte hain | vyaakhyaana dene men bhee chatura hain|

nirmalaa-tab to tujhe bhee khadara pahanana pa.degaa| tujhe to mote kapa.don se chi.dha hai? krishnaa-jab unhen mote kapa.de achchhe lagate hain, to mujhe kyon chi.dha hogee; main ne to charkhaa chalaanaa seekha liya hai|

nirmalaa-sacha! soota nikaala letee hai?

krishnaa-haan bahina, tho.daa-tho.daa nikaala letee hoon|jab vaha khaddara ke itane premee hain; to charakhaa bhee jaroora chalaate honge| main na chalaa sakoongee, to mujhe kitana lajjita honaa pa.degaa|

is tarah baaten karte-karte donon bahinen soee| koee do baje raat ko bachchi roee, to nirmalaa kee neenda khulee| dekha to krishnaa kee chaarapaaee khaalee pa.dee thi| nirmalaa ko aashcharya hua ki itanee raat gae krishnaa kahaan chalee gaee| shaayada paanee-vaanee peene gaee ho| magar paanee to sirahaane rakkhaa hua hai, phir kahaan gaee hai? usane do-teena baara usakaa naama lekara aavaaza dee; par krishnaa kaa pata na tha| tab to nirmalaa ghabaraa uthee| usake mann men bhaanti-bhaanti kee shankaaen hone lageen| sahasaa use khayal aaya ki shaayada apane kamare men na chalee gaee ho| bachchi so gaee, to vaha utha kar krishnaa ke kamare ke dvaara par aaee| usakaa anubhava theeka tha| krishnaa apane kamare men thi| saaraa ghar so raha tha; aur vaha baithee charkhaa chalaa rahi thi| itanee tanmayata se shaayada usane thiyetara bhee na dekha hogaa| nirmalaa danga raha gaee! andara jaakara boli-yaha kyaa kar rahi hai re, yaha charkhaa chalaane kaa samay hai?

krishnaa chaunka kar utha baithee; aur sankocha se sira jhukaa kar boli- tumhaaree neenda kaise khula gaee? paanee-vaanee to mainne rakha diya tha| nirmalaa-main kehti hoon, din ko tujhe samay naheen milata, jo pichhalee raat ko charkhaa lekara baithee hai?

krishnaa-din ko to phurasata hee naheen milatee|

nirmalaa-(soota dekha kar) soota to bahut maheena hai|

krishna-kahaan vahina, yaha soota to motaa hai| main baareeka soota kaata kar unake lie ek saaphaa banaanaa chaahatee hoon| yahee meraa upahaara hogaa|

nirmalaa-baat to toone khooba sochee hai| isase adhika moolyavaana vastu unakee drishti men aur kyaa hogee? achchha utha is vakta; kala kaatana! kaheen beemaara pa.da jaayagee; to yaha sab dharaa raha jaayagaa|

krishnaa-naheen meree bahina, tuma chala kar soo; main abhee aati hoon| nirmalaa ne adhika aagraha naheen kiya- letane chalee gaee| magar kisee tarah needa na aaee| krishnaa kee yaha utsukata aur yaha umanga dekha kar usakaa hridaya kisee alakshita aakaankshaa se aandolita ho uthaa| oha! is samay isakaa hridaya kitana praphullita ho raha hai! anuraaga ne ise kitana unmatta kar rakkhaa hai| tava use apane vivaaha kee yaada aaee| jisa din tilaka gaya tha, usee din se usakee saaree chanchalata, saaree sajeevitaa bidaa ho gaee thi! vaha apanee kotharee men baithee apanee kismata ko rotee thi; aur eeshvara se vinaya karti thi ki praana nikala jayen! aparaadhee jaise danda kee prateekshaa karata hai, usee bhaanti vaha vivaaha kee prateekshaa karti thi, us vivaaha kee-jisamen usake jeevana kee saaree abhilaashaaen vileena ho jaayangee, jab mandapa ke neeche bane hue havana-kunda men usakee aashaaen jala kar bhasma ho jaayengee!!
