# Nirmala — Adhyay 15

**Munshi Premchand**

pandrahvam parichchhed

NAmENirmala ko halanki apne hi ghar ke jhanjhaton se phursat na tha; par NAmEKrishna ke shadi ka sandesha pakad vah kisi tarah na ruk saki. uski mam ne bahut agrah karke bulaya tha. sabse bada akarshan yah tha ki NAmEKrishna ka shadi usi ghar men ho raha tha, jaham NAmENirmala ka shadi pahle tay hua tha. hairani yahi tha ki is bar bina kuchh dahej lie kaise shadi hone par taiyar ho gae. NAmENirmala ko NAmEKrishna ke vishay men badi chinta rahti thi. samjhati thimeri hi tarah vah bhi kisi ke gale marh di jaygi. bahut chahti thi ki mam ki kuchh madad karum, jisse NAmEKrishna ke lie koi yogya dulha mile; lekin idhar vakil sahab ke ghar baith jane aur mahajan ke nalish kar dene se uska hath bhi tanga tha. aisi hal men yah khabar pakad use badi shanti mili. chalne ki taiyari kar di,
vakil sahab steshan tak pahunchane ae. nanhim bachchi se unhen bahut pyaar tha. chhorte hi na the; yaham tak ki NAmENirmala ke sath chalne ko taiyar ho gae lekin shadi se ek mahine pahle unka sasural ja baithna NAmENirmala ko thik na malum hua.

NAmENirmala ne apni mam se ab tak apni musibat-katha na kahi thi. jo bat ho gai, uska rona rokar mam ko takliph dene aur rulane se kya phayda? isalie uski mam samjhati thi, NAmENirmala bade khushi se hai. ab jo NAmENirmala ki surat dekhi, to mano uske dil par dhakka sa lag gaya. larkiya sasural se ghul kar nahin ati; phir NAmENirmala jaisi ladki, jisko sukh ki sabhi samagriyam prapt thim! usne kitni hi larkiyo ko dauj ki chandrama ki tarah sasural jate aur purnachandra van kar ate dekha tha! man men kalpana kar rakkhi thi, NAmENirmala ka ranga nikhar gaya hoga, deh bhar kar sudaul ho gai hogi-angapratyanga ki shobha kuchh aur hi ho gai hogi. ab jo dekha, to vah adhi bhi na rahi thi! na yauvan ki chanchalta thi, na vah vihamsit chhavi, jo dil ko moh leti hai! vah kamaniyta, vah sukumarta jo vilasmay jivan se a jati hai, yaham nam ko na thi. mumh pila, cheshta giri hui, anga shithil, unnisven hi varsh men vuddhi ho gai thi. jav mam-betiyam ro-dhokar shant hui, to mam ne puchha-kyon ri, tujhe vaham khane ko na milta tha? isse kahim achchhi to tu yahim thi. vaham tujhe kya takliph thi?
NAmEKrishna ne hams kar kaha-vaham malikin thi ki nahin. malikin ko duniya bhar ki chintaen rahti haim, khana kab karen?

NAmENirmala -nahin ammam, vaham ka pani mujhe ras nahin ata-tabiyat bhari rahti hai.

mam-vakil sahab nyaute men aenge na? tab puchhungi ki apne phul si ladki le jakar uski yah gat bana dali. achchha, ab yah bata ki tune yaham rupae kyon bheje the? maimne to tujhse kabhi na mange the. lakh gai-gujri hum lekin beti ka paisa khane ki niyat nahin!

NAmENirmala ne chakit hokar puchha-kisne rupae bheje the? ammam! maimne to nahin bheje!

mam-jhuth na bol! tune 500) ke not nahin bheje the?

NAmEKrishna -bheje nahin the, to kya asman se agae? tumhara nam saph likha tha. mohar bhi vahim ki thi.

NAmENirmala -tumhare charan chhukar kahti hum, maimne rupae nahin bheje. yah kab ki bat hai?

mam-are yahi do-dhai mahine hue honge. magar tune nahin bheje, to ae kaham se?

NAmENirmala yah maim kya janum? magar maimne rupae nahin bheje. hamare yaham to jab se javan beta mara hai, kachhari hi nahin jate. mera hath to ap hi tanga tha, rupae kaham se ate?

mam-yah to bade hairani ki bat hai! vaham aur koi
tera saga-sambandhi to nahin hai? vakil sahab ne tujhse chhipa kar to nahin bheje?

NAmENirmala - nahin ammam, mujhe to yakin nahin.

mam-iska pata lagana chahiye. maine sare rupae NAmEKrishna ke gahne kapde men kharch kar dale. yahi badi mushkil hui.

donon ladki men kisi vishay par jhagra uth khada hua; aur NAmEKrishna udhar phaisla karne chali gai, to NAmENirmala ne mam se kaha is shadi ki bat sun kar mujhe bada hairani hua. yah kaise hua ammo?

mam-yaham jo sunta hai, danton ungli davata hai. jin logon ne paki ki-karai vat pher di; aur sirph thore se rupae ke lobh se! ve av vina kuchh lie kaise shadi karne par taiyar ho gaye, samajh men nahin ata. unhomne khud hi chitthi bheja. maimne saph likh diya ki mere pas dene lene ko kuchh nahin hai; kush-ladki hi se apki seva kar sakti hum.

NAmENirmala -iska kuchh javab nahin diya?

mam-shastri ji chitthi lekar gae the. vah to yah kahte the ki av munshi ji kuchh lene ke ichchhuk nahin haim. apni pahli vada- khilaphi par kuchh lajjit bhi haim. munshi ji se to itni udarta ki ummid na thi, magar sunti hum unke bade beta bahut sajan aadmi haim. unhomne kah-sun kar bap ko raji kiya hai.
NAmENirmala -pahle to vah mahashay bhi thaili chahte the na?
mam-ham, magar ab to shastri ji kahte the ki dahej ke nam se chirhte haim. suna hai, yaham shadi na karne par pachhtate bhi the. rupae ke lie bat chhori thi; aur rupae khub pae; par aurat pasand nahin!

NAmENirmala ke man men us aadmi ko dekhne ki badi prabal utkantha hui, jo uski avahelna karke ab uski bahin ka uddhar karna chahta hai. prayashchitt sahi; lekin kitne aise insaan haim, jo is tarah prayashchitt karne ko taiyar haim. unse baten karne ke lie namra shabdon men unka beizzati karne ke lie, apni anupam chhabi dikha kar unhen aur bhi jalane ke lie NAmENirmala ka dil adhir ho utha. rat ko donon bahinen ek hi kamre men soi. muhalle men kin-kin larkiyo ka shadi ho gaya, kaun-kaun si larkori huim; kis-kis ko shadi dhum-dham se hua, kis-kis ke pati ladki ki ichchhanukul mile, kaun kitne aur kaise gahne charhave men laya- inhim vishyon par donon men badi der tak baten hoti rahim. NAmEKrishna bar-bar chahti thi ki bahin ke ghar ka kuchh hal puchh; magar NAmENirmala use kuchh puchhne ka avsar na deti thi. vah janti thi ki yah jo baten puchhegi, uske batane men mujhe sankoch hoga. akhir ek bar NAmEKrishna puchh hi baithi-jija ji bhi aenge na?

NAmENirmala -ane ko kaha to hai.

NAmEKrishna -ab to tumse khush rahte haim na, ya ab bhi vahi hal hai. maim to suna karti thi, duhaj pati apni aurat ko pranon
se bhi priy samjhata hai, yaham vilkul ulti vat dekhi. akhir kis vat par bigrate rahte haim?

NAmENirmala -av maim kisi ke man ki bat kya janum?

NAmEKrishna -maim to samjhati hum, tumhari rukhai se vah chirhte honge. tum to yahim se jali hui gai thim. vaham bhi unhen kuchh kaha hoga?

NAmENirmala --yah vat nahin hai NAmEKrishna; maim saugand khakar kahti hum, jo mere man men unki or se jara bhi mail ho. mujhse jaham tak ho sakta hai, unki seva karti hum. agar unki jagah koi devta bhi hota, to bhi maim isse jyada aur kuchh na kar sakti. unhen bhi mujhse pyaar hai, varavar mera mumh dekhte rahte haim; lekin jo bat unke aur mere kabu se bahar hai, uske lie vah kya kar sakte haim; aur maim kya kar sakti hum? na vah javan ho sakte haim, na maim burhiya ho sakti hum. javan banne ke lie vah na jane kitne ras aur bhasma khate rahte haim, maim burhiya banne ke lie dudh-ghi sav chhore baithi hum. sochti hum, mere duvalepan hi se avastha ka bhed kuchh kam ho jay; lekin na unhen paushtik padarthon se koi phayda hota hai; na mujhe upvaso se! jab se NAmEmansaram ka maut ho gaya hai, tabse unki hal aur bhi kharab ho gai hai.

NAmEKrishna - NAmEmansaram ko to tum bhi bahut pyaar kiya karti thim.

NAmENirmala -vah larka hi aisa tha ki jo dekhta tha, pyaar karta tha. aisi badi-badi doredar okhen maim ne kisi ki nahin dekhi.
kamal ki tarah mumh hardam khila rahta tha. aisa sahsi ki agar avsar a parta, to ag men phand jata. NAmEKrishna, maim tujhse sach kahti hum, jab vah mere pas akar baith jata tha, to maim apne ko bhul jati thi. ji chahta tha, yah hardam samne baitha rahe aur maim dekha karum! mere man men pap ka lesh bhi na tha. agar ek pal ke lie bhi maim ne uski or kisi aur bhav se dekha ho, to meri ankhen phut jay; par na jane kyon use apne pas dekh kar mera dil phula na samata tha. isilie maimne parhne ka svang racha, nahin to vah ghar men ata hi na tha. yah maim janti hum ki agar uske man men pap hota, to maim uske lie sabkuchh kar sakti thi.

krishna-are bahin, chup raho; kaisi baten mumh se nikalti ho.

NAmENirmala -ham, yah bat sunne men buri malum hoti hai aur hai bhi buri; lekin manushya ki prakriti ko to koi badal nahin sakta. tuhi bata-ek pachas varsh ke mard se tera shadi ho jay, to tu kya karegi?

NAmEKrishna -bahin, maim to jahar khakar so rahum. mujhse to uska mumh bhi na dekhte bane!

NAmENirmala -to bas yahi samajh le. us larke ne kabhi meri or ankh utha kar nahin dekha; lekin buddhe shakki to hote hi haim-tumhare jija us larke ke dushman ho gae; aur akhir uski jan lekar hi chhori. jis din use malum ho gaya ki pita ji ke man men meri or se shak hai, usi din se use jvar
chadha, jo jan lekar hi utra. hay! us antim samay ka drishya ankhon se nahin utrata! maim aspatal gai thi, vah jvar men behosh pada tha-uthne ki shakti na thi, lekin jyomhi meri avaj suni, chaunk kar uth baitha aur mam-mam kah kar mere pairon par gir pada! (ro kar ) NAmEKrishna, us samay aisa ji chahta tha ki apne jan nikal kar use de dum. mere pairon par hi vah murchhit ho gaya; aur phir ankhen na kholi. doktar ne uski deh men taja
khun dalne ka prastav kiya tha, yahi sun kar maim dauri gai thi; lekin jab tak doktar log vah kriya shuru karen, uske jan nikal gae!

NAmEKrishna -taja rakt par jane se uski jan bach jati?

NAmENirmala -kaun janta hai! lekin maim to apne rudhir ki antim bund dene ki taiyar thi. us hal men bhi uska mumh-mandal dipak ki tarah chamkata tha. agar vah mujhe dekhte hi daur kar mere pairon par na gir parta-pahle kuchh rakt deh men pahunch jata, to shayad bach jata.

NAmEKrishna -to tumne unhen usi vakt leta kyon na diya?

NAmENirmala -are pagli, tu abhi tak bat nahin samjhi. vah mere pairon par gir kar aur mam-beta ka rishta dikha kar, apne bap ke dil se vah shak nikal dena chahta tha. sirph isilie vah utha tha. mera klesh mitane ke lie usne jan die aur uski vah chah puri ho gai. tumhare jija ji usi
din se sidhe ho gae. ab to unki hal par mujhe daya ati hai. beta-shok unke jan lekaram chhorega. mujh par shak karke mere sath jo anyay kiya hai, ab uska pratishodh kar rahe haim. ab ki unki surat dekh kar tu dar jaygi. burhe baba ho gae haim. kamar bhi kuchh jhuk chali hai.

NAmEKrishna -buddhe log itne shaki kyon hote haim; vahin?

NAmENirmala -yah jakar buddhon se puchh!

NAmEKrishna -maim to samjhati hum, unke dil men hardam ek chor sa baitha rahta hoga ki maim is yuvti ko khush nahin rakh sakta. isilie jara-jara si bat par unhen shak hone lagta hai.

NAmENirmala -janti to hai, phir mujhse kyon puchhti hai?

NAmEKrishna -isilie bechara aurat se davta bhi hoga. dekhne vale samjhate honge ki yah bahut pyaar karta hai.

NAmENirmala tune itne hi dinon men itni baten kaham se sikh li? in baton ko jane de, bata tujhe apna dulha pasand hai? uski tasvir to dekhi hogi?

NAmEKrishna -ham, ai to thi. laum, dekhogi?

ek pal men NAmEKrishna ne tasvir lakar NAmENirmala ke hath men rakh di. NAmENirmala ne muskara kar kaha-tu badi bhagyavan hai!

NAmEKrishna -ammam ji ne bhi bahut pasand kiya.

NAmENirmala -tujhe pasand hai ki nahin, so kah; dusron ki bat na chala.
NAmEKrishna -(lajati hui ) shakla-surat to buri nahin hai, svabhav ka hal ishvar jane. shastri ji to kahte the, aise sushil aur charitravan yuvak kam honge.

NAmENirmala --yaham se teri tasvir bhi gai thi?

NAmEKrishna -gai to thi, shastri ji hi to le gae the.

NAmENirmala -unhen pasand ai?

NAmEKrishna -ab kisi ke man ki bat maim kya janum? shastri ji to kahte the, bahut khush hue the.

NAmENirmala -achchha vata, tume kya uphar dum. abhi se vata de, jismen banva rakkhum.

NAmEKrishna -jo tumhara ji chahe de dena. unhen pustakon se bahut pyaar hai. achchhi-achchhi pustaken mangva dena.

NAmENirmala -unke lie nahin puchhti, tere lie puchhti hum.

NAmEKrishna apne hi lie to maim bhi kah rahi hum.

NAmENirmala -(tasvir ki taraph dekhti hui) kapde sav khaddar ke malum hote haim.

NAmEKrishna -ham, khadar ke bade premi haim. sunti hum. ki pith par khaddar lad kar dehaton men vechne jaya karte haim. vyakhyan dene men bhi hoshiyar haim.

NAmENirmala -tab to tujhe bhi khadar pahnana parega. tujhe to mote kapron se chirh hai?
NAmEKrishna -jab unhen mote kapde achchhe lagte haim, to mujhe kyon chirh hogi; maim ne to charkha chalana sikh liya hai.

NAmENirmala -sach! sut nikal leti hai?

NAmEKrishna -ham bahin, thoda-thoda nikal leti hum. jab vah khaddar ke itne premi haim; to charkha bhi jarur chalate honge. maim na chala sakungi, to mujhe kitna lajjit hona parega.

is tarah baten karte-karte donon bahinen soi. koi do baje rat ko bachchi roi, to NAmENirmala ki nind khuli. dekha to NAmEKrishna ki charapai khali padi thi. NAmENirmala ko hairani hua ki itni rat gae NAmEKrishna kaham chali gai. shayad pani-vani pine gai ho. magar pani to sirhane rakkha hua hai, phir kaham gai hai? usne do-tin bar uska nam lekar avaz di; par NAmEKrishna ka pata na tha. tab to NAmENirmala ghabra uthi. uske man men tarah-tarah ki shankaen hone lagim. sahsa use khyal aya ki shayad apne kamre men na chali gai ho. bachchi so gai, to vah uth kar NAmEKrishna ke kamre ke dvar par ai. uska anubhav thik tha. NAmEKrishna apne kamre men thi. sara ghar so raha tha; aur vah baithi charkha chala rahi thi. itni tanmayta se shayad usne thiyetar bhi na dekha hoga. NAmENirmala danga rah gai! andar jakar boli-yah kya kar rahi hai re, yah charkha chalane ka samay hai?

NAmEKrishna chaunk kar uth baithi; aur sankoch se sir jhuka kar boli- tumhari nind kaise khul gai? pani-vani to maimne rakh diya tha.
NAmENirmala -maim kahti hum, din ko tujhe samay nahin milta, jo pichhli rat ko charkha lekar baithi hai?

NAmEKrishna -din ko to phursat hi nahin milti.

NAmENirmala -(sut dekh kar) sut to bahut mahin hai.

krishna-kaham vahin, yah sut to mota hai. maim barik sut kat kar unke lie ek sapha banana chahti hum. yahi mera uphar hoga.

NAmENirmala -bat to tune khub sochi hai. isse zyada mulyavan vastu unki nazar men aur kya hogi? achchha uth is vakt; kal katna! kahim bimar par jaygi; to yah sab dhara rah jayga.

NAmEKrishna -nahin meri bahin, tum chal kar soo; maim abhi ati hum. NAmENirmala ne zyada agrah nahin kiya- letne chali gai. magar kisi tarah nid na ai. NAmEKrishna ki yah utsukta aur yah umanga dekh kar uska dil kisi alakshit akanksha se andolit ho utha. oh! is samay iska dil kitna praphullit ho raha hai! anurag ne ise kitna unmatt kar rakkha hai. tav use apne shadi ki yad ai. jis din tilak gaya tha, usi din se uski sari chanchalta, sari sajivita bida ho gai thi! vah apni kothri men baithi apni kismat ko roti thi; aur ishvar se narmi karti thi ki jan nikal jayen! apradhi jaise saza ki intzar karta hai, usi tarah vah shadi ki intzar
karti thi, us shadi ki-jismen uske jivan ki sari abhilashaen vilin ho jayangi, jab mandap ke niche bane hue havan-kund men uski ashaen jal kar bhasma ho jayengi!!
