# Nirmala — Adhyay 15

**Munshi Premchand**

pandrahavaan parichchhed

Nirmala ko halaanki apane hee ghar ke jhanyjhaton se avakaash n tha; par krishnaa ke shaadee ka sandeshaa paakar vah kisee tarah n ruk sakee. usakee maataa ne bahut jor dekar kahanaa karake bulaayaa thaa. sabase badaa aakarshan yah tha ki krishnaa ka shaadee usee ghar mein ho rahaa tha, jahaan Nirmala ka shaadee pahale tay huaa thaa. hairaanee yahee tha ki is baar binaa kuchh dahej lie kaise shaadee hone par taiyaar ho gae. Nirmala ko krishnaa ke vishay mein badee fikr rahatee thee. samajhatee thi — meree hee tarah vah bhi kisee ke gale madh dee jaayagee. bahut chaahatee thi ki maataa ki kuchh madad karoon, jisase krishnaa ke lie koee yogy var mile; lekin idhar vakeel Sahab ke ghar baith jaane aur mahaajan ke naalish kar dene se usakaa haath bhi tanggg thaa. aisee dashaa mein yah khabar paakar use badee shaanti milee. chalane ki taiyaaree kar dee, vakeel Sahab steshan tak pahunchaane aae. nanheen bachchi se unhen bahut pyaar thaa. chhodate hee n the; yahan tak ki Nirmala ke saath chalane ko taiyaar ho gae lekin shaadee se ek maheene pahale unakaa sasuraal jaa baithanaa Nirmala ko theek n maaloom huaa.

Nirmala ne apanee maataa se ab tak apanee vipatti-kathaa n kahee thee. jo baat ho gaee, usakaa ronaa rokar maataa ko takaleeph dene aur rulaane se kyaa phaayadaa? isalie usakee maataa samajhatee thi, Nirmala bade khushi se hai. ab jo Nirmala ki soorat dekhee, to maano usake dil par dhakkaa saa lag gayaa. ladakiyaan sasuraal se ghul kar nahin aatee; phir Nirmala jaisee ladakee, jisako sukh ki sabhee saamagriyaan milaa theen! usane kitanee hee ladakiyon ko dauj ki chandramaa ki bhaanti sasuraal jaate aur poornachandr van kar aate dekhaa tha! man mein khayaal kar rakkhee thi, Nirmala ka ranggg nikhar gayaa hogaa, deh bhar kar sudaul ho gaee hogee-angggapratyanggg ki shobhaa kuchh aur hee ho gaee hogee. ab jo dekhaa, to vah aadhee bhi n rahee thi! n yauvan ki chanychalataa thi, n vah vihansit chhavi, jo dil ko moh letee hai! vah kamaneeyataa, vah sukumaarataa jo vilaasamay zindagi se aa jaatee hai, yahan naam ko n thee. munh peelaa, koshish giree huee, anggg shithil, unneesaven hee varsh mein vuddhee ho gaee thee. jav maan-betiyaan ro-dhokar shaant huee, to maataa ne poochhaa-kyon ree, tujhe wahan khaane ko n milataa tha? isase kaheen achchhi to too yaheen thee. wahan tujhe kyaa takaleeph thi? krishnaa ne hans kar kahaa-wahan maalikin thi ki naheen. maalikin ko duniya bhar ki phikraen rahatee hain, khana kab karen?

Nirmala-nahin ammaan, wahan ka paani mujhe raas nahin aataa-tabeeyat bhaaree rahatee hai.

maataa-vakeel Sahab nyaute mein aaengge n? tab poochhoonggee ki aapane phool see ladakee le jaakar usakee yah gat banaa daalee. achchha, ab yah bataa ki toone yahan rupaye kyon bheje the? mainne to tujhase kabhee n maangge the. laakh gaee-gujaree hoon lekin betee ka dhan khaane ki neeyat nahin!

Nirmala ne chakit hokar poochhaa-kisane rupaye bheje the? ammaan! mainne to nahin bheje!

maataa-jhooth n bol! toone 500) ke not nahin bheje the?

krishnaa-bheje nahin the, to kyaa aasamaan se aagae? tumhaaraa naam saaph likhaa thaa. mohar bhi vaheen ki thee.

Nirmala-tumhaare charan chhookar kahatee hoon, mainne rupaye nahin bheje. yah kab ki baat hai?

maataa-are yahee do-dhaaee maheene hue hongge. magar toone nahin bheje, to aae kahaan se?

Nirmala yah main kyaa jaanoon? magar mainne rupaye nahin bheje. hamaare yahan to jab se javaan betaa maraa hai, kachaharee hee nahin jaate. meraa haath to aap hee tanggg tha, rupaye kahaan se aate?

maataa-yah to bade hairaanee ki baat hai! wahan aur koee teraa sagaa-rishtaaee to nahin hai? vakeel Sahab ne tujhase chhipaa kar to nahin bheje?

Nirmala- nahin ammaan, mujhe to bharosaa naheen.

maataa-isakaa pataa lagaanaa chaahie. maine saare rupaye krishnaa ke gahane kapde mein kharch kar daale. yahee badee mushkil huee.

donon ladakee mein kisee vishay par vivaad uth khadaa huaa; aur krishnaa udhar faisla karane chalee gaee, to Nirmala ne maataa se kahaa is shaadee ki baat sun kar mujhe badaa hairaanee huaa. yah kaise huaa ammo?

maataa-yahan jo sunataa hai, daanton unggalee davaataa hai. jin logon ne pakee ki-karaaee vaat pher dee; aur keval thode se rupaye ke lobh se! ve av vinaa kuchh lie kaise shaadee karane par taiyaar ho gaye, samajh mein nahin aataa. unhonne khud hee patr bhejaa. mainne saaph likh diyaa ki mere paas dene lene ko kuchh nahin hai; kush-kanyaa hee se aapakee sevaa kar sakatee hoon.

Nirmala-isakaa kuchh javaab nahin diyaa?

maataa-shaaaurat jee patr lekar gae the. vah to yah kahate the ki av munshee jee kuchh lene ke ichchhuk nahin hain. apanee pahalee vaadaa- khilaaphee par kuchh lajjit bhi hain. munshee jee se to itanee udaarataa ki ummeed n thi, magar sunatee hoon unake bade putr bahut sajan aadmi hain. unhonne kah-sun kar baap ko raajee kiyaa hai.
Nirmala-pahale to vah mahaashay bhi thailee chaahate the n? maataa-haan, magar ab to shaaaurat jee kahate the ki dahej ke naam se chidhate hain. sunaa hai, yahan shaadee n karane par pachhataate bhi the. rupaye ke lie baat chhodee thi; aur rupaye khoob paae; par aurat pasand nahin!

Nirmala ke man mein us mard ko dekhane ki badee prabal utkanthaa huee, jo usakee avahelanaa karake ab usakee bahin ka uddhaar karanaa chaahataa hai. praayashchitt sahee; lekin kitane aise praanee hain, jo is tarah praayashchitt karane ko taiyaar hain. unase baaten karane ke lie namr shabdon mein unakaa nafrat karane ke lie, apanee anupam chhabi dikhaa kar unhen aur bhi jalaane ke lie Nirmala ka dil adheer ho uthaa. raat ko donon bahinen ek hee kamare mein soee. muhalle mein kin-kin ladakiyon ka shaadee ho gayaa, kaun-kaun see ladakoree hueen; kis-kis ko shaadee dhoom-dhaam se huaa, kis-kis ke shauhar kanyaa ki ichchhaanukool mile, kaun kitane aur kaise gahane chadhaave mein laayaa- inheen vishayon par donon mein badee der tak baaten hotee raheen. krishnaa baar-baar chaahatee thi ki bahin ke ghar ka kuchh haal poochh; magar Nirmala use kuchh poochhane ka avasar n detee thee. vah jaanatee thi ki yah jo baaten poochhegee, usake bataane mein mujhe sanggkoch hogaa. aakhir ek baar krishnaa poochh hee baithee-jeejaa jee bhi aaengge n?

Nirmala-aane ko kahaa to hai.

krishnaa-ab to tumase khush rahate hain n, ya ab bhi vahee haal hai. main to sunaa karatee thi, duhaaj shauhar apanee aurat ko praanon se bhi priy samajhataa hai, yahan vilakul ulatee vaat dekhee. aakhir kis vaat par bigadate rahate hain?

Nirmala-av main kisee ke man ki baat kyaa jaanoon?

krishnaa-main to samajhatee hoon, tumhaaree rukhaaee se vah chidhate hongge. tum to yaheen se jalee huee gaee theen. wahan bhi unhen kuchh kahaa hogaa?

Nirmala--yah vaat nahin hai krishnaa; main saugand khaakar kahatee hoon, jo mere man mein unakee or se jaraa bhi mail ho. mujhase jahaan tak ho sakataa hai, unakee sevaa karatee hoon. agar unakee jagah koee devataa bhi hotaa, to bhi main isase jyaadaa aur kuchh n kar sakatee. unhen bhi mujhase pyaar hai, varaavar meraa munh dekhate rahate hain; lekin jo baat unake aur mere kaaboo se baahar hai, usake lie vah kyaa kar sakate hain; aur main kyaa kar sakatee hoon? n vah javaan ho sakate hain, n main budhiyaa ho sakatee hoon. javaan banane ke lie vah n jaane kitane ras aur bhasm khaate rahate hain, main budhiyaa banane ke lie doodh-ghee sav chhode baithee hoon. sochatee hoon, mere duvalepan hee se avasthaa ka bhed kuchh kam ho jaay; lekin n unhen paushtik padaarthon se koee laabh hotaa hai; n mujhe upavaason se! jab se mansaaraam ka dehaant ho gayaa hai, tabase unakee dashaa aur bhi kharaab ho gaee hai.

krishnaa-mansaaraam ko to tum bhi bahut pyaar kiyaa karatee theen.

Nirmala-vah ladakaa hee aisaa tha ki jo dekhataa tha, pyaar karataa thaa. aisee badee-badee doredaar okhen main ne kisee ki nahin dekhee. kamal ki bhaanti munh haradam khilaa rahataa thaa. aisaa himmatee ki agar avasar aa padataa, to aag mein phaand jaataa. krishnaa, main tujhase sach kahatee hoon, jab vah mere paas aakar baith jaataa tha, to main apane ko bhool jaatee thee. jee chaahataa tha, yah haradam saamane baithaa rahe aur main dekhaa karoon! mere man mein paap ka lesh bhi n thaa. agar ek pal ke lie bhi main ne usakee or kisee aur bhaav se dekhaa ho, to meree aankhen phoot jaay; par n jaane kyon use apane paas dekh kar meraa dil phoolaa n samaataa thaa. iseelie mainne padhane ka svaangg rachaa, nahin to vah ghar mein aataa hee n thaa. yah main jaanatee hoon ki agar usake man mein paap hotaa, to main usake lie sabakuchh kar sakatee thee.

krishn-are bahin, chup raho; kaisee baaten munh se nikaalatee ho.

Nirmala-haan, yah baat sunane mein buree maaloom hotee hai aur hai bhi buree; lekin insaan ki prakriti ko to koee badal nahin sakataa.' toohee bataa-ek pachaas varsh ke mard se teraa shaadee ho jaay, to too kyaa karegee?

krishnaa-bahin, main to jahar khaakar so rahoon. mujhase to usakaa munh bhi n dekhate bane!

Nirmala-to bas yahee samajh le. us ladake ne kabhee meree or aankh uthaa kar nahin dekhaa; lekin buddhe shakkee to hote hee hain-tumhaare jeejaa us ladake ke dushman ho gae; aur aakhir usakee jaan lekar hee chhodee. jis din use maaloom ho gayaa ki pitaa jee ke man mein meree or se shak hai, usee din se use jvar chadhaa, jo jaan lekar hee utaraa. haay! us antim samay ka drishy aangkhon se nahin utarataa! main aspataal gaee thi, vah jvar mein behosh padaa tha-uthane ki taakat n thi, lekin jyonhee meree aavaaj sunee, chaungk kar uth baithaa aur maataa-maataa kah kar mere pairon par gir padaa! (ro kar ) krishnaa, us samay aisaa jee chaahataa tha ki apane praan nikaal kar use de doon. mere pairon par hee vah moorchhit ho gayaa; aur phir aankhen n kholee. doktar ne usakee deh mein taajaa
khoon daalane ka prastaav kiyaa tha, yahee sun kar main daudee gaee thi; lekin jab tak doktar log vah kriyaa shuroo karen, usake praan nikal gae!

krishnaa-taajaa rakt pad jaane se usakee jaan bach jaatee?

Nirmala-kaun jaanataa hai! lekin main to apane rudhir ki antim boond dene ki taiyaar thee. us dashaa mein bhi usakaa munh-mandal deepak ki bhaanti chamakataa thaa. agar vah mujhe dekhate hee daud kar mere pairon par n gir padataa-pahale kuchh rakt deh mein pahunch jaataa, to shaayad bach jaataa.

krishnaa-to tumane unhen usee vakt letaa kyon n diyaa?

Nirmala-are pagalee, too abhee tak baat nahin samajhee. vah mere pairon par gir kar aur maataa-putr ka rishtaa dikhaa kar, apane baap ke dil se vah shak nikaal denaa chaahataa thaa. keval iseelie vah uthaa thaa. meraa klesh mitaane ke lie usane praan die aur usakee vah ichchhaa pooree ho gaee. tumhaare jeejaa jee usee din se seedhe ho gae. ab to unakee dashaa par mujhe taras aatee hai. putr-shok unake praan lekarna chhodegaa. mujh par shak karake mere saath jo naainsaaphee kiyaa hai, ab usakaa pratishodh kar rahe hain. ab ki unakee soorat dekh kar too dar jaayagee. boodhe baabaa ho gae hain. kamar bhi kuchh jhuk chalee hai.

krishnaa-buddhe log itane shakee kyon hote hain; vahin?

Nirmala-yah jaakar buddhon se poochh!

krishnaa-main to samajhatee hoon, unake dil mein haradam ek chor saa baithaa rahataa hogaa ki main is javaan ladakee ko khush nahin rakh sakataa. iseelie jaraa-jaraa see baat par unhen shak hone lagataa hai.

Nirmala-jaanatee to hai, phir mujhase kyon poochhatee hai?

krishnaa-iseelie bechaaraa aurat se davataa bhi hogaa. dekhane vaale samajhate hongge ki yah bahut pyaar karataa hai.

Nirmala toone itane hee dinon mein itanee baaten kahaan se seekh lee? in baaton ko jaane de, bataa tujhe apanaa var pasand hai? usakee tasveer to dekhee hogee?

krishnaa-haan, aaee to thee. laaoon, dekhogee?

ek pal mein krishnaa ne tasveer laakar Nirmala ke haath mein rakh dee. Nirmala ne muskaraa kar kahaa-too badee kismatavaan hai!

krishnaa-ammaan jee ne bhi bahut pasand kiyaa.

Nirmala-tujhe pasand hai ki nahin, so kah; doosaron ki baat n chalaa. krishnaa-(lajaatee huee ) shakl-soorat to buree nahin hai, svabhaav ka haal Bhagwan jaane. shaaaurat jee to kahate the, aise susheel aur charitravaan javaan aadmi kam hongge.

Nirmala--yahan se teree tasveer bhi gaee thi?

krishnaa-gaee to thi, shaaaurat jee hee to le gae the.

Nirmala-unhen pasand aaee?

krishnaa-ab kisee ke man ki baat main kyaa jaanoon? shaaaurat jee to kahate the, bahut khush hue the.

Nirmala-achchha vataa, tume kyaa upahaar doon. abhee se vataa de, jisamen banavaa rakkhoon.

krishnaa-jo tumhaaraa jee chaahe de denaa. unhen pustakon se bahut pyaar hai. achchhi-achchhi pustaken manggavaa denaa.

Nirmala-unake lie nahin poochhatee, tere lie poochhatee hoon.

krishnaa apane hee lie to main bhi kah rahee hoon.

Nirmala-(tasveer ki taraph dekhatee huee) kapde sav khaddar ke maaloom hote hain.

krishnaa-haan, khadar ke bade pyaaree hain. sunatee hoon. ki peeth par khaddar laad kar dehaaton mein vechane jaayaa karate hain. vyaakhyaan dene mein bhi chatur hain.

Nirmala-tab to tujhe bhi khadar pahananaa padegaa. tujhe to mote kapadon se chidh hai? krishnaa-jab unhen mote kapde achchhe lagate hain, to mujhe kyon chidh hogee; main ne to charkhaa chalaanaa seekh liyaa hai.

Nirmala-sach! soot nikaal letee hai?

krishnaa-haan bahin, thodaa-thodaa nikaal letee hoon. jab vah khaddar ke itane pyaaree hain; to charakhaa bhi jaroor chalaate hongge. main n chalaa sakoonggee, to mujhe kitanaa lajjit honaa padegaa.

is tarah baaten karate-karate donon bahinen soee. koee do baje raat ko bachchi roee, to Nirmala ki neend khulee. dekhaa to krishnaa ki chaarapaaee khaalee padee thee. Nirmala ko hairaanee huaa ki itanee raat gae krishnaa kahaan chalee gaee. shaayad paani-vaanee peene gaee ho. magar paani to sirahaane rakkhaa huaa hai, phir kahaan gaee hai? usane do-teen baar usakaa naam lekar aavaaj dee; par krishnaa ka pataa n thaa. tab to Nirmala ghabaraa uthee. usake man mein bhaanti-bhaanti ki shanggkaaen hone lageen. sahasaa use khyaal kamaaeeaa ki shaayad apane kamare mein n chalee gaee ho. bachchi so gaee, to vah uth kar krishnaa ke kamare ke dvaar par aaee. usakaa tajurbaa theek thaa. krishnaa apane kamare mein thee. saaraa ghar so rahaa tha; aur vah baithee charkhaa chalaa rahee thee. itanee tanmayataa se shaayad usane thiyetar bhi n dekhaa hogaa. Nirmala danggg rah gaee! andar jaakar bolee-yah kyaa kar rahee hai re, yah charkhaa chalaane ka samay hai?

krishnaa chaungk kar uth baithee; aur sanggkoch se sir jhukaa kar bolee- tumhaaree neend kaise khul gaee? paani-vaanee to mainne rakh diyaa thaa. Nirmala-main kahatee hoon, din ko tujhe samay nahin milataa, jo pichhalee raat ko charkhaa lekar baithee hai?

krishnaa-din ko to phurasat hee nahin milatee.

Nirmala-(soot dekh kar) soot to bahut maheen hai.

krishn-kahaan vahin, yah soot to motaa hai. main baareek soot kaat kar unake lie ek saaphaa banaanaa chaahatee hoon. yahee meraa upahaar hogaa.

Nirmala-baat to toone khoob sochee hai. isase adhik moolyavaan vastu unakee drishti mein aur kyaa hogee? achchha uth is vakt; kal kaatanaa! kaheen beemaar pad jaayagee; to yah sab dharaa rah jaayagaa.

krishnaa-nahin meree bahin, tum chal kar soo; main abhee aatee hoon. Nirmala ne adhik jor dekar kahanaa nahin kiyaa- letane chalee gaee. magar kisee tarah need n aaee. krishnaa ki yah utsukataa aur yah umanggg dekh kar usakaa dil kisee alakshit aakaangksh se aandolit ho uthaa. oh! is samay isakaa dil kitanaa praphullit ho rahaa hai! anuraag ne ise kitanaa unmatt kar rakkhaa hai. tav use apane shaadee ki yaad aaee. jis din tilak gayaa tha, usee din se usakee saaree chanychalataa, saaree sajindaaaa bidaa ho gaee thi! vah apanee kotharee mein baithee apanee kismat ko rotee thi; aur Bhagwan se vinay karatee thi ki praan nikal jaayen! jurmee jaise sajaa ki intezar karataa hai, usee bhaanti vah shaadee ki intezar karatee thi, us shaadee ki-jisamen usake zindagi ki saaree abhilaashaaen vileen ho jaayanggee, jab mandap ke neeche bane hue havan-kund mein usakee ummeedaen jal kar bhasm ho jaayenggee!!
