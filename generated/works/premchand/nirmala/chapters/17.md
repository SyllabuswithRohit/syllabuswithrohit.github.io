# Nirmala — Adhyay 17

**Munshi Premchand**

satrahavaan parichchhed

krishnaa ke shaadee ke baad sudhaa chalee gaee; lekin Nirmala maike hee mein rah gaee. vakeel Sahab baar-baar likhate the; par vah n jaatee thee. wahan jaane ko usakaa jee hee n chaahataa thaa. wahan koee aisee cheej n thi, jo use kheench le jaaya. yahan maataa ki sevaa aur chhote bhaaiyon ki dekh-bhaal mein usakaa samay bade khushi se kat jaataa thaa. vakeel Sahab khud aate, to shaayad vah jaane par raajee ho jaatee; lekin is shaadee mein muhalle ki kaee striyon ne unakee vah durgati ki thi ki bechaare aane ka naam hee n lete the. sudhaa ne bhi kaee baar patr likhaa; par Nirmala ne usase bhi heele-havaale kie. aakhir ek din sudhaa ne naukar ko saath liyaa; aur svayan aa dhamakee!

jab donon gale mil chukeen, to sudhaa ne kahaa — tumhe to wahan jaate maano dar lagataa hai. Nirmala-haan bahin, dar to lagataa hai. byaah ki gaee teen saal mein aaee; ab ki to wahan umr hee khatam ho jaayagee; phir kaun bulaataa hai; aur kaun aataa hai?

sudhaa-aane ko kyaa huaa; jab jee chaahe chalee aanaa. wahan vakeel Sahab bechain ho rahe hain.

Nirmala-bahut bechain, raat ko shaayad neend n aatee ho?

sudhaa-bahin, tumhaaraa kalejaa patthar ka hai. unakee dashaa dekh kar taras aataa hai. kahate the, ghar mein koee poochhane vaalaa nahin, n koee ladakaa n baalaa, kisase jee bahalaaven? jab se doosare makaan mein uth aae hain, bahut dukhee rahate hain.

Nirmala-ladake to Bhagwan ke die do-do hain.

sudhaa-un donon ki to badee shikaayat karate the. jiyaaraam to ab baat hee nahin sunataa-turakee baturakee javaab detaa hai. rahaa chhotaa, vah bhi usee ke kahane mein hai. vechaare bade ladake ko yaad karake royaa karate hain!

Nirmala-jiyaaraam to shareer n tha, vah badamaashee kab se seekh gayaa? meree to koee baat n taalataa tha-ishaare par kaam karataa thaa.

sudhaa-kyaa jaane bahin! sunaa, kahataa hai aap hee ne bhaiyaa ko jahar dekar maar daalaa-aap hatyaare hain. kaee baar tumase shaadee karane ke lie taane de chukaa hai. aisee-aisee baaten kahataa hai ki vakeel saahav ro padate hain. are aur to kyaa kahoon, ek din patthar uthaa kar maarane daudaa tha!

Nirmala ne seeriyas fikr mein pad kar kahaa-yah ladakaa to vadaa shaitaan nikalaa. usase yah kisane kahaa ki usake bhaaee ko unhonne jahar de diyaa hai.

sudhaa-vah tumheen se theek hogaa.

Nirmala ko yah naee fikr paidaa huee! agar jiyaa ka yahee ranggg hai-apane vaap se ladane par taiyaar rahataa hai, to mujhase kyon davane lagaa? vah raat ko badee der tak isee fikr mein doobee rahee. mansaaraam ki aaj use bahut yaad aaee. usake saath jindagee aaraam se kat jaatee. is ladake ka jab apane pitaa ke saamane hee yah haal hai, to unake peechhe usake saath kaise nibaah hogaa. ghar haath se nikal hee gayaa. kuchh n kuchh karj abhee sir par hogaa hee! aamadanee ka yah haal!! Bhagwan hee bedaa paar lagaavengge! aaj pahalee baar Nirmala ko bachchi ki fikr paidaa huee! is vechaaree ka n jaane kyaa haal hogaa? Bhagwan ne yah vipatti bhi sir daal dee! mujhe to isakee zaroorat n thee. paidaa honaa hee lenaa tha, to kisee kismatavaan ke ghar paidaa honaa letee. bachchi usakee chhaatee se lipatee huee so rahee thee. maataa ne usako aur bhi chimataa liyaa, maano koee usake haath se use chheene lie jaataa hai!!

Nirmala ke paas hee sudhaa ki chaarapaaee bhi thee. Nirmala to fikr-saagar mein gotaa khaa rahee thi; aur sudhaa meethee neend ka khushi uthaa rahee thee. kyaa use apane bachcha ki fikr sataatee hai? maut to boodhe aur javaan ka bhed nahin karatee; phir sudhaa ko kyon koee fikr nahin sataatee. use to kabhee bhavishy ki fikr se udaas nahin dekhaa!

sahasaa sudhaa ki neend khul gaee. usane Nirmala ko abhee tak jaagate dekhaa, to bolee-are! abhee tum soee nahin?

Nirmala-neend hee nahin aatee!

sudhaa-aankhen band kar lo, aap hee neend aa jaayagee. main to chaarapaaee par aate hee mar see jaatee hoon. vah jaagate bhi hain, to khabar nahin hotee. n jaane mujhe kyon itanee neend aatee hai? shaayad koee beemaaree hai.

Nirmala-haan, badaa bhaaree beemaaree hai. ise raaj-beemaaree kahate hain. doktar Sahab se kaho-davaa shuroo kar den..

sudhaa-to aakhir jaag kar kyaa sochoon. kabhee-kabhee maike ki yaad aa jaatee hai, to us din jaraa der mein aankh lagatee hai.

Nirmala doktar Sahab ki chaad nahin aatee?

sudhaa-kabhee nahin, unako yaad kyon aae? jaanatee hoon ki tenis khel kar aae hongge; khana khaayaa hogaa aur aaraam se lete hongge.

Nirmala-lo, sohan bhi jaag gayaa. jab tum jaag gaee, to bhalaa vah kyon sone lagaa?

sudhaa-haan vahin, isakee ajeeb aadat hai. mere saath sotaa hai aur mere hee saath jaagataa hai. us paidaa honaa ka koee tapasvee hai. dekho, isake maathe par tilak ka kaisaa nishaan hai. baanho par bhi aise hee nishaan hain. jaroor koee tapasvee hai.

Nirmala-tapasvee log to chandan-tilak nahin lagaane. us paidaa honaa ka koee dhoort pujaaree hogaa. kyon re, too kahaan ka pujaaree tha? bataa!

sudhaa-isakaa vyaah mein bachchi se karoonggee!

Nirmala-chalo bahin, gaalee detee ho. bahin se bhi bhaaee ka vyaah hotaa hai?

sudhaa-main to karoonggee, chaahe koee kuchh kahe. aisee sundar bahoo aur kahaan paaoonggee. jaraa dekho to bahin, isakee deh kuchh garm hai ya mujh hee ko maaloom hotee hai!

Nirmala ne sohan ka maathaa chhookar kahaa-nahin, nahin, deh garm hai. yah jvar kab aa gayaa? doodh to pee rahaa hai n?

sudhaa-abhee soyaa tha, tab to deh thandee thee. shaayad sardee lag gaee, odhaa kar sulaae detee hoon. savere tak theek ho jaayagaa.

saberaa huaa to sohan ki dashaa aur bhi kharaab ho gaee. usakee naak bahane lagee aur bukhaar aur bhi tej ho gayaa. aankhen chadh gaee aur sir jhuk gayaa. n vah haath pair hilaataa tha, n hansataa-bolataa tha; bas chupachaap padaa thaa. aisaa maaloom hotaa tha ki use is vakt kisee ka bolanaa achchha nahin lagataa. kuchh-kuchh khaansee bhi aane lagee. ab to sudhaa ghabaraaee. Nirmala ki bhi Rai huee ki doktar Sahab ko bulaayaa jaay; lekin usakee boodhee maataa ne kahaa-doktar-hakeem ka yahan kuchh kaam naheen. saaph to dekh rahee hoon ki bachche ko najar lag gaee hai. bhalaa doktar aakar kyaa karegaa?

sudhaa-ammaan jee, bhalaa yahan najar kaun lagaa degaa? abhee tak to baahar kaheen gayaa bhi nahin!

maataa-najar koee lagaataa nahin betee, kisee-kisee aadmi ki deeth hee buree hotee hai-aap hee aap lag jaatee hai. kabhee-kabhee maan-baap tak ki najar lag jaatee hai. jabase kamaaeeaa hai, ek baar bhi nahin royaa. chochale bachchon ki yahee gati hotee hai. main to ise humakate dekh kar daree thi ki kuchh n kuchh anisht hone vaalaa hai. aankhen nahin dekhatee ho kitanee chadh gaee hain. yahee najar ki sabase badee pahichaan hai.

budhiyaa maharee aur pados ki panditaain ne is baat ka anumodan kar diyaa. bas, manhagoo ojhaa bulaa liyaa gayaa. manhagoo ne aakar bachche ka munh dekhaa aur hans kar bolaa-maalikin, yah deeth hai aur kuchh naheen. jaraa patalee-patalee teeliyaan to manggavaa deejie. Bhagwan ne chaahaa, to sajhaa tak bachcha hansane-khelane lagegaa.

sarakande ke paanch tukade laae gae. manhagoo ne unhen baraabar karake ek dore se baandh diyaa aur kuchh budabudaa kar usee pole haathon se paanch baar sohan ka sir suhalaayaa. ab jo dekhaa, to paanchon teeliyaan chhotee-badee ho gaee thee. sav striyaan yah kautuk dekh kar danggg rah gaee. ab najar mein kise shak ho sakataa tha? manhagoo ne phir bachche ko teeliyon se suhalaanaa shuroo kiyaa. ab ki teeliyaan baraabar ho gaee. keval thodaa saa antar rah gayaa. yah is baat ka saboot tha ki najar ka asar ab thodaa saa aur rah gayaa hai. mehagoo sabako dilaasaa dekar shaam ko phir aane ka vaayadaa karake chalaa gayaa. bachcha ki dashaa din ko aur bhi kharaab ho gaee. khaansee ka jor ho gayaa. shaam ke samay manhagoo ne aakar phir teeliyon ka tamaashaa kiyaa. is vakt paanchon teeliyaan varaavar nikaleen. striyaan nishchint ho gaee lekin sohan ko saaree raat khaansate gujaree. yahan tak ki kaee vaar usakee aankhen ulat gaee. sudhaa aur Nirmala donon ne baith kar saveraa kiyaa. khair, raat kushal se kat gaee. ab boodhee aurat maataa jee nayaa ranggg laaee. mehagoo najar n utaar sakaa, isalie ab kisee maulavee se phoongk dalavaanaa jarooree ho gayaa. sudhaa phir bhi apane shauhar ko khabar n de sakee. maharee sohan ko ek chaadar se lapet kar ek masjid mein le gaee; aur phoongk dalavaa laaee. shaam ko bhi phoongk chhodee gaee, par sohan ne sir n uthaayaa. raat aa gaee, sudhaa ne aaj man mein pakkaa faisla kiyaa ki raat kushal se beetegee, to subah shauhar ko taar doonggee.

lekin raat kushal se n beetane paaee! aadhee raat jaate-jaate bachcha haath se nikal gayaa!! sudhaa ki zindagi-sampatti dekhate-dekhate usake haathon se chhin gaee!!!

vahee jisake shaadee ka do din pahale vinod ho rahaa tha, aaj saare ghar ko rulaa rahaa hai. jisakee bholee-bhaalee soorat dekh kar maataa ki chhaatee phool uthatee thi, usee ko dekh kar aaj maataa ki chhaatee phatee jaatee hai. saaraa ghar sudhaa ko samajhaataa tha; par usake aansoo n thamate the, sab n hotaa thaa. sabase badaa dukh is baat ka tha ki shauhar ko kaun munh dikhaaoonggee. unhen khabar tak n dee!

raat hee ko taar de diyaa gayaa aur doosare din doktar sinhaa nau bajate-bajate motar par aa pahunche. sudhaa ne unake aane ki khabar paaee, to aur bhi phoot-phoot kar rone lagee. bachcha ki jal-kriyaa huee, doktar Sahab kaee baar andar aae; lekin sudhaa unake paas n gaee. unake saamane kaise jaay? unhen kaun munh dikhaae? usane apanee naadaanee se unake zindagi ka ratn chheen kar dariyaa mein daal diyaa. ab unake paas jaate usakee chhaatee ke tukade-tukade hue jaate the. bachcha ko usakee god mein dekh kar shauhar ki aankhen chamak uthatee theen. bachcha umak kar pitaa ki god mein chalaa jaataa thaa. maataa phir bulaatee, to pitaa ki chhaatee se chimat jaataa tha; aur laakh chumakaarane-dulaarane par baap ki god n chhodataa thaa. tab maan kahatee thi-badaa matalabee hai. aaj vah kise god mein lekar shauhar ke paas jaayagee! usakee soonee god dekhakar kahee vah chillaa kar ro napaden! shauhar ke sammunh jaane ki apeksh use mar jaanaa kaheen aasaan jaan padataa thaa. vah ek pal ke lie bhi Nirmala ko n chhodatee thi ki kaheen shauhar se saamanaa n ho jaay!

Nirmala ne kahaa-bahin, ab jo honaa tha vah to ho hee chukaa; ab unase kab tak bhaagatee phibeemaareeee. raat hee ko chale jaayengge, ammaan kahatee theen.

sudhaa ne sajal aangkhenon se taakate hue kahaa-kaun munh lekar unake paas jaaoon? mujhe dar lag rahaa hai ki unake saamane jaate hee mere paanv n tharraane lagen aur main gir n padoon!

Nirmala-chalo, main tumhaare saath chalatee hoon. tumhe sambhaale rahoonggee.

sudhaa-mujhe chhod kar bhaag to n aa ogee?

Nirmala-nahin-nahin, bhaagenggee naheen.

sudhaa-meraa kalejaa to abhee se umadaa aataa hai. main itanaa ghor vajripaat hone par bhi baithee hoon, mujhe yahee hairaanee ho rahaa hai! sohan ko vah bahut pyaar karate the; bahin! n jaane unake chitt ki kyaa dashaa hogee. main unhen dhaadhas kyaa doonggee, aap hee rotee rahoonggee. kyaa raat hee ko chale jaayengge?

Nirmala-haan, ammaan jee kahatee theen, chhuttee nahin lee hai. donon saheliyaan mardaane kamare ki or chalee; lekin kamare ke dvaar par pahunch kar sudhaa ne Nirmala ko vidaa kar diyaa. akelee kamare mein daakhil huee.

doktar saahav ghavaraa rahe the ki n jaane sudhaa ki kyaa dashaa ho rahee hai! bhaanti-bhaanti ki shanggkaaen man mein aa rahee theen! jaane ko taiyaar to baithe the; lekin jee n chaahataa tha! zindagi shoony saa maaloom hotaa thaa. man hee man kudh rahe the, agar Bhagwan ko itanee jaldee yah padaarth dekar chheen lenaa tha, to diyaa hee kyon tha? unhonne to kabhee santaan ke lie Bhagwan se dua n ki thee. vah aapaidaa honaa nissantaan rah sakate the; par santaan paakar usase vanychit ho jaanaa unhen asahy jaan padataa thaa. 'kyaa sachamuch insaan Bhagwan ka khilaunaa hai? yahee maanav-zindagi ka mahatv hai! vah keval bachchaaon ka. gharondaa hai, jisake banane ka n koee hetu hai, n bigadane kaa. phir bachchaaon ko bhi to apane gharondon se-apanee kaagaj ki naavon se-apane lakadee ke ghodon se mamataa hotee hai! achchhe khilaune ko vah jaan ke peechhe chhipaa kar rakhate hain. agar Bhagwan bachcha hee hai, to vichinn bachcha hai!"

lekin buddhi to Bhagwan ka yah roop maan lenaa nahin karatee. anant srishti ka kartaa udsajaa bachcha nahin ho sakataa. ham use un saare gunon se vibhooshit karate hain, jo hamaaree buddhi ki pahunch se baahar hain. khilaadeepan to un mahaan gunon mein nahin! kyaa hansate-khelate bachchaaon ka praan har lenaa koee khel hai? kyaa Bhagwan aise paishaachik khel khelataa hai.

sahasaa sudhaa dabe paanv kamare mein daakhil huee! doktar Sahab. 'uth khade hue aur usake sameep aakar bole-tum kahaan theen sudhaa? main tumhaaree raah dekh rahaa tha! sudhaa ki aangkhon mein kamaraa tairataa huaa jaan padaa. shauhar ki gardan mein haath daal kar usane unakee chhaatee par sir rakh diyaa aur rone lagee; lekin is ashru-pravaah mein use aseem dhairy aur saantvanaa ka tajurbaa ho rahaa thaa. shauhar ke vakshsthal se lipatee huee vah apane dil mein ek ajeeb sphoorti aur bal ka sanychaar hote hue paatee thi, maano pavan se tharatharaataa huaa deepak anychal ki aad mein aa gayaa ho!

doktar Sahab ne ramanee ke anu-sinychit kapolon ko donon haathon mein lekar kahaa-sudhaa, tum itanaa chhotaa dil kyon karatee ho? sohan apane zindagi mein jo kuchh karane kamaaeeaa tha, vah kar chukaa tha; phir vah kyon baithaa rahataa. jaise koee vriksh jal aur prakaash se badhataa hai. lekin pavan ke praval jhokon hee se sudridh hotaa hai, usee bhoti pranay bhi dukh ke aaghaaton hee se vikaas paataa hai. khushi mein saath hansane vaale bahutere mil jaate hain. raj mein jo saath roye, vahee hamaaraa sachchaa mitr hai. jin pyaariyon ko saath ronaa nahin naseev huaa, ve muhabbat ke maje kyaa jaane? sohan ki maut ne aaj hamaare dvait ko bilakul mitaa diyaa. aaj hee hamane ek doosare ka sachchaa svaroop dekhaa hai!

sudhaa ne sisakate hue kahaa-main najar ke dhokhe mein thee. haay! tum usakaa munh bhi nahin dekhane paae!! n jaane in dinon use itanee samajh kahaan se aa gaee thee. jab mujhe rote dekhataa, to apane takaleeph bhool kar muskaraa detaa. teesare hee din mere laadale ki aankhen band ho gaee. kuchh davaa-darpan bhi n karane paaee. yah kahate-kahate sudhaa ke aansoo phir umad aae. doktar sinhaa ne use seene se lagaa kar taras se kaampatee huee aavaaj mein kahaa-priye, aaj tak koee aisaa bachcha ya boodhaa n maraa hogaa, jisake ghar vaalon ko usake davaa-darpan ki laalasaa pooree ho gaee ho!

sudhaa-Nirmala ne meree badee madad ki! main to ekaadh jhapakee le bhi letee thi; par unakee aankhen nahin jhapakee. raat-raat lie baithee ya tahalaatee rahatee thee. usake ehasaan kabhee n bhooloonggee. kyaa tum aaj hee jaa rahe ho?

doktar-haan, chhuttee lene ka maukaa n thaa. sivil-sarjan shikaar khelane gayaa huaa thaa.

sudhaa-yah sab hameshaa shikaar hee khelaa karate hain?

doktar-raajaaon ka aur kaam hee kyaa hai?

sudhaa-main to aaj n jaane doonggee.

doktar-jee to meraa bhi nahin chaahataa.

sudhaa--to mat jaao; taar de do! main bhi tumhaare saath chaloonggee. Nirmala ko bhi letee chaloonggee..

sudhaa wahan se lautee, to usake dil ka bojh halakaa ho gayaa tha! shauhar ki pyaarapoorn komal vaanee ne usake saare shok aur santaap ko haran kar liyaa thaa. pyaar mein aseem bharosaa hai, aseem dhairy hai. aur aseem bal hai!!
