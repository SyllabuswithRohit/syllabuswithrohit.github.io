# Nirmala — Adhyay 17

**Munshi Premchand**

satrahavaan parichchheda

krishnaa ke vivaaha ke baada sudhaa chalee gaee; lekin nirmalaa maike hee men raha gaee| vakeela saahaba baara-baara likhate the; par vaha na jati thi| vahaan jaane ko usakaa jee hee na chaahata tha| vahaan koee aisee cheeza na thi, jo use kheencha le jaaya| yahaan maataa kee sevaa aur chhote bhaaiyon kee dekha-bhaala men usakaa samay ba.de aananda se kata jata tha| vakeela saahaba Kuda aate, to shaayada vaha jaane par raazee ho jati; lekin is vivaaha men muhalle kee kaee striyon ne unakee vaha durgati kee thi ki bechaare aane kaa naama hee na lete the| sudhaa ne bhee kaee baara patra likha; par nirmalaa ne usase bhee heele-havaale kie| aaKira ek din sudhaa ne naukara ko saatha liya; aur svayan aa dhamakee!

jab donon gale mila chukeen, to sudhaa ne kaha—tumhen to vahaan jaate maano dara lagata hai| nirmalaa-haan bahina, dara to lagata hai| byaaha kee gaee teena saala men aaee; ab kee to vahaan umra hee khatama ho jaayagee; phir kaun bulaataa hai;aur kaun aata hai?

sudhaa-aane ko kyaa hua; jab jee chaahe chalee aana| vahaan vakeela saahaba bechaina ho rahe hain|

nirmalaa-bahut bechaina, raat ko shaayada neenda na aati ho?

sudhaa-bahina, tumhaaraa kalejaa patthara kaa hai| unakee dashaa dekha kar tarasa aata hai| kehte the, ghar men koee poochhane vaalaa naheen, na koee ladka na baala, kisase jee bahalaaven? jab se doosare makaana men utha aae hain, bahut dukhee rahate hain|

nirmalaa-ladke to eeshvara ke die do-do hain|

sudhaa-una donon kee to ba.dee shikaayata karte the| jiyaaraama to ab baat hee naheen sunata-turakee baturakee javaaba detaa hai| raha chhota, vaha bhee usee ke kahane men hai| vechaare ba.de ladke ko yaada karake royaa karte hain!

nirmalaa-jiyaaraama to shareera na tha, vaha badamaashee kab se seekha gaya? meree to koee baat na taalata tha-ishaare par kaama karata tha|

sudhaa-kyaa jaane bahina! suna, kahata hai aapa hee ne bhaiyaa ko jahara dekara maara daalaa-aapa hatyaare hain| kaee baara tumase vivaaha karane ke lie taane de chukaa hai| aisee-aisee baaten kahata hai ki vakeela saahava ro pa.date hain| are aur to kyaa kahoon, ek din patthara uthaa kar maarane dau.daa tha!

nirmalaa ne gambheera chintaa men pa.da kar kaha-yaha ladka to va.daa shaitaana nikalaa| usase yaha kisane kaha ki usake bhaaee ko unhonne jahara de diya hai|

sudhaa-vaha tumheen se theeka hogaa|

nirmalaa ko yaha naee chintaa paidaa hui! agar jiya kaa yahee ranga hai-apane vaapa se la.dane par taiyaara rahata hai,to mujhase kyon davane laga? vaha raat ko ba.dee dera taka isee phikra men doobee rahi| mansaaraama kee aaja use bahut yaada aaee| usake saatha zindagi aaraama se kata jati| is ladke kaa jab apane pitaa ke saamane hee yaha haala hai,to unake peechhe usake saatha kaise nibaaha hogaa| ghar haath se nikala hee gaya| kuchha na kuchha qarza abhee sira par hogaa hee! aamadanee kaa yaha haala!! eeshvara hee be.daa paara lagaavenge! aaja pahalee baara nirmalaa ko bachchi kee phikra paidaa hui! is vechaaree kaa na jaane kyaa haala hoga? eeshvara ne yaha vipatti bhee sira daala dee! mujhe to isakee zaroorat na thi| janma hee lena tha,to kisee bhaagyavaan ke ghar janma letee| bachchi usakee chhaatee se lipatee hui so rahi thi| maataa ne usako aur bhee chimata liya, maano koee usake haath se use chheene lie jata hai!!

nirmalaa ke paasa hee sudhaa kee chaarapaaee bhee thi| nirmalaa to chintaa-saagara men gotaa khaa rahi thi;aur sudhaa meethee neenda kaa aananda uthaa rahi thi| kyaa use apane baalaka kee phikra sataatee hai? mrityu to boo.dhe aur javaana kaa bheda naheen karti;phir sudhaa ko kyon koee chintaa naheen sataatee| use to kabhee bhavishya kee chintaa se udaasa naheen dekha!

sahasaa sudhaa kee neenda khula gaee| usane nirmalaa ko abhee taka jaagate dekha,to boli-are! abhee tuma soee naheen?

nirmalaa-neenda hee naheen aati!

sudhaa-aankhen banda kar lo, aapa hee neenda aa jaayagee| main to chaarapaaee par aate hee mara see jati hoon| vaha jaagate bhee hain, to khabara naheen hoti| na jaane mujhe kyon itanee neenda aati hai? shaayada koee roga hai|

nirmalaa-haan, bada bhaaree roga hai| ise raaja-roga kehte hain| daoktara saahaba se kaho-davaa shuroo kar den| .

sudhaa-to aakhira jaaga kar kyaa sochoon| kabhee-kabhee maike kee yaada aa jati hai,to us din jaraa dera men aankh lagatee hai|

nirmalaa daoktara saahaba kee chaada naheen aati?

sudhaa-kabhee naheen, unako yaada kyon aae? jaanatee hoon ki tenisa khela kar aae honge; khaanaa khaayaa hogaa aur aaraama se lete honge|

nirmalaa-lo,sohana bhee jaaga gaya| jab tuma jaaga gaee,to bhalaa vaha kyon sone laga?

sudhaa-haan vahina,isakee ajeeba aadata hai| mere saatha sotaa hai aur mere hee saatha jaagata hai| us janma kaa koee tapasvee hai| dekho,isake maathe par tilaka kaa kaisaa nishaana hai| baanho par bhee aise hee nishaana hain| jaroora koee tapasvee hai|

nirmalaa-tapasvee log to chandana-tilaka naheen lagaane| us janma kaa koee dhoorta pujaaree hogaa| kyon re,too kahaan kaa pujaaree tha? bata!

sudhaa-isakaa vyaaha men bachchi se karoongee!

nirmalaa-chalo bahina,gaalee detee ho| bahina se bhee bhaaee kaa vyaaha hota hai?

sudhaa-main to karoongee,chaahe koee kuchha kahe| aisee sundara bahoo aur kahaan paaoongee| jaraa dekho to bahina,isakee deha kuchha garma hai yaa mujha hee ko maalooma hoti hai!

nirmalaa ne sohana kaa maathaa chhookara kaha-naheen,naheen,deha garma hai| yaha jvara kab aa gaya? doodha to pee raha hai na?

sudhaa-abhee soyaa tha,tab to deha thandee thi| shaayada sardee laga gaee, o.dhaa kar sulaae detee hoon| savere taka theeka ho jaayagaa|

saberaa hua to sohana kee dashaa aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee| usakee naaka bahane lagee aur bukhaara aur bhee teja ho gaya| aankhen cha.dha gaee aur sira jhuka gaya| na vaha haath pair hilaataa tha, na hansata-bolata tha;basa chupachaapa pada tha| aisaa maalooma hota tha ki use is vakta kisee kaa bolana achchha naheen lagata| kuchha-kuchha khaansee bhee aane lagee| ab to sudhaa ghabaraaee| nirmalaa kee bhee raaya hui ki daoktara saahaba ko bulaayaa jaaya;lekin usakee boo.dhee maataa ne kaha-daoktara-hakeema kaa yahaan kuchha kaama naheen| saapha to dekha rahi hoon ki bachche ko najara laga gaee hai| bhalaa daoktara aakara kyaa karega?

sudhaa-ammaan jee,bhalaa yahaan nazara kaun lagaa dega? abhee taka to baahara kaheen gaya bhee naheen!

maataa-nazara koee lagaataa naheen betee,kisee-kisee aadmi kee deetha hee buree hoti hai-aapa hee aapa laga jati hai| kabhee-kabhee maan-baapa taka kee najara laga jati hai| jabase aaya hai,ek baara bhee naheen royaa| chochale bachchon kee yahee gati hoti hai| main to ise humakate dekha kar daree thi ki kuchha na kuchha anishta hone vaalaa hai| aankhen naheen dekhatee ho kitanee cha.dha gaee hain| yahee najara kee sabase ba.dee pahichaana hai|

bu.dhiya maharee aur pa.dosa kee panditaaina ne is kathana kaa anumodana kar diya| basa,manhagoo ojhaa bulaa liya gaya| manhagoo ne aakara bachche kaa munha dekha aur hansa kar bola-maalikina,yaha deetha hai aur kuchha naheen| jaraa patalee-patalee teeliyaan to mangavaa deejie| bhagavaan ne chaaha,to sajhaa taka bachcha hansane-khelane lagegaa|

sarakande ke paancha tuka.de laae gae| manhagoo ne unhen baraabara karake ek dore se baandha diya aur kuchha budabudaa kar usee pole haathon se paancha baara sohana kaa sira suhalaayaa| ab jo dekha,to paanchon teeliyaan chhotee-ba.dee ho gaee thi| sava striyaan yaha kautuka dekha kar danga raha gaee| ab najara men kise sandeha ho sakata tha? manhagoo ne phir bachche ko teeliyon se suhalaanaa shuroo kiya| ab kee teeliyaan baraabara ho gaee| kevala tho.daa saa antara raha gaya| yaha is baat kaa pramaana tha ki nazara kaa asara ab tho.daa saa aur raha gaya hai| ma.chagoo sabako dilaasaa dekara shaama ko phir aane kaa vaayadaa karake chalaa gaya| baalaka kee dashaa din ko aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee| khaansee kaa jora ho gaya| shaama ke samay manhagoo ne aakara phir teeliyon kaa tamaashaa kiya| is vakta paanchon teeliyaan varaavara nikaleen| striyaan nishchinta ho gaee lekin sohana ko saaree raat khaansate gujaree| yahaan taka ki kaee vaara usakee aankhen ulata gaee| sudhaa aur nirmalaa donon ne baitha kar saveraa kiya| khaira,raat kushala se kata gaee| ab vriddhaa maataa jee nayaa ranga laaee| ma.chagoo nazara na utaara saka,isalie ab kisee maulavee se phoonka dalavaanaa jarooree ho gaya| sudhaa phir bhee apane pati ko soochana na de sakee| maharee sohana ko ek chaadara se lapeta kar ek masjida men le gaee; aur phoonka dalavaa laaee| shaama ko bhee phoonka chho.dee gaee,par sohana ne sira na uthaayaa| raat aa gaee, sudhaa ne aaja mann men nishchaya kiya ki raat kushala se beetegee,to praatahkaala pati ko taara doongee|

lekin raat kushala se na beetane paaee! aadhee raat jaate-jaate bachcha haath se nikala gaya!! sudhaa kee jeevana-sampatti dekhate-dekhate usake haathon se chhina gaee!!!

vahee jisake vivaaha kaa do din pahale vinoda ho raha tha, aaja saare ghar ko rulaa raha hai| jisakee bholee-bhaalee soorata dekha kar maataa kee chhaatee phoola uthatee thi,usee ko dekha kar aaja maataa kee chhaatee phatee jati hai| saaraa ghar sudhaa ko samajhaataa tha;par usake aansoo na thamate the, sab na hota tha| sabase bada dukha is baat kaa tha ki pati ko kaun munha dikhaaoongee| unhen khabara taka na dee!

raat hee ko taara de diya gaya aur doosare din daoktara sinhaa nau bajate-bajate motara par aa pahunche| sudhaa ne unake aane kee khabara paaee, to aur bhee phoota-phoota kar rone lagee| baalaka kee jala-kriya hui, daoktara saahaba kaee baara andara aae; kintu sudhaa unake paasa na gaee| unake saamane kaise jaaya? unhen kaun munha dikhaae? usane apanee naadaanee se unake jeevana kaa ratna chheena kar dariya men daala diya| ab unake paasa jaate usakee chhaatee ke tuka.de-tuka.de hue jaate the| baalaka ko usakee goda men dekha kar pati kee aankhen chamaka uthatee theen| baalaka umaka kar pitaa kee goda men chalaa jata tha| maataa phir bulaatee, to pitaa kee chhaatee se chimata jata tha; aur laakha chumakaarane-dulaarane par baapa kee goda na chho.data tha| tab maan kehti thi-bada matalabee hai| aaja vaha kise goda men lekara pati ke paasa jaayagee! usakee soonee goda dekhakara kahee vaha chillaa kar ro napa.den! pati ke sammukha jaane kee apekshaa use mara jana kaheen aasaana jaana pa.data tha| vaha ek kshana ke lie bhee nirmalaa ko na chho.datee thi ki kaheen pati se saamana na ho jaaya!

nirmalaa ne kaha-bahina, ab jo honaa tha vaha to ho hee chuka; ab unase kab taka bhaagatee phirogee| raat hee ko chale jaayenge, ammaan kehti theen|

sudhaa ne sajala netron se taakate hue kaha-kaun munha lekara unake paasa jaaoon? mujhe dara laga raha hai ki unake saamane jaate hee mere paanva na tharraane lagen aur main gira na pa.doon!

nirmalaa-chalo, main tumhaare saatha chalatee hoon| tumhen sanbhaale rahoongee|

sudhaa-mujhe chho.da kar bhaaga to na aa ogee?

nirmalaa-naheen-naheen, bhaagengee naheen|

sudhaa-meraa kalejaa to abhee se uma.daa aata hai| main itana ghora vajripaata hone par bhee baithee hoon, mujhe yahee aashcharya ho raha hai! sohana ko vaha bahut pyaar karte the; bahina! na jaane unake chitta kee kyaa dashaa hogee| main unhen dha.dhasa kyaa doongee, aapa hee rotee rahoongee| kyaa raat hee ko chale jaayenge?

nirmalaa-haan, ammaan jee kehti theen, chhuttee naheen lee hai| donon saheliyaan mardaane kamare kee ora chalee; lekin kamare ke dvaara par pahuncha kar sudhaa ne nirmalaa ko vidaa kar diya| akelee kamare men daakhila hui|

daoktara saahava ghavaraa rahe the ki na jaane sudhaa kee kyaa dashaa ho rahi hai! bhaanti-bhaanti kee shankaaen mann men aa rahi theen! jaane ko taiyaara to baithe the; lekin jee na chaahata tha! jeevana shoonya saa maalooma hota tha| mann hee mann ku.dha rahe the, agar eeshvara ko itanee jaldee yaha padaartha dekara chheena lena tha, to diya hee kyon tha? unhonne to kabhee santaana ke lie eeshvara se praarthana na kee thi| vaha aajanma nissantaana raha sakate the; par santaana paakara usase vanchita ho jana unhen asahya jaana pa.data tha| 'kyaa sachamucha manushya eeshvara kaa khilaunaa hai? yahee maanava-jeevana kaa mahatva hai! vaha kevala baalakon ka. gharondaa hai, jisake banane kaa na koee hetu hai, na biga.dane kaa| phir baalakon ko bhee to apane gharondon se-apanee kaagaza kee naavon se-apane laka.dee ke gho.don se mamata hoti hai! achchhe khilaune ko vaha jaana ke peechhe chhipaa kar rakhate hain| agar eeshvara baalaka hee hai, to vichinna baalaka hai!"

kintu buddhi to eeshvara kaa yaha roopa sveekaara naheen karti| ananta srishti kaa kartaa uddanda baalaka naheen ho sakata| hama use una saare gunon se vibhooshita karte hain, jo hamaaree buddhi kee pahuncha se baahara hain | khila.deepana to una mahaana gunon men naheen! kyaa hansate-khelate baalakon kaa praana hara lena koee khela hai? kyaa eeshvara aise paishaachika khela khelata hai|

sahasaa sudhaa dabe paanva kamare men daakhila hui! daoktara saahaba . 'utha kha.de hue aur usake sameepa aakara bole-tuma kahaan theen sudha? main tumhaaree raaha dekha raha tha! sudhaa kee aankhon men kamaraa tairata hua jaana pada| pati kee gardana men haath daala kar usane unakee chhaatee par sira rakha diya aur rone lagee; lekin is ashru-pravaaha men use aseema dhairya aur saantvana kaa anubhava ho raha tha| pati ke vakshasthala se lipatee hui vaha apane hridaya men ek vichitra sphoorti aur bala kaa sanchaara hote hue paatee thi, maano pavana se tharatharaataa hua deepaka anchala kee a.da men aa gaya ho!

daoktara saahaba ne ramanee ke anu-sinchita kapolon ko donon haathon men lekara kaha-sudha, tuma itana chhota dil kyon karti ho? sohana apane jeevana men jo kuchha karane aaya tha, vaha kar chukaa tha; phir vaha kyon baithaa rahata| jaise koee vriksha jala aur prakaasha se ba.dhata hai| lekin pavana ke pravala jhokon hee se sudri.dha hota hai, usee bhaoti pranaya bhee dukha ke aaghaaton hee se vikaasa paataa hai| khushi men saatha hansane vaale bahutere mila jaate hain| raja men jo saatha roye, vahee hamaaraa sachchaa mitra hai| jina premiyon ko saatha ronaa naheen naseeva hua, ve muhabbata ke maje kyaa jaane? sohana kee mrityu ne aaja hamaare dvaita ko bilakula mitaa diya|aaja hee hamane ek doosare kaa sachchaa svaroopa dekha hai!

sudhaa ne sisakate hue kaha-main nazara ke dhokhe men thi| haaya! tuma usakaa munha bhee naheen dekhane paae!! na jaane ina dinon use itanee samajha kahaan se aa gaee thi| jab mujhe rote dekhata, to apane kashta bhoola kar muskaraa detaa| teesare hee din mere laadale kee aankhen banda ho gaee| kuchha davaa-darpana bhee na karane paaee| yaha kehte-kehte sudhaa ke aansoo phir uma.da aae| daoktara sinhaa ne use seene se lagaa kar karunaa se kaanpatee hui aavaaja men kaha-priye, aaja taka koee aisaa baalaka yaa vriddha na maraa hoga, jisake ghar vaalon ko usake davaa-darpana kee laalasaa pooree ho gaee ho!

sudhaa-nirmalaa ne meree ba.dee madada kee! main to ekaadha jhapakee le bhee letee thi; par unakee aankhen naheen jhapakee| raat-raat lie baithee yaa tahalaatee rahatee thi| usake ehasaana kabhee na bhooloongee| kyaa tuma aaja hee jaa rahe ho?

daoktara-haan, chhuttee lene kaa maukaa na tha| sivila-sarjana shikaara khelane gaya hua tha|

sudhaa-yaha sab hameshaa shikaara hee khelaa karte hain?

daoktara-raajaaon kaa aur kaama hee kyaa hai?

sudhaa-main to aaja na jaane doongee|

daoktara-jee to meraa bhee naheen chaahata|

sudhaa--to mata jaao; taara de do! main bhee tumhaare saatha chaloongee| nirmalaa ko bhee letee chaloongee||

sudhaa vahaan se lautee, to usake hridaya kaa bojha halakaa ho gaya tha! pati kee premapoorna komala vaanee ne usake saare shoka aur santaapa ko harana kar liya tha| prema men aseema vishvaasa hai, aseema dhairya hai| aur aseema bala hai!!
