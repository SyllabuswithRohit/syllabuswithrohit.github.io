# Chapter 17

satrahavaa.N parichchheda

krishNaa ke vivaaha ke baada sudhaa chalee gaee; lekin nirmalaa maike hee men raha gaee| vakeela saahaba baara-baara likhate the; para vaha na jati thi| vahaa.N jaane ko usakaa jee hee na chaahataa tha| vahaa.N koee aisee cheeja na thi, jo use kheencha le jaaya| yahaa.N maa.N kee sevaa aur chhote bhaaiyon kee dekha-bhaala men usakaa samaya bade khushee se kata jata tha| vakeela saahaba khuda aate, to shaayada vaha jaane para raajee ho jati; lekin is vivaaha men muhalle kee kaee striyon ne unakee vaha durgati kee thi ki bechaare aane kaa naama hee na lete the| sudhaa ne bhee kaee baara patra likhaa; para nirmalaa ne usase bhee heele-havaale kie| aakhira ek din sudhaa ne naukara ko saatha liya; aur svayan aa dhamakee!

jaba donon gale mila chukeen, to sudhaa ne kahaa—tumhen to vahaa.N jaate maano dara lagataa hai|
nirmalaa-haa.N bahina, dara to lagataa hai| byaaha kee gaee teena saala men aaee; aba kee to vahaa.N umra hee khatama ho jaayagee; phir kauna bulaataa hai;aur kauna aata hai?

sudhaa-aane ko kyaa hua; jaba jee chaahe chalee aana| vahaa.N vakeela saahaba bechaina ho rahe hain|

nirmalaa-bahuta bechaina, raat ko shaayada neenda na aatee ho?

sudhaa-bahina, tumhaaraa kalejaa patthara kaa hai| unakee dashaa dekha kar tarasa aata hai| kehte the, ghar men koee poochhane vaalaa naheen, na koee ladakaa na baalaa, kisase jee bahalaaven? jaba se doosare makaana men utha aae hain, bahuta dukhee rahate hain|

nirmalaa-ladake to eeshvara ke die do-do hain|

sudhaa-una donon kee to badee shikaayata karte the| jiyaaraama to aba baat hee naheen sunataa-turakee baturakee javaaba detaa hai| raha chhotaa, vaha bhee usee ke kahane men hai| vechaare bade ladake ko yaada karake royaa karte hain!

nirmalaa-jiyaaraama to shareera na tha, vaha badamaashee kaba se seekha gaya? meree to koee baat na taalataa tha-ishaare para kaama karta tha|

sudhaa-kyaa jaane bahina! sunaa, kehta hai aapa hee ne bhaiyaa ko jahara dekara maara daalaa-aapa hatyaare hain| kaee baara tumase vivaaha karane ke lie taane de chukaa hai| aisee-aisee baaten kehta hai ki
vakeela saahava ro padate hain| are aur to kyaa kahoo.N, ek din patthara uthaa kar maarane daudaa tha!

nirmalaa ne gambheera chintaa men pada kar kahaa-yaha ladakaa to vadaa shaitaana nikalaa| usase yaha kisane kahaa ki usake bhaaee ko unhonne jahara de diya hai|

sudhaa-vaha tumheen se theeka hogaa|

nirmalaa ko yaha naee chintaa paidaa huee! agar jiyaa kaa yahee ranga hai-apane vaapa se ladane para taiyaara rahataa hai,to mujhase kyon davane lagaa? vaha raat ko badee dera taka isee phikra men doobee rahi| mansaaraama kee aaja use bahuta yaada aaee| usake saatha jindagee aaraama se kata jati| is ladake kaa jaba apane pitaa ke saamane hee yaha haala hai,to unake peechhe usake saatha kaise nibaaha hogaa| ghar haath se nikala hee gaya| kuchha na kuchha karja abhee sira para hogaa hee! aamadanee kaa yaha haala!! eeshvara hee bedaa paara lagaavenge! aaja pahalee baara nirmalaa ko bachchee kee phikra paidaa huee! is vechaaree kaa na jaane kyaa haala hogaa? eeshvara ne yaha vipatti bhee sira daala dee! mujhe to isakee jaroorata na thi| janma hee lenaa tha,to kisee bhaagyavaan ke ghar janma letee| bachchee usakee chhaatee se lipatee huee so rahi thi| maa.N ne usako aur bhee chimataa liya, maano koee usake haath se use chheene lie jata hai!!

nirmalaa ke paasa hee sudhaa kee chaarapaaee bhee thi| nirmalaa to chintaa-saagara men gotaa khaa rahi thi;aur sudhaa meethee neenda kaa
khushee uthaa rahi thi| kyaa use apane ladakaa kee phikra sataatee hai? mrityu to boodhe aur javaana kaa bheda naheen karti;phir sudhaa ko kyon koee chintaa naheen sataatee| use to kabhee bhavishya kee chintaa se udaasa naheen dekha!

sahasaa sudhaa kee neenda khula gaee| usane nirmalaa ko abhee taka jaagate dekha,to bolee-are! abhee tuma soee naheen?

nirmalaa-neenda hee naheen aatee!

sudhaa-aa.Nkhen banda kar lo, aapa hee neenda aa jaayagee| main to chaarapaaee para aate hee mara see jati hoo.N| vaha jaagate bhee hain, to khabara naheen hoti| na jaane mujhe kyon itanee neenda aatee hai? shaayada koee roga hai|

nirmalaa-haa.N, badaa bhaaree roga hai| ise raaja-roga kehte hain| doktara saahaba se kaho-davaa shuroo kar den| .

sudhaa-to aakhira jaaga kar kyaa sochoo.N| kabhee-kabhee maike kee yaada aa jati hai,to us din jaraa dera men aa.Nkha lagatee hai|

nirmalaa doktara saahaba kee chaada naheen aatee?

sudhaa-kabhee naheen, unako yaada kyon aae? jaanatee hoo.N ki tenisa khela kar aae honge; khaanaa khaayaa hogaa aur aaraama se lete honge|

nirmalaa-lo,sohana bhee jaaga gaya| jaba tuma jaaga gaee,to bhalaa vaha kyon sone lagaa?

sudhaa-haa.N vahina,isakee ajeeba aadata hai| mere saatha sotaa
hai aur mere hee saatha jaagataa hai| us janma kaa koee tapasvee hai| dekho,isake maathe para tilaka kaa kaisaa nishaana hai| baa.Nho para bhee aise hee nishaana hain| jaroora koee tapasvee hai|

nirmalaa-tapasvee log to chandana-tilaka naheen lagaane| us janma kaa koee dhoorta pujaaree hogaa| kyon re,too kahaa.N kaa pujaaree tha? bataa!

sudhaa-isakaa vyaaha men bachchee se karoo.Ngee!

nirmalaa-chalo bahina,gaalee detee ho| bahina se bhee bhaaee kaa vyaaha hota hai?

sudhaa-main to karoo.Ngee,chaahe koee kuchha kahe| aisee sundara bahoo aur kahaa.N paaoo.Ngee| jaraa dekho to bahina,isakee deha kuchha garma hai yaa mujha hee ko maalooma hoti hai!

nirmalaa ne sohana kaa maathaa chhookara kahaa-naheen,naheen,deha garma hai| yaha jvara kaba aa gaya? doodha to pee raha hai na?

sudhaa-abhee soyaa tha,taba to deha thaNdee thi| shaayada sardee laga gaee, odhaa kar sulaae detee hoo.N| savere taka theeka ho jaayagaa|

saberaa hua to sohana kee dashaa aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee| usakee naaka bahane lagee aur bukhaara aur bhee teja ho gaya| aa.Nkhen chadha gaee aur sira jhuka gaya| na vaha haath pair hilaataa tha, na ha.Nsataa-bolataa tha;basa chupachaapa padaa tha| aisaa maalooma hota tha ki use is vakta kisee kaa bolanaa achchhaa naheen lagataa|
kuchha-kuchha khaa.Nsee bhee aane lagee| aba to sudhaa ghabaraaee| nirmalaa kee bhee raaya huee ki doktara saahaba ko bulaayaa jaaya;lekin usakee boodhee maa.N ne kahaa-doktara-hakeema kaa yahaa.N kuchha kaama naheen| saapha to dekha rahi hoo.N ki bachche ko najara laga gaee hai| bhalaa doktara aakara kyaa karegaa?

sudhaa-ammaa.N jee,bhalaa yahaa.N najara kauna lagaa degaa? abhee taka to baahara kaheen gaya bhee naheen!

maa.N-najara koee lagaataa naheen betee,kisee-kisee aadamee kee deetha hee buree hoti hai-aapa hee aapa laga jati hai| kabhee-kabhee maa.N-baapa taka kee najara laga jati hai| jabase aaya hai,ek baara bhee naheen royaa| chochale bachchon kee yahee gati hoti hai| main to ise humakate dekha kar daree thi ki kuchha na kuchha anishta hone vaalaa hai| aa.Nkhen naheen dekhatee ho kitanee chadha gaee hain| yahee najara kee sabase badee pahichaana hai|

budhiyaa maharee aur padosa kee paNditaaina ne is kathana kaa anumodana kar diya| basa,ma.Nhagoo ojhaa bulaa liya gaya| ma.Nhagoo ne aakara bachche kaa munha dekha aur ha.Nsa kar bola-maalikina,yaha deetha hai aur kuchha naheen| jaraa patalee-patalee teeliyaa.N to ma.Ngavaa deejie| bhagavaan ne chaahaa,to sajhaa taka bachchaa ha.Nsane-khelane lagegaa|

sarakaNde ke paa.Ncha tukade laae gae| ma.Nhagoo ne unhen baraabara karake ek dore se baa.Ndha diya aur kuchha budabudaa kar usee pole
haathon se paa.Ncha baara sohana kaa sira suhalaayaa| aba jo dekha,to paa.Nchon teeliyaa.N chhotee-badee ho gaee thi| sava striyaa.N yaha kautuka dekha kar danga raha gaee| aba najara men kise shaka ho sakataa tha? ma.Nhagoo ne phir bachche ko teeliyon se suhalaanaa shuroo kiya| aba kee teeliyaa.N baraabara ho gaee| kevala thodaa saa antara raha gaya| yaha is baat kaa pramaaNa tha ki najara kaa asara aba thodaa saa aur raha gaya hai| mehagoo sabako dilaasaa dekara shaama ko phir aane kaa vaayadaa karake chalaa gaya| ladakaa kee dashaa din ko aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee| khaa.Nsee kaa jora ho gaya| shaama ke samaya ma.Nhagoo ne aakara phir teeliyon kaa tamaashaa kiya| is vakta paa.Nchon teeliyaa.N varaavara nikaleen| striyaa.N nishchinta ho gaee lekin sohana ko saaree raat khaa.Nsate gujaree| yahaa.N taka ki kaee vaara usakee aa.Nkhen ulata gaee| sudhaa aur nirmalaa donon ne baitha kar saveraa kiya| khaira,raat kushala se kata gaee| aba vriddhaa maa.N jee nayaa ranga laaee| mehagoo najara na utaara sakaa,isalie aba kisee maulavee se phoonka dalavaanaa jarooree ho gaya| sudhaa phir bhee apane pati ko khabara na de sakee| maharee sohana ko ek chaadara se lapeta kar ek masjida men le gaee; aur phoonka dalavaa laaee| shaama ko bhee phoonka chhodee gaee,para sohana ne sira na uthaayaa| raat aa gaee, sudhaa ne aaja mana men phaisalaa kiya ki raat kushala se beetegee,to subaha pati ko taara doongee|

lekin raat kushala se na beetane paaee! aadhee raat jaate-jaate
bachchaa haath se nikala gaya!! sudhaa kee jeevana-sampatti dekhate-dekhate usake haathon se chhina gaee!!!

vahee jisake vivaaha kaa do din pahale vinoda ho raha tha, aaja saare ghar ko rulaa raha hai| jisakee bholee-bhaalee soorata dekha kar maa.N kee chhaatee phoola uthatee thi,usee ko dekha kar aaja maa.N kee chhaatee phatee jati hai| saaraa ghar sudhaa ko samajhaataa tha;para usake aa.Nsoo na thamate the, sab na hota tha| sabase badaa dukha is baat kaa tha ki pati ko kauna mu.Nha dikhaaoo.Ngee| unhen khabara taka na dee!

raat hee ko taara de diya gaya aur doosare din doktara sinhaa nau bajate-bajate motara para aa pahu.Nche| sudhaa ne unake aane kee khabara paaee, to aur bhee phoota-phoota kar rone lagee| ladakaa kee jala-kriyaa huee, doktara saahaba kaee baara andara aae; lekin sudhaa unake paasa na gaee| unake saamane kaise jaaya? unhen kauna mu.Nha dikhaae? usane apanee naadaanee se unake jeevana kaa ratna chheena kar dariyaa men daala diya| aba unake paasa jaate usakee chhaatee ke tukade-tukade hue jaate the| ladakaa ko usakee goda men dekha kar pati kee aa.Nkhen chamaka uthatee theen| ladakaa umaka kar pitaa kee goda men chalaa jata tha| maa.N phir bulaatee, to pitaa kee chhaatee se chimata jata tha; aur laakha chumakaarane-dulaarane para baapa kee goda na chhodataa tha| taba maa.N kehti thi-badaa matalabee hai| aaja vaha kise goda men lekara pati ke paasa jaayagee! usakee soonee goda
dekhakara kahee vaha chillaa kar ro napaden! pati ke sammukha jaane kee apekshaa use mara jana kaheen aasaana jaana padataa tha| vaha ek pala ke lie bhee nirmalaa ko na chhodatee thi ki kaheen pati se saamanaa na ho jaaya!

nirmalaa ne kahaa-bahina, aba jo honaa tha vaha to ho hee chukaa; aba unase kaba taka bhaagatee phirogee| raat hee ko chale jaayenge, ammaan kehti theen|

sudhaa ne sajala netron se taakate hue kahaa-kauna mu.Nha lekara unake paasa jaaoo.N? mujhe dara laga raha hai ki unake saamane jaate hee mere paa.Nva na tharraane lagen aur main gira na padoon!

nirmalaa-chalo, main tumhaare saatha chalatee hoo.N| tumhen sa.Nbhaale rahoo.Ngee|

sudhaa-mujhe chhoda kar bhaaga to na aa ogee?

nirmalaa-naheen-naheen, bhaagengee naheen|

sudhaa-meraa kalejaa to abhee se umadaa aata hai| main itanaa ghora vajripaata hone para bhee baithee hoo.N, mujhe yahee hairaanee ho raha hai! sohana ko vaha bahuta pyaara karte the; bahina! na jaane unake chitta kee kyaa dashaa hogee| main unhen dhaadhasa kyaa doongee, aapa hee rotee rahoo.Ngee| kyaa raat hee ko chale jaayenge?

nirmalaa-haa.N, ammaa.N jee kehti theen, chhuttee naheen lee hai| donon saheliyaa.N mardaane kamare kee ora chalee; lekin kamare ke dvaara para pahu.Ncha kar sudhaa ne nirmalaa ko vidaa kar diya| akelee kamare men daakhila huee|

doktara saahava ghavaraa rahe the ki na jaane sudhaa kee kyaa dashaa
ho rahi hai! taraha-taraha kee shankaae.N mana men aa rahi theen! jaane ko taiyaara to baithe the; lekin jee na chaahataa tha! jeevana shoonya saa maalooma hota tha| mana hee mana kudha rahe the, agar eeshvara ko itanee jaldee yaha padaartha dekara chheena lenaa tha, to diya hee kyon tha? unhonne to kabhee bachchaa ke lie eeshvara se praarthanaa na kee thi| vaha aajanma nissantaana raha sakate the; para bachchaa paakara usase vanchita ho jana unhen asahya jaana padataa tha| 'kyaa sachamucha manushya eeshvara kaa khilaunaa hai? yahee maanava-jeevana kaa mahatva hai! vaha kevala baalakon kaa. gharondaa hai, jisake banane kaa na koee hetu hai, na bigadane kaa| phir baalakon ko bhee to apane gharondon se-apanee kaagaja kee naavon se-apane lakadee ke ghodon se mamataa hoti hai! achchhe khilaune ko vaha jaana ke peechhe chhipaa kar rakhate hain| agar eeshvara ladakaa hee hai, to vichinna ladakaa hai!"

lekin akala to eeshvara kaa yaha roopa sveekaara naheen karti| ananta srishti kaa kartaa uddaNda ladakaa naheen ho sakataa| hama use una saare guNon se vibhooshita karte hain, jo hamaaree akala kee pahu.Ncha se baahara hain | khilaadeepana to una mahaana guNon men naheen! kyaa ha.Nsate-khelate baalakon kaa praaNa hara lenaa koee khela hai? kyaa eeshvara aise paishaachika khela khelataa hai|

sahasaa sudhaa dabe paa.Nva kamare men daakhila huee! doktara saahaba . 'utha khade hue aur usake paasa aakara bole-tuma kahaa.N theen sudhaa? main tumhaaree raaha dekha raha tha!
sudhaa kee aa.Nkhon men kamaraa tairataa hua jaana padaa| pati kee gardana men haath daala kar usane unakee chhaatee para sira rakha diya aur rone lagee; lekin is ashru-pravaaha men use aseema dhairya aur saantvanaa kaa anubhava ho raha tha| pati ke vakshasthala se lipatee huee vaha apane dil men ek vichitra sphoorti aur bala kaa sanchaara hote hue paatee thi, maano pavana se tharatharaataa hua deepaka anchala kee aada men aa gaya ho!

doktara saahaba ne ramaNee ke anu-sinchita kapolon ko donon haathon men lekara kahaa-sudhaa, tuma itanaa chhotaa dil kyon karti ho? sohana apane jeevana men jo kuchha karane aaya tha, vaha kar chukaa tha; phir vaha kyon baithaa rahataa| jaise koee vriksha jala aur prakaasha se badhataa hai| lekin pavana ke pravala jhokon hee se sudridha hota hai, usee bhoti praNaya bhee dukha ke aaghaaton hee se vikaasa paataa hai| khushee men saatha ha.Nsane vaale bahutere mila jaate hain| raja men jo saatha roye, vahee hamaaraa sachchaa mitra hai| jina premiyon ko saatha ronaa naheen naseeva hua, ve muhabbata ke maje kyaa jaane? sohana kee mrityu ne aaja hamaare dvaita ko bilakula mitaa diya|aaja hee hamane ek doosare kaa sachchaa svaroopa dekha hai!

sudhaa ne sisakate hue kahaa-main najara ke dhokhe men thi| haaya! tuma usakaa munha bhee naheen dekhane paae!! na jaane ina dinon use itanee samajha kahaa.N se aa gaee thi| jaba mujhe rote dekhataa, to apane takaleepha bhoola kar muskaraa detaa| teesare hee din mere laadale kee aa.Nkhen banda ho gaee| kuchha davaa-darpana bhee na karane paaee|
yaha kehte-kehte sudhaa ke aa.Nsoo phir umada aae| doktara sinhaa ne use seene se lagaa kar karuNaa se kaa.Npatee huee aavaaja men kahaa-priye, aaja taka koee aisaa ladakaa yaa vriddha na maraa hogaa, jisake ghar vaalon ko usake davaa-darpana kee laalasaa pooree ho gaee ho!

sudhaa-nirmalaa ne meree badee madada kee! main to ekaadha jhapakee le bhee letee thi; para unakee aa.Nkhen naheen jhapakee| raat-raat lie baithee yaa tahalaatee rahatee thi| usake ehasaana kabhee na bhooloongee| kyaa tuma aaja hee jaa rahe ho?

doktara-haa.N, chhuttee lene kaa maukaa na tha| sivila-sarjana shikaara khelane gaya hua tha|

sudhaa-yaha sab hameshaa shikaara hee khelaa karte hain?

doktara-raajaaon kaa aur kaama hee kyaa hai?

sudhaa-main to aaja na jaane doongee|

doktara-jee to meraa bhee naheen chaahataa|

sudhaa--to mata jaao; taara de do! main bhee tumhaare saatha chaloo.Ngee| nirmalaa ko bhee letee chaloo.Ngee||

sudhaa vahaa.N se lautee, to usake dil kaa bojha halakaa ho gaya tha! pati kee premapoorNa komala vaaNee ne usake saare shoka aur dukha ko haraNa kar liya tha| pyaara men aseema yakeena hai, aseema dhairya hai| aur aseema bala hai!!
