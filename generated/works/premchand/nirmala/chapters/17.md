# Nirmala — Adhyay 17

**Munshi Premchand**

satrahvam parichchhed

NAmEKrishna ke shadi ke bad NAmESudha chali gai; lekin NAmENirmala maike hi men rah gai. vakil sahab bar-bar likhte the; par vah na jati thi. vaham jane ko uska ji hi na chahta tha. vaham koi aisi chiz na thi, jo use khinch le jay. yaham mam ki seva aur chhote bhaiyon ki dekh-bhal men uska samay bade khushi se kat jata tha. vakil sahab khud ate, to shayad vah jane par razi ho jati; lekin is shadi men muhalle ki kai striyon ne unki vah durgati ki thi ki bechare ane ka nam hi na lete the. NAmESudha ne bhi kai bar chitthi likha; par NAmENirmala ne usse bhi hile-havale kie. akhir ek din NAmESudha ne naukar ko sath liya; aur svayam a dhamki!

jab donon gale mil chukim, to NAmESudha ne kahatumhen to vaham jate mano dar lagta hai.
NAmENirmala -ham bahin, dar to lagta hai. byah ki gai tin sal men ai; ab ki to vaham umra hi khatam ho jaygi; phir kaun bulata hai; aur kaun ata hai?

NAmESudha -ane ko kya hua; jab ji chahe chali ana. vaham vakil sahab bechain ho rahe haim.

NAmENirmala -bahut bechain, rat ko shayad nind na ati ho?

NAmESudha -bahin, tumhara kaleja patthr ka hai. unki hal dekh kar taras ata hai. kahte the, ghar men koi puchhne vala nahin, na koi larka na bala, kisse ji bahlave? jab se dusre makan men uth ae haim, bahut dukhi rahte haim.

NAmENirmala -larke to ishvar ke die do-do haim.

NAmESudha -un donon ki to badi shikayat karte the. NAmEJiyaram to ab bat hi nahin sunta-turki baturki javab deta hai. raha chhota, vah bhi usi ke kahne men hai. vechare bade larke ko yad karke roya karte haim!

NAmENirmala - NAmEJiyaram to sharir na tha, vah badmashi kab se sikh gaya? meri to koi bat na talta tha-ishare par kam karta tha.

NAmESudha -kya jane bahin! suna, kahta hai ap hi ne bhaiya ko jahar dekar mar dala-ap hatyare haim. kai bar tumse shadi karne ke lie tane de chuka hai. aisi-aisi baten kahta hai ki
vakil sahav ro parte haim. are aur to kya kahum, ek din patthr utha kar marne daura tha!

NAmENirmala ne gambhir chinta men par kar kaha-yah larka to vara shaitan nikla. usse yah kisne kaha ki uske bhai ko unhomne jahar de diya hai.

NAmESudha -vah tumhim se thik hoga.

NAmENirmala ko yah nai chinta paida hui! agar jiya ka yahi ranga hai-apne vap se larne par taiyar rahta hai, to mujhse kyon davne laga? vah rat ko badi der tak isi phikra men dubi rahi. NAmEmansaram ki aj use bahut yad ai. uske sath jindagi aram se kat jati. is larke ka jab apne pita ke samne hi yah hal hai, to unke pichhe uske sath kaise nibah hoga. ghar hath se nikal hi gaya. kuchh na kuchh qarz abhi sir par hoga hi! amdani ka yah hal!! ishvar hi bera par lagavenge! aj pahli bar NAmENirmala ko bachchi ki phikra paida hui! is vechari ka na jane kya hal hoga? ishvar ne yah musibat bhi sir dal di! mujhe to iski zarurat na thi. janma hi lena tha, to kisi bhagyavan ke ghar janma leti. bachchi uski chhati se lipti hui so rahi thi. mam ne usko aur bhi chimta liya, mano koi uske hath se use chhine lie jata hai!!

NAmENirmala ke pas hi NAmESudha ki charapai bhi thi. NAmENirmala to chinta-sagar men gota kha rahi thi; aur NAmESudha mithi nind ka
khushi utha rahi thi. kya use apne larka ki phikra satati hai? maut to burhe aur javan ka bhed nahin karti; phir NAmESudha ko kyon koi chinta nahin satati. use to kabhi ane vala samay ki chinta se udas nahin dekha!

sahsa NAmESudha ki nind khul gai. usne NAmENirmala ko abhi tak jagte dekha, to boli-are! abhi tum soi nahin?

NAmENirmala -nind hi nahin ati!

NAmESudha -ankhen band kar lo, ap hi nind a jaygi. maim to charapai par ate hi mar si jati hum. vah jagte bhi haim, to khabar nahin hoti. na jane mujhe kyon itni nind ati hai? shayad koi bimari hai.

NAmENirmala -ham, bada bhari bimari hai. ise raj-bimari kahte haim. doktar sahab se kaho-dava shuru kar den..

NAmESudha -to akhir jag kar kya sochum. kabhi-kabhi maike ki yad a jati hai, to us din jara der men ankh lagti hai.

NAmENirmala doktar sahab ki chad nahin ati?

NAmESudha -kabhi nahin, unko yad kyon ae? janti hum ki tenis khel kar ae honge; khana khaya hoga aur aram se lete honge.

NAmENirmala -lo, sohan bhi jag gaya. jab tum jag gai, to bhala vah kyon sone laga?

NAmESudha -ham vahin, iski ajib adat hai. mere sath sota
hai aur mere hi sath jagta hai. us janma ka koi tapasvi hai. dekho, iske mathe par tilak ka kaisa nishan hai. bamho par bhi aise hi nishan haim. jarur koi tapasvi hai.

NAmENirmala -tapasvi log to chandan-tilak nahin lagane. us janma ka koi dhurt pujari hoga. kyon re, tu kaham ka pujari tha? bata!

NAmESudha -iska vyah men bachchi se karungi!

NAmENirmala -chalo bahin, gali deti ho. bahin se bhi bhai ka vyah hota hai?

NAmESudha -maim to karungi, chahe koi kuchh kahe. aisi sundar bahu aur kaham paungi. jara dekho to bahin, iski deh kuchh garm hai ya mujh hi ko malum hoti hai!

NAmENirmala ne sohan ka matha chhukar kaha-nahin, nahin, deh garm hai. yah jvar kab a gaya? dudh to pi raha hai na?

NAmESudha -abhi soya tha, tab to deh thandi thi. shayad sardi lag gai, orha kar sulae deti hum. savere tak thik ho jayga.

sabera hua to sohan ki hal aur bhi kharab ho gai. uski nak bahne lagi aur bukhar aur bhi tej ho gaya. ankhen charh gai aur sir jhuk gaya. na vah hath pair hilata tha, na hamsta-bolta tha; bas chupchap pada tha. aisa malum hota tha ki use is vakt kisi ka bolna achchha nahin lagta.
kuchh-kuchh khamsi bhi ane lagi. ab to NAmESudha ghabarai. NAmENirmala ki bhi ray hui ki doktar sahab ko bulaya jay; lekin uski burhi mam ne kaha-doktar-hakim ka yaham kuchh kam nahin. saph to dekh rahi hum ki bachche ko najar lag gai hai. bhala doktar akar kya karega?

NAmESudha -ammam ji, bhala yaham nazar kaun laga dega? abhi tak to bahar kahim gaya bhi nahin!

mam-nazar koi lagata nahin beti, kisi-kisi aadmi ki dith hi buri hoti hai-ap hi ap lag jati hai. kabhi-kabhi mam-bap tak ki najar lag jati hai. jabse aya hai, ek bar bhi nahin roya. chochle bachchon ki yahi gati hoti hai. maim to ise humkate dekh kar dari thi ki kuchh na kuchh anisht hone vala hai. ankhen nahin dekhti ho kitni charh gai haim. yahi najar ki sabse badi pahichan hai.

burhiya mahri aur paros ki panditain ne is kathan ka anumodan kar diya. bas, mamhgu ojha bula liya gaya. mamhgu ne akar bachche ka mumh dekha aur hams kar bola-malikin, yah dith hai aur kuchh nahin. jara patli-patli tiliyam to mangva dijie. bhagavan ne chaha, to sajha tak bachcha hamsne-khelne lagega.

sarakande ke panch tukre lae gae. mamhgu ne unhen barabar karke ek dore se bandh diya aur kuchh budbuda kar usi pole
hathon se panch bar sohan ka sir suhlaya. ab jo dekha, to panchon tiliyam chhoti-badi ho gai thi. sav striyam yah kautuk dekh kar danga rah gai. ab najar men kise shak ho sakta tha? mamhgu ne phir bachche ko tiliyon se suhlana shuru kiya. ab ki tiliyam barabar ho gai. sirph thoda sa antar rah gaya. yah is bat ka sabut tha ki nazar ka asar ab thoda sa aur rah gaya hai. mahgu sabko dilasa dekar sham ko phir ane ka vayda karke chala gaya. larka ki hal din ko aur bhi kharab ho gai. khamsi ka jod ho gaya. sham ke samay mamhgu ne akar phir tiliyon ka tamasha kiya. is vakt panchon tiliyam varavar niklim. striyam nishchint ho gai lekin sohan ko sari rat khamste gujri. yaham tak ki kai var uski ankhen ulat gai. NAmESudha aur NAmENirmala donon ne baith kar savera kiya. khair, rat kushal se kat gai. ab vriddha mam ji naya ranga lai. mahgu nazar na utar saka, isalie ab kisi maulvi se phunk dalvana jaruri ho gaya. NAmESudha phir bhi apne pati ko khabar na de saki. mahri sohan ko ek chadar se lapet kar ek masjid men le gai; aur phunk dalva lai. sham ko bhi phunk chhori gai, par sohan ne sir na uthaya. rat a gai, NAmESudha ne aj man men phaisla kiya ki rat kushal se bitegi, to subah pati ko tar dungi.

lekin rat kushal se na bitne pai! adhi rat jate-jate
bachcha hath se nikal gaya!! NAmESudha ki jivan-jaydad dekhte-dekhte uske hathon se chhin gai!!!

vahi jiske shadi ka do din pahle vinod ho raha tha, aj sare ghar ko rula raha hai. jiski bholi-bhali surat dekh kar mam ki chhati phul uthti thi, usi ko dekh kar aj mam ki chhati phati jati hai. sara ghar NAmESudha ko samjhata tha; par uske amsu na thamte the, sab na hota tha. sabse bada dukh is bat ka tha ki pati ko kaun mumh dikhaungi. unhen khabar tak na di!

rat hi ko tar de diya gaya aur dusre din NAmEDoctor Sinha nau bajte-bajte motar par a pahunche. NAmESudha ne unke ane ki khabar pai, to aur bhi phut-phut kar rone lagi. larka ki jal-kriya hui, doktar sahab kai bar andar ae; lekin NAmESudha unke pas na gai. unke samne kaise jay? unhen kaun mumh dikhae? usne apni nadani se unke jivan ka ratna chhin kar dariya men dal diya. ab unke pas jate uski chhati ke tukre-tukre hue jate the. larka ko uski god men dekh kar pati ki ankhen chamak uthti thim. larka umak kar pita ki god men chala jata tha. mam phir bulati, to pita ki chhati se chimat jata tha; aur lakh chumakarne-dularne par bap ki god na chhorta tha. tab mam kahti thi-bada matlabi hai. aj vah kise god men lekar pati ke pas jaygi! uski suni god
dekhkar kahi vah chilla kar ro napren! pati ke sammukh jane ki apeksha use mar jana kahim asan jan parta tha. vah ek pal ke lie bhi NAmENirmala ko na chhorti thi ki kahim pati se samna na ho jay!

NAmENirmala ne kaha-bahin, ab jo hona tha vah to ho hi chuka; ab unse kab tak bhagti phirogi. rat hi ko chale jayenge, ammam kahti thim.

NAmESudha ne sajal netron se takte hue kaha-kaun mumh lekar unke pas jaum? mujhe dar lag raha hai ki unke samne jate hi mere pamv na tharrane lagen aur maim gir na parum!

NAmENirmala -chalo, maim tumhare sath chalti hum. tumhen sambhale rahungi.

NAmESudha -mujhe chhod kar bhag to na a ogi?

NAmENirmala -nahin-nahin, bhagengi nahin.

NAmESudha -mera kaleja to abhi se umra ata hai. maim itna ghor vajripat hone par bhi baithi hum, mujhe yahi hairani ho raha hai! sohan ko vah bahut pyaar karte the; bahin! na jane unke chitt ki kya hal hogi. maim unhen dharhas kya dungi, ap hi roti rahungi. kya rat hi ko chale jayenge?

NAmENirmala -ham, ammam ji kahti thim, chhutti nahin li hai. donon saheliyam mardane kamre ki or chali; lekin kamre ke dvar par pahunch kar NAmESudha ne NAmENirmala ko vida kar diya. akeli kamre men dakhil hui.

doktar sahav ghavra rahe the ki na jane NAmESudha ki kya hal
ho rahi hai! tarah-tarah ki shankaen man men a rahi thim! jane ko taiyar to baithe the; lekin ji na chahta tha! jivan shunya sa malum hota tha. man hi man kurh rahe the, agar ishvar ko itni jaldi yah padarth dekar chhin lena tha, to diya hi kyon tha? unhomne to kabhi bachcha ke lie ishvar se prarthana na ki thi. vah ajanma nissantan rah sakte the; par bachcha pakad usse vanchit ho jana unhen asahya jan parta tha. kya sachmuch manushya ishvar ka khilauna hai? yahi manav-jivan ka mahatva hai! vah sirph balkon ka. gharonda hai, jiske banne ka na koi hetu hai, na bigrane ka. phir balkon ko bhi to apne gharondon se-apni kagaz ki navon se-apne lakri ke ghoron se mamta hoti hai! achchhe khilaune ko vah jan ke pichhe chhipa kar rakhte haim. agar ishvar larka hi hai, to vichinn larka hai!"

lekin akal to ishvar ka yah rup svikar nahin karti. anant srishti ka karta uddand larka nahin ho sakta. ham use un sare gunon se vibhushit karte haim, jo hamari akal ki pahunch se bahar haim. khilaripan to un mahan gunon men nahin! kya hamste-khelte balkon ka jan har lena koi khel hai? kya ishvar aise paishachik khel khelta hai.

sahsa NAmESudha dabe pamv kamre men dakhil hui! doktar sahab. uth khade hue aur uske pas akar bole-tum kaham thim NAmESudha? maim tumhari rah dekh raha tha!
NAmESudha ki ankhon men kamra tairta hua jan pada. pati ki gardan men hath dal kar usne unki chhati par sir rakh diya aur rone lagi; lekin is amsu-pravah men use asim sabra aur santvana ka anubhav ho raha tha. pati ke vakshasthal se lipti hui vah apne dil men ek vichitra sphurti aur bal ka sanchar hote hue pati thi, mano pavan se tharathrata hua dipak anchal ki ar men a gaya ho!

doktar sahab ne ramni ke anu-sinchit kapolon ko donon hathon men lekar kaha- NAmESudha, tum itna chhota dil kyon karti ho? sohan apne jivan men jo kuchh karne aya tha, vah kar chuka tha; phir vah kyon baitha rahta. jaise koi vriksh jal aur prakash se barhta hai. lekin pavan ke praval jhokon hi se sudrirh hota hai, usi bhoti pranay bhi dukh ke aghaton hi se vikas pata hai. khushi men sath hamsne vale bahutere mil jate haim. raj men jo sath roye, vahi hamara sachcha dost hai. jin premiyon ko sath rona nahin nasiv hua, ve muhabbat ke maje kya jane? sohan ki maut ne aj hamare dvait ko bilkul mita diya. aj hi hamne ek dusre ka sachcha svarup dekha hai!

NAmESudha ne siskate hue kaha-maim nazar ke dhokhe men thi. hay! tum uska mumh bhi nahin dekhne pae!! na jane in dinon use itni samajh kaham se a gai thi. jab mujhe rote dekhta, to apne takliph bhul kar muskara deta. tisre hi din mere ladle ki ankhen band ho gai. kuchh dava-darpan bhi na karne pai.
yah kahte-kahte NAmESudha ke amsu phir umar ae. NAmEDoctor Sinha ne use sine se laga kar daya se kampti hui avaj men kaha-priye, aj tak koi aisa larka ya vriddh na mara hoga, jiske ghar valon ko uske dava-darpan ki lalsa puri ho gai ho!

NAmESudha - NAmENirmala ne meri badi madad ki! maim to ekadh jhapki le bhi leti thi; par unki ankhen nahin jhapki. rat-rat lie baithi ya tahlati rahti thi. uske ehsan kabhi na bhulungi. kya tum aj hi ja rahe ho?

doktar-ham, chhutti lene ka mauka na tha. sivil-sarjan shikar khelne gaya hua tha.

NAmESudha -yah sab hamesha shikar hi khela karte haim?

doktar-rajaon ka aur kam hi kya hai?

NAmESudha -maim to aj na jane dungi.

doktar-ji to mera bhi nahin chahta.

NAmESudha --to mat jao; tar de do! maim bhi tumhare sath chalungi. NAmENirmala ko bhi leti chalungi..

NAmESudha vaham se lauti, to uske dil ka bojh halka ho gaya tha! pati ki premapurn komal vani ne uske sare shok aur dukh ko haran kar liya tha. pyaar men asim yakin hai, asim sabra hai. aur asim bal hai!!
