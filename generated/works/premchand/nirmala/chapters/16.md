# Nirmala — Adhyay 16

**Munshi Premchand**

solahavaan parichchheda

maheenaa katate dera na lagee! vivaaha kaa shubhamuhoorta aa pahuncha! mehamaanon se ghar bhara gaya| munshee totaaraama ek din pahale hee aa gae; aur unake saatha nirmalaa kee sahelee bhee aaee| nirmalaa ne to bahut aagraha na kiya tha—vaha khuda hee aane ko utsuka thi| nirmalaa ko sabase ba.dee utkanthaa yahee thi ki vara ke ba.de bhaaee ke darshana karoongee, aur ho saka, to unakee subuddhi par dhanyavaada doongee!

sudhaa ne hansa kar kaha—tuma unase bola sakogee?

nirmalaa—kyon, bolane men kyaa haani hai| ab to doosaraa hee sambandha ho gaya| aur main na bola sakoongee, to tuma to ho hee!

sudhaa—na bhaaee, mujhase yaha na hogaa| main paraaye marda se naheen bola sakatee| na jaane kaise aadmi hon?

nirmalaa—aadmi to bure naheen hain; aur phir tumhen unase kuchha vivaaha to karana naheen, jaraa saa bolane men kyaa haani hai? daoktara saahaba yahaan hote to main tunhen aajnaa dilaa detee!

sudhaa-jo log hadaya ke uddhaara hote hain,kyaa charitra ke bhee achchhe hote hain? paraaee bee ko ghoorane men to kisee marda ko sankocha naheen hota|

nirmalaa-achchha na bolana, main hee baaten kar loongee,ghoora lenge jitana unase ghoorate banega; basa,ab to raajee hui|

itane men krishna aakara baitha gaee| nirmalaa ne muskaraa kar kaha tab battaa krishna, teraa mann is vakta kyon uchaata ho raha hai?

krishnaa-jeejaa jee bulaa rahe hain,pahale jaakara suna aao, peechhe gape la.daanaa| bahut biga.da rahe hain|

nirmalaa kyaa hai, toone kuchha poochhaa naheen?

krishnaa-kuchha beemaara se maalooma hote hain| bahut dubale ho gae hain|

nirmalaa--to jaraa baitha kar unakaa mann bahalaa detee, yahaan dau.dee kyaa chalee aaee| yaha kaho eeshvara ne kripaa kee, naheen to aisaa hee purusha tujhe bhee milata! jaraa baitha kar baaten to karo| buddhe vaate ba.dee lachchhedaara karte hain| jabaana itane deegiyala naheen hote!

krishnaa naheen bahina,tuma jaao; mujhase do vahaan naheen baithaa jata|

nirmalaa chalee gaee, to sudhaa ne krishnaa se kaha-ab to baaraata aa gaee hogee| dvaara-poojaa kyon naheen hoti? krishnaa-kyaa jaane vahina,shaastree jee saamaana ikatthaa kar rahe hain|

sudhaa-sunaa hai, doolhaa kee bhaavaja ba.dee ka.de svabhaava kee stree hai|

krishnaa-kaise maalooma?

sudhaa-main ne sunaa hai,isalie chetaae detee hoon| chaara vaaten Gama khaakara rahana hogaa|

krishnaa-meree jhaga.dane kee aadata hee naheen| jab meree tarapha se koee shikaayata hee na paavengee,to kyaa anaayaasa hee viga.dengee?

sudhaa- haan, sunaa to aisaa hee hai| jhoothamootha la.daa karti hain|

krishnaa-main to sau baat kee ek baat jaanatee hoon-namrata patthara ko bhee moma kar detee hai|

sahasaa shora machaa-vaaraata aa rahi hai| donon ramaniyaan khi.dakee ke saamane aa baithee| ek kshana men nirmalaa bhee aa pahunchee|

vara ke ba.de bhaaee ko dekhane kee use va.dee utsukata ho rahi thi|

sudhaa ne kaha- kaise pata chalegaa ki ba.de bhaaee kaun hain?

nirmalaa-shaastree jee se poochha to maalooma ho| haathee par to krishnaa ke sasura mahaashaya hain| achchha, daoktara saahaba yahaan kaise aa pahunche| vaha gho.de par kyaa hain, dekhatee naheen ho?

sudhaa-haan, hain to vahee|

nirmalaa-una logon se mitrata hogee| koee sambandha to naheen hai? .

sudhaa-ava bhenta ho, to poochha| mujhe to kuchha naheen maalooma| nirmalaa-paalakee men jo mahaashaya baithe hue hain, vaha to doolhaa ke bhaaee jaise naheen deekhate|

sudhaa-bilakula naheen, maalooma hota hai, saaree deha men peta hee peta hai|

nirmalaa-doosare haathee par kaun baithaa hua hai, samajha men naheen aata|

sudhaa-koee ho, doolhaa kaa bhaaee naheen ho sakata| usakee umra naheen dekhatee ho-chaaleesa ke oopara hogee|

nirmalaa-shaastree jee to is vakta dvaara-poojaa kee phikra men hain, naheen to unase poochhatee|

sanyoga se naaee aa gaya| sandookon kee kunjiyaan nirmalaa hee ke paasa theen| is vakta dvaara-chaara ke lie kuchha rupae kee jaroorata thi, maataa ne bhejaa tha| yaha naaee bhee pandita moteraama jee ke saatha tilaka lekara gaya tha| nirmalaa ne kaha-kyaa abhee rupae chaahie?

naaee-haan vahina jee, chala kar de deejie|

nirmalaa-achchha chalatee hoon| pahale yaha vata, too doolhaa ke ba.de bhaaee ko pahachaanata hai?

naaee-pahachaanata kaahe naheen, vaha kyaa saamane hain|

nirmalaa-kahaan, main to naheen dekhatee?

naaee-are, vaha kyaa gho.de par savaara hain? vahee to hain|

nirmalaa ne chakita hokara kaha-kyaa kahata hai, gho.de par doolhaa ke bhaaee hain! pahachaanata hai yaa atakala se kaha raha hai? naaee-are vahina jee, kyaa itana bhoola jaaoonga? abhee to jala-paana kaa saamaana die chalaa aata hoon|

nirmalaa-are, yaha to daoktara saahaba hain, mere pa.dosa men rahate hain|

naaee-haan haan, vahee to daoktara saahaba hain|

nirmalaa ne sudhaa kee ora dekha kar kaha-sunatee ho bahina isakee baaten?

sudhaa ne hansee roka kar kaha-jhootha bolata hai|

naaee-achchha saahaba, jhootha hee sahee; ab ba.don ke munha kaun lage| abhee shaastree jee se puchhavaa doonga, tab to maaniega?

naaee ke aane men dera hui, to moteraama Kuda aangana men aakara shora machaane lage-is ghar kee maryaada rakhana eeshvara hee ke haath hai| naaee ghante bhara se aaya hua hai, aur abhee taka rupae naheen mile|

nirmalaa-jaraa yahaan chale aaiega; shaastree jee? kitane rupae darakaara hain, nikaala doon?

shaastree jee bhunabhunaate aur zora-zora se haanphate hue oopara aae; aur ek lambee saansa lekara bole-kyaa hai? yaha baaton kaa samay naheen hai| jaldee se rupae nikaala do|

nirmalaa-leejie, nikaala to rahi hoon| ab kyaa munha ke, vala gira pa.den| pahale yaha bataaie ki doolhaa ke ba.de bhaaee kaun hain|

shaastree jee-raama-raama, itanee see baat ke lie mujhe aakaasha par latakaa diya| naaee kyaa na pahachaanata tha? nirmalaa-naaee to kahata hai ki vaha jo gho.de par savaara hain, vahee hai|

shaastree jee-to phir aur kise bata de? vahee to hain hee|

naaee-gha.dee bhara se kaha raha hoon; par bahina jee maanatee hee naheen| nirmalaa ne sudhaa kee ora sneha, mamata, vinoda aur kritrima tiraskaara kee drishti se dekha kar kaha-achchha, to tumhee ab taka mere saatha yaha triya-charitra-khela rahi theen| main jaanatee to tumhen yahaan bulaatee hee naheen| ophkoha! bada gaharaa peta hai tumhaara!! tuma maheenon se mere saatha yaha sharaarata karti chalee aati ho; aur kabhee bhoola se bhee is vishaya kaa ek shabda tumhaare munha se naheen nikalaa | main to do-chaara hee din men ubala pa.datee|

sudhaa-tumhen maalooma ho jaata, to tuma mere yahaan aati hee kyon? '

nirmalaa-gazaba re gazaba, main daoktara saahaba se kaee baara baaten kar chukee hoon| tumhaare oopara yaha saaraa paapa pa.degaa| dekhi krishnaa toone apanee jethaanee kee sharaarata? yaha aisee maayaavinee hain, inase daratee rahana!

krishnaa-main to aisee devee ke charana dho-dhokara maathe cha.dhaaoongee| dhanya bhaaga ki unake darshana hue?

nirmalaa-ab samajha gaee| rupae bhee tumheen ne bhejavaae honge| ab sira hilaayaa to sacha kehti hoon, maara baithoongee|

sudhaa-apane ghar bulaa kar mehamaana kaa apamaana naheen kiya jata| nirmalaa—dekho to abhee kaisi-kaisi khabaren letee hoon| mainne tumhaaraa maana rakhane ko zaraa saa likha diya tha; aur tuma sachamucha aa pahunchee| bhalaa vahaan vaale kyaa kehte honge?

sudhaa—sab se kaha kar aaee hoon|

nirmalaa—ab tumhaare paasa kabhee na aaoongee| itana to ishaaraa kar detee ki daoktara saahaba se paradaa rakhana|

sudhaa—unake dekha lene hee se kaun buraaee ho gaee| na dekhate to apanee kismata ko rote kaise? jaanate kaise ki lobha men pa.da kar kaisi cheeza khodee! ab to tumhen dekha kar laalaa jee haath mala kar raha jaate hain| munha se to kuchha naheen kehte; par mann men apanee bhoola par pachhataate hain|

nirmalaa—ab tumhaare ghar kabhee na jaaoongee|

sudhaa—ab pinda naheen chhoota sakata| mainne kaun tumhaare ghar kee raaha naheen dekhi hai|

dvaara-poojaa samaapta ho chukee thi| mehamaana log baithe jala-paana kar rahe the| munshee totaaraama kee baGala men hee daoktara sinhaa baithe hue the| nirmalaa ne kothe par chika kee a.da se unhen baithe dekha aur kalejaa thaama kar raha gaee| ek aarogya, yauvana aur pratibhaa kaa devata tha, doosara...is vishaya men kuchha na kahana hee uchita hai|

nirmalaa ne daoktara saahaba ko saika.don hee baara dekha tha; par aaja usake hridaya men jo vichaara uthe, ve kabhee na uthe the| baara-baara yahee jee chaahata tha ki bulaa kar khooba phatakaaroon, aise-aise taane maaroon ki vaha bho yaada karen,rulaa-rulaa kar chhodoon magar sahama karake raha jati thi| baaraata janavaase chalee gaee thi| bhojana kee taiyaaree ho rahi thi| nirmalaa bhojana ke thaala chunane men vyasta thi, sahasaa maharee ne aakara kaha-bittee, tumhen sudhaa raanee bulaa rahi hain| tumhaare kamare men baithee hain|

nirmalaa ne thaala chho.da die aur ghabaraaee hui sudhaa ke paasa aaee, magar andara kadama rakhate hee thithaka gaee-daoktara sinhaa kha.de the|

sudhaa ne muskaraa kar kaha-lo bahina,bulaa diya| ab jitana chaaho,phatakaaro| main daravaajaa roke kha.dee hoon,bhaaga naheen sakate|

daoktara saahaba ne gambheera bhaava se kaha-bhaagata kaun hai yahaan to sira jhukaae kha.daa hoon|

nirmalaa ne haath jo.da kar kaha-isee tarah sadaa kripaa-drishti rakhiega,bhoola na jaaiega! yahee meree vinaya hai!!
