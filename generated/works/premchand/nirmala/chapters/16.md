# Nirmala — Adhyay 16

**Munshi Premchand**

solahavaan parichchhed

maheenaa katate der n lagee! shaadee ka shubhamuhoort aa pahunchaa! mehamaanon se ghar bhar gayaa. munshee totaaraam ek din pahale hee aa gae; aur unake saath Nirmala ki sahelee bhi aaee. Nirmala ne to bahut jor dekar kahanaa n kiyaa tha — vah khud hee aane ko utsuk thee. Nirmala ko sabase badee utkanthaa yahee thi ki var ke bade bhaaee ke darshan karoonggee, aur ho sakaa, to unakee subuddhi par dhanyavaad doonggee!

sudhaa ne hans kar kahaa — tum unase bol sakogee?

Nirmala — kyon, bolane mein kyaa haani hai. ab to doosaraa hee rishtaa ho gayaa. aur main n bol sakoonggee, to tum to ho hee!

sudhaa — n bhaaee, mujhase yah n hogaa. main paraaye mard se nahin bol sakatee. n jaane kaise aadmi hon?

Nirmala — aadmi to bure nahin hain; aur phir tumhe unase kuchh shaadee to karanaa nahin, jaraa saa bolane mein kyaa haani hai? doktar Sahab yahan hote to main tunhen aagyaa dilaa detee!

sudhaa-jo log haday ke uddhaar hote hain, kyaa charitr ke bhi achchhe hote hain? paraaee bee ko ghoorane mein to kisee mard ko sanggkoch nahin hotaa.

Nirmala-achchha n bolanaa, main hee baaten kar loonggee, ghoor lengge jitanaa unase ghoorate banegaa; bas, ab to raajee huee.

itane mein krishn aakar baith gaee. Nirmala ne muskaraa kar kahaa tab battaa krishnaa, teraa man is vakt kyon uchaat ho rahaa hai?

krishnaa-jeejaa jee bulaa rahe hain, pahale jaakar sun aao, peechhe gape ladaanaa. bahut bigad rahe hain.

Nirmala kyaa hai, toone kuchh poochhaa nahin?

krishnaa-kuchh beemaar se maaloom hote hain. bahut dubale ho gae hain.

Nirmala--to jaraa baith kar unakaa man bahalaa detee, yahan daudee kyaa chalee aaee. yah kaho Bhagwan ne kripaa ki, nahin to aisaa hee mard tujhe bhi milataa! jaraa baith kar baaten to karo. buddhe vaate badee lachchhedaar karate hain. jabaan itane deegiyal nahin hote!

krishnaa nahin bahin, tum jaao; mujhase do wahan nahin baithaa jaataa.

Nirmala chalee gaee, to sudhaa ne krishnaa se kahaa-ab to baaraat aa gaee hogee. dvaar-poojaa kyon nahin hotee? krishnaa-kyaa jaane vahin, shaaaurat jee saamaan ikatthaa kar rahe hain.

sudhaa-sunaa hai, doolhaa ki bhaavaj badee kade svabhaav ki aurat hai.

krishnaa-kaise maaloom?

sudhaa-main ne sunaa hai, isalie chetaae detee hoon. chaar vaaten gam khaakar rahanaa hogaa.

krishnaa-meree jhagadane ki aadat hee naheen. jab meree taraph se koee shikaayat hee n paavenggee, to kyaa anaayaas hee vigadenggee?

sudhaa- haan, sunaa to aisaa hee hai. jhoothamooth ladaa karatee hain.

krishnaa-main to sau baat ki ek baat jaanatee hoon-narmee patthar ko bhi mom kar detee hai.

sahasaa shor machaa-vaaraat aa rahee hai. donon ramaniyaan khidakee ke saamane aa baithee. ek pal mein Nirmala bhi aa pahunchee.

var ke bade bhaaee ko dekhane ki use vadee utsukataa ho rahee thee.

sudhaa ne kahaa- kaise pataa chalegaa ki bade bhaaee kaun hain?

Nirmala-shaaaurat jee se poochh to maaloom ho. haathee par to krishnaa ke sasur mahaashay hain. achchha, doktar Sahab yahan kaise aa pahunche. vah ghode par kyaa hain, dekhatee nahin ho?

sudhaa-haan, hain to vahee.

Nirmala-un logon se mitrataa hogee. koee rishtaa to nahin hai?.

sudhaa-av bhent ho, to poochha. mujhe to kuchh nahin maalooma. Nirmala-paalakee mein jo mahaashay baithe hue hain, vah to doolhaa ke bhaaee jaise nahin deekhate.

sudhaa-bilakul nahin, maaloom hotaa hai, saaree deh mein pet hee pet hai.

Nirmala-doosare haathee par kaun baithaa huaa hai, samajh mein nahin aataa.

sudhaa-koee ho, doolhaa ka bhaaee nahin ho sakataa. usakee umr nahin dekhatee ho-chaalees ke oopar hogee.

Nirmala-shaaaurat jee to is vakt dvaar-poojaa ki fikr mein hain, nahin to unase poochhatee.

sanyog se naaee aa gayaa. sandookon ki kunyjiyaan Nirmala hee ke paas theen. is vakt dvaar-chaar ke lie kuchh rupaye ki jaroorat thi, maataa ne bhejaa thaa. yah naaee bhi pandit moteraam jee ke saath tilak lekar gayaa thaa. Nirmala ne kahaa-kyaa abhee rupaye chahiye?

naaee-haan vahin jee, chal kar de deejie.

Nirmala-achchha chalatee hoon. pahale yah vataa, too doolhaa ke bade bhaaee ko pahachaanataa hai?

naaee-pahachaanataa kaahe nahin, vah kyaa saamane hain.

Nirmala-kahaan, main to nahin dekhatee?

naaee-are, vah kyaa ghode par savaar hain? vahee to hain.

Nirmala ne chakit hokar kahaa-kyaa kahataa hai, ghode par doolhaa ke bhaaee hain! pahachaanataa hai ya atakal se kah rahaa hai? naaee-are vahin jee, kyaa itanaa bhool jaaoonggaa? abhee to jal-paan ka saamaan die chalaa aataa hoon.

Nirmala-are, yah to doktar Sahab hain, mere pados mein rahate hain.

naaee-haan haan, vahee to doktar Sahab hain.

Nirmala ne sudhaa ki or dekh kar kahaa-sunatee ho bahin isakee baaten?

sudhaa ne hansee rok kar kahaa-jhooth bolataa hai.

naaee-achchha Sahab, jhooth hee sahee; ab badon ke munh kaun lage. abhee shaaaurat jee se puchhavaa doonggaa, tab to maaniegaa?

naaee ke aane mein der huee, to moteraam khud aanggan mein aakar shor machaane lage-is ghar ki maryaad rakhanaa Bhagwan hee ke haath hai. naaee ghante bhar se kamaaeeaa huaa hai, aur abhee tak rupaye nahin mile.

Nirmala-jaraa yahan chale aaiegaa; shaaaurat jee? kitane rupaye darakaar hain, nikaal doon?

shaaaurat jee bhunabhunaate aur jor-jor se haamphate hue oopar aae; aur ek lambee saans lekar bole-kyaa hai? yah baaton ka samay nahin hai. jaldee se rupaye nikaal do.

Nirmala-leejie, nikaal to rahee hoon. ab kyaa munh ke, val gir paden. pahale yah bataaie ki doolhaa ke bade bhaaee kaun hain.

shaaaurat jee-raam-raam, itanee see baat ke lie mujhe aakaash par latakaa diyaa. naaee kyaa n pahachaanataa tha? Nirmala-naaee to kahataa hai ki vah jo ghode par savaar hain, vahee hai.

shaaaurat jee-to phir aur kise bataa de? vahee to hain hee.

naaee-ghadee bhar se kah rahaa hoon; par bahin jee maanatee hee naheen. Nirmala ne sudhaa ki or pyaar, mamataa, vinod aur kritrim nafrat ki drishti se dekh kar kahaa-achchha, to tumhee ab tak mere saath yah triyaa-charitr-khel rahee theen. main jaanatee to tumhe yahan bulaatee hee naheen. ophkoh! badaa gaharaa pet hai tumhaaraa!! tum maheenon se mere saath yah sharaarat karatee chalee aatee ho; aur kabhee bhool se bhi is vishay ka ek shabd tumhaare munh se nahin nikalaa. main to do-chaar hee din mein ubal padatee.

sudhaa-tumhe maaloom ho jaataa, to tum mere yahan aatee hee kyon? '

Nirmala-gajab re gajab, main doktar Sahab se kaee baar baaten kar chukee hoon. tumhaare oopar yah saaraa paap padegaa. dekhee krishnaa toone apanee jethaanee ki sharaarat? yah aisee maayaavinee hain, inase daratee rahanaa!

krishnaa-main to aisee devee ke charan dho-dhokar maathe chadhaaoonggee. dhany bhaag ki unake darshan hue?

Nirmala-ab samajh gaee. rupaye bhi tumheen ne bhejavaae hongge. ab sir hilaayaa to sach kahatee hoon, maar baithoonggee.

sudhaa-apane ghar bulaa kar mehamaan ka beijjatee nahin kiyaa jaataa. Nirmala — dekho to abhee kaisee-kaisee khabaren letee hoon. mainne tumhaaraa maan rakhane ko jaraa saa likh diyaa tha; aur tum sachamuch aa pahunchee. bhalaa wahan vaale kyaa kahate hongge?

sudhaa — sab se kah kar aaee hoon.

Nirmala — ab tumhaare paas kabhee n aaoonggee. itanaa to ishaaraa kar detee ki doktar Sahab se paradaa rakhanaa.

sudhaa — unake dekh lene hee se kaun buraaee ho gaee. n dekhate to apanee kismat ko rote kaise? jaanate kaise ki lobh mein pad kar kaisee cheej khodee! ab to tumhe dekh kar laalaa jee haath mal kar rah jaate hain. munh se to kuchh nahin kahate; par man mein apanee bhool par pachhataate hain.

Nirmala — ab tumhaare ghar kabhee n jaaoonggee.

sudhaa — ab pind nahin chhoot sakataa. mainne kaun tumhaare ghar ki raah nahin dekhee hai.

dvaar-poojaa khatm ho chukee thee. mehamaan log baithe jal-paan kar rahe the. munshee totaaraam ki bagal mein hee doktar sinhaa baithe hue the. Nirmala ne kothe par chik ki aad se unhen baithe dekhaa aur kalejaa thaam kar rah gaee. ek aabeemaareey, yauvan aur pratibhaa ka devataa tha, doosaraa...... is vishay mein kuchh n kahanaa hee theek hai.

Nirmala ne doktar Sahab ko saikadon hee baar dekhaa tha; par aaj usake dil mein jo soch uthe, ve kabhee n uthe the. baar-baar yahee jee chaahataa tha ki bulaa kar khoob phatakaaroon, aise-aise taane maaroon ki vah bho yaad karen, rulaa-rulaa kar chhodoon magar saham karake rah jaatee thee. baaraat janavaase chalee gaee thee. khana ki taiyaaree ho rahee thee. Nirmala khana ke thaal chunane mein vyast thi, sahasaa maharee ne aakar kahaa-bittee, tumhe sudhaa raanee bulaa rahee hain. tumhaare kamare mein baithee hain.

Nirmala ne thaal chhod die aur ghabaraaee huee sudhaa ke paas aaee, magar andar kadam rakhate hee thithak gaee-doktar sinhaa khade the.

sudhaa ne muskaraa kar kahaa-lo bahin, bulaa diyaa. ab jitanaa chaaho, phatakaaro. main daravaajaa roke khadee hoon, bhaag nahin sakate.

doktar Sahab ne seeriyas bhaav se kahaa-bhaagataa kaun hai yahan to sir jhukaae khadaa hoon.

Nirmala ne haath jod kar kahaa-isee tarah sadaa kripaa-drishti rakhiegaa, bhool n jaaiegaa! yahee meree vinay hai!!
