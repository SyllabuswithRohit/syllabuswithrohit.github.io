# Nirmala — Adhyay 16

**Munshi Premchand**

solhavam parichchhed

mahina katte der na lagi! shadi ka shubhamuhurt a pahuncha! mehmano se ghar bhar gaya. munshi NAmETotaram ek din pahle hi a gae; aur unke sath NAmENirmala ki saheli bhi ai. NAmENirmala ne to bahut agrah na kiya thavah khud hi ane ko bekrar thi. NAmENirmala ko sabse badi utkantha yahi thi ki dulha ke bade bhai ke darshan karungi, aur ho saka, to unki subuddhi par dhanyavad dungi!

NAmESudha ne hams kar kahatum unse bol sakogi?

NAmENirmala kyon, bolne men kya nuksan hai. ab to dusra hi rishta ho gaya. aur maim na bol sakungi, to tum to ho hi!

NAmESudha na bhai, mujhse yah na hoga. maim paraye mard se nahin bol sakti. na jane kaise aadmi hon?

NAmENirmala aadmi to bure nahin haim; aur phir tumhen unse kuchh
shadi to karna nahin, jara sa bolne men kya nuksan hai? doktar sahab yaham hote to maim tunhen hukma dila deti!

NAmESudha -jo log haday ke uddhar hote haim, kya svabhav ke bhi achchhe hote haim? parai bi ko ghurne men to kisi mard ko sankoch nahin hota.

NAmENirmala -achchha na bolna, maim hi baten kar lungi, ghur lenge jitna unse ghurte banega; bas, ab to raji hui.

itne men krishna akar baith gai. NAmENirmala ne muskara kar kaha tab batta NAmEKrishna, tera man is vakt kyon uchat ho raha hai?

NAmEKrishna -jija ji bula rahe haim, pahle jakar sun ao, pichhe gape larana. bahut bigar rahe haim.

NAmENirmala kya hai, tune kuchh puchha nahin?

NAmEKrishna -kuchh bimar se malum hote haim. bahut duble ho gae haim.

NAmENirmala --to jara baith kar unka man bahla deti, yaham dauri kya chali ai. yah kaho ishvar ne kripa ki, nahin to aisa hi aadmi tujhe bhi milta! jara baith kar baten to karo. buddhe vate badi lachchhedar karte haim. jaban itne digiyal nahin hote!

NAmEKrishna nahin bahin, tum jao; mujhse do vaham nahin baitha jata.

NAmENirmala chali gai, to NAmESudha ne NAmEKrishna se kaha-ab to barat a gai hogi. dvar-puja kyon nahin hoti?
NAmEKrishna -kya jane vahin, shastri ji saman ikattha kar rahe haim.

NAmESudha -suna hai, dulha ki bhavaj badi kare svabhav ki aurat hai.

NAmEKrishna -kaise malum?

NAmESudha -maim ne suna hai, isalie chetae deti hum. char vaten gam khakar rahna hoga.

NAmEKrishna -meri jhagrane ki adat hi nahin. jab meri taraph se koi shikayat hi na pavengi, to kya anayas hi vigrengi?

NAmESudha - ham, suna to aisa hi hai. jhuthmuth lara karti haim.

NAmEKrishna -maim to sau bat ki ek bat janti hum-namrata patthr ko bhi mon kar deti hai.

sahsa shor macha-varat a rahi hai. donon ramniya khirki ke samne a baithi. ek pal men NAmENirmala bhi a pahunchi.

dulha ke bade bhai ko dekhne ki use vari utsukta ho rahi thi.

NAmESudha ne kaha- kaise pata chalega ki bade bhai kaun haim?

NAmENirmala -shastri ji se puchh to malum ho. hathi par to NAmEKrishna ke sasur mahashay haim. achchha, doktar sahab yaham kaise a pahunche. vah ghore par kya haim, dekhti nahin ho?

NAmESudha -ham, haim to vahi.

NAmENirmala -un logon se mitrata hogi. koi rishta to nahin hai?.

NAmESudha -av bhent ho, to puchh. mujhe to kuchh nahin malum.
NAmENirmala -palki men jo mahashay baithe hue haim, vah to dulha ke bhai jaise nahin dikhte.

NAmESudha -bilkul nahin, malum hota hai, sari deh men pet hi pet hai.

NAmENirmala -dusre hathi par kaun baitha hua hai, samajh men nahin ata.

NAmESudha -koi ho, dulha ka bhai nahin ho sakta. uski umra nahin dekhti ho-chalis ke upar hogi.

NAmENirmala -shastri ji to is vakt dvar-puja ki phikra men haim, nahin to unse puchhti.

samyog se nai a gaya. sandukon ki kunjiyam NAmENirmala hi ke pas thim. is vakt dvar-char ke lie kuchh rupae ki jarurat thi, mam ne bheja tha. yah nai bhi pandit moteram ji ke sath tilak lekar gaya tha. NAmENirmala ne kaha-kya abhi rupae chahiye?

nai-ham vahin ji, chal kar de dijie.

NAmENirmala -achchha chalti hum. pahle yah vata, tu dulha ke bade bhai ko pahachanta hai?

nai-pahachanta kahe nahin, vah kya samne haim.

NAmENirmala -kaham, maim to nahin dekhti?

nai-are, vah kya ghore par savar haim? vahi to haim.

NAmENirmala ne chakit hokar kaha-kya kahta hai, ghore par dulha ke bhai haim! pahachanta hai ya atkal se kah raha hai?
nai-are vahin ji, kya itna bhul jaunga? abhi to jal-pan ka saman die chala ata hum.

NAmENirmala -are, yah to doktar sahab haim, mere paros men rahte haim.

nai-ham ham, vahi to doktar sahab haim.

NAmENirmala ne NAmESudha ki or dekh kar kaha-sunti ho bahin iski baten?

NAmESudha ne hamsi rok kar kaha-jhuth bolta hai.

nai-achchha sahab, jhuth hi sahi; ab badon ke mumh kaun lage. abhi shastri ji se puchhva dunga, tab to maniega?

nai ke ane men der hui, to moteram khud angan men akar shor machane lage-is ghar ki maryad rakhna ishvar hi ke hath hai. nai ghante bhar se aya hua hai, aur abhi tak rupae nahin mile.

NAmENirmala -jara yaham chale aiega; shastri ji? kitne rupae darkar haim, nikal dum?

shastri ji bhunabhunate aur zor-zor se hamphte hue upar ae; aur ek lambi sams lekar bole-kya hai? yah baton ka samay nahin hai. jaldi se rupae nikal do.

NAmENirmala -lijie, nikal to rahi hum. ab kya mumh ke, val gir paren. pahle yah bataie ki dulha ke bade bhai kaun haim.

shastri ji-ram-ram, itni si bat ke lie mujhe akash par latka diya. nai kya na pahachanta tha?
NAmENirmala -nai to kahta hai ki vah jo ghore par savar haim, vahi hai.

shastri ji-to phir aur kise bata de? vahi to haim hi.

nai-ghadi bhar se kah raha hum; par bahin ji manti hi nahin. NAmENirmala ne NAmESudha ki or pyaar, mamta, vinod aur kritrim beizzati ki nazar se dekh kar kaha-achchha, to tumhi ab tak mere sath yah triya-svabhav-khel rahi thim. maim janti to tumhen yaham bulati hi nahin. ophkoh! bada gahra pet hai tumhara!! tum mahinon se mere sath yah shararat karti chali ati ho; aur kabhi bhul se bhi is vishay ka ek shabd tumhare mumh se nahin nikla. maim to do-char hi din men ubal parti.

NAmESudha -tumhen malum ho jata, to tum mere yaham ati hi kyon?

NAmENirmala -gazab re gazab, maim doktar sahab se kai bar baten kar chuki hum. tumhare upar yah sara pap parega. dekhi NAmEKrishna tune apni jethani ki shararat? yah aisi mayavini haim, inse darti rahna!

NAmEKrishna -maim to aisi devi ke charan dho-dhokar mathe charhaungi. dhanya bhag ki unke darshan hue?

NAmENirmala -ab samajh gai. rupae bhi tumhim ne bhejavae honge. ab sir hilaya to sach kahti hum, mar baithungi.

NAmESudha -apne ghar bula kar mehman ka beizzati nahin kiya jata.
NAmENirmala dekho to abhi kaisi-kaisi khabren leti hum. maimne tumhara man rakhne ko zara sa likh diya tha; aur tum sachmuch a pahunchi. bhala vaham vale kya kahte honge?

NAmESudha sab se kah kar ai hum.

NAmENirmala ab tumhare pas kabhi na aungi. itna to ishara kar deti ki doktar sahab se parda rakhna.

NAmESudha unke dekh lene hi se kaun burai ho gai. na dekhte to apni kismat ko rote kaise? jante kaise ki lobh men par kar kaisi chiz khodi! ab to tumhen dekh kar lala ji hath mal kar rah jate haim. mumh se to kuchh nahin kahte; par man men apni bhul par pachhtate haim.

NAmENirmala ab tumhare ghar kabhi na jaungi.

NAmESudha ab pind nahin chhut sakta. maimne kaun tumhare ghar ki rah nahin dekhi hai.

dvar-puja khatma ho chuki thi. mehman log baithe jal-pan kar rahe the. munshi NAmETotaram ki bagal men hi NAmEDoctor Sinha baithe hue the. NAmENirmala ne kothe par chik ki ar se unhen baithe dekha aur kaleja tham kar rah gai. ek arogya, yauvan aur pratibha ka devta tha, dusra...... is vishay men kuchh na kahna hi thik hai.

NAmENirmala ne doktar sahab ko saikron hi bar dekha tha; par aj uske dil men jo soch uthe, ve kabhi na uthe the. bar-bar yahi ji chahta tha ki bula kar khub phatkaru, aise-aise tane marum ki vah
bho yad karen, rula-rula kar chhodum magar saham karke rah jati thi. barat janvase chali gai thi. khana ki taiyari ho rahi thi. NAmENirmala khana ke thal chunne men vyast thi, sahsa mahri ne akar kaha-bitti, tumhen NAmESudha rani bula rahi haim. tumhare kamre men baithi haim.

NAmENirmala ne thal chhod die aur ghabarai hui NAmESudha ke pas ai, magar andar kadam rakhte hi thithak gai- NAmEDoctor Sinha khade the.

NAmESudha ne muskara kar kaha-lo bahin, bula diya. ab jitna chaho, phatkaro. maim darvaja roke khadi hum, bhag nahin sakte.

doktar sahab ne gambhir bhav se kaha-bhagta kaun hai yaham to sir jhukae khada hum.

NAmENirmala ne hath jod kar kaha-isi tarah sada kripa-nazar rakhiega, bhul na jaiega! yahi meri narmi hai!!
