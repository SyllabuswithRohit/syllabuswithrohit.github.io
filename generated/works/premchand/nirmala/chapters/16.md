# Chapter 16

solahavaa.N parichchheda

maheenaa katate dera na lagee! vivaaha kaa shubhamuhoorta aa pahu.Nchaa! mehamaanon se ghar bhara gaya| munshee totaaraama ek din pahale hee aa gae; aur unake saatha nirmalaa kee sahelee bhee aaee| nirmalaa ne to bahuta aagraha na kiya tha—vaha khuda hee aane ko utsuka thi| nirmalaa ko sabase badee utkaNthaa yahee thi ki vara ke bade bhaaee ke darshana karoo.Ngee, aur ho sakaa, to unakee subuddhi para dhanyavaada doo.Ngee!

sudhaa ne ha.Nsa kar kahaa—tuma unase bola sakogee?

nirmalaa—kyon, bolane men kyaa nukasaana hai| aba to doosaraa hee sambandha ho gaya| aur main na bola sakoo.Ngee, to tuma to ho hee!

sudhaa—na bhaaee, mujhase yaha na hogaa| main paraaye marda se naheen bola sakatee| na jaane kaise aadamee hon?

nirmalaa—aadamee to bure naheen hain; aur phir tumhen unase kuchha
vivaaha to karna naheen, jaraa saa bolane men kyaa nukasaana hai? doktara saahaba yahaa.N hote to main tunhen hukma dilaa detee!

sudhaa-jo log hadaya ke uddhaara hote hain,kyaa charitra ke bhee achchhe hote hain? paraaee bee ko ghoorane men to kisee marda ko sankocha naheen hota|

nirmalaa-achchhaa na bolanaa, main hee baaten kar loongee,ghoora lenge jitanaa unase ghoorate banegaa; basa,aba to raajee huee|

itane men krishNa aakara baitha gaee| nirmalaa ne muskaraa kar kahaa taba battaa krishNaa, teraa mana is vakta kyon uchaata ho raha hai?

krishNaa-jeejaa jee bulaa rahe hain,pahale jaakara suna aao, peechhe gape ladaanaa| bahuta bigada rahe hain|

nirmalaa kyaa hai, toone kuchha poochhaa naheen?

krishNaa-kuchha beemaara se maalooma hote hain| bahuta dubale ho gae hain|

nirmalaa--to jaraa baitha kar unakaa mana bahalaa detee, yahaa.N daudee kyaa chalee aaee| yaha kaho eeshvara ne kripaa kee, naheen to aisaa hee aadamee tujhe bhee milataa! jaraa baitha kar baaten to karo| buddhe vaate badee lachchhedaara karte hain| jabaana itane deegiyala naheen hote!

krishNaa naheen bahina,tuma jaao; mujhase do vahaa.N naheen baithaa jata|

nirmalaa chalee gaee, to sudhaa ne krishNaa se kahaa-aba to baaraata aa gaee hogee| dvaara-poojaa kyon naheen hoti?
krishNaa-kyaa jaane vahina,shaastree jee saamaana ikatthaa kar rahe hain|

sudhaa-sunaa hai, doolhaa kee bhaavaja badee kade svabhaava kee aurata hai|

krishNaa-kaise maalooma?

sudhaa-main ne sunaa hai,isalie chetaae detee hoo.N| chaara vaaten gama khaakara rahanaa hogaa|

krishNaa-meree jhagadane kee aadata hee naheen| jaba meree tarapha se koee shikaayata hee na paavengee,to kyaa anaayaasa hee vigadengee?

sudhaa- haa.N, sunaa to aisaa hee hai| jhoothamootha ladaa karti hain|

krishNaa-main to sau baat kee ek baat jaanatee hoo.N-namrataa patthara ko bhee moma kar detee hai|

sahasaa shora machaa-vaaraata aa rahi hai| donon ramaNiyaa.N khidakee ke saamane aa baithee| ek pala men nirmalaa bhee aa pahunchee|

vara ke bade bhaaee ko dekhane kee use vadee utsukataa ho rahi thi|

sudhaa ne kahaa- kaise pataa chalegaa ki bade bhaaee kauna hain?

nirmalaa-shaastree jee se poochha to maalooma ho| haathee para to krishNaa ke sasura mahaashaya hain| achchhaa, doktara saahaba yahaa.N kaise aa pahu.Nche| vaha ghode para kyaa hain, dekhatee naheen ho?

sudhaa-haa.N, hain to vahee|

nirmalaa-una logon se mitrataa hogee| koee sambandha to naheen hai? .

sudhaa-ava bhenta ho, to poochha| mujhe to kuchha naheen maalooma|
nirmalaa-paalakee men jo mahaashaya baithe hue hain, vaha to doolhaa ke bhaaee jaise naheen deekhate|

sudhaa-bilakula naheen, maalooma hota hai, saaree deha men peta hee peta hai|

nirmalaa-doosare haathee para kauna baithaa hua hai, samajha men naheen aata|

sudhaa-koee ho, doolhaa kaa bhaaee naheen ho sakataa| usakee umra naheen dekhatee ho-chaaleesa ke oopara hogee|

nirmalaa-shaastree jee to is vakta dvaara-poojaa kee phikra men hain, naheen to unase poochhatee|

sanyoga se naaee aa gaya| sandookon kee kunjiyaa.N nirmalaa hee ke paasa theen| is vakta dvaara-chaara ke lie kuchha rupae kee jaroorata thi, maa.N ne bhejaa tha| yaha naaee bhee paNdita moteraama jee ke saatha tilaka lekara gaya tha| nirmalaa ne kahaa-kyaa abhee rupae chaahie?

naaee-haa.N vahina jee, chala kar de deejie|

nirmalaa-achchhaa chalatee hoo.N| pahale yaha vataa, too doolhaa ke bade bhaaee ko pahachaanataa hai?

naaee-pahachaanataa kaahe naheen, vaha kyaa saamane hain|

nirmalaa-kahaa.N, main to naheen dekhatee?

naaee-are, vaha kyaa ghode para savaara hain? vahee to hain|

nirmalaa ne chakita hokara kahaa-kyaa kehta hai, ghode para doolhaa ke bhaaee hain! pahachaanataa hai yaa atakala se kaha raha hai?
naaee-are vahina jee, kyaa itanaa bhoola jaaoo.Ngaa? abhee to jala-paana kaa saamaana die chalaa aata hoo.N|

nirmalaa-are, yaha to doktara saahaba hain, mere padosa men rahate hain|

naaee-haa.N haa.N, vahee to doktara saahaba hain|

nirmalaa ne sudhaa kee ora dekha kar kahaa-sunatee ho bahina isakee baaten?

sudhaa ne ha.Nsee roka kar kahaa-jhootha bolataa hai|

naaee-achchhaa saahaba, jhootha hee sahee; aba badon ke mu.Nha kauna lage| abhee shaastree jee se puchhavaa doongaa, taba to maaniegaa?

naaee ke aane men dera huee, to moteraama khuda aa.Ngana men aakara shora machaane lage-is ghar kee maryaada rakhanaa eeshvara hee ke haath hai| naaee ghaNte bhara se aaya hua hai, aur abhee taka rupae naheen mile|

nirmalaa-jaraa yahaa.N chale aaiegaa; shaastree jee? kitane rupae darakaara hain, nikaala doo.N?

shaastree jee bhunabhunaate aur jora-jora se haa.Nphate hue oopara aae; aur ek lambee saa.Nsa lekara bole-kyaa hai? yaha baaton kaa samaya naheen hai| jaldee se rupae nikaala do|

nirmalaa-leejie, nikaala to rahi hoo.N| aba kyaa mu.Nha ke, vala gira paden| pahale yaha bataaie ki doolhaa ke bade bhaaee kauna hain|

shaastree jee-raama-raama, itanee see baat ke lie mujhe aakaasha para latakaa diya| naaee kyaa na pahachaanataa tha?
nirmalaa-naaee to kehta hai ki vaha jo ghode para savaara hain, vahee hai|

shaastree jee-to phir aur kise bataa de? vahee to hain hee|

naaee-ghadee bhara se kaha raha hoo.N; para bahina jee maanatee hee naheen| nirmalaa ne sudhaa kee ora pyaara, mamataa, vinoda aur kritrima tiraskaara kee najara se dekha kar kahaa-achchhaa, to tumhee aba taka mere saatha yaha triyaa-charitra-khela rahi theen| main jaanatee to tumhen yahaa.N bulaatee hee naheen| ophkoha! badaa gaharaa peta hai tumhaaraa!! tuma maheenon se mere saatha yaha sharaarata karti chalee aatee ho; aur kabhee bhoola se bhee is vishaya kaa ek shabda tumhaare mu.Nha se naheen nikalaa | main to do-chaara hee din men ubala padatee|

sudhaa-tumhen maalooma ho jata, to tuma mere yahaa.N aatee hee kyon? '

nirmalaa-gajaba re gajaba, main doktara saahaba se kaee baara baaten kar chukee hoo.N| tumhaare oopara yaha saaraa paapa padegaa| dekhee krishNaa toone apanee jethaanee kee sharaarata? yaha aisee maayaavinee hain, inase daratee rahanaa!

krishNaa-main to aisee devee ke charaNa dho-dhokara maathe chadhaaoo.Ngee| dhanya bhaaga ki unake darshana hue?

nirmalaa-aba samajha gaee| rupae bhee tumheen ne bhejavaae honge| aba sira hilaayaa to sacha kehti hoo.N, maara baithoo.Ngee|

sudhaa-apane ghar bulaa kar mehamaana kaa beijjatee naheen kiya jata|
nirmalaa—dekho to abhee kaisee-kaisee khabaren letee hoo.N| mainne tumhaaraa maana rakhane ko jaraa saa likha diya tha; aur tuma sachamucha aa pahu.Nchee| bhalaa vahaa.N vaale kyaa kehte honge?

sudhaa—sab se kaha kar aaee hoo.N|

nirmalaa—aba tumhaare paasa kabhee na aaoo.Ngee| itanaa to ishaaraa kar detee ki doktara saahaba se paradaa rakhanaa|

sudhaa—unake dekha lene hee se kauna buraaee ho gaee| na dekhate to apanee kismata ko rote kaise? jaanate kaise ki lobha men pada kar kaisee cheeja khodee! aba to tumhen dekha kar laalaa jee haath mala kar raha jaate hain| mu.Nha se to kuchha naheen kehte; para mana men apanee bhoola para pachhataate hain|

nirmalaa—aba tumhaare ghar kabhee na jaaoo.Ngee|

sudhaa—aba piNda naheen chhoota sakataa| mainne kauna tumhaare ghar kee raaha naheen dekhee hai|

dvaara-poojaa khatma ho chukee thi| mehamaana log baithe jala-paana kar rahe the| munshee totaaraama kee bagala men hee doktara sinhaa baithe hue the| nirmalaa ne kothe para chika kee aada se unhen baithe dekha aur kalejaa thaama kar raha gaee| ek aarogya, yauvana aur pratibhaa kaa devataa tha, doosaraa......is vishaya men kuchha na kahanaa hee theeka hai|

nirmalaa ne doktara saahaba ko saikadon hee baara dekha tha; para aaja usake dil men jo socha uthe, ve kabhee na uthe the| baara-baara yahee jee chaahataa tha ki bulaa kar khooba phatakaaroo.N, aise-aise taane maaroo.N ki vaha
bho yaada karen,rulaa-rulaa kar chhodoo.N magara sahama karake raha jati thi| baaraata janavaase chalee gaee thi| khaanaa kee taiyaaree ho rahi thi| nirmalaa khaanaa ke thaala chunane men vyasta thi, sahasaa maharee ne aakara kahaa-bittee, tumhen sudhaa raanee bulaa rahi hain| tumhaare kamare men baithee hain|

nirmalaa ne thaala chhoda die aur ghabaraaee huee sudhaa ke paasa aaee, magara andara kadama rakhate hee thithaka gaee-doktara sinhaa khade the|

sudhaa ne muskaraa kar kahaa-lo bahina,bulaa diya| aba jitanaa chaaho,phatakaaro| main daravaajaa roke khadee hoo.N,bhaaga naheen sakate|

doktara saahaba ne gambheera bhaava se kahaa-bhaagataa kauna hai yahaa.N to sira jhukaae khadaa hoo.N|

nirmalaa ne haath joda kar kahaa-isee taraha sadaa kripaa-najara rakhiegaa,bhoola na jaaiegaa! yahee meree vinaya hai!!
