# Nirmala — Adhyay 16

**Munshi Premchand**

solahavaan parichchhed

maheenaa katate der na lagee! shaadee kaa shubhamuhoort aa pahunchaa! mehamaanon se ghar bhar gayaa. munshee totaaraam ek din pahale hee aa gae; aur unake saath nirml kee sahelee bhee aaee. nirml ne to bahut aagrah na kiyaa thaa—woh khud hee aane ko bekaraar thee. nirml ko sabase ba. dee utkanthaa yahee thee ki doolhaa ke ba. de bhaaee ke darshan karoongee, aur ho sakaa, to unakee subuddhi par dhanyavaad doongee!

sudhaa ne hans kar kahaa—tum unase bol sakogee?

nirml—kyon, bolane men kyaa nukasaan hai. ab to doosr hee rishtaa ho gayaa. aur main na bol sakoongee, to tum to ho hee!

sudhaa—na bhaaee, mujhase yeh na hogaa. main paraaye mard se naheen bol sakatee. na jaane kaise aadamee hon?

nirml—aadamee to bure naheen hain; aur phir tumhen unase kuchh
shaadee to karn naheen, jr saa bolane men kyaa nukasaan hai? daॉktar saahab yahaan hote to main tunhen hukm dilaa detee!

sudhaa-jo log haday ke uddhaar hote hain, kyaa svabhaav ke bhee achchhe hote hain? paraaee bee ko ghoorane men to kisee mard ko sankoch naheen hotaa.

nirmalaa-achchhaa na boln, main hee baaten kar loongee, ghoor lenge jitn unase ghoorate banegaa; bas, ab to raajee huee.

itane men krishn aakar baith gaee. nirml ne muskr kar kahaa tab battaa krishnaa, teraa man is vakt kyon uchaat ho rahaa hai?

krishnaa-jeejaa jee bulaa rahe hain, pahale jaakar sun aao, peechhe gape la. daanaa. bahut biga. d rahe hain.

nirml kyaa hai, toone kuchh poochhaa naheen?

krishnaa-kuchh beemaar se maaloom hote hain. bahut dubale ho gae hain.

nirmalaa--to jr baith kar unakaa man bahl detee, yahaan dau. dee kyaa chalee aaee. yeh kaho eeshvar ne kripaa kee, naheen to aisaa hee aadamee tujhe bhee milataa! jr baith kar baaten to karo. buddhe vaate ba. dee lachchhedaar karate hain. jabaan itane deegiyal naheen hote!

krishnaa naheen bahin, tum jaao; mujhase do vahaan naheen baithaa jaataa.

nirml chalee gaee, to sudhaa ne krishnaa se kahaa-ab to baaraat aa gaee hogee. dvaara-poojaa kyon naheen hotee?
krishnaa-kyaa jaane vahin, shaastree jee saamaan ikatthaa kar rahe hain.

sudhaa-sunaa hai, doolhaa kee bhaavaj ba. dee ka. de svabhaav kee aurat hai.

krishnaa-kaise maaloom?

sudhaa-main ne sunaa hai, isalie chetaae detee hoon. chaar vaaten Gam khaakar rahn hogaa.

krishnaa-meree jhaga. dane kee aadat hee naheen. jab meree taraph se koee shikaayat hee na paavengee, to kyaa anaayaas hee viga. dengee?

sudhaa- haan, sunaa to aisaa hee hai. jhoothamooth la. daa karatee hain.

krishnaa-main to sau baat kee ek baat jaanatee hoon-namrataa patthar ko bhee mom kar detee hai.

sahasaa shor machaa-vaaraat aa rahee hai. donon ramaniyaan khi. dakee ke saamane aa baithee. ek pal men nirml bhee aa pahunchee.

doolhaa ke ba. de bhaaee ko dekhane kee use va. dee utsukataa ho rahee thee.

sudhaa ne kahaa- kaise pataa chalegaa ki ba. de bhaaee kaun hain?

nirmalaa-shaastree jee se poochh to maaloom ho. haathee par to krishnaa ke sasur mahaashay hain. achchhaa, daॉktar saahab yahaan kaise aa pahunche. woh gho. de par kyaa hain, dekhatee naheen ho?

sudhaa-haan, hain to vahee.

nirmalaa-un logon se mitrataa hogee. koee rishtaa to naheen hai?.

sudhaa-av bhent ho, to poochha. mujhe to kuchh naheen maalooma.
nirmalaa-paalakee men jo mahaashay baithe hue hain, woh to doolhaa ke bhaaee jaise naheen deekhate.

sudhaa-bilakul naheen, maaloom hotaa hai, saaree deh men pet hee pet hai.

nirmalaa-doosare haathee par kaun baithaa huaa hai, samajh men naheen aataa.

sudhaa-koee ho, doolhaa kaa bhaaee naheen ho sakataa. usakee umr naheen dekhatee ho-chaalees ke oopar hogee.

nirmalaa-shaastree jee to is vakt dvaara-poojaa kee phikr men hain, naheen to unase poochhatee.

sanyog se naaee aa gayaa. sandookon kee kunjiyaan nirml hee ke paas theen. is vakt dvaara-chaar ke lie kuchh rupae kee jaroorat thee, maan ne bhejaa thaa. yeh naaee bhee pandit moteraam jee ke saath tilak lekar gayaa thaa. nirml ne kahaa-kyaa abhee rupae chaahie?

naaee-haan vahin jee, chal kar de deejie.

nirmalaa-achchhaa chalatee hoon. pahale yeh vataa, too doolhaa ke ba. de bhaaee ko pahachaanataa hai?

naaee-pahachaanataa kaahe naheen, woh kyaa saamane hain.

nirmalaa-kahaan, main to naheen dekhatee?

naaee-are, woh kyaa gho. de par savaar hain? vahee to hain.

nirml ne chakit hokar kahaa-kyaa kahataa hai, gho. de par doolhaa ke bhaaee hain! pahachaanataa hai yaa atakal se kah rahaa hai?
naaee-are vahin jee, kyaa itn bhool jaaoongaa? abhee to jala-paan kaa saamaan die chl aataa hoon.

nirmalaa-are, yeh to daॉktar saahab hain, mere pa. dos men rahate hain.

naaee-haan haan, vahee to daॉktar saahab hain.

nirml ne sudhaa kee ora dekh kar kahaa-sunatee ho bahin isakee baaten?

sudhaa ne hansee rok kar kahaa-jhooth bolataa hai.

naaee-achchhaa saahab, jhooth hee sahee; ab ba. don ke munh kaun lage. abhee shaastree jee se puchhv doongaa, tab to maaniegaa?

naaee ke aane men der huee, to moteraam Kud aangan men aakar shor machaane lage-is ghar kee maryaad rakhn eeshvar hee ke haath hai. naaee ghante bhar se aayaa huaa hai, aur abhee tak rupae naheen mile.

nirmalaa-jr yahaan chale aaiegaa; shaastree jee? kitane rupae darakaar hain, nikaal doon?

shaastree jee bhunabhunaate aur zora-zor se haanphate hue oopar aae; aur ek lambee saans lekar bole-kyaa hai? yeh baaton kaa samay naheen hai. jaldee se rupae nikaal do.

nirmalaa-leejie, nikaal to rahee hoon. ab kyaa munh ke, val gir pa. den. pahale yeh bataaie ki doolhaa ke ba. de bhaaee kaun hain.

shaastree jee-raama-raam, itanee see baat ke lie mujhe aakaash par latakaa diyaa. naaee kyaa na pahachaanataa thaa?
nirmalaa-naaee to kahataa hai ki woh jo gho. de par savaar hain, vahee hai.

shaastree jee-to phir aur kise bataa de? vahee to hain hee.

naaee-gha. dee bhar se kah rahaa hoon; par bahin jee maanatee hee naheen. nirml ne sudhaa kee ora pyaar, mamataa, vinod aur kritrim beizzatee kee nazar se dekh kar kahaa-achchhaa, to tumhee ab tak mere saath yeh triyaa-svabhaava-khel rahee theen. main jaanatee to tumhen yahaan bulaatee hee naheen. ophkoh! ba. daa gahr pet hai tumhaaraa!! tum maheenon se mere saath yeh sharaarat karatee chalee aatee ho; aur kabhee bhool se bhee is vishay kaa ek shabd tumhaare munh se naheen nikl. main to do-chaar hee din men ubal pa. datee.

sudhaa-tumhen maaloom ho jaataa, to tum mere yahaan aatee hee kyon? '

nirmalaa-gazab re gazab, main daॉktar saahab se kaee baar baaten kar chukee hoon. tumhaare oopar yeh saaraa paap pa. degaa. dekhee krishnaa toone apanee jethaanee kee sharaarat? yeh aisee maayaavinee hain, inase daratee rahn!

krishnaa-main to aisee devee ke charan dho-dhokar maathe cha. dhaaoongee. dhany bhaag ki unake darshan hue?

nirmalaa-ab samajh gaee. rupae bhee tumheen ne bhejavaae honge. ab sir hilaayaa to sach kahatee hoon, maar baithoongee.

sudhaa-apane ghar bulaa kar mehamaan kaa beizzatee naheen kiyaa jaataa.
nirml—dekho to abhee kaisee-kaisee khabaren letee hoon. mainne tumhaaraa maan rakhane ko zr saa likh diyaa thaa; aur tum sachamuch aa pahunchee. bhl vahaan vaale kyaa kahate honge?

sudhaa—sab se kah kar aaee hoon.

nirml—ab tumhaare paas kabhee na aaoongee. itn to ishaaraa kar detee ki daॉktar saahab se paradaa rakhanaa.

sudhaa—unake dekh lene hee se kaun buraaee ho gaee. na dekhate to apanee kismat ko rote kaise? jaanate kaise ki lobh men pa. d kar kaisee cheez khodee! ab to tumhen dekh kar laalaa jee haath mal kar rah jaate hain. munh se to kuchh naheen kahate; par man men apanee bhool par pachhataate hain.

nirml—ab tumhaare ghar kabhee na jaaoongee.

sudhaa—ab pind naheen chhoot sakataa. mainne kaun tumhaare ghar kee raah naheen dekhee hai.

dvaara-poojaa khatm ho chukee thee. mehamaan log baithe jala-paan kar rahe the. munshee totaaraam kee baGal men hee daॉktar sinhaa baithe hue the. nirml ne kothe par chik kee aa. d se unhen baithe dekhaa aur kalejaa thaam kar rah gaee. ek aarogy, yauvan aur pratibhaa kaa devataa thaa, doosaraa...... is vishay men kuchh na kahn hee theek hai.

nirml ne daॉktar saahab ko saika. don hee baar dekhaa thaa; par aaj usake dil men jo soch uthe, ve kabhee na uthe the. baara-baar yahee jee chaahataa thaa ki bulaa kar khoob phatakaaroon, aise-aise taane maaroon ki woh
bho yaad karen, rulaa-rulaa kar chhodoon magar saham karake rah jaatee thee. baaraat janavaase chalee gaee thee. khaanaa kee taiyaaree ho rahee thee. nirml khaanaa ke thaal chunane men vyast thee, sahasaa maharee ne aakar kahaa-bittee, tumhen sudhaa raanee bulaa rahee hain. tumhaare kamare men baithee hain.

nirml ne thaal chho. d die aur ghabaraaee huee sudhaa ke paas aaee, magar andar kadam rakhate hee thithak gaee-dॉktar sinhaa kha. de the.

sudhaa ne muskr kar kahaa-lo bahin, bulaa diyaa. ab jitn chaaho, phatakaaro. main daravaajaa roke kha. dee hoon, bhaag naheen sakate.

daॉktar saahab ne ganbheer bhaav se kahaa-bhaagataa kaun hai yahaan to sir jhukaae kha. daa hoon.

nirml ne haath jo. d kar kahaa-isee tarah sadaa kripaa-nazar rakhiegaa, bhool na jaaiegaa! yahee meree narmee hai!!
