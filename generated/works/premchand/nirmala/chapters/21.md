# Nirmala — Adhyay 21

**Munshi Premchand**

ikkeesavaan parichchheda

rookminee ne nirmalaa se tyoriyaan badala kar kaha-kyaa nange paanva hee madarase jaayaga?

nirmalaa ne bachchi ke baala goonthate hue kaha-main kyaa karoon,mere paasa rupae naheen hain|

rukminee-gahane banavaane ko rupae ju.date hain,ladke ke jooton ke lie rupayon men aaga laga jati hai| do to chale hee gae,kyaa teesare ko bhee rulaa-rulaa kar maara daalane kaa iraadaa hai?

nirmalaa ne ek saansa kheencha kar kaha-jisako jeenaa hai jiega, jisako marana hai marega; main kisee ko maarane-jilaane naheen jati?

aajakala ek na ek baat par nirmalaa aur rukminee men roza hee ek jhapata ho jati thi| jab se gahane choree gae hain, nirmalaa kaa svabhaava bilakula badala gaya hai! vaha ek-ek kau.dee ko daanta se paka.dane lagee hai| siyaaraama rote-rote chaahe jaana de de, magar use mithaaee ke lie paise naheen milate; aur vaha vartaava kuchha siyaaraama hee ke saatha naheen hai,nirmalaa svayan apanee jarooraton ko taalatee rahatee hai| dhotee jab taka phata kar taara-taara na ho jaaya, naee dhotee naheen aati, maheenon sira kaa tela naheen mangaayaa jaata, paana khaane kaa use shauka tha, ab kaee-kaee din taka paanadaana khaalee pada rahata hai, yahaan taka ki bachchi ke lie doodha bhee naheen aata| nanhen se shishu kaa bhavishya viraata roopa dhaarana karake usake -vichaara kshetra par mandaraataa rahata hai|

munshee jee ne apane ko sampoornatah nirmalaa ke haathon men saunpa diya hai| usake kisee kaama men dakhala naheen dete| na jaane kyon usase kuchha dave rahate hain| vaha ab bilaa naagaa kachaharee jaate hain| itanee mehanata unhone javaanee men bhee na kee thi| okhen kharaaba ho gaee hain, daoktara sinhaa ne raat ko likhane-pa.dhane kee mumaaniyata kar dee hai, paachana-shakti pahale hee durvala thi, ab aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee hai, dama kee shikaayata bhee paidaa ho chalee hai; par vechaare sabere se aadhee raat taka kaama karte rahate hain| kaama karane ko jee chaahe yaa na chaahe, tabeeyata achchhi ho yaa na ho, kaama karana hee pa.data hai| nirmalaa ko una par jaraa bhee dayaa naheen aati| vahee bhavishya kee bheeshana chintaa usake aantarika sadbhaaavon kaa sarvanaasha kar rahi hai| kisee bhikshuka kee aavaaja suna kar vaha jhallaa pa.datee hai| vaha ek kau.dee bhee kharcha naheen karana chaahatee|

ek din nirmalaa ne siyaaraama ko ghee laane ke lie baajaara bhejaa| bhungee par usakaa vishvaasa na tha,usase ab koee saudaa na mangaatee thi| siyaaraama men kaata-kapata kee aadata na thi| aane ke paune karana na jaanata tha| praayah baajaara kaa saaraa kaama usee ko karana pa.data| nirmalaa ek-ek cheeza ko tolatee,jaraa bhee koee cheeza tola men kama pa.datee, to use lautaa detee| siyaaraama ka, bahut saa samay isee lautaa-pheree men beeta jata tha| baajaara vaale use jaldee koee saudaa na dete| aaja bhee vahee naubata aaee| siyaaraama apane vichaara se to bahut achchha ghee, kaee dookaana dekha kar laaya;lekin nirmalaa ne use soonghate hee kaha-ghee kharaaba hai| lautaa aao|

siyaaraama ne jhunjhalaa kar kaha-isase achchha ghee baajaara men naheen hai, main saaree dookaanen dekha kar laayaa hoon?

nirmalaa-to main jhootha kehti hoon|

siya-yaha main naheen kahata, lekin baniya ab ghee vaapisa na legaa| usane mujhase kaha tha, jisa tarah dekhana chaaho, yaheen dekho; maala tumhaare saamane hai| bohanee-batte ke vakta main saudaa vaapisa na loongaa| main ne soongha kar,chakha kar dekha liya| ab kisa munha se lautaane jaaoon?

nirmalaa ne daanta peesa kar kaha-ghee men saapha charabee milee hui hai; aur tuma kehte ho ghee achchha hai| main ise rasoee men na le jaaoongee, tumhaaraa jee chaahe lautaa do chaahe khaa jaao|

ghee kee haandee vaheen chho.da kar nirmalaa ghar men chalee gaee| siyaaraama krodha aur kshobha se kaatara ho uthaa| vaha kaun munha lekara lautaane jaaya| baniya saapha kaha degaa-main naheen lautaataa| tab vaha kyaa karega? aasapaasa ke dasa-paancha baniye aur sa.daka par chalane vaale aadmi kha.de ho jaayange| una sabhon ke saamane use lajjita honaa pa.degaa| baajaara men yon hee koee baniya use jalda saudaa naheen deta, vaha kisee dookaana par kha.daa naheen hone paataa| chaaron ora se usee par lata.da pa.degee| usane mann hee mann jhunjhalaa kar kaha--pada rahe ghee, main lautaane na jaaoongaa|

maatri-viheena baalaka ke samaana dukhee, deena praanee sansaara men doosaraa naheen hota| aur saare dukha bhoola jaate hain, baalaka ko maataa kee yaada kabhee naheen bhoolatee| siyaaraama ko is samay maataa kee yaada aaee| ammaan hoti to kyaa aaja mujhe yaha sab sahana pa.data| bhaiyaa bhee chale gae,jiyaaraama bhee chale gae, main hee akelaa yaha vipatti sahane ke lie kyon bacha raha? siyaaraama kee aankhon se aansoo kee jha.dee laga gaee| usake shoka-kaatara kantha se ek gahare nishvaasa ke saatha mile hue ye shabda nikala aae-ammaan! tuma mujhe kyon bhoola gaee, kyon mujhe naheen bulaa leteen|

sahasaa nirmalaa phir kamare kee tarapha aaee| usane samajhaa tha siyaaraama chalaa gaya hogaa| use baithe dekha to gusse se boli-tuma abhee taka baithe hee ho? aakhira khaanaa kab banega?

siyaaraama ne okhen ponchha daalee| bola-mujhe skoola jaane ko, dera ho jaayagee| nirmalaa-ek din dera hee ho jaayagee, to kaun haraja hai ? yaha bhee to ghar hee kaa kaama hai|

siya-roja to yahee dhandhaa lagaa rahata hai | kabhee vakta par naheen pahunchata | ghar par bhee pa.dhane kaa vakta naheen milata| koee saudaa ve do-chaara baara lautaae naheen liya jata | daanta to mujha par pa.datee hai; sharmindaa to mujhe honaa pa.data hai, aapako kyaa ?

nirmalaa-haan, mujhe kyaa ? main to tumhaaree dushmana thaharee apana hota tab to use dukha hota | main to eeshvara se manaayaa karti hoon ki tuma pa.dha-likha na sako| mujhamen to saaree buraaiyaan hee buraaiyaan hain tumhaaraa koee kusoora naheen | vimaataa kaa naama hee buraa hota hai| apanee maan visha bhee khilaae to amrita hai | main amrita bhee pilaaoon to visha ho jaaya | tuma logon ke kaarana mittee men mila gaee, rote-rote umra katee jati hai| maalooma hee na hua ki bhagavaana ne kisa lie janma diya tha; aur tumhaaree samajha men main bihaara kar rahi hoon| tumhen sataane se mujhe mazaa aata hai | bhagavaan bhee naheen poochhate ki saaree vipatti kaa anta ho jata|

yaha kehte-kehte nirmalaa ko aankhen bhara aaee| andara chalee gaee | siyaaraama usako rote dekha kar sahama uthaa | use glaani to naheen aaee; haan, yaha shankaa hui ki na jaane kaunasaa danda mile| chupake se haandee uthaa lee; aur ghee lautaane chala, is tarah jaise koee kuttaa kisee nae gaon men jata hai| usee kutte kee bhaanti usakee manogata vedana usake ek-ek bhaaga se prakata ho rahi thi| use dekha kar saadhaarana buddhi kaa manushya bhee anumaana kar sakata tha ki yaha anaatha hai|

siyaaraama jyon-jyon aage ba.dhata tha,aane vaale sangraama ke bhaya se usakee hridaya-gati ba.dhatee jati thi| usane nishchaya kiya-baniye ne ghee na lautaaya,to vahaan;ghee vaheen chho.da kar chalaa aaegaa| makhamaara kar vaniya aapa hee bulaavegaa| baniye ko daatane ke lie bhee usane shabda socha lie| vaha kahegaa-kyon saaha jee, aankhon men dhoola jhonkate ho? dikhaate ho chokhaa maala aur dete ho raddee maala? par yaha nishchaya karane par bhee usake pair aage bahut dheere-dheere uthate the| vaha yaha na chaahata tha ki baniya use aata hua dekhe, vaha akasmaata hee usake saamane pahuncha jana chaahata tha| isalie vaha chakkara kaata kar |doosaree galee se baniye kee dookaana par gaya|

baniye ne use dekhate hee kaha-hamane kaha diya tha, hama saudaa vaapisa na lenge| bolo kaha tha ki naheen?

siyaaraama ne biga.da kar kaha-tumane vaha ghee kahaan diya, jo dikhaayaa tha? dikhaayaa ek maala, diya doosaraa maala; lautaaoge kaise naheen? kyaa kuchha raahajanee hai?

saaha-isase chokhaa ghee baajaara men nikala aave,to jareebaanaa doon| uthaa lo haandee aur do-chaara dookaana dekha aao|

siya-hamen itanee phursata naheen hai| apana ghee lautaa lo|

saaha-ghee na lautegaa|

baniye kee dookaana par ek jataadhaaree saadhu baithaa hua yaha tamaashaa dekha raha tha| utha kar siyaaraama ke paasa aaya;aur haandee kaa ghee soongha kar bola-bachcha,dhee to bahut achchha maalooma hota hai|

saaha ne shaha paakara kaha-baabaajee,hama log to aapahee inako ghatiya saudaa naheen dete| kharaaba maala kyaa jaane-sune gaahakon ko diya jata hai?

saadhu-ghee le jaava bachcha, bahut achchha hai|

siyaaraama ro pada| ghee ko buraa siddha karane ke lie usake paasa aka kyaa pramaana tha? bola-vahee to kehti hain ghee achchha naheen hai; lautaa aao| main to kahata tha ghee achchha hai|

saadhu-kaun kahata hai?

saaha-inakee ammaan kehti hongee| koee saudaa unake mann hee naheen bhaataa| bechaare ladke ko baara-baara dau.daayaa karti hain! sautelee maan hain na! apanee maan ho to kuchha khayal bhee kare| saadhu ne siyaaraama ko sadaya netron se dekha; maano use traana dene ke lie usakaa hridaya vikala ho raha hai| tab karuna svara men bola-tumhaaree maataa kaa svargavaasa hue kitane din hue bachcha?

siyaaraama-chhathaa saala hai|

saadhu-tab to tuma us vakta bahut hee chhote rahe hoge| bhagavaan tumhaaree leelaa kitanee vichitra hai| is dudhamunhe baalaka ko tumane maatri-prema se vanchita kar diya| bada anartha karte ho;bhagavaan! ha,chhah saala kaa baalaka aur raakshasee vimaataa ke paale pa.de!dhanya ho dayaanidhi! saaha jee,baalaka par dayaa karo-dhee lautaa lo;naheen to isakee maataa ise ghar men rahane na degee| bhagavaan kee ichchhaa se tumhaaraa dhee jalda vika jaayagaa| meraa aasheervaada tumhaare saatha rahegaa|

saaha jee ne rupae na vaapasa kie | aakhira ladke ko phir ghee lene aana hee pa.degaa | na jaane din men kitanee baara chakara lagaanaa pa.de aur kisa jaalie se paalaa pa.de| usakee dookaana men jo ghee sabase achchha tha, vaha usane siyaaraama ko diya | siyaaraama dil men socha raha tha, baabaa jee kitane dayaalu hain| inhonne na siphaarisha kee hoti, to saaha jee kyon achchha ghee dete |

siyaaraama ghee lekara chalaa to baabaa jee bhee usake saatha ho lie | raaste men meethee-meethee baaten karane lage :

bachcha, meree maataa bhee mujhe teena saala kaa chho.da kar paraloka sidhaaree thi| tabhee se maatri-viheena baalakon ko dekhata hoon, to meraa hridaya phatane lagata hai|

siyaaraama ne poochhaa-aapake pitaa jee ne bhee doosaraa vivaaha kar liya tha ?

saadhu-haan bachcha, naheen to aaja saadhu kyon hota | pahale to pitaa jee vivaaha na karte the| mujhe bahut pyaar karte the| phir na jaane kyon mann badala gaya-vivaaha kar liya | saadhu hoon, katu vachana munha se naheen nikaalana chaahie; par meree vimaataa jitanee hee sundaree thi, utanee hee kathora thi| mujhe din-din bhara khaane ko na detee, rotaa to maaratee | pitaa jee kee aankhen bhee phir gaee | unhen meree soorata se ghrinaa hone lagee| meraa ronaa suna kar mujhe peetane lagate| anta ko main ek din ghar se nikala kha.daa hua|

siyaaraama ke mann men bhee ghar se nikala bhaagane kaa vichaara kaee baara hua tha| is samay bhee usake mann men yahee vichaara utha raha tha| ba.dee utsukata se bola-ghar se nikala kar aapa kahaan gae?

baabaa jee ne hansa kar kaha-usee din mere saare kashton kaa anta ho gaya| jisa din ghar ke moha-bandhana se chhoota; aur bhaya mann se nikala,usee din maano meraa uddhaara ho gaya| din bhara to main ek pula ke neeche baithaa raha| sandhyaa samay mujhe ek mahaatmaa mila gae| unakaa naama svaamee paramaananda jee tha| ve baala-brahmachaaree the| mujha par unhonne dayaa kee, aur apane saatha rakha liya| unake saatha main desha-deshaantaron men ghoomane lagaa| vaha ba.de achchhe yogee the| mujhe bhee unhonne yoga-vidyaa sikhaaee| ab to mere ko itana abhyaasa ho gaya hai ki jab ichchhaa hoti hai, maataa jee ke darshana kar letaa hoon| unase baaten kar letaa hoon|

siyaaraama ne vispharita netron se dekha kar poochhaa-aapakee maataa kaa to dehaanta ho chukaa tha?

saadhu-to kyaa hua bachcha, yoga-vidyaa men yaha shakti hai ki jisa mrita-aatmaa ko chaahe bulaa le|

siyaao-main yoga-vidyaa seekha loon, to mujhe bhee maataa jee ke darshana honge? saadhu-avashya! abhyaasa se sab kuchha ho sakata hai| hao, yogya guru chaahie| yoga se ba.dee-ba.dee siddhiyaan praapta ho sakatee hain| jitana dhana chaaho,palamaatra men ma.cgaa sakate ho| kaisi hee beemaaree ho,usakee aushadhi bata sakate ho|

siyaa0-aapakaa sthaana kahaan hai?

saadhu-bachcha,mere ko sthaana kaheen naheen hai| desha-deshaantaron men ramata phirata hoon| achchha bachcha,ava tuma jaava,main jaraa snaana-dhyaana karane jaaoongaa|

siya-chalie main bhee usee tarapha chalata hoon| aapake darshanon se jee naheen bharaa|

siya-phir aapake darshana kab honge?

saadhu-kabhee aa jaaoongaa bachcha,tumhaaraa ghar kahaan hai?

siyaaraama prasanna hokara bola-chaliegaa mere ghar;bahut nazadeeka hai| aapakee ba.dee kripaa hogee|

siyaaraama kadama ba.dhaa kar aage-aage chalane lagaa| itana prasanna tha, maano sone kee gatharee lie jata ho| ghar ke saamane pahuncha kar bola-aaie,baithie kuchha dera |

saadhu-naheen bachcha,baithoongaa naheen|phir kala-parason kisee samay aa jaaoonga,yahee tumhaaraa ghar hai?

siyaa0-kale kisa vaktaaiegaa|

saadhu-nishchaya naheen kaha sakata| kisee samay aa jaaoongaa| saadhu aage ba.dhe to tho.dee hee doora par unhen ek doosaraa saadhu milaa| isakaa naama tha hariharaananda|

paramaananda ne poochhaa-kahaan-kahaan.kee shaira kee? koee shikaara phansa?

hariharaananda-idhara to chaaron tarapha ghooma aaya,koee shikaara na milaa| ekaadha milaa bhee to meree hansee u.daane lagaa|

paramaananda-mujhe to ek milata hua jaana pa.data hai| phansa jaaya to jaanen|

hariharaananda-tuma yonhee kaha karte ho| jo aata hai,do-ek din ke vaada nikala bhaagata hai|

paramaananda-abakee bhaagega,dekha lena| isakee maan mara gaee hai| baapa ne doosaraa vivaaha kar liya hai| maan bhee sataayaa karti hai| ghar se oobaa hua hai|

hariharaananda-haan,yaha maamalaa hai to avashya phansegaa| laasaa lagaa diya hai na?

paramaananda-khooba achchhi tarah| yahee tarakeeba sabase achchhi hai|pahale isakaa pata lagaa lena chaahie ki muhalle men kina-kina gharon men vimaataaen hain| unheen gharon men phandaa daalana chaahie|
