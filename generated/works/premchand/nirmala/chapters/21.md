rookmiNee ne nirmalaa se tyoriyaa.N badala kar kahaa-kyaa nange paa.Nva hee madarase jaayagaa?

rukmiNee-gahane banavaane ko rupae judate hain,ladake ke jooton ke lie rupayon men aaga laga jati hai| do to chale hee gae,kyaa teesare ko bhee rulaa-rulaa kar maara daalane kaa iraadaa hai?

nirmalaa ne ek saa.Nsa kheencha kar kahaa-jisako jeenaa hai jiegaa, jisako maranaa hai maregaa; main kisee ko maarane-jilaane naheen jati?

aajakala ek na ek baat para nirmalaa aur rukmiNee men roja hee ek jhapata ho jati thi| jaba se gahane choree gae hain, nirmalaa kaa svabhaava bilakula badala gaya hai! vaha ek-ek kaudee ko daa.Nta se pakadane lagee hai| siyaaraama rote-rote chaahe jaana de de,
magara use mithaaee ke lie paise naheen milate; aur vaha vartaava kuchha siyaaraama hee ke saatha naheen hai,nirmalaa svayan apanee jarooraton ko taalatee rahatee hai| dhotee jaba taka phata kar taara-taara na ho jaaya, naee dhotee naheen aatee, maheenon sira kaa tela naheen ma.Ngaayaa jata, paana khaane kaa use shauka tha, aba kaee-kaee din taka paanadaana khaalee padaa rahataa hai, yahaa.N taka ki bachchee ke lie doodha bhee naheen aata| nanhen se bachchaa kaa bhavishya viraata roopa dhaaraNa karake usake -socha kshetra para ma.Ndaraataa rahataa hai|

munshee jee ne apane ko sampoorNatah nirmalaa ke haathon men saunpa diya hai| usake kisee kaama men dakhala naheen dete| na jaane kyon usase kuchha dave rahate hain| vaha aba bilaa naagaa kachaharee jaate hain| itanee mehanata unhone javaanee men bhee na kee thi| okhen kharaaba ho gaee hain, doktara sinhaa ne raat ko likhane-padhane kee mumaaniyata kar dee hai, paachana-shakti pahale hee durvala thi, aba aur bhee kharaaba ho gaee hai, dama kee shikaayata bhee paidaa ho chalee hai; para vechaare sabere se aadhee raat taka kaama karte rahate hain| kaama karane ko jee chaahe yaa na chaahe, tabeeyata achchhee ho yaa na ho, kaama karna hee padataa hai| nirmalaa ko una para jaraa bhee dayaa naheen aatee| vahee bhavishya kee bheeshaNa chintaa usake aantarika sadbhaavon kaa sarvanaasha kar rahi hai| kisee bhikshuka kee aavaaja suna kar vaha jhallaa padatee hai| vaha ek kaudee bhee kharcha naheen karna chaahatee|

ek din nirmalaa ne siyaaraama ko ghee laane ke lie baajaara
bhejaa| bhungee para usakaa yakeena na tha,usase aba koee saudaa na ma.Ngaatee thi| siyaaraama men kaata-kapata kee aadata na thi| aane ke paune karna na jaanataa tha| praayah baajaara kaa saaraa kaama usee ko karna padataa| nirmalaa ek-ek cheeja ko tolatee,jaraa bhee koee cheeja tola men kama padatee, to use lautaa detee| siyaaraama kaa, bahuta saa samaya isee lautaa-pheree men beeta jata tha| baajaara vaale use jaldee koee saudaa na dete| aaja bhee vahee naubata aaee| siyaaraama apane socha se to bahuta achchhaa ghee, kaee dookaana dekha kar laayaa;lekin nirmalaa ne use soo.Nghate hee kahaa-ghee kharaaba hai| lautaa aao|

siyaaraama ne jhu.Njhalaa kar kahaa-isase achchhaa ghee baajaara men naheen hai, main saaree dookaanen dekha kar laayaa hoo.N?

nirmalaa-to main jhootha kehti hoo.N|

siyaa-yaha main naheen kehta, lekin baniyaa aba ghee vaapisa na legaa| usane mujhase kahaa tha, jisa taraha dekhanaa chaaho, yaheen dekho; maala tumhaare saamane hai| bohanee-batte ke vakta main saudaa vaapisa na loongaa| main ne soongha kar,chakha kar dekha liya| aba kisa mu.Nha se lautaane jaaoo.N?

nirmalaa ne daa.Nta peesa kar kahaa-ghee men saapha charabee milee huee hai; aur tuma kehte ho ghee achchhaa hai| main ise rasoee men na le jaaoo.Ngee, tumhaaraa jee chaahe lautaa do chaahe khaa jaao|

ghee kee haa.Ndee vaheen chhoda kar nirmalaa ghar men chalee gaee| siyaaraama
gussaa aur kshobha se kaatara ho uthaa| vaha kauna mu.Nha lekara lautaane jaaya| baniyaa saapha kaha degaa-main naheen lautaataa| taba vaha kyaa karegaa? aasapaasa ke dasa-paa.Ncha baniye aur sadaka para chalane vaale aadamee khade ho jaaya.Nge| una sabhon ke saamane use lajjita honaa padegaa| baajaara men yon hee koee baniyaa use jalda saudaa naheen detaa, vaha kisee dookaana para khadaa naheen hone paataa| chaaron ora se usee para lataada padegee| usane mana hee mana jhu.Njhalaa kar kahaa--padaa rahe ghee, main lautaane na jaaoo.Ngaa|

maatri-viheena ladakaa ke samaana dukhee, deena praaNee sansaara men doosaraa naheen hota| aur saare dukha bhoola jaate hain, ladakaa ko maa.N kee yaada kabhee naheen bhoolatee| siyaaraama ko is samaya maa.N kee yaada aaee| ammaa.N hoti to kyaa aaja mujhe yaha sab sahanaa padataa| bhaiyaa bhee chale gae,jiyaaraama bhee chale gae, main hee akelaa yaha vipatti sahane ke lie kyon bacha raha? siyaaraama kee aa.Nkhon se aa.Nsoo kee jhadee laga gaee| usake shoka-kaatara galaa se ek gahare nishvaasa ke saatha mile hue ye shabda nikala aae-ammaa.N! tuma mujhe kyon bhoola gaee, kyon mujhe naheen bulaa leteen|

sahasaa nirmalaa phir kamare kee tarapha aaee| usane samajhaa tha siyaaraama chalaa gaya hogaa| use baithe dekha to gusse se bolee-tuma abhee taka baithe hee ho? aakhira khaanaa kaba banegaa?

siyaaraama ne okhen ponchha daalee| bola-mujhe skoola jaane ko, dera ho jaayagee|
nirmalaa-ek din dera hee ho jaayagee, to kauna haraja hai ? yaha bhee to ghar hee kaa kaama hai|

siyaa-roja to yahee dhandhaa lagaa rahataa hai | kabhee vakta para naheen pahu.Nchataa | ghar para bhee padhane kaa vakta naheen milataa| koee saudaa ve do-chaara baara lautaae naheen liya jata | daa.Nta to mujha para padatee hai; sharmindaa to mujhe honaa padataa hai, aapako kyaa ?

nirmalaa-haa.N, mujhe kyaa ? main to tumhaaree dushmana thaharee apanaa hota taba to use dukha hota | main to eeshvara se manaayaa karti hoo.N ki tuma padha-likha na sako| mujhamen to saaree buraaiyaa.N hee buraaiyaa.N hain tumhaaraa koee kusoora naheen | vimaataa kaa naama hee buraa hota hai| apanee maa.N visha bhee khilaae to amrita hai | main amrita bhee pilaaoo.N to visha ho jaaya | tuma logon ke vajaha mittee men mila gaee, rote-rote umra katee jati hai| maalooma hee na hua ki bhagavaana ne kisa lie janma diya tha; aur tumhaaree samajha men main bihaara kar rahi hoo.N| tumhen sataane se mujhe majaa aata hai | bhagavaan bhee naheen poochhate ki saaree vipatti kaa anta ho jata|

yaha kehte-kehte nirmalaa ko aa.Nkhen bhara aaee| andara chalee gaee | siyaaraama usako rote dekha kar sahama uthaa | use pachhataavaa to naheen aaee; haa.N, yaha shankaa huee ki na jaane kaunasaa daNda mile| chupake se haa.Ndee uthaa lee; aur ghee lautaane chalaa, is taraha jaise koee kuttaa kisee nae gaa.Nva men jata hai| usee kutte kee taraha usakee manogata darda usake ek-ek bhaaga se prakata ho rahi
thi| use dekha kar aama akala kaa manushya bhee anumaana kar sakataa tha ki yaha anaatha hai|

siyaaraama jyon-jyon aage badhataa tha,aane vaale sangraama ke dara se usakee dil-gati badhatee jati thi| usane phaisalaa kiya-baniye ne ghee na lautaayaa,to vahaan;ghee vaheen chhoda kar chalaa aaegaa| makhamaara kar vaniyaa aapa hee bulaavegaa| baniye ko daatane ke lie bhee usane shabda socha lie| vaha kahegaa-kyon saaha jee, aa.Nkhon men dhoola jhonkate ho? dikhaate ho chokhaa maala aur dete ho raddee maala? para yaha phaisalaa karane para bhee usake pair aage bahuta dheere-dheere uthate the| vaha yaha na chaahataa tha ki baniyaa use aata hua dekhe, vaha akasmaata hee usake saamane pahu.Ncha jana chaahataa tha| isalie vaha chakkara kaata kar |doosaree galee se baniye kee dookaana para gaya|

baniye ne use dekhate hee kahaa-hamane kaha diya tha, hama saudaa vaapisa na lenge| bolo kahaa tha ki naheen?

siyaaraama ne bigada kar kahaa-tumane vaha ghee kahaa.N diya, jo dikhaayaa tha? dikhaayaa ek maala, diya doosaraa maala; lautaaoge kaise naheen? kyaa kuchha raahajanee hai?

saaha-isase chokhaa ghee baajaara men nikala aave,to jareebaanaa doon| uthaa lo haa.Ndee aur do-chaara dookaana dekha aao|

dekha raha tha| utha kar siyaaraama ke paasa aaya;aur haa.Ndee kaa ghee soongha kar bola-bachchaa,dhee to bahuta achchhaa maalooma hota hai|

saaha ne shaha paakara kahaa-baabaajee,hama log to aapahee inako ghatiyaa saudaa naheen dete| kharaaba maala kyaa jaane-sune gaahakon ko diya jata hai?
