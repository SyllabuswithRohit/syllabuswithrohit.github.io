# Nirmala — Adhyay 21

**Munshi Premchand**

ikkeesavaan parichchhed

rookminee ne Nirmala se tyoriyaan badal kar kahaa-kyaa nanggge paanv hee madarase jaayagaa?

Nirmala ne bachchi ke baal goonthate hue kahaa-main kyaa karoon, mere paas rupaye nahin hain.

rukminee-gahane banavaane ko rupaye judate hain, ladake ke jooton ke lie rupayon mein aag lag jaatee hai. do to chale hee gae, kyaa teesare ko bhi rulaa-rulaa kar maar daalane ka iraadaa hai?

Nirmala ne ek saans kheench kar kahaa-jisako jeenaa hai jiegaa, jisako maranaa hai maregaa; main kisee ko maarane-jilaane nahin jaatee?

aajakal ek n ek baat par Nirmala aur rukminee mein roj hee ek jhapat ho jaatee thee. jab se gahane choree gae hain, Nirmala ka svabhaav bilakul badal gayaa hai! vah ek-ek kaudee ko daant se pakadane lagee hai. siyaaraam rote-rote chaahe jaan de de, magar use mithaaee ke lie paise nahin milate; aur vah vartaav kuchh siyaaraam hee ke saath nahin hai, Nirmala svayan apanee jarooraton ko taalatee rahatee hai. dhotee jab tak phat kar taar-taar n ho jaay, naee dhotee nahin aatee, maheenon sir ka tel nahin manggaayaa jaataa, paan khaane ka use shauk tha, ab kaee-kaee din tak paanadaan khaalee padaa rahataa hai, yahan tak ki bachchi ke lie doodh bhi nahin aataa. nanhen se shishu ka bhavishy viraat roop dhaaran karake usake -soch ilaakaa par mandaraataa rahataa hai.

munshee jee ne apane ko pooraatah Nirmala ke haathon mein saump diyaa hai. usake kisee kaam mein dakhal nahin dete. n jaane kyon usase kuchh dave rahate hain. vah ab bilaa naagaa kachaharee jaate hain. itanee mehanat unhone javaanee mein bhi n ki thee. okhen kharaab ho gaee hain, doktar sinhaa ne raat ko likhane-padhane ki mumaaniyat kar dee hai, paachan-taakat pahale hee durval thi, ab aur bhi kharaab ho gaee hai, dam ki shikaayat bhi paidaa ho chalee hai; par vechaare sabere se aadhee raat tak kaam karate rahate hain. kaam karane ko jee chaahe ya n chaahe, tabeeyat achchhi ho ya n ho, kaam karanaa hee padataa hai. Nirmala ko un par jaraa bhi taras nahin aatee. vahee bhavishy ki bheeshan fikr usake aantarik sadbhaavon ka sarvanaash kar rahee hai. kisee bhikshuk ki aavaaj sun kar vah jhallaa padatee hai. vah ek kaudee bhi kharch nahin karanaa chaahatee.

ek din Nirmala ne siyaaraam ko ghee laane ke lie baajaar bhejaa. bhungggee par usakaa bharosaa n tha, usase ab koee saudaa n manggaatee thee. siyaaraam mein kaat-kapat ki aadat n thee. aane ke paune karanaa n jaanataa thaa. praayah baajaar ka saaraa kaam usee ko karanaa padataa. Nirmala ek-ek cheej ko tolatee, jaraa bhi koee cheej tol mein kam padatee, to use lautaa detee. siyaaraam ka, bahut saa samay isee lautaa-pheree mein beet jaataa thaa. baajaar vaale use jaldee koee saudaa n dete. aaj bhi vahee naubat aaee. siyaaraam apane soch se to bahut achchha ghee, kaee dookaan dekh kar laayaa; lekin Nirmala ne use soongghate hee kahaa-ghee kharaab hai. lautaa aao.

siyaaraam ne jhunjhalaa kar kahaa-isase achchha ghee baajaar mein nahin hai, main saaree dookaanen dekh kar laayaa hoon?

Nirmala-to main jhooth kahatee hoon.

siyaa-yah main nahin kahataa, lekin baniyaa ab ghee vaapis n legaa. usane mujhase kahaa tha, jis tarah dekhanaa chaaho, yaheen dekho; maal tumhaare saamane hai. bohanee-batte ke vakt main saudaa vaapis n loonggaa. main ne soonggh kar, chakh kar dekh liyaa. ab kis munh se lautaane jaaoon?

Nirmala ne daant pees kar kahaa-ghee mein saaph charabee milee huee hai; aur tum kahate ho ghee achchha hai. main ise rasoee mein n le jaaoonggee, tumhaaraa jee chaahe lautaa do chaahe khaa jaao.

ghee ki haandee vaheen chhod kar Nirmala ghar mein chalee gaee. siyaaraam krodh aur kshobh se kaatar ho uthaa. vah kaun munh lekar lautaane jaaya. baniyaa saaph kah degaa-main nahin lautaataa. tab vah kyaa karegaa? aasapaas ke das-paanch baniye aur sadak par chalane vaale aadmi khade ho jaayangge. un sabhon ke saamane use lajjit honaa padegaa. baajaar mein yon hee koee baniyaa use jald saudaa nahin detaa, vah kisee dookaan par khadaa nahin hone paataa. chaaron or se usee par lataad padegee. usane man hee man jhunjhalaa kar kahaa--padaa rahe ghee, main lautaane n jaaoonggaa.

maatri-viheen bachcha ke samaan dukhee, deen praanee sansaar mein doosaraa nahin hotaa. aur saare dukh bhool jaate hain, bachcha ko maataa ki yaad kabhee nahin bhoolatee. siyaaraam ko is samay maataa ki yaad aaee. ammaan hotee to kyaa aaj mujhe yah sab sahanaa padataa. bhaiyaa bhi chale gae, jiyaaraam bhi chale gae, main hee akelaa yah vipatti sahane ke lie kyon bach rahaa? siyaaraam ki aangkhon se aansoo ki jhadee lag gaee. usake shok-kaatar kanth se ek gahare nishvaas ke saath mile hue ye shabd nikal aae-ammaan! tum mujhe kyon bhool gaee, kyon mujhe nahin bulaa leteen.

sahasaa Nirmala phir kamare ki taraph aaee. usane samajhaa tha siyaaraam chalaa gayaa hogaa. use baithe dekhaa to gusse se bolee-tum abhee tak baithe hee ho? aakhir khana kab banegaa?

siyaaraam ne okhen ponchh daalee. bolaa-mujhe skool jaane ko, der ho jaayagee. Nirmala-ek din der hee ho jaayagee, to kaun haraj hai? yah bhi to ghar hee ka kaam hai.

siyaa-roj to yahee dhandhaa lagaa rahataa hai. kabhee vakt par nahin pahunchataa. ghar par bhi padhane ka vakt nahin milataa. koee saudaa ve do-chaar baar lautaae nahin liyaa jaataa. daant to mujh par padatee hai; sharmindaa to mujhe honaa padataa hai, aapko kyaa?

Nirmala-haan, mujhe kyaa? main to tumhaaree dushman thaharee apanaa hotaa tab to use dukh hotaa. main to Bhagwan se manaayaa karatee hoon ki tum padh-likh n sako. mujhamen to saaree buraaiyaan hee buraaiyaan hain tumhaaraa koee kusoor nahin. vimaataa ka naam hee buraa hotaa hai. apanee maan vish bhi khilaae to amaraa huaa hai. main amaraa huaa bhi pilaaoon to vish ho jaay. tum logon ke vajah mittee mein mil gaee, rote-rote umr katee jaatee hai. maaloom hee n huaa ki Bhagwan ne kis lie paidaa honaa diyaa tha; aur tumhaaree samajh mein main bihaar kar rahee hoon. tumhe sataane se mujhe majaa aataa hai. Bhagwan bhi nahin poochhate ki saaree vipatti ka ant ho jaataa.

yah kahate-kahate Nirmala ko aankhen bhar aaee. andar chalee gaee. siyaaraam usako rote dekh kar saham uthaa. use glaani to nahin aaee; haan, yah shanggkaa huee ki n jaane kaunasaa sajaa mile. chupake se haandee uthaa lee; aur ghee lautaane chalaa, is tarah jaise koee kuttaa kisee nae gaon mein jaataa hai. usee kutte ki bhaanti usakee manogat dard usake ek-ek bhaag se saamane ho rahee thee. use dekh kar aam buddhi ka insaan bhi anumaan kar sakataa tha ki yah anaath hai.

siyaaraam jyon-jyon aage badhataa tha, aane vaale sanggaanv ke dar se usakee dil-gati badhatee jaatee thee. usane pakkaa faisla kiyaa-baniye ne ghee n lautaayaa, to wahan; ghee vaheen chhod kar chalaa aaegaa. makhamaar kar vaniyaa aap hee bulaavegaa. baniye ko daatane ke lie bhi usane shabd soch lie. vah kahegaa-kyon saah jee, aangkhon mein dhool jhongkate ho? dikhaate ho chokhaa maal aur dete ho raddee maal? par yah pakkaa faisla karane par bhi usake pair aage bahut dheere-dheere uthate the. vah yah n chaahataa tha ki baniyaa use aataa huaa dekhe, vah akasmaat hee usake saamane pahunch jaanaa chaahataa thaa. isalie vah chakkar kaat kar. doosaree galee se baniye ki dookaan par gayaa.

baniye ne use dekhate hee kahaa-hamane kah diyaa tha, ham saudaa vaapis n lengge. bolo kahaa tha ki nahin?

siyaaraam ne bigad kar kahaa-tumane vah ghee kahaan diyaa, jo dikhaayaa tha? dikhaayaa ek maal, diyaa doosaraa maal; lautaaoge kaise nahin? kyaa kuchh raahajanee hai?

saah-isase chokhaa ghee baajaar mein nikal aave, to jareebaanaa doon. uthaa lo haandee aur do-chaar dookaan dekh aao.

siyaa-hamen itanee phursat nahin hai. apanaa ghee lautaa lo.

saah-ghee n lautegaa.

baniye ki dookaan par ek jataadhaaree saadhu baithaa huaa yah tamaashaa dekh rahaa thaa. uth kar siyaaraam ke paas kamaaeeaa; aur haandee ka ghee soonggh kar bolaa-bachcha, dhee to bahut achchha maaloom hotaa hai.

saah ne shah paakar kahaa-baabaajee, ham log to aapahee inako ghatiyaa saudaa nahin dete. kharaab maal kyaa jaane-sune gaahakon ko diyaa jaataa hai?

saadhu-ghee le jaav bachcha, bahut achchha hai.

siyaaraam ro padaa. ghee ko buraa siddh karane ke lie usake paas ak kyaa saboot tha? bolaa-vahee to kahatee hain ghee achchha nahin hai; lautaa aao. main to kahataa tha ghee achchha hai.

saadhu-kaun kahataa hai?

saah-inakee ammaan kahatee honggee. koee saudaa unake man hee nahin bhaataa. bechaare ladake ko baar-baar daudaayaa karatee hain! sautelee maan hain n! apanee maan ho to kuchh khyaal bhi kare. saadhu ne siyaaraam ko saday aangkhenon se dekhaa; maano use traan dene ke lie usakaa dil vikal ho rahaa hai. tab karun svar mein bolaa-tumhaaree maataa ka svargavaas hue kitane din hue bachcha?

siyaaraam-chhathaa saal hai.

saadhu-tab to tum us vakt bahut hee chhote rahe hoge. Bhagwan tumhaaree leelaa kitanee ajeeb hai. is dudhamunhe bachcha ko tumane maatri-pyaar se vanychit kar diyaa. badaa anarth karate ho; Bhagwan! haa, chhah saal ka bachcha aur raakshsee vimaataa ke paale pade! dhany ho tarasanidhi! saah jee, bachcha par taras karo-dhee lautaa lo; nahin to isakee maataa ise ghar mein rahane n degee. Bhagwan ki ichchhaa se tumhaaraa dhee jald vik jaayagaa. meraa aasheervaad tumhaare saath rahegaa.

saah jee ne rupaye n vaapas kie. aakhir ladake ko phir ghee lene aanaa hee padegaa. n jaane din mein kitanee baar chakar lagaanaa pade aur kis jaalie se paalaa pade. usakee dookaan mein jo ghee sabase achchha tha, vah usane siyaaraam ko diyaa. siyaaraam dil mein soch rahaa tha, baabaa jee kitane tarasalu hain. inhonne n siphaarish ki hotee, to saah jee kyon achchha ghee dete.

siyaaraam ghee lekar chalaa to baabaa jee bhi usake saath ho lie. raaste mein meethee-meethee baaten karane lage:

bachcha, meree maataa bhi mujhe teen saal ka chhod kar paralok sidhaaree thee. tabhee se maatri-viheen bachchaaon ko dekhataa hoon, to meraa dil phatane lagataa hai.

siyaaraam ne poochhaa-aapake pitaa jee ne bhi doosaraa shaadee kar liyaa tha?

saadhu-haan bachcha, nahin to aaj saadhu kyon hotaa. pahale to pitaa jee shaadee n karate the. mujhe bahut pyaar karate the. phir n jaane kyon man badal gayaa-shaadee kar liyaa. saadhu hoon, katu vachan munh se nahin nikaalanaa chahiye; par meree vimaataa jitanee hee sundaree thi, utanee hee sakht thee. mujhe din-din bhar khaane ko n detee, rotaa to maaratee. pitaa jee ki aankhen bhi phir gaee. unhen meree soorat se nafrat hone lagee. meraa ronaa sun kar mujhe peetane lagate. ant ko main ek din ghar se nikal khadaa huaa.

siyaaraam ke man mein bhi ghar se nikal bhaagane ka soch kaee baar huaa thaa. is samay bhi usake man mein yahee soch uth rahaa thaa. badee utsukataa se bolaa-ghar se nikal kar aap kahaan gae?

baabaa jee ne hans kar kahaa-usee din mere saare takaleephon ka ant ho gayaa. jis din ghar ke moh-bandhan se chhootaa; aur dar man se nikalaa, usee din maano meraa uddhaar ho gayaa. din bhar to main ek pul ke neeche baithaa rahaa. sandhyaa samay mujhe ek mahaatmaa mil gae. unakaa naam svaamee paramaanand jee thaa. ve baal-brahmachaaree the. mujh par unhonne taras ki, aur apane saath rakh liyaa. unake saath main desh-deshaantaron mein ghoomane lagaa. vah bade achchhe yogee the. mujhe bhi unhonne yog-vidyaa sikhaaee. ab to mere ko itanaa abhyaas ho gayaa hai ki jab ichchhaa hotee hai, maataa jee ke darshan kar letaa hoon. unase baaten kar letaa hoon.

siyaaraam ne vispharit aangkhenon se dekh kar poochhaa-aapakee maataa ka to dehaant ho chukaa tha?

saadhu-to kyaa huaa bachcha, yog-vidyaa mein yah taakat hai ki jis maraa huaa-aatmaa ko chaahe bulaa le.

siyaao-main yog-vidyaa seekh loon, to mujhe bhi maataa jee ke darshan hongge? saadhu-zaroor! abhyaas se sab kuchh ho sakataa hai. ho, yogy guru chaahie. yog se badee-badee siddhiyaan milaa ho sakatee hain. jitanaa dhan chaaho, palamaatr mein megaa sakate ho. kaisee hee beemaaree ho, usakee davaa bataa sakate ho.

siyaa0-aapakaa jagah kahaan hai?

saadhu-bachcha, mere ko jagah kaheen nahin hai. desh-deshaantaron mein ramataa phirataa hoon. achchha bachcha, av tum jaav, main jaraa snaan-dhyaan karane jaaoonggaa.

siyaa-chalie main bhi usee taraph chalataa hoon. aapake darshanon se jee nahin bharaa.

siyaa-phir aapake darshan kab hongge?

saadhu-kabhee aa jaaoonggaa bachcha, tumhaaraa ghar kahaan hai?

siyaaraam khush hokar bolaa-chaliegaa mere ghar; bahut najadeek hai. aapakee badee kripaa hogee.

siyaaraam kadam badhaa kar aage-aage chalane lagaa. itanaa khush tha, maano sone ki gatharee lie jaataa ho. ghar ke saamane pahunch kar bolaa-aaie, baithie kuchh der.

saadhu-nahin bachcha, baithoonggaa naheen. phir kal-parason kisee samay aa jaaoonggaa, yahee tumhaaraa ghar hai?

siyaa0-kale kis vakt. aaiegaa.

saadhu-pakkaa faisla nahin kah sakataa. kisee samay aa jaaoonggaa. saadhu aage badhe to thodee hee door par unhen ek doosaraa saadhu milaa. isakaa naam tha hariharaananda.

paramaanand ne poochhaa-kahaan-kahaan. ki shair ki? koee shikaar phansaa?

hariharaanand-idhar to chaaron taraph ghoom kamaaeeaa, koee shikaar n milaa. ekaadh milaa bhi to meree hansee udaane lagaa.

paramaanand-mujhe to ek milataa huaa jaan padataa hai. phans jaay to jaanen.

hariharaanand-tum yonhee kahaa karate ho. jo aataa hai, do-ek din ke vaad nikal bhaagataa hai.

paramaanand-abakee bhaagegaa, dekh lenaa. isakee maan mar gaee hai. baap ne doosaraa shaadee kar liyaa hai. maan bhi sataayaa karatee hai. ghar se oobaa huaa hai.

hariharaanand-haan, yah maamalaa hai to zaroor phansegaa. laasaa lagaa diyaa hai n?

paramaanand-khoob achchhi taraha. yahee tarakeeb sabase achchhi hai. pahale isakaa pataa lagaa lenaa chahiye ki muhalle mein kin-kin gharon mein vimaataaen hain. unheen gharon mein phandaa daalanaa chaahie.
