kharcha na honge| yaha aashvaasana paakara ve khushee ke maare phoole na samaae!

isee khabara ne anajaana vaalikaa ko mu.Nha dhaa.Npa kar ek kone men vithaa rakkhaa hai| usake dil men ek vichitra shankaa samaa gaee hai, roma-roma men ek anajaana dara kaa sanchaara ho gaya hai--na jaane kyaa hogaa? usake mana men ve umange naheen hain, jo yuvatiyon kee aa.Nkhon men tirachhee chitavana bana kar, othon para madhura haasya bana kar; aur angon men aalasya vana kar prakata hoti hain| naheen, vahaa.N abhilaashaae.N naheen hain; vahaa.N kevala shankaae.N, chintaae.N aur bheera kalpanaae.N hain| yauvana kaa abhee taka poorNa prakaasha naheen hua hai!

krishNaa kuchha-kuchha jaanatee hai, kuchha-kuchha naheen jaanatee| jaanatee hai, bahina ko achchhe-achchhe gahane milenge, dvaara para baaje bajenge, mehamaana aaenge, naacha hogaa--yaha jaana kar khusha hai; aur yaha bhee jaanatee hai ki vahina sabake gale mila kar roegee, yahaa.N se ro-dho kar vidaa ho jaayagee, main akelee raha jaaoo.Ngee|| yaha jaana kar dukhee hai; para yaha naheen jaanatee ki yaha sab kisalie ho raha hai, maa.N jee aur pitaa jee kyon bahina ko ghar se nikaalane ko itane utsuka ho rahe hain| bahina ne to kisee ko kuchha naheen kahaa, kisee se ladai naheen kee; kyaa isee taraha ek din mujhe bhee log nikaala denge? main bhee isee taraha kone men baitha kar rooo.Ngee; aur kisee ko mujha para dayaa na aaegee? isalie vaha bhayabheeta bhee hai|

shaama kaa samaya tha, nirmalaa chhata para jaakara akelee baithee aakaasha kee ora trishita netron se taaka rahi thi| aisaa mana hota tha ki pankha hote to vaha uda jati; aur ina saaree jhanjhaton se chhoota jati| is samaya bahudhaa donon bahinen kaheen saira karane jaayaa karti theen| bagghee khaalee na hoti, to bageeche hee men tahalaa karateen| isalie krishNaa. use khojatee phiratee thi| jaba kaheen na paayaa, to chhata para aaee; aur use dekhate hee ha.Nsa kar bolee-tuma yahaa.N aakara chhipee baithee ho; aur main tumhen dhoo.Ndhatee phiratee hoo.N| chalo, bagghee taiyaara karaa aaee hoo.N!

nirmalaa ne udaaseena bhaava se kahaa--too jaa, main na jaaoo.Ngee|

krishNaa--naheen, meree achchhee deedee; aaja jaroora chalo| dekho, kaisee thaNdee-thaNdee havaa chala rahi hai|

nirmalaa--meraa mana naheen chaahataa, too chalee jaa|

krishNaa kee aa.Nkhen dabadabaa aaee| kaa.Npatee huee aavaaja se bolee--aaja tuma kyon naheen chalatee? mujhase kyon naheen bolatee? kyon idhara-udhara chhipee-chhipee phiratee ho? meraa jee akele baithe-baithe ghabaraataa hai| tuma na chalogee, to main bhee na jaaoo.Ngee| yaheen tumhaare saatha baithee rahoo.Ngee|

nirmalaa--aur jaba main chalee jaaoo.Ngee, taba kyaa karegee? taba kisake saatha khelegee, kisake saatha ghoomane jaayagee; bataa?

krishNaa--main bhee tumhaare saatha chala.Ngee| akele mujhase yahaa.N na raha jaayagaa|

nirmalaa muskaraa kar bolee--tujhe ammaa.N na jaane dengee|

krishNaa--to main bhee tumhen na jaane doo.Ngee| tuma ammaa.N se kaha kyon naheen deteen ki main na jaaoo.Ngee?

nirmalaa--kaha to rahi hoo.N, koee sunataa hai?

krishNaa--to kyaa yaha tumhaaraa ghar naheen hai?

nirmalaa--naheen; meraa ghar hota to koee kyon jabaradastee nikaala detaa|

krishNaa--isee taraha kisee din main bhee nikaala diya jaayagaa?

nirmalaa--aur naheen kyaa too vaithee rahegee? hama ladakiyaa.N hain, hamaaraa ghar kaheen naheen hota|

krishNaa--chandara bhee nikaala diya jaayagaa?

nirmalaa--chandara to ladakaa hai; use kauna nikaalegaa?

krishNaa--to ladakiyaa.N bahuta kharaaba hoti hongee?

nirmalaa--kharaaba na hoti; to ghar se bhagaaee kyon jaateen|

krishNaa--chandara itanaa badamaasha hai, use koee naheen bhagaataa| hama tuma to koee badamaashee bhee naheen karateen|

ekaaeka chandara dhama-dhama karta hua chhata para aa pahu.Nchaa; aur nirmalaa ko dekha kar bola--achchhaa! aapa yahaa.N baithee hain| oho! aba to baaje bajenge, deedee dulhana banengee, paalakee para chadhegee, oho! oho!!

chandara kaa pooraa naama chandrabhaanu sinhaa tha| nirmalaa se teena saala chhotaa; aur krishNaa se do saala badaa tha|

nirmalaa--chandara! mujhe chidhaaoge, to abhee jaakara ammaa.N se doo.Ngee|

chandra--to chidhatee kyon ho? tuma bhee baaje sunanaa| oho, ho! aba aapa doolhana banengee! kyon kishanee, too baaje sunegee na? vaise baaje toone kabhee na sune honge|

chandra--haa.N haa.N, baiNda se bhee achchhe, hajaara gune achchhe, laakha gune achchhe| tuma jaano kyaa? ek baiNda suna liya, to samajhane lagee, usase achchhe baaje hee naheen hote| baaje bajaane vaale laalalaala bardiyaa.N aur kaalee-kaalee topiyaa.N pahane honge| aise khoobasoorata maalooma honge ki tumase kyaa kahoo.N| aatashabaajiyaa.N bhee hongee; havaaiyaa.N aasamaana men uda jaaya.Ngee; aur vahaa.N taaron men lagengee to laala, peele, hare, neele taare toota-toota kar girenge| badaa majaa aaegaa|
