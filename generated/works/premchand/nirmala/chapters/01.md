# Nirmala — Adhyay 1

**Munshi Premchand**

Nirmala pahalaa parichchhed

yon to baaboo udayabhaanulaal ke gharavaale mein beeson hee praanee the; koee mameraa bhaaee tha, koee phupheraa; koee bhaanyjaa tha, koee bhateejaa; lekin yahan hamen unase koee makasad naheen. vah achchhe vakeel the, lakshmee khush thi; aur kutumb ke daridr praaniyon ko aashray denaa unakaa karttavy hee thaa. hamaaraa rishtaa to log unakee donon kanyaaon se hai, jinamen badee ka naam Nirmala; aur chhotee ka krishnaa thaa. abhee kal tak donon saath-saath gudiyaa bhi main theen. Nirmala ka pandrahavaan saal tha, krishnaa ka dasavaan; phir bhi unake svabhaav mein koee vishesh antar n thaa. donon chanychal, khilaadin aur sair-tamaashe par jaan detee theen. donon gudiyon ka dhoom-dhaam se byaah karatee theen, sadaa kaam se jee churaatee theen. maan pukaaratee rahatee thi; par donon kothe par chhipee baithee rahatee theen ki n jaane kis kaam ke lie bulaatee hain. donon apane bhaaiyon se ladatee theen, naukaron ko daatatee theen; aur baaje ki aavaaj sunate hee dvaar par aakar khadee ho jaatee theen; par aaj ekaaek ek aisee baat ho gaee hai, jisane badee ko badee aur chhotee ko chhotee banaa diyaa hai. krishnaa vahee hai; par Nirmala seeriyas, ekaantapriy aur sharmasheelaa ho gaee hai. idhar maheenon se baaboo udayabhaanulaal Nirmala ke shaadee ki baatacheet kar rahe the. aaj unakee mihanat thikaane lagee hai. baaboo bhaalachandr sinhaa ke jyeshth putr bhuvanamohan sinhaa se baat pakkee ho gaee hai. var ke pitaa ne kah diyaa hai ki aap ki khushi ho dahej den ya n den, mujhe isakee paravaah nahin; haan, baaraat mein jo log jaayan unakaa aadar-satkaar achchhi tarah honaa chahiye, jisamen meree aur aapakee jag-hansaaee n ho. baaboo udayabhaanulaal the to vakeel; par sanychay karanaa n jaanate the. dahej unake saamane kathin mushkil thee. isalie jab var ke pitaa ne svayan kah diyaa ki mujhe dahej ki paravaah nahin, to maano unhe aankhen mil gaee. darate the, n jaane kis-kis ke saamane haath phailaanaa pade, do-teen mahaajanon ko theek kar rakkhaa aur unhe anumaan tha ki haath rokane par bhi bees hajaar se kam kharch n hongge. yah aashvaasan paakar ve khushi ke maare phoole n samaae!

isee khabar ne agyaat vaalikaa ko munh dhaamp kar ek kone mein vithaa rakkhaa hai. usake dil mein ek ajeeb shanggkaa samaa gaee hai, rom-rom mein ek agyaat dar ka sanychaar ho gayaa hai--n jaane kyaa hogaa? usake man mein ve umanggge nahin hain, jo yuvatiyon ki aangkhon mein tirachhee chitavan ban kar, othon par madhur haasy ban kar; aur angggon mein aalasy van kar saamane hotee hain. nahin, wahan abhilaashaaen nahin hain; wahan keval shanggkaaen, phikraen aur bheer khayaalaen hain. yauvan ka abhee tak poorn prakaash nahin huaa hai!

Nirmala ne udaaseen bhaav se kahaa--too jaa, main n jaaoonggee.

krishnaa--nahin, meree achchhi deedee; aaj zaroor chalo. dekho, kaisee thandee-thandee havaa chal rahee hai.

Nirmala--meraa man nahin chaahataa, too chalee jaa.

krishnaa ki aankhen dabadabaa aaee. kaampatee huee aavaaj se bolee--aaj tum kyon nahin chalatee? mujhase kyon nahin bolatee? kyon idhar-udhar chhipee-chhipee phiratee ho? meraa jee akele baithe-baithe ghabaraataa hai. tum n chalogee, to main bhi n jaaoonggee. yaheen tumhaare saath baithee rahoonggee.

Nirmala--aur jab main chalee jaaoonggee, tab kyaa karegee? tab kisake saath khelegee, kisake saath ghoomane jaayagee; bataa?

krishnaa--main bhi tumhaare saath chalanggee. akele mujhase yahan n rahaa jaayagaa. Nirmala muskaraa kar bolee--tujhe ammaan n jaane denggee.

krishnaa--to main bhi tumhe n jaane doonggee. tum ammaan se kah kyon nahin deteen ki main n jaaoonggee?

Nirmala--kah to rahee hoon, koee sunataa hai?

krishnaa--to kyaa yah tumhaaraa ghar nahin hai?

Nirmala--nahin; meraa ghar hotaa to koee kyon jabaradastee nikaal detaa.

krishnaa--isee tarah kisee din main bhi nikaal diyaa jaayagaa?

Nirmala--aur nahin kyaa too vaithee rahegee? ham ladakiyaan hain, hamaaraa ghar kaheen nahin hotaa.

krishnaa--chandar bhi nikaal diyaa jaayagaa?

Nirmala--chandar to ladakaa hai; use kaun nikaalegaa?

krishnaa--to ladakiyaan bahut kharaab hotee honggee?

Nirmala--kharaab n hotee; to ghar se bhagaaee kyon jaateen.

krishnaa--chandar itanaa badamaash hai, use koee nahin bhagaataa. ham tum to koee badamaashee bhi nahin karateen.

ekaaek chandar dham-dham karataa huaa chhat par aa pahunchaa; aur Nirmala ko dekh kar bolaa--achchha! aap yahan baithee hain. oho! ab to baaje bajengge, deedee dulhan banenggee, paalakee par chadhegee, oho! oho!!

chandar ka pooraa naam chandrabhaanu sinhaa thaa. Nirmala se teen saal chhotaa; aur krishnaa se do saal badaa thaa. Nirmala--chandar! mujhe chidhaaoge, to abhee jaakar ammaan se doonggee.

chandr--to chidhatee kyon ho? tum bhi baaje sunanaa. oho, ho! ab aap doolhan banenggee! kyon kishanee, too baaje sunegee n? vaise baaje toone kabhee n sune hongge.

krishnaa--kyaa baind se bhi achchhe hongge?

chandr--haan haan, baind se bhi achchhe, hajaar gune achchhe, laakh gune achchhe. tum jaano kyaa? ek baind sun liyaa, to samajhane lagee, usase achchhe baaje hee nahin hote. baaje bajaane vaale laalalaal bardiyaan aur kaalee-kaalee topiyaan pahane hongge. aise khoobasoorat maaloom hongge ki tumase kyaa kahoon. aatashabaajiyaan bhi honggee; havaaiyaan aasamaan mein ud jaayanggee; aur wahan taaron mein lagenggee to laal, peele, hare, neele taare toot-toot kar girengge. badaa majaa aaegaa.

krishnaa--aur kyaa-kyaa hogaa chandar, bataa de mere bhaiyaa!

chandr--mere saath ghoomane chal, to raaste mein saaree baaten bataa doon. aise-aise tamaashe hongge ki dekh kar teree aankhen khul jaayanggee. havaa mein udatee huee pariyaan honggee; sachamuch ki pariyaan.

krishnaa--achchha chalo; lekin n bataaoge to maaroonggee.

chandrabhaanu aur krishnaa chale gae; par Nirmala akelee baithee rah gaee. krishnaa ke chale jaane se is samay use badaa kshobh huaa. krishnaa jise vah praanon se bhi adhik pyaar karatee thi, aaj itanee nithur ho gaee! akelee chhod kar chalee gaee! baat koee n thi, lekin dukhee dil dukhatee huee aankh hai, jisamen havaa se bhi dard hotee hai. Nirmala badee der tak baithee rotee rahee. bhaaee-bahin, maataa-pitaa sabhee isee bhoti mujhe bhool jaayangge, sab ki aankhen phir jaayanggee! phir shaayad inhen dekhane ko bhi taras jaaoon!

chaaraa mein phool khile hue the. meethee-meethee sugandh aa rahee thee. chait ki sheetal, mand sameer chal rahee thee. aakaash mein taare chhitake hue the. Nirmala inheen shokamay soch mein padee-padee so gaee aur aankh lagate hee usakaa man svapn-desh mein vicharane lagaa. kyaa dekhatee hai ki saamane ek nadee laharen maar rahee hai; aur vah nadee ke kinaare naav ki vaat dekh rahee hai. sandhyaa ka samay hai. andheraa kisee daranggkar jantu ki bhaanti badhataa chalaa aataa hai. vah ghor fikr mein padee huee hai ki kaise yah nadee paar hogee; kaise ghar pahunchoonggee. ro rahee hai ki kaheen raat n ho jaay, nahin to main akelee yahan kaise rahoonggee. ekaaek use ek sundar naukaa ghaat ki or aatee dikhaaee detee hai. vah khushi se uchhal padatee hai; aur jyonhee naav ghaat par aatee hai, vah us par chadhane ke lie badhatee hai. lekin jyonhee naav ke patare par pair rakhanaa chaahatee hain, usakaa mallaah bol uthataa hai--tere lie yahan jagah nahin hai. vah mallaah ki khushaamad karatee hai, usake pairon padatee hai, rotee hai; lekin vah yah kahe jaataa hai--tere lie yahan jagah nahin hai. ek pal mein naav khul jaatee hai. vah chillaa-chillaa kar rone lagatee hai. nadee ke nirjan tat par raat bhar kaise rahegee, yah soch, vah nadee mein kood kar us naav ko pakadanaa chaahatee hai ki itane mein kaheen se aavaaj aatee hai--thaharo-thaharo, nadee gaharee hai, doob jaaogee. vah naav tumhaare lie nahin hai, main aataa hoon; meree naav par baith jaao, main us paar pahunchaa doonggaa. vah darabheet hokar idhar-udhar dekhatee hai ki yah aavaaj kahaan se aaee. thodee der ke baad ek chhotee-see donggee aatee dikhaaee detee hai. usamen n paal hai, n patavaar; aur n mastoola. pendaa phataa huaa hai, takhte toote hue, naav mein paani bharaa huaa hai; aur ek aadmi usamen se paani uleech rahaa hai. vah usase kahatee hai, yah to tootee huee hai, yah kaise paar lagegee? mallaah kahataa hai-- tumhaare lie yahee bhejee gaee hai, aakar baith jaao. vah ek pal sochatee hai--isamen baithoon ya n baithen. ant mein vah pakkaa faisla karatee hai, baith jaaoon. yahan akelee padee rahane se naav mein baith jaanaa phir bhi achchha hai. kisee daranggkar jantu ke pet mein jaane se to yahee achchha hai ki nadee mein doob jaaoon. kaun jaane, naav paar pahunch hee jaay; yah soch kar vah praanon ko mutthee mein lie hue naav par baith jaatee hai. kuchh der tak naav dagamagaatee huee chalatee hai; lekin prati pal usamen paani bharataa jaataa hai. vah bhi mallaah ke saath donon haathon se paani uleechane lagatee hai, yahan tak ki usake haath rah jaate hain; par paani badhataa hee chalaa jaataa hai. aakhir naav chakar khaane lagatee hai; maaloom hotaa hai-ab doobee, ab doovee. tab vah kisee adrishy sahaare ke lie donon haath phailaatee hai, naav neeche se khisak jaatee hai. aur usake pair ukhad jaate hain. vah jor se chillaaee aur chillaate hee usakee aankhen khul gaee. dekhaa to maataa saamane khadee usakaa kandhaa pakad kar hilaa rahee theen.
