# Nirmala — Adhyay 1

**Munshi Premchand**

pahla parichchhed

yon to babu NAmEUdaybhanulal ke parivar men bison hi insaan the; koi mamera bhai tha, koi phuphera; koi bhanja tha, koi bhatija; lekin yaham hamen unse koi maksad nahin. vah achchhe vakil the, lakshmi khush thi; aur kutumb ke garib praniyon ko ashray dena unka pharz hi tha. hamara rishta to log unki donon kanyaon se hai, jinmen badi ka nam NAmENirmala; aur chhoti ka NAmEKrishna tha. abhi kal tak donon sath-sath guriya bhi maim thim. NAmENirmala ka pandrahvam sal tha, NAmEKrishna ka dasvam; phir

bhi unke svabhav men koi khas antar na tha. donon chanchal, khilarin aur sair-tamashe par jan deti thim. donon guriyon ka dhum-dham se byah karti thim, sada kam se ji churati thim. mam pukarti rahti thi; par donon kothe par chhipi baithi rahti thim ki na jane kis kam ke lie bulati haim. donon apne bhaiyon se larti thim, naukron ko datti thim; aur baje ki avaz sunte hi dvar par akar khadi ho jati thim; par aj ekaek ek aisi bat ho gai hai, jisne badi ko badi aur chhoti ko chhoti bana diya hai. NAmEKrishna vahi hai; par NAmENirmala gambhir, ekantapriy aur lajjashila ho gai hai. idhar mahinon se babu NAmEUdaybhanulal NAmENirmala ke shadi ki batchit kar rahe the. aj unki mihnat thikane lagi hai. babu NAmEBhalchandra NAmESinha ke jyeshth beta NAmEBhuvanmohan NAmESinha se bat pakki ho gai hai. dulha ke pita ne kah diya hai ki ap ki khushi ho dahez den ya na den, mujhe iski parvah nahin; ham, barat men jo log jayam unka adar-satkar achchhi tarah hona chahiye, jismen meri aur apki jag-hamsai na ho. babu NAmEUdaybhanulal the to vakil; par sanchay karna na jante the. dahej unke samne mushkil samasya thi. isalie jab dulha ke pita ne svayam kah diya ki mujhe dahej ki parvah nahin, to mano unhe ankhen mil gai. darte the, na jane kis-kis ke samne hath phailana pade, do-tin mahajnon ko thik kar rakkha aur unhe anuman tha ki hath rokne par bhi bis hajar se kam
kharch na honge. yah ashvasan pakad ve khushi ke mare phule na samae!

isi khabar ne anyan valika ko mumh dhamp kar ek kone men vitha rakkha hai. uske dil men ek vichitra shanka sama gai hai, ron-ron men ek anyan dar ka sanchar ho gaya hai--na jane kya hoga? uske man men ve umange nahin haim, jo yuvtiyo ki ankhon men tirchhi chitvan ban kar, othon par mitha hasya ban kar; aur angon men alasya van kar prakat hoti haim. nahin, vaham abhilashaen nahin haim; vaham sirph shankaen, chintaen aur bhir kalpanaen haim. yauvan ka abhi tak purn prakash nahin hua hai!

NAmEKrishna kuchh-kuchh janti hai, kuchh-kuchh nahin janti. janti hai, bahin ko achchhe-achchhe gahne milenge, dvar par baje bajenge, mehman aenge, nach hoga--yah jan kar khush hai; aur yah bhi janti hai ki vahin sabke gale mil kar roegi, yaham se ro-dho kar vida ho jaygi, maim akeli rah jaungi.. yah jan kar dukhi hai; par yah nahin janti ki yah sab kisalie ho raha hai, mam ji aur pita ji kyon bahin ko ghar se nikalne ko itne bekrar ho rahe haim. bahin ne to kisi ko kuchh nahin kaha, kisi se larai nahin ki; kya isi tarah ek din mujhe bhi log nikal denge? maim bhi isi tarah kone men baith kar roungi; aur kisi ko mujh par daya na aegi? isalie vah bhaybhit bhi hai.

sham ka samay tha, NAmENirmala chhat par jakar akeli baithi akash ki or trishit netron se tak rahi thi. aisa man hota tha ki pankha hote to vah ud jati; aur in sari jhanjhaton se chhut jati. is samay bahudha donon bahinen kahim sair karne jaya karti thim. bagghi khali na hoti, to bagiche hi men tahla kartim. isalie NAmEKrishna. use khojti phirti thi. jab kahim na paya, to chhat par ai; aur use dekhte hi hams kar boli-tum yaham akar chhipi baithi ho; aur maim tumhen dhumrhti phirti hum. chalo, bagghi taiyar kara ai hum!

NAmENirmala ne udasin bhav se kaha--tu ja, maim na jaungi.

NAmEKrishna --nahin, meri achchhi didi; aj zarur chalo. dekho, kaisi thandi-thandi hava chal rahi hai.

NAmENirmala --mera man nahin chahta, tu chali ja.

NAmEKrishna ki ankhen dabdaba ai. kampti hui avaz se boli--aj tum kyon nahin chalti? mujhse kyon nahin bolti? kyon idhar-udhar chhipi-chhipi phirti ho? mera ji akele baithe-baithe ghabrata hai. tum na chalogi, to maim bhi na jaungi. yahim tumhare sath baithi rahungi.

NAmENirmala --aur jab maim chali jaungi, tab kya karegi? tab kiske sath khelegi, kiske sath ghumne jaygi; bata?

NAmEKrishna --maim bhi tumhare sath chalangi. akele mujhse yaham na raha jayga.

NAmENirmala muskara kar boli--tujhe ammam na jane dengi.

NAmEKrishna --to maim bhi tumhen na jane dungi. tum ammam se kah kyon nahin detim ki maim na jaungi?

NAmENirmala --kah to rahi hum, koi sunta hai?

NAmEKrishna --to kya yah tumhara ghar nahin hai?

NAmENirmala --nahin; mera ghar hota to koi kyon zabaradasti nikal deta.

NAmEKrishna --isi tarah kisi din maim bhi nikal diya jayga?

NAmENirmala --aur nahin kya tu vaithi rahegi? ham larkiya haim, hamara ghar kahim nahin hota.

NAmEKrishna --chandar bhi nikal diya jayga?

NAmENirmala --chandar to larka hai; use kaun nikalega?

NAmEKrishna --to larkiya bahut kharab hoti hongi?

NAmENirmala --kharab na hoti; to ghar se bhagai kyon jatim.

NAmEKrishna --chandar itna badmash hai, use koi nahin bhagata. ham tum to koi badmashi bhi nahin kartim.

ekaek chandar dham-dham karta hua chhat par a pahuncha; aur NAmENirmala ko dekh kar bola--achchha! ap yaham baithi haim. oho! ab to baje bajenge, didi dulhan banengi, palki par charhegi, oho! oho!!

chandar ka pura nam chandrabhanu NAmESinha tha. NAmENirmala se tin sal chhota; aur NAmEKrishna se do sal bada tha.

NAmENirmala --chandar! mujhe chirhaoge, to abhi jakar ammam se dungi.

chandra--to chirhti kyon ho? tum bhi baje sunna. oho, ho! ab ap dulhan banengi! kyon kishni, tu baje sunegi na? vaise baje tune kabhi na sune honge.

NAmEKrishna --kya baind se bhi achchhe honge?

chandra--ham ham, baind se bhi achchhe, hajar gune achchhe, lakh gune achchhe. tum jano kya? ek baind sun liya, to samjhane lagi, usse achchhe baje hi nahin hote. baje bajane vale lallal bardiyam aur kali-kali topiyam pahne honge. aise khubasurat malum honge ki tumse kya kahum. atashabaziyam bhi hongi; havaiyam asman men ud jayangi; aur vaham taron men lagengi to lal, pile, hare, nile tare tut-tut kar girenge. bada maza aega.

NAmEKrishna --aur kya-kya hoga chandar, bata de mere bhaiya!

chandra--mere sath ghumne chal, to raste men sari baten bata dum. aise-aise tamashe honge ki dekh kar teri ankhen khul jayangi. hava men urti hui pariyam hongi; sachmuch ki pariyam.

NAmEKrishna --achchha chalo; lekin na bataoge to marungi.

chandrabhanu aur NAmEKrishna chale gae; par NAmENirmala akeli baithi rah gai. NAmEKrishna ke chale jane se is samay use bada kshobh hua. NAmEKrishna jise vah pranon se bhi zyada pyaar karti thi, aj itni

nithur ho gai! akeli chhod kar chali gai! bat koi na thi, lekin dukhi dil dukhti hui ankh hai, jismen hava se bhi dard hoti hai. NAmENirmala badi der tak baithi roti rahi. bhai-bahin, mam-pita sabhi isi bhoti mujhe bhul jayange, sab ki ankhen phir jayangi! phir shayad inhen dekhne ko bhi taras jaum!

chadha men phul khile hue the. mithi-mithi sugandh a rahi thi. chait ki shital, mand samir chal rahi thi. akash men tare chhitke hue the. NAmENirmala inhim shokmay vicharon men padi-padi so gai aur ankh lagte hi uska man svapna-desh men vichrane laga. kya dekhti hai ki samne ek nadi lahren mar rahi hai; aur vah nadi ke kinare nav ki vat dekh rahi hai. sham ka samay hai. andhera kisi bhayankar jantu ki tarah barhta chala ata hai. vah ghor chinta men padi hui hai ki kaise yah nadi par hogi; kaise ghar pahunchungi. ro rahi hai ki kahim rat na ho jay, nahin to maim akeli yaham kaise rahungi. ekaek use ek sundar nauka ghat ki or ati dikhai deti hai. vah khushi se uchhal parti hai; aur jyomhi nav ghat par ati hai, vah us par charhne ke lie barhti hai. lekin jyomhi nav ke patre par pair rakhna chahti haim, uska mallah bol uthta hai--tere lie yaham jagah nahin hai. vah mallah ki khushamad karti hai, uske pairon parti hai, roti hai; lekin vah yah kahe jata hai--tere lie yaham jagah nahin hai.

ek pal men nav khul jati hai. vah chilla-chilla kar rone lagti hai. nadi ke nirjan tat par rat bhar kaise rahegi, yah soch, vah nadi men kud kar us nav ko pakrana chahti hai ki itne men kahim se avaz ati hai--thahro-thahro, nadi gahri hai, dub jaogi. vah nav tumhare lie nahin hai, maim ata hum; meri nav par baith jao, maim us par pahuncha dunga. vah bhaybhit hokar idhar-udhar dekhti hai ki yah avaz kaham se ai. thodi der ke bad ek chhoti-si dongi ati dikhai deti hai. usmen na pal hai, na patvar; aur na mastul. penda phata hua hai, takhte tute hue, nav men pani bhara hua hai; aur ek aadmi usmen se pani ulich raha hai. vah usse kahti hai, yah to tuti hui hai, yah kaise par lagegi? mallah kahta hai-- tumhare lie yahi bheji gai hai, akar baith jao. vah ek pal sochti hai--ismen baithum ya na baithen. ant men vah phaisla karti hai, baith jaum. yaham akeli padi rahne se nav men baith jana phir bhi achchha hai. kisi bhayankar jantu ke pet men jane se to yahi achchha hai ki nadi men dub jaum. kaun jane, nav par pahunch hi jay; yah soch kar vah pranon ko mutthi men lie hue nav par baith jati hai. kuchh der tak nav dagamgati hui chalti hai; lekin prati pal usmen pani bharta jata hai. vah bhi mallah ke sath donon hathon se pani ulichne lagti hai, yaham tak ki uske hath rah jate haim; par pani barhta hi chala jata hai.

akhir nav chakar khane lagti hai; malum hota hai-ab dubi, ab duvi. tab vah kisi adrishya sahare ke lie donon hath phailati hai, nav niche se khisak jati hai. aur uske pair ukhar jate haim. vah jod se chillai aur chillate hi uski ankhen khul gai. dekha to mam samne khadi uska kandha pakad kar hila rahi thim.
