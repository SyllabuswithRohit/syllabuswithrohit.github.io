# Nirmala — Adhyay 1

**Munshi Premchand**

yon to baaboo udayabhaanulaal ke parivaar men beeson hee praanee the; koee mameraa bhaaee thaa, koee phupheraa; koee bhaanjaa thaa, koee bhateejaa; lekin yahaan hamen unase koee makasad naheen. woh achchhe vakeel the, lakshmee khush thee; aur kutumb ke gareeb praaniyon ko aashray denaa unakaa pharz hee thaa. hamaaraa rishtaa to log unakee donon kanyaaon se hai, jinamen ba. dee kaa naam nirml; aur chhotee kaa krishnaa thaa. abhee kal tak donon saatha-saath gu. diyaa bhee main theen. nirml kaa pandrahavaan saal thaa, krishnaa kaa dasavaan; phir

bhee unake svabhaav men koee khaas antar na thaa. donon chanchal, khilaa. din aur saira-tamaashe par jaan detee theen. donon gu. diyon kaa dhooma-dhaam se byaah karatee theen, sadaa kaam se jee churaatee theen. maan pukaaratee rahatee thee; par donon kothe par chhipee baithee rahatee theen ki na jaane kis kaam ke lie bulaatee hain. donon apane bhaaiyon se la. datee theen, naukaron ko daatatee theen; aur baaje kee aavaaz sunate hee dvaar par aakar kha. dee ho jaatee theen; par aaj ekaaek ek aisee baat ho gaee hai, jisane ba. dee ko ba. dee aur chhotee ko chhotee bn diyaa hai. krishnaa vahee hai; par nirml ganbheer, ekaantapriy aur lajjaasheelaa ho gaee hai. idhar maheenon se baaboo udayabhaanulaal nirml ke shaadee kee baatacheet kar rahe the. aaj unakee mihanat thikaane lagee hai. baaboo bhaalachandr sinhaa ke jyeshth betaa bhuvanamohan sinhaa se baat pakkee ho gaee hai. doolhaa ke pitaa ne kah diyaa hai ki aap kee khushee ho dahez den yaa na den, mujhe isakee paravaah naheen; haan, baaraat men jo log jaayan unakaa aadara-satkaar achchhee tarah honaa chaahie, jisamen meree aur aapakee jaga-hansaaee na ho. baaboo udayabhaanulaal the to vakeel; par sanchay karn na jaanate the. dahej unake saamane mushkil samasyaa thee. isalie jab doolhaa ke pitaa ne svayan kah diyaa ki mujhe dahej kee paravaah naheen, to maano unhe aankhen mil gaee. darate the, na jaane kisa-kis ke saamane haath phailaanaa pa. de, do-teen mahaajanon ko theek kar rakkhaa aur unhe anumaan thaa ki haath rokane par bhee bees hajaar se kam
kharch na honge. yeh aashvaasan paakar ve khushee ke maare phoole na samaae!

isee khabar ne anajaan vaalikaa ko munh dhaanp kar ek kone men vithaa rakkhaa hai. usake dil men ek vichitr shankaa samaa gaee hai, roma-rom men ek anajaan dar kaa sanchaar ho gayaa hai--n jaane kyaa hogaa? usake man men ve umange naheen hain, jo yuvatiyon kee aankhon men tirachhee chitavan ban kar, othon par meethaa haasy ban kar; aur angon men aalasy van kar prakat hotee hain. naheen, vahaan abhilaashaaen naheen hain; vahaan sirph shankaaen, chintaaen aur bheer kalpanaaen hain. yauvan kaa abhee tak poorn prakaash naheen huaa hai!

krishnaa kuchha-kuchh jaanatee hai, kuchha-kuchh naheen jaanatee. jaanatee hai, bahin ko achchhe-achchhe gahane milenge, dvaar par baaje bajenge, mehamaan aaenge, naach hogaa--yeh jaan kar khush hai; aur yeh bhee jaanatee hai ki vahin sabake gale mil kar roegee, yahaan se ro-dho kar vidaa ho jaayagee, main akelee rah jaaoongee.. yeh jaan kar dukhee hai; par yeh naheen jaanatee ki yeh sab kisalie ho rahaa hai, maan jee aur pitaa jee kyon bahin ko ghar se nikaalane ko itane bekaraar ho rahe hain. bahin ne to kisee ko kuchh naheen kahaa, kisee se la. daaee naheen kee; kyaa isee tarah ek din mujhe bhee log nikaal denge? main bhee isee tarah kone men baith kar rooongee; aur kisee ko mujh par dayaa na aaegee? isalie woh bhayabheet bhee hai.

shaam kaa samay thaa, nirml chhat par jaakar akelee baithee aakaash kee ora trishit netron se taak rahee thee. aisaa man hotaa thaa ki pankh hote to woh u. d jaatee; aur ina saaree jhanjhaton se chhoot jaatee. is samay bahudhaa donon bahinen kaheen sair karane jaayaa karatee theen. bagghee khaalee na hotee, to bageeche hee men tahl karateen. isalie krishnaa. use khojatee phiratee thee. jab kaheen na paayaa, to chhat par aaee; aur use dekhate hee hans kar bolee-tum yahaan aakar chhipee baithee ho; aur main tumhen dhoon. dhatee phiratee hoon. chalo, bagghee taiyaar kr aaee hoon!

nirml ne udaaseen bhaav se kahaa--too jaa, main na jaaoongee.

krishnaa--naheen, meree achchhee deedee; aaj zaroor chalo. dekho, kaisee thandee-thandee hv chal rahee hai.

nirmalaa--meraa man naheen chaahataa, too chalee jaa.

krishnaa kee aankhen dabadabaa aaee. kaanpatee huee aavaaz se bolee--aaj tum kyon naheen chalatee? mujhase kyon naheen bolatee? kyon idhara-udhar chhipee-chhipee phiratee ho? meraa jee akele baithe-baithe ghabaraataa hai. tum na chalogee, to main bhee na jaaoongee. yaheen tumhaare saath baithee rahoongee.

nirmalaa--aur jab main chalee jaaoongee, tab kyaa karegee? tab kisake saath khelegee, kisake saath ghoomane jaayagee; bataa?

krishnaa--main bhee tumhaare saath chalangee. akele mujhase yahaan na rahaa jaayagaa.

nirml muskr kar bolee--tujhe ammaan na jaane dengee.

krishnaa--to main bhee tumhen na jaane doongee. tum ammaan se kah kyon naheen deteen ki main na jaaoongee?

nirmalaa--kah to rahee hoon, koee sunataa hai?

krishnaa--to kyaa yeh tumhaaraa ghar naheen hai?

nirmalaa--naheen; meraa ghar hotaa to koee kyon zabaradastee nikaal detaa.

krishnaa--isee tarah kisee din main bhee nikaal diyaa jaayagaa?

nirmalaa--aur naheen kyaa too vaithee rahegee? hum la. dakiyaan hain, hamaaraa ghar kaheen naheen hotaa.

krishnaa--chandar bhee nikaal diyaa jaayagaa?

nirmalaa--chandar to la. dakaa hai; use kaun nikaalegaa?

krishnaa--to la. dakiyaan bahut kharaab hotee hongee?

nirmalaa--kharaab na hotee; to ghar se bhagaaee kyon jaateen.

krishnaa--chandar itn badamaash hai, use koee naheen bhagaataa. hum tum to koee badamaashee bhee naheen karateen.

ekaaek chandar dhama-dham karataa huaa chhat par aa pahunchaa; aur nirml ko dekh kar bolaa--achchhaa! aap yahaan baithee hain. oho! ab to baaje bajenge, deedee dulhan banengee, paalakee par cha. dhegee, oho! oho!!

chandar kaa pooraa naam chandrabhaanu sinhaa thaa. nirml se teen saal chhotaa; aur krishnaa se do saal ba. daa thaa.

nirmalaa--chandar! mujhe chi. dhaaoge, to abhee jaakar ammaan se doongee.

chandra--to chi. dhatee kyon ho? tum bhee baaje sunanaa. oho, ho! ab aap doolhan banengee! kyon kishanee, too baaje sunegee na? vaise baaje toone kabhee na sune honge.

krishnaa--kyaa baind se bhee achchhe honge?

chandra--haan haan, baind se bhee achchhe, hajaar gune achchhe, laakh gune achchhe. tum jaano kyaa? ek baind sun liyaa, to samajhane lagee, usase achchhe baaje hee naheen hote. baaje bajaane vaale laalalaal bardiyaan aur kaalee-kaalee topiyaan pahane honge. aise khoobasoorat maaloom honge ki tumase kyaa kahoon. aatashabaaziyaan bhee hongee; havaaiyaan aasamaan men u. d jaayangee; aur vahaan taaron men lagengee to laal, peele, hare, neele taare toota-toot kar girenge. ba. daa mazaa aaegaa.

krishnaa--aur kyaa-kyaa hogaa chandar, bataa de mere bhaiyaa!

chandra--mere saath ghoomane chal, to raaste men saaree baaten bataa doon. aise-aise tamaashe honge ki dekh kar teree aankhen khul jaayangee. hv men u. datee huee pariyaan hongee; sachamuch kee pariyaan.

krishnaa--achchhaa chalo; lekin na bataaoge to maaroongee.

chandrabhaanu aur krishnaa chale gae; par nirml akelee baithee rah gaee. krishnaa ke chale jaane se is samay use ba. daa kshobh huaa. krishnaa jise woh praanon se bhee zyaadaa pyaar karatee thee, aaj itanee

nithur ho gaee! akelee chho. d kar chalee gaee! baat koee na thee, lekin dukhee dil dukhatee huee aankh hai, jisamen hv se bhee dard hotee hai. nirml ba. dee der tak baithee rotee rahee. bhaaee-bahin, maan-pitaa sabhee isee bhaॉti mujhe bhool jaayange, sab kee aankhen phir jaayangee! phir shaayad inhen dekhane ko bhee taras jaaoon!

chaaraa men phool khile hue the. meethee-meethee sugandh aa rahee thee. chait kee sheetal, mand sameer chal rahee thee. aakaash men taare chhitake hue the. nirml inheen shokamay vichaaron men pa. dee-pa. dee so gaee aur aankh lagate hee usakaa man svapna-desh men vicharane lagaa. kyaa dekhatee hai ki saamane ek nadee laharen maar rahee hai; aur woh nadee ke kinaare naav kee vaat dekh rahee hai. shaam kaa samay hai. andheraa kisee bhayankar jantu kee tarah ba. dhataa chl aataa hai. woh ghor chintaa men pa. dee huee hai ki kaise yeh nadee paar hogee; kaise ghar pahunchoongee. ro rahee hai ki kaheen raat na ho jaay, naheen to main akelee yahaan kaise rahoongee. ekaaek use ek sundar naukaa ghaat kee ora aatee dikhaaee detee hai. woh khushee se uchhal pa. datee hai; aur jyonhee naav ghaat par aatee hai, woh us par cha. dhane ke lie ba. dhatee hai. lekin jyonhee naav ke patare par pair rakhn chaahatee hain, usakaa mallaah bol uthataa hai--tere lie yahaan jagah naheen hai. woh mallaah kee khushaamad karatee hai, usake pairon pa. datee hai, rotee hai; lekin woh yeh kahe jaataa hai--tere lie yahaan jagah naheen hai.

ek pal men naav khul jaatee hai. woh chillaa-chillaa kar rone lagatee hai. nadee ke nirjan tat par raat bhar kaise rahegee, yeh soch, woh nadee men kood kar us naav ko paka. dn chaahatee hai ki itane men kaheen se aavaaz aatee hai--thaharo-thaharo, nadee gaharee hai, doob jaaogee. woh naav tumhaare lie naheen hai, main aataa hoon; meree naav par baith jaao, main us paar pahunchaa doongaa. woh bhayabheet hokar idhara-udhar dekhatee hai ki yeh aavaaz kahaan se aaee. tho. dee der ke baad ek chhotee-see dongee aatee dikhaaee detee hai. usamen na paal hai, na patavaar; aur na mastoola. pendaa phataa huaa hai, takhte toote hue, naav men paanee bhr huaa hai; aur ek aadamee usamen se paanee uleech rahaa hai. woh usase kahatee hai, yeh to tootee huee hai, yeh kaise paar lagegee? mallaah kahataa hai-- tumhaare lie yahee bhejee gaee hai, aakar baith jaao. woh ek pal sochatee hai--isamen baithoon yaa na baithen. ant men woh phaisl karatee hai, baith jaaoon. yahaan akelee pa. dee rahane se naav men baith jaanaa phir bhee achchhaa hai. kisee bhayankar jantu ke pet men jaane se to yahee achchhaa hai ki nadee men doob jaaoon. kaun jaane, naav paar pahunch hee jaay; yeh soch kar woh praanon ko mutthee men lie hue naav par baith jaatee hai. kuchh der tak naav dagamagaatee huee chalatee hai; lekin prati pal usamen paanee bharataa jaataa hai. woh bhee mallaah ke saath donon haathon se paanee uleechane lagatee hai, yahaan tak ki usake haath rah jaate hain; par paanee ba. dhataa hee chl jaataa hai.

aakhir naav chakar khaane lagatee hai; maaloom hotaa hai-ab doobee, ab doovee. tab woh kisee adrishy sahaare ke lie donon haath phailaatee hai, naav neeche se khisak jaatee hai. aur usake pair ukha. d jaate hain. woh jor se chillaaee aur chillaate hee usakee aankhen khul gaee. dekhaa to maan saamane kha. dee usakaa kandhaa paka. d kar hilaa rahee theen.
