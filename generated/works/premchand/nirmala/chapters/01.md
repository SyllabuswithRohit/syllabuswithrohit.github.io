# Nirmala — Adhyay 1

**Munshi Premchand**

nirmalaa pahalaa parichchheda

yon to baaboo udayabhaanulaala ke parivaara men beeson hee praanee the; koee mameraa bhaaee tha, koee phuphera; koee bhaanjaa tha, koee bhateeja; lekin yahaan hamen unase koee prayojana naheen| vaha achchhe vakeela the, lakshmee prasanna thi; aur kutumba ke daridra praaniyon ko aashraya dena unakaa karttavya hee tha| hamaaraa sambandha to log unakee donon kanyaaon se hai, jinamen ba.dee kaa naama nirmala; aur chhotee kaa krishnaa tha| abhee kala taka donon saatha-saatha gu.diya bhee main theen| nirmalaa kaa pandrahavaan saala tha, krishnaa kaa dasavaan; phir bhee unake svabhaava men koee vishesha antara na tha| donon chanchala, khila.din aur saira-tamaashe par jaana detee theen| donon gu.diyon kaa dhooma-dhaama se byaaha karti theen, sadaa kaama se jee churaatee theen| maan pukaaratee rahatee thi; par donon kothe par chhipee baithee rahatee theen ki na jaane kisa kaama ke lie bulaatee hain| donon apane bhaaiyon se la.datee theen, naukaron ko daatatee theen; aur baaje kee aavaaza sunate hee dvaara par aakara kha.dee ho jati theen; par aaja ekaaeka ek aisee baat ho gaee hai, jisane ba.dee ko ba.dee aur chhotee ko chhotee bana diya hai| krishnaa vahee hai; par nirmalaa gambheera, ekaantapriya aur lajjaasheelaa ho gaee hai| idhara maheenon se baaboo udayabhaanulaala nirmalaa ke vivaaha kee baatacheeta kar rahe the| aaja unakee mihanata thikaane lagee hai| baaboo bhaalachandra sinhaa ke jyeshtha putra bhuvanamohana sinhaa se baat pakkee ho gaee hai| vara ke pitaa ne kaha diya hai ki aapa kee khushi ho daheza den yaa na den, mujhe isakee paravaaha naheen; haan, baaraata men jo log jaayan unakaa aadara-satkaara achchhi tarah honaa chaahie, jisamen meree aur aapakee jaga-hansaaee na ho| baaboo udayabhaanulaala the to vakeela; par sanchaya karana na jaanate the| daheja unake saamane kathina samasyaa thi| isalie jab vara ke pitaa ne svayan kaha diya ki mujhe daheja kee paravaaha naheen, to maano unhe aankhen mila gaee| darate the, na jaane kisa-kisa ke saamane haath phailaanaa pa.de, do-teena mahaajanon ko theeka kar rakkhaa aur unhe anumaana tha ki haath rokane par bhee beesa hajaara se kama kharcha na honge| yaha aashvaasana paakara ve khushi ke maare phoole na samaae!

isee soochana ne ajnaata vaalikaa ko munha dhaanpa kar ek kone men vithaa rakkhaa hai| usake hridaya men ek vichitra shankaa samaa gaee hai, roma-roma men ek ajnaata bhaya kaa sanchaara ho gaya hai--na jaane kyaa hoga? usake mann men ve umange naheen hain, jo yuvatiyon kee aankhon men tirachhee chitavana bana kar, othon par madhura haasya bana kar; aur angon men aalasya vana kar prakata hoti hain| naheen, vahaan abhilaashaaen naheen hain; vahaan kevala shankaaen, chintaaen aur bheera kalpanaaen hain| yauvana kaa abhee taka poorna prakaasha naheen hua hai!

nirmalaa ne udaaseena bhaava se kaha--too ja, main na jaaoongee|

krishnaa--naheen, meree achchhi deedee; aaja zaroora chalo| dekho, kaisi thandee-thandee havaa chala rahi hai|

nirmalaa--meraa mann naheen chaahata, too chalee jaa|

krishnaa kee aankhen dabadabaa aaee| kaanpatee hui aavaaza se boli--aaja tuma kyon naheen chalatee? mujhase kyon naheen bolti? kyon idhara-udhara chhipee-chhipee phiratee ho? meraa jee akele baithe-baithe ghabaraataa hai| tuma na chalogee, to main bhee na jaaoongee| yaheen tumhaare saatha baithee rahoongee|

nirmalaa--aur jab main chalee jaaoongee, tab kyaa karegee? tab kisake saatha khelegee, kisake saatha ghoomane jaayagee; bata?

krishnaa--main bhee tumhaare saatha chalangee| akele mujhase yahaan na raha jaayagaa| nirmalaa muskaraa kar boli--tujhe ammaan na jaane dengee|

krishnaa--to main bhee tumhen na jaane doongee| tuma ammaan se kaha kyon naheen deteen ki main na jaaoongee?

nirmalaa--kaha to rahi hoon, koee sunata hai?

krishnaa--to kyaa yaha tumhaaraa ghar naheen hai?

nirmalaa--naheen; meraa ghar hota to koee kyon zabaradastee nikaala detaa|

krishnaa--isee tarah kisee din main bhee nikaala diya jaayaga?

nirmalaa--aur naheen kyaa too vaithee rahegee? hama ladakiyaan hain, hamaaraa ghar kaheen naheen hota|

krishnaa--chandara bhee nikaala diya jaayaga?

nirmalaa--chandara to ladka hai; use kaun nikaalega?

krishnaa--to ladakiyaan bahut kharaaba hoti hongee?

nirmalaa--kharaaba na hoti; to ghar se bhagaaee kyon jaateen|

krishnaa--chandara itana badamaasha hai, use koee naheen bhagaataa| hama tuma to koee badamaashee bhee naheen karateen|

ekaaeka chandara dhama-dhama karata hua chhata par aa pahuncha; aur nirmalaa ko dekha kar bola--achchha! aapa yahaan baithee hain| oho! ab to baaje bajenge, deedee dulhana banengee, paalakee par cha.dhegee, oho! oho!!

chandara kaa pooraa naama chandrabhaanu sinhaa tha| nirmalaa se teena saala chhota; aur krishnaa se do saala bada tha| nirmalaa--chandara! mujhe chi.dhaaoge, to abhee jaakara ammaan se doongee|

chandra--to chi.dhatee kyon ho? tuma bhee baaje sunana| oho, ho! ab aapa doolhana banengee! kyon kishanee, too baaje sunegee na? vaise baaje toone kabhee na sune honge|

krishnaa--kyaa bainda se bhee achchhe honge?

chandra--haan haan, bainda se bhee achchhe, hajaara gune achchhe, laakha gune achchhe| tuma jaano kya? ek bainda suna liya, to samajhane lagee, usase achchhe baaje hee naheen hote| baaje bajaane vaale laalalaala bardiyaan aur kaalee-kaalee topiyaan pahane honge| aise khoobasoorata maalooma honge ki tumase kyaa kahoon| aatashabaaziyaan bhee hongee; havaaiyaan aasamaana men u.da jaayangee; aur vahaan taaron men lagengee to laala, peele, hare, neele taare toota-toota kar girenge| bada mazaa aaegaa|

krishnaa--aur kyaa-kyaa hogaa chandara, bata de mere bhaiya!

chandra--mere saatha ghoomane chala, to raaste men saaree baaten bata doon| aise-aise tamaashe honge ki dekha kar teree aankhen khula jaayangee| havaa men u.datee hui pariyaan hongee; sachamucha kee pariyaan|

krishnaa--achchha chalo; lekin na bataaoge to maaroongee|

chandrabhaanu aur krishnaa chale gae; par nirmalaa akelee baithee raha gaee| krishnaa ke chale jaane se is samay use bada kshobha hua| krishnaa jise vaha praanon se bhee adhika pyaar karti thi, aaja itanee nithura ho gaee! akelee chho.da kar chalee gaee! baat koee na thi, lekin dukhee hridaya dukhatee hui aankh hai, jisamen havaa se bhee pee.daa hoti hai| nirmalaa ba.dee dera taka baithee rotee rahi| bhaaee-bahina, maataa-pitaa sabhee isee bhaoti mujhe bhoola jaayange, sab kee aankhen phir jaayangee! phir shaayada inhen dekhane ko bhee tarasa jaaoon!

chaaraa men phoola khile hue the| meethee-meethee sugandha aa rahi thi| chaita kee sheetala, manda sameera chala rahi thi| aakaasha men taare chhitake hue the| nirmalaa inheen shokamaya vichaaron men pa.dee-pa.dee so gaee aur aankh lagate hee usakaa mann svapna-desha men vicharane lagaa| kyaa dekhatee hai ki saamane ek nadee laharen maara rahi hai; aur vaha nadee ke kinaare naava kee vaata dekha rahi hai| sandhyaa kaa samay hai| andheraa kisee bhayankara jantu kee bhaanti ba.dhata chalaa aata hai| vaha ghora chintaa men pa.dee hui hai ki kaise yaha nadee paara hogee; kaise ghar pahunchoongee| ro rahi hai ki kaheen raat na ho jaaya, naheen to main akelee yahaan kaise rahoongee| ekaaeka use ek sundara naukaa ghaata kee ora aati dikhaaee detee hai| vaha khushi se uchhala pa.datee hai; aur jyonhee naava ghaata par aati hai, vaha us par cha.dhane ke lie ba.dhatee hai| lekin jyonhee naava ke patare par pair rakhana chaahatee hain, usakaa mallaaha bola uthata hai--tere lie yahaan jagaha naheen hai| vaha mallaaha kee khushaamada karti hai, usake pairon pa.datee hai, rotee hai; lekin vaha yaha kahe jata hai--tere lie yahaan jagaha naheen hai| ek kshana men naava khula jati hai| vaha chillaa-chillaa kar rone lagatee hai| nadee ke nirjana tata par raat bhara kaise rahegee, yaha socha, vaha nadee men kooda kar us naava ko paka.dana chaahatee hai ki itane men kaheen se aavaaza aati hai--thaharo-thaharo, nadee gaharee hai, dooba jaaogee| vaha naava tumhaare lie naheen hai, main aata hoon; meree naava par baitha jaao, main us paara pahunchaa doongaa| vaha bhayabheeta hokara idhara-udhara dekhatee hai ki yaha aavaaza kahaan se aaee| tho.dee dera ke baada ek chhotee-see dongee aati dikhaaee detee hai| usamen na paala hai, na patavaara; aur na mastoola| pendaa phata hua hai, takhte toote hue, naava men paanee bharaa hua hai; aur ek aadmi usamen se paanee uleecha raha hai| vaha usase kehti hai, yaha to tootee hui hai, yaha kaise paara lagegee? mallaaha kahata hai-- tumhaare lie yahee bhejee gaee hai, aakara baitha jaao| vaha ek kshana sochatee hai--isamen baithoon yaa na baithen| anta men vaha nishchaya karti hai, baitha jaaoon| yahaan akelee pa.dee rahane se naava men baitha jana phir bhee achchha hai| kisee bhayankara jantu ke peta men jaane se to yahee achchha hai ki nadee men dooba jaaoon| kaun jaane, naava paara pahuncha hee jaaya; yaha socha kar vaha praanon ko mutthee men lie hue naava par baitha jati hai| kuchha dera taka naava dagamagaatee hui chalatee hai; lekin prati kshana usamen paanee bharata jata hai| vaha bhee mallaaha ke saatha donon haathon se paanee uleechane lagatee hai, yahaan taka ki usake haath raha jaate hain; par paanee ba.dhata hee chalaa jata hai| aakhira naava chakara khaane lagatee hai; maalooma hota hai-ab doobee, ab doovee | tab vaha kisee adrishya sahaare ke lie donon haath phailaatee hai, naava neeche se khisaka jati hai| aur usake pair ukha.da jaate hain| vaha jora se chillaaee aur chillaate hee usakee aankhen khula gaee| dekha to maataa saamane kha.dee usakaa kandhaa paka.da kar hilaa rahi theen|
