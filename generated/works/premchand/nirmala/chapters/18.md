jiyaaraama ko apanee maa.N ke samaya ke doodha kaa svaada to yaada tha naheen, jo is aakshepa kaa javaaba detaa; aur na us samaya kee apanee soorata hee yaada thi, chupakaa raha jata| ina shubhaakaankshaaon kaa asara bhee padanaa svaabhaavika tha| jiyaaraama ko apane ghar vaalon se chidha hoti jati thi| munshee jee makaana neelaama ho jaane ke baada doosare ghar men utha aae, to kiraae kee phikra huee| nirmalaa ne makkhana banda kar diya| jaba vaha aamadanee hee naheen rahi, to vaha kharcha kaise rahataa? donon kahaara alaga kar die gae, jiyaaraama ko
padhaane vaale maastara ko bhee javaaba de diya gaya| jiyaaraama ko yaha katara-jyonta dhuree lagatee thi| java nirmalaa maike chalee gaee, to munshee jee ne doodha bhee banda kar diya| navajaata kanyaa kee chintaa abhee se unake sira savaara ho gaee thi!

jiyaaraama ne bigada kar kahaa-doodha banda karane se to aapakaa mahala vana raha hogaa, khaanaa bhee banda kar deejie!

munshee jee-doodha peene kaa shauka hai, to jaakara duhaa kyon naheen laate| paanee ke paise to mujhase na die jaaya.Nge!

jiyaaraama-main doodha duhaane jaaoo.N, koee skoola kaa ladakaa dekha le tava ?

munshee jee-taba kuchha naheen, kaha denaa apane lie doodha lie jata hoo.N| doodha laanaa koee choree naheen hai|

jiyaaraama-choree naheen hai! aapa hee ko koee doodha laate dekha le, to aapako sharma na aaegee?

munshee jee-bilakula naheen! main ne to inheen haathon se paanee kheenchaa hai, anaaja kee gathariyaa.N laayaa hoo.N| mere baapa lakhapatee naheen the|

jiyaaraama-mere baapa to gareeba naheen hain, main kyon doodha duhaane jaaoo.N? aakhira aapane kahaaron ko kyon javaaba de diya?

munshee jee- kyaa tumhen itanaa bhee naheen soojhataa ki meree aamadanee ava pahalee see naheen rahi, itane naadaana to naheen ho|

jiyaaraama-aakhira aapakee aamadanee kyon kama ho gaee?

munshee jee-jaba tumhen akla hee naheen hai,to kyaa samajhaaoo.N?

jiyaaraama-apane hee haathon na?

munshee jee ne cheekha kar kahaa-are ahamaka! yaha eeshvara kee marjee thi! apane haathon koee apanaa galaa kaatataa hai|

jiyaaraama-eeshvara to aapakaa vivaaha karane na aaya tha|

munshee jee aba jabta na kar sake; laala-laala aa.Nkhen nikaala kar bole-kyaa tuma aaja ladane ke lie kamara baa.Ndha kar aae ho? aakhira kisa birate para? meree rotiyaa.N to naheen chalaate| jaba is kaabila ho jana, to mujhe upadesha denaa| taba main suna loo.Ngaa| abhee tumako mujhe upadesha dene kaa adhikaara naheen hai| kuchha dinon adava aur tameeja seekho| tuma mere salaahakaara naheen ho ki main jo kaama karoo.N, usamen tumase salaaha loo.N| meree paidaa kee huee daulata hai, use jaise chaahoo.N kharcha kar sakataa hoo.N| tumako jabaana kholane kaa bhee haka naheen hai| agar phir tumane mujhase aisee beadabee kee, to nateejaa buraa hogaa| jaba mansaaraama jaisaa ratna khokara mere praaNa na nikale, to tumhaare bagaira main mara na jaaoo.Ngaa; samajha gae!

yaha kadee phatakaara paakara bhee jiyaaraama vahaa.N se na talaa| nihshata bhaava se bola- to kyaa aapa chaahate hain ki hamen chaahe kitanee hee takaleepha ho, mu.Nha na kholen! mujhase to yaha na hogaa|

us din se pitaa aur betaa men kisee na kisee baat para roja hee ek jhapata ho jati | munshee jee jyon-jyon taraha dete the,jiyaaraama
aur bhee shera hota jata tha | ek din jiyaaraama ne rukmiNee se yahaa.N taka kaha daalaa-baapa hai, yaha samajha kar chhoda detaa hoo.N, naheen to mere aise-aise saathee hain ki chaahoo.N to bhare baajaara men pitavaa doon| rukmiNee ne munshee jee se kaha diya| munshee jee ne prakata roopa se to cheparavaaee hee dikhaaee;, para unake mana men shankaa samaa gaee| shaama ko saira karna chhoda diya | yaha naee chintaa savaara ho gaee| isee dara se nirmalaa ko bhee na bulaate the ki yaha shaitaana usake saatha bhee yahee bartaava karegaa| jiyaaraama ek baara dabee javaana kaha bhee chukaa tha-dekhoo, aba kee kaise is ghar men aatee hain | doora hee se na dutkaara doon, to jiyaaraama naama naheen | buddoo miyaa.N kar hee kyaa lenge| munshee jee bhee khooba samajha gae the ki main isakaa kuchha bhee naheen

ek din doktara sinhaa ne jiyaaraama ko bulaa kar samajhaanaa shuroo kiya| jiyaaraama unakaa adaba karta tha| chupachaapa baithaa sunataa raha| jaba doktara saahaba ne anta men poochhaa, aakhira tuma chaahate kyaa ho? to vaha bola-saapha-saapha kaha doon na? buraa to na maaniegaa?

doktara saahaba ne badee mushkila se ha.Nsee roka kar kahaa-tumhaaree hatyaa karane ke lie unhen vyaaha karane kee kyaa jaroorata thi? yaha baat meree samajha men naheen aaee| binaa vivaaha kie bhee to vaha hatyaa kar sakate the!
