# Nirmala — Adhyay 18

**Munshi Premchand**

atharhavam parichchhed

jab hamare upar koi badi musibat a parti hai, to usse hamen sirph dukh hi nahin hotahamen dusron ke tane bhi sahne parte haim. janta ko hamare upar tippaniyam karne ka vah suavsar mil jata hai, jiske lie vah hamesha bechain rahti hai. NAmEmansaram kya mara, mano samaj ko un par avazen kasne ka bahana mil gaya. bhitar ki baten kaun jane? pratyaksh bat yah thi ki yah sab sauteli mam ki kartut hai. charon taraph yahi charcha thiishvar na kare, larkon ko sauteli mam se pala pade. jise apna bana-banaya ghar ujarna hoapne pyare bachchon ki gardan par chhuri pherni ho, vah bachchon ke rahte hue apna dusra byah kare. aisa kabhi nahin dekha ki saut ke ane par ghar tabah na ho gaya ho. vahi bap, jo bachchon par jan deta tha, saut ke ate hi unhim bachchon ka dushman ho jata haiuski mati hi badal jati hai. aisi devi ne janma hi nahin liya, jisne saut ke bachchon ko apna samjha ho!
mushkil yah thi ki log in tippaniyon par hi santusht na hote the. kuchh aise sajjan bhi the, jinhen ab NAmEJiyaram aur NAmESiyaram se khas pyaar ho gaya tha. ve donon balkon se badi hamadardi prakat karte; yaham tak ki do-ek mahilaen to unki mam ke shil aur svabhav ko yad karke amsu bahane lagti thim. hay-hay! bechari kya janti thi ki uske marte hi uske larlon ki yah durdasha hogi! ab dudh-makkhn kahe ko milta hoga?

NAmEJiyaram kahta-milta kyon nahin?

mahila kahti-milta hai, are beta! milna bhi kai tarah ka hota hai. pani vala dudh take ser ka manga kar rakh diya, piyo chahe na piyo-kaun puchhta hai? nahin to bechari naukar se dudh duhva kar mangvati thi, vah to chehra hi kah deta hai. dudh ki surat chhipi nahin rahti-vah surat hi nahin rahi!

NAmEJiyaram ko apni mam ke samay ke dudh ka svad to yad tha nahin, jo is akshep ka javab deta; aur na us samay ki apni surat hi yad thi, chupka rah jata. in shubhakankshaon ka asar bhi parna svabhavik tha. NAmEJiyaram ko apne ghar valon se chirh hoti jati thi. munshi ji makan nilam ho jane ke bad dusre ghar men uth ae, to kirae ki phikra hui. NAmENirmala ne makkhn band kar diya. jab vah amdani hi nahin rahi, to vah kharch kaise rahta? donon kahar alag kar die gae, NAmEJiyaram ko
parhane vale mastar ko bhi javab de diya gaya. NAmEJiyaram ko yah katar-jyont dhuri lagti thi. jav NAmENirmala maike chali gai, to munshi ji ne dudh bhi band kar diya. navjat ladki ki chinta abhi se unke sir savar ho gai thi!

NAmEJiyaram ne bigar kar kaha-dudh band karne se to apka mahal van raha hoga, khana bhi band kar dijie!

munshi ji-dudh pine ka shauk hai, to jakar duha kyon nahin late. pani ke paise to mujhse na die jayange!

NAmEJiyaram -maim dudh duhane jaum, koi skul ka larka dekh le tav?

munshi ji-tab kuchh nahin, kah dena apne lie dudh lie jata hum. dudh lana koi chori nahin hai.

NAmEJiyaram -chori nahin hai! ap hi ko koi dudh late dekh le, to apko sharm na aegi?

munshi ji-bilkul nahin! maim ne to inhim hathon se pani khincha hai, anaj ki gathriya laya hum. mere bap lakhpati nahin the.

NAmEJiyaram -mere bap to garib nahin haim, maim kyon dudh duhane jaum? akhir apne kaharon ko kyon javab de diya?

munshi ji- kya tumhen itna bhi nahin sujhta ki meri amdani av pahli si nahin rahi, itne nadan to nahin ho.

NAmEJiyaram -akhir apki amdani kyon kam ho gai?

munshi ji-jab tumhen akla hi nahin hai, to kya samajhaum?
yaham jindagi se tanga a gaya hum, mukdame kaun le; aur le bhi to taiyar kaun kare? vah dil hi nahin raha. ab to jindagi ke din pure kar raha hum. sare arman lallu ke sath chale gae!

NAmEJiyaram -apne hi hathon na?

munshi ji ne chikh kar kaha-are ahmak! yah ishvar ki marji thi! apne hathon koi apna gala katta hai.

NAmEJiyaram -ishvar to apka shadi karne na aya tha.

munshi ji ab jabt na kar sake; lal-lal ankhen nikal kar bole-kya tum aj larne ke lie kamar bandh kar ae ho? akhir kis birte par? meri rotiyam to nahin chalate. jab is qabil ho jana, to mujhe updesh dena. tab maim sun lunga. abhi tumko mujhe updesh dene ka adhikar nahin hai. kuchh dinon adav aur tamij sikho. tum mere salahkar nahin ho ki maim jo kam karum, usmen tumse salah lum. meri paida ki hui daulat hai, use jaise chahum kharch kar sakta hum. tumko jaban kholne ka bhi hak nahin hai. agar phir tumne mujhse aisi beadbi ki, to natija bura hoga. jab NAmEmansaram jaisa ratna khokar mere jan na nikle, to tumhare bagair maim mar na jaunga; samajh gae!

yah kari phatkar pakad bhi NAmEJiyaram vaham se na tala. nihshat bhav se bola- to kya ap chahte haim ki hamen chahe kitni hi takliph ho, mumh na kholen! mujhse to yah na hoga.
bhai sahab ko adab aur tamiz ka jo inam mila, uski mujhe bhukh nahin! mujh men jahar khakar jan dene ki himmat nahin! aise adav ko dur hi se dandavat karta hum!

munshi ji-tumhen aisi baten karte hue sharm nahin ati?

NAmEJiyaram -larke apne bujurgon hi ki nakal karte haim.

munshi ji ka gussa shant ho gaya! NAmEJiyaram par uska kuchh bhi asar na hoga, iska unhen yakin ho gaya. uth kar tahlane chale gae. aj unhen khabar mil gai ki is ghar ka jaldi hi sarvanash hone vala hai!!

us din se pita aur beta men kisi na kisi bat par roj hi ek jhapat ho jati. munshi ji jyon-jyon tarah dete the, NAmEJiyaram
aur bhi sher hota jata tha. ek din NAmEJiyaram ne NAmERukmini se yaham tak kah dala-bap hai, yah samajh kar chhod deta hum, nahin to mere aise-aise sathi haim ki chahum to bhare bajar men pitva dum. NAmERukmini ne munshi ji se kah diya. munshi ji ne prakat rup se to cheparavai hi dikhai;, par unke man men shanka sama gai. sham ko sair karna chhod diya. yah nai chinta savar ho gai. isi dar se NAmENirmala ko bhi na bulate the ki yah shaitan uske sath bhi yahi bartav karega. NAmEJiyaram ek bar dabi javan kah bhi chuka tha-dekhu, ab ki kaise is ghar men ati haim. dur hi se na dutkar dum, to NAmEJiyaram nam nahin. buddu miyam kar hi kya lenge. munshi ji bhi khub samajh gae the ki maim iska kuchh bhi nahin
kar sakta. koi bahar ka aadmi hota, to use pulis aur kanun ke shikje men kaste. apne larke ko kya karen? sach kaha hai-aadmi harta hai, to apne larkon hi se!

ek din NAmEDoctor Sinha ne NAmEJiyaram ko bula kar samjhana shuru kiya. NAmEJiyaram unka adab karta tha. chupchap baitha sunta raha. jab doktar sahab ne ant men puchha, akhir tum chahte kya ho? to vah bola-saph-saph kah dum na? bura to na maniega?

NAmESinha -nahin, jo kuchh tumhare dil men ho, saph-saph kah do.

NAmEJiyaram -to sunie, jab se bhaiya mare haim, mujhe pita ji ki surat dekh kar gussa ata hai. mujhe aisa malum hota hai ki inhim ne bhaiya ki hatya ki hai; aur ek din mauka pakad ham donon bhaiyon ki bhi hatya karenge. agar inki yah chah na hoti, to byah hi kyon karte?

doktar sahab ne badi mushkil se hamsi rok kar kaha-tumhari hatya karne ke lie unhen vyah karne ki kya jarurat thi? yah bat meri samajh men nahin ai. bina shadi kie bhi to vah hatya kar sakte the!

NAmEJiyaram -kabhi nahin! us vakt to unka dil hi kuchh aur tha- ham logon par jan dete the. ab mumh tak nahin dekhna chahte. inki yahi chah hai ki un donon praniyon ke siva ghar men aur
koi na rahe. av jo larke honge unke raste se ham logon ko hata. dena chahte haim, yahi un donon admiyo ki dili mansha hai. hamen tarah-tarah ki takliphe dekar bhaga dena chahte haim! isilie
ajkal muqdame nahin lete. ham donon bhai aj mar jay, to phir dekhie kaisi vahar hoti hai.

doktar-agar tumhen bhagana hi hota, to koi iljam laga kar ghar se nikal na dete?

jiya0-iske lie pahle hi se taiyar baitha hua hum.

doktar-sunen, kya taiyari ki hai?

jiya0-jab mauka aega, dekh lijiega.

yah kah kar NAmEJiyaram chalta hua. NAmEDoctor Sinha ne bahut pukara; par usne phir kar dekha bhi nahin.

kai din ke bad doktar sahab ki NAmEJiyaram se phir mulakat ho gai. doktar sahab sinema ke premi the, aur NAmEJiyaram ki to jan hi sinema men vasti thi. doktar sahab ne sinema par alochna karke NAmEJiyaram ko vaton men laga liya, aur apne ghar lae. khana ka samay a gaya tha, donon aadmi sath hi khana karne baithe. NAmEJiyaram ko yaham khana bahut svadisht laga; bola-mere yaham to jab se maharaj alag hua, khane ka maja hi jata raha. bua ji paka vaishnavi khana banati haim. jabaradasti kha leta hum par khane ki taraf takne ka ji nahin chahta.
doktar -- mer yaham to jabghar men khana pakta hai, to isse kahim svadisht hota hai. tumhari bua ji pyaz-lahsun na chhuti hongi.

jiya 0-- ham sahab, ubal kar rakh deti haim, lala ji ko iski parvah hi nahin ki koi khata hai ya nahin. isilie to maharaj ko alag kiya hai. agar rupae nahin haim, to roz gahne kahaa se bante haim?

doktar -- yah bat nahin hai NAmEJiyaram, unki amdani sachmuch bahut kam ho gai hai. tum unhen bahut diq karte ho.

jiya 0-- (hams kar) maim unhen diq karta hum! mujhse qasam le lijie, jo kabhi unse bolta bhi hum. mujhe badnam karne ka unhomne bira utha liya hai. besbab, bevjah pichhe pade rahte haim. yaham tak ki mere doston se bhi unhen chirh hai. ap hi sochie, doston ke bagair koi zinda rah sakta hai. maim koi luchcha nahin hum ki luchchon ki suhbat rakkhm; magar ap doston hi ke pichhe mujhe roz sataya karte haim. kal to maimne saf kah diya -- mere dost mere ghar aenge, kisi ko achchha lage ya bura! janab, koi ho; har vakta ki dhaums nahin sah sakta!!

doktar -- mujhe to bhai un par badi daya ati hai. yah zamana unke aram karne ka tha. ek to burhapa us par javan bete ka shok; sehat bhi achchha nahin. aisa aadmi kya kar sakta hai? vah jo kuchh thoda-bahut karta hai, vahi bahut hai. tum abhi aur kuchh nahin kar sakte, to kam se kam apne

bartav se to unhen khush rakh sakte ho. buddhon ko khush karna bahut mushkil kam nahin. yakin mano -- tumhara hams kar volna hi unhen khush karne ko kafi hai. itna puchhne men tumhara kya khach hota hai -- babu ji, apki tabiyat kaisi hai? vah tumhari yah uddandata dekh kar man hi man kurhte rahte haim. maim tumse sach kahta hum, kai bar ro chuke haim! unhomne man lo shadi karne men galti ki. ise vah bhi svikar karte haim; lekin tum apne pharz se kyon mumh morte ho. vah tumhare pita haim, tumhen unki seva karni chahiye. ek bat bhi aisi mumh se na nikalni chahiye, jisse unka dil dukhe. unhen yah khyal karne ka mauka hi kyon do ki sab meri kamai khane vale haim, bat puchhne vala koi nahin. meri umra tumse kahim jyada hai NAmEJiyaram; par aj tak maimne apne pita ji ki kisi bat ka javab nahin diya! vah aj bhi mujhe datte haim, sir jhuka kar sun leta hum. janta hum, yah jo kuchh kahte haim, mere bhale hi ko kahte haim. mam-pita se barh kar hamara hitaishi aur kaun ho sakta hai? unke karz se kaun mukt ho sakta hai?

NAmEJiyaram baitha rota raha! abhi uske sadbhavon ka sampurnatah lop na hua tha. apni durjanta use saf nazar a rahi thi. itni pachhtava use bahut dinon se na ai thi. rokar doktar sahab se kaha -- maim bahut hi lajjit hum. dusron ke bahkane men a gaya tha. ab ap meri zara bhi

shikayat na sunenge. ap pita ji se mere jurm kshama kara dijie. maim sachmuch bada abhaga hum. unhen maimne bahut sataya. unse kahie -- mere jurm kshama kar den, nahin maim mumh men kalikh laga kar kahim nikal jaunga -- dub marunga!

doktar sahab apni updesh-kushlata par phule na samae. NAmEJiyaram ko gale laga kar bida kiya!

NAmEJiyaram ghar pahuncha, to gyarah baj gaye the. munshi ji khana karke abhi bahar ae the. use dekhte hi bole -- jante ho kai baje haim? barah ka vakta hai!

NAmEJiyaram ne badi namrata se kaha -- NAmEDoctor Sinha mil gae. unke sath unke ghar tak chala gaya. unhomne khane ke lie zid ki; majaburan khana pada. isi se der ho gai.

munshi ji -- NAmEDoctor Sinha se dukhre rone gae hoge ya aur koi kam tha?

NAmEJiyaram ki namrata ka chautha bhag ud gaya; bola -- dukhre rone ki meri adat nahin hai.

munshi ji -- zara bhi nahin, tumhare mumh men to zaban hi nahin. mujhse jo log tumhari baten kaha karte haim, vah garha karte honge?

NAmEJiyaram -- aur dinon ki maim nahin kahta; lekin aj NAmEDoctor Sinha ke yaham maimne koi bat aisi nahin ki, jo is vakta apke samne na kar sakum.
munshi ji -- badi khushi ki bat hai. behad khushi hui! aj se guru-diksha le li hai kya?

NAmEJiyaram ki namrata ka ek chaturthamsh aur gayab ho gaya. sir utha kar bola -- aadmi bina guru-diksha lie hue bhi apni buraiyon par lajjit ho sakta hai. apna NAmESudha ra karne ke lie guru-mantra koi zaruri chiz nahin.

munshi ji -- ab to luchche na jama honge?

NAmEJiyaram -- ap kisi ko luchcha kyon kahte haim, jab tak aisa kahne ke lie apke pas koi sabut nahin?

munshi ji -- tumhare dost sab luchche-laphange haim. ek bhi bhala
aadmi nahin. maim tumse kai bar kah chuka ki unhen yaham mat jama kiya karo; par tumne suna nahin. aj maim akhiri bar kahe deta hum ki agar tumne un shohdon ko jama kiya, to mujhe pulis ki madad leni paregi.

NAmEJiyaram ki namrata ka ek chaturthamsh aur gayab ho gaya. pharak kar bola -- achchhi bat hai; pulis ki madad lijie! dekhen pulis kya karti hai? mere doston men adhe se jyada pulis ke afsaron hi ke bete haim. jab ap hi mera NAmESudha ra karne par tule hue haim, to maim bekar kyon takliph uthaum!

yah kahta hua NAmEJiyaram apne kamre men chala gaya, aur ek pal ke bad haramoniyam ke mithe svaron ki avaz bahar ane lagi!
sahridyata ka jalaya hua dipak nirdath vyanga ke ek jhonke se bujh gaya! ara hua ghora chumakarne se jod marne laga tha; par hantar parte hi phir ar gaya aur gari ko pichhe dhakelne laga!!
