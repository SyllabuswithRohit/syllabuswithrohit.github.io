# Nirmala — Adhyay 18

**Munshi Premchand**

athaarahavaan parichchhed

jab hamaare oopar koee badee vipatti aa padatee hai, to usase hamen keval dukh hee nahin hotaa — hamen doosaron ke taane bhi sahane padate hain. janataa ko hamaare oopar tippaniyaan karane ka vah suavasar mil jaataa hai, jisake lie vah hameshaa bechain rahatee hai. mansaaraam kyaa maraa, maano logon ki duniya ko un par aavaajen kasane ka bahaanaa mil gayaa. bheetar ki baaten kaun jaane? pratyaksh baat yah thi ki yah sab sautelee maan ki karatoot hai. chaaron taraph yahee charchaa thi — Bhagwan n kare, ladakon ko sautelee maan se paalaa pade. jise apanaa banaa-banaayaa ghar ujaadanaa ho — apane pyaare bachchon ki gardan par chhuree pheranee ho, vah bachchon ke rahate hue apanaa doosaraa byaah kare. aisaa kabhee nahin dekhaa ki saut ke aane par ghar tabaah n ho gayaa ho. vahee baap, jo bachchon par jaan detaa tha, saut ke aate hee unheen bachchon ka dushman ho jaataa hai — usakee mati hee badal jaatee hai. aisee devee ne paidaa honaa hee nahin liyaa, jisane saut ke bachchon ko apanaa samajhaa ho! mushkil yah thi ki log in tippaniyon par hee saantusht n hote the. kuchh aise sajjan bhi the, jinhen ab jiyaaraam aur siyaaraam se vishesh pyaar ho gayaa thaa. ve donon bachchaaon se badee sahaanubhooti saamane karate; yahan tak ki do-ek mahilaaen to unakee maataa ke sheel aur svabhaav ko yaad karake aansoo bahaane lagatee theen. haay-haay! bechaaree kyaa jaanatee thi ki usake marate hee usake laadalon ki yah durdashaa hogee! ab doodh-makkhan kaahe ko milataa hogaa?

jiyaaraam kahataa-milataa kyon nahin?

mahilaa kahatee-milataa hai, are betaa! milanaa bhi kaee tarah ka hotaa hai. paani vaalaa doodh take ser ka manggaa kar rakh diyaa, piyo chaahe n piyo-kaun poochhataa hai? nahin to bechaaree naukar se doodh duhavaa kar manggavaatee thi, vah to cheharaa hee kah detaa hai. doodh ki soorat chhipee nahin rahatee-vah soorat hee nahin rahee!

jiyaaraam ko apanee maan ke samay ke doodh ka svaad to yaad tha nahin, jo is aakshep ka javaab detaa; aur n us samay ki apanee soorat hee yaad thi, chupakaa rah jaataa. in shubhaakaangkshaon ka asar bhi padanaa svaabhaavik thaa. jiyaaraam ko apane ghar vaalon se chidh hotee jaatee thee. munshee jee makaan neelaam ho jaane ke baad doosare ghar mein uth aae, to kiraae ki fikr huee. Nirmala ne makkhan band kar diyaa. jab vah aamadanee hee nahin rahee, to vah kharch kaise rahataa? donon kahaar alag kar die gae, jiyaaraam ko padhaane vaale maastar ko bhi javaab de diyaa gayaa. jiyaaraam ko yah katar-jyont dhuree lagatee thee. jav Nirmala maike chalee gaee, to munshee jee ne doodh bhi band kar diyaa. navajaat kanyaa ki fikr abhee se unake sir savaar ho gaee thi!

jiyaaraam ne bigad kar kahaa-doodh band karane se to aapakaa mahal van rahaa hogaa, khana bhi band kar deejie!

munshee jee-doodh peene ka shauk hai, to jaakar duhaa kyon nahin laate. paani ke paise to mujhase n die jaayangge!

jiyaaraam-main doodh duhaane jaaoon, koee skool ka ladakaa dekh le tav?

munshee jee-tab kuchh nahin, kah denaa apane lie doodh lie jaataa hoon. doodh laanaa koee choree nahin hai.

jiyaaraam-choree nahin hai! aap hee ko koee doodh laate dekh le, to aapko sharm n aaegee?

munshee jee-bilakul nahin! main ne to inheen haathon se paani kheenchaa hai, anaaj ki gathariyaan laayaa hoon. mere baap lakhapatee nahin the.

jiyaaraam-mere baap to gareeb nahin hain, main kyon doodh duhaane jaaoon? aakhir aapane kahaaron ko kyon javaab de diyaa?

munshee jee- kyaa tumhe itanaa bhi nahin soojhataa ki meree aamadanee av pahalee see nahin rahee, itane naadaan to nahin ho.

jiyaaraam-aakhir aapakee aamadanee kyon kam ho gaee?

munshee jee-jab tumhe akl hee nahin hai, to kyaa samajhaaoon? yahan jindagee se tanggg aa gayaa hoon, mukadame kaun le; aur le bhi to taiyaar kaun kare? vah dil hee nahin rahaa. ab to jindagee ke din poore kar rahaa hoon. saare armaan lalloo ke saath chale gae!

jiyaaraam-apane hee haathon n?

munshee jee ne cheekh kar kahaa-are ahamak! yah Bhagwan ki marjee thi! apane haathon koee apanaa galaa kaatataa hai.

jiyaaraam-Bhagwan to aapakaa shaadee karane n kamaaeeaa thaa.

munshee jee ab jabt n kar sake; laal-laal aankhen nikaal kar bole-kyaa tum aaj ladane ke lie kamar baandh kar aae ho? aakhir kis birate par? meree rotiyaan to nahin chalaate. jab is kaabil ho jaanaa, to mujhe upadesh denaa. tab main sun loonggaa. abhee tumhe mujhe upadesh dene ka hak nahin hai. kuchh dinon adav aur tameej seekho. tum mere salaahakaar nahin ho ki main jo kaam karoon, usamen tumase salaah loon. meree paidaa ki huee daulat hai, use jaise chaahoon kharch kar sakataa hoon. tumhe jabaan kholane ka bhi hak nahin hai. agar phir tumane mujhase aisee beadabee ki, to nateejaa buraa hogaa. jab mansaaraam jaisaa ratn khokar mere praan n nikale, to tumhaare bagair main mar n jaaoonggaa; samajh gae!

yah kadee phatakaar paakar bhi jiyaaraam wahan se n talaa. nihshat bhaav se bolaa- to kyaa aap chaahate hain ki hamen chaahe kitanee hee takaleeph ho, munh n kholen! mujhase to yah n hogaa. bhaaee Sahab ko adab aur tameej ka jo inaam milaa, usakee mujhe bhookh nahin! mujh mein jahar khaakar praan dene ki himmat nahin! aise adav ko door hee se sajaavat karataa hoon!

munshee jee-tumhe aisee baaten karate hue sharm nahin aatee?

jiyaaraam-ladake apane bujurgon hee ki nakal karate hain.

munshee jee ka krodh shaant ho gayaa! jiyaaraam par usakaa kuchh bhi asar n hogaa, isakaa unhen yakeen ho gayaa. uth kar tahalane chale gae. aaj unhen khabar mil gaee ki is ghar ka jaldee hee sarvanaash hone vaalaa hai!!

us din se pitaa aur putr mein kisee n kisee baat par roj hee ek jhapat ho jaatee. munshee jee jyon-jyon tarah dete the, jiyaaraam
aur bhi sher hotaa jaataa tha. ek din jiyaaraam ne rukminee se yahan tak kah daalaa-baap hai, yah samajh kar chhod detaa hoon, nahin to mere aise-aise saathee hain ki chaahoon to bhare baajaar mein pitavaa doon. rukminee ne munshee jee se kah diyaa. munshee jee ne saamane roop se to cheparavaaee hee dikhaaee;, par unake man mein shanggkaa samaa gaee. shaam ko sair karanaa chhod diyaa. yah naee fikr savaar ho gaee. isee dar se Nirmala ko bhi n bulaate the ki yah shaitaan usake saath bhi yahee bartaav karegaa. jiyaaraam ek baar dabee javaan kah bhi chukaa tha-dekhoo, ab ki kaise is ghar mein aatee hain. door hee se n dutkaar doon, to jiyaaraam naam nahin. buddoo miyaan kar hee kyaa lengge. munshee jee bhi khoob samajh gae the ki main isakaa kuchh bhi nahin kar sakataa. koee baahar ka aadmi hotaa, to use pulis aur kaanoon ke shikaje mein kasate. apane ladake ko kyaa karen? sach kahaa hai-aadmi haarataa hai, to apane ladakon hee se!

ek din doktar sinhaa ne jiyaaraam ko bulaa kar samajhaanaa shuroo kiyaa. jiyaaraam unakaa adab karataa thaa. chupachaap baithaa sunataa rahaa. jab doktar Sahab ne ant mein poochhaa, aakhir tum chaahate kyaa ho? to vah bolaa-saaph-saaph kah doon n? buraa to n maaniegaa?

sinhaa-nahin, jo kuchh tumhaare dil mein ho, saaph-saaph kah do.

jiyaaraam-to sunie, jab se bhaiyaa mare hain, mujhe pitaa jee ki soorat dekh kar krodh aataa hai. mujhe aisaa maaloom hotaa hai ki inheen ne bhaiyaa ki hatyaa ki hai; aur ek din maukaa paakar ham donon bhaaiyon ki bhi hatyaa karengge. agar inakee yah ichchhaa n hotee, to byaah hee kyon karate?

doktar Sahab ne badee mushkil se hansee rok kar kahaa-tumhaaree hatyaa karane ke lie unhen vyaah karane ki kyaa jaroorat thi? yah baat meree samajh mein nahin aaee. binaa shaadee kie bhi to vah hatyaa kar sakate the!

jiyaaraam-kabhee nahin! us vakt to unakaa dil hee kuchh aur tha- ham logon par jaan dete the. ab munh tak nahin dekhanaa chaahate. inakee yahee ichchhaa hai ki un donon praaniyon ke sivaa ghar mein aur koee n rahe. av jo ladake hongge unake raaste se ham logon ko hataa. denaa chaahate hain, yahee un donon aadamiyon ki dilee manshaa hai. hamen tarah-tarah ki takaleephen dekar bhagaa denaa chaahate hain! iseelie
aajakal mukadame nahin lete. ham donon bhaaee aaj mar jaay, to phir dekhie kaisee vahaar hotee hai.

doktar-agar tumhe bhagaanaa hee hotaa, to koee iljaam lagaa kar ghar se nikaal n dete?

jiyaa0-isake lie pahale hee se taiyaar baithaa huaa hoon.

doktar-sunen, kyaa taiyaaree ki hai?

jiyaa0-jab maukaa aaegaa, dekh leejiegaa.

yah kah kar jiyaaraam chalataa huaa. doktar sinhaa ne bahut pukaaraa; par usane phir kar dekhaa bhi naheen.

kaee din ke baad doktar Sahab ki jiyaaraam se phir mulaakaat ho gaee. doktar Sahab sinemaa ke pyaaree the, aur jiyaaraam ki to jaan hee sinemaa mein vasatee thee. doktar Sahab ne sinemaa par aalochanaa karake jiyaaraam ko vaaton mein lagaa liyaa, aur apane ghar laae. khana ka samay aa gayaa tha, donon aadmi saath hee khana karane baithe. jiyaaraam ko yahan khana bahut svaadisht lagaa; bolaa-mere yahan to jab se mahaaraaj alag huaa, khaane ka majaa hee jaataa rahaa. buaa jee pakaa vaishnavee khana banaatee hain. jabaradastee khaa letaa hoon par khaane ki taraph taakane ka jee nahin chaahataa. doktar -- mer yahan to jabaghar mein khana pakataa hai, to isase kaheen svaadisht hotaa hai. tumhaaree buaa jee pyaaj-lahasun n chhootee honggee.

jiyaa 0-- haan Sahab, ubaal kar rakh detee hain, laalaa jee ko isakee parvaah hee nahin ki koee khaataa hai ya naheen. iseelie to mahaaraaj ko alag kiyaa hai. agar rupaye nahin hain, to roj gahane kahaae se banate hain?

doktar -- yah baat nahin hai jiyaaraam, unakee aamadanee sachamuch bahut kam ho gaee hai. tum unhen bahut dik karate ho.

jiyaa 0-- (hans kar) main unhen dik karataa hoon! mujhase kasam le leejie, jo kabhee unase bolataa bhi hoon. mujhe badanaam karane ka unhonne beedaa uthaa liyaa hai. besabab, bevajah peechhe pade rahate hain. yahan tak ki mere doston se bhi unhen chidh hai. aap hee sochie, doston ke bagair koee jindaa rah sakataa hai. main koee luchchaa nahin hoon ki luchchon ki suhabat rakkhan; magar aap doston hee ke peechhe mujhe roj sataayaa karate hain. kal to mainne saaph kah diyaa -- mere dost mere ghar aaengge, kisee ko achchha lage ya buraa! janaab, koee ho; har vakt ki dhauns nahin sah sakataa!!

doktar -- mujhe to bhaee un par badee taras aatee hai. yah jamaanaa unake aaraam karane ka thaa. ek to budhaapaa us par javaan bete ka shok; sehat bhi achchha naheen. aisaa aadmi kyaa kar sakataa hai? vah jo kuchh thodaa-bahut karataa hai, vahee bahut hai. tum abhee aur kuchh nahin kar sakate, to kam se kam apane aacharan se to unhen khush rakh sakate ho. buddhon ko khush karanaa bahut kathin kaam naheen. yakeen maano -- tumhaaraa hans kar volanaa hee unhen khush karane ko kaaphee hai. itanaa poochhane mein tumhaaraa kyaa khach hotaa hai -- baaboo jee, aapakee tabeeyat kaisee hai? vah tumhaaree yah udsajaataa dekh kar man hee man kudhate rahate hain. main tumase sach kahataa hoon, kaee baar ro chuke hain! unhonne maan lo shaadee karane mein galatee kee. ise vah bhi maan lenaa karate hain; lekin tum apane karttavy se kyon munh modate ho. vah tumhaare pitaa hain, tumhe unakee sevaa karanee chaahie. ek baat bhi aisee munh se n nikaalanee chahiye, jisase unakaa dil dukhe. unhen yah khyaal karane ka maukaa hee kyon do ki sab meree kamaaee khaane vaale hain, baat poochhane vaalaa koee naheen. meree umr tumase kaheen jyaadaa hai jiyaaraam; par aaj tak mainne apane pitaa jee ki kisee baat ka javaab nahin diyaa! vah aaj bhi mujhe daatate hain, sir jhukaa kar sun letaa hoon. jaanataa hoon, yah jo kuchh kahate hain, mere bhale hee ko kahate hain. maataa-pitaa se badh kar hamaaraa hitaishee aur kaun ho sakataa hai? unake karz se kaun mukt ho sakataa hai?

jiyaaraam baithaa rotaa rahaa! abhee usake sadbhaavon ka pooraatah lop n huaa thaa. apanee durjanataa use saaph najar aa rahee thee. itanee glaani use bahut dinon se n aaee thee. rokar doktar Sahab se kahaa -- main bahut hee lajjit hoon. doosaron ke bahakaane mein aa gayaa thaa. ab aap meree jaraa bhi shikaayat n sunengge. aap pitaa jee se mere jurm kshmaa karaa deejie. main sachamuch badaa abhaagaa hoon. unhen mainne bahut sataayaa. unase kahie -- mere jurm kshmaa kar den, nahin main munh mein kaalikh lagaa kar kaheen nikal jaaoonggaa -- doob maroonggaa!

doktar Sahab apanee upadesh-kushalataa par phoole n samaae. jiyaaraam ko gale lagaa kar bidaa kiyaa!

jiyaaraam ghar pahunchaa, to gyaarah baj gaye the. munshee jee khana karake abhee baahar aae the. use dekhate hee bole -- jaanate ho kai baje hain? baarah ka vakt hai!

jiyaaraam ne badee narmee se kahaa -- doktar sinhaa mil gae. unake saath unake ghar tak chalaa gayaa. unhonne khaane ke lie jid ki; majabooran khana padaa. isee se der ho gaee.

munshee jee -- doktar sinhaa se dukhade rone gae hoge ya aur koee kaam tha?

jiyaaraam ki narmee ka chauthaa bhaag ud gayaa; bolaa -- dukhade rone ki meree aadat nahin hai.

munshee jee -- jaraa bhi nahin, tumhaare munh mein to jabaan hee naheen. mujhase jo log tumhaaree baaten kahaa karate hain, vah gadhaa karate hongge?

jiyaaraam -- aur dinon ki main nahin kahataa; lekin aaj doktar sinhaa ke yahan mainne koee baat aisee nahin ki, jo is vakt aapake saamane n kar sakoon. munshee jee -- badee khushi ki baat hai. behad khushi huee! aaj se guru-deeksh le lee hai kyaa?

jiyaaraam ki narmee ka ek chaturthaansh aur gaayab ho gayaa. sir uthaa kar bolaa -- aadmi binaa guru-deeksh lie hue bhi apanee buraaiyon par lajjit ho sakataa hai. apanaa sudhaar karane ke lie guru-mantr koee zaroori cheej naheen.

munshee jee -- ab to luchche n jamaa hongge?

jiyaaraam -- aap kisee ko luchchaa kyon kahate hain, jab tak aisaa kahane ke lie aapake paas koee saboot nahin?

munshee jee -- tumhaare dost sab luchche-laphanggge hain. ek bhi bhalaa
aadmi naheen. main tumase kaee baar kah chukaa ki unhen yahan mat jamaa kiyaa karo; par tumane sunaa naheen. aaj main aakhiree baar kahe detaa hoon ki agar tumane un shohadon ko jamaa kiyaa, to mujhe pulis ki madad lenee padegee.

jiyaaraam ki narmee ka ek chaturthaansh aur gaayab ho gayaa. phadak kar bolaa -- achchhi baat hai; pulis ki madad leejie! dekhen pulis kyaa karatee hai? mere doston mein aadhe se jyaadaa pulis ke aphasaron hee ke bete hain. jab aap hee meraa sudhaar karane par tule hue hain, to main vyarth kyon takaleeph uthaaoon!

yah kahataa huaa jiyaaraam apane kamare mein chalaa gayaa, aur ek pal ke baad haaramoniyam ke meethe svaron ki aavaaj baahar aane lagee! sadilataa ka jalaayaa huaa deepak nirdath vyanggg ke ek jhongke se bujh gayaa! adaa huaa ghodaa chumakaarane se jor maarane lagaa tha; par hantar padate hee phir ad gayaa aur gaadee ko peechhe dhakelane lagaa!!
