# Nirmala — Adhyay 18

**Munshi Premchand**

athaarahavaan parichchheda

jab hamaare oopara koee ba.dee vipatti aa pa.datee hai, to usase hamen kevala dukha hee naheen hota—hamen doosaron ke taane bhee sahane pa.date hain| janata ko hamaare oopara tippaniyaan karane kaa vaha suavasara mila jata hai, jisake lie vaha hameshaa bechaina rahatee hai| mansaaraama kyaa mara, maano samaaja ko una par aavaazen kasane kaa bahaanaa mila gaya| bheetara kee baaten kaun jaane? pratyaksha baat yaha thi ki yaha sab sautelee maan kee karatoota hai| chaaron tarapha yahee charchaa thi—eeshvara na kare, ladakon ko sautelee maan se paalaa pa.de| jise apana bana-banaayaa ghar uja.dana ho—apane pyaare bachchon kee gardana par chhuree pheranee ho, vaha bachchon ke rahate hue apana doosaraa byaaha kare| aisaa kabhee naheen dekha ki sauta ke aane par ghar tabaaha na ho gaya ho| vahee baapa, jo bachchon par jaana detaa tha, sauta ke aate hee unheen bachchon kaa dushmana ho jata hai—usakee mati hee badala jati hai| aisee devee ne janma hee naheen liya, jisane sauta ke bachchon ko apana samajhaa ho! mushkila yaha thi ki log ina tippaniyon par hee saantushta na hote the| kuchha aise sajjana bhee the, jinhen ab jiyaaraama aur siyaaraama se vishesha sneha ho gaya tha| ve donon baalakon se ba.dee sahaanubhooti prakata karte; yahaan taka ki do-ek mahilaaen to unakee maataa ke sheela aur svabhaava ko yaada karake aansoo bahaane lagatee theen| haaya-haaya! bechaaree kyaa jaanatee thi ki usake marate hee usake la.dalon kee yaha durdashaa hogee! ab doodha-makkhana kaahe ko milata hoga?

jiyaaraama kahata-milata kyon naheen?

mahilaa kehti-milata hai, are beta! milana bhee kaee tarah kaa hota hai| paanee vaalaa doodha take sera kaa mangaa kar rakha diya, piyo chaahe na piyo-kaun poochhata hai? naheen to bechaaree naukara se doodha duhavaa kar mangavaatee thi, vaha to cheharaa hee kaha detaa hai| doodha kee soorata chhipee naheen rahatee-vaha soorata hee naheen rahi!

jiyaaraama ko apanee maan ke samay ke doodha kaa svaada to yaada tha naheen, jo is aakshepa kaa uttara deta; aur na us samay kee apanee soorata hee yaada thi, chupakaa raha jata| ina shubhaakaankshaaon kaa asara bhee pa.dana svaabhaavika tha| jiyaaraama ko apane ghar vaalon se chi.dha hoti jati thi| munshee jee makaana neelaama ho jaane ke baada doosare ghar men utha aae, to kiraae kee phikra hui| nirmalaa ne makkhana banda kar diya| jab vaha aamadanee hee naheen rahi, to vaha kharcha kaise rahata? donon kahaara alaga kar die gae, jiyaaraama ko pa.dhaane vaale maastara ko bhee javaaba de diya gaya| jiyaaraama ko yaha katara-jyonta dhuree lagatee thi| java nirmalaa maike chalee gaee, to munshee jee ne doodha bhee banda kar diya| navajaata kanyaa kee chintaa abhee se unake sira savaara ho gaee thi!

jiyaaraama ne biga.da kar kaha-doodha banda karane se to aapakaa mahala vana raha hoga, bhojana bhee banda kar deejie!

munshee jee-doodha peene kaa shauka hai, to jaakara duhaa kyon naheen laate| paanee ke paise to mujhase na die jaayange!

jiyaaraama-main doodha duhaane jaaoon, koee skoola kaa ladka dekha le tava ?

munshee jee-tab kuchha naheen, kaha dena apane lie doodha lie jata hoon| doodha laanaa koee choree naheen hai|

jiyaaraama-choree naheen hai! aapa hee ko koee doodha laate dekha le, to aapako sharma na aaegee?

munshee jee-bilakula naheen! main ne to inheen haathon se paanee kheenchaa hai, anaaja kee gathariyaan laayaa hoon| mere baapa lakhapatee naheen the|

jiyaaraama-mere baapa to gareeba naheen hain, main kyon doodha duhaane jaaoon? aakhira aapane kahaaron ko kyon javaaba de diya?

munshee jee- kyaa tumhen itana bhee naheen soojhata ki meree aamadanee ava pahalee see naheen rahi, itane naadaana to naheen ho|

jiyaaraama-aakhira aapakee aamadanee kyon kama ho gaee?

munshee jee-jab tumhen akla hee naheen hai,to kyaa samajhaaoon? yahaan zindagi se tanga aa gaya hoon, mukadame kaun le; aur le bhee to taiyaara kaun kare? vaha dil hee naheen raha| ab to zindagi ke din poore kar raha hoon| saare armaana lalloo ke saatha chale gae!

jiyaaraama-apane hee haathon na?

munshee jee ne cheekha kar kaha-are ahamaka! yaha eeshvara kee marjee thi! apane haathon koee apana galaa kaatata hai|

jiyaaraama-eeshvara to aapakaa vivaaha karane na aaya tha|

munshee jee ab jabta na kar sake; laala-laala aankhen nikaala kar bole-kyaa tuma aaja la.dane ke lie kamara baandha kar aae ho? aakhira kisa birate par? meree rotiyaan to naheen chalaate| jab is qaabila ho jaana, to mujhe upadesha dena| tab main suna loongaa| abhee tumako mujhe upadesha dene kaa adhikaara naheen hai| kuchha dinon adava aur tameeja seekho| tuma mere salaahakaara naheen ho ki main jo kaama karoon, usamen tumase salaaha loon| meree paidaa kee hui daulata hai, use jaise chaahoon kharcha kar sakata hoon| tumako jabaana kholane kaa bhee haka naheen hai| agar phir tumane mujhase aisee beadabee kee, to nateejaa buraa hogaa| jab mansaaraama jaisaa ratna khokara mere praana na nikale, to tumhaare bagaira main mara na jaaoonga; samajha gae!

yaha ka.dee phatakaara paakara bhee jiyaaraama vahaan se na talaa| nihshata bhaava se bola- to kyaa aapa chaahate hain ki hamen chaahe kitanee hee takaleepha ho, munha na kholen! mujhase to yaha na hogaa| bhaaee saahaba ko adaba aur tameeza kaa jo inaama mila, usakee mujhe bhookha naheen ! mujha men jahara khaakara praana dene kee himmata naheen! aise adava ko doora hee se dandavat karata hoon!

munshee jee-tumhen aisee baaten karte hue sharma naheen aati ?

jiyaaraama-ladke apane bujurgon hee kee nakala karte hain |

munshee jee kaa krodha shaanta ho gaya ! jiyaaraama par usakaa kuchha bhee asara na hoga, isakaa unhen yakeena ho gaya | utha kar tahalane chale gae | aaja unhen soochana mila gaee ki is ghar kaa sheeghra hee sarvanaasha hone vaalaa hai !!

us din se pitaa aur putra men kisee na kisee baat par roja hee ek jhapata ho jati | munshee jee jyon-jyon tarah dete the,jiyaaraama
aur bhee shera hota jata tha | ek din jiyaaraama ne rukminee se yahaan taka kaha daalaa-baapa hai, yaha samajha kar chho.da detaa hoon, naheen to mere aise-aise saathee hain ki chaahoon to bhare baajaara men pitavaa doon| rukminee ne munshee jee se kaha diya| munshee jee ne prakata roopa se to cheparavaaee hee dikhaaee;, par unake mann men shankaa samaa gaee| shaama ko saira karana chho.da diya | yaha naee chintaa savaara ho gaee| isee bhaya se nirmalaa ko bhee na bulaate the ki yaha shaitaana usake saatha bhee yahee bartaava karegaa| jiyaaraama ek baara dabee javaana kaha bhee chukaa tha-dekhoo, ab kee kaise is ghar men aati hain | doora hee se na dutkaara doon, to jiyaaraama naama naheen | buddoo miyaan kar hee kyaa lenge| munshee jee bhee khooba samajha gae the ki main isakaa kuchha bhee naheen kar sakata| koee baahara kaa aadmi hota, to use pulisa aur kaanoona ke shikaje men kasate| apane ladke ko kyaa karen? sacha kaha hai-aadmi haarata hai, to apane ladakon hee se!

ek din daoktara sinhaa ne jiyaaraama ko bulaa kar samajhaanaa shuroo kiya| jiyaaraama unakaa adaba karata tha| chupachaapa baithaa sunata raha| jab daoktara saahaba ne anta men poochha, aakhira tuma chaahate kyaa ho? to vaha bola-saapha-saapha kaha doon na? buraa to na maaniega?

sinhaa-naheen, jo kuchha tumhaare dil men ho, saapha-saapha kaha do|

jiyaaraama-to sunie, jab se bhaiyaa mare hain, mujhe pitaa jee kee soorata dekha kar krodha aata hai|mujhe aisaa maalooma hota hai ki inheen ne bhaiyaa kee hatyaa kee hai; aur ek din maukaa paakara hama donon bhaaiyon kee bhee hatyaa karenge| agar inakee yaha ichchhaa na hoti, to byaaha hee kyon karte?

daoktara saahaba ne ba.dee mushkila se hansee roka kar kaha-tumhaaree hatyaa karane ke lie unhen vyaaha karane kee kyaa jaroorata thi? yaha baat meree samajha men naheen aaee| binaa vivaaha kie bhee to vaha hatyaa kar sakate the!

jiyaaraama-kabhee naheen! us vakta to unakaa dil hee kuchha aur tha- hama logon par jaana dete the| ab munha taka naheen dekhana chaahate| inakee yahee ichchhaa hai ki una donon praaniyon ke sivaa ghar men aur koee na rahe| ava jo ladke honge unake raaste se hama logon ko hata. dena chaahate hain, yahee una donon aadamiyon kee dilee manshaa hai| hamen tarah-tarah kee takaleephen dekara bhagaa dena chaahate hain! iseelie
aajakala muqadame naheen lete| hama donon bhaaee aaja mara jaaya, to phir dekhie kaisi vahaara hoti hai|

daoktara-agar tumhen bhagaanaa hee hota, to koee iljaama lagaa kar ghar se nikaala na dete?

jiyaa0-isake lie pahale hee se taiyaara baithaa hua hoon|

daoktara-sunen, kyaa taiyaaree kee hai?

jiyaa0-jab maukaa aaega, dekha leejiegaa|

yaha kaha kar jiyaaraama chalata hua| daoktara sinhaa ne bahut pukaara; par usane phir kar dekha bhee naheen|

kaee din ke baada daoktara saahaba kee jiyaaraama se phir mulaakaata ho gaee| daoktara saahaba sinemaa ke premee the, aur jiyaaraama kee to jaana hee sinemaa men vasatee thi| daoktara saahaba ne sinemaa par aalochana karake jiyaaraama ko vaaton men lagaa liya, aur apane ghar laae| bhojana kaa samay aa gaya tha, donon aadmi saatha hee bhojana karane baithe| jiyaaraama ko yahaan bhojana bahut svaadishta laga; bola-mere yahaan to jab se mahaaraaja alaga hua, khaane kaa majaa hee jata raha| buaa jee pakaa vaishnavee bhojana banaatee hain| jabaradastee khaa letaa hoon par khaane kee tarafa taakane kaa jee naheen chaahata| daoktara -- mera yahaan to jabaghara men khaanaa pakata hai, to isase kaheen svaadishta hota hai| tumhaaree buaa jee pyaaza-lahasuna na chhootee hongee|

jiya 0-- haan saahaba, ubaala kar rakha detee hain, laalaa jee ko isakee parvaaha hee naheen ki koee khaataa hai yaa naheen| iseelie to mahaaraaja ko alaga kiya hai| agar rupae naheen hain, to roza gahane kaha.c se banate hain?

daoktara -- yaha baat naheen hai jiyaaraama, unakee aamadanee sachamucha bahut kama ho gaee hai| tuma unhen bahut diqa karte ho|

jiya 0-- (hansa kar) main unhen diqa karata hoon! mujhase qasama le leejie, jo kabhee unase bolata bhee hoon| mujhe badanaama karane kaa unhonne bee.daa uthaa liya hai| besababa, bevajaha peechhe pa.de rahate hain| yahaan taka ki mere doston se bhee unhen chi.dha hai| aapa hee sochie, doston ke baGaira koee zindaa raha sakata hai| main koee luchchaa naheen hoon ki luchchon kee suhabata rakkhan; magar aapa doston hee ke peechhe mujhe roza sataayaa karte hain| kala to mainne saafa kaha diya -- mere dosta mere ghar aaenge, kisee ko achchha lage yaa bura! janaaba, koee ho; hara vakta kee dhaunsa naheen saha sakata!!

daoktara -- mujhe to bhaee una par ba.dee dayaa aati hai| yaha zamaanaa unake aaraama karane kaa tha| ek to bu.dhaapaa us par javaana bete kaa shoka; svaasthya bhee achchha naheen| aisaa aadmi kyaa kar sakata hai? vaha jo kuchha tho.daa-bahut karata hai, vahee bahut hai| tuma abhee aur kuchha naheen kar sakate, to kama se kama apane aacharana se to unhen prasanna rakha sakate ho| buddhon ko prasanna karana bahut kathina kaama naheen| yakeena maano -- tumhaaraa hansa kar volana hee unhen Kusha karane ko kaafee hai| itana poochhane men tumhaaraa kyaa khacha hota hai -- baaboo jee, aapakee tabeeyata kaisi hai? vaha tumhaaree yaha uddandata dekha kar mann hee mann ku.dhate rahate hain| main tumase sacha kahata hoon, kaee baara ro chuke hain! unhonne maana lo shaadee karane men Galatee kee| ise vaha bhee sveekaara karte hain; lekin tuma apane karttavya se kyon munha mo.date ho| vaha tumhaare pitaa hain, tumhen unakee sevaa karanee chaahie| ek baat bhee aisee munha se na nikaalanee chaahie, jisase unakaa dil dukhe| unhen yaha khayal karane kaa maukaa hee kyon do ki sab meree kamaaee khaane vaale hain, baat poochhane vaalaa koee naheen| meree umra tumase kaheen zyada hai jiyaaraama; par aaja taka mainne apane pitaa jee kee kisee baat kaa javaaba naheen diya! vaha aaja bhee mujhe daatate hain, sira jhukaa kar suna letaa hoon| jaanata hoon, yaha jo kuchha kehte hain, mere bhale hee ko kehte hain| maataa-pitaa se ba.dha kar hamaaraa hitaishee aur kaun ho sakata hai? unake rina se kaun mukta ho sakata hai?

jiyaaraama baithaa rotaa raha! abhee usake sadbhaavon kaa sampoornatah lopa na hua tha| apanee durjanata use saafa nazara aa rahi thi| itanee glaani use bahut dinon se na aaee thi| rokara daoktara saahaba se kaha -- main bahut hee lajjita hoon| doosaron ke bahakaane men aa gaya tha| ab aapa meree zaraa bhee shikaayata na sunenge| aapa pitaa jee se mere aparaadha kshamaa karaa deejie| main sachamucha bada abhaagaa hoon| unhen mainne bahut sataayaa| unase kahie -- mere aparaadha kshamaa kar den, naheen main munha men kaalikha lagaa kar kaheen nikala jaaoongaa -- dooba maroonga!

daoktara saahaba apanee upadesha-kushalata par phoole na samaae| jiyaaraama ko gale lagaa kar bidaa kiya!

jiyaaraama ghar pahuncha, to gyaaraha baja gaye the| munshee jee bhojana karake abhee baahara aae the| use dekhate hee bole -- jaanate ho kai baje hain? baaraha kaa vakta hai!

jiyaaraama ne ba.dee namrata se kaha -- daoktara sinhaa mila gae| unake saatha unake ghar taka chalaa gaya| unhonne khaane ke lie zida kee; majabooran khaanaa pada| isee se dera ho gaee|

munshee jee -- daoktara sinhaa se dukha.de rone gae hoge yaa aur koee kaama tha?

jiyaaraama kee namrata kaa chauthaa bhaaga u.da gaya; bola -- dukha.de rone kee meree aadata naheen hai|

munshee jee -- zaraa bhee naheen, tumhaare munha men to zabaana hee naheen| mujhase jo log tumhaaree baaten kaha karte hain, vaha ga.dhaa karte honge?

jiyaaraama -- aur dinon kee main naheen kahata; lekin aaja daoktara sinhaa ke yahaan mainne koee baat aisee naheen kee, jo is vakta aapake saamane na kar sakoon| munshee jee -- ba.dee Kushee kee baat hai| behada Kushee hui! aaja se guru-deekshaa le lee hai kya?

jiyaaraama kee namrata kaa ek chaturthaansha aur gaayaba ho gaya| sira uthaa kar bola -- aadmi binaa guru-deekshaa lie hue bhee apanee buraaiyon par lajjita ho sakata hai| apana sudhaara karane ke lie guru-mantra koee zaroori cheeza naheen|

munshee jee -- ab to luchche na jamaa honge?

jiyaaraama -- aapa kisee ko luchchaa kyon kehte hain, jab taka aisaa kahane ke lie aapake paasa koee pramaana naheen?

munshee jee -- tumhaare dosta sab luchche-laphange hain| ek bhee bhalaa
aadmi naheen| main tumase kaee baara kaha chukaa ki unhen yahaan mata jamaa kiya karo; par tumane sunaa naheen| aaja main aakhiree baara kahe detaa hoon ki agar tumane una shohadon ko jamaa kiya, to mujhe pulisa kee sahaayata lenee pa.degee|

jiyaaraama kee namrata kaa ek chaturthaansha aur Gaayaba ho gaya| pha.daka kar bola -- achchhi baat hai; pulisa kee sahaayata leejie! dekhen pulisa kyaa karti hai? mere doston men aadhe se zyada pulisa ke afasaron hee ke bete hain| jab aapa hee meraa sudhaara karane par tule hue hain, to main vyartha kyon kashta uthaaoon!

yaha kahata hua jiyaaraama apane kamare men chalaa gaya, aur ek kshana ke baada haaramoniyama ke meethe svaron kee aavaaza baahara aane lagee! sahridayata kaa jalaayaa hua deepaka nirdatha vyanga ke ek jhonke se bujha gaya! a.daa hua gho.daa chumakaarane se jora maarane lagaa tha; par hantara pa.date hee phir a.da gaya aur ga.dee ko peechhe dhakelane laga!!
