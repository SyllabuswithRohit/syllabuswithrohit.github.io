# Nirmala — Adhyay 25

**Munshi Premchand**

pachcheesavaan parichchheda

din guzarane lage| ek maheenaa pooraa nikala gaya; lekin munshee jee na laute| koee Kata bhee naheen bhejaa| nirmalaa ko ab nitya yahee chintaa banee rahatee ki vaha lauta kar na aae to kyaa hoga? use isakee chintaa na hoti thi ki una par kyaa beeta rahi hogee, vaha kahaan maare-maare phirate honge, svaasthya kaisaa hoga? use kevala apanee aur isase bhee ba.dha kar bachchi kee chintaa thi| grihasthee kaa nirvaaha kaise hoga? eeshvara kaise be.daa paara lagaavenge? bachchi kaa kyaa haala hoga? usane katara-byonta karake jo rupaye jamaa kar rakkhe the, usamen kuchha na kuchha roza hee kamee hoti jati thi| nirmalaa ko usamen se ek-ek paisaa nikaalate itanee akhara hoti thi, maano koee usakee deha se rakta nikaala raha ho| jhunjhalaa kar munshee jee ko kosatee| ladki kisee cheeza ke lie rotee, to use abhaagina, kalamunhee kaha kar jhallaatee| yahee naheen, rukminee kaa ghar men rahana use aisaa kashtakara jaana pa.data tha, maano vaha usakee gardana par savaara hai | jab hridaya jalata hai, to vaanee bhee agnimaya ho jati hai | nirmalaa ba.dee madhura-bhashinee stree thi; par ab usakee ganana karkashaaon men kee jaa sakatee thi| din bhara usake mukha se jalee-katee baaten hee nikalaa karti theen | usake shabdon kee komalata na jaane kyaa ho gaee | bhaavon men maadhurya kaa kaheen naama hee naheen| bhu.dee bahut dinon se is ghar men naukara thi | svabhaava kee sahanasheelaa thi ; par yaha aathon pahara kee baka-jhaka usase bhee na sahee gaee | ek din usane bhee ghar kee raaha lee| yahaan taka ki jisa bachchi ko vaha praanon se bhee adhika pyaar karti thi, usakee soorata se bhee ghrinaa ho gaee | baat-baat par ghu.daka pa.datee, kabhee-kabhee maara baithatee | rukminee rotee hui baalikaa ko goda men uthaa letee ; aur chumakaara-dulaara kar chupa karti | us anaatha ke lie ab yahee ek aashraya raha gaya tha|

par kadaachit usake bhaagya men yaha sukha bhee naheen bada tha! nirmalaa maamoolee taura se dopahara ko yaa teesare pahara ko sudhaa ke ghar jaayaa karti thi| ek din usakaa jee itana oobaa ki sabere hee jaa pahunchee| sudhaa nadee-snaana karane gaee hui thi| daoktara saahaba aspataala jaane ke lie kapa.de pahana rahe the| maharee apane kaama-dhandhe men lagee hui thi| nirmalaa apanee sahelee ke kamare men jaakara nishchinta baitha gaee| usane samajhaa--sudhaa koee kaama kar rahi hogee,abhee aati hogee| jab baithe-baithe do-teena minita guzara gae,to usane alamaaree se tasveeron kee ek kitaaba utaara lee,aur kesha khole palanga par leta kar chitra dekhane lagee| isee beecha men daoktara saahaba ko kisee jaroorata se nirmalaa ke kamare men aana pada| shaayada apana ainaka dhoon.dhate phirate the| bedha.daka andara chale aae| nirmalaa dvaara kee ora kesha khole letee hui thi| daoktara saahaba ko dekhate hee chaunka kar utha baithee,aur sira dhaankatee hui chaarapaaee se utara kar kha.dee ho gaee| daoktara saahaba ne lautate hue chika ke paasa kha.de hokara kaha-kshamaa karana nirmalaa| mujhe maalooma na tha ki tuma yahaan ho| meree ainaka mere kamare men naheen mila rahi hai| na jaane kahaan utaara kar rakha dee thi| main ne samajhaa shaayada yahaan ho|

nirmalaa ne chaarapaaee ke sirahaane vaale aale par nigaaha daalee to ainaka kee dibiya dikhaaee dee| usane aage ba.dha kar dibiya utaara lee;aur sira jhukaae,deha samete,sankocha se munha phere daoktara saahaba kee ora haath ba.dhaayaa| daoktara saahaba ne nirmalaa ko do ek baara pahale bhee dekha tha; par is samay ke se bhaava kabhee unake mann men na aae the| jisa jvaalaa ko vaha barason se hridaya men davaae hue the, vaha aaja pavana kaa jhonkaa paakara dahaka uthee| unhonne ainaka lene ke lie haath ba.dhaayaa to haath kaopa raha tha| ainaka uthaa kar bhee vaha baahara na gae| vaheen khoe hue se kha.de rahe| nirmalaa ne is ekaanta se bhayabheeta hokara poochhaa-sudhaa kaheen gaee hain kya?

daoktara saahaba ne sira jhukaae hue javaaba diya-haan,jaraa snaana karane chalee gaee hain|

phir bhee daoktara saahaba baahara na gae| vaheen kha.de rahe| nirmalaa ne phir poochhaa-kab taka aaengee?

daoktara saahaba ne sira jhukaae hue kaha-aati hee hongee| phir bhee vaha baahara naheen gae| unake mann men chora dvandva machaa hua tha| auchitya kaa bandhana naheen,bheerata kaa kachchaa taagaa unakee jabaana ko roke hue tha|

nirmalaa ne phir kaha-kaheen ghoomane-ghaamane lagee hongee| main bhee is vakta jati hoon|

bheerutaa kaa kachchaa taagaa bhee toota gaya| nadee ke kagaara par pahuncha kar bhaagatee hui senaa men adbhuta shakti aa jati hai| daoktara saahaba ne sira uthaa kar nirmalaa ko dekha; aur anuraaga men doobe hue svara men bole-naheen nirmala,ava aati hee hongee| abhee na jaao| roja sudhaa kee khaatira se baithatee ho,aaja meree khaatira se baitho| bataao, kab taka is aaga men jalaa karoon,satya kahata hoon nirmala...!

nirmalaa ne aur kuchha naheen sunaa| use aisaa jaana pada maano saaree prithvee chakara khaa rahi hai,maano usake praanon par sahasron vanon
kaa aaghaata ho raha hai| usane jaldee se alaganee par latakatee hui chaadara utaara lee;aur binaa munha se ek shabda nikaale kamare se nikala gaee| daoktara saahaba khisiyaae hue se ronaa munha banaae kha.de rahe| usako rokane kee yaa aur kuchha kahane kee unakee himmata na pa.dee|

nirmalaa jyonhee dvaara par pahunchee,usane sudhaa ko taange se utarate dekha| sudhaa use dekhate hee jaldee se utara kar usakee ora lapakee aur kuchha poochhana chaahatee thi;magar nirmalaa ne use avasara na diya, teera kee tarah jhapata kar chalee gaee| sudhaa ek kshana taka vismaya kee dashaa men kha.dee rahi| baat kyaa hai,usakee samajha men kuchha na shraa sakaa| vaha vyatra ho uthee| jaldee se andara gaee| maharee se poochhaa ki kyaa baat hui hai| use maalooma hua ki kaheen maharee yaa aur kisee naukara ne use koee apamaana-soochaka baat kaha dee hai| vaha aparaadhee kaa pata lagaaegee; aur use kha.de-kha.de nikaala degee| lapakee hui apane kamare men gaee| andara ridama rakhate hee daoktara saahaba ko munha latakaae chaarapaaee par baithe dekha| poochhaa-nirmalaa yahaan aaee thi!

daoktara ne sira khujalaate hue kaha-haan,aaee to thi?

sudhaa-kisee maharee-aharee ne unhen kuchha kaha to naheen? mujhase boli taka naheen,jhapata kar nikala gaee|

daoktara saahaba kee mukha-kaanti malina ho gaee;kaha-yahaan to unhen kisee ne bhee kuchha naheen kaha|

sudhaa-kisee ne kuchha kaha hai! dekho, main poochhatee hoon na| eeshvara jaanata hai,pata paa jaaoongee to kha.de-kha.de nikaala doongee| daoktara saahaba sitapitaate hue bole-maine to kisee ko kuchha kehte naheen munaa| tumhen unhonne dekha hee na hogaa|

sudhaa-vaaha, dekha hee na hoga! unake saamane to main taange se uttaree hoon| unhonne meree ora taakaa bhee; par boleen kuchha naheen| is kamare men aaee thi? daoktara saahaba ke praana sookhe jaa rahe the| hichakichaate hue bole- aaee kyon naheen theen|

sudhaa-tumhen yahaan baithe dekha kar chalee gaee hongee| basa,kisee maharee ne kuchha kaha diya hogaa| neecha jaata hain na,kisee ko baat karane kee tameeza to hai naheen| aree o sundariya,jaraa yahaan to a!

daoktara-use kyon bulaatee ho,vaha yahaan se seedhe daravaaje kee tarafa gaee,mahariyon se to baat taka naheen hui|

sudhaa-to phir tumheen ne kuchha kaha diya hogaa|

daoktara saahaba kaa kalejaa dhaka-dhaka karane lagaa| bole-main bhalaa kyaa kaha deta,kyaa aisaa ganvaara hoon?

sudhaa-tumane unhen aate dekha tab bhee baithe raha gae?

daoktara-main yahaan tha hee naheen| baahara baithaka men apanee ainaka dhoon.dhata raha| jab vahaan na milee,to main ne sochaa shaayada andara ho|'yahaan aaya so unhen baithe dekha| main baahara jana chaahata tha ki unhonne khuda poochhaa-kisee cheeza kee zaroorat hai? main ne kaha-jaraa dekhana yahaan meree ainaka to naheen hai| ainaka isee sirahaane vaale taaka par thi| unhonne uthaa kar de dee| basa,itanee hee to baat hui|

sudhaa-basa,tumhen ainaka dete hee vaha jhalaaee hui baahara chalee gaee! kyon? daoktara-malaaee hui to naheen chalee gaee| jaane lagee to mainne kaha baithie,vaha aati hongee| na baithee to main kyaa karata?

sudhaa ne kuchha socha kar kaha-baat kuchha samajha men naheen aati! main jaraa unake paasa jati hoon| dekhoon baat kyaa hai?

daoktara-to chalee jaana,aisee jaldee kyaa hai! saaraa din to pada hua hai|

sudhaa ne chaadara o.dhate hue kaha-mere peta men khalabalee machee hui hai, tuma kehte ho jaldee kyaa hai?

sudhaa tejee se kadama ba.dhaatee hui nirmalaa ke ghar kee ora chalee;aur paancha minita men jaa pahunchee| dekha to nirmalaa apane kamare men chaarapaaee par pa.dee ro rahi thi; aur bachchi usake paasa kha.dee poochha rahi dhee-ammaan,kyon lotee ho?

sudhaa ne ladki ko goda men uthaa liya aur nirmalaa se boli bahina,sacha bataao kyaa baat hai? mere yahaan kisee ne tumhen kuchha kaha hai? main sabase poochha chukee,koee naheen batalaataa|

nirmalaa aansoo ponchhatee hui volee-kisee ne kuchha kaha naheen vahina, bhalaa vahaan mujhe kaun kuchha kahata?

sudhaa-to phir mujhase boli kyon naheen,aur aate hee aate rone kyon lagon?

nirmalaa-apane naseebon ko ro rahi hoon aur kya?

sudhaa-tuma yon na bataaogee to main qasama rakhaa doongee| nirmalaa-kasama-asama na rakhaanaa bhaaee,mujhe kisee ne kuchha naheen kaha,bhootha kise lagaa hoon?

sudhaa-khaao meree kasama?

nirmalaa-tuma to naahaka jida karti ho|

sudhaa-agar tumane na bataayaa nirmala,to main samajhangee tumhen mujhase zaraa bhee prema naheen hai|basa,sab javaanee jamaa-kharcha hai| main tumase kisee baat kaa paradaa naheen rakhatee;aur tuma mujhe gaira samajhatee ho| mujhe tumhaare oopara bada bharosaa tha| ab jaana gaee ki koee kisee kaa naheen hota|

sudhaa kee aankhen sajala ho gaee| usane bachchi ko goda se utaara diya;aur dvaara kee ora chalee| nirmalaa ne utha kar usakaa haath paka.da liya;aur rotee hui boli--sudha,main tumhaare pairon pa.datee hoon, mata poochho| tumhen suna kar dukha hoga;aur shaayada main phir tumhen apana munha na dikhaa sakoon| main abhaaginee na hoti,to yaha din hee kyon dekhatee| ab to eeshvara se yahee praarthana hai ki,ve sansaara se mujhe uthaa len|abhee yaha durgati ho rahi hai,to aage na jaanen kyaa hoga!

ina shabdon men jo sanketa tha,vaha buddhimatee sudhaa se chhipaa na raha sakaa| vaha samajha gaee ki daoktara saahaba ne kuchha chhe.da-chha.da kee hai| unakaa hichaka-hichaka kar baaten karana;aur usake prashno ko taalana,unakee vaha glaanimaya,kaantiheena mudraa use yaada aa gaee| vaha sira se paancha taka kaanpa uthee;aur binaa kuchha kahe-sune sinhanee kee bhaanti krodha men bharee hui dvaara kee ora chalee| nirmalaa ne use rokana chaaha;par na paa sakee| dekhate-dekhate vaha sa.daka par aa gaee aur ghar kee ora chalee| tab nirmalaa vaheen bhoomi par baitha gaee aur phoota-phoota kar rone lagee! rmalaa din bhara chaarapaaee par pa.dee rahi| maalooma hota hai,usakee deha men praana hee naheen hai| na snaana kiya,na bhojana karane uthee| sandhyaa samay use jvara ho aaya| raat bhara deha tave kee bhaanti tapatee rahi| doosare din bhee jvara na utaraa| haan,kuchha-kuchha kama ho gaya tha| vaha chaarapaaee par letee hui nishchala netron se dvaara kee ora taaka rahi thi| chaaron ora shoonya tha,andara bhee shoonya,baahara bhee shoonya| koee chintaa na thi,na koee smriti,na koee dukha! mastishka men spandana kee shakti hee na rahi thi|

sahasaa sakminee vachchee ko goda men lie aakara kha.dee ho gaee| nirmalaa ne poochhaa-kyaa yaha bahut rotee thi?

sakminee-naheen,yaha to minakee taka naheen| raat bhara chupachaapa pa.dee rahi| sudhaa ne tho.daa saa doodha bheja diya tha,vahee pilaa diya tha| nirmalaa-aheerina doodha na de gaee thi?

rukminee-kehti thi,pichhale paise de do to doon| tumhaaraa jee ab kaisaa hai?

nirmalaa-mujhe kuchha naheen hua hai| kala jaraa deha garama ho aaee thi|

rukminee-daoktara saahaba kaa to buraa haala ho gaya| nirmalaa ne ghaba.daakara poochhaa-kyaa hua kya? kushala se hain na?

rukminee-kushala se hain ki laasha uthaane kee taiyaaree ho rahi hai| koee kahata hai jahara khaa liya tha,koee kahata hai dil kaa chalana banda ho gaya tha| bhagavaana jaanen kyaa hua tha|

nirmalaa ne ek thandee saansa lee;aur rundhe hue kantha se boli-haaya! bhagavaan,sudhaa kee kyaa gati hogee? vaha kaise jiegee!

yaha kehte-kehte vaha ro pa.dee;aur ba.dee dera taka sisakatee rahi| tab chaarapaaee se utara kar sudhaa ke paasa jaane ko taiyaara hui| paanva thara-thara kaopa rahe the,deevaara thaamen kha.dee thi;par jee na maanata tha! na jaane sudhaa ne yahaan se jaakara pati se kyaa kaha? main ne to usase kuchha kaha bhee naheen,na jaane meree baaton kaa vaha kyaa matalava samajhee! haaya! aise roopavaan,aise dayaalu,aise susheela praanee kaa yaha anta! agar nirmalaa ko maalooma hota ki usake krodha kaa yaha bheeshana parinaama hoga,to vaha jahara kaa ghoonta peekara bhee us baat ko hansee men u.daa detee! yaha socha kar ki meree hee nishthurata ke kaarana daoktara saahaba kaa yaha haala hua,nirmalaa ke hridaya ke tuka.de hone lage| aisee vedana hone lagee,maano hridaya men shoola utha raha ho| vaha daoktara saahaba ke ghar chalee|

laasha utha chukee thi| baahara sannaataa chhaayaa hua tha| ghar men striyaan jamaa theen| sudhaa zameen par baithee ro rahi thi| nirmalaa ko dekhate hee vaha jora se chillaa kar ro pa.dee;aur aakara usakee chhaatee se lipata gaee| donon dera taka rotee raheen|

jab auraton kee bhee.da kama hui aur ekaanta ho gaya,to nirmalaa ne poochhaa-yaha kyaa ho gaya bahina,tumane kaha kyaa diya?

sudhaa apane mann ko isee prashna kaa uttara aaja kitanee hee baara de chukee thi| usakaa mann jisa uttara se shaanta ho gaya tha,vahee uttara usane nirmalaa ko diya| boli-chupa bhee to na raha sakatee thi, bahina! krodha kee baat par krodha aata hee hai|

nirmalaa-main ne to tumase koee aisee baat bhee na kahee thi|

sudhaa-tuma kaise kahateen,kaha hee naheen sakatee theen;lekin unhonne jo baat hui thi,vaha kaha dee! us par main ne jo kuchha munha men aaya kaha| jab ek baat dil men aa gaee, to use hua hee samajhana chaahie| avasara aur ghaata mile,to vaha avashya pooree ho| yaha kaha kar koee naheen nikala sakata ki main ne to hansee kee thi| ekaanta men aisaa shabda jabaana par laanaa hee kaha detaa hai ki neeyata buree thi| main ne tumase kabhee kaha naheen bahina;lekin main ne unhen kaee vaara tumhaaree ora bhaankate dekha| us vakta main ne bhee yahee samajhaa ki shaayada mujhe dhokhaa ho raha ho| ava maalooma hua ki us taaka-jhaanka kaa kyaa matalaba tha| agar main ne duniya zyada dekhi hoti,to tumhen apane ghar na aane detee| kama se kama unakee tuma par nigaaha kabhee na pa.dane detee;lekin yaha kyaa jaanatee thi ki purushon ke munha men kuchha aur mann men kuchha aur hota hai| eeshvara ko jo manjoora tha,vaha hua| aise saubhaagya se main vaidhavya ko buraa naheen samajhatee| daridra praanee us dhanee se kaheen sukhee hai,jise usakaa dhana saopa bana kar kaatane dau.de| upavaasa kar lena aasaana hai, vipailaa bhojana karana usase kaheen mushkila|

isee vakta daoktara sinhaa ke chhote bhaaee aur krishnaa ne ghar men pravesha kiya| ghar men koharaama macha gaya! ka maheemaa aur guzara gaya| sudhaa apane devara ke
saatha teesare hee din chalee gaee| ab nirmalaa akelee thi| pahale hansa-bola kar jee bahalaa liya karti thi| ab ronaa hee ek kaama raha gaya| usakaa svaasthya din-din biga.data gaya| puraane makaana kaa kiraayaa adhika tha| doosaraa makaana tho.de kiraae kaa liya| yaha ek tanga galee men tha| andara ek kamaraa tha;aur chhota saa aangana| na prakaasha jaata,na vaayu! durgandha u.daa karti thi| bhojana kaa yaha haala ki paise rahate hue bhee kabhee-kabhee upavaasa karana pa.data tha| baajaara se laave kaun? phir ab ghar men koee marda naheen,koee ladka naheen,to roja bhojana banaane kaa kashta kaun uthaave| auraton ke lie roza bhojana karane kee aavashyakata hee kya? agar ek vakta khaa liya,to do din ke lie chhuttee ho gaee| bachchi ke lie taajaa haluvaa yaa rotiyaan bana jati theen| aisee dashaa men svaasthya kyon na biga.data? chinta, shoka,duraavasthaa--ek ho,to koee kahe; yahaan to traitaapa kaa dhaavaa tha| us par nirmalaa ne davaa khaane kee qasama khaa lee thi| karti hee kya! us tho.de se rupayon men davaa kee gunnjaaisha kahaan thi| jahaan bhojana kaa thikaanaa na tha, vahaan davaa kaa jikra hee kya? din-din sookhatee chalee jati thi!

ek din rukminee ne kaha--bahoo,is tarah kab taka ghulaa karogee, jee hee se to jahaana hai! chalo,kisee vaidya ko dikhaa laaoon|

nirmalaa ne virakta bhaava se kaha--jise rone hee ke lie jeenaa ho, usakaa mara jana hee achchha|

rukminee--bulaane se to mauta naheen aati|

nirmalaa--mauta to binaa bulaae aati hai,bulaane par kyon na aaegee| usake aane men ab bahut din na lagenge| bahina! jai din chalatee hoon, utane saala samajha leejie|

rukminee--dil aisaa chhota mata karo, bahoo! abhee tumane sansaara kaa sukha hee kyaa dekha hai?

nirmalaa--agar sansaara kaa yahee sukha hai, jo itane dinon se dekha rahi hoon, to usase jee bhara gaya| sacha kehti hoon bahina, is bachchi kaa moha mujhe baandhe hue hai; naheen to ab taka kabhee kee chalee gaee hoti| na jaane is bechaaree ke bhaagya men kyaa likhaa hai?

donon mahilaaen rone lageen| idhara jab se nirmalaa ne chaarapaaee paka.da lee hai, rukminee ke hridaya men dayaa kaa sotaa saa khula gaya hai| dvesha kaa lesha bhee naheen raha| koee kaama karti hon, nirmalaa kee aavaaja sunate hee dau.datee hain| ghanton usake paasa kathaa-puraana sunaayaa karti hain| koee aisee cheeza pakaanaa chaahatee hain,jise nirmalaa ruchi se khaae| nirmalaa ko kabhee hansate dekha letee hain,to nihaala ho jati hain;aur bachchi ko to apane gale kaa haara banaae rahatee hain| usee kee neenda sotee hain,usee kee neenda jaagatee hain| vahee baalikaa ab unake jeevana kaa aadhaara hai|

rukminee ne jaraa dera baada kaha-bahoo,tuma itanee niraasha kyon hoti ho,bhagavaan chaahenge to tuma do-chaara din men achchhi ho jaaogee| mere saatha aaja vaidya jee ke paasa chalo| ba.de sajjana hain|

nirmalaa-deedee jee,ab mujhe kisee vaidya-hakeema kee davaa phaayadaa na karegee| aapa meree chintaa na karen| bachchi ko aapakee goda men chho.de jati hoon| agar jeetee-jaagatee bache,to kisee achchhe kula men vivaaha kar deejiegaa| main to isake lie apane jeevana men kuchha na kar sakee,kevala janma dene bhara kee aparaadhinee hoon| chaahe kaoree rakhiega,chaahe visha dekara maara daaliega;par kupaatra ke gale na ma.dhiega,itanee hee aapase meree vinaya hai| main ne aapakee kuchha sevaa na kee, isakaa bada dukha ho raha hai| mujha abhaaginee se kisee ko sukha naheen milaa| jisa par meree chhaayaa bhee pa.da gaee, usakaa sarvanaasha ho gaya| agar svaamee jee kabhee ghar aaven, to unase kahiegaa ki us karama-jalee ke aparaadha kshamaa kar den|

rukminee rotee hui boli-bahoo,tumhaaraa koee aparaadha naheen eeshvara se kehti hoon,tumhaaree ora se mere mann men jaraa bhee maila naheen hai| haan,main ne sadaiva tumhaare saatha kapata kiya| isakaa mujhe marate dama taka dukha rahegaa|

nirmalaa ne kaatara netron se dekhate hue kaha-deedee jee,kahane kee baat naheen;par binaa kahe naheen raha jata| svaamee jee ne hameshaa mujhe avishvaasa kee drishti dekha;lekin mainne kabhee mann men bhee unakee upekshaa naheen kee| jo honaatha,vaha to ho hee chuka;adharma karake apana paraloka kyon biga.datee| poorva-janma men na jaane kaun se paapa kie the,jinakaa yaha praayashchitta karana pada| is janma men kaante botee,to kaun gati hoti?

nirmalaa kee saansa ba.de vega se chalane lagee| phir khaata par leta gaee; aur bachchi kee ora ek aisee drishti se dekha,jo usake jeevana kee sampoorna vipatkathaa kee vrihada aalochana thi| vaanee men itanee saamartha kahaan!

teena din taka nirmalaa kee aankhon se aansuon kee dhaaraa bahatee rahi | vaha na kisee se bolti thi,na kisee kee ora dekhatee thi;
aur na kisee kee kuchha sunatee thi| basa,roye chalee jati thi| us vedana kaa kaun anumaana kar sakata hai?

chauthe din sandhyaa samay vaha vipatti-kathaa samaapta ho gaee| usee samay jab pashu-pakshee apane-apane basere ko lauta rahe the,nirmalaa kaa praana-pakshee bhee,din bhara shikaariyon ke nishaanon,shikaaree chi.diyon ke panjon aur vaayu ke prachanda jhokon se aahata aur vyathita apane basere kee ora u.da gaya!!

muhalle ke log jamaa ho gae| laasha baahara nikaalee gaee| kaun daaha karega,yaha prashna uthaa| log isee chintaa men the ki sahasaa ek boo.dhaa pathika ek bukachaa latakaae aakara kha.daa ho gaya| yahee munshee totaaraama the!!
