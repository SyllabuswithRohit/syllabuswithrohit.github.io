# Nirmala — Adhyay 25

**Munshi Premchand**

pachcheesavaan parichchhed

din gujarane lage. ek maheenaa pooraa nikal gayaa; lekin munshee jee n laute. koee khat bhi nahin bhejaa. Nirmala ko ab nity yahee fikr banee rahatee ki vah laut kar n aae to kyaa hogaa? use isakee fikr n hotee thi ki un par kyaa beet rahee hogee, vah kahaan maare-maare phirate hongge, sehat kaisaa hogaa? use keval apanee aur isase bhi badh kar bachchi ki fikr thee. ghar-baar ka nirvaah kaise hogaa? Bhagwan kaise bedaa paar lagaavengge? bachchi ka kyaa haal hogaa? usane katar-byont karake jo rupaye jamaa kar rakkhe the, usamen kuchh n kuchh roj hee kamee hotee jaatee thee. Nirmala ko usamen se ek-ek paisaa nikaalate itanee akhar hotee thi, maano koee usakee deh se rakt nikaal rahaa ho. jhunjhalaa kar munshee jee ko kosatee. ladakee kisee cheej ke lie rotee, to use abhaagin, kalamunhee kah kar jhallaatee. yahee nahin, rukminee ka ghar mein rahanaa use aisaa takaleephakar jaan padataa tha, maano vah usakee gardan par savaar hai. jab dil jalataa hai, to vaanee bhi agnimay ho jaatee hai. Nirmala badee madhur-bhashinee aurat thi; par ab usakee gananaa karkashaaon mein ki jaa sakatee thee. din bhar usake munh se jalee-katee baaten hee nikalaa karatee theen. usake shabdon ki komalataa n jaane kyaa ho gaee. bhaavon mein maadhury ka kaheen naam hee naheen. bhudee bahut dinon se is ghar mein naukar thi. svabhaav ki sahanasheelaa thi; par yah aathon pahar ki bak-jhak usase bhi n sahee gaee. ek din usane bhi ghar ki raah lee. yahan tak ki jis bachchi ko vah praanon se bhi adhik pyaar karatee thi, usakee soorat se bhi nafrat ho gaee. baat-baat par ghudak padatee, kabhee-kabhee maar baithatee. rukminee rotee huee ladakee ko god mein uthaa letee; aur chumakaar-dulaar kar chup karatee. us anaath ke lie ab yahee ek aashray rah gayaa thaa.

par shaayad usake kismat mein yah sukh bhi nahin badaa tha! Nirmala maamoolee taur se dopahar ko ya teesare pahar ko sudhaa ke ghar jaayaa karatee thee. ek din usakaa jee itanaa oobaa ki sabere hee jaa pahunchee. sudhaa nadee-snaan karane gaee huee thee. doktar Sahab aspataal jaane ke lie kapde pahan rahe the. maharee apane kaam-dhandhe mein lagee huee thee. Nirmala apanee sahelee ke kamare mein jaakar nishchint baith gaee. usane samajhaa--sudhaa koee kaam kar rahee hogee, abhee aatee hogee. jab baithe-baithe do-teen minit gujar gae, to usane alamaaree se tasveeron ki ek kitaab utaar lee, aur kesh khole palanggg par let kar chitr dekhane lagee. isee beech mein doktar Sahab ko kisee jaroorat se Nirmala ke kamare mein aanaa padaa. shaayad apanaa ainak dhoondhate phirate the. bedhadak andar chale aae. Nirmala dvaar ki or kesh khole letee huee thee. doktar Sahab ko dekhate hee chaungk kar uth baithee, aur sir dhaangkatee huee chaarapaaee se utar kar khadee ho gaee. doktar Sahab ne lautate hue chik ke paas khade hokar kahaa-kshmaa karanaa nirmalaa. mujhe maaloom n tha ki tum yahan ho. meree ainak mere kamare mein nahin mil rahee hai. n jaane kahaan utaar kar rakh dee thee. main ne samajhaa shaayad yahan ho.

Nirmala ne chaarapaaee ke sirahaane vaale aale par nigaah daalee to ainak ki dibiyaa dikhaaee dee. usane aage badh kar dibiyaa utaar lee; aur sir jhukaae, deh samete, sanggkoch se munh phere doktar Sahab ki or haath badhaayaa. doktar Sahab ne Nirmala ko do ek baar pahale bhi dekhaa tha; par is samay ke se bhaav kabhee unake man mein n aae the. jis jvaalaa ko vah barason se dil mein davaae hue the, vah aaj pavan ka jhongkaa paakar dahak uthee. unhonne ainak lene ke lie haath badhaayaa to haath kop rahaa thaa. ainak uthaa kar bhi vah baahar n gae. vaheen khoe hue se khade rahe. Nirmala ne is ekaant se darabheet hokar poochhaa-sudhaa kaheen gaee hain kyaa?

doktar Sahab ne sir jhukaae hue javaab diyaa-haan, jaraa snaan karane chalee gaee hain.

phir bhi doktar Sahab baahar n gae. vaheen khade rahe. Nirmala ne phir poochhaa-kab tak aaenggee?

doktar Sahab ne sir jhukaae hue kahaa-aatee hee honggee. phir bhi vah baahar nahin gae. unake man mein chor dvandv machaa huaa thaa. auchity ka bandhan nahin, bheerataa ka kachchaa taagaa unakee jabaan ko roke hue thaa.

Nirmala ne phir kahaa-kaheen ghoomane-ghaamane lagee honggee. main bhi is vakt jaatee hoon.

bheerutaa ka kachchaa taagaa bhi toot gayaa. nadee ke kagaar par pahunch kar bhaagatee huee senaa mein adbhut taakat aa jaatee hai. doktar Sahab ne sir uthaa kar Nirmala ko dekhaa; aur anuraag mein doobe hue svar mein bole-nahin Nirmala, av aatee hee honggee. abhee n jaao. roj sudhaa ki khaatir se baithatee ho, aaj meree khaatir se baitho. bataao, kab tak is aag mein jalaa karoon, sach kahataa hoon Nirmala.........!

Nirmala ne aur kuchh nahin sunaa. use aisaa jaan padaa maano saaree prithvee chakar khaa rahee hai, maano usake praanon par sahasron vanon
ka aaghaat ho rahaa hai. usane jaldee se alaganee par latakatee huee chaadar utaar lee; aur binaa munh se ek shabd nikaale kamare se nikal gaee. doktar Sahab khisiyaae hue se ronaa munh banaae khade rahe. usako rokane ki ya aur kuchh kahane ki unakee himmat n padee.

Nirmala jyonhee dvaar par pahunchee, usane sudhaa ko taangge se utarate dekhaa. sudhaa use dekhate hee jaldee se utar kar usakee or lapakee aur kuchh poochhanaa chaahatee thi; magar Nirmala ne use avasar n diyaa, teer ki tarah jhapat kar chalee gaee. sudhaa ek pal tak hairaanee ki dashaa mein khadee rahee. baat kyaa hai, usakee samajh mein kuchh n shraa sakaa. vah vyatr ho uthee. jaldee se andar gaee. maharee se poochhaa ki kyaa baat huee hai. use maaloom huaa ki kaheen maharee ya aur kisee naukar ne use koee beijjatee-soochak baat kah dee hai. vah jurmee ka pataa lagaaegee; aur use khade-khade nikaal degee. lapakee huee apane kamare mein gaee. andar ridam rakhate hee doktar Sahab ko munh latakaae chaarapaaee par baithe dekhaa. poochhaa-Nirmala yahan aaee thi!

doktar ne sir khujalaate hue kahaa-haan, aaee to thi?

sudhaa-kisee maharee-aharee ne unhen kuchh kahaa to nahin? mujhase bolee tak nahin, jhapat kar nikal gaee.

doktar Sahab ki munh-kaanti malin ho gaee; kahaa-yahan to unhen kisee ne bhi kuchh nahin kahaa.

sudhaa-kisee ne kuchh kahaa hai! dekho, main poochhatee hoon na. Bhagwan jaanataa hai, pataa paa jaaoonggee to khade-khade nikaal doonggee. doktar Sahab sitapitaate hue bole-maine to kisee ko kuchh kahate nahin munaa. tumhe unhonne dekhaa hee n hogaa.

sudhaa-vaah, dekhaa hee n hogaa! unake saamane to main taangge se javaabee hoon. unhonne meree or taakaa bhi; par boleen kuchh naheen. is kamare mein aaee thi? doktar Sahab ke praan sookhe jaa rahe the. hichakichaate hue bole- aaee kyon nahin theen.

sudhaa-tumhe yahan baithe dekh kar chalee gaee honggee. bas, kisee maharee ne kuchh kah diyaa hogaa. neech jaat hain n, kisee ko baat karane ki tameej to hai naheen. aree o sundariyaa, jaraa yahan to aa!

doktar-use kyon bulaatee ho, vah yahan se seedhe daravaaje ki taraph gaee, mahariyon se to baat tak nahin huee.

sudhaa-to phir tumheen ne kuchh kah diyaa hogaa.

doktar Sahab ka kalejaa dhak-dhak karane lagaa. bole-main bhalaa kyaa kah detaa, kyaa aisaa ganvaar hoon?

sudhaa-tumane unhen aate dekhaa tab bhi baithe rah gae?

doktar-main yahan tha hee naheen. baahar baithak mein apanee ainak dhoondhataa rahaa. jab wahan n milee, to main ne sochaa shaayad andar ho.'yahan kamaaeeaa so unhen baithe dekhaa. main baahar jaanaa chaahataa tha ki unhonne khud poochhaa-kisee cheej ki zaroorat hai? main ne kahaa-jaraa dekhanaa yahan meree ainak to nahin hai. ainak isee sirahaane vaale taak par thee. unhonne uthaa kar de dee. bas, itanee hee to baat huee.

sudhaa-bas, tumhe ainak dete hee vah jhalaaee huee baahar chalee gaee! kyon? doktar-malaaee huee to nahin chalee gaee. jaane lagee to mainne kahaa baithie, vah aatee honggee. n baithee to main kyaa karataa?

sudhaa ne kuchh soch kar kahaa-baat kuchh samajh mein nahin aatee! main jaraa unake paas jaatee hoon. dekhoon baat kyaa hai?

doktar-to chalee jaanaa, aisee jaldee kyaa hai! saaraa din to padaa huaa hai.

sudhaa ne chaadar odhate hue kahaa-mere pet mein khalabalee machee huee hai, tum kahate ho jaldee kyaa hai?

sudhaa tejee se kadam badhaatee huee Nirmala ke ghar ki or chalee; aur paanch minit mein jaa pahunchee. dekhaa to Nirmala apane kamare mein chaarapaaee par padee ro rahee thi; aur bachchi usake paas khadee poochh rahee dhee-ammaan, kyon lotee ho?

sudhaa ne ladakee ko god mein uthaa liyaa aur Nirmala se bolee bahin, sach bataao kyaa baat hai? mere yahan kisee ne tumhe kuchh kahaa hai? main sabase poochh chukee, koee nahin batalaataa.

Nirmala aansoo ponchhatee huee volee-kisee ne kuchh kahaa nahin vahin, bhalaa wahan mujhe kaun kuchh kahataa?

sudhaa-to phir mujhase bolee kyon nahin, aur aate hee aate rone kyon lagon?

Nirmala-apane naseebon ko ro rahee hoon aur kyaa?

sudhaa-tum yon n bataaogee to main kasam rakhaa doonggee. Nirmala-kasam-asam n rakhaanaa bhaaee, mujhe kisee ne kuchh nahin kahaa, bhooth kise lagaa hoon?

sudhaa-khaao meree kasam?

Nirmala-tum to naahak jid karatee ho.

sudhaa-agar tumane n bataayaa Nirmala, to main samajhanggee tumhe mujhase jaraa bhi pyaar nahin hai. bas, sab javaanee jamaa-kharch hai. main tumase kisee baat ka paradaa nahin rakhatee; aur tum mujhe gair samajhatee ho. mujhe tumhaare oopar badaa bharosaa thaa. ab jaan gaee ki koee kisee ka nahin hotaa.

sudhaa ki aankhen sajal ho gaee. usane bachchi ko god se utaar diyaa; aur dvaar ki or chalee. Nirmala ne uth kar usakaa haath pakad liyaa; aur rotee huee bolee--sudhaa, main tumhaare pairon padatee hoon, mat poochho. tumhe sun kar dukh hogaa; aur shaayad main phir tumhe apanaa munh n dikhaa sakoon. main abhaaginee n hotee, to yah din hee kyon dekhatee. ab to Bhagwan se yahee dua hai ki, ve sansaar se mujhe uthaa len. abhee yah durgati ho rahee hai, to aage n jaanen kyaa hogaa!

in shabdon mein jo sanggket tha, vah buddhimatee sudhaa se chhipaa n rah sakaa. vah samajh gaee ki doktar Sahab ne kuchh chhed-chhaad ki hai. unakaa hichak-hichak kar baaten karanaa; aur usake savaalo ko taalanaa, unakee vah glaanimay, kaantiheen mudraa use yaad aa gaee. vah sir se paanch tak kaamp uthee; aur binaa kuchh kahe-sune sinhanee ki bhaanti krodh mein bharee huee dvaar ki or chalee. Nirmala ne use rokanaa chaahaa; par n paa sakee. dekhate-dekhate vah sadak par aa gaee aur ghar ki or chalee. tab Nirmala vaheen zameen par baith gaee aur phoot-phoot kar rone lagee! rmalaa din bhar chaarapaaee par padee rahee. maaloom hotaa hai, usakee deh mein praan hee nahin hai. n snaan kiyaa, n khana karane uthee. sandhyaa samay use jvar ho kamaaeeaa. raat bhar deh tave ki bhaanti tapatee rahee. doosare din bhi jvar n utaraa. haan, kuchh-kuchh kam ho gayaa thaa. vah chaarapaaee par letee huee nishchal aangkhenon se dvaar ki or taak rahee thee. chaaron or shoony tha, andar bhi shoony, baahar bhi shoonya. koee fikr n thi, n koee yaad, n koee dukh! dimaag mein spandan ki taakat hee n rahee thee.

sahasaa sakminee vachchee ko god mein lie aakar khadee ho gaee. Nirmala ne poochhaa-kyaa yah bahut rotee thi?

sakminee-nahin, yah to minakee tak naheen. raat bhar chupachaap padee rahee. sudhaa ne thodaa saa doodh bhej diyaa tha, vahee pilaa diyaa thaa. Nirmala-aheerin doodh n de gaee thi?

rukminee-kahatee thi, pichhale paise de do to doon. tumhaaraa jee ab kaisaa hai?

Nirmala-mujhe kuchh nahin huaa hai. kal jaraa deh garam ho aaee thee.

rukminee-doktar Sahab ka to buraa haal ho gayaa. Nirmala ne ghabadaakar poochhaa-kyaa huaa kyaa? kushal se hain n?

rukminee-kushal se hain ki laash uthaane ki taiyaaree ho rahee hai. koee kahataa hai jahar khaa liyaa tha, koee kahataa hai dil ka chalanaa band ho gayaa thaa. Bhagwan jaanen kyaa huaa thaa.

Nirmala ne ek thandee saans lee; aur rundhe hue kanth se bolee-haay! Bhagwan, sudhaa ki kyaa gati hogee? vah kaise jiegee!

yah kahate-kahate vah ro padee; aur badee der tak sisakatee rahee. tab chaarapaaee se utar kar sudhaa ke paas jaane ko taiyaar huee. paanv thar-thar kop rahe the, deevaar thaamen khadee thi; par jee n maanataa tha! n jaane sudhaa ne yahan se jaakar shauhar se kyaa kahaa? main ne to usase kuchh kahaa bhi nahin, n jaane meree baaton ka vah kyaa matalav samajhee! haay! aise roopavaan, aise tarasalu, aise susheel praanee ka yah ant! agar Nirmala ko maaloom hotaa ki usake krodh ka yah bheeshan nateejaa hogaa, to vah jahar ka ghoont peekar bhi us baat ko hansee mein udaa detee! yah soch kar ki meree hee nishthurataa ke vajah doktar Sahab ka yah haal huaa, Nirmala ke dil ke tukade hone lage. aisee dard hone lagee, maano dil mein shool uth rahaa ho. vah doktar Sahab ke ghar chalee.

laash uth chukee thee. baahar sannaataa chhaayaa huaa thaa. ghar mein striyaan jamaa theen. sudhaa zameen par baithee ro rahee thee. Nirmala ko dekhate hee vah jor se chillaa kar ro padee; aur aakar usakee chhaatee se lipat gaee. donon der tak rotee raheen.

jab auraton ki bheed kam huee aur ekaant ho gayaa, to Nirmala ne poochhaa-yah kyaa ho gayaa bahin, tumane kah kyaa diyaa?

sudhaa apane man ko isee savaal ka javaab aaj kitanee hee baar de chukee thee. usakaa man jis javaab se shaant ho gayaa tha, vahee javaab usane Nirmala ko diyaa. bolee-chup bhi to n rah sakatee thi, bahin! krodh ki baat par krodh aataa hee hai.

Nirmala-main ne to tumase koee aisee baat bhi n kahee thee.

sudhaa-tum kaise kahateen, kah hee nahin sakatee theen; lekin unhonne jo baat huee thi, vah kah dee! us par main ne jo kuchh munh mein kamaaeeaa kahaa. jab ek baat dil mein aa gaee, to use huaa hee samajhanaa chaahie. avasar aur ghaat mile, to vah zaroor pooree ho. yah kah kar koee nahin nikal sakataa ki main ne to hansee ki thee. ekaant mein aisaa shabd jabaan par laanaa hee kah detaa hai ki neeyat buree thee. main ne tumase kabhee kahaa nahin bahin; lekin main ne unhen kaee vaar tumhaaree or bhaangkate dekhaa. us vakt main ne bhi yahee samajhaa ki shaayad mujhe dhokhaa ho rahaa ho. av maaloom huaa ki us taak-jhaangk ka kyaa matalab thaa. agar main ne duniya jyaadaa dekhee hotee, to tumhe apane ghar n aane detee. kam se kam unakee tum par nigaah kabhee n padane detee; lekin yah kyaa jaanatee thi ki mardon ke munh mein kuchh aur man mein kuchh aur hotaa hai. Bhagwan ko jo manjoor tha, vah huaa. aise achchhi kismat se main vaidhavy ko buraa nahin samajhatee. daridr praanee us dhanee se kaheen sukhee hai, jise usakaa dhan sop ban kar kaatane daude. upavaas kar lenaa aasaan hai, vipailaa khana karanaa usase kaheen mushkila.

isee vakt doktar sinhaa ke chhote bhaaee aur krishnaa ne ghar mein andar jaanaa kiyaa. ghar mein koharaam mach gayaa! k maheemaa aur gujar gayaa. sudhaa apane devar ke
saath teesare hee din chalee gaee. ab Nirmala akelee thee. pahale hans-bol kar jee bahalaa liyaa karatee thee. ab ronaa hee ek kaam rah gayaa. usakaa sehat din-din bigadataa gayaa. puraane makaan ka kiraayaa adhik thaa. doosaraa makaan thode kiraae ka liyaa. yah ek tanggg galee mein thaa. andar ek kamaraa tha; aur chhotaa saa aanggana. n prakaash jaataa, n vaayu! durgandh udaa karatee thee. khana ka yah haal ki paise rahate hue bhi kabhee-kabhee upavaas karanaa padataa thaa. baajaar se laave kaun? phir ab ghar mein koee mard nahin, koee ladakaa nahin, to roj khana banaane ka takaleeph kaun uthaave. auraton ke lie roj khana karane ki zaroorat hee kyaa? agar ek vakt khaa liyaa, to do din ke lie chhuttee ho gaee. bachchi ke lie taajaa haluvaa ya rotiyaan ban jaatee theen. aisee dashaa mein sehat kyon n bigadataa? fikr, shok, duraavasthaa--ek ho, to koee kahe; yahan to traitaap ka dhaavaa thaa. us par Nirmala ne davaa khaane ki kasam khaa lee thee. karatee hee kyaa! us thode se rupayon mein davaa ki gunnjaaish kahaan thee. jahaan khana ka thikaanaa n tha, wahan davaa ka jikr hee kyaa? din-din sookhatee chalee jaatee thi!

ek din rukminee ne kahaa--bahoo, is tarah kab tak ghulaa kabeemaareeee, jee hee se to jahaan hai! chalo, kisee vaidy ko dikhaa laaoon.

Nirmala ne virakt bhaav se kahaa--jise rone hee ke lie jeenaa ho, usakaa mar jaanaa hee achchhaa.

rukminee--bulaane se to maut nahin aatee.

Nirmala--maut to binaa bulaae aatee hai, bulaane par kyon n aaegee. usake aane mein ab bahut din n lagengge. bahin! jai din chalatee hoon, utane saal samajh leejie.

rukminee--dil aisaa chhotaa mat karo, bahoo! abhee tumane sansaar ka sukh hee kyaa dekhaa hai?

Nirmala--agar sansaar ka yahee sukh hai, jo itane dinon se dekh rahee hoon, to usase jee bhar gayaa. sach kahatee hoon bahin, is bachchi ka moh mujhe baandhe hue hai; nahin to ab tak kabhee ki chalee gaee hotee. n jaane is bechaaree ke kismat mein kyaa likhaa hai?

donon mahilaaen rone lageen. idhar jab se Nirmala ne chaarapaaee pakad lee hai, rukminee ke dil mein taras ka sotaa saa khul gayaa hai. dvesh ka lesh bhi nahin rahaa. koee kaam karatee hon, Nirmala ki aavaaj sunate hee daudatee hain. ghanton usake paas kathaa-puraan sunaayaa karatee hain. koee aisee cheej pakaanaa chaahatee hain, jise Nirmala ruchi se khaae. Nirmala ko kabhee hansate dekh letee hain, to nihaal ho jaatee hain; aur bachchi ko to apane gale ka haar banaae rahatee hain. usee ki neend sotee hain, usee ki neend jaagatee hain. vahee ladakee ab unake zindagi ka aadhaar hai.

rukminee ne jaraa der baad kahaa-bahoo, tum itanee niraash kyon hotee ho, Bhagwan chaahengge to tum do-chaar din mein achchhi ho jaaogee. mere saath aaj vaidy jee ke paas chalo. bade sajjan hain.

Nirmala-deedee jee, ab mujhe kisee vaidy-hakeem ki davaa phaayadaa n karegee. aap meree fikr n karen. bachchi ko aapakee god mein chhode jaatee hoon. agar jeetee-jaagatee bache, to kisee achchhe kul mein shaadee kar deejiegaa. main to isake lie apane zindagi mein kuchh n kar sakee, keval paidaa honaa dene bhar ki jurminee hoon. chaahe koree rakhiegaa, chaahe vish dekar maar daaliegaa; par kupaatr ke gale n madhiegaa, itanee hee aapase meree vinay hai. main ne aapakee kuchh sevaa n ki, isakaa badaa dukh ho rahaa hai. mujh abhaaginee se kisee ko sukh nahin milaa. jis par meree chhaayaa bhi pad gaee, usakaa sarvanaash ho gayaa. agar svaamee jee kabhee ghar aaven, to unase kahiegaa ki us karam-jalee ke jurm kshmaa kar den.

rukminee rotee huee bolee-bahoo, tumhaaraa koee jurm nahin Bhagwan se kahatee hoon, tumhaaree or se mere man mein jaraa bhi mail nahin hai. haan, main ne hameshaa tumhaare saath kapat kiyaa. isakaa mujhe marate dam tak dukh rahegaa.

Nirmala ne kaatar aangkhenon se dekhate hue kahaa-deedee jee, kahane ki baat nahin; par binaa kahe nahin rahaa jaataa. svaamee jee ne hameshaa mujhe bharosaa n honaa ki drishti dekhaa; lekin mainne kabhee man mein bhi unakee upeksh nahin kee. jo honaathaa, vah to ho hee chukaa; adharm karake apanaa paralok kyon bigaadatee. pahale-paidaa honaa mein n jaane kaun se paap kie the, jinakaa yah praayashchitt karanaa padaa. is paidaa honaa mein kaante botee, to kaun gati hotee?

Nirmala ki saans bade veg se chalane lagee. phir khaat par let gaee; aur bachchi ki or ek aisee drishti se dekhaa, jo usake zindagi ki pooraa vipatkathaa ki vrihad aalochanaa thee. vaanee mein itanee saamarth kahaan!

teen din tak Nirmala ki aangkhon se aansuon ki dhaaraa bahatee rahee. vah n kisee se bolatee thi, n kisee ki or dekhatee thi;
aur n kisee ki kuchh sunatee thee. bas, roye chalee jaatee thee. us dard ka kaun anumaan kar sakataa hai?

chauthe din sandhyaa samay vah vipatti-kathaa khatm ho gaee. usee samay jab pashu-pakshee apane-apane basere ko laut rahe the, Nirmala ka praan-pakshee bhi, din bhar shikaariyon ke nishaanon, shikaaree chidiyon ke panyjon aur vaayu ke prachand jhokon se aahat aur vyathit apane basere ki or ud gayaa!!

muhalle ke log jamaa ho gae. laash baahar nikaalee gaee. kaun daah karegaa, yah savaal uthaa. log isee fikr mein the ki sahasaa ek boodhaa pathik ek bukachaa latakaae aakar khadaa ho gayaa. yahee munshee totaaraam the!!
