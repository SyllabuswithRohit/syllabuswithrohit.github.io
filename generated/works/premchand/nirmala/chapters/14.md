# Nirmala — Adhyay 14

**Munshi Premchand**

chaudhavam parichchhed

tinon baten ek hi sath huim NAmENirmala ki ladki ne janma liya, NAmEKrishna ka shadi nishchit hua; aur munshi NAmETotaram ka makan nilam ho gaya! ladki ka janma to am bat thi! halanki NAmENirmala ki nazar men yah uske jivan ki sabse mahan bat thi; lekin shesh donon ghatanaen asadharan thim! NAmEKrishna ka shadi aise sampann gharane men kyon kar thik hua? uski mam ke pas to dahej ke nam kauri bhi na thi; aur idhar burhe NAmESinha sahab jo ab penshan lekar ghar a gaye the, biradri men maha lobhi mashhur the. vah apne beta ka shadi aise garib gharane men karne par kaise razi hue? kisi ko sahsa yakin na ata tha. isse bhi bade hairani ki bat munshi ji ke makan ka nilam hona tha. log munshi ji ko agar lakhpati nahin, to bada aadmi zarur samjhate the. unka makan kaise nilam hua? bat yah thi ki munshi ji ne ek mahajan se
kuchh rupye karz lekar ek gamv rahan rakkha tha. unhen ummid thi ki sal adh sal men yah rupye pata denge. phir das-panch sal men us gamv par bhi kabja kar lenge. vah jamindar asal aur sud ke kuchh rupye ada karne men asamarth ho jayga. isi bharose par munshi ji ne vah mamla kiya tha. gamv bahut bada tha-char-panch sau rupya napha hota tha; lekin man ki sochi man hi men rah gai. munshi ji dil ko bahut samjhane par bhi kachhari na ja sake. beta-shok ne unmen koi kam karne ki shakti hi nahin chhori! kaun aisa dil-shunya pita hai jo apne beta ki gardan par talvar chala kar chitt ko shant karle?

mahajan ke pas jab sal bhar tak sud na pahuncha; aur na uske bar-bar bulane par munshi ji uske pas gaye yaham tak ki pichhli bar unhomne saph-saph kah diya ki ham kisi ke gulam nahin haim; sahu ji jo chahen karen, tab sahu ji ko gussa
a gaya. usne nalish kar di. munshi ji pairvi karne bhi na gaye. ekatrafa digri ho gai! yaham ghar men rupye kaham rakhe the? itne hi dinon men munshi ji ki sakh bhi uth gai thi. vah rupye ka koi pravandh na kar sake. akhir makan nilam par charh gaya. NAmENirmala saur men thi. yah khabar suni, to kaleja sann sa ho gaya! jivan men aur koi sukh na hone par bhi dhanabhav ki chintaon se mukt thi! paisa manav-jivan men agar sarva-pradhan vastu nahin, to vah uske vahut pas ki vastuavashya hai. ab aur
abhavon ke sath yah chinta bhi uske sir savar hui, usne dai dvara kahla bheja mere sab gahne bech kar ghar ko bacha lijiye; lekin munshi ji ne yah prastav kisi tarah svikar na kiya.

us din se munshi ji aur bhi chinta-yast rahne lage. jis paisa ka mumh bhogne ke lie unhomne shadi kiya tha, vah ab bita samay ki smriti-matra tha. vah mare pachhtava ke ab NAmENirmala ko apna mumh tak na dikha sakte the. unhen ab us anyay ka anuman ho raha tha, jo unhomne NAmENirmala ke sath kiya tha, aur ladki ke janma ne to rahi-sahi kasar bhi puri kar di-sarvanash hi kar dala!

barhaven din saur se nikal kar NAmENirmala navjat bachcha ko god men lie pati ke pas gai. vah is abhav men bhi itni khush thi, mano use koi chinta nahin hai! ladki ko dil se laga kar vah apni sari chinta bhul gai thi! bachcha ke viksit aur harsh-pradipt netro ko dekh kar uska dil praphullit ho raha tha! matritva ke is udgar men uske sare klesh vilin ho gae the! vah bachcha ko pati ki god men dekar nihal ho jana chahti thi, lekin munshi ji ladki ko dekh kar saham uthe. god men lene ke lie unka dil hulsaya nahin; par unhone ek bar use karun-netron se dekha; aur phir sir jhuka liya. bachcha ki surat NAmEmansaram se bilkul milti thi!

NAmENirmala ne unke man ka bhav kuchh aur hi samjha! usne
shat-gun pyaar se ladki ko dil se laga liya; mano unse kah rahi hai- agar tum iske bojh se dabe jate ho, to aj se maim is par tumhara saya bhi na parne dungi. jis ratna ko maim ne itni tapasya ke bad paya hai, uska niradar karte hue tumhara dil phat nahin jata? vah usi pal bachcha ko god se chipatae hue apne kamre men chali ai; aur der tak roti rahi! usne pati ki is udasinta ko samjhane ki zara bhi cheshta na ki; nahin to shayad vah unhen itna kathor na samjhati. uske sir par uttaradayitva ka itna bada bhar kaham tha, jo uske pati par a pada tha? kya vah sochne ki cheshta karti, to itna bhi uski samajh men na ata?

munshi ji ko ek hi pal men apni bhul malum ho gai. mam ka dil pyaar men itna anurakt rahta hai ki ane vala samay ki chinta aur badhaen use jara bhi bhaybhit nahin karti! use apne man men ek alaukik shakti ka anubhav hota hai, jo badhaon ko uske samne parast kar deti hai. munshi ji turant daure hue ghar men ae; aur bachcha ko god men lekar bole-mujhe yad ata hai, mansa bhi aisa hi tha-bilkul aisa hi!

NAmENirmala -didi ji bhi to yahi kahti haim!

munshi ji-bilkul vahi badi-badi ankhen aur lal-lal
onth haim. ishvar ne mujhe mera NAmEmansaram is rup men de diya. vahi matha hai, vahi mumh, vahi hath-pamv! ishvar, tumhari lila apar hai!!
sahsa NAmERukmini bhi a gai. munshi ji ko dekhte hi boli--dekho babu, NAmEmansaram hai ki nahin? vahi aya hai! koi lakh kahe, maim na manengi, saph NAmEmansaram hai. sal bhar ke lagbhag ho bhi to gaya.

munshi ji-bahin, ek-ek anga milta hai! vas, bhagavan ne mujhe mera NAmEmansaram de diya. (bachcha se) kyon ri, tu NAmEmansaram hi hai? chhod kar jane ka nam na lena, nahin phir khich launga! kaise nishthur hokar bhage the. najir pakad laya ki nahin? bas kah diya, ab mujhe chhod kar jan ka nam na lena. dekho bahin, kaisa tukur-tukur tak rahi hai?

usi pal munshi ji ne phir se abhilashaon ka bhavan banana shuru kar diya. moh ne unhen phir samsar ki or khincha. manav-jivan! tu kitna pal-bhadra hai; par teri kalpanaen kitni dirghayu! vahi NAmETotaram jo samsar se virakt ho rahe the, jo rat-din maut ka avahan kiya karte the, tinke ka sahara pakad tat par pahunchne ke lie puri shakti se hath-pov mar rahe haim.
