# Chapter 14

chaudahavaa.N parichchheda

teenon baaten ek hee saatha hueen—nirmalaa kee kanyaa ne janma liya, krishNaa kaa vivaaha nishchita hua; aur munshee totaaraama kaa makaana neelaama ho gaya! kanyaa kaa janma to aama baat thi! yadyapi nirmalaa kee najara men yaha usake jeevana kee sabase mahaan ghatanaa thi; lekin shesha donon ghatanaae.N asaadhaaraNa theen! krishNaa kaa vivaaha aise sampanna gharaane men kyon kar theeka hua? usakee maa.N ke paasa to daheja ke naama kaudee bhee na thi; aur idhara boodhe sinhaa saahaba jo aba penshana lekara ghar aa gaye the, biraadaree men mahaa lobhee mashahoora the| vaha apane betaa kaa vivaaha aise daridra gharaane men karane para kaise raajee hue? kisee ko sahasaa yakeena na aata tha| isase bhee bade hairaanee kee baat munshee jee ke makaana kaa neelaama honaa tha| log munshee jee ko agar lakhapatee naheen, to badaa aadamee avashya samajhate the| unakaa makaana kaise neelaama hua? baat yaha thi ki munshee jee ne ek mahaajana se
kuchha rupaye karja lekara ek gaa.Nva rahana rakkhaa tha|unhen ummeeda thi ki saala aadha saala men yaha rupaye pataa denge|phir dasa-paa.Ncha saala men us gaa.Nva para bhee kabjaa kar lenge| vaha jameendaara asala aur sooda ke kuchha rupaye adaa karane men asamartha ho jaayagaa| isee bharose para munshee jee ne vaha maamalaa kiya tha| gaa.Nva bahuta badaa tha-chaara-paa.Ncha sau rupayaa naphaa hota tha;lekin mana kee sochee mana hee men raha gaee | munshee jee dil ko bahuta samajhaane para bhee kachaharee na jaa sake | betaa-shoka ne unamen koee kaama karane kee shakti hee naheen chhodee! kauna aisaa dil-shoonya pitaa hai jo apane betaa kee gardana para talavaara chalaa kar chitta ko shaanta karale?

mahaajana ke paasa jaba saala bhara taka sooda na pahu.Nchaa; aur na usake baara-baara bulaane para munshee jee usake paasa gaye yahaa.N taka ki pichhalee baara unhonne saapha-saapha kaha diya ki hama kisee ke gulaama naheen hain; saahoo jee jo chaahen karen, taba saahoo jee ko gussaa
aa gaya| usane naalisha kar dee| munshee jee pairavee karane bhee na gaye | ekataraphaa digree ho gaee! yahaa.N ghar men rupaye kahaa.N rakhe the? itane hee dinon men munshee jee kee saakha bhee utha gaee thi| vaha rupaye kaa koee pravandha na kar sake| aakhira makaana neelaama para chadha gaya| nirmalaa saura men thi| yaha khabara sunee, to kalejaa sanna saa ho gaya! jeevana men aur koee sukha na hone para bhee dhanaabhaava kee chintaaon se mukta thi! paisaa maanava-jeevana men agar sarva-pradhaana vastu naheen, to vaha usake vahuta paasa kee vastuavashya hai| aba aur
abhaavon ke saatha yaha chintaa bhee usake sira savaara huee, usane daaee dvaaraa kahalaa bhejaa mere sab gahane becha kar ghar ko bachaa leejiye; lekin munshee jee ne yaha prastaava kisee taraha sveekaara na kiya|

us din se munshee jee aur bhee chintaa-yasta rahane lage| jisa paisaa kaa mu.Nha bhogane ke lie unhonne vivaaha kiya tha,vaha aba ateeta kee smriti-maatra tha| vaha maare pachhataavaa ke aba nirmalaa ko apanaa mu.Nha taka na dikhaa sakate the| unhen aba us anyaaya kaa anumaana ho raha tha,jo unhonne nirmalaa ke saatha kiya tha,aur kanyaa ke janma ne to rahi-sahee kasara bhee pooree kar dee-sarvanaasha hee kar daalaa!

baarahaven din saura se nikala kar nirmalaa navajaata bachchaa ko goda men lie pati ke paasa gaee| vaha is abhaava men bhee itanee khusha thi, maano use koee chintaa naheen hai! ladakee ko dil se lagaa kar vaha apanee saaree chintaa bhoola gaee thi! bachchaa ke vikasita aur harsha-pradeepta netro ko dekha kar usakaa dil praphullita ho raha tha! maatritva ke is udgaara men usake saare klesha vileena ho gae the! vaha bachchaa ko pati kee goda men dekara nihaala ho jana chaahatee thi, lekin munshee jee kanyaa ko dekha kar sahama uthe| goda men lene ke lie unakaa dil hulasaayaa naheen; para unhone ek baara use karuNa-netron se dekha; aur phir sira jhukaa liya| bachchaa kee soorata mansaaraama se bilakula milatee thi!

nirmalaa ne unake mana kaa bhaava kuchha aur hee samajhaa! usane
shata-guNa pyaara se ladakee ko dil se lagaa liya; maano unase kaha rahi hai- agar tuma isake bojha se dabe jaate ho, to aaja se main is para tumhaaraa saayaa bhee na padane doongee| jisa ratna ko main ne itanee tapasyaa ke baada paayaa hai, usakaa niraadara karte hue tumhaaraa dil phata naheen jata? vaha usee pala bachchaa ko goda se chipataae hue apane kamare men chalee aaee; aur dera taka rotee rahi! usane pati kee is udaaseenataa ko samajhane kee jaraa bhee cheshtaa na kee; naheen to shaayada vaha unhen itanaa kathora na samajhatee| usake sira para uttaradaayitva kaa itanaa badaa bhaara kahaa.N tha,jo usake pati para aa padaa tha? kyaa vaha sochane kee cheshtaa karti, to itanaa bhee usakee samajha men na aata?

munshee jee ko ek hee pala men apanee bhoola maalooma ho gaee| maa.N kaa dil pyaara men itanaa anurakta rahataa hai ki bhavishya kee chintaa aur baadhaae.N use jaraa bhee bhayabheeta naheen karti! use apane mana men ek alaukika shakti kaa anubhava hota hai, jo baadhaaon ko usake saamane paraasta kar detee hai| munshee jee turanta daude hue ghar men aae; aur bachchaa ko goda men lekara bole-mujhe yaada aata hai, mansaa bhee aisaa hee tha-bilakula aisaa hee!

nirmalaa-deedee jee bhee to yahee kehti hain!

munshee jee-bilakula vahee badee-badee aa.Nkhen aur laala-laala
ontha hain| eeshvara ne mujhe meraa mansaaraama is roopa men de diya| vahee 'maathaa hai, vahee mu.Nha, vahee haath-paa.Nva! eeshvara,tumhaaree leelaa apaara hai!!
sahasaa rukmiNee bhee aa gaee| munshee jee ko dekhate hee bolee--dekho baaboo,mansaaraama hai ki naheen? vahee aaya hai! koee laakha kahe,main na maanengee,saapha mansaaraama hai| saala bhara ke lagabhaga ho bhee to gaya|

munshee jee-bahina,ek-ek anga milataa hai! vasa,bhagavaan ne mujhe meraa mansaaraama de diya| (bachchaa se) kyon ree,too mansaaraama hee hai? chhoda kar jaane kaa naama na lenaa,naheen phir kheecha laaoo.Ngaa! kaise nishthura hokara bhaage the| naajira pakada laayaa ki naheen? basa kaha diya,aba mujhe chhoda kar jaana kaa naama na lenaa| dekho bahina, kaisaa tukura-tukura taaka rahi hai?

usee pala munshee jee ne phir se abhilaashaaon kaa bhavana banaanaa shuroo kar diya| moha ne unhen phir sansaara kee ora kheenchaa| maanava-jeevana! too kitanaa pala-bhadra hai;para teree kalpanaae.N kitanee deerghaayu! vahee totaaraama jo sansaara se virakta ho rahe the,jo raat-din mrityu kaa aavaahana kiya karte the,tinake kaa sahaaraa paakara tata para pahu.Nchane ke lie pooree shakti se haath-pova maara rahe hain|

magara tinake kaa sahaaraa paakara koee tata para pahunchaa hai?
