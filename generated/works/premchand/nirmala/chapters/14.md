# Nirmala — Adhyay 14

**Munshi Premchand**

chaudahavaan parichchheda

teenon baaten ek hee saatha hueen—nirmalaa kee kanyaa ne janma liya, krishnaa kaa vivaaha nishchita hua; aur munshee totaaraama kaa makaana neelaama ho gaya! kanyaa kaa janma to saadhaarana baat thi! yadyapi nirmalaa kee drishti men yaha usake jeevana kee sabase mahaan ghatana thi; lekin shesha donon ghatanaaen asaadhaarana theen! krishnaa kaa vivaaha aise sampanna gharaane men kyon kar theeka hua? usakee maataa ke paasa to daheja ke naama kau.dee bhee na thi; aur idhara boo.dhe sinhaa saahaba jo ab penshana lekara ghar aa gaye the, biraadaree men mahaa lobhee mashahoora the| vaha apane putra kaa vivaaha aise daridra gharaane men karane par kaise raazee hue? kisee ko sahasaa vishvaasa na aata tha| isase bhee ba.de aashcharya kee baat munshee jee ke makaana kaa neelaama honaa tha| log munshee jee ko agar lakhapatee naheen, to bada aadmi avashya samajhate the| unakaa makaana kaise neelaama hua? baat yaha thi ki munshee jee ne ek mahaajana se kuchha rupaye karja lekara ek gaon rahana rakkhaa tha|unhen aashaa thi ki saala aadha saala men yaha rupaye pata denge|phir dasa-paancha saala men us gaon par bhee kabjaa kar lenge| vaha jameendaara asala aur sooda ke kuchha rupaye adaa karane men asamartha ho jaayagaa| isee bharose par munshee jee ne vaha maamalaa kiya tha| gaon bahut bada tha-chaara-paancha sau rupaya naphaa hota tha;lekin mann kee sochee mann hee men raha gaee | munshee jee dil ko bahut samajhaane par bhee kachaharee na jaa sake | putra-shoka ne unamen koee kaama karane kee shakti hee naheen chho.dee! kaun aisaa hridaya-shoonya pitaa hai jo apane putra kee gardana par talavaara chalaa kar chitta ko shaanta karale?

mahaajana ke paasa jab saala bhara taka sooda na pahuncha; aur na usake baara-baara bulaane par munshee jee usake paasa gaye yahaan taka ki pichhalee baara unhonne saapha-saapha kaha diya ki hama kisee ke gulaama naheen hain; saahoo jee jo chaahen karen, tab saahoo jee ko gussaa
aa gaya| usane naalisha kar dee| munshee jee pairavee karane bhee na gaye | ekatarafaa digree ho gaee! yahaan ghar men rupaye kahaan rakhe the? itane hee dinon men munshee jee kee saakha bhee utha gaee thi| vaha rupaye kaa koee pravandha na kar sake| aakhira makaana neelaama par cha.dha gaya| nirmalaa saura men thi| yaha khabara sunee, to kalejaa sanna saa ho gaya! jeevana men aur koee sukha na hone par bhee dhanaabhaava kee chintaaon se mukta thi! dhana maanava-jeevana men agar sarva-pradhaana vastu naheen, to vaha usake vahuta nikata kee vastuavashya hai| ab aur abhaavon ke saatha yaha chintaa bhee usake sira savaara hui, usane daaee dvaaraa kahalaa bhejaa mere sab gahane becha kar ghar ko bachaa leejiye; lekin munshee jee ne yaha prastaava kisee tarah sveekaara na kiya|

us din se munshee jee aur bhee chintaa-yasta rahane lage| jisa dhana kaa mukha bhogane ke lie unhonne vivaaha kiya tha,vaha ab ateeta kee smriti-maatra tha| vaha maare glaani ke ab nirmalaa ko apana munha taka na dikhaa sakate the| unhen ab us anyaaya kaa anumaana ho raha tha,jo unhonne nirmalaa ke saatha kiya tha,aur kanyaa ke janma ne to rahi-sahee kasara bhee pooree kar dee-sarvanaasha hee kar daala!

baarahaven din saura se nikala kar nirmalaa navajaata shishu ko goda men lie pati ke paasa gaee| vaha is abhaava men bhee itanee prasanna thi, maano use koee chintaa naheen hai! baalikaa ko hridaya se lagaa kar vaha apanee saaree chintaa bhoola gaee thi! shishu ke vikasita aur harsha-pradeepta netro ko dekha kar usakaa hridaya praphullita ho raha tha! maatritva ke is udgaara men usake saare klesha vileena ho gae the! vaha shishu ko pati kee goda men dekara nihaala ho jana chaahatee thi, lekin munshee jee kanyaa ko dekha kar sahama uthe| goda men lene ke lie unakaa hridaya hulasaayaa naheen; par unhone ek baara use karuna-netron se dekha; aur phir sira jhukaa liya| shishu kee soorata mansaaraama se bilakula milatee thi!

nirmalaa ne unake mann kaa bhaava kuchha aur hee samajha! usane shata-guna sneha se ladki ko hridaya se lagaa liya; maano unase kaha rahi hai- agar tuma isake bojha se dabe jaate ho, to aaja se main is par tumhaaraa saayaa bhee na pa.dane doongee| jisa ratna ko main ne itanee tapasyaa ke baada paayaa hai, usakaa niraadara karte hue tumhaaraa hridaya phata naheen jaata? vaha usee kshana shishu ko goda se chipataae hue apane kamare men chalee aaee; aur dera taka rotee rahi! usane pati kee is udaaseenata ko samajhane kee zaraa bhee cheshtaa na kee; naheen to shaayada vaha unhen itana kathora na samajhatee| usake sira par uttaradaayitva kaa itana bada bhaara kahaan tha,jo usake pati par aa pada tha? kyaa vaha sochane kee cheshtaa karti, to itana bhee usakee samajha men na aata?

munshee jee ko ek hee kshana men apanee bhoola maalooma ho gaee| maataa kaa hridaya prema men itana anurakta rahata hai ki bhavishya kee chintaa aur baadhaaen use jaraa bhee bhayabheeta naheen karti! use apane antahkarana men ek alaukika shakti kaa anubhava hota hai, jo baadhaaon ko usake saamane paraasta kar detee hai| munshee jee turanta dau.de hue ghar men aae; aur shishu ko goda men lekara bole-mujhe yaada aata hai, mansaa bhee aisaa hee tha-bilakula aisaa hee!

nirmalaa-deedee jee bhee to yahee kehti hain!

munshee jee-bilakula vahee ba.dee-ba.dee aankhen aur laala-laala
ontha hain| eeshvara ne mujhe meraa mansaaraama is roopa men de diya| vahee 'maathaa hai, vahee munha, vahee haath-paanva! eeshvara,tumhaaree leelaa apaara hai!! sahasaa rukminee bhee aa gaee| munshee jee ko dekhate hee boli--dekho baaboo,mansaaraama hai ki naheen? vahee aaya hai! koee laakha kahe,main na maanengee,saapha mansaaraama hai| saala bhara ke lagabhaga ho bhee to gaya|

munshee jee-bahina,ek-ek anga milata hai! vasa,bhagavaan ne mujhe meraa mansaaraama de diya| (shishu se) kyon ree,too mansaaraama hee hai? chho.da kar jaane kaa naama na lena,naheen phir kheecha laaoonga! kaise nishthura hokara bhaage the| naajira paka.da laayaa ki naheen? basa kaha diya,ab mujhe chho.da kar jaana kaa naama na lena| dekho bahina, kaisaa tukura-tukura taaka rahi hai?

usee kshana munshee jee ne phir se abhilaashaaon kaa bhavana banaanaa shuroo kar diya| moha ne unhen phir sansaara kee ora kheenchaa| maanava-jeevana! too kitana kshana-bhadra hai;par teree kalpanaaen kitanee deerghaayu! vahee totaaraama jo sansaara se virakta ho rahe the,jo raat-din mrityu kaa aavaahana kiya karte the,tinake kaa sahaaraa paakara tata par pahunchane ke lie pooree shakti se haath-paova maara rahe hain|

magar tinake kaa sahaaraa paakara koee tata par pahunchaa hai?
