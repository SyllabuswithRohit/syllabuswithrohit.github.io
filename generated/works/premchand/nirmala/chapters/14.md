# Nirmala — Adhyay 14

**Munshi Premchand**

chaudahavaan parichchhed

teenon baaten ek hee saath hueen — Nirmala ki kanyaa ne paidaa honaa liyaa, krishnaa ka shaadee pakkaa huaa; aur munshee totaaraam ka makaan neelaam ho gayaa! kanyaa ka paidaa honaa to aam baat thi! halaanki Nirmala ki drishti mein yah usake zindagi ki sabase mahaan ghatanaa thi; lekin shesh donon ghatanaaen bahut alag theen! krishnaa ka shaadee aise sampann gharaane mein kyon kar theek huaa? usakee maataa ke paas to dahej ke naam kaudee bhi n thi; aur idhar boodhe sinhaa Sahab jo ab penshan lekar ghar aa gaye the, biraadaree mein mahaa lobhee mashahoor the. vah apane putr ka shaadee aise daridr gharaane mein karane par kaise raajee hue? kisee ko sahasaa bharosaa n aataa thaa. isase bhi bade hairaanee ki baat munshee jee ke makaan ka neelaam honaa thaa. log munshee jee ko agar lakhapatee nahin, to badaa aadmi zaroor samajhate the. unakaa makaan kaise neelaam huaa? baat yah thi ki munshee jee ne ek mahaajan se kuchh rupaye karz lekar ek gaon rahna rakkhaa thaa. unhen ummeed thi ki saal aadh saal mein yah rupaye pataa dengge. phir das-paanch saal mein us gaon par bhi kabjaa kar lengge. vah jameendaar asal aur sood ke kuchh rupaye adaa karane mein asamarth ho jaayagaa. isee bharose par munshee jee ne vah maamalaa kiyaa thaa. gaon bahut badaa tha-chaar-paanch sau rupaya naphaa hotaa tha; lekin man ki sochee man hee mein rah gaee. munshee jee dil ko bahut samajhaane par bhi kachaharee n jaa sake. putr-shok ne unamen koee kaam karane ki taakat hee nahin chhodee! kaun aisaa dil-shoony pitaa hai jo apane putr ki gardan par talavaar chalaa kar chitt ko shaant karale?

mahaajan ke paas jab saal bhar tak sood n pahunchaa; aur n usake baar-baar bulaane par munshee jee usake paas gaye yahan tak ki pichhalee baar unhonne saaph-saaph kah diyaa ki ham kisee ke gulaam nahin hain; saahoo jee jo chaahen karen, tab saahoo jee ko gussaa
aa gayaa. usane naalish kar dee. munshee jee pairavee karane bhi n gaye. ekataraphaa digree ho gaee! yahan ghar mein rupaye kahaan rakhe the? itane hee dinon mein munshee jee ki saakh bhi uth gaee thee. vah rupaye ka koee pravandh n kar sake. aakhir makaan neelaam par chadh gayaa. Nirmala saur mein thee. yah khabar sunee, to kalejaa sann saa ho gayaa! zindagi mein aur koee sukh n hone par bhi dhanaabhaav ki phikraon se mukt thi! dhan maanav-zindagi mein agar sarv-pradhaan vastu nahin, to vah usake vahut nikat ki vastujaroor hai. ab aur abhaavon ke saath yah fikr bhi usake sir savaar huee, usane daaee dvaaraa kahalaa bhejaa mere sab gahane bech kar ghar ko bachaa leejiye; lekin munshee jee ne yah prastaav kisee tarah maan lenaa n kiyaa.

us din se munshee jee aur bhi fikr-yast rahane lage. jis dhan ka munh bhogane ke lie unhonne shaadee kiyaa tha, vah ab ateet ki yaad-maatr thaa. vah maare glaani ke ab Nirmala ko apanaa munh tak n dikhaa sakate the. unhen ab us naainsaaphee ka anumaan ho rahaa tha, jo unhonne Nirmala ke saath kiyaa tha, aur kanyaa ke paidaa honaa ne to rahee-sahee kasar bhi pooree kar dee-sarvanaash hee kar daalaa!

baarahaven din saur se nikal kar Nirmala navajaat shishu ko god mein lie shauhar ke paas gaee. vah is abhaav mein bhi itanee khush thi, maano use koee fikr nahin hai! ladakee ko dil se lagaa kar vah apanee saaree fikr bhool gaee thi! shishu ke vikasit aur harsh-pradeept aangkheno ko dekh kar usakaa dil praphullit ho rahaa tha! maatritv ke is udgaar mein usake saare klesh vileen ho gae the! vah shishu ko shauhar ki god mein dekar nihaal ho jaanaa chaahatee thi, lekin munshee jee kanyaa ko dekh kar saham uthe. god mein lene ke lie unakaa dil hulasaayaa nahin; par unhone ek baar use karun-aangkhenon se dekhaa; aur phir sir jhukaa liyaa. shishu ki soorat mansaaraam se bilakul milatee thi!

Nirmala ne unake man ka bhaav kuchh aur hee samajhaa! usane shat-gun pyaar se ladakee ko dil se lagaa liyaa; maano unase kah rahee hai- agar tum isake bojh se dabe jaate ho, to aaj se main is par tumhaaraa saayaa bhi n padane doonggee. jis ratn ko main ne itanee tapasyaa ke baad paayaa hai, usakaa niraadar karate hue tumhaaraa dil phat nahin jaataa? vah usee pal shishu ko god se chipataae hue apane kamare mein chalee aaee; aur der tak rotee rahee! usane shauhar ki is udaaseenataa ko samajhane ki jaraa bhi koshish n ki; nahin to shaayad vah unhen itanaa sakht n samajhatee. usake sir par javaabadaayitv ka itanaa badaa bhaar kahaan tha, jo usake shauhar par aa padaa tha? kyaa vah sochane ki koshish karatee, to itanaa bhi usakee samajh mein n aataa?

munshee jee ko ek hee pal mein apanee bhool maaloom ho gaee. maataa ka dil pyaar mein itanaa anurakt rahataa hai ki bhavishy ki fikr aur baadhaaen use jaraa bhi darabheet nahin karatee! use apane dil mein ek alaukik taakat ka tajurbaa hotaa hai, jo baadhaaon ko usake saamane paraast kar detee hai. munshee jee turant daude hue ghar mein aae; aur shishu ko god mein lekar bole-mujhe yaad aataa hai, mansaa bhi aisaa hee tha-bilakul aisaa hee!

Nirmala-deedee jee bhi to yahee kahatee hain!

Bhagwan, tumhaaree leelaa apaar hai!! sahasaa rukminee bhi aa gaee. munshee jee ko dekhate hee bolee--dekho baaboo, mansaaraam hai ki nahin? vahee kamaaeeaa hai! koee laakh kahe, main n maanenggee, saaph mansaaraam hai. saal bhar ke lagabhag ho bhi to gayaa.

munshee jee-bahin, ek-ek anggg milataa hai! vas, Bhagwan ne mujhe meraa mansaaraam de diyaa. (shishu se) kyon ree, too mansaaraam hee hai? chhod kar jaane ka naam n lenaa, nahin phir kheech laaoonggaa! kaise nishthur hokar bhaage the. naajir pakad laayaa ki nahin? bas kah diyaa, ab mujhe chhod kar jaan ka naam n lenaa. dekho bahin, kaisaa tukur-tukur taak rahee hai?

usee pal munshee jee ne phir se abhilaashaaon ka bhavan banaanaa shuroo kar diyaa. moh ne unhen phir sansaar ki or kheenchaa. maanav-zindagi! too kitanaa pal-bhadr hai; par teree khayaalaen kitanee deerghaayu! vahee totaaraam jo sansaar se virakt ho rahe the, jo raat-din maut ka aavaahan kiyaa karate the, tinake ka sahaaraa paakar tat par pahunchane ke lie pooree taakat se haath-pov maar rahe hain.
