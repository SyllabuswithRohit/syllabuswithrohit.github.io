**Munshi Premchand**

teenon baaten ek hee saath hueen—nirml kee kanyaa ne janm liyaa, krishnaa kaa shaadee nishchit huaa; aur munshee totaaraam kaa makaan neelaam ho gayaa! kanyaa kaa janm to aam baat thee! haalaanki nirml kee nazar men yeh usake jeevan kee sabase mahaan baat thee; lekin shesh donon ghatanaaen asaadhaaran theen! krishnaa kaa shaadee aise sampann gharaane men kyon kar theek huaa? usakee maan ke paas to dahej ke naam kau. dee bhee na thee; aur idhar boo. dhe sinhaa saahab jo ab penshan lekar ghar aa gaye the, biraadaree men mahaa lobhee mashahoor the. woh apane betaa kaa shaadee aise gareeb gharaane men karane par kaise raazee hue? kisee ko sahasaa yakeen na aataa thaa. isase bhee ba. de hairaanee kee baat munshee jee ke makaan kaa neelaam honaa thaa. log munshee jee ko agar lakhapatee naheen, to ba. daa aadamee zaroor samajhate the. unakaa makaan kaise neelaam huaa? baat yeh thee ki munshee jee ne ek mahaajan se
kuchh rupaye karz lekar ek gaanv rahan rakkhaa thaa. unhen ummeed thee ki saal aadh saal men yeh rupaye pataa denge. phir dasa-paanch saal men us gaanv par bhee kabjaa kar lenge. woh jameendaar asal aur sood ke kuchh rupaye adaa karane men asamarth ho jaayagaa. isee bharose par munshee jee ne woh maaml kiyaa thaa. gaanv bahut ba. daa thaa-chaara-paanch sau rupayaa naphaa hotaa thaa; lekin man kee sochee man hee men rah gaee. munshee jee dil ko bahut samajhaane par bhee kachaharee na jaa sake. betaa-shok ne unamen koee kaam karane kee shakti hee naheen chho. dee! kaun aisaa dil-shoony pitaa hai jo apane betaa kee gardan par talavaar chl kar chitt ko shaant karale?

aa gayaa. usane naalish kar dee. munshee jee pairavee karane bhee na gaye. ekatarafaa digree ho gaee! yahaan ghar men rupaye kahaan rakhe the? itane hee dinon men munshee jee kee saakh bhee uth gaee thee. woh rupaye kaa koee pravandh na kar sake. aakhir makaan neelaam par cha. dh gayaa. nirml saur men thee. yeh khabar sunee, to kalejaa sann saa ho gayaa! jeevan men aur koee sukh na hone par bhee dhanaabhaav kee chintaaon se mukt thee! paisaa maanava-jeevan men agar sarva-pradhaan vastu naheen, to woh usake vahut paas kee vastuavashy hai. ab aur
abhaavon ke saath yeh chintaa bhee usake sir savaar huee, usane daaee dvaaraa kahl bhejaa mere sab gahane bech kar ghar ko bachaa leejiye; lekin munshee jee ne yeh prastaav kisee tarah sveekaar na kiyaa.

us din se munshee jee aur bhee chintaa-yast rahane lage. jis paisaa kaa munh bhogane ke lie unhonne shaadee kiyaa thaa, woh ab beetaa samay kee smriti-maatr thaa. woh maare pachhataavaa ke ab nirml ko apn munh tak na dikhaa sakate the. unhen ab us anyaay kaa anumaan ho rahaa thaa, jo unhonne nirml ke saath kiyaa thaa, aur kanyaa ke janm ne to rahee-sahee kasar bhee pooree kar dee-sarvanaash hee kar daalaa!

baarahaven din saur se nikal kar nirml navajaat bachchaa ko god men lie pati ke paas gaee. woh is abhaav men bhee itanee khush thee, maano use koee chintaa naheen hai! la. dakee ko dil se lagaa kar woh apanee saaree chintaa bhool gaee thee! bachchaa ke vikasit aur harsha-pradeept netro ko dekh kar usakaa dil praphullit ho rahaa thaa! maatritv ke is udgaar men usake saare klesh vileen ho gae the! woh bachchaa ko pati kee god men dekar nihaal ho jaanaa chaahatee thee, lekin munshee jee kanyaa ko dekh kar saham uthe. god men lene ke lie unakaa dil hulasaayaa naheen; par unhone ek baar use karuna-netron se dekhaa; aur phir sir jhukaa liyaa. bachchaa kee soorat mansaaraam se bilakul milatee thee!

nirml ne unake man kaa bhaav kuchh aur hee samajhaa! usane
shata-gun pyaar se la. dakee ko dil se lagaa liyaa; maano unase kah rahee hai- agar tum isake bojh se dabe jaate ho, to aaj se main is par tumhaaraa saayaa bhee na pa. dane doongee. jis ratn ko main ne itanee tapasyaa ke baad paayaa hai, usakaa niraadar karate hue tumhaaraa dil phat naheen jaataa? woh usee pal bachchaa ko god se chipataae hue apane kamare men chalee aaee; aur der tak rotee rahee! usane pati kee is udaaseenataa ko samajhane kee zr bhee cheshtaa na kee; naheen to shaayad woh unhen itn kathor na samajhatee. usake sir par uttaradaayitv kaa itn ba. daa bhaar kahaan thaa, jo usake pati par aa pa. daa thaa? kyaa woh sochane kee cheshtaa karatee, to itn bhee usakee samajh men na aataa?

munshee jee ko ek hee pal men apanee bhool maaloom ho gaee. maan kaa dil pyaar men itn anurakt rahataa hai ki aane vaalaa samay kee chintaa aur baadhaaen use jr bhee bhayabheet naheen karatee! use apane man men ek alaukik shakti kaa anubhav hotaa hai, jo baadhaaon ko usake saamane paraast kar detee hai. munshee jee turant dau. de hue ghar men aae; aur bachchaa ko god men lekar bole-mujhe yaad aataa hai, mansaa bhee aisaa hee thaa-bilakul aisaa hee!
