# Nirmala — Adhyay 9

**Munshi Premchand**

navaan parichchhed

mansaaraam ke jaane se ghar soonaa ho gayaa. donon chhote ladake usee skool mein padhate the. Nirmala roj unase mansaaraam ka haal poochhatee. ummeed thi ki chhuttee ke din vah aaegaa; lekin jab chhuttee ke din gujar gae; aur vah n kamaaeeaa, to Nirmala ki tabeeyat ghabaraane lagee. usane usake lie moongg ke lad‌doo banaa rakkhe the. somavaar ko subah bhungggee ko lad‌doo dekar madarase bhejaa. nau baje bhungggee laut aaee. mansaaraam ne lad‌doo jyon ke tyon lautaa die the.

Nirmala ne poochhaa — pahale se kuchh hare hue hain re?

bhungggee — hare-vare to nahin hue, aur sookh gae hain!

Nirmala — kyaa jee achchha nahin hai kyaa?

bhungggee — yah to mainne nahin poochhaa bahoo jee, jhooth kyon boloon. haan, wahan ka kahaar meraa devar lagataa hai. vah kahataa tha ki tumhaare baaboo jee ki khuraak kuchh nahin hai. do phulakiyaan khaakar uth jaate hain. phir din bhar kuchh nahin khaate. har dam padhate rahate hain. Nirmala-toone poochhaa nahin, laddoo kyon lautaae dete ho?

bhungggee-yah to nahin poochhaa bahoo jee, jhooth kyon boloon. unhonne kahaa ise letee jaa, yahan rakhane ka kuchh kaam nahin, main letee aaee.

Nirmala aur kuchh nahin kahate the. poochhaa nahin, kal kyon nahin aae? chhuttee to thee.

bhungggee-bahoo jee, jhooth kyon boloo; yah poochhane ki to mujhe sudh hee n rahee. haan, yah kahate the ki ab too yahan kabhee n aanaa, n mere lie koee cheej laanaa; aur apanee bahoo jee se kah denaa ki mere paas koee chitthee-pattar n bhejen; ladakon se bhi- mere paas koee sandeshaa n bhejen. aur ek baat aisee kahee bahoo jee ki mere munh se nikal nahin sakatee. phir rone lage.

Nirmala-kaun baat thi? kah to.

bhungggee kyaa kahoon bahoo jee, kahate the mere jeene ko dhikkaar hai. yahee kah kar rone lage!

Nirmala ke munh se ek thandee saans nikal gaee. aisaa maaloom huaa, maano kalejaa baithaa jaataa hai. usakaa rom-rom aartanaad se rudan karane lagaa. vah wahan baithee n rah sakee. jaakar bistar par munh dhaamp kar let rahee-aur phoot-phoot kar rone lagee. 'vah bhi jaan gae'usake dil mein baar-baar yahee aavaaj gajane lagee-vah jaan gae! Bhagwan ab kyaa hogaa? jis shak ki aag mein vah bhasm ho rahee thi, ab shat-gun veg se dhadhakane lagee. use apanee koee fikr n thee. zindagi mein ab sukh ki kyaa ummeed thi, jisakee use laalasaa hotee? usane apane man ko is soch se samajhaayaa tha ki yah mere pahale karmongkaa praayashchitt hai. kaun praanee aisaa nirlajj hogaa, jo is dashaa mein bahut din jee sake? farz ki vedee par usane apanaa zindagi aur usakee saaree kaamanaaen hom kar dee theen. dil rotaa rahataa tha; par munh par hansee ka ranggg bharanaa padataa thaa. jisakaa munh dekhane ko jee n chaahataa tha, usake saamane hans-hans kar, baaten karanee padatee theen. jis deh ka sparsh use sarp ke sheetal sparsh ke samaan lagataa tha, usase aalingggit hokar use jitanee nafrat, jitanee marm-dard hotee thi, use kaun jaan sakataa hai? us samay usakee yahee ichchhaa hotee thi, ki dharatee phat jaay; aur main usamen samaa jaaoon! lekin saaree vidambanaa apane hee tak thi, aur apanee fikr karanee usane chhod dee thi; lekin vah mushkil ab bahut daranggkar ho gaee thee. vah apanee aangkhon se mansaaraam ki aatm-dard nahin dekh sakatee thee. mansaaraam jaise manasvee, himmatee javaan aadmi par is aapeksh ka jo asar pad sakataa tha, usakee khayaal hee se usake praan kaamp uthate the. av chaahe us par kitane hee shak kyon n hon, chaahe use aatmahatyaa hee kyon n karanee pade. par vah chup nahin baith sakatee thee. mansaaraam ki bachaav karane ke lie vah vikal ho gaee. usane sanggkoch aur sharm ki chaadar utaar kar phengk dene ka pakkaa faisla kar liyaa.

vakeel Sahab khana karake kachaharee jaane ke pahale ek baar usase zaroor mil liyaa karate the. unake aane ka samay ho gayaa thaa. aa hee rahe hongge, yah soch kar Nirmala dvaar par khadee ho gaee aur unakaa intajaar karane lagee; lekin yah kyaa! vah to baahar chale jaa rahe hain. gaadee jut kar aa gaee, yah hukm vah yaheen se diyaa karate the. to kyaa aaj vah n aaengge, baahar hee baahar chale jaaengge. nahin aisaa nahin hone paavegaa. usane bhungggee se kahaa-jaakar baaboo jee ko bulaa laa. kahanaa ek jarooree kaam hai; sun leejie.

munshee jee jaane ko taiyaar hee the. yah sandeshaa paakar andar aae; par kamare mein n aakar door hee se poochhaa-kyaa baat hai, bhaaee? jaldee kah do, mujhe ek jarooree kaam se jaanaa hai. abhee thodee der huee hedamaastar Sahab ka ek patr kamaaeeaa hai ki mansaaraam ko jvar aa gayaa hai, behatar ho ki aap ghar hee par usakaa ilaaj karen. isalie udhar hee se hotaa huaa kachaharee jaaoonggaa. tumhe koee khaas baat to nahin kahanee hai.

Nirmala par maano vajr gir padaa. aansuon ke aaveg aur kanth-svar mein ghor sanggaanv hone lagaa. donon hee pahale nikalane par tule hue the. do mein se koee ek kadam bhi peechhe hatanaa nahin chaahataa tha. karaath-svar ki kamajorataa aur aansuon ki sabalataa dekh kar yah pakkaa faisla karanaa kathin nahin tha ki ek pal yahee sanggaanv hotaa rahaa, to maidaan kisake haath rahegaa? aakhir donon saath-saath nikale lekin baahar aate hee balavaan ne nirbal ko dabaa liyaa. keval itanaa munh se nikalaa-koee khaas baat nahin thee. aap to udhar jaa hee rahe hain.

munshee jee-mainne ladakon se poochhaa tha to ve kahate the, kal vaithe padh rahe the. aaj n jaane kyaa ho gayaa!

Nirmala ne aavesh se kopate hue kahaa-yah sav aap kar rahe hain.

munshee jee ne sriyoyaan vadal kar kahaa-main kar rahaa hoon! main kyaa kar rahaa hoon?

Nirmala-apane dil se poochhie.

munshee jee-mainne to yahee sochaa tha ki yahan usakaa padhane mein jee nahin lagataa, wahan aur ladakon ke saath khaamakhvaah hee padhegaa. yah to koee buree baat n thi, aur mainne kyaa kiyaa?

Nirmala-khoob sochie, iseelie aapane unhen wahan bhejaa tha? aap ke man mein koee aur baat n thi?

munshee jee jaraa hichakichaae aur apanee kamajorataa ko chhipaane ke lie muskaraane ki koshish karake vole-aur kyaa baat ho sakatee thi? bhalaa tumheen socho!

Nirmala-khair, yahee sahee. ab aap kripaa karake unhen aaj hee lete aaiegaa, wahan rahane se unakee beemaaree badh jaane ka dar hai. yahan deedee jee jitanee beemaaradaaree kar sakatee hain, doosaraa nahin kar sakataa.

ek pal ke baad usane sir neechaa karake phir kahaa-mere vajah n laanaa chaahate hon, to mujhe mere ghar bhej deejie. main wahan aaraam se rahoonggee.

munshee jee ne isakaa kuchh javaab n diyaa. baahar chale gae; aur ek pal mein gaadee skool ki or chalee.

man! teree gati kitanee ajeeb hai, kitanee rahasy se bharee huee, kitanee durmedh? too kitanee jald ranggg badalataa hai? is kalaa mein too nipun hai. aatishabaaj ki charbee ko bhi ranggg badalate kuchh der lagatee hai; par tujhe ranggg badalane mein usakaa lakshansh samay bhi nahin lagataa! jahaan abhee vaatsaly tha, wahan phir shak ne aasan jamaa liyaa.

vah sochate jaate the-kaheen usane bahaanaa to nahin kiyaa hai?
