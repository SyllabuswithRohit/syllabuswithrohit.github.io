# Nirmala — Adhyay 9

**Munshi Premchand**

navam parichchhed

NAmEmansaram ke jane se ghar suna ho gaya. donon chhote larke usi skul men parhte the. NAmENirmala roz unse NAmEmansaram ka hal puchhti. ummid thi ki chhutti ke din vah aega; lekin jab chhutti ke din guzar gae; aur vah na aya, to NAmENirmala ki tabiyat ghabrane lagi. usne uske lie mung ke laddu bana rakkhe the. somvar ko subah bhungi ko laddu dekar madrase bheja. nau baje bhungi laut ai. NAmEmansaram ne laddu jyon ke tyon lauta die the.

NAmENirmala ne puchhapahle se kuchh hare hue haim re?

bhungihare-vare to nahin hue, aur sukh gae haim!

NAmENirmala kya ji achchha nahin hai kya?

bhungiyah to maimne nahin puchha bahu ji, jhuth kyon bolum. ham, vaham ka kahar mera devar lagta hai. vah kahta tha ki tumhare babu ji ki khurak kuchh nahin hai. do phulkiya khakar uth jate haim. phir din bhar kuchh nahin khate. har dam parhte rahte haim.
NAmENirmala -tune puchha nahin, laddu kyon lautae dete ho?

bhungi-yah to nahin puchha bahu ji, jhuth kyon bolum. unhomne kaha ise leti ja, yaham rakhne ka kuchh kam nahin, maim leti ai.

NAmENirmala aur kuchh nahin kahte the. puchha nahin, kal kyon nahin ae? chhutti to thi.

bhungi-bahu ji, jhuth kyon bolu; yah puchhne ki to mujhe sudh hi na rahi. ham, yah kahte the ki ab tu yaham kabhi na ana, na mere lie koi chij lana; aur apni bahu ji se kah dena ki mere pas koi chitthi-pattar na bhejen; larkon se bhi- mere pas koi sandesha na bhejen. aur ek bat aisi kahi bahu ji ki mere mumh se nikal nahin sakti. phir rone lage.

NAmENirmala -kaun bat thi? kah to.

bhungi kya kahum bahu ji, kahte the mere jine ko dhikkar hai. yahi kah kar rone lage!

NAmENirmala ke mumh se ek thandi sams nikal gai. aisa malum hua, mano kaleja baitha jata hai. uska ron-ron artanad se rudan karne laga. vah vaham baithi na rah saki. jakar bistar par mumh dhamp kar let rahi-aur phut-phut kar rone lagi. vah bhi jan gaeuske man men bar-bar yahi avaj gajne lagi-vah jan gae! bhagavan ab kya hoga? jis shak ki ag men vah bhasma ho rahi thi, ab shat-gun veg se dhadhkane lagi. use apni koi chinta na thi. jivan men ab sukh ki
kya ummid thi, jiski use lalsa hoti? usne apne man ko is soch se samjhaya tha ki yah mere pahle karmonka prayashchitt hai. kaun insaan aisa nirlajj hoga, jo is hal men bahut din ji sake? pharz ki vedi par usne apna jivan aur uski sari kamanaen hon kar di thim. dil rota rahta tha; par mumh par hamsi ka ranga bharna parta tha. jiska mumh dekhne ko ji na chahta tha, uske samne hams-hams kar, baten karni parti thim. jis deh ka sparsh use sarp ke shital sparsh ke saman lagta tha, usse alingit hokar use jitni naphrat, jitni marm-dard hoti thi, use kaun jan sakta hai? us samay uski yahi chah hoti thi, ki dharti phat jay; aur maim usmen sama jaum! lekin sari vidambana apne hi tak thi, aur apni chinta karni usne chhod di thi; lekin vah samasya ab bahut bhayankar ho gai thi. vah apni ankhon se NAmEmansaram ki atma-dard nahin dekh sakti thi. NAmEmansaram jaise manasvi, sahsi yuvak par is apeksh ka jo asar par sakta tha, uski kalpana hi se uske jan kamp uthte the. av chahe us par kitne hi shak kyon na hon, chahe use atmahatya hi kyon na karni pade. par vah chup nahin baith sakti thi. NAmEmansaram ki raksha karne ke lie vah vikal ho gai. usne sankoch aur sharm ki chadar utar kar phenk dene ka phaisla kar liya.

vakil sahab khana karke kachhari jane ke pahle ek bar
usse zarur mil liya karte the. unke ane ka samay ho gaya tha. a hi rahe honge, yah soch kar NAmENirmala dvar par khadi ho gai aur unka intajar karne lagi; lekin yah kya! vah to bahar chale ja rahe haim. gari jut kar a gai, yah hukma vah yahim se diya karte the. to kya aj vah na aenge, bahar hi bahar chale jaenge. nahin aisa nahin hone pavega. usne bhungi se kaha-jakar babu ji ko bula la. kahna ek jaruri kam hai; sun lijie.

munshi ji jane ko taiyar hi the. yah sandesha pakad andar ae; par kamre men na akar dur hi se puchha-kya bat hai, bhai? jaldi kah do, mujhe ek jaruri kam se jana hai. abhi thodi der hui hedamastar sahab ka ek chitthi aya hai ki NAmEmansaram ko jvar a gaya hai, behtar ho ki ap ghar hi par uska ilaj karen. isalie udhar hi se hota hua kachhari jaunga. tumhen koi khas bat to nahin kahni hai.

NAmENirmala par mano vajra gir pada. amsuon ke aveg aur gala-avaz men ghor larai hone laga. donon hi pahle niklane par tule hue the. do men se koi ek kadam bhi pichhe hatna nahin chahta tha. karath-avaz ki durbalta aur amsuon ki sablata dekh kar yah phaisla karna mushkil nahin tha ki ek pal yahi larai hota raha, to maidan kiske hath rahega? akhir donon sath-sath nikle lekin bahar ate hi balvan ne nirbal ko daba liya.
sirph itna mumh se nikla-koi khas bat nahin thi. ap to udhar ja hi rahe haim.

munshi ji-maimne larkon se puchha tha to ve kahte the, kal vaithe parh rahe the. aj na jane kya ho gaya!

NAmENirmala ne avesh se kopte hue kaha-yah sav ap kar rahe haim.

munshi ji ne sriyoyam vadal kar kaha-maim kar raha hum! maim kya kar raha hum?

NAmENirmala -apne dil se puchhie.

munshi ji-maimne to yahi socha tha ki yaham uska parhne men ji nahin lagta, vaham aur larkon ke sath khamakhvah hi parhega. yah to koi buri bat na thi, aur maimne kya kiya?

NAmENirmala -khub sochie, isilie apne unhen vaham bheja tha? ap ke man men koi aur bat na thi?

munshi ji jara hichakichae aur apni durbalta ko chhipane ke lie muskarane ki cheshta karke vole-aur kya bat ho sakti thi? bhala tumhim socho!

NAmENirmala -khair, yahi sahi. ab ap kripa karke unhen aj hi lete aiega, vaham rahne se unki bimari barh jane ka dar hai. yaham didi ji jitni bimardari kar sakti haim, dusra nahin kar sakta.

ek pal ke bad usne sir nicha karke phir kaha-mere
vajah na lana chahte hon, to mujhe mere ghar bhej dijie. maim vaham aram se rahungi.

munshi ji ne iska kuchh javab na diya. bahar chale gae; aur ek pal men gari skul ki or chali.

man! teri gati kitni vichitra hai, kitni rahasya se bhari hui, kitni durmedh? tu kitni jald ranga badlata hai? is kala men tu nipun hai. atishbaz ki charbi ko bhi ranga badlate kuchh der lagti hai; par tujhe ranga badlane men uska lakshamsh samay bhi nahin lagta! jaham abhi vatsalya tha, vaham phir shak ne asan jama liya.

vah sochte jate the-kahim usne bahana to nahin kiya hai?
