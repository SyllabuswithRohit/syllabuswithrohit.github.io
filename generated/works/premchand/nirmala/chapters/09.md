# Nirmala — Adhyay 9

**Munshi Premchand**

navaan parichchheda

mansaaraama ke jaane se ghar soonaa ho gaya| donon chhote ladke usee skoola men pa.dhate the| nirmalaa roza unase mansaaraama kaa haala poochhatee| aashaa thi ki chhuttee ke din vaha aaega; lekin jab chhuttee ke din guzara gae; aur vaha na aaya, to nirmalaa kee tabeeyata ghabaraane lagee| usane usake lie moonga ke lad‌doo bana rakkhe the| somavaara ko praatah bhungee ko lad‌doo dekara madarase bhejaa| nau baje bhungee lauta aaee| mansaaraama ne lad‌doo jyon ke tyon lautaa die the|

nirmalaa ne poochhaa—pahale se kuchha hare hue hain re?

bhungee—hare-vare to naheen hue, aur sookha gae hain!

nirmalaa—kyaa jee achchha naheen hai kya?

bhungee—yaha to mainne naheen poochhaa bahoo jee, jhootha kyon boloon| haan, vahaan kaa kahaara meraa devara lagata hai| vaha kahata tha ki tumhaare baaboo jee kee Kuraaka kuchha naheen hai| do phulakiyaan khaakara utha jaate hain| phir din bhara kuchha naheen khaate| hara dama pa.dhate rahate hain| nirmalaa-toone poochhaa naheen,laddoo kyon lautaae dete ho?

bhungee-yaha to naheen poochhaa bahoo jee,jhootha kyon boloon| unhonne kaha ise letee ja,yahaan rakhane kaa kuchha kaama naheen, main letee aaee|

nirmalaa aur kuchha naheen kehte the| poochhaa naheen,kala kyon naheen aae ? chhuttee to thi|

bhungee-bahoo jee,jhootha kyon boloo;yaha poochhane kee to mujhe sudha hee na rahi| haan, yaha kehte the ki ab too yahaan kabhee na aana, na mere lie koee cheeja laana;aur apanee bahoo jee se kaha dena ki mere paasa koee chitthee-pattara na bhejen; ladakon se bhee- mere paasa koee sandeshaa na bhejen| aur ek baat aisee kahee bahoo jee ki mere munha se nikala naheen sakatee| phir rone lage|

nirmalaa-kaun baat thi? kaha to|

bhungee kyaa kahoon bahoo jee,kehte the mere jeene ko dhikkaara hai| yahee kaha kar rone lage!

nirmalaa ke munha se ek thandee saansa nikala gaee| aisaa maalooma hua, maano kalejaa baithaa jata hai| usakaa roma-roma aartanaada se rudana karane lagaa| vaha vahaan baithee na raha sakee| jaakara bistara par munha dhaanpa kar leta rahi-aur phoota-phoota kar rone lagee| 'vaha bhee jaana gae'usake antahkarana men baara-baara yahee aavaaja gajane lagee-vaha jaana gae! bhagavaan ab kyaa hoga? jisa sandeha kee aaga men vaha bhasma ho rahi thi,ab shata-guna vega se dhadhakane lagee| use apanee koee chintaa na thi| jeevana men ab sukha kee kyaa aashaa thi, jisakee use laalasaa hoti ? usane apane mann ko is vichaara se samajhaayaa tha ki yaha mere poorva karmonkaa praayashchitta hai| kaun praanee aisaa nirlajja hoga, jo is dashaa men bahut din jee sake ? kartavya kee vedee par usane apana jeevana aur usakee saaree kaamanaaen homa kar dee theen | hridaya rotaa rahata tha; par mukha par hansee kaa ranga bharana pa.data tha| jisakaa munha dekhane ko jee na chaahata tha, usake saamane hansa-hansa kar, baaten karanee pa.datee theen| jisa deha kaa sparsha use sarpa ke sheetala sparsha ke samaana lagata tha, usase aalingita hokara use jitanee ghrina, jitanee marma-vedana hoti thi, use kaun jaana sakata hai ? us samay usakee yahee ichchhaa hoti thi, ki dharatee phata jaaya; aur main usamen samaa jaaoon! lekin saaree vidambana apane hee taka thi, aur apanee chintaa karanee usane chho.da dee thi;lekin vaha samasyaa ab atyanta bhayankara ho gaee thi| vaha apanee aankhon se mansaaraama kee aatma-pee.daa naheen dekha sakatee thi| mansaaraama jaise manasvee, saahasee yuvaka par is aapeksha kaa jo asara pa.da sakata tha, usakee kalpana hee se usake praana kaanpa uthate the| ava chaahe us par kitane hee sandeha kyon na hon, chaahe use aatmahatyaa hee kyon na karanee pa.de. par vaha chupa naheen baitha sakatee thi| mansaaraama kee rakshaa karane ke lie vaha vikala ho gaee| usane sankocha aur lajjaa kee chaadara utaara kar phenka dene kaa nishchaya kar liya|

vakeela saahaba bhojana karake kachaharee jaane ke pahale ek baara usase avashya mila liya karte the| unake aane kaa samay ho gaya tha| aa hee rahe honge,yaha socha kar nirmalaa dvaara par kha.dee ho gaee aur unakaa intezar karane lagee;lekin yaha kya! vaha to baahara chale jaa rahe hain| ga.dee juta kar aa gaee,yaha hukma vaha yaheen se diya karte the| to kyaa aaja vaha na aaenge, baahara hee baahara chale jaaenge| naheen aisaa naheen hone paavegaa| usane bhungee se kaha-jaakara baaboo jee ko bulaa laa| kahana ek jarooree kaama hai;suna leejie|

munshee jee jaane ko taiyaara hee the| yaha sandeshaa paakara andara aae; par kamare men na aakara doora hee se poochhaa-kyaa baat hai,bhaaee? jaldee kaha do, mujhe ek jarooree kaama se jana hai| abhee tho.dee dera hui hedamaastara saahaba kaa ek patra aaya hai ki mansaaraama ko jvara aa gaya hai, behatara ho ki aapa ghar hee par usakaa ilaaja karen| isalie udhara hee se hota hua kachaharee jaaoongaa| tumhen koee khaasa baat to naheen kahanee hai|

nirmalaa par maano vajra gira pada| aansuon ke aavega aur kantha-svara men ghora sangraama hone lagaa| donon hee pahale nikalane par tule hue the| do men se koee ek kadama bhee peechhe hatana naheen chaahata tha | karaatha-svara kee durbalata aur aansuon kee sabalata dekha kar yaha nishchaya karana kathina naheen tha ki ek kshana yahee sangraama hota raha, to maidaana kisake haath rahega? aakhira donon saatha-saatha nikale lekin baahara aate hee balavaana ne nirbala ko dabaa liya| kevala itana munha se nikalaa-koee khaasa baat naheen thi| aapa to udhara jaa hee rahe hain|

munshee jee-mainne ladakon se poochhaa tha to ve kehte the,kala vaithe pa.dha rahe the| aaja na jaane kyaa ho gaya!

nirmalaa ne aavesha se kaopate hue kaha-yaha sava aapa kar rahe hain|

munshee jee ne sriyoyaan vadala kar kaha-main kar raha hoon! main kyaa kar raha hoon?

nirmalaa-apane dil se poochhie|

munshee jee-mainne to yahee sochaa tha ki yahaan usakaa pa.dhane men jee naheen lagata,vahaan aur ladakon ke saatha khaamakhvaaha hee pa.dhegaa| yaha to koee buree baat na thi, aur mainne kyaa kiya?

nirmalaa-khooba sochie,iseelie aapane unhen vahaan bhejaa tha? aapa ke mann men koee aur baat na thi?

munshee jee jaraa hichakichaae aur apanee durbalata ko chhipaane ke lie muskaraane kee cheshtaa karake vole-aur kyaa baat ho sakatee thi? bhalaa tumheen socho!

nirmalaa-khaira, yahee sahee| ab aapa kripaa karake unhen aaja hee lete aaiega, vahaan rahane se unakee beemaaree ba.dha jaane kaa bhaya hai| yahaan deedee jee jitanee beemaaradaaree kar sakatee hain,doosaraa naheen kar sakata|

ek kshana ke baada usane sira neechaa karake phir kaha-mere kaarana na laanaa chaahate hon,to mujhe mere ghar bheja deejie| main vahaan aaraama se rahoongee|

munshee jee ne isakaa kuchha javaaba na diya| baahara chale gae;aur ek kshana men ga.dee skoola kee ora chalee|

mann! teree gati kitanee vichitra hai,kitanee rahasya se bharee hui, kitanee durmedha? too kitanee jalda ranga badalata hai? is kalaa men too nipuna hai| aatishabaaza kee charbee ko bhee ranga badalate kuchha dera lagatee hai; par tujhe ranga badalane men usakaa lakshaansha samay bhee naheen lagata! jahaan abhee vaatsalya tha,vahaan phir sandeha ne aasana jamaa liya|

vaha sochate jaate the-kaheen usane bahaanaa to naheen kiya hai?
