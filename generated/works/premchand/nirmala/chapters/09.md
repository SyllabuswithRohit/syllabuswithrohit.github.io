**Munshi Premchand**

mansaaraam ke jaane se ghar soonaa ho gayaa. donon chhote la. dake usee skool men pa. dhate the. nirml roz unase mansaaraam kaa haal poochhatee. ummeed thee ki chhuttee ke din woh aaegaa; lekin jab chhuttee ke din guzar gae; aur woh na aayaa, to nirml kee tabeeyat ghabaraane lagee. usane usake lie moong ke lad‌doo bn rakkhe the. somavaar ko subah bhungee ko lad‌doo dekar madarase bhejaa. nau baje bhungee laut aaee. mansaaraam ne lad‌doo jyon ke tyon lautaa die the.

nirml ne poochhaa—pahale se kuchh hare hue hain re?

bhungee—hare-vare to naheen hue, aur sookh gae hain!

nirml—kyaa jee achchhaa naheen hai kyaa?

bhungee—yeh to mainne naheen poochhaa bahoo jee, jhooth kyon boloon. haan, vahaan kaa kahaar meraa devar lagataa hai. woh kahataa thaa ki tumhaare baaboo jee kee Kuraak kuchh naheen hai. do phulakiyaan khaakar uth jaate hain. phir din bhar kuchh naheen khaate. har dam pa. dhate rahate hain.
nirmalaa-toone poochhaa naheen, laddoo kyon lautaae dete ho?

bhungee-yeh to naheen poochhaa bahoo jee, jhooth kyon boloon. unhonne kahaa ise letee jaa, yahaan rakhane kaa kuchh kaam naheen, main letee aaee.

nirml aur kuchh naheen kahate the. poochhaa naheen, kal kyon naheen aae? chhuttee to thee.

bhungee-bahoo jee, jhooth kyon boloo; yeh poochhane kee to mujhe sudh hee na rahee. haan, yeh kahate the ki ab too yahaan kabhee na aanaa, na mere lie koee cheej laanaa; aur apanee bahoo jee se kah denaa ki mere paas koee chitthee-pattar na bhejen; la. dakon se bhee- mere paas koee sandeshaa na bhejen. aur ek baat aisee kahee bahoo jee ki mere munh se nikal naheen sakatee. phir rone lage.

nirmalaa-kaun baat thee? kah to.

bhungee kyaa kahoon bahoo jee, kahate the mere jeene ko dhikkaar hai. yahee kah kar rone lage!

munshee jee jaane ko taiyaar hee the. yeh sandeshaa paakar andar aae; par kamare men na aakar door hee se poochhaa-kyaa baat hai, bhaaee? jaldee kah do, mujhe ek jarooree kaam se jaanaa hai. abhee tho. dee der huee hedamaastar saahab kaa ek chitthee aayaa hai ki mansaaraam ko jvar aa gayaa hai, behatar ho ki aap ghar hee par usakaa ilaaj karen. isalie udhar hee se hotaa huaa kachaharee jaaoongaa. tumhen koee khaas baat to naheen kahanee hai.

nirml par maano vajr gir pa. daa. aansuon ke aaveg aur galaa-aavaaz men ghor la. daaee hone lagaa. donon hee pahale nikalane par tule hue the. do men se koee ek kadam bhee peechhe hatn naheen chaahataa thaa. karaatha-aavaaz kee durbalataa aur aansuon kee sabalataa dekh kar yeh phaisl karn mushkil naheen thaa ki ek pal yahee la. daaee hotaa rahaa, to maidaan kisake haath rahegaa? aakhir donon saatha-saath nikale lekin baahar aate hee balavaan ne nirbal ko dabaa liyaa.
