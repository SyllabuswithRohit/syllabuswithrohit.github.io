# Chapter 9

navaa.N parichchheda

mansaaraama ke jaane se ghar soonaa ho gaya| donon chhote ladake usee skoola men padhate the| nirmalaa roja unase mansaaraama kaa haala poochhatee| ummeeda thi ki chhuttee ke din vaha aaegaa; lekin jaba chhuttee ke din gujara gae; aur vaha na aaya, to nirmalaa kee tabeeyata ghabaraane lagee| usane usake lie moo.Nga ke lad‌doo banaa rakkhe the| somavaara ko subaha bhungee ko lad‌doo dekara madarase bhejaa| nau baje bhungee lauta aaee| mansaaraama ne lad‌doo jyon ke tyon lautaa die the|

nirmalaa ne poochhaa—pahale se kuchha hare hue hain re?

bhungee—hare-vare to naheen hue, aur sookha gae hain!

nirmalaa—kyaa jee achchhaa naheen hai kyaa?

bhungee—yaha to mainne naheen poochhaa bahoo jee, jhootha kyon boloo.N| haa.N, vahaa.N kaa kahaara meraa devara lagataa hai| vaha kehta tha ki tumhaare baaboo jee kee khuraaka kuchha naheen hai| do phulakiyaa.N khaakara utha jaate hain| phir din bhara kuchha naheen khaate| hara dama padhate rahate hain|
nirmalaa-toone poochhaa naheen,laddoo kyon lautaae dete ho?

bhungee-yaha to naheen poochhaa bahoo jee,jhootha kyon boloon| unhonne kahaa ise letee jaa,yahaa.N rakhane kaa kuchha kaama naheen, main letee aaee|

nirmalaa aur kuchha naheen kehte the| poochhaa naheen,kala kyon naheen aae ? chhuttee to thi|

bhungee-bahoo jee,jhootha kyon boloo;yaha poochhane kee to mujhe sudha hee na rahi| haa.N, yaha kehte the ki aba too yahaa.N kabhee na aana, na mere lie koee cheeja laanaa;aur apanee bahoo jee se kaha denaa ki mere paasa koee chitthee-pattara na bhejen; ladakon se bhee- mere paasa koee sandeshaa na bhejen| aur ek baat aisee kahee bahoo jee ki mere mu.Nha se nikala naheen sakatee| phir rone lage|

nirmalaa-kauna baat thi? kaha to|

bhungee kyaa kahoo.N bahoo jee,kehte the mere jeene ko dhikkaara hai| yahee kaha kar rone lage!

nirmalaa ke mu.Nha se ek thaNdee saa.Nsa nikala gaee| aisaa maalooma hua, maano kalejaa baithaa jata hai| usakaa roma-roma aartanaada se rudana karane lagaa| vaha vahaa.N baithee na raha sakee| jaakara bistara para munha dhaa.Npa kar leta rahi-aur phoota-phoota kar rone lagee| 'vaha bhee jaana gae'usake mana men baara-baara yahee aavaaja gajane lagee-vaha jaana gae! bhagavaan aba kyaa hogaa? jisa shaka kee aaga men vaha bhasma ho rahi thi,aba shata-guNa vega se dhadhakane lagee| use apanee koee chintaa na thi| jeevana men aba sukha kee
kyaa ummeeda thi, jisakee use laalasaa hoti ? usane apane mana ko is socha se samajhaayaa tha ki yaha mere pahale karmonkaa praayashchitta hai| kauna praaNee aisaa nirlajja hogaa, jo is dashaa men bahuta din jee sake ? kartavya kee vedee para usane apanaa jeevana aur usakee saaree kaamanaae.N homa kar dee theen | dil rotaa rahataa tha; para mu.Nha para ha.Nsee kaa ranga bharanaa padataa tha| jisakaa munha dekhane ko jee na chaahataa tha, usake saamane ha.Nsa-ha.Nsa kar, baaten karanee padatee theen| jisa deha kaa sparsha use sarpa ke sheetala sparsha ke samaana lagataa tha, usase aalingita hokara use jitanee napharata, jitanee marma-darda hoti thi, use kauna jaana sakataa hai ? us samaya usakee yahee ichchhaa hoti thi, ki dharatee phata jaaya; aur main usamen samaa jaaoo.N! lekin saaree vidambanaa apane hee taka thi, aur apanee chintaa karanee usane chhoda dee thi;lekin vaha samasyaa aba bahuta bhayankara ho gaee thi| vaha apanee aa.Nkhon se mansaaraama kee aatma-peedaa naheen dekha sakatee thi| mansaaraama jaise manasvee, saahasee yuvaka para is aapeksha kaa jo asara pada sakataa tha, usakee kalpanaa hee se usake praaNa kaa.Npa uthate the| ava chaahe us para kitane hee shaka kyon na hon, chaahe use aatmahatyaa hee kyon na karanee pade. para vaha chupa naheen baitha sakatee thi| mansaaraama kee rakshaa karane ke lie vaha vikala ho gaee| usane sankocha aur sharma kee chaadara utaara kar phenka dene kaa phaisalaa kar liya|

vakeela saahaba khaanaa karake kachaharee jaane ke pahale ek baara
usase avashya mila liya karte the| unake aane kaa samaya ho gaya tha| aa hee rahe honge,yaha socha kar nirmalaa dvaara para khadee ho gaee aur unakaa intajaara karane lagee;lekin yaha kyaa! vaha to baahara chale jaa rahe hain| gaadee juta kar aa gaee,yaha hukma vaha yaheen se diya karte the| to kyaa aaja vaha na aae.Nge, baahara hee baahara chale jaae.Nge| naheen aisaa naheen hone paavegaa| usane bhungee se kahaa-jaakara baaboo jee ko bulaa laa| kahanaa ek jarooree kaama hai;suna leejie|

munshee jee jaane ko taiyaara hee the| yaha sandeshaa paakara andara aae; para kamare men na aakara doora hee se poochhaa-kyaa baat hai,bhaaee? jaldee kaha do, mujhe ek jarooree kaama se jana hai| abhee thodee dera huee hedamaastara saahaba kaa ek patra aaya hai ki mansaaraama ko jvara aa gaya hai, behatara ho ki aapa ghar hee para usakaa ilaaja karen| isalie udhara hee se hota hua kachaharee jaaoo.Ngaa| tumhen koee khaasa baat to naheen kahanee hai|

nirmalaa para maano vajra gira padaa| aa.Nsuon ke aavega aur galaa-aavaaza men ghora sangraama hone lagaa| donon hee pahale nikalane para tule hue the| do men se koee ek kadama bhee peechhe hatanaa naheen chaahataa tha | karaatha-aavaaza kee durbalataa aur aa.Nsuon kee sabalataa dekha kar yaha phaisalaa karna mushkila naheen tha ki ek pala yahee sangraama hota raha, to maidaana kisake haath rahegaa? aakhira donon saatha-saatha nikale lekin baahara aate hee balavaana ne nirbala ko dabaa liya|
kevala itanaa mu.Nha se nikalaa-koee khaasa baat naheen thi| aapa to udhara jaa hee rahe hain|

munshee jee-mainne ladakon se poochhaa tha to ve kehte the,kala vaithe padha rahe the| aaja na jaane kyaa ho gaya!

nirmalaa ne aavesha se kopate hue kahaa-yaha sava aapa kar rahe hain|

munshee jee ne sriyoyaa.N vadala kar kahaa-main kar raha hoo.N! main kyaa kar raha hoo.N?

nirmalaa-apane dil se poochhie|

munshee jee-mainne to yahee sochaa tha ki yahaa.N usakaa padhane men jee naheen lagataa,vahaa.N aur ladakon ke saatha khaamakhvaaha hee padhegaa| yaha to koee buree baat na thi, aur mainne kyaa kiya?

nirmalaa-khooba sochie,iseelie aapane unhen vahaa.N bhejaa tha? aapa ke mana men koee aur baat na thi?

munshee jee jaraa hichakichaae aur apanee durbalataa ko chhipaane ke lie muskaraane kee cheshtaa karake vole-aur kyaa baat ho sakatee thi? bhalaa tumheen socho!

nirmalaa-khaira, yahee sahee| aba aapa kripaa karake unhen aaja hee lete aaiegaa, vahaa.N rahane se unakee beemaaree badha jaane kaa dara hai| yahaa.N deedee jee jitanee beemaaradaaree kar sakatee hain,doosaraa naheen kar sakataa|

ek pala ke baada usane sira neechaa karake phir kahaa-mere
vajaha na laanaa chaahate hon,to mujhe mere ghar bheja deejie| main vahaa.N aaraama se rahoo.Ngee|

munshee jee ne isakaa kuchha javaaba na diya| baahara chale gae;aur ek pala men gaadee skoola kee ora chalee|

mana! teree gati kitanee vichitra hai,kitanee rahasya se bharee huee, kitanee durmedha? too kitanee jalda ranga badalataa hai? is kalaa men too nipuNa hai| aatishabaaja kee charbee ko bhee ranga badalate kuchha dera lagatee hai; para tujhe ranga badalane men usakaa lakshaansha samaya bhee naheen lagataa! jahaa.N abhee vaatsalya tha,vahaa.N phir shaka ne aasana jamaa liya|

vaha sochate jaate the-kaheen usane bahaanaa to naheen kiya hai?
