# Swamini

**Munshi Premchand**

1
shivadaas ne bhandaare kee kunjee apanee bahoo raamapyaaree ke saamane phenkakar apanee boodhee aankhon mein aansoo bharakar kahaa- bahoo, aaj se girastee kee dekhabhaal tumhaare oopar hai. meraa sukh bhagavaan se nahin dekh gaya, nahin to kya javaan bete ko yon chheen lete! usakaa kaam karne vaalaa to koi chaahie. ek hal tod doon, to gujaaraa na hogaa. mere hee kukaram se bhagavaan kaa yah kop aay hai, aur main hee apne maathe par use loongaa. birajoo kaa hal ab main hee sanbhaaloongaa. ab ghar kee dekh-rekh karne vaalaa, dharane-uthaane vaalaa tumhaare sivaa doosaraa kaun hai? roo mata betaa, bhagavaan kee jo ichchhaa thi, woh hua; aur jo ichchhaa hogee woh hogaa. hamaaraa-tumhaaraa kya bas hai? mere jeete-jee tumhe koi tedhee aankh se dekh bhee na sakegaa. tum kisee baat kaa soch mata kiya karo. birajoo gaya, to abhee baithaa hee hua hoon.
raamapyaaree aur raamadulaaree do sagee bahanen theen. donon kaa vivaah mathuraa aur birajoo do sage bhaaiyon se hua. donon bahanen naihar kee tarah sasuraal mein bhee pyaar aur khushi se rehne lageen. shivadaas ko penshan milee. din-bhar dvaa ra par gap-shap karte. bharaa-pooraa gharavaale dekhakar khush hote aur adhikatar dharm-charchaa mein lage rehte the; lekin daivagati se badaa ladakaa birajoo bimaar padaa aur aaj use mare hue pandrah din beet gaye. aaj kriyaa-karm se phurasat milee aur shivadaas ne sachche karmaveer kee tarah phir jeevan sangraam ke lie kamar kas lee. man mein use chaahe kitanaa hee du: kha hua ho, use kisee ne rote nahin dekh. aaj apanee bahoo ko dekhakar ek pal ke lie usakee aankhen sajal ho gayeen; lekin usane man ko sanbhaalaa aur rooddh galaa se use dilaasaa dene lagaa. kadaachit usane, sochaa tha, ghar kee svaaminee banakar vidhavaa ke aansoo punchh jaayenge, kam-se-kam use itanaa mushkil parishram na karn padegaa, isalie usane bhandaare kee kunjee bahoo ke saamane phenk dee thi. vaidhavy kee vyathaa ko svaamitv ke garv se dabaa denaa chaahataa tha.
raamapyaaree ne pulakit galaa se kahaa- yah kaise ho sakataa hai daadaa, ki tum mehanat-majadooree karo aur main maalakin banakar baithoon? kaam dhandhe mein lagee rahoongee, to man badalaa rahegaa. baithe-baithe to rone ke sivaa aur kuchh na hogaa.
shivadaas ne samajhaayaa- betaa, daivagati mein to kisee kaa bas nahin, rone-dhone se halakaanee ke sivaa aur kya haath aayegaa? ghar mein bhee to beeson kaam hain. koi saadhu-sant aa jaayan, koi paahunaa hee aa pahunche, to unake sevaa-satkaar ke lie kisee ko ghar par rahanaa hee padegaa.
bahoo ne bahut-se heele kiye, par shivadaas ne ek na sunee.
2
shivadaas ke baahar chale jaane par raamapyaaree ne kunjee uthaayee, to use man mein apoorv gaurav aur uttaradaayitv kaa tajurbaa hua. zara der ke lie pati-viyog kaa du: kha use bhool gaya. usakee chhotee bahan aur devar donon kaam karne gaye hue the. shivadaas baahar tha. ghar bilakul khaalee tha. is vakt woh nishchit hokar bhandaare ko khol sakatee hai. usamen kya-kya saamaan hai, kya-kya vibhooti hai, yah dekhane ke lie usakaa man laalaayit ho uthaa. is ghar mein woh kabhee na aayee thi. jab kabhee kisee ko kuchh denaa yaa kisee se kuchh lenaa hot tha, tabhee shivadaas aakar is kotharee ko kholaa kart tha. phir use bandakar woh taalee apanee kamar mein rakh letaa tha.
raamapyaaree kabhee-kabhee dvaar kee daraaron se bheetar jhaankatee thi, par andhere mein kuchh na dikhaaee detaa. saare ghar ke lie woh kotharee tilism yaa rahasy tha, jisake vishay mein tarah-tarah kee kalpanaaen hoti rehti theen. aaj raamapyaaree ko woh rahasy kholakar dekhane kaa avasar mil gaya. usane baahar kaa dvaar band kar diya, ki koi use bhandaar kholate na dekh le, nahin sochegaa, bejaroorat usane kyon kholaa, tab aakar kaanpate hue haathon se taalaa kholaa. usakee chhaatee dhadak rahi thi ki koi dvaar na khatakhataane lage. andar paanv rakhaa to use kuchh usee tarah kaa, lekin usse kaheen teevr khushi hua, jo use apne gahane-kapade kee pitaaree kholane mein hot tha. matakon mein gud, shakkar, gehoon, jau aadi cheejen rakhee huee theen. ek kinaare bade-bade baratan dhare the, jo shaadee-byaah ke avasar par nikaale jate the, yaa maange diye jate the. ek aale par maalagujaaree kee raseeden aur len-den ke puraje bandhe hue rakhe the. kotharee mein ek vibhooti-see chhaayee thi, maano lakshmee anajaan roop se viraaj rahi hon. us vibhooti kee chhaayaa mein raamapyaaree aadhe ghante tak baithee apanee aatmaa ko tript karti rahi. pratikshan usake dil par mamatv kaa nashaa-saa chhaayaa jaa raha tha. jab woh us kotharee se nikalee, to usake man ke sanskaar badal gaye the, maano kisee ne us par mantr daal diya ho.
usee samay dvaar par kisee ne aavaaj dee. usane turant bhandaare kaa dvaar band kiya aur jaakar sadar daravaajaa khol diya. dekh to padosin jhuniyaa khadee hai aur ek roopayaa udhaar maang rahi hai.
raamapyaaree ne rookhaaee se kahaa- abhee to ek paisaa ghar mein nahin hai jeejee, kriyaa-karm mein sab kharach ho gaya.
jhuniyaa chakaraa gayee. chaudharee ke ghar mein is samay ek roopayaa bhee nahin hai, yah yakeen karne kee baat na thi. jisake yahan saikadon kaa len-den hai, woh sab kuchh kriyaa-karm mein nahin kharch kar sakataa. agar shivadaas ne bahaanaa kiya hot, to use hairaanee na hot. pyaaree to apne aasaan aadat ke lie gaanv mein mashahoor thi. akasar shivadaas kee aankhen bachaakar padosiyon ko ichchhit vastuen de diya karti thi. abhee kal hee usane jaanakee ko ser-bhar doodh diya. yahan tak ki apne gahane tak maange de detee thi. kripan shivadaas ke ghar mein aisee sakharaj bahoo kaa aan gaanv vaale apne achchhee kismat kee baat samajhate the.
jhuniyaa ne chakit hokar kahaa- aisaa na kaho jeejee, bade gaadhe mein padakar aayee hoon, nahin tum jaanatee ho, meree aadat aisee nahin hai. baakee kaa ek roopayaa denaa hai. pyaadaa dvaar par khadaa bakajhak kar raha hai. roopayaa de do, to kisee tarah yah vipatti tale. main aaj ke aathaven din aakar de jaaoongee. gaanv mein aur kaun ghar hai, jahaan maangane jaaoon?
pyaaree tas se mas na huee.
usake jate hee pyaaree saanjh ke lie rasoee-paani kaa intajaam karne lagee. pahale chaaval-daal binanaa apaadh lagataa tha aur rasoee mein jan to soolee par chadhane se kam na tha. kuchh der bahanon mein jhaanv- jhaanv hoti, tab shivadaas aakar kehte, kya aaj rasoee na banegee, to do mein se ek uthatee aur mote- mote tikkad lagaakar rakh detee, maano bailon kaa raatib ho. aaj pyaaree tan-man se rasoee ke intazaam mein lagee huee hai. ab woh ghar kee svaaminee hai.
tab usane baahar nikalakar dekh, kitanaa koodaa-karakat padaa hua hai! budhaoo din-bhar makkhee maaraa karte hain. itanaa bhee nahin hot ki zara jhaadoo hee lagaa den. ab kya inse itanaa bhee na hogaa? dvaar chikanaa hona chaahie ki dekhakar aadamee kaa man khush ho jaay. yah nahin ki ubakaaee aane lage. abhee kah doon, to tinak uthen. achchhaa, munnee naand se alag kyon khadee hai?
usane munnee ke paas jaakar naand mein jhaankaa. durgandh aa rahi thi. theek! maaloom hot hai, maheenon se paani hee nahin badalaa gaya. is tarah to gaay raha chukee. apanaa pet bhar liya, chhuttee huee, aur kisee se kya matalab? haan, doodh sabako achchhaa lagataa hai. daadaa dvaar par baithe chilam pee rahe hain, magar itanaa nahin hot ki chaar ghadaa paani naand mein daal den. majoor rakhaa hai woh bhee teen kaudee kaa. khaane ko dedh ser; kaam karte naanee maratee hai. aaj aat hai to poochhatee hoon, naand mein paani kyon nahin badalaa. rahanaa ho, rahe yaa jaay. aadamee bahut milenge. chaaron ora to log maare-maare phir rahe hain.
aakhir usse na raha gaya. ghadaa uthaakar paani laane chalee.
shivadaas ne pukaaraa- paani kya hogaa bahoo? isamen paani bharaa hua hai.
pyaaree ne kahaa- naand kaa paani sad gaya hai. munnee bhoose mein munh nahin daalatee. dekhate nahin ho, kos-bhar par khadee hai.
shivadaas maarmik bhaav se musakaraaye aur aakar bahoo ke haath se ghadaa le liya.
3
kaee maheene beet gaye. pyaaree ke adhikaar me aate hee us ghar me jaise vasant aa gaya. bheetar-baahar jahaan dekhie, kisee nipun prabandhak ke hastakaushal, suvichaar aur suroochi ke chihn dikhate the. pyaaree ne grihayantr kee aisee chaabhee kas dee thi ki sabhee puraje theek-thaak chalne lage the. khana pahale se achchhaa milataa hai aur samay par milataa hai. doodh zyada hot hai, ghee zyada hot hai, aur kaam zyada hot hai. pyaaree na khud vishraam letee hai, na doosaron ko vishraam lene detee hai. ghar mein aisee barakat aa gayee hai ki jo cheej maango, ghar hee mein nikal aati hai. aadamee se lekar jaanavar tak sabhee svasth dikhaaee dete hain. ab woh pahale kee-see dashaa nahin hai ki koi chithade lapete ghoom raha hai, kisee ko gahane kee dhun savaar hai. haan agar koi roogn aur chintit tathaa malin vesh mein hai, to woh pyaaree hai; phir bhee saaraa ghar usse jalataa hai. yahan tak ki boodhe shivadaas bhee kabhee-kabhee usakee badagoee karte hain. kisee ko pahar raat rahe uthanaa achchhaa nahin lagataa. mehanat se sabhee jee churaate hain. phir bhee yah sab maanate hain ki pyaaree na ho, to ghar kaa kaam na chale. aur to aur, donon bahanon mein bhee ab utanaa apanaapan nahin.
praat: kaal kaa samay tha. dulaaree ne haathon ke kade laakar pyaaree ke saamane patak diye aur ghunnaaee huee bolee- lekar ise bhee bhandaare mein band kar de.
pyaaree ne kade uthaa liye aur komal aavaaz se kahaa- kah to diya, haath mein rupaye aane de, banavaa doongee. abhee aisaa ghis nahin gaya hai ki aaj hee utaarakar phenk diya jaay.
dulaaree ladane ko taiyaar hokar aayee thi. bolee- tere haath mein kaahe ko kabhee rupaye aayenge aur kaahe ko kade banenge. jod- jod rakhane mein majaa aat hai na?
pyaaree ne hensakar kahaa- jod-jod rakhatee hoon to tere hee lie ki mere koi aur baithaa hua hai, ki main sabase zyada khaa-pahan letee hoon. meraa anant kab kaa tootaa padaa hai.
dulaaree- tum na khaao-na pahano, jas to paatee ho. yahan khaane-pahanane ke sivaa aur kya hai? main tumhaaraa hisaab-kitaab nahin jaanatee, mere kade aaj banane ko bhej do.
pyaaree ne aasaan vinod ke bhaav se poochhaa- rupaye na hon, to kahan se laaoon?
dulaaree ne uddandataa ke saath kahaa- mujhe isase koi matalab nahin. main to kade chaahatee hoon.
isee tarah ghar ke sab aadamee apne-apne avasar par pyaaree ko do-chaar khotee-kharee sunaa jate the, aur woh gareeb sabakee dhauns hansakar sahatee thi. svaaminee kaa yah dharm hai ki sabakee dhauns sun le aur kare vahee, jisamen ghar kaa kalyaan ho! svaamitv ke kavach par dhauns, taane, dhamakee kisee kaa asar na hot. usakee svaaminee kee kalpanaa ina aaghaaton se aur bhee svasth hoti thi. woh ghar-gharavaale kee sanchaalikaa hai. sabhee apne-apne du: kha usee ke saamane rote hain, par jo kuchh woh karti hai, vahee hot hai. itanaa use khush karne ke lie kaaphee tha. gaanv mein pyaaree kee saraahanaa hoti thi. abhee umr hee kya hai, lekin saare ghar ko sanbhaale hue hai. chaahatee to sagaaee karake chain se rehti. is ghar ke peechhe apne ko mitaaye detee hai. kabhee kisee se hansatee-bolti bhee nahin, jaise kaayaa palat ho gayee.
kaee din baad dulaaree ke kade banakar aa gaye. pyaaree khud sunaar ke ghar daud-daud gayee.
shaam ho gayee thi. dulaaree aur mathuraa haat se laute. pyaaree ne naye kade dulaaree ko diye. dulaaree nihaal ho gayee. chatapat kade pahane aur daudee huee barauthe mein jaakar mathuraa ko dikhaane lagee. pyaaree barauthe ke dvaar par chhipee khadee yah drishy dekhane lagee. usakee aankhen sajal ho gayeen. dulaaree usse kul teen hee saal to chhotee hai! par donon mein kitanaa antar hai. usakee aankhen maanon us drishy par jam gayeen, dampati kaa woh aasaan khushi, unakaa premaalingan, unakee mugdh mudraa- pyaaree kee takatakee-see bandh gayee, yahan tak ki deepak ke dhundhale prakaash mein woh donon usakee najaron se gaayab ho gaye aur apne hee beetaa samay jeevan kee ek leelaa aankhon ke saamane baar-baar naye-naye roop mein aane lagee.
sahasaa shivadaas ne pukaaraa- badee bahoo! ek paisaa do. tamaakhoo mengavaaoon.
pyaaree kee samaadhi toot gayee. aansoo ponchhatee huee bhandaare mein paisaa lene chalee gayee.
4
ek-ek karake pyaaree ke gahane usake haath se nikalate jate the. woh chaahatee thi, meraa ghar gaanv mein sabase sanpann samajhaa jaay, aur is mahatvaakaankshaa kaa mooly denaa padataa tha. kabhee ghar kee marammat ke lie aur kabhee bailon kee nayee goee khareedane ke lie, kabhee naatedaaron ke vyavahaaron ke lie, kabhee beemaaron kee davaa- daaroo ke lie rupaye kee jaroorat padatee rehti thi, aur jab bahut katarabyont karne par bhee kaam na chalataa to woh apanee koi-na-koi cheej nikaal detee. aur cheej ek baar haath se nikalakar phir na lautatee thi. woh chaahatee, to inamen se kitane hee kharchon ko taal jati; par jahaan izzat kee baat aa padatee thi, woh dil kholakar kharch karti. agar gaanv mein hethee ho gayee, to kya baat rahi! log usee kaa naam to dharenge. dulaaree ke paas bhee gahane the. do-ek cheejen mathuraa ke paas bhee theen, lekin pyaaree unakee cheejen na chhootee. unake khaane-pahanane ke din hain. woh is janjaal mein kyon phensen!
dulaaree ko ladakaa hua, to pyaaree ne dhoom se janmotsav manaane kaa prastaav kiya.
shivadaas ne khilaafat kiya- kya phaayadaa? jab bhagavaan kee raham se sagaaee-byaah ke din aayenge, to dhoom-dhaam kar lenaa.
pyaaree kaa hausalon se bharaa dil bhalaa kyon maanataa! bolee- kaisee baat kehte ho daadaa? pahalauthe ladake ke lie bhee dhoom-dhaam na huee to kab hogee? man to nahin maanataa. phir duniya kya kahegee? naam bade, darshan thode. main tumse kuchh nahin maangatee. apanaa saaraa saranjaam kar loongee.
gahanon ke maathe jaayagee, aur kya? - shivadaas ne chintit hokar kahaa- is tarah ek din dhaagaa bhee na bachegaa. kitanaa samajhaayaa, betaa, bhai-bhaujaaee kisee ke nahin hote. apne paas do cheejen rahengee, to sab munh johenge; nahin koi seedhe baat bhee na karegaa.
pyaaree ne aisaa munh banaayaa, maano woh aisee boodhee baaten bahut sun chukee hai, aur bolee- jo apne hain, woh bhee na poochhen, to bhee apne hee rehte hain. meraa dharam mere saath hai, unakaa dharam unake saath hai. mar jaaoongee to kya chhaatee par laad le jaaoongee?
dhoom-dhaam se janmotsav manaayaa gaya. barahee ke din saaree biraadaree kaa bhoj hua. log khaa-peekar chale gaye, pyaaree din-bhar kee thakee-maandee aangan mein ek taat kaa tukadaa bichhaakar kamar seedhee karne lagee. aankhen jhapak gayeen. mathuraa usee vakt ghar mein aay. navajaat betaa ko dekhane ke lie usakaa chitt vyaakul ho raha tha. dulaaree saur-ghar se nikal chukee thi. garbhaavasthaa mein usakee deh ksheen ho gayee thi, munh bhee utar gaya tha, par aaj svasthataa kee laalimaa munh par chhaayee huee thi. saur ke sanyam aur paushtik khana ne deh ko chikanaa kar diya tha. mathuraa use aangan mein dekhate hee paas aa gaya aur ek baar pyaaree kee ora taakakar usake nidraamagn hone kaa phaisalaa karake usane bachchaa ko god mein le liya aur usakaa munh choomane lagaa.
aahat paakar pyaaree kee aankhen khul gayeen; par usane neend kaa bahaanaa kiya aur adhakhulee aankhon se yah khushi-kreedaa dekhane lagee. maan aur pitaa donon baaree-baaree se ladakaa ko choomate, gale lagaate aur usake munh ko nihaarate the. kitanaa svargeey khushi tha! pyaaree kee trishit laalasaa ek pal ke lie svaaminee ko bhool gayee. jaise lagaam se mukhabaddh, bojh se ladaa hua, haankane vaale ke chaabuk se peedit, daudate-daudate bedam turang hinahinaane kee aavaaj sunakar kanautiyaan khadee kar letaa hai aur paristhiti ko bhoolakar ek dabee huee hinahinaahat se usakaa jawab detaa hai, kuchh vahee dashaa pyaaree kee huee. usakaa maatritv jo pinjare mein band, mook, nishchesht padaa hua tha, paas se aanevaalee maatritv kee chahakaar sunakar jaise jaag padaa aur chintaaon ke us pinjare se nikalane ke lie pankh phadaphadaane lagaa.
mathuraa ne kahaa- yah meraa ladakaa hai.
dulaaree ne ladakaa ko god mein chipataakar kahaa- haan, kyon nahin. tumheen ne to nau maheene pet mein rakhaa hai. saansat to meree huee, baap kahalaane ke lie tum kood pade.
mathuraa- meraa ladakaa na hot, to meree soorat kaa kyon hot. cheharaa-moharaa, rang-roop sab meraa hee-saa hai ki nahin?
dulaaree- isase kya hot hai. beej baniye ke ghar se aat hai. khet kisaan kaa hot hai. upaj baniye kee nahin hoti, kisaan kee hoti hai.
mathuraa- baaton mein tumse koi na jeetegaa. meraa ladakaa badaa ho jaayagaa, to main dvaar par baithakar maje se hukkaa piyaa karoongaa.
dulaaree- meraa ladakaa padhe-likhegaa, koi badaa huddaa paaegaa. tumhaaree tarah din-bhar bail ke peechhe na chalegaa. maalakin se kahanaa hai, kal ek paalanaa banavaa den.
mathuraa- ab bahut sabere na uthaa karn aur chhaatee phaadakar kaam bhee na karn.
dulaaree- yah mahaaraanee jeene dengee?
mathuraa- mujhe to bechaaree par raham aati hai. usake kaun baithaa hua hai? hameen logon ke lie maratee hai. bhaiyaa hote, to ab tak do-teen bachchon kee maan ho gayee hoti.
pyaaree ke galaa mein aansuon kaa aisaa veg uthaa ki use rokane mein saaree deh kaanp uthee. apanaa vanchit jeevan use maroosthal-saa lagaa, jisakee sookhee ret par woh haraa-bharaa baag lagaane kee nishphal cheshtaa kar rahi thi.
5
kuchh dinon ke baad shivadaas bhee mar gaya. udhar dulaaree ke do bachche aur hue. woh bhee adhikatar bachchon ke laalan-paalan mein vyast rehne lagee. khetee kaa kaam majadooron par aa padaa. mathuraa majadoor to achchhaa tha, sanchaalak achchhaa na tha. use svatantr roop se kaam lene kaa kabhee avasar na milaa. khud pahale bhai kee nigaraanee mein kaam kart raha. baad ko baap kee nigaraanee mein kaam karne lagaa. khetee kaa taar bhee na jaanataa tha. vahee majoor usake yahan tikate the, jo mehanat nahin, khushaamad karne mein kushal hote the, isalie pyaaree ko ab din mein do-chaar chakkar haar ke bhee lagaanaa padataa. kehne ko woh ab bhee maalakin thi, par vaastav mein ghar-bhar kee sevikaa thi. majoor bhee usse tyoriyaan badalate, jameendaar kaa pyaadaa bhee usee par dhauns jamaataa. khana mein bhee kiphaayat karanee padatee; ladakon ko to jitanee baar maange, utanee baar kuchh-na-kuchh chaahie. dulaaree to ladakauree thi, use bharapoor khana chaahie. mathuraa ghar kaa saradaar tha, usake is adhikaar ko kaun chheen sakataa tha? majoor bhalaa kyon riyaayat karne lage the. saaree kasar pyaaree par nikalatee thi. vahee ek phaalatoo cheej thi; agar aadhaa pet khaay, to kisee ko nukasaan na ho sakatee thi. tees varsh kee avasthaa mein usake baal pak gaye, kamar jhuk gayee, aankhon kee jot kam ho gayee; magar woh khush thi. svaamitv kaa gaurav ina saare jakhmon par maraham kaa kaam kart tha.
ek din mathuraa ne kahaa- bhaabhee, ab to kaheen paradesh jaane kaa jee hot hai. yahan to kamaaee mein barakat nahin. kisee tarah pet kee rotee chal jati hai. woh bhee ro-dhokar. kaee aadamee poorab se aaye hain. woh kehte hain, wahan do-teen rupaye roj kee majadooree ho jati hai. chaar-paanch saal bhee raha gaya, to maalaamaal ho jaaoongaa. ab aage ladake-baale hue, inake lie kuchh to karn hee chaahie.
dulaaree ne samarthan kiya- haath mein chaar paise honge, ladakon ko padhaayenge-likhaayenge. hamaaree to kisee tarah kat gayee, ladakon ko to aadamee banaanaa hai.
pyaaree yah prastaav sunakar avaak raha gayee. unakaa munh taakane lagee. isake pahale is tarah kee baatacheet kabhee na huee thi. yah dhun kaise savaar ho gayee? use shak hua, shaayad mere vajah yah bhaavanaa utpann huee. bolee- main to jaane ko na kahoongee, aage jaisee ichchhaa ho. ladakon ko padhaane-likhaane ke lie yahan bhee to madarasaa hai. phir kya nity yahee din bane rahenge. do-teen saal bhee khetee ban gayee, to sab kuchh ho jaayagaa.
mathuraa- itane din khetee karte ho gaye, jab ab tak na banee, to ab kya ban jaayagee! is tarah ek din chal denge, man-kee-man mein raha jaayagee. phir ab paurookh bhee to thak raha hai. yah khetee kaun sanbhaalegaa. ladakon ko main chakkee mein jotakar unakee zindagi nahin kharaab karn chaahataa.
pyaaree ne aankhon mein aansoo laakar kahaa- bhaiyaa, ghar par jab tak aadhee mile, saaree ke lie na dhaavanaa chaahie, agar meree ora se koi baat ho, to apanaa ghar-baar apne haath mein karo, mujhe ek tukadaa de denaa, padee rahoongee.
mathuraa aardr galaa hokar bol- bhaabhee, yah tum kya kehti ho. tumhaare hee senbhaale yah ghar ab tak chalaa hai, nahin rasaatal mein chalaa gaya hot. is girastee ke peechhe tumane apne ko mittee mein milaa diya, apanee deh ghulaa daalee. main andhaa nahin hoon. sab kuchh samajhataa hoon. ham logon ko jaane do. bhagavaan ne chaahaa, to ghar phir sanbhal jaayagaa. tumhaare lie ham baraabar kharach-barach bhejate rahenge.
pyaaree ne kahaa- jo aisaa hee hai to tum chale jaao, baal-bachchon ko kahan-kahan baandhe phiroge.
dulaaree bolee- yah kaise ho sakataa hai bahan, yahan dehaat mein ladake kya padhe-likhenge. bachchon ke binaa inakaa jee bhee wahan na lagegaa. daud-daudakar ghar aayenge aur saaree kamaaee rel khaa jaayagee. paradesh mein akele jitanaa kharachaa hogaa, utane mein saaraa ghar aaraam se rahegaa.
pyaaree bolee- to main hee yahan rahakar kya karoongee? mujhe bhee lete chalo.
dulaaree use saath le chalne ko taiyaar na thi. kuchh din jeevan kaa khushi uthaanaa chaahatee thi, agar paradesh mein bhee yah bandhan raha, to jaane se phaayadaa hee kya. bolee- bahan, tum chalateen to kya baat thi, lekin phir yahan kaa kaarobaar to chaupat ho jaayagaa. tum to kuchh-na-kuchh dekhabhaal karti hee rahogee.
ravaanaa hona kee tithi ke ek din pahale hee raamapyaaree ne raat-bhar jaagakar haluaa aur pooriyaan pakaayeen. jab se is ghar mein aayee, kabhee ek din ke lie akele rehne kaa avasar nahin aay. donon bahanen sadaa saath raheen. aaj us bhayankar avasar ko saamane aate dekhakar pyaaree kaa dil baithaa jat tha. woh dekhatee thi, mathuraa khush hai, baal-vrind yaatraa ke khushi mein khana-peenaa tak bhoole hue hain, to usake jee mein aat, woh bhee isee tarah nirdvandv rahe, moh aur mamataa ko pairon se kuchal daale, lekin woh mamataa jis khaadyal ko khaa-khaakar palee thi, use apne saamane se hataaye jate dekhakar kshubdh hone se na rookatee thi. dulaaree to is tarah nishchint hokar baithee thi, maano koi melaa dekhane jaa rahi hai. nayee- nayee cheejon ko dekhane, nayee duniya mein vicharane kee utsukataa ne use kriyaashoony-saa kar diya tha. pyaaree ke sire saare intazaam kaa bhaar tha. dhobee ke ghar se sab kapade aaye hain, yaa nahin, kaun-kaun-se baratan saath jaayenge, saphar-kharch ke lie kitane rupaye kee jaroorat hogee. ek bachche ko khaansee aa rahi thi. doosare ko kaee din se dast aa rahe the, una donon kee aushadhiyon ko peesanaa-kootanaa aadi saikadon hee kaam vyast kie hue the. ladakoree na hokar bhee woh bachchon ke laalan-poshan mein dulaaree se kushal thi. dekho, bachchon ko bahut maaranaa-peetanaa mata. maarane se bachche jiddee yaa behayaa ho jate hain. bachchon ke saath aadamee ko bachchaa ban jan padataa hai. jo tum chaaho ki ham aaraam se pade rahen aur bachche chupachaap baithe rahen, haath-pair na hilaayen, to yah ho nahin sakataa. bachche to aadat ke chanchal hote hain. unhe kisee-na-kisee kaam mein phensaaye rakho. dhele kaa khilaunaa hajaar ghudakiyon se badhakar hot hai. dulaaree ina upadeshon ko is tarah beman hokar sunatee thi, maanon koi sanakakar bak raha ho.
vidaaee kaa din pyaaree ke lie pareekshaa kaa din tha. usake jee mein aat tha kaheen chalee jaay, jisamen woh drishy dekhanaa na pade. haa! ghadee-bhar mein yah ghar soonaa ho jaayagaa. woh din-bhar ghar mein akelee padee rahegee. kisase hensegee-bolegee. yah sochakar usakaa dil kaanp jat tha. jyon-jyon samay paas aat tha, usakee vrittiyaan shithil hoti jaateen theen. woh koi kaam karte-karte jaise kho jati thi aur apalak netron se kisee vastu ko taakane lagatee. kabhee avasar paakar ekaant mein jaakar thodaa-saa ro aati thi. man ko samajhaa rahi thi, yah log apne hote to kya is tarah chale jate. yah to maanane kaa naataa hai; kisee par koi jabaradastee hai? doosaron ke lie kitanaa hee maro, to bhee apne nahin hote. paani tel mein kitanaa hee mile, pir bhee alag hee rahegaa. bachche naye-naye kurate pahane, navaab bane ghoom rahe the. pyaaree unhe pyaar karne ke lie god lenaa chaahatee, to rone kaa-saa munh banaakar chhudaakar bhaag jate. woh kya jaanatee thi ki aise avasar par bahudhaa apne bachche bhee nishthur ho jate hain.
das bajate-bajate dvaar par bailagaadee aa gayee. ladake pahale hee se us par jaa baithe. gaanv ke kitane aurat-puroosh milane aaye. pyaaree ko is samay unakaa aan buraa lag raha tha. woh dulaaree se thodee der ekaant gale milakar ronaa chaahatee thi, mathuraa se haath jodakar kahanaa chaahatee thi, meree khoj-khabar lete rahanaa, tumhaare sivaa meraa sansaar mein kaun hai, lekin is bhambhad mein usako ina baaton kaa maukaa na milaa. mathuraa aur dulaaree donon gaadee mein jaa baithe aur pyaaree dvaar par rotee khadee raha gayee. woh itanee vihrival thi ki gaanv ke baahar tak pahunchaane kee bhee use sudhi na rahi.
6
kaee din tak pyaaree moorchhit-see padee rahi. na ghar se nikalee, na choolhaa jalaayaa, na haath-munh dhoyaa. usakaa halavaahaa jokhoo baar-baar aakar keht - 'maalakin, utho, munh-haath dhoo, kuchh khaao-piyo. kab tak is tarah padee rahogee. is tarah kee tasallee gaanv kee aur striyaan bhee detee theen. par unakee tasallee mein ek tarah kee jalan kaa bhaav chhipaa hua jaan padataa tha.
jokhoo ke aavaaz mein sachchee hamadardee jhalakatee thi. jokhoo kaamachor, baatoonee aur nashebaaj tha. pyaaree use baraabar daantatee rehti thi. do-ek baar use nikaal bhee chukee thi. par mathuraa ke aagrah se phir rakh liya tha. aaj bhee jokhoo kee hamadardee-bharee baaten sunakar pyaaree jhunjhalaatee, yah kaam karne kyon nahin jat. yahan mere peechhe kyon padaa hua hai, magar use jhidak dene ko jee na chaahataa tha. use us samay hamadardee kee bhookh thi. phal kaantedaar vriksh se bhee milen to kya unhe chhod diya jat hai?
dheere-dheere kshobh kaa veg kam hua. jeevan ke vyaapaar hone lage. ab khetee kaa saaraa bhaar pyaaree par tha. logon ne salaah dee, ek hal tod do aur kheton ko uthaa do, par pyaaree kaa garv yon dhol bajaakar apanee paraajay sveekaar na karn tha. saare kaam poorvavat chalne lage. udhar mathuraa ke chitthee-patree na bhejane se usake ghamand ko aur bhee uttejanaa milee. woh samajhataa hai, main usake aasare baithee hoon, usake chitthee bhejane se mujhe koi nidhi na mil jati. use agar meree phikr nahin hai, to main kab usakee paravaah karti hoon.
ghar mein to ab khaas kaam raha nahin, pyaaree saare din khetee-baaree ke kaamon mein lagee rehti. kharabooje boye the. woh khoob phale aur khoob bike. pahale saaraa doodh ghar mein kharch ho jat tha, ab bikane lagaa. pyaaree kee manovrittiyon mein bhee ek vichitr parivartan aa gaya. woh ab saaph kapade pahanatee, maang-chotee kee ora se bhee utanee udaaseen na thi. aabhooshanon mein bhee roochi huee. rupaye haath mein aate hee usane apne giravee gahane chhudaae aur khana bhee sanyam se karne lagee. saagar pahale kheton ko seenchakar khud khaalee ho jat tha. ab nikaas kee naaliyaan band ho gayee theen. saagar mein paani jamaa hone lagaa aur usamen halkee-halkee laharen bhee theen, khile hue kamal bhee the.
ek din jokhoo haar se lautaa, to andheraa ho gaya tha. pyaaree ne poochhaa- ab tak wahan kya kart raha?
jokhoo ne kahaa- chaar kyaariyaan bach rahi thi. mainne sochaa, das mot aur kheench doon. kal kaa jhanjhat kaun rakhe?
jokhoo ab kuchh dinon se kaam mein man lagaane lagaa tha. jab tak maalik usake sir par savaar rehte the, woh heele-bahaane kart tha. ab sab-kuchh usake haath mein tha. pyaaree saare din haar mein thodee hee raha sakatee thi, isalie ab usamen jimmedaaree aa gayee thi.
pyaaree ne lote kaa paani rakhate hue kahaa- achchhaa, haath munh dho daalo. aadamee jaan rakhakar kaam kart hai, haay-haay karne se kuchh nahin hot. khet aaj na hote, kal hote, kya jaldee thi.
jokhoo ne samajhaa, pyaaree bigad rahi hai. usane to apanee samajh mein kaaragujaaree kee thi aur samajhaa tha, taareeph hogee. yahan aalochanaa huee. chidhakar bol- maalakin, daahane-baayen dono ora chalatee ho. jo baat nahin samajhatee ho, usamen kyon koodatee ho? kal ke lie to unchavaa ke khet pade sookh rahe hain. aaj badee mushkil se kuaan khaalee hua tha. savere main pahunchataa, to koi aur aakar na chhenk letaa? phir athavaare tak raah dekhanee padatee. tak tak to saaree ukh bidaa ho jati.
pyaaree usakee saralataa par hensakar bolee- are, to main tujhe kuchh kah thodee rahi hoon, paagal. main to kehti hoon ki jaan rakhakar kaam kar. kaheen beemaar pad gaya, to lene ke dene pad jaayenge.
jokhoo- kaun beemaar pad jaayagaa, main? bees saal mein kabhee sir tak to dukhaa nahin, aage kee nahin jaanataa. kaho raat-bhar kaam kart rahoon.
pyaaree- main kya jaanoon, tumheen antare din baithe rehte the, aur poochhaa jat tha to kehte the- jur aa gaya tha, pet mein darad tha.
jokhoo jhenpataa hua bol- woh baaten jab theen, jab maalik log chaahate the ki ise pees daalen. ab to jaanataa hoon, mere hee maathe hain. main na karoongaa to sab chaupat ho jaayagaa.
pyaaree- mai kya dekh-bhaal nahin karti?
jokhoo- tum bahut karogee, do ber chalee jaaogee. saare din tum wahan baithee nahin raha sakateen.
pyaaree ko usake nishkapat vyavahaar ne mugdh kar diya. bolee- to itanee raat gaye choolhaa jalaaoge. koi sagaaee kyon nahee kar lete?
jokhoo ne munh dhote hue kahaa- tum bhee khoob kehti ho maalakin! apne pet-bhar ko to hot nahin, sagaaee kar loon! savaa ser khaataa hoon ek joon pooraa savaa ser! donon joon ke lie do ser chaahie.
pyaaree- achchhaa, aaj meree rasoee mein khaao, dekhoon kitanaa khaate ho?
jokhoo ne pulakit hokar kahaa- nahin maalakin, tum banaate-banaate thak jaaogee. haan, aadh-aadh ser ke do rotaa banaakar khilaa do, to khaa loon. main to yahee kart hoon. bas, aat saanakar do lit banaataa hoon aur upale par senk letaa hoon. kabhee mathe se, kabhee namak se, kabhee pyaaj se khaa letaa hoon aur aakar pad rehta hoon.
pyaaree- main tumhe aaj phulake khilaaoongee.
jokhoo- tab to saaree raat khaate hee beet jaayagee.
pyaaree- bako mata, chatapat aakar baith jaao.
jokhoo-zara bailon ko saanee-paani detaa jaaoon to baithoon.

jokhoo aur pyaaree mein thanee huee thi.
pyaaree ne kahaa- main kehti hoon, dhaan ropane kee koi jaroorat nahin. jhadee lag jaay, to khet doob jaay. barkhaa band ho jaay, to khet sookh jaay. juaar, baajaraa, san, arahar sab to hain, dhaan na sahee.
jokhoo ne apne vishaal kandhe par phaavadaa rakhate hue kahaa- jab sabakaa hogaa, to meraa bhee hogaa. sabakaa doob jaayagaa, to meraa bhee doob jaayagaa. main kyon kisee se peechhe rahoon? baabaa ke jamaane mein paanch beeghaa se kam nahin ropaa jat tha, birajoo bhaiyaa ne usamen ek-do beeghe aur badhaa diye. mathuraa ne bhee thodaa-bahut har saal ropaa, to main kya sabase gaya-beetaa hoon? main paanch beeghe se kam na laagaaoongaa.

pyaaree ne usake kandhe par se phaavadaa le liya aur bolee- tum pahar raat se pahar raat tak taal mein rahoge, akele meraa jee oobegaa.
jokhoo ko oobane kaa tajurbaa na tha. koi kaam na ho, to aadamee pad kar so rahe. jee kyon oobe? bol- jee oobe to so rahanaa. main ghar rahoongaa tab to aur jee oobegaa. main khaalee baithataa hoon to baar- baar khaane kee soojhatee hai. baaton mein der ho rahi hai aur baadal ghire aate hain.
pyaaree ne kahaa- achchhaa, kal se jan, aaj baitho.
jokhoo ne maanon bandhan mein padakar kahaa- achchhaa, baith gaya, kaho kya kehti ho?
pyaaree ne vinod karte hue poochhaa- kahanaa kya he, main tumse poochhatee hoon, apanee sagaaee kyon nahee kar lete? akele maratee hoon. tab ek se do ho jaaoongee.
jokhoo sharamaataa hua bol- tumane phir vahee bebaat kee baat chhed dee, maalakin! kisase sagaaee kar loon yahan? aisee mehariyaa lekar kya karoongaa, jo gahanon ke lie meree jaan khaatee rahe.
pyaaree- yah to tumane badee kadee shart lagaayee. aisee aurat kahan milegee, jo gahane bhee na chaahe?
jokhoo- yah main thode hee keht hoon ki woh gahane na chaahe; haan, meree jaan na khaay. tumane to kabhee gahanon ke lie hath na kiya, balki apne saare gahane doosaron ke oopar lagaa diye.
pyaaree ke kapolon par halkaa-saa rang aa gaya. bolee- achchhaa, aur kya chaahate ho?
jokhoo- main kehne lagoongaa, to bigad jaaogee.
pyaaree kee aankhon mein sharm kee ek rekhaa najar aayee, bolee- bigadane kee baat kahoge, to zaroor bigadoongee.
jokhoo- to main na kahoongaa.
pyaaree ne use peechhe kee ora thelate hue kahaa- kahoge kaise nahin, main kahalaa ke chhodoongee.
jokhoo- main chaahataa hoon ki woh tumhaaree tarah ho, aisee ganbheer ho, aisee hee baatacheet mein hoshiyaar ho, aisaa hee achchhaa pakaatee ho, aisee hee kiphaayatee ho, aisee hee hansamukh ho. bas, aisee aurat milegee, to karoongaa, nahin isee tarah padaa rahoongaa.
pyaaree kaa munh sharm se aarakt ho gaya. usane peechhe hatakar kahaa- tum bade natakhat ho! hansee- hansee mein sab kuchh kah gaye.
