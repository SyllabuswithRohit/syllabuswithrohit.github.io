# Mantra

**Munshi Premchand**

pandit leelaadhar chaube ki jabaan mein jaadoo tha. jis vakt vah manch par khade ho kar apni vaanee ki sudhaavrishti karne lagte the; shrotaaon ki aatmaaen tript ho jati theen, logon par anuraag kaa nashaa chhaa jat tha. chaubejee ke vyaakhyaanon mein tattav to bahut kam hot tha, shabd-yojanaa bhi bahut sundar na hoti thi; lekin baar-baar duharaane par bhi uska asar kam na hot, balki ghan ki choton ki tarah aur bhi prabhaavotpaadak ho jat tha. hamen to yakeen nahin aat, lekin sunanevaale kehte hain, unhone keval ek vyaakhyaan rat rakhaa hai. aur usi ko vah shabdash: har sabhaa mein ek naye andaaj se duharaayaa karte hain. jaateey gaurav-gaan unake vyaakhyaanon kaa pradhan gun tha; manch par aate hi bhaarat ke praacheen gaurav aur poorvajon ki amar-keerti kaa raag chhed kar sabhaako mugdh kar dete the. yathaa, 'sajjano! hamaaree adhogati ki kathaa sun kar kisakee aankhon se ashrudhaaraa na nikal padegee? hamen praacheen gaurav ko yaad karake shak hone lagta hai ki ham wahi hain, yaa badal gaye. jisane kal sinh se panjaa liya, vah aaj choohe ko dekh kar bil khoj raha hai. is patan ki bhi seemaa hai. door kyon jaaie, mahaaraaj chandragupt ke samay ko hi le leejie. yoonaan kaa suvijn itihaasakaar likhta hai ki us jamaane mein yahan dvaar par taale na daale jaate the, choree kaheen sunane mein na aatee thi, vyabhichaar kaa naam-nishaan na tha, dastaavejon kaa aavishkaar hi na hua tha, purjon par laakhon kaa len-den ho jat tha, nyaay pad par baithe hue karmachaaree makkhiyaan maaraa karte the. sajjano! una dinon koi aadamee javaan na marataa tha. (taaliyaan) haan, una dinon koi aadamee javaan na marataa tha. baap ke saamane bete kaa avasaan ho jan ek abhootapoorv, ek naamumakin, ghatanaa thi. aaj aise kitane maan-pitaa hain, jinake kaleje par javaan bete kaa daag na ho! vah bhaarat nahin raha, bhaarat gaarat ho gaya!'
yah chaube jee ki shailee thi. vah abhi ki adhogati aur durdashaa tathaa bhoot ki samridhdi aur sudashaa kaa raag alaap kar logon mein jaateey svaabhimaan jaagrat kar dete the. isi sidhdi ki badaulat unki netaaon mein gananaa hoti thi. visheshat: hindoo-sabhaa ke to vah karnadhaar hi samajhe jaate the. hindoo-sabhaa ke upaasakon mein koi aisaa utsaahee, aisaa daksh, aisaa neeti-hoshiyaar doosaraa na tha. yon kahie ki sabhaa ke lie unhone apna jeevan hi utsarg kar diya tha. paisaa to unake paas na tha, kam se kam logon kaa soch yahi tha; lekin saahas, dhairy aur samajh jaise amooly ratn unake paas the, aur ye sabhi sabhaa ko arpan the. 'shudhdi' ke to maano praan hi the. hindoo-jaati kaa utthaan aur patan, jeevan aur maran unake soch mein isi sawal par avalambit tha. shuddhi ke sivaa ab hindoo jaati ke punarjeevan kaa aur koi upaay na tha. jaati ki sab naitik, shaareerik, maanasik, saamaajik, aarthik aur dhaarmik beemaariyon ki davaa isi aandolan ki saphalataa mein maryaadit thi, aur vah tan, man se isakaa udyog kiya karte the. chande vasool karne mein chaube jee siddhahast the. eeshvar ne unhen vah 'gun' bataa diya tha ki patthar se bhi tel nikaal sakate the. kanjooson ko to vah aisaa ulate chhure se moodate the ki una mahaashayon ko sadaa ke lie shikshaa mil jati thi! is vishay mein pandit jee saam, daam, dand aur bhed ina chaaron neetiyon se kaam lete the, yahan tak ki raashtr-hit ke lie daakaa aur choree ko bhi kshamy samajhte the.
garamee ke din the. leelaadhar jee kisee sheetal paarvaty pradesh ko jaane ki taiyaariyaan kar rahe the ki sair ki sair ho jaayagee, aur ban padaa to kuchh chandaa bhi vasool kar laayenge. unako jab bhraman ki ichchhaa hoti, to mitron ke saath ek deputeshan ke roop mein nikal khade hote; agar ek hajaar rupaye vasool karake vah isakaa aadhaa sair-sapaate mein kharch bhi kar den, to kisee ki kya nukasaan? hindoo sabhaa ko to kuchh na kuchh mil hi jat tha. vah na udyog karte, to itanaa bhi to na milataa! pandit jee ne ab ki saparivaar jaane kaa phaisalaa kiya tha. jab se 'shudhdi' kaa aavirbhaav hua tha, unki aarthik dashaa, jo pahale bahut shochaneey rehti thi, bahut kuchh samhal gayi thi.
lekin jaati ke upaasakon kaa aisaa achchhi kismat kahan ki shaanti- ghar kaa khushi uthaa saken. unakaa to janm hi maare-maare phirane ke lie hot hai. khabar aayee ki madraas-praant mein tabaleeg vaalon ne toophaan machaa rakhaa hai. hinduon ke gaanv ke gaanv musalamaan hote jaate hain. mullaaon ne bade josh se tabaleeg kaa kaam shuru kiya hai; agar hindoo-sabhaa ne is pravaah ko rokane ki aayojanaa na ki, to sara praant hinduon se shoony ho jaayagaa, kisee shikhaadhaaree ki soorat tak na najar aayegee. hindoo-sabhaa mein khalabalee mach gayi. turant ek khaas adhiveshan hua aur netaaon ke saamane yah samasyaa upasthit ki gayi. bahut soch-soch ke baad phaisalaa hua ki chaube jee par is kaary kaa bhaar rakhaa jaae. unse praarthanaa ki jaae ki vah turant madraas chale jaaeen, aur dharm-vimukh bandhuon kaa uddhaar karen. kehne hi ki der thi. chaube jee to hindoo-jaati ki sevaa ke lie apne ko arpan hi kar chuke the; parvat-yaatraa kaa soch rok diya, aur madraas jaane ko taiyaar ho gaye. hindoo-sabhaa ke mantree ne aankhon mein aansoo bhar kar unse vinay ki ki mahaaraaj, yah beedaa aap hi uthaa sakate hain. aap hi ko paramaatmaa ne itanee saamarthy dee hai. aapake sivaa aisaa koi doosaraa manushy bhaaratavarsh mein nahin hai, jo is ghor vipatti mein kaam aaye. jaati ki deen-heen dashaa par dayaa keejie. chaube jee is praarthanaa ko asveekaar na kar sake. phauran sevakon ki ek mandalee banee aur pandit jee ke netritv mein ravaanaa hui. hindoo-sabhaa ne use badi dhoom se bidaaee kaa bhoj diya. ek udaar raees ne chaube jee ko ek thailee bhent ki, aur relave-steshan par hajaaron aadamee unhen bidaa karne aaye. yaatraa kaa vrittaant likhne ki jaroorat nahin. har ek bade steshan par sevakon kaa sammaanapoorn svaagat hua. kaee jagah thailiyaan mileen. ratalaam ki riyaasat ne ek shaamiyaanaa bhent kiya. badaudaa ne ek motar dee ki sevakon ko paidal chalne kaa takaleeph na uthaanaa pade, yahan tak ki madraas pahunchte-pahunchte sevaa-dal ke paas ek maakool rakam ke atirikt jaroorat ki kitanee cheejen jamaa ho gayeen. wahan aabaadee se door khule hue maidaan mein hindoo-sabhaa kaa padaav padaa. shaamiyaane par raashtreey jhandaa laharaane lagaa. sevakon ne apni-apni vardiyaan nikaaleen, sthaaneey paisaa-kuberon ne daavat ke saamaan bheje, raavatiyaan pad gayeen. chaaron ora aisee chahal-pahal ho gayi, maano kisee raajaa kaa kaimp hai. raat ke aath baje the. achhooton ki ek bastee ke paas, sevak-dal kaa kaimp gais ke prakaash se jagamagaa raha tha. kaee hajaar aadamiyon kaa jamaav tha, jinamen adhikaansh achhoot hi the. unake lie alag taat bichhaa diye gaye the. oonche varn ke hindoo kaaleenon par baithe hue the. pandit leelaadhar kaa dhuaandhaar vyaakhyaan ho raha tha, tum unheen rishiyon ki bachchaa ho, jo aakaash ke neeche ek nayee srishti ki rachanaa kar sakate the! jinake nyaay, samajh aur soch-shakti ke saamane aaj sara sansaar sir jhukaa raha hai.
sahasaa ek boodhe achhoot ne uth kar poochhaa, ham log bhi unheen rishiyon ki bachchaa hain? leelaadhar, nissandeh! tumhari dhaamaniyon mein bhi unheen rishiyon kaa rakt daud raha hai aur yadyapi aaj kaa nirdayee, kathor, vichaaraheen aur sankuchit hindoo-samaaj tumhen avahelanaa ki nazar se dekh raha hai; phir bhi tum kisee hindoo se neech nahin ho, chaahe vah apne ko kitanaa hi oonchaa samajhta ho. boodhaa, tumhari sabhaa ham logon ki sudhi kyon nahin letee?
leelaadhar, hindoo-sabhaa kaa janm abhi thode hi din hue hua hai, aur is alpakaal mein usane jitane kaam kiye hain, us par use abhimaan ho sakataa hai. hindoo-jaati shataabdiyon ke baad gaharee neend se chaunkee hai, aur ab vah samay paas hai, jab bhaaratavarsh mein koi hindoo kisee hindoo ko neech na samajhegaa, jab ve sab ek doosare ko bhai samajhenge. shreeraamachandr ne nishaad ko chhaatee se lagaayaa tha, shabaree ke joothe ber khaaye the... boodhaa, aap jab inheen mahaatmaaon ki bachchaa hain, to phir oonch-neech mein kyon itanaa bhed maanate hain? leelaadhar, isalie ki ham patit ho gaye hain, ajnaan mein padakar una mahaatmaaon ko bhool gaye hain. boodhaa, ab to aapakee nidraa tootee hai, hamaare saath khaanaa karoge?
leelaadhar, mujhe koi aapatti nahin hai.
boodhaa, mere ladake se apni kanyaa kaa vivaah keejiegaa?
leelaadhar, jab tak tumhaare janm-sanskaar na badal jaaen, jab tak tumhaare aahaar-vyavahaar mein parivartan na ho jaae, ham tumse vivaah kaa sambandh nahin kar sakate, maans khaanaa chhodo, madiraa peenaa chhodo, shikshaa grahan karo, tabhee tum uchch-varn ke hinduon mein mil sakate ho.
boodhaa, ham kitane hi aise kuleen braahmanon ko jaanate hain, jo raat-din nashe mein doobe rehte hain, maans ke binaa kaur nahin uthaate; aur kitane hi aise hain, jo ek akshar bhi nahin padhe hain; par aapako unake saath khaanaa karte dekhta hoon. unse vivaah-smbandh karne mein aapako kadaachit inakaar na hogaa. jab aap khud ajnaan mein pade hue hain, to hamaaraa uddhaar kaise kar sakate hain? aapakaa dil abhi tak abhimaan se bharaa hua hai. jaaie, abhi kuchh din aur apni aatmaa kaa sudhaar keejie. hamaaraa uddhaar aapake kiye na hogaa. hindoo-samaaj mein raha kar hamaare maathe se neechta kaa kalank na mitegaa. ham kitane hi vidvaan, kitane hi aachaaravaan ho jaaen, aap hamen yon hi neech samajhte rahenge. hinduon ki aatmaa mar gayi hai, aur uska sthaan ahankaar ne le liya hai! ham ab devataa ki sharan jaa rahe hain, jinake maananevaale hamse gale milne ko aaj hi taiyaar hain. ve yah nahin kehte ki tum apne sanskaar badal kar aao. ham achchhe hain yaa bure, ve isi dashaa mein hamen apne paas bulaa rahe hain. aap agar oonche hain, to oonche bane rahie. hamen udanaa na padegaa. leelaadhar, ek rishi-bachchaa ke munh se aisee baaten sun kar mujhe hairaanee ho raha hai. varn-bhed to rishiyon hi kaa kiya hua hai. use tum kaise mitaa sakate ho?
boodhaa, rishiyon ko mata badanaam keejie. yah sab paakhand aap logon kaa rachaa hua hai. aap kehte hain, tum madiraa peete ho; lekin aap madiraa peene vaalon ki jootiyaan chaatate hain. aap hamse maans khaane ke vajah ghinaate hain; lekin aap go-maans khaanevaalon ke saamane naak ragadate hain. isalie na ki ve aap se balavaan hain! ham bhi aaj raajaa ho jaaen, to aap hamaare saamane haath baandh khade honge. aapake dharm mein wahi oonchaa hai, jo balavaan hai; wahi neech hai, jo nirbal hai. yahi aapakaa dharm hai? yah kah kar boodhaa wahan se chalaa gaya aur usake saath hi aur log bhi uth khade hue. keval chaube jee aur unake dalavaale manch par raha gaye, maano manchagaan khatm ho jaane ke baad uski pratidhavani vaayu mein goonj rahi ho. tabaleegavaalon ne jab se chaube jee ke aane ki khabar sunee thi, is phikr mein the ki kisee upaay se ina sabako yahan se door karn chaahie. chaube jee kaa naam door-door tak prasiddh tha. jaanate the, yah yahan jam gaya, to hamaaree sari ki-karaayee mehnat bekaar ho jaayagee. isake kadam yahan jamane na paayen. mullaaon ne upaay sochna shuru kiya. bahut vaad-vivaad, hujjat aur daleel ke baad phaisalaa hua ki is kaaphir ko katl kar diya jaae. aisaa savaa lootane ke lie aadamiyon ki kya kamee? usake lie to jannat kaa daravaajaa khul jaayagaa, hooren uski balaaen lengee, pharishte usake kadamon ki khaak kaa suramaa banaayenge, rasool usake sar par barakat kaa haath rakhenge, khudaaband-kareem use seene sa lagaaenge aur kahenge, too mera pyaaraa dost hai. do hatte - katte javaanon ne turant beedaa uthaa liya.
raat ke das baj gaye the. hindoo-sabhaa ke kainp mein sannaataa tha. keval chaube jee apni raavatee mein baithe hindoo-sabhaa ke mantree ko patr likh rahe the, yahan sabase badi aavashyakataa paisaa ki hai. rupayaa, rupayaa, rupayaa! jitanaa bhej saken, bhejie. deputeshan bhej kar vasool keejie, mote mahaajanon ki jeb tatolie, bhikshaa maangie. binaa paisaa ke ina abhaagon kaa uddhaar na hogaa. jab tak koi paathashaalaa na khule, koi chikitsaalay na sthaapit ho, koi vaachanaalay na ho, inhen kaise yakeen aayegaa ki hindoo-sabhaa unki hitachintak hai. tabaleegavaale jitanaa kharch kar rahe hain, uska aadhaa bhi mujhe mil jaae, to hindoo-dharm ki pataakaa phaharaane lage. keval vyaakhyaanon se kaam na chalegaa. aseeson se koi jindaa nahin rehta. sahasaa kisee ki aahat paa kar vah chaunk pade. aankhen oopar uthaayeen to dekh, do aadamee saamane khade hain. pandit jee ne shankit ho kar poochhaa, tum
kaun ho? kya kaam hai?
jawab milaa, ham ijaraaeel ke pharishte hain. tumhari rooh kabj karne aaye hain. ijaraaeel ne tumhen yaad kiya hai.
pandit jee yon bahut hi balishth aadamee the, una donon ko ek dhakke mein giraa sakate the. praat:kaal teen paav mohanabhog aur do ser doodh kaa naashtaa karte the. dopahar ke samay paav bhar ghee daal mein khaate, teesare pahar doodhiyaa bhang chhaanate, jisamen ser bhar malaaee aur aadhaa ser badaam milee rehti. raat ko dat kar byaaloo karte; kyonki praat:kaal tak phir kuchh na khaate the. is par turraa yah ki paidal pag bhar bhi na chalte the! paalakee mile, to poochhna hi kya, jaise ghar par palang udaa jaa raha ho. kuchh na ho, to ikkaa to tha hi; yadyapi kaashee mein hi do hi chaar ikkevaale aise the jo unhen dekh kar kah na den ki 'ikkaa khaalee nahin hai.' aisaa manushy narm akhaade mein pat pad kar ooparavaale pahalavaan ko thakaa sakataa tha, chustee aur phurtee ke avasar par to vah ret par nikalaa hua kachhuaa tha. pandit jee ne ek baar kanakhiyon se daravaaje ki taraph dekh. bhaagane kaa koi maukaa na tha. tab unamen saahas kaa sanchaar hua. dar ki paraakaashthaa hi saahas hai. apne sonte ki taraph haath badhaayaa aur garaj kar bole, nikal jaao yahan se!
baat munh se pooree na nikalee thi ki laathiyon kaa vaar padaa. pandit jee moorchchhit ho kar gir pade. shatruon ne paas aa kar dekh, jeevan kaa koi lakshan na tha. samajh gaye, kaam tamaam ho gaya. lootane kaa soch na tha; par jab koi poochhanevaalaa na ho, to haath badhaane mein kya harj? jo kuchh haath lagaa, le-de kar chalte bane.
praat:kaal boodhaa bhi udhar se nikalaa, to sannaataa chhaayaa hua tha, na aadamee, na aadamajaad. chhauladaariyaan bhi gaayab! chakaraayaa, yah maajaraa kya hai? raat hi bhar mein alaadeen ke mahal ki tarah sab kuchh gaayab ho gaya. una mahaatmaaon mein se ek bhi najar nahin aat, jo praat:kaal mohanabhog udaate aur shaam samay bhang ghotate dikhaayee dete the. zara aur paas jaa kar pandit leelaadhar ki raavatee mein jhaankaa, to kalejaa sann se ho gaya! pandit jee jameen par murde ki tarah pade hue the. munh par makkhiyaan bhinak rahi theen. sir ke baalon mein rakt aisaa jam gaya tha, jaise kisee chitrakaar ke brash mein rang. saare kapade lahooluhaan ho rahe the. samajh gaya, pandit jee ke saathiyon ne unhen maar kar apni raah lee. sahasaa pandit jee ke munh se karaahane ki aavaaj nikalee. abhi jaan baakee thi. boodhaa turant daudaa hua gaanv mein aa gaya aur kaee aadamiyon ko laakar pandit jee ko apne ghar uthavaa le gaya. maraham-pattee hone lagee. boodhaa din ke din aur raat ki raat pandit jee ke paas baithaa rehta. usake gharavaale unki shushrooshaa mein lage rehte. gaanv vaale bhi yathaashakti madad karte. is bechaare kaa yahan kaun apna baithaa hua hai? apne hain to ham, begaane hain to ham. hamaare hi uddhaar ke lie to bechaaraa yahan aay tha, nahin to yahan use kyon aan tha? kaee baar pandit jee apne ghar par beemaar pad chuke the, par unake gharavaalon ne itanee tanmayataa se unki teemaaradaaree na ki thi. sara ghar, aur ghar hi nahin; sara gaanv unakaa gulaam banaa hua tha. atithi-sevaa unake dharm kaa ek ang tha. sabhy-svaarth ne abhi us bhaav kaa galaa nahin ghontaa tha. saanp kaa mantr jaananevaalaa dehaatee ab bhi maagh-poos ki andhoree meghaachchhann raatri mein mantr jhaadane ke lie das-paanch kos paidal daudataa hua chalaa jat hai. use dabal phees aur savaaree ki jaroorat nahin hoti. boodhaa mal-mootraa tak apne haathon uthaa kar phenkataa, pandit jee ki ghudakiyaan sunataa, saare gaanv se doodh maang kar unhen pilaataa. par uski tyoriyaan
kabhi mailee na hoteen. agar usake kaheen chale jaane par gharavaale laaparavaahee karte to aa kar sabako daotataa.
mahine bhar ke baad pandit jee chalne-phirane lage aur ab unhen pataa hua ki ina logon ne mere saath kitanaa upakaar kiya hai. inheen logon kaa kaam tha ki mujhe maut ke munh se nikaalaa, nahin to marane mein kya kasar raha gayi thi? unhen anubhav hua ki main jin logon ko neech samajhta tha, aur jinake uddhaar kaa beedaa uthaa kar aay tha ve mujhse kaheen oonche hain. main is paristhiti mein kadaachit rogee ko kisee aspataal mein bhej kar hi apni karttavy -nishthaa par garv kart; samajhta maine dadheechi aur harishchandr kaa munh ujjval kar diya. unake roen-roen se ina dev-tuly praaniyon ke prati aasheervaad nikalne lagaa. teen mahine gujar gaye. na to hindoo-sabhaa ne pandit jee ki khabar lee aur na gharavaalon ne. sabhaa ke munh-patr mein unki mrityu par aansoo bahaaye gaye, unake kaamon ki prashansaa ki gayi, aur unakaa smaarak banaane ke lie chandaa khol diya gaya. gharavaale bhi ro-peet kar baith rahe. udhar pandit jee doodh aur ghee khaa kar chauk-chauband ho gaye. chehare par khoon ki surkhee daud gayi, deh bhar aayee. dehaat ke jalavaayu ne vah kaam kar dikhaayaa jo kabhi malaaee aur makkhan se na hua tha. pahale ki tarah taiyaar to vah na hue; par phurtee aur chustee dugunee ho gayi. motaaee kaa aalasy ab naam ko bhi na tha. unamen ek naye jeevan kaa sanchaar ho gaya.
jaadaa shuru ho gaya tha. pandit jee ghar lautane ki taiyaariyaan kar rahe the. itane mein pleg kaa aakraman hua, aur gaanv ke teen aadamee beemaar ho gaye. boodhaa chaudharee bhi unheen mein tha. gharavaale ina rogiyon ko chhodakar bhaag khade hue. wahan kaa dastoor tha ki jin beemaariyon ko ve log devee kaa kop samajhte the, unake rogiyon ko chhod kar chale jaate the. unhen bachaanaa devataaon vair mol lena tha, aur devataaon se vair kar ke kahan jaate? jis praanee ko devataa ne chun liya, use bhalaa ve usake haathon se chheenane kaa saahas kaise karte? pandit jee ko bhi logon ne saath le jan chaahaa; lekin pandit jee na gaye. unhone gaanv mein raha kar rogiyon ki rakshaa karne kaa phaisalaa kiya. jis praanee ne unhen maut ke panje se chhudaayaa tha; use is dashaa mein chhodakar vah kaise jaate? upakaar ne unki aatmaa ko jagaa diya tha. boodhe chaudharee ne teesare din hosh aane par jab unhen apne paas khade dekh, to bol, mahaaraaj, tum yahan kyon aa gaye? mere lie devataaon kaa hukm aa gaya hai. ab main kisee tarah nahin ruk sakataa. tum kyon apni jaan jokhim mein daalate ho? mujh par dayaa karo, chale jaao. lekin pandit jee par koi asar na hua. vah baaree-baaree se teenon rogiyon ke paas jaate aur kabhi unki giltiyaan senkate, kabhi unhen puraanon ki kathaaen sunaate. gharon mein naaj, baratan aadi sab jyon ke tyon rakhe hue the. pandit jee pathy banaa kar rogiyon ko khilaate. raat ko jab rogee so jaate aur sara gaanv bhaany-bhaany karne lagta to pandit jee ko bhayankar jantu dikhaayee dete. unake kaleje mein dhadakan hone lagti; lekin wahan se talane kaa naam na lete. unhone phaisalaa kar liya tha ki yaa to ina logon ko bachaa hi loongaa yaa ina par apne ko balidaan hi kar doongaa. jab teen din senk-baandh karne par bhi rogiyon ki haalat na sanbhalee, to pandit jee ko badi phikr hui. shahar wahan se bees meel par tha. rel kaa kaheen pataa nahin, raastaa beehad aur savaaree koi nahin. idhar yah dar ki akele rogiyon ki na jaane kya dashaa ho. bechaare bade sankat mein pade. ant ko chauthe din, pahar raat rahe, vah akele shahar ko chal diye aur das bajate-bajate wahan jaa pahunche. aspataal se davaa lene mein badi kathinaaee kaa saamanaa karn padaa. ganvaaron se aspataalavaale davaaon kaa manamaanaa daam vasool karte the. pandit jee ko mupht kyon dene lage? daaktar ne munshee se kahaa, davaa taiyaar nahin hai. pandit jee ne gidagidaa kar kahaa, sarakaar, badi door se aay hoon. kaee aadamee beemaar pade hain. davaa na milegee, to sab mar jaaenge.
munshee ne bigad kar kahaa, kyon sir khaaye jaate ho? kah to diya, davaa taiyaar nahin hai, na to itanee jaldee ho hi sakatee hai. pandit jee bahut deenabhaav se bole, sarakaar, braahman hoon; aapake baal-bachchon ko bhagavaan chiranjeevee karen, dayaa keejie. aapakaa akabaal chamakataa rahe.
