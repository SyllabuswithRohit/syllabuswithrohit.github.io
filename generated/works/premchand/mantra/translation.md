# Mantra

**Munshi Premchand**

pandit leelaadhar chaube kee jabaan mein jaadoo tha| jis vakt vah manch par khade ho kar apanee vaanee kee sudhaavrishti karne lagate the; shrotaaon kee aatmaaen tript ho jati theen, logon par anuraag kaa nashaa chhaa jat tha| chaubejee ke vyaakhyaanon mein tattav to bahut kam hot tha, shabd-yojanaa bhee bahut sundar na hoti thi; lekin baar-baar duharaane par bhee usakaa asar kam na hot, balki ghan kee choton kee tarah aur bhee prabhaavotpaadak ho jat tha| hamen to yakeen nahin aat, lekin sunanevaale kehte hain, unhonne keval ek vyaakhyaan rat rakhaa hai| aur usee ko vah shabdash: har sabhaa mein ek naye andaaj se duharaayaa karte hain| jaateey gaurav-gaan unake vyaakhyaanon kaa pradhan gun tha; manch par aate hee bhaarat ke praacheen gaurav aur poorvajon kee amar-keerti kaa raag chhed kar sabhaako mugdh kar dete the| yathaa, 'sajjano ! hamaaree adhogati kee kathaa sun kar kisakee aankhon se ashrudhaaraa na nikal padegee ? hamen praacheen gaurav ko yaad karake shak hone lagataa hai ki ham vahee hain, yaa badal gaye| jisane kal sinh se panjaa liya, vah aaj choohe ko dekh kar bil khoj raha hai| is patan kee bhee seemaa hai| door kyon jaaie, mahaaraaj chandragupt ke samay ko hee le leejie| yoonaan kaa suvijn itihaasakaar likhataa hai ki us jamaane mein yahan dvaar par taale na daale jaate the, choree kaheen sunane mein na aatee thi, vyabhichaar kaa naam-nishaan na tha, dastaavejon kaa aavishkaar hee na hua tha, purjon par laakhon kaa len-den ho jat tha, nyaay pad par baithe hue karmachaaree makkhiyaan maaraa karte the| sajjano ! una dinon koee aadamee javaan na marataa tha| (taaliyaan) haan, una dinon koee aadamee javaan na marataa tha| baap ke saamane bete kaa avasaan ho jan ek abhootapoorv, ek naamumakin, ghatanaa thi| aaj aise kitane maan-pitaa hain, jinake kaleje par javaan bete kaa daag na ho ! vah bhaarat nahin raha, bhaarat gaarat ho gaya !'
yah chaube jee kee shailee thi| vah abhee kee adhogati aur durdashaa tathaa bhoot kee samridhdi aur sudashaa kaa raag alaap kar logon mein jaateey svaabhimaan jaagrat kar dete the| isee sidhdi kee badaulat unakee netaaon mein gananaa hoti thi| visheshat: hindoo-sabhaa ke to vah karnadhaar hee samajhe jaate the| hindoo-sabhaa ke upaasakon mein koee aisaa utsaahee, aisaa daksh, aisaa neeti-hoshiyaar doosaraa na tha| yon kahie ki sabhaa ke lie unhonne apanaa jeevan hee utsarg kar diya tha| paisaa to unake paas na tha, kam se kam logon kaa soch yahee tha; lekin saahas, dhairy aur samajh jaise amooly ratn unake paas the, aur ye sabhee sabhaa ko arpan the| 'shudhdi' ke to maano praan hee the| hindoo-jaati kaa utthaan aur patan, jeevan aur maran unake soch mein isee savaal par avalambit tha| shuddhi ke sivaa ab hindoo jaati ke punarjeevan kaa aur koee upaay na tha| jaati kee sab naitik, shaareerik, maanasik, saamaajik, aarthik aur dhaarmik beemaariyon kee davaa isee aandolan kee saphalataa mein maryaadit thi, aur vah tan, man se isakaa udyog kiya karte the| chande vasool karne mein chaube jee siddhahast the| eeshvar ne unhen vah 'gun' bataa diya tha ki patthar se bhee tel nikaal sakate the| kanjooson ko to vah aisaa ulate chhure se moodate the ki una mahaashayon ko sadaa ke lie shikshaa mil jati thi ! is vishay mein pandit jee saam, daam, dand aur bhed ina chaaron neetiyon se kaam lete the, yahan tak ki raashtr-hit ke lie daakaa aur choree ko bhee kshamy samajhate the|
garamee ke din the| leelaadhar jee kisee sheetal paarvaty pradesh ko jaane kee taiyaariyaan kar rahe the ki sair kee sair ho jaayagee, aur ban padaa to kuchh chandaa bhee vasool kar laayenge| unako jab bhraman kee ichchhaa hoti, to mitron ke saath ek deputeshan ke roop mein nikal khade hote; agar ek hajaar rupaye vasool karake vah isakaa aadhaa sair-sapaate mein kharch bhee kar den, to kisee kee kyaa nukasaan ? hindoo sabhaa ko to kuchh na kuchh mil hee jat tha| vah na udyog karte, to itanaa bhee to na milataa ! pandit jee ne ab kee saparivaar jaane kaa phaisalaa kiya tha| jab se 'shudhdi' kaa aavirbhaav hua tha, unakee aarthik dashaa, jo pahale bahut shochaneey rahatee thi, bahut kuchh samhal gayee thi|
lekin jaati ke upaasakon kaa aisaa achchhee kismat kahan ki shaanti- ghar kaa khushee uthaa saken| unakaa to janm hee maare-maare phirane ke lie hot hai| khabar aayee ki madraas-praant mein tabaleeg vaalon ne toophaan machaa rakhaa hai| hinduon ke gaanv ke gaanv musalamaan hote jaate hain| mullaaon ne bade josh se tabaleeg kaa kaam shuroo kiya hai; agar hindoo-sabhaa ne is pravaah ko rokane kee aayojanaa na kee, to saaraa praant hinduon se shoony ho jaayagaa, kisee shikhaadhaaree kee soorat tak na najar aayegee| hindoo-sabhaa mein khalabalee mach gayee| turant ek khaas adhiveshan hua aur netaaon ke saamane yah samasyaa upasthit kee gayee| bahut soch-soch ke baad phaisalaa hua ki chaube jee par is kaary kaa bhaar rakhaa jaae| unse praarthanaa kee jaae ki vah turant madraas chale jaaeen, aur dharm-vimukh bandhuon kaa uddhaar karen| kehne hee kee der thi| chaube jee to hindoo-jaati kee sevaa ke lie apne ko arpan hee kar chuke the; parvat-yaatraa kaa soch rok diya, aur madraas jaane ko taiyaar ho gaye| hindoo-sabhaa ke mantree ne aankhon mein aansoo bhar kar unse vinay kee ki mahaaraaj, yah beedaa aap hee uthaa sakate hain| aap hee ko paramaatmaa ne itanee saamarthy dee hai| aapake sivaa aisaa koee doosaraa manushy bhaaratavarsh mein nahin hai, jo is ghor vipatti mein kaam aaye| jaati kee deen-heen dashaa par dayaa keejie| chaube jee is praarthanaa ko asveekaar na kar sake| phauran sevakon kee ek mandalee banee aur pandit jee ke netritv mein ravaanaa huee| hindoo-sabhaa ne use badee dhoom se bidaaee kaa bhoj diya| ek udaar raees ne chaube jee ko ek thailee bhent kee, aur relave-steshan par hajaaron aadamee unhen bidaa karne aaye| yaatraa kaa vrittaant likhane kee jaroorat nahin| har ek bade steshan par sevakon kaa sammaanapoorn svaagat hua| kaee jagah thailiyaan mileen| ratalaam kee riyaasat ne ek shaamiyaanaa bhent kiya| badaudaa ne ek motar dee ki sevakon ko paidal chalne kaa takaleeph na uthaanaa pade, yahan tak ki madraas pahunchate-pahunchate sevaa-dal ke paas ek maakool rakam ke atirikt jaroorat kee kitanee cheejen jamaa ho gayeen| wahan aabaadee se door khule hue maidaan mein hindoo-sabhaa kaa padaav padaa| shaamiyaane par raashtreey jhandaa laharaane lagaa| sevakon ne apanee-apanee vardiyaan nikaaleen, sthaaneey paisaa-kuberon ne daavat ke saamaan bheje, raavatiyaan pad gayeen| chaaron ora aisee chahal-pahal ho gayee, maano kisee raajaa kaa kaimp hai| raat ke aath baje the| achhooton kee ek bastee ke paas, sevak-dal kaa kaimp gais ke prakaash se jagamagaa raha tha| kaee hajaar aadamiyon kaa jamaav tha, jinamen adhikaansh achhoot hee the| unake lie alag taat bichhaa diye gaye the| oonche varn ke hindoo kaaleenon par baithe hue the| pandit leelaadhar kaa dhuaandhaar vyaakhyaan ho raha tha, tum unheen rishiyon kee bachchaa ho, jo aakaash ke neeche ek nayee srishti kee rachanaa kar sakate the ! jinake nyaay, samajh aur soch-shakti ke saamane aaj saaraa sansaar sir jhukaa raha hai|
sahasaa ek boodhe achhoot ne uth kar poochhaa, ham log bhee unheen rishiyon kee bachchaa hain ? leelaadhar, nissandeh ! tumhaaree dhaamaniyon mein bhee unheen rishiyon kaa rakt daud raha hai aur yadyapi aaj kaa nirdayee, kathor, vichaaraheen aur sankuchit hindoo-samaaj tumhen avahelanaa kee nazar se dekh raha hai; phir bhee tum kisee hindoo se neech nahin ho, chaahe vah apne ko kitanaa hee oonchaa samajhataa ho| boodhaa, tumhaaree sabhaa ham logon kee sudhi kyon nahin letee ?
leelaadhar, hindoo-sabhaa kaa janm abhee thode hee din hue hua hai, aur is alpakaal mein usane jitane kaam kiye hain, us par use abhimaan ho sakataa hai| hindoo-jaati shataabdiyon ke baad gaharee neend se chaunkee hai, aur ab vah samay paas hai, jab bhaaratavarsh mein koee hindoo kisee hindoo ko neech na samajhegaa, jab ve sab ek doosare ko bhai samajhenge| shreeraamachandr ne nishaad ko chhaatee se lagaayaa tha, shabaree ke joothe ber khaaye the... boodhaa, aap jab inheen mahaatmaaon kee bachchaa hain, to phir oonch-neech mein kyon itanaa bhed maanate hain ? leelaadhar, isalie ki ham patit ho gaye hain, ajnaan mein padakar una mahaatmaaon ko bhool gaye hain| boodhaa, ab to aapakee nidraa tootee hai, hamaare saath khaanaa karoge ?
leelaadhar, mujhe koee aapatti nahin hai|
boodhaa, mere ladake se apanee kanyaa kaa vivaah keejiegaa ?
leelaadhar, jab tak tumhaare janm-sanskaar na badal jaaen, jab tak tumhaare aahaar-vyavahaar mein parivartan na ho jaae, ham tumse vivaah kaa sambandh nahin kar sakate, maans khaanaa chhodo, madiraa peenaa chhodo, shikshaa grahan karo, tabhee tum uchch-varn ke hinduon mein mil sakate ho|
boodhaa, ham kitane hee aise kuleen braahmanon ko jaanate hain, jo raat-din nashe mein doobe rahate hain, maans ke binaa kaur nahin uthaate; aur kitane hee aise hain, jo ek akshar bhee nahin padhe hain; par aapako unake saath khaanaa karte dekhataa hoon| unse vivaah-smbandh karne mein aapako kadaachit inakaar na hogaa| jab aap khud ajnaan mein pade hue hain, to hamaaraa uddhaar kaise kar sakate hain ? aapakaa dil abhee tak abhimaan se bharaa hua hai| jaaie, abhee kuchh din aur apanee aatmaa kaa sudhaar keejie| hamaaraa uddhaar aapake kiye na hogaa| hindoo-samaaj mein raha kar hamaare maathe se neechataa kaa kalank na mitegaa| ham kitane hee vidvaan, kitane hee aachaaravaan ho jaaen, aap hamen yon hee neech samajhate rahenge| hinduon kee aatmaa mar gayee hai, aur usakaa sthaan ahankaar ne le liya hai ! ham ab devataa kee sharan jaa rahe hain, jinake maananevaale hamse gale milane ko aaj hee taiyaar hain| ve yah nahin kehte ki tum apne sanskaar badal kar aao| ham achchhe hain yaa bure, ve isee dashaa mein hamen apne paas bulaa rahe hain| aap agar oonche hain, to oonche bane rahie| hamen udanaa na padegaa| leelaadhar, ek rishi-bachchaa ke munh se aisee baaten sun kar mujhe hairaanee ho raha hai| varn-bhed to rishiyon hee kaa kiya hua hai| use tum kaise mitaa sakate ho ?
boodhaa, rishiyon ko mata badanaam keejie| yah sab paakhand aap logon kaa rachaa hua hai| aap kehte hain, tum madiraa peete ho; lekin aap madiraa peene vaalon kee jootiyaan chaatate hain| aap hamse maans khaane ke vajah ghinaate hain; lekin aap go-maans khaanevaalon ke saamane naak ragadate hain| isalie na ki ve aap se balavaan hain ! ham bhee aaj raajaa ho jaaen, to aap hamaare saamane haath baandh khade honge| aapake dharm mein vahee oonchaa hai, jo balavaan hai; vahee neech hai, jo nirbal hai| yahee aapakaa dharm hai ? yah kah kar boodhaa wahan se chalaa gaya aur usake saath hee aur log bhee uth khade hue| keval chaube jee aur unake dalavaale manch par raha gaye, maano manchagaan khatm ho jaane ke baad usakee pratidhavani vaayu mein goonj rahi ho| tabaleegavaalon ne jab se chaube jee ke aane kee khabar sunee thi, is phikr mein the ki kisee upaay se ina sabako yahan se door karn chaahie| chaube jee kaa naam door-door tak prasiddh tha| jaanate the, yah yahan jam gaya, to hamaaree saaree kee-karaayee mehanat bekaar ho jaayagee| isake kadam yahan jamane na paayen| mullaaon ne upaay sochanaa shuroo kiya| bahut vaad-vivaad, hujjat aur daleel ke baad phaisalaa hua ki is kaaphir ko katl kar diya jaae| aisaa savaa lootane ke lie aadamiyon kee kyaa kamee ? usake lie to jannat kaa daravaajaa khul jaayagaa, hooren usakee balaaen lengee, pharishte usake kadamon kee khaak kaa suramaa banaayenge, rasool usake sar par barakat kaa haath rakhenge, khudaaband-kareem use seene sa lagaaenge aur kahenge, too meraa pyaaraa dost hai| do hatte - katte javaanon ne turant beedaa uthaa liya|
raat ke das baj gaye the| hindoo-sabhaa ke kainp mein sannaataa tha| keval chaube jee apanee raavatee mein baithe hindoo-sabhaa ke mantree ko patr likh rahe the, yahan sabase badee aavashyakataa paisaa kee hai| rupayaa, rupayaa, rupayaa ! jitanaa bhej saken, bhejie| deputeshan bhej kar vasool keejie, mote mahaajanon kee jeb tatolie, bhikshaa maangie| binaa paisaa ke ina abhaagon kaa uddhaar na hogaa| jab tak koee paathashaalaa na khule, koee chikitsaalay na sthaapit ho, koee vaachanaalay na ho, inhen kaise yakeen aayegaa ki hindoo-sabhaa unakee hitachintak hai| tabaleegavaale jitanaa kharch kar rahe hain, usakaa aadhaa bhee mujhe mil jaae, to hindoo-dharm kee pataakaa phaharaane lage| keval vyaakhyaanon se kaam na chalegaa| aseeson se koee jindaa nahin rahataa| sahasaa kisee kee aahat paa kar vah chaunk pade| aankhen oopar uthaayeen to dekh, do aadamee saamane khade hain| pandit jee ne shankit ho kar poochhaa, tum
kaun ho ? kyaa kaam hai ?
javaab milaa, ham ijaraaeel ke pharishte hain| tumhaaree rooh kabj karne aaye hain| ijaraaeel ne tumhen yaad kiya hai|
pandit jee yon bahut hee balishth aadamee the, una donon ko ek dhakke mein giraa sakate the| praat:kaal teen paav mohanabhog aur do ser doodh kaa naashtaa karte the| dopahar ke samay paav bhar ghee daal mein khaate, teesare pahar doodhiyaa bhang chhaanate, jisamen ser bhar malaaee aur aadhaa ser badaam milee rahatee| raat ko dat kar byaaloo karte; kyonki praat:kaal tak phir kuchh na khaate the| is par turraa yah ki paidal pag bhar bhee na chalate the ! paalakee mile, to poochhanaa hee kyaa, jaise ghar par palang udaa jaa raha ho| kuchh na ho, to ikkaa to tha hee; yadyapi kaashee mein hee do hee chaar ikkevaale aise the jo unhen dekh kar kah na den ki 'ikkaa khaalee nahin hai|' aisaa manushy narm akhaade mein pat pad kar ooparavaale pahalavaan ko thakaa sakataa tha, chustee aur phurtee ke avasar par to vah ret par nikalaa hua kachhuaa tha| pandit jee ne ek baar kanakhiyon se daravaaje kee taraph dekh| bhaagane kaa koee maukaa na tha| tab unamen saahas kaa sanchaar hua| dar kee paraakaashthaa hee saahas hai| apne sonte kee taraph haath badhaayaa aur garaj kar bole, nikal jaao yahan se !
baat munh se pooree na nikalee thi ki laathiyon kaa vaar padaa| pandit jee moorchchhit ho kar gir pade| shatruon ne paas aa kar dekh, jeevan kaa koee lakshan na tha| samajh gaye, kaam tamaam ho gaya| lootane kaa soch na tha; par jab koee poochhanevaalaa na ho, to haath badhaane mein kyaa harj ? jo kuchh haath lagaa, le-de kar chalate bane|
praat:kaal boodhaa bhee udhar se nikalaa, to sannaataa chhaayaa hua tha, na aadamee, na aadamajaad| chhauladaariyaan bhee gaayab ! chakaraayaa, yah maajaraa kyaa hai ? raat hee bhar mein alaadeen ke mahal kee tarah sab kuchh gaayab ho gaya| una mahaatmaaon mein se ek bhee najar nahin aat, jo praat:kaal mohanabhog udaate aur shaam samay bhang ghotate dikhaayee dete the| jaraa aur paas jaa kar pandit leelaadhar kee raavatee mein jhaankaa, to kalejaa sann se ho gaya ! pandit jee jameen par murde kee tarah pade hue the| munh par makkhiyaan bhinak rahi theen| sir ke baalon mein rakt aisaa jam gaya tha, jaise kisee chitrakaar ke brash mein rang| saare kapade lahooluhaan ho rahe the| samajh gaya, pandit jee ke saathiyon ne unhen maar kar apanee raah lee| sahasaa pandit jee ke munh se karaahane kee aavaaj nikalee| abhee jaan baakee thi| boodhaa turant daudaa hua gaanv mein aa gaya aur kaee aadamiyon ko laakar pandit jee ko apne ghar uthavaa le gaya| maraham-pattee hone lagee| boodhaa din ke din aur raat kee raat pandit jee ke paas baithaa rahataa| usake gharavaale unakee shushrooshaa mein lage rahate| gaanv vaale bhee yathaashakti madad karte| is bechaare kaa yahan kaun apanaa baithaa hua hai ? apne hain to ham, begaane hain to ham| hamaare hee uddhaar ke lie to bechaaraa yahan aay tha, nahin to yahan use kyon aan tha ? kaee baar pandit jee apne ghar par beemaar pad chuke the, par unake gharavaalon ne itanee tanmayataa se unakee teemaaradaaree na kee thi| saaraa ghar, aur ghar hee nahin; saaraa gaanv unakaa gulaam banaa hua tha| atithi-sevaa unake dharm kaa ek ang tha| sabhy-svaarth ne abhee us bhaav kaa galaa nahin ghontaa tha| saanp kaa mantr jaananevaalaa dehaatee ab bhee maagh-poos kee andhoree meghaachchhann raatri mein mantr jhaadane ke lie das-paanch kos paidal daudataa hua chalaa jat hai| use dabal phees aur savaaree kee jaroorat nahin hoti| boodhaa mal-mootraa tak apne haathon uthaa kar phenkataa, pandit jee kee ghudakiyaan sunataa, saare gaanv se doodh maang kar unhen pilaataa| par usakee tyoriyaan
kabhee mailee na hoteen| agar usake kaheen chale jaane par gharavaale laaparavaahee karte to aa kar sabako daotataa|
maheene bhar ke baad pandit jee chalne-phirane lage aur ab unhen pataa hua ki ina logon ne mere saath kitanaa upakaar kiya hai| inheen logon kaa kaam tha ki mujhe maut ke munh se nikaalaa, nahin to marane mein kyaa kasar raha gayee thi ? unhen anubhav hua ki main jin logon ko neech samajhataa tha, aur jinake uddhaar kaa beedaa uthaa kar aay tha ve mujhse kaheen oonche hain| main is paristhiti mein kadaachit rogee ko kisee aspataal mein bhej kar hee apanee karttavy -nishthaa par garv kart; samajhataa mainne dadheechi aur harishchandr kaa munh ujjval kar diya| unake roen-roen se ina dev-tuly praaniyon ke prati aasheervaad nikalane lagaa| teen maheene gujar gaye| na to hindoo-sabhaa ne pandit jee kee khabar lee aur na gharavaalon ne| sabhaa ke munh-patr mein unakee mrityu par aansoo bahaaye gaye, unake kaamon kee prashansaa kee gayee, aur unakaa smaarak banaane ke lie chandaa khol diya gaya| gharavaale bhee ro-peet kar baith rahe| udhar pandit jee doodh aur ghee khaa kar chauk-chauband ho gaye| chehare par khoon kee surkhee daud gayee, deh bhar aayee| dehaat ke jalavaayu ne vah kaam kar dikhaayaa jo kabhee malaaee aur makkhan se na hua tha| pahale kee tarah taiyaar to vah na hue; par phurtee aur chustee dugunee ho gayee| motaaee kaa aalasy ab naam ko bhee na tha| unamen ek naye jeevan kaa sanchaar ho gaya|
jaadaa shuroo ho gaya tha| pandit jee ghar lautane kee taiyaariyaan kar rahe the| itane mein pleg kaa aakraman hua, aur gaanv ke teen aadamee beemaar ho gaye| boodhaa chaudharee bhee unheen mein tha| gharavaale ina rogiyon ko chhodakar bhaag khade hue| wahan kaa dastoor tha ki jin beemaariyon ko ve log devee kaa kop samajhate the, unake rogiyon ko chhod kar chale jaate the| unhen bachaanaa devataaon vair mol lenaa tha, aur devataaon se vair kar ke kahan jaate ? jis praanee ko devataa ne chun liya, use bhalaa ve usake haathon se chheenane kaa saahas kaise karte ? pandit jee ko bhee logon ne saath le jan chaahaa; lekin pandit jee na gaye| unhonne gaanv mein raha kar rogiyon kee rakshaa karne kaa phaisalaa kiya| jis praanee ne unhen maut ke panje se chhudaayaa tha; use is dashaa mein chhodakar vah kaise jaate ?
