# Patni Se Pati

**Munshi Premchand**

1
mistar seth ko sabhee hindustaanee cheejon se napharat thi aur unakee sundaree patnee godaavaree ko sabhee videshee cheejon se chidh! magar sabr aur vinay bhaarat kee deviyon kaa gahane hai. godaavaree dil par hajaar jabr karake pati kee laayee huee videshee cheezon kaa vyavahaar karti thi, haalaanki bheetar hee bheetar usakaa dil apanee paravashataa par rotaa tha. woh jis vakt apne chhajje par khadee ho kar sadak par nigaah daudaatee aur kitanee hee mahilaaon ko khaddar kee saadiyaan pahane garv se sir uthaaye chalate dekhatee, to usake bheetar kee dard ek thandee aah ban kar nikal jati thi. use aisaa maaloom hot tha ki mujhse zyada badanaseeb aurat sansaar mein nahin hai. main apne svadeshavaasiyon kee itanee bhee sevaa nahin kar sakatee. shaam ko mistar seth ke aagrah karne par woh kaheen manoranjan yaa sair ke lie jati, to videshee kapade pahane hue nikalate sharm se usakee gardan jhuk jati thi. woh patron mein mahilaaon ke josh-bhare vyaakhyaan padhatee to usakee aankhen jagamagaa uthateen, thodee der ke lie woh bhool jati ki main yahan bandhanon mein jakadee huee hoon.
holee kaa din tha, aath baje raat kaa samay. svadesh ke naam par bike hue anuraagiyon kaa juloos aa kar mistar seth ke makaan ke saamane rukaa aur usee chaude maidaan mein vilaayatee kapadon kee holiyaan lagaane kee taiyaariyaan hone lageen. godaavaree apne kamare mein khidakee par khadee yah samaaroh dekhatee thi aur dil masos kar raha jati thi. ek woh hain, jo yon khush-khush, aajaadee ke nashe se matavaale, garv se sir uthaaye holee lagaa rahe hain, aur ek main hoon ki pinjade mein band pakshee kee tarah phadaphadaa rahi hoon. ina teeliyon ko kaise tod doon? usane kamare mein nigaah daudaayee. sabhee cheejen videshee theen. svadeshee kaa ek soot bhee na tha. yahee cheejen wahan jalaayee jaa rahi theen aur vahee cheejen yahan usake dil mein sanchit pachhataavaa kee tarah sandookon mein rakhee huee theen. usake jee mein ek lahar uth rahi thi ki ina cheejon ko uthaakar usee holee mein daal de, usakee saaree pachhataavaa aur durbalataa jal kar bhasm ho jaay! magar pati kee aprasannataa ke dar ne usakaa haath pakad liya. sahasaa mi. seth ne andar aa kar kahaa-zara ina siraphiron ko dekho, kapade jalaa rahe hain. yah paagalapan, unmaad aur vidroh nahin to aur kya hai. kisee ne sach kahaa hai, hindustaaniyon ko na akl aayee hai na aayegee. koi kal bhee to seedhee nahin.
godaavaree ne kahaa-tum bhee hindustaanee ho?
seth ne garm hokar kahaa-haan, lekin mujhe isakaa hameshaa khed rehta hai ki aise abhaage desh mein kyon paidaa hua. main nahin chaahataa ki koi mujhe hindustaanee kahe yaa samajhe. kam-se-kam mainne aachaar-vyavahaar, vesh-bhooshaa, reeti-neeti, karm-vachan mein koi aisee baat nahin rakhee, jisse hamen koi hindustaanee hone kaa kalank lagaaye. poochhiye, jab hamen aath aane gaj mein badhiyaa kapadaa milataa hai, to ham kyon motaa taat khareeden. is vishay mein har ek ko pooree svaadheenataa honee chaahie. na jaane kyon gavarnament ne ina dushton ko yahan jamaa hone diya. agar mere haath mein adhikaar hot, to sabon ko jahannum raseed kar detaa. tab aate-daal kaa bhaav maaloom hot.
godaavaree ne apne shabdon mein teekshn tiraskaar bhar ke kahaa-tumhe apne bhaaiyon kaa zara bhee khayaal nahin aat? bhaarat ke sivaa aur bhee koi desh hai, jis par kisee doosaree jaati kaa shaasan ho? chhote-chhote raashtr bhee kisee doosaree jaati ke gulaam ban kar nahin rahanaa chaahate. kya ek hindustaanee ke lie yah sharm kee baat nahin hai ki woh apne thode-se phaayade ke lie sarakaar kaa saath de kar apne hee bhaaiyon ke saath zulm kare?
seth ne bhonhe chadhaa kar kahaa-main inhen apanaa bhai nahin samajhataa.
godaavaree-aakhir tumhe sarakaar jo vetan detee hai, woh inheen kee jeb se to aat hai.
seth-mujhe isase koi matalab nahin ki meraa vetan kisakee jeb se aat hai. mujhe jisake haath se milataa hai, woh meraa swami hai. na jaane ina dushton ko kya sanak savaar huee hai. kehte hain, bhaarat aadhyaatmik desh hai. kya adhyaatm kaa yahee aashay hai ki paramaatmaa ke vidhaanon kaa khilaafat kiya jaaye? jab yah maaloom hai ki paramaatmaa kee ichchhaa ke viruddh ek pattee bhee nahin hil sakatee, to yah kaise mumakin hai ki yah itanaa badaa desh paramaatmaa kee marjee ke bagair angarejon ke adheen ho? kyon ina deevaanon ko itanee akl nahin aati ki jab tak paramaatmaa kee ichchhaa na hogee, koi angarejon kaa baal bhee baankaa na kar sakegaa.
godaavaree-to phir kyon naukaree karte ho? paramaatmaa kee ichchhaa hogee, to aap hee aap khana mil jaayegaa. beemaar hote ho, to kyon daude vaidy ke ghar jate ho? paramaatmaa unheen kee madad kart hai, jo apanee madad aap karte hain!
seth-beshak kart hai; lekin apne ghar mein aag lagaa denaa, ghar kee cheejon ko jalaa denaa, aise kaam hain, jinhen paramaatmaa kabhee pasand nahin kar sakataa.
godaavaree-to yahan ke logon ko chupachaap baithe rahanaa chaahie.
seth-nahin ronaa chaahie. is tarah ronaa chaahie jaise bachche maan ke doodh ke lie rote hain.
sahasaa holee jalee, aag kee shikhaaen aasamaan se baaten karne lageen, maano svaadheenataa kee devee agni-kapade dhaaran kiye hue aakaash ke devataaon se gale milane jaa rahi ho.
deenaanaath ne khidakee band kar dee, unake lie yah drishy bhee asahy tha.
godaavaree is tarah khadee rahi, jaise koi gaay kasaaee ke khoonte par khadee ho. usee vakt kisee ke gaane kee aavaaz aayee-
vatan kee dekhie takadeer kab badalatee hai.
godaavaree ke udaasee se bhare hue dil mein ek chot lagee. usane khidakee khol dee aur neeche kee taraph jhaankaa. holee ab bhee jal rahi thi aur ek andhaa ladakaa apanee khanjaree bajaa kar gaa raha tha-
vatan kee dekhie takadeer kab badalatee hai.
woh khidakee ke saamane pahunchaa to godaavaree ne pukaaraa-o andhe! khadaa raha.
andhaa khadaa ho gaya. godaavaree ne sandook kholaa, par usamen use ek paisaa milaa. not aur rupaye the, magar andhe phakeer ko not yaa rupaye dene kaa to sawal hee na tha. paise agar do-chaar mil jate, to is vakt woh zaroor de detee. par wahan ek hee paisaa tha, woh bhee itanaa ghisaa hua ki kahaar baajaar se lautaa laayaa tha. kisee dookaanadaar ne na liya tha. andhe ko woh paisaa dete hue godaavaree ko sharm aa rahi thi. woh zara der tak paise ko haath mein liye sanshay mein khadee rahi. tab andhe ko bulaayaa aur paisaa de diya.
andhe ne kahaa-maan jee kuchh khaane ko deejie. aaj din bhar se kuchh nahin khaayaa.
godaavaree-din bhar maangataa hai, tab bhee tujhe khaane ko nahin milataa?
andhaa-kya karoon maan, koi khaane ko nahin detaa.
godaavaree-is paise kaa chabainaa le kar khaa le.
andhaa-khaa loongaa, maan jee, bhagavaan aapako khush rakhe. ab yaheen sotaa hoon.
doosare din subah kaangres kee taraph se ek aam jalasaa hua. mistar seth ne vilaayatee tooth paaudar vilaayatee brush se daanton par malaa, vilaayatee saabun se nahaayaa, vilaayatee chaay vilaayatee pyaaliyon mein pee, vilaayatee biskut vilaayatee makkhan ke saath khaayaa, vilaayatee doodh piyaa. phir vilaayatee soot dhaaran karake vilaayatee sigaar munh mein dabaa kar ghar se nikale, aur apanee motar saaikil par baith phlaavar sho dekhane chale gaye.
godaavaree ko raat bhar neend nahin aayee thi. duraashaa aur paraajay kee mushkil yantranaa kisee kode kee tarah usake dil par pad rahi thi. aisaa maaloom hot tha ki usake galaa mein koi kadavee cheej atak gayee hai. mistar seth ko apne prabhaav mein laane kee usane woh sab yojanaaen keen, jo ek ramanee kar sakatee hai; par us bhale aadamee par usake saare haav-bhaav, mridu-muskaan aur vaanee-vilaas kaa koi asar na hua. khud to svadeshee vastron ke vyavahaar karne par kya raajee hote, godaavaree ke lie ek khaddar kee saadee laane par bhee sahamat na hue. yahan tak ki godaavaree ne unse kabhee koi cheej maangane kee kasam khaa lee.
gussaa aur pachhataavaa ne usakee sadbhaavanaa ko is tarah vikrit kar diya jaise koi mailee vastu nirmal jal ko dooshit kar detee hai. usane sochaa, jab yah meree itanee-see baat nahin maan sakate, tab phir main kyon inake ishaaron par chaloon, kyon inakee ichchhaaon kee laundee banee rahoon? mainne inake haath kuchh apanee aatmaa nahin bechee hai. agar aaj ye choree yaa gaban karen, to kya main sajaa paaoongee? usakee sajaa ye khud jhelenge. usakaa jurm inake oopar hogaa. inhen apne karm aur vachan kaa akhtiyaar hai, mujhe apne karm aur vachan kaa akhtiyaar. yah apanee sarakaar kee gulaamee karen, angarejon kee chaukhat par naak ragaden, mujhe kya garaj hai ki usamen unakaa sahayog karoon. jisamen aatmaabhimaan nahin, jisane apne ko svaarth ke haathon bech diya, usake prati agar mere man mein bhakti na ho to meraa dosh nahin. yah naukar hain yaa gulaam? naukaree aur gulaamee mein antar hai. naukar kuchh niyamon ke adheen apanaa nirdisht kaam kart hai. woh niyam swami aur sevak donon hee par laagoo hote hain. swami agar beizzatee kare, apashabd kahe to naukar usako sahan karne ke lie majaboor nahin. gulaam ke lie koi shart nahin, usakee daihik gulaamee peechhe hoti hai, maanasik gulaamee pahale hee ho jati hai. sarakaar ne inse kab kahaa hai ki deshee cheejen na khareedo. sarakaaree tikaton tak par yah shabd likhe hote hain, ‘svadeshee cheejen khareedo. ’ isase pataa hai ki sarakaar deshee cheejon kaa nishedh nahin karti, phir bhee yah sahab surkharoo banane kee phikr mein sarakaar se bhee do angul aage badhanaa chaahate hain!
mistar seth ne kuchh jhenpate hue kahaa-kal phlaavar sho dekhane chalogee?
godaavaree ne virakt man se kahaa-nahin!
‘bahut achchhaa tamaashaa hai. ’
‘main kaangres ke jalase mein jaa rahi hoon. ’
mistar seth ke oopar yadi chhat gir padee hoti yaa unhonne bijalee kaa taar haath se pakad liya hot, to bhee woh itane badahavaas na hote. aankhen phaad kar bole-tum kaangres ke jalase mein jaaogee?
‘haan, zaroor jaaoongee. ’
‘main nahin chaahataa ki tum wahan jaao. ’
‘agar tum meree paravaah nahin karte, to meraa dharm nahin ki tumhaaree har ek hukm kaa paalan karoon. ’
mistar seth ne aankhon mein vish bhar kar kahaa-nateejaa buraa hogaa.
godaavaree maano talavaar ke saamane chhaatee khol kar bolee-isakee phikr nahin, tum kisee ke eeshvar nahin ho.
mistar seth khoob garm pade, dhamakiyaan deen, aakhir munh pher kar let rahe. subah phlaavar sho jate samay bhee unhonne godaavaree se kuchh na kahaa.
2
godaavaree jis samay kaangres ke jalase mein pahunchee, to kaee hajaar mardon aur auraton kaa jamaav tha. mantree ne chande kee apeel kee thi aur kuchh log chandaa de rahe the. godaavaree us jagah khadee ho gayee jahaan aur striyaan jamaa theen aur dekhane lagee ki log kya dete hain. adhikaansh log do-do, chaar-chaar aan hee de rahe the. wahan aisaa dhanavaan tha hee kaun? usane apanee jeb tatolee, to ek rupayaa nikalaa. usane samajhaa yah kaaphee hai. isee intajaar mein thi ki jholee saamane aave to usamen daal doon? sahasaa vahee andhaa ladakaa jise ki usane paisaa diya tha, na jaane kidhar se aa gaya aur jyon hee chande kee jholee usake saamane pahunchee, usane usamen kuchh daal diya. sabakee aankhen usakee taraph uth gayeen. sabako kutoohal ho raha tha ki andhe ne kya diya? kaheen ek aadh paisaa mil gaya hogaa. din bhar galaa phaadataa hai, tab bhee to us bechaare ko rotee nahin milatee. agar yahee gaanaa pishvaaz aur saaj ke saath kisee mahaphil mein hot to rupaye barasate; lekin sadak par gaane vaale andhe kee kaun paravaah kart hai.
jholee mein paisaa daal kar andhaa wahan se chal diya aur kuchh door jaa kar gaane lagaa-
vatan kee dekhie takadeer kab badalatee hai.
sabhaapati ne kahaa-mitro, dekhie, yah woh paisaa hai, jo ek gareeb andhaa ladakaa is jholee mein daal gaya hai. meree aankhon mein is ek paise kee keemat kisee ameer ke ek hazaar rupaye se kam nahin. shaayad yahee is gareeb kee saaree bisaat hogee. jab aise gareebon kee hamadardee hamaare saath hai, to mujhe saty kee vijay mein koi shak nahin maaloom hot. hamaare yahan kyon itane phakeer dikhaayee dete hain. yaa to iseelie ki samaaj mein inhen koi kaam nahin milataa yaa daridrataa se paidaa huee beemaariyon ke vajah yah ab is yogy hee nahin raha gaye ki kuchh kaam karen. yaa bhikshaavritti ne inamen koi saamarthy hee nahin chhodee. svaraajy ke sivaa ina gareebon kaa ab uddhaar kaun kar sakataa hai. dekhie, woh gaa raha hai-
vatan kee dekhie takadeer kab badalatee hai.
is peedit dil mein kitanaa utsarg! kya ab bhee koi shak kar sakataa hai ki yah kisakee aavaaj hai? (paisaa oopar uthaa kar) aapamen kaun is ratn ko khareed sakataa hai?
godaavaree ke man mein jijnaasaa huee, kya yah vahee paisaa to nahin hai, jo raat mainne use diya tha? kya usane sachamuch raat ko kuchh nahin khaayaa?
usane jaa kar paas se paise ko dekh, jo mej par rakh diya gaya tha. usakaa dil dhak se ho gaya. yah vahee ghisaa hua paisaa tha.
us andhe kee dashaa, usake tyaag kaa smaran karake godaavaree anurakt ho uthee. kaanpate hue aavaaz mein bolee-mujhe aap yah paisaa de deejie, main paanch rupaye doongee.
sabhaapati ne kahaa-ek bahan is paise ke daam paanch rupaye de rahi hain.
doosaree aavaaj aayee-das rupaye.
teesaree aavaaj aayee-bees rupaye.
godaavaree ne is antim vyakti kee ora dekh. usake munh par aatmaabhimaan jhalak raha tha. maano kah raha ho ki yahan kaun hai, jo meree baraabaree kar sake! godaavaree ke man mein sparddhaa kaa bhaav jaag uthaa. chaahe kuchh ho jaaye, isake haath mein yah paisaa na jaay. samajhataa hai, isane bees rupaye kya kah diye, saare sansaar ko mol le liya.
godaavaree ne kahaa-chaalees rupaye.
us aadamee ne turant kahaa-pachaas rupaye.
hajaaron aankhen godaavaree kee ora uth gayeen maano kah rahi hon, ab aap hee hamaaree laaj rakhie.
godaavaree ne us aadamee kee ora dekh kar dhamakee se mile hue aavaaz mein kahaa-sau rupaye.
dhanee aadamee ne bhee turant kahaa-ek sau bees rupaye.
logon ke chehare par havaaiyaan udane lageen. samajh gaye, isee ke haath vijay rahi. niraash aankhon se godaavaree kee ora taakane lage; magar jyon hee godaavaree ke munh se nikalaa dedh sau, ki chaaron taraph taaliyaan padane lageen. maano kisee dangal ke darshak apne pahalavaan kee vijay par matavaale ho gaye hon.
us aadamee ne phir kahaa-paune do sau.
godaavaree bolee-do sau.
phir chaaron taraph se taaliyaan padeen. pratidvandvee ne ab maidaan se hat jaane hee mein apanee kushal samajhee.
godaavaree vijay ke garv par namrataa kaa pardaa daale hue khadee thi aur hajaaron shubhakaamanaaen us par phoolon kee tarah baras rahi theen.
3
jab logon ko maaloom hua ki yah devee mistar seth kee beevee hain, to unhe eershyaamay khushi ke saath us par raham bhee aayee.
mistar seth abhee phlaavar sho mein hee the ki ek pulis ke aphasar ne unhe yah ghaatak baatacheet sunaayaa. mistar seth sakate mein pad gaye, maano saaree deh shoony pad gayee ho. phir donon mutthiyaan baandh leen. daant peese, onth chabaaye aur usee vakt ghar chale. unakee motar-saaikil kabhee itanee tej na chalee thi.
ghar mein kadam rakhate hee unhonne chinagaariyon-bharee aankhon se dekhate hue kahaa-kya tum mere munh mein kaalikh putavaanaa chaahatee ho?
godaavaree ne shaant bhaav se kahaa-kuchh munh se bhee to kaho yaa gaaliyaan hee diye jaaoge? tumhaare munh mein kaalikh lagegee, to kya mere munh mein na lagegee? tumhaaree jad khudegee, to mere lie doosaraa kaun-saa sahaaraa hai.
mistar seth-saare shahar mein toophaan machaa hua hai. tumane mere rupaye diye kyon?
godaavaree ne usee shaant bhaav se kahaa-isalie ki main use apanaa hee rupayaa samajhatee hoon.
mistar seth daant kitakitaa kar bole-haragiz nahin, tumhe meraa rupayaa kharch karne kaa koi hak nahin hai.
godaavaree-bilakul galat, tumhaare rupaye kharch karne kaa tumhe jitanaa akhtiyaar hai, utanaa hee mujhako bhee hai. haan, jab talaak kaa kaanoon paas karaa loge aur talaak de doge, tab na rahegaa.
mistar seth ne apanaa hait itane jor se mej par phenkaa ki woh ludhakataa hua jameen par gir padaa aur bole-mujhe tumhaaree akl par aphasos aat hai. jaanatee ho tumhaaree is uddandataa kaa kya nateejaa hogaa? mujhse jawab talab ho jaayegaa. batalaao, kya jawab doongaa? jab yah jaahir hai ki kaangres sarakaar se dushmanee kar rahi hai to kaangres kee madad karn sarakaar ke saath dushmanee karn hai.
‘tumane to nahin kee kaangres kee madad! ’
‘tumane to kee! ’
‘isakee sajaa mujhe milegee yaa tumhe? agar main choree karoon, to kya tum jel jaaoge? ’
‘choree kee baat aur hai, yah baat aur hai. ’
‘to kya kaangres kee madad karn choree yaa daake se bhee buraa hai? ’
‘haan, sarakaaree naukar ke lie choree yaa daake se bhee kaheen buraa hai. ’
‘mainne yah nahin samajhaa tha. ’
‘agar tumane yah nahin samajhaa tha, to tumhaaree hee samajh kaa bhram tha. roj akhabaaron mein dekhatee ho, phir bhee mujhse poochhatee ho. ek kaangres kaa aadamee pletaphaarm par bolane khadaa hot hai, to beesiyon saade kapade vaale pulis afasar usakee riport lene baithate hain. kaangres ke saraganaaon ke peechhe kaee-kaee mukhabir lagaa diye jate hain, jinakaa kaam yahee hai ki una par kadee nigaah rakhen. choron ke saath to itanee sakhtee kabhee nahin kee jati. iseelie hajaaron choriyaan aur daake aur khoon roj hote rehte hain, kisee kaa kuchh pataa nahin chalataa, na pulis isakee paravaah karti hai. magar pulis ko jis maamale mein raajaneeti kee gandh bhee aa jati hai phir dekho pulis kee mustaidee. inspektar janaral se lekar kaanstebil tak ediyon tak kaa jor lagaate hain. sarakaar ko choron se dar nahin. chor sarakaar par chot nahin kart. kaangres sarakaar ke akhtiyaar par hamalaa karti hai, isalie sarakaar bhee apanee rakshaa ke lie apne akhtiyaar se kaam letee hai. yah to prakriti kaa niyam hai.
mistar seth aaj daphtar chale, to unake kadam peechhe raha jate the! na jaane aaj wahan kya haal ho. roj kee tarah daphtar mein pahunch kar unhonne chaparaasiyon ko daantaa nahin, klarkon par rob nahin jamaayaa, chupake se jaakar kursee par baith gaye. aisaa maaloom hot tha, koi talavaar sir par latak rahi hai. sahab kee motar kee aavaaj sunate hee unake jaan sookh gaye. roj woh apne kamare mein baithe rehte the. jab sahab aa kar baith jate the, tab aadh ghante ke baad misalen le kar pahunchate the. aaj woh baraamade mein khade the, sahab utare to jhuk kar unhonne salaam kiya. magar sahab ne munh pher liya.
lekin woh himmat nahin haare, aage badh kar pardaa hataa diya, sahab kamare mein gaye, to seth sahab ne pankhaa khol diya, magar jaan sookhee jati thi ki dekhen, kab sir par talavaar giratee hai. sahab jyon hee kursee par baithe, seth ne lapak kar, sigaar-kes aur diyaasalaaee mej par rakh dee.
ekaaek aisaa maaloom hua, maano aasamaan phat gaya ho, sahab garaj rahe the, tum dagaabaaj aadamee hai!
seth ne is tarah sahab kee taraph dekh, jaise unakaa matalab nahin samajhe.
sahab ne phir garaj kar kahaa-tum dagaabaaj aadamee hai.
mistar seth kaa khoon garm ho uthaa, bole-meraa to khayaal hai ki mujhse badaa raajabhakt is desh mein na hogaa.
sahab-tum namakaharaam aadamee hai.
mistar seth ke chehare par surkhee aayee-aap bekaar hee apanee jabaan Karaab kar rahe hain.
sahab-tum shaitaan aadamee hai.
mistar seth kee aankhon mein surkhee aayee-aap meree beijjatee kar rahe hain. aisee baaten sunane kee mujhe aadat nahin hai.
sahab-chup raho, yoo bladee. tumako sarakaar paanch sau rupaye isalie nahin detaa ki tum apne vaaiph ke haath se kaangres kaa chandaa dilavaao. tumako isalie sarakaar rupayaa nahin detaa.
mistar seth ko ab apanee saphaaee dene kaa avasar milaa. bole-main aapako yakeen dilaataa hoon ki meree vaaiph ne saraasar meree marjee ke khilaaph rupaye diye hain. main to us vakt phlaavar sho dekhane gaya tha, jahaan mis phraank kaa guladastaa paanch rupaye mein liya. wahan se lautaa, to mujhe yah khabar milee.
sahab-o! tum hamako bevakooph banaataa hai?
yah baat agni-shilaa kee tarah jyon hee sahab ke dimaag mein ghusee, unake mijaaj kaa paaraa ubaal ke darje tak pahunch gaya. kisee hindustaanee kee itanee majaal ki unhe bevakooph banaaye! woh jo hindustaan ke baadashaah hain, jinake paas bade-bade taalukedaar salaam karne aate hain, jinake naukaron ko bade-bade raees najaraanaa dete hain, unheen ko koi bevakooph banaaye! usake lie woh asahy tha. rool uthaa kar daudaa.
lekin mistar seth bhee majaboot aadamee the. yon woh har tarah kee khushaamad kiya karte the, lekin yah beizzatee sveekaar na kar sake. unhonne rool ko to haath par liya aur ek dag aage badh kar aisaa ghoonsaa sahab ke munh par raseed kiya ki sahab kee aankhon ke saamane andheraa chhaa gaya. woh is mushtiprahaar ke lie taiyaar na the. unhe kaee baar isakaa tajurbaa ho chukaa tha ki netiv bahut shaant, dabboo, aur gamakhor hot hai. visheshakar saahabon ke saamane to usakee jabaan tak nahin khulatee. kursee par baith kar naak kaa khoon ponchhane lagaa. phir mistar seth se ulajhane kee usakee himmat nahin padee, magar dil mein soch raha tha, ise kaise neechaa dikhaaoon.
mistar seth bhee apne kamare mein aa kar is paristhiti par soch karne lage. unhe bilakul khed na tha; balki woh apne saahas par khush the. isakee badamaashee to dekho ki mujh par rool chalaa diya. jitanaa dabataa tha, utanaa hee dabaaye jat tha. mem yaaron ko liye ghoomaa karti hai, usse bolane kee himmat nahin padatee. mujhse sher ban gaya. ab daudegaa kamishnar ke paas. mujhe barakhaast karaaye bagair na chhodegaa. yah sab kuchh godaavaree ke vajah ho raha hai. beijjatee to ho hee gayee. ab rotiyon ko bhee muhataaj hona padaa. mujhse to koi poochhegaa bhee nahin, barakhaastagee kaa paravaanaa aa jaayegaa. apeel kahan hogee? sekretaree hain hindustaanee, magar angarejon se bhee zyada angarej. hom membar bhee hindustaanee hain, magar angarejon ke gulaam. godaavaree ke chande kaa haal sunate hee unhe joodee chadh aayegee. insaaf kee kisee se ummeed nahin, ab yahan se nikal jaane mein hee kushal hai.
unhonne turant ek isteephaa likhaa aur sahab ke paas bhej diya. sahab ne us par likh diya, ‘barakhaast’.
4
dopahar ko jab mistar seth munh latakaaye hue ghar pahunche to godaavaree ne poochhaa-aaj jaldee kaise aa gaye?
mistar seth dahakatee huee aankhon se dekh kar bole-jis baat par lagee theen, woh ho gayee. ab roo, sir par haath rakhake!
godaavaree-baat kya huee, kuchh kaho bhee to?
seth-baat kya huee, usane aankhen dikhaayeen, mainne chaantaa jamaayaa aur isteephaa de kar chalaa aay.
godaavaree-isteephaa dene kee kya jaldee thi?
seth-aur kya sir ke baal nuchavaataa? tumhaaraa yahee haal hai, to aaj nahin, kal alag hona hee padataa.
godaavaree-khair, jo hua achchhaa hee hua. aaj se tum bhee kaangres mein shareek ho jaao.
seth ne onth chabaa kar kahaa-lajaaogee to nahin, oopar se ghaav par namak chhidakatee ho.
godaavaree-lajaaoon kyon, mein to khush hoon ki tumhaaree bediyaan kat gayeen.
seth-aakhir kuchh sochaa hai, kaam kaise chalegaa?
godaavaree-sab soch liya hai. main chal kar dikhaa doongee. haan, main jo kuchh kahoon, woh tum kiye jan. ab tak main tumhaare ishaaron par chalatee thi, ab se tum mere ishaare par chalanaa. main tumse kisee baat kee shikaayat na karti thi; tum jo kuchh khilaate the khaatee thi, jo kuchh pahanaate the pahanatee thi. mahal mein rakhate, mahal mein rehti. jhonpadee mein rakhate, jhonpadee mein rehti. usee tarah tum bhee rahanaa. jo kaam karne ko kahoon woh karn. phir dekhoon kaise kaam nahin chalataa. badappan soot-boot aur thaat-baat mein nahin hai. jisakee aatmaa pavitr ho, vahee oonchaa hai. aaj tak tum mere pati the, aaj se main tumhaaraa pati hoon.
seth jee usakee ora pyaar kee aankhon se dekh kar hans pade.
