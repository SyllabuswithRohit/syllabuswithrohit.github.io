# Agni Samadhi

**Munshi Premchand**

saadhu-santon ke satsang se bure bhee achchhe ho jate hain, lekin payaag kaa badakismatee tha, ki us par satsang kaa ultaa hee asar hua. use gaanje, charas aur bhang kaa chaskaa pad gaya, jisakaa phal yah hua ki ek mehanatee, udyamasheel naujavaan aalasy kaa upaasak ban baithaa. jeevan-sangraam mein yah khushi kahan!
kisee vat-vriksh ke neeche dhoonee jal rahi hai, ek jataadhaaree mahaatmaa viraaj rahe hain, bhaktajan unhe ghere baithe hue hain, aur til-til par charas ke dam lag rahe hain. beech-beech mein bhajan bhee ho jate hain. majooree-dhaatooree mein yah svarg-sukh kahan! chilam bharanaa payaag kaa kaam tha. bhakton ko paralok mein puny-phal kee ummeed thi, payaag ko turant phal milataa tha, chilamon par pahalaa hak usee kaa hot tha. mahaatmaaon ke shreemukh se bhagavat charchaa sunate hue woh khushi se vihval ho uthataa tha, us par aatmavismriti-see chhaa jati thi. woh saurabh, sangeet aur prakaash se bhare hue ek doosare hee sansaar mein pahunch jat tha.
isalie jab usakee aurat rukmin raat ke das-gyaarah baj jaane par use bulaane aati, to payaag ko pratyaksh kroor tajurbaa hot, sansaar use kaanton se bharaa hua jangal-saa deekhataa, visheshat: jab ghar aane par use maaloom hot ki abhee choolhaa nahin jalaa aur chane-chabene kee kuchh phikr karanee hai. woh jaati kaa bhar tha, gaanv kee chaukeedaaree usakee meeraas thi, do rupaye aur kuchh aane vetan milataa tha.
varadee aur saaphaa mupht. kaam tha saptaah mein ek din thaane jan, wahan aphasaron ke dvaar par jhaadoo lagaanaa, astabal saaph karn, lakadee cheeranaa. payaag rakt ke ghoont pee-pee kar ye kaam kart, kyonki avajnaa shaareerik aur aarthik donon hee nazar se mahangee padatee theen. aansoo yon puchhate the ki chaukeedaaree mein yadi koi kaam tha, to itanaa hee, aur maheene mein chaar din ke lie do rupaye aur kuchh aane kam na the. phir gaanv mein bhee agar bade aadamiyon par nahin, to neechon par rob tha.
vetan penshan thi aur jab se mahaatmaaon kaa sampark hua, woh payaag ke jeb-kharch kee mad mein aa gayee. isalie rozee kaa sawal dinondin chintotpaadak roop dhaaran karne lagaa. ina satsangon ke pahale yah dampati gaanv mein majadooree kart tha. rukmin lakadiyaan tod kar baajaar le jati, payaag kabhee aaraa chalaataa, kabhee hal jotataa, kabhee pur haankataa. jo kaam saamane aa jaae, usamen jut jat tha. hansamukh, shramasheel, vinodee, nirdvandv aadamee tha aur aisaa aadamee kabhee bhookhon nahin marataa. us par namr itanaa ki kisee kaam ke lie 'nahin' na kart. kisee ne kuchh kahaa aur woh 'achchhaa bhaiyaa' kah kar daudaa. isalie usakaa gaanv mein maan tha. isee kee badaulat nirudyam hone par bhee do-teen saal use zyaadaa takaleeph na hua. donon joon kee to baat hee kya, jab mahato ko yah ridhdi na praapt thi, jinake dvaar par bailon kee teen-teen jodiyaan bandhaatee theen, to payaag kis ginatee mein tha. haan, ek joon kee daal-rotee mein shak na tha.
lekin ab yah samasyaa din par din vishamatar hoti jati thi. us par vipatti yah thi ki rukmin bhee ab kisee vajah se usakee patiparaayan, utanee sevaasheel, utanee tatpar na thi. nahin, utanee pragalbhataa aur vaachaalataa mein aashcharyajanak vikaas hot jat tha. isalie payaag ko kisee aisee sidhdi kee aavashyakataa thi, jo use rozee kee phikr se mukt kar de aur woh nishchint ho kar bhagavadbhajan aur saadhu-sevaa mein pravrittaa ho jaae.
ek din rukmin baajaar mein lakadiyaan bech kar lautee, to payaag ne kahaa -laa, kuchh paise mujhe de de, dam lagaa aaoon.
rukmin ne munh pher kar kahaa -dam lagaane kee aisee chaat hai, to kaam kyon nahin karte? kya aajakal koi baabaa nahin hain, jaa kar chilam bharo?
payaag ne tyoree chadhaa kar kahaa -bhalaa chaahatee hai to paise de de; nahin to is tarah tang karegee, to ek din kaheen chalaa jaaoongaa, tab royegee.
rukmin angoothaa dikhaa kar bolee - roye meree balaa. tum rehte hee ho, to kaun sone kaa kaur khilaa dete ho? ab bhee chhaatee phaadatee hoon, tab bhee chhaatee phaadoongee.
'to ab yahee phaisalaa hai? '
'haan-haan, kah to diya, mere paas paise nahin hain. '
'gahane banavaane ke lie paise hain aur main chaar paise maangataa hoon, to yon jawab detee hai!
' rukmin tinak kar bolee - gahane banavaatee hoon, to tumhaaree chhaatee kyon phatatee hai? tumane to peetal kaa chhallaa bhee nahin banavaayaa, yaa itanaa bhee nahin dekh jat? payaag us din ghar na aay. raat ke nau baj gaye, tab rukmin ne kivaad band kar liye. samajhee, gaanv mein kaheen chhipaa baithaa hogaa. samajhataa hogaa, mujhe manaane aayegee, meree balaa jati hai. jab doosare din bhee payaag na aay, to rukmin ko phikr huee. gaanv bhar chhaan aayee. chidiyaa kisee bhee daal par na milee. us din usane rasoee nahin banaayee. raat ko letee bhee to bahut der tak aankhen na lageen. shankaa ho rahi thi, payaag sachamuch to virakt nahin ho gaya. usane sochaa, praat: kaal pattaa-pattaa chhaan daaloongee, kisee saadhu-sant ke saath hogaa. jaa kar thaane mein rapat kar doongee. abhee tadakaa hee tha ki rukmin thaane mein chalne ko taiyaar ho gayee. kivaad band karake nikalee hee thi ki payaag aat hua dikhaaee diya. par woh akelaa na tha. usake peechhe-peechhe ek aurat bhee thi. usakee chheent kee saadee, rangee huee chaadar, lambaa ghoonghat aur sharmeelee chaal dekh kar rukmin kaa kalejaa dhak-se ho gaya. woh ek pal hatabuddhi-see khadee rahi, tab badh kar nayee saut ko donon haathon ke beech mein le liya aur use is tarah dheere-dheere ghar ke andar le chalee, jaise koi rogee jeevan se niraash ho kar vish-paan kar raha ho!
jab padosiyon kee bheed chhant gayee to rukmin ne payaag se poochhaa-ise kahan se laaye?
payaag ne hans kar kahaa -ghar se bhaagee jati thi, mujhe raaste mein mil gayee. ghar kaa kaam-dhaandhaa karegee, padee rahegee.
'maaloom hot hai, mujhse tumhaaraa jee bhar gaya. '
payaag ne tirachhee chitavanon se dekh kar kahaa -dut pagalee! ise teree sevaa-tahal karne ko laayaa hoon.
'nayee ke aage puraanee ko kaun poochhataa hai? '
'chal, man jisse mile vahee nayee hai, man jisse na mile vahee puraanee hai. laa, kuchh paisaa ho to de de, teen din se dam nahin lagaayaa, pair seedhe nahin padate. haan, dekh do-chaar din is bechaaree ko khilaa-pilaa de, phir to aap hee kaam karne lagegee.
' rukmin ne pooraa rupayaa laa kar payaag ke haath par rakh diya. doosaree baar kehne kee jaroorat hee na padee. payaag mein chaahe aur koi gun ho yaa na ho, yah maananaa padegaa ki woh shaasan ke mool sidhdaanton se parichit tha. usane bhed-neeti ko apanaa lakshy banaa liya tha. ek maas tak kisee tarah kee vighn-baadhaa na padee. rukmin apanee saaree chaukadiyaan bhool gayee thi. bade tadake uthatee, kabhee lakadiyaan tod kar, kabhee chaaraa kaat kar, kabhee upale paath kar baajaar le jati. wahan jo kuchh milataa, usakaa aadhaa to payaag ke hatthe chadhaa detee. aadho mein ghar kaa kaam chalataa. woh saut ko koi kaam na karne detee. padosiyon se kehti, bahan, saut hai to kya, hai to abhee kal kee bahuriyaa. do-chaar maheene bhee aaraam se na rahegee, to kya yaad karegee. main to kaam karne ko hoon hee.

gaanv bhar mein rukmin ke sheel-aadat kaa bakhaan hot tha, par satsangee ghaagh payaag sab kuchh samajhataa tha aur apanee neeti kee saphalataa par khush hot tha.
ek din bahoo ne kahaa -deedee, ab to ghar mein baithe-baithe jee oobataa hai. mujhe bhee koi kaam dilaa do.
rukmin ne pyaar sinchit aavaaz mein kahaa -kya mere munh mein kaalikh putavaane par lagee huee hai? bheetar kaa kaam kiye jaa, baahar ke lie main hoon hee.
bahoo kaa naam kaushalyaa tha, jo bigad kar siliyaa ho gaya tha. is vakt siliyaa ne kuchh jawab na diya. lekin woh laundiyon kee dashaa ab usake lie asahy ho gayee thi. woh din bhar ghar kaa kaam karte-karte mare, koi nahin poochhataa. rukmin baahar se chaar paise laatee hai, to ghar kee maalakin banee huee hai. ab siliyaa bhee majooree karegee aur maalakin kaa ghamand tod degee.
payaag paison kaa yaar hai, yah baat usse ab chhipee na thi. jab rukmin chaaraa le kar baajaar chalee gayee, to usane ghar kee tattee lagaayee aur gaanv kaa rang-dhang dekhane ke lie nikal padee. gaanv mein braahman, thaakur, kaayasth, baniye sabhee the. siliyaa ne sheel aur sankoch kaa kuchh aisaa svaang rachaa kee sabhee striyaan us par mugdh ho gayeen. kisee ne chaaval diya, kisee ne daal, kisee ne kuchh. nayee bahoo kee aavabhagat kaun na kart? pahale hee daure mein siliyaa ko maaloom ho gaya ki gaanv mein pisanahaaree kaa sthaan khaalee hai aur woh is kamee ko pooraa kar sakatee hai. woh yahan se ghar lautee, to usake sir par gehoon se bharee huee ek tokaree thi.
payaag ne pahar raat hee se chakkee kee aavaaj sunee, to rukmin se bol - aaj to siliyaa abhee se peesane lagee. rukmin baajaar se aat laayee thi. anaaj aur aate ke bhaav mein khaas antar na tha. use hairaanee hua ki siliyaa itane sabere kya pees rahi hai. uth kar kotharee mein aayee, to dekh ki siliyaa andhere mein baithee kuchh pees rahi hai.
usane jaa kar usakaa haath pakad liya aur tokaree ko uthaa kar bolee - tujhase kisane peesane ko kahaa hai? kisakaa anaaj pees rahi hai?
siliyaa ne nishshank ho kar kahaa -tum jaa kar aaraam se sotee kyon nahin. main peesatee hoon, to tumhaaraa kya bigadataa hai! chakkee kee ghumur-ghumur bhee nahin sahee jati? laao, tokaree de do, baithe-baithe kab tak khaaoongee, do maheene to ho gaye.
'mainne to tujhase kuchh nahin kahaa! '
'tum kaho, chaahe na kaho; apanaa dharam bhee to kuchh hai. '
'too abhee yahan ke aadamiyon ko nahin jaanatee. aat to pisaate sabako achchhaa lagataa hai. paise dete rote hain. kisakaa gehoon hai? main sabere usake sir par patak aaoongee.
' siliyaa ne rukmin ke haath se tokaree chheen lee aur bolee - paise kyon na denge? kuchh begaar karti hoon?
'too na maanegee? '
'tumhaaree laundee ban kar na rahoongee. '
yah takaraar sun kar payaag bhee aa pahunchaa aur rukmin se bol - kaam karti hai to karne kyon nahin detee? ab kya janam bhar bahuriyaa hee banee rahegee? ho gaye do maheene.
'tum kya jaano, naak to meree kategee. ' siliyaa bol uthee, to kya koi baithe khilaataa hai? chaukaa-baratan, jhaadoo-bahaaroo, rotee-paani, peesanaa-kootanaa, yah kaun kart hai? paani kheenchate-kheenchate mere haathon mein ghatthe pad gaye. mujhse ab saaraa kaam na hogaa.
payaag ne kahaa -to too hee baajaar jaayaa kar. ghar kaa kaam rehne de! rukmin kar legee. rukmin ne aapatti kee, aisee baat munh se nikaalate laaj nahin aati? teen din kee bahuriyaa baajaar mein ghoomegee, to sansaar kya kahegaa!
siliyaa ne aagrah karake kahaa -sansaar kya kahegaa, kya koi aib karne jati hoon? siliyaa kee digree ho gayee. aadhipaty rukmin ke haath se nikal gaya. siliyaa kee amaladaaree ho gayee. javaan aurat thi. gehoon pees kar uthee to auron ke saath ghaas chheelane chalee gayee, aur itanee ghaas chheelee ki sab dang raha gayeen! gatthaa uthaaye na uthataa tha. jin purushon ko ghaas chheelane kaa badaa abhyaas tha, unse bhee usane baajee maar lee! yah gatthaa baarah aane kaa bikaa. siliyaa ne aat, chaaval, daal, tel, namak, tarakaaree, masaalaa sab kuchh liya, aur chaar aane bachaa bhee liye. rukmin ne samajh rakhaa tha ki siliyaa baajaar se do-chaar aane paise le kar lautegee to use daatoongee aur doosare din se phir baajaar jaane lagoongee. phir meraa raajy ho jaayagaa. par yah saamaan dekhe, to aankhen khul gayeen. payaag khaane baithaa to masaaledaar tarakaaree kaa bakhaan karne lagaa. maheenon se aisee svaadisht vastu mayassar na huee thi. bahut khush hua.
khana karake woh baahar jaane lagaa, to siliyaa barothe mein khadee mil gayee. bol - aaj kitane paise mile?
'baarah aane mile the! '
'sab kharch kar daale? kuchh bache hon to mujhe de de. ' siliyaa ne bache hue chaar aane paise de diye.
payaag paise khanakhanaataa hua bol - toone to aaj maalaamaal kar diya. rukmin to do-chaar paison hee mein taal detee thi.
'mujhe gaad kar rakhanaa thode hee hai. paisaa khaane-peene ke lie hai ki gaadane ke lie? ' 'ab too hee baajaar jaayaa kar, rukmin ghar kaa kaam karegee. ' rukmin aur siliyaa mein sangraam chhid gaya. siliyaa payaag par apanaa aadhipaty jamaaye rakhane ke lie jaan tod kar parishram karti. pahar raat hee se usakee chakkee kee aavaaj kaanon mein aane lagatee. din nikalate hee ghaas laane chalee jati aur zara der sustaa kar baajaar kee raah letee. wahan se laut kar bhee woh bekaar na baithatee, kabhee san kaatatee, kabhee lakadiyaan todatee. rukmin usake intazaam mein baraabar aib nikaalatee aur jab avasar milataa to gobar bator kar upale paathatee aur gaanv mein bechatee. payaag ke donon haathon mein laddoo the. striyaan use zyaadaa se zyaadaa paise dene aur pyaar kaa adhikaansh dekar apne adhikaar mein laane kaa koshish karti rahateen, par siliyaa ne kuchh aisee dridhataa se aasan jamaa liya tha ki kisee tarah hilaaye na hilatee thi. yahan tak ki ek din donon pratiyogiyon mein khullamakhullaa than gayee. ek din siliyaa ghaas le kar lautee to paseene se tar thi. phaagun kaa maheenaa tha; dhoop tej thi. usane sochaa, nahaa kar tab baajaar jaaoongee. ghaas dvaar par hee rakh kar woh taalaab mein nahaane chalee gayee. rukmin ne thodee-see ghaas nikaal kar padosin ke ghar mein chhipaa dee aur gatthe ko dheelaa karake baraabar kar diya. siliyaa nahaa kar lautee to ghaas kam maaloom huee. rukmin se poochhaa.
usane kahaa -main nahin jaanatee.
siliyaa ne gaaliyaan denee shuroo keen, jisane meree ghaas chhuee ho, usakee deh mein keede paden, usake baap aur bhai mar jaaen, usakee aankhen phoot jaaen. rukmin kuchh der tak to jabt kiye baithee rahi, aakhir khoon mein ubaal aa hee gaya. jhallaa kar uthee aur siliyaa ke do-teen tamaache lagaa diye. siliyaa chhaatee peet-peet kar rone lagee. saaraa muhallaa jamaa ho gaya. siliyaa kee subuddhi aur kaaryasheelataa sabhee kee aankhon mein khatakatee thi, woh sabase zyaadaa ghaas kyon chheelatee hai, sabase zyada lakadiyaan kyon laatee hai, itane sabere kyon uthatee hai, itane paise kyon laatee hai, ina kaaranon ne use padosiyon kee hamadardee se vanchit kar diya tha. sab usee ko buraa-bhalaa kehne lageen. mutthee bhar ghaas ke lie itanaa oodham machaa daalaa, itanee ghaas to aadamee jhaad kar phenk detaa hai. ghaas na huee, sonaa hua. tujhe to sochanaa chaahie tha ki agar kisee ne le hee liya, to hai to gaanv-ghar hee kaa. baahar kaa koi chor to aay nahin. toone itanee gaaliyaan deen, to kisako deen? padosiyon hee ko to?
sanyog se us din payaag thaane gaya hua tha. shaam ko thakaa-maandaa lautaa to siliyaa se bol - laa, kuchh paise de de, to dam lagaa aaoon. thak kar choor ho gaya hoon. siliyaa use dekhate hee haay-haay karake rone lagee.
payaag ne ghabaraa kar poochhaa-kya hua? kyon rotee hai? kaheen gamee to nahin ho gayee? naihar se koi aadamee to nahin aay?
'ab is ghar mein meraa rahanaa na hogaa. apne ghar jaaoongee. '
'are, kuchh munh se to bol; hua kya? gaanv mein kisee ne gaalee dee hai? kisane gaalee dee hai? ghar phoonk doon, usakaa chaalaan karavaa doon.
' siliyaa ne ro-ro kar saaree kathaa kah sunaayee.
payaag par aaj thaane mein khoob maar padee thi. jhallaayaa hua tha. woh kathaa sunee, to deh mein aag lag gayee. rukmin paani bharane gayee thi. woh abhee ghadaa bhee na rakhane paayee thi ki payaag us par toot padaa aur maarate-maarate bedam kar diya. woh maar kaa jawab gaaliyon se detee thi aur payaag har ek gaalee par aur jhallaa-jhallaa kar maarataa tha. yahan tak ki rukmin ke ghutane phoot gaye, choodiyaan toot gayeen.
siliyaa beech-beech mein kehti jati thi, vaah re teraa deedaa! vaah re teree jabaan! aisee to aurat hee nahin dekhee. aurat kaahe ko, daain hai, zara bhee munh mein lagaam nahin! lekin rukmin usakee baaton ko maano sunatee hee na thi. usakee saaree shakti payaag ko kosane mein lagee huee thi. payaag maarate-maarate thak gaya, par rukmin kee jabaan na thakee. bas, yahee rat lagee huee thi, too mar jaa, teree mittee nikale, tujhe bhavaanee khaayan, tujhe miragee aaye.
payaag raha-raha kar gussaa se tilamilaa uthataa aur aa kar do-chaar laaten jamaa detaa. par rukmin ko ab shaayad chot hee na lagatee thi. woh jagah se hilatee bhee na thi. sir ke baal khole, jameen par baithee inheen mantron kaa paath kar rahi thi. usake aavaaz mein ab gussaa na tha, keval ek unmaadamay pravaah tha. usakee sab aatmaa hinsaa-chaah kee agni se prajjvalit ho rahi thi.
andheraa hua to rukmin uth kar ek ora nikal gayee, jaise aankhon se aansoo kee dhaar nikal jati hai. siliyaa khana banaa rahi thi. usane use jate dekh bhee, par kuchh poochhaa nahin. dvaar par payaag baithaa chilam pee raha tha. usane bhee kuchh na kahaa. jab phasal pakane lagatee thi, to dedh-do maheene tak payaag ko haar kee dekhabhaal karanee padatee thi. use kisaanon se donon phasalon par hal peechhe kuchh anaaj bandhaa hua tha. maagh hee mein woh haar ke beech mein thodee-see jameen saaph karake ek madaiyaa daal letaa tha aur raat ko khaa-pee kar aag, chilam aur tamaakhoo-charas liye hue isee madaiyaa mein jaa kar padaa rehta tha. chait ke ant tak usakaa yahee niyam rehta tha. aajakal vahee din the. phasal pakee huee taiyaar khadee thi. do-chaar din mein kataaee shuroo honevaalee thi.
payaag ne das baje raat tak rukmin kee raah dekhee. phir yah samajh kar ki shaayad kisee padosin ke ghar so rahi hogee, usane khaa-pee kar apanee laathee uthaayee aur siliyaa se bol - kivaad band kar le, agar rukmin aaye to khol denaa, aur, manaa-junaa kar thodaa-bahut khilaa denaa. tere peechhe aaj itanaa toophaan ho gaya. mujhe na-jaane itanaa gussaa kaise aa gaya. mainne use kabhee phool kee chhadee se bhee na chhuaa tha. kaheen bood-dhaans na maree ho, to kal aaphat aa jaae.
siliyaa bolee - na jaane woh aayegee ki nahin. main akelee kaise rahoongee. mujhe dar lagataa hai.
'to ghar mein kaun rahegaa? soonaa ghar paa kar koi lotaa-thaalee uthaa le jaae to? dar kis baat kaa hai? phir rukmin to aati hee hogee.
' siliyaa ne andar se tattee band kar lee. payaag haar kee ora chalaa. charas kee tarang mein yah bhajan gaataa jat tha,
thaginee! kya nainaa jhamakaave kaddoo kaat mridang banaave,
neeboo kaat majeeraa; paanch taroee mangal gaaven, naache baalam kheeraa.
roopaa pahir ke roop dikhaave, sonaa pahir rijhaave;
gale daal tulasee kee maalaa, teen lok bharamaave. thaginee. ..
sahasaa sivaane par pahunchate hee usane dekh ki saamane haar mein kisee ne aag jalaayee. ek pal mein ek jvaalaa-see dahak uthee. usane chillaa kar pukaaraa, kaun hai wahan? are, yah kaun aag jalaataa hai? oopar uthatee huee jvaalaaon ne apanee aagney jihnaa se jawab diya. ab payaag ko maaloom hua ki usakee madaiyaa mein aag lagee huee hai. usakee chhaatee dhadakane lagee. is madaiyaa mein aag lagaanaa ruee ke dher mein aag lagaanaa tha. havaa chal rahi thi. madaiyaa ke chaaron ora ek haath hat kar pakee huee phasal kee chaadar-see bichhee huee thi. raat mein bhee unakaa sunaharaa rang jhalak raha tha. aag kee ek lapat, keval ek zara-see chinagaaree saare haar ko bhasm kar degee. saaraa gaanv tabaah ho jaayagaa. isee haar se mile hue doosare gaanv ke bhee haar the. woh bhee jal uthenge. oha! lapaten badhatee jaa rahi hain. ab vilamb karne kaa samay na tha. payaag ne apanaa upalaa aur chilam vaheen patak diya aur kandho par lohaband laathee rakh kar betahaashaa madaiyaa kee taraph daudaa. medon se jaane mein chakkar tha, isalie woh kheton mein se ho kar bhaagaa jaa raha tha. prati pal jvaalaa prachandatar hoti jati thi aur payaag ke paanv aur tejee se uth rahe the. koi tej ghodaa bhee is vakt use paa na sakataa tha. apanee tejee par use svayan hairaanee ho raha tha. jaan padataa tha, paanv bhoomi par padate hee nahin. usakee aankhen madaiyaa par lagee huee theen, daahine-baayen se aur kuchh na soojhataa tha. isee ekaagrataa ne usake pairon mein par lagaa diye the. na dam phoolataa tha, na paanv thakate the. teen-chaar pharalaang usane do minat mein tay kar liye aur madaiyaa ke paas jaa pahunchaa. madaiyaa ke aas-paas koi na tha. kisane yah karm kiya hai, yah sochane kaa maukaa na tha. use khojane kee to baat hee aur thi.
payaag kaa shak rukmin par hua. par yah gussaa kaa samay na tha. jvaalaaen kuchaalee baalakon kee tarah thatthaa maarateen, dhakkam-dhakkaa karateen, kabhee daahinee ora lapakateen aur kabhee baayeen taraph. bas, aisaa maaloom hot tha ki lapat ab khet tak pahunchee, ab pahunchee. maano jvaalaaen aagrahapoorvak kyaariyon kee ora badhateen aur naakaam ho kar doosaree baar phir doone veg se lapakatee theen. aag kaise bujhe! laathee se peet kar bujhaane kaa gaun na tha. woh to niree moorkhataa thi. phir kya ho! phasal jal gayee, to phir woh kisee ko munh na dikhaa sakegaa. aah! gaanv mein koharaam mach jaayagaa. sarvanaash ho jaayagaa. usane zyada nahin sochaa. ganvaaron ko sochanaa nahin aat. payaag ne laathee sanbhaalee, jor se ek chhalaang maar kar aag ke andar madaiyaa ke dvaar par jaa pahunchaa, jalatee huee madaiyaa ko apanee laathee par uthaayaa aur use sir par liye sabase chaudee med par gaanv kee taraph bhaagaa.
aisaa jaan padaa, maano koi agni-yaan havaa mein udataa chalaa jaa raha hai. phoos kee jalatee huee dhaajjiyaan usake oopar gir rahi theen, par use isakaa jnaan tak na hot tha. ek baar ek moothaa alag ho kar usake haath par gir padaa. saaraa haath bhun gaya. par usake paanv pal bhar bhee nahin ruke, haathon mein zara bhee hichak na huee. haathon kaa hilanaa khetee kaa tabaah hona tha. payaag kee ora se ab koi shankaa na thi. agar dar tha to yahee ki madaiyaa kaa woh kendr-bhaag jahaan laathee kaa kundaa daal kar payaag ne use uthaayaa tha, na jal jaae; kyonki chhed ke phailate hee madaiyaa usake oopar aa giregee aur agni-samaadhi mein magn kar degee. payaag yah jaanataa tha aur havaa kee chaal se udaa chalaa jat tha. chaar pharalaang kee daud hai.
mrityu agni kaa roop dhaaran kiye hue payaag ke sar par khel rahi hai aur gaanv kee phasal par. usakee daud mein itanaa veg hai ki jvaalaaon kaa munh peechhe ko phir gaya hai aur usakee daahak shakti kaa adhikaansh vaayu se ladane mein lag raha hai. nahin to ab tak beech mein aag pahunch gayee hoti aur haahaakaar mach gaya hot. ek pharalaang to nikal gaya, payaag kee himmat ne haar nahin maanee. woh doosaraa pharalaang bhee pooraa ho gaya. dekhanaa payaag, do pharalaang kee aur kasar hai. paanv zara bhee sust na hon. jvaalaa laathee ke kunde par pahunchee aur tumhaare jeevan kaa ant hai. marane ke baad bhee tumhe gaaliyaan milengee, tum anant kaal tak aahon kee aag mein jalate rahoge. bas, ek minat aur! ab keval do khet aur raha gaye hain. sarvanaash! laathee kaa kundaa nikal gaya. madaiyaa neeche khisak rahi hai, ab koi ummeed nahin.
payaag jaan chhod kar daud raha hai, woh kinaare kaa khet aa pahunchaa. ab keval do sekend kaa aur maamalaa hai. vijay kaa dvaar saamane bees haath par khadaa svaagat kar raha hai. udhar svarg hai, idhar narak. magar woh madaiyaa khisakatee huee payaag ke sir par aa pahunchee. woh ab bhee use phenk kar apanee jaan bachaa sakataa hai. par use praanon kaa moh nahin. woh us jalatee huee aag ko sir par liye bhaagaa jaa raha hai! wahan usake paanv ladakhadaaye! ab yah kroor agni-leelaa nahin dekhee jati. ekaaek ek aurat saamane ke vriksh ke neeche se daudatee huee payaag ke paas pahunchee. yah rukmin thi. usane turant payaag ke saamane aa kar garadan jhukaayee aur jalatee huee madaiyaa ke neeche pahunch kar use donon haathon par le liya. usee dam payaag moorchchhit ho kar gir padaa. usakaa saaraa munh jhulas gaya tha. rukmin usake alaav ko liye ek sekend mein khet ke dode par aa pahunchee, magar itanee door mein usake haath jal gaye, munh jal gaya aur kapadon mein aag lag gayee. use ab itanee sudhi bhee na thi ki madaiyaa ke baahar nikal aaye. woh madaiyaa ko liye hue gir padee. isake baad kuchh der tak madaiyaa hilatee rahi. rukmin haath-paanv phenkatee rahi, phir agni ne use nigal liya. rukmin ne agni-samaadhi le lee. kuchh der ke baad payaag ko hosh aay. saaree deh jal rahi thi. usane dekh, vriksh ke neeche phoos kee laal aag chamak rahi hai. uth kar daudaa aur pair se aag ko hataa diya, neeche rukmin kee adhaajalee laash padee huee thi. usane baith kar donon haathon se munh dhaanp liya aur rone lagaa. praat: kaal gaanv ke log payaag ko uthaa kar usake ghar le gaye. ek saptaah tak usakaa ilaaj hot raha, par bachaa nahin. kuchh to aag ne jalaayaa tha, jo kuchh kasar thi, woh shokaagni ne pooree kar dee.
