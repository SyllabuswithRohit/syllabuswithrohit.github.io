# Prerna

**Munshi Premchand**

meri kakshaa mein sooryaprakaash se zyada oodhamee koi ladakaa na tha, balki yon kaho ki adhyaapan-kaal ke das varshon mein mujhe aisee visham prakriti ke shishy se saabakaa na padaa tha. kapat-kreedaa mein uski jaan basatee thi. adhyaapakon ko banaane aur chidhaane, udyogee baalakon ko chhedane aur rulaane mein hi use khushi aat tha. aise-aise shadayantr rachta, aise-aise phande daalataa, aise-aise bandhan baandhta ki dekhakar hairaanee hot tha. girohabandee mein abhyast tha. khudaaee phaujadaaron ki ek phauj banaa lee thi aur usake aatank se shaalaa par shaasan kart tha. mukhy adhishthaataa ki hukm tal jaay, magar kya majaal ki koi usake hukm ki avajnaa kar sake. skool ke chaparaasee aur ardalee usse thar-thar kaanpate the. inspektar kaa muaainaa hone vaalaa tha, mukhy adhishthaataa ne hukm diya ki ladake nirdisht samay se aadhaa ghanta pahale aa jaayan. matalab yah tha ki ladakon ko muaaine ke baare mein kuchh jarooree baaten bataa dee jaayan, magar das baj gaye, inspektar sahab aakar baith gaye, aur madarase mein ek ladakaa bhi nahin. gyaarah baje sab chhaatr is tarah nikal pade, jaise koi pinjaraa khol diya gaya ho. inspektar sahab ne kaiphiyat mein likhaa disiplin bahut kharaab hai. prinsipal sahab ki kirakiree hui, teechar badanaam hue aur yah sari sharaarat sooryaprakaash ki thi, magar bahut poochh-taachh karne par bhi kisee ne sooryaprakaash kaa naam tak na liya. mujhe apni sanchaalan-vidhi par garv tha. trening kaalej mein is vishay mein maine khyaati praapt ki thi, magar yahan mera sara sanchaalan-kaushal jaise morchaa khaa gaya tha. kuchh akl hi kaam na karti ki shaitaan ko kaise sanmaarg par laayen.
kaee baar adhyaapakon ki baithak hui, par yah girah na khulee. naee shikshaa-vidhi ke anusaar main dandaneeti kaa pakshapaatee na tha, magar yahan ham is neeti se keval isalie virakt the ki kaheen upachaar se bhi rog asaadhay na ho jaay. sooryaprakaash ko skool se nikaal dene kaa prastaav bhi kiya gaya, par ise apni ayogyataa kaa pramaan samajhakar ham is neeti kaa vyavahaar karne kaa saahas na kar sake. bees-baaees anubhavee aur shikshaashaastr ke aachaary ek baarah-terah saal ke uddand ladakaa kaa sudhaar na kar saken, yah soch bahut hi niraashaajanak tha. yon to sara skool usse traahi-traahi kart tha, magar sabase zyada sankat mein main tha, kyonki vah meri kakshaa kaa chhaatr tha aur uski sharaaraton kaa kuphal mujhe bhoganaa padataa tha. main skool aat, to haradam yahi khatakaa lagaa rehta tha ki dekhen aaj kya vipatti aatee hai. ek din maine apni mej ki daraaj kholee, to usamen se ek bada-saa mendhak nikal padaa. main chaunkakar peechhe hataa to klaas mein ek shor mach gaya. uski ora sarosh netron se dekhakar raha gaya. sara ghanta upadesh mein beet gaya aur vah patthaa sir jhukaaye neeche muskaraa raha tha. mujhe hairaanee hot tha ki yah neeche ki kakshaaon mein kaise paas hua tha.
ek din maine gusse se kahaa, 'tum is kakshaa se umr bhar nahin paas ho sakate.'
sooryaprakaash ne avichalit bhaav se kahaa, 'aap mere paas hone ki phikr na karen. main hameshaa paas hua hoon aur abakee bhi hoongaa.'
'naamumakin!'
'naamumakin mumakin ho jaayagaa!'
main hairaanee se uska munh dekhne lagaa. jaheen se jaheen ladakaa bhi apni saphalataa kaa daavaa itane nirvivaad roop se na kar sakataa tha. maine sochaa, vah sawal-patr udaa letaa hogaa. maine pratijnaa ki, abakee isakee ek chaal bhi na chalne doongaa. dekhoon, kitane din is kakshaa mein padaa rehta hai. aap ghabadaakar nikal jaayagaa.
vaarshik pareekshaa ke avasar par maine asaadhaaran dekhabhaal se kaam liya; magar jab sooryaprakaash kaa jawab-patr dekh, to mere hairaanee ki seemaa na rahi. mere do parche the, donon hi mein usake nambar kakshaa mein sabase zyaadaa the. mujhe khoob maaloom tha ki vah mere kisee parche kaa koi sawal bhi hal nahin kar sakataa. main ise siddh kar sakataa tha; magar usake jawab-patron ko kya kart! lipi mein itanaa bhed na tha jo koi shak utpann kar sakataa. maine prinsipal se kahaa,to vah bhi chakaraa gaye; magar unhen bhi jaan-boojhakar makkhee nigalanee padee. main kadaachit svabhaav hi se niraashaavaadee hoon. any adhyaapakon ko main sooryaprakaash ke vishay mein zara bhi chintit na paataa tha. maano aise ladakon kaa skool mein aan koi naee baat nahin, magar mere lie vah ek vikat rahasy tha. agar yahi dhang rahe, to ek din yaa to jel mein hogaa, yaa paagalakhaane mein.
usi saal mera tabaadalaa ho gaya. yadyapi yahan kaa jalavaayu mere anukool tha, prinsipal aur any adhyaapakon se maitree ho gayi thi, magar main apne tabaadale se khush hua; kyonki sooryaprakaash mere raastaa kaa kaantaa na rahegaa. ladakon ne mujhe bidaaee ki daavat dee aur sab-ke-sab steshan tak pahunchaane aaye. us vakt sabhi ladake aankhon mein aansoo bhare hue the. main bhi apne aansuon ko na rok sakaa. sahasaa meri nigaah sooryaprakaash par padee, jo sabase peechhe lajjit khadaa tha. mujhe aisaa maaloom hua ki uski aankhen bhi bheegee theen. mera jee baar-baarachaahta tha ki chalte-chalte usse do-chaar baaten kar loon. shaayad vah bhi mujhse kuchh kehna chahta tha, magar na maine pahale baaten keen, na usane; haalaanki mujhe bahut dinon tak isakaa khed raha. uski jhijhak to kshamaa ke yogy thi; par mera avarodh akshamy tha. mumakin tha, us karunaa aur pachhataavaa ki dashaa mein meri do-chaar nishkapat baaten usake dil par asar kar jaateen; magar inheen khoye
hue avasaron kaa naam to jeevan hai. gaadee mandagati se chalee. ladake kaee kadam tak usake saath daude. main khidakee ke baahar sir nikaale khadaa tha. kuchh der mujhe unake hilate hue roomaal najar aaye. phir ve rekhaaen aakaash mein vileen ho gaeen: magar ek alpakaay moorti ab bhi pletaphaarm par khadee thi. maine anumaan kiya, vah sooryaprakaash hai. us samay mera hraday kisee vikal kaidee ki tarah napharat, maaliny aur udaaseenataa ke bandhanon ko tod-todakar usake gale milne ke lie tadap uthaa.
naye sthaan ki naee chintaaon ne bahut jald mujhe apni ora aakarshit kar liya. pichhale dinon ki yaad ek hasarat banakar raha gayi. na kisee kaa koi khat aay, na maine koi khat likhaa. shaayad duniyaa kaa yahi dastoor hai. varshaa ke baad varshaa ki hariyaalee kitane dinon rehti hai. sanyog se mujhe ingalaind mein vidyaabhyaas karne kaa avasar mil gaya. wahan teen saal lag gaye. wahan se lautaa to ek kaalej kaa prinsipal banaa diya gaya. yah sidhdi mere lie bilakul aashaateet thi. meri bhaavanaa svapn mein bhi itanee door na udee thi; lekin padalipsaa ab kisee aur bhi oonchee daalee par aashray lena chahti thi.
shikshaamantree se rabt-jabt paidaa kiya. mantree mahoday mujh par kripaa rakhte the; magar vaastav mein shikshaa ke maulik sidhdaanton kaa unhen jnaan na tha. mujhe paakar unhone sara bhaar mere oopar daal diya. ghode par vah savaar the, lagaam mere haath mein thi. phal yah hua ki unake raajanaitik vipakshiyon se mera virodh ho gaya. mujh par jaa-bejaa aakraman hone lage. main siddhaant roop se anivaary shikshaa kaa virodhee hoon. mera soch hai ki har ek manushy ki una vishayon mein zyada svaadheenataa honee chaahie, jinakaa usse nij kaa sanbandh hai. mera soch hai ki yoorop mein anivaary shikshaa ki jaroorat hai, bhaarat mein nahin. bhautikataa pashchimee sabhyataa kaa mool tattv hai. wahan kisee kaam ki preranaa, aarthik phaayadaa ke aadhaar par hoti hai. zindagi ki jaroorat zyada hai; isalie jeevan-sangraam bhi zyaadaa bheeshan hai. maan-pitaa bhog ke daas hokar bachchon ko jald-se-jald kuchh kamaane par majaboor karte hain. isakee jagah ki vah mad kaa tyaag karake ek shiling roj ki bachat kar len, ve apne kamasin bachche ko ek shiling ki majadooree karne ke lie dabaayenge. bhaarateey jeevan mein saatvik saralataa hai. ham us vakt tak apne bachchon se majadooree nahin karaate, jab tak ki paristhiti hamen vivash na kar de. daridr se daridr hindustaanee majadoor bhi shikshaa ke upakaaron kaa kaayal hai. usake man mein yah chaah hoti hai ki mera bachchaa chaar akshar padh jaay. isalie nahin ki use koi adhikar milegaa; balki keval isalie ki padhai maanavee sheel kaa ek shrringaar hai. agar yah jaanakar bhi vah apne bachche ko madarase nahin bhejataa, to samajh lena chaahie ki vah majaboor hai. aisee dashaa mein us par kanoon kaa prahaar karn meri nazar mein nyaay-sangat nahin hai. isake sivaay mere soch mein abhi hamaare desh mein yogy shikshakon kaa abhaav hai. ardhd-shikshit aur alpavetan paanevaale adhyaapakon se aap yah ummeed nahin rakh sakate ki vah koi oonchaa aadarsh apne saamane rakh saken. zyaadaa-se-zyaadaa itanaa hi hogaa ki chaar-paanch varsh mein ladakaa ko akshar-jnaan ho jaayagaa. main ise parvat khodakar chuhiyaa nikaalane ke tuly samajhta hoon. vayas praapt ho jaane par yah masalaa ek mahine mein aasaanee se tay kiya jaa sakataa hai.
main anubhav se kah sakataa hoon ki yuvaavasthaa mein ham jitanaa jnaan ek mahine mein praapt kar sakate hain, utanaa baalyaavasthaa mein teen saal mein bhi nahin kar sakate, phir khaamakhvaah bachchon ko madarase mein kaid karne se kya phaayadaa? madarase ke baahar rahakar use svachchh vaayu to milatee, praakritik anubhav to hote. paathashaalaa mein band karake to aap usake maanasik aur shaareerik donon vidhaanon ki jad kaat dete hain. isalie jab praanteey vyavasthaapak-sabhaa mein anivaary shikshaa kaa prastaav pesh hua, to meri preranaa se ministar sahab ne uska virodh kiya. nateejaa yah hua ki prastaav asveekrit ho gaya. phir kya tha. ministar sahab ki aur meri vah le-de shuru hui ki kuchh na poochhie. vyaktagit aakshep kie jaane lage. main gareeb ki beevee tha, mujhe hi sabakee bhaabhee bananaa padaa. mujhe deshadrohee, unnati kaa shatru aur naukarashaahee kaa gulaam kahaa, gaya. mere kaalej mein zara-see bhi koi baat hoti to kaunsil mein mujh par varshaa hone lagti. maine ek chaparaasee ko prithak kiya. sari kaunsil panje jhaadakar mere peechhe pad gayi. aakhir ministar sahab ko majaboor hokar us chaparaasee ko bahaal karn padaa. yah beizzatee mere lie asahy tha. shaayad koi bhi ise sahan na kar sakataa. ministar sahab se mujhe shikaayat nahin. vah majaboor the. haan, is vaataavaran mein kaam karn mere lie dussaadhay ho gaya. mujhe apne kaalej ke aantarik sangathan kaa bhi adhikar nahin. amuk kyon nahin pareekshaa mein bhejaa gaya, amuk ke badale amuk ko kyon nahin chhaatravritti dee gayi, amuk teechar ko amuk kakshaa kyon nahin dee jati, is tarah ke saaraheen aakshepon ne meri naak mein dam kar diya tha. is naee chot ne kamar tod dee. maine isteephaa de diya.
mujhe ministar sahab se itanee ummeed avashy thi ki vah kam-se-kam is vishay mein nyaay-paraayanataa se kaam lenge; magar unhone nyaay ki jagah neeti ko maany samajhaa aur mujhe kaee saal ki bhakt ikaa yah phal milaa ki main padachyut kar diya gaya. sansaar kaa aisaa katu anubhav mujhe ab tak na hua tha. grah bhi kuchh bure aa gaye the, unheen dinon patnee kaa dehaant ho gaya. antim darshan bhi na kar sakaa. shaam-samay nadee-tat par sair karne gaya tha. vah kuchh asvasth theen. lautaa, to unki laash milee. kadaachit hraday ki gati band ho gayi thi. is aaghaat ne kamar tod dee. maan ke prasaad aur aasheervaad se bade-bade mahaan aadamee kritaarth ho gaye hain. main jo kuchh hua, patnee ke prasaad aur aasheervaad se hua; vah mere bhaagy ki vidhaatree theen. kitanaa alaukik tyaag tha, kitanaa vishaal dhairy. unake maadhury mein teekshnataa kaa naam bhi na tha. mujhe yaad nahin aat ki maine kabhi unki bhrikutee sankuchit dekhee ho, vah niraash hona to jaanatee hi na theen. main kaee baar sakht beemaar padaa hoon. vaidy niraash ho gaye hain, par vah apne dhairy aur shaanti se anu-maatr bhi vichalit nahin hueen. unhen yakeen tha ki main apne pati ke jeevan-kaal mein maroongee aur wahi hua bhi. main jeevan mein ab tak unheen ke sahaare khadaa tha! jab vah avalamb hi na raha, to jeevan kahan rehta. khaane aur sone kaa naam jeevan nahin hai. jeevan naam hai, hameshaa aage badhte rehne ki lagan kaa. yah lagan gaayab ho gayi. main sansaar se virakt ho gaya. aur ekaantavaas mein
jeevan ke din vyateet karne kaa phaisalaa karake ek chhote-se gaanv mein jaa basaa. chaaron taraph oonche-oonche teele the, ek ora gangaa bahti thi. maine nadee ke kinaare ek chhota-saa ghar banaa liya aur usi mein rehne lagaa. magar kaam karn to maanavee svabhaav hai. bekaaree mein jeevan kaise katataa. maine ek chhoti-see paathashaalaa khol lee; ek vriksh ki chhaanh mein gaanv ke ladakon ko jamaa kar kuchh padhaayaa kart tha. uski yahan itanee khyaati hui ki aas-paas ke gaanv ke chhaatr bhi aane lage. ek din main apni kakshaa ko padha raha tha ki paathashaalaa ke paas ek motar aakar rukee aur usamen se us jile ke diptee kamishnar utar pade. main us samay keval ek kurtaa aur dhotee pahane hue tha. is vesh mein ek haakim se milate hue sharm aa rahi thi. diptee kamishnar mere paas aaye to maine jhenpate hue haath badhaayaa, magar vah mujhse haath milaane ke badale mere pairon ki ora jhuke aur una par sir rakh diya. main kuchh aisaa sitapitaa gaya ki mere munh se ek shabd bhi na nikalaa. main angrezi achchhi likhta hoon, darshanashaastr kaa bhi aachaary hoon, vyaakhyaan bhi achchhe de letaa hoon. magar ina gunon mein ek bhi shrriddhaa ke yogy nahin. shrriddhaa to jnaaniyon aur saadhuon hi ke adhikar ki vastu hai. agar main braahman hot, to ek baat thi. haalaanki ek siviliyan kaa kisee braahman ke pairon par sir rakhna achintaneey hai. main abhi isi hairaanee mein padaa hua tha ki diptee kamishnar ne sir uthaayaa aur meri taraph dekhakar kahaa, aapane shaayad mujhe pahachaanaa nahin.
itanaa sunate hi mere smriti-aankh khul gaye, bol, 'aapakaa naam sooryaprakaash to nahin hai?'
'jee haan, main aapakaa wahi abhaagaa shishy hoon.'
'baarah-terah varsh ho gaye.'
sooryaprakaash ne muskaraakar kahaa, 'teechar ladakon ko bhool jaate hain, par ladake unhen hameshaa yaad rakhte hain.
maine usi vinod ke bhaav se kahaa, 'tum jaise ladakon ko bhoolanaa naamumakin hai.'
sooryaprakaash ne vineet aavaaz mein kahaa, 'unheen aparaadhon ko kshamaa karaane ke lie sevaa mein aay hoon. main hameshaa aapakee khabar letaa rehta tha. jab aap ingalaind gaye, to maine aapake lie badhaaee kaa patr likhaa; par use bhej na sakaa. jab aap prinsipal hue, main ingalaind jaane ko taiyaar tha. wahan main patrikaaon mein aapake lekh padhta rehta tha. jab lautaa, to maaloom hua ki aapane isteephaa de diya aur kaheen dehaat mein chale gaye hain. is jile mein aaye hue mujhe ek varsh se zyaadaa hua; par isakaa zara bhi anumaan na tha ki aap yahan ekaant-sevan kar rahe hain. is ujaad gaanv mein aapakaa jee kaise lagta hai? itanee hi avasthaa mein aapane vaanaprasth le liya?'
main nahin kah sakataa ki sooryaprakaash ki unnati dekhakar mujhe kitanaa aashcharyamay khushi hua. agar vah mera betaa hot to bhi isase zyaadaa khushi na hot. main use apne jhonpade mein laayaa aur apni raamakahaa,nee kah sunaaee. sooryaprakaash ne kahaa, 'to yah kahie ki aap apne hi ek bhai ke vishvaasaghaat ke shikaar hue. mera anubhav to abhi bahut kam hai; magar itane hi dinon mein mujhe maaloom ho gaya hai, ki ham log abhi apni jimmedaariyon ko pooraa karn nahin jaanate. ministar sahab se bhent hui to poochhoongaa, ki yahi aapakaa dharm tha.'
maine jawab diya-- 'bhai, unakaa dosh nahin. mumakin hai, is dashaa mein main bhi wahi kart, jo unhone kiya. mujhe apni svaarthalipsaa ki sajaa mil gayi, aur usake lie main unakaa rinee hoon. banaavat nahin, saty keht hoon ki yahan mujhe jo shaanti hai, vah aur kaheen na thi. is ekaant-jeevan mein mujhe jeevan ke tattvon kaa vah jnaan hua, jo sanpatti aur adhikar ki daud mein kisee tarah mumakin na tha. itihaas aur bhoogol ke pothe chaatakar aur yoorap ke vidyaalayon ki sharan jaakar bhi main apni mamataa ko na mitaa sakaa; balki yah rog din-din aur bhi asaadhay hot jat tha. aap seedhiyon par paanv rakhe bagair chhat ki oonchaaee tak nahin pahunch sakate. sampatti ki attaalikaa tak pahunchane mein doosaron ki zindagi hi zeenon kaa kaam detee hai. aap kuchalakar hi lakshy tak pahunch sakate hain. wahan saujany aur sahaanubhooti kaa sthaan hi nahin. mujhe aisaa maaloom hot hai ki us vakt main hinstr jantuon se ghiraa hua tha aur meri sari shaktiyaan apni aatmarakshaa mein hi lagee rehti theen. yahan main apne chaaron ora santosh aur saralataa dekhta hoon. mere paas jo log aate hain, koi svaarth lekar nahin aate aur na meri sevaaon mein prashansaa yaa gaurav ki laalasaa hai. yah kahakar maine sooryaprakaash ke chehare ki ora gaur se dekh. kapat musakaan ki jagah pachhataavaa kaa rang tha. shaayad yah dikhaane aay tha ki aap jisakee taraph se itane niraash ho gaye the, vah ab is pad ko sushobhit kar raha hai. vah mujhse apne sadudyog kaa bakhaan karaanaa chahta tha. mujhe ab apni bhool maaloom hui. ek sanpann aadamee ke saamane samridhdi ki nindaa theek nahin. maine turant baat palatakar kahaa, magar tum apna haal to kaho. tumhari yah kaayaa-palat kaise hui? tumhari sharaaraton ko yaad kart hoon to ab bhi roen khade ho jaate hain. kisee devataa ke varadaan ke sivaa aur to kaheen yah vibhooti na praapt ho sakatee thi.'
sooryaprakaash ne musakaraakar kahaa, 'aapakaa aasheervaad tha.' mere bahut aagrah karne par sooryaprakaash ne apna vrittaant sunaanaa shuru kiya aapake chale aane ke kaee din baad mera mameraa bhai skool mein daakhil hua. uski umr aath-nau saal se zyada na thi. prinsipal sahab use hostal mein na lete the aur na maamaa sahab usake thaharane kaa intazaam kar sakate the. unhen is sankat mein dekhakar maine prinsipal sahab se kahaa, use mere kamare mein thaharaa deejiye. prinsipal sahab ne ise niyam-viruddh batalaayaa. is par maine bigadakar usi din hostal chhod diya aur ek kiraaye kaa makaan lekar mohan ke saath rehne lagaa. uski maan kaee saal pahale mar chukee thi. itanaa dubalaa-patalaa, kamajor aur gareeb ladakaa tha ki pahale hi din se mujhe us par dayaa aane lagee. kabhi usake sir mein dard hot, kabhi jvar ho aat.
aaye din koi-na-koi beemaaree khadee rehti thi. idhar saanjh hui aur jhapakiyaan aane lageen. badi mushkil se khaanaa karne uthta. din chadhe tak soyaa kart aur jab tak main god mein uthaakar bithaa na detaa, uthane kaa naam na letaa. raat ko bahudhaa chaunkakar meri chaarapaaee par aa jat. mere gale se lipatakar sotaa. mujhe us par kabhi gussaa na aat. kah nahin sakataa, kyon mujhe usse pyaar ho gaya. main jahaan pahale nau baje sokar uthta tha, ab tadake uth baithta aur usake lie doodh garam kart. phir use uthaakar haath-munh dhulaataa aur naashtaa karaataa. usake svaasthy ke soch se nity vaayu sevan ko le jat. main jo kabhi kitaab lekar na baithta tha, use ghanton padhaayaa kart. mujhe apne daayitv kaa itanaa jnaan kaise ho gaya, isakaa mujhe hairaanee hai. use koi shikaayat ho jati to mere praan nakhon mein samaa jaate. daaktar ke paas daudataa, davaaen laataa aur mohan ki khushaamad karake davaa pilaataa. hameshaa yah phikr lagee rehti thi, ki koi baat uski ichchhaa ke viruddh na ho jaay. is bechaare kaa yahan mere sivaa doosaraa kaun hai. mere chanchal mitron mein se koi use chidhaataa yaa chhedataa to meri tyoriyaan badal jati theen! kaee ladake to mujhe boodhee daaee kahakar chidhaate the; par main hansakar taal detaa tha. main usake saamane ek galat shabd bhi munh se na nikaalataa. yah shankaa hoti thi, ki kaheen meri dekh-dekhee yah bhi kharaab na ho jaay. main usake saamane is tarah rehna chahta tha, ki vah mujhe apna aadarsh samajhe aur isake lie yah maanee hui baat thi ki main apna charitr sudhaaroon. vah mera nau baje sokar uthanaa, baarah baje tak mataragashtee karn, naee-naee sharaaraton ke mansoobe baandhanaa aur adhyaapakon ki aankh bachaakar skool se uda jan, sab aap-hi-aap jat raha. svaasthy aur charitr paalan ke sidhdaanton kaa main shatru tha; par ab mujhse badhakar una niyamon kaa rakshak doosaraa na tha. main eeshvar kaa upahaas kiya kart, magar ab pakkaa aastik ho gaya tha. vah bade aasaan bhaav se poochhta, paramaatmaa sab jagah rehte hain, to mere paas bhi rehte honge. is sawal kaa majaak udaanaa mere lie naamumakin tha. main keht haan paramaatmaa tumhaare, hamaare sabake paas rehte hain aur hamaaree rakshaa karte hain. yah aashvaasan paakar uska cheharaa khushi se khil uthta tha, kadaachit vah paramaatmaa ki sattaa kaa anubhav karne lagta tha. saal hi bhar mein mohan kuchh se kuchh ho gaya. maamaa sahab dobaaraa aaye, to use dekhakar chakit ho gaye. aankhon mein aansoo bharakar bole 'betaa! tumne isako jilaa liya, nahin to main niraash ho chukaa tha. isakaa puneet phal tumhen eeshvar denge. isakee maan svarg mein baithee hui tumhen aasheervaad de rahi hai.' sooryaprakaash ki aankhen us vakt bhi sajal ho gayi theen.
maine poochhaa, 'mohan bhi tumhen bahut pyaar kart hogaa?'
sooryaprakaash ke sajal netron mein hasarat se bharaa hua khushi chamak uthaa,'bol, vah mujhe ek minat ke lie bhi na chhodataa tha. mere saath baithta, mere saath khaataa, mere saath sotaa. main hi uska sabakuchh tha. aah! vah sansaar mein nahin hai. magar mere lie vah ab bhi usi tarah jeetaa-jaagataa hai. main jo kuchh hoon, usi kaa banaayaa hua hoon. agar vah daivee vidhaan ki tarah mera raastaa-pradarshak na ban jat, to shaayad aaj main kisee jel mein padaa hot. ek din maine kah diya tha agar tum roj nahaa na liya karoge to main tumse na boloongaa. nahaane se vah na jaane kyon jee churaataa tha. meri is dhamakee kaa phal yah hua ki vah nity praat:kaal nahaane lagaa. kitanee hi sardee kyon na ho, kitanee hi thandee havaa chale; lekin vah snaan avashy kart tha. dekhta rehta tha, main kis baat se khush hot hoon. ek din main kaee mitron ke saath thiyetar dekhne chalaa gaya, taakeed kar gaya tha ki tum khaanaa khaakar so rehna.
teen baje raat ko lautaa, to dekh ki vah baithaa hua hai! maine poochhaa, 'tum soye nahin? bol, neend nahin aaee.'
us din se maine thiyetar jaane kaa naam na liya. bachchon mein pyaar ki jo ek bhookh hoti hai doodh, mithaaee aur khilaunon se bhi zyada maadak zo maan ki god ke saamane sansaar ki nidhi ki bhi paravaah nahin karti, mohan ki vah bhookh kabhi santusht na hoti thi. pahaadon se takaraane vaalee saaras ki aavaaj ki tarah vah hameshaa uski nason mein goonjaa karti thi. jaise bhoomi par phailee hui lataa koi sahaaraa paate hi usse chipat jati hai, wahi haal mohan kaa tha. vah mujhse aisaa chipat gaya tha ki prithak kiya jat, to uski komal bel ke tukade-tukade ho jaate. vah mere saath teen saal raha aur tab mere jeevan mein prakaash ki ek rekhaa daalakar andhakaar mein vileen ho gaya. us jeern kaayaa mein kaise-kaise aramaan bhare hue the. kadaachit eeshvar ne mere jeevan mein ek avalamb ki srishti karne ke lie use bhejaa tha. makasad pooraa ho gaya to vah kyon rehta.
'garmiyon ki taateel thi. do taateelon mein mohan mere hi saath raha tha. maamaajee ke aagrah karne par bhi ghar na aay. abakee kaalej ke chhaatron ne kaashmeer-yaatraa karne kaa phaisalaa kiya aur mujhe uska adhyaksh banaayaa. kaashmeer yaatraa ki chaah mujhe chirakaal se thi. isi avasar ko ganeemat samajhaa. mohan ko maamaajee ke paas bhejakar main kaashmeer chalaa gaya. do mahine ke baad lautaa, to maaloom hua mohan beemaar hai. kaashmeer mein mujhe baar-baar mohan ki yaad aatee thi aur jee chahta tha, laut jaaoon. mujhe us par itanaa pyaar hai, isakaa andaaj mujhe kaashmeer jaakar hua; lekin mitron ne peechhaa na chhodaa. uski beemaaree ki khabar paate hi main adheer ho uthaa aur doosare hi din usake paas jaa pahunchaa. mujhe dekhte hi usake peele aur sookhe hue chehare par khushi ki sphoorti jhalak padee. main daudakar usake gale se lipat gaya. uski aankhon mein vah dooradrishti aur chehare par vah alaukik aabhaa thi jo mandaraatee hui mrityu ki khabar detee hai. maine aavesh se kaanpate hue aavaaz mein poochhaa, yah tumhari kya dashaa hai mohan? do hi mahine mein yah naubat pahunch gayi? mohan ne aasaan muskaan ke saath kahaa, 'aap kaashmeer ki sair karne gaye the, main aakaash ki sair karne jaa raha hoon.'
'magar yah du:kha ki kahaanee kahakar main ronaa aur rulaanaa nahin chahta. mere chale jaane ke baad mohan itane parishram se padhne lagaa maano tapasyaa kar raha ho. use yah dhun savaar ho gayi thi ki saal bhar ki padhaaee do mahine mein khatm kar le aur skool khulane ke baad mujhse is shram kaa prashansaaroopee upahaar praapt kare. main kis tarah uski peeth thokoongaa, shaabaashee doongaa, apne mitron se bakhaan karoongaa, ina bhaavanaaon ne apne saare baalochit utsaah aur talleenataa se use vasheebhoot kar liya. maamaajee ko daphtar ke kaamon se itanaa phursat kahan ki usake manoranjan kaa dhyaan rakhen. shaayad use pratidin kuchh-na-kuchh padhte dekhakar vah dil mein khush hote the. use khelate dekhakar vah zaroor daotate. padhte dekhakar bhalaa kya kehte. phal yah hua ki mohan ko halkaa-halkaa jvar aane lagaa, lekin us dashaa mein bhi usane padhna na chhodaa. kuchh aur vyatikram bhi hue, jvar kaa prakop aur bhi badhaa; par us dashaa mein bhi jvar kuchh halkaa ho jat, to kitaaben dekhne lagta tha. usake praan mujhamen hi bane rehte the. jvar ki dashaa mein bhi naukaron se poochhta bhaiyaa kaa patr aay? vah kab aayenge? isake sivaa aur koi doosaree chaah na thi. agar mujhe maaloom hot ki meri kaashmeer-yaatraa itanee mahangee padegee, to udhar jaane kaa naam bhi na letaa. use bachaane ke lie mujhse jo kuchh ho sakataa tha, vah maine sab kiya; lekin bukhaar taaiphaayad tha, uski jaan lekar hi utaraa. usake jeevan ke svapn mere lie kisee rishi ke aasheervaad banakar mujhe protsaahit karne lage aur yah usi kaa shubh phal hai ki aaj aap mujhe is dashaa mein dekh rahe hain. mohan ki baal abhilaashaaon ko pratyaksh roop mein laakar yah mujhe santosh hot hai ki shaayad uski pavitr aatmaa mujhe dekhakar khush hoti ho. yahi preranaa thi ki jisane mushkil se mushkil pareekshaaon mein bhi mera bedaa paar lagaayaa; nahin to main aaj bhi wahi mandabuddhi sooryaprakaash hoon, jisakee soorat se aap chidhte the.'
us din se main kaee baar sooryaprakaash se mil chukaa hoon. vah jab is taraph aa jat hai, to binaa mujhse mile nahin jat. mohan ko ab bhi vah apna ishtadev samajhta hai. maanav-prakriti kaa yah ek aisaa rahasy hai, jise main aaj tak nahin samajh sakaa.
