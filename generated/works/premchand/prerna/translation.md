# Prerna

**Munshi Premchand**

meree kakshaa mein sooryaprakaash se jyaadaa oodhamee koee ladakaa na tha, balki yon kaho ki adhyaapan-kaal ke das varshon mein mujhe aisee visham prakriti ke shishy se saabakaa na padaa tha| kapat-kreedaa mein usakee jaan basatee thi| adhyaapakon ko banaane aur chidhaane, udyogee baalakon ko chhedane aur rulaane mein hee use khushee aat tha| aise-aise shadayantr rachataa, aise-aise phande daalataa, aise-aise bandhan baandhataa ki dekhakar hairaanee hot tha| girohabandee mein abhyast tha| khudaaee phaujadaaron kee ek phauj banaa lee thi aur usake aatank se shaalaa par shaasan kart tha| mukhy adhishthaataa kee hukm tal jaay, magar kyaa majaal ki koee usake hukm kee avajnaa kar sake| skool ke chaparaasee aur ardalee usse thar-thar kaanpate the| inspektar kaa muaainaa hone vaalaa tha, mukhy adhishthaataa ne hukm diya ki ladake nirdisht samay se aadhaa ghantaa pahale aa jaayan| matalab yah tha ki ladakon ko muaaine ke baare mein kuchh jarooree baaten bataa dee jaayan, magar das baj gaye, inspektar sahab aakar baith gaye, aur madarase mein ek ladakaa bhee nahin| gyaarah baje sab chhaatr is tarah nikal pade, jaise koee pinjaraa khol diya gaya ho| inspektar sahab ne kaiphiyat mein likhaa disiplin bahut kharaab hai| prinsipal sahab kee kirakiree huee, teechar badanaam hue aur yah saaree sharaarat sooryaprakaash kee thi, magar bahut poochh-taachh karne par bhee kisee ne sooryaprakaash kaa naam tak na liya| mujhe apanee sanchaalan-vidhi par garv tha| trening kaalej mein is vishay mein mainne khyaati praapt kee thi, magar yahan meraa saaraa sanchaalan-kaushal jaise morchaa khaa gaya tha| kuchh akl hee kaam na karti ki shaitaan ko kaise sanmaarg par laayen|
kaee baar adhyaapakon kee baithak huee, par yah girah na khulee| naee shikshaa-vidhi ke anusaar main dandaneeti kaa pakshapaatee na tha, magar yahan ham is neeti se keval isalie virakt the ki kaheen upachaar se bhee rog asaadhay na ho jaay| sooryaprakaash ko skool se nikaal dene kaa prastaav bhee kiya gaya, par ise apanee ayogyataa kaa pramaan samajhakar ham is neeti kaa vyavahaar karne kaa saahas na kar sake| bees-baaees anubhavee aur shikshaashaastr ke aachaary ek baarah-terah saal ke uddand ladakaa kaa sudhaar na kar saken, yah soch bahut hee niraashaajanak tha| yon to saaraa skool usse traahi-traahi kart tha, magar sabase jyaadaa sankat mein main tha, kyonki vah meree kakshaa kaa chhaatr tha aur usakee sharaaraton kaa kuphal mujhe bhoganaa padataa tha| main skool aat, to haradam yahee khatakaa lagaa rahataa tha ki dekhen aaj kyaa vipatti aatee hai| ek din mainne apanee mej kee daraaj kholee, to usamen se ek badaa-saa mendhak nikal padaa| main chaunkakar peechhe hataa to klaas mein ek shor mach gaya| usakee ora sarosh netron se dekhakar raha gaya| saaraa ghantaa upadesh mein beet gaya aur vah patthaa sir jhukaaye neeche muskaraa raha tha| mujhe hairaanee hot tha ki yah neeche kee kakshaaon mein kaise paas hua tha|
ek din mainne gusse se kahaa, 'tum is kakshaa se umr bhar nahin paas ho sakate|'
sooryaprakaash ne avichalit bhaav se kahaa, 'aap mere paas hone kee phikr na karen| main hameshaa paas hua hoon aur abakee bhee hoongaa|'
'naamumakin !'
'naamumakin mumakin ho jaayagaa !'
main hairaanee se usakaa munh dekhane lagaa| jaheen se jaheen ladakaa bhee apanee saphalataa kaa daavaa itane nirvivaad roop se na kar sakataa tha| mainne sochaa, vah savaal-patr udaa letaa hogaa| mainne pratijnaa kee, abakee isakee ek chaal bhee na chalne doongaa| dekhoon, kitane din is kakshaa mein padaa rahataa hai| aap ghabadaakar nikal jaayagaa|
vaarshik pareekshaa ke avasar par mainne asaadhaaran dekhabhaal se kaam liya; magar jab sooryaprakaash kaa javaab-patr dekh, to mere hairaanee kee seemaa na rahi| mere do parche the, donon hee mein usake nambar kakshaa mein sabase zyaadaa the| mujhe khoob maaloom tha ki vah mere kisee parche kaa koee savaal bhee hal nahin kar sakataa| main ise siddh kar sakataa tha; magar usake javaab-patron ko kyaa kart ! lipi mein itanaa bhed na tha jo koee shak utpann kar sakataa| mainne prinsipal se kahaa,to vah bhee chakaraa gaye; magar unhen bhee jaan-boojhakar makkhee nigalanee padee| main kadaachit svabhaav hee se niraashaavaadee hoon| any adhyaapakon ko main sooryaprakaash ke vishay mein jaraa bhee chintit na paataa tha| maano aise ladakon kaa skool mein aan koee naee baat nahin, magar mere lie vah ek vikat rahasy tha| agar yahee dhang rahe, to ek din yaa to jel mein hogaa, yaa paagalakhaane mein|
usee saal meraa tabaadalaa ho gaya| yadyapi yahan kaa jalavaayu mere anukool tha, prinsipal aur any adhyaapakon se maitree ho gaee thi, magar main apne tabaadale se khush hua; kyonki sooryaprakaash mere raastaa kaa kaantaa na rahegaa| ladakon ne mujhe bidaaee kee daavat dee aur sab-ke-sab steshan tak pahunchaane aaye| us vakt sabhee ladake aankhon mein aansoo bhare hue the| main bhee apne aansuon ko na rok sakaa| sahasaa meree nigaah sooryaprakaash par padee, jo sabase peechhe lajjit khadaa tha| mujhe aisaa maaloom hua ki usakee aankhen bhee bheegee theen| meraa jee baar-baarachaahataa tha ki chalate-chalate usse do-chaar baaten kar loon| shaayad vah bhee mujhse kuchh kahanaa chaahataa tha, magar na mainne pahale baaten keen, na usane; haalaanki mujhe bahut dinon tak isakaa khed raha| usakee jhijhak to kshamaa ke yogy thi; par meraa avarodh akshamy tha| mumakin tha, us karunaa aur pachhataavaa kee dashaa mein meree do-chaar nishkapat baaten usake dil par asar kar jaateen; magar inheen khoye
hue avasaron kaa naam to jeevan hai| gaadee mandagati se chalee| ladake kaee kadam tak usake saath daude| main khidakee ke baahar sir nikaale khadaa tha| kuchh der mujhe unake hilate hue roomaal najar aaye| phir ve rekhaaen aakaash mein vileen ho gaeen : magar ek alpakaay moorti ab bhee pletaphaarm par khadee thi| mainne anumaan kiya, vah sooryaprakaash hai| us samay meraa hraday kisee vikal kaidee kee tarah napharat, maaliny aur udaaseenataa ke bandhanon ko tod-todakar usake gale milane ke lie tadap uthaa|
naye sthaan kee naee chintaaon ne bahut jald mujhe apanee ora aakarshit kar liya| pichhale dinon kee yaad ek hasarat banakar raha gaee| na kisee kaa koee khat aay, na mainne koee khat likhaa| shaayad duniyaa kaa yahee dastoor hai| varshaa ke baad varshaa kee hariyaalee kitane dinon rahatee hai| sanyog se mujhe ingalaind mein vidyaabhyaas karne kaa avasar mil gaya| wahan teen saal lag gaye| wahan se lautaa to ek kaalej kaa prinsipal banaa diya gaya| yah sidhdi mere lie bilakul aashaateet thi| meree bhaavanaa svapn mein bhee itanee door na udee thi; lekin padalipsaa ab kisee aur bhee oonchee daalee par aashray lenaa chaahatee thi|
shikshaamantree se rabt-jabt paidaa kiya| mantree mahoday mujh par kripaa rakhate the; magar vaastav mein shikshaa ke maulik sidhdaanton kaa unhen jnaan na tha| mujhe paakar unhonne saaraa bhaar mere oopar daal diya| ghode par vah savaar the, lagaam mere haath mein thi| phal yah hua ki unake raajanaitik vipakshiyon se meraa virodh ho gaya| mujh par jaa-bejaa aakraman hone lage| main siddhaant roop se anivaary shikshaa kaa virodhee hoon| meraa soch hai ki har ek manushy kee una vishayon mein jyaadaa svaadheenataa honee chaahie, jinakaa usse nij kaa sanbandh hai| meraa soch hai ki yoorop mein anivaary shikshaa kee jaroorat hai, bhaarat mein nahin| bhautikataa pashchimee sabhyataa kaa mool tattv hai| wahan kisee kaam kee preranaa, aarthik phaayadaa ke aadhaar par hoti hai| jindagee kee jaroorat jyaadaa hai; isalie jeevan-sangraam bhee zyaadaa bheeshan hai| maan-pitaa bhog ke daas hokar bachchon ko jald-se-jald kuchh kamaane par majaboor karte hain| isakee jagah ki vah mad kaa tyaag karake ek shiling roj kee bachat kar len, ve apne kamasin bachche ko ek shiling kee majadooree karne ke lie dabaayenge| bhaarateey jeevan mein saatvik saralataa hai| ham us vakt tak apne bachchon se majadooree nahin karaate, jab tak ki paristhiti hamen vivash na kar de| daridr se daridr hindustaanee majadoor bhee shikshaa ke upakaaron kaa kaayal hai| usake man mein yah chaah hoti hai ki meraa bachchaa chaar akshar padh jaay| isalie nahin ki use koee adhikaar milegaa; balki keval isalie ki padhai maanavee sheel kaa ek shrringaar hai| agar yah jaanakar bhee vah apne bachche ko madarase nahin bhejataa, to samajh lenaa chaahie ki vah majaboor hai| aisee dashaa mein us par kaanoon kaa prahaar karn meree nazar mein nyaay-sangat nahin hai| isake sivaay mere soch mein abhee hamaare desh mein yogy shikshakon kaa abhaav hai| ardhd-shikshit aur alpavetan paanevaale adhyaapakon se aap yah ummeed nahin rakh sakate ki vah koee oonchaa aadarsh apne saamane rakh saken| zyaadaa-se-zyaadaa itanaa hee hogaa ki chaar-paanch varsh mein ladakaa ko akshar-jnaan ho jaayagaa| main ise parvat khodakar chuhiyaa nikaalane ke tuly samajhataa hoon| vayas praapt ho jaane par yah masalaa ek maheene mein aasaanee se tay kiya jaa sakataa hai|
main anubhav se kah sakataa hoon ki yuvaavasthaa mein ham jitanaa jnaan ek maheene mein praapt kar sakate hain, utanaa baalyaavasthaa mein teen saal mein bhee nahin kar sakate, phir khaamakhvaah bachchon ko madarase mein kaid karne se kyaa phaayadaa ? madarase ke baahar rahakar use svachchh vaayu to milatee, praakritik anubhav to hote| paathashaalaa mein band karake to aap usake maanasik aur shaareerik donon vidhaanon kee jad kaat dete hain| isalie jab praanteey vyavasthaapak-sabhaa mein anivaary shikshaa kaa prastaav pesh hua, to meree preranaa se ministar sahab ne usakaa virodh kiya| nateejaa yah hua ki prastaav asveekrit ho gaya| phir kyaa tha| ministar sahab kee aur meree vah le-de shuroo huee ki kuchh na poochhie| vyaktagit aakshep kie jaane lage| main gareeb kee beevee tha, mujhe hee sabakee bhaabhee bananaa padaa| mujhe deshadrohee, unnati kaa shatru aur naukarashaahee kaa gulaam kahaa, gaya| mere kaalej mein jaraa-see bhee koee baat hoti to kaunsil mein mujh par varshaa hone lagatee| mainne ek chaparaasee ko prithak kiya| saaree kaunsil panje jhaadakar mere peechhe pad gaee| aakhir ministar sahab ko majaboor hokar us chaparaasee ko bahaal karn padaa| yah beizzatee mere lie asahy tha| shaayad koee bhee ise sahan na kar sakataa| ministar sahab se mujhe shikaayat nahin| vah majaboor the| haan, is vaataavaran mein kaam karn mere lie dussaadhay ho gaya| mujhe apne kaalej ke aantarik sangathan kaa bhee adhikaar nahin| amuk kyon nahin pareekshaa mein bhejaa gaya, amuk ke badale amuk ko kyon nahin chhaatravritti dee gaee, amuk teechar ko amuk kakshaa kyon nahin dee jati, is tarah ke saaraheen aakshepon ne meree naak mein dam kar diya tha| is naee chot ne kamar tod dee| mainne isteephaa de diya|
mujhe ministar sahab se itanee ummeed avashy thi ki vah kam-se-kam is vishay mein nyaay-paraayanataa se kaam lenge; magar unhonne nyaay kee jagah neeti ko maany samajhaa aur mujhe kaee saal kee bhakt ikaa yah phal milaa ki main padachyut kar diya gaya| sansaar kaa aisaa katu anubhav mujhe ab tak na hua tha| grah bhee kuchh bure aa gaye the, unheen dinon patnee kaa dehaant ho gaya| antim darshan bhee na kar sakaa| shaam-samay nadee-tat par sair karne gaya tha| vah kuchh asvasth theen| lautaa, to unakee laash milee| kadaachit hraday kee gati band ho gaee thi| is aaghaat ne kamar tod dee| maan ke prasaad aur aasheervaad se bade-bade mahaan aadamee kritaarth ho gaye hain| main jo kuchh hua, patnee ke prasaad aur aasheervaad se hua; vah mere bhaagy kee vidhaatree theen| kitanaa alaukik tyaag tha, kitanaa vishaal dhairy| unake maadhury mein teekshnataa kaa naam bhee na tha| mujhe yaad nahin aat ki mainne kabhee unakee bhrikutee sankuchit dekhee ho, vah niraash honaa to jaanatee hee na theen| main kaee baar sakht beemaar padaa hoon| vaidy niraash ho gaye hain, par vah apne dhairy aur shaanti se anu-maatr bhee vichalit nahin hueen| unhen yakeen tha ki main apne pati ke jeevan-kaal mein maroongee aur vahee hua bhee| main jeevan mein ab tak unheen ke sahaare khadaa tha ! jab vah avalamb hee na raha, to jeevan kahan rahataa| khaane aur sone kaa naam jeevan nahin hai| jeevan naam hai, hameshaa aage badhate rehne kee lagan kaa| yah lagan gaayab ho gaee| main sansaar se virakt ho gaya| aur ekaantavaas mein
jeevan ke din vyateet karne kaa phaisalaa karake ek chhote-se gaanv mein jaa basaa| chaaron taraph oonche-oonche teele the, ek ora gangaa bahatee thi| mainne nadee ke kinaare ek chhotaa-saa ghar banaa liya aur usee mein rehne lagaa| magar kaam karn to maanavee svabhaav hai| bekaaree mein jeevan kaise katataa| mainne ek chhotee-see paathashaalaa khol lee; ek vriksh kee chhaanh mein gaanv ke ladakon ko jamaa kar kuchh padhaayaa kart tha| usakee yahan itanee khyaati huee ki aas-paas ke gaanv ke chhaatr bhee aane lage| ek din main apanee kakshaa ko padhaa raha tha ki paathashaalaa ke paas ek motar aakar rukee aur usamen se us jile ke diptee kamishnar utar pade| main us samay keval ek kurtaa aur dhotee pahane hue tha| is vesh mein ek haakim se milate hue sharm aa rahi thi| diptee kamishnar mere paas aaye to mainne jhenpate hue haath badhaayaa, magar vah mujhse haath milaane ke badale mere pairon kee ora jhuke aur una par sir rakh diya| main kuchh aisaa sitapitaa gaya ki mere munh se ek shabd bhee na nikalaa| main angarejee achchhee likhataa hoon, darshanashaastr kaa bhee aachaary hoon, vyaakhyaan bhee achchhe de letaa hoon| magar ina gunon mein ek bhee shrriddhaa ke yogy nahin| shrriddhaa to jnaaniyon aur saadhuon hee ke adhikaar kee vastu hai| agar main braahman hot, to ek baat thi| haalaanki ek siviliyan kaa kisee braahman ke pairon par sir rakhanaa achintaneey hai| main abhee isee hairaanee mein padaa hua tha ki diptee kamishnar ne sir uthaayaa aur meree taraph dekhakar kahaa, aapane shaayad mujhe pahachaanaa nahin|
itanaa sunate hee mere smriti-aankh khul gaye, bol, 'aapakaa naam sooryaprakaash to nahin hai ?'
'jee haan, main aapakaa vahee abhaagaa shishy hoon|'
'baarah-terah varsh ho gaye|'
sooryaprakaash ne muskaraakar kahaa, 'teechar ladakon ko bhool jaate hain, par ladake unhen hameshaa yaad rakhate hain|
mainne usee vinod ke bhaav se kahaa, 'tum jaise ladakon ko bhoolanaa naamumakin hai|'
sooryaprakaash ne vineet aavaaz mein kahaa, 'unheen aparaadhon ko kshamaa karaane ke lie sevaa mein aay hoon| main hameshaa aapakee khabar letaa rahataa tha| jab aap ingalaind gaye, to mainne aapake lie badhaaee kaa patr likhaa; par use bhej na sakaa| jab aap prinsipal hue, main ingalaind jaane ko taiyaar tha| wahan main patrikaaon mein aapake lekh padhataa rahataa tha| jab lautaa, to maaloom hua ki aapane isteephaa de diya aur kaheen dehaat mein chale gaye hain| is jile mein aaye hue mujhe ek varsh se zyaadaa hua; par isakaa jaraa bhee anumaan na tha ki aap yahan ekaant-sevan kar rahe hain| is ujaad gaanv mein aapakaa jee kaise lagataa hai ? itanee hee avasthaa mein aapane vaanaprasth le liya ?'
main nahin kah sakataa ki sooryaprakaash kee unnati dekhakar mujhe kitanaa aashcharyamay khushee hua| agar vah meraa betaa hot to bhee isase zyaadaa khushee na hot| main use apne jhonpade mein laayaa aur apanee raamakahaa,nee kah sunaaee| sooryaprakaash ne kahaa, 'to yah kahie ki aap apne hee ek bhai ke vishvaasaghaat ke shikaar hue| meraa anubhav to abhee bahut kam hai; magar itane hee dinon mein mujhe maaloom ho gaya hai, ki ham log abhee apanee jimmedaariyon ko pooraa karn nahin jaanate| ministar sahab se bhent huee to poochhoongaa, ki yahee aapakaa dharm tha|'
mainne javaab diya-- 'bhai, unakaa dosh nahin| mumakin hai, is dashaa mein main bhee vahee kart, jo unhonne kiya| mujhe apanee svaarthalipsaa kee sajaa mil gaee, aur usake lie main unakaa rinee hoon| banaavat nahin, saty keht hoon ki yahan mujhe jo shaanti hai, vah aur kaheen na thi| is ekaant-jeevan mein mujhe jeevan ke tattvon kaa vah jnaan hua, jo sanpatti aur adhikaar kee daud mein kisee tarah mumakin na tha| itihaas aur bhoogol ke pothe chaatakar aur yoorap ke vidyaalayon kee sharan jaakar bhee main apanee mamataa ko na mitaa sakaa; balki yah rog din-din aur bhee asaadhay hot jat tha| aap seedhiyon par paanv rakhe bagair chhat kee oonchaaee tak nahin pahunch sakate| sampatti kee attaalikaa tak pahunchane mein doosaron kee jindagee hee zeenon kaa kaam detee hai| aap kuchalakar hee lakshy tak pahunch sakate hain| wahan saujany aur sahaanubhooti kaa sthaan hee nahin| mujhe aisaa maaloom hot hai ki us vakt main hinstr jantuon se ghiraa hua tha aur meree saaree shaktiyaan apanee aatmarakshaa mein hee lagee rahatee theen| yahan main apne chaaron ora santosh aur saralataa dekhataa hoon| mere paas jo log aate hain, koee svaarth lekar nahin aate aur na meree sevaaon mein prashansaa yaa gaurav kee laalasaa hai| yah kahakar mainne sooryaprakaash ke chehare kee ora gaur se dekh| kapat musakaan kee jagah pachhataavaa kaa rang tha| shaayad yah dikhaane aay tha ki aap jisakee taraph se itane niraash ho gaye the, vah ab is pad ko sushobhit kar raha hai| vah mujhse apne sadudyog kaa bakhaan karaanaa chaahataa tha| mujhe ab apanee bhool maaloom huee| ek sanpann aadamee ke saamane samridhdi kee nindaa theek nahin| mainne turant baat palatakar kahaa, magar tum apanaa haal to kaho| tumhaaree yah kaayaa-palat kaise huee ? tumhaaree sharaaraton ko yaad kart hoon to ab bhee roen khade ho jaate hain| kisee devataa ke varadaan ke sivaa aur to kaheen yah vibhooti na praapt ho sakatee thi|'
sooryaprakaash ne musakaraakar kahaa, 'aapakaa aasheervaad tha|' mere bahut aagrah karne par sooryaprakaash ne apanaa vrittaant sunaanaa shuroo kiya aapake chale aane ke kaee din baad meraa mameraa bhai skool mein daakhil hua| usakee umr aath-nau saal se jyaadaa na thi| prinsipal sahab use hostal mein na lete the aur na maamaa sahab usake thaharane kaa intazaam kar sakate the| unhen is sankat mein dekhakar mainne prinsipal sahab se kahaa, use mere kamare mein thaharaa deejiye| prinsipal sahab ne ise niyam-viruddh batalaayaa| is par mainne bigadakar usee din hostal chhod diya aur ek kiraaye kaa makaan lekar mohan ke saath rehne lagaa| usakee maan kaee saal pahale mar chukee thi| itanaa dubalaa-patalaa, kamajor aur gareeb ladakaa tha ki pahale hee din se mujhe us par dayaa aane lagee| kabhee usake sir mein dard hot, kabhee jvar ho aat|
aaye din koee-na-koee beemaaree khadee rahatee thi| idhar saanjh huee aur jhapakiyaan aane lageen| badee mushkil se khaanaa karne uthataa| din chadhe tak soyaa kart aur jab tak main god mein uthaakar bithaa na detaa, uthane kaa naam na letaa| raat ko bahudhaa chaunkakar meree chaarapaaee par aa jat| mere gale se lipatakar sotaa| mujhe us par kabhee gussaa na aat| kah nahin sakataa, kyon mujhe usse pyaar ho gaya| main jahaan pahale nau baje sokar uthataa tha, ab tadake uth baithataa aur usake lie doodh garam kart| phir use uthaakar haath-munh dhulaataa aur naashtaa karaataa| usake svaasthy ke soch se nity vaayu sevan ko le jat| main jo kabhee kitaab lekar na baithataa tha, use ghanton padhaayaa kart| mujhe apne daayitv kaa itanaa jnaan kaise ho gaya, isakaa mujhe hairaanee hai| use koee shikaayat ho jati to mere praan nakhon mein samaa jaate| daaktar ke paas daudataa, davaaen laataa aur mohan kee khushaamad karake davaa pilaataa| hameshaa yah phikr lagee rahatee thi, ki koee baat usakee ichchhaa ke viruddh na ho jaay| is bechaare kaa yahan mere sivaa doosaraa kaun hai| mere chanchal mitron mein se koee use chidhaataa yaa chhedataa to meree tyoriyaan badal jati theen !
