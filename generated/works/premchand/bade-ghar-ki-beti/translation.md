# Bade Ghar Ki Beti

**Munshi Premchand**

1
beneemaadhav sinh gaureepur gaanv ke jameendaar aur nambaradaar the| unake pitaamah kisee samay bade paisaa-dhaany sanpann the| gaanv kaa pakkaa taalaab aur mandir jinakee ab marammat bhee mushkil thi, unheen ke keerti-stanbh the| kehte hain is daravaaje par haathee jhoomataa tha, ab usakee jagah ek boodhee bhains thi, jisake shareer mein asthi-panjar ke sivaa aur kuchh shesh na raha tha; par doodh shaayad bahut detee thi; kyonki ek na ek aadamee haandee lie usake sir par savaar hee rahataa tha| beneemaadhav sinh apanee aadhee se zyaadaa sanpatti vakeelon ko bhent kar chuke the| unakee abhee aay ek hajaar rupaye vaarshik se zyaadaa na thi| thaakur sahab ke do bete the| bade kaa naam shreekanth sinh tha| usane bahut dinon ke parishram aur udyog ke baad bee.e. kee digree praapt kee thi| ab ek daphtar mein naukar tha| chhotaa ladakaa laalabihaaree sinh dohare badan kaa, sajeelaa javaan tha| bharaa hua mukhadaa,chaudee chhaatee| bhains kaa do ser taajaa doodh vah uth kar sabere pee jat tha| shreekanth sinh kee dashaa bilakul vipareet thi| ina netrapriy gunon ko unhonne bee.e. --inheen do aksharon par nyochhaavar kar diya tha| ina do aksharon ne unake shareer ko nirbal aur chehare ko kaantiheen banaa diya tha| isee se vaidyak granthon par unakaa khaas pyaar tha| aayurvedik aushadhiyon par unakaa zyaadaa yakeen tha| shaam-sabere unake kamare se praay: kharal kee sureelee karnamadhur dhvani sunaayee diya karti thi| laahaur aur kalakatte ke vaidyon se badee likhaa-padhee rahatee thi|
shreekanth is angarejee digree ke adhipati hone par bhee angarejee saamaajik prathaaon ke khaas premee na the; balki vah bahudhaa bade jor se usakee nindaa aur tiraskaar kiya karte the| isee se gaonva mein unakaa badaa izzat tha| dashahare ke dinon mein vah bade utsaah se raamaleelaa hote aur svayan kisee na kisee paatr kaa paart lete the| gaureepur mein raamaleelaa ke vahee janmadaataa the| praacheen hindoo sabhyataa kaa gunagaan unakee dhaarmikataa kaa pradhaan ang tha| sammilit kutumb ke to vah ek-maatr upaasak the| aaj-kal striyon ko kutumb ko kutumb mein mil-jul kar rehne kee jo aruchi hoti hai, use vah jaati aur desh donon ke lie haanikaarak samajhate the| yahee vajah tha ki gaonva kee lalanaaen unakee nindak theen ! koee-koee to unhen apanaa shatru samajhne mein bhee sankoch na karti theen ! svayan unakee patnee ko hee is vishay mein unse virodh tha| yah isalie nahin ki use apne saas-sasur, devar yaa jeth aadi napharat thi; balki usakaa soch tha ki yadi bahut kuchh sahane aur tarah dene par bhee parivaar ke saath nirvaah na ho sake, to aaye-din kee kalah se jeevan ko nasht karne kee apekshaa yahee uttam hai ki apanee khichadee alag pakaayee jaay|
aanandee ek bade uchch kul kee ladakee thi| usake baap ek chhotee-see riyaasat ke taallukedaar the| vishaal bhavan, ek haathee, teen kutte, baaj, baharee-shikare, jhaad-phaanoos, aanareree majistret aur rin, jo ek pratishthit taallukedaar ke bhogy padaarth hain, sabhee yahon vidyamaan the| naam tha bhoopasinh| bade udaar-chitt aur pratibhaashaalee aadamee the; par badakismatee se ladakaa ek bhee na tha| saat ladakiyon hueen aur daivayog se sab kee sab jeevit raheen| pahalee umang mein to unhonne teen byaah dil kholakar kiye; par pandrah-bees hajaar rupayon kaa karj sir par ho gaya, to onkhen khuleen, haath samet liya| aanandee chauthee ladakee thi| vah apanee sab bahanon se zyaadaa roopavatee aur gunavatee thi| isase thaakur bhoopasinh use bahut pyaar karte the| sundar bachchaa ko kadaachit usake maan-pitaa bhee zyaadaa chaahate hain| thaakur sahab bade dharm-sankat mein the ki isakaa vivaah kahon karen? na to yahee chaahate the ki rin kaa bojh badhe aur na yahee sveekaar tha ki use apne ko bhaagyaheen samajhanaa pade| ek din shreekanth unake paas kisee chande kaa rupayaa maangane aae| shaayad naagaree-prachaar kaa chandaa tha| bhoopasinh unake svabhaav par reejh gaye aur dhoomadhaam se shreekanthasinh kaa aanandee ke saath byaah ho gaya|
aanandee apne naye ghar mein aayee, to yahan kaa rang-dhang kuchh aur hee dekh| jis teem-taam kee use bachpan se hee aadat padee huee thi, vah yahaan naam-maatr ko bhee na thi| haathee-ghodon kaa to kahanaa hee kyaa, koee sajee huee sundar bahalee tak na thi| reshamee sleepar saath laayee thi; par yahan baag kahan| makaan mein khidakiyaan tak na theen, na jameen par pharsh, na deevaar par tasveeren| yah ek seedhaa-saadaa dehaatee ghar-parivaar kaa makaan tha; lekin aanandee ne thode hee dinon mein apne ko is nayee avasthaa ke aisaa anukool banaa liya, maanon usane vilaas ke saamaan kabhee dekhe hee na the|

2
ek din dopahar ke samay laalabihaaree sinh do chidiyaa liye hue aay aur bhaavaj se bol--jaldee se pakaa do, mujhe bhookh lagee hai| aanandee khaanaa banaakar usakee raah dekh rahi thi| ab vah nayaa vyanjan banaane baithee| haandee mein dekh, to ghee paav-bhar se zyaadaa na tha| bade ghar kee betee, kiphaayat kyaa jaane| usane sab ghee maans mein daal diya| laalabihaaree khaane baithaa, to daal mein ghee na tha, bol--daal mein ghee kyon nahin chhodaa?
aanandee ne kahaa--ghee sab maans mein pad gaya| laalabihaaree jor se bol--abhee parason ghee aay hai| itanaa jald uth gaya?
aanandee ne javaab diya--aaj to kul paav-bhar raha hogaa| vah sab mainne maans mein daal diya|
jis tarah sookhee lakadee jaldee se jal uthatee hai, usee tarah kshudhaa se baavalaa manushy jaraa-jaraa see baat par tinak jat hai| laalabihaaree ko bhaavaj kee yah dhithaaee bahut buree maaloom huee, tinak kar bol--maike mein to chaahe ghee kee nadee bahatee ho !
aurat gaaliyon sah letee hain, maar bhee sah letee hain; par maike kee nindaa unse nahin sahee jati| aanandee munh pher kar bolee--haathee maraa bhee, to nau laakh kaa| vahon itanaa ghee nity naaee-kahaar khaa jaate hain|
laalabihaaree jal gaya, thaalee uthaakar palat dee, aur bol--jee chaahataa hai, jeebh pakad kar kheench loon|
khushee ko bhee gussaa aa gaya| munh laal ho gaya, bolee--vah hote to aaj isakaa majaa chakhaate|
ab apadh, ujadd thaakur se na raha gaya| usakee aurat ek aam jameendaar kee betee thi| jab jee chaahataa, us par haath saaph kar liya kart tha| khadaaoon uthaakar aanandee kee ora jor se phenkee, aur bol--jisake gumaan par bhoolee huee ho, use bhee dekhoongaa aur tumhen bhee|
aanandee ne haath se khadaaoon rokee, sir bach gaya; par angalee mein badee chot aayee| gussaa ke maare havaa se hilate patte kee tarah kaanpatee huee apne kamare mein aa kar khadee ho gayee| aurat kaa bal aur saahas, maan aur maryaadaa pati tak hai| use apne pati ke hee bal aur purushatv kaa ghamand hot hai| aanandee khoon kaa ghoont pee kar raha gayee|
3
shreekanth sinh shanivaar ko ghar aay karte the| vrihaspati ko yah ghatanaa huee thi| do din tak aanandee kop-bhavan mein rahi| na kuchh khaayaa na piyaa, unakee baat dekhatee rahi| ant mein shanivaar ko vah niyamaanukool shaam samay ghar aaye aur baahar baith kar kuchh idhar-udhar kee baaten, kuchh desh-kaal sanbandhee khabar tathaa kuchh naye mukadamon aadi kee charchaa karne lage| yah baatacheet das baje raat tak hot raha| gaanv ke bhadr purushon ko ina baaton mein aisaa khushee milataa tha ki khaane-peene kee bhee sudhi na rahatee thi| shreekanth ko pind chhudaanaa mushkil ho jat tha| ye do-teen ghante aanandee ne bade takaleeph se kaate ! kisee tarah khaanaa kaa samay aay| panchaayat uthee| ekaant hua, to laalabihaaree ne kahaa--bhaiyaa, aap jaraa bhaabhee ko samajhaa deejiegaa ki munh sanbhaal kar baatacheet kiya karen, nahin to ek din anarth ho jaayagaa|
beneemaadhav sinh ne bete kee ora saakshee dee--haan, bahoo-betiyon kaa yah svabhaav achchhaa nahin ki mardon ke moonh lagen|
laalabihaaree--vah bade ghar kee betee hain, to ham bhee koee kurmee-kahaar nahin hai|
shreekanth ne chintit aavaaz se poochhaa--aakhir baat kyaa huee?
laalabihaaree ne kahaa--kuchh bhee nahin; yon hee aap hee aap ulajh padeen| maike ke saamane ham logon ko kuchh samajhatee hee nahin|
shreekanth khaa-peekar aanandee ke paas gaye| vah bharee baithee thi| yah hajarat bhee kuchh teekhe the| aanandee ne poochhaa--chitt to khush hai|
shreekanth bole--bahut khush hai; par tumane aajakal ghar mein yah kyaa upadrav machaa rakhaa hai?
aanandee kee tyoriyon par bal pad gaye, jhunjhalaahat ke maare badan mein jvaalaa-see dahak uthee| bolee--jisane tumse yah aag lagaayee hai, use paaoon, munh jhulas doon|
shreekanth--itanee garam kyon hoti ho, baat to kaho|
aanandee--kyaa kahoon, yah mere bhaagy kaa pher hai ! nahin to ganvaar chhokaraa, jisako chaparaasagiree karne kaa bhee shaoor nahin, mujhe khadaaoon se maar kar yon na akadataa|
shreekanth--sab haal saaph-saaph kahaa, to maaloom ho| mujhe to kuchh pataa nahin|
aanandee--parason tumhaare laadale bhai ne mujhse maans pakaane ko kahaa| ghee haandee mein paav-bhar se zyaadaa na tha| vah sab mainne maans mein daal diya| jab khaane baithaa to kehne lagaa--daal mein ghee kyon nahin hai? bas, isee par mere maike ko buraa-bhalaa kehne lagaa--mujhse na raha gaya| mainne kahaa ki vahon itanaa ghee to naaee-kahaar khaa jaate hain, aur kisee ko jaan bhee nahin padataa| bas itanee see baat par is anyaayee ne mujh par khadaaoon phenk maaree| yadi haath se na rok loon, to sir phat jaay| usee se poochho, mainne jo kuchh kahaa hai, vah sach hai yaa jhooth|
shreekanth kee onkhen laal ho gayeen| bole--yahan tak ho gaya, is chhokare kaa yah saahas! aanandee striyon ke svabhaavaanusaar rone lagee; kyonki onsoo unakee palakon par rahate hain| shreekanth bade dhairyavaan aur shaanti aadamee the| unhen kadaachit hee kabhee gussaa aat tha; striyon ke aansoo aadamee kee krodhaagni bhadakaane mein tel kaa kaam dete hain| raat bhar karavaten badalate rahe| udvignataa ke vajah palak tak nahin jhapakee| praat:kaal apne baap ke paas jaakar bole--daadaa, ab is ghar mein meraa nibaah na hogaa|
is tarah kee vidroh-poorn baaten kehne par shreekanth ne kitanee hee baar apne kaee mitron ko aade haathon liya tha; lekin badakismatee, aaj unhen svayan ve hee baaten apne munh se kahanee padee ! doosaron ko upadesh denaa bhee kitanaa sahaj hai!
beneemaadhav sinh ghabaraa uthe aur bole--kyon?
shreekanth--isalie ki mujhe bhee apanee maan--pratishthaa kaa kuchh soch hai| aapake ghar mein ab anyaay aur hath kaa prakop ho raha hai| jinako badon kaa aadar-izzat karn chaahie, ve unake sir chadhate hain| main doosare kaa naukar thaharaa ghar par rahataa nahin| yahan mere peechhe striyon par khadaaoon aur jooton kee bauchhaaren hoti hain| kadee baat tak phikr nahin| koee ek kee do kah le, wahan tak main sah sakataa hoon lekin yah kabhee nahin nahin ho sakataa ki mere oopar laat-ghoonse paden aur main dam na maarun|
beneemaadhav sinh kuchh javaab na de sake| shreekanth hameshaa unakaa aadar karte the| unake aise tevar dekhakar boodhaa thaakur avaak raha gaya| keval itanaa hee bol--betaa, tum buddhimaan hokar aisee baaten karte ho? striyaan is tarah ghar kaa naash kar detee hai| unako bahut sir chadhaanaa achchhaa nahin|
shreekanth--itanaa main jaanataa hoon, aapake aasheervaad se aisaa bevakooph nahin hoon| aap svayan jaanate hain ki mere hee samajhaane-bujhaane se, isee gaanv mein kaee ghar sanbhal gaye, par jis aurat kee maan-pratishthaa kaa eeshvar ke darabaar mein uttaradaataa hoon, usake prati aisaa ghor anyaay aur pashuvat vyavahaar mujhe asahy hai| aap sach maanie, mere lie yahee kuchh kam nahin hai ki laalabihaaree ko kuchh dand nahin hot|
ab beneemaadhav sinh bhee garamaaye| aisee baaten aur na sun sake| bole--laalabihaaree tumhaaraa bhai hai| usse jab kabhee bhool--chook ho, usake kaan pakado lekin...
shreekanth—laalabihaaree ko main ab apanaa bhai nahin samajhataa|
beneemaadhav sinh--aurat ke peechhe?
shreekanth—jee nahin, usakee kroorataa aur avivek ke kaaran|
donon kuchh der chup rahe| thaakur sahab ladake kaa gussaa shaant karn chaahate the, lekin yah nahin sveekaar karn chaahate the ki laalabihaaree ne koee galat kaam kiya hai| isee beech mein gaanv ke aur kaee sajjan hukke-chilam ke bahaane wahan aa baithe| kaee striyon ne jab yah sunaa ki shreekanth patnee ke peechhe pitaa se ladane kee taiyaar hain, to unhen badaa harsh hua| donon pakshon kee madhur vaaniyon sunane ke lie unakee aatmaaen tilamilaane lageen| gaanv mein kuchh aise kutil manushy bhee the, jo is kul kee neetipoorn gati par man hee man jalate the| ve kahaa karte the—shreekanth apne baap se dabataa hai, iseelie vah dabboo hai| usane padhai padhee, isalie vah kitaabon kaa keedaa hai| beneemaadhav sinh usakee salaah ke binaa koee kaam nahin karte, yah unakee moorkhataa hai| ina mahaanubhaavon kee shubhakaamanaaen aaj pooree hoti dikhaayee deen| koee hukkaa peene ke bahaane aur koee lagaan kee raseed dikhaane aa kar baith gaya| beneemaadhav sinh puraane aadamee the| ina bhaavon ko taad gaye| unhonne phaisalaa kiya chaahe kuchh hee kyon na ho, ina drohiyon ko taalee bajaane kaa avasar na doongaa| turant komal shabdon mein bole--betaa, main tumse baahar nahin hoon| tamhaaraa jo jee chaahe karo, ab to ladake se aparaadh ho gaya|
ilaahaabaad kaa anubhav-rahit jhallaayaa hua grejuet is baat ko na samajh sakaa| use dibeting-klab mein apanee baat par adane kee aadat thi, ina hathakandon kee use kyaa khabar? baap ne jis matalab se baat palatee thi, vah usakee samajh mein na aay| bol—laalabihaaree ke saath ab is ghar mein nahin raha sakataa|
beneemaadhav—betaa, buddhimaan log moorkhon kee baat par dhyaan nahin dete| vah besamajh ladakaa hai| usse jo kuchh bhool huee, use tum bade hokar kshamaa karo|
shreekanth—usakee is dushtataa ko main kabhee nahin nahin sah sakataa| yaa to vahee ghar mein rahegaa, yaa main hee| aapako yadi vah zyaadaa pyaaraa hai, to mujhe vidaa keejie, main apanaa bhaar aap sanbhaal loongaa| yadi mujhe rakhanaa chaahate hain to usse kahie, jahaan chaahe chalaa jaay| bas yah meraa antim phaisalaa hai|
laalabihaaree sinh daravaaje kee chaukhat par chupachaap khadaa bade bhai kee baaten sun raha tha| vah unakaa bahut aadar kart tha| use kabhee itanaa saahas na hua tha ki shreekanth ke saamane chaarapaaee par baith jaay, hukkaa pee le yaa paan khaa le| baap kaa bhee vah itanaa maan na kart tha| shreekanth kaa bhee us par haardik pyaar tha| apne hosh mein unhonne kabhee use ghudakaa tak na tha| jab vah ilaahaabaad se aate, to usake lie koee na koee vastu avashy laate| mugadar kee jodee unhonne hee banavaa dee thi| pichhale saal jab usane apne se dyaudhe javaan ko naagapanchamee ke din dangal mein pachhaad diya, to unhonne pulakit hokar akhaade mein hee jaa kar use gale lagaa liya tha, paonch rupaye ke paise lutaaye the| aise bhai ke munh se aaj aisee dil-vidaarak baat sunakar laalabihaaree ko badee pachhataavaa huee| vah phoot-phoot kar rone lagaa| isamen shak nahin ki apne kiye par pachhataa raha tha| bhai ke aane se ek din pahale se usakee chhaatee dhadakatee thi ki dekhoon bhaiyaa kyaa kehte hain| main unake sammukh kaise jaaoongaa, unse kaise boloongaa, meree aankhe unake saamane kaise uthegee| usane samajhaa tha ki bhaiyaa mujhe bulaakar samajhaa denge| is ummeed ke vipareet aaj usane unhen nirdayataa kee moorti bane hue paayaa| vah bevakooph tha| lekin usakaa man keht tha ki bhaiyaa mere saath anyaay kar rahe hain| yadi shreekanth use akele mein bulaakar do-chaar baaten kah dete; itanaa hee nahin do-chaar tamaache bhee lagaa dete to kadaachit use itanaa du:kha na hot; par bhai kaa yah kahanaa ki ab main isakee soorat nahin dekhanaa chaahataa, laalabihaaree se sahaa na gaya ! vah rotaa hua ghar aay| kothaaree mein jaa kar kapade pahane, aankhe ponchhee, jisamen koee yah na samajhe ki rotaa tha| tab aanandee ke dvaar par aakar bol—bhaabhee, bhaiyaa ne phaisalaa kiya hai ki vah mere saath is ghar mein na rahenge| ab vah meraa munh nahin dekhanaa chaahate; isalie ab main jat hoon| unhen phir munh na dikhaaoongaa ! mujhse jo kuchh aparaadh hua, use kshamaa karn|
yah kehte-kehte laalabihaaree kaa galaa bhar aay|
4
jis samay laalabihaaree sinh sir jhukaaye aanandee ke dvaar par khad tha, usee samay shreekanth sinh bhee aankhe laal kiye baahar se aaye| bhai ko khadaa dekh, to napharat se aankhe pher leen, aur kataraa kar nikal gaye| maanon usakee parachhaahee se door bhaagate hon|
aanandee ne laalabihaaree kee shikaayat to kee thi, lekin ab man mein pachhataa rahi thi vah svabhaav se hee dayaavatee thi| use isakaa tanik bhee dhyaan na tha ki baat itanee badh jaayagee| vah man mein apne pati par jhunjhalaa rahi thi ki yah itane garam kyon hote hain| us par yah dar bhee lagaa hua tha ki kaheen mujhse ilaahaabaad chalne ko kahen, to kaise kyaa karungee| is beech mein jab usane laalabihaaree ko daravaaje par khade yah kehte sunaa ki ab main jat hoon, mujhse jo kuchh aparaadh hua, kshamaa karn, to usakaa raha-sahaa gussaa bhee paanee ho gaya| vah rone lagee| man kaa mail dhone ke lie aankh-jal se upayukt aur koee vastu nahin hai|
shreekanth ko dekhakar aanandee ne kahaa—laalaa baahar khade bahut ro rahe hain|
shreekanth--to main kyaa karoon?
aanandee—bheetar bulaa lo| meree jeebh mein aag lage ! mainne kahan se yah jhagadaa uthaayaa|
shreekanth--main na bulaaoongaa|
aanandee--pachhataaoge| unhen bahut pachhataavaa ho gayee hai, aisaa na ho, kaheen chal den|
shreekanth na uthe| itane mein laalabihaaree ne phir kahaa--bhaabhee, bhaiyaa se meraa pranaam kah do| vah meraa munh nahin dekhanaa chaahate; isalie main bhee apanaa munh unhen na dikhaaoongaa|
laalabihaaree itanaa kah kar laut padaa, aur sheeghrataa se daravaaje kee ora badhaa| ant mein aanandee kamare se nikalee aur usakaa haath pakad liya| laalabihaaree ne peechhe phir kar dekh aur onkhon mein aansoo bhare bol--mujhe jaane do|
aanandee-- kahan jaate ho?
laalabihaaree-- jahaan koee meraa munh na dekhe|
aanandee—- main na jaane doongee?
laalabihaaree—- main tum logon ke saath rehne yogy nahin hoon|
aanandee—-tumhen meree saugandh ab ek pag bhee aage na badhaanaa|
laalabihaaree—jab tak mujhe yah na maaloom ho jaay ki bhaiyaa kaa man meree taraph se saaph ho gaya, tab tak main is ghar mein kabhee nahin na rahoongaa|
aanandee—main eeshvar ko saakshee de kar kehti hoon ki tumhaaree ora se mere man mein tanik bhee mail nahin hai|
ab shreekanth kaa dil bhee pighalaa| unhonne baahar aakar laalabihaaree ko gale lagaa liya| donon bhai khoob phoot-phoot kar roye| laalabihaaree ne sisakate hue kahaa—bhaiyaa, ab kabhee mata kahanaa ki tumhaaraa munh na dekhoongaa| isake sivaa aap jo dand denge, main saharsh sveekaar karoongaa|
shreekanth ne kaanpate hue aavaaz mein kahaa--lalloo! ina baaton ko bilkul bhool jaao| eeshvar chaahegaa, to phir aisaa avasar na aavegaa|
beneemaadhav sinh baahar se aa rahe the| donon bhaaiyon ko gale milate dekhakar khushee se pulakit ho gaye| bol uthe—bade ghar kee betiyaan aisee hee hoti hain| bigadataa hua kaam banaa letee hain|
gaanv mein jisane yah vrittaant sunaa, usee ne ina shabdon mein aanandee kee udaarataa ko saraahaa—‘bade ghar kee betiyaan aisee hee hoti hain|'
