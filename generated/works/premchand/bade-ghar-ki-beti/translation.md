# Bade Ghar Ki Beti

**Munshi Premchand**

1
beneemaadhav sinh gaureepur gaanv ke jameendaar aur nambaradaar the. unake pitaamah kisee samay bade paisaa-dhaany sanpann the. gaanv kaa pakkaa taalaab aur mandir jinakee ab marammat bhi mushkil thi, unheen ke keerti-stanbh the. kehte hain is daravaaje par haathee jhoomataa tha, ab uski jagah ek boodhee bhains thi, jisake shareer mein asthi-panjar ke sivaa aur kuchh shesh na raha tha; par doodh shaayad bahut detee thi; kyonki ek na ek aadamee haandee lie usake sir par savaar hi rehta tha. beneemaadhav sinh apni aadhee se zyaadaa sanpatti vakeelon ko bhent kar chuke the. unki abhi aay ek hajaar rupaye vaarshik se zyaadaa na thi. thaakur sahab ke do bete the. bade kaa naam shreekanth sinh tha. usane bahut dinon ke parishram aur udyog ke baad bee. e. ki digree praapt ki thi. ab ek daphtar mein naukar tha. chhota ladakaa laalabihaaree sinh dohare badan kaa, sajeelaa javaan tha. bharaa hua mukhadaa,chaudee chhaatee. bhains kaa do ser taajaa doodh vah uth kar sabere pee jat tha. shreekanth sinh ki dashaa bilakul vipareet thi. ina netrapriy gunon ko unhone bee. e. --inheen do aksharon par nyochhaavar kar diya tha. ina do aksharon ne unake shareer ko nirbal aur chehare ko kaantiheen banaa diya tha. isi se vaidyak granthon par unakaa khaas pyaar tha. aayurvedik aushadhiyon par unakaa zyaadaa yakeen tha. shaam-sabere unake kamare se praay: kharal ki sureelee karnamadhur dhvani sunaayee diya karti thi. laahaur aur kalakatte ke vaidyon se badi likhaa-padhee rehti thi.
shreekanth is angrezi digree ke adhipati hone par bhi angrezi saamaajik prathaaon ke khaas premee na the; balki vah bahudhaa bade jor se uski nindaa aur tiraskaar kiya karte the. isi se gaonva mein unakaa bada izzat tha. dashahare ke dinon mein vah bade utsaah se raamaleelaa hote aur svayan kisee na kisee paatr kaa paart lete the. gaureepur mein raamaleelaa ke wahi janmadaataa the. praacheen hindoo sabhyataa kaa gunagaan unki dhaarmikataa kaa pradhaan ang tha. sammilit kutumb ke to vah ek-maatr upaasak the. aaj-kal striyon ko kutumb ko kutumb mein mil-jul kar rehne ki jo aruchi hoti hai, use vah jaati aur desh donon ke lie haanikaarak samajhte the. yahi vajah tha ki gaonva ki lalanaaen unki nindak theen! koi-koi to unhen apna shatru samajhne mein bhi sankoch na karti theen! svayan unki patnee ko hi is vishay mein unse virodh tha. yah isalie nahin ki use apne saas-sasur, devar yaa jeth aadi napharat thi; balki uska soch tha ki yadi bahut kuchh sahane aur tarah dene par bhi parivaar ke saath nirvaah na ho sake, to aaye-din ki kalah se jeevan ko nasht karne ki apekshaa yahi uttam hai ki apni khichadee alag pakaayee jaay.
aanandee ek bade uchch kul ki ladakee thi. usake baap ek chhoti-see riyaasat ke taallukedaar the. vishaal bhavan, ek haathee, teen kutte, baaj, baharee-shikare, jhaad-phaanoos, aanareree majistret aur rin, jo ek pratishthit taallukedaar ke bhogy padaarth hain, sabhi yahon vidyamaan the. naam tha bhoopasinh. bade udaar-chitt aur pratibhaashaalee aadamee the; par badakismatee se ladakaa ek bhi na tha. saat ladakiyon hueen aur daivayog se sab ki sab jeevit raheen. pahalee umang mein to unhone teen byaah dil kholakar kiye; par pandrah-bees hajaar rupayon kaa karj sir par ho gaya, to onkhen khuleen, haath samet liya. aanandee chauthee ladakee thi. vah apni sab bahanon se zyaadaa roopavatee aur gunavatee thi. isase thaakur bhoopasinh use bahut pyaar karte the. sundar bachchaa ko kadaachit usake maan-pitaa bhi zyaadaa chahte hain. thaakur sahab bade dharm-sankat mein the ki isakaa vivaah kahon karen? na to yahi chahte the ki rin kaa bojh badhe aur na yahi sveekaar tha ki use apne ko bhaagyaheen samajhna pade. ek din shreekanth unake paas kisee chande kaa rupayaa maangane aae. shaayad naagaree-prachaar kaa chandaa tha. bhoopasinh unake svabhaav par reejh gaye aur dhoomadhaam se shreekanthasinh kaa aanandee ke saath byaah ho gaya.
aanandee apne naye ghar mein aayee, to yahan kaa rang-dhang kuchh aur hi dekh. jis teem-taam ki use bachpan se hi aadat padee hui thi, vah yahan naam-maatr ko bhi na thi. haathee-ghodon kaa to kehna hi kya, koi sajee hui sundar bahalee tak na thi. reshamee sleepar saath laayee thi; par yahan baag kahan. makaan mein khidakiyaan tak na theen, na jameen par pharsh, na deevaar par tasveeren. yah ek seedhaa-saadaa dehaatee ghar-parivaar kaa makaan tha; lekin aanandee ne thode hi dinon mein apne ko is nayee avasthaa ke aisaa anukool banaa liya, maanon usane vilaas ke saamaan kabhi dekhe hi na the.

2
ek din dopahar ke samay laalabihaaree sinh do chidiyaa liye hue aay aur bhaavaj se bol--jaldee se pakaa do, mujhe bhookh lagee hai. aanandee khaanaa banaakar uski raah dekh rahi thi. ab vah nayaa vyanjan banaane baithee. haandee mein dekh, to ghee paav-bhar se zyaadaa na tha. bade ghar ki betee, kiphaayat kya jaane. usane sab ghee maans mein daal diya. laalabihaaree khaane baithaa, to daal mein ghee na tha, bol--daal mein ghee kyon nahin chhodaa?
aanandee ne kahaa--ghee sab maans mein pad gaya. laalabihaaree jor se bol--abhi parason ghee aay hai. itanaa jald uth gaya?
aanandee ne jawab diya--aaj to kul paav-bhar raha hogaa. vah sab maine maans mein daal diya.
jis tarah sookhee lakadee jaldee se jal uthti hai, usi tarah kshudhaa se baavalaa manushy zara-zara see baat par tinak jat hai. laalabihaaree ko bhaavaj ki yah dhithaaee bahut buree maaloom hui, tinak kar bol--maike mein to chaahe ghee ki nadee bahti ho!
aurat gaaliyon sah letee hain, maar bhi sah letee hain; par maike ki nindaa unse nahin sahee jati. aanandee munh pher kar bolee--haathee maraa bhi, to nau laakh kaa. vahon itanaa ghee nity naaee-kahaar khaa jaate hain.
laalabihaaree jal gaya, thaalee uthaakar palat dee, aur bol--jee chahta hai, jeebh pakad kar kheench loon.
khushi ko bhi gussaa aa gaya. munh laal ho gaya, bolee--vah hote to aaj isakaa majaa chakhaate.
ab apadh, ujadd thaakur se na raha gaya. uski aurat ek aam jameendaar ki betee thi. jab jee chahta, us par haath saaph kar liya kart tha. khadaaoon uthaakar aanandee ki ora jor se phenkee, aur bol--jisake gumaan par bhoolee hui ho, use bhi dekhoongaa aur tumhen bhi.
aanandee ne haath se khadaaoon rokee, sir bach gaya; par angalee mein badi chot aayee. gussaa ke maare havaa se hilate patte ki tarah kaanpatee hui apne kamare mein aa kar khadee ho gayi. aurat kaa bal aur saahas, maan aur maryaadaa pati tak hai. use apne pati ke hi bal aur purushatv kaa ghamand hot hai. aanandee khoon kaa ghoont pee kar raha gayi.
3
shreekanth sinh shanivaar ko ghar aay karte the. vrihaspati ko yah ghatanaa hui thi. do din tak aanandee kop-bhavan mein rahi. na kuchh khaayaa na piyaa, unki baat dekhti rahi. ant mein shanivaar ko vah niyamaanukool shaam samay ghar aaye aur baahar baith kar kuchh idhar-udhar ki baaten, kuchh desh-kaal sanbandhee khabar tathaa kuchh naye mukadamon aadi ki charchaa karne lage. yah baatacheet das baje raat tak hot raha. gaanv ke bhadr purushon ko ina baaton mein aisaa khushi milataa tha ki khaane-peene ki bhi sudhi na rehti thi. shreekanth ko pind chhudaanaa mushkil ho jat tha. ye do-teen ghante aanandee ne bade takaleeph se kaate! kisee tarah khaanaa kaa samay aay. panchaayat uthee. ekaant hua, to laalabihaaree ne kahaa--bhaiyaa, aap zara bhaabhee ko samajhaa deejiegaa ki munh sanbhaal kar baatacheet kiya karen, nahin to ek din anarth ho jaayagaa.
beneemaadhav sinh ne bete ki ora saakshee dee--haan, bahoo-betiyon kaa yah svabhaav achchha nahin ki mardon ke moonh lagen.
laalabihaaree--vah bade ghar ki betee hain, to ham bhi koi kurmee-kahaar nahin hai.
shreekanth ne chintit aavaaz se poochhaa--aakhir baat kya hui?
laalabihaaree ne kahaa--kuchh bhi nahin; yon hi aap hi aap ulajh padeen. maike ke saamane ham logon ko kuchh samajhti hi nahin.
shreekanth khaa-peekar aanandee ke paas gaye. vah bharee baithee thi. yah hajarat bhi kuchh teekhe the. aanandee ne poochhaa--chitt to khush hai.
shreekanth bole--bahut khush hai; par tumne aajakal ghar mein yah kya upadrav machaa rakhaa hai?
aanandee ki tyoriyon par bal pad gaye, jhunjhalaahat ke maare badan mein jvaalaa-see dahak uthee. bolee--jisane tumse yah aag lagaayee hai, use paaoon, munh jhulas doon.
shreekanth--itanee garam kyon hoti ho, baat to kaho.
aanandee--kya kahoon, yah mere bhaagy kaa pher hai! nahin to ganvaar chhokaraa, jisako chaparaasagiree karne kaa bhi shaoor nahin, mujhe khadaaoon se maar kar yon na akadataa.
shreekanth--sab haal saaph-saaph kahaa, to maaloom ho. mujhe to kuchh pataa nahin.
aanandee--parason tumhaare laadale bhai ne mujhse maans pakaane ko kahaa. ghee haandee mein paav-bhar se zyaadaa na tha. vah sab maine maans mein daal diya. jab khaane baithaa to kehne lagaa--daal mein ghee kyon nahin hai? bas, isi par mere maike ko buraa-bhalaa kehne lagaa--mujhse na raha gaya. maine kahaa ki vahon itanaa ghee to naaee-kahaar khaa jaate hain, aur kisee ko jaan bhi nahin padataa. bas itanee see baat par is anyaayee ne mujh par khadaaoon phenk maaree. yadi haath se na rok loon, to sir phat jaay. usi se poochho, maine jo kuchh kahaa hai, vah sach hai yaa jhooth.
shreekanth ki onkhen laal ho gayeen. bole--yahan tak ho gaya, is chhokare kaa yah saahas! aanandee striyon ke svabhaavaanusaar rone lagee; kyonki onsoo unki palakon par rehte hain. shreekanth bade dhairyavaan aur shaanti aadamee the. unhen kadaachit hi kabhi gussaa aat tha; striyon ke aansoo aadamee ki krodhaagni bhadakaane mein tel kaa kaam dete hain. raat bhar karavaten badalate rahe. udvignataa ke vajah palak tak nahin jhapakee. praat:kaal apne baap ke paas jaakar bole--daadaa, ab is ghar mein mera nibaah na hogaa.
is tarah ki vidroh-poorn baaten kehne par shreekanth ne kitanee hi baar apne kaee mitron ko aade haathon liya tha; lekin badakismatee, aaj unhen svayan ve hi baaten apne munh se kahanee padee! doosaron ko upadesh dena bhi kitanaa sahaj hai!
beneemaadhav sinh ghabaraa uthe aur bole--kyon?
shreekanth--isalie ki mujhe bhi apni maan--pratishthaa kaa kuchh soch hai. aapake ghar mein ab anyaay aur hath kaa prakop ho raha hai. jinako badon kaa aadar-izzat karn chaahie, ve unake sir chadhte hain. main doosare kaa naukar thaharaa ghar par rehta nahin. yahan mere peechhe striyon par khadaaoon aur jooton ki bauchhaaren hoti hain. kadee baat tak phikr nahin. koi ek ki do kah le, wahan tak main sah sakataa hoon lekin yah kabhi nahin nahin ho sakataa ki mere oopar laat-ghoonse paden aur main dam na maarun.
beneemaadhav sinh kuchh jawab na de sake. shreekanth hameshaa unakaa aadar karte the. unake aise tevar dekhakar boodhaa thaakur avaak raha gaya. keval itanaa hi bol--betaa, tum buddhimaan hokar aisee baaten karte ho? striyaan is tarah ghar kaa naash kar detee hai. unako bahut sir chadhaanaa achchha nahin.
shreekanth--itanaa main jaanataa hoon, aapake aasheervaad se aisaa bevakooph nahin hoon. aap svayan jaanate hain ki mere hi samajhaane-bujhaane se, isi gaanv mein kaee ghar sanbhal gaye, par jis aurat ki maan-pratishthaa kaa eeshvar ke darabaar mein uttaradaataa hoon, usake prati aisaa ghor anyaay aur pashuvat vyavahaar mujhe asahy hai. aap sach maanie, mere lie yahi kuchh kam nahin hai ki laalabihaaree ko kuchh dand nahin hot.
ab beneemaadhav sinh bhi garamaaye. aisee baaten aur na sun sake. bole--laalabihaaree tumhara bhai hai. usse jab kabhi bhool--chook ho, usake kaan pakado lekin...
shreekanth—laalabihaaree ko main ab apna bhai nahin samajhta.
beneemaadhav sinh--aurat ke peechhe?
shreekanth—jee nahin, uski kroorataa aur avivek ke kaaran.
donon kuchh der chup rahe. thaakur sahab ladake kaa gussaa shaant karn chahte the, lekin yah nahin sveekaar karn chahte the ki laalabihaaree ne koi galat kaam kiya hai. isi beech mein gaanv ke aur kaee sajjan hukke-chilam ke bahaane wahan aa baithe. kaee striyon ne jab yah sunaa ki shreekanth patnee ke peechhe pitaa se ladane ki taiyaar hain, to unhen bada harsh hua. donon pakshon ki madhur vaaniyon sunane ke lie unki aatmaaen tilamilaane lageen. gaanv mein kuchh aise kutil manushy bhi the, jo is kul ki neetipoorn gati par man hi man jalate the. ve kahaa karte the—shreekanth apne baap se dabataa hai, iseelie vah dabboo hai. usane padhai padhee, isalie vah kitaabon kaa keedaa hai. beneemaadhav sinh uski salaah ke binaa koi kaam nahin karte, yah unki moorkhta hai. ina mahaanubhaavon ki shubhakaamanaaen aaj pooree hoti dikhaayee deen. koi hukkaa peene ke bahaane aur koi lagaan ki raseed dikhaane aa kar baith gaya. beneemaadhav sinh puraane aadamee the. ina bhaavon ko taad gaye. unhone phaisalaa kiya chaahe kuchh hi kyon na ho, ina drohiyon ko taalee bajaane kaa avasar na doongaa. turant komal shabdon mein bole--betaa, main tumse baahar nahin hoon. tamhaaraa jo jee chaahe karo, ab to ladake se aparaadh ho gaya.
ilaahaabaad kaa anubhav-rahit jhallaayaa hua grejuet is baat ko na samajh sakaa. use dibeting-klab mein apni baat par adane ki aadat thi, ina hathakandon ki use kya khabar? baap ne jis matalab se baat palatee thi, vah uski samajh mein na aay. bol—laalabihaaree ke saath ab is ghar mein nahin raha sakataa.
beneemaadhav—betaa, buddhimaan log moorkhon ki baat par dhyaan nahin dete. vah besamajh ladakaa hai. usse jo kuchh bhool hui, use tum bade hokar kshamaa karo.
shreekanth—uski is dushtataa ko main kabhi nahin nahin sah sakataa. yaa to wahi ghar mein rahegaa, yaa main hi. aapako yadi vah zyaadaa pyaaraa hai, to mujhe vidaa keejie, main apna bhaar aap sanbhaal loongaa. yadi mujhe rakhna chahte hain to usse kahie, jahaan chaahe chalaa jaay. bas yah mera antim phaisalaa hai.
laalabihaaree sinh daravaaje ki chaukhat par chupachaap khadaa bade bhai ki baaten sun raha tha. vah unakaa bahut aadar kart tha. use kabhi itanaa saahas na hua tha ki shreekanth ke saamane chaarapaaee par baith jaay, hukkaa pee le yaa paan khaa le. baap kaa bhi vah itanaa maan na kart tha. shreekanth kaa bhi us par haardik pyaar tha. apne hosh mein unhone kabhi use ghudakaa tak na tha. jab vah ilaahaabaad se aate, to usake lie koi na koi vastu avashy laate. mugadar ki jodee unhone hi banavaa dee thi. pichhale saal jab usane apne se dyaudhe javaan ko naagapanchamee ke din dangal mein pachhaad diya, to unhone pulakit hokar akhaade mein hi jaa kar use gale lagaa liya tha, paanch rupaye ke paise lutaaye the. aise bhai ke munh se aaj aisee dil-vidaarak baat sunakar laalabihaaree ko badi pachhataavaa hui. vah phoot-phoot kar rone lagaa. isamen shak nahin ki apne kiye par pachhta raha tha. bhai ke aane se ek din pahale se uski chhaatee dhadakatee thi ki dekhoon bhaiyaa kya kehte hain. main unake sammukh kaise jaaoongaa, unse kaise boloongaa, meri aankhe unake saamane kaise uthegee. usane samajhaa tha ki bhaiyaa mujhe bulaakar samajhaa denge. is ummeed ke vipareet aaj usane unhen nirdayataa ki moorti bane hue paayaa. vah bevakooph tha. lekin uska man keht tha ki bhaiyaa mere saath anyaay kar rahe hain. yadi shreekanth use akele mein bulaakar do-chaar baaten kah dete; itanaa hi nahin do-chaar tamaache bhi lagaa dete to kadaachit use itanaa du:kha na hot; par bhai kaa yah kehna ki ab main isakee soorat nahin dekhna chahta, laalabihaaree se sahaa na gaya! vah rotaa hua ghar aay. kothaaree mein jaa kar kapade pahane, aankhe ponchhee, jisamen koi yah na samajhe ki rotaa tha. tab aanandee ke dvaar par aakar bol—bhaabhee, bhaiyaa ne phaisalaa kiya hai ki vah mere saath is ghar mein na rahenge. ab vah mera munh nahin dekhna chahte; isalie ab main jat hoon. unhen phir munh na dikhaaoongaa! mujhse jo kuchh aparaadh hua, use kshamaa karn.
yah kehte-kehte laalabihaaree kaa galaa bhar aay.
4
jis samay laalabihaaree sinh sir jhukaaye aanandee ke dvaar par khad tha, usi samay shreekanth sinh bhi aankhe laal kiye baahar se aaye. bhai ko khadaa dekh, to napharat se aankhe pher leen, aur kataraa kar nikal gaye. maanon uski parachhaahee se door bhaagate hon.
aanandee ne laalabihaaree ki shikaayat to ki thi, lekin ab man mein pachhta rahi thi vah svabhaav se hi dayaavatee thi. use isakaa tanik bhi dhyaan na tha ki baat itanee badh jaayagee. vah man mein apne pati par jhunjhalaa rahi thi ki yah itane garam kyon hote hain. us par yah dar bhi lagaa hua tha ki kaheen mujhse ilaahaabaad chalne ko kahen, to kaise kya karungee. is beech mein jab usane laalabihaaree ko daravaaje par khade yah kehte sunaa ki ab main jat hoon, mujhse jo kuchh aparaadh hua, kshamaa karn, to uska raha-sahaa gussaa bhi paani ho gaya. vah rone lagee. man kaa mail dhone ke lie aankh-jal se upayukt aur koi vastu nahin hai.
shreekanth ko dekhakar aanandee ne kahaa—laalaa baahar khade bahut ro rahe hain.
shreekanth--to main kya karoon?
aanandee—bheetar bulaa lo. meri jeebh mein aag lage! maine kahan se yah jhagadaa uthaayaa.
shreekanth--main na bulaaoongaa.
aanandee--pachhataaoge. unhen bahut pachhataavaa ho gayi hai, aisaa na ho, kaheen chal den.
shreekanth na uthe. itane mein laalabihaaree ne phir kahaa--bhaabhee, bhaiyaa se mera pranaam kah do. vah mera munh nahin dekhna chahte; isalie main bhi apna munh unhen na dikhaaoongaa.
laalabihaaree itanaa kah kar laut padaa, aur sheeghrataa se daravaaje ki ora badhaa. ant mein aanandee kamare se nikalee aur uska haath pakad liya. laalabihaaree ne peechhe phir kar dekh aur onkhon mein aansoo bhare bol--mujhe jaane do.
aanandee-- kahan jaate ho?
laalabihaaree-- jahaan koi mera munh na dekhe.
aanandee—- main na jaane doongee?
laalabihaaree—- main tum logon ke saath rehne yogy nahin hoon.
aanandee—-tumhen meri saugandh ab ek pag bhi aage na badhaanaa.
laalabihaaree—jab tak mujhe yah na maaloom ho jaay ki bhaiyaa kaa man meri taraph se saaph ho gaya, tab tak main is ghar mein kabhi nahin na rahoongaa.
aanandee—main eeshvar ko saakshee de kar kehti hoon ki tumhari ora se mere man mein tanik bhi mail nahin hai.
ab shreekanth kaa dil bhi pighalaa. unhone baahar aakar laalabihaaree ko gale lagaa liya. donon bhai khoob phoot-phoot kar roye. laalabihaaree ne sisakate hue kahaa—bhaiyaa, ab kabhi mata kehna ki tumhara munh na dekhoongaa. isake sivaa aap jo dand denge, main saharsh sveekaar karoongaa.
shreekanth ne kaanpate hue aavaaz mein kahaa--lalloo! ina baaton ko bilkul bhool jaao. eeshvar chaahegaa, to phir aisaa avasar na aavegaa.
beneemaadhav sinh baahar se aa rahe the. donon bhaaiyon ko gale milate dekhakar khushi se pulakit ho gaye. bol uthe—bade ghar ki betiyaan aisee hi hoti hain. bigadataa hua kaam banaa letee hain.
gaanv mein jisane yah vrittaant sunaa, usi ne ina shabdon mein aanandee ki udaarataa ko saraahaa—‘bade ghar ki betiyaan aisee hi hoti hain.'
