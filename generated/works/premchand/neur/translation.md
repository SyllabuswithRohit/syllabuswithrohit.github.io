# Neur

**Munshi Premchand**

1
aakaash mein chaandee ke pahaad bhaag rahe the, takaraa rahe the gale mil rahen the, jaise soory megh sangraam chhidaa hua ho. kabhee chhaayaa ho jati thi kabhee tej dhoop chamak uthatee thi. barasaat ke din the. umas ho rahi thi. havaa badan ho gayee thi.
gaavan ke baahar kaee majoor ek khet kee med baandh rahe, the. nange badan paseene mein tar kachhanee kase hue, sab ke sab phaavade se mitatee khodakar med par rakhate jate the. paani se mittee naram ho gayee thi.

gobar-tumane tamakhoo piye binaa kaise raha jat hai neur daadaa? yahan to chaahe rotee ne mile lekin tamaakhoo ke binaa nahin raha jat. deenaa-to yahan se aakar rotee banaaoge daadaa? buchhiyaa kuchh nahin karti? hamse to daadaa aisee mehariy se ek din na pate.

gobar-tumane use sir chadhaa rakhaa hai, nahin to kaam kyo na karti? maje se khaat par baithee chilam peetee rehti hai aur saare gaanv se ladaa karti hai toom boodhe ho gaye, lekin woh to ab bhee javaan banee hai.
deenaa-javaan aurat usakee kya baraabaree karegee? sendur, tikulee, kaajal, mehadee mein to usakaa man basaataa hai. binaa kinaaradaar rangeen dhotee ke use kabhee udekhaa hee nahin us par gahaanon se bhee jee nahin bharataa. tum gaoo ho isase nibaah ho jat hai, nahin to ab tak galee galee thokaren khaatee hoti.
gobar - mujhe to usake banaav singaar par gussaa aataahai. kaat kuchhan karegee; par khaane pahanane ko achchhaa hee chaahie.
neur-tum kya jaano betaa jab woh aayee thi to mere ghar saat hal kee khetee hoti thi. raanee banee baithee rehti thi. jamaanaa badal gaya, to kya hua. usakaa man to vahee hai. ghadee bhar choolhe ke saamane baith jati hai to kya hua! usakaa man to vahee hai. ghadee bhar choolhe ke saamane baith jati hai to aankhe laal ho jati hai aur mood thaamakar pad jati hai. majhase to yah nahee dekh jat. isee din raat ke lie to aadamee shaadee byaah kart hai aur isame kya rakhaa hai. yahan se jaakar rotee banaaungaa paani, laaoogaan, tab do kaur khaayegee. nahin to mujhe kya tha tumhaaree tarah chaar phankee maarakar ek lotaa paani pee letaa. jab se bitiyaa mar gayee. tab se to woh aur bhee last ho gayee. yah badaa bhaaree dhakkaa lagaa. maan kee mamataa ham-tum kya samajhegen betaa! pahale to kabhee kabhee daant bhee detaa tha. abakis munh se daantoon?
deenaa-tum kal ped kaahe ko chadhe the, abhee goolar kaun pakee hai?

ghar pahunchakar neur ne lotaa aur dor uthaayaa aur nahaane chalaa ki aurat ne khaat par lete-lete kahaa- itanee der kyon kar diya karte ho? aadamee kaam ke peechhe paraan thode hee detaa hai? jab majooree sab ke baraabar milatee hai to kyo kaam kaam kepeechhe marate ho?
neur kaa ant: karan ek maadhury se saraabor ho gaya. usake aatmasamarpan se bhare hue pyaar mein main kee gandh bhee to nahin thi. kitanee pyaar! aur kise usake aaraam kee, usake marane jeene kee phikr hai? phir yah kyon na apanee budhiyaa ke lie mare? bol-too una janam mein koi devee rahi hogee budhiyaa, sach.
'achchhaa rehne do yah chaapaloosee. hamaare aage ab kaun baithaa hua hai, jisake lie itanee haay-haay karte ho? '
neur gaj bhar kee chhaatee kiye snaan karne chalaa gaya. lautakar usane motee motee rotiyaan banaayee. aaloo choolhe mein daal diye. unakaa bhurataa banaayaa, phir budhiyaa aur woh dono saath khaane baithe.
budhiyaa-meree jaat se tumhe koi sukh na milaa. pade-pade khaatee hoon aur tumhe tang karti hoon aur isase to kaheen achchhaa tha ki bhagavaan mujhe uthaa lete. '
'bhagavaan aayenge to mai kahoongaa pahale mujhe le chalo. tab is soonee jhopadee mein kaun rahegaa. '
'tum na rahoge, to meree kya dashaa hogee. yah sochakar meree aankho mein andheraa aa jat hai. maine koi badaa pun kiya tha. ki tumhe paayaa tha. kisee aur ke saath meraa bhalaa kya nibaah hot? ' aise meethe sanntosh ke lie neur kya nahin kar daalanaa chaahataa tha.
aalasin lobhin, svaarthin budhiyaanapanee jeebh par keval mithaas rakhakar neur ko nachaatee thi jaise koi shikaaree kantiye mein chaaraa lagaakar machhalee ko khilaataa hai. pahale kaun mare, is vishay par aaj yah pahalee hee baar baatacheet na huee thi. isake pahale bhee kitanee hee baar yah sawal uthaa tha aur yaa hee chhod diya gaya tha; ! lekin na jaane kyon neur ne apanee digree kar lee thi aur use phaisalaa tha ki pahale main jaaoongaa. usake peechhe bhee budhiyaa jab tak raha aaraam se rahe, kisee ke saamane haath na phailaaye, iseelie woh marataa rehta tha, jisame haath mein chaar paise jamaaho jaaye. ' mushkil se mushkil kaam jise koi na kar sake neur kart din bhar phaavade kudaal kaa kaam karne ke baad raat ko woh ookh ke dinon mein kisee kee ookh perataa yaa kheton kee rakhavaalee kart, lekin din nikalate jate the aur jo kuchh kamaataa tha woh bhee nikalaa jat tha. budhiyaa ke bagair woh jeevan. .. nahin, isakee woh kalpanaa hee na kar sakataa tha.
lekin aaj kee baate ne neur ko sashank kar diya. jal mein ek boond rang kee bhaati yah shakaa usake man me samaa kar atirajit hone lagee.

2

gaanv mein neur ko kaam kee kamee na thi, par majooree to vahee milatee thi, jo ab tak milatee aayee thi; is mandee mein woh majooree bhee nahee raha gayee thi. ekaaek gaanv mein ek saadhu kaheen se ghoomate-phirate aa nikale aur neur ke ghar ke saamane hee peepal kee chhaanh me unakee dhunee jal gaee gaanv vaalo ne apanaa dhany bhaagy samajhaa. baabaajee kaa sevaa stkaar karne ke lie sabhee jamaa ho gaye. kaheen se lakadee aa gayee se kaheen se bichhaane ko kambal kaheen se aat-daal. neur ke paas kya tha. ? baabaajee ke lie bhejan banaane kee sevaa usane lee. charas aa gayee, dam lagane lagaa.
do teen din mein hee baabaajee kee keerti phailane lagee. woh aatmadarshee hai bhoot aane vaalaa samay ba baat dete hai. lobh to chhoo nahin gaya. paisaa haath se nahin chhoote aur khana bhee kya karte hai. aath pahar mein ek do baatiyaan khaa lee; lekin munh deepak kee tarah damak raha hai. kitanee meethee baanee hai. ! aasaan dil neur baabaajee kaa sabase badaa bhakt tha. us par kaheen baabaajee kee raham ho gayee. to paaras hee ho jaayagaa. saaraa dukh daliddar mit jaayagaa.
bhaktajan ek-ek karake chale gaye the. khoob kadaake kee thand pad rahi thi keval neur baithaa baabaajee ke paanv dabaa raha tha.
baabaa jee ne kahaa- bachchaa! sansaar maayaa hai isamen kyon phanse ho?
neur ne nat mastak hokar kahaa-ajnaanee hun mahaaraaj, kya karoon?
aurat hai use kis par chhodoon!
'too samajhataa hai too aurat kaa paalan kart hai? '
'aur kaun sahaaraa hai use baabaajee? '
'eeshvar kud nahee hai too hee sab kuchh hai? '
neur ke man mein jaise jnaan-uday ho gaya. tu itanaa abhimaanee ho gaya hai. teraa itanaa dimaag! majadooree karte karte jaan jati hai aur too samajhataa hai mai hee budhiyaa kaa sab kuchh hoon. prabhu jo sansaar kaa paalan karte hai, tu unake kaam mein dakhal dene kaa daavaa kart hai. usake aasaan karte hai. aasthaa kee dhvani see uthakar use dhikkaarane lagee bol-ajnaanee hoon mahaaraaj!
isase zyada woh aur kuchh na kah sakaa. aakhon se gareeb udaasee ke aansu girane lage.
baabaajee ne tejasvitaa se kahaa -'dekhanaa chaahataa hai eeshvar kaa chamatkaar! woh chaahe to pal bhar me tujhe lakhapati kar de. pal bhar mein teree saaree chintaaen. har le! mai usakaa ek tuchchh bhakt hoon kaakavishtaa; lekin mujhemen bhee itanee shakti hai ki tujhe paaras banaa doon. too saaph dil kaa, sachchaa eemaanadaar aadamee hai. moojhe tujhapar raham aati hai. maine is gaanv mein sabako dhyaan se dekh. kisee mein shakti nahin yakeen nahin. tujhame maine bhakt kaa dil paayaa tere paas kuchh chaandee hai?
neur ko jaan pad raha tha ki saamane svarg kaa dvaar hai.
'das paanch rupaye hoge mahaaraaj? '
'kuchh chaandee ke toote phoote gahane nahin hai? '
'gharavaalee ke paas kuchh gahane hai. '
'kal raat ko jitanee chaand mil sake yahan laa aur eeshvar kee prabhutaa dekh. tere saamane mai chaandee kee haandee mein rakhakar isee dhunee mein rakh doongaa praat: kaal aakar haandee nikalaa lenaa; magar itanaa yaad rakhanaa ki una asharphiyo ko agar sharaab peene mein juaa khelane mein yaa kisee doosare bure kaam mein kharch kiya to kodhee ho jaaegaa. ab jaa so raha. haan itanaa aur sun le isakee charchaa kisee se mata karn gharavaalon se bhee nahin. '
neur ghar chalaa, to aisaa khush tha maano eeshvar kaa haath usake sir par hai. raat-bhar use neend nahee aayee. sabere usane kaee aadamiyon se do-do chaar chaar rupaye udhaar lekar pachaas rupaye jode! log usakaa yakeen karte the. kabhee kisee kaa paisaa bhee na dabaataa tha. vaade kaa pakkaa neeyat kaa saaph. rupaye milane mein dikkat na huee. pachees rupaye usake paas the. budhiyaa se gahane kaise le. chaal chalee. tere gahane bahut maile ho gaye hai. khataaee se saaph kar le. raat bhar khataaee mein rehne se nae ho jaayege. budhiyaa chakame mein aa gayee. haandee mein khataaee daalakar gahane bhigo die aur jab raat ko woh so gayee to neur ne rupaye bhee usee haandee me daalaa die aur baabaajee ke paas pahunchaa. baabaajee ne kuchh mantr padhaa. haandee ko chhoonee kee raakh mein rakhaa aur neur ko aasheervaad dekar vidaa kiya.
raat bhar karabaten badalane ke baad neur munh andhere baabaa ke darshan karne gaya. magar baabaajee kaa wahan pataa na tha. adheer hokar usane dhoonee kee jalatee huee raakh tatolee. haandee gaayab thi. chhaatee dhak-dhak karne lagee. badahavaas hokar baabaa ko khojane lagaa. haat kee taraph gaya. taalaab kee ora pahunchaa. das minat, bees minat, aadhaa ghantaa! baabaa kaa kaheen nishaan nahin. bhakt aane lage. baabaa kahaan gae? kambal bhee nahee baratan bhee nahin!
bhakt ne kahaa-ramate saadhuon kaa kya thikaanaa! aaj yahan kal wahan, ek jagah rahe to saadhu kaise? logo se hel-mel ho jaae, bandhan mein pad jaayen.
'siddh the. '
'lobh to chhoo nahin gaya tha. '
neur kahaa hai? us par badee raham karte the. usse kah gaye hoge. '
neur kee talaash hone lagee, kaheen pataa nahin. itane mein budhiyaa neur ko pukaaratee huee ghar mein se nikalee. phir kolaahal mach gaya. budhiyaa rotee thi aur neur ko gaaliyaan detee thi.
neur kheto kee medo se betahaashaa bhaagataa chalaa jat tha. maano us paapee sansaar se nikal jaaegaa.
ek aadamee ne kahaa- neur ne kal mujhse paanch rupaye liye the. aaj saanjh ko dene ko kahaa tha.
doosaraa-hamse bhee do rupaye aaj hee ke vaade par liye the.
budhiyaa royee-daadheejaar mere saare gahane le gaya. pachees rupaye rakhe the. woh bhee uthaa le gaya.
log samajh gaye, baabaa koi dhoort tha. neur ko saajhaa de gaya. aise-aise thag pade hai sansaar mein. neur ke baare mein baare mein kisee ko aisaa shak nahin thi. bechaaraa seedhaa aadamee aa gaya pattee mein. maare laaj ke kaheen chhipaa baithaa hogaa.

teen maheene gujar gaye.
jhaansee jile mein dhasaan nadee ke kinaare ek chhotaa saa gaanv hai- kaasheepur nadee ke kinaare ek pahaadee teelaa hai. usee par kaee din se ek saadhu ne apanaa aasan jamaayaa hai. naate kad kaa aadamee hai, kaale tave kaa-saa rang deh gathee huee. yah neur hai jo saadhu besh mein duniya ko dhokhaa de raha hai. vahee aasaan nishkapat neur hai jisane kabhee paraaye maal kee ora aankh nahin uthaayo jo paseenaa kee rotee khaakar magn tha. ghar kee gaavan kee aur budhiyaa kee yaad ek pal bhee use nahin bhoolatee is jeevan mein phir koi din aayegaa. ki woh apne ghar pahunchegaa aur phir us sansaar me hansataa- khelataa apanee chhotee-chhotee chintaao aur chhotee-chhotee aashaao ke beech khushi se rahegaa. woh jeevan kitanaa sukhamay tha. jitane the. sab apne the sabhee aadar karte the. hamadardee rakhate the. din bhar kee majooree, thodaa-saa anaaj yaa thode se paise lekaar ghar aat tha, to budhiyaa kitane meethe pyaar se usakaa svaagat karti thi. woh saaree mehanat, saaree thakaavat jaise use mithaas mein sanakar aur meethee ho jati thi. haay woh din phir kab aayege? na jaane budhiyaa kaise rehti hogee. kaun use paan kee tarah pheregaa? kaun use pakaakar khilaayegaa? ghar mein paisaa bhee to nahin chhodaa gahane tak dabaa diye. tab use gussaa aat. ki us baabaa ko paa jaay, to kachch heekhaa jaae. haay lobh! lobh!
unake anany bhakto mein ek sundaree naujavaan ladakee bhee thi jisake pati ne use tyaag diya tha. usakaa baap phaujee-penshanar tha, ek padhe likhe aadamee se ladakee kaa vivaah kiya: lekin ladakaa mon ke kehne mein tha aur naujavaan ladakee kee apanee saans se na patatee. woh chaa hatee thi shauhar ke saath saas se alag rahe shauhar apanee maan se alag hone par na raajee hua. woh ruthakar maike chalee aayee. tab se teen saal ho gaye the aur sasuraal se ek baar bhee bulaavaa na aay na patidev hee aaye. naujavaan ladakee kisee tarah pati ko apne vash mein kar lenaa chaahatee thi. mahaatmaaon ke lie tarah pati ko apne vash mein kar lenaa chaahatee thi mahaatmaao ke lie kisee kaa dil pher denaa aisaa kya mushikal hai! haan, unakee raham chaahie.
ek din usane ekaant mein baabaajee se apanee vipati kah sunaayee. neur ko jis shikaar kee toh thi woh aaj milataa hooaa jaan padaa ganbheer bhaav se bol-betee mai na siddh hoon na mahaatmaa na mai sansaar ke jhamelo mein padataa hoon par teree saradhaa aur parem dekhakar tujh par raham aati hau. bhagavaan ne chaahaa to teraa manoradh pooraa ho jaayegaa.
'aap samarth hain aur mujhe aapake oopar yakeen hai. '
'bhagavaan kee jo ichchhaa hogee vahee hogaa. '
'is abhaaginee kee dogee aap vahee hogaa. '
'mere bhagavaan aap hee ho. '
neur ne maano dharm-sankat mein padakar kahaa-lekin betee, us kaam mein badaa anushthaan karn padegaa. aur anushthaan mein saikado hajaaron kaa kharch hai. us par bhee teraa kaaj siddh hogaa yaa nahee, yah mai nahin kah sakataa. haan mujhse jo kuchh ho sakegaa, woh mai kar doongaa. par sab kuchh bhagavaan ke haath mein hai. mai maayaa ko haath se nahin chhootaa; lekin teraa dukh nahee dekh jat.
usee raat ko naujavaan ladakee ne apne sone ke gahanon kee petaaree laakar baabaajee ke charanon par rakh dee baabaajee ne kaanpate hue haathon se petaaree kholee aur chandramaa ke ujjaval prakaash mein aabhooshano ko dekh. unakee baadhe jhapak gayeen yah saaree maayaa unakee hai woh unake saamane haath baadhe khadee kah rahi hai mujhe angeekaar keejie kuchh bhee to karn nahee hai keval petaaree lekar apne sirahaane rakh lenaa hai aur naujavaan ladakee ko aasheervaad dekar vidaa kar denaa hai. praat kaal woh aayegee us vakt woh utanaa door hogen jahaan unakee taage le jaayegee. aisaa aashaateet achchhee kismat! jab woh rupaye se bharee thailiyaan lie gaanv mein pahunchege aur budhiyaa ke saamane rakh dege! oha! isase bade khushi kee to woh kalpanaa bhee nahin kar sakate.
lekin na jaane kyon itanaa zara saa kaam bhee usse nahin ho sakataa tha. woh petaaree ko uthaakar apne sirahaane kanbal ke neeche dabaakar nahin rakh sakataa. hai. kuchh nahin; par usake lie asoojh hai, asaadhy hai woh us petaaree kee ora haath bhee nahee badhaa sakataa hai itanaa kehne me kaun see duniya ulatee jati hai. ki betee ise uthaakar is kambal ke neeche rakh de. jabaan kat to na jaayagee, ;magar ab use maaloom hot ki jabaan par bhee usakaa kaaboo nahee hai. aankho ke ishaare se bhee yah kaam ho sakataa hai. lekin is samay aankhe bheed bagaavat kar rahi hai. man kaa raajaa itane matriyon aur saamanto ke hote hue bhee ashakt hai nireeh hai laakh rupaye kee thailee saamane rakhee ho nangee talavaar haath mein ho gaay majaboot rassee ke saamane bandhee ho, kya us gaay kee garadan par usake haath uthegen. kabhee nahin koi usakee garadan bhale hee kaat le. woh gaoo kee hatyaa nahee kar sakataa. woh parityaaktaa use usee gau kee hatyaa nahee kar sakataa woh papityaaktaa use usee gaoo kee tarah lagar hee thi. jis avasar ko woh teen maheene khoj raha hai use paakar aaj usakee aatmaa kaanp rahi hai. trishnaa kisee vany jantu kee tarah apne sanskaare se aakhetapriy hai lekin janjeero se badhe-badhe usake nakh gir gaye hai aur daatan kamajor ho gaye hain.
usane rote hue kahaa-betee petaaree uthaa le jaao. mai tumhaaree pareekshaa kar raha tha. manorath pooraa ho jaayegaa.
chond nadee ke paar vriksho kee god mein vishraam kar chukaa tha. neur dheere se uthaa aur dhasaan me snaan karake ek ora chal diya. bhabhoot aur tilak se use napharat ho rahi thi use hairaanee ho raha tha ki woh ghar se nikalaa hee kaise? thode upahaas ke dar se! use apne andar ek vichitr ullaas kaa tajurbaa ho raha tha maano woh bediyo se mukt ho gaya ho koi bahut badee chijay praapt kee ho.

aathave din neur gaanv pahunch gaya. ladako ne daudakar uchhal koodakar, usakee lakadee usake haath usakaa svaagat kiya.
ek ladake ne kahaa kaakee to mar gayee daadaa.
neur ke paanv jaise bandh gaye munh ke dono kone neeche jhuke gaye. deenavishaad aakhon mein chamak uthaa kuchh bol nahin, kuchh poochhaa bhee nahin. palbhar jaise nissanjn khadaa raha phir badee tejee se apanee jhopadee kee ora chalaa. baalakavrind bhee usake peechhe daude magar unakee sharaarat aur chanchalataa bhaagachalee thi. jhopadee khulee padee thi budhiyaa kee chaarapaaee jahaa kee tahaan thi. usakee chilam aur naariyal jyo ke jyo dhare hue the. ek kone mein do chaar mitatee aur peetal ke baratan pade hune the ladek baahar hee khade raha gaye jhepadee ke andar kaise jaay wahan budhiyaa baithee hai.
gaanv me bhagadad mach gayee. neur daadaa aa gaye. jhopadee ke dvaar par bheed lag gayee prashano kaa taantaa badh gaya. -toom itane dino kahaan the. daadaa? tumhaare jaane ke baad teesare hee din kaakee chal baseen raat din tumhe gaaliyaan detee thi. marate marate tumhe gariyaatee hee rahi. teesare din aaye to meree padee kthee. tum itane din kahaa rahe?
neur ne koi jawab na diya. keval shuny niraash karun aahat netro se logo kee ora dekhataa raha maano usakee vaanee har leegayee hai. us din se kisee ne use bolte yaa rote-hansate nahin dekh.
gaanv se aadh meel par pakkee sadak hai. achchhee aamadaraphat hai. neur bed sabere jaakar sadak ke kinaare ek ped ke neeche baith jat hai. kisee se kuchh maangataa nahee par raahageer koochh na kuchh de hee dete hai. - chebanaa anaaj paise. sadhyaan sayam woh apanee jhopadee me aa jat hai, chiraag jalaataa hai khana banaataa hai, khana hai aur usee khaat par padaa rehta hai. usake jeevan, mai jo ek sanchaalak shakti thi, woh lupt ho gayee hai woh ab keval jeevadhaaree hai. kitanee gaharee manovyadhaa hai. gaanv mein pleg aay. log ghar chhod chhodakar bhaagane lage neur ko ab kisee kee paravaah na thi. na kisee ko usse dar tha na prem. saaraa gaanv bhaag gaya. neur apanee jhopadee se na nikalaa aur aaj bhee woh usee peu ke neeche sadak ke kinaare usee tarah chup baithaa hua najar aat hai- nishchesht, nirjeev.
