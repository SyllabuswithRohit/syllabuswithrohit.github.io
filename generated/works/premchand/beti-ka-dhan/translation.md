# Beti Ka Dhan

**Munshi Premchand**

betavaa nadee do oonche kagaaron ke beech is tarah munh chhipaaye hue thi jaise nirmal hridayon mein saahas aur utsaah ki maddham jyoti chhipee rehti hai. isake ek kagaar par ek chhota-saa gaanv basaa hai jo apne bhagn jaateey chihnon ke lie bahut hi prasiddh hai. jaateey gaathaaon aur chihnon par mar mitanevaale log is bhaavanasthaan par bade pyaar aur shraddhaa ke saath aate aur gaanv kaa boodhaa kevat sukkhoo chaudharee unhen uski parikramaa karaataa aur raanee ke mahal, raajaa kaa darabaar aur kunvar ki baithak ke mite hue chiddon ko dikhaataa. vah ek uchchhvaas lekar rundhe hue gale se keht, mahaashay! ek vah samay tha ki kevaton ko machhaliyon ke inaam mein asharphiyaan milatee theen. kahaar mahal mein jhaadoo dete hue asharphiyaan bator le jaate the. betavaa nadee roj chadh kar mahaaraaj ke charan chhoone aatee thi. yah prataap aur yah tej tha, lekin aaj isakee yah dashaa hai. ina sundar uktiyon par kisee kaa yakeen jamaanaa chaudharee ke vash ki baat na thi, par sunanevaale uski sahridayataa tathaa anuraag ke zaroor kaayal ho jaate the.
sukkhoo chaudharee udaar aadamee the, lekin jitanaa bada munh tha, utanaa bada graas na tha. teen ladake, teen bahuen aur kaee pautr-pautriyaan theen. ladakee keval ek gangaajalee thi jisakaa abhi tak gaunaa nahin hua tha. chaudharee ki yah sabase pichhalee bachchaa thi. aurat ke mar jaane par usane isako bakaree kaa doodh pilaa-pilaa kar paalaa tha. parivaar mein khaanevaale to itane the, par khetee sirph ek hal ki hoti thi. jyon-tyon kar nirvaah hot tha, lekin sukkhoo ki vriddhaavasthaa aur puraatattv jnaan ne use gaanv mein vah maan aur pratishthaa pradaan kar rakkhee thi, jise dekh kar jhagadoo saahu bheetar hi bheetar jalate the. sukkhoo jab gaanvavaalon ke samaksh, haakimon se haath phenk-phenk kar baaten karne lagta aur khandaharon ko ghumaa-phiraa kar dikhaane lagta tha to jhagadoo saahu jo chaparaasiyon ke dhakke khaane ke dar se kareeb nahin phatakate the tadap-tadap kar raha jaate the. isalie ve sadaa is shubh avasar ki prateekshaa karte rehte the, jab sukkhoo par apne paisaa dvaaraa prabhutv jamaa saken.
is gaanv ke jameendaar thaakur jeetanasinh the, jinakee begaar ke maare gaanvavaalon kaa naakon dam tha. us saal jab jilaa majistret kaa dauraa hua aur vah yahan ke puraatan chihnon ki sair karne ke lie padhaare, to sukkhoo chaudharee ne dabee jabaan se apne gaanvavaalon ki duhkh-kahaanee unhen sunaayee. haakimon se baatacheet karne mein use tanik bhi dar na hot tha. sukkhoo chaudharee ko khoob maaloom tha ki jeetanasinh se raar machaanaa sinh ke munh mein sir dena hai. lekin jab gaanvavaale kehte the ki chaudharee tumhari aise-aise haakimon se mitaaee hai aur ham logon ko raat-din rote katataa hai to phir tumhari yah mitrataa kis din kaam aavegee. paropakaaraay sataam vibhootayah. tab sukkhoo kaa mizaaj aasamaan par chadh jat tha. ghadee bhar ke lie vah jeetanasinh ko bhool jat tha. majistret ne jeetanasinh se isakaa jawab maangaa. udhar jhagadoo saahu ne chaudharee ke is saahasapoorn svaameedroh ki riport jeetanasinh ko dee. thaakur sahab jal kar aag ho gaye. apne kaarinde se bakaayaa lagaan ki bahee maangee. sanyogavash chaudharee ke jimme is saal kaa kuchh lagaan baakee tha. kuchh to paidaavaar kam hui, us par gangaajalee kaa byaah karn padaa. chhoti bahoo nath ki rat lagaaye hue thi; vah banavaanee padee. ina sab kharchon ne haath bilakul khaalee kar diya tha. lagaan ke lie kuchh zyaadaa phikr nahin thi. vah is abhimaan mein bhoolaa hua tha ki jis jabaan mein haakimon ko khush karne ki shakti hai, kya vah thaakur sahab ko apna lakshy na banaa sakegee? boodhe chaudharee idhar to apne garv mein nishchint the aur udhar una par bakaayaa lagaan ki naalish thuk gayi. samman aa pahunchaa. doosare din peshee ki taareekh pad gayi. chaudharee ko apna jaadoo chalaane kaa avasar na milaa.
jin logon ke badhaave mein aa kar sukkhoo ne thaakur se chhedachhaad ki thi, unakaa darshan milna durlabh ho gaya. thaakur sahab ke sahane aur pyaade gaanv mein cheel ki tarah mandaraane lage. unake dar se kisee ko chaudharee ki parachhaaeen kaatane kaa saahas na hot tha. kachaharee wahan se teen meel par thi. barasaat ke din, raaste mein thaur-thaur paani, umadee hui nadiyaan, raastaa kachchaa, bailagaadee kaa nibaah nahin, pairon mein bal nahin, isalie adamapairavee mein mukadame ekataraphaa phaisalaa ho gaya.
kurkee kaa notis pahunchaa to chaudharee ke haath-paanv phool gaye. sari chaturaaee bhool gayi. chupachaap apni khaat par padaa-padaa nadee ki ora taakataa aur apne man mein keht, kya mere jeete jee ghar mittee mein mil jaayagaa. mere ina bailon ki sundar jodee ke gale mein aah! kya doosaron kaa juaa padegaa? yah sochte-sochte uski aankhen bhar aateen. vah bailon se lipat kar rone lagaa, lekin bailon ki aankhon se kyon aansoo jaaree the? ve naand mein munh kyon nahin daalate the? kya unake dil par bhi apne svaamee ke duhkh ki chot pahunch rahi thi!
phir vah apne jhopade ko vikal nayanon se nihaar kar dekhta. aur man mein sochta, kya hamako is ghar se nikalna padegaa? yah poorvajon ki nishaanee kya hamaare jeete jee chhin jaayagee?
kuchh log pareekshaa mein dridh rehte hain aur kuchh log isakee halakee aanch bhi nahin sah sakate. chaudharee apni khaat par udaas pade ghanton apne kuladev mahaaveer aur mahaadev ko manaayaa kart aur unakaa gun gaayaa kart. uski chintaadagdh aatmaa ko aur koi sahaaraa na tha.
isamen koi shak na tha ki chaudharee ki teenon bahuon ke paas gahane the, par aurat kaa gahanaa ookh kaa ras hai, jo perane hi se nikalataa hai. chaudharee jaati kaa ochhaa par svabhaav kaa oonchaa tha. use aisee neech baat bahuon se kehte sankoch hot tha. kadaachit yah neech soch usake dil mein utpann hi nahin hua tha, lekin teenon bete yadi zara bhi samajh se kaam lete to boodhe ko devataaon ki sharan lene ki aavashyakataa na hoti. lekin yahan to baat hi niraalee thi. bade ladake ko ghaat ke kaam se phurasat na thi. baakee do ladake is jatil sawal ko vichitr roop se hal karne ke mansoobe baandh rahe the.
manjhale jheengur ne munh banaa kar kahaa- unh! is gaanv mein kya dharaa hai. jahaan hi kamaaoongaa, vaheen khaaoongaa par jeetanasinh ki moonchhen ek-ek karake chun loongaa.
chhote phakkad ainth kar bole- moonchhen tum chun lena! naak main udaa doongaa. nakataa banaa ghoomegaa.
is par donon khoob hanse aur machhalee maarane chal diye.
is gaanv mein ek boodhe braahman bhi rehte the. mandir mein poojaa karte aur nity apne yajamaanon ko darshan dene nadee paar jaate, par kheve ke paise na dete. teesare din vah jameendaar ke guptacharon ki aankh bachaakar sukkhoo ke paas aaye aur sahaanubhooti ke aavaaz mein bole chaudharee! kal hi tak miyaad hai aur tum abhi tak pade-pade so rahe ho. kyon nahin ghar ki cheej dhoondh-dhaandh kar kisee aur jagah bhej dete? na ho samadhiyaane pathavaa do. jo kuchh bach rahe, wahi sahee. ghar ki mittee khod kar thode hi koi le jaayagaa.
chaudharee letaa tha, uth baithaa aur aakaash ki ora nihaar kar bol- jo kuchh uski ichchhaa hai, vah hogaa. mujhse yah jaal na hogaa.
idhar kaee din ki lagaataar bhakti aur upaasanaa ke vajah chaudharee kaa man shuddh aur pavitr ho gaya tha. use chhal-prapanch se napharat ho gayi thi. pandit jee jo is kaam mein siddhahast the, lajjit ho gaye.
lekin chaudharee ke ghar ke any logon ko eeshvarechchhaa par itanaa bharosaa na tha. dheere-dheere ghar ke bartan-bhaande khisakaaye jaate the. anaaj kaa ek daanaa bhi ghar mein na rehne paayaa. raat ko naav ladee hui jati aur udhar se khaalee lautatee thi. teen din tak ghar mein choolhaa na jalaa. boodhe chaudharee ke munh mein ann ki kaun kahe paani ki ek boond bhi na padee. striaayaan bhaad se chane bhunaa kar chabaateen, aur ladake machhaliyaan bhoon-bhoon kar udaate. lekin boodhe ki is ekaadashee mein yadi koi shareek tha to vah uski betee gangaajalee thi. yah bechaaree apne boodhe baap ko chaarapaaee par nirjal chhatapataate dekh bilakh-bilakh kar rotee.
ladakon ko apne maan-pitaa se vah pyaar nahin hot jo ladakiyon ko hot hai. gangaajalee is soch-soch mein magn rehti thi ki daadaa ki kis tarah madad karoon. yadi ham sab bhai-bahan mil kar jeetanasinh ke paas jaakar dayaa-bhikshaa ki praarthanaa karen to ve avashy maan jaayange; lekin daadaa ko kab yah sveekaar hogaa. vah yadi ek din bade sahab ke paas chale jaayan to sab kuchh baat ki baat mein ban jaay. lekin unki to jaise samajh hi maaree gayi hai. isi udhedabun mein use ek upaay soojh padaa, kumhalaayaa hua mukhaaravind khil uthaa.
pujaaree jee sukkhoo chaudharee ke paas se uth kar chale gaye the aur chaudharee uchch aavaaz se apne soye hue devataaon ko pukaar-pukaar kar bulaa rahe the. nidaan gangaajalee unake paas jaa kar khadee ho gayi. chaudharee ne use dekh kar vismit aavaaz mein poochhaa kyon betee? itanee raat gaye kyon baahar aayee?
gangaajalee ne kahaa- baahar rehna to bhaagy mein likhaa hai, ghar mein kaise rahoon.
sukkhoo ne jor se haank lagaayee- kahan gaye tum krishnamuraaree, mere duhkh haro.
gangaajalee khadee thi, baith gayi aur dheere se bolee- bhajan gaate to aaj teen din ho gaye. ghar bachaane kaa bhi kuchh upaay sochaa ki ise yon hi mittee mein milaa doge? ham logon ko kya ped tale rakhoge?
chaudharee ne vyathit aavaaz se kahaa- betee, mujhe to koi upaay nahin soojhta. bhagavaan jo chaahenge, hogaa. veg chalo giradhar gopaal, kaahe vilamb karo.
gangaajalee ne kahaa- maine ek upaay sochaa hai, kaho to kahoon.
chaudharee uth kar baith gaye aur poochhaa- kaun upaay hai betee?
gangaajalee ne kahaa- mere gahane jhagadoo saahu ke yahan giron rakh do. maine jod liya hai. dene bhar ke rupaye ho jaayange.
chaudharee ne thandee saans le kar kahaa- betee! tumako mujhse yah baat kehte laaj nahin aatee. ved-shaastraa mein mujhe tumhaare gaanv ke kuen kaa paani peenaa bhi manaa hai. tumhari dyodhee mein bhi pair rakhne kaa nishedh hai. kya tum mujhe narak mein dhakelanaa chahti ho?
gangaajalee jawab ke lie pahale hi se taiyaar thi. bolee- main apne gahane tumhen diye thode hi detee hoon. is samay le kar kaam chalaao, chait mein chhudaa dena. chaudharee ne kadak kar kahaa yah mujhse na hogaa.
gangaajalee uttejit ho kar bolee- tumse yah na hogaa to main aap hi jaaoongee, mujhse ghar ki yah durdashaa nahin dekhee jati.
chaudharee ne jhunjhalaa kar kahaa- biraadaree mein kaun munh dikhaaoongaa?
gangaajalee ne chidh kar kahaa- biraadaree mein kaun dhindhoraa peetane jat hai.
chaudharee ne phaisalaa sunaayaa- jagahansaaee ke lie main apna dharm na bigaadoongaa.
gangaajalee bigadakar bolee- meri baat nahin maanoge to tumhaare oopar meri hatyaa padegee. main aaj hi is betavaa nadee mein kood padoongee. tumse chaahe ghar mein aag lagte dekh jaay, par mujhse to na dekh jaayagaa.
chaudharee ne thandee saans le kar kaatar aavaaz mein kahaa- betee, mera dharm naash mata karo. yadi aisaa hi hai to apni kisee bhaavaj ke gahane maang kar laao.
gangaajalee ne gambheer aavaaz mein kahaa- bhaavajon se kaun apna munh nochavaane jaayagaa. unako phikar hoti to kya munh mein dahee jamaa tha, kahateen nahin.
chaudharee niruttar ho gaye. gangaajalee ghar mein jaa kar gahanon ki pitaaree laayee aur ek-ek karake sab gahane chaudharee ke angochhe mein baandh diye. chaudharee ne aankhon mein aansoo bhar kar kahaa- haay raam, is shareer ki kya gati likhee hai! yah kah kar uthe. bahut samhaalane par bhi aankhon mein aansoo na chhipe.
raat kaa samay tha. betavaa nadee ke kinaare-kinaare raastaa ko chhodakar sukkhoo chaudharee gahanon ki gatharee kaankh mein dabaaye is tarah chupake-chupake chal rahe the maano paap ki gatharee liye jaate hain. jab vah jhagadoo saahu ke makaan ke paas pahunche to thahar gaye, aankhen khoob saaph keen, jisse kisee ko yah na bodh ho ki chaudharee rotaa tha.
jhagadoo saahu dhaage ki kamaanee ki ek motee ainak lagaaye baheekhaataa phailaaye hukkaa pee rahe the, aur deepak ke dhundhale prakaash mein una aksharon ko padhne ki bekaar cheshtaa mein lage the jinamen syaahee ki kiphaayat ki gayi thi. baar-baar ainak ko saaph karte aur aankh malate, par chiraag ki battee ukasaanaa yaa doharee battee lagaanaa shaayad isalie theek nahin samajhte the ki tel kaa apavyay hogaa. isi samay sukkhoo chaudharee ne aa kar kahaa jayaraam jee.
jhagadoo saahu ne dekh. pahachaan kar bole- jayaraam chaudharee! kaho mukadame mein kya hua? yah len-den bade jhanjhat kaa kaam hai. din bhar sir uthaane ki chhuttee nahin milatee.
chaudharee ne potalee ko khoob saavadhaanee se chhipaa kar laaparavaahee ke saath kahaa- abhi tak to kuchh nahin hua. kal ijaraayadigaree honevaalee hai. thaakur sahab ne na jaane kab kaa bair nikaalaa hai. hamako do-teen din ki bhi muhalat hoti to digaree na jaaree hone paatee. chhote sahab aur bade sahab donon hamako achchhi tarah jaanate hain. abhi isi saal maine unse nadee kinaare ghanton baaten keen, lekin ek to barasaat ke din, doosare ek din ki bhi muhalat nahin, kya kart! is samay mujhe rupayon ki phikr hai.
jhagadoo saahu ne vismit ho kar poochhaa- tumako rupayon ki phikr! ghar mein bharaa hai, vah kis din kaam aavegaa. jhagadoo saahu ne yah vyangyabaan nahin chhodaa tha. vaastav mein unhen aur saare gaanv ko yakeen tha ki chaudharee ke ghar mein lakshmee mahaaraanee kaa akhand raajy hai.
chaudharee kaa rang badalane lagaa. bole- saahu jee! rupayaa hot to kis baat ki phikr thi? tumse kaun chhipaav hai. aaj teen din se ghar mein choolhaa nahin jalaa, ronaa-peetanaa padaa hai. ab to tumhaare basaaye basoongaa. thaakur sahab ne to ujaadane mein koi kasar na chhodee.
jhagadoo saahu jeetanasinh ko khush rakhna zaroor chahte the, par saath hi chaudharee ko bhi naakhush karn manjoor na tha. yadi sood-dar-sood chhod kar mool tathaa byaaj sahaj vasool ho jaay to unhen chaudharee par mupht kaa ehasaan laadane mein koi aapatti na thi. yadi chaudharee ke aphasaron ki jaan-pahachaan ke vajah saahu jee kaa taiks se galaa chhoot jaay, jo anek upaay karne ahalakaaron ki mutthee garam karne par bhi nity prati unki tond ki tarah badhta hi jaa raha tha to kya poochhna! bole-
-kya kahen chaudharee jee, kharch ke maare aajakal ham bhi tabaah hain. lahane vasool nahin hote. taiks kaa rupayaa dena padaa. haath bilakul khaalee ho gaya. tumhen kitanaa rupayaa chaahie?
chaudharee ne kahaa- sau rupaye ki digaree hai. kharch-barch milaa kar do sau ke lagabhag samajho.
jhagadoo ab apne daanv khelne lage. poochhaa- tumhaare ladakon ne tumhari kuchh bhi madad na ki. vah sab bhi to kuchh na kuchh kamaate hi hain.
saahu jee kaa yah nishaanaa theek padaa ladakon ne laaparavaahee se chaudharee ke man mein jo kutsit bhaav bhare the vah sajeev ho gaye. bole- bhai, ladake kisee kaam ke hote to yah din kyon dekhna padataa. unhen to apne bhog-vilaas se matalab. ghar-ghar-parivaar kaa bojh to mere sir par hai. main ise jaise chaahoon, sanbhaaloon unse kuchh sarokaar nahin, marate dam bhi galaa nahin chhootataa. maroongaa to sab khaal mein bhoosaa bharaa kar rakh chhodenge. 'ghar kaaraj naanaa janjaalaa.'
jhagadoo ne teesaraa teer maaraa- kya bahuon se bhi kuchh na ban padaa.
chaudharee ne jawab diya- bahoo-bete sab apni-apni mauj mein mast hain. main teen din tak dvaar par binaa ann-jal ke padaa tha, kisee ne baat bhi nahin poochhee. kahan ki salaah, kahan ki baatacheet. bahuon ke paas rupaye na hon, par gahane to hain aur ve bhi mere banaaye hue. is durdin ke samay yadi do-do thaan utaar deteen to kya main chhudaa na detaa? sadaa yahi din thode hi rahenge.
jhagadoo samajh gaye ki yah mahaj jabaan kaa saudaa hai aur vah jabaan kaa saudaa bhoolakar bhi na karte the. bole- tumhaare ghar ke log bhi anoothe hain. kya itanaa bhi nahin jaanate ki boodhaa rupaye kahan se laavegaa? ab samay badal gaya. yaa to kuchh jaayadaad likho yaa gahane giron rakho tab jaa kar rupayaa mile. isake binaa rupaye kahan. isamen bhi jaayadaad mein saikadon bakhede pade hain. subheetaa giron rakhne mein hi hai. haan, to jab gharavaalon ko koi isakee phikr nahin to tum kyon bekaar jaan dete ho. yahi na hogaa ki log hansenge so yah laaj kahan tak nibaahoge?
chaudharee ne bahut vineet ho kar kahaa- saahu jee, yah laaj to maare daalatee hai. tumse kya chhipaa hai. ek vah din tha ki hamaare daadaa-baabaa mahaaraaj ki savaaree ke saath chalte the, ab ek din yah ki ghar-ghar ki deevaar tak bikane ki naubat aa gayi hai. kaheen munh dikhaane ko bhi jee nahin chahta. yah lo gahanon ki potalee. yadi lokalaaj na hoti to ise lekar kabhi yahan na aat, lekin yah adharm isi laaj nibaahane ke vajah karn padaa hai.
jhagadoo saahu ne hairaanee mein ho kar poochhaa- yah gahane kisake hain? chaudharee ne sir jhukaa kar badi kathinataa se kahaa- meri betee gangaajalee ke. jhagadoo saahu stambhit ho gaye. bole- are! raam-raam!
chaudharee ne kaatar aavaaz mein kahaa- doob marane ko jee chahta hai.
jhagadoo ne badi dhaarmikataa ke saath sthir ho kar kahaa- shaastraa mein betee ke gaanv kaa ped dekhna manaa hai.
chaudharee ne deergh nihshvaas chhod kar karun aavaaz mein kahaa- na jaane naaraayan kab maut denge. bhai ki teen ladakiyaan byaaheen. kabhi bhool kar bhi unake dvaar kaa munh nahin dekh. paramaatmaa ne ab tak to tek nibaahee hai, par ab na jaane mittee ki kya durdashaa hone vaalee hai.
jhagadoo saahu 'lekhaa jau-jau bakhasheesh sau-sau' ke siddhaant par chalte the. sood ki ek kaudee bhi chhodanaa unake lie haraam tha. yadi ek mahine kaa ek din bhi lag jat to poore mahine kaa sood vasool kar lete. lekin navaraatr mein nity durgaa-paath karavaate the. pitripaksh mein roj braahmanon ko seedhaa baantate the. baniyon ki dharm mein badi nishthaa hoti hai. yadi koi deen braahman ladakee byaahane ke lie unake saamane haath pasaarataa to vah khaalee haath na lautataa, bheekh maangane vaale braahmanon ko chaahe vah kitane hi sande-musande hon, unake daravaaje par phatakaar nahin sunanee padatee thi. unake dharm-shaastraa mein kanyaa ke gaanv ke kuen kaa paani peene se pyaasaa mar jan achchha hai. vah svayan is siddhaant ke bhakt the aur is siddhaant ke any pakshapaatee unake lie mahaamaany devataa the. ve pighal gaye. man mein sochaa, yah manushy to kabhi ochhe vichaaron ko man mein nahin laayaa.
nirday kaal ki thokar se adharm raastaa par utar aay hai, to usake dharm ki rakshaa karn hamaaraa kartavy-dharm hai. yah soch man mein aate hi jhagadoo saahu gaddee se masanad ke sahaare uth baithe aur dridh aavaaz se kahaa wahi paramaatmaa jisane ab tak tumhari tek nibaahee hai, ab bhi nibaahenge. ladakee ke gahane ladakee ko de do. ladakee jaisee tumhari hai vaisee meri bhi hai. yah lo rupaye. aaj kaam chalaao. jab haath mein rupaye aa jaayan, de dena.
chaudharee par is sahaanubhooti kaa gaharaa asar padaa. vah jor-jor se rone lagaa. use apne bhaavon ki dhun mein krishn bhagavaan ki mohinee moorti saamane viraajamaan dikhaayee dee. wahi jhagadoo jo saare gaanv mein badanaam tha, jisakee usane khud kaee baar haakimon se shikaayat ki thi, aaj saakshaat devataa jaan padataa tha. rundhe hue galaa se gadgad ho bol-
- jhagadoo, tumne is samay meri baat, meri laaj, mera dharm, kahan tak kahoon, mera sab kuchh rakh liya. meri doobatee naav paar lagaa dee. krishn muraaree tumhaare is upakaar kaa phal denge aur main to tumhara gun jab tak jeeoongaa, gaataa rahoongaa.
