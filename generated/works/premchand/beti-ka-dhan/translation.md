# Beti Ka Dhan

**Munshi Premchand**

betavaa nadee do oonche kagaaron ke beech is tarah munh chhipaaye hue thi jaise nirmal hridayon mein saahas aur utsaah kee maddham jyoti chhipee rahatee hai| isake ek kagaar par ek chhotaa-saa gaanv basaa hai jo apne bhagn jaateey chihnon ke lie bahut hee prasiddh hai| jaateey gaathaaon aur chihnon par mar mitanevaale log is bhaavanasthaan par bade pyaar aur shraddhaa ke saath aate aur gaanv kaa boodhaa kevat sukkhoo chaudharee unhen usakee parikramaa karaataa aur raanee ke mahal, raajaa kaa darabaar aur kunvar kee baithak ke mite hue chiddon ko dikhaataa| vah ek uchchhvaas lekar rundhe hue gale se keht, mahaashay ! ek vah samay tha ki kevaton ko machhaliyon ke inaam mein asharphiyaan milatee theen| kahaar mahal mein jhaadoo dete hue asharphiyaan bator le jaate the| betavaa nadee roj chadh kar mahaaraaj ke charan chhoone aatee thi| yah prataap aur yah tej tha, lekin aaj isakee yah dashaa hai| ina sundar uktiyon par kisee kaa yakeen jamaanaa chaudharee ke vash kee baat na thi, par sunanevaale usakee sahridayataa tathaa anuraag ke jaroor kaayal ho jaate the|
sukkhoo chaudharee udaar aadamee the, lekin jitanaa badaa munh tha, utanaa badaa graas na tha| teen ladake, teen bahuen aur kaee pautr-pautriyaan theen| ladakee keval ek gangaajalee thi jisakaa abhee tak gaunaa nahin hua tha| chaudharee kee yah sabase pichhalee bachchaa thi| aurat ke mar jaane par usane isako bakaree kaa doodh pilaa-pilaa kar paalaa tha| parivaar mein khaanevaale to itane the, par khetee sirph ek hal kee hoti thi| jyon-tyon kar nirvaah hot tha, lekin sukkhoo kee vriddhaavasthaa aur puraatattv jnaan ne use gaanv mein vah maan aur pratishthaa pradaan kar rakkhee thi, jise dekh kar jhagadoo saahu bheetar hee bheetar jalate the| sukkhoo jab gaanvavaalon ke samaksh, haakimon se haath phenk-phenk kar baaten karne lagataa aur khandaharon ko ghumaa-phiraa kar dikhaane lagataa tha to jhagadoo saahu jo chaparaasiyon ke dhakke khaane ke dar se kareeb nahin phatakate the tadap-tadap kar raha jaate the| isalie ve sadaa is shubh avasar kee prateekshaa karte rahate the, jab sukkhoo par apne paisaa dvaaraa prabhutv jamaa saken|
is gaanv ke jameendaar thaakur jeetanasinh the, jinakee begaar ke maare gaanvavaalon kaa naakon dam tha| us saal jab jilaa majistret kaa dauraa hua aur vah yahan ke puraatan chihnon kee sair karne ke lie padhaare, to sukkhoo chaudharee ne dabee jabaan se apne gaanvavaalon kee duhkh-kahaanee unhen sunaayee| haakimon se baatacheet karne mein use tanik bhee dar na hot tha| sukkhoo chaudharee ko khoob maaloom tha ki jeetanasinh se raar machaanaa sinh ke munh mein sir denaa hai| lekin jab gaanvavaale kehte the ki chaudharee tumhaaree aise-aise haakimon se mitaaee hai aur ham logon ko raat-din rote katataa hai to phir tumhaaree yah mitrataa kis din kaam aavegee| paropakaaraay sataam vibhootayah| tab sukkhoo kaa mizaaj aasamaan par chadh jat tha| ghadee bhar ke lie vah jeetanasinh ko bhool jat tha| majistret ne jeetanasinh se isakaa javaab maangaa| udhar jhagadoo saahu ne chaudharee ke is saahasapoorn svaameedroh kee riport jeetanasinh ko dee| thaakur sahab jal kar aag ho gaye| apne kaarinde se bakaayaa lagaan kee bahee maangee| sanyogavash chaudharee ke jimme is saal kaa kuchh lagaan baakee tha| kuchh to paidaavaar kam huee, us par gangaajalee kaa byaah karn padaa| chhotee bahoo nath kee rat lagaaye hue thi; vah banavaanee padee| ina sab kharchon ne haath bilakul khaalee kar diya tha| lagaan ke lie kuchh zyaadaa phikr nahin thi| vah is abhimaan mein bhoolaa hua tha ki jis jabaan mein haakimon ko khush karne kee shakti hai, kyaa vah thaakur sahab ko apanaa lakshy na banaa sakegee ? boodhe chaudharee idhar to apne garv mein nishchint the aur udhar una par bakaayaa lagaan kee naalish thuk gayee| samman aa pahunchaa| doosare din peshee kee taareekh pad gayee| chaudharee ko apanaa jaadoo chalaane kaa avasar na milaa|
jin logon ke badhaave mein aa kar sukkhoo ne thaakur se chhedachhaad kee thi, unakaa darshan milanaa durlabh ho gaya| thaakur sahab ke sahane aur pyaade gaanv mein cheel kee tarah mandaraane lage| unake dar se kisee ko chaudharee kee parachhaaeen kaatane kaa saahas na hot tha| kachaharee wahan se teen meel par thi| barasaat ke din, raaste mein thaur-thaur paanee, umadee huee nadiyaan, raastaa kachchaa, bailagaadee kaa nibaah nahin, pairon mein bal nahin, isalie adamapairavee mein mukadame ekataraphaa phaisalaa ho gaya|
kurkee kaa notis pahunchaa to chaudharee ke haath-paanv phool gaye| saaree chaturaaee bhool gayee| chupachaap apanee khaat par padaa-padaa nadee kee ora taakataa aur apne man mein keht, kyaa mere jeete jee ghar mittee mein mil jaayagaa| mere ina bailon kee sundar jodee ke gale mein aah ! kyaa doosaron kaa juaa padegaa ? yah sochate-sochate usakee aankhen bhar aateen| vah bailon se lipat kar rone lagaa, lekin bailon kee aankhon se kyon aansoo jaaree the ? ve naand mein munh kyon nahin daalate the ? kyaa unake dil par bhee apne svaamee ke duhkh kee chot pahunch rahi thi !
phir vah apne jhopade ko vikal nayanon se nihaar kar dekhataa| aur man mein sochataa, kyaa hamako is ghar se nikalanaa padegaa ? yah poorvajon kee nishaanee kyaa hamaare jeete jee chhin jaayagee ?
kuchh log pareekshaa mein dridh rahate hain aur kuchh log isakee halakee aanch bhee nahin sah sakate| chaudharee apanee khaat par udaas pade ghanton apne kuladev mahaaveer aur mahaadev ko manaayaa kart aur unakaa gun gaayaa kart| usakee chintaadagdh aatmaa ko aur koee sahaaraa na tha|
isamen koee shak na tha ki chaudharee kee teenon bahuon ke paas gahane the, par aurat kaa gahanaa ookh kaa ras hai, jo perane hee se nikalataa hai| chaudharee jaati kaa ochhaa par svabhaav kaa oonchaa tha| use aisee neech baat bahuon se kehte sankoch hot tha| kadaachit yah neech soch usake dil mein utpann hee nahin hua tha, lekin teenon bete yadi jaraa bhee samajh se kaam lete to boodhe ko devataaon kee sharan lene kee aavashyakataa na hoti| lekin yahan to baat hee niraalee thi| bade ladake ko ghaat ke kaam se phurasat na thi| baakee do ladake is jatil savaal ko vichitr roop se hal karne ke mansoobe baandh rahe the|
manjhale jheengur ne munh banaa kar kahaa- unh ! is gaanv mein kyaa dharaa hai| jahaan hee kamaaoongaa, vaheen khaaoongaa par jeetanasinh kee moonchhen ek-ek karake chun loongaa|
chhote phakkad ainth kar bole- moonchhen tum chun lenaa ! naak main udaa doongaa| nakataa banaa ghoomegaa|
is par donon khoob hanse aur machhalee maarane chal diye|
is gaanv mein ek boodhe braahman bhee rahate the| mandir mein poojaa karte aur nity apne yajamaanon ko darshan dene nadee paar jaate, par kheve ke paise na dete| teesare din vah jameendaar ke guptacharon kee aankh bachaakar sukkhoo ke paas aaye aur sahaanubhooti ke aavaaz mein bole chaudharee ! kal hee tak miyaad hai aur tum abhee tak pade-pade so rahe ho| kyon nahin ghar kee cheej dhoondh-dhaandh kar kisee aur jagah bhej dete ? na ho samadhiyaane pathavaa do| jo kuchh bach rahe, vahee sahee| ghar kee mittee khod kar thode hee koee le jaayagaa|
chaudharee letaa tha, uth baithaa aur aakaash kee ora nihaar kar bol- jo kuchh usakee ichchhaa hai, vah hogaa| mujhse yah jaal na hogaa|
idhar kaee din kee lagaataar bhakti aur upaasanaa ke vajah chaudharee kaa man shuddh aur pavitr ho gaya tha| use chhal-prapanch se napharat ho gayee thi| pandit jee jo is kaam mein siddhahast the, lajjit ho gaye|
lekin chaudharee ke ghar ke any logon ko eeshvarechchhaa par itanaa bharosaa na tha| dheere-dheere ghar ke bartan-bhaande khisakaaye jaate the| anaaj kaa ek daanaa bhee ghar mein na rehne paayaa| raat ko naav ladee huee jati aur udhar se khaalee lautatee thi| teen din tak ghar mein choolhaa na jalaa| boodhe chaudharee ke munh mein ann kee kaun kahe paanee kee ek boond bhee na padee| striaayaan bhaad se chane bhunaa kar chabaateen, aur ladake machhaliyaan bhoon-bhoon kar udaate| lekin boodhe kee is ekaadashee mein yadi koee shareek tha to vah usakee betee gangaajalee thi| yah bechaaree apne boodhe baap ko chaarapaaee par nirjal chhatapataate dekh bilakh-bilakh kar rotee|
ladakon ko apne maan-pitaa se vah pyaar nahin hot jo ladakiyon ko hot hai| gangaajalee is soch-soch mein magn rahatee thi ki daadaa kee kis tarah madad karoon| yadi ham sab bhai-bahan mil kar jeetanasinh ke paas jaakar dayaa-bhikshaa kee praarthanaa karen to ve avashy maan jaayange; lekin daadaa ko kab yah sveekaar hogaa| vah yadi ek din bade sahab ke paas chale jaayan to sab kuchh baat kee baat mein ban jaay| lekin unakee to jaise samajh hee maaree gayee hai| isee udhedabun mein use ek upaay soojh padaa, kumhalaayaa hua mukhaaravind khil uthaa|
pujaaree jee sukkhoo chaudharee ke paas se uth kar chale gaye the aur chaudharee uchch aavaaz se apne soye hue devataaon ko pukaar-pukaar kar bulaa rahe the| nidaan gangaajalee unake paas jaa kar khadee ho gayee| chaudharee ne use dekh kar vismit aavaaz mein poochhaa kyon betee ? itanee raat gaye kyon baahar aayee ?
gangaajalee ne kahaa- baahar rahanaa to bhaagy mein likhaa hai, ghar mein kaise rahoon|
sukkhoo ne jor se haank lagaayee- kahan gaye tum krishnamuraaree, mere duhkh haro|
gangaajalee khadee thi, baith gayee aur dheere se bolee- bhajan gaate to aaj teen din ho gaye| ghar bachaane kaa bhee kuchh upaay sochaa ki ise yon hee mittee mein milaa doge ? ham logon ko kyaa ped tale rakhoge ?
chaudharee ne vyathit aavaaz se kahaa- betee, mujhe to koee upaay nahin soojhataa| bhagavaan jo chaahenge, hogaa| veg chalo giradhar gopaal, kaahe vilamb karo|
gangaajalee ne kahaa- mainne ek upaay sochaa hai, kaho to kahoon|
chaudharee uth kar baith gaye aur poochhaa- kaun upaay hai betee ?
gangaajalee ne kahaa- mere gahane jhagadoo saahu ke yahan giron rakh do| mainne jod liya hai| dene bhar ke rupaye ho jaayange|
chaudharee ne thandee saans le kar kahaa- betee ! tumako mujhse yah baat kehte laaj nahin aatee| ved-shaastraa mein mujhe tumhaare gaanv ke kuen kaa paanee peenaa bhee manaa hai| tumhaaree dyodhee mein bhee pair rakhane kaa nishedh hai| kyaa tum mujhe narak mein dhakelanaa chaahatee ho ?
gangaajalee javaab ke lie pahale hee se taiyaar thi| bolee- main apne gahane tumhen diye thode hee detee hoon| is samay le kar kaam chalaao, chait mein chhudaa denaa| chaudharee ne kadak kar kahaa yah mujhse na hogaa|
gangaajalee uttejit ho kar bolee- tumse yah na hogaa to main aap hee jaaoongee, mujhse ghar kee yah durdashaa nahin dekhee jati|
chaudharee ne jhunjhalaa kar kahaa- biraadaree mein kaun munh dikhaaoongaa ?
gangaajalee ne chidh kar kahaa- biraadaree mein kaun dhindhoraa peetane jat hai|
chaudharee ne phaisalaa sunaayaa- jagahansaaee ke lie main apanaa dharm na bigaadoongaa|
gangaajalee bigadakar bolee- meree baat nahin maanoge to tumhaare oopar meree hatyaa padegee| main aaj hee is betavaa nadee mein kood padoongee| tumse chaahe ghar mein aag lagate dekh jaay, par mujhse to na dekh jaayagaa|
chaudharee ne thandee saans le kar kaatar aavaaz mein kahaa- betee, meraa dharm naash mata karo| yadi aisaa hee hai to apanee kisee bhaavaj ke gahane maang kar laao|
gangaajalee ne gambheer aavaaz mein kahaa- bhaavajon se kaun apanaa munh nochavaane jaayagaa| unako phikar hoti to kyaa munh mein dahee jamaa tha, kahateen nahin|
chaudharee niruttar ho gaye| gangaajalee ghar mein jaa kar gahanon kee pitaaree laayee aur ek-ek karake sab gahane chaudharee ke angochhe mein baandh diye| chaudharee ne aankhon mein aansoo bhar kar kahaa- haay raam, is shareer kee kyaa gati likhee hai ! yah kah kar uthe| bahut samhaalane par bhee aankhon mein aansoo na chhipe|
raat kaa samay tha| betavaa nadee ke kinaare-kinaare raastaa ko chhodakar sukkhoo chaudharee gahanon kee gatharee kaankh mein dabaaye is tarah chupake-chupake chal rahe the maano paap kee gatharee liye jaate hain| jab vah jhagadoo saahu ke makaan ke paas pahunche to thahar gaye, aankhen khoob saaph keen, jisse kisee ko yah na bodh ho ki chaudharee rotaa tha|
jhagadoo saahu dhaage kee kamaanee kee ek motee ainak lagaaye baheekhaataa phailaaye hukkaa pee rahe the, aur deepak ke dhundhale prakaash mein una aksharon ko padhane kee bekaar cheshtaa mein lage the jinamen syaahee kee kiphaayat kee gayee thi| baar-baar ainak ko saaph karte aur aankh malate, par chiraag kee battee ukasaanaa yaa doharee battee lagaanaa shaayad isalie theek nahin samajhate the ki tel kaa apavyay hogaa| isee samay sukkhoo chaudharee ne aa kar kahaa jayaraam jee|
jhagadoo saahu ne dekh| pahachaan kar bole- jayaraam chaudharee ! kaho mukadame mein kyaa hua ? yah len-den bade jhanjhat kaa kaam hai| din bhar sir uthaane kee chhuttee nahin milatee|
chaudharee ne potalee ko khoob saavadhaanee se chhipaa kar laaparavaahee ke saath kahaa- abhee tak to kuchh nahin hua| kal ijaraayadigaree honevaalee hai| thaakur sahab ne na jaane kab kaa bair nikaalaa hai| hamako do-teen din kee bhee muhalat hoti to digaree na jaaree hone paatee| chhote sahab aur bade sahab donon hamako achchhee tarah jaanate hain| abhee isee saal mainne unse nadee kinaare ghanton baaten keen, lekin ek to barasaat ke din, doosare ek din kee bhee muhalat nahin, kyaa kart ! is samay mujhe rupayon kee phikr hai|
jhagadoo saahu ne vismit ho kar poochhaa- tumako rupayon kee phikr ! ghar mein bharaa hai, vah kis din kaam aavegaa| jhagadoo saahu ne yah vyangyabaan nahin chhodaa tha| vaastav mein unhen aur saare gaanv ko yakeen tha ki chaudharee ke ghar mein lakshmee mahaaraanee kaa akhand raajy hai|
chaudharee kaa rang badalane lagaa| bole- saahu jee ! rupayaa hot to kis baat kee phikr thi ? tumse kaun chhipaav hai| aaj teen din se ghar mein choolhaa nahin jalaa, ronaa-peetanaa padaa hai| ab to tumhaare basaaye basoongaa| thaakur sahab ne to ujaadane mein koee kasar na chhodee|
jhagadoo saahu jeetanasinh ko khush rakhanaa jaroor chaahate the, par saath hee chaudharee ko bhee naakhush karn manjoor na tha| yadi sood-dar-sood chhod kar mool tathaa byaaj sahaj vasool ho jaay to unhen chaudharee par mupht kaa ehasaan laadane mein koee aapatti na thi| yadi chaudharee ke aphasaron kee jaan-pahachaan ke vajah saahu jee kaa taiks se galaa chhoot jaay, jo anek upaay karne ahalakaaron kee mutthee garam karne par bhee nity prati unakee tond kee tarah badhataa hee jaa raha tha to kyaa poochhanaa ! bole-
-kyaa kahen chaudharee jee, kharch ke maare aajakal ham bhee tabaah hain| lahane vasool nahin hote| taiks kaa rupayaa denaa padaa| haath bilakul khaalee ho gaya| tumhen kitanaa rupayaa chaahie ?
chaudharee ne kahaa- sau rupaye kee digaree hai| kharch-barch milaa kar do sau ke lagabhag samajho|
jhagadoo ab apne daanv khelane lage| poochhaa- tumhaare ladakon ne tumhaaree kuchh bhee madad na kee| vah sab bhee to kuchh na kuchh kamaate hee hain|
saahu jee kaa yah nishaanaa theek padaa ladakon ne laaparavaahee se chaudharee ke man mein jo kutsit bhaav bhare the vah sajeev ho gaye| bole- bhai, ladake kisee kaam ke hote to yah din kyon dekhanaa padataa| unhen to apne bhog-vilaas se matalab| ghar-ghar-parivaar kaa bojh to mere sir par hai| main ise jaise chaahoon, sanbhaaloon unse kuchh sarokaar nahin, marate dam bhee galaa nahin chhootataa| maroongaa to sab khaal mein bhoosaa bharaa kar rakh chhodenge| 'ghar kaaraj naanaa janjaalaa|'
jhagadoo ne teesaraa teer maaraa- kyaa bahuon se bhee kuchh na ban padaa|
chaudharee ne javaab diya- bahoo-bete sab apanee-apanee mauj mein mast hain| main teen din tak dvaar par binaa ann-jal ke padaa tha, kisee ne baat bhee nahin poochhee| kahan kee salaah, kahan kee baatacheet| bahuon ke paas rupaye na hon, par gahane to hain aur ve bhee mere banaaye hue| is durdin ke samay yadi do-do thaan utaar deteen to kyaa main chhudaa na detaa ? sadaa yahee din thode hee rahenge|
jhagadoo samajh gaye ki yah mahaj jabaan kaa saudaa hai aur vah jabaan kaa saudaa bhoolakar bhee na karte the| bole- tumhaare ghar ke log bhee anoothe hain| kyaa itanaa bhee nahin jaanate ki boodhaa rupaye kahan se laavegaa ? ab samay badal gaya| yaa to kuchh jaayadaad likho yaa gahane giron rakho tab jaa kar rupayaa mile| isake binaa rupaye kahan| isamen bhee jaayadaad mein saikadon bakhede pade hain| subheetaa giron rakhane mein hee hai| haan, to jab gharavaalon ko koee isakee phikr nahin to tum kyon bekaar jaan dete ho| yahee na hogaa ki log hansenge so yah laaj kahan tak nibaahoge ?
chaudharee ne bahut vineet ho kar kahaa- saahu jee, yah laaj to maare daalatee hai| tumse kyaa chhipaa hai| ek vah din tha ki hamaare daadaa-baabaa mahaaraaj kee savaaree ke saath chalate the, ab ek din yah ki ghar-ghar kee deevaar tak bikane kee naubat aa gayee hai| kaheen munh dikhaane ko bhee jee nahin chaahataa| yah lo gahanon kee potalee| yadi lokalaaj na hoti to ise lekar kabhee yahan na aat, lekin yah adharm isee laaj nibaahane ke vajah karn padaa hai|
jhagadoo saahu ne hairaanee mein ho kar poochhaa- yah gahane kisake hain ? chaudharee ne sir jhukaa kar badee kathinataa se kahaa- meree betee gangaajalee ke| jhagadoo saahu stambhit ho gaye| bole- are ! raam-raam !
chaudharee ne kaatar aavaaz mein kahaa- doob marane ko jee chaahataa hai|
jhagadoo ne badee dhaarmikataa ke saath sthir ho kar kahaa- shaastraa mein betee ke gaanv kaa ped dekhanaa manaa hai|
chaudharee ne deergh nihshvaas chhod kar karun aavaaz mein kahaa- na jaane naaraayan kab maut denge| bhai kee teen ladakiyaan byaaheen| kabhee bhool kar bhee unake dvaar kaa munh nahin dekh| paramaatmaa ne ab tak to tek nibaahee hai, par ab na jaane mittee kee kyaa durdashaa hone vaalee hai|
jhagadoo saahu 'lekhaa jau-jau bakhasheesh sau-sau' ke siddhaant par chalate the| sood kee ek kaudee bhee chhodanaa unake lie haraam tha| yadi ek maheene kaa ek din bhee lag jat to poore maheene kaa sood vasool kar lete| lekin navaraatr mein nity durgaa-paath karavaate the| pitripaksh mein roj braahmanon ko seedhaa baantate the| baniyon kee dharm mein badee nishthaa hoti hai| yadi koee deen braahman ladakee byaahane ke lie unake saamane haath pasaarataa to vah khaalee haath na lautataa, bheekh maangane vaale braahmanon ko chaahe vah kitane hee sande-musande hon, unake daravaaje par phatakaar nahin sunanee padatee thi| unake dharm-shaastraa mein kanyaa ke gaanv ke kuen kaa paanee peene se pyaasaa mar jan achchhaa hai| vah svayan is siddhaant ke bhakt the aur is siddhaant ke any pakshapaatee unake lie mahaamaany devataa the| ve pighal gaye| man mein sochaa, yah manushy to kabhee ochhe vichaaron ko man mein nahin laayaa|
nirday kaal kee thokar se adharm raastaa par utar aay hai, to usake dharm kee rakshaa karn hamaaraa kartavy-dharm hai| yah soch man mein aate hee jhagadoo saahu gaddee se masanad ke sahaare uth baithe aur dridh aavaaz se kahaa vahee paramaatmaa jisane ab tak tumhaaree tek nibaahee hai, ab bhee nibaahenge| ladakee ke gahane ladakee ko de do| ladakee jaisee tumhaaree hai vaisee meree bhee hai| yah lo rupaye| aaj kaam chalaao| jab haath mein rupaye aa jaayan, de denaa|
chaudharee par is sahaanubhooti kaa gaharaa asar padaa| vah jor-jor se rone lagaa| use apne bhaavon kee dhun mein krishn bhagavaan kee mohinee moorti saamane viraajamaan dikhaayee dee| vahee jhagadoo jo saare gaanv mein badanaam tha, jisakee usane khud kaee baar haakimon se shikaayat kee thi, aaj saakshaat devataa jaan padataa tha| rundhe hue galaa se gadgad ho bol-
- jhagadoo, tumane is samay meree baat, meree laaj, meraa dharm, kahan tak kahoon, meraa sab kuchh rakh liya| meree doobatee naav paar lagaa dee| krishn muraaree tumhaare is upakaar kaa phal denge aur main to tumhaaraa gun jab tak jeeoongaa, gaataa rahoongaa|
