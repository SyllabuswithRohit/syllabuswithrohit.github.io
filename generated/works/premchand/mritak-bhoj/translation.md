# Mritak Bhoj

**Munshi Premchand**

seth raamanaath ne rog-shayyaa par pade-pade niraashaapoorn nazar se apanee aurat susheelaa kee ora dekhakar kahaa, 'main badaa abhaagaa hoon, sheelaa. mere saath tumhe hameshaa hee dukh bhoganaa padaa. jab ghar mein kuchh na tha, to raat-din ghar-gharavaale ke dhandhon aur bachchon ke lie maratee theen. jab zara kuchh sanbhalaa aur tumhaare aaraam karne ke din aaye, to yon chhode chalaa jaa raha hoon. aaj tak mujhe ummeed thi, par aaj woh ummeed toot gayee. dekho sheelaa, roo mata. sansaar mein sabhee marate hain, koi do saal aage, koi do saal peechhe. ab ghar-gharavaale kaa bhaar tumhaare oopar hai. mainne rupaye nahin chhode; lekin jo kuchh hai, usse tumhaaraa jeevan kisee tarah kat jaayagaa. .. yah raajaa kyon ro raha hai? susheelaa ne aansoo ponchhakar kahaa, ziddee ho gaya hai aur kya. aaj sabere se rat lagaaye hue hai ki main motar loongaa. 50 ru. se kam mein aayegee motar? sethajee ko idhar kuchh dinon se donon baalakon par bahut pyaar ho gaya tha. bole to mangaa do na ek. bechaaraa kab se ro raha hai, kya-kya aramaan dil mein the. sab dhool mein mil gaye. raanee ke lie vilaayatee gudiyaa bhee mangaa do. doosaron ke khilaune dekhakar tarasatee rehti hai. jis paisaa ko praanon se bhee priy samajhaa, woh ant ko daaktaron ne khaayaa. bachche mujhe kya yaad karenge ki baap tha. abhaage baap ne to paisaa ko ladake-ladakee se priy samajhaa. kabhee paise kee cheej bhee laakar nahin dee. '
antim samay jab sansaar kee asaarataa kathor saty banakar aankhon ke saamane khadee ho jati hai, to jo kuchh na kiya, usakaa khed aur jo kuchh kiya, us par pashchaattaap, man ko udaar aur nishkapat banaa detaa hai. susheelaa ne raajaa ko bulaayaa aur use chhaatee se lagaakar rone lagee. woh maatrisneh, jo pati kee kripanataa se bheetar-hee-bheetar tadapakar raha jat tha, is samay jaise khaul uthaa. lekin motar ke lie rupaye kahan the? sethajee ne poochhaa, 'motar loge betaa; apanee ammaan se rupaye lekar bhaiyaa ke saath chale jaao. khoob achchhee motar laanaa. '
raajaa ne maan ke aansoo aur pitaa kaa yah pyaar dekh, to usakaa baalahath jaise pighal gaya. bol, 'abhee nahin loongaa. '
sethajee ne poochhaa, 'kyon? '
'jab aap achchhe ho jaayange tab loongaa. ' sethajee phoot-phootakar rone lage.
teesare din seth raamanaath kaa dehaant ho gaya. dhanee ke jeene se du: kha bahuton ko hot hai, sukh thodon ko. unake marane se du: kha thodon ko hot hai, sukh bahuton ko. mahaabraahmanon kee mandalee alag sukhee hai, panditajee alag khush hain, aur shaayad biraadaree ke log bhee khush hain; isalie ki ek baraabar kaa aadamee kam hua. dil se ek kaantaa door hua. aur patteedaaron kaa to poochhanaa hee kya. ab woh puraanee kasar nikaalenge. hraday ko sheetal karne kaa aisaa avasar bahut dinon ke baad milaa hai. aaj paanchavaan din hai. woh vishaal bhavan soonaa padaa hai. ladake na rote hain, na hansate hain. man maare maan ke paas baithe hain aur vidhavaa aane vaalaa samay kee apaar chintaaon ke bhaar se dabee huee nirjeev-see padee hai. ghar mein jo rupaye bach rahe the, woh daah-kriyaa kee bhent ho gaye aur abhee saare sanskaar baakee pade hain. bhagavaan, kaise bedaa paar lagegaa.
kisee ne dvaar par aavaaj dee. maharaa ne aakar seth dhaneeraam ke aane kee khabar dee. donon ladakaa baahar daude. susheelaa kaa man bhee ek pal ke lie haraa ho gaya. seth dhaneeraam biraadaree ke sarapanch the. abalaa kaa kshubdh
hraday sethajee kee is kripaa se pulakit ho uthaa. aakhir biraadaree ke mukhiyaa hain. ye log anaathon kee khoj-khabar na len to kaun le. dhany hain ye punyaatmaa log jo museebat mein deenon kee rakshaa karte hain. yah sochatee huee susheelaa ghoonghat nikaale barothe mein aakar khadee ho gayee. dekh to dhaneeraamajee ke atirikt aur bhee kaee bhalaa aadamee khade hain.
dhaneeraam bole 'bahoojee, bhai raamanaath kee akaal-mrityu se ham logon ko jitanaa du: kha hua hai, woh hamaaraa dil hee jaanataa hai. abhee unakee umr hee kya thi; lekin bhagavaan kee ichchhaa. ab to hamaaraa yahee dharm hai ki eeshvar
par bharosaa rakhen aur aage ke lie koi raah nikaalen. kaam aisaa karn chaahie ki ghar kee aabaroo bhee banee rahe aur bhaaeejee kee aatmaa santusht bhee ho. ' kuberadaas ne susheelaa ko kanakhiyon se dekhate hue kahaa, 'maryaadaa badee
cheej hai. usakee rakshaa karn hamaaraa dharm hai. lekin kamalee ke baahar paanv nikaalanaa bhee to theek nahin. kitane rupaye hain tere paas, bahoo? kya kahaa, kuchh nahin? '
susheelaa -'ghar mein rupaye kahan hain, sethajee. jo thode-bahut the, woh beemaaree mein uth gaye. '
dhaneeraam -'to yah nayee samasyaa khadee huee. aisee dashaa mein hamen kya karn chaahie, kuberadaasajee? '
kuberadaas -'zaise ho, bhoj to karn hee padegaa. haan, apanee saamarthy dekhakar kaam karn chaahie. main karj lene ko na kahoongaa. haan, ghar mein jitane rupayon kaa intazaam ho sake, usamen hamen koi kasar na chhodanee chaahie. mrit-jeev ke
saath bhee to hamaaraa kuchh farz hai. ab to woh phir kabhee na aayegaa, usse hameshaa ke lie naataa toot raha hai. isalie sabakuchh haisiyat ke mutaabik hona chaahie. braahmanon ko to bhoj denaa hee padegaa jisse ki maryaadaa kaa guzaaraa ho! '
dhaneeraam -'to kya tumhaare paas kuchh bhee nahin hai, bahoojee? do-chaar hajaar bhee nahin! '
susheelaa --'main aapase saty kehti hoon, mere paas kuchh nahin hai. aise samay jhooth boloongee. '
dhaneeraam ne kuberadaas kee ora ardh-avishvaas se dekhakar kahaa, 'tab to yah makaan bechanaa padegaa. '
kuberadaas -'isake sivaa aur kya ho sakataa hai. naak kaatanaa to achchhaa nahin. raamanaath kaa kitanaa naam tha, biraadaree ke stanbh the. yahee is samay ek upaay hai. 10 hajaar ru. mere aate hain. sood-battaa lagaakar koi 15 hajaar ru. mere ho jaayange. baakee bhoj mein kharch ho jaayegaa. agar kuchh bach raha, to baal-bachchon ke kaam aa jaayagaa. '
dhaneeraam -'aapake yahan kitane par bandhak rakhaa tha? '
kuberadaas-' 10 hajaar rupaye par. rupaye saikade sood. '
dhaneeraam -'mainne to kuchh kam sunaa hai. '
kuberadaas -'usakaa to rehananaamaa rakhaa hai. jabaanee baatacheet thode hee hai. main do-chaar hajaar ke lie jhooth nahin boloongaa. '
dhaneeraam-' nahin-nahin, yah main kab keht hoon. to toone sun liya, baaee! panchon kee salaah hai ki makaan bech diya jaay. '
susheelaa kaa chhotaa bhai santalaal bhee isee samay aa pahunchaa. yah antim vaaky usake kaan mein pad gaya. bol uthaa, 'kisalie makaan bech diya jaay? biraadaree ke bhoj ke lie? biraadaree to khaa-peekar raah legee, ina anaathon kee rakshaa kaise hogee? inake aane vaalaa samay ke lie bhee to kuchh sochanaa chaahie. '
dhaneeraam ne kop-bharee aankhon se dekhakar kahaa, 'aapako ina maamalon mein taang adaane kaa koi adhikaar nahin. keval aane vaalaa samay kee phikr karne se kaam nahin chalataa. mritak kaa peechhaa bhee kisee tarah sudharanaa hee padataa hai. aapakaa
kya bigadegaa. hansee to hamaaree hogee. sansaar mein maryaadaa se priy koi vastu nahin! maryaadaa ke lie jaan tak de dete hain. jab maryaadaa hee na rahi, to kya raha. agar hamaaree salaah poochhoge, to ham yahee kahenge. aage baaee kaa akhatiyaar hai, jaisaa chaahe kare; par hamse koi sarokaar na rahegaa. chalie kuberadaasajee, chalen. '
susheelaa ne bhayabheet hokar kahaa, 'bhaiyaa kee baaton kaa soch na keejie, inakee to yah aadat hai. mainne to aapakee baat nahin taalee; aap mere bade hain. ghar kaa haal aapako maaloom hai. main apne swami kee aatmaa ko dukhee karn
nahin chaahatee, lekin jab unake bachche thokaren khaayenge, to kya unakee aatmaa dukhee na hogee? betee kaa byaah karn hee hai. ladake ko padhaanaa-likhaanaa hai hee. braahmanon ko khilaa deejie; lekin biraadaree karne kee mujhamen saamarth nahin hai. '
donon mahaanubhaavon ko jaise thappad lagee itanaa badaa adharm. bhalaa aisee baat bhee jabaan se nikaalee jati hai. panch log apne munh mein kaalikh na lagane denge. duniya vidhavaa ko na hansegee, hansee hogee panchon kee. yah jag-hansaaee woh
kaise sah sakate hain. aise ghar ke dvaar par jhaankanaa bhee paap hai. susheelaa rokar bolee, 'main anaath hoon, naadaan hoon, mujh par gussaa na keejie. aap log hee mujhe chhod denge, to meraa kaise guzaaraa hogaa. '
itane mein do sahab aur aa biraaje. ek bahut mote aur doosare bahut dubale. naam bhee gunon ke anusaar hee bheemachand aur durbaladaas. dhaneeraam ne sankshep mein yah paristhiti unhe samajhaa dee. durbaladaas ne sahradayataa se kahaa, 'to aisaa kyon nahin karte ki ham log milakar kuchh rupaye de den. jab isakaa ladakaa sayaanaa ho jaayagaa, to rupaye mil hee jaayenge. agar na bhee mile to ek mitr ke lie kuchh bal khaa jan koi badee baat nahin. '
santalaal ne khush hokar kahaa, 'itanee raham aap karenge, to kya poochhanaa. '
kuberadaas tyoree chadhaakar bole, 'tum to besir-pair kee baaten karne lage durbaladaasajee! is bakhat ke baajaar mein kisake paas phaalatoo rupaye rakhe hue hain. '
bheemachand-' so to theek hai, baajaar kee aisee mandee to kabhee dekhee nahin; par nibaah to karn chaahie. '
kuberadaas akad gaye. woh susheelaa ke makaan par daant lagaaye hue the. aisee baaton se unake svaarth mein baadhaa padatee thi. woh apne rupaye ab vasool karake chhodenge. bheemachand ne unhe kisee tarah sachet kiya; 'lekin bhoj to denaa hee padegaa. us farz kaa paalan na karn samaaj kee naak kaatanaa hai. '
susheelaa ne durbaladaas mein sahradayataa kaa aabhaas dekh. unakee ora gareeb netron se dekhakar bolee, 'main aap logon se baahar thode hee hoon. aap log maalik hain, jaisaa theek samajhen vaisaa karen. '
durbaladaas -'tere paas kuchh thode-bahut gahane to honge, baaee? '
'haan gahane hain. aadhe to beemaaree mein bik gaye, aadhe bache hain. ' susheelaa ne saare gahane laakar panchon ke saamane rakh diye; 'par yah to mushkil se teen hajaar mein uthenge. ' durbaladaas ne potalee ko haath mein taulakar kahaa, 'teen hajaar ko kaise jaayange. main saadhe teen hajaar dilaa doongaa. '
bheemachand ne phir potalee ko taulakar kahaa, 'meree bolee, chaar hajaar kee hai. '
kuberadaas ko makaan kee bikree kaa sawal chhedane kaa avasar phir milaa, 'chaar hajaar hee mein kya hua jat hai. biraadaree kaa bhoj hai yaa dosh mitaanaa hai. biraadaree mein kam-se-kam das hajaar kaa kharachaa hai. makaan to
nikaalanaa hee padegaa. '
santalaal ne onth chabaakar kahaa, 'main keht hoon, aap log kya itane nirdayee hain! aap logon ko anaath baalakon par bhee raham nahin aati! kya unhe raaste kaa bhikhaaree banaakar chhodenge? ' lekin santalaal kee phariyaad par kisee ne dhyaan na diya. makaan kee baatacheet ab nahin taalee jaa sakatee thi. baajaar mandaa hai. 30 hajaar se besee
nahin mil sakate, 15 hajaar to kuberadaas ke hain. paanch hajaar bachenge. chaar hajaar gahanon se aa jaayange. is tarah 9 hajaar mein badee kiphaayat se brahmabhoj aur biraadaree-bhoj donon nipataa diye jaayange. susheelaa ne donon baalakon ko saamane karake karabaddh hokar kahaa, 'pancho, mere bachchon kaa munh dekhie. mere ghar mein jo kuchh hai; woh aap sab le leejie; lekin makaan chhod deejie mujhe kaheen thikaanaa na milegaa. main aapake pairon padatee hoon makaan is samay na bechen. '
is moorkhataa kaa kya jawab diya jaay. panch log to khud chaahate the ki makaan na bechanaa pade. unhe anaathon se koi dushmanee nahin thi; lekin biraadaree kaa bhoj aur kis tarah kiya jaay. agar vidhavaa kam-se-kam paanch hajaar ru. kaa jogaad aur kar de, to makaan bach sakataa hai, par woh aisaa nahin kar sakatee, to makaan bechane ke sivaa aur koi upaay nahin.
kuber ne ant mein kahaa, 'dekh baaee, baajaar kee dashaa is samay kharaab hai. rupaye kisee se udhaar nahin mil sakate. baal-bachchon ke bhaag mein likhaa hogaa, to bhagavaan aur kisee heele se degaa. heele rojee, bahaane maut. baal-bachchon
kee phikr mata kar. bhagavaan jisako janm dete hain, usakee rozee kee jugat pahale hee se kar dete hain. ham tujhe samajhaakar haar gaye. agar too ab bhee apanaa hath na chhodegee, to ham baat bhee na poochhenge. phir yahan teraa rahanaa mushkil ho jaayagaa. shaharavaale tere peechhe pad jaayange. '
vidhavaa susheelaa ab aur kya karti. panchon se ladakar woh kaise raha sakatee thi. paani mein rahakar magar se kaun bair kar sakataa hai. ghar mein jaane ke lie uthee par vaheen moorchhit hokar gir padee. abhee tak ummeed sanbhaale huee
thi. bachchon ke paalan-poshan mein woh apanaa vaidhavy bhool sakatee thi; par ab to andhakaar tha, chaaron ora. seth raamanaath ke mitron kaa unake ghar par pooraa adhikaar tha. mitron kaa adhikaar na ho to kisakaa ho. aurat kaun hoti hai. jab woh itanee motee-see baat nahin samajhatee ki biraadaree karn aur dhoom-dhaam se dil kholakar karn laajimee baat hai, to usse aur kuchh kahanaa bekaar hai. gahane kaun khareede? bheemachand chaar hajaar daam lagaa chuke the, lekin ab unhe maaloom hua ki unse bhool huee thi. durbaladaas ne teen hajaar lagaaye the. isalie saudaa inheen ke haath hua. is baat par durbaladaas aur bheemachand mein takaraar bhee ho gayee; lekin bheemachand ko munh kee khaanee padee. insaaf durbal ke paksh mein tha. dhaneeraam ne kataaksh kiya 'dekho durbaladaas, maal to le jate ho; par
teen hajaar se besee kaa hai. main neeti kee hatyaa na hone doongaa. '
kuberadaas bole, 'ajee, to ghar mein hee to hai, kaheen baahar to nahin gaya. ek din mitron kee daavat ho jaayagee! '
is par chaaron mahaanubhaav hanse. is kaam se phurasat paakar ab makaan kaa sawal uthaa. kuberadaas 30 hajaar dene par taiyaar the; par kaanoonee kaarravaaee kiye binaa shak kee gunjaaish thi. yah gunjaaish kyonkar rakhee jaay. ek dalaal
bulaayaa gaya. naataa-saa aadamee tha, popalaa munh, koi 50 kee avasthaa. naam tha chokhelaal.
kuberadaas ne kahaa, 'chokhelaalajee se hamaaree tees saal kee dostee hai. aadamee kya ratn hain. '
bheemachand-'dekho chokhelaal, hamen yah makaan bechanaa hai. isake lie koi achchhaa graahak laao. tumhaaree dalaalee pakkee. '
kuberadaas-' baajaar kaa haal achchhaa nahin hai; lekin phir bhee hamen yah to dekhanaa padegaa ki raamanaath ke baal-bachchon ko totaa na ho. (kaan mein) tees se aage na jan. '
bheemachand -'dekhie kuberadaas, yah achchhee baat nahin hai. '
kuberadaas -'to main kya kar raha hoon. main to yahee kah raha tha ki achchhe daam lagavaanaa. '
chokhelaal -'aap logon ko mujhse yah kehne kee jaroorat nahin. main apanaa dharm samajhataa hoon. raamanaathajee mere bhee mitr the. mujhe yah bhee maaloom hai ki is makaan ke banavaane mein ek laakh se kam ek paaee bhee nahin lage, lekin baajaar kaa haal kya aap logon se chhipaa hai. is samay isake 25 hajaar se besee nahin mil sakate. subheete se to koi graahak se das-paanch hajaar aur mil jaayange; lekin is samay to koi graahak bhee mushkil se milenge. lo dahee aur laav dahee kee baat hai. '
dhaneeraam -'25 hajaar ru. to bahut kam hai bhai, aur na sahee 30 hajaar ru. to karaa do. '
chokhelaal -'30 kya main to 40 karaa doon, par koi graahak to mile. aap log kehte hain to main 30 hajaar ru. kee baatacheet karoongaa. '
dhaneeraam -'zab tees hajaar mein hee denaa hai to kuberadaasajee hee kyon na le len. itanaa sastaa maal doosaron ko kyon diya jaay. '
kuberadaas -'aap sab logon kee raay ho, to aisaa hee kar liya jaay. dhaneeraam ne 'haan, haan' kahakar haamee bharee. bheemachand man mein ainthakar raha gaye. yah saudaa bhee pakkaa ho gaya. aaj hee vakeel ne bainaamaa likhaa. turant
rajistree bhee ho gayee. susheelaa ke saamane bainaamaa laayaa gaya, to usane ek thandee saans lee aur sajal netron se us par hastaakshar kar diye. ab use usake sivaa aur kaheen sharan nahin hai. bevaphaa mitr kee tarah yah ghar bhee sukh ke dinon mein saath dekar du: kha ke dinon mein usakaa saath chhod raha hai.
panch log susheelaa ke aangan mein baithe biraadaree ke rukke likh rahe hain aur anaath vidhavaa oopar jharokhe par baithee bhaagy ko ro rahi hai. idhar rukkaa taiyaar hua, udhar vidhavaa kee aankhon se aansoo kee boonden nikalakar rukke par gir padeen. dhaneeraam ne oopar dekhakar kahaa, 'paani kaa chheentaa kahan se aay? '
santalaal -'baaee baithee ro rahi hai. usane rukke par apne rakt ke aansuon kee muhar lagaa dee hai. '
dhaneeraam (oonche aavaaz mein) 'are, to too ro kyon rahi hai, baaee? yah rone kaa samay nahin hai, tujhe to khush hona chaahie ki panch log tere ghar mein aaj yah shubh-kaary karne ke lie jamaa hain. jis pati ke saath toone itane dinon
bhog-vilaas kiya, usee kaa peechhaa sudharane mein too du: kha maanatee hai? '
biraadaree mein rukkaa phiraa. idhar teen-chaar din panchon ne bhoj kee taiyaariyon mein bitaaye. ghee dhaneeraamajee kee aadhat se aay. maidaa, cheenee kee aadhat bhee unheen kee thi. paanchaven din praat: kaal brahm-bhoj hua. shaam-samay biraadaree
kaa jyonaar. susheelaa ke dvaar par bagghiyon aur motaron kee kataaren khadee theen. bheetar mehamaanon kee pangaten theen. aangan, baithak, daalaan, barothaa, oopar kee chhat neeche-oopar mehamaanon se bharaa hua tha. log khana karte the aur panchon ko
saraahate the. kharch to sabhee karte hain; par intajaam kaa saleekaa chaahie. aise svaadisht padaarth bahut kam khaane mein aate hain.
'seth champaaraam ke bhoj ke baad aisaa bhoj raamanaathajee kaa hee hua hai. '
'amritiyaan kaisee kurakuree hain! '
'rasagulle mevon se bhare hain. '
'saaraa shrey panchon ko hai. '
dhaneeraam ne namrataa se kahaa, 'aap bhaaiyon kee raham hai jo aisaa kehte ho. raamanaath se bhai-chaare kaa vyavahaar tha. ham na karte to kaun kart. chaar din se sonaa naseeb nahin hua. '
'aap dhany hain! mitr hon to aise hon. '
'kya baat hai! aapane raamanaathajee kaa naam rakh liya. biraadaree yahee khana-khilaanaa dekhatee hai. rokad dekhane nahin jati. '
mehamaan log bakhaan-bakhaan kar maal udaa rahe the aur udhar kotharee mein baithee huee susheelaa soch rahi thi sansaar mein aise svaarthee log hain! saaraa sansaar svaarthamay ho gaya hai! sab peton par haath pher-pher kar khana kar rahe hain.
koi itanaa bhee nahin poochhataa ki anaathon ke lie kuchh bachaa yaa nahin. ek maheenaa gujar gaya. susheelaa ko ek-ek paise kee tangee ho rahi thi. nakad tha hee nahin, gahane nikal hee gaye the. ab thode se baratan bach rahe the. udhar chhote-chhote bahut-se bil chukaane the. kuchh rupaye daaktar ke, kuchh darajee ke, kuchh baniyon ke. susheelaa ko yah rakamen ghar kaa bachaa-khuchaa saamaan bechakar chukaanee padeen. aur maheenaa pooraa hote-hote usake paas kuchh na bachaa. bechaaraa santalaal ek dookaan par muneem tha. kabhee-kabhee woh aakar ek-aadhaa rupaye de detaa. idhar kharch kaa haath phailaa hua tha. ladake avasthaa ko samajhate the. maan ko chhedate na the, par makaan ke saamane se koi khonchevaalaa nikal jat aur woh doosare ladakon ko phal yaa mithaaee khaate dekhate, to unake munh mein paani bharakar aankhon mein bhar jat tha. aisee lalachaayee huee aankhon se taakate the ki raham aati. vahee bachche, jo thode din pahale meve-mithaaee kee ora taakate na the ab ek-ek paise kee cheej ko tarasate the. vahee bhalaa aadamee, jinhonne biraadaree kaa bhoj karavaayaa tha, ab ghar ke saamane se nikal jate; par koi jhaankataa na tha.
shaam ho gayee thi. susheelaa choolhaa jalaaye rotiyaan senk rahi thi aur donon ladakaa choolhe ke paas rotiyon ko kshudhit netron se dekh rahe the. choolhe ke doosare aile par daal thi. daal ke pakane kaa intajaar tha. ladakee gyaarah saal
kee thi, ladakaa aath saal kaa. mohan adheer hokar bol, ammaan, 'mujhe rookhee rotiyaan hee de do. badee
