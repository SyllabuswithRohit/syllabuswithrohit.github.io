# Suhag Ki Saree

**Munshi Premchand**

1
yah kahanaa bhool hai ki daampaty-sukh ke lie aurat-aadamee ke aadat mein mel hona zarooree hai. shreematee gauraa aur shreemaan kunvar ratanasinh mein koi baat na milatee thi. gauraa udaar thi, ratanasinh kaudee-kaudee ko daanton se pakadate the. woh hansamukh thi, ratanasinh chintaasheel the. woh kul-maryaadaa par jaan detee thi, ratanasinh ise aadambar samajhate the. unake saamaajik vyavahaar aur soch mein bhee ghor antar tha. yahan udaarataa kee baajee ratanasinh ke haath thi. gauraa ko sahabhoj se aapatti thi, vidhavaa-vivaah se napharat aur achhooton ke sawal se virodh. ratanasinh ina sabhee vyavasthaaon ke anumodak the. raajaneetik vishayon mein yah vibhinnataa aur bhee jatil thi! gauraa abhee sthiti ko atal, amar, aparihaary samajhatee thi, isalie woh naram-garam, kaangres, svaraajy, homarool sabhee se virakt thi. kehti-‘‘ye mutthee bhar padhe-likhe aadamee kya banaa lenge, chane kaheen bhaad phod sakate hain? ’’ ratanasinh pakke aashaavaadee the, raajaneetik sabhaa kee pahalee panktiyon mein baithanevaale, karmakshetr mein sabase pahale kadam uthaanevaale, svadeshavratadhaaree aur bahishkaar ke poore anuyaayee. itanee vishamataaon par bhee unakaa daampaty-jeevan sukhamay tha. kabhee-kabhee unamen matabhed avashy ho jat tha, par woh sameer ke woh jhonke the, jo sthir jal ko halakee-halakee laharon se aabhooshit kar dete hain; woh prachand jhonke nahin jinase saagar viplavakshetr ban jat hai. thodee-see sadichchhaa saaree vishamataaon aur matabhedon kaa pratikaar kar detee thi.
2
videshee kapadon kee holiyaan jalaayee jaa rahi theen. svayansevakon ke jatthe bhikhaariyon kee tarah dvaaron par khade ho-ho kar vilaayatee kapadon kee bhikshaa maangate the aur aisaa kadaachit hee koi dvaar tha jahaan unhe niraash hona padataa ho. khaddar aur gaadhe ke din phir gaye the. nayanasukh, nayanadukh, malamal aur tanajeb tanabedh ho gaye the. ratanasinh ne aakar gauraa se kahaa-laao, ab sab videshee kapade sandook se nikaal do, de doon.
gauraa-are to isee ghadee koi saait nikalee jati hai, phir kabhee de denaa.
ratan-vaah, log dvaar par khade kolaahal machaa rahe hain aur tum kehti ho, phir kabhee de denaa.
gauraa-to yah kunjee lo, nikaal kar de do. magar yah sab hai ladakon kaa khel. ghar phoonkane se svaraajy na kabhee milaa hai aur na milegaa.
ratan-mainne kal hee to is vishay par tumse ghanton sirapachchee kee thi aur us samay tum mujhse sahamat ho gayee theen, aaj tum phir vahee shankaaen karne lageen?
gauraa-main tumhaare aprasann ho jaane ke dar se chup ho gayee thi.
ratan-achchhaa, shankaaen phir kar lenaa, is samay jo karn hai woh karo.
gauraa-lekin mere kapade to na loge na?
ratan-sab dene padenge, vilaayat kaa ek soot bhee ghar mein rakhanaa mere pran ko bhang kar degaa.
itane mein raamatahal saaees ne baahar se pukaaraa-sarakaar, log jaldee machaa rahe hain, kehte hain, abhee kaee muhallon kaa chakkar lagaanaa hai. koi gaadhe kaa tukadaa ho to mujhe bhee mil jaay, mainne bhee apne kapade de diye.
kesar maharee kapadon kee ek gatharee lekar baahar jati huee dikhaayee dee. ratanasinh ne poochhaa-kya tum bhee apne kapade dene jati ho?
kesar ne lajaate hue kahaa-haan sarakaar, jab desh chhod raha hai to main kaise pahanoon?
ratanasinh ne gauraa kee ora aadeshapoorn netron se dekh. ab woh vilamb na kar sakee. sharm se sir jhukaaye sandook kholakar kapade nikaalane lagee. ek sandook khaalee ho gaya to usane doosaraa sandook kholaa. sabase oopar ek sanudar reshamee soot rakhaa hua tha jo kunvar sahab ne kisee angarejee kaarakhaane mein silaayaa tha.
gauraa ne poochhaa-kya soot bhee nikaal doon?
ratan-haan-haan, ise kis din ke lie rakhogee?
gauraa-yadi main yah jaanatee ki itanee jaldee havaa badalegee to kabhee yah soot na banavaane detee. saare rupaye khoon ho gaye.
ratanasinh ne kuchh jawab na diya. tab gauraa ne apanaa sandook kholaa aur jalan ke maare svadeshee-videshee sabhee kapade nikaal-nikaal kar phenkane lagee. woh aavesh-pravaah mein aa gayee. unamen kitanee hee bahumooly phainsee jaaket aur saadiyaan theen jinhen kisee samay pahan kar woh phoolee na samaatee thi. baaj-baaj saadiyon ke lie to use ratanasinh se baar-baar takaaje karne pade the. par is samay sab kee sab aankhon mein khatak rahi theen. ratanasinh usake bhaavon ko taad rahe the. svadeshee kapadon kaa nikaalaa jan unhe akhar raha tha, par is samay chup rehne hee mein kushal samajhate the. tis par bhee do-ek baar vaad-vivaad kee naubat aa hee gayee. ek banaarasee saadee ke lie to woh jhagad baithe, use gauraa ke haathon se chheen lenaa chaahaa, par gauraa ne ek na maanee, nikaal hee phenkaa. sahasaa sandook mein se ek kesariyaa rang kee tanajeb kee saadee nikal aayee jis par pakke aanchal aur palle tanke hue the. gauraa ne use jaldee se lekar apanee god mein chhipaa liya.
ratanasinh ne poochhaa-kaisee saadee hai.
gauraa-kuchh nahin, tanajeb kee saadee hai. aanchal pakkaa hai.
ratan-tanajeb kee hai tab to zaroor hee vilaayatee hogee. use alag kyon rakh liya? kya woh banaarasee saadiyon se achchhee hai?
gauraa-achchhee to nahin nhai, par main ise na doongee.
ratan-vaah, vilaayatee cheej ko main na rakhane doongaa. laao idhar.
gauraa-nahin, meree khaatir se ise rehne do.
ratan-tumane meree khaatir se ek bhee cheej na rakhee, main kyon tumhaaree khaatir karoon.
gauraa-pairon padatee hoon, jid na karo.
ratan-svadeshee saadiyon mein se jo chaaho rakh lo, lekin is vilaayatee cheej ko main na rakhane doongaa. isee kapade kee badaulat ham gulaam bane, yah gulaamee kaa daaG main ab nahin rakh sakataa. laao idhar.
gauraa-main ise na doongee, ek baar nahin hazaar baar kehti hoon ki na doongee.
ratan-main ise lekar chhodoongaa, is gulaamee ke patake ko, is daasatv ke bandhan ko kisee tarah na rakhoongaa.
gauraa-naahak jid karte ho.
ratan-aakhir tumako isase kyon itanaa pyaar hai?
gauraa-tum to baal kee khaal nikaalane lagate ho. itane kapade thode hain? ek saadee rakh hee lee to kya?
ratan-tumane abhee tak ina holiyon kaa aashay hee nahin samajhaa.
gauraa-khoob samajhatee hoon. sab dhong hai. chaar din mein josh thandaa pad jaayegaa.
ratan-tum keval itanaa batalaa do ki yah saadee tumhe kyon itanee pyaaree hai, to shaayad main maan jaaoon.
gauraa-yah meree suhaag kee saadee hai.
ratan-(zara der soch kar) tab to main ise kabhee na rakhoongaa. main videshee kapade ko yah shubhasthaan nahin de sakataa. is pavitr sanskaar kaa yah apavitr smriti-chihn ghar mein nahin rakh sakataa. main ise sabase pahale holee kee bhent karoongaa. log kitane hatabuddhi ho gaye the ki aise shubh kaaryon mein bhee videshee vastuon kaa vyavahaar karne mein sankoch na karte the. main ise avashy holee mein doongaa.
gauraa-kaisaa asagun munh se nikaalate ho.
ratan-aisee suhaag kee saadee kaa ghar mein rakhanaa hee ashakun, amangal, anisht aur anarth hai.
gauraa-yon chaahe jabaradastee chheen le jaao, par khushi se na doongee.
ratan-to phir main jabaradastee hee karoongaa. majabooree hai.
yah kah kar woh lapake ki gauraa ke haathon se saadee chheen loon. gauraa ne use majabootee se pakad liya aur ratan kee ora kaatar netron se dekhakar kahaa-tumhe mere sir kee kasam.
kesar maharee bolee-bahoo jee kee ichchhaa hai to rehne deejie.
ratanasinh ke badhe hue haath ruk gaye, munh malin ho gaya. udaas ho kar bole-mujhe apanaa vrat todanaa padegaa. pratijnaa-patr par jhoothe hastaakshar karne padenge. khair, yahee sahee.
3
shaam ho gayee thi. dvaar par svayansevakagan shor machaa rahe the, kunvar sahab jaldee aaie, shreematee jee se bhee kah deejie, hamaaree praarthanaa sveekaar karen. bahut der ho rahi hai. udhar ratanasinh asamanjas mein pade hue the ki pratijnaa-patr par kaise hastaakshar karoon. videshee kapade ghar mein rakh kar svadeshee vrat kaa paalan kyonkar hogaa? aage kadam badhaa chukaa hoon, peechhe nahin hat sakataa. lekin pratijnaa kaa aksharashah paalan karn abheesht bhee to nahin, keval usake aashay par lakshy rahanaa chaahie. is soch se mujhe pratijnaa-patr par hastaakshar karne kaa pooraa adhikaar hai. triyaa-hath ke saamane kisee kee nahin chalatee. yon chaahoon to ek taane mein kaam nikal sakataa hai, par use bahut duhkh hogaa, badee bhaavuk hai, usake bhaavon kaa aadar karn meraa farz hai.
gauraa bhee phikr mein doobee huee thi. suhaag kee saadee suhaag kaa chihn hai, use aag. .. kitane ashakun kee baat hai. ye kabhee-kabhee baalakon kee tarah jid karne lagate hain, apanee dhun mein kisee kee sunate nahin. bigadate hain to maano munh hee nahin seedhaa hot.
lekin woh bechaare bhee to apne siddhaanton se majaboor hain. jhooth se unhe napharat hai. pratijnaa-patr par jhoothee sveekriti likhanee padegee, unakee aatmaa ko badaa duhkh hogaa, ghor dharmasankat mein pade honge, yah bhee to nahin ho sakataa ki saare shahar mein svadeshaanuraagiyon ke siramaur ban kar us pratijnaa-patr par hastaakshar karne se aanaakaanee karen. kaheen munh dikhaane ko jagah na rahegee, log samajhenge, banaa hua hai. par shakun kee cheej kaise doon?
itane mein usane raamatahal saaees ko sir par kapadon kaa gatthar liye baahar jate dekh. kesar maharee bhee ek gatthar sir par rakhe hue thi. peechhe-peechhe ratanasinh haath mein pratijnaa-patr liye jaa rahe the. unake chehare par pachhataavaa kee jhalak thi jaise koi sachchaa aadamee jhoothee gavaahee dene jaa raha ho. gauraa ko dekhakar unhonne aankhen pher leen aur chaahaa ki usakee nigaah bachaakar nikal jaaoon. gauraa ko aisaa jaan padaa ki unakee aankhen dabadabaayee huee hain. woh raah rokakar bolee-zara sunate jaao.
ratan-jaane do, dik na karo; log baahar khade hain.
unhonne chaahaa ki patr ko chhipaa loon; par gauraa ne use unake haath se chheen liya, use gaur se padhaa aur ek pal chintaamagn rehne ke baad bolee-woh saadee bhee lete jaao.
ratan-rehne do, ab to mainne jhooth likh hee diya.
gauraa-main kya jaanatee thi ki tum aisee kadee pratijnaa kar rahe ho.
ratan-yah to main tumse pahale kah chukaa tha.
gauraa-meree bhool thi, maafee kar do aur ise lete jaao.
ratan-jab tum ise denaa ashakun samajhatee ho to rehne do. tumhaaree khaatir thodaa-saa jhooth bolane mein mujhe koi aapatti nahin hai.
gauraa-nahin, lete jaao. amangal ke dar se tumhaaree aatmaa kaa hanan nahin karn chaahatee.
yah kahakar usane apanee suhaag kee saadee uthaakar pati ke haathon mein rakh dee. ratan ne dekh, gauraa ke chehare par ek rang aat hai, ek rang jat hai, jaise koi rogee antarasth visham dard ko dabaane kee cheshtaa kar raha ho. unhe apanee ahridayataa par sharm aayee. haa! keval apne siddhaant kee rakshaa ke lie, apanee aatmaa ke izzat ke lie, main is devee ke bhaavon kaa vadh kar raha hoon! yah atyaachaar hai. saadee gauraa ko de kar bole-tum ise rakh lo, main pratijnaa-patr ko phaade daalataa hoon.
gauraa ne dridhataa se kahaa-tum na le jaaoge to main khud jaa kar de aaoongee.
ratanasinh majaboor ho gaye. saadee lee aur baahar chale aaye.
4
usee din se gauraa ke dil par ek bojh-saa rehne lagaa. woh dil bahalaane ke lie naanaa upaay karti; jalason mein bhaag letee, sair karne jati, manoranjak pustaken padhatee, yahan tak ki kaee baar niyam ke viruddh thiyetaron mein bhee gayee, kisee tarah amangal kalpanaa ko shaant karn chaahatee thi, par yah aashankaa ek meghamandal kee tarah usake dil par chhaayee rehti thi.
jab ek pooraa maheenaa gujar gaya aur usakee maanasik dard dinondin badhatee hee gayee to kunvar sahab ne use kuchh dinon ke lie apne ilaake par le jaane kaa phaisalaa kiya. usakaa man unhe unake aadarsh-pyaar kaa nity tiraskaar kiya kart tha. woh aksar dehaaton mein prachaar kaa kaam karne jaayaa karte the! par ab apne gaanvon se baahar na jate, yaa jate to shaam tak zaroor laut aate. unakee ek din kee der, unakaa aam sir dard aur jukaam use avyavasthit kar dete the. woh bahudhaa bure svapn dekh karti. kisee anisht ke kaalpanik astitv kee chhaayaa use apne chaaron ora mandaraatee huee lagaa hoti thi.
woh to dehaat mein padee huee aashankaaon kee kathaputalee banee huee thi. idhar usakee suhaag kee saadee svadesh-pyaar kee vedee par bhasm hokar riddhi-pradaayinee bhabhoot banee huee thi.
doosare maheene ke ant mein ratanasinh use le kar laut aaye.
5
gauraa ko vaapas aaye teen-chaar din ho chuke the, par asabaab ke sanbhaalane aur niyat sthaan par rakhane mein woh itanee vyast rahi ki ghar se baahar na nikal sakee thi. vajah yah tha ki kesar maharee usake jaane ke doosare hee din chhodakar chalee gayee thi aur abhee utanee hoshiyaar doosaree maharee milee na thi. kunvar sahab kaa saaees raamatahal bhee chhod gaya tha. bechaare kochavaan ko saaees kaa bhee kaam karn padataa tha.
shaam kaa samay tha. gauraa baraamade mein baithee aakaash kee ora ekatak hokar taak rahi thi. chintaagrast praaniyon kaa ekamaatr yahee avalamb hai! sahasaa ratanasinh ne aakar kahaa-chalo, aaj tumhe svadeshee baajaar kee sair karaa laaven. yah meraa hee prastaav tha, par chaar din yahan aaye ho gaye, udhar jaane kaa phursat hee na milaa.
gauraa-meraa to jaane ko jee nahin chaahataa. yaheen baithakar kuchh baaten karo.
ratan-nahin, chalo dekh aaven. ek ghante mein laut aavenge.
ant mein gauraa raajee ho gayee. idhar maheenon se baahar na nikalee thi. aaj use chaaron taraph ek vichitr shobhaa dikhaayee dee. baajaar kabhee itanee raunak par na tha. woh svadeshee baajaar mein pahunchee to julaahon aur koriyon ko apanee-apanee dukaanen sajaaye baithe dekh. sahasaa ek boodhaa koree ne aakar ratanasinh ko salaam kiya. ratanasinh chaunkakar bole-raamatahal, tum ab kahan ho?
raamatahal kaa cheharaa shreesampann tha. usake ang-ang se khuddaaree kee aabhaa jhalak rahi thi. aankhon mein gaurav-jyoti thi. ratanasinh ko kabhee anumaan na hua tha ki astabal saaph karanevaalaa buddhaa raamatahal itanaa saumy, itanaa bhadr aadamee hai. woh bol-sarakaar, ab to apanaa kaarabaar kart hoon. jab se aapakee gulaamee chhodee tab se apne kaam mein lag gaya. aap logon kee nigaah ham gareebon par ho gayee hamaaraa bhee gujar ho raha hai, nahin to aap jaanate hee hain, ki kis haalat mein padaa hua tha. jaat kaa koree hoon, par paapee pet ke lie chamaar ban gaya tha.
ratan-to bhai, ab munh meethaa karaao. yah baajaar lagaane kee meree hee salaah thi, bikree to achchhee hoti hai.
raamatahal-haan sarakaar! aajakal khoob bikree ho rahi hai. maal haatho-haath uda jat hai. yahan baithate hue ek maheenaa ho gaya hai, par aapakee kripaa se logon ke chaar paise the woh bebaak ho gaye. bhagavaan kee raham se rookhaa-sookhaa khana bhee donon samay mil jat hai aur kya chaahie. malakin kee suhaag kee saadee kaa holee mein aan kahie aur baajaar kaa chamakanaa kahie. logon ne kahaa, jab itane bade aadamee ho kar aise shakun kee cheej kee paravaah nahin karte to phir ham videshee kapade kyon rakhen. jis din holee jalee hai usake do-teen din pahale hee sarakaar ilaake par chale gaye the. usake pahale bhee sarakaar kaee dinon tak ghar se bahut kam nikalate the. main to yahee kahoongaa ki yah saaree maayaa usee suhaag kee saadee kee hai.
itane mein ek adhed aurat gauraa ke saamane aa kar bolee-bahoo jee, mujhe bhool to nahin gayeen?
gauraa ne sir uthaayaa to saamane kesar maharee khadee thi. woh sundar saadee pahane hue thi, haath-paanv mein maamoolee gahane bhee the, cheharaa khilaa hua tha. svaadheen jeevan kaa gaurav ek-ek bhaav se prasphutit ho raha tha.
gauraa ne kahaa-itanee jaldee bhool jaaoongee? ab kahan ho? hamen lautane bhee na diya, beech mein hee uda bhaagee.
kesar-kya karoon sarakaar, apanaa kaam chalate dekh kar sabar na ho sakaa. jab tak rojagaar na chalataa tha tab tak laachaaree thi. pet ke lie sevaa-tahal, karam-kukaram sabhee karn padataa tha. ab aap logon kee raham se hamaare bhee din laute hain, ab doosaraa kaam nahin kiya jat. agar baajaar kaa yahee rang raha to apanee kamaaee khaaye na chukegee. yah sab aapakee saadee kee mahimaa hai. usakee badaulat ham gareebon ke kitane hee ghar bas gaye. ek maheenaa pahale ina dookaanavaalon mein se kisee ko rotiyon kaa thikaanaa na tha. koi saaeesee kart tha, koi taase bajaataa tha, yahan tak ki kaee aadamee mehatar kaa kaam karte the. kitane hee bheekh maangate the. ab sab apne dhandhe mein lag gaye hain. sach poochho to tumhaaree suhaag kee saadee ne hamen suhaagin banaa diya, nahin to ham suhaagin hote hue bhee vidhavaaen theen. sach kehti hoon, saikadon jabaanon se nity yahee duaa nikalatee hai ki aapakaa suhaag amar ho, jisane hamaaree raand jaat ko suhaag daan diya.
ratanasinh ek dookaan par baithakar kuchh kapade dekhane lage. gauraa kaa bhaavuk dil khushi se pulakit ho raha tha. usakee saaree amangal kalpanaaen svapnavat vichchhinn hoti jati theen. aankhen sajal ho gayee theen aur suhaag kee devee ashrusanchit netron ke saamane khadee aanchal phailaa kar use aasheervaad de rahi thi.
usane ratanasinh ko bhaktipoorn aankhon se dekh kar kahaa-mere lie bhee ek saadee le lo.
6
jab gauraa yahan se chalee to sadak kee bijaliyaan jal chukee theen. sadakon par khoob prakaash tha. usakaa dil bhee khushi ke prakaash se jagamagaa raha tha.
ratanasinh ne poochhaa-seedhe ghar chaloon?
gauraa-nahin, chhaavanee kee taraph hote chalo.
ratan-baajaar khoob sajaa hua tha.
gauraa-yah jameen le kar ek sthaayee baajaar banavaa do. svadeshee kapadon kee dookaanen hon aur kisee se kiraayaa na liya jaay.
ratan-bahut kharch padegaa.
gauraa-makaan bech do, rupaye hee rupaye ho jaayenge.
ratan-aur rahen, ped tale?
gauraa-nahin, gaanvavaale makaan mein.
ratan-sochoongaa.
gauraa-(zara der mein) ilaake-bhar mein khoob kapaas kee khetee karaao, jo kapaas boye usakee begaar maaph kar do.
ratan-haan, tadabeer achchhee hai, doonee upaj ho jaayegee.
gauraa-(kuchh der sochane ke baad) lakadee binaa daam do to kaisaa ho? jo chaahe, charakhe banavaane ke lie kaat le jaaye.
ratan-loot mach jaayegee.
gauraa-aisee beeemaanee koi na karegaa.
jab usane gaadee se utar kar ghar mein kadam rakhaa to chitt shubh-kalpanaaon se praphullit ho raha tha. maano koi bachhadaa khoonte se chhootakar kilolen kar raha ho.
