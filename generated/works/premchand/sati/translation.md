# Sati

**Munshi Premchand**

muliyaa ko dekhate hue usakaa pati kalloo kuchh bhee nahin hai. phir kya vajah hai ki muliyaa santusht aur khush hai aur kalloo chintit aur sashankit? muliyaa ko kaudee milee hai, use doosaraa kaun poochhegaa? kalloo ko ratn milaa hai, usake saikadon graahak ho sakate hain. khaasakar use apne chachere bhai raajaa se bahut khatakaa rehta hai. raajaa roopavaan hai, rasik hai, baatacheet mein kushal hai, striyon ko rijhaanaa jaanataa hai. isase kalloo muliyaa ko baahar nahin nikalane detaa. us par kisee kee nigaah bhee pad jaay, yah use asahy hai. woh ab raat-din mehanat kart hai, jisse muliyaa ko kisee baat kaa takaleeph na ho. use na jaane kis pahale-janm ke sanskaar se aisee aurat mil gayee hai. us par praanon ko nyochhaavar kar denaa chaahataa hai. muliyaa kaa kabhee sir bhee dukhataa hai, to usakee jaan nikal jati hai. muliyaa kaa bhee yah haal hai ki jab tak woh ghar nahin aat, machhalee kee tarah tadapatee rehti hai. gaanv mein kitane hee naujavaan hain, jo muliyaa se chhedachhaad karte rehte hain; par us naujavaan ladakee kee nazar mein kuroop kaluaa sansaar-bhar ke aadamiyon se achchhaa hai.
ek din raajaa ne kahaa, 'bhaabhee, bhaiyaa tumhaare jog na the. '
muliyaa bolee, 'bhaag mein to woh likhe the; tum kaise milate? '
raajaa ne man mein samajhaa, 'bas, ab maar liya hai. bol, 'vidhi ne yahee to bhool kee. '
muliyaa muskaraakar bolee, 'apanee bhool to vahee sudharegaa. raajaa nihaal ho gaya. '
teej ke din kalloo muliyaa ke lie latthe kee saadee laayaa. chaahataa to tha koi achchhee saadee le; par rupaye na the aur bajaaj ne udhaar na maanaa.
raajaa bhee usee din apne bhaagy kee pareekshaa karn chaahataa tha. ek sundar chundaree laakar muliyaa ko bhent kee.
muliyaa ne kahaa, 'mere lie to saadee aa gayee hai. '
raajaa bol, 'mainne dekhee hai. tabhee to main ise laayaa. tumhaare laayak nahin hai. bhaiyaa ko kiphaayat bhee soojhatee hai, to aisee baaton mein. '
muliyaa kataaksh karake bolee, 'tum samajhaa kyon nahin dete? '
raajaa par ek kulhad kaa nashaa chadh gaya. bol, 'boodhaa totaa nahin padhataa hai. '
muliyaa -'mujhe to latthe kee saadee hee pasand hai. '
raajaa -'zaraa yah chundaree pahanakar dekho, kaisee khilatee hai. '
muliyaa -'zo latthaa pahanaakar khush hot hai, woh chundaree pahan lene se khush na hogaa. unhe chundaree pasand hoti, to chundaree hee laate. '
raajaa -'unhe dikhaane kaa kaam nahin hai? '
muliyaa hairaanee se bolee, 'main kya unse binaa poochhe le loongee? '
raajaa-'isamen poochhane kee kaun-see baat hai. jab woh kaam par chalaa jaay, pahan lenaa. main bhee dekh loongaa. '
muliyaa thatthaa maarakar hansatee huee bolee, 'yah na hogaa, devarajee. kaheen dekh len, to meree saamat hee aa jaay. ise tum liye jaao. '
raajaa ne aagrah karake kahaa, 'ise na logee bhaabhee, to main jahar khaake so rahoongaa. '
muliyaa ne saadee uthaakar aale par rakh dee aur bolee, 'achchhaa lo, ab to khush hue. '
raajaa ne ungalee pakadee'abhee to bhaiyaa nahin hain, zara pahan lo. '
muliyaa ne andar jaakar chundaree pahan lee aur phool kee tarah mahakatee-damakatee baahar aayee.
raajaa ne pahunchaa pakadane ko haath phailaayaa bol, 'aisaa jee chaahataa hai ki tumhe lekar bhaag jaaoon. '
muliyaa usee vinod-bhaav se bolee, 'zaanate ho, tumhaare bhaiyaa kaa kya haal hogaa? '

yah kehte hue usane kivaad band kar liye. raajaa ko aisaa maaloom hua ki thaalee parosakar usake saamane se uthaa lee gayee. muliyaa kaa man baar-baar kart tha ki chundaree kalloo ko dikhaa de; par nateejaa sochakar raha jati thi. usane chundaree rakh kyon lee? use apne oopar gussaa aa raha tha, lekin raajaa ko kitanaa du: kha hot. kya hua usakee chundaree chhan-bhar pahan lene se, usakaa man to raha gaya. lekin usake prashaant maanas-saagar mein yah ek keet aakar use math raha tha. usane kyon chundaree rakh lee? kya yah kalloo ke saath vishvaasaghaat nahin hai? usakaa chitt is soch se vikal ho gaya. usane man ko samajhaayaa, vishvaasaghaat kyon hua; isamen vishvaasaghaat kee kya baat hai. kaun woh raajaa se kuchh bolee, ? zara-saa hans dene se agar kisee kaa dil khush ho jat
hai, to isamen kya buraaee hai?
kalloo ne poochhaa, 'aaj rajjoo kya karne aay tha? '
muliyaa kee deh thar-thar kaanpane lagee. bahaanaa kar gayee, 'tamaakhoo maangane aaye the. '
kalloo ne bheven sikodakar kahaa, 'use andar mata aane diya karo. achchhaa aadamee nahin hai. '
muliyaa-' mainne kah diya tamaakhoo nahin hai, to chale gaye. '
kalloo ne abakee tejasvitaa ke saath kahaa, 'kyon jhooth bolti hai? woh tamaakhoo maangane nahin aay tha. '
muliyaa -'to aur yahan kya karne aate?
kalloo -'chaahe jis kaam se aay ho, tamaakhoo maangane nahin aay. woh jaanataa tha, mere ghar mein tamaakhoo nahin hai. main tamaakhoo ke lie usake ghar gaya tha. '
muliyaa kee deh mein kaato to lahoo nahin. chehare kaa rang uda gaya. sir jhukaakar bolee, 'main kisee ke man kaa haal kya jaanoon? '
aaj teej kaa ratajagaa tha. muliyaa poojaa kaa saamaan kar rahi thi; par is tarah jaise man mein zara bhee utsaah, zara bhee shrriddhaa nahin hai. use aisaa maaloom ho raha hai, usake munh mein kaalimaa put gayee hai aur ab woh kalloo kee aankhon se gir gayee hai. use apanaa jeevan niraadhaar-saa jaan padataa tha. sochane lagee bhagavaan ne mujhe yah roop kyon diya? yah roop na hot to raajaa kyon mere peechhe padataa aur kyon aaj meree yah gat hoti? main kaalee-kuroop rahakar isase kaheen sukhee rehti. tab to man itanaa chanchal na hot. jinhen roop kee kamaaee khaanee ho, woh roop par phoolen, yahan to isane matiyaamet kar diya.
na jaane kab use jhapakee aa gayee, to dekhatee hai, kalloo mar gaya hai aur raajaa ghar mein ghusakar use pakadanaa chaahataa hai. usee dam ek vriddhaa aurat na jaane kidhar se aakar use apanee god mein le letee hai aur kehti hai toone
kalloo ko kyon maar daalaa? muliyaa rokar kehti hai maan, mainne unhe nahin maaraa. vriddhaa kehti hai haan, toone chhuree-kataar se nahin maaraa, us din teraa tap chheen ho gaya aur isee se woh mar gaya.
muliyaa ne chaukannee aankhen khol deen. saamane aangan mein kalloo soyaa hua tha. woh daudee huee usake paas gayee aur usakee chhaatee par sir rakhakar phoot-phootakar rone lagee.
kalloo ne ghabadaakar poochhaa, 'kaun hai? muliyaa! kyon rotee ho? kya dar lag raha hai. main to jaag hee raha hoon. '
muliyaa ne sisakate hue kahaa, 'mujhse aaj ek jurm hua hai. use maafee kar do. '
kalloo uth baithaa, 'kya baat hai? kaho to, rotee kyon ho? '
muliyaa -'raajaa tamaakhoo maangane nahin aay tha. mainne tumse jhooth kahaa tha. '
kalloo hansakar bol, 'woh to main pahale hee samajh gaya tha. '
muliyaa -'woh mere lie chundaree laayaa tha. '
'tumane lautaa dee? '
muliyaa kaanpatee huee bolee, 'mainne le lee. kehte the, main jahar-maahur khaa loongaa. '
kalloo nirjeev kee tarah khaat par gir padaa aur bol, 'to roop mere bas kaa nahin hai. dev ne kuroop banaa diya, to sundar kaise ban jaaoon? '
kalloo ne agar muliyaa ko khaulate hue tel mein daal diya hot, to bhee use itanee dard na hoti. kalloo us din se kuchh khoyaa-khoyaa-saa rehne lagaa. jeevan mein na woh utsaah raha, na woh aanand. hansanaa-bolanaa bhool-saa gaya. muliyaa ne usake saath jitanaa vishvaasaghaat kiya tha, usse kaheen zyada usane samajh liya. aur yah bhram usake hraday mein kevade ke samaan chipat gaya. woh ghar ab usake lie keval letane-baithane kaa sthaan tha aur muliyaa keval khana banaa denevaalee masheen. khushi ke lie woh kabhee-kabhee taadeekhaane chalaa jat, yaa charas ke dam lagaataa. muliyaa usakee dashaa dekh-dekh andar-hee-andar kudhatee thi. woh us baat
ko usake di se nikaal denaa chaahatee thi, isalie usakee sevaa aur man lagaa kar karti. use khush karne ke lie baar-baar koshish karti; par woh jitanaa hee usako kheenchane kee cheshtaa karti thi, utanaa hee woh usse vichalataa
tha, jaise koi ketiye mein phesee huee machhalee ho. kushal yah tha ki raajaa jis angrej ke yahan khaanasaamaa tha, usakaa tabaadalaa ho gaya aur raajaa usake saath chalaa gaya tha, nahin to donon bhaaiyon mein se kisee-na-kisee kaa zaroor khoon ho jat. is tarah saal-bhar beet gaya.
ek din kalloo raat ko ghar lautaa, to use jvar tha. doosare din usakee deh mein daane nikal aaye. muliyaa ne samajhaa, maan hain. maan-manautee karne lagee; magar chaar-paanch din mein hee daane badhakar aavale pad gaye aur maaloom hua ki yah maan nahin hain; upadansh hai. kalloo ke kalushit bhog-vilaas kaa yah phal tha.
rog itanee bhayankarataa se badhane lagaa ki aavalon mein mavaad pad gaya aur unamen se aisee durgandh udane lagee ki paas baithate naak phatatee thi. dehaat mein jis tarah kaa upachaar ho sakataa tha, woh muliyaa karti thi; par koi phaayadaa na hot tha aur kalloo kee dashaa din-din bigadatee jati thi. upachaar kee kasar woh abalaa apanee snehamay sevaa se pooree karti thi. us par ghar-gharavaale chalaane ke lie ab mehanat-majooree bhee karanee padatee thi. kalloo to apne kiye kaa phal bhog raha tha. muliyaa apne farz kaa paalan karne mein maree jaa rahi thi. agar kuchh santosh tha, to yah kalloo kaa bhram usakee is tapasyaa se bhang hot jat tha. use ab yakeen hone lagaa tha ki muliyaa ab bhee usee kee hai. woh agar kisee tarah achchhaa ho jat, to phir use dil mein chhipaakar rakhataa aur usakee poojaa kart.
praat: kaal tha. muliyaa ne kalloo kaa haath-munh dhulaakar davaa pilaayee aur khadee pankhaa dulaa rahi thi ki kalloo ne aansoo-bharee aankhon se dekhakar kahaa, 'muliyaa, mainne us janm mein koi bhaaree tap kiya tha ki too mujhe mil
gayee. tumhaaree jagah mujhe duniya kaa raaj mile to bhee na loon. '
muliyaa ne donon haathon se usakaa munh band kar diya aur bolee, 'is tarah kee baaten karoge, to main rone lagoongee. mere dhany bhaag ki tum-jaisaa swami milaa. ' yah kehte hue usane donon haath pati ke gale mein daal diye aur lipat gayee. phir bolee, 'bhagavaan ne mujhe mere paapon kaa sazaa diya hai. '
kalloo ne utsukataa se poochhaa, 'sach kah do moolaa, raajaa aur tumamen kya maamalaa tha?
muliyaa ne vismit hokar kahaa, 'mere aur usake beech koi aur maamalaa hua ho, to bhagavaan meree durgati karen. usane mujhe chundaree dee thi. woh mainne le lee thi! phir mainne use aag mein jalaa diya. tab se main usse nahin bolee. '
kalloo ne thandee saans kheenchakar kahaa, 'mainne kuchh aur hee samajh rakhaa tha. na-jaane meree mati kahan har gayee thi. tumhe paap lagaakar main aap paap mein phes gaya aur usakaa phal bhog raha hoon. '
usane ro-rokar apne dushkrityon kaa paradaa kholanaa shuroo kiya aur muliyaa aansoo kee ladiyaan bahaakar sunane lagee. agar pati kee phikr na hoti, to usane vish khaa liya hot.

kaee maheene ke baad raajaa chhuttee lekar ghar aay aur kalloo kee ghaatak beemaaree kaa haal sunaa, to dil mein khush hua; teemaaradaaree ke bahaane se kalloo ke ghar aane-jaane lagaa. kalloo use dekhakar munh pher letaa, lekin woh din mein do-chaar baar pahunch hee jat.
ek din muliyaa khana pakaa rahi thi ki raajaa ne rasoee ke dvaar par aakar kahaa, 'bhaabhee, kyon ab bhee mujh par raham na karogee? kitanee beraham ho tum! kai din se tumhe khoj raha hoon, par tum mujhse bhaagatee phiratee ho.
bhaiyaa ab achchhe na honge. inhen garmee ho gayee hai. inake saath kyon apanee zindagi kharaab kar rahi ho? tumhaaree phool-see deh sookh gayee hai. mere saath chalo, kuchh zindagi kee bahaar udaayen. yah javaanee bahut din na rahegee. yah dekho, tumhaare lie ek karanaphool laayaa hoon, zara pahanakar mujhe dikhaa do. '
usane karanaphool muliyaa kee ora badhaa diya. muliyaa ne usakee ora dekh bhee nahin. choolhe kee ora taakatee huee bolee, 'laalaa, tumhaare pairon padatee hoon, mujhe mata chhedo. yah saaree vipatti tumhaaree laayee huee hai. tumheen mere shatru
ho. phir bhee tumhe laaj nahin aati. kehte ho, bhaiyaa ab kis kaam ke hain? mujhe to ab woh pahale se kaheen zyada achchhe lagate hain. jab main na hoti, to woh doosaree sagaaee kar laate, apne haathon thok khaate. aaj main hee unakaa aadhaar hoon. woh mere sahaare jeete hain. agar main is sankat mein unake saath dagaa karoon, to mujhse badhakar adham kaun hogaa. jabaki main jaanatee hoon ki is sankat kaa vajah bhee main hee hoon. '
raajaa ne hansakar kahaa, 'yah to vahee hua, jaise kisee kee daal gir gayee, to usane kahaa, mujhe to sookhee hee achchhee lagatee hai. '
muliyaa ne sir uthaakar usakee ora sajot netron se taakate hue kahaa, 'tum unake pairon kee dhool ke baraabar nahin ho laalaa, kya kehte ho tum? ujale kapade aur chikane mukhade se koi aadamee sundar nahin hot. meree aankhon mein
to unake baraabar koi dikhaayee nahin detaa. '
kalloo ne pukaaraa, 'moolaa, thodaa paani de. ' muliyaa paani lekar daudee.
chalate-chalate karanaphool ko aisaa thukaraayaa ki aangan mein jaa giraa. raajaa ne jaldee se karanaphool uthaa liya aur gussaa mein bharaa hua chal diya.
rog din-par-din badhataa gaya. thikaane se davaa-daaroo hoti, to shaayad achchhaa ho jat; magar akelee muliyaa kya-kya karti? daridrataa mein beemaaree kodh kaa khaaj hai. aakhir ek din paravaanaa aa pahunchaa. muliyaa ghar kaa kaam-dhandhaa karake aayee, to dekh kalloo kee saans chal rahi hai. ghabadaakar bolee, 'kaisaa jee hai tumhaaraa? '
kalloo ne sajal aur deenataa-bharee aankhon se dekh aur haath jodakar sir neechaa kar liya. yahee antim bidaaee thi.
muliyaa usake seene par sir rakhakar rone lagee aur unmaad kee dashaa mein usake aahat hraday se rakt kee boondon ke samaan shabd nikalane lage, 'tumse itanaa bhee na dekh gaya, bhagavan! us par nyaayee aur dayaalu kahalaate ho!
iseelie tumane janm diya! yahee khel khelane ke lie! haay naath! tum to itane nishthur na the! mujhe akelee chhodakar chale jaa rahe ho! haay! ab kaun moolaa kahakar pukaaregaa! ab kisake lie kuen se paani kheenchoongee! kise baithaakar khilaaoongee, pankhaa dulaaoongee! sab sukh har liya, to mujhe bhee kyon nahin uthaa lete! '
saaraa gaanv jamaa ho gaya. sabhee samajhaa rahe the. muliyaa ko sabr na hot tha. yah sab mere vajah hua; yah baat use nahin bhoolatee. haay! haay! use bhagavaan ne saamarthy diya hot, to aaj usakaa sirataaj yon uth jat?
shav kee daah-kriyaa kee taiyaariyaan hone lageen. kalloo ko mare chh: maheene ho gaye. muliyaa apanaa kamaatee hai, khaatee hai aur apne ghar mein padee rehti hai. din-bhar kaam-dhandho se chhuttee nahin milatee. haan, raat ko ekaant mein ro liya karti hai.
udhar raajaa kee aurat mar gayee, magar do-chaar din ke baad woh phir chhailaa banaa ghoomane lagaa. aur bhee chhootaa saand ho gaya. pahale aurat se jhagadaa ho jaane kaa kuchh dar tha. ab woh bhee na raha. abakee naukaree par se lautaa, to seedhe
muliyaa ke ghar pahunchaa. aur idhar-udhar kee baaten karne ke baad bol, 'bhaabhee, ab to meree chaah pooree karogee yaa abhee aur kuchh baakee hai? ab to bhaiyaa bhee nahin rahe. idhar meree gharavaalee bhee sidhaaree! mainne to usakaa gam bhulaa diya. tum kab tak bhaiyaa ke naam ko rotee rahogee? '
muliyaa ne napharat se usakee ora dekhakar kahaa, 'bhaiyaa nahin rahe, to kya hua; bhaiyaa kee yaad to hai, unakaa pyaar to hai, unakee soorat to dil mein hai, unakee baaten to kaanon mein hain. tumhaare lie aur duniya ke lie woh nahin hain, mere lie woh ab bhee vaise hee jeete-jaagate hain. main ab bhee unhe vaise hee baithe dekhatee hoon. pahale to deh kaa antar tha. ab to woh mujhse aur bhee nageech ho gaye hain. aur jyon-jyon din beetenge aur bhee nageech hote jaayange. bhare-poore ghar mein daane kee kaun kadar kart hai. jab ghar khaalee ho jat hai, tab maaloom hot hai ki daanaa kya hai. paisevaale paise kee kadar kya jaanen. paise kee kadar tab hoti hai, jab haath khaalee ho jat hai. tab aadamee ek-ek kaudee daant se pakadataa hai. tumhe bhagavaan ne dil hee nahin diya, tum kya jaano, sohabat kya hai. gharavaalee ko mare abhee chh: maheene bhee nahin hue aur tum saand ban baithe. tum mar gaye hote, to isee tarah woh bhee ab tak kisee ke paas chalee gayee hoti? main jaanatee hoon ki main mar jati, to meraa sirataaj'janm' bhar mere naam ko royaa kart. aise hee purushon kee striyaan una par jaan detee hain. tum-jaise sohadon ke bhaag mein pattal chaatanaa likhaa hai; chaato; magar khabaradaar, aaj se mere ghar mein paanv na rakhanaa, nahin to jaan se haath dhooge! bas, nikal jaao. '
usake munh par aisaa tej, aavaaz mein itanee katutaa thi ki raajaa ko jabaan kholane kaa bhee saahas na hua. chupake se nikal bhaagaa.
