# Ramleela

**Munshi Premchand**

idhar ek muddat se raamaleelaa dekhne nahin gaya. bandaron ke bhadde chehare lagaaye,aadhee taangon kaa paajaamaa aur kaale rang kaa oonchaa kurataa pahane aadamiyon ko daudate, hoo-hoo karte dekh kar ab hansee aatee hai; majaa nahin aat. kaashee ki
leelaa jagadvikhyaat hai. sunaa hai, log door-door se dekhne aate hain. main bhi bade shauk se gaya; par mujhe to wahan ki leelaa aur kisee bajr dehaat ki leelaa mein koi antar na dikhaayee diya. haan, raamanagar ki leelaa mein kuchh saaj-saamaan achchhe hain. raakshason aur bandaron ke chehare peetal ke hain, gadaaen bhi peetal ki hain; kadaachit navaasee bhraataaon ke mukut sachche kaam ke hon; lekin saaj-saamaan ke sivaa wahan bhi wahi hoo-hoo ke sivaa aur kuchh nahin. phir bhi laakhon aadamiyon ki bheed lagee rehti.
lekin ek jamaanaa vah tha, jab mujhe bhi raamaleelaa mein khushi aat tha. khushi to bahut halakaa-saa shabd hai. vah khushi unmaad se kam na tha. sanyogavash una dinon mere ghar se bahut thodee door par raamaleelaa kaa maidaan tha;
aur jis ghar mein leelaa-paatron kaa roop-rang bharaa jat tha, vah to mere ghar se bilakul milaa hua tha. do baje din se paatron ki sajaavat hone lagti thi. main dopahar hi se wahan jaa baithta, aur jis utsaah se daud-daud kar chhote-mote kaam kart, us utsaah se to aaj apni penshan lene bhi nahin jat. ek kotharee mein raajakumaaree kaa shringaar hot tha. unki deh mein raamaraj pees kar potee jati; munh par paaudar lagaayaa jat aur paaudar ke oopar laal, hare, neele rang ki bundakiyaan lagaayee jati theen. sara maathaa, bhaunhen, gaal, thodee bundakiyon se rach uthti theen. ek hi aadamee is kaam mein kushal tha. wahi baaree-baaree se teenon paatron kaa shringaar kart tha. rang ki pyaaliyon mein paani laanaa, raamaraj peesanaa, pankhaa jhalanaa mera kaam tha. jab ina taiyaariyon ke baad vimaan nikalataa, to us par raamachandr jee ke peechhe baith kar mujhe jo ullaas, jo garv, jo romaanch hot tha vah ab laat sahab ke darabaar mein kurasee par baith kar bhi nahin hot. ek baar jab hom-membar sahab ne vyavasthaapak-sabhaa mein mere lie ek prastaav kaa anumodan kiya tha, us vakt mujhe kuchh usi tarah kaa ullaas, garv aur romaanch hua tha. haan, ek baar jab mera jyeshth betaa naayab-tahaseeladaaree mein naamajad hua, tab bhi aisee hi tarangen man mein uthee theen; par inamen aur us baal-vihnalataa mein bada antar hai. tab aisaa maaloom hot tha ki main svarg mein baithaa hoon.
nishaad-naukaa-leelaa kaa din tha. main do-chaar ladakon ke bahakaane mein aakar gullee-dandaa khelne lagaa tha. aaj shringaar dekhne na gaya. vimaan bhi nikalaa; par maine khelna na chhodaa. mujhe apna daanv lena tha. apna daanv chhodane ke lie usse kaheen badhakar aatmatyaag ki jaroorat thi, jitanaa main kar sakataa tha. agar daanv dena hot to main kab kaa bhaag khadaa hot; lekin padaane mein kuchh aur hi baat hoti hai. khair, daanv pooraa hua. agar main chahta, to dhaandhaalee karake das-paanch minat aur padaa sakataa tha, isakee kaaphee gunjaaish thi, lekin ab
isakaa maukaa na tha. main seedhe naale ki taraph daudaa. vimaan jalatat par pahunch chukaa tha. maine door se dekh, mallaah kishtee liye aa raha hai. daudaa, lekin aadamiyon ki bheed mein daudanaa mushkil tha. aakhir jab main bheed hataataa, praan-pan se aage badhta ghaat par pahunchaa, to nishaad apni naukaa khol chukaa tha. raamachandr par meri kitanee shrriddhaa thi! apne paath ki phikr na karake unhen padha diya kart tha, jisamen vah phel na ho jaaen. mujhse umr zyada hone par bhi vah neechee kakshaa mein padhte the. lekin wahi raamachandr naukaa par baithe is tarah munh phere chale jaate the, maano mujhse jaan-pahachaan hi nahin. nakal mein bhi asal ki kuchh na kuchh boo aa hi jati hai. bhakton par jinakee nigaah sadaa hi teekhee rahi hai, vah mujhe kyon ubaarate? main vikal ho kar us bachhade ki tarah koodane lagaa, jisakee garadan par pahalee baar juaa rakhaa gaya ho. kabhi lapak kar naale ki ora jat, kabhi kisee sahaayak ki khoj mein peechhe ki taraph daudataa, par sab ke sab apni dhun mein mast the; meri cheekh-pukaar kisee ke kaanon tak na pahunchee. tab se badi-badi vipattiaayaan jheleen; par us samay jitanaa du:kha hua, utanaa phir
kabhi na hua. maine phaisalaa kiya tha ki ab raamachandr se na kabhi boloongaa, na kabhi khaane ki koi cheej hi doongaa; lekin jyon hi naale ko paar karake vah pul ki ora laute; main daud kar vimaan par chadh gaya, aur aisaa khush hua, maano koi baat hi na hui thi.
raamaleelaa khatm ho gayi thi. raajagaddee honevaalee thi; par na jaane kyon der ho rahi thi. shaayad chandaa kam vasool hua tha. raamachandr ki ina dinon koi baat bhi na poochhta tha. na ghar hi jaane ki chhuttee milatee thi, na khaanaa kaa hi intazaam hot tha. chaudharee sahab ke yahan se ek seedhaa koi teen baje din ko milataa tha. baakee saare din koi paani ko nahin poochhta. lekin meri shrriddhaa abhi tak jyon ki tyon thi. meri nazar mein ab bhi raamachandr hi the. ghar par mujhe khaane ko koi cheej milatee, vah le kar raamachandr ko de aat. unhen khilaane mein mujhe jitanaa khushi milataa tha, utanaa aap khaa jaane mein kabhi na milataa. koi mithaaee yaa phal paate hi main betahaashaa chaupaal ki ora daudataa. agar raamachandr wahan na milate to unhen chaaron ora talaash kart, aur jab tak vah cheej unhen na khilaa letaa, mujhe chain na aat tha. khair, raajagaddee kaa din aay. raamaleelaa ke maidaan mein ek bada-saa shaamiyaanaa taanaa gaya. uski khoob sajaavat ki gayi. veshyaaon ke dal bhi aa pahunche. shaam ko raamachandr ki savaaree nikalee, aur har dvaar par unki aaratee utaaree gayi. shrriddhaanusaar kisee ne rupaye diye, kisee ne paise. mere pitaa pulis ke aadamee the; isalie unhone binaa kuchh diye hi aaratee utaaree. us vakt mujhe jitanee sharm aayee, use bayaan nahin kar sakataa. mere paas us vakt sanyog se ek rupayaa tha. mere maamaa jee dashahare ke pahale aaye the aur
mujhe ek rupayaa de gaye the. us rupaye ko maine rakh chhodaa tha. dashahare ke din bhi use kharch na kar sakaa. maine turant vah rupayaa laa kar aaratee ki thaalee mein daal diya. pitaa jee meri ora gusse mein netron se dekh kar raha gaye.
unhone kuchh kahaa to nahin; lekin munh aisaa banaa liya, jisse prakat hot tha ki meri is dhrishtataa se unake rob mein battaa lag gaya.
raat ke das bajate-bajate yah parikramaa pooree hui. aaratee ki thaalee rupayon aur paison se bharee hui thi. theek to nahin kah sakataa; magar ab aisaa anumaan hot hai ki chaar-paanch sau rupayon se kam na the. chaudharee sahab inse kuchh zyada hi kharch kar chuke the. unhen isakee badi phikr hui ki kisee tarah kam se kam do sau rupaye aur vasool ho jaaen aur isakee sabase achchhi tarakeeb unhen yahi maaloom hui ki veshyaaon dvaaraa mahaphil mein vasoolee ho. jab log aa kar baith jaaen, aur mahaphil kaa rang jam jaae, to aabaadeejaan rasikajanon ki kalaaiyaan pakad-pakad
kar aise haav-bhaav dikhaayen ki log sharamaate-sharamaate bhi kuchh na kuchh de hi maren. aabaadeejaan aur chaudharee sahab mein salaah hone lagee. main sanyog se una donon praaniyon ki baaten sun raha tha. chaudharee sahab ne samajhaa hogaa yah laundaa kya matalab samajhegaa. par yahan eeshvar ki dayaa se akl ke putale the. sari daastaan samajh mein aatee jati thi.
chaudharee - suno aabaadeejaan, yah tumhari jyaadatee hai. hamaaraa aur tumhara koi pahalaa saabikaa to hai nahin. eeshvar ne chaahaa, to yahan hameshaa tumhara aan-jan lagaa rahegaa. ab ki chandaa bahut kam aay, nahin to main tumse itanaa isaraar na kart.
aabaadee. - aap mujhse bhi jameendaaree chaalen chalte hain, kyon, magar yahan hujoor ki daal na galegee. vaah! rupaye to main vasool karoon, aur moonchhon par taav aap den. kamaaee kaa achchha dhang nikaalaa hai. is kamaaee se to vaakaee aap thode dinon mein raajaa ho jaaeenge. usake saamane jameendaaree jhak maaregee! bas, kal hi se ek chakalaa khol deejie! khudaa ki kasam, maalaamaal ho jaaiegaa.
chaudharee - tum dillagee karti ho, aur yahan kaaphiyaa tang ho raha hai.
aabaadee. - to aap bhi to mujhee se ustaadee karte hain. yahan aap-jaise kaaniyon ko roj ungaliyon par nachaatee hoon.
chaudharee - aakhir tumhari manshaa kya hai?
aabaadee. - jo kuchh vasool karoon, usamen aadhaa mera, aadhaa aapakaa. laaie, haath maarie.
chaudharee - yahi sahee.
aabaadee. - achchha, to pahale mere sau rupaye gin deejie. peechhe se aap alaset karne lagenge.
chaudharee - vaah! vah bhi logee aur yah bhi.
aabaadee. - achchha! to kya aap samajhte the ki apni ujarat chhod doongee? vaaharee aapakee samajh! khoob; kyon na ho. deevaanaa bakaare daravesh hushiyaar!
chaudharee - to kya tumne doharee phees lene ki thaanee hai?
aabaadee. - agar aapako sau daphe garaj ho, to. varanaa mere sau rupaye to kaheen gaye hi nahin. mujhe kya kutto ne kaataa hai, jo logon ki jeb mein haath daalatee phiroon?
chaudharee ki ek na chalee. aabaadee ke saamane dabanaa padaa. naach shuru hua. aabaadeejaan balaa ki shokh aurat thi. ek to kamasin, us par haseen. aur uski adaaen to is gazab ki theen ki meri tabeeyat bhi mast hui jati
thi. aadamiyon ke pahachaanane kaa gun bhi usamen kuchh kam na tha. jisake saamane baith gayi, usse kuchh na kuchh le hi liya. paanch rupaye se kam to shaayad hi kisee ne diye hon. pitaa jee ke saamane bhi vah baithee. main maare sharm ke gad gaya. jab usane unki kalaaee pakadee, tab to main saham uthaa. mujhe yakeen tha ki pitaa jee uska haath jhatak denge aur shaayad dutkaar bhi den, lekin yah kya ho raha hai! eeshvar! meri aankhen dhokhaa to nahin khaa rahi hain! pitaa jee moonchhon mein hans rahe hain. aisee mridu-hansee unake chehare par maine kabhi nahin dekhee thi. unki aankhon se anuraag tapakaa padataa tha. unakaa ek-ek rom pulakit ho raha tha; magar eeshvar ne meri laaj rakh lee. vah dekho, unhone dheere se aabaadee ke komal haathon se apni kalaaee chhudaa lee. are! yah phir kya hua? aabaadee to unake gale mein baahen daale detee hai. ab pitaa jee use zaroor peetenge. chudail ko zara bhi sharm nahin.
ek mahaashay ne muskaraa kar kahaa, yahan tumhari daal na galegee, aabaadeejaan! aur daravaajaa dekho.
baat to ina mahaashay ne mere man ki kahin, aur bahut hi theek kahin; lekin na-jaane kyon pitaa jee ne uski ora gusse mein netron se dekh, aur moonchhon par taav diya. munh se to vah kuchh na bole; par unake munh ki aakriti chillaa kar sarosh shabdon mein kah rahi thi, ' too baniyaa, mujhe samajhta kya hai? yahan aise avasar par jaan tak nisaar karne ko taiyaar hain. rupaye ki hakeekat hi kya! tera jee chaahe, aajamaa le. tujhase doonee rakam na de daaloon, to munh na dikhaaoon! ' mahaan hairaanee! ghor anarth! are, jameen too phat kyon nahin jati? aakaash, too phat kyon nahin padataa? are, mujhe maut kyon nahin aa jati! pitaa jee jeb mein haath daal rahe hain. vah koi cheej nikaalee, aur seth jee ko dikhaa kar aabaadeejaan ko de daalee. aah! yah to asharphee hai. chaaron ora taaliyaan
bajane lageen. seth jee ulloo ban gaye yaa pitaa jee ne munh ki khaayee, isakaa phaisalaa main nahin kar sakataa. maine keval itanaa dekh ki pitaa jee ne ek asharphee nikaal kar aabaadeejaan ko dee. unki aankhon mein is samay itanaa garvayukt ullaas tha maano unhone haatim ki kabr par laat maaree ho.
yahi pitaa jee hain, jinhonne mujhe aaratee mein ek rupayaa daalate dekh kar meri ora is tarah se dekh tha, maano mujhe phaad hi khaayenge. mere us paramochit vyavahaar se unake rob mein phark aat tha, aur is samay is ghrinit, kutsit aur nindit vyaapaar par garv aur khushi se phoole na samaate the. aabaadeejaan ne ek manohar muskaan ke saath pitaa jee ko salaam kiya aur aage badhee; magar mujhse wahan na baithaa gaya. maare sharm ke mera mastak jhukaa jat tha; agar meri aankhon-dekhee baat na hoti, to mujhe is par kabhi etabaar na hot. main baahar jo kuchh dekhta-sunataa tha, uski riport ammaan se zaroor kart tha. par is maamale ko maine unse chhipaa rakhaa. main jaanataa tha, unhen yah baat sun kar bada du:kha hogaa. raat bhar gaanaa hot raha. tabale ki dhamak mere kaanon mein aa rahi thi. jee chahta tha, chal kar dekhoon; par saahas na hot tha. main kisee ko munh kaise dikhaaoongaa? kaheen kisee ne pitaa jee kaa jikr chhed diya, to main kya karoongaa? praat:kaal raamachandr ki bidaaee honevaalee thi. main chaarapaaee se uthte hi aankhen malataa hua chaupaal ki ora bhaagaa? dar raha tha ki kaheen raamachandr chale na gaye hon. pahunchaa, to dekh, tavaayaphon ki savaariyaan jaane ko taiyaar hain. beeson aadamee hasaratanaak munh banaaye unhen ghere khade hain. maine unki ora aankh tak na uthaayee. seedhaa raamachandr ke paas pahunchaa. lakshman aur seetaa baithe ro rahe the; aur raamachandr khade kaandho par lutiyaa-dor daale unhen samajhaa rahe the.
mere sivaa wahan aur koi na tha. maine kunthit aavaaz se raamachandr se poochhaa, ' kya tumhari bidaaee ho gayi? '
raamachandr - haan, ho to gayi. hamaaree bidaaee hi kya? chaudharee sahab ne kah diya, jaao, chale jaate hain.
'kya rupayaa aur kapade nahin mile?'
'abhi nahin mile. chaudharee sahab kehte hain, is vakt bachat mein rupaye nahin hain. phir aa kar le jan.
'kuchh nahin milaa?'
'ek paisaa bhi nahin. kehte hain, kuchh bachat nahin hui. maine sochaa tha, kuchh rupaye mil jaaenge to padhne ki kitaaben le loongaa! so kuchh na milaa. raah-kharch bhi nahin diya. kehte hain, kaun door hai, paidal chale jaao!'
mujhe aisaa gussaa aay ki chal kar chaudharee ko khoob aade haathon loon. veshyaaon ke lie rupaye, savaariyaan sab kuchh; par bechaare raamachandr aur unake saathiyon ke lie kuchh bhi nahin! jin logon ne raat ko aabaadeejaan par das-das, bees-bees rupaye nyochhaavar kiye the, unake paas kya inake lie do-do, chaar-chaar aane paise bhi nahin. pitaa jee ne bhi to aabaadeejaan ko ek asharphee dee thi. dekhoon inake naam par kya dete hain! main daudaa hua pitaa jee ke paas gaya. vah kaheen taphateesh par jaane ko taiyaar khade the. mujhe dekh kar bole, kahan ghoom rahe ho? padhne ke vakt tumhen ghoomane ki soojhti hai?
maine kahaa, gaya tha chaupaal. raamachandr bidaa ho rahe the. unhen chaudharee sahab
ne kuchh nahin diya.
'to tumhen isakee kya phikr padee hai?'
'vah jaaenge kaise? paas raah-kharch bhi to nahin hai.'
'kya kuchh kharch bhi nahin diya? yah chaudharee sahab ki beinsaaphee hai.'
'aap agar do rupayaa de den, to main unhen de aaoon. itane mein shaayad vah ghar pahunch jaaen.'
pitaa jee ne teevr nazar se dekh kar kahaa, jaao, apni kitaab dekho, mere paas rupaye nahin hain.
yah kah kar vah ghode par savaar ho gaye. usi din se pitaa jee par se meri shrriddhaa uth gayi. maine phir kabhi unki daota-dapat ki paravaa nahin ki. mera dil keht, aapako mujhako upadesh dene kaa koi adhikar nahin hai. mujhe unki soorat se chidh ho gayi. vah jo kehte, main theek uska ultaa kart. yadyapi isamen meri nukasaan hui; kin mera ant:karan us samay viplavakaaree vichaaron se bharaa hua tha. mere paas do aane paise pade hue the. maine paise uthaa liye aur jaa kar sharamaate-sharamaate raamachandr ko de diye. una paison ko dekh kar raamachandr ko jitanaa harsh hua, vah mere lie aashaateet tha. toot pade, maano pyaase ko paani mil gaya. yahi do aane paise le kar teenon moortiyaan bidaa hueen! keval main hi unake saath kasbe ke baahar tak pahunchaane aay.
unhen bidaa karake lautaa, to meri aankhen sajal theen; par dil khushi se umadaa hua tha.
