# Ramleela

**Munshi Premchand**

idhar ek muddat se raamaleelaa dekhane nahin gaya| bandaron ke bhadde chehare lagaaye,aadhee taangon kaa paajaamaa aur kaale rang kaa oonchaa kurataa pahane aadamiyon ko daudate, hoo-hoo karte dekh kar ab hansee aatee hai; majaa nahin aat| kaashee kee
leelaa jagadvikhyaat hai| sunaa hai, log door-door se dekhane aate hain| main bhee bade shauk se gaya; par mujhe to wahan kee leelaa aur kisee bajr dehaat kee leelaa mein koee antar na dikhaayee diya| haan, raamanagar kee leelaa mein kuchh saaj-saamaan achchhe hain| raakshason aur bandaron ke chehare peetal ke hain, gadaaen bhee peetal kee hain; kadaachit navaasee bhraataaon ke mukut sachche kaam ke hon; lekin saaj-saamaan ke sivaa wahan bhee vahee hoo-hoo ke sivaa aur kuchh nahin| phir bhee laakhon aadamiyon kee bheed lagee rahatee|
lekin ek jamaanaa vah tha, jab mujhe bhee raamaleelaa mein khushee aat tha| khushee to bahut halakaa-saa shabd hai| vah khushee unmaad se kam na tha| sanyogavash una dinon mere ghar se bahut thodee door par raamaleelaa kaa maidaan tha;
aur jis ghar mein leelaa-paatron kaa roop-rang bharaa jat tha, vah to mere ghar se bilakul milaa hua tha| do baje din se paatron kee sajaavat hone lagatee thi| main dopahar hee se wahan jaa baithataa, aur jis utsaah se daud-daud kar chhote-mote kaam kart, us utsaah se to aaj apanee penshan lene bhee nahin jat| ek kotharee mein raajakumaaree kaa shringaar hot tha| unakee deh mein raamaraj pees kar potee jati; munh par paaudar lagaayaa jat aur paaudar ke oopar laal, hare, neele rang kee bundakiyaan lagaayee jati theen| saaraa maathaa, bhaunhen, gaal, thodee bundakiyon se rach uthatee theen| ek hee aadamee is kaam mein kushal tha| vahee baaree-baaree se teenon paatron kaa shringaar kart tha| rang kee pyaaliyon mein paanee laanaa, raamaraj peesanaa, pankhaa jhalanaa meraa kaam tha| jab ina taiyaariyon ke baad vimaan nikalataa, to us par raamachandr jee ke peechhe baith kar mujhe jo ullaas, jo garv, jo romaanch hot tha vah ab laat sahab ke darabaar mein kurasee par baith kar bhee nahin hot| ek baar jab hom-membar sahab ne vyavasthaapak-sabhaa mein mere lie ek prastaav kaa anumodan kiya tha, us vakt mujhe kuchh usee tarah kaa ullaas, garv aur romaanch hua tha| haan, ek baar jab meraa jyeshth betaa naayab-tahaseeladaaree mein naamajad hua, tab bhee aisee hee tarangen man mein uthee theen; par inamen aur us baal-vihnalataa mein badaa antar hai| tab aisaa maaloom hot tha ki main svarg mein baithaa hoon|
nishaad-naukaa-leelaa kaa din tha| main do-chaar ladakon ke bahakaane mein aakar gullee-dandaa khelane lagaa tha| aaj shringaar dekhane na gaya| vimaan bhee nikalaa; par mainne khelanaa na chhodaa| mujhe apanaa daanv lenaa tha| apanaa daanv chhodane ke lie usse kaheen badhakar aatmatyaag kee jaroorat thi, jitanaa main kar sakataa tha| agar daanv denaa hot to main kab kaa bhaag khadaa hot; lekin padaane mein kuchh aur hee baat hoti hai| khair, daanv pooraa hua| agar main chaahataa, to dhaandhaalee karake das-paanch minat aur padaa sakataa tha, isakee kaaphee gunjaaish thi, lekin ab
isakaa maukaa na tha| main seedhe naale kee taraph daudaa| vimaan jalatat par pahunch chukaa tha| mainne door se dekh , mallaah kishtee liye aa raha hai| daudaa, lekin aadamiyon kee bheed mein daudanaa mushkil tha| aakhir jab main bheed hataataa, praan-pan se aage badhataa ghaat par pahunchaa, to nishaad apanee naukaa khol chukaa tha| raamachandr par meree kitanee shrriddhaa thi ! apne paath kee phikr na karake unhen padhaa diya kart tha, jisamen vah phel na ho jaaen| mujhse umr jyaadaa hone par bhee vah neechee kakshaa mein padhate the| lekin vahee raamachandr naukaa par baithe is tarah munh phere chale jaate the, maano mujhse jaan-pahachaan hee nahin| nakal mein bhee asal kee kuchh na kuchh boo aa hee jati hai| bhakton par jinakee nigaah sadaa hee teekhee rahi hai, vah mujhe kyon ubaarate ? main vikal ho kar us bachhade kee tarah koodane lagaa, jisakee garadan par pahalee baar juaa rakhaa gaya ho| kabhee lapak kar naale kee ora jat, kabhee kisee sahaayak kee khoj mein peechhe kee taraph daudataa, par sab ke sab apanee dhun mein mast the; meree cheekh-pukaar kisee ke kaanon tak na pahunchee| tab se badee-badee vipattiaayaan jheleen; par us samay jitanaa du:kha hua, utanaa phir
kabhee na hua| mainne phaisalaa kiya tha ki ab raamachandr se na kabhee boloongaa, na kabhee khaane kee koee cheej hee doongaa; lekin jyon hee naale ko paar karake vah pul kee ora laute; main daud kar vimaan par chadh gaya, aur aisaa khush hua, maano koee baat hee na huee thi|
raamaleelaa khatm ho gayee thi| raajagaddee honevaalee thi; par na jaane kyon der ho rahi thi| shaayad chandaa kam vasool hua tha| raamachandr kee ina dinon koee baat bhee na poochhataa tha| na ghar hee jaane kee chhuttee milatee thi, na khaanaa kaa hee intazaam hot tha| chaudharee sahab ke yahan se ek seedhaa koee teen baje din ko milataa tha| baakee saare din koee paanee ko nahin poochhataa| lekin meree shrriddhaa abhee tak jyon kee tyon thi| meree nazar mein ab bhee raamachandr hee the| ghar par mujhe khaane ko koee cheej milatee, vah le kar raamachandr ko de aat| unhen khilaane mein mujhe jitanaa khushee milataa tha, utanaa aap khaa jaane mein kabhee na milataa| koee mithaaee yaa phal paate hee main betahaashaa chaupaal kee ora daudataa| agar raamachandr wahan na milate to unhen chaaron ora talaash kart, aur jab tak vah cheej unhen na khilaa letaa, mujhe chain na aat tha| khair, raajagaddee kaa din aay| raamaleelaa ke maidaan mein ek badaa-saa shaamiyaanaa taanaa gaya| usakee khoob sajaavat kee gayee| veshyaaon ke dal bhee aa pahunche| shaam ko raamachandr kee savaaree nikalee, aur har dvaar par unakee aaratee utaaree gayee| shrriddhaanusaar kisee ne rupaye diye, kisee ne paise| mere pitaa pulis ke aadamee the; isalie unhonne binaa kuchh diye hee aaratee utaaree| us vakt mujhe jitanee sharm aayee, use bayaan nahin kar sakataa| mere paas us vakt sanyog se ek rupayaa tha| mere maamaa jee dashahare ke pahale aaye the aur
mujhe ek rupayaa de gaye the| us rupaye ko mainne rakh chhodaa tha| dashahare ke din bhee use kharch na kar sakaa| mainne turant vah rupayaa laa kar aaratee kee thaalee mein daal diya| pitaa jee meree ora gusse mein netron se dekh kar raha gaye|
unhonne kuchh kahaa to nahin; lekin munh aisaa banaa liya, jisse prakat hot tha ki meree is dhrishtataa se unake rob mein battaa lag gaya|
raat ke das bajate-bajate yah parikramaa pooree huee| aaratee kee thaalee rupayon aur paison se bharee huee thi| theek to nahin kah sakataa; magar ab aisaa anumaan hot hai ki chaar-paanch sau rupayon se kam na the| chaudharee sahab inse kuchh jyaadaa hee kharch kar chuke the| unhen isakee badee phikr huee ki kisee tarah kam se kam do sau rupaye aur vasool ho jaaen aur isakee sabase achchhee tarakeeb unhen yahee maaloom huee ki veshyaaon dvaaraa mahaphil mein vasoolee ho| jab log aa kar baith jaaen, aur mahaphil kaa rang jam jaae, to aabaadeejaan rasikajanon kee kalaaiyaan pakad-pakad
kar aise haav-bhaav dikhaayen ki log sharamaate-sharamaate bhee kuchh na kuchh de hee maren| aabaadeejaan aur chaudharee sahab mein salaah hone lagee| main sanyog se una donon praaniyon kee baaten sun raha tha| chaudharee sahab ne samajhaa hogaa yah laundaa kyaa matalab samajhegaa| par yahan eeshvar kee dayaa se akl ke putale the| saaree daastaan samajh mein aatee jati thi|
chaudharee - suno aabaadeejaan, yah tumhaaree jyaadatee hai| hamaaraa aur tumhaaraa koee pahalaa saabikaa to hai nahin| eeshvar ne chaahaa, to yahan hameshaa tumhaaraa aan-jan lagaa rahegaa| ab kee chandaa bahut kam aay, nahin to main tumse itanaa isaraar na kart|
aabaadee. - aap mujhse bhee jameendaaree chaalen chalate hain, kyon, magar yahan hujoor kee daal na galegee| vaah ! rupaye to main vasool karoon, aur moonchhon par taav aap den| kamaaee kaa achchhaa dhang nikaalaa hai| is kamaaee se to vaakaee aap thode dinon mein raajaa ho jaaeenge| usake saamane jameendaaree jhak maaregee ! bas, kal hee se ek chakalaa khol deejie ! khudaa kee kasam, maalaamaal ho jaaiegaa|
chaudharee - tum dillagee karti ho, aur yahan kaaphiyaa tang ho raha hai|
aabaadee. - to aap bhee to mujhee se ustaadee karte hain| yahan aap-jaise kaaniyon ko roj ungaliyon par nachaatee hoon|
chaudharee - aakhir tumhaaree manshaa kyaa hai ?
aabaadee. - jo kuchh vasool karoon, usamen aadhaa meraa, aadhaa aapakaa| laaie, haath maarie|
chaudharee - yahee sahee|
aabaadee. - achchhaa, to pahale mere sau rupaye gin deejie| peechhe se aap alaset karne lagenge|
chaudharee - vaah ! vah bhee logee aur yah bhee|
aabaadee. - achchhaa ! to kyaa aap samajhate the ki apanee ujarat chhod doongee ? vaaharee aapakee samajh ! khoob; kyon na ho| deevaanaa bakaare daravesh hushiyaar !
chaudharee - to kyaa tumane doharee phees lene kee thaanee hai ?
aabaadee. - agar aapako sau daphe garaj ho, to| varanaa mere sau rupaye to kaheen gaye hee nahin| mujhe kyaa kutto ne kaataa hai, jo logon kee jeb mein haath daalatee phiroon ?
chaudharee kee ek na chalee| aabaadee ke saamane dabanaa padaa| naach shuroo hua| aabaadeejaan balaa kee shokh aurat thi| ek to kamasin, us par haseen| aur usakee adaaen to is gazab kee theen ki meree tabeeyat bhee mast huee jati
thi| aadamiyon ke pahachaanane kaa gun bhee usamen kuchh kam na tha| jisake saamane baith gayee, usse kuchh na kuchh le hee liya| paanch rupaye se kam to shaayad hee kisee ne diye hon| pitaa jee ke saamane bhee vah baithee| main maare sharm ke gad gaya| jab usane unakee kalaaee pakadee, tab to main saham uthaa| mujhe yakeen tha ki pitaa jee usakaa haath jhatak denge aur shaayad dutkaar bhee den, lekin yah kyaa ho raha hai ! eeshvar ! meree aankhen dhokhaa to nahin khaa rahi hain ! pitaa jee moonchhon mein hans rahe hain| aisee mridu-hansee unake chehare par mainne kabhee nahin dekhee thi| unakee aankhon se anuraag tapakaa padataa tha| unakaa ek-ek rom pulakit ho raha tha; magar eeshvar ne meree laaj rakh lee| vah dekho, unhonne dheere se aabaadee ke komal haathon se apanee kalaaee chhudaa lee| are ! yah phir kyaa hua ? aabaadee to unake gale mein baahen daale detee hai| ab pitaa jee use jaroor peetenge| chudail ko jaraa bhee sharm nahin|
ek mahaashay ne muskaraa kar kahaa , yahan tumhaaree daal na galegee, aabaadeejaan ! aur daravaajaa dekho|
baat to ina mahaashay ne mere man kee kahee, aur bahut hee theek kahee; lekin na-jaane kyon pitaa jee ne usakee ora gusse mein netron se dekh, aur moonchhon par taav diya| munh se to vah kuchh na bole; par unake munh kee aakriti chillaa kar sarosh shabdon mein kah rahi thi , ' too baniyaa, mujhe samajhataa kyaa hai ? yahan aise avasar par jaan tak nisaar karne ko taiyaar hain| rupaye kee hakeekat hee kyaa ! teraa jee chaahe, aajamaa le| tujhase doonee rakam na de daaloon, to munh na dikhaaoon ! ' mahaan hairaanee ! ghor anarth ! are, jameen too phat kyon nahin jati ? aakaash, too phat kyon nahin padataa ? are, mujhe maut kyon nahin aa jati ! pitaa jee jeb mein haath daal rahe hain| vah koee cheej nikaalee, aur seth jee ko dikhaa kar aabaadeejaan ko de daalee| aah ! yah to asharphee hai| chaaron ora taaliyaan
bajane lageen| seth jee ulloo ban gaye yaa pitaa jee ne munh kee khaayee, isakaa phaisalaa main nahin kar sakataa| mainne keval itanaa dekh ki pitaa jee ne ek asharphee nikaal kar aabaadeejaan ko dee| unakee aankhon mein is samay itanaa garvayukt ullaas tha maano unhonne haatim kee kabr par laat maaree ho|
yahee pitaa jee hain, jinhonne mujhe aaratee mein ek rupayaa daalate dekh kar meree ora is tarah se dekh tha, maano mujhe phaad hee khaayenge| mere us paramochit vyavahaar se unake rob mein phark aat tha, aur is samay is ghrinit, kutsit aur nindit vyaapaar par garv aur khushee se phoole na samaate the| aabaadeejaan ne ek manohar muskaan ke saath pitaa jee ko salaam kiya aur aage badhee; magar mujhse wahan na baithaa gaya| maare sharm ke meraa mastak jhukaa jat tha; agar meree aankhon-dekhee baat na hoti, to mujhe is par kabhee etabaar na hot| main baahar jo kuchh dekhataa-sunataa tha, usakee riport ammaan se jaroor kart tha| par is maamale ko mainne unse chhipaa rakhaa| main jaanataa tha, unhen yah baat sun kar badaa du:kha hogaa| raat bhar gaanaa hot raha| tabale kee dhamak mere kaanon mein aa rahi thi| jee chaahataa tha, chal kar dekhoon; par saahas na hot tha| main kisee ko munh kaise dikhaaoongaa ? kaheen kisee ne pitaa jee kaa jikr chhed diya, to main kyaa karoongaa ? praat:kaal raamachandr kee bidaaee honevaalee thi| main chaarapaaee se uthate hee aankhen malataa hua chaupaal kee ora bhaagaa ? dar raha tha ki kaheen raamachandr chale na gaye hon| pahunchaa, to dekh , tavaayaphon kee savaariyaan jaane ko taiyaar hain| beeson aadamee hasaratanaak munh banaaye unhen ghere khade hain| mainne unakee ora aankh tak na uthaayee| seedhaa raamachandr ke paas pahunchaa| lakshman aur seetaa baithe ro rahe the; aur raamachandr khade kaandho par lutiyaa-dor daale unhen samajhaa rahe the|
mere sivaa wahan aur koee na tha| mainne kunthit aavaaz se raamachandr se poochhaa , ' kyaa tumhaaree bidaaee ho gayee ? '
raamachandr - haan, ho to gayee| hamaaree bidaaee hee kyaa ? chaudharee sahab ne kah diya , jaao, chale jaate hain|
'kyaa rupayaa aur kapade nahin mile ?'
'abhee nahin mile| chaudharee sahab kehte hain , is vakt bachat mein rupaye nahin hain| phir aa kar le jan|
'kuchh nahin milaa ?'
'ek paisaa bhee nahin| kehte hain, kuchh bachat nahin huee| mainne sochaa tha, kuchh rupaye mil jaaenge to padhane kee kitaaben le loongaa ! so kuchh na milaa| raah-kharch bhee nahin diya| kehte hain , kaun door hai, paidal chale jaao !'
mujhe aisaa gussaa aay ki chal kar chaudharee ko khoob aade haathon loon| veshyaaon ke lie rupaye, savaariyaan sab kuchh; par bechaare raamachandr aur unake saathiyon ke lie kuchh bhee nahin ! jin logon ne raat ko aabaadeejaan par das-das, bees-bees rupaye nyochhaavar kiye the, unake paas kyaa inake lie do-do, chaar-chaar aane paise bhee nahin| pitaa jee ne bhee to aabaadeejaan ko ek asharphee dee thi| dekhoon inake naam par kyaa dete hain ! main daudaa hua pitaa jee ke paas gaya| vah kaheen taphateesh par jaane ko taiyaar khade the| mujhe dekh kar bole , kahan ghoom rahe ho ? padhane ke vakt tumhen ghoomane kee soojhatee hai ?
mainne kahaa , gaya tha chaupaal| raamachandr bidaa ho rahe the| unhen chaudharee sahab
ne kuchh nahin diya|
'to tumhen isakee kyaa phikr padee hai ?'
'vah jaaenge kaise ? paas raah-kharch bhee to nahin hai|'
'kyaa kuchh kharch bhee nahin diya ? yah chaudharee sahab kee beinsaaphee hai|'
'aap agar do rupayaa de den, to main unhen de aaoon| itane mein shaayad vah ghar pahunch jaaen|'
pitaa jee ne teevr nazar se dekh kar kahaa , jaao, apanee kitaab dekho, mere paas rupaye nahin hain|
yah kah kar vah ghode par savaar ho gaye| usee din se pitaa jee par se meree shrriddhaa uth gayee| mainne phir kabhee unakee daota-dapat kee paravaa nahin kee| meraa dil keht , aapako mujhako upadesh dene kaa koee adhikaar nahin hai| mujhe unakee soorat se chidh ho gayee| vah jo kehte, main theek usakaa ultaa kart| yadyapi isamen meree nukasaan huee; kin meraa ant:karan us samay viplavakaaree vichaaron se bharaa hua tha| mere paas do aane paise pade hue the| mainne paise uthaa liye aur jaa kar sharamaate-sharamaate raamachandr ko de diye| una paison ko dekh kar raamachandr ko jitanaa harsh hua, vah mere lie aashaateet tha| toot pade, maano pyaase ko paanee mil gaya| yahee do aane paise le kar teenon moortiyaan bidaa hueen ! keval main hee unake saath kasbe ke baahar tak pahunchaane aay|
unhen bidaa karake lautaa, to meree aankhen sajal theen; par dil khushee se umadaa hua tha|
