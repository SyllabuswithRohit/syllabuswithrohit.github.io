# Sawa Ser Gehun

**Munshi Premchand**

kisee gaanv mein shankar naam kaa ek kuramee kisaan rehta tha. seedhaa-saadaa gareeb aadamee tha, apne kaam-se-kaam, na kisee ke lene mein, na kisee ke dene mein. chhakkaa-panjaa na jaanataa tha, chhal-prapanch ki use chhoot bhi na lagee thi, thage jaane ki phikr na thi, thagavidyaa na jaanataa tha, khaanaa milaa, khaa liya, na milaa, chabene par kaat dee, chabainaa bhi na milaa, to paani pee liya aur raam kaa naam lekar so raha. lekin jab koi atithi dvaar par aa jat tha to use is nivrittimaarg kaa tyaag karn padataa tha. visheshakar jab saadhu-mahaatmaa padaarpan karte the, to use anivaaryat: saansaarikataa ki sharan lenee padatee thi. khud bhookhaa so sakataa tha, par saadhu ko kaise bhookhaa sulaataa, bhagavaan ke bhakt jo thahare!
ek din shaam samay ek mahaatmaa ne aakar usake dvaar par deraa jamaayaa. tejasvee moorti thi, peetaambar gale mein, jataa sir par, peetal kaa kamandal haath mein, khadaaoon pair mein, ainak aankhon par, pooraa vesh una mahaatmaaon kaa-saa tha jo raeeson ke praasaadon mein tapasyaa, havaagaadiyon par devasthaanon ki parikramaa aur yog-sidhdi praapt karne ke lie ruchikar khaanaa karte hain. ghar mein jau kaa aat tha, vah unhen kaise khilaataa. praacheenakaal mein jau kaa chaahe jo kuchh mahattv raha ho, par abhi yug mein jau kaa khaanaa siddh purushon ke lie dushpaachy hot hai. badi phikr hui, mahaatmaajee ko kya khilaaoon. aakhir phaisalaa kiya ki kaheen se gehoon kaa aat udhaar laaoon, par gaanv-bhar mein gehoon kaa aat na milaa. gaanv mein sab manushy-hi-manushy the, devataa ek bhi na tha, isalie devataaon kaa khaady padaarth kaise milataa. achchhi kismat se gaanv ke vipr mahaaraaj ke yahan se thodaa-saa mil gae. unse savaa ser gehoon udhaar liya aur aurat se kahaa, ki pees de. mahaatmaa ne khaanaa kiya, lambee taanakar soye. praat:kaal aasheervaad dekar apni
raah lee.
vipr mahaaraaj saal mein do baar khalihaanee liya karte the. shankar ne dil mein kahaa,savaa ser gehoon inhen kya lautaaoon, panseree badale kuchh zyada khalihaanee de doongaa, yah bhi samajh jaayange, main bhi samajh jaaoongaa. chait mein jab viprajee
pahunche to unhen dedh panseree ke lagabhag gehoon de diya aur apne ko urin samajhakar uski koi charachaa na ki. viprajee ne phir kabhi na maangaa. aasaan shankar ko kya maaloom tha ki yah savaa ser gehoon chukaane ke lie mujhe doosaraa janm lena padegaa.
saat saal gujar gaye. viprajee vipr se mahaajan hue, shankar kisaan se majoor ho gaya. uska chhota bhai mangal usse alag ho gaya tha. ek saath rahakar donon kisaan the, alag hokar majoor ho gaye the. shankar ne chaahaa ki napharat ki aag bhadakane na paaye, lekin paristhiti ne use vivash kar diya. jis din alag-alag choolhe jale, vah phoot-phootakar royaa. aaj se bhai-bhai shatru ho jaayange, ek royegaa, doosaraa hansegaa, ek ke ghar maatam hogaa to doosare ke ghar gulagule pakenge, pyaar kaa bandhan, khoon kaa bandhan, doodh kaa bandhan aaj tootaa jat hai. usane bhageerath parishram se kul-maryaadaa kaa vriksh lagaayaa tha, use apne rakt se seenchaa tha, usako jad se ukhadataa dekhakar usake hraday ke tukade hue jaate the. saat dinon tak usane daane ki soorat tak na dekhee. din-bhar jeth ki dhoop mein kaam kart aur raat ko munh lapetakar so rehta. is bheeshan dard aur dussah takaleeph ne rakt ko jalaa diya, maans aur majjaa ko ghulaa diya. beemaar padaa to maheenon khaat se na uthaa. ab gujar-basar kaise ho?
paanch beeghe ke aadhe raha gaye, ek bail raha gaya, khetee kya khaak hoti! ant ko yahan tak naubat pahunchee ki khetee keval maryaadaa-rakshaa kaa saadhan-maatr raha gayi, jeevikaa kaa bhaar majooree par aa padaa. saat varsh beet gaye, ek din shankar majooree karake lautaa, to raah mein viprajee ne tokakar kahaa, ' shankar, kal aakar ke apne beej-beng kaa hisaab kar le. tere yahan saadhe paanch man gehoon kabake baakee pade hue hain aur too dene kaa naam nahin letaa, hajam karne kaa man hai kya? '
shankar ne chakit hokar kahaa, maine tumse kab gehoon lie the jo saadhe paanch man ho gaye? tum bhoolate ho, mere yahan kisee kaa chhataank-bhar na anaaj hai,na ek paisaa udhaar. '
vipr -'isi neeyat kaa to yah phal bhog rahe ho ki khaane ko nahin judataa. '
yah kahakar viprajee ne us savaa ser gehoon kaa jikr kiya, jo aaj ke saat varsh pahale shankar ko diye the. shankar sunakar avaak raha gaya. eeshvar maine inhen kitanee baar khalihaanee dee, inhonne mera kaun-saa kaam kiya? jab
pothee-patr dekhne, saait-sagun vichaarane dvaar par aate the, kuchh-na-kuchh 'dakshinaa' le hi jaate the. itanaa svaarth! savaa ser anaaj ko ande ki tarah sekar aaj yah pishaach khadaa kar diya, jo mujhe nigal jaayagaa. itane dinon mein ek baar bhi kah dete to main gehoon taulakar de detaa, kya isi neeyat se chup saadh baithe rahe? bol, ' mahaaraaj, naam lekar to maine utanaa anaaj nahin diya, par kaee baar khalihaanon mein ser-ser, do-do ser diya hai. ab aap aaj saadhe paanch man maangate hain, main kahan se doongaa? '
vipr -'lekhaa jau-jau, bakhasees sau-sau, tumne jo kuchh diya hogaa, uska koi hisaab nahin, chaahe ek ki jagah chaar paseree de do. tumhaare naam bahee mein saadhe paanch man likhaa hua hai; jisse chaahe hisaab lagavaa lo. de do to
tumhara naam chhek doon, nahin to aur bhi badhta rahegaa. '
shankar -'paande, kyon ek gareeb ko sataate ho, mere khaane kaa thikaanaa nahin, itanaa gehoon kisake ghar se laaoongaa? '
vipr -'zisake ghar se chaahe laao, main chhataank-bhar bhi na chhodoongaa, yahan na doge, bhagavaan ke ghar to doge. '
shankar kaanp uthaa. ham padhe-likhe aadamee hote to kah dete, 'achchhi baat hai, eeshvar ke ghar hi denge; wahan ki taul yahan se kuchh badi to na hogee. kam-se-kam isakaa koi pramaan hamaare paas nahin, phir uski kya chintaa. lekin shankar itanaa taarkik, itanaa vyavahaar-hoshiyaar na tha. ek to rin vah bhi braahman kaa bahee mein naam raha gaya to seedhe narak mein jaaoongaa, is khayaal hi se use romaanch ho gaya. bol, ' mahaaraaj, tumhara jitanaa hogaa yaheen doongaa, eeshvar ke yahan kyon doon, is janam mein to thokar khaa hi raha hoon, us janam ke lie kyon kaante booon? magar yah koi niyaav nahin hai. tumne raaee kaa parvat banaa diya, braahman ho ke tumhen aisaa nahin karn chaahie tha. usi ghadee tagaadaa karake le liya hot, to aaj mere sir par itanaa bada bojh kyon padataa. main to de
doongaa, lekin tumhen bhagavaan ke yahan jawab dena padegaa. '
vipr -'wahan kaa dar tumhen hogaa, mujhe kyon hone lagaa. wahan to sab apne hi bhai-bandhu hain. rishi-muni, sab to braahman hi hain; devataa braahman hain, jo kuchh bane-bigadegee, sanbhaal lenge. to kab dete ho? '
shankar -'mere paas rakkhaa to hai nahin, kisee se maang-jaochakar laaoongaa tabhee na doongaa! '
vipr -'main yah na maanoongaa. saat saal ho gaye, ab ek din kaa bhi mulaahijaa na karoongaa. gehoon nahin de sakate, to dastaavej likh do. '
shankar -- ' mujhe to dena hai, chaahe gehoon lo chaahe dastaavej likhaao; kis hisaab se daam rakkhoge? '
vipr -'baajaar-bhaav paanch ser kaa hai, tumhen savaa paanch ser kaa kaat doongaa.'
shankar-' zab de hi raha hoon to baajaar-bhaav kaatoongaa, paav-bhar chhudaakar kyon doshee banoon. '
hisaab lagaayaa to gehoon ke daam 60) hue. 60) kaa dastaavej likhaa gaya, 3) saikade sood. saal-bhar mein na dene par sood ki dar dhaaee rupaye) saikade. aath aane) kaa staamp, chaar aane) dastaavej ki tahareer shankar ko oopar se denee
padee.
gaanv bhar ne viprajee ki nindaa ki, lekin munh par nahin. mahaajan se sabhi kaa kaam padataa hai, usake munh kaun aaye. shankar ne saal bhar tak mushkil tapasyaa ki. meeyaad ke pahale rupaye adaa karne kaa usane vrat-saa kar liya. dopahar ko pahale bhi choolhaa na jalataa tha, chabaine par basar hoti thi, ab vah bhi band hua. keval ladake ke lie raat ko rotiyaan rakh dee jaateen! paise roj kaa tanbaakoo pee jat tha, yahi ek vyasan tha jisakaa vah kabhi na tyaag kar sakaa tha. ab vah vyasan bhi is mushkil vrat ki bhent ho gaya. usane chilam patak dee, hukkaa tod diya aur tamaakhoo ki haandee choor-choor kar daalee. kapade pahale bhi tyaag ki charam seemaa tak pahunch
chuke the, ab vah prakriti ki nyoonatam rekhaaon mein aabaddh ho gaye. shishir ki asthibedhak sheet ko usane aag taapakar kaat diya. is sankalp kaa phal ummeed se badhakar nikalaa. saal ke ant mein usake paas 60) ru.
jamaa ho gaye. usane samajhaa panditajee ko itane rupaye de doongaa aur kahoongaa mahaaraaj, baakee rupaye bhi jald hi aapake saamane haajir karoongaa. 15) ru. ki to aur baat hai, kya panditajee itanaa bhi na maanenge! usane rupaye liye aur le jaakar panditajee ke charan-kamalon par arpan kar diye. panditajee ne vismit hokar
poochhaa, ' kisee se udhaar liye kya? '
shankar -'nahin mahaaraaj, ' aapake asees se abakee majooree achchhi milee. '
vipr -'lekin yah to 60) ru. hi hain! '
shankar -- ' haan mahaaraaj, itane abhi le leejie, baakee main do-teen mahine mein de doongaa, mujhe urin kar deejie. '
vipr -'urin to jabhee hoge jabaki meri kaudee-kaudee chukaa doge. jaakar mere 15) ru. aur laao. '
shankar -'mahaaraaj, itanee dayaa karo; ab saanjh ki rotiyon kaa bhi thikaanaa nahin hai, gaanv mein hoon to kabhi-na-kabhi de hi doongaa. '
vipr -'main yah rog nahin paalataa, na bahut baaten karn jaanataa hoon. agar mere poore rupaye na milenge to aaj se 3) ru. saikade kaa byaaj lagegaa. apne rupaye chaahe apne ghar mein rakkho, chaahe mere yahan chhod jaao.
shankar -'achchha jitanaa laayaa hoon utanaa rakh leejie. main jat hoon, kaheen se 15) ru. aur laane ki phikr kart hoon.
shankar ne sara gaanv chhaan maaraa, magar kisee ne rupaye na diye, isalie nahin ki uska yakeen na tha, yaa kisee ke paas rupaye na the, balki isalie ki panditajee ke shikaar ko chhedane ki kisee ki himmat na thi.
kriyaa ke baad pratikriyaa naisargik niyam hai. shankar saal-bhar tak tapasyaa karne par bhi jab rin se mukt hone mein kaamayaab na ho sakaa to uska sanyam maayoosee ke roop mein parinat ho gaya. usane samajh liya ki jab itanaa takaleeph sahane par bhi saal-bhar mein 60) ru. se zyaadaa na jamaa kar sakaa, to ab aur kaun saa upaay hai jisake dvaaraa isake doone rupaye jamaa hon. jab sir par rin kaa bojh hi laadanaa hai to kya man-bhar kaa aur kya savaa man kaa. uska utsaah ksheen ho gaya, mihanat se napharat ho gayi. ummeed utsaah ki jananee hai, ummeed mein tej hai, bal hai, jeevan hai. ummeed hi sansaar ki sanchaalak shakti hai. shankar aashaaheen hokar udaaseen ho gaya. vah jarooraten jinako usane saal-bhar tak taal rakhaa tha, ab dvaar par khadee honevaalee bhikhaarinee na theen, balki chhaatee par savaar honevaalee pishaachaniyaan theen, jo apni bhent liye binaa jaan nahin chhodateen. kapadon mein chakattiyon ke lagne ki bhi ek seemaa hoti hai. ab shankar ko chitthaa milataa to vah rupaye jamaa na kart, kabhi kapade laataa,
kabhi khaane ki koi vastu. jahaan pahale tamaakhoo hi piyaa kart tha, wahan ab gaanje aur charas kaa chaskaa bhi lagaa. use ab rupaye adaa karne ki koi phikr na thi maano usake oopar kisee kaa ek paisaa bhi nahin aat. pahale joodee chadhee hoti thi, par vah kaam karne avashy jat tha, ab kaam par na jaane ke lie bahaanaa khojaa kart.
is tarah teen varsh nikal gaye. viprajee mahaaraaj ne ek baar bhi takaajaa na kiya. vah hoshiyaar shikaaree ki tarah achook nishaanaa lagaanaa chahte the. pahale se shikaar ko chaunkaanaa unki neeti ke viruddh tha. ek din panditajee ne shankar ko bulaakar hisaab dikhaayaa. 60) ru. jo jamaa the vah minahaa karne par ab bhi shankar ke jimme 120) ru. nikale. shankar -'itane rupaye to usi janm mein doongaa, is janm mein nahin ho sakate. '
vipr -'main isi janm mein loongaa. mool na sahee, sood to dena hi padegaa. '
shankar -- ' ek bail hai, vah le leejie aur mere paas rakkhaa kya hai. '
vipr -'mujhe bail-badhiyaa lekar kya karn hai. mujhe dene ko tumhaare paas bahut kuchh hai. '
shankar -'aur kya hai mahaaraaj? '
vipr -'kuchh hai, tum to ho. aakhir tum bhi kaheen majooree karne jaate hi ho, mujhe bhi khetee ke lie majoor rakhna hi padataa hai. sood mein tum hamaare yahan kaam kiya karo, jab subheetaa ho mool ko de dena. sach to yon hai ki ab
tum kisee doosaree jagah kaam karne nahin jaa sakate jab tak mere rupaye nahin chukaa do. tumhaare paas koi jaayadaad nahin hai, itanee badi gatharee main kis etabaar par chhod doon. kaun isakaa jimmaa legaa ki tum mujhe mahine-mahine sood dete jaaoge. aur kaheen kamaakar jab tum mujhe sood bhi nahin de sakate, to mool ki kaun kahe?'
shankar -'mahaaraaj, sood mein to kaam karoongaa aur khaaoongaa kya? '
vipr -'tumhari gharavaalee hai, ladake hain, kya ve haath-paanv kataake baithenge. raha main, tumhen aadhaa ser jau roj kalevaa ke lie de diya karoongaa. odhane ko saal mein ek kambal paa jaaoge, ek mirajaee bhi banavaa diya karoongaa, aur
kya chaahie. yah sach hai ki aur log tumhen chh: aane dete hain lekin mujhe aisee garaj nahin hai, main to tumhen rupaye bharane ke lie rakhta hoon. '
shankar ne kuchh der tak gaharee phikr mein pade rehne ke baad kahaa, ' mahaaraaj yah to janm-bhar ki gulaamee hui. '
vipr -'Gulaamee samajho, chaahe majooree samajho. main apne rupaye bharaaye binaa tumako kabhi na chhodoongaa. tum bhaagoge to tumhara ladakaa bharegaa. haan, jab koi na rahegaa tab ki baat doosaree hai. '
is phaisalaa ki kaheen apeel na thi. majoor ki jamaanat kaun kart, kaheen sharan na thi, bhaagakar kahan jat, doosare din se usane viprajee ke yahan kaam karn shuru kar diya. savaa ser gehoon ki badaulat umr-bhar ke lie gulaamee
ki bedee pairon mein daalanee padee. us abhaage ko ab agar kisee soch se santosh hot tha to yah tha ki vah mere pahale-janm kaa sanskaar hai. aurat ko ve kaam karne padate the, jo usane kabhi na kiye the, bachche daanon ko tarasate
the, lekin shankar chupachaap dekhne ke sivaa aur kuchh na kar sakataa tha. vah gehoon ke daane kisee devataa ke shaap ki tarah yaavajjeevan usake sir se na utare.
shankar ne viprajee ke yahan 20 varsh tak gulaamee karne ke baad is dussaar sansaar se ravaanaa hona kiya. 120) abhi tak usake sir par savaar the. panditajee ne us gareeb ko eeshvar ke darabaar mein takaleeph dena theek na samajhaa, itane anyaayee, itane nirdayee na the. usake javaan bete ki garadan pakadee. aaj tak vah viprajee ke yahan kaam kart hai. uska uddhaar kab hogaa; hogaa bhi yaa nahin, eeshvar hi jaane.
paathak! is vrittaant ko kapol-kalpit na samajhie. yah saty ghatanaa hai. aise shankaron aur aise vipron se duniyaa khaalee nahin hai.
