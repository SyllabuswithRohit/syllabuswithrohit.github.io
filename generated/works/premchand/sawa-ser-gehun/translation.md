# Sawa Ser Gehun

**Munshi Premchand**

kisee gaanv mein shankar naam kaa ek kuramee kisaan rahataa tha| seedhaa-saadaa gareeb aadamee tha, apne kaam-se-kaam, na kisee ke lene mein, na kisee ke dene mein| chhakkaa-panjaa na jaanataa tha, chhal-prapanch kee use chhoot bhee na lagee thi, thage jaane kee phikr na thi, thagavidyaa na jaanataa tha, khaanaa milaa, khaa liya, na milaa, chabene par kaat dee, chabainaa bhee na milaa, to paanee pee liya aur raam kaa naam lekar so raha| lekin jab koee atithi dvaar par aa jat tha to use is nivrittimaarg kaa tyaag karn padataa tha| visheshakar jab saadhu-mahaatmaa padaarpan karte the, to use anivaaryat: saansaarikataa kee sharan lenee padatee thi| khud bhookhaa so sakataa tha, par saadhu ko kaise bhookhaa sulaataa, bhagavaan ke bhakt jo thahare !
ek din shaam samay ek mahaatmaa ne aakar usake dvaar par deraa jamaayaa| tejasvee moorti thi, peetaambar gale mein, jataa sir par, peetal kaa kamandal haath mein, khadaaoon pair mein, ainak aankhon par, pooraa vesh una mahaatmaaon kaa-saa tha jo raeeson ke praasaadon mein tapasyaa, havaagaadiyon par devasthaanon kee parikramaa aur yog-sidhdi praapt karne ke lie ruchikar khaanaa karte hain| ghar mein jau kaa aat tha, vah unhen kaise khilaataa| praacheenakaal mein jau kaa chaahe jo kuchh mahattv raha ho, par abhee yug mein jau kaa khaanaa siddh purushon ke lie dushpaachy hot hai| badee phikr huee, mahaatmaajee ko kyaa khilaaoon| aakhir phaisalaa kiya ki kaheen se gehoon kaa aat udhaar laaoon, par gaanv-bhar mein gehoon kaa aat na milaa| gaanv mein sab manushy-hee-manushy the, devataa ek bhee na tha, isalie devataaon kaa khaady padaarth kaise milataa| achchhee kismat se gaanv ke vipr mahaaraaj ke yahan se thodaa-saa mil gae| unse savaa ser gehoon udhaar liya aur aurat se kahaa, ki pees de| mahaatmaa ne khaanaa kiya, lambee taanakar soye| praat:kaal aasheervaad dekar apanee
raah lee|
vipr mahaaraaj saal mein do baar khalihaanee liya karte the| shankar ne dil mein kahaa,savaa ser gehoon inhen kyaa lautaaoon, panseree badale kuchh jyaadaa khalihaanee de doongaa, yah bhee samajh jaayange, main bhee samajh jaaoongaa| chait mein jab viprajee
pahunche to unhen dedh panseree ke lagabhag gehoon de diya aur apne ko urin samajhakar usakee koee charachaa na kee| viprajee ne phir kabhee na maangaa| aasaan shankar ko kyaa maaloom tha ki yah savaa ser gehoon chukaane ke lie mujhe doosaraa janm lenaa padegaa|
saat saal gujar gaye| viprajee vipr se mahaajan hue, shankar kisaan se majoor ho gaya| usakaa chhotaa bhai mangal usse alag ho gaya tha| ek saath rahakar donon kisaan the, alag hokar majoor ho gaye the| shankar ne chaahaa ki napharat kee aag bhadakane na paaye, lekin paristhiti ne use vivash kar diya| jis din alag-alag choolhe jale, vah phoot-phootakar royaa| aaj se bhai-bhai shatru ho jaayange, ek royegaa, doosaraa hansegaa, ek ke ghar maatam hogaa to doosare ke ghar gulagule pakenge, pyaar kaa bandhan, khoon kaa bandhan, doodh kaa bandhan aaj tootaa jat hai| usane bhageerath parishram se kul-maryaadaa kaa vriksh lagaayaa tha, use apne rakt se seenchaa tha, usako jad se ukhadataa dekhakar usake hraday ke tukade hue jaate the| saat dinon tak usane daane kee soorat tak na dekhee| din-bhar jeth kee dhoop mein kaam kart aur raat ko munh lapetakar so rahataa| is bheeshan dard aur dussah takaleeph ne rakt ko jalaa diya, maans aur majjaa ko ghulaa diya| beemaar padaa to maheenon khaat se na uthaa| ab gujar-basar kaise ho ?
paanch beeghe ke aadhe raha gaye, ek bail raha gaya, khetee kyaa khaak hoti ! ant ko yahan tak naubat pahunchee ki khetee keval maryaadaa-rakshaa kaa saadhan-maatr raha gayee, jeevikaa kaa bhaar majooree par aa padaa| saat varsh beet gaye, ek din shankar majooree karake lautaa, to raah mein viprajee ne tokakar kahaa, ' shankar, kal aakar ke apne beej-beng kaa hisaab kar le| tere yahan saadhe paanch man gehoon kabake baakee pade hue hain aur too dene kaa naam nahin letaa, hajam karne kaa man hai kyaa ? '
shankar ne chakit hokar kahaa, mainne tumse kab gehoon lie the jo saadhe paanch man ho gaye ? tum bhoolate ho, mere yahan kisee kaa chhataank-bhar na anaaj hai,na ek paisaa udhaar| '
vipr -'isee neeyat kaa to yah phal bhog rahe ho ki khaane ko nahin judataa| '
yah kahakar viprajee ne us savaa ser gehoon kaa jikr kiya, jo aaj ke saat varsh pahale shankar ko diye the| shankar sunakar avaak raha gaya| eeshvar mainne inhen kitanee baar khalihaanee dee, inhonne meraa kaun-saa kaam kiya ? jab
pothee-patr dekhane, saait-sagun vichaarane dvaar par aate the, kuchh-na-kuchh 'dakshinaa' le hee jaate the| itanaa svaarth ! savaa ser anaaj ko ande kee tarah sekar aaj yah pishaach khadaa kar diya, jo mujhe nigal jaayagaa| itane dinon mein ek baar bhee kah dete to main gehoon taulakar de detaa, kyaa isee neeyat se chup saadh baithe rahe ? bol, ' mahaaraaj, naam lekar to mainne utanaa anaaj nahin diya, par kaee baar khalihaanon mein ser-ser, do-do ser diya hai| ab aap aaj saadhe paanch man maangate hain, main kahan se doongaa ? '
vipr -'lekhaa jau-jau, bakhasees sau-sau, tumane jo kuchh diya hogaa, usakaa koee hisaab nahin, chaahe ek kee jagah chaar paseree de do| tumhaare naam bahee mein saadhe paanch man likhaa hua hai; jisse chaahe hisaab lagavaa lo| de do to
tumhaaraa naam chhek doon, nahin to aur bhee badhataa rahegaa| '
shankar -'paande, kyon ek gareeb ko sataate ho, mere khaane kaa thikaanaa nahin, itanaa gehoon kisake ghar se laaoongaa ? '
vipr -'zisake ghar se chaahe laao, main chhataank-bhar bhee na chhodoongaa, yahan na doge, bhagavaan ke ghar to doge| '
shankar kaanp uthaa| ham padhe-likhe aadamee hote to kah dete, 'achchhee baat hai, eeshvar ke ghar hee denge; wahan kee taul yahan se kuchh badee to na hogee| kam-se-kam isakaa koee pramaan hamaare paas nahin, phir usakee kyaa chintaa| lekin shankar itanaa taarkik, itanaa vyavahaar-hoshiyaar na tha| ek to rin vah bhee braahman kaa bahee mein naam raha gaya to seedhe narak mein jaaoongaa, is khayaal hee se use romaanch ho gaya| bol, ' mahaaraaj, tumhaaraa jitanaa hogaa yaheen doongaa, eeshvar ke yahan kyon doon, is janam mein to thokar khaa hee raha hoon, us janam ke lie kyon kaante booon ? magar yah koee niyaav nahin hai| tumane raaee kaa parvat banaa diya, braahman ho ke tumhen aisaa nahin karn chaahie tha| usee ghadee tagaadaa karake le liya hot, to aaj mere sir par itanaa badaa bojh kyon padataa| main to de
doongaa, lekin tumhen bhagavaan ke yahan javaab denaa padegaa| '
vipr -'wahan kaa dar tumhen hogaa, mujhe kyon hone lagaa| wahan to sab apne hee bhai-bandhu hain| rishi-muni, sab to braahman hee hain; devataa braahman hain, jo kuchh bane-bigadegee, sanbhaal lenge| to kab dete ho ? '
shankar -'mere paas rakkhaa to hai nahin, kisee se maang-jaochakar laaoongaa tabhee na doongaa ! '
vipr -'main yah na maanoongaa| saat saal ho gaye, ab ek din kaa bhee mulaahijaa na karoongaa| gehoon nahin de sakate, to dastaavej likh do| '
shankar -- ' mujhe to denaa hai, chaahe gehoon lo chaahe dastaavej likhaao; kis hisaab se daam rakkhoge ? '
vipr -'baajaar-bhaav paanch ser kaa hai, tumhen savaa paanch ser kaa kaat doongaa|'
shankar-' zab de hee raha hoon to baajaar-bhaav kaatoongaa, paav-bhar chhudaakar kyon doshee banoon| '
hisaab lagaayaa to gehoon ke daam 60) hue| 60) kaa dastaavej likhaa gaya, 3) saikade sood| saal-bhar mein na dene par sood kee dar dhaaee rupaye) saikade| aath aane) kaa staamp, chaar aane) dastaavej kee tahareer shankar ko oopar se denee
padee|
gaanv bhar ne viprajee kee nindaa kee, lekin munh par nahin| mahaajan se sabhee kaa kaam padataa hai, usake munh kaun aaye| shankar ne saal bhar tak mushkil tapasyaa kee| meeyaad ke pahale rupaye adaa karne kaa usane vrat-saa kar liya| dopahar ko pahale bhee choolhaa na jalataa tha, chabaine par basar hoti thi, ab vah bhee band hua| keval ladake ke lie raat ko rotiyaan rakh dee jaateen ! paise roj kaa tanbaakoo pee jat tha, yahee ek vyasan tha jisakaa vah kabhee na tyaag kar sakaa tha| ab vah vyasan bhee is mushkil vrat kee bhent ho gaya| usane chilam patak dee, hukkaa tod diya aur tamaakhoo kee haandee choor-choor kar daalee| kapade pahale bhee tyaag kee charam seemaa tak pahunch
chuke the, ab vah prakriti kee nyoonatam rekhaaon mein aabaddh ho gaye| shishir kee asthibedhak sheet ko usane aag taapakar kaat diya| is sankalp kaa phal ummeed se badhakar nikalaa| saal ke ant mein usake paas 60) ru.
jamaa ho gaye| usane samajhaa panditajee ko itane rupaye de doongaa aur kahoongaa mahaaraaj, baakee rupaye bhee jald hee aapake saamane haajir karoongaa| 15) ru. kee to aur baat hai, kyaa panditajee itanaa bhee na maanenge ! usane rupaye liye aur le jaakar panditajee ke charan-kamalon par arpan kar diye| panditajee ne vismit hokar
poochhaa, ' kisee se udhaar liye kyaa ? '
shankar -'nahin mahaaraaj, ' aapake asees se abakee majooree achchhee milee| '
vipr -'lekin yah to 60) ru. hee hain ! '
shankar -- ' haan mahaaraaj, itane abhee le leejie, baakee main do-teen maheene mein de doongaa, mujhe urin kar deejie| '
vipr -'urin to jabhee hoge jabaki meree kaudee-kaudee chukaa doge| jaakar mere 15) ru. aur laao| '
shankar -'mahaaraaj, itanee dayaa karo; ab saanjh kee rotiyon kaa bhee thikaanaa nahin hai, gaanv mein hoon to kabhee-na-kabhee de hee doongaa| '
vipr -'main yah rog nahin paalataa, na bahut baaten karn jaanataa hoon| agar mere poore rupaye na milenge to aaj se 3) ru. saikade kaa byaaj lagegaa| apne rupaye chaahe apne ghar mein rakkho, chaahe mere yahan chhod jaao|
shankar -'achchhaa jitanaa laayaa hoon utanaa rakh leejie| main jat hoon, kaheen se 15) ru. aur laane kee phikr kart hoon|
shankar ne saaraa gaanv chhaan maaraa, magar kisee ne rupaye na diye, isalie nahin ki usakaa yakeen na tha, yaa kisee ke paas rupaye na the, balki isalie ki panditajee ke shikaar ko chhedane kee kisee kee himmat na thi|
kriyaa ke baad pratikriyaa naisargik niyam hai| shankar saal-bhar tak tapasyaa karne par bhee jab rin se mukt hone mein kaamayaab na ho sakaa to usakaa sanyam maayoosee ke roop mein parinat ho gaya| usane samajh liya ki jab itanaa takaleeph sahane par bhee saal-bhar mein 60) ru. se zyaadaa na jamaa kar sakaa, to ab aur kaun saa upaay hai jisake dvaaraa isake doone rupaye jamaa hon| jab sir par rin kaa bojh hee laadanaa hai to kyaa man-bhar kaa aur kyaa savaa man kaa| usakaa utsaah ksheen ho gaya, mihanat se napharat ho gayee| ummeed utsaah kee jananee hai, ummeed mein tej hai, bal hai, jeevan hai| ummeed hee sansaar kee sanchaalak shakti hai| shankar aashaaheen hokar udaaseen ho gaya| vah jarooraten jinako usane saal-bhar tak taal rakhaa tha, ab dvaar par khadee honevaalee bhikhaarinee na theen, balki chhaatee par savaar honevaalee pishaachaniyaan theen, jo apanee bhent liye binaa jaan nahin chhodateen| kapadon mein chakattiyon ke lagane kee bhee ek seemaa hoti hai| ab shankar ko chitthaa milataa to vah rupaye jamaa na kart, kabhee kapade laataa,
kabhee khaane kee koee vastu| jahaan pahale tamaakhoo hee piyaa kart tha, wahan ab gaanje aur charas kaa chaskaa bhee lagaa| use ab rupaye adaa karne kee koee phikr na thi maano usake oopar kisee kaa ek paisaa bhee nahin aat| pahale joodee chadhee hoti thi, par vah kaam karne avashy jat tha, ab kaam par na jaane ke lie bahaanaa khojaa kart|
is tarah teen varsh nikal gaye| viprajee mahaaraaj ne ek baar bhee takaajaa na kiya| vah hoshiyaar shikaaree kee tarah achook nishaanaa lagaanaa chaahate the| pahale se shikaar ko chaunkaanaa unakee neeti ke viruddh tha| ek din panditajee ne shankar ko bulaakar hisaab dikhaayaa| 60) ru. jo jamaa the vah minahaa karne par ab bhee shankar ke jimme 120) ru. nikale| shankar -'itane rupaye to usee janm mein doongaa, is janm mein nahin ho sakate| '
vipr -'main isee janm mein loongaa| mool na sahee, sood to denaa hee padegaa| '
shankar -- ' ek bail hai, vah le leejie aur mere paas rakkhaa kyaa hai| '
vipr -'mujhe bail-badhiyaa lekar kyaa karn hai| mujhe dene ko tumhaare paas bahut kuchh hai| '
shankar -'aur kyaa hai mahaaraaj ? '
vipr -'kuchh hai, tum to ho| aakhir tum bhee kaheen majooree karne jaate hee ho, mujhe bhee khetee ke lie majoor rakhanaa hee padataa hai| sood mein tum hamaare yahan kaam kiya karo, jab subheetaa ho mool ko de denaa| sach to yon hai ki ab
tum kisee doosaree jagah kaam karne nahin jaa sakate jab tak mere rupaye nahin chukaa do| tumhaare paas koee jaayadaad nahin hai, itanee badee gatharee main kis etabaar par chhod doon| kaun isakaa jimmaa legaa ki tum mujhe maheene-maheene sood dete jaaoge| aur kaheen kamaakar jab tum mujhe sood bhee nahin de sakate, to mool kee kaun kahe ?'
shankar -'mahaaraaj, sood mein to kaam karoongaa aur khaaoongaa kyaa ? '
vipr -'tumhaaree gharavaalee hai, ladake hain, kyaa ve haath-paanv kataake baithenge| raha main, tumhen aadhaa ser jau roj kalevaa ke lie de diya karoongaa| odhane ko saal mein ek kambal paa jaaoge, ek mirajaee bhee banavaa diya karoongaa, aur
kyaa chaahie| yah sach hai ki aur log tumhen chh: aane dete hain lekin mujhe aisee garaj nahin hai, main to tumhen rupaye bharane ke lie rakhataa hoon| '
shankar ne kuchh der tak gaharee phikr mein pade rehne ke baad kahaa, ' mahaaraaj yah to janm-bhar kee gulaamee huee| '
vipr -'Gulaamee samajho, chaahe majooree samajho| main apne rupaye bharaaye binaa tumako kabhee na chhodoongaa| tum bhaagoge to tumhaaraa ladakaa bharegaa| haan, jab koee na rahegaa tab kee baat doosaree hai| '
is phaisalaa kee kaheen apeel na thi| majoor kee jamaanat kaun kart, kaheen sharan na thi, bhaagakar kahan jat, doosare din se usane viprajee ke yahan kaam karn shuroo kar diya| savaa ser gehoon kee badaulat umr-bhar ke lie gulaamee
kee bedee pairon mein daalanee padee| us abhaage ko ab agar kisee soch se santosh hot tha to yah tha ki vah mere pahale-janm kaa sanskaar hai| aurat ko ve kaam karne padate the, jo usane kabhee na kiye the, bachche daanon ko tarasate
the, lekin shankar chupachaap dekhane ke sivaa aur kuchh na kar sakataa tha| vah gehoon ke daane kisee devataa ke shaap kee tarah yaavajjeevan usake sir se na utare|
shankar ne viprajee ke yahan 20 varsh tak gulaamee karne ke baad is dussaar sansaar se ravaanaa honaa kiya| 120) abhee tak usake sir par savaar the| panditajee ne us gareeb ko eeshvar ke darabaar mein takaleeph denaa theek na samajhaa, itane anyaayee, itane nirdayee na the| usake javaan bete kee garadan pakadee| aaj tak vah viprajee ke yahan kaam kart hai| usakaa uddhaar kab hogaa; hogaa bhee yaa nahin, eeshvar hee jaane|
paathak ! is vrittaant ko kapol-kalpit na samajhie| yah saty ghatanaa hai| aise shankaron aur aise vipron se duniyaa khaalee nahin hai|
