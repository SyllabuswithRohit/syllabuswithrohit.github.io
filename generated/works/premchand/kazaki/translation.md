# Kazaki

**Munshi Premchand**

meree baal-smritiyon mein 'kajaakee' ek na mitane vaalaa vyakti hai. aaj chaalees saal gujar gaye; kajaakee kee moorti abhee tak aankhon ke saamane naach rahi hai. main una dinon apne pitaa ke saath aajamagadh kee ek tahaseel mein tha. kajaakee jaati kaa paasee tha, badaa hee hansamukh, badaa hee saahasee, badaa hee jindaadil. woh roj shaam ko daak kaa thailaa lekar aat, raat-bhar rehta aur sabere daak lekar chalaa jat. shaam ko phir udhar se daak lekar aa jat.

main din bhar ek udvign dashaa mein usakee raah dekh kart. jyon hee chaar bajate, vyaakul ho kar, sadak par aa kar, khadaa ho jat, aur thodee der mein kajaakee kandho par ballam rakhe, usakee jhunjhunee bajaataa, door se daudataa hua aat dikhalaayee detaa. woh saanvale rang kaa gatheelaa, lambaa javaan tha. shareer saanche mein aisaa dhalaa hua ki hoshiyaar moortikaar bhee usamen koi dosh na nikaal sakataa. usakee chhotee-chhotee moonchhen, usake sudaul chehare par bahut hee achchhee maaloom hoti theen. mujhe dekh kar woh aur tej daudane lagataa, usakee jhunjhunee aur tejee se bajane lagatee, aur mere dil mein aur jor se khushi kee dhadakan hone lagatee. harshaatirek mein main bhee daud padataa aur ek pal mein kajaakee kaa kandhaa meraa sinhaasan ban jat. woh sthaan meree abhilaashaaon kaa svarg tha. svarg ke nivaasiyon ko bhee shaayad woh aandolit khushi na milataa hogaa jo mujhe kajaakee ke vishaal kandhon par milataa tha. sansaar meree aankhon mein tuchchh ho jat aur jab kajaakee mujhe kandho par liye hue daudane lagataa, tab to aisaa maaloom hot, maano main havaa ke ghode par udaa jaa raha hoon.

kajaakee daakakhaane mein pahunchataa, to paseene se tar rehta; lekin aaraam karne kee aadat na thi. thailaa rakhate hee woh ham logon ko le kar kisee maidaan mein nikal jat, kabhee hamaare saath khelataa, kabhee birahe gaa kar sunaataa aur kabhee kahaaniyaan sunaataa. use choree aur daake, maar-peet, bhoot-pret kee saikadon kahaaniyaan yaad theen. main ye kahaaniyaan sunakar vismayapoorn khushi mein magn ho jat; usakee kahaaniyon ke chor aur daakoo sachche yoddhaa hote the, jo ameeron ko loot kar gareeb-dukhee praaniyon kaa paalan karte the. mujhe una par napharat ke badale shrriddhaa hoti thi.

ek din kajaakee ko daak kaa thailaa le kar aane mein der ho gayee. sooryaast ho gaya aur woh dikhalaayee na diya. main khoyaa hua-saa sadak par door tak aankhen phaad-phaad kar dekhataa tha; par woh parichit rekhaa na dikhalaayee padatee thi. kaan lagaa kar sunataa tha; 'jhun-jhun' kee woh aamodamay dhavani na sunaayee detee thi. prakaash ke saath meree ummeed bhee malin hoti jati thi. udhar se
kisee ko aate dekhataa, to poochhataa, kajaakee aat hai? par yaa to koi sunataa hee na tha, yaa keval sir hilaa detaa tha. sahasaa 'jhun-jhun' kee aavaaj kaanon mein aayee. mujhe andhere mein chaaron ora bhoot hee dikhalaayee dete the, yahan tak ki maan jee ke kamare mein taak par rakhee huee mithaaee bhee andheraa ho jaane ke baad, mere lie tyaajy ho jati thi; lekin woh aavaaj sunate hee main usakee taraph jor se daudaa. haan, woh kajaakee hee tha. use dekhate hee meree vikalataa gussaa mein badal gayee. main use maarane lagaa, phir rooth karake alag khadaa ho gaya.

kajaakee ne hans kar kahaa —maaroge, to main ek cheej laayaa hoon, woh na doongaa. mainne saahas karake kahaa —jaao, mata denaa, main loongaa hee nahin. kajaakee, abhee dikhaa doon, to daud kar god mein uthaa loge. mainne pighal kar kahaa —achchhaa, dikhaa do. kajaakee, to aa kar mere kandho par baith jaao bhaag chaloon. aaj bahut der ho gayee hai. baaboo jee bigad rahe honge. mainne akad kar kahaa —pahile dikhaa. meree vijay huee. agar kajaakee ko der kaa dar na hot aur woh ek minat bhee aur ruk sakataa, to shaayad paansaa palat jat. usane koi cheej dikhalaayee, jise woh ek haath se chhaatee se chipataaye hue tha; lambaa munh tha, aur do aankhen chamak rahi theen.

mainne use daud kar kajaakee kee god se le liya. yah hiran kaa bachchaa tha. aah! meree us khushi kaa kaun anumaan karegaa? tab se mushkil pareekshaaen paas keen, achchhaa pad bhee paayaa, raayabahaadur bhee hua; par woh khushi phir na haasil huee. main use god mein liye, usake komal sparsh kaa khushi uthaataa ghar kee ora daudaa. kajaakee ko aane mein kyon itanee der huee isakaa khayaal hee na raha.

mainne poochhaa—yah kahan milaa, kajaakee?
kajaakee, bhaiyaa, yahan se thodee door par ek chhotaa-saa jangal hai. usamen bahut-se hiran hain. meraa bahut jee chaahataa tha ki koi bachchaa mil jaae, to tumhe doon. aaj yah bachchaa hiranon ke jhund ke saath dikhalaayee diya. main jhund kee ora daudaa, to sab ke sab bhaage. yah bachchaa bhee bhaagaa; lekin mainne peechhaa na chhodaa. aur hiran to bahut door nikal gaye, yahee peechhe raha gaya. mainne ise pakad liya. isee se itanee der huee.

yon baaten karte ham donon daakakhaane pahunche. baaboo jee ne mujhe na dekh, hiran ke bachche ko bhee na dekh, kajaakee hee par unakee nigaah padee. bigad kar bole — aaj itanee der kahan lagaayee? ab thailaa le kar aay hai, use lekar kya karoon? daak to chalee gayee. bataa, toone itanee der kahan lagaayee? kajaakee ke munh se aavaaj na nikalee.

baaboo jee ne kahaa —tujhe shaayad ab naukaree nahin karanee hai. neech hai na, pet bharaa to motaa ho gaya! jab bhookhon marane lagegaa, to aankhen khulengee.
kajaakee chupachaap khadaa raha.
baaboo jee kaa gussaa aur badhaa. bole — achchhaa, thailaa rakh de aur apne ghar kee raah le. sooar, ab daak le ke aay hai. teraa kya bigadegaa, jahaan chaahegaa, majooree kar legaa. maathe to mere jaayagee, jawab to mujhse talab hogaa.
kajaakee ne ruaanse ho kar kahaa —sarakaar, ab kabhee der na hogee.
baaboo jee, aaj kyon der kee, isakaa jawab de?

kajaakee ke paas isakaa koi jawab na tha. hairaanee to yah tha ki meree bhee jabaan band ho gayee. baaboo jee bade gussevar the. unhe kaam karn padataa tha, isee se baat-baat par jhunjhalaa padate the. main to unake saamane kabhee jat hee na tha. woh bhee mujhe kabhee pyaar na karte the. ghar mein keval do baar ghante-ghante bhar ke lie khana karne aate the, baakee saare din daphtar mein likhaa karte the. unhonne baar-baar ek sahakaaree ke lie aphasaron se vinay kee thi; par isakaa kuchh asar na hua tha. yahan tak ki taateel ke din bhee baaboo jee daphtar hee mein rehte the. keval maan jee unakaa gussaa shaant karn jaanatee theen; par woh daphtar mein kaise aateen. bechaaraa kajaakee usee vakt mere dekhate-dekhate nikaal diya gaya. usakaa ballam, chaparaas aur saaphaa chheen liya gaya aur use daakakhaane se nikal jaane kaa naadiree hukm sunaa diya. aah! us vakt meraa aisaa jee chaahataa tha ki mere paas sone kee lankaa hoti, to kajaakee ko de detaa aur baaboo jee ko dikhaa detaa ki aapake nikaal dene se kajaakee kaa baal bhee baankaa nahin hua. kisee yoddhaa ko apanee talavaar par jitanaa ghamand hot hai, utanaa hee ghamand kajaakee ko apanee chaparaas par tha. jab woh chaparaas kholane lagaa, to usake haath kaanp rahe the aur aankhon se aansoo bah rahe the.

aur is saare upadrav kee jad woh komal vastu thi, jo meree god mein munh chhipaaye aise chain se baithee huee thi, maano maan kee god mein ho. jab kajaakee chalaa to main dheere-dheere usake peechhe-peechhe chalaa. mere ghar ke dvaar par aa kar kajaakee ne kahaa —bhaiyaa, ab ghar jaao; saanjh ho gayee.
main chupachaap khadaa apne aansuon ke veg ko saaree shakti se dabaa raha tha. kajaakee phir bol — bhaiyaa, main kaheen baahar thode hee chalaa jaaoongaa. phir aaoongaa aur tumhe kandho par baithaa kar kudaaoongaa. baaboo jee ne naukaree le lee hai, to kya itanaa bhee na karne denge! tumako chhod kar main kaheen na jaaoongaa, bhaiyaa! jaakar ammaan se kah do, kajaakee jat hai. usakaa kahaa-sunaa maaph karen.

main daudaa hua ghar gaya, lekin ammaan jee ko kuchh kehne ke badale bilakh-bilakh kar rone lagaa. ammaan jee rasoee ke baahar nikal kar poochhane lageen, kya hua betaa? kisane maaraa! baaboo jee ne kuchh kahaa hai? achchhaa, raha to jaao, aaj ghar aate hain, to poochhatee hoon. jab dekho, mere ladake ko maaraa karte hain. chup raho betaa, ab tum unake paas kabhee mata jan.

mainne badee mushkil se aavaaj sanbhaal kar kahaa —kajaakee. .. ammaan ne samajhaa, kajaakee ne maaraa hai; boleen, achchhaa, aane do kajaakee ko, dekho, khade-khade nikalavaa detee hoon. harakaaraa ho kar mere raajaa betaa ko maare! aaj hee to saaphaa, ballam, sab chhinavaaye letee hoon. vaah!
mainne jaldee se kahaa —nahin, kajaakee ne nahin maaraa. baaboo jee ne use nikaal diya hai; usakaa saaphaa, ballam chheen liya, chaparaas bhee le lee.
ammaan, yah tumhaare baaboo jee ne bahut buraa kiya. woh bechaaraa apne kaam mein itanaa chaukas rehta hai. phir use kyon nikaalaa?
mainne kahaa —aaj use der ho gayee thi.

yah kah kar mainne hiran ke bachche ko god se utaar diya. ghar mein usake bhaag jaane kaa dar na tha. ab tak ammaan jee kee nigaah bhee us par na padee thi. use phudakate dekh kar woh sahasaa chaunk padeen aur lapak kar meraa haath pakad liya ki kaheen yah bhayankar jeev mujhe kaat na khaay! main kahan to phoot-phoot kar ro raha tha aur kahan ammaan kee ghabaraahat dekh kar khilakhilaa kar hans padaa.
ammaan, are, yah to hiran kaa bachchaa hai! kahan milaa? mainne hiran ke bachche kaa saaraa itihaas aur usakaa bheeshan nateejaa aadi se ant tak kah sunaayaa, ammaan, yah itanaa tej bhaagataa tha ki koi doosaraa hot, to pakad hee na sakataa. san-san, havaa kee tarah udataa chalaa jat tha. kajaakee paanch-chh: ghante tak isake peechhe daudataa raha. tab kaheen jaa kar bachchaa mile. ammaan jee, kajaakee kee tarah koi duniya bhar mein nahin daud sakataa, isee se to der ho gayee. isalie baaboo jee ne bechaare ko nikaal diya, chaparaas, saaphaa, ballam, sab chheen liya. ab bechaaraa kya karegaa? bhookhon mar jaayagaa. ammaan ne poochhaa—kahan hai kajaakee, zara use bulaa to laao. mainne kahaa —baahar to khadaa hai. keht tha, ammaan jee se meraa kahaa-sunaa maaph karavaa denaa.

ab tak ammaan jee mere vrittaant ko dillagee samajh rahi theen. shaayad woh samajhatee theen ki baaboo jee ne kajaakee ko dotaa hogaa; lekin meraa antim vaaky sunakar sanshay hua ki sachamuch to kajaakee barakhaast nahin kar diya gaya. baahar aa kar 'kajaakee! kajaakee' pukaarane lageen, par kajaakee kaa kaheen pataa na tha. mainne baar-baar pukaaraa; lekin kajaakee wahan na tha.
khana to mainne khaa liya, bachche shok mein khana nahin chhodate, khaas kar jab rabadee bhee saamane ho; magar badee raat tak pade-pade sochataa raha, mere paas rupaye hote, to ek laakh rupaye kajaakee ko de detaa aur keht, baaboo jee se kabhee mata bolanaa. bechaaraa bhookhon mar jaayagaa! dekhoon, kal aat hai ki nahin. ab kya karegaa aa kar? magar aane ko to kah gaya hai. main kal use apne saath khana khilaaoongaa. yahee havaaee kile banaate-banaate mujhe neend aa gayee. doosare din main din bhar apne hiran ke bachche kee sevaa-satkaar mein vyast raha.

pahale usakaa naamakaran sanskaar hua. 'munnoo' naam rakhaa gaya. phir mainne usakaa apne sab hamajoliyon aur sahapaathiyon se parichay karaayaa. din hee bhar mein woh mujhse itanaa hil gaya ki mere peechhe-peechhe daudane lagaa. itanee hee der mein mainne use apne jeevan mein ek mahattvapoorn sthaan de diya. apne aane vaalaa samay mein bananevaale vishaal bhavan mein usake lie alag kamaraa banaane kaa bhee phaisalaa kar liya; chaarapaaee, sair karne kee phitan aadi kee bhee aayojanaa kar lee.

lekin shaam hote hee main sab kuchh chhod-chhaad kar sadak par jaa khadaa hua aur kajaakee kee baat johane lagaa. jaanataa tha ki kajaakee nikaal diya gaya hai, ab use yahan aane kee koi jaroorat nahin rahi. phir na-jaane mujhe kyon yah ummeed ho rahi thi ki woh aa raha hai. ekaaek mujhe khayaal aay ki kajaakee bhookhon mar raha hogaa. main turant ghar aay. ammaan diya-battee kar rahi theen. mainne chupake se ek tokaree mein aat nikaalaa; aat haathon mein lapete, tokaree se girate aate kee ek lakeer banaataa hua bhaagaa. jaa kar sadak par khadaa hua hee tha ki kajaakee saamane se aat dikhalaayee diya. usake paas ballam bhee tha, kamar mein chaparaas bhee thi, sir par saaphaa bhee bandhaa hua tha. ballam mein daak kaa thailaa bhee bandhaa hua tha. main daud kar usakee kamar se chipat gaya aur vismit ho kar bol — tumhe chaparaas aur ballam kahan se mil gaya, kajaakee? kajaakee ne mujhe uthaa kar kandho par baithaalate hue kahaa —woh chaparaas kis kaam kee thi, bhaiyaa? woh to gulaamee kee chaparaas thi, yah puraanee khushi kee chaparaas hai. pahale sarakaar kaa naukar tha, ab tumhaaraa naukar hoon. yah kehte-kehte usakee nigaah tokaree par padee, jo vaheen rakhee thi.

bol — yah aat kaisaa hai, bhaiyaa?
mainne sakuchaate hue kahaa —tumhaare hee lie to laayaa hoon. tum bhookhe hoge, aaj kya khaayaa hogaa?
kajaakee kee aankhen to main na dekh sakaa, usake kandho par baithaa hua tha;
haan, usakee aavaaj se maaloom hua ki usakaa galaa bhar aay hai. bol — bhaiyaa, kya rookhee hee rotiyaan khaaoongaa? daal, namak, ghee, aur to kuchh nahin hai. main apanee bhool par bahut lajjit hua. sach to hai, bechaaraa rookhee rotiyaan kaise khaayagaa? lekin namak, daal, ghee kaise laaoon? ab to ammaan chauke mein hongee. aat le kar to kisee tarah bhaag aay tha (abhee tak mujhe na maaloom tha ki meree choree pakad lee gayee; aate kee lakeer ne suraag de diya hai). ab ye teen-teen cheejen kaise laaoongaa? ammaan se maangoongaa, to kabhee na dengee. ek-ek paise ke lie to ghanton rulaatee hain, itanee saaree cheejen kyon dene lageen? ekaaek mujhe ek baat yaad aayee. mainne apanee kitaabon ke baston mein kaee aane paise rakh chhode the. mujhe paise jamaa karake rakhane mein badaa khushi aat tha. maaloom nahin ab woh aadat kyon badal gayee. ab bhee vahee haalat hoti to shaayad itanaa phaakemast na rehta. baaboo jee mujhe pyaar to kabhee na karte the; par paise khoob dete the, shaayad apne kaam mein vyast rehne ke vajah, mujhse pind chhudaane ke lie isee nuskhe ko sabase aasaan samajhate the. inakaar karne mein mere rone aur machalane kaa dar tha. is baadhaa ko woh door hee se taal dete the. ammaan jee kaa aadat isase theek pratikool tha. unhe mere rone aur machalane se kisee kaam mein baadhaa padane kaa dar na tha. aadamee lete-lete din bhar ronaa sun sakataa hai; hisaab lagaate hue jor kee aavaaj se dhyaan bant jat hai. ammaan mujhe pyaar to bahut karti theen, par paise kaa naam sunate hee unakee tyoriyaan badal jati theen. mere paas kitaaben na theen. haan, ek bastaa tha, jisamen daakakhaane ke do-chaar phaarm tah karake pustak roop mein rakhe hue the. mainne sochaa, daal, namak aur ghee ke lie kya utane paise kaaphee na honge? meree to mutthee mein nahin aate. yah phaisalaa karake mainne kahaa —achchhaa, mujhe utaar do, to main daal aur namak laa doon, magar roj aay karoge na?

kajaakee, bhaiyaa, khaane ko doge, to kyon na aaoongaa.
mainne kahaa —main roj khaane ko doongaa.
kajaakee bol — to main roj aaoongaa.
main neeche utaraa aur daud kar saaree poonjee uthaa laayaa. kajaakee ko roj bulaane ke lie us vakt mere paas kohanoor heeraa bhee hot, to usako bhent karne mein mujhe pasopesh na hot.
kajaakee ne vismit ho kar poochhaa—ye paise kahan paaye, bhaiyaa?
mainne garv se kahaa —mere hee to hain.
kajaakee, tumhaaree ammaan jee tumako maarengee, kahengee, kajaakee ne phusalaa kar mangavaa liye honge. bhaiyaa, ina paison kee mithaaee le lenaa aur matake mein rakh denaa. main bhookhon nahin marataa. mere do haath hain. main bhalaa bhookhon mar sakataa hoon?

mainne bahut kahaa ki paise mere hain, lekin kajaakee ne na lie. usane badee der tak idhar-udhar kee sair karaayee, geet sunaaye aur mujhe ghar pahunchaa kar chalaa gaya. mere dvaar par aate kee tokaree bhee rakh dee.
mainne ghar mein kadam rakhaa hee tha ki ammaan jee ne dot kar kahaa —kyon re chor, too aat kahan le gaya tha? ab choree karn seekhataa hai? bataa, kisako aat de aay, nahin to teree khaal udhed kar rakh doongee.
meree naanee mar gayee. ammaan gussaa mein sinhanee ho jati theen. sitapitaa kar bol — kisee ko to nahin diya.
ammaan, toone aat nahin nikaalaa? dekh kitanaa aat saare aangan mein bikharaa padaa hai?
main chup khadaa tha. woh kitanaa hee dhaamakaatee theen, chumakaaratee theen, par meree jabaan na khulatee thi. aanevaalee vipatti ke dar se jaan sookh rahe the. yahan tak ki yah bhee kehne kee himmat na padatee thi ki bigadatee kyon ho, aat to dvaar par rakhaa hua hai, aur na uthaa kar laate hee banataa tha, maano kriyaa-shakti hee lupt ho gayee ho, maano pairon mein hilane kee saamarthy hee nahin. sahasaa kajaakee ne pukaaraa, bahoo jee, aat dvaar par rakhaa hua hai. bhaiyaa mujhe dene ko le gaye the.

yah sunate hee ammaan dvaar kee ora chalee gayeen. kajaakee se woh paradaa na karti theen. unhonne kajaakee se koi baat kee yaa nahin, yah to main nahin jaanataa; lekin ammaan jee khaalee tokaree liye hue ghar mein aayeen. phir kotharee mein jaakar sandook se kuchh nikaalaa aur dvaar kee ora gayeen. mainne dekh ki unakee mutthee band thi. ab mujhse wahan khade na raha gaya.
ammaan jee ke peechhe-peechhe main bhee gaya. ammaan ne dvaar par kaee baar pukaaraa; magar kajaakee chalaa gaya tha.
mainne badee adheerataa se kahaa —main jaa kar khoj laaoon, ammaan jee? ammaan jee ne kivaaden band karte hue kahaa —tum andhere mein kahan jaaoge, abhee to yaheen khadaa tha. mainne kahaa ki yaheen rahanaa; main aati hoon. tab tak na-jaane kahan khisak gaya. badaa sankochee hai! aat to letaa hee na tha. mainne jabaradastee usake angochhe mein baandh diya. mujhe to bechaare par badee raham aati hai. na-jaane bechaare ke ghar mein kuchh khaane ko hai ki nahin. rupaye laayee thi ki de doongee; par na-jaane kahan chalaa gaya. ab to mujhe bhee saahas hua. mainne apanee choree kee pooree kathaa kah daalee. bachchon ke saath samajhadaar bachche ban kar maan-baap una par jitanaa asar daal sakate hain, jitanee shikshaa de sakate hain, utane boodhe ban kar nahin. ammaan jee ne kahaa —tumane mujhse poochh kyon na liya? kya main kajaakee ko thodaa-saa aat na detee?

mainne isakaa jawab na diya. dil mein kahaa —is vakt tumhe kajaakee par raham aa gayee hai, jo chaahe de daalo; lekin main maangataa, to maarane daudateen. haan, yah soch kar chitt khush hua ki ab kajaakee bhookhon na maregaa. ammaan jee use roj khaane ko dengee aur woh roj mujhe kandho par bithaa kar sair karaayegaa. doosare din main din bhar munnoo ke saath khelataa raha. shaam ko sadak par jaa kar khadaa ho gaya. magar andheraa ho gaya aur kajaakee kaa kaheen pataa nahin. diye jal gaye, raaste mein sannaataa chhaa gaya; par kajaakee na aay! main rotaa hua ghar aay. ammaan jee ne poochhaa—kyon rote ho, betaa? kya kajaakee nahin aay? main aur jor se rone lagaa. ammaan jee ne mujhe chhaatee se lagaa liya. mujhe aisaa maaloom hua ki unakaa bhee galaa gadgad ho gaya hai. unhonne kahaa —betaa, chup ho jaao, main kal kisee harakaare ko bhej kar kajaakee ko bulavaaoongee. main rote hee rote so gaya. sabere jyon hee aankhen khuleen, mainne ammaan jee se kahaa —kajaakee ko bulavaa do. ammaan ne kahaa —aadamee gaya hai, betaa! kajaakee aat hogaa. khush ho kar khelane lagaa. mujhe maaloom tha ki ammaan jee jo baat kehti hain, use pooraa zaroor karti hain. unhonne sabere hee ek harakaare ko bhej diya tha. das baje jab main munnoo ko liye hue ghar aay, to maaloom hua ki kajaakee apne ghar par nahin milaa. woh raat ko bhee ghar na gaya tha. usakee aurat ro rahi thi ki na-jaane kahan chale gaye. use dar tha ki woh kaheen bhaag gaya hai.
baalakon kaa dil kitanaa komal hot hai, isakaa anumaan doosaraa nahin kar sakataa. unamen apne bhaavon ko vyakt karne ke lie shabd nahin hote. unhe yah bhee pataa nahin hot ki kaun-see baat unhe vikal kar rahi hai, kaun-saa kaantaa unake dil mein khatak raha hai, kyon baar-baar unhe ronaa aat hai, kyon woh man maare baithe rehte hain, kyon khelane mein jee nahin lagataa? meree bhee yahee dashaa thi. kabhee ghar mein aat, kabhee baahar jat, kabhee sadak par jaa pahunchataa. aankhen kajaakee ko dhoondh rahi theen. woh kahan chalaa gaya? kaheen bhaag to nahin gaya? teesare pahar ko main khoyaa hua-saa sadak par khadaa tha. sahasaa mainne kajaakee ko ek galee mein dekh. haan, woh kajaakee hee tha. main usakee ora chillaataa hua daudaa; par galee mein usakaa pataa na tha, na-jaane kidhar gaayab ho gaya.

mainne galee ke is sire se us sire tak dekh; magar kaheen kajaakee kee gandhaa tak na milee. ghar jaa kar mainne ammaan jee se yah baat kahee. mujhe aisaa jaan padaa ki woh yah baat sun kar bahut chintit ho gayeen. isake baad do-teen din tak kajaakee na dikhalaayee diya. main bhee ab use kuchh-kuchh bhoolane lagaa. bachche pahale jitanaa pyaar karte hain, baad ko utane hee nishthur bhee ho jate hain. jis khilaune par jaan dete hain, usee ko do-chaar din ke baad patak kar phod bhee daalate hain. das-baarah din aur beet gaye. dopahar kaa samay tha. baaboo jee khana khaa rahe the. main munnoo ke pairon mein peenas kee paijaniyaan baandh raha tha. ek aurat ghoonghat nikaale hue aayee aur aangan mein khadee ho gayee. usake kapade phate hue aur maile the, par goree, sundar aurat thi.

usane mujhse poochhaa—bhaiyaa, bahoo jee kahan hai?
mainne usake paas jaa kar usakaa munh dekhate hue kahaa —tum kaun ho, kya bechatee ho?
aurat, kuchh bechatee nahin hoon, tumhaare lie ye kamalagatte laayee hoon. bhaiyaa, tumhe to kamalagatte bahut achchhe lagate hain na?
mainne usake haathon se latakatee huee potalee ko utsuk netron se dekh kar poochhaa—kahan se laayee ho? dekhen.
aurat, tumhaare harakaare ne bhejaa hai, bhaiyaa!
mainne uchhal kar poochhaa—kajaakee ne?
aurat ne sir hilaa kar 'haan' kahaa aur potalee kholane lagee. itane mein ammaan jee bhee rasoee se nikal aayeen. usane ammaan ke pairon kaa sparsh kiya.
ammaan ne poochhaa—too kajaakee kee gharavaalee hai?
aurat ne sir jhukaa liya.
ammaan, aajakal kajaakee kya kart hai.
aurat ne ro kar kahaa —bahoo jee, jis din se aapake paas se aat le kar gaye hain, usee din se beemaar pade hain. bas, bhaiyaa-bhaiyaa kiya karte hain.
bhaiyaa hee mein unakaa man basaa rehta hai. chaunk-chaunk kar 'bhaiyaa! bhaiyaa! ' kehte hue dvaar kee ora daudate hain. na jaane unhe kya ho gaya hai, bahoo jee! ek din mujhse kuchh kahaa na sunaa, ghar se chal diye aur ek galee mein chhip kar bhaiyaa ko dekhate rahe. jab bhaiyaa ne unhe dekh liya, to bhaage. tumhaare paas aate hue lajaate hain.
mainne kahaa —haan-haan, mainne us din tumse jo kahaa tha ammaan jee!
ammaan, ghar mein kuchh khaane-peene ko hai?
aurat, haan bahoo jee, tumhaare aasiravaad se khaane-peene kaa du: kha nahin hai.
aaj sabere uthe aur taalaab kee ora chale gaye. bahut kehti rahi, baahar mata jaao, havaa lag jaayagee. magar na maane! maare kamajoree ke pair kaanpane lagate hain, magar taalaab mein ghus kar ye kamalagatte tod laaye. tab mujhse kahaa —le jaa, bhaiyaa ko de aa. unhe kamalagatte bahut achchhe lagate hain. kushal-chhem poochhatee aan.
mainne potalee se kamalagatte nikaal liye the aur maje se chakh raha tha.
ammaan ne bahut aankhen dikhaayeen, magar yahan itanee sabr kahan!
ammaan ne kahaa —kah denaa sab kushal hai.
mainne kahaa —yah bhee kah denaa ki bhaiyaa ne bulaayaa hai. na jaaoge to phir tumse kabhee na bolenge, haan!
baaboo jee khana khaa kar nikal aaye the. tauliye se haath-munh ponchhate hue bole — aur yah bhee kah denaa ki sahab ne tumako bahaal kar diya hai.
jaldee jaao, nahin to koi doosaraa aadamee rakh liya jaayagaa.
aurat ne apanaa kapadaa uthaayaa aur chalee gayee. ammaan ne bahut pukaaraa, par woh na rukee. shaayad ammaan jee use seedhaa denaa chaahatee theen.
ammaan ne poochhaa—sachamuch bahaal ho gaya?
baaboo jee, aur kya jhoothe hee bulaa raha hoon. mainne to paanchaven hee din bahaalee kee riport kee thi.

ammaan, yah tumane achchhaa kiya.
baaboo jee, usakee beemaaree kee yahee davaa hai.
praat: kaal main uthaa, to kya dekhataa hoon ki kajaakee laathee tekataa hua chalaa aa raha hai. woh bahut dubalaa ho gaya tha, maaloom hot tha, boodhaa ho gaya hai. haraa-bharaa ped sookh kar thoonthaa ho gaya tha. main usakee ora daudaa aur usakee kamar se chimat gaya. kajaakee ne mere gaal choome aur mujhe uthaa kar kandho par baithaalane kee cheshtaa karne lagaa; par main na uth sakaa. tab woh jaanavaron kee tarah bhoomi par haathon aur ghutanon ke bal khadaa ho gaya aur main usakee peeth par savaar ho kar daakakhaane kee ora chalaa. main us vakt phoolaa na samaataa tha aur shaayad kajaakee mujhse bhee zyada khush tha. baaboo jee ne kahaa —kajaakee, tum bahaal ho gaye. ab kabhee der na karn. kajaakee rotaa hua pitaa jee ke pairon par gir padaa; magar shaayad mere bhaagy mein donon sukh bhoganaa na likhaa tha, munnoo milaa, to kajaakee chhootaa; kajaakee aay to munnoo haath se gaya aur aisaa gaya ki aaj tak usake jaane kaa du: kha hai. munnoo meree hee thaalee mein khaataa tha. jab tak main khaane na baithoon, woh bhee kuchh na khaataa tha. use bhaat se bahut hee ruchi thi; lekin jab tak khoob ghee na padaa ho, use santosh na hot tha. woh mere hee saath sotaa tha aur mere hee saath uthataa bhee tha. saphaaee to use itanee pasand thi ki mal-mootraa tyaag karne ke lie ghar se baahar maidaan mein nikal jat tha. kutton se use chidh thi, kutton ko ghar mein na ghusane detaa. kutte ko dekhate hee thaalee se uth jat aur use daud kar ghar se baahar nikaal detaa tha. kajaakee ko daakakhaane mein chhod kar jab main khana khaane gaya, to munnoo bhee aa baithaa. abhee do-chaar hee kaur khaaye the ki ek badaa-saa jhabaraa kuttaa aangan mein dikhaayee diya. munnoo use dekhate hee daudaa. doosare ghar mein jaa kar kuttaa choohaa ho jat hai. jhabaraa kuttaa use aate dekh kar bhaagaa. munnoo ko ab laut aan chaahie tha; magar woh kuttaa usake lie yamaraaj kaa doot tha. munnoo ko use ghar se nikaal kar bhee santosh na hua. woh use ghar ke baahar maidaan mein bhee daudaane lagaa. munnoo ko shaayad khayaal na raha ki yahan meree amaladaaree nahin hai. woh us kshetraa mein pahunch gaya tha, jahaan jhabare kaa bhee utanaa hee adhikaar tha, jitanaa munnoo kaa. munnoo kutton ko bhagaate-bhagaate kadaachit apne baahubal par ghamand karne lagaa tha. woh yah na samajhataa tha ki ghar mein usakee peeth par ghar ke swami kaa dar kaam kiya kart hai. jhabare ne is maidaan mein aate hee ulat kar munnoo kee garadan dabaa dee. bechaare munnoo ke munh se aavaaj tak na nikalee. jab padosiyon ne shor machaayaa, to main daudaa. dekh, to munnoo maraa padaa hai aur jhabare kaa kaheen pataa nahin.
