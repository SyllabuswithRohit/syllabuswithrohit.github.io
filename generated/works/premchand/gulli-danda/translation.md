# Gulli Danda

**Munshi Premchand**

1
hamaare angrezi dost maanen yaa na maanen main to yahi kahoongaa ki gullee-dandaa sab khelon kaa raajaa hai. ab bhi kabhi ladakon ko gullee-dandaa khelate dekhta hoon, to jee
lot-pot ho jat hai ki inake saath jaakar khelne lagoon. na laan ki jaroorat, na kort ki, na net ki, na thaapee ki. maje se kisee ped se ek tahanee kaat lee, gullee banaa lee, aur
do aadamee bhi aa jaae, to khel shuru ho gaya.

vilaayatee khelon mein sabase bada aib hai ki usake saamaan mahange hote hain. jab tak kam-se-kam ek saikadaa na kharch keejie, khilaadiyon mein shumaar hi nahin ho paataa. yahan
gullee-dandaa hai ki banaa harr-phitakaree ke chokhaa rang detaa hai; par ham angrezi cheejon ke peechhe aise deevaane ho rahe hain ki apni sabhi cheejon se aroochi ho gayi. skoolon mein
harek ladake se teen-chaar rupaye saalaanaa keval khelne ki phees lee jati hai. kisee ko yah nahin soojhta ki bhaarateey khel khilaaen, jo binaa daam-kaudee ke khele jaate hain.
angrezi khel unake lie hain, jinake paas paisaa hai. gareeb ladakon ke sir kyon yah vyasan madhte ho? theek hai, gullee se aankh phoot jaane kaa dar rehta hai, to kya kriket se
sir phoot jaane, tillee phat jaane, taang toot jaane kaa dar nahin rehta! agar hamaare maathe mein gullee kaa daag aaj tak banaa hua hai, to hamaare kaee dost aise bhi hain, jo thaapee
ko baisaakhee se badal baithe. yah apni-apni roochi hai. mujhe gullee hi sab khelon se achchhi lagti hai aur bachpan ki meethee smritiyon mein gullee hi sabase meethee hai.

vah praat:kaal ghar se nikal jan, vah ped par chadhakar tahaniyaan kaatanaa aur gullee-dande banaanaa, vah utsaah, vah khilaadiyon ke jamaghte, vah padanaa aur padaanaa, vah ladaaee-jhagade,
vah aasaan svabhaav, jisse chhoot-achhoot, ameer-gareeb kaa bilkul bhed na rehta tha, jisamen ameeraanaa chochalon ki, pradarshan ki, abhimaan ki gunjaaish hi na thi, yah usi vakt
bhoolegaa jab... jab.... gharavaale bigad rahe hain, pitaajee chauke par baithe veg se rotiyon par apna gussaa utaar rahe hain, ammaan ki daud keval dvaar tak hai, lekin unki
soch-dhaaraa mein mera andhakaaramay aane vaalaa samay tootee hui naukaa ki tarah dagamagaa raha hai; aur main hoon ki padaane mein mast hoon, na nahaane ki sudhi hai, na khaane ki. gullee hai to
zara-see, par usamen duniyaa-bhar ki mithaaiyon ki mithaas aur tamaashon kaa khushi bharaa hua hai.

mere hamajoliyon mein ek ladakaa gaya naam kaa tha. mujhse do-teen saal bada hogaa. dubalaa, bandaron ki-see lambee-lambee, patalee-patalee ungaliyaan, bandaron ki-see chapalataa, wahi
jhallaahat. gullee kaisee hi ho, par is tarah lapakataa tha, jaise chhipakalee keedon par lapakatee hai. maaloom nahin, usake maan-baap the yaa nahin, kahan rehta tha, kya khaataa tha; par tha
hamaare gullee-klab kaa chaimpiyan. jisakee taraph vah aa jaae, uski jeet nishchit thi. ham sab use door se aate dekh, uska daudakar svaagat karte the aur apna goiyaan banaa lete
the.

ek din main aur gaya do hi khel rahe the. vah padaa raha tha. main pad raha tha, magar kuchh vichitr baat hai ki padaane mein ham din-bhar mast raha sakate hai; padanaa ek minat kaa bhi
akharataa hai. maine galaa chhudaane ke lie sab chaalen chaleen, jo aise avasar par shaastr-vihit na hone par bhi kshamy hain, lekin gaya apna daanv lie bagair mera pind na chhodataa
tha.

main ghar ki ora bhaagaa. anunay-vinay kaa koi asar na hua tha.

gaya ne mujhe daudakar pakad liya aur dandaa taanakar bol-mera daanv dekar jaao. padaayaa to bade bahaadur banake, padane ke ber kyon bhaage jaate ho.

'tum din-bhar padaao to main din-bhar padataa rahoon?'

'haan, tumhen din-bhar padanaa padegaa.'

'na khaane jaaoon, na peene jaaoon?'

'haan! mera daanv diye binaa kaheen nahin jaa sakate.'

'main tumhara gulaam hoon?'

'haan, mere gulaam ho.'

'main ghar jat hoon, dekhoon mera kya kar lete ho!'

'ghar kaise jaaoge; koi dillagee hai. daanv diya hai, daanv lenge.'

'achchha, kal maine amarood khilaayaa tha. vah lautaa do.

'vah to pet mein chalaa gaya.'

'nikaalo pet se. tumne kyon khaayaa mera amarood?'

'amarood tumne diya, tab maine khaayaa. main tumse maangane na gaya tha.'

'jab tak mera amarood na doge, main daanv na doongaa.'

main samajhta tha, nyaay meri ora hai. aakhir maine kisee svaarth se hi use amarood khilaayaa hogaa. kaun ni:svaarth kisee ke saath salook kart hai. bhikshaa tak to svaarth ke
lie dete hain. jab gaya ne amarood khaayaa, to phir use mujhse daanv lene kaa kya adhikar hai? rishvat dekar to log khoon pachaa jaate hain, yah mera amarood yon hi hajam kar
jaaegaa? amarood paise ke paanchavaale the, jo gaya ke baap ko bhi naseeb na honge. yah saraasar anyaay tha.

gaya ne mujhe apni ora kheenchte hue kahaa-mera daanv dekar jaao, amarood-samarood main nahin jaanataa.

mujhe nyaay kaa bal tha. vah anyaay par dataa hua tha. main haath chhudaakar bhaaganaa chahta tha. vah mujhe jaane na detaa! maine use gaalee dee, usane usse kadee gaalee dee, aur
gaalee-hi nahin, ek chaantaa jamaa diya. maine use daant kaat liya. usane meri peeth par dandaa jamaa diya. main rone lagaa! gaya mere is astr kaa mukaabalaa na kar sakaa. maine
turant aansoo ponchh daale, dande ki chot bhool gaya aur hansataa hua ghar jaa pahunchaa! main thaanedaar kaa ladakaa ek neech jaat ke launde ke haathon pita gaya, yah mujhe us samay bhi
apamaanajanak maaloom haaa; lekin ghar mein kisee se shikaayat na ki.

2

unheen dinon pitaajee kaa wahan se tabaadalaa ho gaya. naee duniyaa dekhne ki khushi mein aisaa phoolaa ki apne hamajoliyon se bichhud jaane kaa bilakul du:kha na hua. pitaajee du:khee
the. vah badi aamadanee ki jagah thi. ammaanjee bhi du:khee theen yahan sab cheej sastee theen, aur muhalle ki striyon se gharaav-saa ho gaya tha, lekin main saare khushi ke phoolaa na
samaataa tha. ladakon mein jeet udaa raha tha, wahan aise ghar thode hi hote hain. aise-aise oonche ghar hain ki aasamaan se baaten karte hain. wahan ke angrezi skool mein koi
maastar ladakon ko peete, to use jehal ho jaae. mere mitron ki phailee hui aankhen aur chakit mudraa batalaa rahi thi ki mujhse unki nigaah mein kitanee spardghaa ho rahi thi!
maano kah rahe the-too bhaagavaan ho bhai, jaao. hamen to isi oojad graam mein jeenaa bhi hai aur maranaa bhi.

bees saal gujar gae. maine injeeniyaree paas ki aur usi jile kaa dauraa kart hua usi kasbe mein pahunchaa aur daakabangale mein thaharaa. us sthaan ko dekhte hi itanee madhur
baal-smritiyaan dil mein jaag utheen ki maine chhadee uthaaee aur kasbe ki sair karne nikalaa. aankhen kisee pyaase pathik ki tarah bachpan ke una kreedaa-sthalon ko dekhne ke
lie vyaakul ho rahi theen; par us parichit naam ke sivaa wahan aur kuchh parichit na tha. jahaan khandahar tha, wahan pakke makaan khade the. jahaan baragad kaa puraanaa ped tha, wahan ab
ek sundar bageechaa tha. sthaan ki kaayaa palat ho gayi thi. agar usake naam aur sthiti kaa jnaan na hot, to main use pahachaan bhi na sakataa. bachpan ki sanchit aur amar smritiyaan
baanhen khole apne una puraane mitron se gale milne ko adheer ho rahi theen; magar vah duniyaa badal gayi thi. aisaa jee hot tha ki us dharatee se lipatakar rooon aur kahoon, tum mujhe
bhool gayi! main to ab bhi tumhara wahi roop dekhna chahta hoon.

sahasaa ek khulee jagah mein maine do-teen ladakon ko gullee-dandaa khelate dekh. ek pal ke lie main apne ko bilkul bhool gaya. bhool gaya ki main ek oonchaa aphasar hoon, saahabee
thaath mein, raub aur adhikar ke aavaran mein.

jaakar ek ladake se poochhaa-kyon bete, yahan koi gaya naam kaa aadamee rehta hai?

ek ladake ne gullee-dandaa sametakar sahame hue aavaaz mein kahaa-kaun gaya? gaya chamaar?

maine yon hi kahaa-haan-haan wahi. gaya naam kaa koi aadamee hai to? shaayad wahi ho.

'haan, hai to.'

'zara use bulaa sakate ho?'

ladakaa daudataa hua gaya aur ek pal mein ek paanch haath ke kaale dev ko saath lie aat dikhaaee diya. main door se hi pahachaan gaya. uski ora lapakanaa chahta tha ki usake gale lipat
jaaoon, par kuchh sochakar raha gaya. bol-kaho gaya, mujhe pahachaanate ho?

gaya ne jhukakar salaam kiya-haan maalik, bhalaa pahachaanoongaa kyon nahin! aap maje mein ho?

'bahut maje mein. tum apni kaho.'

'diptee sahab kaa saaees hoon.'

'mataee, mohan, durgaa sab kahan hain? kuchh khabar hai?

'mataee to mar gaya, durgaa aur mohan donon daakiyaa ho gae hain. aap?'

'main to jile kaa injeeniyar hoon.'

'sarakaar to pahale hi bade jaheen the?

'ab kabhi gullee-dandaa khelate ho?'

gaya ne meri ora sawal-bharee onkhon se dekh-ab gullee-dandaa kya kheloongaa sarakaar, ab to dhandhe se chhuttee nahin milatee.

'aao, aaj ham-tum khelen. tum padaanaa, ham padenge. tumhara ek daanv hamaare oopar hai. vah aaj le lo.'

gaya badi mushkil se raajee hua. vah thaharaa take kaa majadoor, main ek bada aphasar. hamaaraa aur uska kya jod? bechaaraa jhenp raha tha. lekin mujhe bhi kuchh kam jhenp na thi;
isalie nahin ki main gaya ke saath khelne jaa raha tha, balki isalie ki log is khel ko ajoobaa samajhakar isakaa tamaashaa banaa lenge aur achchhi-khaasee bheed lag jaaegee. us bheed mein vah
khushi kahan rahegaa, par khele bagair to raha nahin jat. aakhir phaisalaa hua ki donon jane bastee se bahut door khelenge aur bachpan ki us mithaaee ko khoob ras le-lekar khaaenge.
main gaya ko lekar daakabangale par aay aur motar mein baithakar donon maidaan ki ora chale. saath mein ek kulhaadee le lee. main ganbheer bhaav dhaaran kie hue tha, lekin gaya ise abhi
tak majaak hi samajh raha tha. phir bhi usake munh par utsukataa yaa khushi kaa koi chihn na tha. shaayad vah ham donon mein jo antar ho gaya tha, yahi sochne mein magan tha.

maine poochhaa-tumhen kabhi hamaaree yaad aatee thi gaya? sach kehna.

gaya jhenpataa hua bol-main aapako yaad kart hajoor, kis laayak hoon. bhaag mein aapake saath kuchh din khelna bada tha; nahin meri kya ginatee?

maine kuchh udaas hokar kahaa-lekin mujhe to baraabar, tumhari yaad aatee thi. tumhara vah dandaa, jo tumne taanakar jamaayaa tha, yaad hai na?

gaya ne pachhataate hue kahaa-vah ladakapan tha sarakaar, uski yaad na dilaao.

'vaah! vah mere baal-jeevan ki sabase raseelee yaad hai. tumhaare us dande mein jo ras tha, vah to ab na aadar-izzat mein paataa hoon, na paisaa mein.'

itanee der mein ham bastee se koi teen meel nikal aaye. chaaron taraph sannaataa hai. pashchim ora koson tak bheemataal phailaa hua hai, jahaan aakar ham kisee samay kamal pushp tod le jaate
the aur usake jhoomak banaakar kaanon mein daal lete the. jeth ki shaam kesar mein doobee chalee aa rahi hai. main lapakakar ek ped par chadh gaya aur ek tahanee kaat laayaa. chatapat
gullee-dandaa ban gaya.

khel shuru ho gaya. maine guchchee mein gullee rakhakar uchhaalee. gullee gaya ke saamane se nikal gayi. usane haath lapakaayaa, jaise machhalee pakad raha ho. gullee usake peechhe jaakar
giree. yah wahi gaya hai, jisake haathon mein gullee jaise aap hi aakar baith jati thi. vah daahine-baaen kaheen ho, gullee uski hathelee mein hi pahunchti thi. jaise gulliyon par
vasheekaran daal detaa ho. nayee gullee, puraanee gullee, chhoti gullee, badi gullee, nokadaar gullee, sapaat gullee sabhi usse mil jati thi. jaise usake haathon mein koi
chumbak ho, gulliyon ko kheench letaa ho; lekin aaj gullee ko usse vah pyaar nahin raha. phir to maine padaanaa shuru kiya. main tarah-tarah ki dhaandhaliyaan kar raha tha.
abhyaas ki kasar beeemaanee se pooree kar raha tha. huch jaane par bhi dandaa khele jat tha. haalaanki shaastr ke anusaar gaya ki baaree aanee chaahie thi. gullee par ochhee chot padatee
aur vah zara door par gir padatee, to main jhapatakar use khud uthaa letaa aur dobaaraa taand lagaataa. gaya yah sari be-kaayadagiyaan dekh raha tha; par kuchh na bolta tha, jaise use vah sab
kaayade-kanoon bhool gae. uska nishaanaa kitanaa achook tha. gullee usake haath se nikalakar tan se dande se aakar lagti thi. usake haath se chhootakar uska kaam tha dande se takaraa jan,
lekin aaj vah gullee dande mein lagti hi nahin! kabhi daahine jati hai, kabhi baaen, kabhi aage, kabhi peechhe.

aadh ghante padaane ke baad ek gullee dande mein aa lagee. maine dhaandhalee ki-gullee dande mein nahin lagee. bilkul paas se gayi; lekin lagee nahin.

gaya ne kisee tarah kaa asantosh prakat nahin kiya.

'na lagee hogee.'

'dande mein lagti to kya main beeemaanee kart?'

'nahin bhaiyaa, tum bhalaa beeemaanee karoge?'

bachpan mein majaal tha ki main aisaa ghapalaa karake jeetaa bachta! yahi gaya gardan par chadh baithta, lekin aaj main use kitanee aasaanee se dhokhaa die chalaa jat tha. gadhaa hai! sari baaten
bhool gaya.

sahasaa gullee phir dande se lagee aur itanee jor se lagee, jaise bandook chhootee ho. is pramaan ke saamane ab kisee tarah ki dhaandhalee karne kaa saahas mujhe is vakt bhi na ho sakaa,
lekin kyon na ek baar sabako jhooth bataane ki cheshtaa karoon? mera haraj ki kya hai. maan gaya to vaah-vaah, nahin do-chaar haath padanaa hi to padegaa. andheraa kaa bahaanaa karake
jaldee se chhudaa loongaa. phir kaun daanv dene aat hai.

gaya ne vijay ke ullaas mein kahaa-lag gayi, lag gayi. tan se bolee.

maine anajaan banane ki cheshtaa karake kahaa-tumne lagte dekh? maine to nahin dekh.

'tan se bolee hai sarakaar!'

'aur jo kisee eent se takaraa gayi ho?

mere munh se yah vaaky us samay kaise nikalaa, isakaa mujhe khud hairaanee hai. is saty ko jhuthalaanaa vaisaa hi tha, jaise din ko raat bataanaa. ham donon ne gullee ko dande mein
jor se lagte dekh tha; lekin gaya ne mera kathan sveekaar kar liya.

'haan, kisee eent mein hi lagee hogee. dande mein lagti to itanee aavaaj na aatee.'

maine phir padaanaa shuru kar diya; lekin itanee pratyaksh dhaandhalee kar lene ke baad gaya ki saralataa par mujhe dayaa aane lagee; iseelie jab teesaree baar gullee dande mein lagee, to
maine badi udaarataa se daanv dena tay kar liya.

gaya ne kahaa-ab to andheraa ho gaya hai bhaiyaa, kal par rakho.

maine sochaa, kal bahut-saa samay hogaa, yah na jaane kitanee der padaae, isalie isi vakt muaamalaa saaph kar lena achchha hogaa.

'nahin, nahin. abhi bahut ujaalaa hai. tum apna daanv le lo.'

'gullee soojhegee nahin.'

'kuchh paravaah nahin.'

gaya ne padaanaa shuru kiya; par use ab bilakul abhyaas na tha. usane do baar taand lagaane kaa iraadaa kiya; par donon hi baar huch gaya. ek minit se kam mein vah daanv kho
baithaa. maine apni dil ki vishaalataa kaa parichay diya.

'ek daanv aur khel lo. tum to pahale hi haath mein huch gae.'

'nahin bhaiyaa, ab andheraa ho gaya.'

'tumhara abhyaas chhoot gaya. kabhi khelate nahin?'

'khelne kaa samay kahan milataa hai bhaiyaa!'

ham donon motar par jaa baithe aur chiraag jalate-jalate padaav par pahunch gae. gaya chalte-chalte bol-kal yahan gullee-dandaa hogaa. sabhi puraane khilaadee khelenge. tum bhi aaoge? jab
tumhen phurasat ho, tabhee khilaadiyon ko bulaaoon.

maine shaam kaa samay diya aur doosare din maich dekhne gaya. koi das-das aadamiyon ki mandalee thi. kaee mere ladakapan ke saathee nikale! adhikaansh yuvak the, jinhen main pahachaan na
sakaa. khel shuru hua. main motar par baithaa-baithaa tamaashaa dekhne lagaa. aaj gaya kaa khel, uska naipuny dekhakar main chakit ho gaya. taand lagaataa, to gullee aasamaan se baaten
karti. kal ki-see vah jhijhak, vah hichakichaahat, vah bedilee aaj na thi. ladakapan mein jo baat thi, aaj usane praudhta praapt kar lee thi. kaheen kal isane mujhe is tarah padaayaa hot,
to main zaroor rone lagta. usake dande ki chot khaakar gullee do sau gaj ki khabar laatee thi.

padane vaalon mein ek yuvak ne kuchh dhaandhalee ki. usane apne soch mein gullee lapak lee thi. gaya kaa kehna tha-gullee jameen me lagakar uchhalee thi. is par donon mein taal thokane ki
naubat aaee hai. yuvak dab gaya. gaya kaa tamatamaayaa hua cheharaa dekhakar dar gaya. agar vah dab na jat, to zaroor maar-peet ho jati.

main khel mein na tha; par doosaron ke is khel mein mujhe wahi ladakapan kaa khushi aa raha tha, jab ham sab kuchh bhoolakar khel mein mast ho jaate the. ab mujhe maaloom hua ki kal gaya ne
mere saath khelaa nahin, keval khelne kaa bahaanaa kiya. usane mujhe dayaa kaa paatr samajhaa. maine dhaandhalee ki, beeemaanee ki, par use zara bhi gussaa na aay. isalie ki vah khel na
raha tha, mujhe khelaa raha tha, mera man rakh raha tha. vah mujhe padaakar mera kachoomar nahin nikaalanaa chahta tha. main ab aphasar hoon. yah aphasaree mere aur usake beech mein deevaar ban gayi
hai. main ab uska lihaaj paa sakataa hoon, adab paa sakataa hoon, saahachary nahin paa sakataa. ladakapan tha, tab main uska samakaksh tha. yah pad paakar ab main keval uski dayaa yogy hoon.
vah mujhe apna jod nahin samajhta. vah bada ho gaya hai, main chhota ho gaya hoon.
