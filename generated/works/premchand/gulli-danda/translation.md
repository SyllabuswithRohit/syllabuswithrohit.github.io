# Gulli Danda

**Munshi Premchand**

1
hamaare angrejee dost maanen yaa na maanen main to yahee kahoongaa ki gullee-dandaa sab khelon kaa raajaa hai| ab bhee kabhee ladakon ko gullee-dandaa khelate dekhataa hoon, to jee
lot-pot ho jat hai ki inake saath jaakar khelane lagoon| na laan kee jaroorat, na kort kee, na net kee, na thaapee kee| maje se kisee ped se ek tahanee kaat lee, gullee banaa lee, aur
do aadamee bhee aa jaae, to khel shuroo ho gaya|

vilaayatee khelon mein sabase badaa aib hai ki usake saamaan mahange hote hain| jab tak kam-se-kam ek saikadaa na kharch keejie, khilaadiyon mein shumaar hee nahin ho paataa| yahan
gullee-dandaa hai ki banaa harr-phitakaree ke chokhaa rang detaa hai; par ham angarejee cheejon ke peechhe aise deevaane ho rahe hain ki apanee sabhee cheejon se aroochi ho gaee| skoolon mein
harek ladake se teen-chaar roopaye saalaanaa keval khelane kee phees lee jati hai| kisee ko yah nahin soojhataa ki bhaarateey khel khilaaen, jo binaa daam-kaudee ke khele jaate hain|
angarejee khel unake lie hain, jinake paas paisaa hai| gareeb ladakon ke sir kyon yah vyasan madhate ho? theek hai, gullee se aankh phoot jaane kaa dar rahataa hai, to kyaa kriket se
sir phoot jaane, tillee phat jaane, taang toot jaane kaa dar nahin rahataa! agar hamaare maathe mein gullee kaa daag aaj tak banaa hua hai, to hamaare kaee dost aise bhee hain, jo thaapee
ko baisaakhee se badal baithe| yah apanee-apanee roochi hai| mujhe gullee hee sab khelon se achchhee lagatee hai aur bachpan kee meethee smritiyon mein gullee hee sabase meethee hai|

vah praat:kaal ghar se nikal jan, vah ped par chadhakar tahaniyaan kaatanaa aur gullee-dande banaanaa, vah utsaah, vah khilaadiyon ke jamaghate, vah padanaa aur padaanaa, vah ladaaee-jhagade,
vah aasaan svabhaav, jisse chhoot-achhoot, ameer-gareeb kaa bilkul bhed na rahataa tha, jisamen ameeraanaa chochalon kee, pradarshan kee, abhimaan kee gunjaaish hee na thi, yah usee vakt
bhoolegaa jab ... jab ...| gharavaale bigad rahe hain, pitaajee chauke par baithe veg se rotiyon par apanaa gussaa utaar rahe hain, ammaan kee daud keval dvaar tak hai, lekin unakee
soch-dhaaraa mein meraa andhakaaramay aane vaalaa samay tootee huee naukaa kee tarah dagamagaa raha hai; aur main hoon ki padaane mein mast hoon, na nahaane kee sudhi hai, na khaane kee| gullee hai to
jaraa-see, par usamen duniyaa-bhar kee mithaaiyon kee mithaas aur tamaashon kaa khushee bharaa hua hai|

mere hamajoliyon mein ek ladakaa gaya naam kaa tha| mujhse do-teen saal badaa hogaa| dubalaa, bandaron kee-see lambee-lambee, patalee-patalee ungaliyaan, bandaron kee-see chapalataa, vahee
jhallaahat| gullee kaisee hee ho, par is tarah lapakataa tha, jaise chhipakalee keedon par lapakatee hai| maaloom nahin, usake maan-baap the yaa nahin, kahan rahataa tha, kyaa khaataa tha; par tha
hamaare gullee-klab kaa chaimpiyan| jisakee taraph vah aa jaae, usakee jeet nishchit thi| ham sab use door se aate dekh, usakaa daudakar svaagat karte the aur apanaa goiyaan banaa lete
the|

ek din main aur gaya do hee khel rahe the| vah padaa raha tha| main pad raha tha, magar kuchh vichitr baat hai ki padaane mein ham din-bhar mast raha sakate hai; padanaa ek minat kaa bhee
akharataa hai| mainne galaa chhudaane ke lie sab chaalen chaleen, jo aise avasar par shaastr-vihit na hone par bhee kshamy hain, lekin gaya apanaa daanv lie bagair meraa pind na chhodataa
tha|

main ghar kee ora bhaagaa| anunay-vinay kaa koee asar na hua tha|

gaya ne mujhe daudakar pakad liya aur dandaa taanakar bol-meraa daanv dekar jaao| padaayaa to bade bahaadur banake, padane ke ber kyon bhaage jaate ho|

'tum din-bhar padaao to main din-bhar padataa rahoon?'

'haan, tumhen din-bhar padanaa padegaa|'

'na khaane jaaoon, na peene jaaoon?'

'haan! meraa daanv diye binaa kaheen nahin jaa sakate|'

'main tumhaaraa gulaam hoon?'

'haan, mere gulaam ho|'

'main ghar jat hoon, dekhoon meraa kyaa kar lete ho!'

'ghar kaise jaaoge; koee dillagee hai| daanv diya hai, daanv lenge|'

'achchhaa, kal mainne amarood khilaayaa tha| vah lautaa do|

'vah to pet mein chalaa gaya|'

'nikaalo pet se| tumane kyon khaayaa meraa amarood?'

'amarood tumane diya, tab mainne khaayaa| main tumse maangane na gaya tha|'

'jab tak meraa amarood na doge, main daanv na doongaa|'

main samajhataa tha, nyaay meree ora hai| aakhir mainne kisee svaarth se hee use amarood khilaayaa hogaa| kaun ni:svaarth kisee ke saath salook kart hai| bhikshaa tak to svaarth ke
lie dete hain| jab gaya ne amarood khaayaa, to phir use mujhse daanv lene kaa kyaa adhikaar hai? rishvat dekar to log khoon pachaa jaate hain, yah meraa amarood yon hee hajam kar
jaaegaa? amarood paise ke paanchavaale the, jo gaya ke baap ko bhee naseeb na honge| yah saraasar anyaay tha|

gaya ne mujhe apanee ora kheenchate hue kahaa-meraa daanv dekar jaao, amarood-samarood main nahin jaanataa|

mujhe nyaay kaa bal tha| vah anyaay par dataa hua tha| main haath chhudaakar bhaaganaa chaahataa tha| vah mujhe jaane na detaa! mainne use gaalee dee, usane usse kadee gaalee dee, aur
gaalee-hee nahin, ek chaantaa jamaa diya| mainne use daant kaat liya| usane meree peeth par dandaa jamaa diya| main rone lagaa! gaya mere is astr kaa mukaabalaa na kar sakaa| mainne
turant aansoo ponchh daale, dande kee chot bhool gaya aur hansataa hua ghar jaa pahunchaa! main thaanedaar kaa ladakaa ek neech jaat ke launde ke haathon pita gaya, yah mujhe us samay bhee
apamaanajanak maaloom haaa; lekin ghar mein kisee se shikaayat na kee|

2

unheen dinon pitaajee kaa wahan se tabaadalaa ho gaya| naee duniyaa dekhane kee khushee mein aisaa phoolaa ki apne hamajoliyon se bichhud jaane kaa bilakul du:kha na hua| pitaajee du:khee
the| vah badee aamadanee kee jagah thi| ammaanjee bhee du:khee theen yahan sab cheej sastee theen, aur muhalle kee striyon se gharaav-saa ho gaya tha, lekin main saare khushee ke phoolaa na
samaataa tha| ladakon mein jeet udaa raha tha, wahan aise ghar thode hee hote hain| aise-aise oonche ghar hain ki aasamaan se baaten karte hain| wahan ke angarejee skool mein koee
maastar ladakon ko peete, to use jehal ho jaae| mere mitron kee phailee huee aankhen aur chakit mudraa batalaa rahi thi ki mujhse unakee nigaah mein kitanee spardghaa ho rahi thi!
maano kah rahe the-too bhaagavaan ho bhai, jaao| hamen to isee oojad graam mein jeenaa bhee hai aur maranaa bhee|

bees saal gujar gae| mainne injeeniyaree paas kee aur usee jile kaa dauraa kart hua usee kasbe mein pahunchaa aur daakabangale mein thaharaa| us sthaan ko dekhate hee itanee madhur
baal-smritiyaan dil mein jaag utheen ki mainne chhadee uthaaee aur kasbe kee sair karne nikalaa| aankhen kisee pyaase pathik kee tarah bachpan ke una kreedaa-sthalon ko dekhane ke
lie vyaakul ho rahi theen; par us parichit naam ke sivaa wahan aur kuchh parichit na tha| jahaan khandahar tha, wahan pakke makaan khade the| jahaan baragad kaa puraanaa ped tha, wahan ab
ek sundar bageechaa tha| sthaan kee kaayaa palat ho gaee thi| agar usake naam aur sthiti kaa jnaan na hot, to main use pahachaan bhee na sakataa| bachpan kee sanchit aur amar smritiyaan
baanhen khole apne una puraane mitron se gale milane ko adheer ho rahi theen; magar vah duniyaa badal gaee thi| aisaa jee hot tha ki us dharatee se lipatakar rooon aur kahoon, tum mujhe
bhool gaee! main to ab bhee tumhaaraa vahee roop dekhanaa chaahataa hoon|

sahasaa ek khulee jagah mein mainne do-teen ladakon ko gullee-dandaa khelate dekh| ek pal ke lie main apne ko bilkul bhool gaya| bhool gaya ki main ek oonchaa aphasar hoon, saahabee
thaath mein, raub aur adhikaar ke aavaran mein|

jaakar ek ladake se poochhaa-kyon bete, yahan koee gaya naam kaa aadamee rahataa hai?

ek ladake ne gullee-dandaa sametakar sahame hue aavaaz mein kahaa-kaun gaya? gaya chamaar?

mainne yon hee kahaa-haan-haan vahee| gaya naam kaa koee aadamee hai to? shaayad vahee ho|

'haan, hai to|'

'jaraa use bulaa sakate ho?'

ladakaa daudataa hua gaya aur ek pal mein ek paanch haath ke kaale dev ko saath lie aat dikhaaee diya| main door se hee pahachaan gaya| usakee ora lapakanaa chaahataa tha ki usake gale lipat
jaaoon, par kuchh sochakar raha gaya| bol-kaho gaya, mujhe pahachaanate ho?

gaya ne jhukakar salaam kiya-haan maalik, bhalaa pahachaanoongaa kyon nahin! aap maje mein ho?

'bahut maje mein| tum apanee kaho|'

'diptee sahab kaa saaees hoon|'

'mataee, mohan, durgaa sab kahan hain? kuchh khabar hai?

'mataee to mar gaya, durgaa aur mohan donon daakiyaa ho gae hain| aap?'

'main to jile kaa injeeniyar hoon|'

'sarakaar to pahale hee bade jaheen the?

'ab kabhee gullee-dandaa khelate ho?'

gaya ne meree ora savaal-bharee onkhon se dekh-ab gullee-dandaa kyaa kheloongaa sarakaar, ab to dhandhe se chhuttee nahin milatee|

'aao, aaj ham-tum khelen| tum padaanaa, ham padenge| tumhaaraa ek daanv hamaare oopar hai| vah aaj le lo|'

gaya badee mushkil se raajee hua| vah thaharaa take kaa majadoor, main ek badaa aphasar| hamaaraa aur usakaa kyaa jod? bechaaraa jhenp raha tha| lekin mujhe bhee kuchh kam jhenp na thi;
isalie nahin ki main gaya ke saath khelane jaa raha tha, balki isalie ki log is khel ko ajoobaa samajhakar isakaa tamaashaa banaa lenge aur achchhee-khaasee bheed lag jaaegee| us bheed mein vah
khushee kahan rahegaa, par khele bagair to raha nahin jat| aakhir phaisalaa hua ki donon jane bastee se bahut door khelenge aur bachpan kee us mithaaee ko khoob ras le-lekar khaaenge|
main gaya ko lekar daakabangale par aay aur motar mein baithakar donon maidaan kee ora chale| saath mein ek kulhaadee le lee| main ganbheer bhaav dhaaran kie hue tha, lekin gaya ise abhee
tak majaak hee samajh raha tha| phir bhee usake munh par utsukataa yaa khushee kaa koee chihn na tha| shaayad vah ham donon mein jo antar ho gaya tha, yahee sochane mein magan tha|

mainne poochhaa-tumhen kabhee hamaaree yaad aatee thi gaya? sach kahanaa|

gaya jhenpataa hua bol-main aapako yaad kart hajoor, kis laayak hoon| bhaag mein aapake saath kuchh din khelanaa badaa tha; nahin meree kyaa ginatee?

mainne kuchh udaas hokar kahaa-lekin mujhe to baraabar, tumhaaree yaad aatee thi| tumhaaraa vah dandaa, jo tumane taanakar jamaayaa tha, yaad hai na?

gaya ne pachhataate hue kahaa-vah ladakapan tha sarakaar, usakee yaad na dilaao|

'vaah! vah mere baal-jeevan kee sabase raseelee yaad hai| tumhaare us dande mein jo ras tha, vah to ab na aadar-izzat mein paataa hoon, na paisaa mein|'

itanee der mein ham bastee se koee teen meel nikal aaye| chaaron taraph sannaataa hai| pashchim ora koson tak bheemataal phailaa hua hai, jahaan aakar ham kisee samay kamal pushp tod le jaate
the aur usake jhoomak banaakar kaanon mein daal lete the| jeth kee shaam kesar mein doobee chalee aa rahi hai| main lapakakar ek ped par chadh gaya aur ek tahanee kaat laayaa| chatapat
gullee-dandaa ban gaya|

khel shuroo ho gaya| mainne guchchee mein gullee rakhakar uchhaalee| gullee gaya ke saamane se nikal gaee| usane haath lapakaayaa, jaise machhalee pakad raha ho| gullee usake peechhe jaakar
giree| yah vahee gaya hai, jisake haathon mein gullee jaise aap hee aakar baith jati thi| vah daahine-baaen kaheen ho, gullee usakee hathelee mein hee pahunchatee thi| jaise gulliyon par
vasheekaran daal detaa ho| nayee gullee, puraanee gullee, chhotee gullee, badee gullee, nokadaar gullee, sapaat gullee sabhee usse mil jati thi| jaise usake haathon mein koee
chumbak ho, gulliyon ko kheench letaa ho; lekin aaj gullee ko usse vah pyaar nahin raha| phir to mainne padaanaa shuroo kiya| main tarah-tarah kee dhaandhaliyaan kar raha tha|
abhyaas kee kasar beeemaanee se pooree kar raha tha| huch jaane par bhee dandaa khele jat tha| haalaanki shaastr ke anusaar gaya kee baaree aanee chaahie thi| gullee par ochhee chot padatee
aur vah jaraa door par gir padatee, to main jhapatakar use khud uthaa letaa aur dobaaraa taand lagaataa| gaya yah saaree be-kaayadagiyaan dekh raha tha; par kuchh na bolataa tha, jaise use vah sab
kaayade-kaanoon bhool gae| usakaa nishaanaa kitanaa achook tha| gullee usake haath se nikalakar tan se dande se aakar lagatee thi| usake haath se chhootakar usakaa kaam tha dande se takaraa jan,
lekin aaj vah gullee dande mein lagatee hee nahin! kabhee daahine jati hai, kabhee baaen, kabhee aage, kabhee peechhe|

aadh ghante padaane ke baad ek gullee dande mein aa lagee| mainne dhaandhalee kee-gullee dande mein nahin lagee| bilkul paas se gaee; lekin lagee nahin|

gaya ne kisee tarah kaa asantosh prakat nahin kiya|

'na lagee hogee|'

'dande mein lagatee to kyaa main beeemaanee kart?'

'nahin bhaiyaa, tum bhalaa beeemaanee karoge?'

bachpan mein majaal tha ki main aisaa ghapalaa karake jeetaa bachataa! yahee gaya gardan par chadh baithataa, lekin aaj main use kitanee aasaanee se dhokhaa die chalaa jat tha| gadhaa hai! saaree baaten
bhool gaya|

sahasaa gullee phir dande se lagee aur itanee jor se lagee, jaise bandook chhootee ho| is pramaan ke saamane ab kisee tarah kee dhaandhalee karne kaa saahas mujhe is vakt bhee na ho sakaa,
lekin kyon na ek baar sabako jhooth bataane kee cheshtaa karoon? meraa haraj kee kyaa hai| maan gaya to vaah-vaah, nahin do-chaar haath padanaa hee to padegaa| andheraa kaa bahaanaa karake
jaldee se chhudaa loongaa| phir kaun daanv dene aat hai|

gaya ne vijay ke ullaas mein kahaa-lag gaee, lag gaee| tan se bolee|

mainne anajaan banane kee cheshtaa karake kahaa-tumane lagate dekh? mainne to nahin dekh|

'tan se bolee hai sarakaar!'

'aur jo kisee eent se takaraa gaee ho?

mere munh se yah vaaky us samay kaise nikalaa, isakaa mujhe khud hairaanee hai| is saty ko jhuthalaanaa vaisaa hee tha, jaise din ko raat bataanaa| ham donon ne gullee ko dande mein
jor se lagate dekh tha; lekin gaya ne meraa kathan sveekaar kar liya|

'haan, kisee eent mein hee lagee hogee| dande mein lagatee to itanee aavaaj na aatee|'

mainne phir padaanaa shuroo kar diya; lekin itanee pratyaksh dhaandhalee kar lene ke baad gaya kee saralataa par mujhe dayaa aane lagee; iseelie jab teesaree baar gullee dande mein lagee, to
mainne badee udaarataa se daanv denaa tay kar liya|

gaya ne kahaa-ab to andheraa ho gaya hai bhaiyaa, kal par rakho|

mainne sochaa, kal bahut-saa samay hogaa, yah na jaane kitanee der padaae, isalie isee vakt muaamalaa saaph kar lenaa achchhaa hogaa|

'nahin, nahin| abhee bahut ujaalaa hai| tum apanaa daanv le lo|'

'gullee soojhegee nahin|'

'kuchh paravaah nahin|'

gaya ne padaanaa shuroo kiya; par use ab bilakul abhyaas na tha| usane do baar taand lagaane kaa iraadaa kiya; par donon hee baar huch gaya| ek minit se kam mein vah daanv kho
baithaa| mainne apanee dil kee vishaalataa kaa parichay diya|

'ek daanv aur khel lo| tum to pahale hee haath mein huch gae|'

'nahin bhaiyaa, ab andheraa ho gaya|'

'tumhaaraa abhyaas chhoot gaya| kabhee khelate nahin?'

'khelane kaa samay kahan milataa hai bhaiyaa!'

ham donon motar par jaa baithe aur chiraag jalate-jalate padaav par pahunch gae| gaya chalate-chalate bol-kal yahan gullee-dandaa hogaa| sabhee puraane khilaadee khelenge| tum bhee aaoge? jab
tumhen phurasat ho, tabhee khilaadiyon ko bulaaoon|

mainne shaam kaa samay diya aur doosare din maich dekhane gaya| koee das-das aadamiyon kee mandalee thi| kaee mere ladakapan ke saathee nikale! adhikaansh yuvak the, jinhen main pahachaan na
sakaa| khel shuroo hua| main motar par baithaa-baithaa tamaashaa dekhane lagaa| aaj gaya kaa khel, usakaa naipuny dekhakar main chakit ho gaya| taand lagaataa, to gullee aasamaan se baaten
karti| kal kee-see vah jhijhak, vah hichakichaahat, vah bedilee aaj na thi| ladakapan mein jo baat thi, aaj usane praudhataa praapt kar lee thi| kaheen kal isane mujhe is tarah padaayaa hot,
to main jaroor rone lagataa| usake dande kee chot khaakar gullee do sau gaj kee khabar laatee thi|

padane vaalon mein ek yuvak ne kuchh dhaandhalee kee| usane apne soch mein gullee lapak lee thi| gaya kaa kahanaa tha-gullee jameen me lagakar uchhalee thi| is par donon mein taal thokane kee
naubat aaee hai| yuvak dab gaya| gaya kaa tamatamaayaa hua cheharaa dekhakar dar gaya| agar vah dab na jat, to jaroor maar-peet ho jati|

main khel mein na tha; par doosaron ke is khel mein mujhe vahee ladakapan kaa khushee aa raha tha, jab ham sab kuchh bhoolakar khel mein mast ho jaate the| ab mujhe maaloom hua ki kal gaya ne
mere saath khelaa nahin, keval khelane kaa bahaanaa kiya| usane mujhe dayaa kaa paatr samajhaa| mainne dhaandhalee kee, beeemaanee kee, par use jaraa bhee gussaa na aay| isalie ki vah khel na
raha tha, mujhe khelaa raha tha, meraa man rakh raha tha| vah mujhe padaakar meraa kachoomar nahin nikaalanaa chaahataa tha| main ab aphasar hoon| yah aphasaree mere aur usake beech mein deevaar ban gaee
hai| main ab usakaa lihaaj paa sakataa hoon, adab paa sakataa hoon, saahachary nahin paa sakataa| ladakapan tha, tab main usakaa samakaksh tha| yah pad paakar ab main keval usakee dayaa yogy hoon|
vah mujhe apanaa jod nahin samajhataa| vah badaa ho gaya hai, main chhotaa ho gaya hoon|
