# Doodh Ka Daam

**Munshi Premchand**

ab bade-bade shaharon mein daaiyaan, narsen aur ledee daaktar, sabhee paidaa ho gayee hain; lekin dehaaton mein jachchekhaanon par abhee tak bhanginon kaa hee prabhutv hai aur paas aane vaalaa samay mein isamen koee tabdeelee hone kee ummeed nahin| baaboo maheshanaath apne gaanv ke jameendaar the, shikshit the aur jachchekhaanon mein sudhaar kee aavashyakataa ko maanate the, lekin isamen jo baadhaaen theen, una par kaise vijay paate ? koee nars dehaat mein jaane par raajee na huee aur bahut kehne-sunane se raajee bhee huee, to itanee lambee-chaudee phees maangee ki baaboo sahab ko sir jhukaakar chale aane ke sivaa aur kuchh na soojhaa| ledee daaktar ke paas jaane kee unhen himmat na padee| usakee phees pooree karne ke lie to shaayad baaboo sahab ko apanee aadhee jaayadaad bechanee padatee; isalie jab teen kanyaaon ke baad vah chauthaa ladakaa paidaa hua, to phir vahee goodad tha aur vahee goodad kee bahoo| bachche aksar raat hee ko paidaa hote hain| ek din aadhee raat ko chaparaasee ne goodad ke dvaar par aisee haank lagaayee ki paas-pados mein bhee jaag pad gayee| ladakee na thi ki maree aavaaj se pukaarataa|
goodad ke ghar mein is shubh avasar ke lie maheenon se taiyaaree ho rahi thi| dar tha to yahee ki phir betee na ho jaay, nahin to vahee bandhaa hua ek rupayaa aur ek saadee milakar raha jaayagee| is vishay mein aurat-aadamee
mein kitanee hee baar jhagadaa ho chukaa tha, shart lag chukee thi| aurat kehti thi, 'agar abakee betaa na ho to munh na dikhaaoon; haan-haan, munh na dikhaaoon, saare lachchhan bete ke hain| aur goodad keht tha, 'dekh lenaa, betee hogee aur beech khet betee hogee| betaa nikale to moonchhen mundaa loon, haan-haan, moonchhen mudaa loon|' shaayad goodad samajhataa tha ki is tarah apanee aurat mein betaa-chaah ko balavaan karake vah bete kee avaaee ke lie raastaa saaph kar raha hai|
bhoongee bolee, 'ab moonchh mundaa le daadheejaar ! kehti thi, betaa hogaa| sunataa hee na tha| apanee hee rat lagaaye jat tha| main aaj teree moonchhen moonnoongee, khoontee tak to rakhoongee hee nahin|'
goodad ne kahaa, 'achchhaa moond lenaa bhaleemaanas ! moonchhen kyaa phir nikalengee hee nahin ? teesare din dekh lenaa, phir jyon-kee-tyon hain, magar jo kuchh milegaa,usamen aadhaa rakhaa loongaa, kahe detaa hoon|
bhoongee ne angoothaa dikhaayaa aur apne teen maheene ke ladakaa ko goodad ke supurd kar sipaahee ke saath chal khadee huee|
goodad ne pukaaraa, 'aree ! sun to, kahan bhaagee jati hai ? mujhe bhee badhaaee bajaane jan padegaa| ise kaun sanbhaalegaa ?'
bhoongee ne door hee se kahaa, 'ise vaheen dhaaratee par sulaa denaa| main aake doodh pilaa jaaoongee|'

maheshanaath ke yahan ab bhee bhoongee kee khoob khaatiradaariyaan hone lageen| sabere hareeraa milataa, dopahar ko pooriyaan aur halavaa, teesare pahar ko phir aur raat ko phir aur goodad ko bhee bharapoor parosaa milataa tha| bhoongee apne bachche ko din-raat mein ek-do baar se jyaadaa na pilaa sakatee thi| usake lie oopar ke doodh kaa intazaam tha| bhoongee kaa doodh baaboosaahab kaa bhaagyavaan ladakaa peetaa tha| aur yah silasilaa baarahaven din bhee na band hua| maalakin motee-taajee devee thi; par abakee kuchh aisaa sanyog ki unhen doodh hua hee nahin| teenon ladakiyon kee baar itane ipharaat se doodh hot tha ki ladakiyon ko badahajamee ho jati thi| ab kee ek boond nahin| bhoongee daaee bhee thi aur doodh-pilaaee bhee| maalakin kahateen 'bhoongee, hamaare bachche ko paal de, phir jab tak too jiye, baithee khaatee rahanaa| paanch beeghe maaphee dilavaa doongee| naatee-pote tak chain karenge| aur bhoongee kaa laadalaa oopar kaa doodh hajam na kar sakane ke vajah baar-baar ulatee kart aur din-din dubalaa hot jat tha|
bhoongee kehti, 'bahoojee, moondan mein choode loongee, kahe detee hoon|'
bahoojee, javaab deteen, 'haan haan, choode lenaa bhai, dhamakaatee kyon hai ? chaandee ke legee yaa sone ke|'
'vaah bahoojee! chaandee ke choode pahan ke kise munh dikhaaoongee aur kisakee hansee hogee ?'
'achchhaa, sone ke lenaa bhai, kah to diya|'
'aur byaah mein kanthaa loongee aur chaudharee (goodad) ke lie haathon ke tode|'
'vah bhee lenaa, bhagavaan vah din to dikhaave|'
ghar mein maalakin ke baad bhoongee kaa raajy tha| mahariyaan, maharaajin, naukar- chaakar sab usakaa rob maanate the| yahan tak ki khud bahoojee bhee usse dab jati theen| ek baar to usane maheshanaath ko bhee daantaa tha| hansakar taal gaye| baat chalee thi bhangiyon kee| maheshanaath ne kahaa, tha duniyaa mein aur chaahe jo kuchh ho jaay, bhangee bhangee hee rahenge| inhen aadamee banaanaa mushkil hai| is par bhoongee ne kahaa, tha maalik, bhangee to badon-badon ko aadamee banaate
hain, unhen koee kyaa aadamee banaaye| yah gustaakhee karake kisee doosare avasar par bhalaa bhoongee ke sir ke baal
bach sakate the ? lekin aaj baaboosaahab thathaakar hanse aur bole bhoongee baat bade pate kee kehti hai|
bhoongee kaa shaasanakaal saal-bhar se aage na chal sakaa| devataaon ne ladakaa ke bhangin kaa doodh peene par aapatti kee, moteraam shaastree to praayashchitt kaa prastaav kar baithe| doodh to chhudaa diya gaya; lekin praayashchitt kee baat hansee mein uda gayee| maheshanaath ne phatakaarakar kahaa, 'praayashchitt kee khoob kahee shaastreejee, kal tak usee bhangin kaa khoon peekar palaa, ab usamen chhoot ghus gayee| vaah re aapakaa dharm| shaastreejee shikhaa phatakaarakar bole yah saty hai,vah kal tak bhangin kaa rakt peekar palaa| maans khaakar palaa, yah bhee saty hai; lekin kal kee baat kal thi, aaj kee baat aaj| jagannaathapuree mein chhoot-achhoot sab ek pangat mein khaate hain; par yahan to nahin khaa sakate| beemaaree mein to ham bhee kapade pahane khaa lete hain, khichadee tak khaa lete hain baaboojee;lekin achchhe ho jaane par to nem kaa paalan karn hee padataa hai| aapaddharm kee baat nyaaree hai|
'to isakaa yah arth hai ki dharm badalataa rahataa hai kabhee kuchh, kabhee kuchh ?'
'aur kyaa ! raajaa kaa dharm alag, prajaa kaa dharm alag, ameer kaa dharm alag, gareeb kaa dharm alag, raaje-mahaaraaje jo chaahen khaayan, jisake saath chaahen khaayan, jisake saath chaahen shaadee-byaah karen, unake lie koee bandhan nahin| samarth aadamee hain| bandhan to madhyavaalon ke lie hai|'
praayashchitt to na hua; lekin bhoongee ko gaddee se utaranaa padaa ! haan, daan-dakshinaa itanee milee kee vah akele le na jaa sakee aur sone ke choode bhee mile| ek kee jagah do nayee, sundar saadiyaan maamoolee nainasukh kee nahin,
jaisee ladakiyon kee baar milee theen| isee saal pleg ne jor baandh aur goodad pahale hee chapet mein aa gaya| bhoongee
akelee raha gayee; par ghar-parivaar jyon-kee-tyon chalatee rahi| log taak lagaaye baithe the ki bhoongee ab gayee| phalaan bhangee se baatacheet huee, phalaan chaudharee aaye,lekin bhoongee na kaheen aayee, na kaheen gayee, yahan tak ki paanch saal beet gaye aur usakaa ladakaa mangal, durbal aur sadaa rogee rehne par bhee, daudane lagaa| suresh ke saamane piddee-saa lagataa tha|
ek din bhoongee maheshanaath ke ghar kaa paranaalaa saaph kar rahi thi| maheenon se galeej jamaa ho raha tha| aangan mein paanee bharaa rehne lagaa tha| paranaale mein ek lambaa motaa baans daalakar jor se hilaa rahi thi| pooraa daahinaa haath
paranaale ke andar tha ki ekaaek usane chillaakar haath baahar nikaal liya aur usee vakt ek kaalaa saanp paranaale se nikalakar bhaagaa| logon ne daudakar use maar to daalaa; lekin bhoongee ko na bachaa sake| samajhe, paanee kaa saanp hai, vishailaa na hogaa, isalie pahale kuchh gaphalat kee gayee| jab vish deh mein phail gaya aur laharen aane lageen, tab pataa chalaa ki vah paanee kaa saanp nahin, gehunvan tha|
mangal ab anaath tha| din-bhar maheshabaaboo ke dvaar par mandaraayaa kart| ghar mein joothan itanaa bachataa tha ki aise-aise das-paanch ladakaa pal sakate the| khaane kee koee kamee na thi| haan, use tab buraa jaroor lagataa tha, jab use
mittee ke kasoron mein oopar se khaanaa diya jat tha| sab log achchhe-achchhe baratanon mein khaate hain, usake lie mittee ke kasore ! yon use is bhedabhaav kaa bilakul jnaan na hot tha, lekin gaanv ke ladake chidhaa-chidhaakar usakaa beizzatee karte rahate the| koee use apne saath khelaataa bhee na tha| yahan tak ki jis taat par vah sotaa tha, vah bhee achhoot tha| makaan ke saamane ek neem kaa ped tha| isee ke neeche mangal kaa deraa tha| ek phataa-saa taat kaa tukadaa,do mittee ke kasore aur ek dhotee, jo suresh baaboo kee utaaran thi, jaadaa, garamee, barasaat harek mausam mein vah jagah ek-see aaraamadeh thi aur bhaagy kaa balee mangal jhulasatee huee loo, galate hue jaade aur moosalaadhaar varshaa mein bhee jindaa aur pahale se kaheen svasth tha| bas, usakaa koee apanaa tha, to gaanv kaa ek kuttaa, jo apne sahavargiyon ke julm se dukhee hokar mangal kee sharan aa padaa tha| donon ek hee khaanaa khaate, ek
hee taat par sote, tabiyat bhee donon kee ek-see thi aur donon ek-doosare ke svabhaav ko jaan gaye the| kabhee aapas mein jhagadaa na hot|
gaanv ke dhaarmaatmaa log baaboosaahab kee is udaarataa par hairaanee karte| theek dvaar ke saamane pachaas haath bhee na hogaa mangal kaa padaa rahanaa unhen solahon aane dharm-viruddh jaan padaa| chhi: ! yahee haal raha, to thode hee dinon
mein dharm kaa ant hee samajho| bhangee ko bhee bhagavaan ne hee rachaa hai, yah ham bhee jaanate hain| usake saath hamen kisee tarah kaa anyaay na karn chaahie,yah kise nahin maaloom ? bhagavaan kaa to naam hee patit-paavan hai; lekin samaaj kee maryaadaa bhee koee vastu hai ! us dvaar par jaate hue sankoch hot hai| gaanv ke maalik hain, jan to padataa hee hai; lekin bas yahee samajh lo ki napharat hoti hai|
mangal aur taamee mein gaharee banatee thi| mangal keht dekho bhai taamee, jaraa aur khisakakar soo| aakhir main kahan letoon ? saaraa taat to tumane gher liya| taamee koon-koon kart, dum hilaataa aur khisak jaane ke badale aur oopar chadh aat evan mangal kaa munh chaatane lagataa| shaam ko vah ek baar roj apanaa ghar dekhane aur thodee der rone jat| pahale saal phoos kaa chhappar gir padaa, doosare saal ek deevaar giree aur ab keval aadhee-aadhee deevaaren khadee theen, jinakaa ooparee bhaag nokadaar ho gaya tha| yahee use pyaar kee sampatti milee thi| vahee smriti, hee aakarshan, vahee pyaar use ek baar us oojad mein kheench le jat tha aur taamee hameshaa usake saath hot tha| mangal nokadaar deevaar par baith jat aur jeevan ke beete aur aanevaale svapn dekhane lagataa aur baar-baar uchhalakar usakee god mein baithane kee naakaam cheshtaa kart|
ek din kaee ladake khel rahe the| mangal bhee pahunchakar door khadaa ho gaya| yaa to suresh ko us par dayaa aayee, yaa khelanevaalon kee jodee pooree na padatee thi, kah nahin sakate| jo kuchh bhee ho, tajaveej kee ki aaj mangal ko bhee
khel mein shareek kar liya jaay| yahan kaun dekhane aat hai|
'kyon re mangal, khelegaa|'
mangal bol, 'naa bhaiyaa, kaheen maalik dekh len, to meree chamadee udhod dee jaay| tumhen kyaa, tum to alag ho jaaoge|'
suresh ne kahaa, 'to yahan kaun aat hai dekhane be ? chal, ham log savaar-savaar khelenge| too ghodaa banegaa, ham log tere oopar savaaree karake daudaayenge ?'
mangal ne shankaa kee, 'main baraabar ghodaa hee rahoongaa, ki savaaree bhee karoongaa ?yah bataa do|'
yah savaal tedhaa tha| kisee ne is par soch na kiya tha| suresh ne ek pal soch karake kahaa, 'tujhe kaun apanee peeth par bithaayegaa, soch ?'
'aakhir too bhangee hai ki nahin ?'
mangal bhee kadaa ho gaya| bol, 'main kab keht hoon ki main bhangee nahin hoon, lekin tumhen meree hee maan ne apanaa doodh pilaakar paalaa hai| jab tak mujhe bhee savaaree karne ko na milegee, main ghodaa na banoongaa| tum log bade chaghad ho|
aap to maje se savaaree karoge aur main ghodaa hee banaa rahoon|'
suresh ne daantakar kahaa,'tujhe ghodaa bananaa padegaa' aur mangal ko pakadane daudaa| mangal bhaagaa| suresh ne daudaayaa| mangal ne kadam aur tej kiya| suresh ne bhee jor lagaayaa; magar vah bahut khaa-khaakar thul-thul ho gaya tha| aur daudane mein usakee saans phoolane lagatee thi| aakhir usane rukakar kahaa, 'aakar ghodaa bano mangal, nahin to kabhee paa jaaoongaa, to buree tarah peetoongaa|'
'tumhen bhee ghodaa bananaa padegaa|'
'achchhaa ham bhee ban jaayange|'
'tum peechhe se nikal jaaoge| pahale tum ghodaa ban jaao| main savaaree kar loon, phir main banoongaa|'
suresh ne sachamuch chakamaa denaa chaahaa tha| mangal kaa yah mutaalabaa sunakar saathiyon se bol, 'dekhate ho isakee badamaashee, bhangee hai na !'
teenon ne mangal ko gher liya aur use jabaradastee ghodaa banaa diya| suresh ne chatapat usakee peeth par aasan jamaa liya aur tikatik karake, 'bol,chal ghode, chal !
mangal kuchh der tak to chalaa, lekin us bojh se usakee kamar tootee jati thi| usane dheere se peeth sikodee aur suresh kee raan ke neeche se sarak gaya| suresh mahoday lad se gir pade aur bhonpoo bajaane lage| maan ne sunaa, suresh kaheen ro raha hai| suresh kaheen roye, to unake tej kaanon mein jaroor bhanak pad jati thi aur usakaa ronaa bhee bilakul niraalaa hot tha, jaise chhotee laain ke injan kee aavaaj| maharee se bolee, 'dekh to, suresh kaheen ro raha hai, poochh to kisane maaraa hai|'

itane mein suresh khud aankhen malataa hua aay| use jab rone kaa avasar
milataa tha, to maan ke paas phariyaad lekar jaroor aat tha| maan mithaaee yaa meve dekar aansoo ponchh detee theen| aap the to aath saal ke, magar the bilakul gaavadee| had se jyaadaa pyaar ne usakee samajh ke saath vahee kiya tha, jo had se jyaadaa khaanaa ne usakee deh ke saath|
maan ne poochhaa, 'kyon rotaa hai suresh, kisane maaraa ?'
suresh ne rokar kahaa, 'mangal ne chhoo diya|'
maan ko yakeen na aay| mangal itanaa nireeh tha ki usse kisee tarah kee sharaarat kee shankaa na hoti thi; lekin jab suresh kasamen khaane lagaa, to yakeen karn laajim ho gaya| mangal ko bulaakar daantaa, 'kyon re mangal, ab tujhe badamaashee soojhane lagee| mainne tujhase kahaa, tha, suresh ko kabhee mata chhoonaa, yaad hai ki nahin, bol|'
mangal ne dabee aavaaj se kahaa, 'yaad kyon nahin hai|'
'to phir toone use kyon chhuaa ?'
'mainne nahin chhuaa|'
'toone nahin chhuaa, to vah rotaa kyon tha ?'
'gir pade, isase rone lage|'
choree aur seenaajoree| deveejee daant peesakar raha gayeen| maarateen, to usee dam snaan karn padataa| chhadee to haath mein lenee hee padatee aur chhoot kaa vidyut-pravaah is chhadee ke raaste unakee deh mein paivast ho jat, isalie jahaan
tak gaaliyaan de sakeen, deen aur hukm diya ki -'abhee-abhee yahan se nikal jaa| phir jo is dvaar par teree soorat najar aayee, to khoon hee pee jaaoongee| mupht kee rotiyaan khaa-khaakar sharaarat soojhatee hai,' aadi|
mangal mein gairat to kyaa thi, haan, dar tha| chupake se apne sakore uthaaye, taat kaa tukadaa bagal mein dabaayaa, dhotee kandho par rakhee aur rotaa hua wahan se chal padaa| ab vah yahan kabhee na aayegaa| yahee to hogaa ki bhookhon mar
jaayegaa| kyaa haraj hai ? is tarah jeene se phaayadaa hee kyaa ? gaanv mein usake lie aur kahan thikaanaa tha ? bhangee ko kaun panaah detaa ? usee khandahar kee ora chalaa, jahaan bhale dinon kee smritiyaan usake aansoo ponchh sakatee theen aur khoob phoot-phootakar royaa| usee pal taamee bhee use dhoondhataa hua pahunchaa aur donon phir apanee vyathaa
bhool gaye|
lekin jyon-jyon din kaa prakaash ksheen hot jat tha, mangal kee pachhataavaa bhee ksheen hoti jati thi| bachpan ko bechain karne vaalee bhookh deh kaa rakt pee-peekar aur bhee balavaan hoti jati thi| aankhen baar-baar kasoron kee ora uth jaateen| wahan ab tak suresh kee joothee mithaaiyaan mil gayee hoteen| yahan kyaa dhool phaanke ? usane taamee se salaah kee Kaaoge kyaa taamee ? main to bhookhaa let rahoongaa| taamee ne koon-koon karake shaayad kahaa, 'is tarah kaa beizzatee to jindagee bhar sahanaa hai| yon himmat haaroge, to kaise kaam chalegaa ? mujhe dekho na, kabhee
kisee ne dandaa maaraa, chillaa uthaa, phir jaraa der baad dum hilaataa hua usake paas jaa pahunchaa| ham-tum donon iseelie bane hain, bhai !' mangal ne kahaa,'to tum jaao, jo kuchh mile khaa lo, meree paravaah na karo|'
taamee ne apanee shvaan-bhaashaa mein kahaa, 'akelaa nahin jat, tumhen saath lekar chaloongaa|'
'main nahin jat|'
'to main bhee nahin jat|'
'bhookhon mar jaaoge|'
'to kyaa tum jeete rahoge ?'
'meraa kaun baithaa hai, jo royegaa ?'
'yahan bhee vahee haal hai bhai, kvaar mein jis kutiyaa se pyaar kiya tha, usane bevaphaaee kee aur ab kalloo ke saath hai| khairiyat yahee huee ki apne bachche letee gayee, nahin to meree jaan gaadhe mein pad jati| paanch-paanch bachchon ko
kaun paalataa ?'
ek pal ke baad bhookh ne ek doosaree yukti soch nikaalee| 'maalakin hamen khoj rahi hongee, kyaa taamee ?'
'aur kyaa ? baaboojee aur suresh khaa chuke honge| kahaar ne unakee thaalee se joothan nikaal liya hogaa aur hamen pukaar raha hogaa|'
'baaboojee aur suresh donon kee thaaliyon mein ghee khoob rahataa hai aur vah meethee-meethee cheej haan malaaee|'
'sab-kaa-sab ghoore par daal diya jaayagaa|'
'dekhen, hamen khojane koee aat hai ?'
'khojane kaun aayegaa; kyaa koee purohit ho ? ek baar 'mangal-mangal' hogaa aur bas, thaalee paranaale mein undel dee jaayegee|'
'achchhaa, to chalo chalen| magar main chhipaa rahoongaa, agar kisee ne meraa naam lekar na pukaaraa; to main laut aaoongaa| yah samajh lo|'
donon wahan se nikale aur aakar maheshanaath ke dvaar par andhere mein dabakakar khade ho gaye; magar taamee ko sabr kahan ? vah dheere se andar ghus gaya| dekh, maheshanaath aur suresh thaalee par baith gaye hain| barothe mein dheere se baith gaya, magar dar raha tha ki koee dandaa na maar de| naukar mein baatacheet ho rahi thi| ek ne kahaa, 'aaj mangalavaa nahin dikhaayee detaa| maalakin ne daantaa tha, isase bhaagaa hai saait|'
doosare ne javaab diya, 'achchhaa hua, nikaal diya gaya| sabere-sabere bhangee kaa munh dekhanaa padataa tha|
mangal aur andhere mein khisak gaya| ummeed gahare jal mein doob gayee| maheshanaath thaalee se uth gaye| naukar haath dhulaa raha hai| ab hukkaa peeyenge aur soyenge| suresh apanee maan ke paas baithaa koee kahaa,nee sunataa-sunataa
so jaayagaa ! gareeb mangal kee kise phikr hai ? itanee der ho gayee, kisee ne bhool se bhee na pukaaraa|
kuchh der tak vah niraash-saa wahan khadaa raha, phir ek lambee saans kheenchakar jan hee chaahataa tha ki kahaar pattal mein thaalee kaa joothan le jat najar aay| mangal andhere se nikalakar prakaash mein aa gaya| ab man ko kaise roke ?
kahaar ne kahaa, 'are, too yahan tha ? hamane samajhaa ki kaheen chalaa gaya| le, khaa le; main phenkane le jaa raha tha|'
mangal ne deenataa se kahaa, 'main to badee der se yahan khadaa tha|'
'to bol, kyon nahin ?'
'maare dar ke|'
'achchhaa, le khaa le|'
usane pattal ko oopar uthaakar mangal ke phaile hue haathon mein daal diya| mangal ne usakee ora aisee aankhon se dekh, jisamen deen kritajnataa bharee huee thi| taamee bhee andar se nikal aay tha| donon vaheen neem ke neeche pattal
mein khaane lage| mangal ne ek haath se taamee kaa sir sahalaakar kahaa, 'dekh, pet kee aag aisee hoti hai ! yah laat kee maaree huee rotiyaan bhee na milateen,to kyaa karte ?' taamee ne dum hilaa dee|
'suresh ko ammaan ne paalaa tha|'
taamee ne phir dum hilaayee|
'log kehte hain, doodh kaa daam koee nahin chukaa sakataa aur mujhe doodh kaa yah daam mil raha hai|'
taamee ne phir dum hilaayee|
