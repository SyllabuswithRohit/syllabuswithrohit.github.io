# Doodh Ka Daam

**Munshi Premchand**

ab bade-bade shaharon mein daaiyaan, narsen aur ledee daaktar, sabhi paidaa ho gayi hain; lekin dehaaton mein jachchekhaanon par abhi tak bhanginon kaa hi prabhutv hai aur paas aane vaalaa samay mein isamen koi tabdeelee hone ki ummeed nahin. baaboo maheshanaath apne gaanv ke jameendaar the, shikshit the aur jachchekhaanon mein sudhaar ki aavashyakataa ko maanate the, lekin isamen jo baadhaaen theen, una par kaise vijay paate? koi nars dehaat mein jaane par raajee na hui aur bahut kehne-sunane se raajee bhi hui, to itanee lambee-chaudee phees maangee ki baaboo sahab ko sir jhukaakar chale aane ke sivaa aur kuchh na soojhaa. ledee daaktar ke paas jaane ki unhen himmat na padee. uski phees pooree karne ke lie to shaayad baaboo sahab ko apni aadhee jaayadaad bechanee padatee; isalie jab teen kanyaaon ke baad vah chauthaa ladakaa paidaa hua, to phir wahi goodad tha aur wahi goodad ki bahoo. bachche aksar raat hi ko paidaa hote hain. ek din aadhee raat ko chaparaasee ne goodad ke dvaar par aisee haank lagaayee ki paas-pados mein bhi jaag pad gayi. ladakee na thi ki maree aavaaj se pukaarataa.
goodad ke ghar mein is shubh avasar ke lie maheenon se taiyaaree ho rahi thi. dar tha to yahi ki phir betee na ho jaay, nahin to wahi bandhaa hua ek rupayaa aur ek saadee milakar raha jaayagee. is vishay mein aurat-aadamee
mein kitanee hi baar jhagadaa ho chukaa tha, shart lag chukee thi. aurat kehti thi, 'agar abakee betaa na ho to munh na dikhaaoon; haan-haan, munh na dikhaaoon, saare lachchhan bete ke hain. aur goodad keht tha, 'dekh lena, betee hogee aur beech khet betee hogee. betaa nikale to moonchhen mundaa loon, haan-haan, moonchhen mudaa loon.' shaayad goodad samajhta tha ki is tarah apni aurat mein betaa-chaah ko balavaan karake vah bete ki avaaee ke lie raastaa saaph kar raha hai.
bhoongee bolee, 'ab moonchh mundaa le daadheejaar! kehti thi, betaa hogaa. sunataa hi na tha. apni hi rat lagaaye jat tha. main aaj teri moonchhen moonnoongee, khoontee tak to rakhoongee hi nahin.'
goodad ne kahaa, 'achchha moond lena bhaleemaanas! moonchhen kya phir nikalengee hi nahin? teesare din dekh lena, phir jyon-ki-tyon hain, magar jo kuchh milegaa,usamen aadhaa rakhaa loongaa, kahe detaa hoon.
bhoongee ne angoothaa dikhaayaa aur apne teen mahine ke ladakaa ko goodad ke supurd kar sipaahee ke saath chal khadee hui.
goodad ne pukaaraa, 'aree! sun to, kahan bhaagee jati hai? mujhe bhi badhaaee bajaane jan padegaa. ise kaun sanbhaalegaa?'
bhoongee ne door hi se kahaa, 'ise vaheen dhaaratee par sulaa dena. main aake doodh pilaa jaaoongee.'

maheshanaath ke yahan ab bhi bhoongee ki khoob khaatiradaariyaan hone lageen. sabere hareeraa milataa, dopahar ko pooriyaan aur halavaa, teesare pahar ko phir aur raat ko phir aur goodad ko bhi bharapoor parosaa milataa tha. bhoongee apne bachche ko din-raat mein ek-do baar se zyada na pilaa sakatee thi. usake lie oopar ke doodh kaa intazaam tha. bhoongee kaa doodh baaboosaahab kaa bhaagyavaan ladakaa peetaa tha. aur yah silasilaa baarahaven din bhi na band hua. maalakin motee-taajee devee thi; par abakee kuchh aisaa sanyog ki unhen doodh hua hi nahin. teenon ladakiyon ki baar itane ipharaat se doodh hot tha ki ladakiyon ko badahajamee ho jati thi. ab ki ek boond nahin. bhoongee daaee bhi thi aur doodh-pilaaee bhi. maalakin kahateen 'bhoongee, hamaare bachche ko paal de, phir jab tak too jiye, baithee khaatee rehna. paanch beeghe maaphee dilavaa doongee. naatee-pote tak chain karenge. aur bhoongee kaa laadalaa oopar kaa doodh hajam na kar sakane ke vajah baar-baar ulatee kart aur din-din dubalaa hot jat tha.
bhoongee kehti, 'bahoojee, moondan mein choode loongee, kahe detee hoon.'
bahoojee, jawab deteen, 'haan haan, choode lena bhai, dhamakaatee kyon hai? chaandee ke legee yaa sone ke.'
'vaah bahoojee! chaandee ke choode pahan ke kise munh dikhaaoongee aur kisakee hansee hogee?'
'achchha, sone ke lena bhai, kah to diya.'
'aur byaah mein kanthaa loongee aur chaudharee (goodad) ke lie haathon ke tode.'
'vah bhi lena, bhagavaan vah din to dikhaave.'
ghar mein maalakin ke baad bhoongee kaa raajy tha. mahariyaan, maharaajin, naukar- chaakar sab uska rob maanate the. yahan tak ki khud bahoojee bhi usse dab jati theen. ek baar to usane maheshanaath ko bhi daantaa tha. hansakar taal gaye. baat chalee thi bhangiyon ki. maheshanaath ne kahaa, tha duniyaa mein aur chaahe jo kuchh ho jaay, bhangee bhangee hi rahenge. inhen aadamee banaanaa mushkil hai. is par bhoongee ne kahaa, tha maalik, bhangee to badon-badon ko aadamee banaate
hain, unhen koi kya aadamee banaaye. yah gustaakhee karake kisee doosare avasar par bhalaa bhoongee ke sir ke baal
bach sakate the? lekin aaj baaboosaahab thathaakar hanse aur bole bhoongee baat bade pate ki kehti hai.
bhoongee kaa shaasanakaal saal-bhar se aage na chal sakaa. devataaon ne ladakaa ke bhangin kaa doodh peene par aapatti ki, moteraam shaastree to praayashchitt kaa prastaav kar baithe. doodh to chhudaa diya gaya; lekin praayashchitt ki baat hansee mein uda gayi. maheshanaath ne phatakaarakar kahaa, 'praayashchitt ki khoob kahin shaastreejee, kal tak usi bhangin kaa khoon peekar palaa, ab usamen chhoot ghus gayi. vaah re aapakaa dharm. shaastreejee shikhaa phatakaarakar bole yah saty hai,vah kal tak bhangin kaa rakt peekar palaa. maans khaakar palaa, yah bhi saty hai; lekin kal ki baat kal thi, aaj ki baat aaj. jagannaathapuree mein chhoot-achhoot sab ek pangat mein khaate hain; par yahan to nahin khaa sakate. beemaaree mein to ham bhi kapade pahane khaa lete hain, khichadee tak khaa lete hain baaboojee;lekin achchhe ho jaane par to nem kaa paalan karn hi padataa hai. aapaddharm ki baat nyaaree hai.
'to isakaa yah arth hai ki dharm badalataa rehta hai kabhi kuchh, kabhi kuchh?'
'aur kya! raajaa kaa dharm alag, prajaa kaa dharm alag, ameer kaa dharm alag, gareeb kaa dharm alag, raaje-mahaaraaje jo chaahen khaayan, jisake saath chaahen khaayan, jisake saath chaahen shaadee-byaah karen, unake lie koi bandhan nahin. samarth aadamee hain. bandhan to madhyavaalon ke lie hai.'
praayashchitt to na hua; lekin bhoongee ko gaddee se utaranaa padaa! haan, daan-dakshinaa itanee milee ki vah akele le na jaa sakee aur sone ke choode bhi mile. ek ki jagah do nayee, sundar saadiyaan maamoolee nainasukh ki nahin,
jaisee ladakiyon ki baar milee theen. isi saal pleg ne jor baandh aur goodad pahale hi chapet mein aa gaya. bhoongee
akelee raha gayi; par ghar-parivaar jyon-ki-tyon chalti rahi. log taak lagaaye baithe the ki bhoongee ab gayi. phalaan bhangee se baatacheet hui, phalaan chaudharee aaye,lekin bhoongee na kaheen aayee, na kaheen gayi, yahan tak ki paanch saal beet gaye aur uska ladakaa mangal, durbal aur sadaa rogee rehne par bhi, daudane lagaa. suresh ke saamane piddee-saa lagta tha.
ek din bhoongee maheshanaath ke ghar kaa paranaalaa saaph kar rahi thi. maheenon se galeej jamaa ho raha tha. aangan mein paani bharaa rehne lagaa tha. paranaale mein ek lambaa motaa baans daalakar jor se hilaa rahi thi. pooraa daahinaa haath
paranaale ke andar tha ki ekaaek usane chillaakar haath baahar nikaal liya aur usi vakt ek kaalaa saanp paranaale se nikalakar bhaagaa. logon ne daudakar use maar to daalaa; lekin bhoongee ko na bachaa sake. samajhe, paani kaa saanp hai, vishailaa na hogaa, isalie pahale kuchh gaphalat ki gayi. jab vish deh mein phail gaya aur laharen aane lageen, tab pataa chalaa ki vah paani kaa saanp nahin, gehunvan tha.
mangal ab anaath tha. din-bhar maheshabaaboo ke dvaar par mandaraayaa kart. ghar mein joothan itanaa bachta tha ki aise-aise das-paanch ladakaa pal sakate the. khaane ki koi kamee na thi. haan, use tab buraa zaroor lagta tha, jab use
mittee ke kasoron mein oopar se khaanaa diya jat tha. sab log achchhe-achchhe baratanon mein khaate hain, usake lie mittee ke kasore! yon use is bhedabhaav kaa bilakul jnaan na hot tha, lekin gaanv ke ladake chidhaa-chidhaakar uska beizzatee karte rehte the. koi use apne saath khelaataa bhi na tha. yahan tak ki jis taat par vah sotaa tha, vah bhi achhoot tha. makaan ke saamane ek neem kaa ped tha. isi ke neeche mangal kaa deraa tha. ek phta-saa taat kaa tukadaa,do mittee ke kasore aur ek dhotee, jo suresh baaboo ki utaaran thi, jaadaa, garamee, barasaat harek mausam mein vah jagah ek-see aaraamadeh thi aur bhaagy kaa balee mangal jhulasatee hui loo, galate hue jaade aur moosalaadhaar varshaa mein bhi jindaa aur pahale se kaheen svasth tha. bas, uska koi apna tha, to gaanv kaa ek kuttaa, jo apne sahavargiyon ke julm se dukhee hokar mangal ki sharan aa padaa tha. donon ek hi khaanaa khaate, ek
hi taat par sote, tabiyat bhi donon ki ek-see thi aur donon ek-doosare ke svabhaav ko jaan gaye the. kabhi aapas mein jhagadaa na hot.
gaanv ke dhaarmaatmaa log baaboosaahab ki is udaarataa par hairaanee karte. theek dvaar ke saamane pachaas haath bhi na hogaa mangal kaa padaa rehna unhen solahon aane dharm-viruddh jaan padaa. chhi:! yahi haal raha, to thode hi dinon
mein dharm kaa ant hi samajho. bhangee ko bhi bhagavaan ne hi rachaa hai, yah ham bhi jaanate hain. usake saath hamen kisee tarah kaa anyaay na karn chaahie,yah kise nahin maaloom? bhagavaan kaa to naam hi patit-paavan hai; lekin samaaj ki maryaadaa bhi koi vastu hai! us dvaar par jaate hue sankoch hot hai. gaanv ke maalik hain, jan to padataa hi hai; lekin bas yahi samajh lo ki napharat hoti hai.
mangal aur taamee mein gaharee banatee thi. mangal keht dekho bhai taamee, zara aur khisakakar soo. aakhir main kahan letoon? sara taat to tumne gher liya. taamee koon-koon kart, dum hilaataa aur khisak jaane ke badale aur oopar chadh aat evan mangal kaa munh chaatane lagta. shaam ko vah ek baar roj apna ghar dekhne aur thodee der rone jat. pahale saal phoos kaa chhappar gir padaa, doosare saal ek deevaar giree aur ab keval aadhee-aadhee deevaaren khadee theen, jinakaa ooparee bhaag nokadaar ho gaya tha. yahi use pyaar ki sampatti milee thi. wahi smriti, hi aakarshan, wahi pyaar use ek baar us oojad mein kheench le jat tha aur taamee hameshaa usake saath hot tha. mangal nokadaar deevaar par baith jat aur jeevan ke beete aur aanevaale svapn dekhne lagta aur baar-baar uchhalakar uski god mein baithane ki naakaam cheshtaa kart.
ek din kaee ladake khel rahe the. mangal bhi pahunchakar door khadaa ho gaya. yaa to suresh ko us par dayaa aayee, yaa khelanevaalon ki jodee pooree na padatee thi, kah nahin sakate. jo kuchh bhi ho, tajaveej ki ki aaj mangal ko bhi
khel mein shareek kar liya jaay. yahan kaun dekhne aat hai.
'kyon re mangal, khelegaa.'
mangal bol, 'naa bhaiyaa, kaheen maalik dekh len, to meri chamadee udhod dee jaay. tumhen kya, tum to alag ho jaaoge.'
suresh ne kahaa, 'to yahan kaun aat hai dekhne be? chal, ham log savaar-savaar khelenge. too ghodaa banegaa, ham log tere oopar savaaree karake daudaayenge?'
mangal ne shankaa ki, 'main baraabar ghodaa hi rahoongaa, ki savaaree bhi karoongaa? yah bataa do.'
yah sawal tedhaa tha. kisee ne is par soch na kiya tha. suresh ne ek pal soch karake kahaa, 'tujhe kaun apni peeth par bithaayegaa, soch?'
'aakhir too bhangee hai ki nahin?'
mangal bhi kadaa ho gaya. bol, 'main kab keht hoon ki main bhangee nahin hoon, lekin tumhen meri hi maan ne apna doodh pilaakar paalaa hai. jab tak mujhe bhi savaaree karne ko na milegee, main ghodaa na banoongaa. tum log bade chaghad ho.
aap to maje se savaaree karoge aur main ghodaa hi banaa rahoon.'
suresh ne daantakar kahaa,'tujhe ghodaa bananaa padegaa' aur mangal ko pakadane daudaa. mangal bhaagaa. suresh ne daudaayaa. mangal ne kadam aur tej kiya. suresh ne bhi jor lagaayaa; magar vah bahut khaa-khaakar thul-thul ho gaya tha. aur daudane mein uski saans phoolane lagti thi. aakhir usane rukakar kahaa, 'aakar ghodaa bano mangal, nahin to kabhi paa jaaoongaa, to buree tarah peetoongaa.'
'tumhen bhi ghodaa bananaa padegaa.'
'achchha ham bhi ban jaayange.'
'tum peechhe se nikal jaaoge. pahale tum ghodaa ban jaao. main savaaree kar loon, phir main banoongaa.'
suresh ne sachamuch chakamaa dena chaahaa tha. mangal kaa yah mutaalabaa sunakar saathiyon se bol, 'dekhte ho isakee badamaashee, bhangee hai na!'
teenon ne mangal ko gher liya aur use jabaradastee ghodaa banaa diya. suresh ne chatapat uski peeth par aasan jamaa liya aur tikatik karake, 'bol,chal ghode, chal!
mangal kuchh der tak to chalaa, lekin us bojh se uski kamar tootee jati thi. usane dheere se peeth sikodee aur suresh ki raan ke neeche se sarak gaya. suresh mahoday lad se gir pade aur bhonpoo bajaane lage. maan ne sunaa, suresh kaheen ro raha hai. suresh kaheen roye, to unake tej kaanon mein zaroor bhanak pad jati thi aur uska ronaa bhi bilakul niraalaa hot tha, jaise chhoti laain ke injan ki aavaaj. maharee se bolee, 'dekh to, suresh kaheen ro raha hai, poochh to kisane maaraa hai.'

itane mein suresh khud aankhen malataa hua aay. use jab rone kaa avasar
milataa tha, to maan ke paas phariyaad lekar zaroor aat tha. maan mithaaee yaa meve dekar aansoo ponchh detee theen. aap the to aath saal ke, magar the bilakul gaavadee. had se zyada pyaar ne uski samajh ke saath wahi kiya tha, jo had se zyada khaanaa ne uski deh ke saath.
maan ne poochhaa, 'kyon rotaa hai suresh, kisane maaraa?'
suresh ne rokar kahaa, 'mangal ne chhoo diya.'
maan ko yakeen na aay. mangal itanaa nireeh tha ki usse kisee tarah ki sharaarat ki shankaa na hoti thi; lekin jab suresh kasamen khaane lagaa, to yakeen karn laajim ho gaya. mangal ko bulaakar daantaa, 'kyon re mangal, ab tujhe badamaashee soojhane lagee. maine tujhase kahaa, tha, suresh ko kabhi mata chhoonaa, yaad hai ki nahin, bol.'
mangal ne dabee aavaaj se kahaa, 'yaad kyon nahin hai.'
'to phir toone use kyon chhuaa?'
'maine nahin chhuaa.'
'toone nahin chhuaa, to vah rotaa kyon tha?'
'gir pade, isase rone lage.'
choree aur seenaajoree. deveejee daant peesakar raha gayeen. maarateen, to usi dam snaan karn padataa. chhadee to haath mein lenee hi padatee aur chhoot kaa vidyut-pravaah is chhadee ke raaste unki deh mein paivast ho jat, isalie jahaan
tak gaaliyaan de sakeen, deen aur hukm diya ki -'abhi-abhi yahan se nikal jaa. phir jo is dvaar par teri soorat najar aayee, to khoon hi pee jaaoongee. mupht ki rotiyaan khaa-khaakar sharaarat soojhti hai,' aadi.
mangal mein gairat to kya thi, haan, dar tha. chupake se apne sakore uthaaye, taat kaa tukadaa bagal mein dabaayaa, dhotee kandho par rakhee aur rotaa hua wahan se chal padaa. ab vah yahan kabhi na aayegaa. yahi to hogaa ki bhookhon mar
jaayegaa. kya haraj hai? is tarah jeene se phaayadaa hi kya? gaanv mein usake lie aur kahan thikaanaa tha? bhangee ko kaun panaah detaa? usi khandahar ki ora chalaa, jahaan bhale dinon ki smritiyaan usake aansoo ponchh sakatee theen aur khoob phoot-phootakar royaa. usi pal taamee bhi use dhoondhta hua pahunchaa aur donon phir apni vyathaa
bhool gaye.
lekin jyon-jyon din kaa prakaash ksheen hot jat tha, mangal ki pachhataavaa bhi ksheen hoti jati thi. bachpan ko bechain karne vaalee bhookh deh kaa rakt pee-peekar aur bhi balavaan hoti jati thi. aankhen baar-baar kasoron ki ora uth jaateen. wahan ab tak suresh ki joothee mithaaiyaan mil gayi hoteen. yahan kya dhool phaanke? usane taamee se salaah ki Kaaoge kya taamee? main to bhookhaa let rahoongaa. taamee ne koon-koon karake shaayad kahaa, 'is tarah kaa beizzatee to zindagi bhar sahanaa hai. yon himmat haaroge, to kaise kaam chalegaa? mujhe dekho na, kabhi
kisee ne dandaa maaraa, chillaa uthaa, phir zara der baad dum hilaataa hua usake paas jaa pahunchaa. ham-tum donon iseelie bane hain, bhai!' mangal ne kahaa,'to tum jaao, jo kuchh mile khaa lo, meri paravaah na karo.'
taamee ne apni shvaan-bhaashaa mein kahaa, 'akelaa nahin jat, tumhen saath lekar chaloongaa.'
'main nahin jat.'
'to main bhi nahin jat.'
'bhookhon mar jaaoge.'
'to kya tum jeete rahoge?'
'mera kaun baithaa hai, jo royegaa?'
'yahan bhi wahi haal hai bhai, kvaar mein jis kutiyaa se pyaar kiya tha, usane bevaphaaee ki aur ab kalloo ke saath hai. khairiyat yahi hui ki apne bachche letee gayi, nahin to meri jaan gaadhe mein pad jati. paanch-paanch bachchon ko
kaun paalataa?'
ek pal ke baad bhookh ne ek doosaree yukti soch nikaalee. 'maalakin hamen khoj rahi hongee, kya taamee?'
'aur kya? baaboojee aur suresh khaa chuke honge. kahaar ne unki thaalee se joothan nikaal liya hogaa aur hamen pukaar raha hogaa.'
'baaboojee aur suresh donon ki thaaliyon mein ghee khoob rehta hai aur vah meethee-meethee cheej haan malaaee.'
'sab-kaa-sab ghoore par daal diya jaayagaa.'
'dekhen, hamen khojane koi aat hai?'
'khojane kaun aayegaa; kya koi purohit ho? ek baar 'mangal-mangal' hogaa aur bas, thaalee paranaale mein undel dee jaayegee.'
'achchha, to chalo chalen. magar main chhipaa rahoongaa, agar kisee ne mera naam lekar na pukaaraa; to main laut aaoongaa. yah samajh lo.'
donon wahan se nikale aur aakar maheshanaath ke dvaar par andhere mein dabakakar khade ho gaye; magar taamee ko sabr kahan? vah dheere se andar ghus gaya. dekh, maheshanaath aur suresh thaalee par baith gaye hain. barothe mein dheere se baith gaya, magar dar raha tha ki koi dandaa na maar de. naukar mein baatacheet ho rahi thi. ek ne kahaa, 'aaj mangalavaa nahin dikhaayee detaa. maalakin ne daantaa tha, isase bhaagaa hai saait.'
doosare ne jawab diya, 'achchha hua, nikaal diya gaya. sabere-sabere bhangee kaa munh dekhna padataa tha.
mangal aur andhere mein khisak gaya. ummeed gahare jal mein doob gayi. maheshanaath thaalee se uth gaye. naukar haath dhulaa raha hai. ab hukkaa peeyenge aur soyenge. suresh apni maan ke paas baithaa koi kahaa,nee sunataa-sunataa
so jaayagaa! gareeb mangal ki kise phikr hai? itanee der ho gayi, kisee ne bhool se bhi na pukaaraa.
kuchh der tak vah niraash-saa wahan khadaa raha, phir ek lambee saans kheenchakar jan hi chahta tha ki kahaar pattal mein thaalee kaa joothan le jat najar aay. mangal andhere se nikalakar prakaash mein aa gaya. ab man ko kaise roke?
kahaar ne kahaa, 'are, too yahan tha? hamne samajhaa ki kaheen chalaa gaya. le, khaa le; main phenkane le jaa raha tha.'
mangal ne deenataa se kahaa, 'main to badi der se yahan khadaa tha.'
'to bol, kyon nahin?'
'maare dar ke.'
'achchha, le khaa le.'
usane pattal ko oopar uthaakar mangal ke phaile hue haathon mein daal diya. mangal ne uski ora aisee aankhon se dekh, jisamen deen kritajnataa bharee hui thi. taamee bhi andar se nikal aay tha. donon vaheen neem ke neeche pattal
mein khaane lage. mangal ne ek haath se taamee kaa sir sahalaakar kahaa, 'dekh, pet ki aag aisee hoti hai! yah laat ki maaree hui rotiyaan bhi na milateen,to kya karte?' taamee ne dum hilaa dee.
'suresh ko ammaan ne paalaa tha.'
taamee ne phir dum hilaayee.
'log kehte hain, doodh kaa daam koi nahin chukaa sakataa aur mujhe doodh kaa yah daam mil raha hai.'
taamee ne phir dum hilaayee.
