# Sajjanta Ka Dand

**Munshi Premchand**

aam manushy kee tarah shaahajahaanpur ke distrikt injeeniyar saradaar shivasinh mein bhee bhalaaiyaan aur buraaiyaan donon hee abhee theen. bhalaaee yah thi ki unake yahan insaaf aur raham mein koi antar na tha. buraaee yah thi ki woh sarvathaa nirlobh aur nihsvaarth the. bhalaaee ne maatahaton ko nidar aur aalasee banaa diya tha, buraaee ke vajah us vibhaag ke sabhee adhikaaree unakee jaan ke dushman ban gaye the.
subah kaa samay tha. woh kisee pul kee nigaraanee ke lie taiyaar khade the. magar saaees abhee tak meethee neend le raha tha. raat ko use achchhee tarah sahej diya tha ki pau phatane ke pahale gaadee taiyaar kar lenaa. lekin subah bhee huee, soory bhagavaan ne darshan bhee diye, sheetal kiranon mein garamee bhee aayee, par saaees kee neend abhee tak nahin tootee.
saradaar sahab khade-khade thak kar ek kursee par baith gaye. saaees to kisee tarah jaagaa, lekin ardalee ke chaparaasiyon kaa pataa nahin. jo sahab daak lene gaye the woh ek thaakuradvaaraa mein khade charanaamrit kee pareekshaa kar rahe the. jo thekedaar ko bulaane gaye the woh baabaa raamadaas kee sevaa mein baithe gaanje kaa dam lagaa rahe the.
dhoop tej hoti jati thi. saradaar sahab jhunjhalaa kar makaan mein chale gaye aur apanee patnee se bole, itanaa din chadh aay, abhee tak ek chaparaasee kaa bhee pataa nahin. inake maare to mere naak mein dam aa gaya hai.
patnee ne deevaar kee ora dekh kar deevaar se kahaa, yah sab unhe sir chadhaane kaa phal hai.
saradaar sahab chidh kar bole, kya karoon, unhe phaansee de doon?
saradaar sahab ke paas motarakaar kaa to kahanaa hee kya, koi phitan bhee na thi. woh apne ikke se hee khush the, jise unake naukar-chaakar apanee bhaashaa mein udanakhatolaa kehte the. shahar ke log use itanaa aadar-soochak naam na dekar chhakadaa kahanaa hee theek samajhate the. is tarah saradaar sahab any vyavahaaron mein bhee bade mitavyayee the. unake do bhai ilaahaabaad mein padhate the. vidhavaa maan banaaras mein rehti theen. ek vidhavaa bahin bhee unheen par avalambit thi. inake sivaa kaee gareeb ladakon ko chhaatravrittiyaan bhee dete the. inheen kaaranon se woh sadaa khaalee haath rehte! yahan tak ki unake kapadon par bhee is aarthik dashaa ke chihn dikhaayee dete the! lekin yah sab takaleeph sah kar bhee woh lobh ko apne paas phatakane na dete the! jin logon par unakaa pyaar tha woh unakee sajjanataa ko saraahate the aur unhe devataa samajhate the. unakee sajjanataa se unhe koi nukasaan na hoti thi, lekin jin logon se unake vyaavasaayik sambandh the woh unake sadbhaavon ke graahak na the, kyonki unhe nukasaan hoti thi. yahan tak ki unhe apanee sahadharminee se bhee kabhee-kabhee apriy baaten sunanee padatee theen.
ek din woh daphtar se aaye to unakee patnee ne snehapoorn dhang se kahaa, tumhaaree yah sajjanataa kis kaam kee, jab saaraa sansaar tumako buraa kah raha hai.
saradaar sahab ne dridhataa se jawab diya, sansaar jo chaahe kahe paramaatmaa to dekhataa hai.
raamaa ne yah jawab pahale hee soch liya. woh bolee, main tumse vivaad to karti nahin, magar zara apne dil mein soch karake dekho ki tumhaaree is sachaaee kaa doosaron par kya asar padataa hai? tum to achchhaa vetan paate ho. tum agar haath na badhaao to tumhaaraa guzaaraa ho sakataa hai? rookhee rotiyaan mil hee jaayangee, magar ye das-das paanch-paanch rupaye ke chaparaasee, muharrir, daphtaree bechaare kaise gujar karen. unake bhee baal-bachche hain. unake bhee kutumb-gharavaale hain. shaadee-gamee, tithi-tyohaar yah sab unake paas lage hue hain. bhalamanasee kaa bhesh banaaye kaam nahin chalataa. bataao unakaa gujar kaise ho? abhee raamadeen chaparaasee kee gharavaalee aayee thi. rote-rote aanchal bheegataa tha. ladakee sayaanee ho gayee hai. ab usakaa byaah karn padegaa. braahman kee jaati hajaaron kaa kharch. bataao usake aansoo kisake sir padenge?
ye sab baaten sach theen. inse saradaar sahab ko inakaar nahin ho sakataa tha. unhonne svayan is vishay mein bahut kuchh soch kiya tha. yahee vajah tha ki woh apne maatahaton ke saath badee naramee kaa vyavahaar karte the. lekin saralataa aur shaaleenataa kaa aatmik gaurav chaahe jo ho, unakaa aarthik mol bahut kam hai. woh bole, tumhaaree baaten sab yathaarth hain, lekin main majaboor hoon. apne niyamon ko kaise todoon? yadi meraa vash chale to main una logon kaa vetan badhaa doon. lekin yah nahin ho sakataa ki main khud loot machaaoon aur unhe lootane doon.
raamaa ne vyangyapoorn shabdon mein kahaa, to yah hatyaa kis par padegee?
saradaar sahab ne teevr ho kar jawab diya, yah una logon par padegee jo apanee haisiyat aur aamadanee se zyaadaa kharch karn chaahate hain. aradalee banakar kyon vakeel ke ladake se ladakee byaahane ko thaanate hain. daphtaree ko yadi tahaluve kee jaroorat ho to yah kisee paap kaary se kam nahin. mere saaees kee aurat agar chaandee kee sil gale mein daalanaa chaahe to yah usakee moorkhataa hai. is jhoothee badaaee kaa uttaradaataa main nahin ho sakataa.
injeeniyaron kaa thekedaaron se kuchh aisaa hee sambandh hai jaise madhu-makkhiyon kaa phoolon se. agar woh apne niyat bhaag se zyaadaa paane kee cheshtaa na karen to unse kisee ko shikaayat nahin ho sakatee. yah madhu-ras kameeshan kahalaataa hai. rishvat lok aur paralok donon kaa hee sarvanaash kar detee hai. usamen dar hai, choree hai, badamaashee hai. magar kameeshan ek manohar vaatikaa hai, jahaan na manushy kaa dar hai, na paramaatmaa kaa dar, yahan tak ki wahan aatmaa kee chhipee huee chutakiyon kaa bhee gujar nahin hai. aur kahan tak kahen usakee ora badanaamee aankh bhee nahin uthaa sakatee. yah woh balidaan hai jo hatyaa hote hue bhee dharm kaa ek ansh hai. aisee avasthaa mein yadi saradaar shivasinh apne ujjval charitr ko is dhabbe se saaph rakhate the aur us par ghamand karte the to maafee ke paatr the.
maarch kaa maheenaa beet raha tha. cheeph injeeniyar sahab jile mein muaayanaa karne aa rahe the. magar abhee tak imaaraton kaa kaam apoorn tha. sadaken kharaab ho rahi theen, thekedaaron ne mittee aur kankad bhee nahin jamaa kiye the.
saradaar sahab roj thekedaaron ko taakeed karte the, magar isakaa kuchh phal na hot tha.
ek din unhonne sabako bulaayaa. woh kehne lage, tum log kya yahee chaahate ho ki main is jile se badanaam hokar jaaoon. mainne tumhaare saath koi buraa salook nahin kiya. main chaahataa to aapase kaam chheen kar khud karaa letaa, magar mainne aapako nukasaan pahunchaanaa theek na samajhaa. usakee mujhe yah sajaa mil rahi hai. khair!
thekedaar log yahan se chale to baaten hone lageen. mistar gopaaladaas bole, ab aate-daal kaa bhaav maaloom ho jaayagaa.
shaahabaaz khaan ne kahaa, kisee tarah isakaa janaajaa nikale to yahan se. ..
seth chunneelaal ne pharamaayaa, injeeniyar se meree jaan-pahachaan hai. main usake saath kaam kar chukaa hoon. woh unhe khoob lathedegaa.
is par boodhe haridaas ne upadesh diya, yaaro, svaarth kee baat hai. nahin to sach yah hai ki yah manushy nahin, devataa hai. bhalaa aur nahin to saal bhar mein kameeshan ke 10 hajaar to hote honge. itane rupayon ko theekare kee tarah tuchchh samajhanaa kya koi sahaj baat hai? ek ham hain ki kaudiyon ke peechhe eemaan bechate phirate hain. jo bhalaa aadamee aadamee hamse ek paaee kaa ravaadaar na ho, sab tarah ke takaleeph uthaa kar bhee jisakee neeyat daanvaandol na ho, usake saath aisaa neech aur kutil barataav karn padataa hai. ise apne abhaagy ke sivaa aur kya samajhen.
shaahabaaz khaan ne pharamaayaa haan; isamen to koi shak nahin ki yah shakhs nekee kaa pharishtaa hai.
seth chunneelaal ne gambheerataa se kahaa, khaan sahab! baat to vahee hai, jo tum kehte ho. lekin kiya kya jaay? nekaneeyatee se to kaam nahin chalataa. yah duniya to chhal-kapat kee hai.
mistar gopaaladaas bee. e. paas the. woh garv ke saath bole, inhen jab is tarah rahanaa tha to naukaree karne kee kya jaroorat thi? yah kaun nahin jaanataa kee neeyat ko saaph rakhanaa achchhee baat hai. magar yah bhee to dekhanaa chaahie ki isakaa doosaron par kya asar padataa hai. hamako to aisaa aadamee chaahie jo khud khaaye aur hamen bhee khilaave. khud haluvaa khaay, hamen rookhee rotiyaan hee khilaave. woh agar ek rupayaa kameeshan legaa to usakee jagah paanch kaa phaayadaa kar degaa. ina sahab ke yahan kya hai? iseelie aap jo chaahen kahen, meree to kabhee inse nibh nahin sakatee.
shaahabaaz khaan bole, haan, nek aur paak-saaph rahanaa zaroor achchhee cheej hai, magar aisee nekee hee se kya jo doosaron kee jaan le le.
boodhe haridaas kee baaton kee jin logon ne pushti kee thi woh sab gopaaladaas kee haan mein haan milaane lage! nirbal aatmaaon mein sachaaee kaa prakaash juganoo kee chamak hai.
saradaar sahab ke ek betee thi. usakaa vivaah merath ke ek vakeel ke ladake se thaharaa tha. ladakaa honahaar tha. jaati-kul kaa oonchaa tha. saradaar sahab ne kaee maheene kee daud-dhoop mein is vivaah ko tai kiya tha. aur sab baaten tai ho chukee theen, keval dahej kaa phaisalaa nahin hua tha. aaj vakeel sahab kaa ek patr aay. usane is baat kaa bhee phaisalaa kar diya, magar yakeen, ummeed aur vachan ke bilakul pratikool. pahale vakeel sahab ne ek jile ke injeeniyar ke saath kisee tarah kaa thaharaav bekaar samajhaa. badee sastee udaarataa prakat kee. is lajjit aur ghrinit vyavahaar par khoob aansoo bahaaye. magar jab zyada poochhataachh karne par saradaar sahab ke paisaa-vaibhav kaa bhed khul gaya tab dahej kaa thaharaanaa zarooree ho gaya. saradaar sahab ne aashankit haathon se patr kholaa, paanch hajaar rupaye se kam par vivaah nahin ho sakataa. vakeel sahab ko bahut khed aur sharm thi ki woh is vishay mein spasht hone par majaboor kiye gaye. magar woh apne khaanadaan ke kaee boodhe khurraat, vichaaraheen, svaarthaandh mahaatmaaon ke haathon bahut tang the. unakaa koi vansh na tha. injeeniyar sahab ne ek lambee saans kheenchee saaree aashaaen mittee mein mil gayeen. kya sochate the, kya ho gaya. vikal ho kar kamare mein tahalane lage.
unhonne zara der peechhe patr ko uthaa liya aur andar chale gaye. vichaaraa ki yah patr raamaa ko sunaaven, magar phir khayaal aay ki yahan hamadardee kee koi ummeed nahin. kyon apanee nirbalataa dikhaaoon? kyon bevakooph banoon? woh binaa baaton ke baat na karegee. yah soch kar woh aangan se laut gaye.
saradaar sahab aadat ke bade dayaalu the aur komal dil aapattiyon mein sthir nahin raha sakataa. woh duhkh aur pachhataavaa se bhare hue soch rahe the ki mainne aise kaun se bure kaam kiye hain jinakaa mujhe yah phal mil raha hai. barason kee daud-dhoop ke baad jo kaary siddh hua tha woh pal maatr mein nasht ho gaya. ab woh meree saamarthy se baahar hai. main use nahin samhaal sakataa. chaaron ora andhakaar hai. kaheen ummeed kaa prakaash nahin. koi meraa sahaayak nahin. unake aankh sajal ho gaye.
saamane mej par thekedaaron ke bil rakhe hue the. woh kaee saptaahon se yon hee pade the. saradaar ne unhe khol kar bhee na dekh tha. aaj is aatmik pachhataavaa aur nairaashy kee avasthaa mein unhonne ina bilon ko satrishn aankhon se dekh. zara se ishaare par ye saaree kathinaaiyaan door ho sakatee hain. chaparaasee aur klark keval meree sammati ke sahaare sab kuchh kar lenge. mujhe jabaan hilaane kee bhee jaroorat nahin. na mujhe lajjit hee hona padegaa. ina vichaaron kaa itanaa praabaly hua ki woh vaastav mein bilon ko uthaa kar gaur se dekhane aur hisaab lagaane lage ki unamen kitanee nikaasee ho sakatee hai.
magar jaldee hee aatmaa ne unhe jagaa diya aah! main kis bhram mein padaa hua hoon? kya us aatmik pavitrataa ko, jo meree janm-bhar kee kamaaee hai, keval thode se paisaa par arpan kar doon? jo main apne sahakaariyon ke saamane garv se sir uthaaye chalataa tha, jisse motarakaar vaale bhraatrigan aankhen nahin milaa sakate the, vahee main aaj apne us saare gaurav aur maan ko, apanee pooraa aatmik jaayadaad ko das-paanch hajaar rupayon par tyaag doon. aisaa kabhee nahin nahin ho sakataa.
ab us kuvichaar ko paraast karne ke lie, jisane kshanamaatr ke lie una par vijay paa lee thi, woh us sunasaan kamare mein jor se thathaa kar hanse. chaahe yah hansee una bilon ne aur kamare kee deevaaron ne na sunee ho, magar unakee aatmaa ne avashy sunee. us aatmaa ko ek mushkil pareekshaa mein paar paane par param khushi hua.
saradaar sahab ne una bilon ko uthaa kar mej ke neeche daal diya. phir unhe pairon se kuchalaa. tab is vijay par muskaraate hue woh andar gaye.
bade injeeniyar sahab niyat samay par shaahajahaanpur aaye. usake saath saradaar sahab kaa badakismatee bhee aay. jile ke saare kaam adhoore pade hue the. unake khaanasaamaa ne kahaa hujoor! kaam kaise pooraa ho? saradaar sahab thekedaaron ko bahut tang karte hain. hedaklark ne daphtar ke hisaab ko bhram aur bhoolon se bharaa hua paayaa. unhe saradaar sahab kee taraph se na koi daavat dee gayee na koi bhent. to kya woh saradaar sahab ke naatedaar the jo galatiyaan na nikaalate.
jile ke thekedaaron ne ek bahumooly daalee sajaayee aur use bade injeeniyar sahab kee sevaa mein le kar haajir hue. woh bole, hujoor! chaahe gulaamon ko golee maar den, magar saradaar sahab kaa zulm ab nahin sahaa jat. kehne ko to kameeshan nahin lete magar sach poochhie to jaan le lete hain.
cheeph injeeniyar sahab ne muaaine kee kitaab mein likhaa, 'saradaar shivasinh bahut eemaanadaar aadamee hain. unakaa charitr ujjval hai, magar woh itane bade jile ke kaary kaa bhaar nahin sambhaal sakate. '
nateejaa yah hua ki woh ek chhote-se jile mein bhej diye gaye aur unakaa darajaa bhee ghataa diya gaya.
saradaar sahab ke mitron aur snehiyon ne bade samaaroh se ek jalasaa kiya. usamen unakee dharmanishthaa aur svatantrataa kee prashansaa kee. sabhaapati ne sajalanetr ho kar kampit aavaaz mein kahaa, saradaar sahab ke viyog kaa duhkh hamaare dil mein sadaa khatakataa rahegaa. yah ghaav kabhee na bharegaa.
magar 'pheyaravel dinar' mein yah baat siddh ho gayee ki svaadisht padaarthon ke saamane viyog kaa duhkh dussah nahin.
yaatraa ke saamaan taiyaar the. saradaar sahab jalase se aaye to raamaa ne unhe bahut udaas aur malinamukh dekh. usane baar-baar kahaa tha ki bade injeeniyar ke khaanasaamaa ko inaam do, hed klark kee daavat karo; magar saradaar sahab ne usakee baat na maanee thi. isalie jab usane sunaa ki unakaa darajaa ghataa aur badalee bhee huee tab usane badee nirdayataa se apne vyangy-baan chalaaye. magar is vakt unhe udaas dekh kar usse na raha gaya. bolee, kyon itane udaas ho? saradaar sahab ne jawab diya, kya karoon hansoon? raamaa ne gambheer aavaaz se kahaa, hansanaa hee chaahie. roye to woh jisane kaudiyon par apanee aatmaa bhrasht kee ho jisane rupayon par apanaa dharm bechaa ho. woh buraaee kaa sazaa nahin hai. yah bhalaaee aur sajjanataa kaa sazaa hai, ise saanand jhelanaa chaahie.
yah kah usane pati kee ora dekh to netron mein sachchaa anuraag bharaa hua dikhaayee diya. saradaar sahab ne bhee usakee ora snehapoorn nazar se dekh. unakee hridayeshvaree kaa mukhaaravind sachche aamod se vikasit tha. use gale lagaa kar woh bole, raamaa! mujhe tumhaaree hee hamadardee kee jaroorat thi, ab main is sazaa ko saharsh sahoongaa.
