# Godaan — Adhyay 25

**Munshi Premchand**

25.
NAmEBhola idhar dusri sagai laye the. aurat ke bagair unka jivan niras tha. jab tak NAmEJhunia thi, unhen huqqa-pani de deti thi. samay se khane ko bula le jati thi. ab bechare anath-se ho gaye the. bahuon ko ghar ke kam-dham se chhutti na milti thi. unki kya seva-satkar karti; isalie ab sagai paramavashyak ho gayi thi. samyog se ek javan vidhva mil gayi, jiske pati ka maut hue sirph tin mahine hue the. ek larka bhi tha. NAmEBhola ki lar tapak padi. jhatpat shikar mar laye. jab tak sagai na hui, uska ghar khod dala. abhi tak uske ghar men jo kuchh tha, bahuon ka tha. jo chahti thim, karti thim, jaise chahti thim, rahti thim. jangi jab se apni aurat ko lekar lakhanau chala gaya tha, kamta ki bahu hi ghar ki svamini thi. panch-chhah mahinon men hi usne tis-chalis, pae apne hath men kar liye the. ser-adh ser dudh-dahi chori se bech leti thi. ab svamini hui uski sauteli sas. uska niyantran bahu ko bura lagata tha aur aye din donon men takrar hoti rahti thi. yaham tak ki aurton ke pichhe NAmEBhola aur kamta men bhi kaha-suni ho gayi. jhagra itna barha ki algaujhe ki naubat a gayi. aur yah riti sanatan se chali ayi hai ki algaujhe ke samay mar-pit zarur ho. yaham us riti ka palan kiya gaya. kamta javan aadmi tha. NAmEBhola ka us par jo kuchh dabab tha, vah pita ke nate tha; magar nayi aurat lakar bete se adar pane ka ab use koi haq na raha tha. kam-se-kam kamta ise svikar na karta tha. usne NAmEBhola ko patakkar kai laten jamayim aur ghar se nikal diya. ghar ki chizen na chhune dim. gamvvalo men bhi kisi ne NAmEBhola ka paksh na liya. nayi sagai ne unhen nakku bana diya tha. rat to unhomne kisi tarah ek per ke niche kati, subah hote hi nokheram ke pas ja pahunche aur apni fariyad sunayi. NAmEBhola ka gamv bhi unhim ke ilaqe men tha aur ilaqe-bhar ke malik-mukhiya jo kuchh the, vahi the. nokheram ko NAmEBhola par to kya daya ati; par unke sath ek chatpati, rangili aurat dekhi to chatpat ashray dene par razi ho gaye. jaham unki gayen bandhti thim, vahim ek kothri rahne ko de di. apne janvaron ki dekh-bhal, sani-bhuse ke lie unhen ekaek ek jankar aadmi ki zarurat malum hone lagi. NAmEBhola ko tin rupya mahina aur ser-bhar rozana par naukar rakh liya. nokheram nate, mote, khalvat, lambi nak aur chhoti-chhoti ankhomvale samvle aadmi the. bada-sa paggar bandhte, nicha kurta pahnate aur jaron men lihaf orhkar bahar ate-jate the. unhen tel ki malish karane men bada khushi ata tha, isalie unke kapde hamesha maile, chikat rahte the. unka parivar bahut bada tha. sat bhai aur unke bal-bachche sabhi unhim par ashrit the. us par svayam unka larka naven darje men angrezi parhta tha aur uska babuai thath nibhana koi asan kam na tha. NAmERai Sahib se unhen sirph barah rupae tanakhvah milta tha; magar kharch sau rupae se kauri kam na tha. isalie asami kisi tarah unke changul men phams jay to bina use achchhi tarah chuse chhorte na the. pahle chhah rupae tanakhvah milta tha, tab asamiyon se itni noch-khasot na karte the; jab se barah rupae ho gaye the, tab se unki trishna aur bhi barh gayi thi; isalie NAmERai Sahib unki taraqqi na karte the. gamv men aur to sabhi kisi-na-kisi rup men unka davab mante the; yaham tak ki NAmEDatadin aur NAmEJhinguri simh bhi unki khushamad karte the, sirph NAmEPateshwari unse tal thokne ko hamesha taiyar rahte the. nokheram ko agar yah jon tha ki ham brahman haim aur kayasthon ko ungli par nachate haim, to NAmEPateshwari ko bhi ghamand tha ki ham kayasth haim, qalam ke badshah, is maidan men koi hamse kya bazi le jayga. phir vah zamindar ke naukar nahin, sarkar ke naukar haim, jiske raj men suraj kabhi nahin dubta. nokheram agar ekadshi ko vrat rakhte haim aur panch brahmanon ko khana karate haim to NAmEPateshwari har punarmasi ko satyanarayan ki katha sunenge aur das brahmanon ko khana karayenge. jab se unka jetha larka sazaval ho gaya tha, nokheram is tak men rahte the ki unka larka kisi tarah dasvam pas kar le, to use bhi kahim naqal-navisi dila den. isalie hukkam ke pas fasli saugaten lekar barabar salami karte rahte the. ek aur bat men NAmEPateshwari unse barhe hue the. logon ka khayal tha ki vah apni vidhva kaharin ko rakhe hue haim. ab nokheram ko bhi apni shan men yah kasar puri karne ka avsar milta hua jan pada. NAmEBhola ko dharhas dete hue bole -- tum yaham aram se raho NAmEBhola, kisi bat ka khatka nahin. jis chiz ki zarurat ho, hamse akar kaho. tumhari gharvali hai, uske lie bhi koi na koi kam nikal ayega. bakharon men anaj rakhna, nikalna, pachhorna, phatkana kya thoda kam hai?
NAmEBhola ne araj ki -- sarkar, ek bar kamta ko bulakar puchh lo, kya bap ke sath bete ka yahi saluk hona chahiye. ghar hamne banvaya, gayen-bhaimsen hamne lim. ab usne sab kuchh hathiya liya aur hamen nikal bahar kiya. yah anyay nahin to kya hai. hamare malik to tumhim ho. tumhare darbar se iska faisla hona chahiye.
nokheram ne samjhaya -- NAmEBhola, tum usse larkar pesh na paoge; usne jaisa kiya hai, uski saza use bhagavan denge. beimani karke koi aj tak phalibhut hua hai? samsar men anyay na hota, to ise narak kyon kaha jata. yaham nyay aur dharm ko kaun puchhta hai? bhagavan sab dekhte haim. samsar ka ratti-ratti hal jante haim. tumhare man men is samay kya bat hai, yah unse kya chhipa hai? isi se to antarjami kahlate haim. unse bachkar koi kaham jayga? tum chup hoke baitho. bhagavan ki chah hui, to yaham tum usse bure na rahoge.
yaham se uthkar NAmEBhola ne NAmEHori ke pas jakar apna dukhra roya. NAmEHori ne apni biti sunayi -- larkon ki ajkal kuchh na puchho NAmEBhola bhai. mar-markar palo; javan hon, to dusman ho jayam. mere hi NAmEGobar ko dekho. mam se larkar gaya, aur salon ho gaye, na chitthi, na pattar. uske lekhe to mam-bap mar gaye. bitiya ka byah sir par hai; lekin usse koi matlab nahin. khet rehan rakhkar do sau rupae liye haim. izzat-abru ka nibah to karna hi hoga.
kamta ne bap ko nikal bahar to kiya; lekin ab use malum hone laga ki buddha kitna kamkaji aadmi tha. sabere uthkar sani-pani karna, dudh duhna, phir dudh lekar bazar jana, vaham se akar phir sani-pani karna, phir dudh duhna; ek pakhvare men uska huliya bigar gaya. aurat-aadmi men larai hui. aurat ne kaha -- maim jan dene ke lie tumhare ghar nahin ayi hum. meri roti tumhen bhari ho, to maim apne ghar chali jaum.
kamta dara, yah kahim chali jay, to roti ka thikana bhi na rahe, apne hath se thokna pade. akhir ek naukar rakha; lekin usse kam na chala. naukar khali-bhusa chura-churakar bechne laga. use alag kiya. phir aurat-aadmi men larai hui. aurat ruthkar maike chali gayi. kamta ke hath-pamv phul gaye. harkar NAmEBhola ke pas aya aur chirauri karne laga -- dada, mujhse jo kuchh bhul-chuk hui ho kshama karo. ab chalkar ghar sambhalo, jaise tum rakhoge, vaise hi rahunga.
NAmEBhola ko yaham majuron ki tarah rahna akhar raha tha. pahle mahine-do-mahine uski jo khatir hui, vah ab na thi. nokheram kabhi-kabhi usse chilam bharne ya charapai bichhane ko bhi kahte the. tab bechara NAmEBhola zahar ka ghunt pikar rah jata tha. apne ghar men larai-danga bhi ho, to kisi ki tahal to na karni paregi. uski aurat NAmENohari ne yah prastav suna to ainthkar boli -- jaham se lat khakar aye, vaham phir jaoge? tumhen laj bhi nahin ati.
NAmEBhola ne kaha -- to yahim kaun simhasan par baitha hua hum.
NAmENohari ne matakkar kaha -- tumhen jana ho to jao, maim nahin jati.
NAmEBhola janta tha, NAmENohari khilaf hona karegi. iska vajah bhi vah kuchh-kuchh samjhata tha, kuchh dekhta bhi tha, uske yaham se bhagne ka ek vajah yah bhi tha. yaham uski to koi bat na puchhta tha; par NAmENohari ki badi khatir hoti thi. pyade aur shahne tak uska dabav mante the. uska javab sunkar NAmEBhola ko gussa aya; lekin karta kya? NAmENohari ko chhorkar chale jane ka himmat usmen hota to NAmENohari bhi jhakh markar uske pichhe-pichhe chali jati. akele use yaham apne ashray men rakhne ki himmat nokheram men na thi. vah tatti ki ar se shikar khelanevale jiv the, magar NAmENohari NAmEBhola ke svabhav se parichit ho chuki thi.
NAmEBhola minnat karke bola -- dekh NAmENohari, dik mat kar. ab to vaham bahuen bhi nahin haim. tere hi hath men sab kuchh rahega. yaham majuri karne se biradri men kitni badnami ho rahi hai, yah soch!
NAmENohari ne thenga dikhakar kaha -- tumhen jana hai jao, maim tumhen rok to nahin rahi hum. tumhen bete ki laten pyari lagti hongi, mujhe nahin lagtim. maim apni mazduri men magan hum.
NAmEBhola ko rahna pada aur kamta apni aurat ki khushamad karke use mana laya. idhar NAmENohari ke vishay men kanabtiya hoti rahim -- NAmENohari ne aj gulabi sari pahni hai. ab kya puchhna hai, chahe roz ek sari pahne. saiyam bhaye kotval ab dar kahe ka. NAmEBhola ki ankhen phut gayi haim kya? shobha bada hamsor tha. sare gamv ka vidushak, balki narad. har ek bat ki toh lagata rahta tha. ek din NAmENohari use ghar men mil gayi. kuchh hamsi kar baitha. NAmENohari ne nokheram se jar diya. shobha ki chaupal men talbi hui aur aisi dant padi ki umra-bhar na bhulega.
ek din lala NAmEPateshwari prasad ki shamat a gayi. gamiryon ke din the. lala bagiche men baithe am turva rahe the. NAmENohari bani-thani udhar se nikli. lala ne pukara -- nohra rani, idhar ao, thore se am leti jao, bade mithe haim.
NAmENohari ko bhram hua, lala mera uphas kar rahe haim. use ab ghamand hone laga tha. vah chahti thi, log use zamindarin samjhen aur uska izzat karen. ghamandi aadmi prayah shakki hua karta hai. aur jab man men chor ho to shakkipan aur bhi barh jata hai. vah meri or dekhkar kyon hamsa? sab log mujhe dekhkar jalte kyon haim? maim kisi se kuchh mangne nahin jati. kaun badi satavanti hai! zara mere samne aye, to dekhum. itne dinon men NAmENohari gamv ke gupt rahasyon se parichit ho chuki thi. yahi lala kaharin ko rakhe hue haim aur mujhe hamste haim. inhen koi kuchh nahin kahta. bade aadmi haim na. NAmENohari garib hai, jat ki hethi hai; isalie sabhi uska uphas karte haim. aur jaisa bap hai, vaisa hi beta. inhim ka ramesri to NAmESilia ke pichhe pagal bana phirta hai. chamariyon par to giddh ki tarah tutte haim, us par dava hai ki ham unche haim. usne vahim khade hokar kaha -- tum dani kab se ho gaye lala! pao to dusron ki thali ki roti ura jao. aj bade amvale hue haim. mujhse chher ki to achchha na hoga, kahe deti haim.
o ho! is ahirin ka itna mizaj! nokheram ko kya phams liya, samjhati hai sari duniya par uska raj hai. bole -- tu to aisi tinak rahi hai NAmENohari, jaise ab kisi ko gamv men rahne na degi. zara zaban sambhalkar baten kiya kar, itni jald apne ko na bhul ja.

NAmENohari ko lal mirch-sa laga. jo kuchh mumh men aya baka -- darhijar, lampat, mumhjhaumsa aur jane kya-kya kaha aur usi gussa men bhari hui kothri men gayi aur apne bartan-bhamre nikal-nikalkar bahar rakhne lagi. nokheram ne suna to ghabraye hue aye aur puchha -- vah kya kar rahi hai NAmENohari, kapde-latte kyon nikal rahi hai? kisi ne kuchh kaha hai kya?
NAmENohari madon ke nachane ki kala janti thi. apne jivan men usne yahi vidya sikhi thi. nokheram parhe-likhe aadmi the. qanun bhi jante the. dharm ki pustaken bhi bahut parhi thim. bade-bade vakilon, bairistaron ki jutiyam sidhi ki thim; par is bevkuph NAmENohari ke hath ka khilauna bane hue the. bhaumhen sikorkar boli -- samay ka pher hai, yaham a gayi; lekin apni abru na gavaungi.
brahman satej ho utha. munchhen khadi karke bola -- teri or jo take uski ankhen nikal lum.
NAmENohari ne lohe ko lal karke ghan jamaya -- lala patesri jab dekho mujhse bebat ki bat kiya karte haim. maim harajai thore hi hum ki koi mujhe paise dikhaye. gamv-bhar men sabhi aurten to haim, koi unse nahin bolta. jise dekho, mujhi ko chherta hai.
nokheram ke sir par bhut savar ho gaya. apna mota danda uthaya aur andhi ki tarah harahrate hue bag men pahunchkar lage lalakarne -- a ja bada mard hai to. munchhen ukhar lunga, khodkar gar dunga. nikal a samne. agar phir kabhi NAmENohari ko chhera to khun pi jaunga. sari patavargiri nikal dunga. jaisa khud hai, vaisa hi dusron ko samjhata hai. tu hai kis ghamand men?
lala NAmEPateshwari sir jhukaye, dam sadhe jaravat khade the. zara bhi zaban kholi aur shamat ayi. unka itna beizzati jivan men kabhi na hua tha. ek bar logon ne unhen tal ke kinare rat ko gherkar khub pita tha; lekin gamv men uski kisi ko khabar na hui thi. kisi ke pas koi sabut na tha; lekin aj to sare gamv ke samne unki izzat utar gayi. kal jo aurat gamv men ashay mangti ayi thi, aj sare gamv par uska atank tha. ab kiski himmat hai jo use chher sake. jab NAmEPateshwari kuchh nahin kar sake, to dusron ki bisat hi kya! ab NAmENohari gamv ki rani thi. use ate dekhkar kisan log uske raste se hat jate the. yah khula hua rahasya tha ki uski thodi-si puja karke nokheram se bahut kam nikal sakta hai. kisi ko batvara karana ho, lagan ke lie muhlat mangni ho, makan banane ke lie zameen ki zarurat ho, NAmENohari ki puja kiye bagair uska kam siddh nahin ho sakta. kabhi-kabhi yah achchhe-achchhe asamiyon ko dant deti thi. asami hi nahin, ab karkun sahab par bhi rob jamane lagi thi. NAmEBhola uske ashrit bankar na rahna chahte the. aurat ki kamai khane se zyada adham unki nazar men dusra kam na tha. unhen kul tin, paye mahvar milte the, yah bhi unke hath na lagte.
