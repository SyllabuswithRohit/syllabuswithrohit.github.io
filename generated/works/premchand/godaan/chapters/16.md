NAmERai Sahib ko khabar mili ki ilaqe men ek vardat ho gayi hai aur NAmEHori se gamv ke panchon ne jurmana vasul kar liya hai, to fauran nokheram ko bulakar javab-talab kiya -- kyon unhen, iski ittala nahin di gayi. aise namakahram dagabaz aadmi ke lie unke darbar men jagah nahin hai. nokheram ne itni galiyam khayim, to zara garm hokar bole -- maim akela thoda hi tha. gamv ke aur panch bhi to the. maim akela kya kar leta. NAmERai Sahib ne unki tond ki taraf bhale-jaisi nukili nazar se dekha -- mat bako ji! tumhen usi vaqta kahna chahiye tha, jab tak sarkar ko ittala na ho jay, maim panchon ko jurmana na vasul karne dunga. panchon ko mere aur meri riaya ke bich men dakhal dene ka haq kya hai? is damr-bandh ke siva ilaqe men aur kaun-si amdani hai? vasuli sarkar ke ghar gayi. baqaya asamiyon ne daba liya. tab maim kaham jaum? kya khaum, tumhara sir! yah lakhon rupae sal ka kharch kaham se aye? khed hai ki do pushton se karindagiri karne par mujhe aj tumhen yah bat batlani parti hai. kitne rupae vasul hue the NAmEHori se? nokheram ne sitpita kar kaha -- assi rupae! naqad? naqad uske pas kaham the huzur! kuchh anaj diya, baqi men apna ghar likh diya. NAmERai Sahib ne svarth ka paksh chhorkar NAmEHori ka paksh liya -- achchha to apne aur bagulabhgat panchon ne milkar mere ek matbar asami ko tabah kar diya. maim puchhta hum, tum logon ko kya haq tha ki mere ilaqe men mujhe ittala diye bagair mere asami se jurmana vasul karte. isi bat par agar maim chahum, to apko aur us jaliye patvari aur us dhurt pandit ko sat-sat sal ke lie jel bhijva sakta hum. apne samajh liya ki ap hi ilaqe ke badshah haim. maim kahe deta hum, aj sham tak jurmane ki puri raqam mere pas pahunch jay; varna bura hoga. maim ek-ek se chakki pisavakar chhorunga. jaie, ham, NAmEHori ko aur uske larke ko mere pas bhej dijiega. nokheram ne dabi zaban se kaha -- uska larka to gamv chhorkar bhag gaya. jis rat ko yah vardat hui, usi rat ko bhaga. NAmERai Sahib ne rosh se kaha -- jhuth mat bolo. tumhen malum hai, jhuth se mere badan men ag lag jati hai. maimne aj tak kabhi nahin suna ki koi yuvak apni premika ko uske ghar se lakar phir khud bhag jay. agar use bhagna hi hota, to vah us ladki ko lata kyon? tum logon ki ismen bhi zarur koi shararat hai. tum ganga men dubkar bhi apni safai do, to manne ka nahin. tum logon ne apne samaj ki pyari maryada ki raksha ke lie use dhamkaya hoga. bechara bhag na jata, to kya karta! nokheram iska prativad na kar sake. malik jo kuchh kahen vah thik hai. vah yah bhi na kah sake ki ap khud chalkar jhuth-sach ki janch kar len. bade admiyo ka gussa pura samarpan chahta hai. apne khilaf ek shabd bhi nahin sun sakta. panchon ne NAmERai Sahib ka yah faisla suna, to nasha hiran ho gaya. anaj to abhi tak jyon ka tyon pada tha; par rupae to kab ke gayab ho gaye. NAmEHori ka makan rehan likha gaya tha; par us makan ko dehat men kaun puchhta tha. jaise hindu aurat pati ke sath ghar ki svamini hai, aur pati tyag de, to kahim ki nahin rahti, usi tarah yah ghar NAmEHori ke lie lakh rupae ka hai; par uski asli qimat kuchh bhi nahin. aur idhar NAmERai Sahib bina rupae lie manne ke nahin. yahi NAmEHori jakar ro aya hoga. NAmEPateshwari lal sabse zyada bhaybhit the. unki to naukri hi chali jaygi. charon sajjan is gahan samasya par soch kar rahe the, par kisi ki akla kam na karti thi. ek dusre par dosh rakhta tha. phir khub jhagra hua. NAmEPateshwari ne apni lambi shankashil gardan hilakar kaha -- maim mana karta tha ki NAmEHori ke vishay men hamen chuppi sadhkar rah jana chahiye. gay ke mamle men sabko tavan dena pada. is mamle men tavan hi se gala na chhutega, naukri se hath dhona parega; magar tum logon ko rupae ki padi thi. nikalo bis-bis rupae. ab bhi kushal hai. kahim NAmERai Sahib ne rapat kar di, to sab jane bandh jaoge. NAmEDatadin ne brahmatej dikhakar kaha -- mere pas bis rupae ki jagah bis paise bhi nahin haim. brahmanon ko bhoj diya gaya, hon hua. kya ismen kuchh kharach hi nahin hua? NAmERai Sahib ki himmat hai ki mujhe jel le jayam? brahma bankar ghar ka ghar mita dunga. abhi unhen kisi brahman se pala nahin pada. NAmEJhinguri simh ne bhi kuchh isi ashay ke shabd kahe. vah NAmERai Sahib ke naukar nahin haim. unhomne NAmEHori ko mara nahin, pita nahin, koi dabav nahin dala. NAmEHori agar prayishchat karna chahta tha, to unhomne iska avsar diya. iske lie koi un par jurm nahin laga sakta; magar nokheram ki gardan itni asani se na chhut sakti thi. yaham maze se baithe raj karte the. tanakhvah to das rupae se zyada na tha; par ek hazar sal ki upar ki amdani thi, saikron admiyo par hukumat, char-char pyade hazir, begar men sara kam ho jata tha, thanedar tak kursi dete the, yah chain unhen aur kaham tha! aur NAmEPateshwari to naukri ke badaulat mahajan bane hue the. kaham ja sakte the? do-tin din isi chinta men pade rahe ki kaise is vipitt se niklen. akhir unhen ek rasta sujh hi gaya. kabhi-kabhi kachhari men unhen dainik bijli dekhne ko mil jati thi. yadi ek gumnam chitthi uske sampadak ki seva men bhej diya jay ki NAmERai Sahib kis tarah asamiyon se jurmana vasul karte haim to bacha ko lene ke dene par jayam. nokheram bhi sahmat ho gaye. donon ne milkar kisi tarah ek chitthi likha aur rajistari bhej diya. sampadak NAmEOnkarnath to aise patron ki tak men rahte the. chitthi pate hi turant NAmERai Sahib ko khabar di. unhen ek aisa khabar mila hai, jis par yakin karne ki unki chah nahin hoti; par samvaddata ne aise sabut diye ki sahsa avishvas bhi nahin kiya ja sakta. kya yah sach hai ki NAmERai Sahib ne apne ilaqe ke ek asami se assi rupae tavan isalie vasul kiye ki uske beta ne ek vidhva ko ghar men dal liya tha? sampadak ka pharz unhen mazbur karta hai ki vah muamle ki janch karen aur janta ke hitarth use prakashit kar den. NAmERai Sahib is vishay men jo kuchh kahna chahen, sampadak ji use bhi prakashit kar denge. sampadkaji dil se chahte haim ki yah khabar galat ho; lekin usmen kuchh bhi satya hua, to vah use prakash men lane ke lie majbur ho jayange. maitri unhen pharz-rasta se nahin hata sakti. NAmERai Sahib ne yah khabar payi, to sir pit liya. pahle to unki aisi uttejna hui ki jakar NAmEOnkarnath ko ginkar pachas hantar jamayen aur kah den, jaham vah chitthi chhapna vaham yah khabar bhi chhap dena; lekin iska natija sochkar man ko shant kiya aur turant unse milne chale. agar der ki, aur NAmEOnkarnath ne vah batchit chhap diya, to unke sare yash men kalima put jaygi. NAmEOnkarnath sair karke laute the aur aj ke chitthi ke lie sampadkiy lekh likhne ki chinta men baithe hue the; par man pakshi ki tarah abhi ura-ura phirta tha. unki dharmapatni ne rat men unhen kuchh aisi baten kah dali thim jo abhi tak kanton ki tarah chubh rahi thim. unhen koi garib kah le, abhaga kah le, buddhu kah le, vah zara bhi bura na mante the; lekin yah kahna ki unmen purushatva nahin hai, yah unke lie asahya tha. aur phir apni patni ko yah kahne ka kya haq hai? usse to yah ummid ki jati hai ki koi is tarah ka akshep kare, to uska mumh band kar de. beshak vah aisi khabren nahin chhapte, aisi tippaniyam nahin karte ki sir par koi afat a jay. phunk-phunkkar qadam rakhte haim. in kale kanunon ke yug men vah aur kar hi kya sakte haim; magar vah kyon samp ke bil men hath nahin dalte? isilie to ki unke gharvalo ko takliph na uthane pade. aur unki sahishnuta ka unhen yah puraskar mil raha hai? kya andher hai! unke pas rupae nahin haim, to banarsi sari kaise manga den? daktar seth aur profesar bhatiya aur na jane kis-kis ki striyam banarsi sari pahnati haim, to vah kya karen? kyon unki patni in sarivaliyon ko apni khaddar ki sari se lajjit nahin karti? unki khud to yah adat hai ki kisi bade aadmi se milne jate haim, to mote se mote kapde pahan lete haim aur kuchh koi alochna kare to uska mumhtor javab dene ko taiyar rahte haim. unki patni men kyon vahi atmabhiman nahin hai? vah kyon dusron ka that-bat dekhkar vichlit ho jati hai? use samjhana chahiye ki vah ek desh-bhakt aadmi ki patni hai. desh-bhakt ke pas apni bhakti ke siva aur kya jaydad hai. isi vishay ko aj ke agralekh ka vishay banane ki kalpana karte-karte unka dhyan NAmERai Sahib ke muamle ki or ja pahuncha. NAmERai Sahib khabar ka kya javab dete haim, yah dekhna hai. agar vah apni safai dene men kamyab ho jate haim, tab to koi bat nahin, lekin agar vah yah samjhen ki NAmEOnkarnath dabav, dar, ya mulahje men akar apne pharz se mumh pher lenge to yah unka bhram hai. is sare tap aur sadhan ka puraskar unhen iske siva aur kya milta hai ki avsar parne par vah in qanuni dakaiton ka bhanda-phor karen. unhen khub malum hai ki NAmERai Sahib bade prabhavshali jiv haim. kaumsil ke membar to haim hi. adhikariyon men bhi unka kafi rusukh hai. vah chahen, to un par jhuthe muqdame chalva sakte haim, apne gundon se rah chalte pitva sakte haim; lekin onkar in baton se nahin darta. jab tak uski deh men jan hai, vah atatayiyon ki khabar leta rahega. sahsa motarkar ki avaz sun kar vah chaunke. turant kagaz lekar apna lekh shuru kar diya. aur ek hi pal men NAmERai Sahib ne unke kamre men qadam rakkha. NAmEOnkarnath ne na unka svagat kiya, na kushal-kshen puchha, na kursi di. unhen is tarah dekha mano koi mulazim unki adalat men aya ho aur rob se mile hue avaz men puchha -- apko mera purza mil gaya tha? maim vah chitthi likhne ke lie badhya nahin tha, mera pharz yah tha ki svayam uski tahaqiqat karta; lekin murauvat men siddhanton ki kuchh na kuchh hatya karni hi parti hai. kya us batchit men kuchh satya hai? NAmERai Sahib uska satya hona asvikar na kar sake. halam ki abhi tak unhen jurmane ke rupae nahin mile the aur vah unke pane se saf inkar kar sakte the; lekin vah dekhna chahte the ki yah mahashay kis pahlu par chalte haim. NAmEOnkarnath ne khed prakat karte hue kaha -- tab to mere lie us batchit ko prakashit karne ke siva aur koi rasta nahin hai. mujhe iska duhkh hai ki mujhe apne ek param hitaishi dost ki alochna karni par rahi hai; lekin pharz ke age vyakti koi chiz nahin. sampadak agar apna pharz na pura kar sake, to use is asan par baithne ka koi haq nahin hai. NAmERai Sahib kursi par dat gaye aur pan ki gilauriyam mumh men bharkar bole -- lekin yah apke haq men achchha na hoga. mujhe jo kuchh hona hai, pichhe hoga, apko turant saza mil jayga; agar ap mitron ki parvah nahin karte, to maim bhi usi kaimre ka aadmi hum. NAmEOnkarnath ne shahid ka gaurav dharan karke kaha -- iska to mujhe kabhi dar nahin hua. jis din maimne chitthi-sampadan ka bhar liya, usi din pranon ka moh chhod diya, aur mere pas ek sampadak ki sabse shandar maut yahi hai ki vah nyay aur satya ki raksha karta hua apna balidan kar de. achchhi bat hai. maim apki chunauti svikar karta hum. maim ab tak apko dost samjhata aya tha; magar ab ap larne hi par taiyar haim, to larai hi sahi. akhir maim apke chitthi ka panchguna chanda kyon deta hum. sirph isilie ki vah mera gulam bana rahe. mujhe paramatma ne rais banaya hai. pachahattar rupya deta hum; isilie ki apka mumh band rahe. jab ap ghate ka rona rote haim aur madad ki apil karte haim, aur aisi shayad hi koi timahi jati ho, jab apki apil na niklati ho, to maim aise mauqe par apki kuchh na kuchh madad kar deta hum. kisalie! dipavli, dashara, holi men apke yaham baina bhejta hum, aur sal men pachchis bar apki davat karta hum, kisalie! ap rishvat aur pharz donon sath-sath nahin nibha sakte. NAmEOnkarnath uttejit hokar bole, -- maimne kabhi rishvat nahin li. NAmERai Sahib ne phatkara -- agar yah bartav rishvat nahin hai to rishvat kya hai? zara mujhe samjha dijie. kya ap samjhate haim, apko chhorkar aur sabhi gadhe haim jo nihsvarth-bhav se apka ghata pura karte haim. nikalie apni bahi aur batalaie ab tak apko meri riyasat se kitna mil chuka hai. mujhe yakin hai, hazaron ki raqam niklegi; agar apko svadeshi-svadeshi chillakar videshi davaon aur vastuon ka vijnyapan chhapne men sharam nahin ati, to maim apne asamiyon se damr, tavan aur jumarna lete sharamaum? yah na samajhie ki ap hi kisanon ke hit ka bira uthaye hue haim. mujhe kisanon ke sath jalna-marna hai, mujhse barhkar dusra unka hitechchhu nahin ho sakta; lekin meri guzar kaise ho! afsaron ko davten kaham se dum, sarkari chande kaham se dum, khandan ke saikron admiyo ki zarurten kaise puri karum. mere ghar ka kya kharch hai, yah shayad ap jante haim. to kya mere ghar men rupye phalte hai? maimne kisi talluqedar ko vade ka pakka dekha hi nahin. dada ek talluqedar ke naukar the. sal-sal bhar talab nahin milti thi. use chhorkar dusre ki naukri ki. usne do sal tak ek pai na di. ek bar dada garam pade, to markar bhaga diya. inke vadon ka koi qarar nahin.

bheja karo. kah denge, kal ana. kal apne ilaqe par chale jayange. tin mahine men lautenge.
NAmEOnkarnath samshay men par gaye. thik to hai, kahim NAmERai Sahib pichhe se mukar gaye, to vah kya kar lenge. phir bhi dil mazbut karke kaha -- aisa nahin ho sakta. kam-se-kam NAmERai Sahib ko maim itna dhokhebaz nahin samjhata. mera unke yaham kuchh baqi nahin hai.
gomti ne usi shak ke bhav se kaha -- isi se to maim tumhen buddh kahti hum. zara kisi ne hamadardi dikhayi aur tum phul uthe. ye mote rais haim. inke pet men aise kitne vade hazam ho sakte haim. jitne vade karte haim, agar sab pura karne lagen, to bhikh mangne ki naubat a jay. mere gamv ke thakur sahab to do-do, tin-tin sal-tak baniyon ka hisab na karte the. naukron ka hisab to nam ke lie dete the. sal-bhar kam liya, jab naukar ne tanakhvah manga, markar nikal diya. kai bar isi nadihendi men skul se unke larkon ke nam kat gaye. akhir unhomne larkon ko ghar bula liya. ek bar rel ka tikat udhar manga tha. yah NAmERai Sahib bhi to unhim ke bhaiband haim. chalo khana karo aur chakki piso, jo tumhare bhagya men likha hai. yah samajh lo ki ye bade aadmi tumhen phatakarte rahen, vahi achchha hai. yah tumhen ek paisa denge, to uska chauguna apne asamiyon se vasul kar lenge. abhi unke vishay men jo kuchh chahte ho, likhte ho. tab to thakurasohati hi kahni paregi. pandit ji khana kar rahe the; par kaur mumh men phamsa hua jan parta tha. akhir bina dil ka bojh halka kiye khana karna mushkil ho gaya. bole -- agar rupae na diye, to aisi khabar lunga ki yad karenge. unki choti mere hath men hai. gamv ke log jhuthi khabar nahin de sakte. sachchi khabar dete to unki jan niklati hai, jhuthi khabar kya denge! NAmERai Sahib ke khilaf ek riport mere pas ayi hai. chhap dum, bacha ko ghar se niklana mushkil ho jay. mujhe yah khairat nahin de rahe haim, bade dabsat men parkar is rah par aye haim. pahle dhamkiya dikha rahe the, jab dekha isse kam na chalega, to yah chadha phenka. maimne bhi socha, ek inke thik ho jane se to desh se anyay mita jata nahin, phir kyon na is dan ko svikar kar lum. maim apne adarsh se gir gaya hum zarur; lekin itne par bhi NAmERai Sahib ne daga ki, to maim bhi shathta par utar aunga. jo garibon ko lutta hai, usko lutne ke lie apni atma ko bahut samjhana na parega.
