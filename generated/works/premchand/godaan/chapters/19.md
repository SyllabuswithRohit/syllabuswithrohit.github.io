# Godaan — Adhyay 19

**Munshi Premchand**

19.
mirza NAmEKhurshed ka hata klab bhi hai, kachhari bhi, akhara bhi. din bhar jamghat laga rahta hai. muhalle men akhare ke lie kahim jagah nahin milti thi. mirza ne ek chhappar dalavakar akhara banava diya hai; vaham nitya sau-pachas larintaye a jutte haim. mirzaji bhi unke sath zor karte haim. muhalle ki panchayten bhi yahim hoti haim. miyam-bibi aur sas-bahu aur bhai-bhai ke jhagre-tante yahim chukaye jate haim. muhalle ke samajik jivan ka yahi kendra hai aur rajanitik andolan ka bhi. aye din sabhaen hoti rahti haim. yahim svayamsevak tikte haim, yahim unke program bante haim, yahim se nagar ka rajanitik sanchalan hota hai. pichhle jalse men NAmEmalati nagar-kangres-kameti ki sabhanetri chun li gayi hai. tab se is sthan ki raunaq aur bhi barh gayi hai. NAmEGobar ko yaham rahte sal bhar ho gaya. ab vah sidha-sadha gramin yuvak nahin hai. usne bahut kuchh duniya dekh li aur samsar ka rang-dhang bhi kuchh-kuchh samjhane laga hai. mul men vah ab bhi dehati hai, paise ko dant se pakrata hai, svarth ko kabhi nahin chhorta, aur mehnat se ji nahin churata, na kabhi himmat harta hai; lekin shahar ki hava use bhi lag gayi hai. usne pahle mahine to sirph majuri ki or adha pet khakar thore se rupae bacha liye. phir vah kachalu aur matar aur dahi-bade ke khonche lagane laga. idhar zyada phayda dekha, to naukri chhod di. gamiryon men sharbat aur baraf ki dukan bhi khol di. len-den men khada tha isalie uski sakh jam gayi. jare aye, to usne sharbat ki dukan utha di aur garm chay pilane laga. ab uski rozana amdani dhai-tin rupae se kam nahin. usne angrezi faishan ke bal katva lie haim, mahin dhoti aur pamp-shu pahnata hai, ek lal uni chadar kharid li aur pan sigret ka shauqin ho gaya hai. sabhaon men ane-jane se use kuchh-kuchh rajanitik jnyan bhi ho chala hai. rashtra aur varg ka arth samjhane laga hai. samajik rurhiyon ki pratishtha aur lok-ninda ka dar ab usmen bahut kam rah gaya hai. aye din ki panchayton ne use nissankoch bana diya hai. jis bat ke pichhe vah yaham ghar se dur, mumh chhipaye pada hua hai, usi tarah ki, balki usse bhi kahim nindaspad baten yaham nitya hua karti haim, aur koi bhagta nahin. phir vahi kyon itna dare aur mumh churaye! itne dinon men usne ek paisa bhi ghar nahin bheja. vah mam-pita ko rupae-paise ke mamle men itna hoshiyar nahin samjhata. ve log to rupae pate hi akash men urne lagenge. dada ko turant gaya karne ki aur ammam ko gahne banvane ki dhun savar ho jaygi. aise bekar ke kamon ke lie uske pas rupae nahin haim. ab vah chhota-mota mahajan hai. paros ke ekkevalon garivanon aur dhobiyon ko sud par rupae udhar deta hai. is das-gyarah mahine men hi usne apni mehnat aur kifayat aur purusharth se apna sthan bana liya hai aur ab NAmEJhunia ko yahim lakar rakhne ki bat soch raha hai. tisre pahar ka samay hai. vah sarak ke nal par nahakar aya hai aur sham ke lie alu ubal raha hai ki mirza NAmEKhurshed akar dvar par khade ho gaye. NAmEGobar ab unka naukar nahin hai; par adab usi tarah karta hai aur unke lie jan dene ko taiyar rahta hai. dvar par jakar puchha -- kya hukma hai sarkar?
mirza ne khade-khade kaha -- tumhare pas kuchh rupae hon, to de do. aj tin din se botal khali padi hui hai, ji bahut bechain ho raha hai.
NAmEGobar ne iske pahle bhi do-tin bar mirzaji ko rupae diye the; par ab tak vasul na kar saka tha. taqaza karte darta tha aur mirzaji rupae lekar dena na jante the. unke hath men rupae tikte hi na the. idhar aye udhar gayab. yah to na kah saka, maim rupae na dunga ya mere pas rupae nahin haim, sharab ki ninda karne laga -- ap ise chhod kyon nahin dete sarkar? kya iske pine se kuchh fayda hota hai?
mirzaji ne kothri ke andar khat par baithte hue kaha -- tum samjhate ho, maim chhorna nahin chahta aur shauq se pita hum. maim iske bagair zinda nahin rah sakta. tum apne rupae ke lie na daro, maim ek-ek kauri ada kar dunga.
NAmEGobar avichlit raha -- maim sach kahta hum malik! mere pas is samay rupae hote to apse inkar karta?
do rupae bhi nahin de sakte?
is samay to nahin haim.
meri anguthi giro rakh lo.
NAmEGobar ka man lalcha utha; magar bat kaise badle. bola -- yah ap kya kahte haim malik, rupae hote to apko de deta, anguthi ki kaun bat thi?
mirza ne apne avaz men bada bechara agrah bharkar kaha -- maim phir tumse kabhi na mangunga NAmEGobar! mujhse khada nahin hua ja raha hai. is sharab ki badaulat maimne lakhon ki haisiyat bigar di aur bhikhari ho gaya. ab mujhe bhi zid par gayi hai ki chahe bhikh hi mangni pade, ise chhorunga nahin.
jab NAmEGobar ne abki bar inkar kiya, to mirza sahab nirash hokar chale gaye. shahar men unke hazaron milne vale the. kitne hi unki badaulat ban gaye the. kitnon hi ko garhe samay par madad ki thi; par aise se vah milna bhi na pasand karte the. unhen aise hazaron latke malum the, jisse vah samay-samay par rupyon ke dher laga dete the; par paise ki unki nigah men koi qadra na thi. unke hath men rupae jaise katte the. kisi na kisi bahane urakar hi unka chitt shant hota tha. NAmEGobar alu chhilne laga. sal-bhar ke andar hi vah itna kaiyam ho gaya tha aur paisa jorne men itna kushal ki achraj hota tha. jis kothri men vah rahta hai, vah mirza sahab ne di hai. is kothri aur baramde ka kiraya badi asani se panch rupya mil sakta hai. NAmEGobar lagbhag sal bhar se usmen rahta hai; lekin mirza ne na kabhi kiraya manga na usne diya. unhen shayad khayal bhi na tha ki is kothri ka kuchh kiraya bhi mil sakta hai. thodi der men ek ikkevala rupye mangne aya. aladin nam tha, sir ghuta hua, khichri daadhi, aur kana. uski ladki bida ho rahi thi. panch rupae ki use badi zarurat thi. NAmEGobar ne ek ana rupya sud par rupae de diye. aladin ne dhanyavad dete hue kaha -- bhaiya, ab bal-bachchon ko bula lo. kab tak hath se thokte rahoge.
NAmEGobar ne shahar ke kharch ka rona roya -- thodi amdani men ghar-parivar kaise chalegi?
aladin biri jalata hua bola -- kharach allah dega bhaiya! socho, kitna aram milega. maim to kahta hum, jitna tum akele kharach karte ho, usi men ghar-parivar chal jaygi. aurat ke hath men badi barakkat hoti hai. khuda qasam, jab maim akela yaham rahta tha, to chahe kitna hi kamaum kha-pi sab barabar. biri-tamakhu ko bhi paisa na rahta. us par hairani. thake-mande ao, to ghore ko khilao aur tahalao. phir nanabai ki dukan par dauro. nak men dam a gaya. jab se gharvali a gayi hai, usi kamai men uski rotiyam bhi nikal ati haim aur aram bhi milta hai. akhir aadmi aram ke lie hi to kamata hai. jab jan khapakar bhi aram na mila, to zindagi hi garat ho gayi. maim to kahta hum, tumhari kamai barh jaygi bhaiya! jitni der men alu aur matar ubalte ho, utni der men do-char pyale chay bech loge. ab chay barhon mas chalti hai! rat ko letoge to gharvali pamv dabayegi. sari thakan mit jaygi.
yah bat NAmEGobar ke man men baith gayi. ji uchat ho gaya. ab to vah NAmEJhunia ko lakar hi rahega. alu chulhe par charhe rah gaye, aur usne ghar chalne ki taiyari kar di; magar yad aya ki holi a rahi hai; isalie holi ka saman bhi leta chale. kripan logon men utsavon par dil kholkar kharch karne ki jo ek pravritti hoti hai, vah usmen bhi sajag ho gayi. akhir isi din ke lie to kauri-kauri jod raha tha. vah mam, bahnon aur NAmEJhunia ke lie ek-ek jori sari le jayga. NAmEHori ke lie ek dhoti aur ek chadar. NAmESona ke lie tel ki shishi le jayga, aur ek jora chappal. NAmERupa ke lie japani churiyam aur NAmEJhunia ke lie ek pitari, jismen tel, sindur aur aina hoga. bachche ke lie top aur frak jo bazar men bana banaya milta hai. usne rupae nikale aur bazar chala. dophar tak sari chizen a gayim. bistar bhi bandh gaya, muhallevalon ko khabar ho gayi, NAmEGobar ghar ja raha hai. kai mard-aurten use bida karne aye. NAmEGobar ne unhen apna ghar saumpte hue kaha -- tumhim logon par chhore jata hum. bhagavan ne chaha to holi ke dusre din lautunga.
ek yuvti ne muskarakar kaha -- mehriya ko bina liye na ana, nahin ghar men na ghusne paoge.
dusri praurha ne shiksha di -- ham, aur kya, bahut dinon tak chulha phunk chuke. thikane se roti to milegi!
NAmEGobar ne sabko ram-ram kiya. hindu bhi the, musalman bhi the, sabhi men mitrabhav tha, sab ek-dusre ke duhkh-dard ke sathi. roza rakhanevale roza rakhte the. ekadshi rakhanevale ekadshi. kabhi-kabhi vinod-bhav se ek-dusre par chhinte bhi ura lete the. NAmEGobar aladin ki namaz ko utha-baithi kahta, aladin pipal ke niche sthapit saikron chhote-bade shivling ko batkhare banata; lekin sampradayik naphrat ka nam bhi na tha. NAmEGobar ghar ja raha hai. sab use hamsi-khushi bida karna chahte haim. itne men bhure ekka lekar a gaya. abhi din-bhar ka dhava markar aya tha. khabar mili, NAmEGobar ghar ja raha hai. vaise hi ekka idhar pher diya. ghore ne apatti ki. use kai chabuk lagaye. NAmEGobar ne ekke par saman rakha, ekka barha, pahunchane vale gali ke mor tak pahunchane aye, tab NAmEGobar ne sabko ram-ram kiya aur ekke par baith gaya. sarak par ekka sarpat daura ja raha tha. NAmEGobar ghar jane ki khushi men mast tha. bhure use ghar pahunchane ki khushi men mast tha. aur ghora tha panidar, ghora chala ja raha tha. bat ki bat men steshan a gaya. NAmEGobar ne khush hokar ek rupya kamre se nikal kar bhure ki taraf barhakar kaha -- lo, gharvali ke lie mithai lete jana.
bhure ne kritajnyata-bhare beizzati se uski or dekha -- tum mujhe gair samjhate ho bhaiya! ek din zara ekke par baith gaye to maim tumse inam lunga. jaham tumhara pasina gire, vaham khun girane ko taiyar hum. itna chhota dil nahin paya hai. aur le bhi lum, to gharvali mujhe jita chhoregi?
NAmEGobar ne phir kuchh na kaha. lajjit hokar apna asbab utara aur tikat lene chal diya.
