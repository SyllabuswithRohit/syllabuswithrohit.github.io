# Godaan — Adhyay 2

**Munshi Premchand**

2.
semri aur belari donon avadh-prant ke gamv haim. zile ka nam batane ki koi zarurat nahin. NAmEHori belari men rahta hai, NAmERai Sahib amarpal simh semri men. donon gamvon men sirph panch mil ka antar hai. pichhle satyagrah-larai men NAmERai Sahib ne bada yash kamaya tha. kaumsil ki membari chhorkar jel chale gaye the. tab se unke ilaqe ke asamiyon ko unse badi shraddha ho gayi thi. yah nahin ki unke ilaqe men asamiyon ke sath koi khas riyayat ki jati ho, ya damr aur begar ki karai kuchh kam ho; magar yah sari badnami mukhtaron ke sir jati thi. NAmERai Sahib ki kirti par koi kalank na lag sakta tha. vah bechare bhi to usi intzam ke gulam the. zabte ka kam to jaise hota chala aya hai, vaisa hi hoga. NAmERai Sahib ki sajjanta us par koi asar na dal sakti thi; isalie amdani aur adhikar men jau-bhar ki bhi kami na hone par bhi unka yash mano barh gaya tha. asamiyon se vah hams kar bol lete the. yahi kya kam hai? simh ka kam to shikar karna hai; agar vah garjane aur gurarne ke badle mithi boli bol sakta, to use ghar baithe manmana shikar mil jata. shikar ki khoj men jangal men na bhatkana parta.
NAmERai Sahib rashtravadi hone par bhi hukkam se mel-jol banaye rakhte the. unki nazren aur daliyam aur karmachariyon ki dasturiyam jaisi ki taisi chali ati thim. sahitya aur sangit ke premi the, drama ke shauqin, achchhe vakta the, achchhe lekhak, achchhe nishane-baz. unki patni ko mare aj das sal ho chuke the; magar dusri shadi na ki thi. hams-bolkar apne vidhur jivan ko bahlate rahte the.
NAmEHori dyorhi par pahuncha to dekha jeth ke dashhare ke avsar par honevale dhanush-yajnya ki badi zoron se taiyariyam ho rahi haim. kahim rang-manch ban raha tha, kahim mandap, kahim mehmano ka atithya-ghar, kahim dukandaro ke lie dukanen. dhup tez ho gayi thi; par NAmERai Sahib khud kam men lage hue the. apne pita se jaydad ke sath-sath unhomne ram ki bhakti bhi payi thi aur dhanush-yajnya ko natak ka rup dekar use shisht manoranjan ka sadhan bana diya tha. is avsar par unke yar-dost, hakim-hukkam sabhi nimantrit hote the. aur do-tin din ilaqe men badi chahal-pahal rahti thi. NAmERai Sahib ka parivar bahut bahut bada tha. koi derh sau sardar ek sath khana karte the. kai chacha the, darjanon chachere bhai, kai sage bhai, bisiyon nate ke bhai. ek chacha sahab radha ke ananya upasak the aur barabar vrindaban men rahte the. bhakti-ras ke kitne hi kavita rach dale the aur samay-samay par unhen chhapavakar doston ki bhent kar dete the. ek dusre chacha the, jo ram ke paramabhakt the aur farsi-bhasha men ramayan ka anuvad kar rahe the. riyasat se sabke vasike bandhe hue the. kisi ko koi kam karne ki zarurat na thi.
NAmEHori mandap men khada soch raha tha ki apne ane ki khabar kaise de ki sahsa NAmERai Sahib udhar hi a nikle aur use dekhte hi bole --are! tu a gaya NAmEHori, maim to tujhe bulavanevala tha. dekh, abki tujhe raja janak ka mali banna parega. samajh gaya na, jis vaqta janki ji mandir men puja karne jati haim, usi vaqta tu ek guladasta liye khada rahega aur janki ji ki bhent karega. galti na karna aur dekh, asamiyon se takid karke kah dena ki sab-ke-sab shagun karne ayen. mere sath kothi men a, tujhse kuchh baten karni haim. vah age-age kothi ki or chale, NAmEHori pichhe-pichhe chala. vahim ek ghane vriksh ki chhaya men ek kursi par baith gaye aur NAmEHori ko zameen par baithne ka ishara karke bole -- samajh gaya, maimne kya kaha. karkun ko to jo kuchh karna hai, vah karega hi, lekin asami jitne man se asami ki bat sunta hai, karkun ki nahin sunta. hamen inhim panch-sat dinon men bis hazar ka intzam karna hai. kaise hoga, samajh men nahin ata. tum sochte hoge, mujh take ke aadmi se malik kyon apna dukhra le baithe. kisse apne man ki kahum? na jane kyon tumhare upar yakin hota hai. itna janta hum ki tum man men mujh par hamsoge nahin. aur hamso bhi, to tumhari hamsi maim varadasht kar sakunga. nahin sah sakta unki hamsi, jo apne barabar ke haim, kyunki unki hamsi men irshya, vyang aur jalan hai. aur ve kyon na hamsenge. maim bhi to unki durdasha aur musibat aur patan par hamsta hum, dil kholkar, taliyam bajakar. jaydad aur sahridyata men vair hai. ham bhi dan dete haim, dharm karte haim. lekin jante ho, kyon? sirph apne barabarvalo ko nicha dikhane ke lie. hamara dan aur dharm kora ahankar hai, vishuddh ahankar. ham men se kisi par digri ho jay, qurqi a jay, baqaya malaguzari ki illat men havalat ho jay, kisi ka javan beta mar jay, kisi ki vidhva bahu nikal jay, kisi ke ghar men ag lag jay, koi kisi veshya ke hathon ullu ban jay, ya apne asamiyon ke hathon pit jay, to uske aur sabhi bhai us par hamsenge, baglen bajayenge, mano sare samsar ki sampada mil gayi hai. aur milenge to itne pyaar se, jaise hamare pasine ki jagah khun bahane ko taiyar haim. are, aur to aur, hamare chachere, phuphere, mamere, mausere bhai jo isi riyasat ki badaulat mauj ura rahe haim, kavita kar rahe haim aur jue khel rahe haim, sharaben pi rahe haim aur aiyashi kar rahe haim, vah bhi mujhse jalte haim, aur aj mar jaum to ghi ke chirag jalayen. mere duhkh ko duhkh samajhanevala koi nahin. unki nazron men mujhe dukhi hone ka koi adhikar hi nahin hai. maim agar rota hum, to duhkh ki hamsi urata hum. maim agar bimar hota hum, to mujhe sukh hota hai. maim agar apna byah karke ghar men jhagra nahin barhata to yah meri nich svarthaprata hai; agar byah kar lum, to vah vilasandhata hogi. agar sharab nahin pita to meri kanjusi hai. sharab pine lagum, to vah praja ka rakt hoga. agar aiyashi nahin karta, to arsik hum, aiyashi karne lagum, to phir kahna hi kya. in logon ne mujhe bhog-vilas men phamsane ke lie kam chalen nahin chalim aur ab tak chalte jate haim. unki yahi chah hai ki maim andha ho jaum aur ye log mujhe lut len, aur mera dharm yah hai ki sab kuchh dekhkar bhi kuchh na dekhum. sab kuchh jankar bhi gadha bana rahum.
NAmERai Sahib ne gari ko age barhane ke lie do bire pan khaye aur NAmEHori ke mumh ki or takne lage, jaise uske manobhavon ko parhna chahte hon.
NAmEHori ne himmat batorkar kaha -- ham samjhate the ki aisi baten hamim logon men hoti haim, par jan parta hai, bade admiyo men unki kami nahin hai.
NAmERai Sahib ne mumh pan se bharkar kaha -- tum hamen bada aadmi samjhate ho? hamare nam bade haim, par darshan thore. garibon men agar irshya ya vair hai to svarth ke lie ya pet ke lie. aisi irshya aur vair ko maim kshamya samjhata hum. hamare mumh ki roti koi chhin le to uske gale men ungli dalkar nikalna hamara dharm ho jata hai. agar ham chhod den, to devta haim. bade admiyo ki irshya aur vair sirph khushi ke lie hai. ham itne bade aadmi ho gaye haim ki hamen nichta aur kutilta men hi nihsvarth aur param khushi milta hai. ham devatapan ke us darje par pahunch gaye haim jab hamen dusron ke rone par hamsi ati hai. ise tum chhoti sadhna mat samjho. jab itna bada kutumb hai, to koi-na-koi to hamesha bimar rahega hi. aur bade admiyo ke bimari bhi bade hote haim. vah bada aadmi hi kya, jise koi chhota bimari ho. mamuli jvar bhi a jay, to hamen sarsam ki dava di jati hai, mamuli phumsi bhi nikal aye, to vah zaharbad ban jati hai. ab chhote sarjan aur majhole sarjan aur bade sarjan tar se bulaye ja rahe haim, masihulamulk ko lane ke lie dilli aadmi bheja ja raha hai, bhishagacharya ko lane ke lie kalakatta. udhar devalay men durgapath ho raha hai aur jyotishacharya kundli ka soch kar rahe haim aur tantra ke acharya apne anushthan men lage hue haim. raja sahab ko yamraj ke mumh se nikalne ke lie daur lagi hui hai. doktar aur daktar is tak men rahte haim ki kab sir men dard ho aur kab unke ghar men sone ki varsha ho. aur ye rupae tumse aur tumhare bhaiyon se vasul kiye jate haim, bhale ki nok par. mujhe to yahi hairani hota hai ki kyon tumhari ahon ka davanal hamen bhasma nahin kar dalta; magar nahin, hairani karne ki koi bat nahin. bhasma hone men to bahut der nahin lagti, dard bhi thodi hi der ki hoti hai. ham jau-jau aur angul-angul aur por-por bhasma ho rahe haim. us hahakar se bachne ke lie ham pulis ki, hukkam ki, adalat ki, vakilon ki sharan lete haim. aur rupvati aurat ki tarah sabhi ke hathon ka khilauna bante haim. duniya samjhati hai, ham bade sukhi haim. hamare pas ilaqe, mahal, savariyam, naukar-chakar, qaraz, veshyaen, kya nahin haim, lekin jiski atma men bal nahin, abhiman nahin, vah aur chahe kuchh ho, aadmi nahin hai. jise dushman ke dar ke mare rat ko nind na ati ho, jiske duhkh par sab hamsen aur ronevala koi na ho, jiski choti dusron ke pairon ke niche dabi ho, jo bhog-vilas ke nashe men apne ko bilkul bhul gaya ho, jo hukkam ke talve chatta ho aur apne adhinon ka khun chusta ho, use maim sukhi nahin kahta. vah to samsar ka sabse abhaga insaan hai. sahab shikar khelne ayen ya daure par, mera pharz hai ki unki dum ke pichhe laga rahum. unki bhaumhon par shikan padi aur hamare jan sukhe. unhen khush karne ke lie ham kya nahin karte. magar vah pachra sunane lagum to shayad tumhen yakin na aye. daliyon aur rishvaton tak to khair ganimat hai, ham sijde karne ko bhi taiyar rahte haim. muftakhori ne hamen apang bana diya hai, hamen apne purusharth par leshamatra bhi yakin nahin, sirph afsaron ke samne dum hila-hilakar kisi tarah unke kripapatra bane rahna aur unki madad se apni praja par atank zamana hi hamara udyam hai. pichhalaguon ki khushamad ne hamen itna abhimani aur tunakamizaj bana diya hai ki hammen shil, narmi aur seva ka lop ho gaya hai. maim to kabhi-kabhi sochta hum ki agar sarkar hamare ilaqe chhinkar hamen apni rozi ke lie mehnat karna sikha de to hamare sath mahan upkar kare, aur yah to phaisla hai ki ab sarkar bhi hamari raksha na karegi. hamse ab uska koi svarth nahin niklata. lakshan kah rahe haim ki bahut jald hamare varg ki hasti mit janevali hai. maim us din ka svagat karne ko taiyar baitha hum. ishvar vah din jald laye. vah hamare uddhar ka din hoga. ham paristhatiyon ke shikar bane hue haim. yah hal hi hamara sarvanash kar rahi hai aur jab tak jaydad ki yah beri hamare pairon se na niklegi, jab tak yah abhishap hamare sir par mandrata rahega, ham manvata ka vah pad na pa sakenge jis par pahunchna hi jivan ka antim lakshya hai.
NAmERai Sahib ne phir gilauri-dan nikala aur kai gilauriyam nikalkar mumh men bhar lim. kuchh aur kahne vale the ki ek chaprasi ne akar kaha -- sarkar begaron ne kam karne se inkar kar diya hai. kahte haim, jab tak hamen khane ko na milega ham kam na karenge. hamne dhamkaya, to sab kam chhorkar alag ho gaye.
NAmERai Sahib ke mathe par bal par gaye. ankhen nikalkar bole --chalo, maim in dushton ko thik karta hum. jab kabhi khane ko nahin diya, to aj yah nayi bat kyon? ek ane roz ke hisab se majuri milegi, jo hamesha milti rahi hai; aur is majuri par unhen kam karna hoga, sidhe karen ya terhe.
phir NAmEHori ki or dekhkar bole -- tum ab jao NAmEHori, apni taiyari karo. jo bat maimne kahi hai, uska khayal rakhna. tumhare gamv se mujhe kam-se-kam panch sau ki ummid hai.
NAmERai Sahib jhallate hue chale gaye. NAmEHori ne man men socha, abhi yah kaisi-kaisi niti aur dharam ki baten kar rahe the aur ekaek itne garam ho gaye!
surya sir par a gaya tha. uske tej se abhibhut hokar vrikshon ne apna pasar samet liya tha. akash par mityala gard chhaya hua tha aur samne ki prithvi kampti hui jan parti thi.
NAmEHori ne apna danda uthaya aur ghar chala. shagun ke rupye kaham se ayenge, yahi chinta uske sir par savar thi.
