NAmEKhanna ne bat katkar kaha -- nahin govindi, paisa kamane ke lie apne men samskar chahiye. sirph kaushal se paisa nahin milta. iske lie bhi tyag aur tapasya karni parti hai. shayad itni sadhna men ishvar bhi mil jay. hamari sari atmik aur bauddhik aur sharirik shaktiyon ke samanjasya ka nam paisa hai.
govindi ne vipakshi na bankar madhyasth bhav se kaha -- maim manti hum ki paisa ke lie thodi tapasya nahin karni parti; lekin phir bhi hamne use jivan men jitne mahatva ki vastu samajh rakha hai, utna mahatva usmen nahin hai. maim to khush hum ki tumhare sir se yah bojh tala. ab tumhare larke aadmi honge, svarth aur abhiman ke putle nahin. jivan ka sukh dusron ko sukhi karne men hai, unko lutne men nahin. bura na manna, ab tak tumhare jivan ka arth tha atmaseva, bhog aur vilas. daiv ne tumhen us sadhan se vanchit karke tumhen zyada unche aur pavitra jivan ka rasta khol diya hai. yah siddhi prapt karne men agar kuchh takliph bhi ho, to uska svagat karo. tum ise musibat samjhate hi kyon ho? kyon nahin samjhate, tumhen anyay se larne ka yah avsar mila hai. mere soch men to pirak hone se pirit hona kahim shreshth hai. paisa khokar agar ham apni atma ko pa saken, to yah koi mahanga sauda nahin hai. nyay ke sainik bankar larne men jo gaurav, jo khushi hai, kya use itni jald bhul gaye?
govindi ke pile, sukhe mumh par tej ki aisi chamak thi, mano usmen koi vilakshan shakti a gayi ho, mano uski sari muk sadhna pragalbh ho uthi ho. NAmEmehta uski or bhakti-purn netron se tak rahe the, NAmEKhanna sir jhukaye ise daivi prerna samjhane ki cheshta kar rahe the aur NAmEmalati man men lajjit thi. govindi ke soch itne unche, uska dil itna bahut bada aur uska jivan itna ujjval hai!
