teenon aadamee kamare men daakhila hue| dvaara khulane kee aahata paate hee govindee bheetara se aakara bolee -- kyaa aapa log vaheen se aa rahe hain? mahaaraaja to badee buree khabara laayaa|
khannaa ke mana men aisaa prabala, na rukanevaalaa, toophaanee aavesha uthaa ki govindee ke charaNon para gira pade, aur use aa.Nsuon se dho den| bhaaree gale se bole -- haa.N priye, hama tabaaha ho gaye| unakee nirjeeva, niraasha aahata aatmaa saantvanaa ke lie vikala ho rahi thi; sachchee pyaara men doobee huee saantvanaa ke lie, us rogee kee taraha jo jeevana-sootra ksheeNa ho jaane para bhee vaidya ke mu.Nha kee ora ummeeda-bharee aa.Nkhon se taaka raha ho| vahee govindee jisa para unhonne hameshaa julma kiya, jisakaa hameshaa beijjatee kiya, jisase hameshaa bevaphaaee kee, jise hameshaa jeevana kaa bhaara samajhaa, jisakee mrityu kee hameshaa chaaha karte rahe, vahee is samaya jaise anchala men aasheervaada aur mangala aur abhaya liye una para vaara rahi thi, jaise una charaNon men hee unake jeevana kaa svarga ho, jaise vaha unake abhaage mastaka para haath rakhakara hee unakee praaNaheena dhamaniyon men phir rakta kaa sanchaara kar degee| mana kee is durbala dashaa men, is ghora vipatti men, maano vaha unhen galaa se lagaa lene ke lie khadee thi| naukaa para baithe hue jala-vihaara karte samaya hama jina chattaanon ko ghaataka samajhate hain, aur chaahate hain ki koee inhen khoda kar phenka detaa, unheen se, naukaa toota jaane para, hama chimata jaate hain|
govindee ne unhen ek sophaa para baithaa diya aur pyaara-komala aavaaja men bolee -- to tuma itanaa dil chhotaa kyon karte ho? paisaa ke lie, jo saare paapa kee jada hai? us paisaa se hamen kyaa sukha tha? sabere se aadhee raat taka ek-na-ek jhanjhata -- aatmaa kaa sarvanaasha! ladake tumase baat karane ko tarasa jaate the, tumhen sambandhiyon ko patra likhane taka kee phurasata na milatee thi| kyaa badee ijjata thi? haa.N, thi; kyonki duniyaa aaja taka paisaa kee poojaa karti chalee aayee hai| use tumase koee kaama naheen| jaba taka tumhaare paasa lakshmee hai, tumhaare saamane poo.Nchha hilaayegee| kala utanee hee bhakti se doosaron ke dvaara para sijade karegee| tumhaaree tarapha taakegee bhee naheen| satpurusha paisaa ke aage sira naheen jhukaate| vaha dekhate hain, tuma kyaa ho; agar tumamen sachchaaee hai, nyaaya hai, tyaaga hai, purushaartha hai, to ve tumhaaree poojaa karenge| naheen tumhen samaaja kaa luteraa samajhakara mu.Nha phera lenge; balki tumhaare dushmana ho jaaya.Nge! main galata to naheen kehti mehataajee?
mehataa ne maano svarga-svapna se chaunkakara kahaa -- galata? aapa vahee kaha rahi hain, jo sansaara ke mahaan purushon ne jeevana kaa saatvika anubhava karane ke baada kahaa hai| jeevana kaa sachchaa aadhaara yahee hai|
govindee ne mehataa ko sambodhita karake kahaa -- dhanee kauna hota hai, isakaa koee socha naheen karta| vahee jo apane kaushala se doosaron ko bevakoopha banaa sakataa hai ...|
khannaa ne baat kaatakara kahaa -- naheen govindee, paisaa kamaane ke lie apane men sanskaara chaahie| kevala kaushala se paisaa naheen milataa| isake lie bhee tyaaga aur tapasyaa karanee padatee hai| shaayada itanee saadhanaa men eeshvara bhee mila jaaya| hamaaree saaree aatmika aur bauddhika aur shaareerika shaktiyon ke saamanjasya kaa naama paisaa hai|
govindee ne vipakshee na banakara madhyastha bhaava se kahaa -- main maanatee hoo.N ki paisaa ke lie thodee tapasyaa naheen karanee padatee; lekin phir bhee hamane use jeevana men jitane mahatva kee vastu samajha rakhaa hai, utanaa mahatva usamen naheen hai| main to khusha hoo.N ki tumhaare sira se yaha bojha talaa| aba tumhaare ladake aadamee honge, svaartha aur abhimaana ke putale naheen| jeevana kaa sukha doosaron ko sukhee karane men hai, unako lootane men naheen| buraa na maananaa, aba taka tumhaare jeevana kaa artha tha aatmasevaa, bhoga aur vilaasa| daiva ne tumhen us saadhana se vanchita karake tumhen jyaadaa oo.Nche aur pavitra jeevana kaa raastaa khola diya hai| yaha siddhi praapta karane men agar kuchha takaleepha bhee ho, to usakaa svaagata karo| tuma ise vipatti samajhate hee kyon ho? kyon naheen samajhate, tumhen anyaaya se ladane kaa yaha avasara milaa hai| mere socha men to peedaka hone se peedita honaa kaheen shreshtha hai| paisaa khokara agar hama apanee aatmaa ko paa saken, to yaha koee maha.Ngaa saudaa naheen hai| nyaaya ke sainika banakara ladane men jo gaurava, jo ullaasa hai, kyaa use itanee jalda bhoola gaye?
govindee ke peele, sookhe mu.Nha para teja kee aisee chamaka thi, maano usamen koee vilakshaNa shakti aa gayee ho, maano usakee saaree mooka saadhanaa pragalbha ho uthee ho| mehataa usakee ora bhakti-poorNa netron se taaka rahe the, khannaa sira jhukaaye ise daivee preraNaa samajhane kee cheshtaa kar rahe the aur maalatee mana men lajjita thi| govindee ke socha itane oo.Nche, usakaa dil itanaa bahuta badaa aur usakaa jeevana itanaa ujjvala hai!
