# Godaan — Adhyay 29

**Munshi Premchand**

29.
NAmENohari un aurton men na thi, jo neki karke dariya men dal deti hai. usne neki ki hai, to uska khub dhindhora pitegi aur usse jitna yash mil sakta hai, usse kuchh zyada hi pane ke lie hath-pamv maregi. aise aadmi ko yash ke badle apyash aur badnami hi milti hai. neki na karna badnami ki bat nahin. apni chah nahin hai, ya samarthya nahin hai. iske lie koi hamen bura nahin kah sakta. magar jab ham neki karke uska ehsan jatane lagte haim, to vahi jiske sath hamne neki ki thi, hamara dushman ho jata hai, aur hamare ehsan ko mita dena chahta hai. vahi neki agar karanevalon ke dil men rahe, to neki hai, bahar nikal aye to badi hai. NAmENohari charon or kahti phirti thi -- bechara NAmEHori badi musibat men tha, beti ke byah ke lie zameen rehan rakh raha tha. maimne unki yah hal dekhi, to mujhe daya ayi. NAmEDhania se to ji jalta tha, vah ramr to mare ghamand ke dharti par pamv hi nahin rakhti. bechara NAmEHori chinta se ghula jata tha. maimne socha, is musibat men iski kuchh madad kar dum. akhir aadmi hi to aadmi ke kam ata hai. aur NAmEHori to ab koi gair nahin hai, mano chahe mano, vah tumhare natedar ho chuke. rupae nikal kar de diye; nahin, ladki ab tak baithi hoti. NAmEDhania bhala yah zit kab sunne lagi thi. rupae khairat diye the? badi denevali! sud mahajan bhi lega, tum bhi logi. ehsan kahe ka! dusron ko deti, sud ki jagah mul bhi gayab ho jata; hamne liya hai, to hath men rupae ate hi nak par rakh denge. hamim the ki tumhare ghar ka bis uthake pi gaye, aur kabhi mumh par nahin laye. koi yaham dvar par nahin khada hone deta tha. hamne tumhara marjad bana diya, tumhare mumh ki lali rakh li. rat ke das baje gaye the. savan ki andheri ghata chhayi thi. sare gamv men andhakar tha. NAmEHori ne khana karke tamakhu piya aur sone ja raha tha ki NAmEBhola akar khada ho gaya. NAmEHori ne puchha -- kaise chale NAmEBhola mahto! jab isi gamv men rahna hai, to kyon alag chhota-sa ghar nahin bana lete? gamv men log kaisi-kaisi kutsa uraya karte haim, kya yah tumhen achchha lagta hai? bura na manna, tumse rishta ho gaya hai, isalie tumhari badnami nahin suni jati, nahin mujhe kya karna tha. NAmEDhania usi samay lote men pani lekar NAmEHori ke sirhane rakhne ayi. sunkar boli -- dusra mard hota, to aisi aurat ka sir kat leta. NAmEHori ne danta -- kyon be-bat ki bat karti hai. pani rakh de aur ja. aj tu hi kurah chalne lage, to maim tera sir kat lunga? katne degi? NAmEDhania use pani ka ek chhinta markar boli -- kurah chale tumhari bahan, maim kyon kurah chalne lagi. maim to duniya ki bat kahti hum, tum mujhe galiyam dene lage. ab mumh mitha ho gaya hoga. aurat chahe jis raste jay, mard tukur-tukur dekhta rahe. aise mard ko maim mard nahin kahti. NAmEHori dil men kata jata tha. NAmEBhola usse apna dukh-dard kahne aya hoga. vah ulte usi par tut padi. zara garm hokar bola -- tu jo sare din apne hi man ki kiya karti hai, to maim tera kya bigar leta hum. kuchh kahta hum to katne daurti hai. yahi soch. NAmEDhania ne lallo-chappo karna na sikha tha, boli -- aurat ghi ka ghara lurhka de, ghar men ag laga de, mard sah lega; lekin uska kurah chalna koi mard na sahega. NAmEBhola dukhit avaz men bola -- tu bahut thik kahti hai NAmEDhania! besak mujhe uska sir kat lena chahiye tha, lekin ab utna paurukh to nahin raha. tu chalkar samjha de, maim sab kuchh karke har gaya. jab aurat ko bas men rakhne ka buta na tha, to sagai kyon ki thi? isi chhichhaledar ke lie? kya sochte the, vah akar tumhare pamv dabayegi, tumhen chilam bhar-bhar pilayegi aur jab tum bimar paroge to tumhari seva karegi? to aisi vahi aurat kar sakti hai, jisne tumhare sath javani ka sukh uthaya ho. meri samajh men yahi nahin ata ki tum use dekhkar lattu kaise ho gaye. kuchh dekh-bhal to kar liya hota ki kis svabhav ki hai, kis rang-dhang ki hai. tum to bhukhe siyar ki tarah tut pade. ab to tumhara dharam yahi hai ki gamrase se uska sir kat lo. phamsi hi to paoge. phamsi is chhichhaledar se achchhi. NAmEBhola ke khun men kuchh sphurti ayi. bola -- to tumhari yahi salah hai? NAmEDhania boli -- ham, meri salah hai. ab sau pachas baras to jioge nahin. samajh lena itni hi umir thi. NAmEHori ne ab ki zor se phatkara -- chup rah, badi ayi hai vaham se satavanti banke. zabaradasti chiriya tak to pinjre men rahti nahin, aadmi kya rahega. tum use chhod do NAmEBhola aur samajh lo, mar gayi aur jakar apne bal-bachchon men aram se raho. do roti khao aur ram ka nam lo. javani ke sukh ab gaye. vah aurat chanchal hai, badnami aur jalan ke siva tum usse koi sukh na paoge. NAmEBhola NAmENohari ko chhod de, namumkin! NAmENohari is samay bhi uski or rosh-bhari ankhon se tarerti hui jan parti thi; lekin nahin, NAmEBhola ab use chhod hi dega. jaisa kar rahi hai, uska phal bhoge. ankhon men amsu a gaye. bola -- NAmEHori bhaiya, is aurat ke pichhe meri jitni samsat ho rahi hai, maim hi janta hum. isi ke pichhe kamta se meri larai hui. burhape men yah dag bhi lagna tha, vah lag gaya. mujhe roz tana deti hai ki tumhari to ladki nikal gayi. meri ladki nikal gayi, chahe bhag gayi; lekin apne aadmi ke sath padi to hai, uske sukh-dukh ki sathin to hai. uski tarah to maimne aurat hi nahin dekhi. dusron ke sath to hamsti hai, mujhe dekha to kuppe-sa mumh phula liya. maim garib aadmi thahra, tin-char ane roz ki majuri karta hum. dudh-dahi, mamsamchhali, rabri-malai kaham se laum! NAmEBhola yaham se kasam karke apne ghar gaye. ab beton ke sath rahenge, bahut dhakke kha chuke; lekin dusre din subah NAmEHori ne dekha, to NAmEBhola NAmEDulari sahaain ki dukan se tamakhu lie chale ja rahe the. NAmEHori ne pukarna thik na samjha. asakti men aadmi apne bas men nahin rahta. vaham se akar NAmEDhania se bola -- NAmEBhola to abhi vahim hai. NAmENohari ne sachmuch in par koi jadu kar diya hai. NAmEDhania ne nak sikorkar kaha -- jaisi behya vah hai, vaisa hi behya yah hai. aise mard ko to chullu-bhar pani men dub marna chahiye. ab vah sekhi na jane kaham gayi. NAmEJhunia yaham ayi, to uske pichhe danda lie phir rahe the. izzat bigri jati thi. ab izzat nahin bigrati! NAmEHori ko NAmEBhola par daya a rahi thi. bechara is kulta ke pher men parkar apni zindagi barbad kiye dalta hai. chhorkar jay bhi, to kaise? aurat ko is tarah chhorkar jana kya sahaj hai? yah churail use vaham bhi to chain se na baithne degi! kahim panchayat karegi, kahim roti-kapde ka dava karegi. abhi to gamv hi ke log jante haim. kisi ko kuchh kahte sankoch hota hai. kanaphuskiya karke hi rah jate haim. tab to duniya bhi NAmEBhola hi ko bura kahegi. log yahi to kahenge, ki jab mard ne chhod diya, to bechari abla kya kare? mard bura ho, to aurat ki gardan kat lega. aurat buri ho, to mard ke mumh men kalikh laga degi. iske do mahine bad ek din gamv men yah khabar phaili ki NAmENohari ne mare juton ke NAmEBhola ki chand ganji kar di. varsha khatma ho gayi thi aur rabi bone ki taiyariyam ho rahi thim. NAmEHori ki ukh to nilam ho gayi thi. ukh ke bij ke lie use rupae na mile aur ukh na boi gayi. udhar dahina bail bhi baithaum ho gaya tha aur ek naye bail ke bina kam na chal sakta tha. NAmEPunia ka ek bail nale men girkar mar gaya tha, tab se aur bhi archan par gayi thi. ek din NAmEPunia ke khet men hal jata, ek din NAmEHori ke khet men. kheton ki jutai jaisi honi chahiye, vaisi na ho pati thi. NAmEHori hal lekar khet men gaya; magar NAmEBhola ki chinta bani hui thi. usne apne jivan men kabhi yah na suna tha ki kisi aurat ne apne pati ko jute se mara ho. juton se kya thappar ya ghumse se marne ki bhi koi bat use yad na ati thi; aur aj NAmENohari ne NAmEBhola ko juton se pita aur sab log tamasha dekhte rahe. is aurat se kaise us abhage ka gala chhute! ab to NAmEBhola ko kahim dub hi marna chahiye. jab zindagi men badnami aur durdasa ke siva aur kuchh na ho, to aadmi ka mar jana hi achchha. kaun NAmEBhola ke nam ko ronevala baitha hai. bete chahe kriya-karam kar den; lekin loklaj ke bas, amsu kisi ki ankh men na ayega. tirsana ke bas men parkar aadmi is tarah apni zindagi chaupat karta hai. jab koi ronevala hi nahin, to phir zindagi ka kya moh aur marne se kya darna! ek yah NAmENohari hai aur ek yah chamarin hai NAmESilia! dekhne-sunne men usse lakh darje achchhi. chahe to do ko khilakar khaye aur radhika bani ghume; lekin majuri karti hai, bhukhon marti hai aur matai ke nam par baithi hai, aur vah bedard bat bhi nahin puchhta. kaun jane, NAmEDhania mar gayi hoti, to aj NAmEHori ki bhi yahi dasa hoti. uski maut ki kalpana hi se NAmEHori ko romanch ho utha. NAmEDhania ki murti mansik netron ke samne akar khadi ho gayi -- seva aur tyag ki devi; zaban ki tez, par mon jaisa dil; paise-paise ke pichhe jan denevali, par maryada-raksha ke lie apna sarvasva hon kar dene ko taiyar. javani men vah kam rupvati na thi. NAmENohari uske samne kya hai. chalti thi, to rani-si lagti thi. jo dekhta tha, dekhta hi rah jata tha. yah NAmEPateshwari aur NAmEJhinguri tab javan the. donon NAmEDhania ko dekhkar chhati par hath rakh lete the. dvar ke sau-sau chakkar lagate the. NAmEHori unki tak men rahta tha; magar chherne ka koi bahana na pata tha. un dinon ghar men khane-pine ki badi tangi thi. pala par gaya tha aur kheton men bhusa tak na hua tha. log jharaberiyam kha-khakar din katte the. NAmEHori ko qahat ke kaimp men kam karne jana parta tha. chhah paise roz milte the. NAmEDhania ghar men akeli hi rahti thi; lekin kabhi kisi ne use kisi chhaila ki or takte nahin dekha. NAmEPateshwari ne ek bar kuchh chher ki thi. uska aisa mumhtor javab diya ki ab tak nahin bhule.
sahsa usne NAmEmatadin ko apni or ate dekha. qasai kahim ka, kaisa tilak lagaye hue hai, mano bhagavan ka asli bhagat hai. ranga hua siyar! aise bahman ko palagan kaun kare. NAmEmatadin ne pas akar kaha -- tumhara dahina to burha ho gaya NAmEHori, abki sinchai men na thahrega. koi panch sal hue honge ise laye?
NAmEHori ne dayen bail ki pith par hath rakhkar kaha -- kaisa panchvam, yah athvam chal raha hai bhai! ji to chahta hai, ise pimsin de dum; lekin kisan aur kisan ke bailan ko jamraj hi pimsin den, to mile. iski gardan par jua rakhte mera man kachotta hai. bechara sochta hoga, ab bhi chhutti nahin, ab kya mera har jotega kya? lekin apna koi qabu nahin. tum kaise chale? ab to ji achchha hai?
NAmEmatadin idhar ek mahine se maleriya jvar men pada raha tha. ek din to uski nari chhut gayi thi. charapai se niche utar diya gaya tha. tab se uske man men yah prerna hui thi ki NAmESilia ke sath atyachar karne ka use yah saza mila hai. jab usne NAmESilia ko ghar se nikala, tab vah garbhavti thi. use tanik bhi daya na ayi. pura pet men bachcha lekar bhi vah majuri karti rahi. agar NAmEDhania ne us daya na ki hoti to mar gayi hoti. kaisi-kaisi musibten jhelkar ji rahi hai. majuri bhi to is hal men nahin kar sakti. ab lajjit aur dravit hokar vah NAmESilia ko NAmEHori ke haste do rupae dene aya hai; agar NAmEHori use vah rupae de de, to vah uska bahut upkar manega.
NAmEHori ne kaha -- tumhim jakar kyon nahin de dete?
NAmEmatadin ne bechara-bhav se kaha -- mujhe uske pas mat bhejo NAmEHori mahto! kaun-sa mumh lekar jaum? dar bhi lag raha hai ki mujhe dekhkar kahim phatkar na sunane lage. tum mujh par itni daya karo. abhi mujhse chala nahin jata; lekin isi rupae ke lie ek jajman ke pas kos-bhar daura gaya tha. apni karni ka phal bahut bhog chuka. is bamhanai ka bojh ab nahin uthaye uthta. luk-chhipkar chahe jitna kukarm karo, koi nahin bolta. paratachchh kuchh nahin kar sakte, nahin kul men kalank lag jayga. tum use samjha dena, dada, ki mera jurm kshama kar de. yah dharam ka bandhan bada kara hota hai. jis samaj men janme aur pale, uski maryada ka palan to karna hi parta hai. aur kisi jati ka dharam bigar jay, use koi bises nuksan nahin hoti; bamhan ka dharam bigar jay, to vah kahim ka nahin rahta. uska dharam hi uske purvajon ki kamai hai. usi ki vah roti khata hai. is paraschit ke pichhe hamare tin sau bigar gaye. to jab bedhram hokar hi rahna hai, to phir jo kuchh karna hai paratachchh karunga. samaj ke nate aadmi ka agar kuchh dharam hai, to manushya ke nate bhi to uska kuchh dharam hai. samaj-dharam palne se samaj adar karta hai; magar manushya-dharam palne se to ishvar khush hota hai.
sham-samay jab NAmEHori ne NAmESilia ko darte-darte rupae diye, to vah jaise apni tapasya ka vardan pa gayi. duhkh ka bhar to vah akeli utha sakti thi. sukh ka bhar to akele nahin uthta. kise yah khushakhbari sunaye? NAmEDhania se vah apne dil ki baten nahin kar sakti. gamv men aur koi insaan nahin, jisse uski ghanishthata ho. uske pet men chuhe daur rahe the. NAmESona hi uski saheli thi. NAmESilia usse milne ke lie atur ho gayi. rat-bhar kaise sabra kare? man men ek andhi-si uth rahi thi. ab vah anath nahin hai. NAmEmatadin ne uski bamh phir pakad li. jivan-rasta men uske samne ab andheri, vikral mukhvali khai nahin hai; lahalhata hua hara-bhara maidan hai, jismen jharne ga rahe haim aur hiran kulelen kar rahe haim. uska rutha hua pyaar aj unmatt ho gaya hai. NAmEmatadin ko usne man men kitna pani pi-pikar kosa tha. ab vah unse kshamadan mangegi. usse sachmuch badi bhul hui ki usne usko sare gamv ke samne apamanit kiya. vah to chamarin hai, jat ki hethi, uska kya bigra? aj das-bis lagakar biradri ko roti de de, phir biradri men le li jaygi. un bechare ka to sada ke lie dharam nas ho gaya. vah marzad ab unhen phir nahin mil sakta. vah gussa men kitni andhi ho gayi thi ki sabse unke pyaar ka dhindhora pitti phiri. unka to dharam bhirasht ho gaya tha, unhen to gussa tha hi, uske sir par kyon bhut savar ho gaya? vah apne hi ghar chali jati, to kaun burai ho jati. ghar men use koi bandh to na leta. desh NAmEmatadin ki puja isilie to karta hai ki vah nen-dharam se rahte haim. vahi dharam nasht ho gaya, to vah kyon na uske khun ke pyase ho jate? zara der pahle tak uski nazar men sara dosh NAmEmatadin ka tha. aur ab sara dosh apna tha. sahridyata ne sahridyata paida ki. usne bachche ko chhati se lagakar khub pyaar kiya. ab use dekhkar sharm aur pachhtava nahin hoti. vah ab sirph uski daya ka chitthi nahin. vah ab uske pura matri pyaar aur garva ka aphsar hai.
kartik ki ruphali chandni prakriti par mitha sangit ki tarah chhai hui thi. NAmESilia ghar se nikli. vah NAmESona ke pas jakar yah sukh-batchit sunayegi. ab usse nahin raha jata. abhi to sanjh hui hai. dongi mil jaygi. vah qadam barhati hui chali. nadi par akar dekha, to dongi us par thi. aur manjhi ka kahim pata nahin. chand ghulkar jaise nadi men baha ja raha tha. vah ek pal khadi sochti rahi. phir nadi men ghus padi. nadi men kuchh aisa zyada pani to kya hoga. us khushi ke sagar ke samne vah nadi kya chiz thi? pani pahle to ghutnon tak tha, phir kamar tak aya aur ant men gardan tak pahunch gaya.
