# Godaan — Adhyay 34

**Munshi Premchand**

34.
NAmESilia ka larka ab do sal ka ho raha tha aur sare gamv men daur lagata tha. apne sath ek vichitra bhasha laya tha, aur usi men bolta tha, chahe koi samjhe ya na samjhe. uski bhasha men ta, la aur gha ki kasrat thi aur sa, ra adi varn gayab the. us bhasha men roti ka nam tha oti, dugh ka tut, sag ka chhag aur kauri ka tauli. janvaron ki boliyon ki aisi naqal karta hai ki hamste-hamste logon ke pet men bal par jata hai. kisi ne puchha -- ramu, kutta kaise bolta hai? ramu gambhir bhav se kahta -- bhon-bhon, aur katne daurta. billi kaise bole? aur ramu myamv-myamv karke ankhen nikalkar takta aur panjon se nochta. bada mast larka tha. jab dekho khelne men magan rahta, na khane ki sudhi thi, na pine ki. god se use chirh thi. uske sabse sukhi pal vah hote, jab vah dvar ke nim ke niche manon dhul bator kar usmen lotta, sir par charhata, uski dheriyam lagata, gharaunde banata. apni umra ke larkon se uski ek pal na patti. shayad unhen apne sath khelane ke yogya hi na samjhata tha. koi puchhta -- tumhara nam kya hai? chatpat kahta -- lamu.

NAmEHori ke ghar men umra to nahin kat sakti thi. NAmEmatadin ko kai sau rupae kharch karne ke bad ant men kashi ke panditon ne phir se brahman bana diya. us din bada bhari havan hua, bahut-se brahmanon ne khana kiya aur bahut se mantra aur shlok parhe gaye. NAmEmatadin ko shuddh NAmEGobar aur gomutra khana-pina pada. NAmEGobar se uska man pavitra ho gaya. mutra se uski atma men ashuchita ke kitanu mar gaye. lekin ek tarah se is prayashchit ne use sachmuch pavitra kar diya. havan ke bahut tez agni-kund men uski manvata nikhar gayi aur havan ki jvala ke prakash se usne dharm-stambhon ko achchhi tarah parakh liya. us din se use dharm ke nam se chirh ho gayi. usne janeu utar phenka aur purohiti ko ganga men duba diya. ab vah pakka khetihar tha. usne yah bhi dekha ki halanki vidvanon ne uska brahmanatva svikar kar liya; lekin janta ab bhi uske hath ka pani nahin piti, usse muhurt puchhti hai, sait aur lagna ka soch karvati hai, use parva ke din dan bhi de deti hai, par usse apne bartan nahin chhulati. jis din NAmESilia ke larka ka janma hua usne duni matra men bhang pi, aur garva se jaise uski chhati tan gayi, aur ungliya bar-bar munchhon par parne lagim. bachcha kaisa hoga? usi ke jaisa? kaise dekhe? uska man masoskar rah gaya.
tisre din NAmERupa khet men usse mili. usne puchha -- rupiya, tune NAmESilia ka larka dekha?
rupiya boli -- dekha kyon nahin. lal-lal hai khub mota, badi-badi ankhen haim, sir men jhabrale bal haim, tukur-tukur takta hai.
NAmEmatadin ke dil men jaise vah larka a baitha tha, aur hath-pamv phenk raha tha. uski ankhon men nasha-sa chha gaya. usne us kishori NAmERupa ko god men utha liya, phir kandhe par bitha liya, phir utarkar uske kapolon ko chum liya. NAmERupa bal sambhalti hui dhith hokar boli -- chalo, maim tumko dur se dikha dum. osare men hi to hai. NAmESilia bahan na jane kyon hardam roti rahti hai. NAmEmatadin ne mumh pher liya. uski ankhen sajal ho ayi thim, aur oth kamp rahe the. us rat ko jab sara gamv so gaya aur per andhakar men dub gaye, to vah NAmESilia ke dvar par aya aur pura pranon se larka ka rona suna, jismen sari duniya ka sangit, khushi aur madhurya bhara hua tha. NAmESilia bachche ko NAmEHori ke ghar men khatole par sulakar majuri karne chali jati. NAmEmatadin kisi-na-kisi bahane se NAmEHori ke ghar ata aur kankhiyo se bachche ko dekhkar apna kaleja aur ankhen aur jan shital karta. NAmEDhania muskara kar kahti -- lajate kyon ho, god men le lo, pyaar karo, kaisa kath ka kaleja hai tumhara. bilkul tumko pada hai. NAmEmatadin ek-do rupya NAmESilia ke lie phenkkar bahar nikal ata. larka ke sath uski atma bhi barh rahi thi, khil rahi thi, chamak rahi thi. ab uske jivan ka bhi maksad tha, ek vrat tha. usmen samyam a gaya, gambhirta a gayi, dayitva a gaya. ek din ramu khatole par leta hua tha. NAmEDhania kahim gayi thi. NAmERupa bhi larkon ka shor sunkar khelne chali gayi. ghar akela tha. usi vaqta NAmEmatadin pahuncha. larka nile akash ki or dekh-dekh hath-pamv phenk raha tha, humak raha tha, jivan ke us khushi ke sath jo abhi usmen taza tha. NAmEmatadin ko dekhkar vah hams pada. NAmEmatadin pyaar-vihval ho gaya. usne larka ko uthakar chhati se laga liya. uski sari deh aur dil aur jan romanchit ho uthe, mano pani ki lahron men prakash ki rekhaen kamp rahi hon. bachche ki gahri, nirmal, athah, mod-bhari ankhon men jaise uske jivan ka satya mil gaya. use ek tarah ka dar-sa laga, mano vah nazar uske dil men chubhi jati ho -- vah kitna apavitra hai, ishvar ka vah prasad kaise chhu sakta hai. usne larka ko sashank man ke sath phir lita diya. usi vaqta NAmERupa bahar se a gayi aur vah bahar nikal gaya. ek din khub ole gire. NAmESilia ghas lekar bazar gayi hui thi. NAmERupa apne khel men magna thi. ramu ab baithne laga tha. kuchh-kuchh bakvam chalne bhi laga tha. usne jo angan men binaule bichhe dekhe, to samjha, batase phaile hue haim. kai uthakar khaye aur angan men khub khela. rat ko use jvar a gaya. dusre din nimoniya ho gaya. tisre din sham samay NAmESilia ki god men hi larka ke jan nikal gaye. lekin larka markar bhi NAmESilia ke jivan ka kendra bana raha. uski chhati men dudh ka ubal-sa ata aur anchal bhing jata. usi pal ankhon se amsu bhi nikal parte. pahle sab kamon se chhutti pakad rat ko jab vah ramu ko hiye se lagakar stan uske mumh men de deti to mano uske pranon men larka ki sphurti bhar jati. tab vah pyare-pyare git gati, mithe-mithe svapna dekhti aur naye-naye samsar rachti, jiska raja ramu hota. ab sab kamon se chhutti pakad vah apni suni jhompri men roti thi aur uske jan tarpate the, ud jane ke lie, us lok men jaham uska lal is samay bhi khel raha hoga. sara gamv uske duhkh men sharik tha. ramu kitna chonchal tha, jo koi bulata, usi ki god men chala jata. markar aur pahunch se bahar hokar vah aur bhi priy ho gaya tha, uski chhaya usse kahim sundar, kahim chonchal, kahim lubhavni thi. NAmEmatadin us din khul pada. parda hota hai hava ke lie. andhi men parde uthake rakh diye jate haim ki andhi ke sath ud na jayam. usne shav ko donon hatheliyon par utha liya aur akela nadi ke kinare tak le gaya, jo ek mil ka pat chhorkar patli-si dhar men sama gayi thi. ath din tak uske hath sidhe na ho sake. us din vah zara bhi nahin lajaya, zara bhi nahin jhijhka. aur kisi ne kuchh kaha bhi nahin; balki sabhi ne uske himmat aur drirhta ki tarif ki. NAmEHori ne kaha -- yahi marad ka dharam hai. jiski bamh pakri, use kya chhorna! NAmEDhania ne ankhen nachakar kaha -- mat bakhan karo, ji jalta hai. yah marad hai? maim aise marad ko namrad kahti hum. jab bamh pakri thi, tab kya dudh pita tha ki NAmESilia brahmani ho gayi thi? ek mahina bit gaya. NAmESilia phir majuri karne lagi thi. sham ho gayi thi. punarmasi ka chand vihamsta-sa nikal aya tha. NAmESilia ne kate hue khet men se gire hue jau ke bal chunkar tokri men rakh liye the aur ghar jana chahti thi ki chand par nigah par gayi aur dard-bhari smritiyon ka mano srot khul gaya. anchal dudh se bhing gaya aur mumh amsuon se. usne sir latka liya aur jaise rudan ka khushi lene gayi. sahsa kisi ki ahat pakad vah chaunk padi.
