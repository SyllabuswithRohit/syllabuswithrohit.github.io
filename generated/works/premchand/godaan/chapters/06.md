# Godaan — Adhyay 6

**Munshi Premchand**

6.
jeth ki udas aur garm sham semri ki sarkon aur galiyon men pani ke chhirkav se shital aur khush ho rahi thi. mandap ke charon taraf phulon aur paudhon ke gamle saja diye gaye the aur bijli ke pankhe chal rahe the. NAmERai Sahib apne karkhane men bijli banva lete the. unke sipahi pili vardiyam date, nile safe bandhe, janta par rob jamate phirte the. naukar ujle kurte pahne aur kesriya pag bandhe, mehmano aur mukhiyon ka adar-satkar kar rahe the. usi vaqta ek motar simh-dvar ke samne akar ruki aur usmen se tin mahanubhav utre. vah jo khaddar ka kurta aur chappal pahne hue haim unka nam pindat NAmEOnkarnath hai. ap dainik-chitthi bijli ke yashasvi sampadak haim, jinhen desh-chinta ne ghula dala hai. dusre mahashap jo kot-paint men haim, vah haim to vakil, par vakalat na chalne ke vajah ek bima-kampani ki dalali karte haim aur talluqedaron ko mahajnon aur bainkon se qaraz dilane men vakalat se kahim zyada kamai karte haim. inka nam hai shyamabihari tankha aur tisre sajjan jo reshmi achkan aur tang pajama pahne hue haim, mistar bi. NAmEmehta, yunivarsiti men darshanashastra ke tichar haim. ye tinon sajjan NAmERai Sahib ke sahapathiyon men haim aur shagun ke jashna men nimantrit hue haim. aj sare ilaqe ke asami ayenge aur shagun ke rupae bhent karenge. rat ko dhanush-yajnya hoga aur mehmano ki davat hogi. NAmEHori ne panch rupae shagun ke de diye haim aur ek gulabi mirazai pahne, gulabi pagri bandhe, ghutne tak kachhni kachhe, hath men ek khurpi liye aur mumh par paudar lagvaye raja janak ka mali ban gaya hai aur garur se itna phul utha hai mano yah sara jashna usi ke purusharth se ho raha hai. NAmERai Sahib ne mehmano ka svagat kiya. dohre badan ke unche aadmi the, gatha hua sharir, tejasvi chehra, uncha matha, gora rang, jis par sharbati reshmi chadar khub khil rahi thi. pindat NAmEOnkarnath ne puchha -- abki kaun-sa natak khelne ka soch hai? mere ras ki to yaham vahi vastu hai. NAmERai Sahib ne tinon sajjanon ko apni ravti ke samne kusiryon par baithate hue kaha -- pahle to dhanush-yajnya hoga, uske bad ek prahsan. natak koi achchha na mila. koi to itna lamba ki shayad panch ghanton men bhi khatam na ho aur koi itna klisht ki shayad yaham ek vyakti bhi uska arth na samjhe. akhir maimne svayam ek prahsan likh dala, jo do ghanton men pura ho jayga. NAmEOnkarnath ko NAmERai Sahib ki rachna-shakti men bahut shak tha. unka khyal tha ki pratibha to garibi hi men chamkati hai dipak ki tarah, jo andhere hi men apna prakash dikhata hai. dhyan na dena ke sath, jise chhipane ki bhi unhomne cheshta nahin ki, pandit NAmEOnkarnath ne mumh pher liya. mistar tankha in bematlab ki baton men na parna chahte the, phir bhi NAmERai Sahib ko dikha dena chahte the ki is vishay men unhen kuchh bolne ka adhikar hai. bole -- natak koi bhi achchha ho sakta hai, agar uske abhineta achchhe hon. achchha-se-achchha natak bure abhinetaon ke hath men parkar bura ho sakta hai. jab tak stej par shikshit abhinetriyam nahin atim, hamari natya-kala ka uddhar nahin ho sakta. abki to apne kaumsil men prashnon ki dhum macha di. maim to dave ke sath kah sakta hum ki kisi membar ka rikard itna shandar nahin hai. darshan ke tichar mistar NAmEmehta is prashamsa ko sahan na kar sakte the. khilaf hona to karna chahte the par siddhanta ki ar men. unhomne hal hi men ek pustak kai sal ke mehnat se likhi thi. uski jitni dhum honi chahiye thi, uski shatamsh bhi nahin hui thi. isse bahut dukhi the. bole -- bhai, maim prashnon ka kayal nahin. maim chahta hum hamara jivan hamare siddhanton ke anukul ho. ap krishkon ke shubhechchhu haim, unhen tarah-tarah ki riyayat dena chahte haim, zamindaron ke adhikar chhin lena chahte haim, bilk unhen ap samaj ka shap kahte haim, phir bhi ap zamindar haim, vaise hi zamindar jaise hazaron aur zamindar haim. agar apki dharna hai ki krishkon ke sath riyayat honi chahiye, to pahle ap khud shuru karen -- kashtakaron ko bagair nazrane lie patte likh den, begar band kar den, izafa lagan ko tilanjli de den, charavar zameen chhod den. mujhe un logon se zara bhi hamadadir nahin hai, jo baten to karte haim kamyuniston ki-si, magar jivan hai raison ka-sa, utna hi vilasmay, utna hi svarth se bhara hua. NAmERai Sahib ko aghat pahuncha. vakil sahab ke mathe par bal par gaye aur sampadkaji ke mumh men jaise kalikh lag gayi. vah khud samishtavad ke pujari the, par sidhe ghar men ag na lagana chahte the. tankha ne NAmERai Sahib ki vakalat ki -- maim samjhata hum, NAmERai Sahib ka apne asamiyon ke sath jitna achchha bartav hai, agar sabhi zamindar vaise hi ho jayam, to yah saval hi na rahe. NAmEmehta ne hathaure ki dusri chot jamayi -- manta hum, apka apne asamiyon ke sath bahut achchha batarva hai, magar saval yah hai ki usmen svarth hai ya nahin. iska ek vajah kya yah nahin ho sakta ki maddhim anch men khana svadisht pakta hai? gur se maranevala zahar se maranevale ki apeksha kahim kamyab ho sakta hai. maim to sirph itna janta hum, ham ya to samyavadi haim ya nahin haim. haim to uska bartav karen, nahin haim, to bakna chhod den. maim naqli zindagi ka virodhi hum. agar mams khana achchha samjhate ho to khulkar khao. bura samjhate ho, to mat khao, yah to meri samajh men ata hai; lekin achchha samjhana aur chhipkar khana, yah meri samajh men nahin ata. maim to ise kayrata bhi kahta hum aur dhurtata bhi, jo sach men ek haim. NAmERai Sahib sabha-hoshiyar aadmi the. beizzati aur aghat ko sabra aur udarta se sahne ka unhen abhyas tha. kuchh asamanjas men pade hue bole -- apka soch bilkul thik hai NAmEmehta ji. ap jante haim, maim apki safagoi ka kitna adar karta hum, lekin ap yah bhul jate haim ki anya yatraon ki tarah vicharon ki yatra men bhi parav hote haim, aur ap ek parav ko chhorkar dusre parav tak nahin ja sakte. manav-jivan ka itihas iska pratyaksh sabut hai. maim us vatavran men pala hum, jaham raja ishvar hai aur zamindar ishvar ka mantri. mere svargavasi pita asamiyon par itni daya karte the ki pale ya sukhe men kabhi adha aur kabhi pura lagan maf kar dete the. apne bakhar se anaj nikalkar asamiyon ko khila dete the. ghar ke gahne bechkar kanyaon ke shadi men madad dete the; magar usi vaqta tak, jab tak praja unko sarkar aur dhamarvatar kahti rahe, unhen apna devta samajhkar unki puja karti rahe. praja ka palan unka sanatan-dharm tha, lekin adhikar ke nam par vah kauri ka ek dant bhi phorkar dena na chahte the. maim usi vatavran men pala hum aur mujhe garva hai ki maim bartav men chahe jo kuchh karum, vicharon men unse age barh gaya hum aur yah manne lag gaya hum ki jab tak kisanon ko ye riyayten adhikar ke rup men na milengi, sirph sadbhavna ke adhar par unki hal sudhar nahin sakti. svechchha agar apna svarth chhod de, to apvad hai. maim khud sadbhavna karte hue bhi svarth nahin chhod sakta aur chahta hum ki hamare varg ko shasan aur niti ke bal se apna svarth chhorne ke lie mazbur kar diya jay. ise ap kayrata kahenge, maim ise vivshata kahta hum. maim ise svikar karta hum ki kisi ko bhi dusre ke mehnat par mote hone ka adhikar nahin hai. upjivi hona ghor sharm ki bat hai. karm karna pranimatra ka dharm hai. samaj ki aisi intzam, jismen kuchh log mauj karen aur zyada log pisen aur khapen, kabhi sukhad nahin ho sakti. punji aur shiksha, jise maim punji hi ka ek rup samjhata hum, inka qila jitni jald tut jay, utna hi achchha hai. jinhen pet ki roti mayassar nahin, unke afsar aur niyojak das-das panch-panch hazar phatkare, yah hasyaspad hai aur lajjaspad bhi. is intzam ne ham zamindaron men kitni vilasita, kitna durachar, kitni paradhinta aur kitni nirlajjata bhar di hai, yah maim khub janta hum; lekin maim in karnon se is intzam ka khilaf hona nahin karta. mera to yah kahna hai ki apne svarth ki nazar se bhi iska anumodan nahin kiya ja sakta. is shan ko nibhane ke lie hamen apni atma ki itni hatya karni parti hai ki hammen atmabhiman ka nam bhi nahin raha. ham apne asamiyon ko lutne ke lie mazbur haim. agar afsaron ko qimti-qimti daliyam na den, to bagi samjhe jayam, shan se na rahen, to kanjus kahlaye. pragti ki zara-si ahat pate hi ham kamp uthte haim, aur afsaron ke pas fariyad lekar daurte haim ki hamari raksha kijie. hamen apne upar yakin nahin raha, na purusharth hi rah gaya. bas, hamari hal un bachchon ki-si hai, jinhen chammach se dudh pilakar pala jata hai, bahar se mote, andar se durbal, satvahin aur muhtaj.
NAmEmehta ne tali bajakar kaha -- hiyar, hiyar! apki zaban men jitni akal hai, kash uski adhi bhi dimag men hoti! khed yahi hai ki sab kuchh samjhate hue bhi ap apne vicharon ko bartav men nahin late.
NAmEOnkarnath bole -- akela chana bhar nahin phor sakta, mistar NAmEmehta! hamen samay ke sath chalna bhi hai aur use apne sath chalana bhi. bure kamon men hi sahyog ki zarurat nahin hoti. achchhe kamon ke lie bhi sahyog utna hi zaruri hai. ap hi kyon ath sau rupae mahine harpate haim, jab apke karoron bhai sirph ath rupae men apna guzara kar rahe haim? NAmERai Sahib ne upri khed, lekin bhitri santosh se sampadkaji ko dekha aur bole -- vyaktigat baton par alochna na kijie sampadak ji! ham yaham samaj ki intzam par soch kar rahe haim.
mistar NAmEmehta usi thanthe man se bole -- nahin-nahin, maim ise bura nahin samjhata. samaj vyakti hi se banta hai. aur vyakti ko bhulkar ham kisi intzam par soch nahin kar sakte. maim isliye itna tanakhvah leta hum ki mera is intzam par yakin nahin hai. sampadkaji ko achambha hua -- achchha, to ap abhi intzam ke samarthak haim?
maim is siddhanta ka samarthak hum ki samsar men chhote-bade hamesha rahenge, aur unhen hamesha rahna chahiye. ise mitane ki cheshta karna manav-jati ke sarvanash ka vajah hoga.

NAmERai Sahib ne mehmano ko pan aur ilaychi dete hue kaha -- akal agar svarth se mukt ho, to hamen uski prabhuta manne men koi apitt nahin. samajvad ka yahi adarsh hai. ham sadhu-mahatmaon ke samne isilie sir jhukate haim ki unmen tyag ka bal hai. isi tarah ham akal ke hath men adhikar bhi dena chahte haim, izzat bhi, netritva bhi; lekin jaydad kisi tarah nahin. akal ka adhikar aur izzat vyakti ke sath chala jata hai, lekin uski jaydad vish bone ke lie, uske bad aur bhi prabal ho jati hai. akal ke bagair kisi samaj ka sanchalan nahin ho sakta. ham sirph is bichchhu ka dank tod dena chahte haim.
