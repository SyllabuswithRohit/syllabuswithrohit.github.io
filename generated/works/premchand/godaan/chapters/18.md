# Godaan — Adhyay 18

**Munshi Premchand**

18.
NAmEKhanna aur govindi men nahin patti. kyon nahin patti, yah batana mushkil hai. jyotish ke hisab se unke grahon men koi khilaf hona hai, halanki shadi ke samay grah aur nakshatra khub mila liye gaye the. kam-shastra ke hisab se is anban ka aur koi rahasya ho sakta hai, aur manovijnyan vale kuchh aur hi vajah khoj sakte haim. ham to itna hi jante haim ki unmen nahin patti. NAmEKhanna dhanvan haim, rasik haim, milansar haim, rupvan haim achchhe khase parhe-likhe haim aur nagar ke vishisht purushon men haim. govindi apsara na ho, par rupvati zarur hai; gehuma rang lajjashil ankhen jo ek bar samne uthkar phir jhuk jati haim, kapolon par lali na ho par chikanapan hai, gat komal, ang-vinyas, sudaul, gol bamhen, mumh par ek tarah ki aruchi, jismen kuchh garva ki jhalak bhi hai, mano samsar ke bartav aur vyapar ko hey samjhati hai. NAmEKhanna ke pas vilas ke upri sadhnon ki kami nahin, avval darje ka bangla hai, avval darje ka farnichar, avval darje ki kar aur apar paisa; par govindi ki nazar men jaise in chizon ka koi kimat nahin. is khade sagar men vah pyasi padi rahti hai. bachchon ka lalan-palan aur ghar-parivar ke chhote-mote kam hi uske lie sab kuchh haim. vah inmen itni vyast rahti hai ki bhog ki or uska dhyan nahin jata. akarshan kya vastu hai aur kaise utpann ho sakta hai, iski or usne kabhi soch nahin kiya. vah aadmi ka khilauna nahin hai, na uske bhog ki vastu, phir kyon akarshak banne ki cheshta kare; agar aadmi uska asli saundarya dekhne ke lie ankhen nahin rakhta, kaminiyon ke pichhe mara-mara phirta hai to vah uska badakismati hai. vah usi pyaar aur nishtha se pati ki seva kiye jati hai jaise naphrat aur moh-jaisi bhavanaon ko usne jit liya hai. aur yah apar jaydad to jaise uski atma ko kuchlati rahti hai. in adambaron aur pakhandon se mukt hone ke lie uska man hamesha lalchaya karta hai. apne asan aur svabhavik jivan men vah kitni sukhi rah sakti thi, iska vah nitya svapna dekhti rahti hai. tab kyon NAmEmalati uske rasta men akar badhak ho jati! kyon veshyaon ke mujre hote, kyon yah shak aur banavat aur ashanti uske jivan-rasta men kanta banti! bahut pahle jab vah ladki-skul men parhti thi, use kavita ka bimari lag gaya tha, jaham dukh aur dard hi jivan ka tatva hai, jaydad aur vilas to sirph isalie hai ki uski holi jalayi jay, jo manushya ko asatya aur ashanti ki or le jata hai. vah ab kabhi-kabhi kavita rachti thi; lekin sunaye kise? uski kavita sirph man ki tarang ya bhavna ki uran na thi, uske ek-ek shabd men uske jivan ki vyatha aur uske amsuon ki thandi jalan bhari hoti thi -- kisi aise pradesh men ja basne ki lalsa, jaham vah pakhandon aur vasanaon se dur apni shant kutiya men asan khushi ka upbhog kare. NAmEKhanna uski kavitaen dekhte, to unka mazaq urate aur kabhi-kabhi pharkar phenk dete. aur jaydad ki yah divar din-din unchi hoti jati thi aur dampati ko ek dusre se dur aur prithak karti jati thi. NAmEKhanna apne grahkon ke sath jitna hi mitha aur namra tha, ghar men utna hi karva aur uddand. aksar gussa men govindi ko apashabd kah baithta, shishtata uske lie duniya ko thagne ka ek sadhan thi, man ka samskar nahin. aise avsaron par govindi apne ekant kamren men ja baithti aur rat ki rat roya karti aur NAmEKhanna divankhane men mujre sunta ya klab men jakar sharaben urata. lekin yah sab kuchh hone par bhi NAmEKhanna uske sarvasva the. vah dalit aur apamanit hokar bhi NAmEKhanna ki laundi thi. unse laregi, jalegi, royegi; par rahegi unhim ki. unse prithak jivan ki vah koi kalpana hi na kar sakti thi. aj mistar NAmEKhanna kisi bure aadmi ka mumh dekhkar uthe the. sabere hi chitthi khola, to unke kai stakon ka dar gir gaya tha, jismen unhen kai hazar ki nuksan hoti thi. shakkar mil ke mazduro ne hartal kar di thi aur danga-fasad karne par amada the. nafe ki ummid se chandi kharidi thi; magar uska dar aj aur bhi zyada gir gaya tha. NAmERai Sahib se jo sauda ho raha tha aur jismen unhen khase nafe ki ummid thi, vah kuchh dinon ke lie talta hua jan parta tha. phir rat ko bahut pi jane ke vajah is vaqta sir bhari tha aur deh tut rahi thi. idhar shofar ne kar ke injan men kuchh kharabi paida ho jane ki bat kahi thi aur lahaur men unke baink par ek divani muqdama dayar ho jane ka khabar bhi mila tha. baithe man men jhunjhla rahe the ki usi vaqta govindi ne akar kaha -- bhishma ka jvar aj bhi nahin utra, kisi daktar ko bula do. bhishma unka sabse chhota beta tha, aur janma se hi durbal hone ke vajah use roz ek-na-ek shikayat bani rahti thi. aj khamsi hai, to kal bukhar; kabhi pasli chal rahi hai, kabhi hare-pile dast a rahe haim. das mahine ka ho gaya tha! par lagta tha panch-chhah mahine ka. NAmEKhanna ki dharna ho gayi thi ki yah larka bachega nahin; isalie uski or se udasin rahte the; par govindi isi vajah use aur sab bachchon se zyada chahti thi.
NAmEKhanna ne pita ke pyaar ka bhav dikhate hue kaha -- bachchon ko davaon ka adi bana dena thik nahin, aur tumhen dava pilane ka maraz hai. zara kuchh hua aur daktar bulao. ek roz aur dekho, aj tisra hi din to hai. shayad aj ap-hi-ap utar jay.
govindi ne agrah kiya -- tin din se nahin utra. gharelu davaen karke har gayi.
NAmEKhanna ne puchha -- achchhi bat hai bula deta hum, kise bulaum?
bula lo daktar nag ko.
achchhi bat hai, unhim ko bulata hum, magar yah samajh lo ki nam ho jane se hi koi achchha daktar nahin ho jata. nag fis chahe jitni le len, unki dava se kisi ko achchha hote nahin dekha. vah to marizon ko svarg bhejne ke lie mashhur haim.
to jise chaho bula lo, maimne to nag ko isalie kaha tha ki vah kai bar a chuke haim.
mis NAmEmalati ko kyon na bula lum? fis bhi kam aur bachchon ka hal ledi daktar jaisa samjhegi, koi mard daktar nahin samajh sakta.
govindi ne jalkar kaha -- maim mis NAmEmalati ko daktar nahin samjhati.
NAmEKhanna ne bhi tez ankhon se dekhkar kaha -- to vah inglaind ghas khodne gayi thi, aur hazaron admiyo ko aj jivan-dan de rahi hai; yah sab kuchh nahin hai?
hoga, mujhe un par bharosa nahin hai. vah mardon ke dil ka ilaj kar len. aur kisi ki dava unke pas nahin hai.
bas than gayi. NAmEKhanna garjane lage. govindi barsane lagi. unke bich men NAmEmalati ka nam a jana mano larai ka altimetam tha. NAmEKhanna ne sare kagzon ko zameen par phenkkar kaha -- tumhare sath zindagi talakh ho gayi. govindi ne nukile avaz men kaha -- to NAmEmalati se byah kar lo na! abhi kya bigra hai, agar vaham dal gale.
tum mujhe kya samjhati ho?
yahi ki NAmEmalati tum-jaison ko apna gulam banakar rakhna chahti hai, pati banakar nahin.
tumhari nigah men maim itna zalil hum?
aur unhimne iske viruddh sabut dene shuru kiya. NAmEmalati jitna unka adar karti hai, utna shayad hi kisi ka karti ho. NAmERai Sahib aur raja sahab ko mumh tak nahin lagati; lekin unse ek din bhi mulaqat na ho, to shikayat karti hai....
govindi ne in pramanon ko ek phunk men ura diya -- isilie ki vah tumhen sabse bada ankhon ka andha samjhati hai, dusron ko itna asani se bevquf nahin bana sakti.
NAmEKhanna ne ding mari -- vah chahen to aj NAmEmalati se shadi kar sakte haim. aj, abhi...
magar govindi ko bilkul yakin nahin hai -- tum sat janma nak ragro, to bhi vah tumse shadi na karegi. tum uske tattu ho, tumhen ghas khilayegi, kabhi-kabhi tumhara mumh sahalayegi, tumhare putthon par hath pheregi; lekin isalie ki tumhare upar savari ganthe. tumhare jaise ek hazar buddhu uski jeb men haim. govindi aj bahut barhi jati thi. malum hota hai, aj vah unse larne par taiyar hokar ayi hai. daktar ke bulane ka to sirph bahana tha. NAmEKhanna apni yogyata aur dakshata aur purushatva par itna bada akshep kaise sah sakte the!
tumhare khayal men maim buddhu aur bevkuph hum, to ye hazaron kyon mere dvar par nak ragrate haim? kaun raja ya talluqedar hai, jo mujhe dandvat nahin karta. saikron ko ullu bana kar chhod diya.
yahi to NAmEmalati ki visheshta hai ki jo auron ko sidhe ustare se mumrta hai, use vah ulte chhure se mumrti hai.
tum NAmEmalati ki chahe jitni burai karo, tum uski pamv ki dhul bhi nahin ho.
meri nazar men vah veshyaon se bhi gayi biti hai; kyunki vah parde ki ar se shikar khelti hai.
donon ne apne-apne agni-ban chhod diye. NAmEKhanna ne govindi ko chahe dusri kathor se kathor bat kahi hoti, use itni buri na lagti; par NAmEmalati se uski yah ghrinit tulna uski sahishnuta ke lie bhi asahya thi. govindi ne bhi NAmEKhanna ko chahe jo kuchh kaha hota, vah itne garm na hote; lekin NAmEmalati ka yah beizzati vah nahin sah sakte. donon ek dusre ke komal sthalon se parichit the. donon ke nishane thik baithe aur donon tilmila uthe. NAmEKhanna ki ankhen lal ho gayim. govindi ka mumh lal ho gaya. NAmEKhanna avesh men uthe aur uske donon kan pakarkar zor se ainthe aur tin-char tamache laga diye. govindi roti hui andar chali gayi. zara der men daktar nag aye aur sivil sarjan em. tad aye aur bhishagacharya nilakanth shastri aye; par govindi bachche ko liye apne kamre men baithi rahi. kisne kya kaha, kya tashkhish ki, use kuchh malum nahin. jis musibat ki kalpana vah kar rahi thi, vah aj uske sir par a gayi. NAmEKhanna ne aj jaise usse nata tod liya, jaise use ghar se khaderkar dvar band kar liya. jo rup ka bazar lagakar baithti hai, jiski parachhaim bhi vah apne upar parne nahin dena chahti. vah us par paroksh rup se shasan kare. yah na hoga. NAmEKhanna uske pati haim, unhen usko samjhane-bujhane ka adhikar hai, unki mar ko bhi vah shirodharya kar sakti hai; par NAmEmalati ka shasan! namumkin! magar bachche ka jvar jab tak shant na ho jay, vah hil nahin sakti. atmabhiman ko bhi pharz ke samne sir jhukana parega. dusre din bachche ka jvar utar gaya tha. govindi ne ek tanga mangvaya aur ghar se nikli. jaham uska itna anadar hai, vaham ab vah nahin rah sakti. aghat itna kathor tha ki bachchon ka moh bhi tut gaya tha. unke prati uska jo dharm tha, use vah pura kar chuki hai. shesh jo kuchh hai, vah NAmEKhanna ka dharm hai. ham, god ke larka ko vah kisi tarah nahin chhod sakti. vah uski jan ke sath hai. aur is ghar se vah sirph apne jan lekar niklegi. aur koi chiz uski nahin hai. inhen yah dava hai ki vah uska palan karte haim. govindi dikha degi ki vah unke ashray se nikalkar bhi zinda rah sakti hai. tinon bachche us samay khelne gaye the. govindi ka man hua, ek bar unhen pyaar kar le; magar vah kahim bhagi to nahin jati. bachchon ko usse pyaar hoga, to uske pas ayenge, uske ghar men khelenge. vah jab zarurat samjhegi, khud bachchon ko dekh aya karegi. sirph NAmEKhanna ka ashray nahin lena chahti. sanjh ho gayi thi. park men raunaq thi. log hari ghas par lete hava ka khushi lut rahe the. govindi hazarataganj hoti hui chiriyaghar ki taraf muri hi thi ki kar par NAmEmalati aur NAmEKhanna samne se ate hue dikhayi diye. use malum hua, NAmEKhanna ne uski taraf ishara karke kuchh kaha aur NAmEmalati muskarayi. nahin, shayad yah uska bhram ho. NAmEKhanna NAmEmalati se uski ninda na karenge; magar kitni besharm hai. suna hai iski achchhi praikitas hai ghar ki bhi sampann hai phir bhi yon apne ko bechti phirti hai. na jane kyon byah nahin kar leti; lekin usse byah karega hi kaun? nahin, yah bat nahin. purushon men bhi aise bahut ho gaye haim, jo use pakad apne ko dhanya manenge; lekin NAmEmalati khud kisi ko pasand kare. aur vyah men kaun-sa sukh rakha hua hai. bahut achchha karti hai, jo byah nahin karti. abhi sab uske gulam haim. tab vah ek ki laundi hokar rah jaygi. bahut achchha kar rahi hai. abhi to yah mahashay bhi uske talve chatte haim. kahim inse byah kar le, to us par shasan karne lagen; magar inse vah kyon byah karegi? aur samaj men do-char aisi striyam bani rahen, to achchha; purushon ke kan to garm karti rahen. aj govindi ke man men NAmEmalati ke prati badi hamadardi utpann hui. vah NAmEmalati par akshep karke uske sath anyay kar rahi hai. kya meri hal ko dekhkar uski ankhen na khulti hongi. vivahit jivan ki durdasha ankhon dekhkar agar vah is jal men nahin phamsti, to kya bura karti hai! chiriyaghar men charon taraf sannata chhaya hua tha. govindi ne tanga rok diya aur bachche ko lie hari dub ki taraf chali; magar do hi tin qadam chali thi ki chappal pani men dub gaye. abhi thodi der pahle lan sincha gaya tha aur ghas ke niche pani bah raha tha. us utavli men usne pichhe na phirkar ek qadam aur age rakha to pamv kichar men san gaye. usne pamv ki or dekha. ab yaham pamv dhone ke lie pani kaham se milega? uski sari manovyatha lupt ho gayi. pamv dhokar saf karne ki nayi chinta hui. uski soch-dhara ruk gayi. jab tak pamv na saf ho jayam vah kuchh nahin soch sakti. sahsa use ek lamba paip ghas men chhipa nazar aya, jismen se pani bah raha tha. usne jakar pamv dhoye, chappal dhoye, hath-mumh dhoya, thoda-sa pani chullu men lekar piya aur paip ke us par sukhi zameen par ja baithi. udasi men maut ki yad turant a jati hai. kahim vah vahim baithe-baithe mar jay, to kya ho? tangevala turant jakar NAmEKhanna ko khabar dega. NAmEKhanna sunte hi khil uthenge; lekin duniya ko dikhane ke lie ankhon par rumal rakh lenge. bachchon ke lie khilaune aur tamashe mam se pyare haim. yah hai uska jivan, jiske lie koi char bund amsu bahanevala bhi nahin. tab use vah din yad aya, jab uski sas jiti thi aur NAmEKhanna uranchhu na hue the, tab use sas ka bat-bat par bigrana bura lagta tha; aj use sas ke us gussa men pyaar ka ras ghula jan par raha tha. tab vah sas se ruth jati thi aur sas use dularkar manati thi. aj vah mahinon ruthi padi rahe. kise parva hai? ekaek uska man urkar mam ke charnon men ja pahuncha. hay! aj ammam hotim, to kyon uski yah durdasha hoti! uske pas aur kuchh na tha, pyaar-bhari god to thi, pyaar-bhara anchal to tha, jismen mumh dalkar vah ro leti; lekin nahin, vah royegi nahin, us devi ko svarg men dukhi na banayegi, mere lie vah jo kuchh zyada se zyada kar sakti thi, vah kar gayi? mere karmo ki sathin hona to unke vash ki bat na thi. aur vah kyon roye? vah ab kisi ke adhin nahin hai, vah apne guzar-bhar ko kama sakti hai. vah kal hi gandhi-ashram se chizen lekar bechna shuru kar degi. sharm kis bat ki? yahi to hoga, log ungli dikhakar kahenge -- vah ja rahi hai NAmEKhanna ki bibi; lekin is shahar men rahum kyon? kisi dusre shahar men kyon na chali jaum, jaham mujhe koi janta hi na ho. das-bis rupae kama lena aisa kya mushkil hai. apne pasine ki kamai to khaungi, phir to koi mujh par rob na jamayega. yah mahashay isilie to itna mizaj karte haim ki vah mera palan karte haim. maim ab khud apna palan karungi. sahsa usne NAmEmehta ko apni taraf ate dekha. use uljhan hui. is vaqta vah pura ekant chahti thi. kisi se bolne ki chah na thi; magar yaham bhi ek mahashay a hi gaye. us par bachcha bhi rone laga tha.
NAmEmehta ne pas akar hairani ke sath puchha -- ap is vaqta yaham kaise a gayim?
govindi ne larka ko chup karate hue kaha -- usi tarah jaise ap a gaye.
NAmEmehta ne muskarakar kaha -- meri bat na chalaie. dhobi ka kutta na ghar ka na ghat ka. laie, maim bachche ko chup kar dum.
apne yah kala kab sikhi?
abhyas karna chahta hum. iski pariksha jo hogi.
achchha! pariksha ke din qarib a gaye?
yah to meri taiyari par hai. jab taiyar ho jaunga, baith jaunga. chhoti-chhoti upadhiyon ke lie ham parh-parhkar ankhen phor liya karte haim. yah to jivan-vyapar ki pariksha hai.
achchhi bat hai, maim bhi dekhungi ap kis gred men pas hote haim.
yah kahte hue usne bachche ko unki god men de diya. unhomne bachche ko kai bar uchhala, to vah chup ho gaya. balkon ki tarah ding markar bole -- dekha apne, kaisa mantar ke zor se chup kar diya. ab maim bhi kahim se bachcha launga.
govindi ne vinod kiya -- bachcha hi laiega, ya uski mam bhi?
NAmEmehta ne vinod-bhari mayusi se sar hilakar kaha -- aisi aurat to kahim milti hi nahin.
kyon, mis NAmEmalati nahin haim? sundari, shikshit, gunvati, manoharini; aur ap kya chahte haim?
mis NAmEmalati men vah ek bat bhi nahin hai jo maim apni aurat men dekhna chahta hum.
govindi ne is kutsa ka khushi lete hue kaha -- usmen kya burai hai, sunum. bhaumre to hamesha ghere rahte haim. maimne suna hai, ajkal purushon ko aisi hi aurten pasand ati haim. NAmEmehta ne bachche ke hathon se apni munchhon ki raksha karte hue kaha -- meri aurat kuchh aur hi dhang ki hogi. vah aisi hogi, jiski maim puja kar sakunga.
govindi apni hamsi na rok saki -- to ap aurat nahin, koi pratima chahte haim. aurat to aisi apko shayad kahim mile.
ji nahin, aisi ek devi isi shahar men hai.
sach! maim bhi uske darshan karti, aur usi tarah banne ki cheshta karti.
ap use khub janti haim. vah ek lakhpati ki patni hai, par vilas ko tuchchh samjhati hai; jo dhyan na dena aur anadar sah kar bhi apne pharz se vichlit nahin hoti, jo matritva ki vedi par apne ko balidan karti hai, jiske lie tyag hi sabse bada adhikar hai, aur jo is yogya hai ki uski pratima banakar puji jay.
govindi ke dil men khushi ka kampan hua. samajhkar bhi na samjhane ka abhinay karti hui boli -- aisi aurat ki ap tarif karte haim. magar meri samajh men to vah daya ki chitthi hai. vah adarsh nari hai aur jo adarsh nari ho sakti hai, vahi adarsh patni bhi ho sakti hai.
NAmEmehta ne hairani se kaha -- ap uska beizzati karti haim.
lekin vah adarsh is yug ke lie nahin hai.
vah adarsh sanatan hai aur amar hai. manushya use vikrit karke apna sarvanash kar raha hai.
govindi ka man khila ja raha tha. aisi phureriyam vaham kabhi na uthi thim. jitne admiyo se uska parichay tha, unmen NAmEmehta ka sthan sabse uncha tha. unke mumh se yah protsahan pakad vah matvali hui ja rahi thi. usi nashe men boli -- to chalie, mujhe un ke darshan kara dijie.
NAmEmehta ne larka ke kapolon men mumh chhipakar kaha -- vah to yahim baithi hui haim.
kaham, maim to nahin dekh rahi hum.
usi devi se bol raha hum.
govindi ne zor se qahqaha mara -- apne aj mujhe banane ki than li, kyon?
NAmEmehta shraddhanat hokar kaha -- deviji, ap mere sath anyay kar rahi haim, aur mujhse zyada apne sath. samsar men aise bahut kam insaan haim jinke prati mere man men shraddha ho. unhim men ek ap haim. apka sabra aur tyag aur shil aur pyaar anupam hai. maim apne jivan men sabse bade sukh ki jo kalpana kar sakta hum, vah ap jaisi kisi devi ke charnon ki seva hai. jis naritva ko maim adarsh manta hum, ap uski sajiv pratima haim.
govindi ki ankhon se khushi ke amsu nikal pade; is shraddha-kavach ko dharan karke vah kis musibat ki samna na karegi. uske ron-ron men jaise mridu-sangit ki dhvani nikal padi. usne apne ramanitva ka khushi man men dabakar kaha -- ap darshanik kyon hue NAmEmehta ji? apko to kavi hona chahiye tha.
NAmEmehta sarlata se hamskar bole -- kya ap samjhati haim, bina darshanik hue hi koi kavi ho sakta hai? darshan to sirph bich ki mamzil hai.
to abhi ap kavitva ke raste men haim; lekin ap yah bhi jante haim, kavi ko samsar men kabhi sukh nahin milta?
jise samsar duhkh kahta hai, vaham kavi ke lie sukh hai. paisa aur aishvarya, rup aur bal, vidya aur akal, ye vibhutiyam samsar ko chahe kitna hi mohit kar len, kavi ke lie yaham zara bhi akarshan nahin hai, uske mod aur akarshan ki vastu to bujhi hui ashaen aur miti hui smritiyam aur tute hue dil ke amsu haim. jis din in vibhutiyon men uska pyaar na rahega, us din vah kavi na rahega. darshan jivan ke in rahasyon se sirph vinod karta hai, kavi unmen lay ho jata hai. maimne apki do-char kavitaen parhi haim aur unmen jitni pulak, jitna kampan, jitni mitha vyatha, jitna rulanevala unmad paya hai, vah maim hi janta hum. prakriti ne hamare sath kitna bada anyay kiya hai ki ap-jaisi koi dusri devi nahin banayi.
