daataadeena ne isakaa bhaavaartha kiya -- yaha kehte hain ki horee ne apane khushee se baila mujhe de diye| hamee ko ulloo banaate hain| horee ne sakuchaate hue kahaa -- yaha mujhase kahane lage yaa to jhuniyaa ko ghar se nikaala do, yaa mere rupae do, naheen to main baila khola le jaaoo.Ngaa| mainne kahaa, main bahu ko to na nikaaloo.Ngaa, na mere paasa rupae hain; agar tumhaaraa dharama kahe, to baila khola lo| basa, mainne inake dharama para chhoda diya aur inhonne baila khola liye|
pateshvaree ne mu.Nha latakaakara kahaa -- jaba tumane dharama para chhoda diya, taba koee kee jabaradastee| usake dharama ne kahaa, liye jata hai| jaao bhaiyaa, baila tumhaare hain|
daataadeena ne samarthana kiya -- haa.N, jaba dharama kee baat aa gayee, to koee kyaa kahe| sab ke sab horee ko tiraskaara kee aa.Nkhon se dekhate paraasta hokara lauta pade aur vijayee bholaa shaana se gardana uthaaye bailon ko le chalaa|
