NAmERai Sahib ki zabaradasti hai, nahin is samay kisi ke samne kyon hath phailana parta. NAmEJhinguri simh ne jab se uske dvar par gay dekhi thi, us par dant lagaye hue gay ka dil-daul aur gathan kah raha tha ki usmen panch ser se kam dudh nahin hai. man men soch liya tha, NAmEHori ko kisi ardab men dalkar gay ko ura lena chahiye. aj vah avsar a gaya. bole -- achchha bhai, tumhare pas kuchh nahin hai, ab razi hue. jitne rupae chaho, le jao lekin tumhare bhale ke lie kahte haim, kuchh gahne-gathe hon, to giro rakhkar rupae le lo. istam likhoge, to sud barhega aur jhamele men par jaoge. NAmEJhinguri simh ne hamadardi ka rang mumh par potkar kaha -- to ek bat karo, yah nayi gay jo laye ho, ise hamare hath bech do. sud istam sab jhagron se bach jao; char aadmi jo dam kahen, vah hamse le lo. ham jante haim, tum use apne shauq se laye ho aur bechna nahin chahte; lekin yah musibat to talna hi parega.
