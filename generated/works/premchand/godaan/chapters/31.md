NAmERai Sahib kamp uthe. unke man men bhi is tarah ki bat ayi thi; lekin unhomne use akar na lene diya tha. samskar donon vyaktiyon ke ek-se the. guphavasi manushya donon hi vyaktiyon men zinda tha. NAmERai Sahib ne use upar vastron se dhank diya tha. raja sahab men vah nagna tha. apna barappan siddh karne ke us avsar ko NAmERai Sahib chhod na sake. jaise lajjit hokar bole -- lekin yah bisvim sadi hai, barhavim nahin. rudrapal ke upar iski kya pratikriya hogi, maim nahin kah sakta; lekin manvata ki nazar se....
raja sahab ne bat katkar kaha -- ap manvata liye phirte haim aur yah nahin dekhte ki samsar men aj manushya ki pashuta hi uski manvata par vijay pa rahi hai. nahin, rashtron men laraiyam kyon hotim? panchayton se mamle na tay ho jate? jab tak manushya rahega, uski pashuta bhi rahegi.
chhoti-moti bahas chhir gayi aur shadi ke rup men akar ant men vitanda ban gayi aur raja sahab naraz hokar chale gaye. dusre din NAmERai Sahib ne bhi nainital ko ravana hona kiya. aur uske ek din bad rudrapal ne saroj ke sath inglaind ki rah li. ab unmen pita-beta ka nata na tha. pratidvandvi ho gaye the. mistar tankha ab rudrapal ke salahkar aur pairokar the. unhomne rudrapal ki taraf se NAmERai Sahib par hisab-fahmi ka dava kiya. NAmERai Sahib par das lakh ki digri ho gayi. unhen digri ka itna duhkh na hua jitna apne beizzati ka. beizzati se bhi barhkar duhkh tha jivan ki sanchit abhilashaon ke dhul men mil jane ka aur sabse bada duhkh tha is bat ka ki apne bete ne hi daga di. ajnyakari beta ke pita banne ka gaurav badi nirdayta ke sath unke hath se chhin liya gaya tha.
magar abhi shayad unke duhkh ka pyala bhara na tha. jo kuchh kasar thi, vah ladki aur damad ke rishta-vichchhed ne puri kar di. am hindu balikaon ki tarah minakshi bhi bezban thi. bap ne jiske sath byah kar diya, uske sath chali gayi; lekin aurat-aadmi men pyaar na tha. digvijaysimh aiyash bhi the, sharabi bhi. minakshi bhitar hi bhitar kurhti rahti thi. pustakon aur patrikaon se man bahlaya karti thi. digvijay ki avastha to tis se zyada na thi. parha-likha bhi tha; magar bada magrur, apni kul-pratishtha ki ding maranevala, svabhav ka bedard aur kripan. gamv ki nich jati ki bahu-betiyon par dore dala karta tha. sohbat bhi nichon ki thi, jinki khushamdon ne use aur bhi khushamadapasand bana diya tha. minakshi aise vyakti ka izzat dil se na kar sakti thi. phir patron men striyon ke adhikaron ki charcha parh-parhkar uski ankhen khulne lagi thim. vah zanana klab men ane-jane lagi. vaham kitni hi shikshit unche kul ki mahilaen ati thim. unmen vot aur adhikar aur svadhinta aur nari-jagriti ki khub charcha hoti thi, jaise purushon ke viruddh koi shadayantra racha ja raha ho. adhiktar vahi deviyam thim jinki apne purushon se na patti thi, jo nayi shiksha pane ke vajah purani mayardaon ko tod dalna chahti thim. kai yuvtiya bhi thim, jo digriyam le chuki thim aur vivahit jivan ko atmasamman ke lie ghatak samajhkar naukriyo ki talash men thim. unhim men ek mis sultan thim, jo vilayat se bar-ait-la hokar ayi thim aur yaham paradanshin mahilaon ko qanuni salah dene ka vyavsay karti thim. unhim ki salah se minakshi ne pati par guzare ka dava kiya. vah ab uske ghar men na rahna chahti thi. guzare ki minakshi ko zarurat na thi. maike men vah bade aram se rah sakti thi; magar vah digvijaysimh ke mumh men kalikh lagakar yaham se jana chahti thi. digvijaysimh ne us par ulta badachlani ka akshep lagaya. NAmERai Sahib ne is jhagra ko shant karne ki bharsak bahut cheshta ki; par minakshi ab pati ki surat bhi nahin dekhna chahti thi. halanki digvijaysimh ka dava kharij ho gaya aur minakshi ne us par guzare ki digri payi; magar yah beizzati uske jigar men chubhta raha. vah alag ek kothi men rahti thi, aur samishtavadi andolan men pramukh bhag leti thi, par vah jalan shant na hoti thi.
ek din vah gussa men akar hantar liye digvijaysimh ke bangle par pahunchi. shohde jama the aur veshya ka nach ho raha tha. usne ranachandi ki tarah pishachon ki is chandal chaukri men pahunchkar tahlaka macha diya. hantar kha-khakar log idhar-udhar bhagne lage. uske tej ke samne vah nich shohde kya tikte; jab digvijaysimh akele rah gaye, to usne un par sarasar hantar jamane shuru kiye aur itna mara ki kumvar sahab bedam ho gaye. veshya abhi tak kone men dabki khadi thi. ab uska nambar aya. minakshi hantar tankar jamana hi chahti thi ki veshya uske pairon par gir padi aur rokar boli -- dulahinyi, aj ap meri jan bakhsh den. maim phir kabhi yaham na aungi. maim nirapradh hum.
minakshi ne uski or naphrat se dekhkar kaha -- ham, tu nirapradh hai. janti hai na, maim kaun hum! chali ja. ab kabhi yaham na ana. ham striyam bhog-vilas ki chizen haim hi, tera koi dosh nahin!
veshya ne uske charnon par sir rakhkar avesh men kaha -- paramatma apko sukhi rakhe. jaisa apka nam sunti thi, vaisa hi paya.
sukhi rahne se tumhara kya ashay hai?
ap jo samjhen maharaniji!
nahin, tum batao.
veshya ke jan nakhon men sama gaye. kaham se kaham ashirvad dene chali. jan bach gayi thi, chupke se apni rah leni chahiye thi, dua dene ki sanak savar hui. ab kaise jan bache. darti-darti boli -- huzur ka eqbal barhe, nam barhe.
minakshi muskarayi -- ham, thik hai.
vah akar apni kar men baithi, hakim-zila ke bangle par pahunchkar is kand ki khabar di aur apni kothi men chali ayi. tab se aurat-aadmi donon ek dusre ke khun ke pyase the. digvijaysimh rivalvar liye uski tak men phira karte aur vah bhi apni raksha ke lie do pahalvan thakuron ko apne sath liye rahti thi. aur NAmERai Sahib ne sukh ka jo svarg banaya tha, use apni zindagi se hi dhvams hote dekh rahe the. aur ab samsar se nirash hokar unki atma antamurkhi hoti jati thi. ab tak abhilashaon se jivan ke lie prerna milti rahti thi. udhar ka rasta band ho jane par unka man ap hi ap bhakti ki or jhuka, jo abhilashaon se kahim barhkar satya tha. jis nayi jaydad ke asre qaraz liye the, vah jaydad qaraz ki purauti kiye bina hi hath se nikal gayi thi aur vah bojh sir par lada hua tha. ministari se zarur achchhi raqam milti thi; magar vah sari ki sari us maryada ka palan karne men hi ud jati thi aur NAmERai Sahib ko apna rajsi that nibhane ke lie vahi asamiyon par izafa aur bedkhali aur nazrana karna aur lena parta tha, jisse unhen naphrat thi. vah praja ko takliph na dena chahte the. unki hal par unhen daya ati thi; lekin apni zarurton se hairan the. mushkil yah thi ki upasna aur bhakti men bhi unhen shanti na milti thi. vah moh ko chhorna chahte the; par moh unhen na chhorta tha aur is khinch-tan men unhen beizzati, pachhtava aur ashanti se chhutkara na milta tha. aur jab atma men shanti nahin, to deh kaise svasth rahti? nirog rahne ka sab upay karne par bhi ek na ek badha gale padi rahti thi. rasoi men sabhi tarah ke pakvan bante the; par unke lie vahi mung ki dal aur phulke the. apne aur bhaiyon ko dekhte the jo unse bhi zyada maqruj, apamanit aur shokagrast the, jinke bhog-vilas men, that-bat men kisi tarah ki kami na thi; magar is tarah ki behayai unke bas men na thi. unke man ke unche samskaron ka dhvams na hua tha. par-dard, makkari, nirlajjata aur atyachar ko vah talluqedari ki shobha aur rob-dab ka nam dekar apni atma ko santusht na kar sakte the, aur yahi unki sabse badi har thi.
