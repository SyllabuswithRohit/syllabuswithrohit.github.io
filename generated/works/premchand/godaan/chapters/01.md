# Godaan — Adhyay 1

**Munshi Premchand**

1.
NAmEHori ne donon bailon ko sani-pani dekar apni aurat NAmEDhania se kaha -- NAmEGobar ko ukh gorne bhej dena. maim na jane kab lautum. zara meri lathi de de.
NAmEDhania ke donon hath NAmEGobar se bhare the. uple pathkar ayi thi. boli -- are, kuchh ras-pani to kar lo. aisi jaldi kya hai.
NAmEHori ne apne jhuriryon se bhare hue mathe ko sikorkar kaha -- tujhe ras-pani ki padi hai, mujhe yah chinta hai ki aber ho gayi to malik se bhent na hogi. asnan-puja karne lagenge, to ghanton baithe bit jayga.
isi se to kahti hum, kuchh kuchh khana kar lo. aur aj na jaoge to kaun haraz hoga. abhi to parson gaye the.
tu jo bat nahin samjhati, usmen tang kyon arati hai bhai! meri lathi de de aur apna kam dekh. yah isi milte-julte rahne ka parsad hai ki ab tak jan bachi hui hai. nahin kahim pata na lagta ki kidhar gaye. gamv men itne aadmi to haim, kis par bedkhali nahin ayi, kis par kurki nahin ayi. jab dusre ke pamvon-tale apni gardan dabi hui hai, to un pamvon ko sahlane men hi kushal hai.
NAmEDhania itni bartav-kushal na thi. uska soch tha ki hamne zamindar ke khet jote haim, to vah apna lagan hi to lega. uski khushamad kyon karen, uske talve kyon sahlaye. halanki apne vivahit jivan ke in bis barson men use achchhi tarah anubhav ho gaya tha ki chahe kitni hi katar-byont karo, kitna hi pet-tan kato, chahe ek-ek kauri ko dant se pakro; magar lagan bebaq hona mushkil hai. phir bhi vah har na manti thi, aur is vishay par aurat-aadmi men aye din larai chhira rahta tha. uski chhah santanon men ab sirph tin zinda haim, ek larka NAmEGobar koi solah sal ka, aur do larkiya NAmESona aur NAmERupa, barah aur ath sal ki. tin larke bachpan hi men mar gaye. uska man aj bhi kahta tha, agar unki davadaru hoti to ve bach jate; par vah ek dhele ki dava bhi na mangva saki thi. uski hi umra abhi kya thi. chhattisvam hi sal to tha; par sare bal pak gaye the, chehre par jhurriyam par gayi thim. sari deh dhal gayi thi, vah sundar gehuam rang samvla gaya tha aur ankhon se bhi kam sujhne laga tha. pet ki chinta hi ke vajah to. kabhi to jivan ka sukh na mila. is chirasthayi jinarvastha ne uske atma-izzat ko udasinta ka rup de diya tha. jis ghar-parivar men pet ki rotiyam bhi na milen, uske lie itni khushamad kyon? is hal se uska man barabar bagavat kiya karta tha. aur do char ghurkiya kha lene par hi use yatharth ka jnan hota tha.
usne parast hokar NAmEHori ki lathi, mirajai, jute, pagri aur tamakhu ka batua lakar samne patak diye.
NAmEHori ne uski or ankhen tarer kar kaha -- kya sasural jana hai jo panchon posak layi hai? sasural men bhi to koi javan sali-salhaj nahin baithi hai, jise jakar dikhaum.
NAmEHori ke gahre samvle, pichke hue chehre par muskarahat ki mriduta jhalak padi. NAmEDhania ne lajate hue kaha -- aise hi to bade sajile javan ho ki sali-salhajen tumhen dekh kar rijh jayangi!
NAmEHori ne phati hui mirajai ko badi savdhani se tah karke khat par rakhte hue kaha -- to kya tu samjhati hai, maim burha ho gaya? abhi to chalis bhi nahin hue. mard sathe par pathe hote haim.
jakar sise men mumh dekho. tum-jaise mard sathe par pathe nahin hote. dudh-ghi anjan lagane tak ko to milta nahin, pathe honge! tumhari hal dekh-dekhkar to maim aur bhi sukhi jati hum ki bhagavan yah burhapa kaise katega? kiske dvar par bhikh mangenge?
NAmEHori ki vah kshanik mriduta yatharth ki is anch men jaise jhulas gayi. lakri sambhalta hua bola -- sathe tak pahunchne ki naubat na ane payegi NAmEDhania! iske pahle hi chal denge.
NAmEDhania ne beizzati kiya -- achchha rahne do, mat asubh mumh se nikalo. tumse koi achchhi bat bhi kahe, to lagte ho kosne.
NAmEHori lathi kandhe par rakhkar ghar se nikla, to NAmEDhania dvar par khadi use der tak dekhti rahi. uske in mayusi-bhare shabdon ne NAmEDhania ke chot khaye hue dil men atankmay kampan-sa dal diya tha. vah jaise apne naritva ke sampunar tap aur vrat se apne pati ko abhay-dan de rahi thi. uske man se jaise ashirvadon ka vyuh-sa nikal kar NAmEHori ko apne andar chhipaye leta tha. vipannata ke is athah sagar men sohag hi vah trin tha, jise pakre hue vah sagar ko par kar rahi thi. in asangat shabdon ne yatharth ke pas hone par bhi mano jhatka dekar uske hath se vah tinke ka sahara chhin lena chaha bilk yatharth ke pas hone ke vajah hi unmen itni dard-shakti a gayi thi. kana kahne se kane ko jo duhkh hota hai, vah kya do ankhomvale aadmi ko ho sakta hai?
NAmEHori qadam barhaye chala jata tha. pagadandi ke donon or ukh ke paudhon ki lahrati hui hariyali dekh kar usne man men kaha -- bhagavan kahim gaum se barkha kar den aur damri bhi subhite se rahe, to ek gay zarur lega. deshi gayen to na dudh den na unke bachhve hi kisi kam ke hon. bahut hua to teli ke kolhu men chale. nahin, vah pachhaim gay lega. uski khub seva karega. kuchh nahin to char-panch ser dudh hoga. NAmEGobar dudh ke lie taras-taras kar rah jata hai. is umir men na khaya-piya, to phir kab khayega. sal-bhar bhi dudh pi le, to dekhne layaq ho jay. bachhve bhi achchhe bail niklenge. do sau se kam ki gomi na hogi. phir, gau se hi to dvar ki NAmESobha hai. sabere-sabere gau ke darshan ho jayam to kya kahna. na jane kab yah sadh puri hogi, kab vah shubh din ayega!
har ek grihasth ki tarah NAmEHori ke man men bhi gau ki lalsa chirkal se sanchit chali ati thi. yahi uske jivan ka sabse bada svapna, sabse badi sadh thi. baink sud se chain karne ya zameen kharidne ya mahal banvane ki bahut bada akankshaen uske nanhen-se dil men kaise samatim.
jeth ka surya amon ke jhurmut men se nikalkar akash par chhayi hui lalima ko apne rajat-pratap se tej pradan karta hua upar charh raha tha aur hava men gamir ane lagi thi. donon or kheton men kam karanevale kisan use dekhkar ram-ram karte aur izzat-bhav se chilam pine ka nimantran dete the; par NAmEHori ko itna phursat kaham tha. uske andar baithi hui izzat-lalsa aisa adar pakad uske sukhe mumh par garva ki jhalak paida kar rahi thi. malikon se milte-julte rahne hi ka to yah prasad hai ki sab uska adar karte haim. nahin use kaun puchhta? panch bighe ke kisan ki bisat hi kya? yah kam adar nahin hai ki tin-tin, char-char halvale mahto bhi uske samne sir jhukate haim.
ab vah kheton ke bich ki pagadandi chhorkar ek khaleti men a gaya tha, jaham barsat men pani bhar jane ke vajah tari rahti thi aur jeth men kuchh hariyali nazar ati thi. as-pas ke gamvon ki gauen yaham charne aya karti thim. us samay men bhi yaham ki hava men kuchh tazgi aur thandak thi. NAmEHori ne do-tin samsen zor se lim. uske ji men aya, kuchh der yahim baith jay. din-bhar to lu-lapat men marna hai hi. kai kisan is gaddhe ka patta likhane ko taiyar the. achchhi raqam dete the; par ishvar bhala kare NAmERai Sahib ka ki unhomne saf kah diya, yah zameen janvaron ki charai ke lie chhod di gayi hai aur kisi dam par bhi na uthayi jaygi. koi svarthi zamindar hota, to kahta, gayen jayam bhar men, hamen rupae milte haim, kyon chhoren. par NAmERai Sahib abhi tak purani maryada nibhate ate haim. jo malik praja ko na pale, vah bhi koi aadmi hai?
sahsa usne dekha, NAmEBhola apni gayen liye isi taraf chala a raha hai. NAmEBhola isi gamv se mile hue purve ka gvala tha aur dudh-makkhn ka vyavsay karta tha. achchha dam mil jane par kabhi-kabhi kisanon ke hath gayen bech bhi deta tha. NAmEHori ka man un gayon ko dekh kar lalcha gaya. agar NAmEBhola vah agevali gay use de to kya kahna! rupae age pichhe deta rahega. vah janta tha ghar men rupae nahin haim, abhi tak lagan nahin chukaya ja saka, bisesar sah ka dena bhi baqi hai, jis par ane rupae ka sud charh raha hai; lekin daridrata men jo ek tarah ki aduradarshita hoti hai, vah nirlajjata jo taqaze, gali aur mar se bhi bhaybhit nahin hoti, usne use protsahit kiya. barson se jo sadh man ko andolit kar rahi thi, usne use vichlit kar diya. NAmEBhola ke pas jakar bola -- ram-ram NAmEBhola bhai, kaho kya rang-dhang hai. suna abki mele se nayi gayen laye ho.
NAmEBhola ne rukhai se javab diya. NAmEHori ke man ki bat usne tar li thi -- ham, do bachhiyen aur do gayen laya. pahalevali gayen sab sukh gayi thim. bandhi par dudh na pahunche to guzar kaise ho. NAmEHori ne anevali gay ke putthe par hath rakhkar kaha -- dudhar to malum hoti hai. kitne men li?
NAmEBhola ne shan jamayi -- abki bazar bada tez raha mahto, iske assi rupae dene pade. ankhen nikal gayim. tis-tis rupae to donon kaloron ke diye. tis par gahak rupae ka ath ser dudh mangta hai.
bada bhari kaleja hai tum logon ka bhai, lekin phir laye bhi to vah mal ki yaham das-panch gamvon men to kisi ke pas niklegi nahin.
NAmEBhola par nasha charhne laga. bola -- NAmERai Sahib iske sau rupae dete the. donon kaloron ke pachas-pachas rupae, lekin hamne na diye. bhagavan ne chaha, to sau rupae isi byan men pit lunga.
ismen kya shak hai bhai! malik kya khake lenge. nazrane men mil jay, to bhale le len. yah tumhim logon ka gudar hai ki anjuli-bhar rupae taqdir ke bharose gin dete ho. yahi ji chahta hai ki iske darsan karta rahum. dhanya hai tumhara jivan ki gauon ki itni seva karte ho. hamen to gay ka NAmEGobar bhi mayassar nahin. girast ke ghar men ek gay bhi na ho, to kitni sharm ki bat hai. sal-ke-sal bit jate haim, goras ke darsan nahin hote. gharvali bar-bar kahti hai, NAmEBhola bhaiya se kyon nahin kahte. maim kah deta hum, kabhi milenge to kahunga. tumhare subhav se badi parsan rahti hai. kahti hai, aisa mard hi nahin dekha ki jab baten karenge, nichi ankhen karke, kabhi sir nahin uthate.
NAmEBhola par jo nasha charh raha tha, use is bharpur pyale ne aur gahra kar diya. bola -- bhala aadmi vahi hai, jo dusron ki bahu-beti ko apni bahu-beti samjhe. jo dusht kisi mehriya ki or take, use goli mar dena chahiye.
yah tumne lakh rupye ki bat kah di bhai. bas sajjan vahi, jo dusron ki abru ko apni abru samjhe.
jis tarah mard ke mar jane se aurat anath ho jati hai, usi tarah aurat ke mar jane se mard ke hath-pamv tut jate haim. mera to ghar ujar gaya mahto, koi ek lota pani denevala bhi nahin.
gat varsh NAmEBhola ki aurat lu lag jane se mar gayi thi. yah NAmEHori janta tha, lekin pachas baras ka khankhar NAmEBhola bhitar se itna snigdh hai, vah na janta tha. aurat ki lalsa uski ankhon men sajal ho gayi thi. NAmEHori ko asan mil gaya. uski vyavaharik kisan-akal sajag ho gayi.
purani masal jhuthi thodi hai -- bin gharni ghar bhut ka dera. kahim sagai nahin thik kar lete?
tak men hum mahto, par koi jaldi phamsta nahin. sau-pachas kharach karne ko bhi taiyar hum. jaisi bhagavan ki ichchha.
ab maim bhi firk men rahunga. bhagavan chahenge, to jaldi ghar bas jayga.
bas yahi samajh lo ki ubar jaunga bhaiya! ghar men khane ko bhagavan ka diya bahut hai. char paseri roz dudh ho jata hai, lekin kis kam ka.
mere sasural men ek mehriya hai. tin-char sal hue, uska aadmi use chhod-kar kalakatte chala gaya. bechari pisai karke guzar kar rahi hai. bal-bachcha bhi koi nahin. dekhne-sunne men achchhi hai. bas, lachchhmi samajh lo.
NAmEBhola ka sikura hua chehra jaise chikna gaya. ummid men kitni NAmESudha hai. bola -- ab to tumhara hi asra hai mahto! chhutti ho, to chalo ek din dekh ayen.
maim thik-thak karke tab tumse kahunga. bahut utavli karne se bhi kam bigar jata hai.
jab tumhari chah ho tab chalo. utavli kahe ki. is kabri par man lalchaya ho, to le lo.
yah gay mere man ki nahin hai dada. maim tumhen nuqsan nahin pahunchana chahta. apna dharam yah nahin hai ki mitron ka gala dabayen. jaise itne din bite haim, vaise aur bhi bit jayenge.
tum to aisi baten karte ho NAmEHori, jaise ham-tum do haim. tum gay le jao, dam jo chahe dena. jaise mere ghar rahi, vaise tumhare ghar rahi. assi rupae men li thi, tum assi rupye hi de dena. jao.
lekin mere pas nagad nahin hai dada, samajh lo.
to tumse nagad mangta kaun hai bhai!
NAmEHori ki chhati gaz-bhar ki ho gayi. assi rupae men gay mamhgi na thi. aisa achchha dil-daul, donon jun men chhah-sat ser dudh, sidhi aisi ki bachcha bhi duh le. iska to ek-ek bachha sau-sau ka hoga. dvar par bandhegi to dvar ki shobha barh jaygi. use abhi koi char sau rupae dene the; lekin udhar ko vah ek tarah se mufta samjhata tha. kahim NAmEBhola ki sagai thik ho gayi to sal do sal to vah bolega bhi nahin. sagai na bhi hui, to NAmEHori ka kya bigrata hai. yahi to hoga, NAmEBhola bar-bar tagada karne ayega, bigrega, galiyam dega. lekin NAmEHori ko iski zyada sharm na thi. is bartav ka vah adi tha. kisan ke jivan ka to yah prasad hai. NAmEBhola ke sath vah chhal kar raha tha aur yah vyapar uski mayarda ke anukul tha. ab bhi len-den men uske lie likha-parhi hone aur na hone men koi antar na tha. sukhe-bure ki vipadaen uske man ko bhi, banaye rahti thim. ishvar ka raudra rup hamesha uske samne rahta tha. par yah chhal uski niti men chhal na tha. yah sirph svarth-siddhi thi aur yah koi buri bat na thi. is tarah ka chhal to vah din-rat karta rahta tha. ghar men do-char rupye pade rahne par bhi mahajan ke samne qasmen kha jata tha ki ek pai bhi nahin hai. san ko kuchh gila kar dena aur rui men kuchh binaule bhar dena uski niti men jayaj tha. aur yaham to sirph svarth na tha, thoda-sa manoranjan bhi tha. buddhon ka burhbhas hasyaspad vastu hai aur aise buddhon se agar kuchh ainth bhi liya jay, to koi dosh-pap nahin.
NAmEBhola ne gay ki paghiya NAmEHori ke hath men dete hue kaha -- le jao mahto, tum bhi yad karoge. byate hi chhah ser dudh le lena. chalo, maim tumhare ghar tak pahuncha dum. sait tumhen anyan samajhkar raston men kuchh dik kare. ab tumse sach kahta hum, malik nabbe rupae dete the, par unke yaham gauon ki kya qadar. mujhse lekar kisi hakim-hukkam ko de dete. hakimon ko gau ki seva se matlab. vah to khun chusna-bhar jante haim. jab tak dudh deti, rakhte, phir kisi ke hath bech dete. kiske palle parti kaun jane. rupya hi sab kuchh nahin hai bhaiya, kuchh apna dharam bhi to hai. tumhare ghar aram se rahegi to. yah na hoga ki tum ap khakar so raho aur gau bhukhi khadi rahe. uski seva karoge, chumakaroge. gau hamen asirvad degi. tumse kya kahum bhaiya, ghar men changul bhar bhi bhusa nahin raha. rupae sab bazar men nikal gaye. socha tha mahajan se kuchh lekar bhusa le lenge; lekin mahajan ka pahla hi nahin chuka. usne inkar kar diya. itne janvaron ko kya khilaven, yahi chinta mare dalti hai. chutki-chutki bhar khilaum, to man-bhar roz ka kharach hai. bhagavan hi par lagayen to lage.
NAmEHori ne hamadardi ke avaz men kaha -- tumne hamse pahle kyon nahin kaha? hamne ek gari bhusa bech diya.
NAmEBhola ne matha thokkar kaha -- isilie nahin kaha bhaiya ki sabse apna duhkh kyon roum. bantta koi nahin, hamste sab haim. jo gayen sukh gayi haim unka gam nahin, patti-satti khilakar jila lunga; lekin ab yah to ratib bina nahin rah sakti. ho sake, to das-bis rupye bhuse ke lie de do.
kisan pakka svarthi hota hai, ismen shak nahin. uski ganth se rishvat ke paise badi mushkil se niklate haim, bhav-tav men bhi vah chaukas hota hai, byaj ki ek-ek pai chhurane ke lie vah mahajan ki ghanton chirauri karta hai, jab tak pakka yakin na ho jay, vah kisi ke phuslane men nahin ata, lekin uska pura jivan prakriti se sthayi sahyog hai. vrikshon men phal lagte haim, unhen janta khati hai; kheti men anaj hota hai, vah samsar ke kam ata hai; gay ke than men dudh hota hai, vah khud pine nahin jati dusre hi pite haim; meghon se varsha hoti hai, usse prithvi tript hoti hai. aisi sangti men kutsit svarth ke lie kaham sthan. NAmEHori kisan tha aur kisi ke jalte hue ghar men hath senkna usne sikha hi na tha.
NAmEBhola ki musibat-katha sunte hi uski manovritti badal gayi. paghiya ko NAmEBhola ke hath men lautata hua bola -- rupae to dada mere pas nahin haim, ham thoda-sa bhusa bacha hai, vah tumhen dunga. chalkar uthva lo. bhuse ke lie tum gay bechoge, aur maim lunga. mere hath na kat jayenge?
