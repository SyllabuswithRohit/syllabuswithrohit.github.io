bholaa ne gad gada galaa se kahaa -- to kisee ko bheja doo.N bhoose ke lie?

bholaa kee aa.Nkhon men aa.Nsoo bhara aaye| bola -- tumane aaja mujhe ubaara liya horee bhaaee! mujhe aba maalooma hua ki main sansaara men akelaa naheen hoo.N| meraa bhee koee hitoo hai| ek pala ke baada usane phir kahaa -- us baat ko bhoola na jana|
