# Godaan — Adhyay 26

**Munshi Premchand**

26.
lala NAmEPateshwari patvari-samuday ke sad gunon ke sakshat avtar the. vah yah na dekh sakte the ki koi asami apne dusre bhai ki inch bhar bhi zameen daba le. na vah yahi dekh sakte the ki asami kisi mahajan ke rupae daba le. gamv ke sab praniyon ke hiton ki raksha karna unka param dharm tha. samjhaute ya mel-jol men unka yakin na tha, yah to nijirvita ke lakshan haim! vah to sangharsh ke pujari the, jo sajivta ka lakshan hai. aye din is jivan ko uttejna dene ka koshish karte rahte the. ek-na-ek phuljhari chhorte rahte the. mangru sah par in dinon unki khas kripa-drisht thi. mangru sah gamv ka sabse dhani aadmi tha; par sthaniy rajniti men bilkul bhag na leta tha. rob ya adhikar ki lalsa use na thi. makan bhi uska gamv ke bahar tha, jaham usne ek bag aur ek kuam aur ek chhota-sa shiv-mandir banva liya tha. bal-bachcha koi na tha; isalie len-den bhi kam kar diya tha aur adhiktar puja-path men hi laga rahta tha. kitne hi asamiyon ne uske rupae hazam kar lie the; par usne kisi par nalish-fariyad na ki. NAmEHori par bhi uske sud-byaj milakar koi derh sau ho gaye the; magar na NAmEHori ko karz chukane ki koi chinta thi aur na use vasul karne ki. do-char bar usne taqaza kiya, ghurka-danta bhi; magar NAmEHori ki hal dekhkar chup ho baitha. abki samyog se NAmEHori ki ukh gamv bhar ke upar thi. kuchh nahin to uske do-dhai sau sidhe ho jayange, aisa logon ka anuman tha. NAmEPateshwari prasad ne mangru ko sujhaya ki agar is vaqta NAmEHori par dava kar diya jay to sab rupae vasul ho jayam. mangru itna dayalu nahin, jitna kamchor tha. jhanjhat men parna na chahta tha; magar jab NAmEPateshwari ne zimma liya ki use ek din bhi kachhari na jana parega, na koi dusra takliph hoga, baithe-baithaye uski digri ho jaygi, to usne nalish karne ki ijazat de di, aur adalat-kharch ke lie rupae bhi de diye. NAmEHori ko khabar bhi na thi ki kya khichri pak rahi hai. kab dava dayar hua, kab digri hui, use vilkul pata na chala. qurqa-amin uski ukh nilam karne aya, tab use malum hua. sara gamv khet ke kinare jama ho gaya. NAmEHori mangru sah ke pas daura aur NAmEDhania NAmEPateshwari ko galiyam dene lagi. uski sahaj-akal ne bata diya ki NAmEPateshwari hi ki karastani hai, magar mangru sah puja par the, mil na sake aur NAmEDhania galiyon ki varsha karke bhi NAmEPateshwari ka kuchh bigar na saki. udhar ukh derh sau rupae men nilam ho gayi aur boli bhi ho gayi mangru sah hi ke nam. koi dusra aadmi na bol saka. NAmEDatadin men bhi NAmEDhania ki galiyam sunne ka himmat na tha.
NAmEDhania ne NAmEHori ko uttejit karke kaha -- baithe kya ho, jakar patvari se puchhte kyon nahin, yahi dharam hai tumhara gamv-ghar ke admiyo ke sath?
NAmEHori ne dinta se kaha -- puchhne ke lie tune mumh bhi rakha ho. teri galiyam kya unhomne na suni hongi?
jo gali khane ka kam karega, use galiyam milengi hi.
tu galiyam bhi degi aur bhai-chadha bhi nibhayegi?
dekhungi, mere khet ke nagich kaun jata hai.
milvale akar kat le jayange, tu kya karegi, aur maim kya karunga. galiyam dekar apni jibh ki khujli chahe mita le.
mere jite-ji koi mera khet kat le jayga?
ham-ham, tere aur mere jite-ji. sara gamv milkar bhi use nahin rok sakta. ab vah chiz meri nahin, mangru sah ki hai.
mangru sah ne mar-markar jeth ki duphari men sinchai aur gorai ki thi?
vah sab tune kiya; magar ab vah chiz mangru sah ki hai. ham unke karazdar nahin haim?
ukh to gayi; lekin uske sath hi ek nayi samasya a padi. NAmEDulari isi ukh par rupae dene par taiyar hui thi. ab vah kis jamanat par rupae de? abhi uske pahle hi ke do sau pade hue the. socha tha, ukh ke purane rupae mil jayange, to naya hisab chalne lagega. uski nazar men NAmEHori ki sakh do sau tak thi. isse zyada dena jokhim tha. sahalag sir par tha. tithi nishchit ho chuki thi. gauri mahto ne sari taiyariyam kar li hongi. ab shadi ka talna namumkin tha. NAmEHori ko aisa gussa ata tha ki jakar NAmEDulari ka gala daba de. jitni chirauri-binti ho sakti thi, vah kar chuka; magar vah patthr ki devi zara bhi na pasiji. usne chalte-chalte hath bandh kar kaha -- NAmEDulari, maim tumhare rupae lekar bhag na jaunga. na itni jald mara hi jata hum. khet haim, per-palon haim, ghar haim, javan beta hai. tumhare rupae mare na jayange, meri izzat ja rahi hai, ise sambhalo; magar NAmEDulari ne daya ko vyapar men milana svikar na kiya; agar vyapar ko vah daya ka rup de sakti, to use koi apatti na hoti. par daya ko vyapar ka rup dena usne na sikha tha.
NAmEHori ne ghar akar NAmEDhania se kaha -- ab? NAmEDhania ne usi par dil ka gubar nikala -- yahi to tum chahte the. NAmEHori ne zakhmi ankhon se dekha -- mera hi dosh hai?
kisi ka dosh ho, hui tumhare man ki.
teri chah hai ki zameen rehan rakh dum?
zameen rehan rakh doge, to karoge kya?
majuri.
magar zameen donon ko ek-si pyari thi. usi par to unki izzat aur abru avalimbat thi. jiske pas zameen nahin, vah grihasth nahin, majur hai. NAmEHori ne kuchh javab na pakad puchha -- to kya kahti hai?
NAmEDhania ne ahat gala se kaha -- kahna kya hai. gauri barat lekar ayange. ek jun khila dena. sabere beti bida kar dena. duniya hamsegi, hams le. bhagavan ki yahi chah hai, ki hamari nak kate, mumh men kalikh lage to ham kya karenge.
sahsa NAmENohari chundri pahne samne se jati hui dikhai di. NAmEHori ko dekhte hi usne zara-sa ghunghat nikal liya. usse samdhi ka nata manti thi. NAmEDhania se uska parichay ho chuka tha. usne pukara -- aj kidhar chali samdhin? ao, baitho.
NAmENohari ne digvijay kar liya tha aur ab janmat ko apne paksh men bator lene ka koshish kar rahi thi. akar khadi ho gayi.
NAmEDhania ne use sir se pamv tak alochna ki ankhon se dekhkar kaha -- aj idhar kaise bhul parim?
NAmENohari ne katar avaz men kaha -- aise hi tum logon se milne chali ayi. bitiya ka byah kab tak hai?
NAmEDhania snigdh bhav se boli -- bhagavan ke adhin hai, jab ho jay.
maimne to suna, isi sahalag men hoga. tithi thik ho gayi hai?
ham, tithi to thik ho gayi hai.
mujhe bhi nevta dena.
tumhari to ladki hai, nevta kaisa?
dahej ka saman to mangva liya hoga. zara maim bhi dekhum.
NAmEDhania asamanjas men padi, kya kahe. NAmEHori ne use sambhala -- abhi to koi saman nahin mangvaya hai, aur saman kya karna hai, kus-ladki to dena hai. NAmENohari ne avishvas-bhari ankhon se dekha -- kus-ladki kyon doge mahto, pahli beti hai, dil kholkar karo. NAmEHori hamsa; mano kah raha ho, tumhen charon or hara dikhayi deta hoga; yaham to sukha hi pada hua hai.
rupae-paise ki tangi hai, kya kholkar karum. tumse kaun parda hai.
beta kamata hai, tum kamate ho; phir bhi rupae-paise ki tangi? kise yakin ayega.
beta hi layaq hota, to phir kahe ko rona tha. chitthi-pattar tak bhejta nahin, rupae kya bhejega. yah dusra sal hai, ek chitthi nahin.
itne men NAmESona bailon ke chare ke lie hariyali ka ek gattha sir par liye, yauvan ko apne anchal se churati, ladki-si asan, ayi aur gattha vahim patakkar andar chalo gayi. NAmENohari ne kaha -- ladki to khub sayani ho gayi hai.
NAmEDhania boli -- ladki ki barh remr ki barh hai. nahin hai abhi kai din ki!
dulha to thik ho gaya hai na?
ham, dulha to thik hai. rupae ka bandobast ho gaya, to isi mahine men byah kar denge. NAmENohari dil ki ochhi thi. idhar usne jo thore-se rupae jore the, ve uske pet men uchhal rahe the; agar vah NAmESona ke byah ke lie kuchh rupae de de, to kitna yash milega. sare gamv men uski charcha ho jaygi. log chakit hokar kahenge, NAmENohari ne itne rupae de die. badi devi hai. NAmEHori aur NAmEDhania donon ghar-ghar uska bakhan karte phirenge. gamv men uska man-izzat kitna barh jayga. vah ungli dikhanevalon ka mumh si degi. phir kiski himmat hai, jo us par hamse, ya us par avazen kase. abhi sara gamv uska dushman hai. tab sara gamv uska hitaishi ho jayga. is kalpana se uski mudra khil gayi.
thore-bahut se kam chalta ho, to mujhse lo; jab hath men rupae a jayam to de dena.
NAmEHori aur NAmEDhania donon hi ne uski or dekha. nahin, NAmENohari dillagi nahin kar rahi hai. donon ki ankhon men hairani tha, kritajnyata thi, shak tha aur sharm thi. NAmENohari utni buri nahin hai, jitna log samjhate haim. NAmENohari ne phir kaha -- tumhari aur hamari izzat ek hai. tumhari hamsi ho to kya meri hamsi na hogi? kaise bhi hua ho, par ab to tum hamare samdhi ho.
NAmEHori ne sakuchate hue kaha -- tumhare rupae to ghar men hi haim, jab kam parega le lage. aadmi apnon hi ka bharosa to karta hai; magar upar se intazam ho jay, to ghar ke rupae kyon chhue.
NAmEDhania ne anumodan kiya -- ham, aur kya.
NAmENohari ne apanapan jataya -- jab ghar men rupae haim, to baharvalo ke samne hath kyon phailao. sud bhi dena parega, us par istam likho, gavahi karao, dasturi do, khusamad karo. ham, mere rupae men chhut lagi ho, to dusri bat hai.
NAmEHori ne sambhala -- nahin, nahin NAmENohari, jab ghar men kam chal jayga, to bahar kyon hath phailayenge; lekin apasvali bat hai. kheti-badi ka bharosa nahin. tumhen jaldi koi kam pada aur ham rupae na juta sake, to tumhen bhi bura lagega aur hamari jan bhi musibat men paregi. isse kahta tha. nahin, ladki to tumhari hai.
mujhe abhi rupae ki aisi jaldi nahin hai.
to tumhim se lenge. kanyadan ka phal bhi kyon bahar jay.
kitne rupae chahiye?
tum kitne de sakogi?
sau men kam chal jayga?
NAmEHori ko lalach aya. bhagavan ne chhappar pharkar rupae diye haim, to jitna le sake, utna kyon na le!
sau men bhi chal jayga. panch sau men bhi chal jayga. jaisa hausla ho.
mere pas kul do sau rupae haim, vah maim de dungi.
to itne men badi khuspheli se kam chal jayga. anaj ghar men hai; magar thakurain, aj tumse kahta hum, maim tumhen aisi lachchhmi na samjhata tha. is zamane men kaun kiski madad karta hai, aur kiske pas hai. tumne mujhe dubte se bacha liya.
diya-batti ka samay a gaya tha. thandak parne lagi thi. zameen ne nili chadar orh li thi. NAmEDhania andar jakar angithi layi. sab tapne lage. pual ke prakash men chhabili, rangili, kulta NAmENohari unki samne vardan-si baithi thi. is samay uski un ankhon men kitni sahridyata thi; kapolon par kitni sharm, othon par kitni satprerna! kuchh der tak idhar-udhar ki baten karke NAmENohari uth khadi hui aur yah kahti hui ghar chali -- ab der ho rahi hai. kal tum akar rupae le lena mahto!
chalo, maim tumhen pahuncha dum. nahin-nahin, tum baitho, maim chali jaungi.
ji to chahta hai, tumhen kandhe par baithakar pahunchaum.
nokheram ki chaupal gamv ke dusre sire par thi, aur bahar-bahar jane ka rasta saf tha. donon usi raste se chale. ab charon or sannata tha. NAmENohari ne kaha -- tanik samjha dete ravat ko. kyon sabse larai kiya karte haim. jab inhim logon ke bich men rahna hai, to aise rahna chahiye na ki char aadmi apne ho jayam. aur inka hal yah hai ki sabse larai, sabse jhagra. jab tum mujhe parde men nahin rakh sakte, mujhe dusron ki majuri karni parti hai, to yah kaise nibh sakta hai ki maim na kisi se hamsum, na bolum, na koi meri or take, na hamse. yah sab to parde men hi ho sakta hai. puchho, koi meri or takta ya ghurta hai to maim kya karum. uski ankhen to nahin phor sakti. phir mel-muhabbat se aadmi ke sau kam niklate haim. jaisa samay dekho, vaisa bartav karo. tumhare ghar hathi jhumta tha, to ab vah tumhare kis kam ka. ab to tum tin rupae ke majur ho. mere ghar to bhaims lagti thi, lekin ab to majurin hum; magar unki samajh men koi bat ati hi nahin. kabhi larkon ke sath rahne ki sochte haim, kabhi lakhanau jakar rahne ki sochte haim. nak men dam kar rakha hai mere.
NAmEHori ne thakurasuhati ki -- yah NAmEBhola ki sarasar nadani hai. burhe hue, ab to unhen samajh ani chahiye. maim samjha dunga.
