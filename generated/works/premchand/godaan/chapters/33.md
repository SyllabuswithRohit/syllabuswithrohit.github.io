# Godaan — Adhyay 33

**Munshi Premchand**

33.
daktar NAmEmehta parikshak se pariksharthi ho gaye haim. NAmEmalati se dur-dur rahkar unhen aisi shak hone lagi hai ki use kho na baithen. kai mahinon se NAmEmalati unke pas na ayi thi aur jab vah vikal hokar uske ghar gaye, to mulaqat na hui. jin dinon rudrapal aur saroj ka premkand chalta raha, tab to NAmEmalati unki salah lene prayah ek-do bar roz ati thi; par jab se donon inglaind chale gaye the, unka ana-jana band ho gaya tha. ghar par bhi mushkil se milti. aisa malum hota tha, jaise vah unse bachti hai, jaise balapurvak apne man ko unki or se hata lena chahti hai. jis pustak men vah in dinon lage hue the, vah age barhne se inkar kar rahi thi, jaise unka manoyog lupt ho gaya ho. ghar-intzam men to vah kabhi bahut kushal na the. sab milkar ek hazar rupae se zyada mahine men kama lete the; magar bachat ek dhele ki bhi na hoti thi. roti-dal khane ke siva aur unke hath kuchh na tha. takalluf agar kuchh tha to vah unki kar thi, jise vah khud draiv karte the. kuchh rupae kitabon men ud jate the, kuchh chandon men, kuchh garib chhatron ki paravrish men aur apne bag ki sajavat men jisse unhen ishqa-sa tha. tarah-tarah ke paudhe aur vanaspatiyam videshon se mahange damon mangana aur unko palna; yahi unka mansik chatorapan tha ya ise dimagi aiyashi kahen; magar idhar kai mahinon se us bagiche ki or se bhi vah kuchh virakt-se ho rahe the aur ghar ka intazam aur bhi badtar ho gaya tha. khate do phulke aur kharch ho jate sau se upar! achkan purani ho gayi thi; magar isi par unhomne karake ka jara kat diya. nayi achkan silvane ki taufiq na hui thi. kabhi kabhi bina ghi ki dal khakar uthna parta. kab ghi ka kanastar mangaya tha, iski unhen yad hi na thi, aur maharaj se puchhen bhi to kaise. vah samjhega nahin ki us par avishvas kiya ja raha hai? akhir ek din jab tin nirashaon ke bad chauthi bar NAmEmalati se mulaqat hui aur usne inki yah halat dekhi, to usse na raha gaya. boli -- tum kya abki jara yon hi kat doge? vah achkan pahnate tumhen sharm bhi nahin ati?
NAmEmalati unki patni na hokar bhi unke itne pas thi ki yah saval usne usi sahaj bhav se kiya, jaise apne kisi atmiy se karti. NAmEmehta ne bina jhempe hue kaha -- kya karum NAmEmalati, paisa to bachta hi nahin.
NAmEmalati ko achraj hua -- tum ek hazar se zyada kamate ho, aur tumhare pas apne kapde banvane ko bhi paise nahin? meri amdani kabhi char sau se zyada na thi; lekin maim usi men sari ghar-parivar chalati hum aur kuchh bacha leti hum. akhir tum kya karte ho?
maim ek paisa bhi faltu nahin kharch karta. mujhe koi aisa shauq bhi nahin hai.
achchha, mujhse rupae le jao aur ek jori achkan banva lo.
NAmEmehta ne lajjit hokar kaha -- abki banva lunga. sach kahta hum.
ab ap yaham ayen to aadmi bankar ayen.
yah to badi kari shart hai.
kari sahi. tum jaison ke sath bina karai kiye kam nahin chalta.
magar vaham to sanduq khali tha aur kisi dukan par be paise jane ka himmat na parta tha! NAmEmalati ke ghar jayam to kaun mumh lekar? dil men tarap-tarap kar rah jate the. ek din nayi musibat a padi. idhar kai mahine se makan ka kiraya nahin diya tha. pachahattar rupae mahvar barhte jate the. makandar ne jab bahut taqaze karne par bhi rupae vasul na kar paye, to notis de di; magar notis rupye garhne ka koi jantar to hai nahin. notis ki tarikh nikal gayi aur rupae na pahunche. tab makandar ne mazbur hokar nalish kar di. vah janta tha, NAmEmehta ji bade, sajjan aur paropkari aadmi haim; lekin isse zyada bhalamnasi vah kya karta ki chhah mahine baitha raha. NAmEmehta ne kisi tarah ki pairvi na ki, ekatrafa digri ho gayi, makandar ne turat digri jari karayi aur qurqa-amin NAmEmehta sahab ke pas pahle khabar dene aya; kyunki uska larka yunivarsiti men parhta tha aur use NAmEmehta kuchh vazifa bhi dete the. samyog se us vaqta NAmEmalati bhi baithi thi. boli -- kaisi qurqi hai? kis bat ki?
amin ne kaha -- vahi kiraye ki digri jo hui thi. maimne kaha, huzur ko ittala de dum. char-panch sau ka mamla hai, kaun-si badi raqam hai. das din men bhi rupae de dijie, to koi haraz nahin. maim mahajan ko das din tak ulajhae rahunga.
jab amin chala gaya to NAmEmalati ne beizzati-bhare avaz se puchha -- ab yaham tak naubat pahunch gai! mujhe hairani hota hai ki tum itne mote-mote granth kaise likhte ho. makan ka kiraya chhah-chhah mahine se baqi pada hai aur tumhen khabar nahin.
NAmEmehta sharm se sir jhukakar bole -- khabar kyon nahin hai; lekin rupae bachte hi nahin. maim ek paisa bhi bekar nahin kharch karta.
koi hisab-kitab bhi likhte ho?
hisab kyon nahin rakhta. jo kuchh pata hum, vah sab daraz karta jata hum, nahin inakamataiksavale zinda na chhoren.
aur jo kuchh kharch karte ho vah.
uska to koi hisab nahin rakhta.
kyon?
kaun likhe? bojh-sa lagta hai.
aur yah pothe kaise likh dalte ho?
usmen to khas kuchh nahin karna parta. qalam lekar baith jata hum. har vaqta kharch ka khata to kholkar nahin baithta.
to rupae kaise ada karoge?
kisi se qaraz le lunga. tumhare pas hon to de do.
maim to ek hi shart par de sakti hum. tumhari amdani sab mere hathon men aye aur kharch bhi mere hath se ho.
NAmEmehta khush hokar bole -- vah, agar yah bhar le lo, to kya kahna; muslon dhol bajaum.
NAmEmalati ne digri ke rupae chuka diye aur dusre hi din NAmEmehta ko vah bangla khali karne par mazbur kiya. apne bangle men usne unke lie do bade-bade kamre de diye. unke khana adi ka intzam bhi apni hi ghar-parivar men kar diya. NAmEmehta ke pas aur saman to zyada na tha; magar kitaben kai gari thim. unke donon kamre pustakon se bhar gaye. apna bagicha chhorne ka unhen zarur qalaq hua; lekin NAmEmalati ne apna pura ahata unke lie chhod diya ki jo phul-pattiyam chahen lagayen. NAmEmehta to nishchint ho gaye; lekin NAmEmalati ko unki kamai-kharch par niyantran karne men badi mushkil ka samna karna pada. usne dekha, kamai to ek hazar se zyada hai; magar vah sari ki sari guptadan men ud jati hai. bis-pachchis larke unhim se vazifa pakad skul men parh rahe the. vidhavaon ki tadad bhi isse kam na thi. is kharch men kaise kami kare, yah use na sujhta tha. sara dosh usi ke sir marha jayga, sara apyash usi ke hisse parega. kabhi NAmEmehta par jhunjhlati, kabhi apne upar, kabhi prathiryon ke upar, jo ek asan, udar insaan par apna bhar rakhte zara bhi na sakuchate the. yah dekhkar aur bhi jhunjhalahat hoti thi ki in dan lene valon men kuchh to iske chitthi hi na the. ek din usne NAmEmehta ko are hathon liya. NAmEmehta ne uska akshep sunkar nishchint bhav se kaha -- tumhen akhtiyar hai, jise chahe do, jise chahe na do. mujhse puchhne ki koi zarurat nahin. ham, javab bhi tumhim ko dena parega.
NAmEmalati ne chirhkar kaha -- ham, aur kya, yash to tum lo, apyash mere sir marho. maim nahin samjhati, tum kis tark se is dan-pratha ka samarthan kar sakte ho. manushya-jati ko is pratha ne jitna kamchor aur muftakhor banaya hai aur uske atmagaurav par jaisa aghat kiya hai, utna anyay ne bhi na kiya hoga; balki mere khyal men anyay ne manushya-jati men bagavat ki bhavna utpann karke samaj ka bada upkar kiya hai.
NAmEmehta ne svikar kiya -- mere bhi yahi khayal haim.
tumhara yah khayal nahin hai.
nahin NAmEmalati, maim sach kahta hum.
to soch aur bartav men itna bhed kyon?
NAmEmalati ne tisre mahine bahuton ko nirash kiya. kisi ko saf javab diya, kisi se mazburi jatai, kisi ki fajihat ki. mistar NAmEmehta ka bajat to dhire-dhire thik ho gaya; magar isse unko ek tarah ki pachhtava hui. NAmEmalati ne jab tisre mahine men tin sau ki bachat dikhayi, tab vah usse kuchh bole nahin; magar unki nazar men uska gaurav kuchh kam zarur ho gaya. nari men dan aur tyag hona chahiye. uski yahi sabse badi vibhuti hai. isi adhar par samaj ka bhavan khada hai. vanik-akal ko vah zaruri burai hi samjhate the. jis din NAmEmehta ki achkanen ban kar ayim aur nayi ghadi ayi, vah sankoch ke mare kai din bahar na nikle. atma-seva se bada unki nazar men dusra jurm na tha. magar rahasya ki bat yah thi ki NAmEmalati unko to lekhe-dayorhe men kaskar bandhna chahti thi. unke paisa-dan ke dvar band kar dena chahti thi; par khud jivan-dan dene men apne samay aur sadashyata ko donon hathon se lutati thi. amiron ke ghar to vah bina fis liye na jati thi; lekin garibon ko mufta dekhti thi, mufta dava bhi deti thi. donon men antar itna hi tha, ki NAmEmalati ghar ki bhi thi aur bahar ki bhi; NAmEmehta sirph bahar ke the, ghar unke lie na tha. nijatva donon mitana chahte the. NAmEmehta ka rasta saf tha. un par apni zan ke siva aur koi zimmedari na thi. NAmEmalati ka rasta mushkil tha, us par dayitva tha, bandhan tha jise vah tod na sakti thi, na torna chahti thi. us bandhan men hi use jivan ki prerna milti thi. use ab NAmEmehta ko pas se dekhkar yah anubhav ho raha tha ki vah khule jangal men vicharanevale jiv ko pinjre men band nahin kar sakti. aur band kar degi, to vah katne aur nochne daurega. pinjre men sab tarah ka sukh milne par bhi uske jan hamesha jangal ke lie hi tarpate rahenge. NAmEmehta ke lie gharbari duniya ek anyani duniya thi, jiski riti-niti se vah parichit na the. unhomne samsar ko bahar se dekha tha aur use makt aur fareb se hi bhara samjhate the. jidhar dekhte the, udhar hi buraiyam nazar ati thim; magar samaj men jab gaharai men jakar dekha, to unhen malum hua ki in buraiyon ke niche tyag bhi hai pyaar bhi hai, himmat bhi hai, sabra bhi hai; magar yah bhi dekha ki vah vibhutiyam haim to zarur, par durlabh haim, aur is shak aur shak men jab NAmEmalati ka andhakar se niklata hua devirup unhen nazar aya, tab vah uski or utavalepan ke sath, sara sabra khokar tute aur chaha ki use aise jatan se chhipakar rakhen ki kisi dusre ki ankh bhi us par na pade. yah dhyan na raha ki yah moh hi vinash ki jar hai. pyaar-jaisi nirmam vastu kya dar se bandhkar rakhi ja sakti hai? vah to pura yakin chahti hai, puri svadhinta chahti hai, puri zimmedari chahti hai. uske pallavit hone ki shakti uske andar hai. use prakash aur kshetra milna chahiye. vah koi divar nahin hai, jis par upar se iten rakhi jati haim. usmen to jan hai, phailne ki asim shakti hai. jab se NAmEmehta is bangle men aye haim, unhen NAmEmalati se din men kai bar milne ka avsar milta hai. unke dost samjhate haim, yah unke shadi ki taiyari hai. sirph rasma ada karne ki der hai. NAmEmehta bhi yahi svapna dekhte rahte haim. agar NAmEmalati ne unhen sada ke lie thukra diya hota, to kyon un par itna pyaar rakhti. shayad vah unhen sochne ka avsar de rahi hai, aur vah khub sochkar isi phaisla par pahunche haim ki NAmEmalati ke bina vah adhe haim. vahi unhen purnata ki or le ja sakti hai. bahar se vah vilasini hai, bhitar se vahi manovritti shakti ka kendra hai; magar hal badal gayi hai. tab NAmEmalati pyasi thi, ab NAmEmehta pyas se vikal haim. aur ek bar javab pa jane ke bad unhen us saval par NAmEmalati se kuchh kahne ka himmat nahin hota, halanki unke man men ab shak ka lesh nahin raha. NAmEmalati ko pas se dekhkar unka akarshan barhta hi jata hai dur se pustak ke jo akshar lipe-pute lagte the, pas se vah spasht ho gaye haim, unmen arth hai paigam hai. idhar NAmEmalati ne apne bag ke lie NAmEGobar ko mali rakh liya tha. ek din vah kisi mariz ko dekhkar a rahi thi ki raste men petrol na raha. vah khud draiv kar rahi thi. fikra hui petrol kaise aye? rat ke nau baj gaye the aur magh ka jara par raha tha. sarkon par sannata ho gaya tha. koi aisa aadmi nazar na ata tha, jo kar ko dhakel kar petrol ki dukan tak le jay. bar-bar naukar par jhunjhla rahi thi. haramkhor kahim ka. bekhbar pada rahta hai. samyog se NAmEGobar udhar se a nikla. NAmEmalati ko khade dekhkar usne halat samajh li aur gari ko do falang thel kar petrol ki dukan tak laya. NAmEmalati ne khush hokar puchha -- naukri karoge? NAmEGobar ne dhanyavad ke sath svikar kiya. pandrah rupae tanakhvah tay hua. mali ka kam use pasand tha. yahi kam usne kiya tha aur usmen maza hua tha. mil ki majuri men tanakhvah zyada milta tha; par us kam se use uljhan hoti thi. dusre din se NAmEGobar ne NAmEmalati ke yaham kam karna shuru kar diya. use rahne ko ek kothri bhi mil gayi. NAmEJhunia bhi a gayi. NAmEmalati bag men ati to use NAmEJhunia ka larka dhul-mitti men khelta milta. ek din NAmEmalati ne use ek mithai de di. bachcha us din se parach gaya. use dekhte hi uske pichhe lag jata aur jab tak mithai na leta, uska pichha na chhorta. ek din NAmEmalati bag men ayi to larka na dikhai diya. NAmEJhunia se puchha to malum hua bachche ko jvar a gaya hai. NAmEmalati ne ghabarakar kaha -- jvar a gaya! to mere pas kyon nahin layi? chal dekhum. larka khatole par jvar men achet pada tha. khaprail ki us kothri men itni sil, itna andhera, aur is thand ke dinon men bhi itni machchhr ki NAmEmalati ek minat bhi vaham na thahar saki; turant akar thamarmitar liya aur phir jakar dekha, ek sau char tha! NAmEmalati ko dar hua, kahim chechak na ho. bachche ko abhi tak tika nahin laga tha. aur agar is sili kothri men raha, to dar tha, kahim jvar aur na barh jay. sahsa larka ne ankhen khol dim aur NAmEmalati ko khadi pakad karun netron se uski or dekha aur uski god ke lie hath phailaye. NAmEmalati ne use god men utha liya aur thapkiya dene lagi. larka NAmEmalati ke god men akar jaise kisi bade sukh ka anubhav karne laga. apni jalti hui ungliyo se uske gale ki motiyon ki mala pakarkar apni or khinchne laga. NAmEmalati ne nekles utarkar uske gale men dal di. larka ki svarthi prakriti is hal men bhi sajag thi. nekles pakad ab use NAmEmalati ki god men rahne ki koi zarurat na rahi. yaham uske chhin jane ka dar tha. NAmEJhunia ki god is samay zyada surikshat thi. NAmEmalati ne khile hue man se kaha -- bada chalak hai. chiz lekar kaisa bhaga! NAmEJhunia ne kaha -- de do beta, men sahab ka hai. larka ne har ko donon hathon se pakad liya aur mam ki or rosh se dekha. NAmEmalati boli -- tum pahne raho bachcha, maim mangti nahin hum. usi vaqta bangle men akar usne apna baithak ka kamra khali kar diya aur usi vaqta NAmEJhunia us naye kamre men dat gayi. mangal ne us svarg ko kutuhal-bhari ankhon se dekha. chhat men pankha tha, rangin balb the, divaron par tasviren thim. der tak un chizon ko taktaki lagaye dekhta raha. NAmEmalati ne bade pyaar se pukara -- mangal! mangal ne muskarakar uski or dekha, jaise kah raha ho -- aj to hamsa nahin jata men sahab! kya karum. apse kuchh ho sake to kijie. NAmEmalati ne NAmEJhunia ko bahut-si baten samajhaim aur chalte-chalte puchha -- tere ghar men koi dusri aurat ho, to NAmEGobar se kah de, do-char din; ke lie bula lave. mujhe chechak ka dar hai. kitni dur hai tera ghar? NAmEJhunia ne apne gamv ka nam aur pata bataya. andaz se attharah-bis kos honge. NAmEmalati ko belari yad tha. boli -- vahi gamv to nahin, jiske pachchhim taraf adh mil par nadi hai?
ham-ham men sahab, vahi gamv hai. apko kaise malum?
ek bar ham log us gamv men gaye the. NAmEHori ke ghar thahre the. tu use janti hai?
vah to mere sasur haim men sahab. meri sas bhi mili hongi.
ham-ham, badi samajhdar aurat malum hoti thi. mujhse khub baten karti rahi. to NAmEGobar ko bhej de, apni mam ko bula laye.
vah unhen bulane nahin jayenge.
kyon?
kuchh aisa karan hai.
NAmEJhunia ko apne ghar ka chauka-bartan, jharu-baharu, roti-pani sabhi kuchh karna parta. din ko to donon chana-chabena khakar rah jate, rat ko jab NAmEmalati a jati, to NAmEJhunia apna khana pakati aur NAmEmalati bachche ke pas baithti. vah bar-bar chahti ki bachche ke pas baithe; lekin NAmEmalati use na ane deti. rat ko bachche ka jvar tez hota jata aur vah bechain hokar donon hath upar utha leta. NAmEmalati use god men lekar ghanton kamre men tahlati. chauth din use chechak nikal ayi. NAmEmalati ne sare ghar ko tika lagaya, khud tika lagvaya, NAmEmehta ko bhi lagaya. NAmEGobar, NAmEJhunia, maharaj, koi na bacha. pahle din to dane chhote the aur alag-alag the. jan parta tha, chhoti mam haim. dusre din jaise khil uthe aur angur ke dane ke barabar ho gaye aur phir kai-kai dane milkar bade-bade amvle jaise ho gaye. mangal jalan aur khujli aur dard se bechain hokar karun avaz men karahta aur bechara, ashay netron se NAmEmalati ki or dekhta. uska karahna bhi praurhon ka-sa tha, aur nazar men bhi praurhta thi, jaise vah ekaek javan ho gaya ho. is asahya dard ne mano uske nasmajh shishupan ko mita dala ho. uski bachcha-akal mano sajnyan hokar samajh rahi thi ki NAmEmalati hi ke jatan se vah achchha ho sakta hai. NAmEmalati jyon hi kisi kam se chali jati, vah rone lagta. NAmEmalati ke ate hi chup ho jata. rat ko uski bechaini barh jati aur NAmEmalati ko prayah sari rat baithna par jata; magar vah na kabhi jhunjhlati, na chirhti. ham, NAmEJhunia par use kabhi-kabhi zarur gussa ata, kyunki vah ajnyan ke vajah jo na karna chahiye, vah kar baithti. NAmEGobar aur NAmEJhunia donon ki astha jhar-phunk men zyada thi; yaham usko koi avsar na milta. us par NAmEJhunia do bachche ki mam hokar bachche ka palan karna na janti thi, mangal dik karta, to use dantti-kosti. zara-sa bhi phursat pati, to zameen par so jati aur sabere se pahle na uthti; aur NAmEGobar to us kamre men ate jaise darta tha. NAmEmalati vaham baithi hai, kaise jay? NAmEJhunia se bachche ka hal-haval puchh leta aur khakar par rahta. us chot ke bad vah pura svasth na ho paya tha. thoda-sa kam karke bhi thak jata tha. un dinon jab NAmEJhunia ghas bechti thi aur vah aram se pada rahta tha, vah kuchh hara ho gaya tha; magar idhar kai mahine bojh dhone aur chune-gare ka kam karne se uski hal gir gayi thi. us par yaham kam bahut tha. sare bag ko pani nikalkar sinchna, kyariyon ko gorna, ghas chhilna, gayon ko chadha-pani dena aur duhna. aur jo malik itna dayalu ho, uske kam men kan-chori kaise kare? yah ehsan usse ek pal bhi aram se na baithne deta, aur jab NAmEmehta khud khurpi lekar ghanton bag men kam karte to vah kaise aram karta? vah khud sukhta tha; par bag hara ho raha tha. mistar NAmEmehta ko bhi larka se pyaar ho gaya tha. ek din NAmEmalati ne use god men lekar unki munchh ukhrava di thi. dusht ne munchhon ko aisa pakra tha ki samul hi ukhar lega. NAmEmehta ki ankhon men amsu bhar aye the. NAmEmehta ne bigarkar kaha tha -- bada shaitan launda hai. NAmEmalati ne unhen danta tha -- tum munchhen saf kyon nahin kar lete?
meri munchhen mujhe pranon se priy haim.
abki pakad lega, to ukharkar hi chhorega.
to maim iske kan bhi ukhar lunga.
mangal ko unki munchhen ukharne men koi khas maza aya tha. vah khub khilakhilakar hamsa tha aur munchhon ko aur zor se khincha tha; magar NAmEmehta ko bhi shayad munchhen ukharvane men maza aya tha; kyunki vah prayah do ek bar roz usse apni munchhon ki rassakshi kara liya karte the. idhar jab se mangal ko chechak nikal ayi thi, NAmEmehta ko bhi badi chinta ho gayi thi. aksar kamre men jakar mangal ko vyathit ankhon se dekha karte. uske kashton ki kalpana karke unka komal dil hil jata tha. unke daur-dhup se vah achchha ho jata, to prithvi ke us chhod tak daur lagate; rupae kharch karne se achchha hota, to chahe bhikh hi mangna parta, vah use achchha karke hi rahte; lekin yaham koi bas na tha. use chhute bhi unke hath kampte the. kahim uske able na tut jayam. NAmEmalati kitne komal hathon se use uthati hai, kandhe par uthakar kamre men tahlati hai aur kitne pyaar se use bahalakar dudh pilati hai, yah vatsalya NAmEmalati ko unki nazar men na jane kitna uncha utha deta hai.
