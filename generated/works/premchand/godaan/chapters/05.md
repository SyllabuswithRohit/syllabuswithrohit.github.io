NAmEJhunia ka vanchit man, jise bhabhiyon ke vyang aur has-vilas ne aur bhi lolup bana diya tha, uske kaumarya hi par lalcha utha. aur us kumar men bhi patta kharkate hi kisi soye hue shikari janvar ki tarah yauvan jag utha.
NAmEGobar ne avran-hin rasikta ke sath kaha -- agar bhikshuk ko bhikh milne ki asa ho, to vah din-bhar aur rat-bhar data ke dvar par khada rahe.
NAmEJhunia ne kataksh karke kaha -- to yah kaho tum bhi matlab ke yar ho. NAmEGobar ki dhamniyo ka rakt prabal ho utha. bola -- bhukha aadmi agar hath phailaye to use kshama kar dena chahiye.
NAmEJhunia aur gahre pani men utri -- bhikshuk jab tak das dvare na jay, uska pet kaise bharega. maim aise bhikshukon ko mumh nahin lagati. aise to gali-gali milte haim. phir bhikshuk deta kya hai, asis! asison se to kisi ka pet nahin bharta. mand-akal NAmEGobar NAmEJhunia ka ashay na samajh saka. NAmEJhunia chhoti-si thi tabhi se grahkon ke ghar dudh lekar jaya karti thi. sasural men use grahkon ke ghar dudh pahunchana parta tha. ajkal bhi dahi bechne ka bhar usi par tha. use tarah-tarah ke manushyon se sabiqa par chuka tha. do-char rupae uske hath lag jate the, ghadi-bhar ke lie manoranjan bhi ho jata tha; magar yah khushi jaise mangni ki chiz ho. usmen tikav na tha, samarpan na tha, adhikar na tha. vah aisa pyaar chahti thi, jiske lie vah jiye aur mare, jis par vah apne ko samarpit kar de. vah sirph jugnu ki chamak nahin, dipak ka sthayi prakash chahti thi. vah ek grihasth ki ladki thi, jiske grihinitva ko rasikon ki lagavatabaziyon ne kuchal nahin paya tha. NAmEGobar ne chah se uddipt mumh se kaha -- bhikshuk ko ek hi dvar par bharpet mil jay, to kyon dvar-dvar ghume?
NAmEJhunia ne saday bhav se uski or taka. kitna NAmEBhola hai, kuchh samjhata hi nahin. bhikshuk ko ek dvar par bharpet kaham milta hai. use to chutki hi milegi. sarbas to tabhi paoge, jab apna sarbas doge.
mere pas kya hai NAmEJhunia?
tumhare pas kuchh nahin hai? maim to samjhati hum, mere lie tumhare pas jo kuchh hai, vah bade-bade lakhaptiyo ke pas nahin hai. tum mujhse bhikh na mangkar mujhe mol le sakte ho.
NAmEGobar use chakit netron se dekhne laga. NAmEJhunia ne phir kaha -- aur jante ho, dam kya dena hoga? mera hokar rahna parega. phir kisi ke samne hath phailaye dekhungi, to ghar se nikal dungi. NAmEGobar ko jaise andhere men tatolte hue ichchhit vastu mil gayi. ek vichitra dar-mishrit khushi se uska ron-ron pulkit ho utha. lekin yah kaise hoga? NAmEJhunia ko rakh le, to rakheli ko lekar ghar men rahega kaise. biradri ka jhanjhat jo hai. sara gamv kamv-kamv karne lagega. sabhi dusman ho jayange. ammam to ise ghar men ghusne bhi na degi. lekin jab aurat hokar yah nahin darti, to aadmi hokar vah kyon dare. bahut hoga, log use alag kar denge. vah alag hi rahega. NAmEJhunia jaisi aurat gamv men dusri kaun hai? kitni samajhdari ki baten karti hai. kya janti nahin ki maim uske jog nahin hum. phir bhi mujhse pyaar karti hai. meri hone ko razi hai. gamvvale nikal denge, to kya samsar men dusra gamv hi nahin hai? aur gamv kyon chhore? NAmEmatadin ne chamarin baitha li, to kisi ne kya kar liya. NAmEDatadin dant katakatakar rah gaye. NAmEmatadin ne itna zarur kiya ki apna dharam bacha liya. ab bhi bina asnan-puja kiye mumh men pani nahin dalte. donon jun apna khana ap pakate haim aur ab to alag khana nahin pakate. NAmEDatadin aur vah sath baithkar khate haim. NAmEJhinguri simh ne bamhani rakh li, unka kisi ne kya kar liya? unka jitna adar-man tab tha, utna hi aj bhi hai; balki aur barh gaya. pahle naukri khojte phirte the. ab uske rupae se mahajan ban baithe. thakurai ka rob to tha hi, mahajni ka rob bhi jam gaya. magar phir khyal aya, kahim NAmEJhunia dillagi na kar rahi ho. pahle iski or se nishchint ho jana zaruri tha. usne puchha -- man se kahti ho jhuna ki khali lalach de rahi ho? maim to tumhara ho chuka; lekin tum bhi ho jaogi?
tum mere ho chuke, kaise janum?
tum jan bhi chaho, to de dum.
jan dene ka arath bhi samjhate ho?
tum samjha do na.
jan dene ka arath hai, sath rahkar nibah karna. ek bar hath pakarkar umir bhar nibah karte rahna, chahe duniya kuchh kahe, chahe mam-bap, bhai-band, ghar-dvar sab kuchh chhorna pade. mumh se jan denevale bahuton ko dekh chuki. bhauron ki tarah phul ka ras lekar ud jate haim. tum bhi vaise hi na ud jaoge? bar ke ek hath men gay ki paghiya thi. dusre hath se usne NAmEJhunia ka hath pakad liya. jaise bijli ke tar par hath gaya ho. sari deh yauvan ke pahle sparsh se kamp uthi. kitni mulayam, gudgudi, komal kalai! NAmEJhunia ne uska hath hataya nahin, mano is sparsh ka uske lie koi mahatva hi na ho. phir ek pal ke bad gambhir bhav se boli -- aj tumne mera hath pakra hai, yad rakhna.
khub yad rakhunga jhuna aur marte dam tak nibahunga.
NAmEJhunia avishvas-bhari muskan se boli -- isi tarah to sab kahte haim NAmEGobar! balki isse bhi mithe, chikne shabdon men. agar man men kapat ho, mujhe bata do. sachet ho jaum. aison ko man nahin deti. unse to khali hams-bol lene ka nata rakhti hum. barson se dudh lekar bazar jati hum. ek-se-ek babu, mahajan, thakur, vakil, amle, afsar apna rasiyapan dikhakar mujhe phamsa lena chahte haim. koi chhati par hath rakhkar kahta hai, NAmEJhunia, tarsa mat; koi mujhe rasili, nasili chitvan se ghurta hai, mano mare pyaar ke behosh ho gaya hai, koi rupae dikhata hai, koi gahne. sab meri gulami karne ko taiyar rahte haim, umir bhar, balki us janam men bhi, lekin maim un sabon ki nas pahachanti hum. sab-ke-sab bhaumre ras lekar ud janevale. maim bhi unhen lalchati hum, tirchhi nazron se dekhti hum, musakrati hum. vah mujhe gadhi banate haim, maim unhen ullu banati hum. maim mar jaum, to unki ankhon men amsu na ayega. vah mar jayam, to maim kahungi, achchha hua, nigora mar gaya. maim to jiski ho jaungi, uski janam-bhar ke lie ho jaungi, sukh men, duhkh men, sampat men, bipat men, uske sath rahungi. harajai nahin hum ki sabse hamsti-bolti phirum. na rupae ki bhukhi hum, na gahne-kapde ki. bas bhale aadmi ka sang chahti hum, jo mujhe apna samjhe aur jise maim bhi apna samjhum. ek pandit ji bahut tilak-mudra lagate haim. adh ser dudh lete haim. ek din unki gharvali kahim nevte men gayi thi. mujhe kya malum. aur dinon ki tarah dudh liye bhitar chali gayi. vaham pukarti hum, bahuji, bahuji! koi bolta hi nahin. itne men dekhti hum to panditji bahar ke kivar band kiye chale a rahe haim. maim samajh gayi iski niyat kharab hai. maimne dantkar puchha -- tumne kivar kyon band kar liye? kya bahuji kahim gayi haim? ghar men sannata kyon hai? usne kaha -- vah ek nevte men gayi haim; aur meri or do pag aur barh aya. maimne kaha -- tumhen dudh lena ho to lo, nahin maim jati hum. bola -- aj to tum yaham se na jane paogi jhuni rani, roz-roz kaleje par chhuri chalakar bhag jati ho, aj mere hath se na bachogi. tumse sach kahti hum, NAmEGobar, mere roen khade ho gaye. NAmEGobar avesh men bola -- maim bachcha ko dekh paum, to khodkar zameen men gar dum. khun chus lum. tum mujhe dikha to dena. suno to, aison ka mumh torne ke lie maim hi kafi hum. meri chhati dhak-dhak karne lagi. yah kuchh badmasi kar baithe, to kya karungi. koi chillana bhi to na sunega; lekin man men yah phaisla na kar liya tha ki meri deh chhui, to dudh ki bhari hamri uske mumh par patak dungi. bala se char-panch ser dudh jayga, bacha ko yad to ho jaygi. kaleja mazbut karke boli -- is pher men na rahna panditji! maim ahir ki ladki hum. munchh ka ek-ek bal chunva lungi. yahi likha hai tumhare pothi-patre men ki dusron ki bahu-beti ko apne ghar men band karke beizzat karo. isilie tilak-mudra ka jal bichhaye baithe ho? laga hath jorne, pairon parne -- ek premi ka man rakh dogi, to tumhara kya bigar jayga, jhuna rani! kabhi-kabhi garibon par daya kiya karo, nahin bhagavan puchhenge, maimne tumhen itna rupdhan diya tha, tumne usse ek brahman ka upkar bhi nahin kiya, to kya javab dogi? bole, maim vipra hum, rupae-paise ka dan to roz hi pata hum, aj rup ka dan de do. maimne yon hi uska man parkhane ko kah diya, maim pachas rupae lungi. sach kahti hum NAmEGobar, turant kothri men gaya aur das-das ke panch not nikalkar mere hathon men dene laga aur jab maimne not zameen par gira diye aur dvar ki or chali, to usne mera hath pakad liya. maim to pahle hi se taiyar thi. hamri uske mumh par de mari. sir se pamv tak sarabor ho gaya. chot bhi khub lagi. sir pakarkar baith gaya aur laga hay-hay karne. maimne dekha, ab yah kuchh nahin kar sakta, to pith men do laten jama dim aur kivar kholkar bhagi. NAmEGobar ko uski daya buri lagi -- yah tumne kya kiya? uski aurat se jakar kah kyon nahin diya? juton se pitti. aise pakhandiyon par daya na karni chahiye. tum mujhe kal unki surat dikha do, phir dekhna kaisi marammat karta hum.
