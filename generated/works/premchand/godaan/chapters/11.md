# Godaan — Adhyay 11

**Munshi Premchand**

11.
aise asadharan kand par gamv men jo kuchh halchal machna chahiye tha, vah macha aur mahinon tak machta raha. NAmEJhunia ke donon bhai lathiyam liye NAmEGobar ko khojte phirte then. NAmEBhola ne qasam khayi ki ab na NAmEJhunia ka mumh dekhenge aur na is gamv ka. NAmEHori se unhomne apni sagai ki jo batchit ki thi, vah ab tut gayi thi. ab vah apni gay ke dam lenge aur naqad aur ismen vilamb hua to NAmEHori par dava karke uska ghar-dvar nilam kara lenge. gamvvalo ne NAmEHori ko jati-bahar kar diya. koi uska huqqa nahin pita, na uske ghar ka pani pita hai. pani band kar dene ki kuchh batchit thi; lekin NAmEDhania ka chandi-rup sab dekh chuke the; isliye kisi ki age ane ki himmat na padi. NAmEDhania ne sabko suna-sunakar kah diya -- kisi ne use pani bharne se roka, to uska aur apna khun ek kar degi.
is lalkar ne sabhi ke pitte pani kar diye. sabse dukhi hai NAmEJhunia, jiske vajah yah sab upadrav ho raha hai, aur NAmEGobar ki koi khoj-khabar na milna is duhkh ko aur bhi darun bana raha hai. sare din mumh chhipaye ghar men padi rahti hai. bahar nikle to charon or se vagbanon ki aisi varsha ho ki jan bachana mushkil ho jay. din-bhar ghar ke dhandhe karti rahti hai aur jab avsar pati hai, ro leti hai. hardam thar-thar kampti rahti hai ki kahim NAmEDhania kuchh kah na baithe. akela khana to nahin paka sakti; kyunki koi uske hath ka khayega nahin, baqi sara kam usne apne upar le liya. gamv men jaham char aurat-aadmi jama ho jate haim, yahi kutsa hone lagti hai. ek din NAmEDhania hat se chali a rahi thi ki raste men pandit NAmEDatadin mil gaye. NAmEDhania ne sir nicha kar liya aur chahti thi ki katarakar nikal jay; par panditji chherne ka avsar pakad kab chukanevale the. chher hi to diya -- NAmEGobar ka kuchh sar-paigam mila ki nahin NAmEDhania? aisa kaput nikla ki ghar ki sari marjad bigar di.
NAmEDhania ke man men svayam yahi bhav ate rahte the. udas man se boli -- bure din ate haim baba, to aadmi ki mati phir jati hai, aur kya kahum.
NAmEDatadin bole -- tumhen is dushta ko ghar men na rakhna chahiye tha. dudh men makkhi par jati hai, to aadmi use nikalkar phenk deta hai, aur dudh pi jata hai. socho, kitni badnami aur jag-hamsai ho rahi hai. vah kulta ghar men na rahti, to kuchh na hota. larkon se is tarah ki bhul-chuk hoti rahti hai. jab tak biradri ko bhat na doge, bamhanon ko bhoj na doge, kaise uddhar hoga? use ghar men na rakhte, to kuchh na hota. NAmEHori to pagal hai hi, tu kaise dhokha kha gayi.
NAmEDatadin ka larka NAmEmatadin ek chamarin se phamsa hua tha. ise sara gamv janta tha; par vah tilak lagata tha, pothi-patre banchta tha, katha-bhagvat kahta tha, dharm-samskar karata tha. uski pratishtha men zara bhi kami na thi. vah nitya snan-puja kar ke apne papon ka prayashchit kar leta tha. NAmEDhania janti thi, NAmEJhunia ko ashray dene hi se yah sari vipitt ayi hai. use na jane kaise daya a gayi, nahin usi rat ko NAmEJhunia ko nikal deti, to kyon itna uphas hota; lekin yah dar bhi hota tha ki tab uske lie nadi ya kuam ke siva aur thikana kaham tha. ek jan ka kimat dekar -- ek nahin do pranon ka -- vah apne marjad ki raksha kaise karti? phir NAmEJhunia ke pet men bachcha men jo larka hai, vah ghaniya hi ke dil ka tukra to hai. hamsi ke dar se uske jan kaise le leti! aur phir NAmEJhunia ki namrata aur dinta bhi use nirastra karti rahti thi. yah jali-bhuni bahar se ati; par jyomhi NAmEJhunia lote ka pani lakar rakh deti aur uske pamv dabane lagti, uska gussa pani ho jata. bechari apni sharm aur duhkh se ap dabi hui hai, use aur kya dabaye, mare ko kya mare. usne tivra avaz men kaha -- hamko kul-paratistha itni pyari nahin hai maharaj, ki uske pichhe ek jiv ki hatya kar dalte. byahta na sahi; par uski bamh to pakri hai mere bete ne hi. kis mumh se nikal deti. vahi kam bade-bade karte haim, muda unse koi nahin bolta, unhen kalank hi nahin lagta. vahi kam chhote aadmi karte haim, to unki marjad bigar jati hai, nak kat jati hai. bade admiyo ko apni nak dusron ki jan se pyari hogi, hamen to apni nak itni pyari nahin.
NAmEDatadin har mananevale jiv na the. vah is gamv ke narad the. yaham ki vaham, vaham ki yaham, yahi unka vyavsay tha. vah chori to na karte the, usmen jan-jokhim tha; par chori ke mal men hissa bantane ke samay zarur pahunch jate the. kahim pith men dhul na lagne dete the. zamindar ko aj tak lagan ki ek pai na di thi, qurqi ati, to kuen men girne chalte, nokheram ke kiye kuchh na banta; magar asamiyon ko sud par rupae udhar dete the. kisi aurat ko koi gahne banvana hai, NAmEDatadin uski seva ke lie hazir haim. shadi-byah tay karne men unhen bada khushi ata hai, yash bhi milta hai, dikshana bhi milti hai. bimari men dava-daru bhi karte haim, jhar-phunk bhi, jaisi mariz ki chah ho. aur sabha-hoshiyar itne haim ki javanon men javan ban jate haim, balkon men larka aur burhon men burhe. chor ke bhi dost haim aur sah ke bhi. gamv men kisi ko un par yakin nahin hai; par unki vani men kuchh aisa akarshan hai ki log bar-bar dhokha khakar bhi unhim ki sharan jate haim. sir aur daadhi hilakar bole -- yah tu thik kahti hai NAmEDhania! dhamartma logon ka yahi dharam hai; lekin lok-riti ka nibah to karna hi parta hai.
isi tarah ek din lala NAmEPateshwari ne NAmEHori ko chhera. vah gamv men punyatma mashhur the. purnamasi ko nitya satyanarayan ki katha sunte; par patvari hone ke nate khet begar men jutvate the, sinchai begar men karvate the aur asamiyon ko ek dusre se larakar raqmen marte the. sara gamv unse kampta tha! garibon ko das-das, panch-panch qaraz dekar unhomne kai hazar ki jaydad bana li thi. fasal ki chizen asamiyon se lekar kachhari aur pulis ke amlon ki bhent karte rahte the. isse ilaqe bhar men unki achchhi dhak thi. agar koi unke hatthe nahin chadha, to vah daroga gandasimh the, jo hal men is ilaqe men aye the. paramarthi bhi the. bukhar ke dinon men sarkari kunain bantkar yash kamate the, koi bimar aram ho, to uski kushal puchhne zarur jate the. chhote-mote jhagre apas men hi tay kara dete the. shadi-byah men apni palki, qalin, aur mahfil ke saman mangni dekar logon ka ubar kar dete the. mauqa pakad na chukte the, par jiska khate the, uska kam bhi karte the. bole -- yah tumne kya bimari pal liya NAmEHori?
NAmEHori ne pichhe phirkar puchha -- tumne kya kaha lala -- maimne suna nahin.
NAmEPateshwari pichhe se qadam barhate hue barabar akar bole, yahi kah raha tha ki NAmEDhania ke sath kya tumhari akal bhi ghas kha gayi. NAmEJhunia ko kyon nahin uske bap ke ghar bhej dete, sent-ment men apni hamsim kara rahe ho. na jane kiska larka lekar a gayi aur tumne ghar men baitha liya. abhi tumhari do-do larkiya byahne ko baithi hui haim, socho kaise bera par hoga. NAmEHori is tarah ki alochanaen, aur shubh kamanaen sunte-sunte tang a gaya tha. khinn hokar bola -- yah sab maim samjhata hum lala! lekin tumhim batao, maim kya karum! maim NAmEJhunia ko nikal dum, to NAmEBhola use rakh lenge? agar vah razi hon, to aj maim use unke ghar pahuncha dum, agar tum unhen razi kar do, to janam-bhar tumhara ausan manum; magar vaham to unke donon larke khun karne ko utaru ho rahe haim. phir maim use kaise nikal dum. ek to nalayaq aadmi mila ki uski bamh pakarkar daga de gaya. maim bhi nikal dunga, to is hal men vah kahim mehnat-majuri bhi to na kar sakegi. kahim dub-dhas mari to kise jurm lagega. raha larkiyo ka byah so bhagavan malik haim. jab uska samay ayega, koi na koi rasta nikal hi ayega. ladki to hamari biradri men aj tak kabhi kumari nahin rahi. biradri ke dar se hatyare ka kam nahin kar sakta.
NAmEHori namra svabhav ka aadmi tha. sada sir jhukakar chalta aur char baten gam kha leta tha. NAmEHeera ko chhorkar gamv men koi uska ahit na chahta tha, par samaj itna bada anarth kaise sah le! aur uski mutamardi to dekho ki samjhane par bhi nahin samjhata. aurat-aadmi donon jaise samaj ko chunauti de rahe haim ki dekhen koi unka kya kar leta hai. to samaj bhi dikha dega ki uski mayarda toranevale sukh ki nind nahin so sakte. usi rat ko is samasya par soch karne ke lie gamv ke vidhataon ki baithak hui. NAmEDatadin bole -- meri adat kisi ki ninda karne ki nahin hai. samsar men kya kya kukarm nahin hota; apne se kya matlab. magar vah ramr NAmEDhania to mujhse larne par utaru ho gayi. bhaiyon ka hissa dabakar hath men char paise ho gaye, to ab kupath ke siva aur kya sujhegi. nich jat, jaham pet-bhar roti khayi aur terhe chale, isi se to sastaron men kaha hai -- nich jat latiyaye achchha.
NAmEPateshwari ne nariyal ka kash lagate hue kaha -- yahi to inmen burai hai ki char paise dekhe aur ankhen badlim. aj NAmEHori ne aisi hekri jatayi ki maim apna-sa mumh lekar rah gaya. na jane apne ko kya samjhata hai. ab socho, is aniti ka gamv men kya phal hoga. NAmEJhunia ko dekhkar dusri vidhavaon ka man barhega ki nahin? aj NAmEBhola ke ghar men yah bat hui. kal hamare-tumhare ghar men bhi hogi. samaj to dar ke bal se chalta hai. aj samaj ka ankus jata rahe, phir dekho samsar men kya-kya anarth hone lagte haim.
NAmEJhinguri simh do striyon ke pati the. pahli aurat panch larke-larkiya chhorkar mari thi. us samay inki avastha paintalis ke lagbhag thi; par apne dusra byah kiya aur jab usse koi bachcha na hui, to tisra byah kar dala. ab inki pachas ki avastha thi aur do javan patniyam ghar men baithi hui thim. un donon hi ke vishay men tarah-tarah ki baten phail rahi thim; par thakur sahab ke dar se koi kuchh kah na sakta tha, aur kahne ka avsar bhi to ho. pati ki ar men sab kuchh jayaz hai. musibat to usko hai, jise koi ar nahin. thakur sahab striyon par bada kathor shasan rakhte the aur unhen ghamand tha ki unki patniyon ka ghunghat tak kisi ne na dekha hoga. magar ghunghat ki ar men kya hota hai, uski unhen kya khabar? bole -- aisi aurat ka to sir kat le. NAmEHori ne is kulta ko ghar rakhkar samaj men vish boya hai. aise aadmi ko gamv men rahne dena sare gamv ko bhrasht karna hai. NAmERai Sahib ko iski khabar deni chahiye. saf-saf kah dena chahiye, agar gamv men yah aniti chali to kisi ki abru salamat na rahegi.
pandit nokheram karkun bade kulin brahman the. inke dada kisi raja ke divan the! par apna sab kuchh bhagavan ke charnon men bhent karke sadhu ho gaye the. inke bap ne bhi ram-nam ki kheti men umra kat di. nokheram ne bhi vahi bhakti tarke men payi thi. subah puja par baith jate the aur das baje tak baithe ram-nam likha karte the; magar bhagavan ke samne se uthte hi unki manvata is avrodh se vikrit hokar unke man, vachan aur karm sabhi ko vishakt kar deti thi. is prastav men unke adhikar ka beizzati hota tha. phule hue galon men dhamsi hui ankhen nikalkar bole -- ismen NAmERai Sahib se kya puchhna hai. maim jo chahum, kar sakta hum. laga do sau rupye damr. ap gamv chhorkar bhagega. idhar bedkhali bhi dayar kiye deta hum.
NAmEPateshwari ne kaha -- magar lagan to bebaq kar chuka hai?
NAmEJhinguri simh ne samarthan kiya -- ham, lagan ke lie hi to hamse tis rupae liye haim.
nokheram ne ghamand ke sath kaha -- lekin abhi rasid to nahin di. sabut kya hai ki lagan bebaq kar diya.
sarvasammati se yahi tay hua ki NAmEHori par sau rupae tavan laga diya jay. sirph ek din gamv ke admiyo ko batorkar unki mamzuri le lene ka abhinay zaruri tha. mumkin tha, ismen das-panch din ki der ho jati. par aj hi rat ko NAmEJhunia ke larka paida ho gaya. aur dusre hi din gamvvalo ki panchayat baith gayi. NAmEHori aur NAmEDhania, donon apni qismat ka faisla sunne ke lie bulae gaye. chaupal men itni bhir thi ki kahim til rakhne ki jagah na thi. panchayat ne faisla kiya ki NAmEHori par sau rupae naqad aur tis man anaj damr lagaya jay. NAmEDhania bhari sabha men rakumardhe hue gala se boli -- pancho, garib ko satakar sukh na paoge, itna samajh lena. ham to mit jayange, kaun jane, is gamv men rahen ya na rahen, lekin mera sarap tumko bhi zarur se zarur lagega. mujhse itna kara jaribana isliye liya ja raha hai ki maimne apni bahu ko kyon apne ghar men rakha. kyon use ghar se nikalkar sarak ki bhikharin nahin bana diya. yahi nyay hai, aim?
NAmEPateshwari bole -- vah teri bahu nahin hai, harajai hai.
NAmEHori ne NAmEDhania ko danta -- tu kyon bolti hai NAmEDhania! panch men paramesar rahte haim. unka jo nyay hai, vah sir ankhon par; agar bhagavan ki yahi chah hai ki ham gamv chhorkar bhag jayam, to hamara kya bas. pancho, hamare pas jo kuchh hai, vah abhi khalihan men hai. ek dana bhi ghar men nahin aya, jitna chaho, le lo. sab lena chaho, sab le lo. hamara bhagavan malik hai, jitni kami pade, usmen hamare donon bail le lena. NAmEDhania dant katakatakar boli -- maim ek dana na anaj dungi, na ek kauri damr. jismen buta ho, chalkar mujhse le. achchhi dillagi hai. socha hoga damr ke bahane iski sab jaijat le lo aur nazrana lekar dusron ko de do. bag-bagicha bechkar maze se tar mal urao. NAmEDhania ke jite-ji yah nahin hone ka, aur tumhari lalsa tumhare man men hi rahegi. hamen nahin rahna hai biradri men. biradri men rahkar hamari mukut na ho jaygi. ab bhi apne pasine ki kamai khate haim, tab bhi apne pasine ki kamai khayange.
NAmEHori ne uske samne hath jorkar kaha -- NAmEDhania, tere pairon parta hum, chup rah. ham sab biradri ke chakar haim, uske bahar nahin ja sakte. vah jo damr lagati hai, use sir jhukakar mamzur kar. nakku bankar jine se to gale men phamsi laga lena achchha hai. aj mar jayam, to biradri hi to is mitti ko par lagayegi? biradri hi taregi to tarenge. pancho, mujhe apne javan bete ka mumh dekhna nasib na ho, agar mere pas khalihan ke anaj ke siva aur koi chiz ho. maim biradri se daga na karunga. panchon ko mere bal-bachchon par daya aye, to unki kuchh paravris karen, nahin mujhe to unki hukma palni hai. NAmEDhania jhallakar vaham se chali gayi aur NAmEHori pahar rat tak khalihan se anaj dho-dhokar NAmEJhinguri simh ki chaupal men dher karta raha. bis man jau tha, panch man gehum aur itna hi matar, thoda-sa chana aur telhan bhi tha. akela aadmi aur do grihisthayon ka bojh. yah jo kuchh hua, NAmEDhania ke purusharth se hua. NAmEJhunia bhitar ka sara kam kar leti thi aur NAmEDhania apni larkiyo ke sath kheti men jut gayi thi. donon ne socha tha, gehum aur telhan se lagan ki ek qist ada ho jaygi aur ho sake to thoda-thoda sud bhi de denge. jau khane ke kam men ayega. lange-tange panch-chhah mahine kat jayange tab tak juar, makka, samvam, dhan ke din a jayenge. vah sari ummid mitti men mil gayi. anaj to hath se gaye hi, sau rupae ki gathri aur sir par lad gayi. ab khana ka kahim thikana nahin. aur NAmEGobar ka kya hal hua, bhagavan jane. na hal na haval. agar dil itna kachcha tha, to aisa kam hi kyon kiya; magar honhar ko kaun tal sakta hai. biradri ka vah atank tha ki apne sir par ladkar anaj dho raha tha, mano apne hathon apni qabra khod raha ho. zamindar, sahukar, sarkar kiska itna rob tha? kal bal-bachche kya khayange, iski chinta pranon ko sokhe leti thi; par biradri ka dar pishach ki tarah sir par savar ankus diye ja raha tha. biradri se prithak jivan ki vah koi kalpana hi na kar sakta tha. shadi-byah, mumran-chhedan, janma-maran sab kuchh biradri ke hath men hai. biradri uske jivan men vriksh ki tarah jar jamaye hue thi aur uski nasen uske ron-ron men bindhi hui thim. biradri se nikalkar uska jivan vishrinkhal ho jayga -- tar-tar ho jayga. jab khalihan men sirph derh-do man jau rah gaya, to NAmEDhania ne daurkar uska hath pakad liya aur boli -- achchha, ab rahne do. dho to chuke biradri ki laj. bachchon ke lie bhi kuchh chhoroge ki sab biradri ke bhar men jhonk doge. maim tumse har jati hum. mere bhagya men tumhim jaise buddhu ka sang likha tha! NAmEHori ne apna hath chhurakar tokri men shesh anaj bharte hue kaha -- yah na hoga NAmEDhania, panchon ki ankh bachakar ek dana bhi rakh lena mere lie haram hai. maim le jakar sab-ka-sab vaham dher kar deta hum. phir panchon ke man men daya upjegi, to kuchh mere bal-bachchon ke lie denge. nahin bhagavan malik haim.
NAmEDhania tilamilakar boli -- yah panch nahin haim, rakshas haim, pakke rachhas! yah sab hamari jagah-zameen chhinkar mal marna chahte haim. damr to bahana hai. samjhati jati hum; par tumhari ankhen nahin khultim. tum in pishachon se daya ki asa rakhte ho. sochte ho, das-panch man nikalkar tumhen de denge. mumh dho rakho.
jab NAmEHori ne na mana aur tokri sir par rakhne laga to NAmEDhania ne donon hathon se puri shakti ke sath tokri pakad li aur boli -- ise to maim na le jane dungi, chahe tum meri jan hi le lo. mar-markar hamne kamaya, pahar rat-rat ko sincha, agora, isliye ki panch log muchhon par tav dekar bhog lagayen aur hamare bachche dane-dane ko tarsen. tumne akele hi sab kuchh nahin kar liya hai. maim bhi apni bachchiyon ke sath sati hui hum. sidhe se tokri rakh do, nahin aj sada ke lie nata tut jayga. kahe deti hum. NAmEHori soch men par gaya. NAmEDhania ke kathan men satya tha. use apne bal-bachchon ki kamai chhinkar tavan dene ka kya adhikar hai? vah ghar ka malik isalie hai ki sabka palan kare, isalie nahin ki unki kamai chhinkar biradri ki nazar men surkha- bane. tokri uske hath se chhut gayi. dhire se bola -- tu thik kahti hai NAmEDhania! dusron ke hisse par mera koi zor nahin hai. jo kuchh bacha hai, vah le ja, maim jakar panchon se kahe deta hum.
NAmEDhania anaj ki tokri ghar men rakhkar apni donon larkiyo ke sath pote ke janmotsav men gala phar-pharkar sohar ga rahi thi, jismen sara gamv sun le. aj yah pahla mauqa tha ki aise shubh avsar par biradri ki koi aurat na thi. saur se NAmEJhunia ne kahla bheja tha, sohar gane ka kam nahin hai; lekin NAmEDhania kab manne lagi. agar viradri ko uski parva nahin hai, to vah bhi biradri ki parva nahin karti. usi vaqta NAmEHori apne ghar ko assi rupae par NAmEJhinguri simh ke hath giron rakh raha tha. damr ke rupae ka iske siva vah aur koi intzam na kar sakta tha. bis rupae to telhan, gehum aur matar se mil gaye. shesh ke lie ghar likhna par gaya. nokheram to chahte the ki bail bikva lie jayam; lekin NAmEPateshwari aur NAmEDatadin ne iska khilaf hona kiya. bail bik gaye, to NAmEHori kheti kaise karega? biradri uski jaydad se rupae vasul kare; par aisa to na kare ki vah gamv chhorkar bhag jay. is tarah bail bach gaye. NAmEHori rehannama likhkar koi gyarah baje rat ghar aya to, NAmEDhania ne puchha -- itni rat tak vaham kya karte rahe?
NAmEHori ne julahe ka gussa daadhi par utarte hue kaha -- karta kya raha, is launde ki karni bharta raha. abhaga ap to chingari chhorkar bhaga, ag mujhe bujhani par rahi hai. assi rupae men ghar rehan likhna pada. karta kya! ab huqqa khul gaya. biradri ne jurm kshama kar diya.
NAmEDhania ne oth chabakar kaha -- na huqqa khulta, to hamara kya bigra jata tha. char-panch mahine nahin kisi ka huqqa piya, to kya chhote ho gaye? maim kahti hum, tum itne bhondu kyon ho? mere samne to bade buddhiman bante ho, bahar tumhara mumh kyon band ho jata hai? le-de ke bap-dadon ki nisani ek ghar bach raha tha, aj tumne uska bhi vara-nyara kar diya. isi tarah kal yah tin-char bighe zameen hai, ise bhi likh dena aur tab gali-gali bhikh mangna. maim puchhti hum, tumhare mumh men jibh na thi ki un panchon se puchhte, tum kaham ke bade dhamartma ho, jo dusron par damr lagate phirte ho, tumhara to mumh dekhna bhi pap hai.
NAmEHori ne danta -- chup rah, bahut charh-charh na bol. biradri ke chakkar men abhi padi nahin hai, nahin mumh se bat na niklati.
NAmEDhania uttejit ho gayi -- kaun-sa pap kiya hai, jiske lie biradri se daren, kisi ki chori ki hai, kisi ka mal kata hai? mehriya rakh lena pap nahin hai, ham, rakh ke chhod dena pap hai. aadmi ka bahut sidha hona bhi bura hai. uske sidhepan ka phal yahi hota hai ki kutte bhi mumh chatne lagte haim. aj udhar tumhari vah-vah ho rahi hogi ki biradri ki kaisi marjad rakh li. mere bhag phut gaye the ki tum jaise mard se pala pada. kabhi sukh ki roti na mili.
maim tere bap ke pamv parne gaya tha? vahi tujhe mere gale bandh gaya.
patthr par gaya tha unki akkal par aur unhen kya kahum? na jane kya dekhkar lattu ho gaye. aise koi bade sundar bhi to na the tum.
