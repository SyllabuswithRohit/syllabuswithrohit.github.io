'achchhaa maana lo, tumhaaraa anumaana theeka hai, to phir? main tumase ek shubha-kaarya men madada maa.Ngane aayee hoo.N, apane vyavahaara kee pareekshaa dene aayee hoo.N| aur agar tuma samajhate ho, kuchha chandaa dekara tuma yasha aur dhanyavaada ke sivaa aur kuchha paa sakate ho, to tuma bhrama men ho| '
khannaa paraasta ho gaye| vaha aise sakare kone men pha.Nsa gaye the, jahaa.N idhara-udhara hilane kaa bhee sthaana na tha| kyaa vaha usase yaha kahane kaa saahasa rakhate hain ki mainne aba taka tumhaare oopara hajaaron rupae lutaa diye, kyaa usakaa yahee puraskaara hai? sharma se unakaa mu.Nha chhotaa-saa nikala aaya, jaise sikuda gaya ho! jhenpate hue bole -- meraa aashaya yaha na tha maalatee, tuma bilakula galata samajheen|
maalatee ne parihaasa ke aavaaja men kahaa -- khudaa kare, mainne galata samajhaa ho, kyonki agar main use sacha samajha loo.Ngee, to tumhaare saaye se bhee bhaagoo.Ngee| main rupavatee hoo.N| tuma bhee mere aneka chaahanevaalon men se ek ho| vaha meree kripaa thi ki jahaa.N main auron ke upahaara lautaa detee thi, tumhaaree saamaanya-se-saamaanya cheejen bhee dhanyavaada ke saatha sveekaara kar letee thi, aur jaroorata padane para tumase rupae bhee maa.Nga letee thi, agar tumane apane dhanonmaada men isakaa koee doosaraa artha nikaala liya, to main tumhen kshamaa karoo.Ngee| yaha aadamee-prakriti kaa apavaada naheen; magara yaha samajha lo ki paisaa ne aaja taka kisee naaree ke dil para vijaya naheen paayee, aur na kabhee paayegaa|
khannaa ek-ek shabda para maano gaja-gaja bhara neeche dha.Nsate jaate the| aba aur jyaadaa chota sahane kaa unamen jeevata na tha| lajjita hokara bole -- maalatee, tumhaare pairon padataa hoo.N, aba aur jaleela na karo| aur na sahee to mitra-bhaava to banaa rahane do| yaha kehte hue unhonne daraaja se chekabuka nikaalaa aur ek hajaara likhakara darate darate maalatee kee tarapha badhaayaa|
maalatee ne cheka lekara nirdaya vyanga kiya -- yaha mere vyavahaara kaa moolya hai yaa vyaayaamashaalaa kaa chandaa?
khannaa sajala aa.Nkhon se bole -- aba meree jaana bakhsho maalatee, kyon mere mu.Nha men kaalikha pota rahi ho|
maalatee ne jora se kahakahaa maaraa -- dekho, daa.Nta bhee bataaee aur ek hajaara rupae bhee vasoola kiye| aba to tuma kabhee aisee sharaarata na karoge?
'kabhee naheen, jeete jee kabhee naheen| '
'kaana pakado| phra phra kaana pakadataa hoo.N; magara aba tuma dayaa karake jaao aur mujhe ekaanta men baithakara sochane aur rone do| tumane aaja mere jeevana kaa saaraa khushee....| '
maalatee aur jora se ha.Nsee -- dekho khannaa, tuma meraa bahuta beijjatee kar rahe ho aur tuma jaanate ho, roopa beijjatee naheen saha sakataa| mainne to tumhaare saatha bhalaaee kee aur tuma use buraaee samajhate ho|
khannaa vidroha bharee aa.Nkhon se dekhakara bole -- tumane mere saatha bhalaaee kee hai yaa ulatee chhooree se meraa galaa retaa hai?
'kyon, main tumhen loota-lootakara apanaa ghar bhara rahi thi| tuma us loota se bacha gaye| '
'kyon ghaava para namaka chhidaka rahi ho maalatee! main bhee aadamee hoo.N| '
