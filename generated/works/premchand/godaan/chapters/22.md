# Godaan — Adhyay 22

**Munshi Premchand**

22.
idhar kuchh dinon se NAmERai Sahib ki ladki ke shadi ki batchit ho rahi thi. uske sath hi elekshan bhi sir par a pahuncha tha; magar in sabon se zaruri unhen divani men ek muqdama dayar karna tha jiski kort-fis hi pachas hazar hoti thi, upar ke kharch alag. NAmERai Sahib ke sale jo apni riyasat ke ekamatra malik the, ain javani men motar lar jane ke vajah gat ho gaye the, aur NAmERai Sahib apne kumar beta ki or se us riyasat par adhikar pane ke lie qanun ki sharan lena chahte the. unke chachere salon ne riyasat par kabza jama liya tha aur NAmERai Sahib ko usmen se koi hissa dene par taiyar na the. NAmERai Sahib ne bahut chaha ki apas men samjhauta ho jay aur unke chachere sale makul guzara lekar hat jayen, yaham tak ki vah us riyasat ki adhi amdani chhorne par taiyar the; magar salon ne kisi tarah ka samjhauta svikar na kiya, aur sirph lathi ke zor se riyasat men tahsil-vasul shuru kar di. NAmERai Sahib ko adalat ki sharan jane ke siva koi rasta na raha. muqdame men lakhon ka kharch tha; magar riyasat bhi bis lakh se kam ki jaydad na thi. vakilon ne phaisla rup se kah diya tha ki apki shartiya digri hogi. aisa mauqa kaun chhod sakta tha? mushkil yahi tha ki yah tinon kam ek sath a pade the aur unhen kisi tarah tala na ja sakta tha. ladki ki avastha 18 varsh ki ho gayi thi aur sirph hath men rupae na rahne ka vajah ab tak uska shadi tal jata tha. kharch ka anuman ek lakh ka tha. jiske pas jate, vahi bada-sa mumh kholta; magar hal men ek bada achchha avsar hath a gaya tha. kumvar digvijaysimh ki patni yakshma ki bhent ho chuki thi, aur kumvar sahab apne ujre ghar ko jald se jald basa lena chahte the. sauda bhi vare se tay ho gaya aur kahim shikar hath se nikal na jay, isalie isi lagna men shadi hona paramavashyak tha. kumvar sahab durvasanaon ke bhandar the. sharab, ganja, afim, madak, charas, aisa koi nasha na tha, jo vah na karte hon. aur aiyashi to rais ki shobha hai. vah rais hi kya, jo aiyash na ho. paisa ka upbhog aur kiya hi kaise jay? magar in sab durgunon ke hote hue bhi vah aise pratibhavan the ki achchhe-achchhe vidvan unka loha mante the. sangit, natyakla, hastarekha, jyotish, yog, lathi, kushti, nishanebazi adi kalaon men apna jod na rakhte the. iske sath hi bade dabang aur nirbhik the. rashtriy andolan men dil kholkar sahyog dete the; ham, gupt rup se. adhikariyon se yah bat chhipi na thi, phir bhi unki badi pratishtha thi aur sal men ek-do bar gavarnar sahab bhi unke mehman ho jate the. aur abhi avastha tis-battis se zyada na thi aur sehat to aisa tha ki akele ek bakra khakar hazam kar dalte the. NAmERai Sahib ne samjha, billi ke bhagon chhinka tuta. abhi kumvar sahab shodshi se nivritt bhi na hue the ki NAmERai Sahib ne batchit shuru kar di. kumvar sahab ke lie shadi sirph apna prabhav aur shakti barhane ka sadhan tha. NAmERai Sahib kaumsil ke membar the hi; yon bhi prabhavshali the. rashtriy larai men apne tyag ka parichay dekar shraddha ke chitthi bhi ban chuke the. shadi tay hone men koi badha na ho sakti thi. aur vah tay ho gayi. raha elekshan. yah sone ki hamsiya thi, jise na uglate banta tha, na niglate. ab tak vah do bar nirvachit ho chuke the aur donon hi bar un par ek-ek lakh ki chapat padi thi; magar abki ek raja sahab usi ilaqe se khade ho gaye the aur danke ki chot ailan kar diya tha ki chahe har ek votar ko ek-ek hazar hi kyon na dena pade, chahe pachas lakh ki riyasat mitti men mil jay; magar ray amarapalsimh ko kaumsil men na jane dunga. aur unhen adhikariyon ne apni madad ka ashvasan bhi de diya tha. NAmERai Sahib vicharshil the, hoshiyar the, apna nafa-nuqsan samjhate the; magar rajput the. aur potron ke rais the. vah chunauti pakad maidan se kaise hat jayam? yon unse raja suryapratapsimh ne akar kaha hota, bhai sahab, ap to do bar kaumsil men ja chuke, abki mujhe jane dijie, to shayad NAmERai Sahib ne unka svagat kiya hota. kaumsil ka moh ab unhen na tha; lekin is chunauti ke samne tal thonkne ke siva aur koi rah hi na thi. ek masalhat aur bhi thi. mistar tankha ne unhen yakin dilaya tha ki ap khade ho jayam, pichhe raja sahab se ek lakh ki thaili lekar baith jaiega. unhomne yaham tak kaha tha ki raja sahab badi khushi se ek lakh de denge; meri unse batchit ho chuki hai; par ab malum hua, raja sahab NAmERai Sahib ko parast karne ka gaurav nahin chhorna chahte aur iska mukhya vajah tha, NAmERai Sahib ki ladki ki shadi kumvar sahab se thik hona. do prabhavshali gharanon ka samyog vah apni pratishtha ke lie hanikarak samjhate the.
udhar NAmERai Sahib ko sasurali zaydad milne ki bhi ummid thi. raja sahab ke pahlu men yah kanta bhi buri tarah khatak raha tha. kahim vah zaydad inhen mil gayi -- aur qanun NAmERai Sahib ke paksh men tha hi -- tab to raja sahab ka ek pratidvandi khada ho jayga; isalie unka dharm tha ki NAmERai Sahib ko kuchal dalen aur unki pratishtha dhul men mila den. bechare NAmERai Sahib bade musibat nen par gaye the. unhen yah shak hone laga tha ki sirph apna matlab nikalne ke lie mistar tankha ne unhen dhokha diya. yah khabar mili thi ki ab raja sahab ke pairokar ho gaye haim. yah NAmERai Sahib ke ghav par namak tha. unhomne kai bar tankha ko bulaya tha; magar vah ya to ghar par milte hi na the, ya ane ka vada karke bhul jate the. akhir aj khud unse milne ka irada karke vah unke pas ja pahunche.
samyog se mistar tankha ghar par mil gaye; magar NAmERai Sahib ko pure ghante-bhar unki intzar karni padi. yah vahi mistar tankha haim, jo NAmERai Sahib ke dvar par ek bar roz haziri diya karte the. aj itna mizaj ho gaya hai. jale baithe the. jyomhi mistar tankha saje-sajaye, mumh men sigar dabaye kamre men aye aur hath barhaya ki NAmERai Sahib ne bamgola chhod diya -- maim ghante-bhar se yaham baitha hua hum aur ap niklate-niklate ab nikle haim. maim ise apni tauhin samjhata hum!
mistar tankha ne ek sofe par baithkar nishchint bhav se dhuam urate hue kaha -- mujhe iska khed hai. maim ek zaruri kam men laga tha. apko fon karke mujhse samay thik kar lena chahiye tha.
ag men ghi par gaya; magar NAmERai Sahib ne gussa ko dabaya. vah larne na aye the. is beizzati ko pi jane ka hi avsar tha. bole -- ham, yah galti hui. ajkal apko bahut kam fursat rahti hai, shayad.
ji ham, bahut kam, varna maim zarur ata.
maim usi muamle ke bade men ap se puchhne aya tha. samjhauta ki to koi ummid nahin malum hoti. udhar to jang ki taiyariyam bade zoron se ho rahi haim.
raja sahab ko to ap jante hi haim, jhakkar aadmi haim, pure sanki. koi na koi dhun un par savar rahti hai. ajkal yahi dhun hai ki NAmERai Sahib ko nicha dikhakar rahenge. aur unhen jab ek dhun savar ho jati hai, to phir kisi ki nahin sunte, chahe kitna hi nuqsan uthana pade. koi chalis lakh ka bojh sir par hai, phir bhi vahi dam-kham hai, vahi alalle-talalle kharch haim. paise ko to kuchh samjhate hi nahin. naukron ka tanakhvah chhah-chhah mahine se baqi pada hua hai; magar NAmEHeera -mahal ban raha hai. sangamarmar ka to farsh hai. pachchikari aisi ho rahi hai ki ankhen nahin thahratim. afsaron ke pas roz daliyam jati rahti haim. suna hai, koi angrez mainejar rakhne vale haim.
phir apne kaise kah diya tha ki ap koi samjhauta kara denge.
mujhse jo kuchh ho sakta tha vah maimne kiya. iske siva maim aur kya kar sakta tha. agar koi vyakti apne do-char lakh rupae phunkne hi par tula hua ho, to mera kya bas!
NAmERai Sahib ab gussa na sambhal sake -- khaskar jab un do-char lakh rupae men se das-bis hazar apke hatthe charhne ki bhi ummid ho.
mistar tankha kyon dabte. bole -- NAmERai Sahib, ab saf-saf na kahalavaie. yaham na maim samnyasi hum, na ap. ham sabhi kuchh na kuchh kamane hi nikle haim. ankh ke andhon aur ganth ke puron ki talash apko bhi utni hi hai, jitni mujhko. apse maimne khade hone ka prastav kiya. ap ek lakh ke lobh se khade ho gaye; agar goti lal ho jati, to aj ap ek lakh ke malik hote aur bina ek pai qaraz liye kumvar sahab se rishta bhi ho jata aur muqdama bhi dayar ho jata; magar apke badakismati se vah chal pat par gayi. jab ap hi thath par rah gaye, to mujhe kya milta. akhir maimne jhak markar unki punchh pakri. kisi na kisi tarah yah vaitrani to par karni hi hai.
NAmERai Sahib ko aisa avesh a raha tha ki is dusht ko goli mar den. isi badmash ne sabz bag dikhakar unhen khada kiya aur ab apni safai de raha hai, pith men dhul bhi nahin lagne deta, lekin hal zaban band kiye hue thi.
to ab apke kiye kuchh nahin ho sakta?
aisa hi samajhie.
maim pachas hazar par bhi samjhauta karne ko taiyar hum.
raja sahab kisi tarah na manenge.
pachchis hazar par to man jayange?
koi ummid nahin. vah saf kah chuke haim.
vah kah chuke haim ya ap kah rahe haim.
ap mujhe jhutha samjhate haim?
NAmERai Sahib ne naram avaz men kaha -- maim apko jhutha nahin samjhata; lekin itna zarur samjhata hum ki ap chahte, to muamla ho jata.
to ap ka khyal hai, maimne samjhauta nahin hone diya?
nahin, yah mera matlab nahin hai. maim itna hi kahna chahta hum ki ap chahte to kam ho jata aur maim is jhamele men na parta.
mistar tankha ne ghadi ki taraf dekhkar kaha -- to NAmERai Sahib, agar ap saf kahlana chahte haim, to sunie -- agar apne das hazar ka chek mere hath men rakh diya hota, to aj phaisla ek lakh ke malik hote. ap shayad chahte honge, jab apko raja sahab se rupae mil jate, to ap mujhe hazar-do-hazar de dete. to maim aisi kachchi goli nahin khelta. ap raja sahab se rupae lekar tijori men rakhte aur mujhe angutha dikha dete. phir maim apka kya bana leta? batalaie? kahim nalish-fariyad bhi to nahin kar sakta tha.
NAmERai Sahib ne ahat netron se dekha -- ap mujhe itna beiman samjhate haim?
tankha ne kursi se uthte hue kaha -- ise beimani kaun samjhata hai. ajkal yahi chaturai hai. kaise dusron ko ullu banaya ja sake, yahi kamyab niti hai; aur ap iske acharya haim.
NAmERai Sahib ne mutthi bandhkar kaha -- maim?
ji ham, ap! pahle chunav men maimne ji-jan se apki pairvi ki. apne badi mushkil se ro dhokar panch sau rupae diye, dusre chunav men apne ek sari-si tuti-phuti kar dekar apna gala chhuraya. dudh ka jala chhanchh bhi phunk-phunkkar pita hai.
vah kamre se nikal gaye aur kar lane ka hukma diya? NAmERai Sahib ka khun khaul raha tha. is ashishtata ki bhi koi had hai. ek to ghante-bhar intazar karaya aur ab itni bemurauvti se pesh akar unhen zabaradasti ghar se nikal raha hai; agar unhen yakin hota ki vah mistar tankha ko patkani de sakte haim, to kabhi na chukte; magar tankha dil-daul men unse savaye the. jab mistar tankha ne harn bajaya, to vah bhi akar apni kar par baithe aur sidhe mistar NAmEKhanna ke pas pahunche. nau baj rahe the; magar NAmEKhanna sahab abhi tak mithi nind ka khushi le rahe the. vah do baje rat ke pahle kabhi na sote the aur nau baje tak NAmESona svabhavik hi tha. yaham bhi NAmERai Sahib ko adha ghanta baithna pada; isalie jab koi sarhe nau baje mistar NAmEKhanna muskarate hue nikle to NAmERai Sahib ne dant batai -- achchha! ab sarkar ki nind khuli hai, sarhe nau baje. rupae jama kar liye haim na, jabhi yah befikri hai. meri tarah talukkedar hote, to ab tak ap bhi kisi dvar par khade hote. baithe-baithe sir men chakkar a jata. mistar NAmEKhanna ne sigret-kes unki taraf barhate hue khush mumh se kaha -- rat sone men badi der ho gayi. is vaqta kidhar se a rahe haim?
NAmERai Sahib ne thore se shabdon men apni sari kathinaiyam bayan kar dim. dil men NAmEKhanna ko galiyam dete the, jo unka sahpathi hokar bhi hamesha unhen thagne ki fikra kiya karta tha; magar mumh par uski khushamad karte the. NAmEKhanna ne aisa bhav banaya, mano unhen badi chinta ho gayi hai, bole -- meri to salah hai; ap elekshan ko goli maren, aur apne salon par muqdama dayar kar den. rahi shadi, vah to tin din ka tamasha hai. uske pichhe zerbar hona munasib nahin. kumvar sahab mere dost haim, len-den ka koi saval na uthne payega.
NAmERai Sahib ne vyang karke kaha -- ap yah bhul jate haim. mistar NAmEKhanna ki maim bainkar nahin, talluqedar hum. kumvar sahab dahej nahin mangte, unhen ishvar ne sab kuchh diya hai, lekin ap jante haim, yah meri akeli ladki hai aur uski mam mar chuki hai. vah aj zinda hoti to shayad sara ghar lutakar bhi use santosh na hota. tab shayad maim use hath rokkar kharch karne ka adesh deta; lekin ab to maim uski mam bhi hum, bap bhi hum. agar mujhe apne dil ka rakt nikalkar bhi dena pade, to maim khushi se de dunga. is vidhur-jivan men maimne bachcha-pyaar men hi apni atma ki pyas bujhai hai. donon bachchon ke pyaar men hi apne patni-vrat ka palan kiya hai. mere lie yah namumkin hai ki is shubh avsar par apne dil ke arman na nikalum. maim apne man ko to samjha sakta hum par jise maim patni ka adesh samjhata hum, use nahin samjhaya ja sakta. aur elekshan ke maidan se bhagna bhi mere lie mumkin nahin hai. maim janta hum, maim harunga. raja sahab se mera koi mukabla nahin; lekin raja sahab ko itna zarur dikha dena chahta hum ki amarapalsimh narm chadha nahin hai.
aur muqdama dayar karna to zaruri hi hai?
usi par to sara daromdar hai. ab ap batalaie, ap meri kya madad kar sakte haim?
mere dairektaron ka is vishay men jo hukma hai, vah ap jante haim. aur raja sahab bhi hamare dairektar haim, yah bhi apko malum hai. pichhla vasul karne ke lie bar-bar takid ho rahi hai. koi naya muamla to shayad hi ho sake.
NAmERai Sahib ne mumh latakakar kaha -- ap to mera donga hi dubaye dete haim mistar NAmEKhanna!
mere pas jo kuchh nij ka hai, vah apka hai; lekin baink ke muamle men to mujhe apne svamiyon ke adeshon ko manna hi parega.
agar yah zaydad hath a gayi, aur mujhe iski puri ummid hai, to pai-pai ada kar dunga.
ap batla sakte haim, is vaqta ap kitne pani men haim?
NAmERai Sahib ne hichkate hue kaha -- panch-chhah lakh samajhie. kuchh kam hi honge. NAmEKhanna ne avishvas ke bhav se kaha -- ya to apko yad nahin hai, ya ap chhipa rahe haim. NAmERai Sahib ne zor dekar kaha -- ji nahin, maim na bhula hum, aur na chhipa raha hum. meri zaydad is vaqta kam se kam pachas lakh ki hai aur sasural ki zaydad bhi isse kam nahin hai. itni zaydad par das-panch lakh ka bojh kuchh nahin ke barabar hai.
lekin yah ap kaise kah sakte haim ki sasuralvali zaydad par bhi qaraz nahin hai.
jaham tak mujhe malum hai, vah zaydad be-dag hai.
aur mujhe yah khabar mili hai ki us zaydad par das lakh se kam ka bhar nahin hai. us zaydad par to ab kuchh milne se raha, aur apki zaydad par bhi mere khayal men das lakh se kam dena nahin hai. aur vah zaydad ab pachas lakh ki nahin mushkil se pachis lakh ki hai. is hal men koi baink apko qaraz nahin de sakta. yon samajh lijie ki ap jvalamukhi ke mumh par khade haim. ek halki si thokar apko patal men pahuncha sakti hai. apko is mauqe par bahut sambhalkar chalna chahiye.
NAmERai Sahib ne unka hath apni taraf khinchkar kaha -- yah sab maim khub samjhata hum, mitravar! lekin jivan ki traijedi aur iske siva kya hai ki apki atma jo kam karna nahin chahti, vahi apko karna pade. apko is mauqe par mere lie kam se kam do lakh ka intazam karna parega.
NAmEKhanna ne lambi sams lekar kaha -- mai gad! do lakh. namumkin, bilkul namumkin!
maim tumhare dvar par sar patakkar jan de dunga, NAmEKhanna itna samajh lo. maimne tumhare hi bharose yah sare program bandhe haim. agar tumne nirash kar diya, to shayad mujhe zahar kha lena pade. maim suryapratapsimh ke samne ghutne nahin tek sakta. ladki ka shadi abhi do char mahine tal sakta hai. muqdama dayar karne ke lie abhi kafi vaqta hai; lekin yah elekshan sir par a gaya hai, aur mujhe sabse badi fikt yahi hai.
NAmEKhanna ne chakit hokar kaha -- to ap elekshan men do lakh laga denge?
elekshan ka saval nahin hai bhai, yah izzat ka saval hai. kya apki ray men meri izzat do lakh ki bhi nahin. meri sari riyasat bik jay, gam nahin; magar suryapratapsimh ko maim asani se vijay na pane dunga.
NAmEKhanna ne ek minat tak dhuam nikalne ke bad kaha -- baink ki jo hal hai vah maimne apko samne rakh di. baink ne ek tarah se len-den ka kam band kar diya hai. maim koshish karunga ki apke sath khas riayat ki jay; lekin yah ap jante haim. par mera kamishan kya rahega? mujhe apke lie khas taur par sifarish karni paregi; raja sahab ka anya dairektaron par kitna prabhav hai, yah bhi ap jante haim. mujhe unke khilaf gut-bandi karni paregi. yon samajh lijie ki meri zimmedari par hi muamla hoga.
NAmERai Sahib ka mumh gir gaya. NAmEKhanna unke antarang mitron men the. sath ke parhe hue, sath ke baithanevale. aur yah unse kamishan ki ummid rakhte haim, itne bemuravvati? akhir vah jo itne dinon se NAmEKhanna ki khushamad karte haim, vah kis din ke lie? bag men phal nikle, shak-bhaji paida ho, sab se pahle NAmEKhanna ke pas dali bhejte haim. koi jashna ho, koi jalsa ho, sabse pahle NAmEKhanna ko nimantran dete haim. uska yah javab ho. udas man se bole -- apki jo chah ho; lekin maim apko apna bhai samjhata tha.
NAmEKhanna ne kritajnyata ke bhav se kaha -- yah apki kripa hai. maimne bhi hamesha apko apna bada bhai samjha hai aur ab bhi samjhata hum. kabhi apse koi parda nahin rakha, lekin vyapar ek dusra kshetra hai. yaham koi kisi ka dost nahin, koi kisi ka bhai nahin. jis tarah maim bhai ke nate apse yah nahin kah sakta ki mujhe dusron se zyada kamishan dijie, usi tarah apko bhi mere kamishan men riyayat ke lie agrah na karna chahiye. maim apko yakin dilata hum, ki maim jitni riayat ap ke sath kar sakta hum, utna karunga. kal ap daftar ke vaqta ayen aur likha-parhi kar len. bas, bijnes khatma. apne kuchh aur suna! NAmEmehta sahab ajkal NAmEmalati par be-tarah rijhe hue haim. sari filasfi nikal gayi. din men ek-do bar zarur haziri de ate haim, aur sham ko aksar donon sath-sath sair karne niklate haim. yah to meri hi shan thi ki kabhi NAmEmalati ke dvar par salami karne na gaya. shayad ab usi ki kasar nikal rahi hai. kaham to yah hal tha ki jo kuchh haim, mistar NAmEKhanna haim. koi kam hota, to NAmEKhanna ke pas dauri ati. jab rupyon ki zarurat parti to NAmEKhanna ke nam purza ata. aur kaham ab mujhe dekhkar mumh pher leti haim. maimne khas unhim ke lie frams se ek ghadi mangavai thi. bade shauq se lekar gaya; magar nahin li. abhi kal mevon ki dali bheji thi -- kashmir se mangvaye the -- vapas kar di. mujhe to hairani hota hai ki aadmi itni jald kaise itna badal jata hai.
NAmERai Sahib man men to unki beqadri par khush hue; par hamadardi dikhakar bole -- agar yah bhi man len ki NAmEmehta se uska pyaar ho gaya hai, to bhi bartav torne ka koi vajah nahin hai.
NAmEKhanna vyathit avaz men bole -- yahi to ranj hai bhai sahab! yah to maim shuru se janta tha vah mere hath nahin a sakti! maim ap se satya kahta hum, maim kabhi is dhokhe men nahin pada ki NAmEmalati ko mujhse pyaar hai. pyaar-jaisi chiz unse mil sakti hai, iski maimne kabhi ummid hi nahin ki. maim to sirph unke rup ka pujari tha. samp men vish hai, yah jante hue bhi ham use dudh pilate haim. tote se zyada nithur jiv aur kaun hoga; lekin sirph uske rup aur vani par mugdh hokar log use palte haim aur sone ke pinjre men rakhte haim. mere lie bhi NAmEmalati usi tote ke saman thi. afsos yahi hai ki maim pahle kyon na chet gaya. iske pichhe maimne apne hazaron rupae barbad kar diye bhai sahab! jab uska rukka pahuncha, maimne turant rupae bheje. meri kar aj bhi uski savari men hai. uske pichhe maimne apna ghar chaupat kar diya bhai sahab! dil men jitna ras tha, vah usar ki or itne veg se daura ki dusri taraf ka udyan bilkul sukha rah gaya. barson ho gaye, maimne govindi se dil kholkar bat bhi nahin ki. uski seva aur pyaar aur tyag se mujhe usi tarah aruchi ho gayi thi, jaise ajirn ke rogi ko mohanbhog se ho jati hai.
