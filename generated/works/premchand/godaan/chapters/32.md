# Godaan — Adhyay 32

**Munshi Premchand**

32.
mirazaa khurshed ne aspataal se nikalakar ek nayaa kaam shuroo kar diyaa thaa. nishchint baithn unake svabhaav men na thaa. yeh kaam kyaa thaa? nagar kee veshyaaon kee ek naataka-mandalee banaanaa. apane achchhe dinon men unhonne Koob aiyaashee kee thee aur ina dinon aspataal ke ekaant men ghaavon kee pee. daaen sahate-sahate unakee aatmaa nishthaavaan ho gayee thee. us jeevan kee yaad karake unhen gaharee manovyathaa hotee thee. us vaqt agar unhen samajh hotee, to woh praaniyon kaa kitn upakaar kar sakate the; kitanon ke shok aur daridrataa kaa bhaar halakaa kar sakate the; magar woh paisaa unhonne aiyaashee men u. daayaa. yeh koee nayaa aavishkaar naheen hai ki sankaton men hee hamaaree aatmaa ko jaagriti milatee hai. bu. dhaape men kaun apanee javaanee kee bhoolon par dukhee naheen hotaa. kaash, woh samay jnaan yaa shakti ke sanchay men lagaayaa hotaa, sukritiyon kaa kosh bhar liyaa hotaa, to aaj chitt ko kitanee shaanti milatee. vahee unhen isakaa vedanaamay anubhav huaa ki sansaar men koee apn naheen, koee unakee maut aansoo bahaanevaalaa naheen. unhen raha-rahakar jeevan kee ek puraanee baat yaad aatee thee. basare ke ek gaanv men jab woh kaimp men maleriyaa se grast pa. de the, ek graameen baalaa ne unakee teemaaradaaree kitane aatma-samarpan se kee thee. achchhe ho jaane par jab unhonne rupae aur aabhooshanon se usake ehasaanon kaa badl denaa chaahaa thaa, to usane kis tarah aankhon men aansoo bharakar sir neechaa kar liyaa thaa aur una upahaaron ko lene se inakaar kar diyaa thaa. ina nason kee sushrooshaa men niyam hai, intazaam hai, sachchaaee hai, magar woh pyaar kahaan, woh tanmayataa kahaan jo us baalaa kee abhyaasaheen, alha. d sevaaon men thee? woh anuraaga-moorti kab kee unake dil se mit chukee thee. woh usase phir aane kaa vaadaa karake kabhee usake paas na gaye. vilaas ke unmaad men kabhee usakee yaad hee na aayee. aayee bhee to usamen sirph dayaa thee, pyaar na thaa. maaloom naheen, us baalaa par kyaa guzaree? magar aajakal usakee woh aatur, namr, shaant, aasaan mudraa baraabar unakee aankhon ke saamane phiraa karatee thee. kaash usase shaadee kar liyaa hotaa aaj jeevan men kitn rah hotaa. aur usake prati anyaay ke duhkh ne us pooraa varg ko unakee sevaa aur hamadardee kaa paatr bn diyaa. jab tak nadee baa. dh par thee usake gandale, tej, phenil pravaah men prakaash kee kiranen bikharakar rah jaatee theen. ab pravaah sthir aur shaant ho gayaa thaa aur rashmiyaan usakee tah tak pahunch rahee theen.
mirazaa saahab vasant kee is sheetal shaam men apane jhonpa. de ke baraamade men do vaaraanganaaon ke saath baithe kuchh baatacheet kar rahe the ki mistar mehataa pahunche. mirazaa ne ba. de tapaak se haath milaayaa aur bole -- main to aapakee Kaatiradaaree kaa saamaan liye aapakee raah dekh rahaa hoon.
donon sundariyaan muskaraayeen. mehataa kat gaye. mirazaa ne donon auraton ko vahaan se chale jaane kaa sanket kiyaa aur mehataa ko masanad par baithaate hue bole -- main to Kud aapake paas aanevaalaa thaa. mujhe aisaa maaloom ho rahaa hai ki main jo kaam karane jaa rahaa hoon, woh aapakee madad ke baGair pooraa na hogaa. aap sirf meree peeth par haath rakh deejie aur lalakaarate jaaiye -- haan mirazaa, ba. dhe chal patthe.
mehataa ne hansakar kahaa -- aap jis kaam men haath lagaayenge, usamen hum-jaise kitaabee kee. don kee madad kee zaroorat na hogee. aapakee umr mujhase zyaadaa hai duniyaa bhee aapane Koob dekhee hai aur chhote-se-chhote aadamiyon par apn asar daal sakane kee jo shakti aap men hai, woh mujhamen hotee, to mainne Kudaa jaane kyaa kiyaa hotaa. mirazaa saahab ne tho. de-se shabdon men apanee nayee skeem unase bayaan kee. unakee dhaarn thee ki roop ke baazaar men vahee striyaan aatee hain, jinhen yaa to apane ghar men kisee vajah se izzata-poorn aashray naheen milataa, yaa jo aarthik kashton se mazaboor ho jaatee hain, aur agar yeh donon savaal hal kar diye jaayan, to bahut kam auraten is tarah patit hon.
mehataa ne any vichaaravaan sajjanon kee tarah is savaal par kaafee soch kiyaa thaa aur unakaa Kayaal thaa ki mukhyatah man ke sanskaar aur bhoga-laalasaa hee auraton ko is ora kheenchatee hai. isee baat par donon mitron men bahas chhi. d gayee. donon apane-apane paksh par a. d gaye. mehataa ne mutthee baandhakar hv men patakate hue kahaa -- aapane is savaal par thande dil se Gaur naheen kiyaa. rozee ke lie aur bahut se zariye hain. magar aish kee bhookh rotiyon se naheen jaatee. usake lie duniyaa ke achchhe-se-achchhe padaarth chaahie. jab tak samaaj kee intazaam oopar se neeche tak badal na daalee jaay, is tarah kee mandalee se koee faayadaa na hogaa.
mirazaa ne moonchhen kha. dee keen -- aur main kahataa hoon ki woh mahaz rozee kaa savaal hai. haan, yeh savaal sabhee aadamiyon ke lie ek-saa naheen hai. mazadoor ke lie woh mahaz aate-daal aur ek phoos kee jhopa. dee kaa savaal hai. ek vakeel ke lie woh ek kaar aur bangale aur Kidamatagaaron kaa savaal hai. aadamee mahaz rotee naheen chaahataa, aur bhee bahuta-see cheezen chaahataa hai. agar auraton ke saamane bhee woh savaal taraha-tarah kee sooraton men aataa hai to unakaa kyaa qusoor hai?
daaktar mehataa agar zr gaur karate, to unhen maaloom hotaa ki unamen aur mirazaa men koee bhed naheen, sirph shabdon kaa hera-pher hai; par bahas kee garmee men Gaur karane kaa sabr kahaan? garm hokar bole -- muaaf keejie, mirazaa saahab, jab tak duniyaa men daulatavaale rahenge, veshyaaen bhee rahengee. mandalee agar kaamayaab bhee ho jaay, haalaanki mujhe usamen bahut shak hai, to aap dasa-paanch auraton se zyaadaa usamen kabhee na le sakenge, aur woh bhee tho. de dinon ke lie. sabhee auraton men naaty karane kee shakti naheen hotee, usee tarah jaise sabhee aadamee kavi naheen ho sakate. aur yeh bhee maan len ki veshyaaen aapakee mandalee men sthaayee roop se tik jaayangee, to bhee baazaar men unakee jagah Kaalee na rahegee. ja. d par jab tak kulhaa. de na chalenge, pattiyaan to. dane se koee nateejaa naheen. daulatavaalon men kabhee-kabhee aise log nikal aate hain, jo sab kuchh tyaag kar Kudaa kee yaad men jaa baithate hain; magar daulat kaa raajy badastoor qaayam hai. usamen zr bhee kamazoree naheen aane paaee.
mirazaa ko mehataa kee hathadharmee par duhkh huaa. itn pa. dhaa-likhaa vichaaravaan aadamee is tarah kee baaten kare! samaaj kee intazaam kyaa aasaanee se badal jaayagee? woh to sadiyon kaa muaaml hai. tab tak kyaa yeh anarth hone diyaa jaay? usakee roka-thaam na kee jaay, ina abalaaon ko madon kee lipsaa kaa shikaar hone diyaa jaay? kyon na sher ko pinjare men band kar diyaa jaay ki woh daant aur naaKoon hote hue bhee kisee ko nukasaan na pahunchaa sake. kyon us vaqt tak chupachaap baithaa rahaa jaay, jab tak sher ahinsaa kaa vrat na le le? daulatavaale aur jis tarah chaahen apanee daulat u. daayen, mirazaajee ko Gam naheen. sharaab men doob jaayan, kaaron kee maalaa gale men daal len, qile banavaayen dharmashaalaayen aur masaziden kha. dee karen, unhen koee paravaah naheen. abalaaon kee zindagee na Karaab karen. yeh mirazaajee naheen dekh sakate. woh roop ke baazaar ko aisaa Kaalee kar denge ki daulatavaalon kee ashafiryon par koee thookanevaalaa bhee na mile. kyaa jin dinon sharaab kee dookaanon kee piketing hotee thee, achchhe-achchhe sharaabee paanee pee-peekar dil kee aag naheen bujhaate the?
mehataa ne mirazaa kee bevaqoofee par hansakar kahaa -- aapako maaloom honaa chaahie ki duniyaa men aise mulk bhee hain jahaan veshyaaen naheen hain. magar ameeron kee daulat vahaan bhee dilachaspiyon ke saamaan paidaa kar letee hai.
mirazaajee bhee mehataa kee ja. dataa par hanse -- jaanataa hoon meharabaan, jaanataa hoon. aapakee duaa se duniyaa dekh chukaa hoon; magar yeh hindustaan hai, yoorop naheen hai.
'insaan kaa svabhaav saaree duniyaa men ek-saa hai. '
'magar yeh bhee maaloom rahe ki hara-ek qaum men ek aisee cheez hotee hai, jise usakee aatmaa kah sakate hain. asamat (sateetv) hindustaanee tahazeeb kee aatmaa hai. '
'apane munh miyaan-mitthoo ban leejie. '
'daulat kee aap itanee buraaee karate hain, phir bhee khannaa kee himaayat karate naheen thakate. na kahiegaa. '
mehataa kaa tej bidaa ho gayaa. namr bhaav se bole -- mainne khannaa kee himaayat us vaqt kee hai, jab woh daulat ke panje se chhoot gaye hain, aur aajakal usakee haalat aap dekhen, to aapako dayaa aayegee. aur main kyaa himaayat karoongaa, jise apanee kitaabon aur skool se chhuttee naheen; zyaadaa-se-zyaadaa sookhee hamadardee hee to kar sakataa hoon. himaayat kee hai mis maalatee ne ki khannaa ko bachaa liyaa. insaan ke dil kee gaharaaiyon men tyaag aur qubaarnee kee kitanee taaqat chhipee hotee hai, isakaa mujhe ab tak tajarabaa na huaa thaa. aap bhee ek din khannaa se mil aaie. phoolaa na samaaiegaa. is vaqt use jis cheez kee sabase zyaadaa zaroorat hai, woh hamadardee hai.
mirazaa ne jaise apanee chaah ke viruddh kahaa -- aap kahate hain, to jaaoongaa. aapake saath jahannum men jaane men bhee mujhe urjaa naheen; magar mis maalatee se to aapakee shaadee honevaalee thee. ba. dee garm Kabar thee.
mehataa ne jhenpate hue kahaa -- tapasyaa kar rahaa hoon. dekhie kab varadaan mile.
'ajee woh to aap par maratee thee. '
'mujhe bhee yahee vaham huaa thaa; magar jab mainne haath ba. dhaakar use paka. dn chaahaa, to dekhaa. woh aasamaan men jaa baithee hai. us oonchaaee tak to kyaa main pahunchoongaa, aarazoo-minnat kar rahaa hoon ki neeche aa jaaya. aajakal to woh mujhase bolatee bhee naheen. '
yeh kahate hue mehataa zor se rotee huee hansee hanse aur uth kha. de hue.
mirazaa ne poochhaa -- ab phir kab mulaaqaat hogee?
'abakee aapako takaleef karanee pa. degee. khannaa ke paas jaaiegaa zaroor!
'jaaoongaa. '
mirazaa ne khi. dakee se mehataa ko jaate dekhaa. chaal men woh tezee na thee, jaise kisee chintaa men doobe hue hon.
