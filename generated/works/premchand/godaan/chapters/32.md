# Godaan — Adhyay 32

**Munshi Premchand**

32.
mirza NAmEKhurshed ne aspatal se nikalkar ek naya kam shuru kar diya tha. nishchint baithna unke svabhav men na tha. yah kam kya tha? nagar ki veshyaon ki ek natak-mandli banana. apne achchhe dinon men unhomne khub aiyashi ki thi aur in dinon aspatal ke ekant men ghavon ki piraen sahte-sahte unki atma nishthavan ho gayi thi. us jivan ki yad karke unhen gahri manovyatha hoti thi. us vaqta agar unhen samajh hoti, to vah praniyon ka kitna upkar kar sakte the; kitnon ke shok aur daridrata ka bhar halka kar sakte the; magar vah paisa unhomne aiyashi men uraya. yah koi naya avishkar nahin hai ki sankton men hi hamari atma ko jagriti milti hai. burhape men kaun apni javani ki bhulon par dukhi nahin hota. kash, vah samay jnyan ya shakti ke sanchay men lagaya hota, sukritiyon ka kosh bhar liya hota, to aj chitt ko kitni shanti milti. vahi unhen iska vedanamay anubhav hua ki samsar men koi apna nahin, koi unki maut amsu bahanevala nahin. unhen rah-rahkar jivan ki ek purani bat yad ati thi. basre ke ek gamv men jab vah kaimp men maleriya se grast pade the, ek gramin bala ne unki timardari kitne atma-samarpan se ki thi. achchhe ho jane par jab unhomne rupae aur abhushnon se uske ehsano ka badla dena chaha tha, to usne kis tarah ankhon men amsu bharkar sir nicha kar liya tha aur un upharo ko lene se inkar kar diya tha. in nason ki sushrusha men niyam hai, intzam hai, sachchai hai, magar vah pyaar kaham, vah tanmayta kaham jo us bala ki abhyashin, alhar sevaon men thi? vah anurag-murti kab ki unke dil se mit chuki thi. vah usse phir ane ka vada karke kabhi uske pas na gaye. vilas ke unmad men kabhi uski yad hi na ayi. ayi bhi to usmen sirph daya thi, pyaar na tha. malum nahin, us bala par kya guzri? magar ajkal uski vah atur, namra, shant, asan mudra barabar unki ankhon ke samne phira karti thi. kash usse shadi kar liya hota aj jivan men kitna rah hota. aur uske prati anyay ke duhkh ne us pura varg ko unki seva aur hamadardi ka chitthi bana diya. jab tak nadi barh par thi uske gandale, tej, phenil pravah men prakash ki kirnen bikharkar rah jati thim. ab pravah sthir aur shant ho gaya tha aur rashmiyam uski tah tak pahunch rahi thim.
mirza sahab vasant ki is shital sham men apne jhompre ke baramde men do varanganaon ke sath baithe kuchh batchit kar rahe the ki mistar NAmEmehta pahunche. mirza ne bade tapak se hath milaya aur bole -- maim to apki khatirdari ka saman liye apki rah dekh raha hum.
donon sundariyam muskarayim. NAmEmehta kat gaye. mirza ne donon aurton ko vaham se chale jane ka sanket kiya aur NAmEmehta ko masnad par baithate hue bole -- maim to khud apke pas anevala tha. mujhe aisa malum ho raha hai ki maim jo kam karne ja raha hum, vah apki madad ke bagair pura na hoga. ap sirfa meri pith par hath rakh dijie aur lalakarte jaiye -- ham mirza, barhe chal patthe.
NAmEmehta ne hamskar kaha -- ap jis kam men hath lagayenge, usmen ham-jaise kitabi kiron ki madad ki zarurat na hogi. apki umra mujhse zyada hai duniya bhi apne khub dekhi hai aur chhote-se-chhote admiyo par apna asar dal sakne ki jo shakti ap men hai, vah mujhmen hoti, to maimne khuda jane kya kiya hota. mirza sahab ne thore-se shabdon men apni nayi skim unse bayan ki. unki dharna thi ki rup ke bazar men vahi striyam ati haim, jinhen ya to apne ghar men kisi vajah se izzat-purn ashray nahin milta, ya jo arthik kashton se mazbur ho jati haim, aur agar yah donon saval hal kar diye jayam, to bahut kam aurten is tarah patit hon.
NAmEmehta ne anya vicharavan sajjanon ki tarah is saval par kafi soch kiya tha aur unka khayal tha ki mukhyatah man ke samskar aur bhog-lalsa hi aurton ko is or khinchti hai. isi bat par donon mitron men bahas chhir gayi. donon apne-apne paksh par ar gaye. NAmEmehta ne mutthi bandhkar hava men patkate hue kaha -- apne is saval par thande dil se gaur nahin kiya. rozi ke lie aur bahut se zariye haim. magar aish ki bhukh rotiyon se nahin jati. uske lie duniya ke achchhe-se-achchhe padarth chahiye. jab tak samaj ki intzam upar se niche tak badal na dali jay, is tarah ki mandli se koi fayda na hoga.
mirza ne munchhen khadi kim -- aur maim kahta hum ki vah mahaz rozi ka saval hai. ham, yah saval sabhi admiyo ke lie ek-sa nahin hai. mazdur ke lie vah mahaz ate-dal aur ek phus ki jhopri ka saval hai. ek vakil ke lie vah ek kar aur bangle aur khidamatgaro ka saval hai. aadmi mahaz roti nahin chahta, aur bhi bahut-si chizen chahta hai. agar aurton ke samne bhi vah saval tarah-tarah ki surton men ata hai to unka kya qusur hai?
daktar NAmEmehta agar zara gaur karte, to unhen malum hota ki unmen aur mirza men koi bhed nahin, sirph shabdon ka her-pher hai; par bahas ki garmi men gaur karne ka sabra kaham? garm hokar bole -- muaf kijie, mirza sahab, jab tak duniya men daulatvale rahenge, veshyaen bhi rahengi. mandli agar kamyab bhi ho jay, halanki mujhe usmen bahut shak hai, to ap das-panch aurton se zyada usmen kabhi na le sakenge, aur vah bhi thore dinon ke lie. sabhi aurton men natya karne ki shakti nahin hoti, usi tarah jaise sabhi aadmi kavi nahin ho sakte. aur yah bhi man len ki veshyaen apki mandli men sthayi rup se tik jayangi, to bhi bazar men unki jagah khali na rahegi. jar par jab tak kulhare na chalenge, pattiyam torne se koi natija nahin. daulatvalo men kabhi-kabhi aise log nikal ate haim, jo sab kuchh tyag kar khuda ki yad men ja baithte haim; magar daulat ka rajya badastur qayam hai. usmen zara bhi kamzori nahin ane pai.
mirza ko NAmEmehta ki hathadharmi par duhkh hua. itna parha-likha vicharavan aadmi is tarah ki baten kare! samaj ki intzam kya asani se badal jaygi? vah to sadiyon ka muamla hai. tab tak kya yah anarth hone diya jay? uski rok-tham na ki jay, in abalaon ko madon ki lipsa ka shikar hone diya jay? kyon na sher ko pinjre men band kar diya jay ki vah dant aur nakhun hote hue bhi kisi ko nuksan na pahuncha sake. kyon us vaqta tak chupchap baitha raha jay, jab tak sher ahimsa ka vrat na le le? daulatvale aur jis tarah chahen apni daulat urayen, mirzaji ko gam nahin. sharab men dub jayam, karon ki mala gale men dal len, qile banvaye dharmashalayen aur maszide khadi karen, unhen koi parvah nahin. abalaon ki zindagi na kharab karen. yah mirzaji nahin dekh sakte. vah rup ke bazar ko aisa khali kar denge ki daulatvalo ki ashafiryon par koi thukanevala bhi na mile. kya jin dinon sharab ki dukanon ki piketing hoti thi, achchhe-achchhe sharabi pani pi-pikar dil ki ag nahin bujhate the?
NAmEmehta ne mirza ki bevqufi par hamskar kaha -- apko malum hona chahiye ki duniya men aise mulk bhi haim jaham veshyaen nahin haim. magar amiron ki daulat vaham bhi dilachaspiyon ke saman paida kar leti hai.
mirzaji bhi NAmEmehta ki jarta par hamse -- janta hum meharban, janta hum. apki dua se duniya dekh chuka hum; magar yah hindustan hai, yurop nahin hai.
imsan ka svabhav sari duniya men ek-sa hai.
magar yah bhi malum rahe ki har-ek qaum men ek aisi chiz hoti hai, jise uski atma kah sakte haim. asmat (satitva) hindustani tahzib ki atma hai.
apne mumh miyam-mitthu ban lijie.
daulat ki ap itni burai karte haim, phir bhi NAmEKhanna ki himayat karte nahin thakte. na kahiega.
NAmEmehta ka tej bida ho gaya. namra bhav se bole -- maimne NAmEKhanna ki himayat us vaqta ki hai, jab vah daulat ke panje se chhut gaye haim, aur ajkal uski halat ap dekhen, to apko daya ayegi. aur maim kya himayat karunga, jise apni kitabon aur skul se chhutti nahin; zyada-se-zyada sukhi hamadardi hi to kar sakta hum. himayat ki hai mis NAmEmalati ne ki NAmEKhanna ko bacha liya. imsan ke dil ki gaharaiyon men tyag aur qubarni ki kitni taqat chhipi hoti hai, iska mujhe ab tak tajraba na hua tha. ap bhi ek din NAmEKhanna se mil aie. phula na samaiega. is vaqta use jis chiz ki sabse zyada zarurat hai, vah hamadardi hai.
mirza ne jaise apni chah ke viruddh kaha -- ap kahte haim, to jaunga. apke sath jahannum men jane men bhi mujhe urja nahin; magar mis NAmEmalati se to apki shadi honevali thi. badi garm khabar thi.
NAmEmehta ne jhempte hue kaha -- tapasya kar raha hum. dekhie kab vardan mile.
aji vah to ap par marti thi.
mujhe bhi yahi vaham hua tha; magar jab maimne hath barhakar use pakrana chaha, to dekha. vah asman men ja baithi hai. us unchai tak to kya maim pahunchunga, arzu-minnat kar raha hum ki niche a jay. ajkal to vah mujhse bolti bhi nahin.
yah kahte hue NAmEmehta zor se roti hui hamsi hamse aur uth khade hue.
mirza ne puchha -- ab phir kab mulaqat hogi?
abki apko taklif karni paregi. NAmEKhanna ke pas jaiega zarur!
jaunga.
mirza ne khirki se NAmEmehta ko jate dekha. chal men vah tezi na thi, jaise kisi chinta men dube hue hon.
