NAmEDhania itni shah pakad boli -- pandit dada, vah usi ka kam hai. NAmESobha ke ghar se khurpi mangkar laya aur koi jari khodkar gay ko khila di. us rat ko jo jhagra hua tha, usi din se vah khar khaye baitha tha.

NAmEDhania boli -- maharaj, uske qasam ka bharosa nahin. chatpat kha lega. jab isne jhuthi qasam kha li, jo bada dharmatma banta hai, to NAmEHeera ka kya vishvas.
ab NAmEGobar bola -- kha le jhuthi qasam. bams ka ant ho jay. burhe jite rahen. javan jikar kya karenge!
NAmERupa ek pal men akar boli -- kaka ghar men nahin hai, pandit dada! kaki kahti haim, kahim chale gaye haim. NAmEDatadin ne lambi daadhi phatakarkar kaha -- tune puchha nahin, kaham chale gaye kiya? ghar men chhipa baitha na ho. dekh to NAmESona, bhitar to nahin baitha hai.

NAmEPunia kahti hai lutiya-dor aur danda sab lekar gaye haim. NAmEDhania shital dil se boli -- mumh men kalikh lagakar kahim bhaga hoga.

NAmEDhania ne shak ki -- ganga jata to rupae kyon le jata, aur ajkal koi parab bhi to nahin hai?

NAmEHeera zarur kahim bhag gaya. dekha hoga ki bhed khul gaya, ab jehal jana parega, hatya alag lagegi. bas, kahim bhag gaya. NAmEDatadin, NAmEJhinguri simh, nokheram, unke charon pyade, mangru sah aur lala NAmEPateshwari, sabhi a pahunche aur darogaji ke samne hath bandhkar khade ho gaye. NAmEHori ki talbi hui. jivan men yah pahla avsar tha ki vah daroga ke samne aya. aisa dar raha tha, jaise phamsi ho jayegi. NAmEDhania ko pitte samay uska ek-ek ang pharak raha tha. daroga ke samne kachhue ki tarah bhitar simta jata tha. daroga ne use alochak netron se dekha aur uske dil tak pahunch gaye. admiyo ki nas pahachanne ka unhen achchha abhyas tha. kitabi manovijnyan men kore, par vyavaharik manovijnyan ke marmajna the. yaqin ho gaya, aj achchhe ka mumh dekhkar uthe haim. aur NAmEHori ka chehra kahe deta tha, ise sirph ek ghurki kafi hai. daroga ne puchha -- tujhe kis par shubha hai?
NAmEHori ne zameen chhui aur hath bandhkar bola -- mera subha kisi par nahin hai sarkar, gay apni maut se mari hai. buddhi ho gayi thi.
NAmEDhania bhi akar pichhe khadi thi. turant boli -- gay mari hai tumhare bhai NAmEHeera ne. sarkar aise bauram nahin haim ki jo kuchh tum kah doge, vah man lenge. yaham janch-tahaqiqat karne aye haim.
darogaji ne puchha -- yah kaun aurat hai?
kai admiyo ne darogaji se kuchh batchit karne ka achchhi kismat prapt karne ke lie chadha-upri ki. ek sath bole aur apne man ko is kalpana se santosh diya ki pahle maim bola -- NAmEHori ki gharvali hai sarkar!
to ise bulao, maim pahle isi ka bayan likhunga. vah kaham hai NAmEHeera?
