# Godaan — Adhyay 15

**Munshi Premchand**

15.
NAmEmalati bahar se titli hai, bhitar se madhumakkhi. uske jivan men hamsi hi hamsi nahin hai, sirph gur khakar kaun ji sakta hai! aur jiye bhi to vah koi sukhi jivan na hoga. vah hamsti hai, isalie ki use iske bhi dam milte haim. uska chahkana aur chamkana, isalie nahin hai ki vah chahkane ko hi jivan samjhati hai, ya usne nijatva ko apni ankhon men itna barha liya hai ki jo kuchh kare, apne hi lie kare. nahin, vah kyunki chahkati hai aur vinod karti hai ki isse uske pharz ka bhar kuchh halka ho jata hai. uske bap un vichitra jivon men the, jo sirph zaban ki madad se lakhon ke vare-nyare karte the. bade-bade zamindaron aur raison ki jaydade bikvana, unhen qaraz dilana ya unke muamlon ko afsaron se milkar tay kara dena, yahi unka vyavsay tha. dusre shabdon men, dalal the. is varg ke log bade pratibhavan hote haim. jis kam se kuchh milne ki ummid ho, vah utha lenge, kisi na kisi tarah use nibha bhi denge. kisi raja ki shadi kisi rajakumari se thik karva di aur das-bis hazar usi men mar liye. yahi dalal jab chhote-chhote saude karte haim, to taut kahe jate haim, aur ham unse naphrat karte haim. bade-bade kam karke vahi taut rajaon ke sath shikar khelta hai aur gavarnaron ki mez par chay pita hai. mistar kaul unhim bhagyavanon men se the. unke tin larkiya hi larkiya thim. unka soch tha ki tinon ko inglaind bhejkar shiksha ke shikhar par pahuncha den. anya bahut se bade admiyo ki tarah unka bhi khayal tha ki inglaind men shiksha pakad aadmi kuchh aur ho jata hai. shayad vaham ke jal-vayu men akal ko tez kar dene ki koi shakti hai; magar unki yah chah ek-tihai se zyada puri na hui. NAmEmalati inglaind men hi thi ki un par falij gira aur bekam kar gaya. ab badi mushkil se do admiyo ke sahare uthte-baithte the. zaban to bilkul band hi ho gayi. aur jab zaban hi band ho gayi, to amdani bhi band ho gayi. jo kuchh thi, zaban hi ki kamai thi. kuchh bacha rakhne ki unki adat na thi. aniymit kamai thi aur aniymit kharch tha; isalie idhar kai sal se bahut tanghal ho rahe the. sara dayitva NAmEmalati par a pada. NAmEmalati ke char-panch sau rupae men vah bhog-vilas aur that-bat to kya nibhta! ham, itna tha ki donon larkiyo ki shiksha hoti jati thi aur bhalemanson ki tarah zindagi basar hoti thi. NAmEmalati subah se pahar rat tak daurti rahti thi. chahti thi ki pita satvikta ke sath rahen, lekin pitaji ko sharab-kavab ka aisa chaska pada tha ki kisi tarah gala na chhorta tha. kahim se kuchh na milta, to ek mahajan se apne bangle par pronot likhkar hazar do hazar le lete the. mahajan unka purana dost tha, jisne unki badaulat len-den men lakhon kamaye the, aur murauvat ke mare kuchh bolta na tha. uske pachis hazar charh chuke the, aur jab chahta, quqir kara sakta tha; magar mitrata ki laj nibhata jata tha. atmaseviyon men jo nirlajjata a jati hai, vah kaul men bhi thi. taqaze hua karen, unhen parva na thi. NAmEmalati unke apavyay par jhunjhlati rahti thi; lekin uski mam jo sakshat devi thim aur is yug men bhi pati ki seva ko nari-jivan ka mukhya hetu samjhati thim, use samjhati rahti thi; isalie ghar-larai na hone pata tha. sham ho gayi thi. hava men abhi tak gamir thi. akash men dhundh chhaya hua tha. NAmEmalati aur uski donon bahnen bangle ke samne ghas par baithi hui thim. pani na pane ke vajah vaham ki dub jal gayi thi aur bhitar ki mitti nikal ayi thi.
NAmEmalati ne puchha -- mali kya bilkul pani nahin deta?
manjhli bahan saroj ne kaha -- pada-pada soya karta hai suar. jab kaho, to bis bahane nikalne lagta hai.
saroj bi. e. men parhti thi, dubli-si, lambi, pili, rukhi, katu. use kisi ki koi bat pasand na ati thi. hamesha aib nikalti rahti thi. daktaron ki salah thi ki vah koi mehnat na kare, aur pahar par rahe; lekin ghar ki hal aisi na thi ki use pahar par bheja ja sakta. sabse chhoti varda ko saroj se isliye naphrat tha ki sara ghar saroj ko hathon-hath liye rahta tha; vah chahti thi jis bimari men itna svad hai, vah use hi kyon nahin ho jati. gori-si, garvashil, svasth, chanchal ankhomvali ladki thi, jiske mumh par pratibha ki jhalak thi. saroj ke siva use sare samsar se hamadardi thi. saroj ke kathan ka khilaf hona karna uska svabhav tha. boli -- din-bhar dadaji bazar bhejte rahte haim, fursat hi kaham pata hai. marne ko chhutti to milti nahin, pada-pada soyega!
saroj ne danta -- dadaji use kab bazar bhejte haim ri, jhuthi kahim ki!
roz bhejte haim, roz. abhi to aj hi bheja tha. kaho to bulakar puchhva dum?
puchhavayegi, bulaum?
NAmEmalati dari. donon guth jayangi, to baithna mushkil kar dengi. bat badalkar boli -- achchha khair, hoga. aj daktar NAmEmehta ka tumhare yaham bhashan hua tha, saroj?

NAmEmalati ne kataksh kiya -- ledi huqqu ne? is vishay men vah bhi kuchh bolne ka himmat rakhti haim! tumhen daktar sahab ka bhashan adi se ant tak sunna chahiye tha. unhomne dil men larkiyo ko kya samjha hoga?
pura bhashan sunne ka sabra kise tha? vah to jaise ghav par namak chhirkate the.
phir unhen bulaya hi kyon? akhir unhen aurton se koi vair to hai nahin. jis bat ko ham satya samjhate haim, usi ka to prachar karte haim. aurton ko khush karne ke lie vah unki-si kahanevalon men nahin haim aur phir abhi yah kaun janta hai ki striyam jis raste par chalna chahti haim vahi satya hai. bahut mumkin hai, age chal kar hamen apni dharna badlani pade.
usne frams, jarmani aur itli ki mahilaon ke jivan adarsh batlaye aur kaha -- jaldi hi vimems lig ki or se NAmEmehta ka bhashan honevala hai. saroj ko kutuhal hua.
magar ap bhi to kahti haim ki striyon aur purushon ke adhikar saman hone chahiye.
ab bhi kahti hum; lekin dusre pakshavale kya kahte haim, yah bhi to sunna chahiye. mumkin hai; hamim galti par hon.
yah lig is nagar ki nayi samstha hai aur NAmEmalati ke udyog se khuli hai. nagar ki sabhi shikshit mahilaen usmen sharik haim. NAmEmehta ke pahle bhashan ne mahilaon men badi halchal macha di thi aur lig ne phaisla kiya tha, ki unka khub dandashikan javab diya jay. NAmEmalati hi par yah bhar dal gaya tha. NAmEmalati kai din tak apne paksh ke samarthan men yuktiyam aur sabut khojti rahi. aur bhi kai deviyam apne bhashan likh rahi thim. us din jab NAmEmehta sham ko lig ke hal men pahunche, to jan parta tha hal phat jayga. unhen garva hua. unka bhashan sunne ke lie itna utsah! aur vah utsah sirph mumh par aur ankhon men na tha. aj sabhi deviyam sone aur resham se ladi hui thim, mano kisi barat men ayi hon. NAmEmehta ko parast karne ke lie puri shakti se kam liya tha aur yah kaun kah sakta hai ki jagamagahat shakti ka ang nahin hai. NAmEmalati ne to aj ke lie naye faishan ki sari nikali thi, naye kat ke jampar banvaye the aur rang-rogan aur phulon se khub saji hui thi, mano uska shadi ho raha ho. vimems lig men itna jashna aur kabhi na hua tha. daktar NAmEmehta akele the, phir bhi deviyon ke dil kamp rahe the. satya ki ek chingari asatya ke ek pahar ko bhasma kar sakti hai. sabse pichhe ki saf men mirza aur NAmEKhanna aur sampadkaji bhi viraj rahe the. ray-sahab bhashan shuru hone ke bad aye aur pichhe khade ho gaye.
mirza ne kaha -- a jaie ap bhi, khade kab tak rahiega.
NAmERai Sahib bole -- nahin bhai, yaham mera dam ghutne lagega.
to maim khada hota hum. ap baithie.
NAmERai Sahib ne unke kandhe dabaye -- takalluf nahin, baithe rahie. maim thak jaunga, to apko utha dunga aur baith jaunga, achchha mis NAmEmalati sabhanetri huim. NAmEKhanna sahab kuchh inam dilavaie.
NAmEKhanna ne roni surat banakar kaha -- ab mistar NAmEmehta par hi nigah hai. maim to gir gaya.
mistar NAmEmehta ka bhashan shuru hua -- deviyo, jab maim is tarah apko sambodhit karta hum, to apko koi bat khatkati nahin. ap is izzat ko apna adhikar samjhati haim; lekin apne kisi mahila ko purushon ke prati devta ka bartav karte suna hai? use ap devta kahen, to vah samjhega, ap use bana rahi haim. apke pas dan dene ke lie daya hai, shraddha hai, tyag hai. aadmi ke pas dan ke lie kya hai? vah devta nahin, levta hai. vah adhikar ke lie himsa karta hai, larai karta hai, jhagra karta hai..
taliyam bajim.
NAmERai Sahib ne kaha -- aurton ko khush karne ka isne kitna achchha dhang nikala.
bijli sampadak ko bura laga -- koi nayi bat nahin. maim kitni hi bar yah bhav vyakt kar chuka hum.
NAmEmehta age barhe -- isalie jab maim dekhta hum, hamari unnat vicharomvali deviyam us daya aur shraddha aur tyag ke jivan se asantusht hokar larai aur jhagra aur himsa ke jivan ki or daur rahi haim aur samajh rahi haim ki yahi sukh ka svarg hai, to maim unhen badhai nahin de sakta.
misez NAmEKhanna ne NAmEmalati ki or sagarva netron se dekha. NAmEmalati ne gardan jhuka li.
NAmEKhurshed bole -- ab kahie. NAmEmehta diler aadmi hai. sachchi bat kahta hai aur mumh par.
bijli sampadak ne nak sikori -- ab vah din lad gaye, jab deviyam in chakmon men a jati thim. unke adhikar harpate jao aur kahte jao, ap to devi haim, lakshami haim, mam haim.
NAmEmehta age barhe -- aurat ko aadmi ke rup men, aadmi ke karm men, rat dekhkar mujhe usi tarah dard hoti hai, jaise aadmi ko aurat ke rup men, aurat ke karm karte dekhkar. mujhe yakin hai, aise purushon ko ap apne yakin aur pyaar ka chitthi nahin samjhati aur maim apko yakin dilata hum, aisi aurat bhi aadmi ke pyaar aur shraddha ka chitthi nahin ban sakti.
NAmEKhanna ke chehre par dil ki khushi chamak uthi. NAmERai Sahib ne chutki li -- ap bahut khush haim NAmEKhanna ji!
NAmEKhanna bole -- NAmEmalati milen, to puchhum, ab kahie.
NAmEmehta age barhe -- maim praniyon ke vikas men aurat ke pad ko purushon ke pad se shreshth samjhata hum, usi tarah jaise pyaar aur tyag aur shraddha ko himsa aur larai aur jhagra se shreshth samjhata hum. agar hamari deviyam srishti aur palan ke dev-mandir se himsa aur jhagra ke danav-kshetra men ana chahti haim, to usse samaj ka kalyan na hoga. maim is vishay men drirh hum. aadmi ne apne abhiman men apni danvi kirti ko zyada mahatva diya. vah apne bhai ka svatva chhinkar aur uska rakt bahakar samjhane laga, usne bahut badi vijay payi. jin shishuon ko deviyon ne apne rakt se sirja aur pala unhen bam aur mashingan aur sahasron tainkon ka shikar banakar vah apne ko vijeta samjhata hai. aur jab hamari hi matayen uske mathe par kesar ka tilak lagakar aur use apni asison ka kavach pahanakar himsa-kshetra men bhejti haim, to hairani hai ki aadmi ne vinash ko hi samsar ke kalyan ki vastu samjha aur uski himsa-pravritti din-din barhti gayi aur aj ham dekh rahe haim ki yah danvata bahut tez hokar sab samsar ko raundti, praniyon ko kuchlati, hari-bhari khetiyon ko jalati aur gulzar bistayon ko viran karti chali jati hai. deviyo, maim ap se puchhta hum, kya ap is danavlila men sahyog dekar, is larai-kshetra men utarkar samsar ka kalyan karengi? maim apse vinti karta hum, nash karanevalon ko apna kam karne dijie, ap apne dharm ka palan kiye jaie.
NAmEKhanna bole -- NAmEmalati ki to gardan nahin uthti.
NAmERai Sahib ne in vicharon ka samarthan kiya -- NAmEmehta kahte to yatharth hi haim.
bijli sampadak bigre -- magar koi nayi bat to nahin kahi. nari-andolan ke virodhi inhim ut-patang baton ki sharan liya karte haim. maim ise manta hi nahin ki tyag aur pyaar se samsar ne unnati ki. samsar ne unnati ki paurush se, parakram se, akal-bal se, tej se.
NAmEKhurshed ne kaha -- achchha, sunne dijiega ya apni hi gaye jaiega?
NAmEmehta ka bhashan jari tha -- deviyo, maim un logon men nahin hum, jo kahte haim, aurat aur aadmi men saman shaktiyam haim, saman pravrittiyam haim, aur unmen koi vibhinnata nahin hai; isse bhayankar asatya ki maim kalpana nahin kar sakta. yah vah asatya hai, jo yug-yugantaron se sanchit anubhav ko usi tarah dhank lena chahta hai, jaise badal ka ek tukra surya ko dhank leta hai. maim apko sachet kiye deta hum ki ap is jal men na phamsen. aurat aadmi se utni hi shroshtha hai, jitna prakash andhere se. manushya ke lie kshama aur tyag aur ahimsa jivan ke uchchatam adarsh haim. nari is adarsh ko prapt kar chuki hai. aadmi dharm aur adhyatma aur rishiyon ka ashraya lekar us lakshya par pahunchne ke lie sadiyon se zor mar raha hai; par kamyab nahin ho saka. maim kahta hum, uska sara adhyatma aur yog ek taraf aur nariyon ka tyag ek taraf. taliyam bajim. hal hil utha. NAmERai Sahib ne gadgad hokar kaha -- NAmEmehta vahi kahte haim, jo inke dil men hai.
NAmEOnkarnath ne tika ki -- lekin baten sabhi purani haim, sari huim.
