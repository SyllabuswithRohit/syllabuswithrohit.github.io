# Nasha

**Munshi Premchand**

1
eeshvaree ek bade jameendaar kaa ladakaa tha aur main gareeb klark kaa, jisake paas mehnat-majooree ke sivaa aur koi jaayadaad na thi. ham donon mein paraspar bahasen hoti rehti theen. main jameendaaree ki buraaee kart, unhen hinsak pashu aur khoon choosane vaalee jonk aur vrikshon ki chotee par phoolane vaalaa banjhaa keht. vah jameendaaron kaa paksh letaa, par svabhaavat: uska pahaloo kuchh kamajor hot tha, kyonki usake paas jameendaaron ke anukool koi daleel na thi. vah keht ki sabhi manushya baraabar nahin haate, chhote-bade hameshaa hote rahenge. lachar daleel thi. kisee maanusheey yaa naitik niyam se is vyavastha kaa auchitya siddh karn mushkil tha. main is vaad-vivaad ki garmee-garmee mein aksar tej ho jat aur lagne vaalee baat kah jat, lekin eeshvaree haarakar bhi muskaraataa rehta tha maine use kabhi garm hote nahin dekh. shaayad isakaa vajah yah tha ki vah apne paksh ki kamajoree samajhta tha.
naukaron se vah seedhe munh baat nahin kart tha. ameeron mein jo ek bedardee aur uddantaa hoti hai, isamen use bhi prachur bhaag milaa tha. naukar ne bistar lagaane mein zara bhi der ki, doodh jaroorat se zyada garm yaa thandaa hua, saaikil achchhi tarah saaph nahin hui, to vah aape se baahar ho jat. sustee yaa badatameejee use zara bhi baradaashta na thi, par doston se aur visheshakar mujhse uska vyavahaar sauhaard aur namrataa se bharaa hua hot tha. shaayad uski jagah main hot, to mujhse bhi vaheen kathorataaen paidaa ho jaateen, jo usamen theen, kyonki mera lokaprem siddhaanton par nahin, nijee dashaaon par tikaa hua tha, lekin vah meri jagah hokar bhi shaayad ameer hi rehta, kyonki vah prakriti se hi vilaasee aur aishvary-priy tha.
abakee dashahare ki chhuttiyon mein maine nishchay kiya ki ghar na jaaoongaa. mere paas kiraae ke lie rupaye na the aur na gharavaalon ko takaleeph dena chahta tha. main jaanataa hoon, ve mujhe jo kuchh dete hain, vah unki haisiyat se bahut zyada hai, usake saath hi pareekshaa kaa khyaal tha. abhi bahut kuchh padhna hai, bordig haaus mein bhoot ki tarah akele pade rehne ko bhi jee na chahta tha. isalie jab eeshvaree ne mujhe apne ghar kaa nevataa diya, to main binaa aagrah ke raajee ho gaya. eeshvaree ke saath pareekshaa ki taiyaaree khoob ho jaaegee. vah ameer hokar bhi mehanatee aur jaheen hai.
usane usake saath hi kahaa-lekin bhai, ek baat kaa khyaal rakhna. wahan agar jameendaaron ki nindaa ki, to muaamilaa bigad. jaaegaa aur mere gharavaalon ko buraa lagegaa. vah log to aasaamiyon par isi daave se shaasan karte hain ki eeshvar ne asaamiyon ko unki sevaa ke lie hi paidaa kiya hai. asaamee mein koi maulik bhed nahin hai, to jameendaaron kaa kaheen pataa na lage.
maine kahaa-to kya tum samajhte ho ki main wahan jaakar kuchh aur ho jaaoongaa?
'haan, main to yahi samajhta hoon.
'tum galat samajhte ho.'
eeshvaree ne isakaa koi jawab na diya. kadaachit usane is muaamale ko mare vivek par chhod diya. aur bahut achchha kiya. agar vah apni baat par adataa, to main bhi jid pakad letaa.
2
sekand klaas to kya, mainnen kabhi intar klaas mein bhi saphar na kiya tha. ab ki sekand klaas mein saphar kaa saubhaagya praaiz hua. gaadee to nau baje raat ko aatee thi, par yaatraa ke harsh mein ham shaam ko steshan jaa pahunche. kuchh der idhar-udhar sair karne ke baad riphreshament-room mein jaakar ham logon ne bhejan kiya. meri vesh-bhooshaa aur rang-dhang se paarakhee khaanasaamon ko yah pahachaanane mein der na lagee ki maalik kaun hai aur pichhalaggoo kaun; lekin na jaane kyon mujhe unki gustaakhee buree lag rahi thi. paise eeshvaree ki jeb se gae. shaayad mere pitaa ko jo vetan milataa hai, usse zyada ina khaanasaamon ko inaam-ikaraam mein mil jat ho. ek athannee to chalte samay eeshvaree hi ne dee. phir bhi main una sabhon se usi tatparataa aur vinay ki apekshaa kart tha, jisse ve eeshvaree ki sevaa kar rahe the. kyon eeshvaree ke hukma par sab-ke-sab daudate hain, lekin main koi cheej maangataa hoon, to utanaa utsaah nahin dikhaate! mujhe khaanaa mein kuchh svaad na milaa. yah bhed mere dhyaan ko sampoorn roop se apni ora kheenche hue tha.
gaadee aayee, ham dono savaar hue. khaanasaamon ne eeshvaree ko salaam kiya. meri ora dekh bhi nahin.
eeshvaree ne kahaa—kitane tameejadaar hain ye sab? ek hamaare naukar hain ki koi kaam karne kaa dhang nahin.
maine khatte man se kahaa—isi tarah agar tum apne naukaron ko bhi aath aane roj inaam diya karo, to shaayad inse zyada tameejadaar ho jaaen.
'to kya tum samajhte ho, yah sab keval inaam ke laalach se itanaa adab karte hain.
'jee nahin, kadaapit nahin! tameej aur adab to inake rakta mein mil gaya hai.'
gaadee chalee. daak thi. koshish se chalee to prataapagadh jaakar rookee. ek aadamee ne hamaaraa kamaraa kholaa. main turant chillaa uthaa, doosaraa darja hai-sekand klaas hai.
us musaaphir ne dibbe ke andar aakar meri ora ek vichitr upekshaa ki nazar se dekhakar kahaa—jee haan, sevak itanaa samajhta hai, aur beech vaale barthade par baith gaya. mujhe kitanee lajjaa aaee, kah nahin sakataa.
bhor hote-hote ham log muraadaabaad pahunche. steshan par kaee aadamee hamaaraa svaagat karne ke lie khade the. paanch begaar. begaaron ne hamaaraa lagej uthaayaa. donon bhadr puroosh peechhe-peechhe chale. ek musalamaan tha riyaasat alee, doosaraa braahman tha raamaharakh. donon ne meri ora parichit netron se dekh, maano kah rahe hain, tum kauve hokar hans ke saath kaise?
riyaasat alee ne eeshvaree se poochhaa—yah baaboo sahab kya aapake saath padhte hain?
eeshvaree ne jawab diya—haan, saath padhte bhi hain aur saath rehte bhi hain. yon kahie ki aap hi ki badaulat main ilaahaabaad padaa hua hoon, nahin kab kaa lakhanaoo chalaa aay hot. ab ki main inhen ghaseet laayaa. inake ghar se kaee taar aa chuke the, magar maine inakaaree-jawab dilavaa die. aakhiree taar to arjent tha, jisakee phees chaar aane prati shabda hai, par yahan se unakaa bhi jawab inakaaree hi tha.
donon sajjanon ne meri ora chakit netron se dekh. aatankit ho jaane ki cheshtaa karte jaan pade.
riyaasat alee ne arddhashankaa ke svar mein kahaa—lekin aap bade saade libaas mein rehte hain.
eeshvaree ne shankaa nivaaran ki—mahaatmaa gaandhee ke bhakta hain sahab. khaddar ke sivaa kuchh pahane hi nahin. puraane saare kapade jalaa daale. yon kahaa ki raajaa hain. dhaaee laakh saalaanaa ki riyaasat hai, par aapakee soorat dekho to maaloom hot hai, abhi anaathaalay se pakadakar aaye hain.
raamaharakh bole—ameeron kaa aisaa svabhaav bahut kam dekhne mein aat hai. koi bhaonpa hi nahin sakataa.
riyaasat alee ne samarthan kiya—aapane mahaaraajaa chaongalee ko dekh hot to daonton tale ungalee dabaate. ek gaadhe ki mirjaee aur chamaraundhe joote pahane baajaaron mein ghoomaa karte the. sunate hain, ek baar begaar mein pakade gae the aur unheen ne das laakh se kaalej khol diya.
main man mein kataa jaa raha tha; par na jaane kya baat thi ki yah saphed jhooth us vakta mujhe haasyaaspad na jaan padaa. usake pratyek vaakya ke saath maanon main us kalpit vaibhav ke sameepatar aat jat tha.
main shahasavaar nahin hoon. haan, ladakapan mein kaee baar laddoo ghodon par savaar hua hoon. yahan dekh to do kalon-raas ghode hamaare lie taiyaar khade the. meri to jaan hi nikal gayi. savaar to hua, par botiyon kaonpa raheen theen. maine chehare par shikan na padane diya. ghode ko eeshvaree ke peechhe daal diya. khairiyat yah hui ki eeshvaree ne ghode ko tej na kiya, varanaa shaayad main haath-paonra tudavaakar lautataa. mumakin hai, eeshvaree ne samajh liya ho ki yah kitane paani mein hai.
3
eeshvaree kaa ghar kya tha, kilaa tha. imaamabaade kaa—saa phaatak, dvaar par paharedaar tahalataa hua, naukaron kaa koi sisaab nahin, ek haathee ba. cndhaa hua. eeshvaree ne apne pitaa, chaachaa, taaoo aadi sabase mera parichay karaayaa aur usi atishyokti ke saath. aisee havaa baondhee ika kuchh na poochhie. naukar-chaakar hi nahin, ghar ke log bhi mera sammaan karne lage. dehaat ke jameendaar, laakhon kaa munaaphaa, magar pulis kaansatebil ko aphasar samajhne vaale. kaee mahaashay to mujhe hujoor-hujoor kehne lage!
jab zara ekaanta hua, tau maine eeshvaree se kahaa—tum bade shaitaan ho yaar, meri mittee kyon paleed kar rahe ho?
eeshvaree ne dridh muskaan ke saath kahaa—ina gadhon ke saamane yahi chaal jarooree thi, varanaa seedhe munh bolte bhi nahin.
zara der ke baad naaee hamaare paanv dabaane aay. kunvar log steshan se aaye hain, thak gae honge. eeshvaree ne meri ora ishaaraa karake kahaa—pahale kunvar sahab ke paanv dabaa.
main chaarapaaee par letaa hua tha. mere jeevan mein aisaa shaayad hi kabhi hua ho ki kisee ne mere paanv dabaae hon. main ise ameeron ke chochale, raeeson kaa gadhaapan aur bade aadamiyon ki mutamaradee aur jaane kya-kya kahakar eeshvaree kaa parihaas kiya kart aur aaj main potadon kaa raees banane kaa svaang bhar raha tha.
itane mein das baj gae. puraanee sabhyataa ke log the. nayee roshanee abhi keval pahaad ki chotee tak pahunch paayee thi. andar se khaanaa kaa bulaavaa aay. ham snaan karne chale. main hamenshaa apni dhotee khud chhaant liya kart hoon; magar yahan maine eeshvaree ki hi tarah apni dhotee bhi chhod dee. apne haathon apni dhotee chhaantate sharm aa rahi thi. andar khaanaa karne chale. hostal mein joote pahale mej par jaa datate the. yahan paonva dhonaa aavashyak tha. kahaar paani liye khadaa tha. eeshvaree ne paonva badhaa die. kahaar ne usake paonva dhoe. maine bhi paonva badhaa die. kahaar ne mere paonva bhi dhoe. mera vah soch na jaane kahan chalaa gaya tha.
4
sochaa tha, wahan dehaat mein ekaagr hokar khoob padhenge, par yahan sara din sair-sapaate mein kat jat tha. kaheen nadee mein bajare par sair kar rahe hain, kaheen machhliyon yaa chidiyon kaa shikaar khel rahe hain, kaheen pahalavaanon ki kushtee dekh rahe hain, kaheen shataranj par jamen hain. eeshvaree khoob ande mangavaataa aur kamare mein 'stov' par aamalet banate. naukaron kaa ek jattha hameshaa ghere rehta. apne haanth-paonva hilaane ki koi jaroorat nahin. keval jabaan hilaa dena kaaphee hai. nahaane baitho to aadamee nahalaane ko haajir, leto to aadamee pankhaa jhalane ko khade.
mahaatmaa gaandhee kaa kunvar chelaa mashahoor tha. bheetar se baahar tak meri dhaak thi. naashte mein zara bhi der na hone paae, kaheen kunvar sahab naaraaj na ho jaaen; bichhaavan theek samay par lag jaae, kunvar sahab ke sone kaa samay aa gaya. main eeshvaree se bhi zyada naajuk dimaag ban gaya tha yaa banane par majaboor kiya gaya tha. eeshvaree apne haath se bistar bichhaale lekin kunvar mehamaan apne haathon kaiset apna bichhaavan bichhaa sakate hain! unki mahaanataa mein battaa lag jaaegaa.
ek din sachamuch yahi baat ho gayi. eeshvaree ghar mein tha. shaayad apni maan se kuchh baatacheet karne mein der ho gayi. yahan das baj gae. meri aankhen neend se jhapak rahi theen, magar bistar kaiset lagaaoon? kunvar jo thaharaa. koi saadhe gyaarah baje maharaa aay. bada munh lagaa naukar tha. ghar ke dhandhon mein mera bistar lagaane ki use sudhi hi na rahi. ab jo yaad aaee, to bhaagaa hua aay. maine aisee daonta bataaee ki usane bhi yaad kiya hogaa.
eeshvaree meri daonta sunakar baahar nikal aay aur bol—tumne bahut achchha kiya. yah sab haraamakhor isi vyavahaar ke yogya hain.
isi tarah eeshvaree ek din ek jagah daavat mein gaya hua tha. shaam ho gayi, magar laimpa mej par rakhaa hua tha. diyaasalaaee bhi thi, lekin eeshvaree khud kabhi laimpa nahin jalaataa tha. phir kunvar sahab kaise jalaaen? main jhunjhalaa raha tha. khabar-patr aay rakhaa hua tha. jee udhar lagaa hua tha, par laimpa nadaarad. daivayog se usi vakta munshee riyaasat alee aa nikale. main unheen par ubal padaa, aisee phatakaar bataaee ki bechaaraa ulloo ho gaya— tum logon ko itanee phikr bhi nahin ki laimpa to jalavaa do! maaloom nahin, aise kaamachor aadamiyon kaa yahan kaise gujar hot hai. mere yahan ghante-bhar nirvaah na ho. riyaasat alee ne kaonpate hue haathon se laimpa jalaa diya.
wahan ek thaakur aksar aay kart tha. kuchh manachalaa aadamee tha, mahaatmaa gaandhee kaa param bhakta. mujhe mahaatmaajee kaa chelaa samajhakar mera bada lihaaj kart tha; par mujhse kuchh poochhte sankoch kart tha. ek din mujhe akelaa dekhakar aay aur haath baandhakar bol—sarakaar to gaandhee baabaa ke chele hain na? log kehte hain ki yah suraaj ho jaaegaa to jameendaar na rahenge.
maine shaan jamaaee—jameendaaron ke rehne ki jaroorat hi kya hai? yah log gareebon kaa khoon choosane ke sivaa aur kya karte hai?
thaakur ne pir poochhaa—to kyon, sarakaar, sab jameendaaron ki jameen chheen lee jaaegee. mainnen kahaa-bahut-se log to khushi se de denge. jo log khushi se na denge, unki jameen chheenanee hi padegee. ham log to taiyaar baithe hue hain. jyon hi svaraajya hua, apne ilaake asaamiyon ke naam hibaa kar denge.
main kurasee par paonva latakaae baithaa tha. thaakur mere paonva dabaane lagaa. phir bol—aajakal jameendaar log bada julum karte hain sarakaar! hamen bhi hujoor, apne ilaake mein thodee-see jameen de den, to chalakar vaheen aapakee sevaa mein rahen.
maine kahaa—abhi to mera koi akhtiyaar nahin hai bhai; lekin jyon hi akhtiyaar milaa, main sabase pahale tumhen bulaaoongaa. tumhen motar-draaivaree sikhaa kar apna draaivar banaa loongaa.
sunaa, us din thaakur ne khoob bhang pee aur apni stree ko khoob peetaa aur gaonva mahaajan se ladane par taiyaar ho gaya.
5
chhuttee is tarah tamaam hui aur ham phir prayaag chale. gaonva ke bahut-se log ham logon ko pahunchaane aaye. thaakur to hamaare saath steshan tak aay. mainen bhi apna paart khoob saphaaee se khelaa aur apni kuberochit vinay aur devatva ki muhar harek dil par lagaa dee. jee to chahta tha, harek naukar ko achchha inaam doon, lekin vah saamarthy kahan thi? vaapasee tikat tha hi, keval gaadee mein baithanaa tha; par gaadee gaayee to thasaathas bharee hui. durgaapoojaa ki chhuttiyon bhogakar sabhi log laut rahe the. sekand klaas mein til rakhne ki jagah nahin. intaravyoo klaas ki haalat usse bhi badatar. yah aakhiree gaadee thi. kisee tarah rook na sakate the. badi mushkil se teesare darje mein jagah milee. hamaare aishvary ne wahan apna rang jamaa liya, magar mujhe usamen baithanaa buraa lag raha tha. aaye the aaraam se lete-lete, jaa rahe the sikude hue. pahaloo badalane ki bhi jagah na thi.
kaee aadamee padhe-likhe bhi the! ve aapas mein angrezi raajya ki taareeph karte jaa rahe the. ek mahaashya bole—aisaa nyaay to kisee raajya mein nahin dekh. chhote-bade sab baraabar. raajaa bhi kisee par anyaay kare, to adaalat uski gardan dabaa detee hai.
doosare sajjan ne samarthan kiya—are sahab, aap khud baadashaah par daavaa kar sakate hain. adaalat mein baadashaah par digree ho jati hai.
ek aadamee, jisakee peeth par bada gatthar bandhaa tha, kalakatte jaa raha tha. kaheen gatharee rakhne ki jagah na milatee thi. peeth par baondhe hue tha. isase bechain hokar baar-baar dvaar par khadaa ho jat. main dvaar ke paas hi baithaa hua tha. uska baar-baar aakar mere munh ko apni gatharee se ragadanaa mujhe bahut buraa lag raha tha. ek to havaa yon hi kam thi, doosare us ganvaar kaa aakar mere munh par khadaa ho jan, maano mera galaa dabaanaa tha. main kuchh der tak jabta kie baithaa raha. ekaaek mujhe gussaa aa gaya. maine use pakadakar peechhe thel diya aur do tamaache jor-jor se lagaae.
usanen aankhen nikaalakar kahaa—kyon maarate ho baaboojee, hamne bhi kiraayaa diya hai!
maine uthakar do-teen tamaache aur jad die.
gaadee mein toophaan aa gaya. chaaron ora se mujh par bauchhaar padane lagee.
'agar itane naajuk mijaaj ho, to avval darje mein kyon nahin baithe.'
'koi bada aadamee hogaa, to apne ghar kaa hogaa. mujhe is tarah maarate to dikhaa detaa.'
'kya kasoor kiya tha bechaare ne. gaadee mein saans lene ki jagah nahin, khidakee par zara saonsa lene khadaa ho gaya, to us par itanaa gussaa! ameer hokar kya aadamee apni insaaniyat bilkul kho detaa hai.
'yah bhi angrezi raaj hai, jisakaa aap bakhaan kar rahe the.'
ek graameen bol—daphtar mao ghus paavat nahin, us pai ittaa mijaaj.
eeshvaree ne angrezi me kahaa- What an idiot you are, Bir!
aur mera nashaa ab kuchh-kuchh utarataa hua maaloom hot tha.
