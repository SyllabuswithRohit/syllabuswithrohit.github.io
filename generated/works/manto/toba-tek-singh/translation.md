# Toba Tek Singh

**Saadat Hasan Manto**

batware ke do teen saal baad pakistan aur Hindustan ki hukumaton ko khayal aaya ki akhlaqi qaidiyon ki tarah paaglon ka tabadlah bhi hona chahiye yauni jo musliman paagal, Hindustan ke paagal khaanon mein hain anhein pakistan pahuncha diya jaaye aur jo hindo aur sikh, pakistan ke paagal khaanon mein hain anhein Hindustan ke hawalay kardiya jaaye.

maloom nahin yeh baat maqool thi yaa ghair maqool, behar haal daanish mandon ke faislay ke mutabiq adhar adhar oonchi satah ki conferencein hoyein aur balaakhir ek din paaglon ke tabadle ke liye muqarar hogiya. achchhi tarah chhaan bayne ki gayee. woh musliman paagal jinn ke lawahaqeen Hindustan hi mein thay, wahein rehney diye gaye thay. jo baaqi thay, un ko sarhad parr rawanah kardiya gayaa. yahan pakistan mein chonkah qareeb qareeb tamaam hindo, sikh jaa chakay the asee liye kasee ko rakhnay rakhanay ka sawal hi naha paida hawaa. jitnay hindo, sikh paagal the sab ke sab police ki hifazat mein barder parr pahuncha diye gayey.

adhar ka maloom nahein, lekin adhar lahoor ke paagal khaaney mein jab is tabadle ki khabar pahunchi tuu badi dilchasp chah megoyiyan honay lagein. ek musliman paagal jo baarah burse se harr roz baqayedgi ke saath zamindar padhta thaa, is se jab is ke ek dost ne poocha, molabi saab! yeh pakistan kya hotaa hay? tuu is ne baday ghoor woo fikr ke baad jawab diyaa, Hindustan mein ek aisi jagah hai jehaan ustaray bantay haine. yeh jawab sunn kar is ka dost mutmain hogiya.

asee tarah ek sikh paagal ne ek dosray sikh paagal se poocha, sardaar jee humein Hindustan kyon bheja raha hay... humein tuu wahan ki boli nahin aatee.

dosra maskaraya, mujhe tuu hindustudon ki boli aatee hay... hindustani baday shaitani, akad akad phirtay haine.

ek din nahatay nahatay ek musliman paagal nay''pakistan zindah baad ka naarah is zoor se buland kya ki farsh parr phisal kar giraa aur bay hosh hogiya.

baaz paagal aisey bhi the jo paagal nahin thay. un mein aksariyat aisey qatilon ki thi jinn ke rishtah daaron ne afsaron ko day dalaa kirr, paagal khaaney bhajwa diya tha ki phansi ke phanday se bach jayein. yeh kuchh kuchh samajhte the ki Hindustan kyon taqsim hawaa hai aur yeh pakistan kya hay. lekin saheeh waaqiaat se woh bhi khabar thay. akhbaron se kuchh pataa nahin chalta tha aur pehrah daar spahi un padh aur jaahil thay. un ki guftagoon se bhi woh koi natijah baraamad nahin karsakte thay. un ko sarf atnaa maloom tha ki ek aadmi mohammad alee jinaah hai jiss ko qaidaazam kehte haine. is ne muslimanon ke liye ek alaihdah milk banaya hai jiss ka naam pakistan hay... yeh kahan hay, is ka mahal waqoo kya hay, is ke mutaliq woh kuchh nahin jaantay thay. yahee wajah hai ki paagal khaaney mein woh sab paagal jinn ka dimagh poori tarah maaof nahin hawaa thaa, is mukhsame mein giraftar the ki woh pakistan mein hain yaa Hindustan mein... agrahindustan mein hain tuu pakistan kahan hai! agar woh pakistan mein hain tuu yeh kisay hosakta hai ki woh kuchh arsey pehley yahein rehtay hoay bhi Hindustan mein the!

ek paagal tuu pakistan aur Hindustan, aur Hindustan aur pakistan ke chakar mein kuchh aisa giraftar hawaa ki aur zyada paagal hogiya. jhaado deetay ek din darkhat parr chadh gaya aur tehni parr beeth kar do ghantay muslisil taqrir karta raha jo pakistan aur Hindustan ke naazik masilay parr thee. spahiyon ne use neechey utarnay ko kaha towah aur oopar chadh gayaa. draya dhamkaya gaya tuu is ne kahaa, mein Hindustan mein rehna chahta hoon naha pakistan mein... mein is darkhat parr hi rehon gaa.

badi mashkalon ke baad jab is ka dorah sard padaa tuu woh neechey utraa aur apne hindo sikh doston se gulay mill mill kar ronay lagaa. is khayal se is ka dil bhar aaya tha ki woh use chhodkar Hindustan chale jayein gay.

ek ayem. aiss. see. paas redio engineer mein, jo musliman tha aur dosray paaglon se balkal alg thalag, baagh ki ek khaas rosh parr, saaraa din khamush tehalta rehta thaa, yeh tabdili namodar hoyee ki is ne tamaam kapday ataar kar dafadar ke hawalay kardiye aur nung dhadang saaray baagh mein chalna phirna sharoo kardiya.

chaniyot ke ek motay musliman paagal ne jo muslim leeg ka srgarm kaarkan rahh chakaa tha aur din mein pandrah solah martabah nahaya karta thaa, yakk lakht yeh aadat turk kardi. is ka naam mohammad alee thaa. chunancha is ne ek din apne janglay mein aalaan kardiya ki woh qaidaazam mohammad alee jinaah hay. is ki dekha dekhi ek sikh paagal maaster taaraa sangh bunn gayaa. qareeb tha ki is janglay mein khoon kharabah how jaaye magar donon ko khatarnaak paagal qaraar day kar alaihdah alaihdah bund kardiya gayaa.

lahoor ka ek naujawan hindo wakeel tha jo mohabbat mein nakaam how kar paagal hogiya thaa. jab is ne suna ki amritsar Hindustan mein chala gaya hai tuu use bahut dikh hawaa. asee shehar ki ek hindo ladki se use mohabbat hoyee thee. gow is ne is wakeel ko thukra diya thaa, magar dewangi ki haalat mein bhi woh is ko nahin bhula thaa. chunancha woh un tamaam hindo aur muslim leaders ko gaaliyan deeta tha jinhon ne mill milaa kar Hindustan ke do takday kardiye... is ki mahbubah hindustani bunn gayi aur woh pakistani.

jab tabadle ki baat sharoo hoyee tuu wakeel ko kayee paaglon ne samjhaya ki woh dil bura naha karay, is kohindustan bhej diya jaaye gaa. is Hindustan mein jehaan is ki mahbubah rehti hay. magar woh lahoor chhodna nahin chahta tha is liye ki is ka khayal tha ki amritsar mein is ki practs nahin chale gee.

europen waard mein anglo indeen paagal thay. un ko jab maloom hawaa ki Hindustan ko aazaad karkay angrez chale gaye hain tuu un ko bahut sadmah hawaa. woh chhap chhap kar ghanton aaps mein is aham masilay parr guftagu karte rehtay ki paagal khaaney mein ab un ki haisiyat kiss qasm ki hogi. europen waard rahe gaa yaa adaa diya jaaye gaa. break faast milaa karay gaa yaa nahein. kya anhein double roty ke bajaye buildy indeen chapati tuu zahar maar nahin karna paray gee.

ek sikh tha jiss ko paagal khaaney mein daakhil hoay pandrah burse how chakay thay. harr waqt is ki zabaan se yeh ajeeb waghareeb alfaz sunnay mein aatay thay. opaddi gargardi aneks di bay dhiyanadi mung di daal aff di laltin. din ko sotaa tha naha raat kuu. pahridaron ka yeh kehna tha ki pandrah burse ke taweel arsey mein woh ek lahzay ke liye bhi nahin soyaa. leeta bhi nahin thaa. albatah kabhi kabhi kasee dewaar ke saath teek lagaa leeta thaa.

harr waqt khadaa rehney se is ke paaon sooj gaye thay. pandaliyan bhi phul gayi thein, magar is jasmani taklif ke bawajood lait kar aaraam nahin karta thaa. Hindustan, pakistan aur paaglon ke tabadle ke mutaliq jab kabhi paagal khaaney mein guftagu hoti thi woh ghoor se santaa thaa. koi is se poochta tha ki is ka kya khayal hai tuu woh badi sanjeedgi se jawab deeta, opaddi garh garh di aneks di bay dhiyana di mung di waal aaf di pakistan government.

lekin baad mein aaf di pakistan government ki jagah aaf di tobah teek sangh government ne lay li aur is ne dosray paaglon se poochna sharoo kya ki tobah teek sangh kahan hai jehaan ka woh rehney waala hay. lekin kasee ko bhi maloom nahin tha ki woh pakistan mein hai yaa Hindustan mein. jo batanay ki koshish karte thay, khood is aljhao mein giraftar how jaatay the ki siyalkot pehley Hindustan mein hotaa tha parr ab suna hai ki pakistan mein hay, kya patah hai ki lahoor jo ab pakistan mein hai kill Hindustan mein chala jaaye. yaa saaraa Hindustan hi pakistan bunn jaaye aur yeh bhi kaun seenay parr haath rakh kar kahah saktaa tha ki Hindustan aur pakistan donon kasee din saray se ghaib hi how jayein.

is sikh paagal ke kees chhadray how kar bahut mukhtasir rahh gaye thay. chonkah bahut kam nahata thaa, is liye daadhi aur sarr ke baal aaps mein jumm gaye thay, jiss ke baais is ki shakal badi bhayanak hogayi thee. magar aadmi bay zarar thaa. pandrah barson mein is ne kabhi kasee se jhagda fasaad nahin kya thaa. paagal khaaney ke jo paranay mulazim thay, woh is ke mutaliq jaantay the ki tobah teek sangh mein is ki kayee zaminein thein. achchha khaata peeta zamindar tha ki achanak dimagh alt gayaa.

is ke rishtah daar lohay ki motee motee zanjiron mein use baandh kar laa eyey aur paagal khaaney mein daakhil kiraa gayey. mahinay mein ek barmulaqat ke liye yeh loag aatay the aur is ki khair khairiyat driyafat karkay chale jaatay thay. ek madat takk yeh silsilah jaari rehaa. parr jab pakistan, Hindustan ki garbad sharoo hoyee tuu un ka aana bund hogiya.

is ka naam bashan sangh tha magar use tobah teek sangh kehte thay. is ko yeh qataan maloom nahin tha ki din kaun saa hay, mahinah kaun saa hai yaa katnay saal beeth chakay haine. lekin harr mahinay jab is ke azeez woo aqaarib is se milnay ke liye aatay the tuu use apne aap patah chull jaataa thaa. chunancha woh dafadar se kehta ki is ki mulaqat aarahi hay. is din woh achchhi tarah nahata, badan parr khoob saabin ghasta aur sarr mein teel lagaa kar kangha kartaa, apne kapday jo woh kabhi istemaal nahin karta thaa, nikalwa ke pehanta aur yuon sajj kar milnay waalon ke paas jaataa. woh is se kuchh poochtay tuu woh khamush rehta yaa kabhi kabhaar oopar di gargad di aneks di bay dhiyana di mung di waal aaf di laltin kahah deeta.

is ki ek ladki thi johar mahinay ek angli badhti badhti pandrah barson mein jawaan hogayi thee.bashan sangh is ko pehchanta hi nahin thaa. woh bachee thi jab bhi apne baap ko deekh kar rotee thee, jawaan hoyee tab bhi is ki aankhon se aanso behtay thay.

pakistan aur Hindustan ka qasah sharoo hawaa tuu is ne dosray paaglon se poochna sharoo kya ki tobah teek sangh kahan hay. jab atminan bakhash jawab naha milaa tuu is ki kareed din badan badhti gayee. ab mulaqat bhi nahin aatee thee. pehley tuu use apne aap patah chull jaataa tha ki milnay waalay aarhay haine, parr ab jeesay is ke dil ki aawaz bhi bund hogayi thi jo use un ki aamad ki khabar day diya karti thee.

is ki badi khwahish thi ki woh loag aayein jo is se humdardi ka izhaar karte the aur is ke liye phal, mithayiyan aur kapday laatay thay. woh agar un se poochta ki tobah teek sangh kahan hay? tuu woh use yaqeenan bataa deetay ki pakistan mein hai yaa Hindustan mein keunkah is ka khayal tha ki woh tobah teek sangh hi se aatay haine, jehaan is ki zaminein haine.

paagal khaaney mein ek paagal aisa bhi tha jo khood ko Khuda kehta thaa. is se jab ek roz bashan sangh ne poochha ki tobah teek sangh pakistan mein hai yaa Hindustan mein tuu is ne hasb aadat qahqaha lagaya aur kahaa, woh pakistan mein hai naha Hindustan mein. is liye ki hum ne ubhee takk hakam nahin diyaa.

bashan sangh ne is Khuda se kayee martabah manat samajat se kaha ki woh hakam deday taakah jhanjhat khatam how, magar woh bahut masruf tha is liye ki use aur bay shamaar hakam deenay thay. ek din tung aakar woh is parr burse padaa, opad di garh garh di aneks di bay dhiyana di mung di daal aaf waahay goroji daa khaalisa aind waahay goroji ki fatah... jo bolay sonhaal, sath saree akaal.

is ka shayed yeh matlab tha ki tum musliman ke Khuda how... sikhon ke Khuda hotay tuu zaroor meri santay.

tabadle se kuchh din pehley tobah teek sangh ka ek musliman jo is ka dost thaa, mulaqat ke liye aayaa. pehley woh kabhi nahin aaya thaa. jab bashan sangh ne use dekha tuu ek taraf hutt gaya aur waps jaanay lagaa, magar spahiyon ne use rooka, yeh tum se milnay aaya hay... tumhara dost fazal deen hay.

bashan sangh ne fazal deen ko ek nazar dekha aur kuchh badbadanay lagaa. fazal deen ne aagay badh kar is ke kandhay parr haath rakhaa, mein bahut dunon se soch raha tha ki tum se milon lekin farsat hi naha milly... tumharay sab aadmi khairiyat se Hindustan chale gayey... majh se jitni madad hosaki, mein ne kii... tumhari betty ruup coor... woh kuchh kehte kehte rook gayaa. bashan sangh kuchh yaad karnay lagaa. betty ruup coor! fazal deen ne rakk rakk kar kahaa, haan... wah... woh bhi theek thaak hay... un ke saath hi chali gayee.

bashan sangh khamush rehaa. fazal deen ne kehna sharoo kiyaa, unhon ne majh se kaha tha ki tumhari khair khairiyat poochta rehon... ab mein ne suna hai ki tum Hindustan jaarhay how... bhai balbeer sangh aur bhai wadhawa sangh se mera salaam kehna... aur behen amrat coor se bhee... bhai balbeer se kehna fazal deen raazi khushi hay... do bhuri bhensein jo woh chhod gaye thay, un mein se ek ne kataa diya hay... aur dosri ke cutty hoyee thi parr woh chh din ki how ke marr gayee... aur...miray laiq jo khadmat how kehna, mein harr waqt tayaar hoan... aur yeh tumharay liye thoday se maronday laaya hoan.

bashan sangh ne marondon ki potli lay kar paas khaday spahi ke hawalay kardi aur fazal deen se poocha, tobah teek sangh kahan hay? fazal deen ne qadray hairat se kahaa, kahan hay...wahein hai jehaan thaa. bashan sangh ne phir poocha, pakistan mein yaa Hindustan mein?

Hindustan mein...nahein nahein, pakistan mein. fazal deen bokhla saa gayaa.

bashan sangh badbadata hawaa chala gayaa.''opad di garh garh di aneks di bay dhiyana di mung di daal aaf pakistan aind Hindustan aaf di daraftay manah.

tabadle ki tayariyan makmal how chaki thein. adhar se adhar aur adhar se adhar aaney waalay paaglon ki fehristein pahunch gayi thein aur tabadle ka din bhi muqarar how chakaa thaa. sakht sardiyan thein, jab lahoor ke paagal khaaney se hindo, sikh paaglon se bharee hoyee laariyan police ke muhafiz daad satay ke saath rawanah hoyein. mutaliqah afsar bhi humrah thay. wahgah ke barder parr tarfin ke sprintendent ek dosray se milay aur ibtadayi karwayi khatam honay ke baad tabadlah sharoo how gaya jo raat bhar jaari rehaa.

paaglon ko laariyon se nikalna aur un ko dosray afsaron ke hawalay karna bada kathan kaam thaa. baaz tuu baahir nikaltay hi nahin thay. jo nikalnay pararza mund hoay thay, un ko sanbhalna mashkal how jaataa tha kyon ki adhar adhar bhaag uthtay thay, jo nangay thay, un ko kapday pehnaye jaatay woh phaad kar apne tunn se jadaa kar deetay. koi gaaliyan buck raha hay. koi gaa raha hay. aaps mein larh jhagad rahe haine. ruu rahe haine, bluck rahe haine. kaan padee aawaz sanayi nahin deeti thee. paagal auraton ka shoar woo ghogha alg tha aur sardi atnee kadaake ki thi ki daanat se daanat bujj rahe thay.

paaglon ki aksariyat is tabadle ke haqh mein nahin thee, is liye ki un ki samajh mein nahin aataa tha ki anhein apni jagah se akhaad kar kahan phainka jaarha hay. woh chand jo kuchh soch samajh saktay thay.'pakistan zindah baad aur pakistan mardah baad ke naaray lagaa rahe thay. do teen martabah fasaad hotay hotay bachaa kyon ki baaz muslimanon aur sikhon ko yeh naarah sunn kar taish aagiya thaa.

jab bashan sangh ki baari aayi aur wahgah ke is paar mutaliqah afsar is ka naam register mein darj karnay lagaa tuu is ne poocha, tobah teek sangh kahan hay... pakistan mein yaa Hindustan mein?

mutaliqah afsar hansa, pakistan mein.

yeh sunn kar bashan sangh achhal kar ek taraf hataa aur dodh kar apne baaqi maandah saathiyon ke paas pahunch gayaa.

pakistani spahiyon ne use pakad liya aur dosri taraf lay jaanay lagay, magar is ne chalnay se ankaar kardiya, tobah teek sangh yahan hay... aur zoor zoor se chalanay lagaa, opad di garh garh di aneks di bay dhiyana di mung di daal aaf tobah teek sangh aind pakistan.

use bahut samjhaya gaya ki dekho ab tobah teek sangh Hindustan mein chala gaya hay... agar nahin gaya tuu use foraan wahan bhej diya jaaye gaa, magar woh naha maanaa. jab is ko zabardasti dosri taraf lay jaanay ki koshish ki gayi tuu woh darmiyan mein ek jagah is andaaz mein apni soji hoyee taangon parr khadaa hogiya jeesay ab use koi taaqat wahan se nahin halaske gee.

aadmi chonkah bay zarar tha is liye is se mazeed zabardasti naha ki gayee. is ko wahein khadaa rehney diya gaya aur tabadle ka baaqi kaam hotaa rehaa.

soraj nikalnay se pehley saakat wasamat bashan sangh ke halq se ek fluck shagaaf cheekh nikli. adhar adhar se kayee afsar doday aaye aur dekha ki woh aadmi jo pandrah burse takk din raat apni taangon parr khadaa raha thaa, aundhay manah leeta hay. adhar khaar daar taaron ke peeche Hindustan thaa... adhar waisay hi taaron ke peeche pakistan. darmiyan mein zameen ke is takday parr jiss ka koi naam nahin thaa, tobah teek sangh padaa thaa.
