# Khol Do

**Saadat Hasan Manto**

amritasara se sshapeshala trena dopahara do baje ko chalee aur aatha ghanton ke baada mugalapuraa pahunchee| raaste men kaee aadmi maare gae| kaee ghaayala hue aur kuchha idhara udhara bhataka gae| subaha dasa baje kainpa kee thandee zameen par jab siraajuddeena ne aankhen kholeen aur apane chaaron tarapha mardon, auraton aur bachchon kaa ek mutalaatima samundara dekha to usakee sochane samajhane kee taakata aur bhee kamazora ho gaeen| vo dera taka gadale aasamaana ko takatakee baandhe dekhata raha| yoon to kainpa men hara tarapha shora machaa tha| lekin boodhe siraajuddeena ke kaana jaise banda the| use kuchha sunaaee naheen detaa tha| koee use dekhata to ye khayal karata ki vo kisee gaharee phikra men garka hai magar aisaa naheen tha| usake hosha shala the| usakaa saaraa pooraa badana aur mann khaaleepana men latakaa tha| gadale aasamaana kee tarapha binaa kisee iraade ke dekhate dekhate siraajuddeena kee nigaahen sooraja se takaraaeen| teja roshanee usake pooraa badana aur mann ke poore badana men utara gaee aur vo jaaga uthaa| oopara tale usake dimaaga par kaee tasveeren dauda gaeen| loota, aaga... bhaagama bhaaga... steshana... goliyaan... raat aur sakeena... siraajuddeena ekadama utha khadaa hua aur paagalon kee tarah usane apane chaaron tarapha phaile hue insaanon ke samundara ko khangaalana shuroo kiya| poore teena ghante vo sakeenaa sakeenaa pukaarata kainpa men bhatakata raha| magar use apanee javaana ikalautee betee kaa koee pata na milaa| chaaron tarapha ek dhaandalee see machee thi| koee apana bachcha dhoondha raha tha, koee maan| koee beevee aur koee betee| siraajuddeena thaka haara kar ek tarapha baitha gaya aur yaadadaashta par jora de kar sochane lagaa ki sakeenaa usase kab aur kahaan judaa hui| lekin sochate sochate usakaa dimaaga sakeenaa kee maan kee laasha par jama jata jisakee saaree antadiyaan baahara nikalee hui theen| isase aage vo aur kuchha na socha sakata| sakeenaa kee maan mara chukee thi| usane siraajuddeena kee aankhon ke saamane dama todaa tha| lekin sakeenaa kahaan thi jisake ke baare men usakee maan ne marate hue kaha tha, mujhe chhodo aur sakeenaa ko lekara jaldee yahaan se bhaaga jaao| sakeenaa usake saatha hee thi| donon nange paanva bhaaga rahe the| sakeenaa kaa dupattaa gira padaa tha| use uthaane ke lie usane rukana chaahaa tha magar sakeenaa ne chillaa kar kaha tha, abbaa jee... chhodie| lekin usane dupattaa uthaa liya tha... ye sochate-sochate usane apane kota kee ubharee hui jeba kee tarapha dekha aur usamen haath daala kar ek kapadaa nikaala... sakeenaa kaa vahee dupattaa tha... lekin sakeenaa kahaan thi? siraajuddeena ne apane thake hue dimaaga par bahut jora diya magar vo kisee nateeje par na pahuncha sakaa| kyaa vo sakeenaa ko apane saatha steshana taka le aaya tha? kyaa vo usake saatha hee gaadee men savaara thi? raaste men jab gaadee rokee gaee thi aur dangaaee andara ghusa aae the to kyaa vo behosha hogayaa tha jo vo sakeenaa ko uthaa karale gae? siraajuddeena ke dimaaga men savaala hee savaala the, javaaba koee bhee naheen tha| usako hamadardee kee jaroorata thi| lekin chaaron tarapha jitane bhee insaana phaile hue the sabako hamadardee kee jaroorata thi| siraajuddeena ne ronaa chaahaa magar aankhon ne usakee madada na kee| aansoo jaane kahaan gaayaba ho gae the| chhah roja ke baada jab hosha kisee kadara durusta hue to siraajuddeena una logon se milaa jo usakee madada karane ke lie taiyaara the| aatha naujavaana the, jinake paasa laaree thi, bandooken theen| siraajuddeena ne unako laakha laakha duaaen deen aur sakeenaa kaa shakla aur pahachaana bataaya, “goraa ranga hai aur bahut hee khoobasoorata hai... mujha par naheen apanee maan par thi... umra satraha barasa ke kareeba hai... aankhen badee badee... baala kaale, daahine gaala par motaa saa tila... meree ikalautee ladki hai| dhoondha laao, tumhaaraa khudaa bhalaa karegaa|” madada karane vaale naujavaanon ne bade josha ke saatha boodhe siraajuddeena ko yakeena dilaayaa ki agar usakee betee jindaa hui to chanda hee dinon men usake paasa hogee| aathon naujavaana ne koshisha kee| jaana hatheliyon par rakha kar vo amritasara gae| kaee auraton, kaee mardon aur kaee bachchon ko nikaala nikaala kar unhonne surakshita jagahon par pahunchaayaa| dasa roja gujara gae magar unhen sakeenaa kaheen na milee| ek roja vo usee madada ke lie laaree par amritasara jaa rahe the ki chhah harata ke paasa sadaka par unhen ek ladki dikhaaee dee| laaree kee aavaaja suna kar vaha bidakee aur bhaagana shuroo kar diya| madada karane vaaleon ne motara rokee aur sabake sab usake peechhe bhaage| ek kheta men unhonne ladki ko pakada liya| dekha to bahut khoobasoorata thi| daahine gaala par motaa tila tha| ek ladke ne usase kaha, “ghabaraao naheen... kyaa tumhaaraa naama sakeenaa hai?” ladki kaa ranga aur bhee peelaa hogayaa| usane koee javaaba na diya, lekin jab saare ladakon ne use dama dilaasaa diya to usakee dara doora hui aur usane maana liya ki vo siraajuddeena kee betee sakeenaa hai| aatha madada karane vaale naujavaanon ne hara tarah sakeenaa kee dekhabhaala kee| use khaanaa khilaaya, doodha pilaayaa aur laaree men bithaa diya| ek ne apana kota utaara kar use de diya kyunki dupattaa na hone ke baaisa vo bahut ulajhana mahasoosa kar rahi thi aur baara baara baanhon se apane seene ko dhaankane kee naakaama koshisha men vyasta thi| kaee din gujara gae... siraajuddeena ko sakeenaa kee koee khabara na milee| vo din bhara alaga-alaga kaimpon aur daphtaron ke chakkara kaatata rahata| lekin kaheen se bhee usakee betee kaa pata na chalaa| raat ko vo bahut dera taka una madada karane vaale naujavaanon kee kaamayaabee ke lie duaaen maangata rahata| jinhonne usako yakeena dilaayaa tha ki agar sakeenaa jindaa hui to chanda dinon hee men vo use dhoondha nikaalenge| ek roja siraajuddeena ne kainpa men una naujavaana madada karane vaaleon ko dekha, laaree men baithe the| siraajuddeena bhaagaa bhaagaa unake paasa gaya| laaree chalane hee vaalee thi ki usane poochha, “beta, meree sakeenaa kaa pata chala?” sab ne ek saatha ho kar kaha, “chala jaaega, chala jaaegaa|” aur laaree chalaa dee| siraajuddeena ne ek baara phir una naujavaanon kee kaamayaabee ke lie duaa maangee aur usakaa jee kisee kadara halkaa hogayaa| shaama ke kareeba kainpa men jahaan siraajuddeena baithaa tha| usake paasa hee kuchha gadabada see hui| chaara aadmi kuchha uthaa kar laa rahe the| usane poochha kiya to maalooma hua ki ek ladki relave laaina ke paasa behosha padee thi| log use uthaa kar laae hain| siraajuddeena unake peechhe peechhe ho liya| logon ne ladki ko haspataala vaalon ke havaale kiya aur chale gae| kuchha dera vo aise hee haspataala ke baahara gadhe hue lakadee ke khanbe ke saatha laga kar khadaa raha| phir dheere dheere andara chalaa gaya| kamare men koee bhee naheen tha| ek strechara tha jisa par ek laasha padee thi| siraajuddeena chhote chhote kadama uthaataa usakee tarapha badhaa| kamare men achaanaka roshanee hui| siraajuddeena ne laasha ke peelaa chehare par chamakata hua tila dekha aur chillaaya, “sakeena!” daaktara ne jisane kamare men roshanee kee thi siraajuddeena se poochha, “kyaa hai?” siraajuddeena ke galaa se sirpha is kadara nikala saka, “jee main... jee main... isakaa baapa hoon!” daaktara ne strechara par padee hui laasha kee tarapha dekha| usakee nabja tatolee aur siraajuddeena se kaha, “khidakee khola do|” sakeenaa ke murdaa badana men harakata paidaa hui| bejaana haathon se usane shalavaara kaa na.daa kholaa aur shalavaara neeche sarakaa dee| boodhaa siraajuddeena khushi se chillaaya, “jindaa hai... meree betee jindaa hai...” daaktara sara se pair taka paseene men garka ho gaya|
