# Thanda Gosht

**Saadat Hasan Manto**

eeshara sinha jab ek ladki par haath saapha karane ke iraade se use uthaa kar laataa hai to dekhata hai ki vo mara chukee hai| is shadeeda sadame se vo apanee mardaanagee kho baithata hai| ladki ko vaheen chhodakara vo hotala men apanee daashtaa kulavanta ke paasa jata hai| kulavanta ke laakha koshisha ke baapooraa badana aur mann vo khuda ko jinsee taura par taiyaara naheen kar paataa| eeshara sinha joonhee hotala ke kamare men andara hua, kulavanta kaura palanga par se uthee| apanee teja teja aankhon se usakee tarapha ghoora ke dekha aur daravaaje kee chatakhnee banda kar dee| raat ke baaraha baja chuke the, shehar kaa mujaaphaata ek ajeeba pura-rahasya chupee men garka tha| kulavanta kaura palanga par aalatee paalatee maara kar baitha gaee| eeshara sinha jo gaalibana apane paraagandaa khayaalaata ke ulajhe hue dhaage khola raha, haath men kripaana liye ek kone men khadaa tha| chanda lamhaata isee tarah chupee men gujara gae| kulavanta kaura ko thodee dera ke baada apana aasana pasanda na aaya, aur vo donon taangen palanga se neeche latakaa kar hilaane lagee| eeshara sinha phir bhee kuchha na bola| kulavanta kaura bhare bhare haath pairon vaalee aurat thi| chaude chakale koolhe, thala-thala karane vaale goshta se bharapoora kuchha bahut hee zyada oopara ko uthaa hua seena, teja aankhen| baalaaee honta par baalon kaa suramaee gubaara, thodhee kee saakhta se pata chalata tha ki bade dhadalle kee aurat hai| eeshara sinha go sara nevadhaae ek kone men chupachaapa khadaa tha| sara par usakee kasa kar baandhee hui pagadee dheelee horahee thi| usake haath jo kripaana thaame hue the, thode thode kaanpatee the, magar usake kada-o-kaamata aur khadda-o-khaala se pata chalata tha ki kulavanta kaura jaisee aurat ke lie maujoon tareena marda hai| chanda aur lamahaata jab isee tarah chupee se gujara gae to kulavanta kaura chhalaka padee, lekin teja teja aankhon ko bachaa kar vo sirpha is kadara kaha sakee, “eeshara sayyaan|” eeshara sinha ne gardana uthaa kar kulavanta kaura kee tarapha dekha, magar usakee nigaahon kee goliyon kee taaba na laa kar munha doosaree tarapha moda liya| kulavanta kaura chillaaee, “eeshara sayyaan|” lekin phaurana hee aavaaja bheencha lee aur palanga par se uthakara usakee jaaniba jaate hue boli, “kahaan rahe tuma itane din?” eeshara sinha ne khushka honton par jabaana pheree, “mujhe maalooma naheen|” kulavanta kaura bhannaa gaee, “ye bhee koee maan yaa javaaba hai?” eeshara sinha ne kripaana ek tarapha phenka dee aur palanga par leta gaya| aisaa maalooma hota tha ki vo kaee dinon kaa beemaara hai| kulavanta kaura ne palanga kee tarapha dekha, jo ab eeshara sinha se labaalaba bharaa tha| usake dil men hamadardee kaa jajbaa paidaa ho gaya| isalie usake maathe par haath rakha kar usane bade pyaar se poochha, “jaanee kyaa hua hai tumhen?” eeshara sinha chhata kee tarapha dekha raha tha, usase nigaahen hata kar usane kulavanta kaura ke maanoosa chehare ko tatolana shuroo kiya, “kulavanta!” aavaaja men darda tha| kulavanta kaura saaree kee saaree simata kar apane baalaaee honta men aagaee, “haan jaanee,” kaha kar vo usako daanton se kaatane lagee| eeshara sinha ne pagadee utaara dee| kulavanta kaura kee tarapha sahaaraa lene vaalee nigaahon se dekha, usake goshta bhare koolhe par jora se dhappaa maaraa aur sara ko jhatakaa de kar apane aapa se kaha, “ye kudee kaa dimaaga hee kharaaba hai|” jhatakaa dene se usake kesa khula gae| kulavanta kaura ungaliyon se unamen kanghee karane lagee| aisaa karte hue usane bade pyaar se poochha, “eeshara sayyaan, kahaan rahe tuma itane din?” “bure kee maan ke ghar|” eeshara sinha ne kulavanta kaura ko ghoora ke dekha aur achaanaka donon haathon se usake ubhare hue seene ko masalane laga, “kasama vaahaguru kee badee jaanadaara aurat hai|” kulavanta kaura ne ek adaa ke saatha eeshara sinha ke haath ek tarapha jhataka die aur poochha, “tumhen meree kasama bataao, kahaan rahe?... shehar gae the?” eeshara sinha ne ek hee lapeta men apane baalon kaa joodaa banaate hue javaaba diya, “naheen|” kulavanta kaura chida gaee, “naheen tuma jaroora shehar gae the... aur tumane bahut saa rupaya lootaa hai jo mujha se chhupaa rahe ho|” “vo apane baapa kaa tukhma na ho jo tuma se jhoota bole|” kulavanta kaura thodee dera ke lie chupa hogaee, lekin phaurana hee bhadaka uthee| “lekin meree samajha men naheen aata, us raat tumhen kyaa hua?... achchhe bhale mere saatha lete the, mujhe tumane vo saare gahane pahana rakhe the jo tuma shehar se loota kar laae the| meree bhapiyaan le rahe the, par jaane ek dama tumhen kyaa hua, uthe aur kapade pahana kar baahara nikala gae|” eeshara sinha kaa ranga peelaa hogayaa| kulavanta kaura ne ye tabdeelee dekhate hee kaha, “dekha kaise ranga neelaa pada gaya... eeshara sayyaan, kasama vaahaguru kee, jaroora kuchha daala men kaalaa hai?” “teree jaana kee kasama, kuchha bhee naheen|” eeshara sinha kee aavaaja bejaana thi| kulavanta kaura kaa shaka aur zyada majaboota hogaya, baalaaee honta bheencha kar usane ek ek shabda par jora dete hue kaha, “eeshara sayyaan, kyaa baat hai| tuma vo naheen ho jo aaja se aatha roja pahale the?” eeshara sinha ek dama utha baitha, jaise kisee ne us par hamalaa kiya tha| kulavanta kaura ko apane tanomanda baajooon men sameta kar usane pooree kuvvata ke saatha use bhanbhodana shuroo kar diya| “jaanee main vahee hoon... ghata ghata paa japhiyaan, teree nikale hadaan dee garmee...” kulavanta kaura ne virodha na kee, lekin vo shikaayata karti rahi, “tumhen us raat ho kyaa gaya tha?” “bure kee maan kaa vo hogayaa tha|” “bataaoge naheen?” “koee baat ho to bataaoon|” “mujhe apane haathon se jalaao agar jhoota bolo|” eeshara sinha ne apane baajoo usakee gardana men daala die aur honta usake honton men gaada die| moonchhon ke baala kulavanta kaura ke nathanon men ghuse to use chheenka aagaee| donon hansane lage| eeshara sinha ne apanee sadaree utaara dee aur kulavanta kaura ko shahavata bharee najaron se dekha kar kaha, “aa jaao, ek baajee taasha kee ho jaye!” kulavanta kaura ke baalaaee honta par paseene kee nanhee nanhee boonden phoota aaeen, ek adaa ke saatha usane apanee aankhon kee putaliyaan ghumaaeen aur kaha, “chala daphaana ho|” eeshara sinha ne usake bhare hue koolhe par jora se chutakee bharee| kulavanta kaura tadapa kar ek tarapha hata gaee| “na kar eeshara sayyaan, mere darda hota hai|” eeshara sinha ne aage badha kar kulavanta kaura kaa baalaaee honta apane daanton tale dabaa liya aur kichakichaane lagaa| kulavanta kaura bilkula pighala gaee| eeshara sinha ne apana kurata utaara ke phenka diya aur kaha, “lo, phir ho jaye turupa chaala...” kulavanta kaura kaa baalaaee honta kapakapaane laga, eeshara sinha ne donon haathon se kulavanta kaura kee kameeja kaa gheraa pakadaa aur jisa tarah bakare kee khaala utaarate hain, isee tarah usako utaara kar ek tarapha rakha diya, phir usane ghoora ke usake nange badana ko dekha aur jora se usake baajoo par chutakee bharate hue kaha, “kulavanta, kasama vaahaguru kee, badee karaaree aurat hai too|” kulavanta kaura apane baajoo par ubharate hue laala dhabbe ko dekhane lagee, “bada jaalima hai too eeshara sayyaan|” eeshara sinha apanee ghanee kaalee moonchhon men muskuraaya, “hone de aaja julma?” aur ye kaha kar usane aur julma dhaane shuroo kie| kulavanta kaura kaa baalaaee honta daanton tale kichakichaayaa| kaana kee lavon ko kaata, ubhare hue seene ko bhanbhoda, ubhare hue koolhon par aavaaja paidaa karane vaale chaante maare| gaalon ke munha bhara bhara ke bose liye| choosa choosa kar usakaa saaraa seenaa thookon se latheda diya| kulavanta kaura teja aancha par chadhee hui haandee kee tarah ubalane lagee| lekin eeshara sinha una saare heelon ke baapooraa badana aur mann khuda men haraarata paidaa na kar sakaa| jitane gura aur jitane daava use yaada the| sab ke sab usane pita jaane vaale pahalavaana kee tarah kaama karadie, par koee kaaragara na hua| kulavanta kaura ne jisake badana ke saare taara tana kar khudabakhuda baja rahe the| gaira jarooree chhedachhaada se tanga aakara kaha, “eeshara sayyaan, kaaphee phenta chukaa hai, ab pattaa phenka!” ye sunate hee eeshara sinha ke haath se jaise taasha kee saaree gaddee neeche phisala gaee| haanpata hua vo kulavanta kaura ke pahaloo men leta gaya aur usake maathe par sarda paseene ke lepa hone lage| kulavanta kaura ne use garamaane kee bahut koshisha kee| magar naakaama rahi, ab taka sab kuchha munha se kahe binaa hota raha tha lekin jab kulavanta kaura ke muntajira baamala aajaa ko sakhta naaummeedee hui to vo jhallaakara palanga se neeche utara gaee| saamane khoontee par chaadara padee thi, usako utaara kar usane jaldee jaldee odha kar aur nathune phulaa kar, biphare hue lahaje men kaha, “eeshara sayyaan, vo kaun haraamajaadee hai, jisake paasa too itane din raha kar aaya hai| jisane tujhe nichoda daalaa hai?” eeshara sinha palanga par letaa haanpata raha aur usane koee javaaba na diya| kulavanta kaura gusse se ubalane lagee, “main poochhatee hoon? kaun hai chaddoo... kaun hai vo ulphatee... kaun hai vo chora patta?” eeshara sinha ne thake hue lahaje men javaaba diya, “koee bhee naheen kulavanta, koee bhee naheen|” kulavanta kaura ne apane bhare hue koolhon par haath rakha kar ek pakkaa iraadaa ke saatha kaha, “eeshara sayyaan, main aaja jhoota-sacha jaana ke rahoongee... khaa vaahaguru jee kee kasama... kyaa usakee taha men koee aurat naheen?” eeshara sinha ne kuchha kahana chaaha, magar kulavanta kaura ne usakee ijaajata na dee| “kasama khaane se pahale socha le ki main saradaara nihaala sinha kee betee hoon... tikkaa botee kar doongee, agar too ne jhoota bola... le ab khaa vaahaguru jee kee kasama... kyaa isakee taha men koee aurat naheen?” eeshara sinha ne bade dukha ke saatha isbaata men sara hilaaya, kulavanta kaura bilkula divaanee hogaee| lapaka kar kone men se kripaana uthaaee, myaana ko kele ke chhilake kee tarah utaara kar ek tarapha phenkaa aur eeshara sinha par vaara kar diya| aana kee aana men lahoo ke phavvaare chhoota pade| kulavanta kaura kee isase bhee tasallee na hui to usane vahashee billiyon kee tarah eeshara sinha ke kesa nochane shuroo kar die| saatha hee saatha vo apanee naamaalooma sauta ko motee motee gaaliyaan detee raheen| eeshara sinha ne thodee dera ke baada kamazoree bharee binatee kee, “jaane de ab kulavanta! jaane de|” aavaaja men balaa kaa darda tha, kulavanta kaura peechhe hata gaee| khoona, eeshara sinha ke gale se uda uda kar usakee moonchhon par gira raha tha, usane apane kaanpatee honta khole aur kulavanta kaura kee tarapha shukrie aur gile kee milee julee nigaahon se dekha, “meree jaana! tuma ne bahut jaldee kee... lekin jo hua theeka hai|” kulavanta kaura kaa hasada phir bhadaka, “magar vo kaun hai tumhaaree maan?” lahoo eeshara sinha kee jabaana taka pahuncha gaya, jab usane usakaa jaayakaa chakhaa to usake badana par jhurajhuree see dauda gaee| “aur main... aur main... bheenee yaa chhah aadamiyon ko katla kar chukaa hoon... isee kripaana se...” kulavanta kaura ke dimaaga men sirpha doosaree aurat thi, “main poochhatee hoon, kaun hai vo haraamajaadee?” eeshara sinha kee aankhen dhundalaa rahi theen, ek halkee see chamaka unamen paidaa hui aur usane kulavanta kaura se kaha, “gaalee na de us bhadavee ko|” kulavanta chillaaee, “main poochhatee hoon, vo hai kaun?” eeshara sinha ke gale men aavaaja rundha gaee, “bataataa hoon|” ye kaha kar usane apanee gardana par haath pheraa aur us par apana jeetaa jeetaa khoona dekha kar muskuraaya, “insaana maan yaa bhee ek ajeeba cheeja hai|” kulavanta kaura usake javaaba kee muntajira thi| “eeshara sayyaan, too matalaba kee baat kar|” eeshara sinha kee muskuraahata usakee lahoo bharee moonchhon men aur zyada phaila gaee, “matalaba hee kee baat kar raha hoon... galaa chiraa hai maan yaa mera... ab dheere-dheere hee saaree baat bataaoongaa|” aur jab vo baat banaane lagaa to usake maathe par thande paseene ke lepa hone lage| “kulavanta! meree jaana... main tumhen naheen bata sakata, mere saatha kyaa hua? insaana kudiya bhee ek ajeeba cheeja hai... shehar men loota machee to sabakee tarah mainne bhee usamen hissaa liya... gahane-paate aur rupaye-paise jo bhee haath lage vo mainne tumhen de die... lekin ek baat tumhen na bataaee|” eeshara sinha ne ghaava men darda mahasoosa kiya aur karaahane lagaa| kulavanta kaura ne usakee tarapha dhyaana na dee aur badee nirdayata se poochha, “kaun see baat?” eeshara sinha ne moonchhon par jamate hue lahoo ko phoonka ke jariye se udaate hue kaha, “jisa makaana par mainne dhaavaa bola tha... usamen saata... usamen saata aadmi the... chhah mainne katla kar die... isee kripaana se jisa se too ne mujhe... chhoda use... suna... ek ladki thi bahut sundara... usako uthaa main apane saatha le aaya|” kulavanta kaura, chupa sunatee rahi| eeshara sinha ne ek baara phir phoonka maara ke moonchhon par se lahoo udaaya, “kulavanta jaanee, main tuma se kyaa kahoon, kitanee sundara thi... main use bhee maara daalata, par mainne kaha, naheen, eeshara sayyaan, kulavanta kaura ke to hara roja maje letaa hai, ye mevaa bhee chakha dekha|” kulavanta kaura ne sirpha is kadara kaha, “hoon...!” aur main use kandhe par daala kar chala diya... raaste men... kyaa kaha raha tha main?... haan raaste men... nahara kee patadee ke paasa, thohada kee jhaadiyon tale mainne use litaa diya... pahale sochaa ki phentoon, lekin phir khayal aaya ki naheen... ye kehte kehte eeshara sinha kee jabaana sookha gaee| kulavanta kaura ne thooka nigala kar apana galaa tara kiya aur poochha, “phir kyaa hua?” eeshara sinha ke galaa se mushkila se ye shabda nikale, “mainne... mainne pattaa phenka... lekin... lekin|” usakee aavaaja dooba gaee| kulavanta kaura ne use jhanjhoda, “phir kyaa hua?” eeshara sinha ne apanee banda hoti hui aankhen kholeen aur kulavanta kaura ke badana ke tarapha dekha, jisakee botee botee thiraka rahi thi| vo... vo maree hui thi... laasha thi... bilkula thandaa goshta... jaanee mujhe apana haath de... kulavanta kaura ne apana haath eeshara sinha ke haath par rakha, jo barpha se bhee zyada thandaa tha|
